# Contract Magic and True Names

Contract magic works by binding identity, obligation, consent, custody, and enforcement into metaphysical form. It is strongest when a contract can touch what a being truly is: name, office, debt, oath, bloodline, guilt, title, or freely given promise.

## True Names

A true name is not just a label. It is a usable handle on identity. Full custody of a true name can let a binder:

- summon,
- constrain,
- redirect,
- classify,
- assign office,
- impose debt,
- or force a being into a mantle.

The most dangerous contract systems compartmentalize names so no single custodian holds the whole key.

## Devils as Imposed Mantles

In the current cosmology, "devil" is not simply a species category. A devil can be any significant power forced into a contractual job-mantle by binding. The mantle reshapes behavior, duties, limits, and self-understanding.

This makes contract magic useful, terrifying, and spiritually dangerous.

## Core Components

- **Text:** clauses, loopholes, definitions, exclusions.
- **Seal:** the enforcement interface.
- **Collateral:** blood, name, land, relic, memory, debt, office, hostage, lineage.
- **Witness:** court, patron, spirit, state, saint, archive, or bound enforcer.
- **Breach logic:** what happens when the contract fails.

## Failure Modes

- loophole predation,
- inherited bondage,
- identity deformation,
- coerced consent,
- false witnesses,
- name theft,
- contract fossils that outlive their moral context,
- and legal systems that call domination "order."

## Moral Frame

Oath, law, and covenant can be good when ordered toward truth and justice. Predatory contract magic is evil when it uses law to erase personhood, sanctify enslavement, or make coercion look righteous.
