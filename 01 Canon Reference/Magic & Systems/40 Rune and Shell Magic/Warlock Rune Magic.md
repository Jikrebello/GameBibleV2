# Warlock Rune Magic

Warlock rune magic is engineered elemental magic. Warlocks use runes arranged into set scripts to draw elemental force from the [[Elemental Shell and the Four Planes]] and materialize it inside the [[Material Realm Overview]].

It is the basis of most formal war magic: battle casting, siege magic, elemental artillery, tactical shielding, breachwork, arena combat, and military spellcraft.

[[Harmonic Shellcraft]] reaches the same broad Elemental Shell through breath, interval, refrain, and tuned conductors. It is a parallel arcane discipline rather than a Warlock specialization. A Warlock writes a bounded instruction; a Bard sustains and changes one through performed harmonic structure.

## What Warlocks Are

In common speech, "warlock" can mean several related things:

- an elemental script-caster trained to draw from the Shell,
- a combat mage employed by a state, guild, war college, or mercenary company,
- a pact-agent extending an Astral patron's anchor network,
- or a dangerous outsider who uses forbidden scripts without institutional oversight.

This note defines the first meaning: the warlock as an elemental script-caster.

## Scripts

A script is a prepared runic formula that tells elemental force how to enter the Material Realm.

Scripts usually define:

- **source:** which Shell quadrant or blended expression is drawn,
- **shape:** bolt, wall, mist, lance, shield, burst, field, seal, engine,
- **duration:** instant, sustained, pulsed, anchored, delayed,
- **conductors:** wand, gauntlet, staff, ring, etched plate, siege frame,
- **safety limits:** vents, grounding marks, cut-off clauses, backlash channels.

The better the script, the less reality has to guess.

## Instruments

Warlocks rarely cast with bare hands. Instruments stabilize the draw:

- **wands:** precise, fast, limited capacity,
- **gauntlets:** modular combat casting and quick script rotation,
- **staves:** longer ritual reach and field control,
- **etched armor:** defensive scripts and elemental grounding,
- **siege frames:** large-scale scripts for walls, gates, trenches, ships, and artillery.

The Crucible of Vorthara is the clearest current example of institutionalized elemental war magic.

## Common Expressions

- **Fire:** flame walls, heat lances, explosive charges, forge-bursts, denial fields.
- **Ice/Water:** frost barriers, immobilizing ground, fog, pressure jets, cold preservation.
- **Air/Lightning:** signal bolts, storm arcs, speed bursts, sound disruption, shock weapons.
- **Earth/Acid:** stone reinforcement, corrosive breachwork, armor failure, dust veils, ground locks.

"Acid" is usually a war college term for corrosive water-earth-fire interactions, not a separate fifth element.

## Costs and Limits

Warlock magic is powerful because it is technical, not because it is free.

- Scripts require training, literacy, instruments, and rehearsal.
- Strong scripts consume prepared materials or damage instruments.
- Improvised scripts are dangerous.
- Battlefield emotion can distort precision.
- Overdraw causes Shell burn: burns, frostbite, nerve shock, mineralization, lung damage, memory disruption, or elemental contamination.
- Large workings can thin the local boundary and create persistent hazards.

## Moral and Political Risks

Warlock rune magic is not inherently evil, but it is easy to militarize.

Its dangers are:

- treating elemental force as merely technical,
- escalating warfare beyond ordinary restraint,
- selling battlefield power to the highest bidder,
- creating Shell scars that civilians inherit,
- and confusing power with authority.

## Institutions

Warlock traditions naturally produce:

- war colleges,
- military licensing,
- mercenary companies,
- duel arenas,
- siege bureaus,
- script registries,
- illegal script markets,
- counter-warlock orders,
- and religious debates over whether Shell force should be touched at all.
