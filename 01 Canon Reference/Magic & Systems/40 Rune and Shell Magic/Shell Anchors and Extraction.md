# Shell Anchors and Extraction

A permanent Shell anchor is a runic installation that holds a narrow Material reference frame against one of the four joined Shell planes. It does not open a road into an elemental civilization. It builds a temporary worksite inside otherwise abstract force.

These fourteen are the only installations able to support repeatable threshold delves in 1360 AR. Lesser taps draw force without creating a traversable platform.

## Platform Anatomy

Every permanent anchor requires:

- a Material datum that defines direction, duration, and human scale;
- an Astral ballast mass that prevents the platform drifting away from its world;
- a quadrant lattice that specifies what force may enter;
- return marks maintained independently of the extraction machinery;
- dump channels that ground excess force into ordinary matter;
- trained crews on both sides of the threshold.

The platform itself creates apparent terrain. A Water anchor develops channels and pressure basins. An Earth anchor builds ledges and weight wells. Air produces spans, charge fronts, and sound corridors. Fire produces radiant chambers, release slopes, and fuel-less heat fronts.

## Strategic Products

- **Forge-charge** can be transported only inside vented ceramic or rune-metal cells. A cracked cell produces heat without flame and may form a Fire Body.
- **Pressure brine** stores directed flow and cold. It powers pumps and cutters but can erase a poorly grounded boundary between connected vessels.
- **Stormglass** is glass or crystal prepared to retain charge, vibration, or signal. Broken stores attract Storm Kites.
- **Weight-seed** is compressed structural instruction held in mineral pellets. Badly placed seed can continue reinforcing until a road, mine, or room seals itself.

Mixed products are manufactured by joining stored outputs or operating a carefully opposed anchor. They are not evidence of additional elemental planes.

## Politics

Vorthara possesses the broadest technical knowledge. Ironvale converts a narrower anchor base into guns, armor, and fortress industry. The Qahirid court ties extraction to siege foundries and centralized military households. Thalmyria treats its anchors as strategic inheritance and keeps them under military, monastic, and provincial witness.

Other states buy output, lease crews, recover Atherian taps, or operate unsafe temporary draws. Sestaran finance reaches every bloc without giving the Concord direct control of all anchors.

## Failure and Responsibility

Extraction is a morally neutral technique. Failure is not automatic punishment from a sentient plane. Responsibility attaches to choices such as:

- overdraw despite instrument warnings;
- concealing a damaged return lattice;
- placing forced labor on the Shell side;
- venting into inhabited districts;
- converting a civilian anchor into an indiscriminate weapon;
- abandoning a platform while its ecology can still propagate.

## Hidden occupation — authorial history

During the 1320s–1340s, abandoned delvers and fugitive households extended unused maintenance chambers on the Shell side of Blackwall Compression Yard. Their concealed quarter survives by diverting capacity from that existing installation. Its separate Astral emergency tether is not a fifteenth Material anchor. The operators' discovery, the households' survival and any future intervention remain unresolved in 1360. See [[Constructed Shell Settlements#The hidden quarter at Blackwall]].
