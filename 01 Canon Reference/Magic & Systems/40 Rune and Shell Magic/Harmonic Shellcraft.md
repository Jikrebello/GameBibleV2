# Harmonic Shellcraft

Harmonic Shellcraft is an arcane method of drawing bounded elemental relations from the [[Elemental Shell and the Four Planes]]. It uses breath, interval, rhythm, repeated musical structure, and a prepared instrument where [[Warlock Rune Magic]] uses written runes and geometric scripts.

Both arts address the same broad source. They are not interchangeable techniques.

## Working Principle

A harmonic working has five parts:

1. **Conductor:** a tuned flute, horn, reed, drum, string, bell, or resonant chamber.
2. **Root relation:** the elemental family being addressed.
3. **Refrain:** the repeated instruction that sustains a field or bodily effect.
4. **Lead:** a person or object receiving a more exact part of the working.
5. **Cadence:** the closure that releases, changes, or safely ends the draw.

A bard does not create power through artistic feeling alone. Training, breath, physical acoustics, instrument preparation, and a coherent harmonic grammar tell the Shell what material relation to express.

## Common Expressions

| Harmonic form | Material expression |
|---|---|
| Fog refrain | suspended water, air pressure, cooling, concealment, and bounded shelter |
| Marching refrain | breath timing, air pressure, attention, and reduced waste in coordinated movement |
| Mending refrain | stabilization, warmth, circulation, and restoration of immediately recoverable bodily function |
| Elemental cadence | temporary group weapon relation using fire, ice, lightning, or corrosive mineral suspension |
| Counterphrase | destructive interference against a spell cadence, roar, signal, or conducted working |
| Weakness descant | reveals or amplifies an existing elemental or affliction vulnerability |
| Pressure note | short air-impact, stagger cone, or evasive burst used for self-defence |

Harmonic healing restores only the immediately recoverable portion of injury defined in [[Health Recovery Afflictions and Encumbrance]]. It cannot restore lasting Health loss, revive the dead, replace destroyed organs or limbs, cure disease, cleanse spiritual corruption, or imitate [[White Flame Doctrine|White Flame]].

## Resonance and Strain

Sustained refrains consume breath and Stamina. Clean coordination between the bard and allies creates **Resonance**: stored vibrational agreement in the instrument, surrounding field, and active cadence.

Resonance can be spent on:

- changing or layering refrains;
- placing an elemental relation across several allied weapons;
- interrupting a hostile working;
- issuing a brief battle compulsion;
- producing a strong closing crescendo.

Poor tuning, excessive volume, damaged conductors, contradictory refrains, and sustained overdraw cause:

- bleeding lips, damaged lungs, vertigo, deafness, nerve tremors, or burst vessels;
- cracked keys, warped reeds, split bells, or instrument failure;
- unstable fog, misplaced enchantment, or an uncontrolled pressure wave;
- persistent local vibration that interferes with later scripts.

Harmonic Shellcraft uses a different strain vocabulary from Arcane Burn, but it cannot evade the physical cost of drawing through the Shell.

## Battle Compulsion

Some air-and-pressure cadences entrain hearing, balance, attention, and immediate motor impulse. They may produce fascination, fear, sleep, emotional pressure, or one brief simple command.

Permitted battlefield examples include:

- drop the weapon;
- turn away;
- kneel;
- run;
- release the captive;
- jump from a vessel when that command is being used as lethal force.

The working is temporary and legible. It cannot:

- rewrite memory;
- manufacture affection, loyalty, belief, forgiveness, or consent;
- create a continuing order;
- make an unwilling agreement valid;
- command a target that cannot hear or physically understand the impulse;
- control constructs, slimes, ordinary corpse residue, or other beings without the relevant living sensory system.

A lethal command is legally and morally lethal force. Battlefield custom may tolerate it against active enemies. Use against civilians, dependants, captives, companions, or unwilling performers remains assault, coercion, or worse according to the act.

## Institutions

Harmonic traditions arise around:

- shipboard signal companies;
- marching and casualty bards;
- salon and theatre schools;
- naval fog crews;
- civic bell and warning offices;
- private travelling companies;
- counter-signal and anti-compulsion training.

Sermora has the most visible current tradition because canal acoustics, fleet coordination, privateering, processions, salons, and signal law reward portable ensembles. Other cultures possess related hymn, horn, drum, and bell arts without sharing Sermoran institutions.

## Moral Risks

- An audience is not the same thing as consent.
- A leader's ability to coordinate does not give ownership of performers.
- Saving lives through compulsion does not make all compulsion harmless.
- Artistic credit, instrument custody, wages, and the right to refuse remain real claims.
- A beautiful working may still enact a wicked purpose.
