# White Flame Doctrine

The White Flame is the Lord's coherence operating inside created reality through the authority of the Redeemer. It is not ordinary elemental fire, not Shell fire, and not a neutral magical substance.

## What It Does

The White Flame:

- stabilizes thin spaces,
- burns corruption without merely burning matter,
- strengthens soul-routing toward judgement and communion,
- exposes falsehood and spiritual deformation,
- sanctifies saints and relics,
- resists Idol mapping and predatory contract re-typing,
- and points creation toward Restoration.

## What It Is Not

- It is not a peer to warlock fire.
- It is not a technology anyone can master by technique alone.
- It is not morally neutral.
- It is not automatically obedient to Church politics.

## Human Access

Mortals encounter the White Flame through:

- saints,
- relics,
- true hymns and psalms,
- sacramental action,
- martyrdom,
- exorcism,
- sanctified places,
- and rare moments of direct divine intervention.

## Institutional Risk

Because the White Flame is real, institutions compete to control its public meaning. Churches can preserve doctrine, test sainthood, guard relics, and protect souls. They can also forge signs, politicize sanctity, suppress inconvenient saints, or confuse institutional victory with divine approval.

## Cultural Contrast

- **Eclessia:** tends to approach White Flame through institution, canon law, relic courts, saints, pilgrimage, proof, and public doctrine.
- **Thalmyria:** tends to approach White Flame through asceticism, suffering, mysticism, holy fire, imperial memory, and martial sanctification.

Both may be genuinely connected to the Redeemer. Both may also be tempted to possess what can only be served.
