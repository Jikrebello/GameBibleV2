# Thin Spaces

Thin spaces are places where the Material Realm no longer holds its ordinary boundaries cleanly. Another layer presses through: Faewild, Shadowlands, Astral drift, Shell force, or spiritual routing.

## What Causes Them

- old rituals,
- mass death,
- relic misuse,
- saintly or corrupt miracles,
- warlock overdraw,
- Shell leakage,
- patron anchors,
- Idol feeding networks,
- Menhir pacts,
- broken contracts,
- or historical wounds repeated in the same place.

## How They Feel

Thin spaces should feel locally lawful, not random. A place becomes strange according to the layer pressing through.

- **Faewild thinness:** seasons behave like courts; beasts remember oaths; paths demand etiquette.
- **Shadowlands thinness:** shame, hunger, violence, or desire becomes architecture.
- **Astral thinness:** maps fail, names rot, rooms reorder, debts become spatial.
- **Shell thinness:** fire, flood, stone, storm, or breath behaves with impossible purity.
- **Soul-route thinness:** the dead linger, judgement echoes, burial customs gain force.

## Story Rule

A thin space should reveal what is wrong, old, holy, hungry, unpaid, or unresolved in a place.

## Game Use

Thin spaces justify dungeons, anomalies, local mechanics, monster ecologies, relic sites, haunted roads, and political disputes over who gets to interpret the phenomenon.
