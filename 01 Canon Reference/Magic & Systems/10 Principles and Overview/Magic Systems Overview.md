# Magic Systems Overview

Magic in the World Egg is not one substance. It is a family of different access methods into different layers of reality. Each system has its own source, method, cost, institutions, and moral danger.

## Major Families

| System | Source | Method | Common Users | Primary Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[White Flame Doctrine]] | The Lord's coherence in created reality | sanctity, sainthood, relics, hymn-prayer, sacrament | Church, saints, relic-keepers, holy orders | presumption, false sanctity, political capture |
| [[Contract Magic and True Names]] | identity, oath, law, name, metaphysical obligation | contracts, seals, custody of names, enforcement clauses | Ahl-e-Ahd, banks, jurists, binders | enslavement, loophole predation, identity deformation |
| [[Warlock Rune Magic]] | [[Elemental Shell and the Four Planes]] | runic scripts, instruments, formulae, trained casting | warlocks, war colleges, siege magi, mercenaries | Shell burn, runaway elements, battlefield atrocity |
| [[Patrons and Astral Rent]] | Astral anchors and leased stability | pacts, rent, patronage, anchor extension | patrons, warlocks, debt-agents, occult clients | debt, repossession, identity dependency |
| Menhir/Druidic Magic | Faewild living law | pact, taboo, place, season, bloodline, rite | druids, pact clans, forest peoples | becoming local law's instrument |
| Idol/Shade Magic | Shadowlands predatory meanings | vice architecture, psychic feeding, cult patterning | shades, cults, corrupt courts, vice networks | possession by appetite, identity collapse |

## Design Rule

Every magic system should answer five questions:

1. What layer does it draw from?
2. What method gives access?
3. What does it cost?
4. What institution grows around it?
5. What kind of sin, abuse, or failure does it invite?

## Game Rule of Thumb

If a power is useful in war, someone has built a school, guild, office, heresy, black market, countermeasure, or legal regime around it.
