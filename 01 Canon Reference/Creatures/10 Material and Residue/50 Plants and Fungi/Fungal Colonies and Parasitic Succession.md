# Fungal Colonies and Parasitic Succession

Material fungi can coordinate through chemical signals, rootlike mycelial webs, and host behavior without possessing a soul or reflective mind. Their apparent planning is distributed biological response. [[Mnemonic Lichen]] and other engineered memory organisms remain crafted exceptions.

## Spore Colonies

Spore colonies combine irritant fruiting bodies, underground mycelium, and simple host-directed chemistry. Mature beds alter airflow, light, and animal behavior to spread spores.

- **Lifecycle:** spores establish in damp organic soil; the hidden web may persist for years between fruiting seasons.
- **Signs:** animals walking the same route before dying, colored dust on the lee side of objects, sweet or metallic taste, and growth aligned to airflow rather than light.
- **Threat:** hallucination, coughing, temporary blindness, compulsive movement toward humid ground, and sudden fruiting when a body falls.
- **Control:** masks, dry heat, changing airflow, digging beyond the visible edge, and burning only after containment.
- **Materials:** dyes, medicine, poison, fermentation cultures, and forensic evidence connecting distant chambers.

## Seed-Thralls

Seed-thralls arise when a parasitic fungus kills a living host, consumes it, and grows a mobile fruiting body through the remaining frame. The human soul, if the host was human, departs at death. The moving body is not the victim and contains no trapped person.

- **Lifecycle:** infection begins in lungs or wounds; after death, fibrous cords operate joints long enough to reach a humid fruiting site.
- **Signs:** fine roots behind teeth or eyelids, movements optimized for balance rather than habit, no response to a private memory, and spores released when struck.
- **Threat:** approach familiar shelter, grapple, burst under pressure, and seed enclosed rooms. It does not plan beyond chemical cues.
- **Control:** quarantine, dry heat, respirators, removing bodies from humid ground, and destroying the hidden nursery rather than only walkers.
- **Materials:** medicinal compounds before fruiting; afterward the body is contamination, not salvage.

## Intelligence Boundary

The family uses `intelligence: cunning` because a large colony can redirect hosts, close a route with fruiting bodies, and respond to repeated disturbance. It has no moral agency, language, culture, or unified self. A fungus that preserves memories, negotiates, or intentionally conceals itself requires another continuity and its own dossier.

## Geography

Warm northern caves support the fastest succession. Central fens and temperate ruins support seasonal colonies. In the southern cold belt, spores remain dormant in frozen bodies and become dangerous when halls, mines, or siege camps thaw them indoors.

## Regional Names

Walking bloom, grave flower, cave breath, widow dust, and seed-dead describe local symptoms rather than formal species. Calling a seed-thrall “undead” is understandable folk usage but metaphysically inaccurate.

## Story Hooks

- thawing Grobi corpses seed a southern winter hall;
- two mines accuse one another of sabotage while sharing the same underground mycelium;
- a seed-thrall walks home because warmth and humidity, not memory, draw it there;
- healers need a living sample before the next fruiting cycle;
- a colony demonstrates genuine memory, exposing an engineered organism hidden beneath natural infection.
