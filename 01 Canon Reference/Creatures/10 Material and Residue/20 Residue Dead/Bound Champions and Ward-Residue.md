# Bound Champions and Ward-Residue

Martial residue forms where training, command, defensive magic, and repeated violence leave a role strong enough to continue after the human soul has departed. **Bound champions** copy a fighter through regalia or battlefield duty. **Wall-wardens** are decayed defensive systems animated by residue. Neither contains a human soul unless a separate soulbinding is proven.

## Bound Champions

A bound champion is persona or routine residue anchored to armor, weapon, banner, mount gear, oath-stone, or the geometry of a battlefield.

- **Origin:** a final defense, repeated ceremonial watch, atrocity, oath, or deliberate attempt to preserve a warrior's skill.
- **Signs:** empty armor taking weight, an obsolete salute, damage appearing only near the remembered wound, and a weapon returning to one position after removal.
- **Threat:** use copied expertise, challenge the strongest visible opponent, command reflex dead, and reform around surviving regalia.
- **Limits:** cannot invent tactics outside its experience unless it becomes autonomous; often accepts a valid relief, surrender, or completed order.
- **Defeat:** destroy or lawfully retire the anchor, complete the duty, expose a false order, or disperse the residue after preserving testimony.
- **Materials:** historical arms and banners remain culturally owned objects rather than monster parts.

A genuine human soul imprisoned in armor uses `bound-human-soul` and requires release, not this classification.

## Wall-Wardens

Wall-wardens arise when old defensive scripts absorb generations of alarm, drill, and death until they continue after their command structure fails. Masonry, doors, statues, armor racks, chains, and murder-holes may act as one distributed guardian.

- **Origin:** Atherian undercrofts, border forts, sealed arsenals, tomb gates, and repeatedly besieged walls.
- **Signs:** repairs made from the wrong historical period, hinges moving before a command, dust avoiding one patrol line, and challenge words answered by architecture.
- **Threat:** divide intruders, seal exits, animate weapons, redirect corridors, and identify everyone as the last recorded enemy.
- **Limits:** literal boundary, obsolete recognition rules, damaged signal network, and inability to distinguish legitimate succession without an accepted token.
- **Defeat:** restore command, present valid relief, sever signal stones, dismantle the anchor geometry, or destroy enough distributed bodies that the pattern cannot close.
- **Materials:** command plaques, ward-metal, signal stone, and architectural evidence.

## Intelligence and Moral Agency

Most martial residue has limited agency inside a role. It can choose a tactic or interpret an order but cannot freely abandon the identity organizing it. A champion that learns, moves anchors, and pursues private ends may be [[Self-Anchored Dead|self-anchored]].

## Regional Names

Empty knight, last captain, banner-dead, gate mind, old wall, and iron watch are common terms. “Graveknight” is creator shorthand only when the being is residue rather than a held soul, vampire, or Giant office-dead.

## Story Hooks

- a fort's wall-warden recognizes the enemy's modern descendants as its lawful garrison;
- a champion can be defeated only by proving that its last command was forged;
- salvagers remove the banner-anchor and cause the residue to occupy their wagon;
- an army wants to reactivate a guardian whose old recognition rules include civilians as enemies;
- a soulbound prisoner hides among ordinary martial residue and is nearly destroyed with it.
