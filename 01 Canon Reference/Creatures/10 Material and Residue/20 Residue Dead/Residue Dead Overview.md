# Residue Dead Overview

**Residue dead are what death leaves capable of moving after the human soul has departed.** They arise when memory, emotion, bodily habit, magical saturation, or a place-anchor organizes shed residue into repetition. They do not prove failed judgement and are not automatically the deceased returned.

## The Four Grades

| Grade | Capacity | Typical forms | Moral agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reflex** | Responds to heat, motion, sound, blood, or intrusion | rattle-bones, corpse-walkers | None |
| **Routine** | Repeats learned work, patrol, grief, or defense | drowned walkers, wall-wardens | None or negligible |
| **Persona echo** | Speaks and reasons inside copied memory and one anchoring role | barrow-shades, Keeners, Hollow Sentries | Limited |
| **Autonomous residue-born mind** | Learns beyond the original pattern and recognizes itself as a new being | self-anchored dead; mature ghouls through a separate Idol ecology | Full where genuine choice exists |

Advancing in grade does not recover the routed soul. A persona echo with perfect memories remains an echo. An autonomous residue mind is a newly organized being, not the dead human awakening late.

## What Creates Residue Dead

- burial failure, exposed remains, and mass death;
- repeated violence or grief saturating one place;
- damaged defensive wards and funerary mechanisms;
- deliberate grave-script or necromantic organization;
- thin-space pressure copying roles or memories;
- objects, names, or routines that provide an unusually strong anchor.

Residue is common enough for sexton-work and delving. A specific Idol, patron, Undertow route, or cosmic breach should be added only when evidence supports it.

## Classification Questions

1. **Substrate:** corpse, place, object, name, role, or repeated act.
2. **Anchor:** what lets the pattern remain coherent.
3. **Grade:** reflex, routine, persona, or autonomous.
4. **Compulsion:** what action the pattern returns to.
5. **Growth:** whether it can learn beyond copied memory.
6. **Release:** destruction, completion, truth, reburial, deconsecration, or removal of an imposed script.

## Genuine Human Souls

A manifestation is treated as a real surviving human person only when evidence establishes `retained-human-soul` or `bound-human-soul`. Such cases require their own dossiers and release conditions. Memory, speech, emotion, family recognition, and apparent pain are evidence worth investigating but are not sufficient by themselves.

Destroying ordinary residue does not kill the deceased. It may still be morally serious because remains, testimony, community memory, and a new autonomous intelligence can matter.

## Burial and Law

Eclessian and Thalmyric churches agree that ordinary residue is not the soul. Sextons disperse reflex dead, clergy settle stronger anchors, and courts investigate deliberate animation as desecration and public danger. Folk and Ättar practices describe the distinction differently but often recognize the same practical divide between a dead kinsperson and a memory wearing their path.
