# Anchored Hauntings

Anchored hauntings are persona-grade residue organized around a place, event, role, or repeated emotion. They can speak, recognize people, and solve problems inside the copied pattern. They are still not the routed deceased.

Their apparent personality deserves careful treatment. An echo can suffer within its pattern, provide truthful testimony, misremember, manipulate, or ask for release. Limited agency means the pattern can choose among available actions without becoming capable of an unrestricted new life.

## Barrow-Shades

Barrow-shades are the cold standing echoes of old interments and memorial places.

- **Anchor:** burial stone, grave boundary, named object, or repeated watch.
- **Signs:** cold aligned to masonry, a figure visible only from the graveward side, old challenge phrases, and offerings moved into obsolete arrangements.
- **Threat:** enforce a forgotten boundary, drain warmth, call reflex dead, and make intruders feel the weight or posture of burial.
- **Release:** restore a name, repair or lawfully move the boundary, complete the watch, or disperse the residue after evidence is preserved.
- **Use:** historical witness, barrow guardian, inheritance obstacle, and proof that a grave was disturbed.

## Keeners

Keeners are grief echoes formed when public mourning, suppressed guilt, or repeated lament becomes one voice wearing a familiar shape.

- **Anchor:** anniversary, courtroom, death-room, execution place, lament, or community lie.
- **Signs:** the same phrase spoken by unrelated mourners, a cry heard only after someone denies the death, and glass or breath vibrating without wind.
- **Threat:** amplify grief into panic, force testimony, deafen, isolate a named target, and strengthen when a crowd repeats the lie that formed it.
- **Release:** establish the truth, complete a proper lament, stop ritual repetition, or disperse the sound-anchor. Killing the visible shape only silences one appearance.
- **Use:** wrongful-death mystery, public guilt, false saint testimony, and dangerous evidence.

## House Echoes

House echoes arise from domestic routine, abuse, hospitality, craft, or secrecy repeated until rooms remember the role better than later occupants do.

- **Anchor:** threshold, hearth, family object, work rhythm, hidden room, or assigned household role.
- **Signs:** doors opening for a dead schedule, place settings returning, tools completing one task, and voices unable to discuss anything outside the house's remembered life.
- **Threat:** assign visitors roles, separate “family” from “stranger,” repeat punishment, lock routes, and animate ordinary objects through residue.
- **Release:** break or fulfill the routine, name concealed harm, remove the anchoring object, alter the architecture, or close the house after preserving testimony.
- **Use:** haunted home, workshop accident, false inheritance claimant, and domestic history made active.

## Combat and Conversation

Silver, consecration, and White Flame can disrupt manifested residue, but observation usually identifies a cheaper and more complete answer. A haunting that speaks outside its role, learns across visits, or freely abandons its anchor may be [[Self-Anchored Dead|an autonomous residue mind]] or a different continuity entirely.

## Regional Names

Cold guest, stone widow, bench-cry, old tenant, after-house, and wake voice are local names. “Ghost” remains an everyday word covering residue, bound souls, counterfeits, and things witnesses never understood.

## Story Hooks

- a barrow-shade guards a boundary no current map records;
- a Keener at a trial strengthens whenever the true beneficiary lies;
- a house echo treats a visiting child as the missing heir;
- a family recognizes private memories and assumes the soul has returned;
- releasing a haunting destroys the only surviving testimony to a public crime.
