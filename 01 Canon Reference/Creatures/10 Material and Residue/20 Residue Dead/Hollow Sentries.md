# Hollow Sentries

Hollow Sentries are after-images produced by [[The Hollow Muster]]. They combine a dead soldier's final station with memories copied from a living person, allowing the thin space to place a convincing guard into the present while the original repeats the old duty below.

They are not whole returned souls and should not be treated as a reproducible people.

## Encounter Role

Hollow Sentries serve as infiltrators, patrols, social mysteries, command hazards, and combatants who can often be ended by breaking the assigned role rather than destroying the image.

## Signs

- correct uniform with an obsolete stitch, badge, or method of wear;
- a patrol repeated at identical intervals despite changed conditions;
- use of an old challenge-and-answer sequence;
- recognition of dead officers but confusion around current family;
- a wound visible only near the place or stimulus of death;
- inability to describe unobserved time between memorized actions;
- equipment that resists being left behind or exchanged;
- no spontaneous opinion outside the copied person's known habits.

## Behavior and Tactics

- obey the final order attached to the station;
- attempt detention, alarm, or reinforcement before killing;
- use the keep's passages and procedures as remembered in 1338;
- exchange apparent position with another echo when a bell sounds;
- become more violent when faced with contradictory uniforms or commands;
- protect the confession register and cracked bell as parts of their own continuity.

They can speak, plan within a duty, and display emotion contained in copied memory. They cannot grow beyond the available pattern while anchored.

## Breaking a Sentry

Useful methods include:

- give a present order from a living commander accepted by the sentry's role;
- confront it with the rescued living original;
- remove the current name or pattern object from the register;
- complete the interrupted final duty;
- expose the forged no-quarter order;
- lead it beyond the geometry where its post exists;
- silence or correct the bell signal.

Destroying an image prevents immediate action but does not release the original if the name remains anchored.

## Variants

- **Copied Sentry:** current face and routine fitted to an old post.
- **Broken Veteran:** largely 1338 memory, visibly wounded, capable of testimony between repetitions.
- **Muster Hound:** war dog and handler memory compressed into one pursuit shape.
- **Prepared Double:** deliberately supplied with broad memories, as with Sabine Krail's escape image.
- **Officer Echo:** able to issue orders that temporarily bind lesser sentries.

## Ecology and Distribution

Hollow Sentries belong specifically to Hollow Keep's rare combination of mass death, contradictory accepted orders, a detailed identity register, and repeated military duty.

Similar after-images may arise at other thin spaces, but they should reflect their own wound rather than duplicate this exact creature family automatically.

## Materials and Faction Use

- name tabs and pattern objects can identify a living original;
- old uniforms retain testimony about the massacre but decay when removed;
- Lantern investigators seek controlled release and proof of Sabine's abuse;
- hardline commanders want reliable replacement guards;
- Freeflame witnesses fear the Church will classify echoes as relic property;
- Accord Wardens prioritize finding the living original before destroying a double.

## Regional Names

Hollow Keep survivors call them **Hollow Sentries**. Brauvalian testimony also uses **second guards** and **bell doubles**; Freeflame pamphlets prefer **muster echoes** to avoid implying that a human soul has returned.

## Quest Uses

- a guard finishes every shift but has not gone home in weeks;
- two forts report the same sentry on the same night;
- an echo asks the party to complete an order that was itself a lie;
- a copied officer commands living troops who do not know their commander is below;
- destroying a haunted post leaves its original victim trapped in a repetition elsewhere;
- Sabine sells a prepared double to someone who wants a perfect alibi.
