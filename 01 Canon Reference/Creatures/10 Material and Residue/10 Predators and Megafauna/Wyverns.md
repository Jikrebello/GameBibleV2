# Wyverns

Wyverns are large two-legged flying predators with hooked wings, a balancing tail, and venom delivered through a tail spur. They are biological animals. They are neither [[Drakes Overview|drakes]] nor lesser [[True Dragons|true dragons]], despite centuries of heraldry placing all three beneath one word.

## Ecology and Lifecycle

Wyverns nest on cliffs that combine rising air, an inaccessible ledge, and nearby herd or sea-bird prey. A pair usually raises one surviving juvenile every few years. Coastal animals take fish, seals, and birds; inland animals strike goats, horses, and young great-horns. They soar whenever possible and become clumsy in dense forest or enclosed ruins.

Egg theft, carcass disposal, and roadbuilding can shift a wyvern's hunting circuit toward settlements. Most “sudden” attacks follow a human change to prey or nesting ground.

## Signs

- paired hind-foot tracks with long wing-knuckle marks after landing;
- bones dropped and broken on exposed stone;
- pale venom scoring around a puncture rather than a bite;
- livestock refusing open slopes during the warmest rising winds;
- white streaks beneath a cliff ledge and a sour carrion smell above it.

## Threat Pattern

Wyverns circle beyond easy missile range, strike mounts or isolated targets, and climb before the ground can close. A nesting parent uses false passes to draw threats away from the ledge. It does not duel for pride. Nets, covered approaches, crosswinds, narrow terrain, and threatening the escape route are more useful than trying to match it in open air.

## Weaknesses and Permanent Defeat

Wing membranes tear easily once pinned, and the animal cannot launch from level ground while heavily burdened. Antivenom must be matched to region; northern cliff strains and warm-strait strains differ enough to make imported remedies unreliable. Death is permanent and leaves no heart-gem, court-law, or soul-route event.

## Materials and Human Use

Venom supports medicine, hunting poisons, and assassination. Wing leather is light but degrades in wet weather. Military attempts to breed riding wyverns produce more dead handlers than cavalry; a trained juvenile remains an apex animal responding to food, fear, and familiar routine.

## Regional Names

- **hook-tail** along the Sestaran and Saltborn coasts;
- **goat-shadow** in Vlastravan and Caldrian heights;
- **sun-spear** on northern desert escarpments;
- **wyvern** in creator-facing and Eclessian military reference.

## Story Hooks

- a new road cuts directly below the only surviving nest in a mountain district;
- an alchemist pays for live venom while concealing that the antidote is untested;
- a wyvern carries an expedition satchel to its ledge with the rest of the body;
- soldiers blame a wyvern for carefully selected attacks committed by a human rider;
- fleeing animals reveal a landslide before the threatened village believes the scouts.
