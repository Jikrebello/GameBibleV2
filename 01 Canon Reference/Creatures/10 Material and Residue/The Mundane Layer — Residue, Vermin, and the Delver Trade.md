# The Mundane Layer - Residue, Vermin, and the Delver Trade

The Material Realm is a grounded world before it is a supernatural one. Most dangerous journeys involve weather, hunger, bad roads, human violence, ordinary animals, disease, structural collapse, and a limited set of native wonders. The forms in this catalogue give ruins and frontiers a dependable adventure ecology without making every encounter a cosmic revelation.

## Encounter Pyramid

- **Mundane:** animals, vermin, human opponents, ordinary residue, terrain, disease, and common crafted hazards. This is the broad base of play.
- **Aligned:** a layer, ancient system, or supernatural faction has materially shaped the danger, but its cause remains local and comprehensible.
- **Cosmological:** a creature or event can alter a region, soul-route, court, or plane-level condition. Such encounters should remain rare enough to matter.

An old crypt can contain rats, bad air, a corpse-walker, and stolen goods without concealing an Idol. A forest road can be threatened by a warg pack because food is scarce. Ordinary causes are not lesser worldbuilding; they are what let true ruptures feel like ruptures.

## Material Continuity

Living Material creatures use `biological-pattern`. They are organisms with bodies, lifecycles, needs, and ecologies. Intelligence and moral agency vary by creature, but none naturally enters the human soul-road.

Ordinary bears, boars, wolves, crocodilians, sharks, large cats, and similar animals remain ordinary fauna. They need no monster dossier unless a region, condition, or story makes a particular population unusual.

## Residue Principle

Human death separates three things that folklore often confuses:

1. the body;
2. memory and emotional residue left in body, place, object, or witness;
3. the genuine human soul, which ordinarily routes toward [[The Vestibule of Weighing]].

Residue can animate without retrieving the person who died. A familiar voice, habit, face, or memory is not proof that the deceased remains. Only `retained-human-soul` or `bound-human-soul` establishes the genuine human person as still present.

### Four Residue Grades

- **Reflex:** one stimulus produces one response, such as grasping, pacing, striking, or reliving a fall.
- **Repeated routine:** a longer pattern repeats around a route, duty, household rhythm, or mode of death.
- **Persona echo:** memory and manner create a convincing imitation capable of limited conversation and adaptation. It is still not the deceased.
- **Autonomous residue-born mind:** residue organizes into a new enduring intelligence. It may possess moral agency, but it is a new being rather than the return of the original human soul.

Ending residue means dispersing, settling, exhausting, or severing its anchor. This does not murder the routed dead. Burial, truthful witness, repaired architecture, consecration, and removal of repeated stimuli often matter as much as force.

## Geographic Distribution

The known world lies in the southern hemisphere. Moving south means colder forests, taiga, frozen rivers, permafrost, and increasingly severe seasonal scarcity.

- Frostmaws, cold-adapted wargs, and some Harridan populations belong chiefly to the southern cold belt.
- Great-Horns occupy cool northeastern Tarkhan grasslands and high continental steppe; elevation and winter exposure explain that range without placing them in the deep southern permafrost.
- Road-Borers and Stonebacks range through temperate construction corridors, uplands, and quarry country.
- Marsh-Jaws, Pitcher Maws, large reptilian fauna, and the warmest fungal successions become common toward northern wetlands and warmer river systems.
- Sea Serpents, Great Cephalopods, and Reefbacks follow currents, shelves, spawning grounds, and shipping rather than a simple north-south band.
- Residue follows human death, failed rites, architecture, and memory pressure rather than climate, though frozen ground can preserve an anchor for centuries.

Regional names should make these creatures feel native. The underlying family remains stable even when one coast calls a Reefback a chapel-isle and another calls it a breathing shoal.

## Honest Dungeon Ecology

Common delving sites include barrow-fields, Atherian undercrofts, Giant ruins, abandoned mines, plague galleries, collapsed siegeworks, failed colonies, drained canals, and forts reused by several regimes. Their populations arise in layers:

- ordinary animals and vermin enter through food, warmth, water, and shelter;
- human occupants, bandits, squatters, and salvage crews use accessible rooms;
- residue repeats death, duty, or habitation around surviving anchors;
- plants, fungi, and natural slimes consume what the site provides;
- genuinely aligned or cosmological dangers appear only where the site's history supports them.

This layering lets an expedition read a place. Corpse-beetles imply carrion. Fresh wall scratches imply a large living animal. Repeated clean footprints with no return may imply routine residue. An impossible season or altered law points beyond the Material catalogue.

## Delver Trade

Barrow-runners, tomb-sweeps, mine-clearers, salvage crews, sextons, ratters, ward-breakers, and ruin guides form a low-status but necessary trade. Some work under ducal writ, university commission, church license, town bounty, or landholder charter. Others work without permission and sell what they can carry.

Competent crews bring rope, wedges, lamps, masks, chalk, poles, salt, burial cloth, simple wards, tools for shoring, and region-specific antidotes before they bring exotic relics. They learn signs, retreat routes, ownership customs, and the difference between a dangerous echo and a held human soul.

Common disputes concern salvage share, disturbed graves, concealed contamination, land rights, unsafe commissions, and artifacts that should never enter ordinary trade. These are human problems around monsters, not proof that every expedition belongs to a cosmic conspiracy.

## Deferred Ecologies

This wave deliberately excludes forms whose origin belongs elsewhere:

- Menhir plants and mythic court-fauna;
- alchemical and Vortharan waste-forms;
- Shell seepage and bound elementals;
- Astral consumption organisms;
- black-mud anti-coherence;
- engineered Giant and Nythalum fungi.

Those families should remain in their native origin sections even when encountered in a Material ruin.
