# Flensed Devotees

Flensed Devotees are damned human souls who made suffering into permission, proof, or worship. Some inflicted pain to strip others of identity. Others treated their own endurance as authority over everyone who had not suffered as they had.

Their manifestations appear anatomically opened without dying: names, loyalties, and remembered relationships hang from the body like removed skins, leaving one chosen devotion exposed at the center.

## Refusal and Signs

- **Refusal:** "Pain has made me pure, so whatever pain serves me is justified."
- **Signs:** scars appearing in ritual order, victims forgetting relationships before facts, instruments warm without use, and crowds ranking testimony by suffering rather than truth.
- **Anchor:** a torture instrument, cult discipline, prison custom, spectacle, or institution that continues to call mutilation purification.

## Threat Pattern

Flensed Devotees transfer pain without transferring injury, peel names from speech, make victims obey whichever identity remains, and turn inflicted wounds into temporary armor. They seek witnesses because unobserved suffering cannot support their claimed authority.

They possess full moral agency. A history of being tortured may explain their formation but does not excuse choosing to reproduce torture as doctrine.

## Weaknesses and Permanent Defeat

- restore a stripped name or relationship in the Devotee's presence;
- refuse the claim that suffering grants moral authority while still acknowledging the suffering as real;
- destroy the local instrument and end its continuing practice;
- separate the manifestation from an audience that validates it;
- break the manifested body after the anchor has been exposed.

The human soul returns to Hell. It is neither annihilated nor redeemed by defeat.

## Materials and Regional Names

Flensed skins vanish unless they were Material artifacts before manifestation. Preserved instruments may reveal names and injuries, making them evidence too important to destroy casually.

Names include flensed devotee, red saint, skinless master, pain father, opened witness, and the pure wound.

## Story Hooks

- A prison's reform fails because its ceremonial lash still anchors a Flensed Devotee.
- A healer can return stolen names but must experience the pain attached to each one.
- A cult's apparent victim is the damned founder using their own suffering to command descendants.
- Destroying an instrument would end attacks but conceal generations of official torture.
- A Devil mantle has been imposed on a Flensed Devotee, and breaking the office reveals a still-hostile bearer.
