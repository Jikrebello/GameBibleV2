# Crowned Refusers and Hollow Prophets

These damned forms turn authority and revelation into instruments of completed refusal. They remain human souls. Their rank reflects the number of living systems that still repeat their rejection, not a stable infernal nobility.

## Crowned Refusers

Crowned Refusers are rulers, patriarchs, judges, commanders, and household tyrants who treated office as ownership of other persons and refused every authority above their own will.

- **Refusal:** "What I ruled became mine, and no judgement can stand over me."
- **Signs:** empty seats commanding obedience, titles surviving the institutions that granted them, servants repeating orders in sleep, and rooms becoming physically lower than a throne.
- **Threat:** impose obsolete ranks, convert protection into ownership, make subordinates carry wounds meant for the ruler, and manifest dead institutions as temporary courts.
- **Anchor:** an unrelinquished title, captive regalia, continuing oath of personal obedience, or community that still benefits from the tyranny.
- **Defeat:** invalidate the local claim, release living and dead subjects, destroy or lawfully transfer the regalia, and break the manifestation. Final judgement remains unchanged.

## Hollow Prophets

Hollow Prophets used genuine pain, partial truth, or apparent revelation to teach that communion, repentance, and moral obligation were traps. They appear as eloquent human forms whose chest and mouth open onto wet darkness.

- **Refusal:** "The wound proves that goodness is false and separation is wisdom."
- **Signs:** a doctrine explaining every counterexample as persecution, true testimony stripped of its conclusion, followers losing the ability to name any good they did not author, and sermons heard from empty mouths.
- **Threat:** reframe rescue as domination, turn confession into despair, imitate signs without their source, and make a crowd repeat one conclusion in different words.
- **Anchor:** a text, martyr legend, ritual platform, broadcast instrument, or unresolved injustice being used to sustain the false conclusion.
- **Defeat:** preserve the true grievance while exposing the false metaphysical answer, restore suppressed testimony, sever the anchor, and destroy the manifestation.

## Limits and Materials

Neither form can change divine judgement, create revelation, or own a human soul. Their authority operates through consent, fear, inherited structure, and damaged routes.

Regalia and texts left behind are contaminated human artifacts rather than pieces of the soul. They can be evidence, relics of crime, or renewed anchors.

Regional names include drowned king, empty crown, black bishop, hollow prophet, mouth of the mire, and throne without a country.

## Story Hooks

- A deposed tyrant's crown begins issuing orders through everyone who wears it.
- A Hollow Prophet is correct about a church crime and deliberately wrong about what the crime proves.
- Destroying a throne would end a manifestation but erase evidence needed by its victims.
- A Crowned Refuser offers military order against a worse invasion in exchange for renewed obedience.
- Two rival sects unknowingly sustain the same Hollow Prophet by preserving opposite halves of its lie.
