# Mirebound and Grudge-Bearers

These are damned human souls whose completed refusal takes a repeatable manifestation in Hell and at compatible Material anchors. Their bodies are expressions of judgement and chosen fixation, not new biological species.

## Mirebound

Mirebound are people who made refusal passive: they would not act, witness, repent, protect, or choose while continuing to consume the shelter created by others. Their manifestations are human outlines sunk to the chest in black mud, dragging a household, office, or crowd's accumulated debris behind them.

- **Refusal:** "Nothing could have been done, and therefore nothing was required of me."
- **Signs:** wet footprints that begin at a place of duty, tools too heavy only when needed, repeated claims that no choice exists, and doors slowly sinking into sound floors.
- **Threat:** increase weight, spread exhaustion, make rescue feel futile, and bind victims together so each person's delay burdens the others.
- **Anchor:** concealed abandonment, denied witness, or a site where beneficiaries still profit from the Mirebound person's refusal.
- **Defeat:** expose the abandoned duty, complete what can still be completed, sever the local anchor, and break the manifestation. The soul returns to Hell; it is not redeemed.

## Grudge-Bearers

Grudge-Bearers preserve a real or imagined injury as permission to reject every good not centered upon revenge. Their forms are lean carriers bent beneath weapons, letters, trophies, and names that grow heavier whenever forgiveness or proportion is mentioned.

- **Refusal:** "Because I was wronged, no claim upon me remains."
- **Signs:** old injuries appearing on uninvolved people, lists that add names by association, weapons pulling toward descendants, and mud hardening around memorials.
- **Threat:** transfer remembered pain, mark entire groups as substitutes, compel pursuit, and turn truthful testimony into fuel by stripping it of proportion.
- **Anchor:** an inherited feud, falsified memorial, unburied victim, or institution using grievance to authorize new harm.
- **Defeat:** distinguish the original wrong from later substitutes, restore truthful memory, deny the manifestation a replacement victim, and break its body or name-route.

## Continuity and Remains

Both forms retain genuine human souls and full moral agency. Their suffering does not make their predation righteous. No harvestable corpse remains after manifestation; mud, weapons, and written accusations collapse into temporary residue or return to their Material source.

Regional names include mirebound, the seated dead, grudge-bearer, injury knight, wet claimant, and the one who adds names.

## Story Hooks

- A Mirebound manifestation makes every rescue from a collapsed mine physically harder until an old abandonment is admitted.
- A Grudge-Bearer marks the descendants of both murderer and victim as one enemy line.
- A cult mistakes the suffering of a damned soul for proof that its accusations are holy.
- Breaking a manifestation frees a town from haunting but returns the damned person to a greater Hell office.
- The only surviving witness is someone who profited from refusing to act.
