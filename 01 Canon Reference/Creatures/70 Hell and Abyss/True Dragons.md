# True Dragons

True dragons are unique Abyssal catastrophes. They are not the adult form of a drake, not a color-coded species, and not ordinary fauna from another plane. Each dragon is a particular refusal or wound given sovereign appetite and physical consequence.

## Drake and Dragon

[[Drakes Overview|Drakes]] are Menhir court-fauna with recognizable lineages, ecologies, reproduction, and heart-gems. A true dragon enters through a layered breach, distorts the laws around it, and may make a region disappear from reliable history. Rural speech often uses the same word for both. The error is understandable and lethal.

## Design Requirements

Every true dragon requires a dedicated dossier answering:

- What refusal or wound gives it continuity?
- What ritual, history, or geography lets it manifest?
- What does its presence make physically or morally possible?
- Which ordinary signs precede full arrival?
- Which anchors must be broken to prevent recurrence?
- What lies does it teach those who serve it?

True dragons may appear serpentine, winged, many-limbed, aquatic, skeletal, radiant, or almost human. Appearance does not create a family. Their powers arise from the wound they embody.

## Defeat

Destroying a manifested body can interrupt a dragon. Permanent victory requires closing or transforming the Abyssal contradiction that permits it to exist. This normally demands historical truth, physical action, spiritual resistance, and the destruction of several anchors rather than one weapon or damage type.
