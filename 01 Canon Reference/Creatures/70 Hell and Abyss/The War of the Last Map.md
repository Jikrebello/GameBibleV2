# The War of the Last Map

The War of the Last Map is the recurring conflict between powers that require Hell to remain administratively usable and the Abyssal wounds that dissolve the distinctions administration requires. It is the World Egg's structural analogue to an endless infernal war, but it is neither law against chaos nor a struggle between two fiendish species.

## Cause

Mapped dominions require named prisoners, stable borders, repeatable offices, enforceable contracts, roads with destinations, and causes with recognizable effects. Abyssal wounds break or contradict those things. A map-lord can exploit and imprison only while persons, places, commands, and consequences remain distinguishable.

Damned rulers therefore contain wounds to preserve their own dominions. They then bind, measure, divide, and direct those wounds into rival territory. A weaponized contradiction destroys one enemy road while opening another wound. An impossible command creates the prisoners used to mark a replacement border. A devil office survives the ruler who commissioned it and continues a campaign whose original objective no longer exists.

The war continually recreates what it claims to contain.

## Why It Cannot End Infernally

Containment is not healing. Closing an Abyssal wound ordinarily requires some combination of truthful acknowledgement, restored names, restitution, surrendered authority, released captives, repaired relationships, and refusal of the lie that opened it.

Mapped-Hell rulers reject those remedies because the same acts would invalidate their titles, empty their prisons, release their roads, and expose the refusals sustaining their dominions. They prefer permanent mobilization to a victory that would require relinquishment. Abyssal wounds, for their part, possess no shared diplomacy, government, or peace condition.

No side is secretly good or necessary to Creation. Infernal order preserves usable tyranny; Abyssal anti-place destroys relation and causal standing.

## Participants

The war has coalitions, clients, defectors, profiteers, and temporary fronts rather than two permanent armies.

- **Damned map-lords and Greater Sinners** build forts, courts, prisons, roads, and command systems. Many bear devil offices, but their human souls remain distinct from those offices.
- **Devil-office systems** continue mandates, recruit new bearers, and impose war roles upon persons who did not hold them while alive.
- **Abyssal wounds** recur according to their individual contradiction. They are not a demon nation and do not obey one ruler.
- **True dragons** are sovereign wound-beings. They function as catastrophes, contested seals, and potential weapons rather than a draconic faction.
- **Idol Archmasks** are foreign courts. They lease fronts or harvest the conduct produced by war while remaining rooted in exactly one Idol Throne.
- **Patron brokers** rent routes, bodies, refuge, supply, and betrayal without becoming native infernal authorities.
- **Fallen spirits** may prolong a particular campaign through their own rejected commissions.
- **Native Depth fauna** feed, migrate, and reproduce across fronts without acquiring the moral allegiance mortals attribute to them.

The Adversary exploits every form of refusal in the conflict but does not administer every combatant as a dark emperor.

## Human Participation

Living Sinners are the principal Material intermediaries. Abyssal Loyalists open contradictions in the name of liberation. Instrumentalists purchase offices, routes, and weapons. Heresarchs turn infernal effectiveness into rival revelation. After judgement, a damned Greater Sinner may become a map-lord or office-bearer without ceasing to be a human soul.

Mortals who believe they are choosing the disciplined side against monsters are offered usable tyranny. Those choosing the liberating side against jailers are offered the destruction of standing, relation, and coherent choice. Both conceal the people used as boundaries, receivers, test subjects, and proof costs.

## Idol Incursions

Idol Archmasks do not form the Abyssal side. They enter particular theatres to cultivate their own feeding architectures:

- Wrath leases disciplined war fronts and makes ending a completed defence appear dishonorable.
- Greed prices passage, custody, armament, pardon, and evacuation.
- Gluttony converts famine, corpses, and shared atrocity into communion.
- Envy counterfeits rescue, command, intelligence, and rightful succession.
- Pride claims authorship of victories and offers restored majesty through stolen offices.

Lust and Sloth require no token representatives. An Idol participates only where an actual feeding route exists.

## Saints, Angels, and Victory

Heaven maintains no permanent army defending Hell's frontier. A saint or angel may receive a bounded commission to protect named prisoners, preserve testimony, recover an unlawfully intercepted soul-route, close one wound, or defeat one manifested power. The intervention validates only that commission. It does not recognize a map-lord's title or make Hell the lesser righteous side.

Local victory is possible. A front can end when its prisoners are released, offices broken, evidence restored, contradiction closed, and Material aperture sealed. The wider war persists because other rulers and wounds continue making the same refusals elsewhere. No universal final front or mortal strategy is authorially fixed.

## Design Controls

- Give every front one specific contradiction, map, belligerent interest, human cost, and possible closure.
- Do not treat `devil`, `demon`, `archdevil`, `Sinner`, `Archmask`, and `true dragon` as ranks in one species ladder.
- A true dragon's body can be interrupted; its contradiction must be closed for permanent victory.
- An Archmask remains foreign to Hell and rooted in one Idol Throne.
- A Sinner's soul remains distinct from office, body, court, Mask, Patron role, residue, and legend.
- No created participant can annihilate a human soul or alter the Lord's judgement.
- No conflict establishes a universal infernal capital, council, bloodline, or good faction.
