# Hell and Abyssal Beings Overview

`Fiend` is a useful creator classification for hostile beings associated with Hell, the Depths, damnation, and the Abyss. It is not one ancestry or empire.

## Five Provenances

1. **Refusing human souls:** the damned, whose human souls persist through refusal and judgement.
2. **Transformed damned:** human souls forced or self-shaped into predatory bodies and roles.
3. **Native Depth fauna:** nonhuman ecologies that feed, hunt, and reproduce without routable souls.
4. **Imposed offices:** jailers, tempters, collectors, and contractual mantles. `Devil` belongs here as an office-name, not a species.
5. **Abyssal wound-beings:** active contradictions whose body is the expression of a tear in creation.

These provenances can cooperate without forming a unified infernal government. Rival economies of refusal, appetite, contract, and soul predation may exploit one another. Native animals may be harmful without moral culpability; damned persons and full-agency predators remain answerable for chosen evil.

## Foreign Powers and Political Titles

Idol Archmasks, Patron agents, manifested Sinners, and rare fallen created spirits can occupy territory or command forces in Hell without acquiring infernal provenance. An Archmask remains an `idol-pattern` person rooted in exactly one Idol Throne. A Patron remains a separately continuous actor working through anchors and rent. A fallen spirit remains a `created-spirit` person. Location, appearance, and allegiance do not change continuity.

`Archdevil` is an in-world political grade for a power controlling several infernal offices, roads, prisons, or works. It is not a sixth provenance. Many archdevils are damned human rulers bearing offices; the human soul, office, manifested body, dominion, cult persona, and legend remain distinct.

## Design Controls

- A damned human remains a human soul. Judgement is final, but the soul cannot be annihilated by another creature.
- A Depth animal can be cunning and dangerous without participating in mortal guilt.
- An imposed office has a mandate, wording, chain of authority, means of removal, and an underlying bearer whose continuity does not change.
- An Abyssal wound recurs until the opening that expresses it is closed.
- Seduction, fear, pain, destruction, and legal coercion are strategies or ecologies, not imported species names.
- A human crime may open the contradiction from which an Abyssal wound or true dragon forms. The resulting wound is not the perpetrator's soul and does not absorb the victims.

## Public Language

Churches, villagers, warlocks, and occult courts use `demon`, `devil`, `hell-beast`, and many regional names imprecisely. Creator notes must identify actual provenance and continuity even when characters cannot.
