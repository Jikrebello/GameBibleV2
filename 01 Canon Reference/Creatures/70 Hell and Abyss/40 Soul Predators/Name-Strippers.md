# Name-Strippers

Name-Strippers are native Depth predators that feed on the attachments by which a human soul recognizes itself in relation to others: names, remembered duties, beloved places, and acknowledged bonds. They cannot consume the soul itself or alter judgement.

They resemble low many-limbed animals covered in thin membranes. Each membrane carries a borrowed voice or face until digested.

## Ecology and Lifecycle

Name-Strippers gather near damaged routes, mass death, true-name repositories, and places where identity has already been administratively erased. They reproduce by laying membrane clusters in discarded records and grave wrappings.

Juveniles feed on residue. Adults can reach a routed or retained human soul through a breach. Their cunning is predatory rather than doctrinal; they learn which voice makes prey stop moving.

## Signs

- names missing while other words remain intact;
- survivors recognizing affection but not its object;
- grave markers scraped from the inside;
- thin translucent skins repeating private forms of address;
- records preserving dates and offices but losing persons.

## Threat Pattern

They imitate trusted voices, strip one attachment at a time, hide among discarded identities, and retreat after feeding rather than fighting to death. A heavily fed Stripper can wear several social identities but cannot reproduce the human person's moral interior.

## Weaknesses and Permanent Defeat

- repeated truthful naming by independent witnesses;
- relationships enacted materially rather than asserted only in words;
- White Flame or commissioned soul-road work that separates stolen attachment from predator;
- destroying membrane nests and the adult body;
- restoring records and graves so new hatchlings lose their feeding ground.

Death is bodily and permanent. Stolen names and attachments can be recovered from fresh membranes. Digested material can be restored through truthful witness and divine aid because the soul was never annihilated.

## Materials and Regional Names

Empty membrane can hold a disguise or voice briefly. Using occupied membrane as a tool continues the violation.

Names include name-stripper, face leech, widow eater, relation hound, and the thing that leaves the date.

## Story Hooks

- A town remembers every war casualty except who loved them.
- A Name-Stripper wears the identity of a saint but cannot imitate the saint's freely chosen mercy.
- An archive infestation is mistaken for political censorship until the missing names begin speaking.
- A vampire hires hunters because a Stripper is consuming the attachments that stabilize its retained soul.
- A recovered membrane contains two names believed to belong to one person.
