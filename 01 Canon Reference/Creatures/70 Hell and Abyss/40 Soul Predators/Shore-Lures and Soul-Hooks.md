# Shore-Lures and Soul-Hooks

These forms exploit damaged passage between human death and the lawful shore. They can delay, divert, hold, or injure a soul. They cannot erase it, change its judgement, or turn a nonhuman pattern into a human soul.

## Shore-Lures

Shore-Lures are damned human souls that manifest as a familiar guide, safe bank, lamp, relative, priest, or remembered road. They invite the newly dead away from the lawful route by presenting refusal as rest, reunion, or justified delay.

- **Refusal:** the Lure rejected its own judgement and now seeks company as evidence that the lawful shore is false.
- **Signs:** dead people describing mutually incompatible guides, funerary lamps casting light away from exits, residue using knowledge it never possessed, and water audible beneath dry floors.
- **Threat:** imitate trust, isolate a soul from commissioned guides, turn grief into a false route, and anchor itself through a living mourner's refusal to release the dead.
- **Defeat:** identify the borrowed relationship, restore the true death and destination to memory, close the false shore, and break the manifestation. The Lure returns to Hell.

## Soul-Hooks

Soul-Hooks are impersonal imposed mechanisms attached to names, graves, battle standards, contracts, or damaged route infrastructure. They catch passing human souls and hold them for an operator or office.

- **Signs:** a dead person's residue settling while funerary signs remain incomplete, hooks or barbs appearing in script, several deaths pulling toward one object, and the same name recurring in unrelated custody records.
- **Threat:** snag, reel, transfer, suspend, and strip attachments needed for a soul to recognize the lawful route.
- **Agency:** none. Moral responsibility belongs to creator, operator, beneficiary, and anyone who knowingly preserves the mechanism.
- **Defeat:** release every held soul, cut the naming line, destroy or lawfully dismantle the office, and repair the route before breaking the anchor.

Destroying a loaded Hook without release can scatter held souls into damaged passage. They remain real and recoverable, but rescue becomes harder.

## Manifestation and Permanent Containment

A Shore-Lure reaches the dead through a damaged route and a relationship it can imitate; truthful separation and route repair remove its local access before the manifestation is broken. A Soul-Hook persists through its naming line, anchor and operator. Permanent dismantling requires releasing every captive, severing all three and repairing the lawful route. Neither procedure annihilates, judges or mechanically redeems a human soul.

## Relationship to the Undertow

[[The Undertow]] is a predatory route network, not a species. It may deploy Hooks or bargain with Lures, but neither form proves Undertow involvement. Hell, patrons, necromancers, and mortal states can build similar interception sites.

## Materials and Regional Names

Hook fragments retain true-name danger. Shore-Lure lamps and tokens are temporary signs unless they began as Material objects.

Names include shore-lure, false ferryman, dead lamp, soul-hook, grave barb, name gaff, and black anchor.

## Story Hooks

- A beloved relative at a deathbed is a Shore-Lure using accurate memories stolen from residue.
- A battlefield standard holds hundreds of souls while the nation calls it sacred protection.
- Destroying a Soul-Hook now saves one captive but scatters many others.
- The Undertow is blamed for a device built by a local human office.
- A Shore-Lure tells the truth about a mourner's guilt and lies about what repentance requires.
