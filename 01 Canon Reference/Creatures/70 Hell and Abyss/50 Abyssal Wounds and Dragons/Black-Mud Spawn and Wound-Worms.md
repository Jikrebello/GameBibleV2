# Black-Mud Spawn and Wound-Worms

These are lesser bodies produced by active Abyssal contradictions. They do not reproduce as ordinary animals. Each recurrence indicates that the enabling wound remains open.

## Black-Mud Spawn

Black-Mud Spawn are temporary organs of anti-coherence: crawling hands without owners, mouths forming in puddles, larval silhouettes, or bodies whose boundaries exchange material whenever they touch.

- **Signs:** substances refusing to separate, footprints sharing toes, tools becoming partly flesh, and mud returning after complete removal.
- **Threat:** smear boundaries, join victims to terrain, exchange injuries, and make categories such as open and closed physically unreliable.
- **Weakness:** clear naming, dry separation, White Flame, intact thresholds, and restoring the distinction the local atrocity denied.
- **Permanent defeat:** destroy present bodies and close the wound. Otherwise new Spawn condense from the next black-mud bloom.

## Wound-Worms

Wound-Worms are eel-like gaps moving through matter and sequence. The visible rim is damaged reality rather than skin.

- **Signs:** tunnels with no displaced material, injuries appearing before contact, corridors missing one repeated interval, and sounds arriving in the wrong order.
- **Threat:** burrow through barriers, remove the connection between two structures, emerge from old injuries, and leave a route other wound-beings can follow.
- **Weakness:** force the route into one witnessed sequence, seal both ends simultaneously, restore removed material or testimony, and apply White Flame to the causal edge rather than the apparent body.
- **Permanent defeat:** close the moving tear and its originating contradiction. Cutting the visible rim only changes direction.

## Agency and Remains

Neither form possesses moral agency. Both are objectively hostile to created coherence. Their remains collapse into contaminated mud, missing material, or scars in objects. None contains a human soul.

Regional names include black spawn, mud hands, smearlings, wound-worm, gap eel, and the tunnel that takes no earth.

## Story Hooks

- Black-Mud Spawn merge a prison wall with prisoners after officials deny anyone was held there.
- A Wound-Worm makes a road bypass one village in both space and public memory.
- Killing every visible Spawn fails because the concealed atrocity remains officially denied.
- A warlock tries to use a Wound-Worm as a siege tunnel.
- White Flame closes the breach but leaves Material evidence that powerful people want destroyed.
