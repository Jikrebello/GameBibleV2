# The General Below

The General Below is a unique [[True Dragons|true dragon]] and the sovereign horror beneath [[The Deep Muster]]. It embodies **eternal conscription**: the refusal to release service at discharge, surrender, death, or the end of any rightful cause.

It is not a Giant, the soul of a dead general, a composite of soldiers, or the adult form of a drake. Qianglong's Unending Muster gave an Abyssal refusal hierarchy, bodies, campaign memory, and physical means to approach the Material Realm.

## Form and Presence

The General has no biologically stable shape. Its manifestation should combine a dragon's scale with the architecture of command: a body assembled from marching depth, ranked armor, banners without wind, roads that bend into ribs, and a head or crown that is always positioned below the person looking at it.

Its approaching presence produces:

- discharged soldiers reporting for commands no living officer issued;
- corpses and empty armor aligning by obsolete rank;
- roads that return travelers to a muster point;
- rolls adding the living under the names of the dead;
- bells, drums, and horns sounding from buried formations;
- wounds presenting as honorable service marks;
- officers treating every civilian as unassigned manpower;
- victories that generate immediate orders for another campaign.

## Intelligence and Lie

The General is sovereignly intelligent and fully responsible. It teaches that service is the only proof a life mattered, that release is betrayal, and that a community honors its dead by continuing to use them.

Duty, sacrifice, discipline, defense, and remembrance are real goods. The General's lie is that those goods erase the person, make command permanent, and convert death into fresh ownership.

## Threat Pattern

The General does not begin by attacking as an animal. It classifies.

- people become recruits, deserters, officers, labor, or supplies;
- buildings become barracks, depots, hospitals, or objectives;
- roads become fronts;
- relationships become chains of command;
- every death expands available material;
- every act of resistance is recorded as proof that a larger army is required.

When physically manifest, it coordinates residue formations, folds distance through campaign routes, imposes rank upon movement, and rebuilds damaged anatomy from active Muster functions.

## Anchors

Five regional anchors let contradictory formations act as one campaign: Cold-Iron Muster Shaft, Watch-Hill Command Crypt, River Arsenal Replacement Forge, Tuskway Banner Vault, and Fen Ossuary. [[Muster Anchor Matrix]] controls their exact functions. Three must be destroyed to make the final campaign feasible; all five remove every regional boss power.

Gerhold's accepted living command is a separate present-day condition and is always severed before the finale. The campaign archive, local altar feeds, and deepest refusal architecture are then addressed inside the Deep Muster. Breaking a manifested body without interrupting reformation and destroying the altar only delays the dragon.

## Material Avatar

The first complete battlefield body is assembled from corpse residue and campaign matter rather than souls: an eel-like coil carrying marching bodies through black mud, a lamprey mouth turning command into hunger and pressure, crab-armored replacement limbs, and wing-like banners and siege frames.

The confrontation has three controlling phases:

1. break the current avatar while surviving any powers retained by active regional anchors;
2. sever three altar feeds and prevent the campaign archive from issuing another formation;
3. reject the offered supreme rank and destroy the altar by physical, White-Flame, warlock, law-gem, or combined action.

## Defeat

Permanent victory requires the player to stop materialization and destroy or transform the command contradiction. Historical truth, physical action, burial or release, counterscript work, and rejection of false rank may all contribute. No human soul must be destroyed or abandoned to win because no human soul is contained in the General.

Every completed Grenzburg campaign stops the General Below and destroys the Material altar. Closure methods differ in casualties, preserved knowledge, surviving architecture, faction credit, and contamination. The site may remain dangerous; the General never remains an active recurring boss.

## Moral Ruling

The General cannot be responsibly harnessed as a permanent defense. An agreement that gives it prisoners, corpses, debtors, criminals, or volunteers as endless soldiers accepts its central evil. Temporary deception may create a tactical opening, but no stable bargain becomes a good ending.
