# The Two-Mouthed Deep

The Two-Mouthed Deep is a unique [[True Dragons|true dragon]] born from the crime remembered at the **Double Gate**. During a war whose participants and exact territory remain unassigned, one ruling command issued two simultaneous decrees under the same lawful seal.

The outer decree ordered every displaced household and straggling soldier to enter the fortified refuge. Refusal to enter was desertion. The inner decree classified everyone entering without a seal that the authorities had ceased issuing as an infiltrator. Entry was treason. Guards, judges, and executioners knowingly enforced both orders. Those who remained outside were killed as deserters; those who entered were killed as enemies; dependants were imprisoned or divided as property. The contradiction allowed the ruling command to seize supplies, eliminate unwanted populations, and declare every killing the lawful consequence of a victim's own choice.

The dragon embodies **authority making obedience impossible and then calling every possible response guilty**. It is not either decree, the office that issued them, any ruler's soul, or the dead of the Double Gate. It is the Abyssal wound opened when one authority deliberately made contradiction its instrument of innocence.

## Form, Signs, and Presence

The Deep manifests as one immense black eel-dragon whose forebody divides into two crowned heads. The mouths share sensation, memory, wounds, appetite, and continuity. One commands approach, confession, surrender, and enclosure. The other condemns whatever approaches, confesses, surrenders, or enters. Neither is an innocent personality imprisoned by the other.

Its coil passes through two gates that open onto the same drowned tribunal. Armor, walls, and black water divide around it and rejoin behind it without agreeing which side is inside.

Its presence produces:

- commands whose formally valid fulfilment constitutes a separate crime;
- doors that record entry as flight and departure as unlawful entry;
- prisoners punished for silence and for answering the required question;
- messengers delivering mutually exclusive orders bearing one authentic seal;
- institutions splitting into rival departments which remain answerable to one concealed authority;
- families and military units mutilating themselves to demonstrate obedience to both mouths;
- maps in which two opposed jurisdictions occupy the same ground and deny the same people standing.

## Intelligence and Lie

The Deep is sovereignly intelligent and fully responsible. It does not suffer indecision. It understands both commands and chooses the contradiction because contradiction transfers apparent guilt from the issuer to the person trapped beneath it.

It teaches that authority cannot be guilty if every subject is already in violation; that a command need not be possible to obey in order to be just; and that victims prove their corruption by selecting whichever death they prefer.

Law, obedience, emergency command, guarded refuge, and military discipline remain real goods. The dragon's lie is that authority becomes innocent when it removes every lawful course of action.

## Threat Pattern

The Deep does not ordinarily begin by destroying a settlement. It gives two constituencies different complete accounts of the same order, causes each to enforce its account against the other, and preserves the common authorization behind both.

When physically manifest, one mouth compels movement while the other punishes it. The body can turn fortifications into circular jurisdictions, drown courts in black water, and make a completed act satisfy the prohibition against itself. Killing one head interrupts one command channel and injures the single dragon; it neither kills half the continuity nor frees an innocent twin.

## The Double Gate and Far-Side Basin

Survivors, defecting guards, and clerks who preserved both decrees eventually exposed the common command. They denied the ruling office's claim that each massacre had been committed by the other gate, forced the manifested body through the joined threshold, and bounded its far-side expression within a drowned contradiction basin at the Abyssal margin.

Three continuing anchors prevent full Material return:

1. **The Joined Seal:** the broken authority seal is preserved beside both decrees and may never again authenticate an order.
2. **The Roll of Opposite Verdicts:** the dead from inside and outside are recorded together with the mutually exclusive charges used to kill them.
3. **The Open Middle:** the former execution ground between the gates remains a place of passage and asylum in which no office may punish a person merely for entering or leaving.

The seal weakens whenever the decrees are separated, one group of victims is blamed for the other's death, the Joined Seal is returned to office, or a present authority knowingly constructs a command that makes every available response criminal.

## Permanent Defeat

Destroying a manifested body returns the Deep to its contradiction basin. Permanent victory requires preserving both sides of the evidence, naming the common authority behind the incompatible commands, dismantling offices that reproduce the Double Gate's logic, protecting people who refuse an impossible order, and closing the historical wound while the dragon is manifest.

No human soul lies inside either mouth. The Dragon can hold, route, injure, and misclassify souls where passage has already been unlawfully intercepted, but it cannot divide a soul, reverse judgement, or make Heaven accept its contradictory verdicts.

## Infernal and Idol Relations

Mapped-Hell rulers fear the Deep because stable roads, prisons, contracts, and offices cannot survive its full arrival. They nevertheless direct brief projections into rival jurisdictions and recreate its logic in their own commands. [[The War of the Last Map]] therefore contains and feeds the same Dragon.

The [[Black-Coil Archfiends - Source Signature and Full Ecology Review|Surgeon of the Wound Arsenal]] seeks reproducible measurements from the basin. The Dragon is neither the Surgeon's creation nor property. Idol Archmasks may exploit the resulting fear, appetite, grievance, or hunger, but no Idol Throne owns the Deep.

## Materials and Regional Names

Fragments of a projected body resemble wet black tablets bearing a different legible command on either face. Reading one side causes the reader to overlook the other. These fragments are active anchors and administrative weapons, not ordinary scales.

Names include Two-Mouthed Deep, dragon of impossible obedience, lord of the contrary gates, double-verdict worm, and the sovereign below both laws.

## Story Hooks

- A court discovers that two hostile legal codes still bear impressions from the Joined Seal.
- A fortress begins executing those who obey an evacuation order and those who remain behind.
- One copy of the Roll preserves only the entrants, allowing a cult to blame those outside for the original massacre.
- The Surgeon offers a city a weapon that will force an invading army to obey two incompatible commanders.
- The Open Middle is fortified during a real emergency, weakening the seal even though the defenders' fear is justified.
