# Occlusion Beasts and Null-Maws

These greater wound-beings arise where people deliberately made suffering unseeable or treated created persons and relationships as if they had never existed.

## Occlusion Beasts

Occlusion Beasts are cunning predators whose bodies occupy whatever a witness is prevented from noticing. They resemble large many-jointed animals only in reflection, peripheral vision, or reconstructed evidence.

- **Signs:** tracks everyone steps around without seeing, wounds attributed to impossible accidents, missing space in drawings, and witnesses agreeing too quickly that nothing happened.
- **Threat:** move through blind procedure, attack from officially nonexistent space, shift attention away from victims, and make observation costly or socially dangerous.
- **Agency:** limited. They hunt intelligently but do not normally construct moral doctrine.
- **Defeat:** establish independent witnesses, make the hidden space public and physically marked, destroy the manifested body from several viewpoints, and repair the system that enforced non-observation.

## Null-Maws

Null-Maws are sapient wounds organized around the claim that a person, relationship, event, or consequence can be made truly nothing by sufficient power. Their visible form is a mouth or absence surrounded by fragments of everything it has failed to erase.

- **Signs:** names becoming grammatically impossible, maps folding around one district, consequences without accepted causes, and objects losing every recorded owner.
- **Threat:** sever relationships between cause and result, consume records and attachments, make allies unable to address one another, and offer servants freedom from consequence through self-erasure.
- **Agency:** full. A Null-Maw understands the created good it attacks and chooses to continue.
- **Defeat:** restore names, causes, witnesses, and material continuity; refuse its offer of consequence-free action; destroy every anchor; then close the wound beneath the body.

No Null-Maw can annihilate a human soul. It can obscure the soul from mortal rescue, mutilate memory and attachments, or remove Material evidence. Divine judgement and the soul's existence remain beyond its power.

## Materials and Regional Names

Occlusion scars can conceal an object from casual notice but reproduce the wound's logic. Null fragments are absences in records or matter and are never safe treasure.

Names include blind beast, unlooked hound, occlusion, null-maw, history eater, and the hole with witnesses around it.

## Story Hooks

- An Occlusion Beast lives in a palace room servants are ordered not to acknowledge.
- A Null-Maw offers soldiers immunity from guilt if they erase the unit they harmed.
- Restoring one victim's name makes an entire hidden district visible again.
- Investigators can see the Beast only through testimony from people society refuses to believe.
- A ruler preserves a Null anchor because it keeps an old atrocity outside legal memory.
