# The Unwritten Worm

The Unwritten Worm formed at [[Orthea]] around 520 BR after a city league destroyed a rebellious district, killed surviving witnesses, burned its rolls, and made continued denial a condition of office. The rulers did not merely conceal excesses. They attempted to make the people and campaign causally unrecordable.

The dragon embodies **atrocity converted into enforced absence**. It is not the souls of the murdered and cannot destroy them. It is the wound made by choosing that victims, witnesses, causes, and consequences should have no place in reality.

## Form, Signs, and Presence

The Worm appears as a vast pale serpent defined by missing ink, cut stone, and the edges of absent space. Segments of its body pass through maps, buildings, and memories without occupying the interval between them.

Its presence produces:

- names disappearing from records while grammar remains;
- wounds with no accepted weapon or battle;
- roads crossing a district that no traveler remembers entering;
- families retaining grief but losing its object;
- orders whose consequences survive after every issuer vanishes from history.

## Intelligence and Lie

The Worm is sovereignly intelligent and fully responsible. It teaches that sufficiently complete control of record becomes innocence, that only documented people possess claims, and that destroying causal evidence removes moral consequence.

Its servants do not need to deny that suffering occurred. They need only make attribution impossible until action becomes socially unthinkable.

## Threat Pattern

The Worm removes connections rather than matter. It separates attacker from wound, name from person, district from map, command from commander, and witness from audience. It attacks archives, testimony networks, graves, and living relationships before direct confrontation.

It can make a victim difficult for mortals to locate or remember. It cannot erase the human soul, escape divine judgement, or turn a true event into moral nothingness.

## The Orthean Seal

Defecting soldiers, surviving witnesses, and rival scribes reconstructed enough of the campaign to give the wound boundaries. They collapsed the archive-valley around the Worm and established three anchors:

1. **The Witness Stones:** independent accounts cut into different strata so no one archive controls them.
2. **The Roll of Commands:** reconstructed names and orders linking authority to action.
3. **The Empty Street:** an unbuilt line through the valley preserving the destroyed district's place on the map.

The seal weakens when records are centralized and purged, witnesses are treated as enemies of order, or the Empty Street is built over.

## Permanent Defeat

Destroying a manifested body only returns it to the missing interval beneath Orthea. Permanent victory requires reconstructing causal truth, restoring names and places, protecting independent witnesses, destroying every occlusion anchor, and closing the wound while refusing the Worm's offer of consequence-free power.

## Materials and Regional Names

Manifested fragments appear as blank scales that remove ownership and provenance from nearby objects. They are active anchors and should never enter an archive collection.

Names include Unwritten Worm, archive dragon, pale absence, history eater, and the serpent beneath the blank street.

## Story Hooks

- A ruler builds over the Empty Street to prove the old legend false.
- Soldiers return with wounds from a campaign no military roll records.
- A family remembers a murdered ancestor only when standing on a Witness Stone.
- Blank scales enter a university archive and detach every artifact from its excavation history.
- A partial recurrence is stopped by publishing evidence that will also destabilize a present government.
