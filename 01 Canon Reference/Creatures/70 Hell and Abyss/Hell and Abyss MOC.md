# Hell and Abyss MOC

Historical reading: [[Infernal Kings and the Wars of the Depth]], within the [[World Historical Atlas MOC]].

Hell is the mapped destination and ecology of completed refusal. The Abyss lies beyond it as anti-place and causal injury. `Fiend` remains a useful creator term, but no single ancestry, empire, or chain of command unites the beings catalogued here.

## Encounter Rules

- Hell's native ecology is cold black Earth and Water. Metaphysical fire and brimstone identify Wrath rather than infernal provenance.
- Defeating a damned manifestation does not annihilate, redeem, or reverse judgement upon the human soul beneath it.
- Native Depth fauna are soulless biological ecologies. Their danger is real without assigning moral guilt to animal behavior.
- A devil is an imposed office. Break the mantle, then identify what remains beneath it.
- Soul predators can hold, mutilate, obscure, or exploit a human soul but cannot erase it or alter the Lord's judgement.
- An Abyssal wound recurs until its enabling contradiction is closed. Destroying a body is only interruption.
- Infernal beings can be dragged through names, contracts, anchors, or damaged routes. This is coercion, never safe domestication.

## The War of the Last Map

Mapped dominions require stable roads, prisoners, contracts, borders, offices, and causal sequence. Abyssal wounds dissolve those distinctions. Damned rulers therefore contain wounds to preserve the tyrannies they can administer, then redirect those same wounds into rival territory. They cannot end the war without truthful acknowledgement, restitution, surrendered claims, and released captives; their defining refusals make those remedies intolerable. The war consequently recreates what it claims to contain.

This is not a war between two infernal species or morally balanced nations. Wounds possess no common government. Idol Archmasks, Patron brokers, fallen spirits, Greater Sinners, and native fauna enter particular fronts for their own purposes. Saints and angels intervene only under bounded commission to protect persons, testimony, or lawful soul passage, never to preserve Hell's dominion.
