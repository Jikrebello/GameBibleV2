# Bone-Reef Crawlers and Hell-Drakes

These are larger native animals of Hell's mapped ecology. Neither is related to Menhir drakes or true dragons.

## Bone-Reef Crawlers

Bone-Reef Crawlers are broad crustacean animals that cement discarded bone, shell, weapons, and stone to their backs. A resting colony resembles a reef or battlefield midden above the mud.

- **Signs:** heaps changing orientation overnight, cleaned bone sorted by size, clicking under shallow water, and salvage disappearing without drag marks.
- **Ecology:** Crawlers filter mud, eat larvae and carrion, and use hard remains as camouflage and brood shelter. No souls inhabit the attached bones.
- **Threat:** rise beneath explorers, pin limbs under the armored back, cut with several small claws, and collapse into a defensive mound.
- **Weakness:** soft underside, dry slopes, loud repeated vibration, and removing enough shell weight to expose the brood seam.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death and destruction or relocation of eggs.

## Hell-Drakes

Hell-Drake is the common creator name for long-bodied Depth predators with vestigial wings, heat-sensing pits, and jaws adapted to black mud. In-world names usually emphasize mire, eel, or bone rather than dragonhood.

- **Signs:** serpentine trenches, mud sprayed high on walls, molted jaw plates, and prey crushed before being swallowed.
- **Ecology:** solitary apex animals that move between mire pools through submerged tunnels. They reproduce rarely and guard warm egg banks.
- **Threat:** burst from depth, coil around structures, spray choking mud, use vestigial wings to lunge or brake, and retreat through collapsing tunnels.
- **Weakness:** clean flame dries sensory membranes, rigid narrow gates prevent coiling, and cold stone slows the animal.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death. It leaves no law-gem, soul event, office, or Abyssal recurrence.

## Materials and Names

Crawler shell carries contamination but makes excellent layered armor after cleansing. Hell-Drake jaw plates and sensory pits are prized by trackers and black-mud alchemists.

Names include bone reef, ossuary crab, midden crawler, mire-wing, pit wyrm, carrion coil, and Hell-Drake.

## Story Hooks

- A treasure mound is a brooding Crawler colony whose attached bones include vital evidence.
- A captured Hell-Drake escapes when the devil office controlling it is broken.
- Miners breach a warm egg bank beneath a leftward shrine.
- A cult presents a Hell-Drake as proof that a sealed true dragon has returned.
- Cleansing Crawler shell reveals that some bones came from a recent Material massacre.
