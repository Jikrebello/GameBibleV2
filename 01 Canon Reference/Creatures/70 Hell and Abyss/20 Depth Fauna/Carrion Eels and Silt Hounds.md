# Carrion Eels and Silt Hounds

These predators follow warmth, coherent flesh, and movement through Hell's mapped mire. They can cross into the Material Realm only through wet leftward breaches or deliberate transport.

## Carrion Eels

Carrion Eels are long armored scavengers with lamprey jaws and sensory pits that detect the boundary between living tissue and unstable manifestation.

- **Signs:** parallel furrows through mud, circular feeding wounds, water clouded from below, and carcasses stripped only at joints.
- **Ecology:** they consume carrion, mire larvae, and damaged manifested bodies. Large females brood eggs inside bone cavities.
- **Threat:** strike from opaque water, fasten to a limb, roll prey into mud, and release when exposed to dry air.
- **Weakness:** prolonged dryness, bright heat, vibration away from water, and damage to the sensory crown.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death and destruction of brood cavities.

## Silt Hounds

Silt Hounds are six-limbed pursuit animals with broad feet, sealed eyes, and nostrils that track coherent identity through obscuring mud. Devil offices and damned hunters sometimes capture them, but they are not servants by nature.

- **Signs:** tracks appearing only in wet surfaces, breathing heard below floor level, doors marked with a fan of silt, and prey scents vanishing from ordinary dogs.
- **Ecology:** packs den in raised bone and reed mounds. They follow Carrion Eels during flood seasons and hunt anything that retains a stable warm body.
- **Threat:** relay pursuit, drive prey toward water, spit blinding silt, and hold a target for larger predators.
- **Weakness:** dry stone, crossed scent fields, sudden elevation, and destroying the lead Hound's scent mark.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death. A broken pack retreats unless trained or office-bound.

## Materials and Names

Eel armor resists contaminated water. Hound scent nodules can track one coherent body for several days and are illegal in most soul-route investigations without Church custody.

Names include carrion eel, black lamprey, grave eel, silt hound, mire dog, and blind pursuer.
