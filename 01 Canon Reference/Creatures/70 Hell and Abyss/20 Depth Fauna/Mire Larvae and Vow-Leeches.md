# Mire Larvae and Vow-Leeches

Mire Larvae and Vow-Leeches are living animals of the Depth, found in black-mud shallows, corpse banks and places where repeated oaths have placed bodies under strain. Larvae consume carrion and unstable residue; Vow-Leeches feed upon the residue of sustained intention. Neither contains a human soul or embodies a damned person, and both can be destroyed by ordinary bodily means.

## Mire Larvae

Mire Larvae are pale segmented scavengers that hatch in warm black mud around carrion, discarded manifestations, and concentrated emotional residue.

- **Signs:** mud breathing in small pulses, clean bones beneath untouched clothing, crescent feeding marks, and a sweet mineral smell.
- **Ecology:** larvae digest unstable flesh and residue, then pupate into short-lived mud flies that spread eggs along wet routes.
- **Threat:** mass beneath a target, enter wounds, generate heat, and collapse a mud crust under weight.
- **Control:** dryness, salt concentration, cold, clean running water, and removing the carrion bed.
- **Permanent defeat:** ordinary bodily death and destruction of egg mud.

## Vow-Leeches

Vow-Leeches are flat dark parasites attracted to the repeated bodily and metaphysical stress of maintaining an oath. They feed on the residue of intention, not the human soul or the validity of the promise.

- **Signs:** unexplained exhaustion after repeating a vow, black rings beneath armor or jewelry, promises becoming harder to recall in the same room, and leech tracks leading toward written names.
- **Ecology:** they cluster around prisons, military musters, coerced labor, false marriages, and damaged contract sites.
- **Threat:** attach invisibly, weaken sustained intention, and make a victim abandon a difficult act at its most costly moment.
- **Control:** remove them physically, expose the coerced or contradictory oath, cross clean fire or fast water, and destroy their damp nesting medium.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death. Ending an unjust oath removes the food source but does not magically kill every leech.

## Materials and Names

Larval fat stabilizes some black-mud samples at severe contamination risk. Dried Vow-Leeches can reveal which clause or promise fed them, but contract magicians also use them to sabotage resolve.

Names include mire grub, bog maggot, refusal fly, vow-leech, promise sucker, and oath worm.
