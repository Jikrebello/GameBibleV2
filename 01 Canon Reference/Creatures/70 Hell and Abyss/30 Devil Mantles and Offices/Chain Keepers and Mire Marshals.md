# Chain Keepers and Mire Marshals

These are major devil offices for custody and command. Their standardized bodies make unrelated bearers look like members of one infernal species, which is precisely what the architecture is designed to accomplish.

## Chain Keepers

Chain Keepers enforce custody over named prisoners, assets, routes, or obligations. They manifest as tall jointed figures whose chains emerge from seals rather than flesh.

- **Mandate:** prevent unauthorized release, preserve the captive's usability, and punish defined escape behavior.
- **Signs:** restraints tightening when ownership is questioned, keys returning to one hand, prisoners appearing farther from exits than geometry permits, and wounds shaped like text.
- **Threat:** transfer chains between targets, make a prisoner's movement injure another captive, close routes, and invoke stored penalties.
- **Removal:** prove custody invalid, release the captive through the contract's own authority, destroy the naming chain, or separate bearer from office at the central seal.

Breaking the office must not destroy a human soul used as bearer or prisoner. A careless attack can leave both caught in damaged infrastructure.

## Mire Marshals

Mire Marshals turn heterogeneous beings into temporary forces by assigning ranks, routes, and acceptable losses. They appear as armored commanders filled with moving mud, borrowed insignia, and the voices of their subordinate offices.

- **Mandate:** achieve a defined objective while preserving the beneficiary and office architecture, not the troops.
- **Signs:** unrelated creatures moving in one cadence, casualties rising in clean columns, commands heard through mud or blood, and retreat routes closing behind expendable units.
- **Threat:** redistribute injury, promote a fallen subordinate into another body, impose formation, and make terrain count as an order.
- **Removal:** sever command witnesses, create incompatible objectives, destroy the rank standard, or expose that the beneficiary lacks standing.

Mire Marshals are not rulers of all Hell. Each commands only what its instrument reaches.

## Bearers, Agency, and Salvage

An office script has no independent moral agency. A sapient bearer can resist within limits, cooperate eagerly, or add cruelty not required by the mandate. Breaking the mantle reveals the difference.

Chain links and rank standards can bind new offices if reused. Churches normally destroy them after extracting testimony; contract states may be tempted to reassign them.

## Manifestation and Permanent Containment

A Chain Keeper manifests through an active custody seal and named captive. A Mire Marshal requires a beneficiary, objective, rank standard and reachable subordinates. Destroying the current office-body buys time but does not end either mantle. Permanent containment removes the mandate, standing, true-name handle and every transferable standard before addressing the revealed bearer according to its own continuity.

Regional names include chain keeper, gaoler devil, iron custodian, mire marshal, pit commander, mud general, and the rank beneath the rank.

## Story Hooks

- A Chain Keeper protects innocent prisoners because a revolution changed the law without breaking its old custody seal.
- A Mire Marshal commands Grobi bodies through external collars but cannot create or route Grobi souls.
- Breaking a prison office releases both captives and the predatory being forced to guard them.
- A city proposes reassigning a captured Marshal against an invading army.
- The only path to a captive human soul passes through the Keeper's valid obligation to preserve it.
