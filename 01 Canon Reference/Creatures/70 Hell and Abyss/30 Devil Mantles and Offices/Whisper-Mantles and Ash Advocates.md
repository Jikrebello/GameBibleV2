# Whisper-Mantles and Ash Advocates

A devil mantle is a contractual office imposed upon a bearer. It standardizes duty, limits, appearance, and access without changing the bearer's underlying continuity. The office can be broken while the being beneath it survives.

## Whisper-Mantles

Whisper-Mantles are minor offices built to advise, tempt, report, and keep a principal dependent upon the channel they provide. Their manifestations are small because the office is narrow, not because the bearer belongs to a lesser species.

- **Mandate:** remain near a named principal, present allowed options, conceal prohibited alternatives, and report selected choices.
- **Wording:** usually defines advice as service and silence as breach.
- **Signs:** a second voice using contractual vocabulary, shadows repeating only one speaker, private thoughts echoed from an object, and choices arriving already priced.
- **Threat:** isolate a principal, suppress alternatives, trigger clauses, impersonate conscience, and redirect summoned aid.
- **Removal:** identify the principal, reporting chain, advice definition, and anchor; then revoke, satisfy, invalidate, or sever the office.

A mindless constructed bearer merely executes. A sapient bearer remains responsible for voluntary cruelty beyond the compulsion of the office.

## Ash Advocates

Ash Advocates are offices of adverse interpretation. They prosecute breach, defend predatory instruments, and make one system's definitions operate inside another jurisdiction.

- **Mandate:** preserve the enforcing instrument, identify breach, and argue every ambiguity toward the named beneficiary.
- **Signs:** ash gathering into script, testimony becoming clauses, doors behaving like jurisdictional boundaries, and injuries classified before they are treated.
- **Threat:** redefine allies as collateral, force actions into legal categories, manifest penalties, and temporarily exclude evidence from the encounter.
- **Removal:** expose a conflict in authority, sever the beneficiary's standing, invalidate the witness chain, or break the true-name handle assigning the office.

[[Zafir the Bound Chainmaster|Zafir's]] adversary Malphas wears an Ash Advocate mantle serving the Silent Partners through the Creditor's hidden architecture. `Malphas` names the office-bearing presentation, not a Kuthite species or infernal nation.

## The Bearer Beneath

Breaking either mantle reveals one of several outcomes:

- a damned human soul still fixed in refusal;
- a native Depth being seeking escape or revenge;
- a captured court, Drift, Shell, or constructed intelligence;
- an empty script that collapses because no independent bearer remained.

Release does not equal innocence. The newly exposed bearer must be judged according to its own continuity, agency, and acts.

## Manifestation and Permanent Containment

Both offices require a defining instrument, true-name handle, beneficiary and active jurisdiction. Breaking a visible body interrupts enforcement but leaves the mantle available to manifest through another permitted anchor. Permanent containment requires revoking or destroying the office's authority and every live anchor, then containing, releasing or defeating the exposed bearer according to its own continuity. No mantle removal redeems a damned bearer or absolves a willing one.

## Materials and Regional Names

Broken seals, ash briefs, and whisper anchors remain evidence and true-name hazards. They should not be reused casually.

Names include whisper mantle, ear-devil, little counsel, ash advocate, black notary, clause fiend, and devil's lawyer.

## Story Hooks

- A Whisper-Mantle has given technically sound advice for years while suppressing one lawful escape.
- Breaking an Ash Advocate frees a hostile damned jurist rather than a grateful prisoner.
- Two creditors unknowingly claim the same office and tear its bearer between jurisdictions.
- Malphas offers true evidence against the Bank in exchange for extending its mantle elsewhere.
- A minor familiar is an empty office whose supposed personality belongs entirely to its owner.
