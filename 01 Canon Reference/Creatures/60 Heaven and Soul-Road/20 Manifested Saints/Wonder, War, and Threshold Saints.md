# Wonder, War, and Threshold Saints

These profiles describe unusually forceful saintly manifestations. The distinction is one of commission, not spiritual caste: the same redeemed person may serve differently on separate occasions if the Lord so appoints.

## Wonder-Working Saints

Wonder-working saints appear where ordinary means have failed but mercy still serves a larger truth. They may arrest plague in one ward, restore speech needed for testimony, reveal clean water, or stabilize a body long enough for human healers to act.

Their wonders are bounded. They do not make mortality optional, erase every consequence, or operate as repeatable cures. A true wonder normally creates an obligation among the living: tend the sick, confess the concealed cause, share the water, or finish the rescue.

**Counterfeit warning:** an apparition that sells healing, cultivates dependency, or demands moral surrender is not a saint.

## War and Threshold Saints

This single service profile joins two closely related commissions: defense within a crisis and defense of the boundary through which people must survive it.

**War service** manifests when armed defense serves a just commission. These saints rally a breaking line, expose an infiltrator, shield noncombatants, break an Idol's panic, or lead an escape through encirclement. Their glory may be terrible, but they do not sanctify cruelty, vengeance, or indiscriminate killing.

**Signs:** disciplined courage; clear recognition of protected persons; weapons made effective against a specific supernatural threat; avian pinions, fire, or battle scars associated with the saint's earthly witness.

**Tactics:** war saints create openings, deny hostile supernatural advantages, and direct mortals toward achievable duties. They rarely win a whole war in place of its participants.

**Threshold service** defends places where Material life, death, and hostile layers press together. A saint may hold a hospice door against a false psychopomp, keep a thin space closed during evacuation, guide the dying away from an Idol's lure, or stand beside the Boatman's servants at a damaged route.

They do not judge souls or replace the Boatman. Their authority concerns protection, witness, and the completion of a specific passage.

## Permanent Defeat and Materials

Breaking a manifestation ends its current intervention but cannot destroy the redeemed soul. The appearance leaves no celestial organs or commodity materials. Relics already connected to the saint may become active during the commission, but their power cannot be industrialized or detached from moral purpose.

## Regional Names and Institutional Use

Wonder-working saints are called **oil saints**, **healing elders**, or **bearers of the clean flame**. War and threshold service produces titles such as **banner saint**, **wall intercessor**, **hospice keeper**, and **saint at the last door**. Armies and churches may invoke those titles sincerely or propagandistically; the commission's conduct, not the banner, establishes what appeared.
