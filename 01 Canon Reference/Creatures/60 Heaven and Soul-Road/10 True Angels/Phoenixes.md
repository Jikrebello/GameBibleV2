# Phoenixes

Phoenixes are singular true angels commissioned where judgement and restoration must occur together on a regional or historical scale. They are not elemental birds, immortal wildlife, reincarnating pets, or saints promoted into another species.

## Form

A phoenix's core form is an immense avian intelligence of white fire, many pinions, and a heart-like clarity around which damaged reality becomes sharply legible. Witnesses may see a burning raptor, a sun-bright migration, a person-shaped blaze with wings, or a whole sky folding into one descending bird. The stable fact is **fire without appetite**: corruption burns, created good remains itself, and hidden damage becomes visible.

Each phoenix is an individual. “Phoenix-grade” describes the scale of commission, but the family is not a ladder of interchangeable stat blocks.

## Commission

Phoenixes are sent to:

- destroy an Abyssal incursion that lesser containment cannot hold;
- purify a landscape whose corruption reproduces through ordinary remedies;
- expose and judge a great false holiness;
- preserve a remnant through catastrophe while ending the structure that trapped it;
- enact a public proof that refusal is not creation's final condition.

Their arrival does not mean every person or institution on the “correct” side is vindicated. Phoenix fire distinguishes created good from the corruption attached to it, often exposing allies as painfully as enemies.

## Signs

- ordinary flame goes silent and points toward the corruption source;
- shadows sharpen around lies but do not vanish around honest grief;
- birds lift in one coordinated direction regardless of species;
- wounds cease worsening while remaining fully felt;
- relics, false relics, Shell fire, and Idol radiance become visibly different substances;
- ash falls upward before the manifestation arrives.

## Threat Pattern

A phoenix strips concealment, separates captives from corrupt structures, burns self-reproducing metaphysical harm, and denies the enemy replacement bodies. It may carry survivors through destructive purification or require mortals to remove them first. The encounter is as much evacuation, truth, and moral choice as combat.

## Limits and Defeat

A phoenix cannot be summoned, harvested, bred, or reduced to a repeatable resurrection method. It does not restore the dead as a convenience or erase human judgement. Its commission has boundaries and may leave ordinary rebuilding entirely to mortals.

Breaking a Material phoenix-body requires apex force or severing the manifestation path. The created spirit survives ordinary defeat, but the interrupted purification may fail catastrophically. Permanent destruction requires a unique cosmic circumstance and cannot be inferred from possessing “phoenix ash.”

Phoenixes are true angels and therefore capable of refusal in principle. No active fallen phoenix is canonized in this wave.

## Materials and Counterfeits

After a true departure, ash becomes ordinary mineral ash once its witness-work is complete. Feathers may remain as temporary signs or relic-like testimony, not inexhaustible fuel. Markets selling phoenix blood, eggs, or rebirth ash trade in fraud, elemental material, stolen relic effects, or something worse.

## Regional Names

Eclessians favor **White Phoenix** or **Bird of the Final Fire**. Thalmyric hymnody uses **Many-Winged Rekindler**. Other cultures describe the same grade through sun-birds, returning migration, or the fire that leaves truth standing.

## Story Hooks

- a city asks for phoenix intervention while refusing to evacuate the people its purification would expose;
- counterfeit ash performs real miracles because another power answers the fraud;
- an old battlefield proves a phoenix once acted there, but histories reverse whom it judged;
- the party must keep one clean road open through a regional burning;
- an enemy breaks the commission-body and leaves a half-purified ecology more unstable than before.
