# True Angels Overview

True angels are spirits created by the Lord for heavenly service. They are not human souls, fey, patrons, elementals, or thoughts produced by worship. Their continuity is `created-spirit`.

## Nature

True angels possess intelligence, affection, memory, identity, and full moral agency without bearing a routable human soul. They do not reproduce into a biological population. Lesser offices recur as recognizable **choirs** because similar commissions produce similar forms; each member remains an individual. Greater angels and phoenixes are singular beings.

Angels may fall through chosen refusal. Loyal angels encountered under heavenly commission are genuinely good, but goodness is allegiance and action rather than an automatic species trait.

## Form

Each choir has a stable core anatomy. True angelic forms are often avian but need not resemble winged humans: nested wings, eyes set in pinions, wheel-like flight, feathered flame, raptor masks, many-jointed silhouettes, or voices carried by moving air are common.

Witnesses receive a cultural vestment around that core. Armor, robes, gendered voice, musical scale, tools, and familiar animal references may change. The commission, stable signs, and underlying being do not.

## Choirs and Singular Orders

- [[Attendant and Herald Angels|Attendants]] sustain small local works; **heralds** carry bounded communication.
- [[Warden, Mercy, and Witness Angels|Wardens]] defend; **mercies** restore; **witnesses** reveal and preserve truth.
- [[Host and Greater Angels|Host angels]] wage open spiritual war; **greater angels** lead singular historical commissions.
- [[Phoenixes]] are the highest intervention-grade true angels, bearing judgement and restoration together.

These are service profiles, not immutable species. An angel may receive different work over its history without climbing an evolutionary ladder.

## Saints and Authority

Angels are not automatically senior to [[Manifested Saints Overview|saints]]. A saint can command angels when placed over a mission. Angels can lead angelic hosts but possess no jurisdiction over a saint's testimony merely through greater force.

## Worship and Petition

True angels reject worship. Mortals may ask the Lord for aid and may address a known servant while it is present, but cannot summon or bind heavenly service. A being that accepts worship, sells its appearance, or submits to a repeatable command rite fails the ordinary test of a loyal angel.

## Defeat

Breaking a Material commission-body forces withdrawal or ends that local action. It does not normally destroy the created spirit. Permanent destruction requires exceptional cosmic means and must be stated in a unique dossier rather than assumed from combat.
