# Host and Greater Angels

Host angels fight open supernatural war. Greater angels are singular created spirits entrusted with historical or cosmological commissions on the scale of kingdoms, churches, or the World Egg's boundaries. Neither category exists to provide a routine high-level monster encounter.

## Host Angels

Host angels recur in recognizable martial choirs. They oppose Abyssal incursions, mass soul interception, large Idol manifestations, fallen created spirits, and supernatural armies that mortal force cannot contain alone.

- **Core form:** raptor-like bodies assembled from armor, pinions, flame, and storm; many appear as several winged fighters sharing one moving geometry.
- **Signs:** battle lines become sharply visible, friendly concealment that depends on lies fails alongside enemy illusion, weapons ring before contact, and fear becomes distinguishable from cowardice.
- **Threat pattern:** break supernatural formation, isolate commanders, carry combat through air or thin space, shield mortal withdrawal, and destroy the mechanism sustaining a hostile host.
- **Discipline:** host angels do not seek glory, loot, territory, or political rule. They withdraw when their assigned enemy is contained even if mortals want them to win the rest of the war.
- **Defeat:** a commission-body can be broken by great force, interrupted manifestation, or severance from the battlefront. The angelic spirit survives ordinary defeat.

Host angels can lead other angels inside an assigned battle. They possess no automatic command over manifested saints. A saint placed over the campaign may direct the host.

During [[The Great Unbinding]], specialized Host servants operated beneath one Phoenix's regional commission. They broke selected supernatural formations, severed recurrence and generative routes, shielded mortal withdrawal, and then withdrew. They did not administer conquered lands or turn a bounded liberation commission into general authorization for human war.

## Greater Angels

Each greater angel is unique. Functional titles describe them better than species: the bearer of a particular trumpet, defender of a gate, captain of a named host, keeper of a revelation, or opponent appointed against one great refusal.

- **Core form:** singular and stable, usually too large or complex for one witness to perceive. Multiple wings, eyes, wheels, voices, weapons, and landscapes may be aspects of one body rather than carried equipment.
- **Signs:** a whole region briefly shares one orientation; hidden supernatural powers reveal their allegiance; lesser commissions align without coercion; natural light and White Flame become distinguishable from every imitation.
- **Threat pattern:** reshape the terms of a cosmic battle, close a great breach, defeat an apex created enemy, carry a message that reorders history, or hold a frontier while mortals complete the act entrusted to them.
- **Limits:** still created, still commissioned, and not omniscient. A greater angel cannot replace the Lord, create souls, annul human freedom, or declare personal desire to be divine command.
- **Defeat and fall:** breaking a Material body remains possible at extreme cost. A greater angel can fall through chosen refusal; such an event is an epochal betrayal, not combat corruption.

This wave establishes the family without inventing unattached named greater angels. A singular dossier should be created only when history or an adventure requires the individual.

## Materials and Mortal Claims

Claims to own a greater angel's weapon, feather, or command are presumptively false. A temporary instrument may remain as a relic-like witness, but its authority cannot be inherited by purchase or conquest. Mortal armies commonly paint host imagery on banners; resemblance is not commission.

## Regional Names

Host choirs are remembered as **the High Host**, **spear-wings**, **white companies**, or **the storm in order**. Greater angels receive titles from the commission witnessed by a culture, such as **the Gate-Bearer** or **the Voice Over Kings**; a title does not prove that two chronicles describe different beings.

## Story Hooks

- a host arrives to hold one breach and refuses to defend the ruler's nearby capital;
- a saint commands an angelic battle line, scandalizing clergy who confuse origin with rank;
- evidence suggests an ancient greater angel abandoned a commission without proving where it went;
- mortal generals imitate a host signal and draw an enemy they cannot survive;
- the party must complete a human-scale task while the visible cosmic battle prevents everyone else from noticing it.
