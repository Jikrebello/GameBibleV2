# Warden, Mercy, and Witness Angels

These choirs protect created goods under direct commission. Their service is morally coherent but not interchangeable: a warden preserves, a mercy restores, and a witness makes truth durable.

## Warden Angels

Wardens defend a person, place, relic, community, road, or boundary. Their commission names what must be preserved and what may be allowed to pass.

- **Core form:** layered shield-wings, hooked feet set against invisible ground, and eyes facing every approach; larger manifestations may resemble a gate made from a living bird.
- **Signs:** thresholds become physically definite, hostile disguises fail at the boundary, wind moves outward from the protected center, and innocent occupants know where safety ends.
- **Threat pattern:** interpose, divide combatants, deny passage, carry attacks into empty space, and force intruders to confront the condition they violate.
- **Weakness:** work outside the stated protection receives no special authority. A warden can be bypassed by genuinely ceasing the prohibited act or proving the protected claim false.
- **End:** the charge is safe, lawfully surrendered, destroyed beyond recovery, or the manifestation is broken.

## Mercy Angels

Mercies restore coherence where injury, disease, corruption, or despair has damaged a created good. They do not abolish mortality, erase consent, or make every wound consequence-free.

- **Core form:** broad pale wings folded like a shelter, many delicate hands or beaks performing one precise work, and heatless white fire visible beneath translucent anatomy.
- **Signs:** bleeding stops while pain remains legible, contaminated tissue separates from healthy flesh, hidden abuse becomes impossible to conceal from the patient, and caregivers recover steadiness.
- **Threat pattern:** burn corruption without burning sound matter, immobilize an abuser, expose poison, and return stolen function. Against deliberate predation, mercy can be severe because restoration requires removing what continues the harm.
- **Weakness:** cannot force a person to accept healing, restore what the commission does not encompass, or turn death into routine reversal.
- **End:** the patient or place is stabilized, refuses the offered restoration, or the commission-body is broken.

## Witness Angels

Witnesses preserve or disclose truth when death, power, magic, or public lies would otherwise erase it. They do not independently invent a verdict or act as a neutral court above the Lord.

- **Core form:** a vertical wheel or fan of eyes and scriptlike feathers around an empty center where the witnessed event appears without embellishment.
- **Signs:** testimony retains exact distinctions, altered records show their seams, witnesses remember what they truly perceived rather than what they rehearsed, and destroyed evidence casts a temporary outline.
- **Threat pattern:** strip illusion, prevent false speech from gaining force, bind a scene against alteration, and turn concealed actions outward for those authorized to see.
- **Weakness:** a witness reveals only what falls within the commission. It does not answer unrelated questions, expose private truth for curiosity, or replace moral judgement with footage.
- **End:** the truth reaches its appointed witness, is freely rejected, or the manifestation is broken after preserving enough evidence to continue without it.

## Materials and Misuse

No choir leaves a harvestable angelic body. Feathers, shields, oil, tears, or script reported after departure are signs or temporary instruments. Institutions may preserve them as evidence or relic-adjacent objects, but cannot manufacture another appearance from them.

## Regional Names

Warden angels are **gate-wings**, **shield choirs**, or **keepers at the line**. Mercy angels are **white hands**, **cool pinions**, or **the breath after fever**. Witness angels are **many-eyed ones**, **unforgotten voices**, or **keepers of the clear word**. Regional language changes vestment and emphasis, not identity.

Wardens are often mistaken for proof that a claimant is righteous; they may protect a guilty person whose survival is necessary for testimony. Mercies are mistaken for endorsements of every institution around a healing. Witnesses are mistaken for universal surveillance. The commission is narrower than the propaganda built around it.

## Story Hooks

- a warden bars both assassins and rescuers from the same condemned prisoner;
- a mercy offers restoration that will expose the patient's concealed crime;
- a witness preserves a battle's truth but only one hated enemy is authorized to receive it;
- a church claims ownership of a feather-sign after the commission has ended;
- breaking an angelic guard saves a town now but opens the protected threshold beneath it.
