# Soul-Hunters and Threshold Wardens

Soul-hunters and threshold wardens are forceful road mechanisms used when a valid human passage is under active attack. Their frightening appearance reflects resistance to interception, not hostility toward the dead.

## Origin and Continuity

Like guides and separators, these are `infrastructure-office` manifestations rather than persons. Angels or saints may accompany, direct, or embody related work, but the profile here concerns the impersonal mechanism itself.

## Signs

- False doors slam shut in sequence behind a fleeing presence.
- Hooks, hounds, wings, lantern-spears, or boundary posts take shape from the route.
- A death chamber becomes impossible for an intruding shadow to leave.
- Predatory voices lose every stolen name except their own.
- The threshold brightens for the dying while darkening for the interceptor.

## Conduct and Tactics

**Soul-hunters** mark and pursue a being that has trespassed upon a routed passage. They deny disguises, sever stolen residue, and drive the target toward a place where commissioned agents or mortals can oppose it.

**Threshold wardens** hold a crossing long enough for the genuine soul to pass. They bar counterfeit psychopomps, Idol lures, predatory residue, and unlawful bindings. They do not prevent natural death or return the dead to life.

Both offices are exact rather than inventive. They will ignore unrelated danger and cannot be bribed into hunting a mortal enemy. A human antagonist becomes their target only by interfering with the route itself.

## Defeat and Permanent Resolution

An office-body can be dispersed by sufficient force or by destroying the physical conditions through which it manifests. Permanent safety comes from ending the interception, repairing the threshold, or releasing the unlawful binding. Destroying the warden while leaving the breach open merely removes protection.

No usable corpse remains. Marks left by a hunter may identify an interceptor, reveal a stolen name, or guide mortals to the point where the soul-road was breached.

## Regional Names

Soul-hunters are remembered as **black hounds of the true road**, **hooked wings**, or **name-pursuers**. Threshold wardens are **last gateposts**, **deathbed shields**, or **lamps against the false shore**. Frightening imagery does not make them damned creatures, and local names do not establish separate species.

## Story Uses

- A terrifying hunter is mistaken for the cause of a haunting while it pursues the true predator.
- Defenders must keep a hospice intact until a warden completes the passage.
- A necromancer destroys the visible office and exposes the route behind it.
- A hunter pins an interceptor in the Material Realm, creating a brief chance for permanent defeat.
