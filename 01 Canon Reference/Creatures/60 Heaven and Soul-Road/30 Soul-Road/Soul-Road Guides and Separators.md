# Soul-Road Guides and Separators

Road guides and separators are impersonal manifestations of sacred infrastructure. They appear when a human soul must be distinguished from the residue, compulsions, copied memories, or attached forces surrounding death.

## Origin and Continuity

They are not people, angels, saints, or minor gods. Their continuity is an `infrastructure-office`: a bounded function made visible for as long as the route requires it. When the task ends or the manifestation is broken, no hidden person dies.

## Signs

- A single lit way appears where every false route loops.
- Corpse memories repeat behind a line they cannot cross.
- Knotted cords, feathers, doors, or stepping stones arrange themselves into a sequence.
- A dying person's true name can be heard apart from voices imitating it.
- Water or darkness divides cleanly around an unseen passage.

## Conduct and Tactics

Guides mark, open, and stabilize. Separators strip residue and foreign attachments away from the routed soul without erasing the person's identity. When resisted, the office repeats its distinction with increasing force: closing false doors, cutting copied names loose, or fixing an interceptor in place for a commissioned agent to confront.

The office cannot judge a person, answer unrelated questions, or be redirected to another destination. Attempts to command it either fail or damage the visible route without changing the Lord's authority over it.

## Failure and Restoration

A hostile power may obscure signs, overwhelm a threshold with false identities, or destroy the current manifestation. Restoration requires removing the obstruction, recovering the person's proper name or testimony, repairing the physical death-place, or receiving direct heavenly aid. The routed soul is endangered by delay and interception, not converted into the office.

## Materials and Story Uses

No harvestable body remains. Temporary signs may persist long enough to expose a forged identity, locate a hidden corpse, distinguish a vampire's retained soul from residue dead, or show where a soul-route has been wounded.

## Regional Names

Funeral traditions call these manifestations **line-lamps**, **name-dividers**, **last stepping stones**, **shore cords**, or **the clear door**. The names describe one bounded infrastructure profile rather than small psychopomp species.
