# Sapphire Drakes

Sapphire drakes are cold-water, lake, and mountain-river drakes. They hunt from below, nest in flooded caves, and collect polished stone, drowned tools, and bells. They are perfect for lake towns, ferry routes, monastery reservoirs, and submerged ruins.

## Creature Role

- **Encounter shape:** territorial hazard, lair guardian, regional pressure, and occasional trophy hunt.
- **Signs:** missing livestock, altered local wildlife, shed scales, changed weather or terrain around the lair.
- **Human use:** scales, teeth, eggs, and lair materials can drive black markets, noble hunts, military requisitions, or saintly prohibitions.

## Game Hooks

- A settlement wants the drake killed, but the creature is suppressing something worse.
- A faction wants a live egg for prestige, weaponry, or ritual leverage.
- The drake's lair sits on an old road, relic site, or thin-space wound.
- Local folklore misidentifies the drake as a saint sign, Menhir omen, or Idol punishment.
