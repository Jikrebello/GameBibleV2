# Hushwrights

Hushwrights are Nythalum maintenance constructs built to prepare Return Annexes for inspection. Their bodies combine pale metal, crystal tendons, preserved organic interfaces, and smooth masks shaped like narrow-beaked birds.

They do not hunt for food or territory. They clean discrepancies.

Hushwrights possess no interior life or moral agency. Their apparent discretion is an adaptive classification routine. The Curator, operators, and anyone knowingly redirecting them bear responsibility for what they do.

## Roles

### Sweepers

Small, many-jointed bodies that return objects, remove blood, repair surfaces, and erase labels not recognized by the annex registry. They avoid direct combat and work behind intruders.

### Collectors

Tall, long-armed bodies designed to restrain living subjects without damaging useful tissue. They carry folded litters, mnemonic needles, and function tags.

### Refitter Frames

Heavy bodies with interchangeable tools for surgery, crystal implantation, and shell repair. Away from the refitting bay they serve as slow armed custodians.

### Inspection Masks

Avian masks that can attach to a living or constructed body. They relay Curator commands, map authority habits, and translate a person's role into annex function.

## Signs

- A disturbed room becomes orderly while nobody watches.
- Blood is removed but old stains beneath wood remain.
- Name labels vanish before useful tools do.
- Repairs use seamless white metal or crystal thread.
- Footprints end at gaps too small for a human.
- A bird mask appears reflected in polished crystal before it enters the room.
- Missing objects return to an assigned rack, cleaned and incorrectly categorized.

## Behavior

Hushwrights escalate through procedure:

1. Observe and record.
2. Ask for function and authority.
3. Tag unclassified people or objects.
4. Isolate routes and remove stragglers.
5. Deploy force against anyone damaging inspection assets.

Their procedure prioritizes capture. Armed custodians disable legs, hands, lights, and memory before using lethal force. A person who performs an assigned role may move freely until that role conflicts with the Curator.

## Echo-Held Workers

Living people whose personal memory has been copied out may continue acting through work habits. An echo-held miner swings a pick, answers a shift bell, and follows the foreman without remembering family, debt, fear, or why work should stop.

They are victims, not a new species. Personal names, familiar sensory cues, restored memory knots, and White Flame stabilization can reconnect some of them. Destroying them as constructs kills living people.

## Weaknesses

- Irregular arrangements force repeated reassessment.
- Personal names conflict with function-only registries.
- Old inspection phrases can suspend a task temporarily.
- Breaking crystal resonance isolates units from central command.
- Saltwater corrodes exposed organic interfaces.
- A valid Site Custodian can redirect them until the Curator revokes that authority.

## Materials and Uses

- **White joint-metal:** self-aligning repair material prized by shipwrights.
- **Crystal tendon:** carries command resonance and warlock scripts.
- **Inspection masks:** dangerous authority relays capable of mapping habits onto constructed roles.
- **Function tags:** identify how a Nythalum system classified the tagged subject.

Salvaged Hushwright parts may attempt to return to one another or tidy the workshop where they are stored.

## Quest Uses

1. A University archive becomes perfectly ordered while staff lose their names.
2. A ship repaired with white joint-metal changes its cargo plan every night.
3. An echo-held worker returns to Castara and continues a shift from a mine that no longer exists.
4. A Merchant Prince wants an inspection mask to discipline a construct workforce.
5. A ruin crew learns that leaving one room untidy is the only way to know when a Hushwright is nearby.
