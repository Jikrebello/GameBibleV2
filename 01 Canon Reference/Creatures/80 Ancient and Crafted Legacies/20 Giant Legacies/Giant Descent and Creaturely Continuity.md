# Giant Descent and Creaturely Continuity

This note is the authorial ruling for Titan and Giant descent, personhood, death, survival, repentance, and false return. It controls later dynastic histories and creature dossiers. Blood, memory, office, legendary identity, and creaturely continuity must remain separate questions.

## Governing Formula

> **Blood is graduated; continuity is categorical. Blood may dilute. A soul does not fractionalize.**

A being with Giant ancestry is authorially one of the following: a Titan with `titan-continuity`, a Giant with `giant-continuity`, or a human person with `human-soul`. No one is partly ensouled, possesses a percentage of the Image, or moves gradually between these categories through breeding.

## First Conceptions and Titan-Firstborn

A **Titan** or **Titan-Firstborn** is the direct child of a human woman and one of three bounded parents:

- a generative manifestation of a Menhir;
- an Idol Throne acting through one of its dependent Masks;
- an autonomous Archmask whose separate agency and continuity have already been established.

The full Menhir or Idol sovereignty does not enter the Material Realm as an ordinary body. A dependent Idol Mask is an organ of its Throne, not a separate parent: the resulting Titan descends from the Idol sovereignty acting through that Mask. An autonomous Archmask may act in its own right, but does not thereby become a god or a second Idol.

The Titan is a new created person. It is not a Mask, Menhir court-being, severed piece of its parent, or human soul trapped in a monstrous body. Its birth does not divide, diminish, redeem, replace, or reproduce the parent sovereignty. Direct conception is exceptional and cannot be guaranteed by blood concentration, coercive rite, accurate genealogy, or possession of an earlier Titan's remains. Every alleged direct conception must be classified individually in authorial material; no dynasty or ritual receives the category by presumption.

The human mother remains fully human. Consent, coercion, captivity, complicity, and survival are historical and moral questions about particular acts; ancestry itself creates neither guilt nor holiness. Giant courts routinely concealed coercion, blamed mothers, and converted exceptional births into hereditary political claims. Their propaganda is not the authorial metaphysics.

## Later Giant Descent

### High Giants and God-Kin

**High Giants**, also called **God-Kin**, descend from Titan-Firstborn through unions with other Titans, Giants, or humans. They possess `giant-continuity`. They may inherit immense bodies, long life, altered senses, domain pressure, true-name constraints, and access to ancestral works, but they are neither gods nor extensions of their ancestors.

### Dynastic Giants

**Dynastic Giants** are later bearers of `giant-continuity`. Dilution usually reduces stature, raw force, inherited law, and ease of access to ancestral structures. It may also make later Giants more adaptable than their tightly defined Titan forebears. A weak or human-sized Giant remains a Giant if its actual continuity is `giant-continuity`.

### Giant-Blooded Humans

A **giant-blooded human** is a complete Image-bearing human person with `human-soul`. Such a person may be enormous, long-lived, visibly altered, capable of inherited powers, recognized by Giant instruments, or descended from a famous Titan. None of those facts makes the soul partial or converts the person into a Giant.

A mixed lineage can produce human persons and Giants in different generations. Courts may influence bodies, fertility, inherited traits, and access conditions, but they cannot manufacture the Image or guarantee the child's creaturely continuity. Authorial notes must state the result even when every in-world test remains disputed.

## Genealogy, Patronage, and Imperial Claims

Ancient Titanic ancestry can be genuine without validating a dynasty's complete theology. A court may descend remotely from a real Titan while falsely claiming that every ruler is the immediate, pure, and exclusive child of one named Menhir or Idol.

Menhir correspondence proves compatibility with natural law, not moral approval or rightful government. Idol patronage may alter bodies, blood rites, offices, and public imagery without making every later descendant a direct child of the Idol. Crowns, gates, relics, and ancestor engines may test a bounded access condition; none proves holiness, universal sovereignty, or possession of a human soul.

## Titan-Continuity: One Life Distributed

A Titan's living body remains one `titan-continuity` across separated authentic pieces. Ordinary bodily destruction can incapacitate, scatter, imprison, pulverize, or radically limit the Titan, but it cannot by itself complete the Titan's true death.

- Every authentic fragment belongs to the same person. Fragments are not children, independent souls, or separate Titans, even when reduced to particles or made dormant.
- A fragment may preserve sensation, intention, domain pressure, bodily memory, or only latent orientation toward the whole. A unique dossier states what an incomplete collection can express.
- Restoring all authentic fragments into their proper living relation reconstitutes the Titan unless a divine commission has ended or permanently locked that possibility. An incomplete collection may produce influence, a partial body, or a malformed attempted return, but cannot restore the whole Titan.
- A copied organ, manufactured substitute, office relic, memory image, or Titan-shaped construct is not a living fragment.
- Damage to one authentic fragment wounds the one Titan. Apparent destruction ordinarily reduces or suppresses that portion rather than turning it into an unrelated dead object.
- Binding may block communication, movement, nourishment, manifestation, or reassembly without killing the Titan.

A Titan truly dies only when the Lord directly ends that continuity or a specifically commissioned heavenly severance completes the assigned judgement. The same authority may instead permanently lock reconstitution while leaving the Titan alive and bound. Mortals can scatter, starve, suppress, and maintain bindings for ages, but cannot infer true death from bodily ruin. Once `titan-continuity` has genuinely ended, collecting remains or reconstructing memories can produce residue, an office, a biological work, or a constructed imitation, but never the dead Titan.

Human victims do not become pieces of a Titan by being consumed, sacrificed, copied, buried within it, or used to feed reconstitution. Their souls retain the human route.

## Giant-Continuity: Embodied and Uninterrupted

A High or Dynastic Giant is ordinarily one embodied nonhuman person with `giant-continuity`. Its children are new persons rather than continuations of the parent.

Ordinary bodily death ends `giant-continuity` when the Giant's living operation irreversibly stops. Healing, transformation, bodily regeneration, bodily escape, machine transfer, recurrence, or another mechanism preserves the same person only where a controlling dossier establishes an uninterrupted living route. Two canonical special cases illustrate the rule:

- an embodied Ansharan Vampire Giant remains the same Giant through unbroken bodily continuity;
- a Nythalum Continuant remains the same Giant only when conscious operation passes into and among machines without any interval of mere storage.

After interruption, memory cannot restart the person. Qianglong caste recurrences, Ishkatar title reconstructions, Xolotl blood memories, Nythalum backups, Ansharan court simulations, inherited offices, and performed legends may resemble a dead Giant while remaining something else. Each uses its actual continuity, normally `constructed-pattern`, `residue`, `infrastructure-office`, `biological-pattern`, `idol-pattern`, or an explicitly bound bearer.

## Egg White Crossing and Patronage

A living Titan, Giant, or giant-blooded human may cross bodily into the Egg White if a valid route and anchor permit it. Location and ownership do not change creaturely continuity:

- a Titan remains `titan-continuity`;
- a Giant remains `giant-continuity`;
- a giant-blooded human remains `human-soul`;
- an office, residue, Mask, or Reconstruction remains its own nonhuman continuity.

**Patron** is a role performed by an anchor-owner, not a species or afterlife. A surviving Giant may become a Patron without becoming a human soul, god, Idol, Menhir, or merged collective ancestor. Defeat must address both the person's continuity and any anchor, tether, proxy, or demiplane through which it acts.

An apparent escaped ancestor may instead be a copied ruler, inherited office, residue intelligence, Idol Mask, or later claimant using the dynasty's anchor. Memory, command recognition, and successful bargains do not prove that the original crossed.

Nythalum's imperial Ascension Project was physical travel to the physical Moon. Its Moonbase and Continuants are not Egg White flight, a translunar Astral current, or a concealed Second Ascension. A later Nythalum individual can encounter or enter the Egg White as a separate event without changing what the historical project was.

## Agency, Guilt, and Repentance

Titans and Giants capable of understanding their acts possess real moral agency. They can choose tyranny, restraint, loyalty, betrayal, mercy, or genuine repentance. Their lack of a human soul does not make suffering unreal or moral choice meaningless.

Repentance is a real turning from evil. It may require release of captives, truthful confession, restitution, surrender of a predatory office, destruction of harmful works, or acceptance of a just binding. It does not:

- manufacture the Image, enter human redemption, or open the human soul-road;
- make a Titan or Giant a human saint;
- erase victims, inherited damage, legal consequence, or the need for repair;
- redeem an Idol Throne or make Menhir law morally authoritative;
- turn an office, legend-persona, Mask, or copy into the repentant person;
- guarantee bodily survival or an authorially specified afterlife.

The final disposition of a nonhuman person belongs to the Lord and is not administered through the Vestibule merely because the being repented. A non-conscious Reconstruction cannot repent, sin, or receive forgiveness; it can only perform a representation of remorse. Responsibility remains with accountable makers, owners, commanders, and any genuine person acting through it.

## Authorial Classification Test

When an ancient claimant appears, answer in this order:

1. Is a complete human soul actually present?
2. Is the claimant one uninterrupted Titan or Giant life?
3. If distributed, which pieces and routes remain biologically or metaphysically alive?
4. Did any interval reduce the claimant to stored memory or records?
5. Is an office, residue, Mask, blood pattern, or constructed system speaking instead?
6. Does an Egg White anchor preserve the original, or merely authorize a successor claimant?
7. What permanently ends this exact continuity without harming an unrelated human soul?

Resemblance, inherited blood, self-description, private memory, a responsive artifact, and recognition by servants are evidence. None is decisive alone.

## Hard Boundaries

- No Titan, Giant, ancestor, or Patron is God. All are created and contingent.
- No Menhir, Idol, Giant, rite, machine, office, or bloodline can create the Image.
- Giant ancestry is not hereditary guilt, moral inferiority, or proof of rightful rule.
- A human soul never becomes Titan continuity, Giant continuity, residue, office, or Mask through remembrance or consumption.
- A dead Giant does not return merely because its memories, title, body-plan, or descendants survive.
- A Titan's distributed life must be established by authorial continuity, not inferred from any ancient body part.
- Repentance never erases historical consequence, and punishment never proves every victim personally guilty.
- Nythalum's physical Moon history cannot be reinterpreted as an Astral escape programme.
