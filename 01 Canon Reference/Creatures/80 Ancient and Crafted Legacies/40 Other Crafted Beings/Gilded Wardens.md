# Gilded Wardens

Gilded Wardens are Ishkatar funerary and treasury constructs built to weigh offerings, move grave goods, restrain intruders, and preserve the ownership claims of dead rulers and priests.

Their gilding is a control surface and status display rather than solid wealth. Removing it carelessly can activate recovery behavior in every linked warden.

## Encounter Role

Wardens function as readable moving hazards, guards, pursuit bodies, and heavy set-piece machines. They should reward understanding the assigned route, object, or category they enforce.

## Common Forms

- **Tally Hands:** small multi-armed inventory constructs that mark and retrieve objects.
- **Serpent-Mask Guards:** human-sized wardens that restrain unregistered entrants.
- **Processional Coils:** massive runner-mounted serpents that collect marked people or cargo along fixed ceremonial roads.
- **Coffin Wardens:** heavy guardians assembled from linked funerary masks and sarcophagus plates.

## Signs

- parallel grooves in floors too regular for carts;
- gold leaf worn bright at joints and sensor plates;
- paired serpent heads facing doors, scales, or storage niches;
- objects returned to exact outlines in dust;
- title marks appearing on gear after a metallic touch;
- deep scraping that stops when an inventory is spoken aloud.

## Behavior and Tactics

- scan for marks, seals, concealed goods, and assigned standing;
- recover property before attacking unrelated targets;
- use nets, clamps, closing doors, and bodily displacement;
- patrol fixed routes until a receiver changes their task;
- combine into larger guardian forms around a high-value object;
- ignore witnesses or declared kin until those people obstruct recovery.

Wardens do not bargain, but their instructions can be satisfied, contradicted, physically jammed, or superseded by a valid seal.

## Weaknesses

- route-bound behavior can be mapped and exploited;
- false weight or altered inventory creates command conflicts;
- exposed gold tally mirrors coordinate several models at once;
- receiver seals grant control but can also transfer the estate's claim to the bearer;
- loss of a title plaque leaves a warden unable to identify its protected object;
- glass dust abrades ancient runner channels and articulation.

## Ecology and Distribution

Gilded Wardens occur in Ishkatar tombs, treasury roads, temple storehouses, and ruined irrigation estates. Desert exposure often leaves them immobile until light, vibration, or the removal of grave goods resumes an interrupted inventory.

They are constructs rather than living reptile peoples and should not be conflated with drakes, Naga Giants, or later serpent cultists.

## Materials and Faction Use

- tally mirrors can coordinate constructs or identify marked artifacts;
- command plaques are valuable to ruin crews and dangerous in markets;
- joint alloys resist heat and abrasive sand;
- Covenant golemists study their category logic;
- Avarix cultists use recovered plates as false proof of inherited authority;
- salvagers strip decorative leaf only after disabling the receiving network.

## Quest Uses

- a warden follows a sold grave object through several owners and cities;
- a processional road reactivates beneath a modern caravan route;
- two receivers issue contradictory inventories and split a guardian household;
- a museum reconstructs a coffin warden from pieces believed to be unrelated;
- the safest way through a ruin is to become part of its ceremonial procession.
