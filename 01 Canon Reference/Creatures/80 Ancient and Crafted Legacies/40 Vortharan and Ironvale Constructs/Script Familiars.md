# Script Familiars

Script Familiars are compact physical constructs that classify speech, gesture, tools, routes, and familiar persons in order to assist a trained operator. Their animal forms make movement and communication legible: brass birds carry signal slips, ceramic hounds follow marked trails, spider frames inspect masonry, and floating lens cages observe a warlock's script.

Unlike [[Script-Bound Servitors]], a Script Familiar's continuing identity lies in a crafted instruction core rather than a Shell body. Both can simulate affection, alarm, curiosity, and loyalty. Neither possesses experience or moral agency.

## Signs and Lifecycle

- a maker's mark and owner relation are physically inspectable;
- expressive behavior repeats around the same classification problem;
- “sleep” is charging, maintenance, or archive compression;
- copied cores produce equally convincing siblings with no divided personhood.

Familiars last as long as their core remains legible and replacement bodies are available. They learn by adding classifications, not by becoming conscious.

## Behavior and Tactics

Most avoid battle, warn an operator, map routes, fetch marked equipment, test hazards, or record conditions. Guard models blind, trip, distract, mark targets, and sacrifice their bodies to preserve a core or task object. Stolen owner marks can redirect them; contradictory affection routines can be exploited but do not cause emotional pain.

## Failure and Permanent Defeat

Memory saturation, ambiguous pronouns, corrupted owner lists, and repeated emergency rules produce dangerous drift. Deleting the active core and every synchronized copy ends a familiar. Breaking one body does not if its core escaped or was backed up.

## Materials and Regional Names

Valuable parts include classification plates, miniature joints, voice reeds, optical records, and ownership seals. Names include *script pets*, *brass companions*, *workshop imps*, and *little masters*.
