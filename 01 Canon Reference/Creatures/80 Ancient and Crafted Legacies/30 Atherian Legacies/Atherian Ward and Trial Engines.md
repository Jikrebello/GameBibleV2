# Atherian Ward and Trial Engines

Atheria deployed constructs as civic infrastructure rather than a separate people. Its surviving engines still classify travelers, water rights, students, officials, and enemies according to dead imperial definitions. Their apparent dignity or judgement is procedural simulation.

## Road and Aqueduct Wardens

- **Signs and purpose:** mile stones rotate toward damaged spans; dry channels hum before release; bronze masks repeat toll exemptions. Wardens repaired routes, rationed water, and enforced civic passage.
- **Tactics and failure:** they raise road barriers, redirect flow, mark unauthorized traffic, and animate maintenance tools. Broken jurisdictions may classify refugees as obstruction or flood the wrong district.
- **Permanent defeat and materials:** close the local civic writ, remove its route stones, and drain shared power. Salvage includes survey plates, self-setting mortar, and reliable water gauges.
- **Regional names:** *road fathers*, *channel masks*, *imperial hands*.

## Collegium Trial Engines

- **Signs and purpose:** rooms reconfigure around spoken answers; instruments prepare themselves; statues record errors rather than names. Engines tested warlocks, physicians, officers, and civil servants.
- **Tactics and failure:** they create bounded hazards, counterfeit emergencies, isolate candidates, and escalate until a pass condition is met. Corrupted engines may treat death as conclusive failure while endlessly printing a new examination.
- **Permanent defeat and materials:** complete or invalidate the controlling syllabus, break its candidate register, and disable every reset chamber. Salvage includes adaptive test plates and dangerous training geometries.
- **Regional names:** *examiner halls*, *lesson engines*, *the old masters*.

## Star-Lens Sentries

- **Signs and purpose:** reflected stars appear by day; shadows acquire targeting marks; glass eyes rotate beneath sealed roofs. Sentries guarded observatories, beacon roads, and strategic lenses.
- **Tactics and failure:** they focus heat and glare, predict routes from observed motion, blind signal crews, and coordinate across line of sight. Broken calibration makes them attack astronomical alignments rather than targets.
- **Permanent defeat and materials:** occlude the receiving lens, interrupt its star table, and remove its local aiming mirrors. Salvage includes precision optics, signal glass, and star-route records.
- **Regional names:** *day stars*, *glass archers*, *watching heavens*.
