# Ogres

Ogres are the heavy-bodied siege and labor caste among the Grobi peoples. They are not stupid by default; they are slow to trust, difficult to redirect once committed, and terrifyingly useful in any society built around migration, hauling, fortification, or breaking gates.

## Creature Role

- **Battlefield role:** shock troops, shield-breakers, living rams, and pack-haulers.
- **Social role:** clan anchors, winter laborers, bodyguards, and symbols of a leader's logistical power.
- **Weakness:** hunger, heat stress, slow recovery from injury, and dependence on smaller Grobi for scouting and negotiation.

## Culture Notes

Ogres are often treated by outsiders as monsters and by Grobi factions as necessary kin. A war-chief who wastes ogres loses legitimacy. A matron who can feed and protect them gains bargaining power.

## Game Hooks

- An injured ogre child forces players to decide whether the migration is only an invasion or also a refugee crisis.
- A war-chief is using ogres as disposable siege engines, angering a survival faction inside the Grobi host.
- A bank, duke, or army wants captured ogres turned into forced labor.
- An ogre remembers an older route, ruin, or bargain that smaller Grobi have forgotten.
