# Oathwyrms

Oathwyrms are immense solitary reptiles whose bodies grow around a long-lived boundary compact. They fill the creator-facing role of linnorms without becoming a species of true dragon, Giant, or devil.

Their ancestors were Menhir-shaped fauna. An individual becomes an Oathwyrm only after surviving long enough for one territorial promise, curse, or prohibition to organize its memory and growth. Each is therefore biologically related but metaphysically singular.

## Law and Agency

- **Domain:** one named boundary, prohibition, refuge, succession, or vengeance.
- **Compulsion:** test every claimant against the compact it carries.
- **Taboo:** the exact act the founding compact forbade.
- **Bargain:** passage, protection, weather knowledge, or release from its hunt in exchange for assuming a defined obligation.
- **Price:** the obligation outlives convenience and may outlive the bargainer.

Oathwyrms are sapient and possess full moral agency. An ancient wrong can establish a real claim without excusing torture, inherited collective punishment, or deliberate lies about what the oath requires.

## Signs and Ecology

An Oathwyrm eats like an apex animal but arranges its territory like a legal memory. Carcasses mark boundaries. Shed scales bear repeated symbols. Weather or animal routes stop at one line. Local stories agree on a prohibition while disagreeing about why it exists.

Southern Oathwyrms favor glacial valleys, cold coasts, and permafrost halls. Warmer northern examples inhabit exposed ridges and desert escarpments. Their presence follows old boundaries more strongly than climate.

## Threat Pattern

They use immense bodies, breath shaped by habitat, constriction, terrain collapse, and a dying curse tied to the compact. Their curse is not automatic moral authority. It is court-law released without a living body to interpret it.

## Weaknesses and Permanent Defeat

- recover the compact's complete wording and expose any false clause the Wyrm added;
- fulfill, transfer, or lawfully dissolve the obligation under equivalent witness;
- separate the creature from the boundary that feeds its regeneration;
- destroy its body, oath-organ, and the physical anchor that would regrow the curse;
- survive the dying curse by repairing the underlying wrong rather than merely resisting its effect.

Killing the Wyrm without settling its compact leaves a curse-site capable of selecting a new carrier.

## Materials and Regional Names

Oath scales preserve one prohibition. The oath-organ can anchor a vast ward or curse and is among the most dangerous forms of creature salvage. Bones remain aligned to the boundary for centuries.

Names include Oathwyrm, linnorm, curse-wyrm, pass father, coast coil, and hall-serpent.

## Story Hooks

- An Oathwyrm protects a refugee road whose founding people no longer exist.
- A king claims the Wyrm recognizes his dynasty, but its compact names an office rather than a bloodline.
- Slayers kill a Wyrm and release its inherited curse across every village that used the pass.
- A dying Oathwyrm seeks someone willing to assume a just duty it no longer trusts itself to enforce.
- An apparent true-dragon cult is actually preserving the anchor of an Oathwyrm's boundary.
