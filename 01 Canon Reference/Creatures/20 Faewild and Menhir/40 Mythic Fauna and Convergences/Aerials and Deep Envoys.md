# Aerials and Deep Envoys

The Third Circle produces beings adapted to conditions that ordinary life cannot inhabit. Aerials belong to Zephyraxis-shaped storm systems. Deep Envoys belong to local Thalassor ecologies. Neither family constitutes a universal civilization or speaks for a Menhir.

## Aerials

Aerials are court-law intelligences formed in long-lived pressure fronts, mountain-wave systems, and storms that repeatedly follow one track. At rest they resemble transparent ribbons, avian silhouettes, or people assembled from cloud scraps and debris held in suspension.

- **Domain:** pressure differential, altitude, migration, and structural testing.
- **Compulsion:** move from pressure toward release while carrying loose things with them.
- **Taboo:** a path sealed at both ends without a vent or declared refuge.
- **Bargain and price:** a safe wind, lifted burden, or weather warning in exchange for opening a route, carrying storm-seed, or surrendering a spoken message to the sky.
- **Signs:** speech stripped from mouths, hanging objects leaning into still air, birds changing height together, and dust climbing walls.
- **Threat:** vertical displacement, sudden pressure loss, thrown debris, and separating travelers by altitude.
- **Defeat:** ground the pressure route, equalize the enclosed space, release what the Aerial carries, or break the storm-anchor.

Most Aerials possess cunning and limited agency. Stable elders that understand consequence and negotiate across seasons possess full agency.

## Deep Envoys

Deep Envoys are pressure-adapted biological peoples and solitary organisms associated with particular trenches, wreck-fields, or anchor complexes. They possess gills, pressure-flesh, bioluminescent signaling, and tool traditions suited to darkness and crushing water.

They form local households and work groups. There is no worldwide Deep Empire, common sovereign, or single attitude toward the surface. Some harvest wrecks, some maintain pressure anchors, some lure prey, and some trade warnings or salvage.

- **Domain:** not a legal office; their ecology follows Thalassor-shaped depth, current, and pressure.
- **Compulsion:** biological need for pressure, mineral salts, and stable current paths.
- **Taboo:** regional and cultural, never a universal racial law.
- **Signs:** weighted knots, arranged wreckage, repeating cold lights, tools cut for pressure-gloved hands, and currents maintained through artificial channels.
- **Threat:** attacks from vertical darkness, line cutting, pressure shock, entanglement, and retreat into depths humans cannot follow.
- **Defeat:** bodily death is permanent. Closing or negotiating access to a pressure anchor may end a conflict without destroying a community.

Sapient Deep Envoys possess full moral agency. Predatory ecology does not excuse slavery, murder, or treating every shipwrecked person as salvage.

## Materials and Regional Names

Aerial condensate briefly stores pressure. Deep Envoy tools, pressure membranes, and current maps are practical artifacts, not proof of worship.

Regional names include sky wrack, gale folk, wind riders, deep envoys, trench keepers, blackwater people, and the inaccurate collective term Deep Ones.

## Story Hooks

- An Aerial carries the final words of a lost expedition but releases them only at the storm's destination.
- A Deep Envoy work group repairs a pressure anchor beneath a harbor whose pilots think they are preparing an invasion.
- Wreckers imitate trench lights and provoke retaliation against the wrong coast.
- A stable Aerial demands that a mountain pass remain open during a fatal winter storm.
- Two unrelated Deep Envoy communities are treated as one enemy after a single predatory raid.
