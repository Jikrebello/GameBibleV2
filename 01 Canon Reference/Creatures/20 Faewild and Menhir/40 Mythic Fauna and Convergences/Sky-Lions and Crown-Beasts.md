# Sky-Lions and Crown-Beasts

These are living animals shaped over generations where Menhir ecologies overlap. Their unusual bodies are stable, reproductive, and biological. Beauty, rarity, or association with a court does not make them morally good or secretly human.

## Sky-Lions

Sky-Lions combine a great cat's hindquarters and hunting behavior with a broad avian chest, hooked forelimbs, and wings. Regional populations resemble eagles, vultures, lammergeiers, hawks, or cold-coast sea birds.

- **Ecology:** cliff-nesting pair or pride predators that hunt goats, Great-Horn calves, horses, and exposed pack animals.
- **Signs:** split tracks ending at a ledge, plucked carcasses, whitewash above thermal vents, and bone-lined launch shelves.
- **Threat:** stoop attacks, forced falls, separation of mounts from riders, and carrying prey toward inaccessible nests.
- **Weakness:** poor turning inside dense woods, vulnerable launch windows, nest loyalty, and exhaustion when forced to remain aloft without thermals.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death. Relocation of eggs or prey routes can move a pride, though stealing eggs creates a long pursuit.

Trained Sky-Lions are rare military and courier mounts. Training does not make them tame livestock, and broken animals remain dangerous.

## Crown-Beasts

Crown-Beast is a creator family for court-marked herbivores and omnivores: white harts, moon-mares, antlered boars, winged coursers, glass-horned goats, and similar regional phenotypes. A local population normally expresses one Menhir pressure rather than every noble trait at once.

- **Ecology:** migration, rut, browsing, seed movement, and predator support remain primary.
- **Signs:** tracks following impossible seasonal lines, vegetation cut to one height, shed horn or feather carrying a weak court tendency, and ordinary animals yielding a route.
- **Threat:** stampede, territorial rut, lure into court-thin ground, and herds that enforce a migration corridor through settlement.
- **Weakness:** ordinary biological needs, season, herd behavior, and terrain.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death, though removing a herd may destabilize the court ecology it carried.

## Materials and Law

Wing spars, horn, hide, milk, and court-marked bone are valuable. Sustainable shed material is widely traded. Poaching breeding animals can collapse both a mundane herd and the local law it stabilizes.

Regional names include sky-lion, cliff griffon, horse-eagle, white crown, moon-mare, king's hart, winged courser, and bright-horn.

## Story Hooks

- A courier corps needs one Sky-Lion egg returned before its parents begin hunting road traffic.
- A Crown-Beast migration crosses a newly fortified border and both armies interpret it as an omen.
- Poachers kill a white hart that was keeping a thin-space path closed.
- A supposed sacred mount is an ordinary animal painted to resemble a Crown-Beast.
- A starving village can survive only by hunting a court-marked herd whose route sustains the forest.
