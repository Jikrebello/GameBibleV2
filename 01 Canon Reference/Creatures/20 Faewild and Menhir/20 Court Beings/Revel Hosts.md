# Revel Hosts

Revel Hosts are court-beings of invitation, music, appetite, repetition, and the dangerous refusal to let a gathering end. They appear in whatever bodies best fit the celebration: horned dancers, masked musicians, ribbon-limbed acrobats, or elegant guests whose feet never touch the floor.

They overlap with [[The Cloven]] at forest festivals, but they are not the same family. Cloven express living land and succession. Revel Hosts form around the social and rhythmic law of the gathering itself.

## Formation and Persistence

A Host condenses when invitation, synchronized performance, shared appetite, and an unfinished ending repeat at a Faewild-thin gathering. Minor masks and musicians may last one night; established Hosts preserve themselves through recurring festivals, inherited invitations, or a master rhythm carried between venues. Closing the gathering ends that manifestation, while an intact invitation-anchor can assemble it again.

## Court Law

- **Domain:** invitation, synchronized movement, shared appetite, and formal closure.
- **Compulsion:** intensify a gathering until someone completes or breaks its ending rite.
- **Taboo:** refusing a freely accepted invitation while continuing to consume the host's food, music, shelter, or status.
- **Bargain:** skill, courage, news, or one honest performance can buy passage through a revel.
- **Price:** the guest leaves behind a habit, song, appetite, public embarrassment, or obligation to close another unfinished gathering.

No compulsion excuses coercion. A Host that drugs guests, prevents meaningful refusal, or deliberately dances people to death has chosen predation.

## Signs and Encounter Pattern

Music continues after instruments stop. Food returns to platters with bites missing. Dancers repeat a sequence while growing older. Roads lead back to the same lanterns. Invitations contain no ending hour.

Hosts use rhythm to separate action from intention. They force movement, exchange masks, make allies mirror one another, and turn applause into physical pressure. Violence strengthens a feast built around contest but weakens one built around courtship or mourning.

## Weaknesses and Permanent Defeat

- identify the gathering's true purpose and perform its proper closing;
- withdraw consent before accepting further benefit;
- name every absent or dead guest whom the Host has erased from the celebration;
- break the master rhythm with a truthful counter-song or unsynchronized labor;
- destroy the invitation-anchor after all trapped guests have crossed its threshold.

Worked iron only helps when used as an ordinary tool to fix a boundary, bell, or closing gate. Waving a blade does not cancel hospitality.

## Materials and Names

Masks retain one borrowed mannerism. Revel strings can hold a rhythm without a musician. Feast salt exposes a guest who has eaten without being invited.

Regional names include horned host, ribbon court, last dancers, satyrs, midsummer company, and the guests after midnight.
