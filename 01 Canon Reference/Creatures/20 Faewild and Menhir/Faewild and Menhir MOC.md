# Faewild and Menhir MOC

Historical reading: [[Menhir Courts through the Ages]], within the [[World Historical Atlas MOC]].

The Faewild is creation under concentrated natural and mythic law. Its beings are not automatically benevolent, corrupt, or divine. Menhir are intelligent but morally blind sovereignties; their courts are ecologies and observed affiliations rather than universal governments.

## Governing Rules

- **Origin:** [[The Great Rebellion and the Yolk Exiles]] controls the angelic cohorts and their flight into the yolks.
- **Sovereignty:** [[Menhir Sovereignty and Bargain]] controls present agency, moral blindness, bargains, manifestations, and limits.
- **History:** [[The Faewild Schism]] controls Trium's fracture and the observed Circles.
- **Natural law is not moral truth:** hunger, winter, tide, metamorphosis, and pursuit can be lawful and terrible without becoming verdicts.
- **Agency varies below the sovereign:** a sapient court person remains responsible for chosen cruelty; fauna and bounded law-organisms may not possess that capacity.
- **Court is observational:** Folk courts may include courtiers, place minds, animals, residue, patrons, and counterfeits. They are not states.
- **Iron is situational:** worked iron stabilizes some Material boundaries but is not a universal poison.
- **Continuity controls defeat:** court-law must be satisfied, displaced, exposed, or broken. Biological court-fauna die as living creatures.
- **No hidden nations:** sapient families form local households, retinues, and solitary powers without adding a second geopolitical map.
