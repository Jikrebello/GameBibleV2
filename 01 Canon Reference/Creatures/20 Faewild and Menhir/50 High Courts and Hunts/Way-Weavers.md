# Way-Weavers

Way-Weavers are Hecatara-aligned court persons who perceive routes and the consequences attached to taking them. They do not control fate, judge souls, or know every possible future. They see how present roads, choices, tolls, and boundaries constrain what can follow.

Their stable forms resemble tall figures woven from road dust, keys, knotted cord, and strips of maps that show only places the witness could still reach.

## Formation and Persistence

A Way-Weaver forms where several consequential routes remain open long enough for Hecatara's law to acquire a stable observer. It persists through remembered crossings, carried route-keys, and choices whose alternatives remain materially possible. Closing every route can end a manifestation; an individual reforms only if its own key-thread and unresolved crossing survive.

## Court Law

- **Domain:** route consequence, crossroads, toll, access, and the cost of keeping options open.
- **Compulsion:** make a concealed route or consequence legible before a choice becomes irreversible.
- **Taboo:** declaring that no choice existed while hiding an available road.
- **Bargain:** reveal a route, an escape, or the likely cost of a decision.
- **Price:** close another option, surrender the right to retrace one path, carry a toll to a future crossing, or let another traveler use the route first.

Way-Weavers possess full agency. They may state a consequence truthfully while arranging choices to benefit themselves. Precision is not moral neutrality.

## Signs and Encounter Pattern

- road dust forming branching diagrams indoors;
- keys fitting doors that do not yet exist;
- maps losing routes after someone refuses them;
- speech separating into several audible endings;
- knots tightening whenever a hidden option is denied.

Way-Weavers close exits, exchange destinations, make a toll follow the traveler, and force combatants to experience the immediate consequence of their chosen approach. They are strongest where several real routes remain.

## Binding and Misuse

Contract courts prize Way-Weavers because they can expose concealed paths through an agreement. Capturing one does not create heavenly authority or infallible judgement. A bound Weaver can be forced to display consequences while being prevented from showing alternatives.

One corrupt Eclessian canon court holds such a captive and calls it an angelic witness. This is the bound being encountered through [[Zafir the Bound Chainmaster|Zafir's]] Grenzburg arc. It is a Way-Weaver, not a true angel.

## Weaknesses and Permanent Defeat

- reduce the encounter to one genuinely available route;
- expose an option the Weaver concealed from its own declaration;
- pay or reject the stated toll before taking benefit;
- cut every route-anchor and return stolen keys or maps;
- release the Weaver from an imposed office, after which it remains an independent court-being rather than disappearing.

Map-thread and route keys retain one use. Harvesting them from a captive Weaver extends the captivity.

## Regional Names

Way-Weaver, crossroad wife, key sister, road norn, fork speaker, and the one who shows the other door.

## Story Hooks

- A Way-Weaver proves a condemned prisoner had another choice, but revealing it exposes the judge's secret road.
- A border court binds a Weaver to make every refugee route return to the same gate.
- The party can escape a siege by permanently surrendering the road home.
- A Weaver asks help recovering routes cut from its body and sold as maps.
- Three apparently contradictory prophecies are consequences of three still-open choices, not competing destinies.
