# Hunt Hounds and Hunt Riders

There is no single Wild Hunt. High Hunts are temporary retinues assembled when Orionis, a Horseman pressure, or another Menhir court makes pursuit the dominant law across a region.

## Ecology and Formation

Hunt Hounds are bred court-fauna with ordinary bodily lifecycles, though a court route can accelerate growth or imprint a quarry-sign. Hunt Riders are temporary court-law offices assumed by existing sapient courtiers. A Hunt begins with a witnessed declaration, route and quarry-sign; it persists only while those three remain connected.

## Hunt Hounds

Hunt Hounds are biological court-fauna bred or gathered for supernatural pursuit. Regional forms include moon hounds, ash-mouthed summer curs, antlered forest dogs, and storm-running longhounds.

- **Signs:** tracks that ignore terrain, prey paths converging, distant baying heard through sealed walls, and animals facing one direction at once.
- **Threat:** scent across disguise, relay pursuit without sleep, drive quarry toward a chosen boundary, and pin rather than kill when the Hunt demands capture.
- **Weakness:** each pack follows a declared quarry-sign. Remove, transfer, or truthfully disprove that sign and the pack loses supernatural certainty.
- **Permanent defeat:** bodily death. A slain pack can be replaced only if the Hunt still possesses the quarry-sign and a court route.

Hounds have animal intelligence and no moral agency. Their Riders remain responsible for how they are used.

## Hunt Riders

Hunt Riders are sapient court-law persons who assume a temporary office in a named pursuit. Their forms reflect season and court: bark-armored riders, pale winter figures, storm silhouettes, or masked hunters on Crown-Beasts.

- **Domain:** pursuit, selection, mastery, and the proof produced by capture.
- **Compulsion:** continue until the named quarry is caught, released by proper authority, or proven not to fit the declaration.
- **Taboo:** substituting an easier victim after naming the quarry.
- **Bargain and price:** a Rider may delay, redirect, or admit a mortal hunter in exchange for a true trail, a surrendered refuge, or future service as quarry-beater.
- **Threat:** path compression, quarry marks, coordinated Hounds, and making safe ground count as open trail.

Riders possess full agency. A valid hunt for a murderer does not permit killing everyone who shelters them, and a court declaration cannot make an innocent person guilty.

## Breaking a Hunt

A Hunt ends when its declaration is fulfilled, exposed as false, lawfully released, or deprived of every route into the hunted territory. Killing the Master may scatter one manifestation but does not settle the quarry-sign. Worked iron helps bar a threshold only when the Hunt's own law recognizes that threshold as closed.

Hound teeth keep scent; Rider masks hold one quarry-sign; hunt horns can open a route once before cracking.

## Regional Names

High Hunt, wild hunt, winter riders, antler host, sky chase, moon hounds, ash curs, and the riders behind weather.

## Story Hooks

- A Hunt names a genuine murderer who is hiding among innocent refugees.
- A false quarry-sign turns a seasonal Hunt into an assassination weapon.
- One Rider refuses an unlawful substitution and asks mortals to prove the declaration corrupt.
- A slain Hound's tooth continues pointing toward someone the party believed innocent.
- A town can close its gates to the Hunt only by surrendering an ancient right of refuge.
