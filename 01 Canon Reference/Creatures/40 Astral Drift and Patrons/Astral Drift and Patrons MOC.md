## Creator Language

- **Patron** is a role held by an anchor-owner, not a species or moral alignment.
- **Genie** is a regional umbrella for Anchor-Lords, not an elemental people.
- **Lich** is creator shorthand for several persistence projects. Every named case must declare its actual continuity.
- [[Severed Exalts]] are singular named beings, not a reusable family.
