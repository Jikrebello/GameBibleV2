# Patron Servitors

These forms recur because anchor-owners need agents, bodies, and route control. Patron is a role, not an origin or moral alignment. A benevolent refuge, predatory rent lord, honest merchant, Anchor-Lord, human lich, or institutional demiplane may use the same broad servitor form for different ends.

## Tether Hounds

Tether Hounds are low, many-legged Astral fauna bred or condensed to follow a relationship between an anchor and what belongs to it.

- **Origin and agency:** animal astral-pattern with limited agency. Training can redirect a hound, but its world remains scent-like tether relation.
- **Ecology:** they den in route knots, feed on shed coherence, and reproduce when one anchor maintains many remote assets.
- **Signs:** tension marks appear around doors, a distant pulling sound follows a marked person, and discarded collateral gathers into nests.
- **Threat pattern:** track across layers, bite a tether rather than flesh, drag prey toward the owner, and become stronger when targets carry patron-granted power.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** distinguish the valid tether from imposed ownership, ground or lawfully sever it, then defeat the hound away from the owner's current. Cutting every tether blindly may strand innocent dependents.
- **Materials:** tether whiskers identify the direction and emotional character of an owner relationship.
- **Regional names:** rent hounds, cord dogs, claim runners, and home-pullers.

## Proxy Bodies

Proxy Bodies are temporary Material or demiplane forms through which an anchor-owner can act at a distance. They may resemble the owner, an idealized envoy, a local person, a statue, a weather pattern, or a deliberately impersonal suit.

- **Origin and agency:** cunning astral-pattern with no independent moral agency. The owner remains responsible for directed acts. A severed Proxy can continue a short instruction but never becomes a new person.
- **Ecology:** a Proxy requires mass at the destination, a return tether, and periodic instruction. Damage increases rent and may expose the owner's continuity.
- **Signs:** gestures arrive a fraction before speech, the body has no history outside its task, wounds leak route-light, and mirrors show the distant anchor.
- **Threat pattern:** changes anatomy within the licensed template, withdraws through the tether, spends local mass for sudden power, and attempts to make opponents attack the anchor relationship rather than the body.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** identify the tether, isolate the local mass, sever communication, and dismantle the body. This ends the manifestation, not the owner. If the owner is a human soul, construct, Drift person, or other continuity, its dossier governs further defeat.
- **Materials:** proxy matter retains the last instruction and can prove who exercised control if examined before it frays.
- **Regional names:** borrowed bodies, far hands, lease faces, and speaking suits.

## Current Shepherds

Current Shepherds are sapient native Astral persons adapted to tending lanes, separating predator flows, and moving unstable settlements. Some serve patrons by oath, wages, dependence, coercion, or shared interest; others work independently.

- **Origin and agency:** sapient astral-pattern with full moral agency. Service never erases personal responsibility or makes the Shepherd an extension of the patron.
- **Ecology:** Shepherds maintain current tools, living lane marks, and small flocks of Protons that clear or thicken routes. Their bodies resemble layered veils around a stable core and change with the currents they tend.
- **Signs:** route turbulence parts into parallel streams, proton shoals move in deliberate patterns, and a voice arrives from the safest direction rather than the speaker.
- **Threat pattern:** redirects movement, folds pursuit into cross-currents, releases managed fauna, and moves allies through brief safe lanes. A Shepherd avoids fighting where route collapse would kill bystanders.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** ordinary bodily defeat disperses the person unless a patron retains a return pattern. Counter the route tools, not an assumed elemental affinity. Negotiation depends on the Shepherd's own obligations and choices.
- **Materials:** current crooks and route veils are personal equipment, not biological harvest.
- **Regional names:** lane shepherds, current hands, veil pilots, and road tenders.
