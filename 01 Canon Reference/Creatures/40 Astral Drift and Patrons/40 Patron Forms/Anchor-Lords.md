# Anchor-Lords

Anchor-Lords are native Astral persons whose bodies, names, and owned anchors have become stable enough to support palace-demiplanes. Mortal travelers often call them genies. They are not elementals, one species, or a hidden nation.

## Continuity and Personhood

An Anchor-Lord uses astral-pattern continuity and possesses full moral agency. It is not a human soul, although some imitate human courts or trace their first stable forms to human stories. Killing a manifestation does not necessarily destroy the person if the palace anchor retains a return pattern.

Each Lord must be treated as an individual. Some are just rulers, some predators, some hospitable merchants, some tyrants, and many are internally consistent in ways that still harm mortals. Astral origin creates no moral alignment.

## Palace Ecology

An Anchor-Lord becomes identifiable when personal continuity, metaphysical mass, and a governing rule reinforce one another. The palace is therefore body, habitat, treasury, and political instrument at once.

Common palace anchors include a cup that always returns, a folded wind, a name spoken by every guest, a route knot, a fossil law, or a repeated act of shelter. The Lord pays upkeep through service, trade, ritual, harvested coherence, or the lawful function of the realm.

## Signs

- architecture responds to etiquette before spoken command;
- the host's anatomy echoes doors, weather, or furnishings;
- gifts alter access to rooms rather than merely changing ownership;
- every exit expresses the same governing rule;
- the Lord remains locally present while no single body is visible.

## Powers and Threat Pattern

Anchor-Lords edit routes, hospitality, scale, weather-like Drift pressure, and limited local reality within their palace law. They can create Proxy Bodies, employ Current Shepherds, and lease shelter or power. Outside the anchor regime they are weaker, expensive to manifest, and dependent on tethers.

They cannot create human souls, reverse judgement, become elemental sovereigns, or make an immoral demand good by expressing it as palace law.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Every named Anchor-Lord requires a stated anchor, governing rule, upkeep, and return condition. To defeat one permanently, separate the person from the palace's mass, close its return pattern, and destroy or lawfully transfer the anchor. Collapsing a palace without evacuation may kill dependents and scatter dangerous contents into Drift.

## Materials and Trade

Palace matter, route favors, shelter rights, and reality edits are valuable. An Anchor-Lord's hospitality may be genuinely protective and its price genuinely fair; investigators must judge the actual terms and consequences rather than assuming every Astral bargain is infernal.

## Regional Names

Genie, cup lord, wind prince, palace mind, anchor sovereign, and white king are local umbrellas. None identifies ancestry or moral character.

## Story Uses

- A fair Anchor-Lord cannot shelter more refugees without evicting a violent but lawfully admitted client.
- A palace tyrant's death would collapse the only stable route between two communities.
- Rival Lords ask mortals to adjudicate a border because Material witness provides density neither can manufacture.
