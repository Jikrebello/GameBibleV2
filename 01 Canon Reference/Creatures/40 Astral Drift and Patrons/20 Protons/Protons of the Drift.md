# Protons of the Drift

Proton is the Vortharan umbrella for small native Astral patterns that behave like fauna. They are not atoms, souls, elementals, or one lineage. Each species condenses around a repeatable pressure in unfinished reality.

## Lacuna Rays

Flat, translucent shapes that swim through missing information and widen it into navigable space.

- **Ecology:** graze on omitted names, blank map areas, forgotten instructions, and deliberately unrecorded rooms.
- **Signs:** text develops smooth gaps, shadows cross blank walls, and travelers forget why one route mattered.
- **Threat pattern:** erase route knowledge, hide predators inside absences, and fold around a victim until companions cannot describe where the person stands.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** restore the omitted fact from independent evidence and pin the Ray inside a fully described boundary. Bodies dissolve into clear film.
- **Materials:** lacuna film can conceal one written line until the film dries.

## Anchor Leeches

Heavy black-gold organisms that attach to relics, names, buildings, and people with high metaphysical mass.

- **Ecology:** feed on stability and excrete brittle counterfeit ballast. Dense colonies form around underfunded demiplanes.
- **Signs:** anchors feel lighter, upkeep costs climb without visible damage, and surfaces acquire circular pressure scars.
- **Threat pattern:** drain protections, leap to the strongest carried relic, and burst into a cloud of false weights when struck.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** isolate the true anchor, provide a sacrificial inert ballast, then destroy the colony away from a tether.
- **Materials:** leech pearls indicate how much stability was consumed but cannot replace it.

## Mnemonic Kites

Winged ribbon forms that feed on memories shed during Astral travel.

- **Ecology:** follow caravans, grief processions, and repeated routes. They prefer memories a traveler rehearses to remain coherent.
- **Signs:** familiar stories lose sensory detail, colored ribbons appear overhead, and companions remember the same event from an outside view.
- **Threat pattern:** peel rehearsed memory from a target, replay it as decoy terrain, and lure travelers toward places associated with loss.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** stop rehearsal, establish present sensory anchors, and cut the Kite's tail-knot. Captured memories can be returned imperfectly.
- **Materials:** tail knots contain compressed scenes but no soul or personal identity.

## Causality Crabs

Many-legged patterns with shells displaying effects before causes occur.

- **Ecology:** scavenge around failed experiments, nested pockets, repeated time structures, and routes whose return precedes departure.
- **Signs:** wounds bruise before impact, doors close before a hand moves, and debris gathers beneath an intact object.
- **Threat pattern:** exchange cause and consequence locally, make a defense trigger its own threat, and retreat into the event that attracted them.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** establish a simple witnessed sequence, remove recursive triggers, and break the shell only after the predicted effect has been prevented.
- **Materials:** shell plates preserve one cause-effect pair and are useful in forensic demonstrations.

## Glass-Spawn Shoals

Schools of knife-like translucent forms condensed from divine debris, broken demiplane walls, and minute anchor shards.

- **Ecology:** graze on microfractures and become more dangerous around hurried repairs. They are both scavengers and a source of further breakage.
- **Signs:** glitter moving against currents, tiny cuts appearing under clothing, and patched walls sounding like surf.
- **Threat pattern:** strip coatings from tethers, flow through narrow seams, reflect hostile magic unpredictably, and form larger hunting shapes under pressure.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** draw the shoal into a low-entropy basin, ground it with dull mass, and fuse or crush the shards.
- **Materials:** sorted spawn glass is valuable for wards and route lenses.

## Vow-Wasps

Insect-like patterns whose hives are made from repeated promises and whose stings compel completion of the exact phrase they carry.

- **Ecology:** form where vows are used as structural upkeep. They prefer precise repeated promises regardless of whether the promise is wise or just.
- **Signs:** speech hardens into amber threads, wasp comb appears around signatures, and minor promises become painfully difficult to abandon.
- **Threat pattern:** sting targets with a remembered commitment, build barriers from contradictory vows, and swarm anyone attempting to revise a structural promise.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** distinguish the valid obligation from the phrase, release or fulfill it through the proper witnesses, then dissolve the comb. Casual oath-breaking feeds the hive.
- **Materials:** vow-comb can stabilize one bounded promise but does not make an unjust promise morally valid.
