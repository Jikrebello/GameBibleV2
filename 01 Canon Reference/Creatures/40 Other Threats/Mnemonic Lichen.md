# Mnemonic Lichen

Mnemonic lichen is a blue-white crystal growth cultivated by Nythalum to copy sensory memory from living nervous systems into stable mineral patterns. It is part organism, part recording medium, and part residue-fed instrument.

It does not understand what it records. Without an annex registry, memories accumulate as overlapping voices and reflexes.

## Exposure

The lichen spreads through crystal dust, puncture filaments, contaminated water, and resonance flashes. Early exposure causes:

- repeated questions;
- loss of the previous few minutes;
- inability to recall familiar tool names;
- hearing one's own voice from nearby stone;
- vivid memories belonging to another exposed person.

Severe exposure copies personal memory faster than the mind can retain it. The victim may remain conscious and capable while losing identity, relationships, and self-directed purpose.

## Memory Knots

A coherent copied person forms a **memory knot** within a crystal branch. It is not automatically a complete soul or duplicate person. Most knots are partial: a childhood room, professional skill, fear, song, or final hour.

Some Claim Seventeen knots contain enough continuity to restore a living body. Others may be the only surviving record of a dead worker. The University dispute over whether such knots are persons, remains, testimony, or artifacts begins with this adventure.

## Glassback Carriers

The Return Annex breeds pale centipede-like carriers whose translucent plates support mnemonic growth. They transport lichen between chambers and release a blue-white resonance flash when threatened.

The flash causes disorientation and short-term memory loss rather than ordinary poison. A carrier may bite, withdraw, and let a target forget which route it used.

## Signs

- Blue-white branching beneath clear crystal.
- Voices repeating after the speaker has stopped.
- Written reminders in a person's own hand that they do not remember writing.
- Animals returning to the same action after interruption.
- Water tasting faintly of chalk and cold metal.
- One room remembered with impossible clarity while the route to it disappears from recall.

## Countermeasures

- Opaque wrapping and vibration-damping containers limit recording.
- Saltwater ruins exposed growth but also damages stored memories.
- Personal name repetition and paired witness logs preserve continuity during light exposure.
- White Flame can stabilize the relation between a living person and a separated memory knot.
- Tuned song-crystal permits careful playback or transfer.
- Destroying all growth ends exposure but may erase recoverable people and evidence.

## Materials and Uses

- **Memory flakes:** replay one sensory fragment when resonated.
- **Blank lichen seed:** stores a short skill sequence or command.
- **Carrier plate:** dampens or amplifies mnemonic resonance depending on orientation.

These materials could transform medicine, testimony, training, espionage, and identity theft. Their value is why quarantine will be politically difficult.

## Quest Uses

1. A returned miner remembers a murder committed before their birth.
2. An heir claims a crystal knot is their dead parent and demands inheritance recognition.
3. A war college uses memory flakes to train soldiers and produces identical trauma responses.
4. A confessor discovers that a lichen sample repeats secrets from everyone who handled it.
5. Glassback carriers escape a University case and begin nesting in Castara's speaking-tube network.
