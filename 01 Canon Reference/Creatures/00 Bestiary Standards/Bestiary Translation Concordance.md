# Bestiary Translation Concordance

This is the creator-only crosswalk for the supplied Pathfinder monster taxonomy. It records coverage decisions, not imported canon. Every named example in a source row inherits that row's result unless a later World Egg note explicitly separates it.

## Result Key

- `existing`: a controlling World Egg family already exists.
- `expand`: retain the encounter niche by deepening an existing family.
- `new-family`: build a native family dossier during its assigned wave.
- `template`: express the idea as a condition applied across origins.
- `unique`: reserve it for one or a few named beings.
- `merge`: fold its useful traits into another native family.
- `exclude`: do not translate the concept as a native category.

## Native Conditions

The first controlled condition set is [[Creature Conditions and Templates|residue-fed, Menhir-aligned, Idol-fed, Shell-scarred, patron-leased, saint-touched, Giant-made, soulbound, elder, skinwalking, layer-touched, and swarm]]. Adventure-specific conditions such as [[Collateral Beasts]], [[Form-Ruins]], and [[Vault-Claimed]] remain with their adventures.
