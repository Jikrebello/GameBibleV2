# Creature Conditions and Templates

A condition modifies a base creature without replacing its primary origin or family. Apply one only when it changes signs, behavior, continuity, weakness, or story use in a meaningful way.

## Controlled Conditions

| Condition | What it changes | Continuity rule |
|---|---|---|
| Residue-fed | Memory, repetition, or death-site appetite accumulates around a living or constructed form | Does not add a human soul |
| Menhir-aligned | A court or place-law reshapes behavior and body | Usually remains base continuity or becomes `court-law` if fully remade |
| Idol-fed | Vice ecology reinforces a compulsion and rewards its spread | Does not create a human soul |
| Shell-scarred | Elemental leakage alters body, habitat, or attack pattern | Base life remains unless fully replaced by `shell-pattern` |
| Patron-leased | Stability, power, or embodiment depends on an Astral rent relation | Record the lease and what happens at default |
| Saint-touched | A bounded heavenly intervention leaves a protective or restorative mark | Never manufactures a soul |
| Giant-made | A dead empire altered, bred, or built the subject | Record which empire and continuity project |
| Soulbound | A genuine human soul has been deliberately imprisoned | Continuity becomes `bound-human-soul` |
| Elder | Age and accumulated continuity produce exceptional intelligence or power | Does not change origin; may increase moral agency |
| Skinwalking | A living body assumes another animal or creaturely form | Source may be curse, craft, Menhir law, or Idol corruption |
| Layer-touched | Contact with another layer creates a stable secondary expression | Primary origin remains visible in metadata |
| Swarm | Many small bodies act as one encounter ecology | Each member retains its base continuity |

## Use Rules

- Do not use a template to hide an unclear origin.
- Do not infer a human soul from copied memories, speech, or human shape.
- Record whether the condition is inherited, contagious, chosen, imposed, or environmental.
- Record whether removing the condition kills, restores, releases, or merely changes the subject.
- Unique adventure transformations may keep local names and mechanics without entering this global list.

[[Collateral Beasts]], [[Form-Ruins]], and [[Vault-Claimed]] are local examples of adventure conditions rather than independent creature families.
