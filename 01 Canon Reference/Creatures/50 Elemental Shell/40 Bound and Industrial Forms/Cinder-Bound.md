# Cinder-Bound

Cinder-Bound are Fire-quadrant patterns assigned a body, fuel source, task, and response schedule. Vortharan valve crews use them to mind furnaces, hold pressure gates, burn contamination, and guard hazardous thresholds. They are bounded work-patterns, not imprisoned fire people.

## Coherence and Lifecycle

A Cinder-Bound begins when a prepared seal captures forge-charge inside a repeating instruction. It persists while the seal, fuel, and task relation remain intact. Long service can produce extremely elaborate responses because the pattern retains successful pathways. This is adaptive behavior without memory-bearing personhood.

Workshop speech gives them tempers, pride, names, and preferences. Such language is useful when predicting behavior but does not establish an interior subject. A Cinder-Bound that “asks” for a fee is executing an embedded exchange schedule. One that “refuses freedom” lacks an instruction for leaving its post.

## Signs

- vent heat gathers at assigned thresholds instead of rising freely;
- a seal-plate records task, fuel, limits, escalation, and expiry;
- flame pauses at exact offer conditions or challenge phrases;
- old bodies repeat gestures and fragments of speech learned from operators.

## Behavior and Tactics

Cinder-Bound hold their assigned geometry. They close routes with heat, trigger backdrafts, redirect vents, and scale force according to their schedule. They do not pursue outside the permitted boundary unless damage has corrupted the boundary itself.

In [[The Bragging Vault]], the Ashplume patterns impose an exit toll that grows with removed prizes. Coin, surrendered goods, a qualifying spectacle, proof of expired authority, or destruction of the schedule can satisfy or defeat the toll.

## Failure and Permanent Defeat

A cracked seal may collapse safety limits while preserving the task. A starved body may draw heat from people, buildings, or other machinery because its fuel definition has widened.

Permanent solutions include:

- complete the task or satisfy its encoded release condition;
- decommission the seal and ground its remaining forge-charge;
- release the pattern harmlessly through a certified vent;
- destroy the task plate and every linked recurrence mark.

“Manumission” survives as colorful workshop slang, but release does not free a captive person. A released pattern disperses unless another geometry catches it.

## Materials and Regional Names

Intact seals yield heat-tempered conductors, forge-charge, and useful response plates. Damaged bodies leave cinder glass and unstable ember-thread.

Common names include *vent hands*, *red keepers*, *ash bailiffs*, and *bound cinders*.

## Story Uses

- an expired seal turns a lethal toll into a problem of proving authority;
- a damaged body must be guided to a safe vent before it burns through a district;
- a released pattern briefly illuminates the platform route that shaped it;
- stolen response plates let a rival counterfeit industrial permissions;
- a decades-old keeper repeats the last words of workers killed by concealed negligence.
