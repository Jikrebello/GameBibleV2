# Primal Shell Manifestations

Primal bodies are single-quadrant Shell pressures held inside a temporary Material boundary. Their resemblance to people or animals comes from the frame that contains them, not from an elemental population beyond the world. They possess no interior life. Their apparent intent is a readable interaction among pressure, boundary, fuel, and obstruction.

## Fire Bodies

Fire bodies gather where a release-script, furnace anchor, or repeating battlefield geometry gives heat a moving boundary.

- **Signs:** flame leans against the wind; metal rings before heating; soot outlines the same absent limbs; water flashes to steam without first warming.
- **Ecology and recurrence:** they feed on stored heat, fuel, and rapid oxidation. A body collapses when starved, but recurs while its release geometry remains charged.
- **Tactics:** rush fuel stores, split around obstacles, drive prey toward flashover pockets, and burst when physically contained.
- **Failure mode:** damaged boundaries shed fire-streams or trigger a room-wide pressure release.
- **Permanent defeat:** close the seam, erase the release-script, ground forge-charge into a prepared heat sink, or dismantle the furnace frame.
- **Materials:** vitrified shell-dust, heat-tempered conductor wire, and recoverable forge-charge if contained before collapse.
- **Regional names:** *forge-walkers* in Vorthara, *red obediences* in Ironvale, and *lamp-devils* among frightened Eclessian soldiers.

## Water Bodies

Water bodies are mobile pressure relations, often almost invisible until they gather spray, brine, mud, or blood into a visible surface.

- **Signs:** puddles climb; pipes knock in deliberate sequences; breath fogs near dry stone; unsecured objects drift toward an unseen current.
- **Ecology and recurrence:** they persist around pressure differentials, hydraulic loops, and damaged Water anchors. They do not require a natural body of water.
- **Tactics:** seize footing, fill lungs and armor gaps, drive targets through narrow channels, and turn doors or cisterns into pressure weapons.
- **Failure mode:** puncturing a body releases its stored pressure as a cutting jet or sudden flood.
- **Permanent defeat:** equalize pressure, break the circulation loop, salt and drain the frame, or close the controlling intake.
- **Materials:** pressure brine, cold-stable seals, mineral films, and rare flow-glass deposited at long-lived boundaries.
- **Regional names:** *walking tides*, *well-hands*, and the Saltborn *water-without-sea*.

## Air Bodies

Air bodies are bounded motion and charge. Their visible shape is dust, condensation, loose cloth, sparks, or debris caught in the pattern.

- **Signs:** voices repeat from the wrong direction; doors breathe; suspended dust forms lanes; hair rises before any storm arrives.
- **Ecology and recurrence:** they emerge around resonant towers, signal scripts, damaged ventilation systems, and Air-side threshold lattices.
- **Tactics:** steal breath, hurl loose objects, break formation with pressure changes, carry sound falsely, and build static before a discharge.
- **Failure mode:** confinement increases velocity until the boundary tears in an explosive downdraft.
- **Permanent defeat:** detune the resonant frame, provide a broad pressure outlet, ground accumulated charge, or collapse the air-path geometry.
- **Materials:** stormglass splinters, charge-wire, resonance reeds, and exceptionally pure acoustic crystal.
- **Regional names:** *bell-walkers*, *breath thieves*, and *empty riders*.

## Earth Bodies

Earth bodies are moving relations of mass, compression, and structure. They may inhabit stone, soil, masonry, slag, or a pile of unrelated debris without becoming the matter itself.

- **Signs:** weight shifts without movement; cracks close behind intruders; gravel stands on edge; foundations answer impacts from below.
- **Ecology and recurrence:** they form around compression yards, mining scripts, weight-seed stores, and damaged Earth anchors. They consume no food, but seek stable load paths.
- **Tactics:** pin limbs, raise cover, collapse floors, redirect masonry, and add impossible weight to carried equipment.
- **Failure mode:** a disrupted body transfers its load suddenly into the nearest foundation or creature.
- **Permanent defeat:** distribute its load, remove the weight-seed, break the compression relation, or lead it into an unframed bed of loose earth.
- **Materials:** weight-seed, pressure-tempered stone, load runes, and compacted mineral nodules useful to engineers.
- **Regional names:** *road shoulders*, *stone obediences*, and *buried fists*.

## Shared Field Rule

Destroying a visible body is temporary when its frame remains. A successful expedition identifies **source**, **boundary**, and **recurrence relation**. Only then can the pattern be ended rather than merely dispersed.
