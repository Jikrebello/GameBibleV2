# Sloth Masks and Dusk-Beasts

Sloth's ecology grows around neglected duties, empty routines and institutions that have mistaken the absence of disturbance for peace. Drowse-Leeches consume urgency, Hush-Walkers preserve work after its purpose has disappeared, and Dusk Colossi arise from the accumulated abdication of whole settlements or offices. Their influence makes consequence feel distant and necessary action seem like an unreasonable demand.

## Drowse-Leeches

Drowse-Leeches are soft, translucent Shadow fauna that hang in the air like strips of wet cloth. A dark pulse moves through them whenever someone remembers a duty and decides to address it later.

- **Origin and agency:** reflexive fauna with animal intelligence and no moral agency.
- **Ecology:** they gather around deferred warnings, unvisited patients, unanswered letters, unfinished repairs, and watch schedules no one checks. They consume urgency rather than physical energy.
- **Signs:** clocks lose their striking weight, urgent ink fades first, sleepers wake physically rested but unable to begin, and small hazards become strangely easy to step around.
- **Threat pattern:** attach to the shadow, blunt alarm, make a victim perceive the next action as premature, and shed larvae into unattended tasks. They flee decisive coordinated motion.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** complete or consciously reassign the duty, name the consequence aloud, and expose the brood to dawn or White Flame while its host acts. Killing a leech without addressing the duty hatches its stored delay into several young.
- **Materials:** dried leech-skin can preserve sleep during a crisis but also dull the first warning after waking.
- **Regional names:** later-worms, soft bells, dusk ribbons, and duty drinkers.

## Hush-Walkers

Hush-Walkers are Masks shaped like quiet workers whose faces are smooth except for a small closed door. They maintain the visible routine of an institution while removing every act that would make the routine serve its purpose.

- **Origin and agency:** cunning Mask with limited agency, anchored to a schedule, office, or repeated abdication.
- **Ecology:** a Walker files forms, sweeps halls, rings bells, turns pages, and delivers empty trays. It rewards politeness and discourages any question about outcomes.
- **Signs:** workspaces immaculate while work remains undone, footsteps following the night round with no witness, sealed messages delivered back to their sender, and staff unable to name who made the last decision.
- **Threat pattern:** isolates active people through procedure, closes routes as being out of hours, repeats a victim's own reasonable excuses, and substitutes harmless activity for rescue or defense.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** identify the office's actual purpose, put a living bearer in authority, complete the neglected work, and break the schedule-object that lets performance replace duty. Destroying the building merely moves the routine to the evacuation camp.
- **Materials:** the tiny face-door can replay every decision passed away within the institution.
- **Regional names:** quiet clerks, closed servants, dusk ushers, and the always-present.

## Dusk Colossi

Dusk Colossi are autonomous Archmasks built from the accumulated abdication of a large institution or settlement. One may resemble a walking watchtower, a seated judge made of empty benches, or a giant sleeper roofed in abandoned homes.

- **Origin and agency:** sapient Archmask with full moral agency. It understands that others suffer and calls their demand for action a violation of peace.
- **Ecology:** a Colossus creates a calm zone in which emergencies lose priority, leaders surrender office, and people arrange themselves around not waking the sovereign.
- **Signs:** warning fires emit no heat, crowds lower their voices near catastrophe, roads curve around unfinished work, and the same dusk persists past dawn.
- **Threat pattern:** suppresses coordinated action, absorbs abandoned offices as armor, relocates consequences beyond sight, and offers sanctuary in exchange for permanent withdrawal from responsibility.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** restore the institution's essential duty through several independent bearers, permit honest rest without surrender, wake those used as ballast, and strike the Colossus while the founding task is being completed. Mere noise or forced wakefulness feeds its claim that action is cruelty.
- **Materials:** dusk stone can quiet pain or panic for minutes; prolonged use erodes initiative.
- **Regional names:** still giants, sleeping ministries, evening fathers, and the unrisen.
