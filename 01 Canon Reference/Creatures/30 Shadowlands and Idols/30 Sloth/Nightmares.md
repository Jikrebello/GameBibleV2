# Nightmares

Nightmares are large Dusk fauna shaped like black horses only because human riders, roads, and cavalry dreams give that body extraordinary route-value. Their hides absorb detail, their breath makes distance feel futile, and their hooves leave cold impressions resembling work abandoned halfway.

They are associated with [[Sloth]] because they turn fear into surrender before action, not because sleep or fright is itself sinful.

## Origin and Ecology

Nightmares form where a population repeatedly dreams of approaching danger while accepting that no preparation can matter. They graze on abandoned plans, patrol routes no longer walked, warnings no one answers, and ambitions enjoyed more vividly in imagination than pursued in life.

A Nightmare is cunning and can select a rider or easier hunting ground. Its agency remains limited by the Dusk ecology: it can choose how to induce surrender, but not question why surrender is its food.

## Signs

- many sleepers share the same road but remember traveling alone;
- hoofprints stop before an unfinished task and resume beyond it;
- horses refuse an apparently empty stable;
- watchfires cast the shadow of a mounted figure without a rider;
- plans are postponed until one recurring dream has reached its ending.

## Threat Pattern

A Nightmare separates travelers through false distances, crosses from one sleeper's dream into another's route, and exhales a cold dread that makes the next useful action seem already too late. It accepts riders who prefer the image of decisive power to the work of leadership. A mounted pair can cross dusk, shadow, and abandoned purpose with terrible speed.

It does not burn souls. Dark flame sometimes seen around its mane is Dusk-light consuming intention, not Hellfire and not injury to a human soul.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Wake the linked sleepers, complete or consciously abandon the route's neglected purpose, and sever any rider's bridle before killing the body. A Nightmare dispersed while the communal surrender remains can reform at the next shared dream. White Flame preserves coherent identity and wakes intention, but somebody must still perform the delayed work.

A captured Nightmare cannot be ethically converted into routine cavalry. Bridling it creates a feeding bond in which the rider's unrealized plans become its stable.

## Materials and Remains

Mane shadow can preserve a dream for later examination. Hoof glass reveals which unfinished route anchored the animal. Both decay at sunrise unless sealed, and both tempt handlers to study danger instead of answering it.

## Regional Names

Dusk horses, later-steeds, black roaders, surrender mares, and dream cavalry are common names. The church term nightmare covers several unrelated dream predators, so field signs remain decisive.

## Story Uses

- A garrison dreams vividly of victory while every winter preparation remains undone.
- A penitent former rider seeks help destroying a bridle that returns to each new ambition.
- A Nightmare carries a missing child between the dreams of adults who all assumed someone else was searching.
