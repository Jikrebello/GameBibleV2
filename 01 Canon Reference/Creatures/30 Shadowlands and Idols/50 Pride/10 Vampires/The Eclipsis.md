# The Eclipsis

Eclipsis are the veil bloodline: retained-soul vampires who bend recognition, emotion, and social memory. Their power does not create a private reality. It makes people privilege a prepared appearance, overlook a contradiction, or remember the emotional meaning chosen by the vampire.

## Origin and Lifecycle

An Eclipsis waking rite requires the recipient to be publicly mourned, disowned, acquitted, married, crowned, or otherwise given a decisive social identity while the body lies near death. The sire's blood anchors itself to the gap between the person and that public image.

Eclipsis feed most effectively through intimacy, admiration, fear, or prolonged attention. Their lineage-anchors are commonly portraits, mirrored rooms, masks, reputational archives, or a living circle trained to repeat the same account of them.

## Signs

- several witnesses agree on mood and status but cannot agree on face, clothing, or words;
- portraits become more convincing than memories of the subject;
- a scandal vanishes socially while physical evidence remains untouched;
- mirrors show the prepared persona a moment before the vampire enters;
- friends defend a person they cannot describe outside the role assigned to them.

## Society and Agency

Eclipsis work as diplomats, courtiers, performers, confessors, spies, and protectors of hidden vampire households. They may prevent panic or shield innocent dependents from collective reprisal. They also excel at making victims doubt harm that plainly occurred. Concealment can protect life; it does not make coercion or erased testimony good.

## Threat Pattern and Tactics

They cultivate an expected role before direct contact, then punish anyone who perceives beyond it. In conflict they redirect attention, amplify one emotion, produce convincing sensory overlays, and cause allies to misidentify priority targets. Their strongest effects require prior observation, blood contact, or repeated social reinforcement; an Eclipsis cannot effortlessly rewrite an unknown crowd.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Sunlight separates body from cultivated image. White Flame, contemporaneous records, witnesses comparing accounts in person, and deliberate naming of contradictions weaken the veil. Destroying the body is temporary while its portrait, mirror, or living repetition-circle maintains identity. Permanent defeat breaks that anchor and releases the retained human soul.

## Materials and Remains

Eclipsis mirror-silver can reveal imposed personas when properly cleansed. Masks and portraits retain emotional pressure and are useful forensic evidence but dangerous display pieces. Their blood can preserve one chosen impression while suppressing context, making it unreliable without independent testimony.

## Regional Names

Eclessian pamphlets say **veil-bloods**; Thalmyric confessors use **the borrowed faces**; Sestaran files call them **mirror kin**. Eclipsis is the creator-facing bloodline name.

## Story Uses

- The town remembers a dead noble as merciful although every account book records predation.
- A vampire hides refugees from a purge but demands that one witness's identity be surrendered to maintain the veil.
- Breaking a portrait-anchor will reveal a household and expose innocent dependents alongside their predator.
