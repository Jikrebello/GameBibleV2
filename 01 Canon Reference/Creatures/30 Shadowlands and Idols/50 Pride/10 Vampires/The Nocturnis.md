# The Nocturnis

Nocturnis are the veiled bloodline: retained-soul vampires adapted to concealment, surveillance, and the possession of secrets. They are not literal shadows and do not draw power from Sloth's realm. Their gifts arise from Ansharan blood-memory made narrow enough to hide a body, suppress a trace, or read what blood has witnessed.

## Origin and Lifecycle

The Nocturnis transformation requires a sire's blood, near-death, and the concealment of one decisive truth during the waking rite. That hidden fact becomes the first knot in the new vampire's lien. Elders accumulate sensory memories from blood and can mistake that archive for omniscience.

Nocturnis feed most effectively from a person whose habits they have studied. They form small cells, information households, or solitary archives rather than a universal secret order. A cell's lineage-anchor is usually a witness book, sealed mask, hidden room, or cache of blood-memory.

## Signs

- witnesses remember a conversation but not who occupied one chair;
- locks are opened without damage while dust remains undisturbed;
- a victim's blood is taken in small, precise amounts over many nights;
- rooms contain redundant exits and records written from several observers' viewpoints;
- shadows lag only because the vampire suppresses attention, not because darkness is alive.

## Society and Agency

Nocturnis may serve mortal rulers, vampire houses, churches, criminal networks, or themselves. Some genuinely prevent wars by exposing plots; others manufacture threats to justify permanent secrecy. Their blood gives no mandate to decide what everyone else is allowed to know.

## Threat Pattern and Tactics

They map routines, compromise messengers, remove evidence, and make opponents doubt which facts are shared. In a fight they extinguish attention rather than light: a watcher overlooks movement, loses the sequence of blows, or fails to connect a face with an earlier sighting. Blood from a fresh wound lets them recover a few recent sensory impressions. Prepared escape routes and mortal informants matter more than brute force.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Sunlight and White Flame force suppressed traces into coherence. Independent witnesses, redundant records, and actions performed without predictable routine blunt Nocturnis advantages. Destroying the body is temporary while its witness-cache or hidden lineage-anchor survives. Permanent defeat exposes the first concealed truth, breaks the anchor, and releases the retained human soul.

## Materials and Remains

Nocturnis blood can preserve a brief sensory record but contaminates it with the vampire's framing. Their masks, witness books, and blackened lenses are valuable evidence and dangerous tools for spies. Cleansed remnants can support forensic work; uncleansed ones invite false certainty.

## Regional Names

Eclessian hunters call them **veil-bloods**; Thalmyric chronicles use **the unreflected**; Sestaran intelligence books say **quiet kin**. Nocturnis is the creator-facing bloodline name.

## Story Uses

- An investigator discovers that the city's best informant has been editing every faction's evidence.
- A witness-cache can prove a massacre, but opening it also gives the dead vampire a route back.
- A Nocturnis cell offers accurate warning of an invasion in exchange for one person being erased from the record.
