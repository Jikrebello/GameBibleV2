# The Sanguine

Sanguine are the chalice bloodline: retained-soul vampires whose inheritance specializes in vitality transfer, blood-memory, lineage rites, and the making or maintenance of other vampires. They can read residue carried in blood; they do not converse with routed souls or govern the dead.

## Origin and Lifecycle

The Sanguine waking rite mixes a sire's blood with blood from the intended recipient's family or closest living bond. This gives the new vampire unusually strong access to inherited memory and makes careless transformation dangerous to an entire household. Sanguine lineages keep chalices, lancets, preserved hearts, or family vessels as anchors.

They require regular blood and can transfer vitality between bodies. Such healing never creates life from nothing: the strength comes from the Sanguine, a donor, stored blood, or another living subject. Willing donation can mitigate predation but does not dissolve the blood lien holding the vampire's soul.

## Signs

- wounds close while another person nearby becomes pale or weak;
- cups and surgical tools are marked with generations of names;
- family illnesses recur according to feeding nights rather than season;
- old blood reveals flashes of posture, terror, or sensation but never a complete speaking soul;
- a household treats one physician-priest as both healer and unquestionable ancestor.

## Society and Agency

Sanguine often become physicians, lineage keepers, ritualists, or makers of new vampires. Some preserve genuine medical knowledge and provide care unavailable elsewhere. Others create dependency by causing the weakness they later cure. Their ability to help does not entitle them to blood, bodies, obedience, or descendants.

## Threat Pattern and Tactics

They prepare encounters by marking donors, storing vitality, and learning kinship. In combat they drain strength, close their own wounds, wake blood residue as misleading sensory echoes, and transfer injury into prepared vessels. Sanguine prefer ritual spaces where every drop has been collected and can be reassigned.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Sunlight spoils stored blood-law and makes transferred vitality unstable. White Flame distinguishes healing from theft and exposes whose life paid for each effect. Destroying a Sanguine body is temporary while its lineage vessel remains fed. Permanent defeat empties or consecrates the vessel, breaks the lien, and releases the retained soul toward judgement.

## Materials and Remains

Properly secured Sanguine instruments can diagnose blood curses and inherited poisons. Their preserved blood is potent medicine, evidence, and a route for recurrence; using it without purification risks accepting the old lineage claim. Ash from a released Sanguine no longer carries that claim.

## Regional Names

Eclessian records use **chalice-bloods**; Thalmyric hunters say **red cantors**; Sestaran physicians call them **cup-keepers** when discretion is required. Sanguine is the creator-facing bloodline name.
