# Bloodlines Overview

This overview contributes no additional catalogue form. Coverage belongs to the five dedicated bloodline dossiers: [[The Regalis]], [[The Nocturnis]], [[The Sanguine]], [[The Eclipsis]], and [[The Militis]].

## Shared Continuity

All five bloodlines descend from Ansharan Giant blood craft and retain the transformed human's genuine soul. They are not children of [[Pride|the Sovereign]], separate species, or inevitable servants of one worldwide vampire government. Pride strengthens their inherited hierarchy and feeds upon chosen domination; individual vampires remain morally accountable.

A bloodline shapes appetite, gifts, preferred anchors, and recurring social roles. It does not erase personality or force allegiance. Houses of the same line may feud, cooperate, ignore one another, or serve mortal institutions for very different reasons.

## Comparative Register

| Bloodline | Inheritance | Typical anchor | Preferred pressure | Encounter use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Regalis]] | rank, household command, deference | coronet, seal, chair, ancestor reliquary | dependence presented as rightful order | patron, ruler, social controller |
| [[The Nocturnis]] | concealment, surveillance, blood-memory | witness book, mask, hidden room | secrecy presented as necessary protection | infiltrator, assassin, information broker |
| [[The Sanguine]] | vitality transfer, lineage rite, inherited memory | chalice, lancet, heart vessel | healing presented as entitlement to bodies | ritualist, healer, maker, controller |
| [[The Eclipsis]] | recognition, emotion, social memory | portrait, mirror, repetition-circle | concealment presented as mercy or consensus | deceiver, diplomat, identity predator |
| [[The Militis]] | endurance, war-form, blood obedience | standard, muster roll, gate, weapon | protection presented as ownership | commander, guardian, shock combatant |

## Shared Limits

- Sunlight exposes Ansharan blood-law through stronger day jurisdiction.
- White Flame reveals the retained human soul and weakens its lien.
- A destroyed body can return while its lineage-anchor remains active.
- Permanent defeat breaks the lien and releases the soul toward judgement.
- Neither bloodline, age, repentance, nor destruction guarantees the soul's final judgement.
- Blood-memory and residue are not permission to speak for routed dead.

## Use in Stories

Vampires work best when the bloodline clarifies the kind of pressure an adventure explores. A Regalis plot should concern hierarchy and dependence; a Nocturnis plot, secrecy and evidence; a Sanguine plot, bodies and inherited obligation; an Eclipsis plot, identity and testimony; a Militis plot, protection and command. Their shared origin need not make every story about Pride or Anshara.
