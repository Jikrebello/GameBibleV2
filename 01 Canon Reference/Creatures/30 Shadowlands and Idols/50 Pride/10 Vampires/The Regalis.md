# The Regalis

Regalis are the crown-blood inheritance of Anshara: vampires whose blood lien specializes in rank, household possession, and compelled deference. They remain human persons with retained souls. Their conviction that command proves superiority is an inherited temptation and a chosen creed, not a divine right.

## Origin and Lifecycle

A Regalis is made when a sire brings a human close to death, gives concentrated lineage blood, and formally receives the dying person into a household. The rite establishes a blood lien whether the recipient consented or was coerced. A new Regalis wakes hungry, acutely aware of social rank, and unable to route at death while the lien endures.

Regalis can subsist on stored or freely given blood, but coercive feeding produces stronger command effects and therefore tempts their houses toward cultivated dependency. Old Regalis often preserve a lineage-anchor such as a coronet, seal, chair, or ancestor reliquary through which a destroyed body may slowly rebuild.

## Signs

- servants answer ordinary questions by naming who authorized them;
- family portraits include faces no civic record can place;
- doors, seating, meals, and even sleeping rooms are ordered by rank;
- witnesses feel an involuntary urge to stand, bow, or wait for permission;
- sealed rooms hold fresh blood, old heraldry, and genealogies revised around one unaging patron.

## Society and Agency

Regalis build houses rather than one worldwide court. A house may shelter dependents, patronize art, enforce peace, or preserve a community through war; it may also turn every benefit into proof that obedience is owed. These real goods do not excuse blood servitude, coerced feeding, or the conversion of people into hereditary property.

Their command is strongest over people who have drunk their blood, accepted repeated household protection, or publicly acknowledged their rank. It cannot erase moral agency or turn an unrelated crowd into perfect puppets.

## Threat Pattern and Tactics

Regalis isolate opponents socially before fighting. They use retainers, false hospitality, inherited claims, and staged public humiliation to make resistance appear illegitimate. In violence they command marked dependents, force hesitation through rank-pressure, and withdraw toward prepared household ground. An exposed Regalis remains physically formidable but prefers witnesses who can be made to ratify the result.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Sunlight exposes the hidden claim and burns Regalis flesh. White Flame, sincere repudiation by coerced dependents, and destruction of the lineage-anchor weaken its household jurisdiction. Killing the body without breaking that anchor permits return. Permanent defeat breaks the blood lien and releases the retained soul toward judgement; it does not guarantee salvation.

## Materials and Remains

Regalis blood can expose kinship, command marks, and false succession when handled by a trained investigator. House seals and coronets retain dangerous residue of acknowledged rank. Such objects are evidence or ritual anchors, not harmless treasure, and should be cleansed or secured after the lien is broken.

## Regional Names

Eclessian hunters say **crown-bloods**; Thalmyric accounts use **red voivodes**; Sestaran dossiers prefer **first-house vampires**. Regalis is the creator-facing bloodline name.
