# Dusk Barrows

The **Dusk Barrows** hang beneath the Gale-Crown Ranges where southern permafrost meets the Grobi Wastes. Folk winter stories call them **the Under-Cairns**.

The settlement follows burial shafts, frozen battle pits, lava-cut caverns, and old cairn fields rather than one horizontal floor. Many entrances remain sealed for generations until thaw, war, or Menhir pressure opens them.

## Material Body

Permafrost preserves bodies and residue while slowing ordinary decay. Generations of Folk-Grobi warfare, failed halls, winter migration, and unclaimed battlefield dead created a deep resource frontier for ghoul lineages.

Grobi bodies can sustain physical appetite but do not carry routed human souls or the same human burial attachments. The Barrows therefore prize human dead, names, and relics and raid northward or westward despite abundant Grobi carrion.

The physical settlement includes:

- vertical burial shafts linked by rope, bone, and frozen hide;
- preserved halls occupied upside-down along cavern ceilings;
- Grave-Runner kennels and pursuit galleries;
- thaw chambers used to awaken cached bodies and residue;
- relic markets trading objects recovered from sealed cairns;
- war tunnels opening toward Gale-Crown passes and Grobi hunger corridors.

## Feast-Shadow: The Hanging Dusk

The Shadowlands body is a vertical twilight where every grave hangs above an unseen appetite. Falling means moving toward the city center regardless of physical direction.

Its anchoring organ is the **Dusk Spindle**, formed from the repeated act of lowering named dead into cairns and hauling them back without permission. The Spindle guides Grave-Runners through memory of descent rather than mapped tunnels.

## Rule and Rivalry

The Barrows are ruled through the **Chain of Cairns**, a hierarchy of lineages that each control one vertical route and must contribute hunters to shared raids. Elite Grave-Runner masters can challenge an elder by retrieving a body or relic from a shaft the incumbent cannot reach.

The city considers Nihil soft with archives and the Fever Deeps wasteful with infection. It purchases names from both while denying either access to its preserved hunting stock.

## Access and Signs

- cairn stones become warm while the ground remains frozen;
- ropes descend farther than the depth of the grave;
- Grave-Runner tracks stop beneath an intact sheet of ice;
- relics vanish from sealed barrows while bodies remain temporarily untouched;
- an aurora casts shadows downward into the earth;
- Folk memory-chains omit the same generation across otherwise unrelated clans.

## Threat Pattern

The Under-Cairns raid for preserved human residue, navigational memory, and objects that identify forgotten burial fields. Hunters exploit Grobi attacks as cover but do not command the Grobi. They may recover a threatened corpse from battlefield scavengers only to consume it under controlled conditions later.

## Permanent Defeat

Permanent destruction requires collapsing the major Material shafts, restoring the violated cairn chains, and breaking the Dusk Spindle while its corresponding descent rites are interrupted. Fire alone can release dangerous residue and thaw new routes. A successful campaign must manage heat, burial, and structural collapse together.

## Story Uses

- a thawing pass exposes a shaft both Folk defenders and Grobi raiders need to cross;
- Grave-Runners steal memorial objects to locate bodies whose cairns have never been found;
- an old clan memory-chain was deliberately altered to hide the bargain that first opened the Under-Cairns;
- a southern expedition must descend through the city to close a route before winter migration begins.
