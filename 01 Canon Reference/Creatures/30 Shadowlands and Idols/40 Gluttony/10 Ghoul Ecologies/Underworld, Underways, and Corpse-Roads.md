# Underworld, Underways, and Corpse-Roads

This note controls the word **underworld** wherever ghoul geography is concerned. It prevents physical depth, the Shadowlands, Hell, and the human soul-road from collapsing into one convenient subterranean cosmology.

## The Material Underworld

The Material Realm contains ordinary caverns, mines, volcanic tubes, cisterns, tomb galleries, buried settlements, fault chambers, and deep biological ecologies. These systems are discontinuous. Some extend for many miles; none forms one worldwide underdark.

Travel below ground remains physical. Air, water, food, collapse, heat, cold, direction, and excavation matter. A tunnel beneath one realm does not automatically connect to another continent or plane.

## Jirahar Underways

[[Prophet's Roads and the Underways|Jirahar Underways]] are engineered local systems: tower passages, drains, mine routes, concealed stairs, caches, and linked host houses. They are not ancient world tunnels and do not naturally connect to ghoul cities.

A ghoul breach into an Underway is a local security disaster, not evidence that the Jirahar road network was secretly built upon a universal corpse-road.

## Other Things Called Underworld

- **The Shadowlands** are the left yolk of predatory meaning, fear, appetite, imitation, and identity pressure.
- **Hell and its Depth** are the mapped ecology of completed refusal. Physical depth does not lead there by ordinary excavation.
- **The Vestibule and soul-road** concern routed human souls and divine commission. They are not underground places.
- **The Abyss** is anti-place and causal injury, not a deeper cave layer.

In-world traditions may call all four “below.” Authorial notes keep them separate.

## Corpse-Roads

A **corpse-road** forms when repeated burial violation, residue disturbance, bodily transport, ghoul feeding, and Gluttony pressure make one route easier for predation to repeat.

A corpse-road usually requires:

- a physical passage, sequence of burial sites, or transport corridor;
- repeated movement of bodies, names, grave goods, or residue;
- an established ghoul lineage or feeding practice;
- a Shadowlands echo organized around appetite;
- neglected boundaries that allow the two patterns to reinforce one another.

The road is neither a soul-route nor a magical railway. It makes ghoul movement, residue disturbance, and local Shadowlands overlap easier. Distance, danger, and physical obstruction remain real.

## Double-Layer Settlements

The three great ghoul cities possess two mutually reinforcing bodies.

### Material Body

The physical settlement contains lairs, corpse stores, water, tools, routes, workshops, nurseries, fortifications, and the remains of earlier civilizations. Its residents are embodied residue-born predators.

### Feast-Shadow

The corresponding Shadowlands district is made from accumulated appetite, violated remembrance, recurring feeding, and Gluttony's pressure. Streets correspond by function rather than measurement. A sealed tomb may be a gate in the Feast-shadow; a banquet hall may correspond to an entire Material cemetery.

The Devourer shapes these conditions but does not reside as municipal ruler. Each city has its own politics and can oppose the others.

## Movement Between Layers

Transition requires a feeding anchor: a corpse gate, fever cradle, name archive, mass grave, or similarly repeated conjunction. Crossing is easier during war, plague, famine, or burial collapse, but those events do not automatically create a road.

The Feast-shadows can approach one another when mass death strengthens Gluttony's heartland. They still do not form a safe or stable intercity route. A ghoul expedition attempting such travel risks arriving in the wrong age, appetite, body cache, or rival dominion.

## Destruction and Recurrence

Destroying only the Material city leaves its Feast-shadow capable of guiding survivors, fever, and cached residue back into the ruins. Destroying only the Feast-shadow leaves an embodied population able to rebuild its appetite architecture.

Permanent defeat requires:

1. ending the city's named feeding anchor in the Material body;
2. breaking the corresponding Feast-shadow organ;
3. closing or redirecting its corpse-roads;
4. restoring burial names and boundaries where possible;
5. preventing surviving lineages from carrying the anchor elsewhere.

This releases no captive human population of souls. The original souls associated with ghoul residue have already routed.
