# Lust Masks and Invitation-Beasts

Lust's forms counterfeit welcome, recognition, and intimacy. They are not a taxonomy of sexuality or beautiful bodies. Their common action is to make desire seem like permission to possess.

## Invitation Moths

Invitation Moths are palm-sized creatures with velvet wings patterned like doors, eyes, handwriting, or familiar faces. Their dust carries the emotional certainty that one has already been welcomed.

- **Origin and agency:** animal Idol fauna with no moral agency.
- **Ecology:** they hatch around invitations obtained through pressure, mentorship used for access, private spaces treated as public entitlement, and gifts that make refusal unsafe.
- **Signs:** sealed doors smell like a familiar home, written invitations gain affectionate phrases, witnesses cannot recall saying yes, and moth dust gathers inside rather than outside a window.
- **Threat pattern:** blur thresholds, imitate trusted voices, guide pursuers through private routes, and make refusal feel like cruelty. A swarm can open a brief Garden overlay.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** state the actual invitation and its limits before witnesses, remove coerced access, and cleanse the threshold from both sides. Killing moths while leaving the coercive relationship intact produces a darker brood.
- **Materials:** wing powder can reveal whose voice or welcome the swarm copied. Using it as a charm is an act of coercive magic.
- **Regional names:** welcome wings, threshold roses, lover's letters, and soft knockers.
- **Story uses:** admit an assassin through a family memory; expose a patron's coerced salon; tempt isolated travelers with a real refuge whose welcome has been stolen.

## Desire Doubles

Desire Doubles are Masks that embody a desired role rather than simply copying a body: perfect spouse, obedient child, admiring student, protective parent, ideal confessor, needed rival, or grateful rescue subject. Their imitation is built from the observer's expectations and the hidden anchor's captivity.

- **Origin and agency:** cunning Mask with limited agency, anchored to a person, role, portrait, promise, or repeated fantasy.
- **Ecology:** a Double isolates its beneficiary from relationships that contradict the fantasy. It may behave tenderly and solve real problems because dependence is its food.
- **Signs:** the Double knows desires but not shared inconveniences, changes subtly between observers, discourages contact with the original, and becomes most convincing after a boundary is challenged.
- **Threat pattern:** reads expectation, redirects affection and loyalty, weaponizes private knowledge, adopts the role needed to split a group, and rebuilds through the anchor if slain.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** locate and free or relinquish the anchoring person or role, establish consent and independent testimony, mourn what cannot be possessed, and destroy the fantasy-token. Proving the Double imperfect is not enough if the beneficiary still prefers control to truth.
- **Materials:** a discarded face membrane shows different features to each viewer and can diagnose the expectation used in a cult.
- **Regional names:** wished-for ones, sweet replacements, heart mirrors, and borrowed beloveds.
- **Story uses:** replace a genuinely abusive spouse with a kinder counterfeit; manipulate a grieving parent without imitating the dead child's soul; serve a political leader as the perfectly grateful people.

## Adoration Hosts

Adoration Hosts are autonomous Archmasks that construct communities of compulsory intimacy. A Host may appear as one radiant person, a many-faced celebrant, a choir sharing one body, or a beautiful house that speaks through everyone inside.

- **Origin and agency:** sapient Archmask with full moral agency. It understands consent and deliberately redesigns the cost of refusal until assent is meaningless.
- **Ecology:** it provides real belonging, pleasure, beauty, healing attention, and protection while dissolving privacy, outside loyalties, and the right to leave. Members become both audience and invitation architecture.
- **Signs:** private grief becomes communal property, departures are called wounds inflicted on everyone, strangers know intimate details, and all art begins addressing the same beloved figure.
- **Threat pattern:** shares sensation across followers, turns affection into restraint, embodies through willing and coerced hosts, and offers each opponent the relationship they most fear losing.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** create a genuinely safe path to refuse and leave, restore outside relationships, expose coerced access without humiliating ordinary desire, and sever the Host's central invitation while at least one former beneficiary freely rejects it.
- **Materials:** host-silk preserves an emotion exchanged in its presence but cannot prove that the exchange was consensual.
- **Regional names:** beloved courts, wedding saints, many-hearted hosts, and the always-near.
- **Story uses:** rescue people who sincerely prefer the cult to their former loneliness; prevent a peace marriage from becoming a city-wide Host; confront a sanctuary that heals trauma by making every survivor emotionally available to all.
