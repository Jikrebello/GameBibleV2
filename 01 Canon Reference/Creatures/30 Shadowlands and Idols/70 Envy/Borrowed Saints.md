# Borrowed Saints

## Classification

Idol-fed Shadowlands counterfeits formed from sustained false apparition work. They are most commonly cultivated by [[The Weeping Courts]].

## What They Are

A Borrowed Saint begins as theater: prepared testimony, concealed voices, manipulated dreams, staged tears, and an image copied from accepted sacred art. Repeated devotion gives that image a stable place in communal imagination. Envy and comparison then feed it through [[Envy|Invidia]] until the performance can answer without its operators.

It is not the soul of the saint imitated, a mistaken angel, or a culturally alternative route to holiness. It is a predatory image wearing the expectations directed toward it.

## Signs

- revelations repeat language from submitted letters, confessions, or petitions;
- the apparition praises its own neglected worshippers by diminishing recognized holy places;
- private grief becomes public proof of favor;
- images weep toward reflective surfaces or hidden glass channels;
- miracles intensify when witnesses argue over who is more blessed;
- the apparition resists ordinary questions about the Redeemer, repentance, or service while speaking fluently about recognition.

## Ecology and Lifecycle

1. **Staged Image:** entirely operated by people and devices.
2. **Answering Reflection:** can repeat, recombine, and reveal harvested memories.
3. **Fed Apparition:** produces minor physical effects, healing, tears, lights, or dream visitations.
4. **Living Witness:** attempts to overwrite a chosen person whose identity grants the shrine legitimacy.
5. **Court:** becomes a permanent local feeding architecture able to produce lesser grief-doubles.

## Behavior

Borrowed Saints rarely attack first. They isolate doubters, reward comparison, expose private pain, and make dependence feel like recognition. When physically threatened, they manifest grief-doubles shaped like absent loved ones, turn reflections against witnesses, and move through connected weeping glass.

## Weaknesses

- Return stolen letters, memories, and testimony to their owners.
- Break or purify the reflective network connecting shrine and workshop.
- Deny comparison without denying the worshippers' real suffering.
- Name the ordinary human sources behind supposedly impossible knowledge.
- White Flame and genuine relics expose the image's lack of routed personhood.
- Preserve the intended living witness's identity through relationships and memories the apparition did not harvest.

## Materials

Weeping glass retains emotional and spoken residue. Properly handled, it can identify operators or restore a stolen final memory. Broken carelessly, it releases grief-doubles or forces nearby people to relive what it contains.

## Permanent Defeat

End the staged testimony network, return stolen memories and letters, preserve the intended living witness's identity, and sever every connected piece of weeping glass while the counterfeit is exposed to truthful public witness. Destroying only the apparition leaves the cult's comparison architecture able to grow another image.

## Regional Names

Eclessian investigators use **borrowed saint**; Thalmyric clergy say **weeping counterfeit**; Sestaran stage-workers call an early form an **answering image**. None of these names grants the Mask saintly continuity.

## Story Uses

- A village apparition whose miracles become real only after investigators expose the original fraud.
- Rival pilgrimage sites accusing each other while one hidden Court feeds from the comparison.
- A genuine shrine infiltrated by a counterfeit image copying its saint.
- A living witness asking the party to kill them before the shrine completes the overwrite.
