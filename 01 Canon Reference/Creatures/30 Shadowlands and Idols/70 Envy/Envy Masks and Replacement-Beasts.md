# Envy Masks and Replacement-Beasts

Envy's forms do not merely want what another person owns. They make another person's good feel like evidence that the observer has been robbed, then offer replacement as repair.

## Comparison Leeches

Comparison Leeches are thin green-black Shadow fauna with two faces: one resembles the host at their worst, the other a chosen rival at their best. They attach to reflections, portraits, written evaluations, or the underside of the tongue.

- **Origin and agency:** reflexive Idol fauna with animal intelligence and no moral agency.
- **Ecology:** a leech feeds whenever unlike goods are forced onto one scale. It breeds in rankings that conceal different conditions, public comparison rituals, and repeated resentment toward a named person.
- **Signs:** praise produces pain in a bystander, mirrors split into paired images, achievements become impossible to enjoy unless someone loses, and evaluations acquire the same rival's name.
- **Threat pattern:** transfers confidence and attention, makes allies perceive unequal treatment as deliberate theft, redirects healing or praise toward the chosen comparison, and hides within the host's own voice.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** identify the imposed comparison, name the real injustice separately, acknowledge the rival's good without surrendering the host's dignity, and destroy the paired reflection. Empty reassurance does not remove the leech.
- **Materials:** preserved twin faces reveal the comparison selected by whoever seeded the infestation.
- **Regional names:** green measures, spite tongues, twin worms, and praise drinkers.

## Replacement Doubles

Replacement Doubles are Masks grown to occupy a role, relationship, reputation, or history while the original is pushed into obscurity. Unlike Lust's Desire Doubles, they are designed chiefly to make the original's existence feel unnecessary.

- **Origin and agency:** cunning Mask with limited agency, anchored to stolen testimony, a copied object, edited record, or community agreement to forget.
- **Ecology:** the Double studies what witnesses resented about the original and performs a more convenient version. It feeds on every statement that the replacement is what the person should have been.
- **Signs:** old friends remember incompatible first meetings, joint work bears one name, a copied person casts no reflection beside the original, and records improve as memories worsen.
- **Threat pattern:** steals access and recognition, copies skills through witness belief, turns the original's flaws into physical weaknesses, and re-forms while its replacement narrative remains publicly accepted.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** restore the original's name and truthful history without hiding real faults, expose the edited witness chain, and destroy the copied anchor in both Material and overlay. Killing the Double in secret confirms the replacement story.
- **Materials:** copy-skin records every trait witnesses demanded the original lose.
- **Regional names:** better siblings, second heirs, green twins, and the always-there.

## Stolen-Fate Lords

Stolen-Fate Lords are autonomous Archmasks that govern counterfactual lives. They appear as robed magistrates, orchard keepers, mourning nobles, or many-shadowed figures carrying keys to homes that were never theirs.

- **Origin and agency:** sapient Archmask with full moral agency. It knows no fate was stolen merely because lives differ, yet weaponizes grief and injustice to authorize erasure.
- **Ecology:** a Lord builds a jurisdiction from unrealized inheritances, alternative victories, unborn dynasties, and achievements reassigned to more acceptable claimants.
- **Signs:** maps show districts that never existed, people receive letters from lives they did not live, the successful dream of apologizing for existing, and missing names leave physically cold gaps in records.
- **Threat pattern:** exchanges roles between people, imports skills from counterfactual selves, makes restored truths feel like theft from the replacement, and retreats into any widely desired alternate history.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** repair the real injustice without validating an invented victim, restore erased people and records, accept losses that cannot be transferred, and close the counterfactual anchor through truthful collective witness.
- **Materials:** fate keys open one route toward a life someone desired, but never guarantee return and always identify a person that route would displace.
- **Regional names:** green lords, might-have kings, orchard judges, and inheritance thieves.

## Relation to Borrowed Saints

[[Borrowed Saints]] counterfeit holiness through signs, reputation, and displaced devotion. They may work beside Replacement Doubles, but remain a separate established form with their own defeat conditions.
