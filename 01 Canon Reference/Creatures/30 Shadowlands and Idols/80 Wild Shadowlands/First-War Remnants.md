# First-War Remnants

First-War remnants are Shadowlands continuities produced by the violence, desertion, and forced yolk fusion of the [[The Great Rebellion and the Yolk Exiles|Great Rebellion]]. They are not surviving angelic persons. A true fallen angel remains a named created-spirit governed by [[Fallen Angels and Broken Commissions]].

## Broken Standards

Broken Standards are moving banners, weapons, insignia, and command calls whose original bearers and armies no longer exist. They repeat affiliation without understanding purpose.

- **Origin and agency:** mindless shadow-pattern fauna formed around discarded allegiance.
- **Ecology:** a Standard gathers anything seeking a side: lost masks, frightened travelers, abandoned weapons, and unresolved battle residue. Its following becomes hostile to whatever is not marked.
- **Signs:** colors appear where no pigment remains, compasses point toward a command voice, old injuries align into formations, and objects arrange themselves into opposing camps.
- **Threat pattern:** brands allies and enemies, animates abandoned arms, forces movement into obsolete formations, and changes allegiance when a stronger surviving symbol is displayed.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** identify the dead commission or betrayal it repeats, lower rather than capture the Standard, separate followers from its mark, and unmake the central insignia at a place belonging to neither army.
- **Materials:** standard thread can reveal old routes of the rebellion but tries to classify every bearer.
- **Regional names:** first banners, side-makers, war cloth, and empty colors.
- **Story uses:** turn a ruin expedition into two compulsory armies; reveal an ancient route into an Idol heartland; attach itself to a modern crusade whose symbols resemble the lost host.

## Commission Husks

Commission Husks are discarded manifestation-bodies from orders that entered the yolks. They resemble empty angelic armor, folded wings, giant masks, or architectural organs still attempting the last command they received.

- **Origin and agency:** cunning shadow-pattern Mask with limited agency. It is an abandoned function, not the spirit that once wore it.
- **Ecology:** a Husk anchors to a narrow surviving instruction such as guard this threshold, announce this truth, deny this passage, shelter these names, or strike the marked enemy. Shadow pressure distorts scope without creating personhood.
- **Signs:** avian shadows without bodies, commands spoken in an unknown grammar but understood as obligation, doors obeying obsolete ranks, and light outlining an absent occupant.
- **Threat pattern:** enforces the surviving commission literally, copies visitors into missing roles, deploys fragments of former heavenly anatomy, and repairs itself from symbols associated with the instruction.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** reconstruct the bounded commission, fulfill or formally terminate it under truthful witness, then dismantle the empty body. Treating the Husk as a fallen angel lets its function recruit an apparent personality.
- **Materials:** commission splinters can stabilize one matching task but are dangerous when used outside its original limit.
- **Regional names:** empty angels, wing shells, old offices, and command skins.
- **Story uses:** guard refugees dead since creation; mistake a modern saint for its missing bearer; preserve the only reliable account of an Idol's original commission.

## Unhoused Witnesses

Unhoused Witnesses are autonomous Shadow persons formed from native beings and memory pressure around those who saw the second desertion but belonged to no sovereign afterward. Their knowledge is ancient, partial, and shaped by survival.

- **Origin and agency:** sapient shadow-pattern Archmasks with full moral agency. They are new persons continuous from Shadow ecology, not the original dead witnesses or angels.
- **Ecology:** each Witness maintains an unclaimed refuge, route, or testimony by refusing all seven Idol jurisdictions. Some protect fugitives; others sell truths in ways calculated to restart the First War.
- **Signs:** a site lacks every Idol's weather, stories contain sensory detail older than human language, and a stranger recognizes commission anatomy but not later mortal names.
- **Threat pattern:** hides behind incompatible accounts, redirects sovereign forms into one another, weaponizes truthful fragments, and can exile visitors from one Shadow route at the cost of exposing them to another.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** each Witness requires an individual anchor and chosen purpose. A culpable Witness can be defeated by exposing the testimony it falsified, ending the refuge or route that sustains it, and confronting it as an autonomous person rather than a relic.
- **Materials:** witness tokens preserve one scene from a nonhuman viewpoint but cannot supply a complete cosmology.
- **Regional names:** houseless ones, old witnesses, camp-between, and those-without-a-face.
- **Story uses:** guide mortals through an Idol war for a dangerous price; expose a church cosmology as partly wrong without vindicating the Idols; protect a refugee court while sacrificing outsiders to keep it hidden.
