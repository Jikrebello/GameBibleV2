# Before-Shapes

Before-Shapes are the creator-facing category for primordial Shadowlands life that predates humanity, the Great Rebellion, and the seven Idol dominions. The category is deliberately narrow: it prevents every inexplicable Shadow encounter from becoming a secret Idol plot while refusing to turn the unknown into a new pantheon.

## Origin and Continuity

A Before-Shape is a sovereign-scale shadow-pattern, not a human soul, fallen angel, Idol, patron, or Abyssal wound. Whether separate encounters describe individuals, phases, organs, or environmental expressions cannot usually be established from Material observation.

Its recorded intelligence is sovereign because it can alter routes, preserve long projects, and respond to intrusion. Its moral agency is limited in the register because investigators can attribute choices but cannot prove that it apprehends human goods and obligations in a comparable way. A specific story may establish fuller agency for one named encounter.

## Ecology

Before-Shapes occupy regions the Idols route around, bury, or claim to have conquered. They feed upon unchosen possibility, unrealized forms, relations that never acquired names, and meaning prior to human interpretation. They do not gain special nourishment from sin and may treat a cult, saint, Archmask, or animal as equivalent disturbances.

They neither form a nation nor maintain a shared hierarchy. Two sightings need not imply common allegiance.

## Signs

- shadows precede the objects that will cast them;
- observers agree on motion but not anatomy;
- a route arrives at a place for which no traveler had a concept;
- Idol fauna become quiet or lose their characteristic compulsion;
- an encounter leaves consequences that cannot be restated as fear, vice, rent, court law, residue, or refusal.

## Threat Pattern

A Before-Shape may remove distinctions needed for navigation, force objects into unrealized versions of themselves, preserve a person as one possibility among many, or move a whole site beneath an Idol border. It does not communicate through ordinary temptation. When it answers, it uses alteration, juxtaposition, repeated impossible outcomes, or temporary anatomy.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

There is no universal banishment. A local manifestation can be ended by identifying the exact relation making contact possible, restoring stable names and boundaries, and severing that relation from both sides. Destroying a visible body ends only the observed phase. White Flame can preserve human identity during withdrawal but does not grant dominion over elder Shadow.

Permanent destruction of a Before-Shape has never been authorially confirmed. Named adventures must specify whether their objective is escape, closure, displacement, dormancy, or an actual unique defeat.

## Materials and Remains

Before-Shape residue loses stable properties when classified. Samples may temporarily remember a form that a nearby object could have taken. Vortharan researchers value such material; responsible custodians treat storage as an active thin-space hazard.

## Regional Names

First shadows, elder shade, things before faces, the unimagined, and under-dreamers are mortal labels rather than true species names.

## Story Uses

- An Idol court requests mortal help because something beneath its realm is erasing vice distinctions and court geography.
- A missing town survives as several unrealized versions, none clearly the original.
- A scholar's correct classification causes the specimen to become the dangerous form the classification predicted.
