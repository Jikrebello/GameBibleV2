# Dreadforms

Dreadforms are wild Shadowlands predators organized around fear rather than one Idol's vice economy. Fear can make a route for them without being sinful. Cowardice, cruelty, or institutional exploitation may strengthen a manifestation, but a frightened victim does not feed them through moral guilt.

## Isolation Stalkers

Isolation Stalkers are long-limbed, soundless predators visible only when no other witness can see the observer. Their bodies resemble the negative space behind the nearest shelter.

- **Origin and agency:** animal Shadow fauna with no moral agency.
- **Ecology:** they hunt people cut off from reciprocal notice: lost travelers, shunned children, solitary sentries, and survivors whom everyone assumes someone else has checked.
- **Signs:** one set of footprints gains a second shadow, voices fail to carry toward companions, camp objects move only while a person is alone, and windows show an occupied room from outside but empty space within.
- **Threat pattern:** separates groups through false sounds, attacks only outside mutual sight, steals evidence that a victim was present, and retreats when accurately observed by two independent witnesses.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** establish reciprocal sight or call-and-response, mark the missing person's route, and corner the Stalker where two witnesses can describe it simultaneously. Killing it alone leaves no proof and permits recurrence.
- **Materials:** a dried eye-film shows the last place where someone was unnoticed.
- **Regional names:** no-one beasts, behind-men, lone shadows, and second sleepers.

## Helplessness Choirs

Helplessness Choirs are Masks formed from many mouths suspended in a dark membrane. They sing accurate descriptions of danger while removing each listener's confidence that action can matter.

- **Origin and agency:** cunning Shadow Mask with limited agency, anchored to a failed rescue, repeated surrender, or public spectacle of powerlessness.
- **Ecology:** a Choir grows where warnings are real and ignored until people learn that noticing pain has no consequence. It repeats the voices of those who tried first.
- **Signs:** alarms harmonize instead of summoning help, plans end with the same hopeless phrase, tools feel like stage props, and witnesses can describe a crisis perfectly but not name one possible action.
- **Threat pattern:** suppresses initiative, makes failed attempts echo before they occur, turns commands into lament, and embodies through any crowd that agrees nothing can be done.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** perform one materially effective rescue or protective act, name its limits honestly, recover the first ignored warning, and break the acoustic anchor. Empty encouragement cannot overcome a Choir built from real failure.
- **Materials:** throat membranes preserve suppressed warnings and can provide testimony after careful separation.
- **Regional names:** no-help choirs, mourning walls, surrender songs, and many mouths.

## Rejection Mimes

Rejection Mimes are autonomous Shadow persons that take the form an observer believes a community would most certainly exclude. They stage invitations, tests, and humiliations until victims reject others preemptively or surrender belonging entirely.

- **Origin and agency:** sapient Archmask with full moral agency. It understands welcome and deliberately manufactures rejection.
- **Ecology:** a Mime creates a court from gatekeepers, excluded people, false applicants, and communities terrified of infiltration. It can exploit real danger without being the cause of that danger.
- **Signs:** entry requirements change after compliance, familiar faces perform private contempt they later deny, invitations contain one impossible condition, and a stranger knows the exact words of an old exclusion.
- **Threat pattern:** impersonates feared outsiders and trusted gatekeepers, turns defensive measures into public cruelty, makes each side see the other's rejection first, and escapes through anyone who chooses humiliation over a bounded refusal.
- **Weakness and permanent defeat:** establish a truthful boundary with a real route for appeal or mercy, expose the staged rejection, restore the person used as exemplar, and deny the Mime both indiscriminate welcome and indiscriminate exclusion.
- **Materials:** mime-skin records every identity the local community considered inherently unworthy.
- **Regional names:** shut-door saints, unwelcome guests, last applicants, and threshold mockers.
