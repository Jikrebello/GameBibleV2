# Trolls

Trolls are the rare stable **fifth stage of Grobi growth**. An ogre normally collapses under the food demand, heat, pain, and tissue instability required to grow further. A troll crosses that threshold and establishes a new equilibrium: wounds close almost as quickly as they are made, organs migrate around trauma, and hunger becomes the body's governing intelligence.

They are not a Giant lineage and do not possess routable human souls. Their continuity is the same Wrath-rewritten life found throughout the Grobi ladder, driven to a terminal form.

## Ecology and Formation

Troll formation requires all of the following:

- an unusually stable ogre body;
- sustained access to dense food over several seasons;
- repeated survival of catastrophic injury;
- prolonged exposure to Wrath-saturated battlefields, massacre grounds, or warband worship;
- enough dominance to consume rivals before the growth crisis reverses.

Most candidates die, split into malformed tissue, or starve back toward ogre form. A successful troll no longer readily reverts. Its regenerative organs consume its own reserves, nearby flesh, and sometimes living prey even while the creature appears dormant.

## Intelligence and Agency

Trolls preserve cunning, recognition, territorial memory, and a brutal ability to learn from failed hunts. Their enormous metabolic burden narrows attention toward food, pain, dominance, and the destruction of threats. Some retain fragments of a former orc leader's tactics or speech. None becomes mindless merely by reaching the fifth stage, but most have less practical freedom than a hobgoblin or orc.

## Signs

- prey stripped of fat and marrow before muscle;
- severed Grobi flesh trying to crawl toward the largest surviving mass;
- deep prints accompanied by handholds torn into frozen ground;
- trees pushed over and chewed where bark carries salt or fungus;
- warbands abandoning full stores without fighting;
- a hot, iron-sweet smell under winter air.

## Threat Pattern

Trolls pursue food through fortifications rather than around them. They use uprooted trees, boulders, corpses, and smaller Grobi as tools. A troll learns which defender carries fire, acid, White Flame, or a disabling warlock script and changes its approach on the next assault.

Regeneration is not universal invulnerability. Fire can overwhelm regrowth, caustic compounds can denature the stage-glands, sustained cold can slow the process, and starvation eventually forces the body to consume itself. Dismemberment without destroying or separating the regenerative organs creates several dangerous masses rather than a clean kill.

## Permanent Defeat

A slain troll must be burned or chemically rendered until no stage-gland remains capable of organizing tissue. A battlefield left unwatched can produce a crawling remnant, a contaminated scavenger, or a new growth around a corpse pile.

## Materials and Use

Troll stage-glands are coveted by Vortharan fleshworkers and military alchemists. Extracts can accelerate healing, induce uncontrolled tumors, or cause a subject's body to consume itself. Most churches and frontier authorities ban the trade. Grobi warbands use rendered troll fat as terror medicine while avoiding living trolls whenever possible.

## Regional Names

Ättar accounts use **last-stage** and **the hunger that stands**; Folk frontier speech favors **winter-eater**; Eclessian bestiaries retain **troll**. Named specimens acquire local titles but do not form a separate lineage.
