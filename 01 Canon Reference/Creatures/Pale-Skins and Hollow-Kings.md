# Pale-Skins and Hollow-Kings

The Pale-Skins are the deep-dwelling remnant of the pre-human barrow-builders beneath the Grenzburg frontier. They are not Grobi. They are older, colder, quieter, and more politically dangerous because they can use Grobi hunger as a tool without sharing Grobi life.

The Hollow-Kings are their undying ruler-caste: embalmed sovereigns, memory-vessels, or throne-bound intelligences trying to rekindle buried engines such as the Heart-Stone.

## Cosmological Placement

Their exact corruption remains intentionally unresolved in-world, but authorial use should keep three pressures visible:

- **Material ancestry:** they were once a real subterranean/barrow-building people, not demons by default.
- **Shadowlands pressure:** their society has been thinned by hunger, secrecy, identity loss, and predatory memory architecture.
- **Astral or artifact debt:** their oldest rulers may be paying rent on ancient stability systems they no longer understand.

They are a perfect Grenzburg threat because they sit between archaeology, politics, horror, and metaphysics.

## Signs

- Pale fungal gardens arranged like court gardens.
- Grobi markings scratched over older barrow glyphs, as if a surface warband has been taught what to fear.
- Missing survey teams found alive but unable to remember the same route twice.
- Stone doors warm in winter, cold in summer.
- Human bones sorted by profession rather than body.
- Children's songs from villages echoing underground before the children are born.
- Pale figures seen by torchlight who retreat like courtiers, not animals.

## Ecology and Society

Pale-Skins live in deep galleries, barrow undercrofts, drowned halls, old ventilation shafts, and the rootwork beneath ancient cairns. They farm fungus, marrow-slurry, blind fish, grave-mold, and possibly memory itself.

Their society is hierarchical and ceremonial:

- **Skin-bearers:** scouts and abductors who can pass through narrow shafts and mimic surface distress calls.
- **Mourners:** ritual handlers who preserve bodies, names, and useful grief.
- **Engine-keepers:** technicians or priests who maintain buried mechanisms.
- **Throne-servants:** court officials who carry fragments of a Hollow-King's will.
- **Hollow-Kings:** ancient sovereigns whose bodies are less important than their command protocols, name-hoards, and throne engines.

## Tactics

Pale-Skins avoid fair fights. They prefer:

- separating groups in tunnels,
- turning Grobi warbands toward surface targets,
- collapsing familiar routes,
- bargaining through prisoners,
- staging false rescue cries,
- starving a settlement's decision-making before attacking its walls,
- using the Heart-Stone or lesser engines to amplify fear, hunger, memory, or obedience.

If forced into combat, they fight with hooks, barbed needles, choking powders, weighted nets, and disciplined retreats toward prepared rooms.

## Lairs

A Pale-Skin lair should feel like a court buried under a grave:

- audience halls too low for human posture,
- wells with voices in them,
- ossuary ledgers,
- sealed nurseries,
- throne chambers built around machines,
- fungal light that dims when names are spoken,
- Grobi tribute pits near the outer galleries.

## Materials and Loot

- **Pale bone needles:** useful for surgery, torture, and forbidden memory rites.
- **Barrow glass:** lens material for warlock scripts and relic inspections.
- **Throne wax:** embalming compound that preserves flesh and old contracts.
- **Name tablets:** dangerous archives; Church, Bank, and Jirahar scholars all want different pieces.
- **Engine fragments:** Heart-Stone-adjacent components that can stabilize or corrupt Shell scripts.

## Faction Use

- **Bank:** wants barrow glass, engine fragments, and recoverable survey rights.
- **Church:** wants proof of whether the Hollow-Kings are undead, Idol-corrupt, or merely ancient.
- **Duke:** wants the threat contained without admitting Grenzburg sits on a buried state.
- **Folk:** may remember old taboos about the barrows and resent anyone digging near them.
- **Grobi:** fear, trade with, obey, raid, or avoid Pale-Skins depending on hunger and leadership.

## Hooks

- A Grobi prisoner begs not to be returned south because "the pale court counts us."
- A Hollow-King offers to close one Grobi corridor if given names from Grenzburg's death records.
- The Heart-Stone was not stolen upward; it was answering a throne beneath the barrow.
- A missing Bank surveyor returns with perfect maps and no shadow.
- Mael knows the Lakekeepers have an old prohibition against opening one particular barrow door.
