# Peoples of the Ancient Aurin

The Enathi, Serathi and Vardeni were related Orphaned peoples of the Aurin basin, its hill districts and adjoining uplands. They developed among the survivors of Arsuvan and neighbouring human communities after Giant rule collapsed. Their histories link river cultivation, urban crafts, pastoral life and metal production to the formation of Caleran and its hinterland.

Between about 2260 and 1900 BR, related regional communities developed into the peoples later called Enathi, Serathi and Vardeni. The names became useful gradually, as speech, marriage, festivals and political loyalties acquired recognisable local forms. Their shared past included service under Arsuvan, resistance to its rulers and contact with human settlements that had survived beyond its most closely controlled districts. Ancestry ran through many households.

## Regional societies

The [[Enathi]] cultivated lowland fields and maintained the boats and landings through which heavy goods travelled. The [[Serathi]] developed competing hill towns, ancestor houses, workshops and centres of religious learning. The [[Vardeni]] connected mountain farms, seasonal pastures and ore workings. Every society included households engaged in several occupations, and marriages carried skills across these regional differences.

The three peoples shared related speech, many domestic practices and memories of Giant rule. River grain sustained upland settlements; upland metal and animals supported the river economy; hill-city workshops and markets linked both. Marriages moved knowledge along these routes. Religious differences usually followed a particular town, teacher or powerful house more closely than a clean boundary between peoples.

Their descendants would enter Atherian society under unequal circumstances. Some became citizens, landowners or temple patrons. Others were enslaved, dispossessed or driven into less accessible country. Many retained local affiliations alongside an Atherian language or civic identity. Their contribution to later heartland society cannot be reduced to a single conquered population disappearing into its conquerors.

## Settlement and later identity

[[Sarven, Nerava, Thesra and Olveth]] introduce the principal places through which these relationships can be followed. Caleran at the estuary brought inland producers into contact with shipping households. The newcomers' Ark-derived ancestry differed from that of the older Aurin communities; their descendants formed mixed settlements through trade, adoption and marriage as well as conquest and enslavement.

The spread of Atherian speech and civic institutions changed public identities over generations. Local ancestry remained important in household observances, marriage connections and claims to property. A family could become Atherian in language and citizenship while continuing to honour Enathi or Serathi forebears.
