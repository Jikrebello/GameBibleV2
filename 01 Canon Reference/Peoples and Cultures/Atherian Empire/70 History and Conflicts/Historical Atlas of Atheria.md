# Historical Atlas of Atheria

The history of Atheria extends from the southern departure of Ark-derived households to an empire whose institutions and ruins shaped the later known world. Its early centuries brought settlers into contact with independent Orphaned societies. Maritime households, hill cities and river settlements then developed forms of public life which Caleran's republic joined and expanded.

## Historical Atlas — wider inheritance

Atherian identity changed through southern settlement, maritime allegiance, city membership and republican or imperial citizenship. Its populations included descendants of the Glass People, earlier inhabitants, adopted household members, freed captives and people incorporated by conquest. The first expansion encountered occupied fields, working harbours and societies with their own experience of the Giant collapse.

Massacre, slavery, hostage-taking and land seizure accompanied treaties, intermarriage and local defeats of settler forts. Later membership could create genuine belonging while preserving unequal rights. These different experiences remain important to the histories of the empire's successors.

## Southern separation and settlement, c. 2130–1900 BR

Southern leagues left the communities nearer the Glass over several generations. Commanders, craftsmen and households sought permanent fields, defensible crossings and useful older works. Relatives remained connected through marriage, trade and journeys between camps and settlements. The growing distance made common decisions more difficult and gave local commitments increasing importance.

[[Southern Settlement and Maritime Atheria]] follows these departures into inhabited country. The first substantial Atherian arrivals in the Aurin basin encountered the [[Peoples of the Ancient Aurin|Enathi, Serathi and Vardeni]]. Their ancestors had survived the fall of [[Arsuvan - The Giant Kingdom of the Aurin|Arsuvan]], a regional Giant kingdom with a palace economy, agricultural estates and upland connections.

## Maritime societies, c. 1900–1400 BR

Ship-owning houses connected timber, metal, grain and captive labour across distant shores. A successful fleet required food and repairs ashore as well as skill at sea. Harbour families could use that dependence to restrain a commander or find themselves subjected to military seizure.

The Aurin supplied one group of these relationships. [[Arkenan and Saronikan Cities before the Empire]] describes another, centred on the eastern islands and Thalassian harbours. Erenost's grain seizure, Pyrassa's organised voyages and Irenai's teaching traditions arose from their own regional circumstances. These communities developed alongside Caleran rather than receiving every institution from it.

## Cities and royal households, c. 1400–800 BR

Larger settlements concentrated workshops, food stores, religious specialists and public buildings. Their governments had to maintain water, transport and defence while negotiating the claims of prominent houses. [[The First Atherian City-States]] introduces this wider urban development.

[[Caleran - Foundation and Royal Households]] follows the estuary city through its founding coalition, Avren's murder of Caleth, later river kings and the reign of Ilaron and Tarsenna. The couple's [[The Common Sky and the Formation of Astratheon|Common Sky settlement]] gave different religious constituencies a shared public form. Their festivals and endowments outlived the royal household and became resources of subsequent governments.

## The Compact and republic, c. 800–350 BR

The twelve-city Celestial Compact coordinated a calendar, arbitration and military contributions. Caleran became its principal meeting place, and republican assemblies, a senate and annual magistracies developed over subsequent generations. Expansion made citizenship a powerful means of incorporation, distributed unevenly among allies, subject communities and individual households.

[[The Celestial Compact and the Caleran Republic]] follows the relationship between these institutions and the land, military service and provincial wealth on which they depended. [[Food, Work and Citizenship in Republican Atheria]] describes the households whose work sustained them. By the final republican decades, powerful commanders could maintain loyalties which outlasted their public offices and resist the institutions intended to restrain them.

## Empire and later history

Aurelius Thalion's seizure of Caleran around 350 BR began the transition to hereditary imperial command. The existing [[01 Canon Reference/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/The Rise of the Atherian Empire Overview.md|account of imperial rise]] and [[01 Canon Reference/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/The Atherian Empire – Age of Glory.md|Age of Glory]] describe the next period.

The Redeemer entered this imperial world. Persecution, Conversion, regional decay, frontier accommodation and the Fall changed its political and religious inheritance. Thalmyrics and Eclessians later claimed that inheritance through different histories, not as two ancient biological branches. Atherian ruins therefore belong to many present peoples, including those conquered by the builders.
