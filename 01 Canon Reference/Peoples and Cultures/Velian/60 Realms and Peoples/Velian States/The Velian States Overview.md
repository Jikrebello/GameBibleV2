# The Velian States Overview

The Velian States are a belt of cities, river districts, estates, churches, forts, and client courts formed under Banu Mizan rule and never securely recovered by Thalmyria. They are a border civilization, not empty lost provinces.

## People and Formation

The [[Velian Culture Overview|Velians]] are an Atherian-descended people whose institutions developed through the Velian Church, municipal law, Covenant protection compacts, translation houses, water courts, and six centuries of mixed rule. Later Qahirid governments hardened tribute, garrisons, bondage, and name custody without erasing local institutions.

## Rule

No single government controls the belt. Velian princes, city councils, bishops, Covenant governors, Zanthiri patrons, Thalmyric-backed claimants, estate houses, and militias rule different roads and districts. Some towns pay two powers; others preserve autonomy by making themselves too useful to destroy.

## Redeemer Faith

The Velian Church is the principal native communion. Thalmyric-rite parishes survive in contested districts and among recent settlers. Eclessian congregations are largely foreign. Under [[The People of the Lamp|People of the Lamp]] law, worship and communal courts receive real protection alongside legal inferiority, levies, restrictions, and periodic persecution.

## Present Conflicts

Bondage, conversion pressure, resistance taxation, collaboration accusations, mixed marriage, water rights, Jirahar protection, Thalmyric ancestral claims, and the possibility that liberation merely changes the external master.
