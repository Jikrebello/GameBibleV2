# Thalmyric Canon Register

This is the creator-facing control sheet for Thalmyria. Authority descends through [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Gazetteer of the Known World]], [[Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR)]], [[Named Figures of the Known World]], [[The Great Split — Two Crowns, One Fire]], [[White Flame Doctrine]], this register, and then regional dossiers.

## Three Sacred Cities

- **Veridun** is the living imperial capital, refuge-city, coronation cathedral, and seat of [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/Kingdom of Veridun/Emperor Caedros IV Aetherion.md|Emperor Caedros IV Aetherion]]. The Great Lamp and Virelian Lamp remain there. The Virelian Lamp is the dead hearth-lamp of Radomir I's burned hall at Strazgorod, which lit itself in the hands of his fire-keeper Zdena before Patriarch Makarios (1061); her descendants tend it in the cathedral close, and under Vesari hearth-law whoever keeps a house's fire is its lord. Nikandros I refused the Sanctum's demand to try the Great Lamp in its relic court as the price of Eclessian soldiers (1227): "a lamp that is tried is a lamp that is owned."
- **Caleran** is the recovered Atherian capital, site of Jareth's martyrdom, the Pillar of Concordance and the Eternal Ember, and present seat of Patriarch Sophron. The Ember was kindled from Amaryah's lamp at Veridun Minor. Idris impounded it in 905 as a deposit held for the final audit; soldiers could not put it out, its Covenant keepers guarded it, ledgered its fuel and in 957 held it against their own amir, and Veridun paid the invoice in full on the recovery of 957. Theodosia's Rekindling (1052) relit every lamp in the empire from it, so Thalmyria's whole fire "burns on a receipt". The Redeemer's execution and Resurrection took place at Second Qiryath.
- **Virelos** is the mountain rebellion's cradle and the coronation city of the Aetherion line.

The title **Patriarch of Veridun** survives from the exile years. The Patriarchate returned to Caleran after the Reconquest without surrendering the title that legitimized the refuge Church.

## Fixed Historical Chain

Thalmyria's military provinces and soldier-tenure descend from the kindled marches of 612–905, each held under one charter-oath renewed by the emperor in person before a frontier witness on whom the Fire came. Caleran falls in 905, taken in Idris's name by the engine-master Barzin and the breach-court train after Corvenas III's public call on the Fire fails on the wall, and Corvenas III surrenders his person and relics and dies a captive in 908; Veridun survives the Veil in 906, the smoke of ovens baked against the council's order; the Mountain Rebellion runs 908-921; [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/70 History and Conflicts/The Battle of Caelmount.md|Caelmount]] (the Broken Road, 917–918) proves the resistance can destroy a field army, at the price of the valley's terraces; Caedros I takes Virelos in 921 to feed Caelmount and is crowned there in 922; the Burning Banners unify the refuges in 922-949; Caleran is recovered in 957, when Amir Durayd flees after the Ember's Covenant keepers hold it against him, and Caedros I dies at the Pillar of Concordance; Caedros II completes the first coastal campaigns by 962; and [[The Long Reconquest (908-1052 AR)|the Long Reconquest]] takes the dozen separately sworn amirates, most of them at their canal heads, until Theodosia's Rekindling in 1052.

The restored church's lamps gutter again. Leovar III's First Breaking (from 987) breaks the war-lamps and icon-lamps and hoods the Great Lamp; Ioan of Velia's *Sayings for the Foot of a Lamp*, stamped on clay lamps in Velian kilns, survive in the rubble. In the dry years after 1008 families lift hidden lamps from sunken wells; Leovar III dies of a drought well's flux (1011); on the Night of the Thousand Lamps (1013) the households unhood the Great Lamp themselves and Leovar IV yields. Leovar IV impounds every lamp in crown lamp-houses after his Master of Lamps is burned to death forcing a wayhouse lamp before Meliton (1029); the keepers sell the oil and the lamps are found dead at his bier (1049); Theodosia relights every lamp from the Ember (1052).

Caedros III (1121–1167), married and godfather to the districts, settles frontier land emptied by the Two Winters as military districts owing a lance and a lamp, and wins the War of the Southern Deeds (1131–1139) when the district lamps kindle in defence of their homes. To bar his son Orestan after the killings at Vresna, he leaves his succession to the Fire (the Kolonna Declaration, 1139); at the Unanswered Funeral (1167) no claimant's lamp kindles, Orestan is blinded for a counterfeit, and the district armies take Despina of Kolonna's kindled lamp as their warrant. At Qarath's Well (1214) Markellos's army drinks from a sworn well whose guest-peace turns the water in men who take up arms against their host; Suhrab sends the emperor home as a guest with the bill for the water, the Southern Deeds districts. Sergian's Forfeiture of the Drinkers takes the farms of every holder who drank and deposes Markellos to make the bill his own debt; the Court of Breaches answers that the bill is the Throne's, which does not die and is not deposed. No emperor has signed a Covenant instrument since 1215.

Markellos pours out the cup at Virelos (1225) and gives his claim to Nikandros, who had read the well-curb at Qarath and did not drink; every emperor since pours out a cup at his coronation. Nikandros I, of a Kedravan cadet branch of House Aetherion, refuses the Sanctum's test of the Great Lamp (1227) and recovers half the Qarath districts when plague-widowed Covenant garrisons march home (1238–1246); Evandros the Sower (1263–1290) and Stavren the Patient (1290–1329) follow; Caedros IV, a provincial Aetherion adopted by Stavren in 1328, is crowned c. 1329.

The Velian States and Zanthir remain outside secure imperial rule. The defeat at Qarath's Well and the post-1214 civil wars deepen the older demographic wound without creating it.

## Moral and Theological Rule

The Covenant occupation imposed predatory contract rule, forced movement, and legal bondage. Its evil does not make every occupation-descended family culpable. Thalmyric defense and liberation can be righteous; expulsion, massacre, or treating ancestry as guilt remains evil.

The White Flame sanctifies and restores. Pain does not buy salvation. Thalmyric asceticism is meant to strip self-deception and train attention; suffering sought as proof of holiness is spiritual vanity.

## Geographic Rule

Thalmyria lies west-central. The Covenant presses from the north and northwest; Eclessia lies east; the Thalassian shore lies east and southeast. Because the world is southern-hemisphere oriented, Kedrava and the deeper southern highlands are colder than the northern lowlands.
