# The Making of the Thalmyrics

Thalmyria is not simply old Atheria with a new flag. It is an old heartland population remade by conversion, invasion, mountain refuge, occupation, return, and the absorption of peoples who survived the same wars differently.

## The Living Layers

**Atherian heartlanders** preserve the oldest urban speech, court forms, municipal law, and family claims. They are strongest around Caleran, Veridun, Virelos, Arkena, and the old road cities.

**Mountain-refuge peoples** carried villages, monasteries, icons, livestock, and military households into the volcanic and southern uplands after 905. Their provincial speech, extended kinship customs, village assemblies, and seasonal pastoral life became central to the living empire rather than a marginal survival.

**Reconquest settlers** descend from soldiers, freed captives, returned refugees, and families granted abandoned land for service. Vlastrava and many military provinces are built from this layer.

**Occupation-descended communities** include surviving locals under Covenant contracts, garrison families, rewarded clients, transplanted laborers, and their mixed descendants. Some aided the return; some resisted it; most inherited circumstances they did not choose.

**Velians** belong to the border civilization formed beneath Banu Mizan and later Qahirid rule. Their churches, water courts, markets, and family law cannot be reduced to either occupier or captive.

**Saltborn** are Atherian-Kedravan coastfolk of the western outer shore, transformed through centuries of Ättar raid, settlement, baptism, and naval service.

## The Imperial Boast

Thalmyrics habitually claim that every useful art began in their country: law at Caleran, theology at Veridun, philosophy at Arkena, cavalry in Vlastrava, ships in Saronika, wine in Elenara, and resistance in Kedrava. The boast has enough truth to endure and enough exaggeration to irritate every neighbor.

The old heartland really did preserve institutions older than most present states. It also borrowed, absorbed, renamed, and retroactively claimed the work of provincial, Jirahar, Sestaran, Velian, and occupation-era communities.

## Regional Expression

- The imperial core favors Atherian-derived names, urban ceremony, law, icons, and court education.
- Mountain and inland regions favor extended households, village patron feasts, epic song, wood and stone churches, and military kinship.
- River valleys preserve vineyard, monastery, and market-town cultures.
- Islands and coasts speak through city councils, sailors' confraternities, shipyards, and mixed ancestry.
- Frontier communities organize memory around which generation fled, returned, collaborated, or was displaced.

## What Makes a Thalmyric

A Thalmyric recognizes the Patriarchate, the Aetherion continuity, the Burning Year, and the claim that the old empire survived by passing through fire. Blood is not sufficient. Saltborn guards, occupation-descended converts, mountain peasants, old Caleran houses, and returned Velians can all belong while arguing fiercely over who belongs first.
