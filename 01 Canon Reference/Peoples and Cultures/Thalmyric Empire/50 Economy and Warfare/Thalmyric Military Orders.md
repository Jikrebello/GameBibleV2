# Thalmyric Military Orders

Military orders combine vows, property, hospitals, archives, and specialist service. They supplement the imperial army and remain answerable to both Church discipline and secular law.

## Order of the Returning Standard

The Returning Standard preserves Reconquest roads, recovered archives, imperial memorials, and disputed return sites. Its members include mounted escorts, archivists, engineers, and relic guards. It inherits the useful service traditions formerly divided among Phoenix and Ashen-Star orders.

Its temptation is to treat every ancestral claim as superior to every living community.

## Order of the Long Watch

The Long Watch maintains frontier chapels, evacuation routes, scouts, signal towers, hospitals, and prisoner-exchange stations. It absorbs the legitimate functions of the former Eternal Vigil.

Its members spend more time preventing raids and moving civilians than fighting battles. War parties distrust its negotiated relationships across the border.

## Iron Martyrs

The Iron Martyrs began among maimed veterans and hospital clergy. They guard corrupted battlefields, dangerous relics, ossuaries, and weapons that cannot safely return to ordinary arsenals.

They reject the claim that injury proves sanctity. Their rule requires veterans to receive care before being permitted to take permanent vows.

## Ash Guard

The Ash Guard is not a Church order. It is the emperor's household guard, recruited from Saltborn and baptized Ättar oath-families whose loyalty is sworn to the sovereign's person.
