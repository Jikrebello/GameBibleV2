# Thalmyric Economy and the Old Heartland

Thalmyria possesses old infrastructure, skilled cities, fertile volcanic districts, and a chronic shortage of people, capital, and uncontested land. It is sophisticated without being as rich as its monuments imply.

## Cityward Economy

Veridun, Caleran, Virelos, Naverion, Drasovar, and the regional capitals gather guilds, schools, courts, hospitals, garrisons, aqueduct service, and Church charity. Refuge-descended neighborhoods retain village names long after the villages themselves disappear.

Caleran is the heartland's largest sacred, pilgrimage, antiquities, and hospital economy. Its 350,000 permanent residents depend on river and maritime grain, seven historic aqueduct lines, metropolitan return villages, Church reserves, and a port whose stoppage becomes an imperial concern within days.

Cities offer work and water. The countryside offers land entangled in title, service, ruin law, and infrastructure no household can repair alone.

## Agriculture

Northern lowlands and sheltered valleys produce wheat, olives, wine, fruit, and vegetables. Highland households rely on barley, sheep, goats, timber, honey, and seasonal movement. Southern Kedrava is colder, with pine highlands, short seasons, and hardy grains.

Monasteries preserve terraces, seed stocks, mills, vineyards, and guest houses. Their land wealth gives them real public value and considerable political power.

## Antiquities Economy

Declared finds move through assessors, bishops, city museums, Crown archives, and licensed auction. Ordinary coins, tile, masonry, and scrap circulate legally. Relics, strategic archives, active contracts, and sealed mechanisms do not.

Smugglers sell to Sestaran collectors, noble cabinets, warlock colleges, and foreign courts. Forged provenance is often more profitable than excavation.

## War and Debt

Frontier armies consume grain, iron, animals, timber, cloth, and coin. Sestaran lenders hold paper on great houses, naval yards, city waterworks, and imperial tax farms. Thalmyric pride condemns foreign finance while depending on it.

## Regional Strengths

- Saronika builds ships, trades, fishes, and manages naval customs.
- Vlastrava supplies soldiers, horses, iron, and fortification.
- Elenara produces wine, grain, medicine, manuscripts, and monastic craft.
- Arkena exports education, bronze work, navigation, oil, and antiquities expertise.
- Kedrava supplies timber, livestock, sailors, mountain troops, and preserved foods.
- Saltborn districts provide pilots, keelwrights, fish, salt, and coast guards.

## Present Pressures

Abandoned aqueduct branches, war taxation, veteran land demands, Medori debt, relic smuggling, and return litigation prevent simple recovery. Imperial programs that “restore” a village often create a new fortified town nearby because rebuilding the old legal and water systems costs more than starting again.
