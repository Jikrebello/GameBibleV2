# Zanthir and the Unfinished Reconquest

Thalmyric law calls Zanthir and the Velian belt unrecovered imperial land. This is a claim of succession, not a description of present sovereignty.

## The Imperial Case

The regions belonged to Atheria, contain old churches and imperial infrastructure, and entered Covenant rule through conquest, bondage, and imposed legal systems. Thalmyria maintains claimants, exile houses, military plans, and Church missions.

## The Civilian Reality

Six centuries have produced living Velian and Zanthiri societies. Many residents oppose bondage without desiring annexation. Others rely on contract-bound water, title, inheritance, and trade systems that cannot be erased in a victory ceremony.

## Court Factions

Frontier militants argue that Marzuq's weakness and Badis's divided tribute create the best opportunity in generations. Veterans, Prokopia, Sophron, merchants, and frontier communities count the people, roads, debt, and retaliation a crusade would consume.

Caedros IV's biography points toward war; his experience points away from believing war will end cleanly.

## The Moral Boundary

Liberating captives, ending name custody, and defending threatened communities are righteous aims. Treating ancestry as guilt, erasing local law indiscriminately, or using holy sites to excuse conquest are not.
