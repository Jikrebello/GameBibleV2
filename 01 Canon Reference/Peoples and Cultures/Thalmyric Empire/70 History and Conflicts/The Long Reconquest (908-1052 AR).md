# The Long Reconquest (908-1052 AR)

The Long Reconquest is the 144-year struggle by which the mountain refuges became the Thalmyric Empire and recovered the core Atherian heartland. Caleran's return in 957 was its sacred midpoint, not its conclusion.

## Flight and Occupation, 905-908 AR

The fall of Caleran scattered the heartland. Urban households fled toward mountain monasteries, rural estates, fortified valleys, eastern ports, and Eclessia. Covenant authorities secured roads, ports, irrigation works, and archives before attempting complete territorial control.

Occupation repopulation mixed garrison households, rewarded clients, contract-bound survivors, imported laborers, and forcibly transplanted subjects. Land and water rights were rewritten into Covenant instruments. This policy created functioning occupied districts while ensuring that later return would never be a simple restoration.

## Mountain Rebellion, 908-921 AR

Caedros Aetherion joined surviving soldiers, refuge councils, monasteries, village militias, and dispossessed houses. The resistance initially fought through ambush, evacuation, road denial, and the preservation of people rather than territory.

[[The Battle of Caelmount]] in 917 proved a Covenant field force could be defeated in the mountains. Virelos was secured in 921, giving the rebellion a defensible political center.

## Crown and Burning Banners, 922-949 AR

Caedros was crowned at Virelos in 922. The [[The War of the Burning Banners|War of the Burning Banners]] brought independent refuge armies, monastery leagues, and noble claimants under one crown. The result was not administrative unity but a shared command, tax, and return policy.

## The Caleran Campaign, 950-962 AR

The Caleran campaign produced the first large body of disputed reports concerning White Flame manifestations in battle. Church authorities distinguish these rare events from the rune-script Shell-fire used by imperial warlocks. Caleran was recovered in 957. Caedros I died at the Pillar of Concordance as witnesses reported the White Flame descending; Caedros II completed the immediate campaigns by 962.

The Eternal Ember had remained lit under Covenant guard and property law. Veridun paid the occupation ledger in full, preserving both the Ember and the humiliating fact that its enemy had kept the account.

## Province by Province, 962-1052 AR

The next ninety years were fought through roads, irrigation districts, walled towns, marriage settlements, amnesties, sieges, and competing land registers. Many returned families found their ancestral villages inhabited by other families with legal claims of their own. Some settlements negotiated continuity; others suffered expulsion, massacre, or repeated changes of ruler.

The First and Second Breakings weakened the return by turning imperial force inward. Empress Theodosia ended the Second Breaking and secured the surviving core road network in 1052. The Feast of the Rekindling commemorates religious restoration and the practical reconnection of the heartland.

## What Was Not Recovered

The Velian States remained fragmented beneath Covenant-aligned rulers, local churches, mixed communities, and resistance networks. Zanthir survived as an autonomous emirate through tribute and diplomacy. Thalmyric maps call both unfinished provinces; their inhabitants do not agree.

## The Demographic Inheritance

Four generations of flight and return concentrated population around walls, markets, monasteries, garrisons, and restored aqueducts. Many villages never revived. Others carried occupation-descended populations that return law refused to recognize. The modern empty interior is therefore a political landscape, not untouched wilderness.
