# Challenges and Future Tensions

Thalmyria's future is threatened by the same forces that make it powerful: militarized faith, imperial memory, frontier pressure, war debt, and a political culture that treats compromise as spiritual danger.

## Active Tensions

- Mystics versus militarists inside the Church.
- Veterans and settlers demanding land after crusades.
- Imperial centralization versus local noble privilege.
- The cost of maintaining permanent readiness against the Ahl-e-Ahd.
- The temptation to make every political problem a holy emergency.
