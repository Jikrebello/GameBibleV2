# Challenges and Future Tensions

Thalmyria's strength and its dangers grow from the same inheritance: old institutions, sacred memory, recovered territory, a permanent frontier, and the visible remains of a greater empire beneath nearly every settled district.

## The Unsettled Crown

Caedros IV has no living accepted heir. Alexane, Sophios, and an adopted commander each promise a different empire. Provincial rulers are already turning appointments, marriages, and military budgets into succession positions.

## Zanthir and the War Party

Nikandros's death gave renewed force to those demanding a final campaign. Veterans who know the cost, frontier communities that would become the battlefield, and treasury officials who cannot finance it form an unstable peace coalition.

## The Empty Interior

Cities and fortified corridors grow while many villages shrink. Abandoned terraces, broken aqueduct branches, and unsafe roads surrender land to smugglers, local strongmen, and the dead. Resettlement schemes repeatedly underestimate how much infrastructure a village needs before families will stay.

## The Buried Empire

Imperial, Church, landholder, and finder claims overlap whenever antiquities emerge. Most finds are mundane; the rare archive, relic, weapon cache, or functioning system can redraw a title, embarrass a saint cult, bankrupt a noble house, or begin a dungeon expedition.

## Debt and Dependence

Sestaran loans maintain fleets, granaries, and reconstruction. Credit prevents immediate collapse while moving future customs revenue and antiquities concessions beyond imperial control. Prokopia's chancellery seeks solvency without allowing creditors to own the recovery.

## Crown and Church

The Holy Synod and emperor require one another but dispute appointments, military preaching, relic law, monastic land, and Caleran's influence over Veridun. Neither side is united: contemplatives, administrators, militants, provincial bishops, and court clergy form changing coalitions.

## Return and Belonging

Mixed occupation-descended communities remain vulnerable to purist politics. The state must decide whether loyalty, law, ancestry, or suffering defines belonging. Every answer protects someone and exposes someone else.

## Campaign Fronts

- **Succession:** a court appointment becomes the first irreversible alignment.
- **Frontier:** a manufactured atrocity could force the Zanthir war.
- **Interior:** reopening one abandoned district requires choosing whose old claims survive.
- **Antiquities:** a buried census contradicts the property order of an entire province.
- **Debt:** a Sestaran rescue package demands control of a recovered imperial harbor.
- **Church:** an authentic miracle is claimed by a movement preaching a false theology of pain.
