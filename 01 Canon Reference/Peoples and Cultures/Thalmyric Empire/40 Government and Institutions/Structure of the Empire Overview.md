The **Thalmyric Empire** is a vast confederation of semi-autonomous realms, each bound by oath and sacrament to the **Imperial Crown** and the authority of the **Thalmyric Church**. Though distinct in culture, terrain, and historical development, all constituent states share the vision of the **Reconquest**, the restoration of lost lands, and the expansion of the **Redeemer’s flame** throughout the known world.

Each realm plays a unique role—military, spiritual, naval, or economic—in maintaining the empire’s strength. Their political structures vary from monarchies to ecclesiastical duchies to maritime councils, but all are unified by their loyalty to the **Emperor of Flame and Crown** and the guidance of the **Patriarch of Veridun**.

---

### **I. [[The Kingdom of Veridun Overview]]**

The **heart of the empire**, Veridun is the spiritual, political, and cultural nucleus of Thalmyric civilization. Its capital, **Caleran**, houses both the **Imperial Court** and the **Patriarchate of Veridun**.

- **Terrain**: Rugged highlands, volcanic ridges, ancient ruins.
- **Notable Cities**: Caleran (capital), Virelos (scholarly bastion), Emberhold (military fortress).
- **Features**: Fortified monasteries, burning spires, high temples.
- **Role**: Central authority, origin of imperial theology, administrative core.

Veridun's warriors are known as **Firewardens**, elite paladins and strategoi trained in both statecraft and holy war.

---

### **II. [[The Kingdom of Saronis Overview]]**

The **naval engine** of the empire, Saronis commands the islands and coastal strongholds of the **Thalassian Sea**.

- **Terrain**: Archipelagos, wind-beaten cliffs, coral fortresses.
- **Notable Cities**: Navarein (naval capital), Isles of Elmor, Port Erenthos.
- **Features**: Lighthouse monasteries, floating shrines, sea-bound crusader fleets.
- **Role**: Maritime dominance, trade enforcement, naval reconquest.

The **Saronic Admiralty** is both military and religious, maintaining fleets of **Sea-Paladins** and storm temples where sailors are blessed before each voyage.

---

### **III. [[The Kingdom of Valrathis Overview]]**

The **military bulwark** of the empire, Valrathis is famed for its **cavalry legions**, crusading traditions, and massive fortresses.

- **Terrain**: Plains, rolling hills, fortified valleys.
- **Notable Cities**: Duras Vahn (crusader capital), Flamegate, Redfield.
- **Features**: Holy siege academies, relic armories, knightly estates.
- **Role**: Core of the Reconquest, command of frontier legions.

Home to many **military orders**, including the **Order of the Phoenix Ascendant** and the **Iron Martyrs**, Valrathis’ nobles are warrior-kings bred for holy war.

---

### **IV. [[The Duchy of Elenor Overview]]**

A **mountain-bound ecclesiastical duchy**, Elenor is known for its spiritual severity and fierce defense of the faith.

- **Terrain**: Alpine valleys, sacred peaks, isolated monasteries.
- **Notable Sites**: High Cloister of Eluneth, Chapel of Chains, Ash Ridge.
- **Features**: Monastic fortresses, flaming prayer wheels, holy ascetics.
- **Role**: Spiritual backbone, supply of clergy, mystic orders.

The **Duke-Abbot** of Elenor rules both as a noble and archpriest, commanding both knights and monks in defense of sacred ground.

---

### **V. [[The Velian States Overview]]**

Now fragmented and **under occupation** by the Ahl-e-Ahd, the Velian States were once the **richest and most culturally diverse** regions of the empire.

- **Terrain**: Urban sprawl, river deltas, flowering plains.
- **Notable Cities**: Zareth (Ahl-e-Ahd governorate), Old Myrh, the Citadel of Shifting Light.
- **Features**: Blended temples, contract courts, resistance enclaves.
- **Role**: Symbol of lost glory, ground-zero of reconquest efforts.

Many inhabitants live double lives: **publicly oath-bound**, privately loyal to the Redeemer. Secret Thalmyric rites are whispered beneath occupied spires.

---

### **VI. [[Emirate of Zanthir Overview]]**

The **northernmost stronghold** of the Ahl-e-Ahd and the ultimate target of the Reconquest. Zanthir is as much symbol as city.

- **Terrain**: River-girded marble city, spice gardens, mirror towers.
- **Notable Sites**: Palace of Qabir, the Sapphire Court, Vault of Oaths.
- **Features**: Contract libraries, fire-resistant domes, slave-markets.
- **Role**: Ahl-e-Ahd cultural heartland, focal point of ideological resistance.

The **retaking of Zanthir** is prophesied by some Thalmyrics to signal the opening flames of the Redeemer’s return.

---

### **VII. [[Principality of Caedmon’s Reach Overview]]**

A **rugged, sea-facing principality**, Caedmon’s Reach is renowned for its **peasant militias, knight-villeins, prophetic mariners**, and a unique system of **agrarian monasticism**.

- **Terrain**: Windblown cliffs, salt plains, pine-cloaked highlands.
- **Notable Cities**: Aestra’s Landing, Chapel Haven, the Lighthouse of Grale.
- **Features**: Crusader seaports, sacred cartography schools, monastic granges.
- **Role**: Naval skirmishers, holy exploration, frontier settlement.

The principality was initially poor and sparsely defended, but **grange monasteries** led by Cinderian monks brought advanced agriculture, cattle rearing, and irrigation. Militant religious orders followed, founding **peasant-knightly enclaves**. Their navy, though small, is famed for daring raids and spiritual resilience. Here, **every farmhand dreams of becoming a martyr-sailor**.

---

### **VIII. [[The Thematic of Archenia Overview]]**

**Archenia** is an archipelago of **philosopher-priests, island city-states**, and ancient flame-temples. It is ruled by the **Metropolitan League**, a council of high archons, each representing a city or sacred academy.

- **Terrain**: Island chains, olive hills, volcanic springs, ancient ruins.
- **Notable Cities**: Veros (city of debates), Pyraxa (home of flame-philosophers), Irannis, Delkos, Cyramae.
- **Features**: Heated amphitheaters, geometric fire-temples, bronze oracle-altars, floating processions.
- **Role**: Theological refinement, preservation of classical flame-doctrines, arcane experimentation.

Though distant and often internally fractious, the Archenians are respected for their **intellectual rigor**, **devotion to structured worship**, and role as **devil’s advocates** in theological councils. Their island-priests are known to host **symposia of fire**, where wine, logic, and sacred chants flow together.

---

### **Conclusion: One Empire, Many Flames**

The Thalmyric Empire is not a monolith, but a living flame composed of many tongues—each realm contributing its voice, its heat, and its sacred duty. Bound by fire, faith, and the Redeemer’s call, they stand together against the coming dark—**each a facet of the ever-burning flame.**
