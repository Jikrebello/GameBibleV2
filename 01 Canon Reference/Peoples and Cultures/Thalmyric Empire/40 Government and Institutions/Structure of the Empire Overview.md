# Structure of the Empire Overview

Thalmyria is an empire of one crown, one imperial law, one Church communion, and many inherited governments. Refuge leagues and Reconquest settlements were never erased; they became provinces, kingdoms, duchies, city privileges, military districts, and Church exemptions.

## The Emperor

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/Kingdom of Veridun/Emperor Caedros IV Aetherion.md|Emperor Caedros IV Aetherion]] commands foreign policy, the army, imperial appointments, strategic roads, currency, and the return law. He rules from Veridun and is crowned at Virelos.

The Emperor is neither priest nor divine. His duty is to preserve an empire devoted to the Redeemer under moral judgment, not to embody the White Flame.

## The Chancellery

Grand Chancellor Prokopia directs treasury, provincial accounts, appointments, correspondence, and the imperial calendar. The Master of Lamps administers the Rekindling relay and palace. The office was created after 1012 to keep the crown lamp-houses, where households could leave their lamps out of the Breakers' reach, and was soon used to impound them: from 1029 its officers sealed every lamp but the hooded Great Lamp and the Ember in those houses, where its clerks sold the oil and the lamps died. In 1052 it was remade to run the relay that relit them all from the Ember. The chancellor has kept the imperial seals under two locks since 1214, when a chancellor pressed a sick emperor's seal to the Forfeiture of the Drinkers; one key is the emperor's. Provincial courts use imperial law alongside local charters and Church jurisdiction.

## The Holy Synod

Patriarch Sophron and the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/20 Faith and Worldview/The Patriarchate of Veridun.md|Holy Synod of Veridun]] govern doctrine, clergy, sacraments, glorification, and ecclesiastical discipline from Caleran. The Synod may bless or refuse imperial campaigns but does not command the army.

[[Zanthir and the Unfinished Reconquest]] records the imperial claim.

## Cities and Countryside

Chartered cities possess courts, guilds, walls, water rights, and representation unavailable to most villages. Rural districts answer through landholders, monasteries, military tenure, and provincial assemblies.

Military tenure is older than the empire. Before the Fall about a dozen kindled marches held the old frontier, each under a single charter sworn in the Fire and renewed at every accession by the emperor in person, kneeling before a frontier monk on whom the Flame came; march land was held for service and paid no tax. Thalmyria's military provinces descend from those marches. Caedros III renewed the pattern after the Two Winters, settling the emptied frontier as military districts in which each holding owed a lance in war and a lamp lit from the Rekindling relay every year. Population and authority therefore continue to move cityward.

## Antiquities Jurisdiction

The Crown, Church, cities, landholders, and licensed finders possess overlapping rights defined in [[The Layers Beneath Thalmyria]]. Provincial governors cannot quietly appropriate a discovered archive without risking both imperial prosecution and ecclesiastical censure.
