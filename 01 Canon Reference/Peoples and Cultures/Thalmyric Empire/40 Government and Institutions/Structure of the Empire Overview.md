# Structure of the Empire Overview

Thalmyria is an empire of one crown, one imperial law, one Church communion, and many inherited governments. Refuge leagues and Reconquest settlements were never erased; they became provinces, kingdoms, duchies, city privileges, military districts, and Church exemptions.

## The Emperor

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/Kingdom of Veridun/Emperor Caedros IV Aetherion.md|Emperor Caedros IV Aetherion]] commands foreign policy, the army, imperial appointments, strategic roads, currency, and the return law. He rules from Veridun and is crowned at Virelos.

The Emperor is neither priest nor divine. His duty is to preserve an empire devoted to the Redeemer under moral judgment, not to embody the White Flame.

## The Chancellery

Grand Chancellor Prokopia directs treasury, provincial accounts, appointments, correspondence, and the imperial calendar. The Master of Lamps administers the Rekindling relay and palace. Provincial courts use imperial law alongside local charters and Church jurisdiction.

## The Holy Synod

Patriarch Sophron and the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/20 Faith and Worldview/The Patriarchate of Veridun.md|Holy Synod of Veridun]] govern doctrine, clergy, sacraments, glorification, and ecclesiastical discipline from Caleran. The Synod may bless or refuse imperial campaigns but does not command the army.

## Constituent Realms

- [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/Kingdom of Veridun/The Kingdom of Veridun Overview.md|The Kingdom of Veridun Overview]]: imperial capital, recovered Caleran, Virelos, and the densest heartland.
- [[The Maritime Kingdom of Saronika Overview]]: navy, eastern ports, islands, and Sestaran-facing trade.
- [[The Kingdom of Vlastrava Overview]]: northern fortresses, veteran estates, and the Covenant frontier.
- [[The Duchy of Elenara Overview]]: river valleys, vineyards, monasteries, and return archives.
- [[The Military Province of Arkena Overview]]: island city councils, academies, and naval administration.
- [[Principality of Kedrava Overview]]: southern mountain refuges, cold outer coast, and militia valleys.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/The Saltborn Coast Overview.md|The Saltborn Coast Overview]]: a cultural and maritime layer crossing several jurisdictions.

## Unrecovered Lands

The [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Velian/60 Realms and Peoples/Velian States/The Velian States Overview.md|Velian States]] remain contested and culturally mixed. Zanthir is an autonomous Ahl-e-Ahd emirate under [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/60 Realms and Peoples/Emirate of Zanthir/Emirate of Zanthir Overview.md|Emir Badis]], not a Thalmyric province. [[Zanthir and the Unfinished Reconquest]] records the imperial claim.

## Cities and Countryside

Chartered cities possess courts, guilds, walls, water rights, and representation unavailable to most villages. Rural districts answer through landholders, monasteries, military tenure, and provincial assemblies. Population and authority therefore continue to move cityward.

## Antiquities Jurisdiction

The Crown, Church, cities, landholders, and licensed finders possess overlapping rights defined in [[The Layers Beneath Thalmyria]]. Provincial governors cannot quietly appropriate a discovered archive without risking both imperial prosecution and ecclesiastical censure.
