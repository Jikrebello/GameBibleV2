# Kalzaret, Bastion of the Returning Road

Kalzaret is a Vlastravan frontier town built around an Atherian road station, occupation caravan court, Reconquest fortress, and modern truce market. Its hybrid name preserves the occupation spelling that imperial language reform never displaced.

## The Town

Walls enclose a garrison, mixed neighborhoods, Long Watch hospital, caravan yards, two principal churches, contract court archive, and a market operating under truce articles. Farms cluster within signal range.

## Historical Layers

The Atherian road lies beneath the main street. Occupation cisterns still supply water. Reconquest tunnels connect the fortress to old quarries. A sealed name vault beneath the court cannot be destroyed without threatening several current titles.

## Present Conflict

Soldiers want stronger walls; merchants want a longer truce; return families want property; occupation-descended households want recognition; war parties want an incident. The town survives because no single faction receives everything.
