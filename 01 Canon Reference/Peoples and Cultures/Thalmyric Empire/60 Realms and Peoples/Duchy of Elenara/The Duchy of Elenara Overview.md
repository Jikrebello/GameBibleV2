# The Duchy of Elenara Overview

Elenara is the central river and monastery duchy: vineyards, grain terraces, market towns, hospitals, archive houses, and abandoned estates beneath green hills. Its living culture grew from river-valley villages, pastoral uplands, monastery estates, and Atherian provincial law.

## Historical Formation

Rural and monastic refuges absorbed urban families after 905. Occupation rule concentrated around river estates and irrigation works. Return claimants later found both surviving villages and imposed populations in possession of their land.

## Government

Duchess Mirena Ielen rules with town councils, landed houses, bishops, and powerful monasteries. Imperial return law and Church charters give almost every major estate several plausible histories.

## Settlement and Economy

Cities and monastery towns grow while high terraces empty. Wine, grain, fruit, medicine, manuscripts, wool, mills, and pilgrimage hospitality support the duchy. Abandoned aqueduct branches make some fertile valleys unusable.

## Faith and Ruins

Monasteries preserve icons, seed, occupation testimony, and records that can overturn noble title. Beneath churches lie Atherian villas, martyr rooms, and hidden lamp caches from the Breakings.

## Present Conflicts

Archive access, monastery taxation, cityward migration, ruin ownership, noble debt, and whether empty estates should go to veterans, return claimants, or current tenants.

## Story Uses

Monastery mysteries, vineyard inheritance, ghost villages, forged charters, buried archives, relic assessment, and city politics driven by rural families.
