# Principality of Kedrava Overview

Kedrava is the southern mountain and western outer-coast principality of Aestrin, the Kedra monastery-port, and Grael Beacon. It preserves the strongest village memory of refuge and resistance. Its colder highlands, pine valleys, peasant militias, agrarian monasteries, and [[The Saltborn Coast Overview|Saltborn maritime rim]] stand apart from the monumental imperial core.

## Historical Formation

Kedravan valleys received refugees after Caleran's fall and supplied food, guides, sailors, and militias to the Mountain Rebellion. Their service predates the imperial crown, a fact local assemblies cite whenever Veridun demands more.

## Government

Prince Radovan Kedrin rules through village assemblies, monastery estates, coast councils, and militia captains. His authority is strongest on imperial roads and weakest in valleys whose charters derive directly from the refuge years.

## Settlement and Economy

Cold uplands support hardy grain, sheep, timber, cattle, honey, preserved fish, and seasonal labor. Villages shrink as young people enter the army, monasteries, ports, or Veridun. Coast settlements supply sailors and explorers rather than great fleets.

## Faith and Ruins

Refuge monasteries preserve plain timber churches, painted interiors, granary caves, resistance tunnels, and hidden archives. Pre-Atherian hill sites survive beyond the densest imperial construction.

## Present Conflicts

Central taxation, depopulation, monastery privilege, Saltborn service rights, militia independence, Ättar raiding, and resentment that Veridun celebrates Kedravan sacrifice while investing elsewhere.

## Story Uses

Mountain assemblies, refuge tunnels, cold pilgrimages, coastal beacons, village feuds, monastery granaries, and evidence that a celebrated imperial victory belonged to local fighters.
