# The Saltborn Coast Overview

The Saltborn are the coast people of **Kedrava's rugged western and southwestern shore**, where Thalmyria faces the outer sea and the principal Ättar routes. They are an Atherian-Kedravan population transformed by centuries of Ättar raiding, settlement, baptism, naval service, and intermarriage.

They are Thalmyric in faith and allegiance, Kedravan in political inheritance, Ättar in substantial ancestry, and entirely their own at sea. Smaller Saltborn districts exist in Saronika, Caleran, and other imperial ports through naval migration, but the eastern Thalassian shore is not their homeland.

## Where They Are

The homeland follows Kedrava's steep outer coast beneath cold pine highlands and refuge monasteries.

- **Halmenth**, at the western mouth of the **Celion**, is the chief port, the site of the Council of 411 AR, and the drowning-place of Saint Naris. [[Celion Light Station]] stands six miles west on Saint Naris's Tooth and marks the outer reef passage.
- **Soligrad** and its fishing settlements face the tidal waters associated with the **Thirty Sisters**.
- Inland roads climb toward Kedravan halls, resistance monasteries, pasture valleys, and the imperial heartland.
- West and south lie the direct sea roads to the Lion-Mane Coast, Qazmarid's strait, and the distant Nsizwa routes.

## How They Were Made

1. **Atherian-Kedravan coast.** Imperial beacon ports, fisheries, garrisons, monasteries, and ship stations formed the first durable maritime layer.
2. **Ättar raiding age, c. 400-540 AR and after.** Lion-Mane and other crews repeatedly struck the western shore. Raids became winter settlements, military service, intermarriage, and rival coast dynasties.
3. **Fall and occupation.** The coast endured Qazmari and other Covenant pressure through the western strait while mountain refuges preserved local resistance.
4. **Reconquest and baptism.** Kedravan rulers and the Patriarchate incorporated Saltborn halls through service, baptism, land grants, and naval obligations rather than erasing them.
5. **Imperial dispersion.** Saltborn crews later settled in Saronikan, Arkenan, and Caleran districts as sailors, pilots, guards, and shipwrights.

## Custom and Faith

Saltborn people keep saints' names beside inherited totem bynames, braid sea-knots into wedding crowns, and serve mead outside church doors. These customs do not make them secret old-rite worshippers.

Saint Naris is the coast's great martyr. Naris chapels pray for those lost at sea and maintain registers of unreturned ships. The claim that Naris personally routes drowned souls remains a popular belief under Synodal review.

A few lineages preserve moon-scars, emergency rage, changeling stories, or illicit skinwalker renewal. [[Baptized Ättar Halls|Baptized Ättar tradition]] holds that conversion ended the lawful child-price. Any family still offering children to renew transformation acts against both Church teaching and the settled Ättar rule of renunciation.

## The Thirty Sisters

Soligrad's waters carry formation-bells linked to the Thirty Sisters and drowned Atherian works. Some lineages can hear those bells beneath ordinary surf. The inheritance is cultivated sensitivity, not inherited guilt, and may reflect generations of exposure to the Undertow's channels.

## Imperial Service

Saltborn crews are central to Kedrava's coast defence and serve throughout the imperial navy. Baptized Ättar oath-families and Saltborn households also supply the Ash Guard, binding themselves to the crowned emperor rather than inland court factions.
