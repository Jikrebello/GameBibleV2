# The Velian States Overview

The Velian States are a belt of cities, river districts, estates, churches, and client courts formed under Banu Mizan rule and never securely recovered by Thalmyria. They are not empty lost provinces. They are a border civilization with six centuries of mixed institutions.

## Historical Formation

The states began as first-wave conquest clients after 601 AR. Water courts, translation houses, local churches, Covenant contracts, Jirahar quarters, and Atherian municipal traditions developed together. Later Qahirid rule hardened military and name custody without erasing the older Banu Mizan inheritance.

The Long Reconquest reached portions of the belt but never established durable control. Towns changed allegiance, paid tribute both ways, or negotiated their own survival.

## Government and Faith

No single government rules the whole belt. Client princes, city councils, Covenant officials, bishops of the Velian Church, Thalmyric-backed claimants, and local militias control different districts.

The Velian Church follows the condemned Man-Bearer settlement from Halmenth and remains distinct from both Patriarchate and Covenant religion.

## Settlement and Economy

Dense river cities and irrigated towns survive where contracts maintain water and roads. Rural communities can be bound to infrastructure whose destruction would harm civilians before it harms rulers.

## Present Conflicts

Collaboration accusations, resistance taxation, dual tribute, mixed marriage, water rights, Jirahar protection, Thalmyric ancestral claims, and the possibility that “liberation” replaces one external master with another.

## Story Uses

Espionage, water-court disputes, insurgency, prisoner exchange, contract release, divided families, old churches, and cities that must be saved without accepting the legal architecture holding them together.
