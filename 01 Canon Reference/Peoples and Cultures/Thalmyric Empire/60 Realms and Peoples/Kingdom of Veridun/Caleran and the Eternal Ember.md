# Caleran and the Eternal Ember

Caleran is the old capital of Atheria, the site of the Redeemer's execution and resurrection signs, the Pillar of Concordance, the Eternal Ember, and the present seat of the Patriarch of Veridun.

## The Recovered City

Caleran lies on the northwestern lowland coast at the Aurin's mouth. Atherian forums, baths, palaces, temples, aqueducts, and road monuments remain embedded in the modern city. Converted churches, occupation courts, Reconquest walls, and pilgrimage districts occupy the same ground.

The city fell in 905 and returned in 957. It never again became the ordinary imperial court because Veridun's refuge government had become indispensable and the return remained incomplete.

## The Eternal Ember

The Ember remained lit throughout occupation under Covenant guard and property law. It is neither ordinary fuel nor an object the Patriarch can command. Its continuity anchors Thalmyric memory while the occupation invoice prevents a simple triumphalist story.

## The Patriarchal Seat

Patriarch Sophron and the Holy Synod govern from the Cathedral of the Eternal Ember. The title “of Veridun” preserves the refuge succession. Major synods alternate between Caleran and Veridun when crown and Church require mutual presence.

## Below Caleran

Every building project risks opening an older city. Known underlayers include senate tunnels, martyr crypts, Star temples, name vaults, siege works, sealed occupation courts, and forgotten aqueduct branches.

## Story Uses

Patriarchal politics, pilgrimage, contested excavation, old-city crime, impounded sacred objects, active contracts, urban collapse, and evidence capable of changing the accepted history of Atheria.
