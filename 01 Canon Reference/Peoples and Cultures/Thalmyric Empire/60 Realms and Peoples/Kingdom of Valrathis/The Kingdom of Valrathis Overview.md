# The Kingdom of Valrathis Overview

The Kingdom of Valrathis is a hard military realm on the edges of Thalmyric power: iron roads, fortress towns, veteran orders, and border populations shaped by generations of crusade.

## Role in the Setting

Valrathis is the empire's clenched fist. It produces soldiers, siege engineers, and commanders who distrust soft diplomacy. Its nobility measures virtue in endurance, discipline, and willingness to burn corruption out before it spreads.

## Conflicts

- Military orders competing with royal authority.
- Border civilians exhausted by permanent mobilization.
- Officers hiding atrocities under the language of necessity.
- Reformers trying to prevent Valrathis from becoming a holy barracks-state.

## Game Use

Use Valrathis for war stories, fortress politics, military trials, monster-frontier patrols, and questions about when righteous violence becomes its own idol.
