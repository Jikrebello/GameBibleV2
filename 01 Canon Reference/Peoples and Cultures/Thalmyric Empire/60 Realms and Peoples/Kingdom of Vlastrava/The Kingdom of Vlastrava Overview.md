# The Kingdom of Vlastrava Overview

Vlastrava is the northern military kingdom of Drasovar, Zharin Gate, and the Chervena Fields. Its frontier culture joins cavalry households, fortified towns, veteran villages, epic memory, and a population tired of being treated as the empire's permanent shield.

## Historical Formation

Occupation authorities settled garrisons and contract clients along the northern roads. Reconquest armies recovered the region through repeated sieges and return grants. Old and occupation-descended communities remain intermingled beneath military law.

## Government

King Dragovan II Vlastar rules with fortress commanders, veteran assemblies, landed houses, bishops, and imperial inspectors. He supports readiness while resisting Flame-Marshals who would turn Vlastrava into the launch ground for Zanthir.

## Settlement and Economy

Walls matter more than administrative borders. People cluster in fortress towns and veteran colonies; exposed villages serve seasonally or lie empty. Horses, iron, grain, leather, arms, and military contracts sustain the realm.

## Faith and Ruins

Frontier churches keep ossuaries, return banners, and occupation testimony. Atherian forts lie beneath later castles. Some recovered villages preserve Covenant water works or family contracts that cannot be destroyed without harming current residents.

## Present Conflicts

Veteran land, return claims, officer impunity, civilian exhaustion, mixed ancestry, warlock licensing, and pressure for a new crusade.
