# Emirate of Zanthir Overview

The Emirate of Zanthir is the Ahl-e-Ahd-aligned frontier emirate built from occupied imperial provinces, contract-bound garrisons, and local collaborator houses. It is the pressure valve between Thalmyria, the Velian States, and the wider Ahl-e-Ahd world.

## Role in the Setting

Zanthir should feel like a border regime where law is weaponized. Every road, bridge, irrigation right, and tax privilege is backed by contract magic and name-custody. It is not simply a conquered province; it is an experiment in turning occupation into enforceable metaphysics.

## Conflicts

- Puppet nobles who want legitimacy without full Ahl-e-Ahd control.
- Contract masters who see the province as a proving ground.
- Thalmyric revanchists who treat Zanthir as unfinished holy war.
- Local families caught between survival, collaboration, and rebellion.

## Game Use

Use Zanthir for espionage, contract loophole quests, prisoner exchanges, border fortresses, and moral compromises around collaborators who may be keeping civilians alive.
