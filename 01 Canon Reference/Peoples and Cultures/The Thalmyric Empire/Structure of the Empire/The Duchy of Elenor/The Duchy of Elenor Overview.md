# The Duchy of Elenor Overview

The Duchy of Elenor is a cultivated Thalmyric heartland duchy: vineyards, monasteries, river towns, veteran estates, and old Atherian ruins repurposed into churches and court seats.

## Role in the Setting

Elenor is where Thalmyria looks civilized, lawful, and beautiful. It supplies grain, wine, clergy, scribes, and legitimacy. Beneath that order are inheritance feuds, monastery politics, and quiet arguments over whether the empire is a sacred restoration or a military machine.

## Conflicts

- Noble houses competing for imperial marriages and church offices.
- Monasteries guarding inconvenient histories of the old Atherian Empire.
- Peasants burdened by war taxes for distant frontiers.
- Relic cults that can become local political parties.

## Game Use

Use Elenor for court intrigue, monastery mysteries, succession disputes, hidden archives, and stories about the cost of maintaining imperial holiness.
