# Peoples and Cultures — Authorial Navigation

This control describes the organisation and governing sources of the culture library. The reader-facing entrance is [[Cultures MOC|Peoples and Cultures]].

## Shelf structure

Where sufficient material exists, a culture shelf uses **10 Foundations**, **20 Faith and Worldview**, **30 Society and Daily Life**, **40 Government and Institutions**, **50 Economy and Warfare**, **60 Realms and Peoples**, and **70 History and Conflicts**. Smaller culture shelves remain intentionally shallower.

Overview articles are the normal reader entrance. They must distinguish ancestry, cultural belonging, religion, citizenship and political allegiance; represent internal differences; and summarise historical formation, ordinary life, institutions, faith and the present situation before directing readers to specialist articles.

## Controlling sources

The [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ethnic Groups Overview.md|Peoples and Ancestry Register]] controls broad ancestry relationships. The [[01 Canon Reference/History/Peoples in the World Historical Atlas.md|Peoples through History]] controls their chronological relationship to one another.

The [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/10 Foundations/Eclessia Cultural Thesis.md|Eclessian Cultural Thesis]] and [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/10 Foundations/Thalmyria Cultural Thesis.md|Thalmyric Cultural Thesis]] preserve early design foundations. The [[Sestaran Canon Register]], [[Folk Canon Register]], [[Ättar Canon Register]], [[Tarkhan Canon Register]], [[Ahl-e-Ahd Canon Register]] and [[Jirahar Canon Register]] record controlling details for their respective shelves.

When a culture is expanded, its articles should explain its understanding of reality, the institutions created by that understanding, the goods it preserves, the temptations and evils it faces, the lives of ordinary people and the historical pressures that produced its present form.

## Editorial standards

All culture work follows the [[World Egg Encyclopedia - Editorial Style Guide]] and the package gates in the [[World Egg Encyclopedia - Standards and Corpus Quality Audit]]. Parent and child accounts must agree, and a parent overview must retain a substantive summary rather than becoming a directory of links.
