# Raiding, Slavery, Ransom, and Adoption

Raiding is an economic institution as well as war. Animals, portable wealth, tools, weapons, hostages, and people can all be taken. Slavery and captive sale are normal sources of Tarkhan wealth and are objectively evil.

## Captive Outcomes

- **Ransom:** kin, city, employer, or religious community pays for return. A ransomed captive does not become kin.
- **Exchange:** opposing camps trade named prisoners, hostages, or skilled people under witnessed terms.
- **Retention:** captives perform herding, domestic, craft, sexual, agricultural, or caravan labor under coercion.
- **Sale:** Mazrakan, illicit Sestaran brokers, Covenant markets, and mobile factors convert captives into distant slavery.
- **Manumission:** a holder releases a person through payment, service, a major horse gift, satisfaction of declared word-price, or free decision.
- **Adoption:** an assembly fire-names a person into a clan, creating kin obligations and free standing. Adoption is not merely prolonged service.

## Descent

A child born to an enslaved mother is unfree unless publicly fire-named and adopted into a clan. Acknowledged paternity alone is insufficient. Adoption of the child does not free the mother, though it may create pressure, ransom obligations, or a later claim.

## Law and Abuse

Free Tarkhan cannot lawfully be sold by their own clan, but debt, disputed ancestry, false accusation, and defeated splinter status create abuse. Captives may earn trust or become indispensable without becoming free. Affection does not cancel ownership.

## Political Effects

White-Horde raids create the largest current captive flow into northern Covenant markets. Iron officers increasingly use registered military service to regularize bondage. Ash raids target roads and isolated settlements, while their formal peace with Castara requires the return of named protected travelers.
