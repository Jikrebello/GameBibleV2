# Tarkhan and the Wider World

## Covenant

The [[Nine Wells of Tergai]] make the frontier sacred, economic, and unresolved. White-Horde raids are the immediate threat to Iqashar; Iron-Horde companies are the slower danger because their armed households are entering frontier administration. Mazrakan trades animals, hides, escorts, captives, and intelligence while claiming neutrality at its gates.

## Sestaran Concord

Castara buys the annual Ash Road Peace. Vardessa hosts horse fairs and protected fires. Aversia sells arms and demands secure freight. Sermora transports mercenaries and remounts. Fiorenzian insurers profit from both the toll and the fear of it. Outside recognized member territory, the Seven-Seal Muster does not make a caravan war collective.

## Jirahar

Migdal Melach and Road Companies negotiate passage through old word-law. Jirahar caravans pay declared prices, hire escorts, trade animals, and sometimes shelter defeated camps. Way-Gates cannot replace ordinary travel to markets without anchors.

## Eclessia and Thalmyria

Eclessian eastern roads meet Ash traders, raiders, mercenaries, and displaced camps. Thalmyric contact is more distant and commonly mediated through Covenant or Sestaran markets. Both churches maintain small missions; converts remain Tarkhan.

## Folk and Ättar

Folk and Tarkhan exchange animals, metal, guides, medicines, and feud stories at the edge of forest and grass. Ättar contacts bring horses, mercenary work, distant goods, and occasional slave exchange. Neither relationship creates a single stable frontier.

## Abroad

Tarkhan beyond the dry belt appear as hired cavalry, caravan guards, falconers, slaves, gladiators, scouts, traders, hostages, and exiles. Their legal status matters more than outsiders' assumption that every rider represents a Horde.
