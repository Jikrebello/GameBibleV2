# Historical Spine of the Tarkhan

## Orphaned Reassembly in the Dry Belt

Tarkhan ancestors descend principally from dry-belt subject communities, fugitives, client-state survivors and mobile groups left outside the Glass Ark. They survive and reassemble across the northern desert, seasonal lake country and eastern grasslands. Western clans develop camels, well law, caravan brokerage and mobile pastoralism. Eastern communities combine hunting, small herds and foot-mobile migration. Later arrivals are absorbed, but the people is not a fourth branch of the Prophet's camp.

## The Nine Wells, 558 AR

During Mujalib's unification, Covenant forces capture the [[Nine Wells of Tergai]]. Fighting, expulsion, and imposed access instruments replace Tarkhan seasonal tenure. Later Covenant histories rename the seizure the First Submission and describe it as voluntary. White-Horde reclamation and Iron-Horde service both grow from this unresolved wound.

## First Catch, 905-920 AR

The Atherian remount network collapses after Caleran falls. Eastern Tarkhan capture and breed the scattered stock. [[First Catch and Grey Sister]] transforms travel, hunting, war, and status within three generations while western camel life continues.

## Horse Centuries, 920-1260 AR

Clans expand their circuits, trade horses, raid border provinces, and hire out as scouts and cavalry. No ruler keeps more than a regional coalition for long. The Nine Wells harden as the western frontier; the northern Sestaran routes develop recurring grass-tolls.

## Black Wind, 1261-1303 AR

[[Qarayel and the Black Wind|Qarayel]] gathers the Kurultai of the Long Grass in 1261 and raises the Nine-Mane Standard. Between 1265 and 1300 he compels Iqashar, Velian fairs, and Sestaran routes to pay without attempting permanent occupation. He dies undefeated in 1303. The grave remains undisclosed by policy and ritual.

## Partition and Road Peace, 1304-1360 AR

The Partition of the Herds creates White, Iron, Moon, and Ash coalitions. Castara's growing inland route is devastated in 1333; in 1334 the city buys the first [[Castaran Long Road|Ash Road Peace]]. By 1360 White raids threaten Covenant wells, Iron companies occupy imperial contracts, Sorqai's age makes succession dangerous, and Chilgir's debt makes the Castaran peace unstable.

## Historical Atlas — wider inheritance

Tarkhan societies are active Orphaned histories, not a pressure invented to divide Ark descendants. Western camel-and-well circuits and eastern hunting and herding developed through different ecologies. Their relations with Near-Glass peoples included marriage, ransom, trade, raids and contested water long before either the Covenant or Atheria's Fall.

The Nine Wells seizure in 558 AR violently altered western life. The First Catch, c. 905–920, supplied horses from scattered imperial studs to already-skilled eastern communities. It did not create Tarkhan civilization from nothing. Grey Sister's line and the cavalry transformation belong to that later period; earlier rider traditions must not import its horses backward.

Qarayel's 1261 unification and the Black Wind years changed the wider world's costs of movement. Extraction without occupation still meant burned fairs, threatened dependants and coercion. After his death in 1303, successor hordes developed distinct policies. The grave remains protected, and no new heroic cycle solves it.
