# Qarayel and the Black Wind

**Qarayel, the Black Wind** rose from a minor clan and united the Tarkhan banners at the Kurultai of the Long Grass in 1261 AR. He carried the Nine-Mane Standard and made extraction from roads, wells, fairs, and frightened provinces more profitable than occupying cities.

## Rule

Qarayel rewarded scouts, hostage keepers, interpreters, herd masters, and negotiators as carefully as warriors. His host shifted between horse and camel country and refused campaigns whose prestige depended on fixed siege. From 1265 to 1300, Iqashar paid escort fees, Velian fairs burned and paid, and Sestaran routes accepted the grass-toll.

He could be generous under guest-right and ruthless outside it. Captive sale, punitive grazing, hostage-taking, and public devastation financed the peace he offered afterward.

## Death and Grave

Qarayel died in 1303, undefeated and without an accepted successor. Burial riders concealed the grave and did not return, while a thousand horses confused the ground. The Moon Horde forbids the search because discovery would become a claim to universal rule. The grave has never been located.

## Legacy

The Partition divided his coalition among descendants and rival claims. Every Horde invokes a different Qarayel: lawful ancestor, conqueror, protector of movement, or warning against settled dependency.
