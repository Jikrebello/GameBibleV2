# First Catch and Grey Sister

The **First Catch** names the grassland horse revolution from 905 to 920 AR. As Atherian frontier commands collapsed, remount studs escaped, were abandoned, or were driven north by surviving handlers. Eastern Tarkhan clans captured and bred them.

Tarkhan memory gives the first willing capture to **Grey Sister**, a pale mare who drank from a hunter's hand rather than fleeing. Later genealogies connect the finest sacred lines to her. The underlying Atherian stock had been hymn-blessed for imperial war; the blessing persists weakly and unevenly in descendants.

## Consequences

- cart and foot migration becomes mounted migration within three generations;
- mounted hunting and remount wealth reorder prestige;
- eastern raids reach settled roads previously beyond practical range;
- western clans import horses but retain camels for dry country;
- horse genealogy becomes both sacred record and high finance;
- a foal accepted as Grey Sister's blood can seal peace like a relic translation.

The First Catch does not mean the Tarkhan began in 905 or that every clan had been foot-mobile. Well-Tarkhan camel civilization was already ancient.
