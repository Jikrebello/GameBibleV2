# The Making of the Tarkhan

The Tarkhan formed from Orphaned human communities which survived the collapse of [[Vathra and the Great Unyoking|Vathra]] across the northern dry belt. Their ancestors included well workers, eastern herders and hunters, camel keepers, tether-town craftspeople, fugitives, soldiers and freed captives. Broken seasonal lakes and long distances made movement essential to survival.

Western communities moved between deep wells with camels and small stock. Their customs attached pasture and water access to remembered labour, graves, negotiated seasons and the defence of a route. Tergai well houses, Narashi caravan families and Taleri craft towns contributed to this western world. These communities became the Well-Tarkhan.

Farther northeast, open grass replaced hard desert. Families followed wild herds, kept sheep and cattle, and moved camps by sled, cart, and foot. The Fall of Atheria released remount stock into this country. The [[First Catch and Grey Sister|First Catch]] did not create the Tarkhan; it transformed one part of an old civilization.

The Vathran crown had kept horses for chariots and elite couriers. Most escaped animals died during the Great Unyoking, and the surviving lines remained too scarce to support mounted pastoral life. Ancient Tarkhan transport rested upon camels, carts, sledges and human carriers until the First Catch transformed the eastern grasslands.

Horse wealth spread west without displacing camels. Marriage, fosterage, trade, exile, and banner service carried customs in both directions. A clan may call itself Well-Tarkhan in one generation and Grass-Tarkhan in another if drought, war, or patronage changes its circuit.

Qarayel's conquests gave outsiders the misleading idea of one steppe nation. The Partition restored the older truth: Tarkhan unity is made temporarily at fires, assemblies, and common campaigns. It is not inherited as a permanent state.
