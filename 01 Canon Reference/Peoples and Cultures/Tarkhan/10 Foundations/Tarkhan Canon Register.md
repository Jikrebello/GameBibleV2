# Tarkhan Canon Register

This register controls authorial facts about the Tarkhan. Authority descends through [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Gazetteer of the Known World]], this register, [[The Tarkhan Tribes Overview]], and then specialist dossiers.

## Fixed Identity

- Well-Tarkhan and Grass-Tarkhan are ecological identities within one mutually intelligible people.
- Well-Tarkhan camel culture predates the Atherian Fall. The First Catch is specifically the eastern grassland horse revolution.
- The four Hordes are regional-dynastic coalitions containing mixed clans, not ethnic nations.
- Khanship, permanent formal war command, and ordinary raid leadership are male institutions. Women may control substantial property, lead households, become wind-speakers, and exercise regency.
- Sorqai is khatun-regent, elder, and custodian of the Standard. She is not precedent for unrestricted female khanship.

## Fixed History

- The Tarkhan are principally an Orphaned people descended from northern communities that survived under, beside and against [[Vathra and the Great Unyoking|Vathra]] outside the Glass Ark.
- Vathra was a lesser Giant crown whose flying Material cities collapsed during the Great Unyoking. It was not a sixth Imperial Horizon and was not Nythalum.
- Tergai well houses, Elkari grass families, Taleri tether towns and Narashi caravan houses contributed to ancient Tarkhan formation. Their common name developed during the Hearth-Crown centuries.
- Ancient Tarkhan society used camels, carts, sledges and walking herds. A few chariot and riding horses survived among elite households without producing the mounted civilisation of the Horse Centuries.
- Tarkhan ancestors deepened and maintained the [[Nine Wells of Tergai]]. Covenant forces seized them violently in 558 AR. The later voluntary-submission account is false.
- Kharuz of the Stone Wells and the Ahdari water keeper Tavisha defeated Lethren Ostar at Rethmar in 381 BR. Rethmar was a mixed winter gathering rather than a Tarkhan capital or another name for Tergai.
- The First Catch occurs from 905 to 920 AR. Grey Sister begins the sacred remount genealogy.
- Qarayel unites the banners in 1261, dominates the Black Wind Years from 1265 to 1300, dies in 1303, and is followed by the Partition in 1304.
- The first Ash Road Peace is concluded in 1334 after an attack on Castara's new inland route.

## Fixed Faith and Magic

- Kokh is a Tarkhan composite reading of several sky-aligned Menhir powers, not one loving national god.
- Falcon sky-riding is a pact-mediated sensory overlay through lesser courtiers of Orionis. It is not possession, bodily flight, spell projection, or soul travel.
- A wind-speaker bonds to one falcon. Distance, weather, landscape, wards, and thin spaces limit the link; the bird keeps its own will.
- Wind-speakers may read death residue but cannot freely recall routed souls.

## Fixed Law and Moral Rule

- Word-price declares the compensation or consequence attached to a sworn promise; it does not make every act purchasable or lawful.
- Three-day guest-right protects an honest guest and obliges one who accepts the fire.
- Slavery, slave trading, coerced labor, and captive concubinage are common and objectively evil.
- A child born to an enslaved mother remains unfree unless publicly fire-named and adopted. That act does not free the mother.
- Ransom, manumission, exchange, and adoption are distinct legal outcomes.

## Fixed Geography and Politics

- White is northwestern, Iron west-central at Iqashar and Mazrakan, Moon central, and Ash northeastern toward Castara.
- White raids are the immediate Covenant danger. Iron settlement inside frontier institutions is the slower strategic danger.
- The Castaran Long Road crosses Ash country north of Eclessia toward Vardessa and Aversia. Its annual peace is an escort compact in Castaran law and tribute in most foreign accounts.
- The Seven-Seal Defensive Muster does not protect caravans outside recognized Castaran territory.

## Superseded Claims

The old claim that Tarkhan civilisation was wholly foot-mobile before 905 is obsolete: camels, carts and sledges were fundamental, while rare horses served chariot and elite households. Any claim that ancient Tarkhan were already a mass mounted people is equally obsolete. Grass hunting does not define every northeastern clan. The Moon Horde does not establish general female khanship. Falcon-trance is not unrestricted animal possession. Direct historical comparison labels are creator-only.
