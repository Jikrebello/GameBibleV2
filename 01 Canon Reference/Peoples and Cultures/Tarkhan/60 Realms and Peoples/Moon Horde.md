# Moon Horde

The Moon Horde keeps the central migration circuit, old assembly grounds, sacred horse lines, and the **Nine-Mane Standard**. Its authority is ritual and constitutional as much as military.

## Ruler and Office

**Khatun-regent Sorqai**, Mother of Banners, governs through age, lineage, accepted regency, and custody of the Standard. Her office is exceptional within a strongly patriarchal system. Male banner-lords command the Horde's campaigns; Sorqai controls whether they can claim universal legitimacy.

## Economy and War

Horse breeding, pastoral herds, ritual gifts, passage through the central circuit, arbitration, and falconry support the coalition. Moon riders excel at reconnaissance and coalition warfare but possess fewer concentrated raiding bands than White or Ash.

## Faith

The oldest sky rites and most respected wind-speaker lineages gather here. The Horde forbids deliberate search for Qarayel's grave, arguing that ambition must not manufacture revelation. Falcon sky-riders are uncommon even here and guarded as specialists.

## Foreign Relationship

Every Horde needs the Standard, so every foreign court watches Sorqai. The Covenant seeks a lawful kurultai it can influence; Sestaran agents seek her road judgements; Jirahar caravans value Moon enforcement of ancient peace-prices.

## Present Conflict

Sorqai is old and has not named a successor with equal acceptance. Custody of the Standard, the grave prohibition, and the limits of regency may split the coalition before any khan can call a lawful kurultai.

## Story Uses

- guard a sky-rider whose falcon saw an impossible camp;
- prove a claimant forged one mane of the Standard;
- stop a grave expedition without revealing why its map is convincing;
- carry Sorqai's ruling through territory whose khan rejects her jurisdiction.
