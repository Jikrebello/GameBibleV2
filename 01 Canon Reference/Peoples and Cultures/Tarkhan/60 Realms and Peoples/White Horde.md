# White Horde

The White Horde is the richest coalition of the northwestern grass and desert fringe. It contains horse-pastoral clans, camel-breeding well clans, dispossessed families, and raiding banners united by Qarayel's eldest line.

## Ruler and Claim

**Khan Batuqai** is patient, wealthy, and burdened by an old paternity dispute in his descent from Temuchar. He answers insult by accumulating followers rather than demanding immediate recognition. His best argument is that the largest herds and strongest raid host already obey him.

## Economy and War

Horses, camels, sheep, tolls, captured people, ransom, hides, and seasonal trade support the Horde. White banners are the principal raiders and slavers striking the Covenant approaches. They specialize in well ambush, deep desert movement, dispersed horse attack, and rapid captive withdrawal.

## Faith

Old sky rites remain strong. Well songs name the Nine Mother-Wells as stolen ancestral ground. Wind-speakers often regard Iron conversions and imperial contracts as submission disguised as employment.

## Foreign Relationship

Iqashar treats White raids as its immediate northern military danger. Mazrakan buys White animals and sells goods that later equip raids. The Moon Horde sympathizes with the wells claim but fears Batuqai using reclamation to seize the Standard.

## Present Conflict

Batuqai must choose between a great campaign at the Nine Wells, a succession bid after Sorqai's death, or restraint long enough to let Iron settlement discredit Tegrul. Land-poor splinters raid without permission and force him to accept blame or surrender authority.
