# Well-Tarkhan

The Well-Tarkhan occupy the western desert, stony uplands, and grass-desert margin north of Iqashar. The name describes a way of life organized around camels, water rights, and long dry circuits.

## Mobility and Wealth

Camels carry households and cargo across country that exhausts horses. Sheep, goats, cloth, salt, hides, transport, guides, and access to water sustain most camps. Horse ownership remains prestigious, especially among wealthy White and Iron banners, but it does not replace the camel economy.

## Well Law

Rights attach to remembered digging, maintenance, graves, seasonal use, hospitality, and negotiated access. A clan may own draw gear or defend approaches without claiming that water itself was created by its khan. The Covenant seizure of the Nine Wells violated this order by turning shared seasonal tenure into fortified imperial jurisdiction.

## Society and Faith

Well-Tarkhan camps are often multilingual and closely involved with Covenant trade. Iron-Horde communities contain the largest Al-Ahdiya following. White-Horde wind-speakers preserve older well songs and treat many imperial converts as compromised rather than foreign.

## Present Conflicts

- White raids contest Qahirid forts and take captives.
- Iron contracts provide access while settling armed households inside the frontier.
- Drought pushes land-poor clans into routes claimed by both Hordes.
- Pilgrims at the Nine Wells demand security that often means excluding the descendants of their diggers.
