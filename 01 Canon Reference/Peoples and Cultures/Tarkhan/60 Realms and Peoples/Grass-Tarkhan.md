# Grass-Tarkhan

The Grass-Tarkhan occupy the northeastern steppe, seasonal lake country, and broad roadless plains between the central sacred circuit and Castara. Their horse culture descends from the [[First Catch and Grey Sister|First Catch]], but their older hunting and herding traditions remain visible.

## Three Economies

- Pastoral clans keep horses, sheep, cattle, and goats and move for pasture recovery.
- Hunting clans follow migratory [[Great-Horns]], taking meat, hide, sinew, bone, and horn under seasonal rules.
- Mixed clans keep domestic herds while joining one or two great-horn drives each year.

No single model defines all Grass-Tarkhan. Herd-followers may be rich in preserved meat and poor in horses; horse lords may depend on hunters for route knowledge.

## Political Life

Moon banners dominate the central sacred circuit and carry the strongest wind-speaker traditions. Ash banners control northeastern road peace, toll, and foreign employment. Clan circuits overlap both coalitions, so a marriage or drought can change which Horde receives a family's riders.

## Castaran Contact

The [[Castaran Long Road]] brings silver, tools, grain, arms, and foreign law into Ash country. It also blocks grazing, creates fortified depots, and invites Castaran claims beyond its actual territory. The annual peace protects declared caravans, not every traveler or settlement.

## Present Conflicts

- herd migration crosses new road depots and farms;
- Ash debt threatens the escort compact;
- Moon wind-speakers oppose unrestricted foreign surveying;
- Aversian buyers arm rival banners while condemning raids on their cargo.
