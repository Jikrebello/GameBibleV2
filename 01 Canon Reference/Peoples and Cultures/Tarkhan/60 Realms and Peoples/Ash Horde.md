# Ash Horde

The Ash Horde controls the northeastern grasslands crossed by the [[Castaran Long Road]]. It combines horse-pastoral clans, great-horn hunters, road escorts, raiding banners, and foreign mercenary companies.

## Ruler and Government

**Khan Chilgir** is young, ambitious, and indebted through intermediaries connected to the Under-Ledger. He must distribute Castaran payments among banners that can abandon the peace, while preventing creditors from turning seasonal revenue into control of his court.

## Economy and War

Road silver, grain, tools, horse sales, tolls, escorts, raiding, hunting products, and mercenary work support the Horde. Ash forces use falcon scouts, mounted archery, false retreat, night attack, and intimate knowledge of the road's water and forage.

## Faith

Sky observance remains common but less institutionally strict than in Moon country. Foreign service has created Redeemer, Al-Ahdiya, and skeptical households without dissolving clan law.

## Foreign Relationship

Castara calls the annual payment an escort compact. Aversia calls it tribute while selling the Ash weapons. Vardessa relies on declared word-prices for its fairs. Sermora hires Ash lances and transports remounts. None of these relations grants the Concord ownership of the grass.

## Present Conflict

The Ash Road Peace depends on payment, Chilgir's distribution, and the obedience of named banners. A missed installment ends collective protection. Unauthorized raids test whether Chilgir can enforce restitution without appearing to serve Castara.
