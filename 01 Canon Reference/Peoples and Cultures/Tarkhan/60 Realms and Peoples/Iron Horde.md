# Iron Horde

The Iron Horde occupies the mixed horse-and-camel frontier north of Iqashar and Mazrakan. It descends from Qarayel's second line and has made contracted military service its road to wealth.

## Ruler and Government

**Khan Tegrul** seals agreements in his own name and places relatives over companies that once answered only to temporary banner captains. His camp includes Aqidin, interpreters, quartermasters, hostage households, and scribes capable of working Covenant courts.

## Economy and War

Imperial pay, escort contracts, remount breeding, camel transport, frontier estates, market patronage, and captured labor support the Horde. Iron companies can fight as mobile Tarkhan hosts or integrate with Covenant infantry, engineers, and supply columns.

## Faith

Al-Ahdiya has a substantial and sincere Iron following. Converts express obligation through witness, word-price, discipline, and service. Old-rite households remain numerous, and conflict concerns jurisdiction and succession more often than private belief.

## Foreign Relationship

Iqashar needs Iron mobility but fears the companies becoming a military estate able to appoint officials, control wells, and decide succession. Mazrakan's Council wants Iron protection divided among competing patrons. White leaders call the Horde collaborators while buying its weapons.

## Present Conflict

Tegrul's strategic opportunity is institutional capture: settle armed households, inherit unpaid posts, marry frontier elites, and turn emergency powers into custom. His danger is becoming dependent on a Qahirid court that can withhold pay or take his heirs as honored hostages.

## Story Uses

- a frontier governor dies and his Iron deputy refuses to surrender the seal;
- an unpaid company invokes Tarkhan word-price against Iqashar;
- a convert's burial starts a jurisdiction dispute between Aqid and wind-speaker;
- expose a contract designed to make temporary pasture an inheritable military estate.
