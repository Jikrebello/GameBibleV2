# Four Hordes of the Partition

The four Hordes descend from the coalition that Qarayel held under the Nine-Mane Standard. Each is a layered allegiance of clans, clients, banner-lords, captives, and subject camps. Their borders move with pasture and tribute.

| Horde | Present ruler | Principal range | Strategic character |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[White Horde]] | Khan Batuqai | Northwestern grass and desert fringe | Raiding, slave-taking, contested succession claim, Nine-Wells reclamation |
| [[Iron Horde]] | Khan Tegrul | Iqashar-Mazrakan frontier | Contract cavalry, mixed remounts, Al-Ahdiya influence, institutional settlement |
| [[Moon Horde]] | Khatun-regent Sorqai | Central sacred circuit | Nine-Mane custody, wind-speakers, old sky rites, kurultai legitimacy |
| [[Ash Horde]] | Khan Chilgir | Northeastern grass and Castaran road | Tolls, escorts, raids, horse trade, foreign debt |

The Hordes can cooperate against invasion without accepting one another's succession claims. A full kurultai requires Sorqai's Standard, recognized delegates, safe fire, and enough common danger to keep armed rivals in one place.

Three routes might create a new unifier: Sorqai's lawful release of the Standard, discovery of Qarayel's grave, or a common enemy strong enough to compel a kurultai. Each route can also begin a civil war.
