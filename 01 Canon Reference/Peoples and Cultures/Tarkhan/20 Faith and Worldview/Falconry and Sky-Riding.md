# Falconry and Sky-Riding

Falconry is ordinary Tarkhan craft. **Sky-riding** is the rare pact discipline by which a wind-speaker shares a bonded falcon's perception. Lesser hunting courtiers of Orionis carry the connection; the wind-speaker does not possess the bird.

## Bond

A bond grows through one bird and one keeper over seasons of feeding, flight, recall, sleep watches, and bounded rites. A borrowed bird cannot be used. The falcon keeps its own fear, appetite, judgement, and will, and can refuse a lethal direction.

## Trance

The wind-speaker sits or lies still while trusted companions guard the body. Sight, motion, and fragments of sound overlay the human senses. The rider may draw the bird's attention or urge direction but cannot speak through it, cast through it, seize its muscles, or carry another soul.

In clear air, an experienced pair can scout roughly twenty miles for no more than an hour. Storm, mountain walls, heavy wards, crowded cities, and thin spaces shorten or distort the link. Skilled enemies can mislead a bird with decoys without touching the magic.

## Cost and Failure

Ordinary use brings cold, nausea, disorientation, and temporary difficulty judging human distance. Injury to the falcon produces mirrored shock. Its death during trance can leave the wind-speaker blind, comatose, or afflicted by **sky-hunger**: a compulsion to seek height, exposure, and borrowed motion.

The vulnerable body is the discipline's central military weakness. Killing or moving it can break the bond catastrophically. For that reason, reconnaissance pairs travel with dedicated guards and never scout from an unsecured camp.

## Uses

- locate herds, wells, smoke, and moving columns;
- inspect a pass or road before a caravan commits;
- follow a raid without matching its speed;
- verify weather fronts and grass fires;
- discover that an apparent camp has no heat, animals, or ordinary tracks.
