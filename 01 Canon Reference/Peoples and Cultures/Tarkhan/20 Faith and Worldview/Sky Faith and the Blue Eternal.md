# Sky Faith and the Blue Eternal

Tarkhan call the order above the open country **Kokh, the Blue Eternal**. Sun, moon, storm, wind, hunt, and the moving stars are understood as its household: powers with their own tempers whose agreement mortals cannot assume.

The faith promises no universal tenderness. Clear weather can bless a herd and expose an army. Rain can save a camp and drown the next valley. Tarkhan worship therefore emphasizes right attention, inherited limits, gifts, and truthful reading rather than confidence that the sky loves its petitioners.

## Wind-Speakers

Wind-speakers are selected through apprenticeship, recurring dreams, survival of exposure, unusual animal bonds, or recognition by an elder. They advise on camp movement, weather, burial, oath timing, migration, and spiritual contamination. Women and men may hold the office.

They can interpret residue left around the dead but cannot freely summon a routed soul. Voices answering without authenticated signs are treated as Shades, patrons, staged fraud, or dangerous masks until proved otherwise.

## The Horse and the Hunt

Grey Sister is honored as the mare who accepted the First Catch, not as a goddess. Her descendants carry a fading Atherian blessing tracked through chanted genealogy. Falcon rites fall under the hunting household and are mediated through lesser courtiers associated with [[Names of the Menhir, Masks of the Idols|Orionis]].

## Other Faiths

Iron-Horde converts interpret Al-Ahdiya through word-price, witness, and mounted service. Small Redeemer missions survive by hospitality and medicine. Jirahar teachers are respected for portable law and disciplined memory. Conversion changes allegiance and ritual; it does not erase Tarkhan ancestry or clan obligation.

## Authorial Truth

Kokh is a composite Tarkhan interpretation of several sky-aligned Menhir powers. Its practical observations often work, but no single loving tribal god stands behind the whole system.
