# Peoples and Cultures

The peoples of the known world belong to overlapping histories of survival, migration, conquest, conversion and intermarriage. Some descend principally from the people formed in the Great Glass Desert. Others survived the fall of Giant rule in their own countries. Many present societies arose much later through the joining of these populations under empires, churches, cities and shared institutions.

Culture, ancestry, religion, citizenship and political allegiance are related but do not share identical boundaries. This page introduces the principal living societies, gives one clear first article for each and then supplies longer routes through ancient history and the present world.

## Living peoples and societies

### Ahl-e-Ahd

The Ahl-e-Ahd, or People of the Covenant, comprise a civilisation joined by Al-Ahdiya, literary Ahdari, shared legal traditions and a network of roads, markets and places of pilgrimage. Ahdari, Iqashari, Qahuri, Qazmari and Zanthiri populations retain distinct countries and ancestries within this wider religious and political world.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/The Ahl-e-Ahd Overview.md|The Ahl-e-Ahd Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/10 Foundations/The Making of the Covenant Peoples.md|formation]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Covenant.md|historical spine]].

### Ättar

The Ättar inhabit the halls, fortified valleys and maritime settlements of the southern cold belt. Household and hall membership, four totem-kindreds and the inherited bargain with Mother Moon shape societies whose rulers and alliances remain strongly regional.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/Ättar Overview.md|Ättar Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/10 Foundations/The Making of the Ättar.md|formation]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Ättar.md|historical spine]].

### Eclessians

Eclessia is a group of eastern kingdoms, duchies, cities and marches shaped by Atherian provincial life and the Eclessian Church. Its peoples formed through different mixtures of provincial, Folk, Markish and Vesari populations, and now live amid the political and religious divisions produced by the Great Split and the Freeflame Reformation.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/Eclessia Overview.md|Eclessia Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/10 Foundations/The Making of the Eclessians.md|formation]], [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/30 Society and Daily Life/Life Paths/Lives of Eclessia — The Common Spine.md|ordinary lives]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Eclessia.md|historical atlas]].

### Folk

The Folk are the related clan peoples of the southern forests, mountain ranges, cold coasts and Eclessian marches. Their communities range from longhouse kindreds to fortified confederacies, river leagues and coastal fleets, joined by traditions of kinship, hearth-law and winter shelter.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/The Folk Overview.md|The Folk Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/10 Foundations/The Making of the Folk.md|formation]], [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/30 Society and Daily Life/The Folk of the Frontier and the Wolfsmere Lineage.md|frontier life]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Folk.md|historical spine]].

### Jirahar

The Jirahar are an ancient dispersed people whose communities inhabit tower-citadels, foreign quarters, roads, rivers and permanent sea fleets. Maternal descent, conversion, the Orakha tradition, schools and the journeys of the Keepers sustain belonging across many countries and under many rulers.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/The Jirahar Overview.md|The Jirahar Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/30 Society and Daily Life/Four Ways of Jirahar Life.md|the Four Ways]], [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/10 Foundations/The Making of the Jirahar.md|formation]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Jirahar.md|historical spine]].

### Nsizwa

The Nsizwa inhabit the far southwestern savannas, escarpments and highland plateaux governed from Mzalu's Hold. Cattle, cultivation, metalwork, military service and the remembrance of the dead sustain their regional order, while the nature of particular ancestor-shaped apparitions remains disputed.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Nsizwa/The Nsizwa Overview.md|The Nsizwa Overview]]. **Then follow:** the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Nsizwa/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of the Nsizwa.md|historical atlas]].

### Sestarans

The Sestaran Concord joins seven independent city-states for defence while leaving each member its own government, citizenship and religious arrangements. The cities occupy ports, islands, mountains and inland fairs, and their populations reflect different histories of settlement, trade, refuge, conquest and intermarriage.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/The Sestaran Concord Overview.md|The Sestaran Concord Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/10 Foundations/The Making of the Sestaran Cities.md|formation]], [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/30 Society and Daily Life/Life Paths/Lives of the Concord — The Common Spine.md|ordinary lives]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Sestaran Concord.md|historical spine]].

### Tarkhan

The Tarkhan are a northern nomadic civilisation extending across desert wells and northeastern grasslands. Clan law, guest-right, related speech and sky observance connect communities whose movements depend on different animals, water systems and seasonal routes.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Tarkhan/The Tarkhan Tribes Overview.md|The Tarkhan Tribes Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Tarkhan/10 Foundations/The Making of the Tarkhan.md|formation]], [[The Tarkhan before the Horse Hordes|ancient history]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Tarkhan/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Tarkhan.md|historical spine]].

### Thalmyrics

The Thalmyric Empire occupies the western heartland of old Atheria. Its people descend from imperial heartlanders, provincial communities, refugees, occupation-era populations and later settlers whose lives were joined by the Fall, the mountain refuge and the Long Reconquest.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/Thalmyric Empire Overview.md|Thalmyric Empire Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/10 Foundations/The Making of the Thalmyrics.md|formation]], [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/30 Society and Daily Life/Lives of Thalmyria — Seven Roads from Cradle to Grave.md|ordinary lives]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Thalmyria.md|historical atlas]].

### Velians

The Velians are an Atherian-descended people of the desert-margin towns, river districts and cultivated estates of the Velian States and Zanthir. Their communities developed around a distinct communion of the Redeemer's faith during centuries of life between Covenant, Atherian and Thalmyric powers.

**Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Velian/Velian Culture Overview.md|Velian Culture Overview]]. **Then follow:** the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Velian/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of the Velians.md|historical atlas]] or [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Velian/60 Realms and Peoples/Velian States/The Velian States Overview.md|the Velian States]].

## Historical civilisations and political worlds

The societies below no longer survive in their earlier political form. They include Giant kingdoms and crownlands, river empires, sanctuary cities, harbour republics, upland crowns and the later Atherian Empire. Their descendants, buildings and institutions may remain important to living peoples, but ancient citizenship and present cultural identity are not interchangeable.

### Giant-age kingdoms and crownlands

**Arsuvan.** Arsuvan was a regional Giant kingdom of the Aurin basin and adjoining uplands. Iskaren and Velketh rulers joined agricultural estates, mines and coastal exchange through military households and the labour of human dependants. Its collapse left communities that contributed to the later Enathi, Serathi and Vardeni peoples. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/20 Prehistory/Arsuvan - The Giant Kingdom of the Aurin.md|Arsuvan — The Giant Kingdom of the Aurin]].

**Vathra.** Vathra was a confederated Giant kingdom whose flying crown-cities ruled the northern dry belt through human-maintained wells, anchor towers, herds, workshops and roads. The War of Altitude and the human-led Great Unyoking destroyed it between approximately 2300 and 2260 BR. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Vathra and the Great Unyoking.md|Vathra and the Great Unyoking]]. **Then follow:** the [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Historical Atlas of Vathra and the Northern Ground.md|Historical Atlas of Vathra and the Northern Ground]].

**Nhaldur.** Nhaldur was a southern Giant penal crownland of ring-fortresses, mines, labour camps, hostage houses and geothermal estates. Its fall scattered prisoners, lesser Giants and giant-blooded humans into the southern cold world, while the First Rewriting at Kharvos created the earliest Grobi. **Start with:** the public [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Historical Atlas of the Southern Cold World.md|Historical Atlas of the Southern Cold World]], which places Nhaldur within the histories of the Folk, Ättar and Grobi frontier.

### Early human cities and empires

**Ancient Qahur.** The Sacred Basin supported a river civilisation from the collapse of Giant rule to the opening of Atheria's imperial age. Town, temple, household and irrigation district remained important identities through repeated reunions under competing Twin-Reed Crowns. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/70 History and Conflicts/Ancient Qahur and the Twin-Reed Crowns.md|Ancient Qahur and the Twin-Reed Crowns]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Qahur and the Gateway before Atheria.md|Qahur and the Gateway, c. 2165–350 BR]].

**Gateway Republic.** The Gateway Republic was a maritime state centred on Qarzeth between approximately 1200 and 520 BR. Ports, inland communities, elected Harbour Judges and unequal civic institutions connected the desert approaches to Thalassian shipping until war with Caleran destroyed the capital. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/The Gateway Republic.md|The Gateway Republic]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Qahur and the Gateway before Atheria.md|Qahur and the Gateway, c. 2165–350 BR]].

**Qiryath.** First Qiryath grew from Jirahar road houses, towers, public stores and portable courts into a sanctuary city. Arqesh destroyed it in 742 BR; deportees, villages and road families later rebuilt the city without recovering its earlier sovereignty. **Start with:** the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria.md|Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria]].

**Arqesh.** House Ubar joined the canal cities of the Ubar riverlands through water control, royal estates, sacred kingship and the forced movement of conquered households. Its administration preserved canals, measures and learning used by later states, while also enabling starvation, enslavement and deportation. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/The Arqeshite River Empire.md|The Arqeshite River Empire]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Arqesh - River Cities, Kings and Deportation.md|Arqesh — River Cities, Kings and Deportation]].

**Vashar.** House Tiravan united the highland country east of the Ubar during Arqesh's fall. The resulting Crown of Roads and Dawns protected the return to Qiryath, ruled through roads, royal relatives and subject institutions, and remained an independent peer when Atheria unified. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Vashar and the Crown of Roads and Dawns.md|Vashar and the Crown of Roads and Dawns]].

**Ortheia.** Ortheia was an Atherian-speaking upland kingdom whose royal households drew sustained military strength from valleys, mines, forests and pasture roads. Valeron II and Eryndor III imposed a temporary hegemony on the Celestial Compact and carried war across the eastern inner sea. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Ortheia and the Upland Kings.md|Ortheia and the Upland Kings]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/The Eastern Sea Wars.md|The Eastern Sea Wars]].

**Elmaric island worlds.** The central island peoples formed related communities without one state. The Elmarai Isles, populous Seravon and independent sanctuary of Ilamar joined through short crossings, grain, kinship, captivity and war. **Start with:** [[Historical Atlas of the Elmaric Sea before 350 BR]] for the period or [[Elmaric Island Peoples before 350 BR]] for the people. **Then follow:** [[Elmarai Isles before 350 BR]], [[Seravon before 350 BR]], [[Ilamar before 350 BR]] and the histories of [[Caelovar]] and [[Marelith]].

**Vorthara.** Escaped human slaves and later outsiders built a refuge and pass town inside and around the Black Spire. By 350 BR it held independent precincts for trade, death-work and rival cults, but no common city government. **Start with:** [[Vorthara before 350 BR]]. **Then follow:** [[Vortharan Working Precincts before 350 BR]], [[The Pale Hand before 350 BR]] and [[The Citadel of Vorthara Overview|the present city]].

**Atherian city-states and empire.** The Atherian world developed from southern war-settlers and maritime communities through city-states, the Caleran republic and hereditary empire. Its roads, cities, laws, churches and provincial identities remain powerful inheritances throughout Thalmyria, Eclessia, Sestara and the frontier countries. **Start with:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Atherian Empire/The Atherian Empire Overview.md|The Atherian Empire Overview]]. **Then follow:** [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/The First Atherian City-States.md|the first city-states]], [[01 Canon Reference/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/The Rise of the Atherian Empire Overview.md|the rise of the empire]] or the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Atherian Empire/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Atheria.md|historical atlas]].

Not every ancient people formed one state or civilisation. The older Folk, Ättar, Tarkhan and Ahdari worlds remain attached to their living culture entries above and to the historical routes below. The [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Peoples before Atherian Unification/Peoples before Atherian Unification.md|ancient peoples directory]] distinguishes language, ancestry, local belonging and political membership across all of these societies.
