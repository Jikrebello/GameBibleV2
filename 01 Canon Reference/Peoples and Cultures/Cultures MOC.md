# Peoples and Cultures

The peoples of the known world belong to overlapping histories of survival, migration, conquest, conversion and intermarriage. Some descend principally from the people formed in the Great Glass Desert. Others survived the fall of Giant rule in their own countries. Many present societies arose much later through the joining of these populations under empires, churches, cities and shared institutions.

This page provides a direct entrance to each principal culture. The [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ethnic Groups Overview.md|Peoples and Ancestry Register]] explains the distinction between ancestry, cultural belonging, religion and political allegiance. Readers who want to follow these relationships through time can use [[01 Canon Reference/History/Peoples in the World Historical Atlas.md|Peoples through History]] or the larger [[01 Canon Reference/History/World Historical Atlas MOC.md|World Historical Atlas]].

## Living peoples and societies

### Ahl-e-Ahd

The Ahl-e-Ahd, or People of the Covenant, comprise a civilisation joined by Al-Ahdiya, literary Ahdari, shared legal traditions and a network of roads, markets and places of pilgrimage. Ahdari, Iqashari, Qahuri, Qazmari and Zanthiri populations retain distinct countries and ancestries within this wider religious and political world.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/The Ahl-e-Ahd Overview.md|Overview]] · [[The Ahdari before the Covenant|Ancient Ahdari]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/10 Foundations/The Making of the Covenant Peoples.md|Historical formation]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Covenant.md|History]]

### Ättar

The Ättar inhabit the halls, fortified valleys and maritime settlements of the southern cold belt. Household and hall membership, four totem-kindreds and the inherited bargain with Mother Moon shape societies whose rulers and alliances remain strongly regional.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/Ättar Overview.md|Overview]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/10 Foundations/The Making of the Ättar.md|Historical formation]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Ättar.md|History]] · [[Halls, Jarls, and Chief-Kings|Halls and rulers]] · [[Mother Moon's Bargain|The Moon bargain]] · [[Winter War and the Grobi Frontier|The Grobi frontier]]

### Eclessians

Eclessia is a group of eastern kingdoms, duchies, cities and marches shaped by Atherian provincial life and the Eclessian Church. Its peoples formed through different mixtures of provincial, Folk, Markish and Vesari populations, and now live amid the political and religious divisions produced by the Great Split and the Freeflame Reformation.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/Eclessia Overview.md|Overview]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/10 Foundations/The Making of the Eclessians.md|Historical formation]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Eclessia.md|History]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/30 Society and Daily Life/Life Paths/Lives of Eclessia — The Common Spine.md|Ordinary lives]]

### Folk

The Folk are the related clan peoples of the southern forests, mountain ranges, cold coasts and Eclessian marches. Their communities range from longhouse kindreds to fortified confederacies, river leagues and coastal fleets, joined by traditions of kinship, hearth-law and winter shelter.

### Jirahar

The Jirahar are an ancient dispersed people whose communities inhabit tower-citadels, foreign quarters, roads, rivers and permanent sea fleets. Maternal descent, conversion, the Orakha tradition, schools and the journeys of the Keepers sustain belonging across many countries and under many rulers.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/The Jirahar Overview.md|Overview]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/10 Foundations/The Making of the Jirahar.md|Historical formation]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Jirahar.md|History]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria.md|Qiryath before Atheria]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/30 Society and Daily Life/Four Ways of Jirahar Life.md|Four ways of life]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/60 Realms and Peoples/Tower Towns and the Prophet's Roads.md|Tower towns and roads]]

### Nsizwa

The Nsizwa inhabit the far southwestern savannas, escarpments and highland plateaux governed from Mzalu's Hold. Cattle, cultivation, metalwork, military service and the remembrance of the dead sustain their regional order, while the nature of particular ancestor-shaped apparitions remains disputed.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Nsizwa/The Nsizwa Overview.md|Overview]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Nsizwa/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of the Nsizwa.md|History]]

### Sestarans

The Sestaran Concord joins seven independent city-states for defence while leaving each member its own government, citizenship and religious arrangements. The cities occupy ports, islands, mountains and inland fairs, and their populations reflect different histories of settlement, trade, refuge, conquest and intermarriage.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/The Sestaran Concord Overview.md|Overview]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/10 Foundations/The Making of the Sestaran Cities.md|Historical formation]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Sestaran Concord.md|History]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/30 Society and Daily Life/Life Paths/Lives of the Concord — The Common Spine.md|Ordinary lives]] · [[The Free Port of Orsena|Orsena]] · [[The Free City of Vardessa|Vardessa]]

### Tarkhan

The Tarkhan are a northern nomadic civilisation extending across desert wells and northeastern grasslands. Clan law, guest-right, related speech and sky observance connect communities whose movements depend on different animals, water systems and seasonal routes.

### Thalmyrics

The Thalmyric Empire occupies the western heartland of old Atheria. Its people descend from imperial heartlanders, provincial communities, refugees, occupation-era populations and later settlers whose lives were joined by the Fall, the mountain refuge and the Long Reconquest.

**Principal articles:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/Thalmyric Empire Overview.md|Overview]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/10 Foundations/The Making of the Thalmyrics.md|Historical formation]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Thalmyria.md|History]] · [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/30 Society and Daily Life/Lives of Thalmyria — Seven Roads from Cradle to Grave.md|Ordinary lives]]

### Velians

The Velians are an Atherian-descended people of the desert-margin towns, river districts and cultivated estates of the Velian States and Zanthir. Their communities developed around a distinct communion of the Redeemer's faith during centuries of life between Covenant, Atherian and Thalmyric powers.

## Historical civilisation

### Atherian Empire

The Atherian Empire was the central imperial civilisation of the known world. It developed from southern war-settlers and maritime communities through city-states, the Caleran republic and hereditary empire. Its roads, cities, laws, churches and provincial identities remain powerful inheritances throughout Thalmyria, Eclessia, Sestara and the frontier countries.

## Peoples through history

The chronological history begins with the human communities subjected to Giant rule and those which survived its collapse outside the Glass refuge. The Great Prophet's followers became one people during their refuge in the desert. After his death, successive migrations and two gradual fissions contributed to Atherian, Jirahar and ancient Ahdari histories, while Orphaned societies continued to develop in their own lands.

Later conquest and migration produced new civic and cultural identities. Atherian citizenship joined people of many ancestries; the Fall and Long Reconquest transformed the imperial provinces; and the Covenant, Thalmyric, Eclessian, Sestaran and Velian worlds emerged through different combinations of inheritance and political change.

Follow the complete sequence in [[01 Canon Reference/History/Peoples in the World Historical Atlas.md|Peoples through History]].
