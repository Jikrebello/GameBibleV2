# The Qahirid Suzerainty and Iqashar

Iqashar is the Covenant's imperial capital: a northern escarpment metropolis commanding the best roads to the desert, Al-Qahur, and the Tarkhan approaches. It is the seat of Sultan-Caliph Marzuq III and the Sublime Threshold.

## Court

Marzuq rules with Sayyida Nurhan, who controls household finance and marriage; Guard-Master Temuz, who commands the Ahdanite Guard; and the unnamed Custodian of the Vault of Names. Princes Kavus and Ardavan lead rival succession households.

## Society

Iqashari administrators and merchants dominate the city, but Ahdari clans, Qahuri pilgrims, Sealed-Blood minorities, Jirahar, Tarkhan envoys, People of the Lamp, and foreign factors maintain quarters and institutions. Imperial culture is deliberately Ahdari in ceremony and Iqashari in administration.

## Economy and Power

Customs, caravan tolls, textiles, metalwork, weapons, court consumption, provincial revenue, and military contracts sustain the capital. Its weakness is logistical: authority travels by road and water station, and distant rulers can obey the wording of an order while defeating its purpose.

## Northern Frontier

Iqashar faces the Tarkhan through wells rather than a clean border. The [[Nine Wells of Tergai]] remain ancestral Tarkhan ground held by Qahirid forts. Iron Horde companies guard routes and increasingly occupy frontier offices; White Horde and dispossessed bands raid the approaches. Horse warfare dominates the grass, while ancient Well-Tarkhan camel traditions control the driest country. [[Mazrakan, Market of the Nine Measures]] converts part of that conflict into trade.

## Present Crisis

Marzuq is clever, indolent, and dependent upon the Guard that placed him. The coming succession may trigger a Cull of Brothers, Sealed-Blood revolt, military intervention, or Tarkhan opportunism.
