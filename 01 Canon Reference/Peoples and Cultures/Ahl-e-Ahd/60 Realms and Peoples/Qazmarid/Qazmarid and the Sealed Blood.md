# Qazmarid and the Sealed Blood

Qazmarid occupies the western strait on a separate landmass. It is not a Thalmyric border province. Its ports, hill fortresses, orchard valleys, and naval stations make it an independent counterweight to Iqashar.

## Rule and Faith

Caliph-Wasi Zahran claims descent from Ruqaiya bint Mujalib and custody of the Counter-Instrument. A hierarchy of Wasiyin, lineage courts, shrine foundations, and fortress governors supports him. The Sealed Blood is the state confession and the Mourning of the Wells its central public rite.

## Society and Economy

Qazmari clans, maritime houses, orchard towns, sailors, artisans, and soldiers provide the state's base. Strait tolls, shipping, citrus, oil, horses, salt, and fortified trade sustain it. The court sponsors exiles and dissidents from Iqashar while suppressing Qazmari critics who ask why sacred descent should escape public witness.

## Minorities

Witnessed-Seal subjects retain courts and worship but are excluded from the highest offices and periodically accused of Qahirid allegiance. Redeemer-faithful communities are smaller than in Zanthir and receive narrower protections.

## Foreign Policy

Zahran denies Marzuq's universal title, competes for routes through the western strait, and uses Sealed-Blood minorities as influence networks. Qahirid invasion is limited by sea, distance, and the risk of turning Zahran into the martyr he claims already to be.
