# Mazrakan, Market of the Nine Measures

Mazrakan stands where Iqashar's paved caravan road reaches the approaches to the [[Nine Wells of Tergai]]. Its charter promises hard commercial peace: feuds, imperial warrants, and banner disputes stop at the market gates unless the city's courts recognize them.

## Government

The **Council of Nine Measures** represents caravan houses, resident guilds, water interests, foreign communities, and licensed dangerous trades. An imperial Gate-Prefect protects Qahirid revenue without directly governing the wards. Community compacts administer worship and family law. The Market Watch keeps public order and can awaken contract-bound golems stationed beneath major squares.

## The Market

Mazrakan sells grain, animals, textiles, spices, medicines, weapons, books, art, maps, labor, information, and passage. Restricted enclosures trade relics, dangerous creatures, bound spirits, names, slaves, and contraband under licenses that often legalize what wealth demands.

The city is more than vice. Camel yards, horse fairs, food streets, performers, bathhouses, artisans, caravan hostels, foreign shrines, mercenary courts, schools, crowded homes, and neighborhoods built around shared wells make it a place where ordinary people live.

## Peoples and Frontier

Iqashari factors, Ahdari caravan clans, Well-Tarkhan camel houses, Grass-Tarkhan horse traders, Qahuri grain houses, Jirahar brokers, People of the Lamp, Sestaran agents, and foreign adventurers meet here. Iron Horde companies sell escort service; their enemies sell stolen remounts two streets away under altered brands. White-Horde captives also pass through licensed and illicit markets.

Jirahar Road Companies usually reach Mazrakan through [[Migdal Melach]] and Tarkhan passage-price country. The market has no Way-Gate anchor, which prevents the Council of Nine Measures from turning commercial regulation into control of the Prophet's Roads.

## Conflicts

- The Gate-Prefect wants imperial customs to override the charter.
- Tarkhan patrons demand representation equal to settled merchant houses.
- Abolitionists target licensed slave and name markets.
- Bound creatures disappear before sale, suggesting either liberation or procurement by something worse.
- A dormant market golem has begun enforcing a clause absent from every public copy of the charter.
