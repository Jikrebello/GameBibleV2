# The People of the Lamp

**People of the Lamp** is the Covenant legal name for recognized communities devoted to the Redeemer. It is a status of protected inequality, not a single church or culture.

## Recognized Communions

- The [[The Velian Church|Velian Church]] is the largest native communion. Its ancient Banu Mizan compacts protect congregations, married clergy, courts, schools, and property.
- Thalmyric Church parishes survive in disputed districts, old Atherian settlements, migrant quarters, and frontier towns. Officials often suspect them of loyalty to Veridun.
- Eclessian old-Church congregations are mostly merchants, diplomats, captives' descendants, and expatriates in major cities.
- Freeflame congregations are small and closely watched. Their decentralized preaching and refusal of fixed custodians make communal recognition difficult.

## Protection and Inferiority

Recognized communities may worship, maintain clergy, own property, educate children, and use internal courts for marriage and inheritance. They owe protection levies and communal surety. Public processions, new churches, bells, weapons, senior office, and testimony against privileged Covenant parties may be restricted.

Forced conversion is formally unlawful. Coercive debt, hostage arrangements, confiscation, inheritance pressure, abduction disguised as guardianship, and local violence regularly hollow out that promise. Velian protections are strongest in Zanthir, inconsistent in the client states, and weakest under Qazmari rulers hostile to their Witnessed-Seal history.

Conversion to Al-Ahdiya can bring opportunity but does not automatically erase ancestry or client status. Leaving Al-Ahdiya for a Redeemer communion is treated as breach and may carry severe penalties.

## Distinct From Jirahar Status

Jirahar communities are governed through tower, Road, Sea, Turning, guest, and sanctuary instruments. They are not People of the Lamp and must not be folded into Redeemer communal law.
