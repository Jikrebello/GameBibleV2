# The Monomyth According to the Ahl-e-Ahd

The Covenant recension of the Exodus is called **The Journey of the Last Contract**. It preserves genuine memories of liberation while interpreting them through institutions that arose much later.

## Creation and Breach

The Lord orders creation and entrusts humanity with stewardship. The Adversary persuades humanity that power can be separated from rightful obligation. The First Breach disorders mortal life and permits false masters to claim what was never theirs.

## The Giant Bonds

The Giants enslave humanity through blood, debt, land, appetite, and machines. Covenant reciters describe every Giant tyranny as an unlawful instrument whose witnesses were fear and whose consideration was stolen life.

## The Great Prophet

The Great Prophet bears the Divine Quill, breaks the Giant bonds, and leads the freed peoples across the Great Glass Desert. He refuses the bargains of desert powers and teaches that water, bread, sanctuary, and names cannot be taken without obligation.

At the promised refuge he disobeys the Lord and is lost. The people scatter. Covenant teaching treats this as proof that even a liberator remains answerable to the order he serves.

## The Peoples of the Roads

The story compresses many ancestors into one caravan. Ahdari reciters emphasize wells and guest-law; Qahuri versions emphasize water labor; Iqashari versions emphasize witnessed civic order; Qazmari versions emphasize the betrayed household. Modern historians treat these differences as evidence that the Covenant peoples existed before they shared one faith.

## Mujalib and the Final Covenant

Generations later, Mujalib receives the revelation of Malak al-Ahd. He does not replace the Great Prophet but claims to restore the law the scattered peoples forgot. The Covenant of the 4,444 brings water to Al-Qahur and begins the sacred history of Al-Ahdiya.

The scripture ends with an unresolved warning: an instrument can restrain a tyrant, but anyone who mistakes the instrument for the Lord has already prepared a new chain.
