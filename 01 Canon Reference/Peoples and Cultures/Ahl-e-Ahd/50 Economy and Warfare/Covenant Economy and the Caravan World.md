# Covenant Economy and the Caravan World

The Covenant economy joins river grain, oasis cultivation, herding, maritime trade, workshops, pilgrimage, military estates, and caravan roads.

## Regional Roles

- Iqashar supplies administration, finished goods, finance, arms, and imperial demand.
- Al-Qahur supplies grain, dates, linen, fish, pilgrimage, and military-estate revenue.
- Qazmarid controls strait shipping, orchards, salt, horses, and western routes.
- Zanthir brokers Thalassian trade, translation, insurance, spices, and intelligence.
- Mazrakan joins imperial roads to Tarkhan animals, hides, escorts, captives, and grassland trade.

## Contracts and Credit

Most commerce uses ordinary witnessed agreements. Metaphysical enforcement is expensive and reserved for high-value cargoes, state procurement, dangerous entities, and politically important debt. Insurance and caravan surety spread risk while allowing powerful houses to own claims on distant harvests and households.

## Labor

Free cultivators, tenants, guild workers, caravan wage laborers, apprentices, household servants, debt-bondspeople, slaves, and military dependants work beside one another. Slavery and name custody are major sources of wealth, not decorative villainy. Manumission and free labor exist without making the system benign.

## Strategic Weakness

Water stations, canals, fodder, guarded roads, and shipping lanes are the empire's real arteries. A ruler who controls seals but loses wells governs parchment.
