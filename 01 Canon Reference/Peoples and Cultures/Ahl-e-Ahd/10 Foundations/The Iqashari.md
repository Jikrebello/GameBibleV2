# The Iqashari

The Iqashari descend from old northern escarpment towns, irrigated pockets, and caravan cities absorbed by the early Covenant. They now dominate imperial administration, long-distance finance, workshops, and the educated professions of Iqashar.

Iqashari identity is urban and municipal. People name their ward, school, guild, and patron before an ancient tribe. Courtyard houses, public fountains, bath culture, savory grain dishes, dyed cotton, formal debate, and fiercely competitive education mark respectable life. Marriage joins households and careers as much as bloodlines; adoption and professional sponsorship are common routes into elite families.

Provincials stereotype Iqashari as polished, evasive, and unable to survive beyond a paved road. Iqashari answer that the empire runs because somebody keeps the registers, granaries, arsenals, and drains working.
