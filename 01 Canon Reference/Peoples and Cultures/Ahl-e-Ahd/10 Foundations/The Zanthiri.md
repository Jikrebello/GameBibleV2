# The Zanthiri

The Zanthiri formed in the frontier created by the First Unsealing. Banu Mizan soldiers, Ahl-e-Ahd settlers, local converts, Velians, freed households, sailors, translators, and merchant diasporas produced a people whose loyalties cannot be reduced to conquest ancestry.

Zanthiri commonly speak both Covenant and Velian registers. Their food, music, clothing, and architecture combine desert-court, Atherian municipal, and Thalassian coastal forms. Mixed neighborhoods organize around wells, docks, churches, law schools, guilds, and patron houses rather than clean ethnic quarters.

Imperial ideologues call them unreliable; Thalmyric militants call them collaborators. Zanthiri politics begins with rejecting both simplifications while arguing bitterly over Emir Badis, bondage, tribute, and the future of the frontier.
