# The Qahuri

The Qahuri are the cultivators, water engineers, shrine communities, boatmen, and market towns of the sacred basin. Their irrigation councils and seasonal labor obligations predate Mujalib, although later law credits every surviving channel to the Covenant of the 4,444.

Qahuri life follows flood, planting, pilgrimage, and harvest. Extended households pool fields and water shares; village matrons and canal elders hold practical authority that military landlords ignore at their peril. Food centers on river grain, dates, lentils, fish, herbs, and festival sweets sold to pilgrims. Linen, reed work, painted ceramics, and devotional recitation distinguish the basin.

The military houses consider themselves guardians of Qahuri order. Many Qahuri consider them armed tenants who forgot the lease had an end.
