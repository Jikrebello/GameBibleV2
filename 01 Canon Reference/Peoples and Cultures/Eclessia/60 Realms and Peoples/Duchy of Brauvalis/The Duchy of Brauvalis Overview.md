# The Duchy of Brauvalis Overview

Brauvalis is a central Markish duchy of abbey valleys, brewing towns, cloth guilds, herb gardens, ceramics, and negotiated privilege. Duke Reinhardt remains old Church, but the territory's proximity to Korrheim and Ironvale makes confessional compromise a condition of survival.

## Historical Formation

Brauvalis grew from Gervin's middle inheritance around monastic estates, river markets, and chartered craft towns. No ruler could govern without abbots and guilds, so ducal authority developed through negotiated taxes and privileges.

## The Grey Sweat

Abbey infirmaries and brewing houses became plague hospitals. Surviving workers won wages and guild access; indebted monasteries leased land and production rights. The Great Abbey Tax Revolt of 1321 began as a dispute over whether emergency exemptions had become permanent.

## Reformation and Accord

Freeflame congregations spread through craft districts and along the River Weiss. Reinhardt's predecessors suppressed some and negotiated with others. The Accord confirms old-Church rule while preserving specified Freeflame districts and guild worship through local articles.

The arrangement is legal but fragile. Neither the Sanctum nor Ironvale accepts every exemption, and a new duke could invoke territorial confession to revoke them.

## Government and Faith

Reinhardt rules with noble estates, abbots, and town guilds. Old-Church monasteries remain spiritually and economically important. Reform-minded guilds demand audited tithes, vernacular scripture, and control of their charitable funds.

## Economy and Society

Ale, hops, medicines, cloth, ceramics, timber, and hospitality circulate through Brauvalian markets. Brewing is food preservation, medicine, wages, tax base, and ritual hospitality before it is luxury. The renamed **Order of Saint Veyskar** maintains the oldest licensed monastic breweries.

The poor eastern [[Wanemoor District]] adds wet timber hills, shallow iron workings, charcoal roads, and reed fens to this economy. Its district seat, [[Wanenbruck]], governs mine and saw traffic before the land descends toward isolated villages such as [[Merewick]]. Wanemoor's Grey Sweat galleries and burial grounds remain public obligations even where later leases placed mines or yards around them.

Central [[Hellerbruck]] stands on the River Weiss above a high limestone quarry. Its bridge, breweries, pottery yards, and bell foundry make it a prosperous charter town rather than an eastern extraction district. The ruined founder ridge and old upper quarry are protected historic ground whose final boundaries have not yet been mapped.

## Warfare

Brauvalis maintains ducal cavalry, guild militias, river guards, and fortified abbeys. It hires Korrheim companies in crisis and buys Ironvale weapons while fearing both neighbors' political influence.

## Present Tensions

- Reinhardt's succession may reopen the confessional settlement.
- Guilds and abbeys dispute tax, charity, and production licenses.
- The Black Keg network moves untaxed drink, medicine, pamphlets, and fugitives.
- Veridancian wine interests challenge Brauvalian naming and tariff privileges.
