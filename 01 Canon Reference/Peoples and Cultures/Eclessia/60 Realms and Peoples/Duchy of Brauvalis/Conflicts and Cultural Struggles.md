# Conflicts and Cultural Struggles

## Guild Rivalries

The **Brewers' League** represents town producers; the **Amber Brotherhood** protects monastic and licensed houses. Clothiers and potters dispute fuel, river frontage, and tariff preference. Herbalists contest Church alchemists over who may certify plague medicines and relic-adjacent remedies.

These are political institutions as well as trades. Guild burial funds, militias, and congregations became sources of power after the Grey Sweat.

## Church and Ducal Conflicts

- **Tithe War, 1309-1314 AR:** brewing towns withheld levies from clerics who defended the assurance system. Freeflame organizers entered through the dispute.
- **Great Abbey Tax Revolt, 1321 AR:** monasteries claimed plague-era exemptions against ducal reconstruction taxes. The settlement forced audited charitable accounts.
- **Forbidden Brew Controversy, 1348 AR:** an old medicinal recipe was condemned after Freeflame houses used it in memorial rites. Reinhardt licensed the drink as medicine while prohibiting sacramental claims.

## Trade Conflicts

The **Veridancian Wine Dispute**, active since 1268 AR, concerns tariffs, naming rights, and which drink may be sold at pilgrim fairs. The **Ironvale Merchants' War, 1326-1334 AR** combined embargo, pamphlet smuggling, and attacks on River Weiss warehouses during the Reform Wars.

The **Black Keg** began as untaxed drink traffic. It now moves medicine, banned books, fugitives, forged parish records, and occasionally weapons. Its cells share routes rather than ideology.

## Persons and Festivals

Father Aldric of Kellersheim organized plague hospitals and is locally venerated, though his universal sainthood case remains incomplete. Dame Lisbeth von Falkenheim won formal guild standing for women managing inherited breweries. Gustav Dornfeld standardized measures after the Trade War.

The Grand Tapping, Night of the Golden Tankard, and Feast of Hops remain civic celebrations. Old Church and Freeflame households attend together and disagree over which blessings are worship.

## Present Conflict

Duke Reinhardt's compromise depends on personal authority. Abbeys fear secular audit; guilds fear revoked worship; heirs court both the Sanctum and Ironvale. A mundane succession could therefore reopen a religious war.
