# Conflicts and Underground Movements

## Black Powder and Shell Scripts

The **Red Anvil Consortium** favors bombards, warlock scripts, and aggressive export. The **Silvered Gauntlet** joins displaced cavalry interests, cautious engineers, and Freeflame elders who fear both battlefield slaughter and Shell breaches.

The argument is not technology against superstition. Failed scripts can materialize uncontrolled force, while foreign sales finance Ironvale's freedom.

## Labor

The **Greystone Riots of 1355 AR** began after a mine collapse and falsified safety inspection. The Blackened Guard restored order with lethal force, exposing the distance between guild citizenship and worker power.

The **Hammerford Strike of 1360 AR** demands elected safety wardens, transparent injury funds, and limits on debt deductions. It remains unresolved.

The **Sons of the Forge** seek reform within the Freeflame constitution. The **Ashen Chain** argues that guild masters have become a new landed class and prepares sabotage. Neither is automatically a Left Hand cult, though hostile networks recruit inside both.

## Arms and Finance

The **Rustborn Legion** is a deniable company moving experimental weapons to foreign buyers. The **Veiled Consortium** brokers stolen designs, false inspection seals, and political influence. Medori credit appears behind several contracts without proving direct control of every crime.

## Old Regime Networks

Surviving ducal clients preserve Rainald's household memory while disagreeing over restoration. Old-Church families keep private chapels. Some merely want protected worship; others cooperate with foreign powers to overturn the Dominion.

## Story Uses

A strike infiltrated by saboteurs, an unsafe script blamed on labor, an old-Church chapel hiding neither weapons nor innocence, a weapon shipment to Grenzburg, and a guild election purchased through invisible debt.
