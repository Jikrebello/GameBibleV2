# Relics, Forbidden Knowledge, and Hidden Horrors

This register identifies the origins and costs of the Marches' principal dangerous objects. Public stories may preserve uncertainty; creator-facing use should not collapse them into generic ancient evil.

## Relics

**Ebon Crown of Kael'Ryth:** a contract mantle worn by the fortress commander who tried to impose one identity on the dead. It grants tactical memories by gradually replacing the wearer's judgment with those of former commanders.

**Hollow Grimoire:** a collection of incomplete Shell scripts from Dornwald. Missing control lines make apparent successes progressively unstable.

**Thornheart Dagger:** a Menhir-bound tool for cutting diseased growth and severing corrupted pacts. Used outside its covenant, it treats persons as infections.

**Wraith Lantern of Uldrekh:** a Weeping Court object that displays the dead a viewer most wants to see. It learns grief through repeated use and eventually supplies instructions in the beloved voice.

## Knowledge Sites

The ruined libraries of Valtorheim preserve Atherian provincial archives, plague-era additions, and later forged genealogies. Their surviving wards protect documents rather than readers.

The Hollowborn vault contains route maps, seal keys, and keeper testimony. It is not a treasury. Opening it without the sequence of living witnesses invalidates several seals at once.

## Active Horrors

**Sleeper in the Mire:** a dormant Menhir-adjacent power constrained by an old ecological pact. Drainage and peat cutting weaken its boundary.

**Drowned Knights of Kael'Ryth:** preserved identities caught in the keep's failed soul-route. They reenact command because the Ebon Crown still defines them as a garrison.

**Forest That Breathes:** a region where Faewild law has overgrown material boundaries. It is dangerous but not inherently Adversary-aligned.

**Maw That Whispers:** a Left Hand breach offering erasure of guilt in exchange for the memories that make restitution possible.
