# The Sylvan Marches Overview

The Sylvan Marches are Eclessia's southeastern cold frontier: forest lordships, charter towns, Folk sanctuaries, abandoned parishes, Grobi corridors, and Atherian ruins under the nominal authority of High Warden Elias Dreymann. Southward travel enters harsher taiga, frozen waterways, and permafrost.

## Historical Formation

The Marches preserve a strong Folk substrate beneath Adalmarid settlement, including old-rite Enaidfae and Chapel-Folk communities. The First Southern Crusade, Great Southern Purge, and March of the Redeemer's Sword established forts and parishes but never erased older land claims. Authority remains negotiated at the distance a garrison can actually travel.

## The Grey Sweat

Entire parish belts were abandoned. Migrants, runaways, discharged soldiers, Freeflame exiles, and Folk families later resettled the gaps under incompatible titles. Missing registers make the Marches a refuge and a jurisdictional nightmare.

## Reformation and Accord

The Marches are old-Church territory in law. In practice, itinerant Freeflame elders, isolated priests, Folk rites, and household worship coexist beyond consistent enforcement. The Accord protects the High Warden's claim more effectively than any single confession.

## Government and Faith

Elias Dreymann governs from Marchburg through wardens, local lords, town charters, rangers, and seasonal assemblies. Grimmholt, under Baroness Katarina Wolfsden, anchors a western forest road. Chapel-Folk and River-Clans negotiate sanctuary and passage where marcher courts cannot compel them.

## Society and Economy

Timber, resin, charcoal, furs, salvage, and winter provisioning sustain the region. Settlement follows rivers and fortified roads. The cold makes failed harvests and Grobi movement connected problems rather than separate monster events.

## Threats

Grobi warbands enter along hunger corridors; their leaders and motives differ, but predation remains predation. Drakes are Faewild-shaped apex creatures whose heart-gems attract hunters, armies, and artifact merchants. Folk resistance defends real sovereignty and can also exact ruthless vengeance. Old ruins contain Atherian, Menhir, and Left Hand dangers rather than one undefined lost civilization.

## Present Tensions

- Competing plague-era titles over inhabited land.
- Sanctuary agreements threatened by lords, settlers, or Folk retaliation.
- Grobi migration intensified by pressure farther south.
- Relic salvagers waking systems they mistake for treasure.
- Church and Freeflame agents turning frontier necessity into confessional competition.

## Story Uses

Missing settlements, winter convoys, disputed land, Grobi diplomacy, drake hunts, haunted battlefields, false relics, Menhir obligations, and ruins whose history implicates living families.
