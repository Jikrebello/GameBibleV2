## **1. The Lost Ruins of the Forgotten Civilization**

### **Overview**
Scattered across the Sylvan Marches lie the **remnants of an ancient, pre-Atherian civilization**, one that **worshipped the Adversary** and has since faded into oblivion. The **exact nature of this lost empire is shrouded in mystery**, but the ruins they left behind **hum with dark energy**, hiding **forgotten horrors, forbidden knowledge, and ancient curses**.

### **Notable Ruins and Their Secrets**

#### **The Sunken Spires of Valtorheim**

- A city that once **stood atop the marshes**, now **half-submerged in mud and tangled roots**.
- Its **central temple contains murals depicting apocalyptic visions**, where the world is **consumed by a tidal wave of darkness**.
- Scholars and treasure hunters **who venture inside rarely return**, and those who do speak of **whispering voices and shadowy figures watching from the drowned corridors**.
- Strange **luminescent glyphs** glow beneath the water at night, seemingly **leading deeper into the submerged ruins**.
- Local legends claim **something ancient stirs beneath the silt**, waiting to be awakened.

#### **The Black Gate of Dornwald**

- A colossal **obsidian archway**, standing alone in the heart of the **Dornwald Forest**, covered in **runes no living scholar has been able to decipher**.
- Legends claim that **those who pass beneath it vanish**, drawn into a realm of **endless night**.
- The Hollowborn have **warned against disturbing the Gate**, believing it to be **a threshold between the world of the living and something far worse**.
- Occasionally, **faint echoes of battle** can be heard near the Gate—spectral remnants of an ancient war.
- Inquisitors have attempted to **seal the Gate with holy sigils**, yet the runes always **reappear, untouched by time**.

#### **The Weeping Stones of Uldrekh**

- A set of **ancient monoliths**, each carved with the **agonized faces of long-forgotten kings**.
- At night, the stones are said to **“weep” a dark, viscous fluid**, which, when consumed, grants **visions of the past… or nightmares from the abyss**.
- Local rangers avoid the area, citing **unsettling dreams and whispers in the wind**.
- The Hollowborn believe the monoliths were **prisons for traitor-lords**, their souls eternally trapped within the stone.
- Some who drink the fluid **gain unnatural knowledge**, while others are **driven mad by whispers of the dead**.

#### **The Temple of the Rooted God**

- Hidden deep in the **twisting brambles of the Hollowwood**, this **buried temple is entwined with colossal tree roots**, as if the forest itself is attempting to **consume it**.
- Inside, strange **statues of robed figures** line the halls, their **faces obscured by wooden masks that seem to shift when unobserved**.
- The Hollowborn claim that **something still lives within the temple**, its voice carried on the rustling leaves.
- Those who spend too long inside find themselves **lost in the woods for days, no matter how close to the edge they should be**.

#### **The Maw of Unmaking**

- A **massive, circular pit**, rimmed with obsidian-like shards that **cut deeper than steel**.
- A **constant, eerie hum emanates from the chasm**, disorienting those who linger near it.
- Some scholars theorize this was **a sacrificial site** where the Adversary’s followers performed **dark rituals to unmake existence itself**.
- Rangers have reported **twisted figures crawling from the pit on moonless nights**, only to dissolve with the dawn.

#### **The Drowned Keep of Kael’Ryth**

- A fortress submerged beneath a cursed lake, its **spire occasionally breaching the surface during certain celestial alignments**.
- The Keep was said to be **home to an order of knights who turned against the Atherian Empire**, only to be swallowed by the earth.
- Even now, their **spirits patrol the ruins, locked in eternal battle**, repeating the final moments of their doomed stand.
- The water itself is **poisoned**, but some claim that beneath the keep lies **a vault containing relics of unspeakable power**.

## **2. Legendary Rangers and Protectors of the Marches**

### **The Wardens of the Deepwood**
- The **oldest and most respected ranger order**, dedicated to **keeping the balance between civilization and the wild**.
- Trained in **stealth, archery, and tracking**, they know every hidden path through the forest.
- Some whisper that **the Hollowborn secretly guide their actions**, though the Wardens deny such claims.

### **Famous Rangers and Their Deeds**

#### **Eldric “The Ghost of the Glen”**
- A legendary **marksman and survivalist**, said to have **single-handedly held off a Fäskogarfolk warband for three days**.
- Vanished into the **Deepwood after a failed political coup in Marchburg**, now a folk hero among exiles.
- Sightings of a **lone figure clad in dark leather, bow in hand, are considered a sign of good fortune**.

#### **Sister Ysolde, the Silver Huntress**
- A former **priestess turned monster hunter**, wielding **blessed silver arrows** against the horrors of the Marches.
- Known for **tracking and slaying a rogue drake that had terrorized the hamlets of Blackbriar**.
- Has a **bounty on her head from the Church**, accused of **dabbling in forbidden magic**.

#### **Garrik the Hollow**
- A ranger **who was once taken by the Hollowborn**, only to return years later, **changed but still loyal to his duty**.
- His **eyes glow faintly in the dark**, and he is said to **sense the presence of the ruins’ ancient evils before they awaken**.
- Feared by some, revered by others, he remains an **enigmatic guardian of the Marches**.

## **3. Major Battles Against the Fäskogarfolk**

### **The Eternal War of the Marches**
The **Fäskogarfolk**, wild and untamed, see the **Eclessian settlers as trespassers upon sacred land**. For centuries, the two cultures have clashed in an **endless cycle of raids, skirmishes, and border wars**.

### **Notable Conflicts**

#### **The Battle of Raven’s Hollow (120 years ago)**
- The **largest recorded battle** between Marchers and Fäskogarfolk, where a massive **warhost of elk-riders and shamans** attempted to **burn Marchburg to the ground**.
- Defended by **a desperate alliance of rangers, knights, and mercenaries**, the battle **lasted for five days** before the invaders were driven back.
- Many survivors of the battle still **report strange visions** near the battlefield, claiming that **spirits of the fallen still wander the charred remains of Raven’s Hollow**.

#### **The Bloodmoon Massacre (45 years ago)**
- A brutal retaliation where **a warband of Fäskogarfolk was cornered and slaughtered by Marcher forces** under the command of **Baron Viktor Dreymann**.
- The massacre **poisoned the rivers with blood for weeks**, and many claim that **the land has never fully recovered from the slaughter**.
- Some say the **souls of the fallen still cry out for vengeance**, leading to an increase in **Fäskogarfolk war activity every Bloodmoon**.

#### **The Ghostwood Raids (Ongoing)**
- Small, coordinated **hit-and-run attacks** by Fäskogarfolk warbands, targeting **supply routes, logging camps, and isolated hamlets**.
- Difficult to track and predict, as they **vanish into the deep forests before counterattacks can be launched**.
- Some fear that these **increasingly frequent raids are a sign of an even larger invasion to come**.

## **4. The Hollowborn and Their Secrets**

### **The Guardians of Forgotten Knowledge**
The Hollowborn are a **mysterious order of mystics, druids, and scholars**, living deep within the **haunted groves of the Marches**. They claim to **protect knowledge too dangerous for mortal hands**.

### **Rumors and Legends About the Hollowborn**
- Some say they **worship neither the Lord nor the Adversary**, but rather an **ancient force older than both**.
- Others believe they **hold the keys to the secrets of the lost civilization**, carefully ensuring that **its dark magic does not fall into the wrong hands**.
- Their members rarely interact with outsiders, but those who do are often **gifted with unnatural insight or foresight**.

### **The Hollowborn Trials**
- Those who seek **forbidden knowledge** may **petition the Hollowborn**, but they must pass the **Trials of the Deepwood**.
- These trials involve **navigating the ruins, confronting spectral guardians, and deciphering lost inscriptions**.
- Few return from these trials unchanged, and fewer still **retain their sanity if they uncover the truth of the lost empire**.

## **5. The Future of the Sylvan Marches**
With **conflicts raging, forgotten horrors awakening, and hidden knowledge waiting to be uncovered**, the fate of the Sylvan Marches remains uncertain.

- **Will the Marchers stand united against the rising threats, or will old feuds divide them?**
- **What secrets do the Hollowborn protect, and should they ever be revealed?**
- **Are the increasing Fäskogarfolk raids a prelude to something greater—perhaps a final war for the soul of the Marches?**

The answers lie **deep within the forests, beneath the ruins, and in the whispered warnings of those who have seen too much.**
