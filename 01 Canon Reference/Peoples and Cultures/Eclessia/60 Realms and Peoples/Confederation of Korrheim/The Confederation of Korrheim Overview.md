## **1. Overview**
The **Confederation of Korrheim** is a rugged, fiercely independent region of **mountain-dwelling warriors, siege engineers, and self-governing cantons**. Unlike the great feudal kingdoms of Eclessia, Korrheim is **not ruled by a single monarch**—instead, it is a loose alliance of **city-states, fortress-settlements, and warrior-brotherhoods** bound together by a shared commitment to **defending their homeland and maintaining autonomy from outside rule**.

Surrounded by **natural defenses of towering peaks, deep ravines, and labyrinthine tunnels**, Korrheim is nearly **impossible to invade**, making its people legendary for their **defensive warfare and expert siege engineering**. The **mountain clans and mercenary companies of Korrheim are highly sought after**, providing elite fighters, tactical specialists, and defensive architects to rulers across Eclessia.

Despite their reputation as warriors, the people of Korrheim are also renowned **engineers, builders, and strategists**, known for crafting **fortifications that can withstand any siege** and developing **advanced mechanical innovations**.

While their unity has made them strong, **political rivalries, mercenary feuds, and disputes over trade rights** constantly threaten the stability of the confederation. The question remains: **Can Korrheim remain united, or will its independence fracture under the weight of internal strife and foreign influence?**

## **2. Government & Political Structure**

### **Decentralized Rule & The Council of Heldenheim**
- Korrheim is governed by a **rotating council of representatives** from each of the **free cities and warrior cantons**.
- **No central ruler or noble class**—leadership is determined by **military merit, trade influence, or ancestral lineage**.
- Decisions are made through **heated debates and majority votes**, with city-states retaining their **own laws and traditions**.

### **Key Political Factions & Power Struggles**

#### **The Free Cities of Heldenheim & Ostwald**
- Act as the **economic and diplomatic heart of Korrheim**, controlling **trade with foreign powers**.
- Favor **mercantile expansion and negotiated alliances** over military aggression.
- Opposed by the **Warrior Cantons**, who believe in **strength through arms** rather than diplomacy.

#### **The Warrior Cantons (Falkendorf, Sturmgard, Grimhart)**
- Governed by **warrior-brotherhoods, knightly orders, and paladin militias**.
- Favor **military action, defensive fortifications, and maintaining Korrheim’s independence at all costs**.
- Frequently clash with **mercantile factions over foreign trade policies**.

#### **The Guild of Siege Engineers & Builders**
- A powerful faction responsible for **constructing Korrheim’s legendary fortresses, war machines, and underground tunnel networks**.
- Seeks to **expand technological advancements**, pushing for **more funding and research into war machinery**.
- Their innovations sometimes cause tension with **traditionalist warrior clans**, who view their work as too mechanized and unorthodox.

### **Ongoing Political Disputes**
- **Mercenary Contracts vs. Homeland Defense:** Some cantons want to **prioritize selling mercenary services**, while others argue for **keeping elite fighters in Korrheim to strengthen defenses**.
- **Trade with Foreign Powers:** Free cities push for **expanding trade**, but warrior clans fear **too much foreign influence will weaken Korrheim’s identity**.
- **Church Influence:** The Eclessian Church wants **a stronger presence in Korrheim**, but many warrior-brotherhoods reject outside religious control.

## **3. Military & Warfare**

### **The Iron Hosts of Korrheim** _(Mercenary Companies & Warrior Orders)_
- Korrheim’s **mercenaries are among the most elite in Eclessia**, renowned for their **discipline, adaptability, and mountain warfare tactics**.
- Major mercenary factions include:
    - **The Blackmount Guard** – Specializes in **fortress defense and counter-siege tactics**.
    - **The Stormforged Legion** – Experts in **heavy infantry and hammer-and-shield formations**.
    - **The Winterfang Riders** – Elite **mountain cavalry and skirmishers**, trained in **ambush warfare and swift strikes**.

### **Defensive Warfare & Siege Engineering**
- Korrheim’s **mountain strongholds** are **impenetrable fortresses**, built with **hidden tunnels, reinforced gates, and collapsible bridges**.
- Siege engineers develop **ballistae, trebuchets, and boiling oil traps**, making assaults on Korrheim a **nightmare for any invader**.
- In battle, Korrheim warriors favor **defensive shield walls, pike formations, and guerrilla tactics**, using the terrain to their advantage.

### **Historical Conflicts & Campaigns**
- **The Battle of Sturmgard Pass (110 years ago):** A Korrheimer army **held off a foreign invasion** in a narrow mountain pass, using a combination of **rockslides and ambush tactics**.
- **The Siege of Heldenheim (60 years ago):** The only recorded attempt to conquer Korrheim’s capital failed when the defenders **collapsed tunnels beneath the enemy camp**, forcing the invaders to retreat.
- **The Falkendorf Rebellion (20 years ago):** A civil conflict between **mercenary warlords and the Council of Heldenheim**, nearly plunging the confederation into civil war.

## **4. Economy & Trade**

### **Key Exports & Trade Strengths**
- **Mercenary Services:** Korrheim exports its **elite soldiers, siege engineers, and military strategists** to foreign rulers.
- **Mining & Blacksmithing:** Produces **high-quality iron, steel, and weapons**, with underground forges hidden deep in the mountains.
- **Fortifications & Engineering:** Korrheimer architects are hired to **design and build castles, walls, and defensive outposts**.
- **Alpine Agriculture & Brewing:** Known for **hardy livestock, mountain-honey mead, and herbal medicinal brews**.

### **Trade Conflicts & Economic Challenges**
- **Foreign Mercenary Dependence:** Some argue that **selling Korrheim’s warriors weakens homeland defenses**, while others say **it enriches the confederation**.
- **Ironvale Trade Rivalry:** Korrheim’s metalworks compete with **Ironvale’s industrial output**, leading to **trade disputes over steel prices and mining rights**.
- **Tensions with the Church:** The Eclessian Church wants **a stronger economic foothold in Korrheim**, but warrior factions **oppose outside religious control over trade policies**.

## **5. The Future of Korrheim**
As Korrheim stands at a crossroads, key questions remain:

- **Will mercenary expansion weaken homeland security, or ensure Korrheim’s prosperity?**
- **Can the warrior cantons and free cities remain united, or will political divisions break the confederation apart?**
- **Will technological advancements in siege engineering redefine Korrheim’s role in future wars?**

Surrounded by towering peaks and battle-hardened warriors, Korrheim remains **one of the most resilient and enigmatic forces in Eclessia**—but whether it will stand the test of time depends on **the unity of its people and the strength of its warriors.**
