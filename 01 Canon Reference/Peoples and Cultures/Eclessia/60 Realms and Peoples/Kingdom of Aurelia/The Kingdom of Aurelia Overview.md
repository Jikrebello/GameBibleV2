# The Kingdom of Aurelia Overview

Aurelia is Eclessia's deep-southern Latinate heartland and the temporal country of the Sanctum Divinitatis. It is called a kingdom by inheritance, but in 1360 AR it functions as a Primarchal State: Lord Primarch Alistair III governs through clerical estates, cathedral cities, noble clients, and an immense legal archive.

## Historical Formation

Aurelia preserves the densest Atherian provincial inheritance in Eclessia. Adalmar's grandson Aldwin received its line at the Partition of Drossfeld in 1049. As the royal house weakened, the Sanctum converted coronation rights, estates, and custody of the Crown of Embers into direct temporal rule.

## The Grey Sweat

Eastern mortality reached roughly one person in three. Cathedral estates lost tenants, parish books failed, and orphaned benefices fell into litigation. Hospitals and burial confraternities became powerful civic institutions. Bruckmoor's false apparition forced the relic courts to separate evidence, custody, and offerings.

The assurance offices nevertheless expanded after the plague. Under Alessior VI, grief became a financial base for monumental rebuilding and war.

## Reformation and Accord

Ansgar's indictment names the Aurellian assurance regime directly. Aurelia answered the Reformation first with suppression and then with the Council of Veridance's reforms. The Korrheim Accord confirms Aurelia as old-Church territory, but underground Freeflame circles, banned printers, and a gray assurance market persist.

## Government and Faith

Alistair III balances traditionalist cardinals, Veridancian reformers, relic judges, noble estate holders, and creditors. The High Council advises but does not elect away his temporal authority. Aurelia's strongest claim is custodial: it keeps the Crown of Embers and therefore the legal possibility of a reunited Eclessian empire.

## Society and Economy

Pilgrimage, administration, schools, hospitals, luxury workshops, and Church estates dominate. Lay notaries increasingly challenge clerical records. Southern cold makes the countryside harsher than Aurelia's monumental court culture suggests; poor harvests expose how dependent the capital is on Virelian grain.

## Warfare

Aurelia relies on noble clients, order houses, urban guards, and contracted forces rather than a vast royal army. Its most consequential weapon is legitimacy: recognition, excommunication, relic judgment, and access to the Crown.

## Present Tensions

- Reform the Church without conceding that Ansgar was right about its structure.
- Suppress assurance fraud without exposing how deeply it financed the Sanctum.
- Keep Virelian grain and Medori credit flowing without becoming their dependent.
- Prevent the Unburnt Psalter from receiving a test neither confession can control.

## Story Uses

Plague-title litigation, forbidden printing, relic trials, clerical espionage, pilgrimage crime, disputed sainthood, and succession schemes around the Crown of Embers.
