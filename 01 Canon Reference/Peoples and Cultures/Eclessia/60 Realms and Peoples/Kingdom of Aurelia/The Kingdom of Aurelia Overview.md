# The Kingdom of Aurelia Overview

Aurelia is Eclessia's deep-southern Latinate heartland and the temporal country of the Sanctum Divinitatis. It is called a kingdom by inheritance, but in 1360 AR it functions as a Primarchal State: Lord Primarch Alistair III governs through clerical estates, cathedral cities, noble clients, and an immense legal archive.

## Historical Formation

Aurelia preserves the densest Atherian provincial inheritance in Eclessia. Adalmar's grandson Aldwin held it, with Aurelium and the keeping of the unworn Crown of Embers, when the lot at Drossfeld came up blank in 1041. In the Two Winters his descendant Ermbold refused to carry the crown to the Sanctum for examination ("a hat, not a relic") and in 1102 sent it to the Primarch in a grain cart. The crown passed into the Sanctum's trust in 1104. Aldwin's line kept the Aurelian kingdom until its last king died in 1166, when the Primarchs took the royal estates into the same trust as the crown and began to rule Aurelia directly.

## The Grey Sweat

Eastern mortality reached roughly one person in five, and more than half the parish clergy. Cathedral estates lost tenants, parish books failed, and orphaned benefices fell into litigation. In 1240 the plague-thinned conclave elected Aimery of Corvel, who died that night unsung; his twin brother Aubert wore his ring for twenty months and issued the Ferry Rule, which let any baptised person sing the funeral where no priest lived. When Aimery's unsent figure was seen at the conclave garden wall in 1242, Aubert confessed, and two colleges of cardinals split over whether his acts stood until Lord Primarch Gelasin II, elected by both, closed the Schism of the Twin Cardinals that year. Hospitals and burial confraternities became powerful civic institutions. Bruckmoor's false apparition forced the relic courts to separate evidence, custody, and offerings.

In 1252 Gelasin II gathered the old assurance letters into the **Chancery of Sendings**: Sending Houses were endowed to sing named rites for named dead, and families bought sealed letters promising them. Where the rites were sung, the dead stopped walking, and the trade spread on that evidence. Under Alessior II, an ascetic who taught that the Chancery's seal itself placed a soul in the Church's keeping, and his Cardinal-Treasurer Silvio Quarne, grief became the financial base for a bought election, and sealed letters given free to the poor taught whole parishes to stop singing for their dead. Letters were printed, priced by age and sold faster than any House could sing them. In 1285 an apparition of Saint Gerlind condemned the singers of Aurelium's Saint-Leon quarter, who sang the dead out for nothing, and a riot burned the quarter; Lantern investigators later traced the apparition to the Cardinal-Treasurer's office.

## Reformation and Accord

Ansgar's indictment names the Aurellian assurance regime directly. Aurelia answered the Reformation first with suppression and then with the Council of Veridance's reforms. The Korrheim Accord confirms Aurelia as old-Church territory, but underground Freeflame circles, banned printers, and a gray assurance market persist.

## Government and Faith

Alistair III balances traditionalist cardinals, Veridancian reformers, relic judges, noble estate holders, and creditors. The High Council advises but does not elect away his temporal authority. Aurelia's strongest claim is custodial: it keeps the Crown of Embers and therefore the legal possibility of a reunited Eclessian empire.

## Society and Economy

Pilgrimage, administration, schools, hospitals, luxury workshops, and Church estates dominate. Lay notaries increasingly challenge clerical records. Southern cold makes the countryside harsher than Aurelia's monumental court culture suggests; poor harvests expose how dependent the capital is on Virelian grain.

## Warfare

Aurelia relies on noble clients, order houses, urban guards, and contracted forces rather than a vast royal army. Its most consequential weapon is legitimacy: recognition, excommunication, relic judgment, and access to the Crown.

## Present Tensions

- Reform the Church without conceding that Ansgar was right about its structure.
- Suppress assurance fraud without exposing how deeply it financed the Sanctum.
- Keep Virelian grain and Medori credit flowing without becoming their dependent.
- Prevent the Unburnt Psalter from receiving a test neither confession can control.
