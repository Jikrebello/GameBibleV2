# The Kingdom of Virelia Overview

Virelia is Eclessia's eastern grain kingdom: Vesari river plains beneath an elective Latinate-Markish crown, ruled in 1360 AR by King Gregor IV Radomir. Its sabre-right nobility prizes political liberty for itself while enforcing the harshest bound labor in Eclessia.

## Historical Formation

Virelia descends from independent Vesari princedoms, not an Eclessian colony. At the Choosing of Strazgorod in 1061, Prince Radomir I accepted the Sanctum for an Adalmarid marriage and military alliance. The hierarchy became western while village piety retained icons, standing vigils, hearth observances, and memory of Veridun.

## The Grey Sweat

Where labor scarcity freed tenants elsewhere, Virelian magnates imposed a second serfdom. Estate courts restricted movement, increased demesne labor, and tied grain contracts to hereditary obligation. Runaways formed river gangs, frontier communities, and the Unwritten camps beyond reliable registration.

## Reformation and Accord

Roughly one-third of the Council of Sabres now follows recognized Freeflame practice. The crown remains old Church, but every royal election can change the public rite under the Korrheim Accord. Magnates use confession to court foreign allies, seize estates, and discipline rivals.

## Government and Faith

The Council of Sabres elects the king from eligible noble houses. Gregor IV balances House Radomir, mercantile Arvidan, frontier Siercan, and rough-land Lovec interests. Bishops answer west while many villages practice a faith their own clergy only partly understand.

## Society and Economy

Virelia exports grain, timber, horses, cattle, and river tolls. Magnate estates dominate, but chartered towns, Jirahar traders, Freeflame printers, and runaway networks complicate their control. Parish-register gaps let estates claim people whose ancestry cannot be proved.

## Warfare

Winged lancers provide noble shock cavalry; estate levies and hired riders sustain longer campaigns. Hetmans command by negotiated confederation rather than simple royal hierarchy. The eastern frontier requires fast troops, while internal policing consumes increasing manpower.

## Present Tensions

- Gregor's eventual succession could change Eclessia's confessional balance.
- Grain wealth depends on bondage that drives flight and revolt.
- The eastern candidate at every election revives the possibility of alignment with Veridun.
- Freeflame preaching spreads fastest among the Unwritten, where territorial confession has little meaning.

## Story Uses

Election intrigue, runaway recovery, forged lineage, river piracy, confessional cavalry companies, estate revolt, and the Virelian Lamp in Veridun.
