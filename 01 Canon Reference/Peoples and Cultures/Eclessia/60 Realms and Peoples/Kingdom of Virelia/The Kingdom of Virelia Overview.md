# The Kingdom of Virelia Overview

Virelia is Eclessia's eastern grain kingdom: Vesari river plains beneath an elective Latinate-Markish crown, ruled in 1360 AR by King Gregor IV Radomir. Its sabre-right nobility prizes political liberty for itself while enforcing the harshest bound labor in Eclessia.

## Historical Formation

Virelia descends from independent Vesari princedoms, not an Eclessian colony. At the Choosing of Strazgorod in 1061 the envoys' lamp kindled in Veridun's cathedral and Patriarch Makarios would not let it go home; Prince Radomir I accepted the Sanctum instead, with the hand of Lotharic's heiress and western swords against the steppe. Their son ruled the Vesari princedoms and Lotharic's eastern kingdom together as the first king of Virelia. The hierarchy became western while village piety retained icons, standing vigils, hearth observances, and memory of Veridun.

The crown was won in blood at home. Between 1068 and 1102 Radomir II's officers purged the old Vesari hearth-houses that had contested his throne: they burned the ancestral faces painted above door lintels, renamed the houses' children and forbade one kin-name, which is still not spoken. Then the Two Winters of 1101–1103 starved the purged districts, and Radomir II's sons died of the famine-fever. In 1103 the Council of Sabres revived the old Vesari moot-right and elected a king, and it has elected every king since. Veridun offered to send the Virelian Lamp home if the eastern candidate won that first election; he lost by three sabres.

## The Grey Sweat

Where labor scarcity freed tenants elsewhere, Virelian magnates imposed a second serfdom through the **bond of the clean village**. The magnates held the patronage of most parishes; a village whose priest had died was unclean and could be sealed, and its survivors were moved onto demesne estates under bond in exchange for a priest and burial in rite. Estate courts restricted movement, increased demesne labor, and tied grain contracts to hereditary obligation. Runaways formed river gangs, frontier communities, and the Unwritten camps beyond reliable registration.

## Reformation and Accord

Roughly one-third of the Council of Sabres now follows recognized Freeflame practice. The crown remains old Church, but every royal election can change the public rite under the Korrheim Accord. Magnates use confession to court foreign allies, seize estates, and discipline rivals.

## Government and Faith

The Council of Sabres elects the king from eligible noble houses. Gregor IV balances House Radomir, mercantile Arvidan, frontier Siercan, and rough-land Lovec interests. Bishops answer west while many villages practice a faith their own clergy only partly understand.

## Society and Economy

Virelia exports grain, timber, horses, cattle, and river tolls. Magnate estates dominate, but chartered towns, Jirahar traders, Freeflame printers, and runaway networks complicate their control. Parish-register gaps let estates claim people whose ancestry cannot be proved.

## Warfare

Winged lancers provide noble shock cavalry; estate levies and hired riders sustain longer campaigns. Hetmans command by negotiated confederation rather than simple royal hierarchy. The eastern frontier requires fast troops, while internal policing consumes increasing manpower.

## Present Tensions

- Gregor's eventual succession could change Eclessia's confessional balance.
- Grain wealth depends on bondage that drives flight and revolt.
- The eastern candidate at every election revives the possibility of alignment with Veridun.
- Freeflame preaching spreads fastest among the Unwritten, where territorial confession has little meaning.
