# Eclessian Military Orders

Eclessian orders are chartered institutions, not a single army. Some guard roads or shrines; others hold estates, maintain hospitals, investigate supernatural threats, or serve frontier vows. The Reform Wars reduced their universal reach and forced each to declare how it relates to territorial confession.

## Order of the Radiant Path

The Radiant Path maintains pilgrim roads, bridges, and fortified hospices. Its mounted brethren escort high-value processions; its Pathwardens patrol on foot with spear and crossbow. The order lost houses in Freeflame territory but retains cross-border passage through carefully secular service contracts.

## Inquisitors of Saint Malveris

Saint Malveris's inquisitors specialize in forbidden objects, fraudulent relics, and cult networks. After the Council of Veridance, their independent prisons were curtailed and their supernatural investigations placed under Lantern review. Traditionalists resent the oversight; reformers doubt it goes far enough.

## Knights of Saint Aveline

The Avelines defend the southern marches and maintain a penitential memory of earlier campaigns against the Folk. Their charter now distinguishes armed raiders, Grobi warbands, and lawful Folk sanctuaries. Chapters that ignore the distinction risk both secular prosecution and spiritual corruption.

## Order of the Blessed Watch

The Blessed Watch protects pilgrims, plague cemeteries, hospitals, and endangered parish archives. Its Shield Brethren train in warding and evacuation rather than conquest. It is the order most often trusted across confessional borders.

## Order of the Vigilant Flame

The Vigilant Flame once acted openly against sedition and heresy. Its burnings and property seizures during the Reform Wars made it a symbol of coercive old-Church power. The order survives under strict episcopal warrants, smaller and angrier, with several former chapters operating as unauthorised companies.

## Order of the Lantern

Founded by Lord Primarch Celestin IX during the Council of Veridance, the Lantern trains clergy, investigators, teachers, linguists, and field theologians. It carries arms when evidence requires protection but is not principally a knightly order. Father Ivo the Lamed designed it to understand an opponent's doctrine accurately before judging it.

## Confessional Limits

Recognized Freeflame realms do not admit an armed clerical order merely because the Sanctum chartered it. Orders therefore travel under diplomatic safe-conducts, hospital licenses, mercenary articles, or local invitations. This creates fertile ground for forged warrants and deniable intervention.
