# Eclessian Canon Register

This is the creator-facing control sheet for Eclessia. When active notes disagree, authority descends through: [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR)]], [[Gazetteer of the Known World]], the three controlling events, [[The Making of the Eclessians]], and then regional dossiers.

## Fixed Historical Chain

The Fall of Caleran (905) creates the eastern emergency; the Third Crusade (906-908) stops the advance at Caldria; Adalmar unites the eastern kingdoms (975-1019); the Great Split (1015) makes their western Christian inheritance institutionally separate from Thalmyria; Drossfeld (1049) creates the successor lines; the Choosing (1061) binds Virelia to the Sanctum; the Crown of Embers enters Church trust (1104); [[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/The Grey Sweat.md|The Grey Sweat]] (1232-1247) transforms labor, law, and religious confidence; [[Freeflame Reformation]] (1297 onward) breaks the Church monopoly; and [[The Korrheim Accord]] (1343) freezes territorial confession without granting individual liberty.

## Moral and Institutional Rule

The old Church possesses valid sacraments, genuine saints, and institutions capable of mercy. Its assurance regime nevertheless committed real fraud. Freeflame reform names that fraud truthfully and creates literate, disciplined communities; it also enables confiscation, coercive princes, and merciless rigor. Neither diagnosis requires moral equivalence between sanctity, corruption, and the Left Hand.

## Geographic Rule

Eclessia lies east of Thalmyria. Its Western-Christian cultural inheritance is not a compass direction. Within the southern hemisphere, travel south toward Aurelia, the Sylvan Marches, and Grenzburg becomes colder; deep southern woodland grades toward taiga and permafrost.
