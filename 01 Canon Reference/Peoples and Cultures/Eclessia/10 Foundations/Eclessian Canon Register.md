# Eclessian Canon Register

This is the creator-facing control sheet for Eclessia. When active notes disagree, authority descends through: [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR)]], [[Gazetteer of the Known World]], the three controlling events, [[The Making of the Eclessians]], and then regional dossiers.

## Fixed Historical Chain

The Fall of Caleran (905) creates the eastern emergency; the Stand at Crown Gate (906-908) stops the advance at Caldria; a march banner that kindles for the miller's widow Hedwen at Kaltfurt (974) makes Adalmar king, and he unites the eastern kingdoms (975-1019); his Folk Wars (985-994) stop at Wolfsmere, where he drowns the clans' oath-keepers at a recital of their boundaries (993) and the lake rises the next winter (994); the Peace of the Bent Oak (999) is a judgement of the bishops' court against him.

The Sanctum's relic court credits the Kaltfurt sign to Adalmar (1006), but Valerin II will not crown him over Hedwen's living testimony. The Great Split (1015) begins as Octavin III's demand that Veridun lift the hood from its Great Lamp and submit it to examination; Makarios nails it shut, and their western Christian inheritance is institutionally separate from Thalmyria from then on. Octavin crowns Adalmar Emperor of the Atherians at the ford of Kaltfurt (1017), the only emperor the east ever crowns; Wolmar and every later heir are recorded silent, and the Crown of Embers lies unworn. At Drossfeld (1041) the lot drawn to choose a claimant for examination comes up blank, and each lord keeps what he holds; the smudged tablet of the middle march starts its border disputes. The Choosing (1061) binds Virelia to the Sanctum: Grass-Tarkhan riders burn Radomir I's hall at Strazgorod (1059), his fire-keeper Zdena carries the dead hearth-lamp to Veridun to be relit from the Ember, it lights itself in her hands, and Veridun keeps it. Vesari hearth-law makes whoever keeps a house's fire its lord, so Radomir takes baptism, Eclessian swords and a fire from the Sanctum's own altar instead, and in 1064 the kingless lords of the eastern march choose him king in a moot of sabres. There is no dynastic marriage. There is no embassy and no test of faiths. Ermbold the Idle, king of Aurelia, refuses to carry the unworn crown to its examination in the Two Winters and sends it to the Primarch in a grain cart (1102); he is not deposed, and the Crown of Embers enters Church trust (1104).

[[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/The Grey Sweat.md|The Grey Sweat]] (1232-1247) kills priests faster than parishioners and transforms labor, law, and religious confidence; the Schism of the Twin Cardinals (1240–1242) leaves the Ferry Rule's dead in doubt; the Chancery of Sendings (1252) sells sealed rites for the dead, and under Alessior II (1274–1289), whose Bull of the Sufficient Seal removes every limit on sales and whose alms give letters away, unsung letters become dead paper; [[Freeflame Reformation]] (1290 onward) begins when Ansgar, a Sending House chaplain, drowns with forty-one miners and his margins are read at their funeral; the voided book-test at Aldersbruck (1299) leaves the Unburnt Psalter and the second fee; the Ashenmen swear in the Weiss (1306) and fight out of the mines; the Council of Veridance (1319–1324), with Ferro as its witness, repudiates the Seal, orders the Great Arrears (1320) and charters the Lantern from the night-singers of Saint-Leon (1324); its levy turns Duke Rainald, whose Burning of the Seals (1329) ends the Risings and opens the princes' war; and [[The Korrheim Accord]] (1343), built on the Falkendorf burial articles, forced by Ironvale's Iron Fast and drawn by the Night Count, gives every yard to the rite that kept its dead sent and freezes territorial confession, sworn on the Weiss, without granting individual liberty.

Fixed points on that road: Aimery of Corvel, elected in 1240, dies unsung on his election night, and his twin Aubert buries him in the conclave garden and reigns twenty months in his ring; the Ferry Rule (1241) lets any baptised person who knows the names of the dead sing the funeral where no priest lives, and Gelasin II confirms it for the plague years only. Alessior II is an ascetic Malvora of Sestara who believes his own Seal doctrine and learns after his election (1274) that Bartolo Medori's credit bought it; he dies in debt in 1289, and the Medori take the Chancery's registers as surety. Ansgar is never tried: he drowns in the Blackwater in 1290, the fire is put to his psalter in 1299, and the legate voids the test when it does not burn. The first unbought funeral is read by the tally-keeper Irmel, whose river-burying congregations are the first of the Unsworn. Lucian Ferro writes *The Order of the Open Flame* (c. 1313) on the Sanctum's commission as the first half of a refutation he never delivers, sits as the Council's unvoting witness (1319–1324), refuses Duke Rainald's invitation (1330) and dies in 1339. The Lantern is older than the Reformation; its priest, Father Anselin of Saint-Leon, raised among the night-singers of the Aurelian quarter burned in 1285, dies untried in a Malverine prison-house in 1315. No prince chose his rite at the Accord. The older forms "Alessior VI" and "Ivo the Lamed" are retired, and the priest's name is Anselin, not Ivo, from 15 November 2026.

## Moral and Institutional Rule

The old Church possesses valid sacraments, genuine saints, and institutions capable of mercy. Its assurance regime nevertheless committed real fraud. Freeflame reform names that fraud truthfully and creates literate, disciplined communities; it also enables confiscation, coercive princes, and merciless rigor. Neither diagnosis requires moral equivalence between sanctity, corruption, and the Left Hand.

## Geographic Rule

Eclessia lies east of Thalmyria. Its Western-Christian cultural inheritance is not a compass direction. Within the southern hemisphere, travel south toward Aurelia, the Sylvan Marches, and Grenzburg becomes colder; deep southern woodland grades toward taiga and permafrost.
