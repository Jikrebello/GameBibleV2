# Eclessian Canon Register

This is the creator-facing control sheet for Eclessia. When active notes disagree, authority descends through: [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR)]], [[Gazetteer of the Known World]], the three controlling events, [[The Making of the Eclessians]], and then regional dossiers.

## Fixed Historical Chain

The Fall of Caleran (905) creates the eastern emergency; the Third Crusade (906-908) stops the advance at Caldria; a march banner that kindles for the miller's widow Hedwen at Kaltfurt (974) makes Adalmar king, and he unites the eastern kingdoms (975-1019); his Folk Wars (982-1008) stop at Wolfsmere, where the lake rises the winter after the Drowning (993-994).

The Sanctum's relic court credits the Kaltfurt sign to Adalmar (1001), and Silvane II crowns him Emperor of the Atherians (1002), discharging the trust over the eastern oaths held since 711. The Great Split (1015), begun as Bonifac III's demand to examine Veridun's Great Lamp, makes their western Christian inheritance institutionally separate from Thalmyria. Drossfeld (1049) creates the successor lines by lot; the smudged wax of the third lot starts the middle march's border disputes. The Choosing (1061) binds Virelia to the Sanctum: Grass-Tarkhan riders burn Radomir I's hall at Strazgorod (1059), his fire-keeper Zdena carries the dead hearth-lamp to Veridun to be relit from the Ember, it lights itself in her hands, and Veridun keeps it. Vesari hearth-law makes whoever keeps a house's fire its lord, so Radomir takes baptism, Lotharic's heiress and Eclessian swords from the Sanctum instead. There is no embassy and no test of faiths. Ermbold the Idle, last Adalring emperor, is deposed in the Two Winters (1102), and the Crown of Embers enters Church trust (1104).

[[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/The Grey Sweat.md|The Grey Sweat]] (1232-1247) transforms labor, law, and religious confidence; the Schism of the Twin Cardinals (1240–1242) leaves the Ferry Rule's dead in doubt; the Chancery of Sendings (1252) sells sealed rites for the dead, and under Alessior II (1281–1296), whose Bull of the Sufficient Seal removes every limit on sales, unsung letters become dead paper; [[Freeflame Reformation]] (1297 onward), begun with the Grievances sung over drowned miners at Brenholt and the unbought funerals, breaks the Church monopoly; the voided book-test at Aldersbruck and the flood that takes Ansgar (1305) leave the Unburnt Psalter; the Ashenmen swear in the Weiss (1306), fight out of the mines, and the Risings end with the Burning of the Seals (1329); the Council of Veridance, with Ferro as its witness, repudiates the Seal and orders the Great Arrears (1337) and charters the Lantern from Ivo of Saint-Leon's night-singers (1338); and [[The Korrheim Accord]] (1343), forced by Ironvale's Iron Fast, freezes territorial confession, sworn on the Weiss, without granting individual liberty.

Fixed points on that road: Aimery of Corvel, elected in 1240, dies unsung on his election night, and his twin Aubert buries him in the conclave garden and reigns twenty months in his ring; the Ferry Rule (1241) lets any baptised person who knows the names of the dead sing the funeral where no priest lives, and Gelasin II confirms it for the plague years only. Alessior II is an ascetic Malvora of Sestara who believes his own Seal doctrine and learns after his election (1281) that Bartolo Medori's credit bought it; he dies in debt in 1296, and the Medori take the Chancery's registers as surety. Ansgar is neither burned nor brought to a verdict: the fire is put to his psalter, the legate voids the test when it does not burn, and the spring flood takes him from the toll-tower on the Aldersbruck bridge. Lucian Ferro writes *The Order of the Open Flame* (c. 1316) on the Sanctum's commission as the first half of a refutation he never delivers, refuses Duke Rainald's invitation (1330) and sits as the Council's unvoting witness until his death in 1352. The Lantern's founder is Father Ivo of Saint-Leon, raised among the night-singers of the Aurelian quarter burned in 1285. The older forms "Alessior VI" and "Ivo the Lamed" are retired.

## Moral and Institutional Rule

The old Church possesses valid sacraments, genuine saints, and institutions capable of mercy. Its assurance regime nevertheless committed real fraud. Freeflame reform names that fraud truthfully and creates literate, disciplined communities; it also enables confiscation, coercive princes, and merciless rigor. Neither diagnosis requires moral equivalence between sanctity, corruption, and the Left Hand.

## Geographic Rule

Eclessia lies east of Thalmyria. Its Western-Christian cultural inheritance is not a compass direction. Within the southern hemisphere, travel south toward Aurelia, the Sylvan Marches, and Grenzburg becomes colder; deep southern woodland grades toward taiga and permafrost.
