# Eclessian Canon Register

This is the creator-facing control sheet for Eclessia. When active notes disagree, authority descends through: [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR)]], [[Gazetteer of the Known World]], the three controlling events, [[The Making of the Eclessians]], and then regional dossiers.

## Fixed Historical Chain

The Fall of Caleran (905) creates the eastern emergency; the Third Crusade (906-908) stops the advance at Caldria; a march banner that kindles for the miller's widow Hedwen at Kaltfurt (974) makes Adalmar king, and he unites the eastern kingdoms (975-1019); his Folk Wars (982-1008) stop at Wolfsmere, where the lake rises the winter after the Drowning (993-994).

The Sanctum's relic court credits the Kaltfurt sign to Adalmar (1001), and Silvane II crowns him Emperor of the Atherians (1002), discharging the trust over the eastern oaths held since 711. The Great Split (1015), begun as Bonifac III's demand to examine Veridun's Great Lamp, makes their western Christian inheritance institutionally separate from Thalmyria. Drossfeld (1049) creates the successor lines by lot; the smudged wax of the third lot starts the middle march's border disputes. The Choosing (1061) binds Virelia to the Sanctum after Veridun keeps the envoys' kindled lamp. Ermbold the Idle, last Adalring emperor, is deposed in the Two Winters (1102), and the Crown of Embers enters Church trust (1104).

[[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/The Grey Sweat.md|The Grey Sweat]] (1232-1247) transforms labor, law, and religious confidence; the Chancery of Sendings (1252) sells sealed rites for the dead, and under Alessior II (1281–1296) unsung letters become dead paper; [[Freeflame Reformation]] (1297 onward) breaks the Church monopoly; the Ashenmen swear in the Weiss (1306) and the Risings end with the Burning of the Seals (1329); the Council of Veridance orders the Great Arrears (1337); and [[The Korrheim Accord]] (1343) freezes territorial confession, sworn on the Weiss, without granting individual liberty.

## Moral and Institutional Rule

The old Church possesses valid sacraments, genuine saints, and institutions capable of mercy. Its assurance regime nevertheless committed real fraud. Freeflame reform names that fraud truthfully and creates literate, disciplined communities; it also enables confiscation, coercive princes, and merciless rigor. Neither diagnosis requires moral equivalence between sanctity, corruption, and the Left Hand.

## Geographic Rule

Eclessia lies east of Thalmyria. Its Western-Christian cultural inheritance is not a compass direction. Within the southern hemisphere, travel south toward Aurelia, the Sylvan Marches, and Grenzburg becomes colder; deep southern woodland grades toward taiga and permafrost.
