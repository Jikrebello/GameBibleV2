# Eclessian Church Overview

The Eclessian Church is the western institutional inheritance of the Redeemer's faith: sacramental, juridical, relic-bearing, and governed from the Sanctum Divinitatis in Aurelium. Its priests are celibate, its bishops hold territorial sees, and its Lord Primarch claims the first teaching seat and custody of the Crown of Embers.

The Church is not spiritually empty. Its sacraments work, its saints can be tested, and many of its hospitals, schools, monasteries, and parishes preserve real goods. Its gravest error is the recurring belief that stewardship of grace permits ownership of it.

## Authority and Offices

- **Lord Primarch Alistair III** governs from Aurelium through the Primarchal chancery and the High Council.
- **Bishops** supervise dioceses, ordain priests, judge clerical discipline, and negotiate constantly with territorial rulers after the [[The Korrheim Accord|Korrheim Accord]].
- **Relic courts** test claims of sainthood, apparitions, and sanctified objects. Their modern procedure descends from the Bruckmoor scandal during [[The Grey Sweat]].
- **Parish priests** keep the registers by which Eclessians prove birth, marriage, burial, legitimacy, and title.
- **Monastic houses** maintain worship, hospitals, libraries, estates, and specialist crafts. Their property and exemptions remain central confessional disputes.
- **The Order of the Lantern** trains clergy, investigates false sanctity, conducts missions, and supplies the Counter-Reform's quiet intelligence.

## Doctrine

The Church teaches that the Lord's White Flame restores rather than merely empowers; that Isfir the Redeemer opened a true road through death; that saints may intercede without replacing the Lord; and that relics can bear the impress of genuine sanctity. It rejects the Thalmyric claim that imperial suffering and the Eternal Ember grant Veridun an equal or superior universal authority.

The Church also teaches that ordered office protects the faithful from private revelation, false relics, predatory patrons, and rulers who would make doctrine a household possession. The [[Freeflame Reformation]] attacks the point where this defense became monopoly.

## The Assurance Scandal

Intercession and burial rites can aid a soul. The late assurance offices converted that truth into a saleable guarantee no mortal office could make. Lord Primarch Alessior VI expanded the trade to finance war, debt, and monumental rebuilding. The failure of those promises during the Grey Sweat prepared the public to hear Ansgar's indictment.

The Council of Veridance abolished assurance sales, codified relic testing, rebuilt seminary education, and founded the Lantern. Gray assurance markets survive through euphemism, private chaplaincies, and forged certificates, particularly around Aurelia.

## The Confessional Settlement

Since 1343 AR, the Church no longer governs every Eclessian territory. The Korrheim Accord recognizes old-Church and princely Freeflame settlements while allowing rulers to determine public rite. The Church retains Aurelia, Veridancia, Caldria, Virelia, Brauvalis, the Sylvan Marches, and Grenzburg in law, though actual practice varies sharply.

The Accord grants no individual liberty. Old-Church households in Ironvale, Freeflame households in Aurelia, mixed marriages, wandering preachers, and unaffiliated radicals survive through local toleration, concealment, or exile.

## Present Divisions

- **Primarchal traditionalists** defend broad clerical privilege and the reunification mandate.
- **Veridancian reformers** accept the Council's discipline and seek institutional renewal without abandoning hierarchy.
- **Relic rigorists** fear that reform has weakened necessary defenses against real supernatural fraud.
- **Temporal clerics** use office, land, and law as instruments of family power.
- **Parish clergy** often keep communities alive while their superiors argue over jurisdiction.
