# Mysticism and Heresy

Eclessia distinguishes sanctity, disputed revelation, confessional dissent, political sedition, and supernatural predation. Officials often collapse those categories for convenience; this note does not.

## Recognized Sanctity

The White Flame can genuinely kindle through a living saint, a relic, communal repentance, or sacramental action. The old Church tests such claims through relic courts. Freeflame congregations reject the courts' monopoly but maintain their own examinations of character, doctrine, witnesses, and consequence.

Authentic mystics do not become harmless merely because they are authentic. A true warning can overturn a succession, expose a bishop, or require restitution from an entire town.

## Disputed Mysticism

Hermits, dreamers, Ash-Walker descendants, battlefield visionaries, and Folk converts occupy uncertain ground. The Lantern asks what a revelation demands, what it feeds upon, whether witnesses agree, and whether its effects restore persons or consume them.

The Bruckmoor apparition established the central warning: grief, repetition, and apparent consolation can sustain a predatory power that imitates holiness.

## Recognized Freeflame

The Order of the Open Flame is a rival confession protected where recognized by the Korrheim Accord. Old-Church polemic may call it heresy, but it is not Left Hand worship. Its sacral claims can be mistaken; its followers are not thereby servants of the Adversary.

Radical congregations excluded from the Accord include icon-breakers, commonwealth preachers who deny princely confession, and rigorists who treat every sacrament administered by a corrupt priest as void. Their legal exposure does not determine their metaphysical condition.

## Fraud, Cult, and Predation

False relic sellers, invented saints, forged assurances, and staged visions are mortal frauds unless something answers them. The Weeping Courts cultivate grief; the Hollow Flame turns justified anger toward severance; Undertow networks exploit the unregistered dead; other Left Hand systems feed appetite, shame, debt, or refusal.

These powers are not alternative denominations. They damage agency and route souls toward predatory economies even when their first offer appears fair.

## Investigation and Jurisdiction

The Order of the Lantern investigates doctrine, relics, and supernatural evidence. Bishops retain ordinary jurisdiction in old-Church lands; rulers and Freeflame elders contest it elsewhere. Secular courts judge murder, fraud, trespass, and sedition, though clerical immunity and confessional borders make every boundary negotiable.

Burning a suspect is not a metaphysical test. Coerced confession is unreliable, public panic feeds several hostile powers, and destroying an object before understanding its route can release what it contained.
