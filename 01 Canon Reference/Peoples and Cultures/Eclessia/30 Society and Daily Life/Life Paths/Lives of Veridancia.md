# Lives of Veridancia

The counter-reform boomtown duchy: silk, print, seminaries, and the Council's long afterglow. If Aurelia is the Church's palace, Veridancia is its workshop — the place the old faith goes to *retool*.

## The Farmer

Prosperous by Eclessian standards: mulberry terraces and vineyards over wheat, cash tenancy, market gardens feeding the swollen towns. The Sweat hit lighter here and the recovery built the silk economy on scarce-labor wages. His distinctive fork: the **putting-out loom** — a merchant's silk frame in the cottage doubles income and quietly makes the family answerable to the Silk Duchess's factors instead of the manor. Half the duchy's villages have crossed that line without noticing.

## The Seminarian (the duchy's signature road)

The **Lantern seminaries** are the Counter-Reformation's engine and the poor boy's sharpest ladder: entry by examination and parish sponsorship, formation ferocious (languages, rhetoric, the Bruckmoor file, disputation against live Freeflame texts — the Lantern teaches the enemy's books *accurately*, which scandalizes the old guard and wins the debates). The forks: mission territory (the marches, the Folk edge, Zanthir's study-house — glory and graves) versus the teaching bench versus the quiet third door some take after formation: conviction *by* the texts they were trained to refute. The Lantern loses perhaps one in forty that way and considers the price of honest training worth it. The Freeflame considers those forty-to-one odds their best recruiting pamphlet.

## The Printer and the Merchant

Veridancia and Ironvale run the presses of the east — here, under license: catechisms, council decrees, devotional bestsellers, and the *approved* refutations. The printer's road is prosperous and razor-edged: the same shop that sets the Lantern's Latin can set a banned pamphlet in a night-run, and the margin on contraband is triple. The merchant road runs through the **Silk Duchess's consortium**, the recognized court-facing merchant league: guild wedding to guild, factor postings abroad, and the class's great ambition — a son in the seminaries, a daughter into the lesser nobility, the family arms one generation from respectability.

## The Priest and the Bishop

The best-trained parish clergy in Eclessia (the Council's reforms began here): seminary-formed, register-exact, preaching-competent — the old lazy benefice-priest is extinct in this duchy and merely endangered elsewhere. The bishop's road is the intellectual's: Veridancia's see argues the Kindling Question with Veridun's Hierophants by published exchange, and its holder is traditionally the Church's chief pen. Duke Adrien III finds hosting the Church's arsenal splendid for prestige and exhausting for taxes.

## The Criminal

Book-running is the signature trade: banned Freeflame texts north, banned Vetti south, the *Mirror for Sovereigns* under every third counter. The censors' index is, as everywhere, the underworld's bestseller list. Otherwise: silk-theft rings, license-forgery, and the gray trade in seminary examination places, which the Lantern hunts with special fury because it strikes at their one honest door.

## The Strange-Dose

Moderate and *institutional*: the duchy's wonders are certified, printed, and distributed — a Veridancian meets the strange in an approved woodcut before ever meeting it in a field, which shapes a piety more literate and less raw than the marches'.

## Hooks

A night-run pamphlet traced to the Lantern's own press; a seminarian's crisis mid-mission in Folk country; the Silk Duchess's factor found with an Under-Ledger second book.
