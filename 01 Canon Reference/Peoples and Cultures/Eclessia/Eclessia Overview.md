# Eclessia Overview

Eclessia is the eastern, church-bound inheritance of Atheria: a family of kingdoms, duchies, free cities, and frontier marches sharing one religious ancestry while disputing who may speak for it. Its institutional character is expressed through cathedral authority, parish life, relic courts, reform movements, feudal law, and competing crowns.

## Cultural Thesis

Eclessia answers disorder through visible institutions: parish registers, relic courts, councils, universities, monasteries, guild law, knightly orders, and public doctrine. These institutions preserve real goods and real sanctity. They can also mistake custody of truth for ownership of it.

See [[Eclessia Cultural Thesis]] and [[The Making of the Eclessians]].

## Present Crisis

The [[Freeflame Reformation]] has broken the old Church's monopoly without ending the faith it challenged. The [[The Korrheim Accord]] freezes confession by territory, leaving mixed families, border parishes, reformist princes, and the Sanctum to live inside its silences. Behind both stands the older schism with Thalmyria and the still-unfulfilled reunification mandate.

[[The Grey Sweat]] also remains politically alive: its labor shortage weakened serfdom in much of Eclessia while Virelia's sabre-right nobility bound survivors more tightly to the land.

Current rulers and political alignments are indexed in [[Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR)]].

## Regions

- [[The Kingdom of Aurelia Overview]]: the Primarchal crown, relic courts, pilgrimage economy, and institutional old Church.
- [[The Duchy of Veridancia Overview]]: universities, vineyards, seminaries, printers, and reform argument.
- [[The Duchy of Caldria Overview]]: knightly orders, war industry, and the militarized frontier faith.
- [[The Kingdom of Virelia Overview]]: grain estates, elective sabre-right nobility, eastern folk piety, and bound labor.
- [[The Ironvale Dominion Overview]]: foundries, guild government, black powder, and Freeflame power.
- [[The Confederation of Korrheim Overview]]: canton politics, hired companies, engineering, and the Accord's namesake.
- [[The Duchy of Brauvalis Overview]]: brewing towns, monastic crafts, and confessional compromise.
- [[The Sylvan Marches Overview]]: the southern frontier where parishes thin into Folk land and the cold deepens.

Eclessian stories naturally arise from contested legitimacy, confessional borders, relic evidence, noble debt, guild autonomy, peasant flight, reform preaching, inquisitorial jurisdiction, and the gap between an institution's holy purpose and the people who wield it.
