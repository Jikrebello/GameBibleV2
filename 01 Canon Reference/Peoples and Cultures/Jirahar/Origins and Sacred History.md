The Jirahar are a semi-nomadic people who claim **direct descent from the Great Prophet**, the divine leader who led humanity out of the **Great Glass Desert** at the beginning of days. They believe they are the only people who have remained faithful to the original laws and traditions of the **Path of the Chosen** (**Ra'adat al-Mukhtarin**).

### **The Age of Bondage: Humanity Under the Giants**

According to Jiraharin sacred history, humanity was once united under divine guidance, following the **Path of the One Above All**. However, in the time before the Great Prophet, humanity strayed from the Path, falling into the hands of the **Giants**, also known as **Mamalik al-Jababira**—great and terrible beings who enslaved mankind through brute strength, deception, and sorcery. The Giants ruled with absolute authority, dividing the world into vast domains where they forced the lesser peoples to labor and offer tribute.

Each Giant kingdom imposed a different form of oppression:

- **The Iron Lords of Qanglun** – Who ruled through war and militarization, drafting humans into their endless campaigns of conquest.
- **The Serpent Kings of Ishkar** – Who used wealth and deception, binding people into economic and magical servitude through unbreakable contracts.
- **The Obsidian Giants of Xulat** – Who demanded blood sacrifices, consuming the life force of their enslaved subjects.
- **The Machine Lords of Nythalum** – Who sought to transcend flesh and forced humanity into servitude to their ever-growing mechanical dominion.

For centuries, the Jirahar’s ancestors, along with the rest of humanity, toiled under these oppressors, their identities and faiths slowly eroded. Many tribes and nations succumbed entirely, **forgetting the Path** and accepting the rule of the Giants as the natural order. However, a small group of the faithful clung to the old ways, preserving fragments of their sacred knowledge in hidden places. These remnants would one day become the Jirahar.

### **The Coming of the Great Prophet: Al-Mukhtar al-Khalas**

It was in this time of despair that the **Great Prophet**, known in the oldest Jiraharin texts as **Al-Mukhtar al-Khalas** (_The Chosen One of Salvation_), was born. Raised in secret among the last faithful, he was said to have received **divine revelation from the One Above All**, who commanded him to lead his people out of bondage and restore the Path.

Legends say that the Prophet traveled across the lands of the Giants, confronting each of the oppressors in turn. Through divine miracles and great trials, he broke their power:

- In **Qanglun**, he called down the mountains upon the fortresses of the warlords, toppling their strongholds and setting their enslaved armies free.
- In **Ishkar**, he turned gold to sand and cast famine upon the serpent kings, breaking the chains of their debt-based rule.
- In **Xulat**, he caused the great obsidian altars to shatter, and the blood-drinkers perished as their magic was undone.
- In **Nythalum**, he shattered the spires of the Machine Lords, cutting them off from their celestial engines and bringing their rule to ruin.

As he moved from land to land, more and more people followed him, joining his great **Exodus**. These were the first true **Children of the Path**.

### **The Crossing of the Great Glass Desert**

After breaking the rule of the Giants, the Prophet led his followers into the **Great Glass Desert**, a vast and uncharted wasteland of shimmering, crystallized sand. The desert was both a physical trial and a spiritual purification; only the truly faithful could survive the passage. Here, the Prophet received his final revelations and completed the **Scrolls of the Path**, inscribing the divine laws that would govern the Jirahar forevermore.

Throughout the journey, the Jirahar faced tests of faith:

- **Trials of Thirst** – Those who doubted the Path saw only barren wasteland and perished, while the faithful found wells where none should exist.
- **The Storm of the Unbelievers** – A great tempest rose, separating those who truly believed from those whose faith wavered.
- **The Calling of the Stars** – The Prophet taught his people how to navigate by the heavens, showing them that even in the darkest nights, the divine presence remained with them.

Eventually, they reached the far side of the desert, settling in a land unknown to those who had remained behind. Here, they established the first **Tower Towns**, places of refuge where the **Law of the Path** would be kept sacred and untainted.

### **The Death of the Prophet and the Scattering of the Peoples**

Before reaching the final destination, the Prophet received a divine command: **the Jirahar were to build a great city, a sanctuary of faith where they would dwell for three generations before spreading the Path once more.** However, fearing that stagnation would lead to corruption, the Prophet disobeyed, urging his people to continue their journey.

For his disobedience, a great **storm of wrath** descended upon the land. The Prophet was taken up by the storm, and his body was never found. This event marked the end of the Exodus and the beginning of the Jirahar’s true journey—a people forever traveling, never settling, always keeping the faith alive.

In the generations that followed, most of those who had been freed **assimilated into foreign lands**, adopting the ways of outsiders and losing their connection to the Path. Only a small remnant, the Jirahar, remained faithful, preserving the laws and customs given to them in the desert.

### **The Eternal Journey and the Awaited One**

The Jirahar believe that their journey will not end until the coming of **Al-Muntazar**, the **Awaited One**, the final Great Prophet who will **unite all Jirahar, restore the pure law, and lead them to dominion over the world**. Until that time, they must remain vigilant, ensuring that their customs, language, and traditions remain unchanged, resisting the influences of the foreign faiths that seek to corrupt them.

This belief in their **divine destiny** has fueled both their **resistance to assimilation** and their **conflicts with the great religious and political powers of the world**, particularly the **Church of the Redeemer** and the **Ahl-e-Ahd**. They see themselves as **a people in exile, waiting for the day when they will reclaim their rightful place as the masters of the world.**

Thus, the Jirahar continue their sacred journey, ever-moving, ever-faithful, bound to the laws and prophecies that have guided them since the days of the Great Prophet.
