The Jirahar religion, known as **Ra'adat al-Mukhtarin** or **Al-Sabil** (_The Path of the Chosen_), is a **deeply legalistic, ritualistic, and hierarchical** system of faith, centered on the **preservation of divine law** as revealed by the **Great Prophet**. It dictates every aspect of Jiraharin life, from **daily customs and purity laws** to **trade, marriage, and warfare**.

The Jirahar believe that they alone have **kept the true faith**, while the rest of the world has **strayed into corruption and heresy**. The **Scrolls of the Path**, their sacred scriptures, contain the full **divinely ordained laws**, and breaking from these is considered a **grievous sin**. Their faith is maintained through a vast system of **oral law, legal interpretation, and judicial debate**, ensuring that the Path remains intact across generations and changing circumstances.

---

### **Sacred Texts and Laws**

At the core of Jiraharin faith are the **Scrolls of the Path**, a collection of **holy writings, legal decrees, historical records, and prophecies** that form the foundation of their belief system. These scrolls are seen as **immutable divine laws**, set down by the Great Prophet and preserved by the Keepers of the Scrolls.

#### **The Three Foundational Scrolls**

1. **The Scroll of Laws** (_Sahifat al-Ahkam_)
    
    - The **most authoritative** of all Jiraharin texts, dictating the **laws of purity, trade, contracts, family life, diet, prayer, and justice**.
    - Includes detailed **ritualistic laws**, down to the proper way to prepare food, conduct trade, and engage in legal disputes.
    - Every Jiraharin action, from **greetings to business deals**, must adhere to **codified ritual practices**.
2. **The Scroll of Journeys** (_Sahifat al-Rihlat_)
    
    - The **historical and mythological account** of the Great Prophet's **exodus** through the **Great Glass Desert** and the trials that shaped the Jirahar.
    - Contains moral lessons and **parables** drawn from the Prophet's struggles against the **Giants, false gods, and corrupt peoples**.
    - Used as a **mystical roadmap**, symbolizing the spiritual journey that all Jirahar must walk in their own lives.
3. **The Scroll of Prophecies** (_Sahifat al-Nubuwwat_)
    
    - Foretells **the final days of exile**, when **Al-Muntazar**, the Awaited One, will arrive to restore **Jiraharin dominion** over the world.
    - Warns of **corrupt priests, false rulers, and treacherous outsiders** who will seek to deceive the Jirahar before the final war of purification.
    - Contains **visions and cryptic signs** that scholars debate endlessly, trying to decipher **when and where Al-Muntazar will arise**.

#### **Additional Texts and Commentaries**

- **The Lesser Path** (_Al-Sabil al-Saghir_) – A collection of **customary laws and social norms**, governing etiquette, conduct, and trade practices.
- **The Scroll of Judgments** (_Sahifat al-Qada'_) – Records of past **legal rulings** by the Keepers of the Scrolls, forming a **precedent-based legal system**.
- **The Hidden Words** (_Al-Kalimat al-Khafiya_) – A **mystical and esoteric text**, studied only by the most learned, containing secret interpretations of divine law.

---

### **Oral Tradition and Legal Debate**

The Jiraharin faith is not just a **set of static laws** but a **living legal tradition** that adapts to new situations through **interpretation, debate, and precedent**. This tradition is preserved in the **Ta'arifat al-Tariq** (_Interpretations of the Path_), a vast **oral and written commentary** that **expands upon the sacred laws** while maintaining their divine essence.

At the heart of Jiraharin **legalism** is **Jahd al-Kalima** (_The Struggle of the Word_), an **intellectual and spiritual battle** waged by **scholars, judges, and merchants** to determine **how the law applies to contemporary life**.

#### **Major Components of Legal Interpretation**

1. **Hikayat** (_Narratives_) – Stories and parables about the **Great Prophet and early Jirahar**, teaching moral lessons.
2. **Masail** (_Legal Cases_) – Complex **hypothetical and real cases**, where scholars debate how to apply religious law to new situations.
3. **Tafsir al-Sabil** (_Commentaries on the Path_) – **Legal interpretations and debates**, determining how different rulings should be followed.
4. **Amthal** (_Proverbs and Ethical Teachings_) – **Wisdom texts**, shaping the ethical and moral outlook of the Jirahar.

#### **The Keepers of the Scrolls: Guardians of Religious Law**

The **Keepers of the Scrolls** are the **supreme religious authorities** of the Jirahar, responsible for:\n- Preserving **ritual law and historical traditions**.

- **Overseeing legal cases** and issuing **fatwas** (binding rulings).
- Teaching the **next generation** of scholars and judges.
- Studying **the Prophecy of Al-Muntazar**, seeking signs of his arrival.

---

### **The Prophecy of Al-Muntazar**

At the heart of Jiraharin faith is the **belief in Al-Muntazar**, the **final prophet and ruler**, who will **lead the Jirahar to their ultimate destiny**.

#### **The Role of Al-Muntazar**

- **A warrior-prophet**, who will **conquer the world and establish divine law**.
- **A legal scholar**, restoring the pure, original **Scrolls of the Path**.
- **A purifier**, who will **destroy the false faiths** that have led humanity astray.

#### **Signs of His Coming**

The **Scroll of Prophecies** describes cryptic **omens** that will precede Al-Muntazar’s arrival, including:

- **The Collapse of False Thrones** – The Church of the Redeemer and Ahl-e-Ahd will **fracture and weaken**.
- **The Gathering of the Scattered** – Jiraharin exiles will **return from all corners of the world**.
- **The Blackened Sun** – A **celestial event** will signal the **beginning of the final war**.

#### **The Final War and the Age of the Chosen**

Once Al-Muntazar arises, the Jirahar believe he will:\n- **Unite all Jirahar under one law.**

- **Lead them into battle against the false faiths.**
- **Establish a divine empire**, where the **Law of the Path** governs all nations.

Until that day, the Jirahar remain in exile, preserving their **purity, faith, and legal traditions**, waiting for the moment when they will reclaim their divine inheritance.

---

### **Conclusion**

The Jiraharin faith is **an intricate blend of law, prophecy, and ritual**, sustaining their unique identity and **resistance to assimilation**. Their system of **oral law, religious courts, and scholarly debate** mirrors traditions like the **Talmud**, ensuring that their faith remains both **unchanging and adaptable**.

Above all, their belief in **Al-Muntazar** fuels their **hope, defiance, and ultimate destiny**—a future where they **rise above all other peoples, reclaiming their place as the Chosen of the One Above All**.
