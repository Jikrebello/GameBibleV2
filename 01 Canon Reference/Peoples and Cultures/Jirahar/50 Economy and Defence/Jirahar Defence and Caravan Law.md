# Jirahar Defence and Caravan Law

Jirahar communities defend themselves through tower watches, travelling companies, hired escorts and negotiated protection from host powers. The diaspora maintains no common army, and military authority expands only for a particular journey or emergency. Caravan law therefore defines who commands during danger, what may be requisitioned and how officers must answer for abandoned people, property and violence once safety has been restored.

## Convoy Law

A travelling company appoints a route captain, rear warden, animal master, quartermaster, and record keeper. Authority expands during immediate danger and contracts again in camp. Captains must account for abandoned cargo, lost people, forced requisition, and violence after the crisis.

## Tower Defence

Visible towers control narrow approaches while deep settlements provide water, stores, fallback chambers, and concealed exits. Defenders rely on gates, collapsible passages, disciplined watches, missile positions, and knowledge of the Underways. Ancient devices are rare and never the ordinary basis of survival.

## Fleet Defence

Sea Fleets travel in mutual-aid groups, share storm signals, and maintain boarding watches. Some accept lawful letters of escort or reprisal from host states; others refuse any commission that would turn a family fleet into a foreign navy.

## Arms

Weapons vary by region: bows, crossbows, spears, sabres, ship weapons, hunting arms, and purchased powder weapons where available. Warlock support is hired or trained locally rather than an ethnic specialty.

## Sanctuary and Retreat

Companies prepare caches, friendly yards, hidden slips, mountain passes, and host households. Way-Gates are a last strategic option because opening reveals location, requires two benches, and can transfer danger to the receiving tower.

## Moral Boundary

Protecting a caravan does not authorize robbery of surrounding communities. Collective punishment, hostage-taking, predatory lending under arms, and pre-emptive killing remain crimes even when justified as survival.
