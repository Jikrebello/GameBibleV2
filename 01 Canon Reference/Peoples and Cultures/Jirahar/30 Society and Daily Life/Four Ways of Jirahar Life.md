# Four Ways of Jirahar Life

The Four Ways are recognized forms of communal life, not hereditary castes. A family may change Ways through apprenticeship, marriage, confiscation, opportunity, disaster, or calling.

## Tower Houses

Tower Houses live in the great citadels or tower-affiliated quarters. They maintain courts, schools, archives, cisterns, workshops, burial halls, and gates. Tower life offers security and depth while creating hierarchy, crowding, and dependence on charters and imported food.

## Road Companies

Road Companies travel with wagons, pack animals, tents, portable shrines, workshops, classrooms, and guarded record chests. They trade animals and goods, repair metal and wheels, perform, carry messages, practice medicine, take seasonal work, and connect communities beyond gate anchors.

## Sea Fleets

Sea Fleets are permanent shipboard communities rather than tower merchants temporarily aboard. Family vessels travel in mutual-aid flotillas, carrying cargo, pilgrims, fishers, navigators, artisans, and shipboard courts. Migdal Yamma is their principal winter anchor, not their capital.

## Turning Companies

Turning Companies build wagon bodies, cargo boxes, and household fittings that can be loaded onto prepared barges. They travel roads between watersheds, refit at river yards, and continue by water. Their engineers know slips, ferries, flood calendars, towpaths, and the legal seams between inland jurisdictions.

## Great Treks

A Great Trek occurs when whole communities move together because a charter fails, famine closes a region, prophecy draws pilgrims, or persecution makes ordinary departure impossible. Treks combine all Four Ways and may use a Way-Gate if two Keeper courts accept the burden.

## Transfer Between Ways

No person loses descent by changing Way. Apprenticeship and marriage are the usual transfers. Tower prejudice against road families and road suspicion of tower privilege remain real social tensions without creating separate peoples.
