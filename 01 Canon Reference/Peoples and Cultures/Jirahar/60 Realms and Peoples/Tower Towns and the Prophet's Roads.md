# Tower Towns and the Prophet's Roads

The Jirahar hold no continuous state. Their geography consists of **verticals and vectors**: five living tower-citadels, the Empty City, host quarters, physical routes, and the threshold corridors between fixed anchors.

## Deep Cities

A great tower's visible masonry is a gatehouse, watchpoint, public court, and declaration of charter. The inhabited city descends beneath it. Generations excavate dwelling halls, cisterns, workshops, schools, stables, kitchens, assembly chambers, burial galleries, refuges, scroll vaults, and hidden exits.

The deepest secure chamber contains the Way-Gate. Most underground works are ordinary engineering maintained through constant labor. Functioning ancient mechanisms and supernatural chambers are exceptional.

## The Five Living Towers

- [[Migdal Qesheth]]: sacred precedence, High Keeper, desert margin near Zanthir.
- [[Migdal Dahava]]: Velian finance, arbitration, and archive clearing.
- [[Migdal Yamma]]: Thalassian harbor, Sea Fleet wintering, and ship law.
- [[Migdal Melach]]: Iqashar road, Tarkhan peace-price, and caravan exchange.
- [[Migdal Talga]]: cold Eclessian march, frontier refuge, and the thinnest charter.

## The Empty City

[[Qiryath Reqa]] contains the ruins of the First and Second Qiryath. Its perpetual charter permits pilgrimage and preservation but forbids rebuilding before the Mesakha. Its silent gate remains the missing anchor.

## Roads and Underways

[[Prophet's Roads and the Underways]] distinguishes three systems:

- physical caravan, river, and sea routes;
- local engineered tunnels, concealed passes, caches, and safehouses;
- Way-Gates following Exodus threshold corridors.

## No Common Capital

Qesheth is first in sacred dignity, not a sovereign capital. Each tower maintains its own council, charter, court, stores, and gate bench. Joint action depends on negotiated consent.
