# Migdal Yamma

**Migdal Yamma**, the Sea Tower, rises above a protected inlet on western Kedrava's outer coast near Halmenth. It is the principal winter harbor of the Sea Fleets and their nearest fixed anchor to the Ättar sea-road.

## Above and Below

The visible lighthouse tower, sea wall, cranes, and courts occupy little land. Rock-cut docks, tide chambers, warehouses, rope walks, workshops, housing, freshwater cisterns, schools, and burial galleries descend behind the cliffs. Some lower halls can admit shallow vessels at high water.

## Government

Fleet delegates hold more seats than old land households. Harbor masters, shipwrights, Keepers, and resident families bargain with a foreign-charter bench. Captains command vessels, not the tower.

## Economy

Ship repair, navigation, fishing, convoy brokerage, maritime insurance, salt goods, pilgrimage, charts, translation, and seasonal harbor work sustain Yamma. Sermoran merchants are partners and competitors.

## Sacred Role

Yamma preserves the names of those lost without burial. Its mourning court faces open water and records testimony from every surviving vessel.

## Gate

The gate lies above the tide chambers. It cannot connect to ships at sea. Fleet households use it mainly when storms, war, or persecution make a harbor evacuation unavoidable.

## Present Conflict

Halmenth wants naval inspection rights in the lower docks, while fleet captains fear that one foreign search will become permanent control.

## Story Uses

- a lost fleet's names arrive in a sealed gate wagon without survivors;
- a Sermoran privateer claims sanctuary with stolen Jirahar passengers aboard;
- dredging exposes a pre-Atherian chamber beneath the tidal works.
