# Migdal Dahava

**Migdal Dahava**, the Gold Tower, occupies a fortified river hill in the Velian belt. Its name refers as much to trusted measure and recoverable record as to bullion.

## Above and Below

A gilded copper roof and compact court complex mark the surface. Beneath them spread counting halls, family vaults, workshops, dormitories, kitchens, arbitration rooms, cisterns, burial galleries, and fire-separated archives. The city is designed to keep records and people alive through siege, not to display treasure.

## Government

Household delegates share authority with advocate benches, craft guilds, and Keepers. Clearing houses are influential but do not govern alone. Velian officials maintain a charter court at the outer gate.

## Economy

Dahava clears letters, stores deposits, finances caravans, authenticates instruments across legal systems, trains translators, and supports bookbinders, assayers, metalworkers, and river merchants. It competes with the Medori while corresponding with them.

## Sacred Role

Dahava preserves deportation and ransom registers. Its annual remembrance names people recovered from slavery, debt seizure, and lost companies.

## Gate

The gate specializes in emergency redistribution of households and portable reserves. Its bench refuses speculative cargo openings, a policy merchants constantly challenge.

## Present Conflict

Two Velian princes claim authority to confirm the tower's charter, while a Medori-backed road project could make Dahava richer and strategically dependent.
