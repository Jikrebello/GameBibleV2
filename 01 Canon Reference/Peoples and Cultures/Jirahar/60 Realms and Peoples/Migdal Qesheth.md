# Migdal Qesheth

**Migdal Qesheth**, the Tower of the Bow, stands on the dry margin near Zanthir under an old emirial charter. It is first in sacred dignity and the seat of High Keeper Zerahiah.

## Above and Below

Three narrow towers and a walled caravan court rise above ground. Below lies a much larger city of cool dwelling galleries, water chambers, courts, schools, kitchens, archives, guest halls, stables, and layered refuges. The deepest bow-shaped hall protects the master gate bench and the largest surviving collection of common rulings.

## Government

A council of old households, craft delegates, quarter representatives, and senior Keepers governs. Zerahiah presides over sacred and transregional questions but cannot tax or command the city alone.

## Economy

Qesheth supports pilgrims, legal education, manuscript authentication, desert transport, water engineering, textiles, metal repair, and Zanthiri trade. Its prestige attracts gifts and litigants while making ordinary housing expensive.

## Sacred Role

The bow signifies a people bent by exile but not broken. Qesheth coordinates the Keeper circuit and scrutiny of Mesakha claimants. It does not replace Qiryath.

## Gate

Qesheth holds the best documented active gate and acts as the principal routing court. Other towers resent any suggestion that this makes it owner of the Roads.

## Present Conflict

Zanthiri officials want emergency access written into the next charter renewal. The gate bench insists that host protection purchases no right of passage.
