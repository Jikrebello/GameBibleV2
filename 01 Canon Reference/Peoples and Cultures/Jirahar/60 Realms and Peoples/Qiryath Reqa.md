# Qiryath Reqa

**Qiryath Reqa**, the Empty City, contains the remains of both sacred tower cities. It stands in Eclessian territory under a perpetual charter that permits Jirahar custody and pilgrimage while forbidding reconstruction before the Mesakha.

## Archaeological Layers

- early caravan court and First Tower foundations;
- Arqeshite siege cuts, destruction fill, and deportation records;
- Second Tower courts, expanded sanctuary, cisterns, schools, and dwellings;
- 70 AR siege works, burn layers, collapsed galleries, and imperial occupation;
- diminished post-destruction settlement;
- Vestian clearance, sealed entrances, and later pilgrim paths.

The city was always larger below ground than above. Most deep halls are collapsed, flooded, sealed, or legally forbidden. Excavation is both archaeology and sacrilege.

## The Sanctuary

Sacrifice continued from the Second Tower's consecration until 70 AR. After Isfir's resurrection, ritual fire and procedure continued but the authenticated sign of divine acceptance never returned.

## Custody

Jirahar custodians maintain paths, stabilize masonry, receive pilgrims, bury discovered remains, and prevent removal. Eclessian officers guard the outer boundary and repeatedly dispute what counts as preservation rather than rebuilding.

## Silent Gate

The gate chamber lies beneath the Second Tower ruin. It has not opened since 70 AR. The Keepers prohibit experimental activation. A true response would be treated as possible Mesakha evidence and an international emergency.

## Present Conflict

Pilgrim numbers are rising after reports of warmth beneath the gate stair. Eclessian clergy want an investigation; Jirahar custodians suspect staged prophecy or an attempt to void the charter.
