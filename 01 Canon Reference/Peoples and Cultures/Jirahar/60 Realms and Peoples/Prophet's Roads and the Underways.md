# Prophet's Roads and the Underways

## Physical Roads

Most Jirahar movement is ordinary and difficult. Road Companies use passes, ferries, caravan tracks, seasonal pasture, market roads, and negotiated camps. Sea Fleets follow currents and harbor law. Turning Companies connect watersheds by road and prepared river yards.

## Underways

Underways are engineered local networks: tower tunnels, drains, mine passages, cliff stairs, concealed slips, border culverts, safe cellars, caches, and linked host houses. Some run miles beneath a ridge or city. None forms a continuous tunnel across the known world.

Route knowledge is divided so capture of one guide does not expose a whole chain. Many passages belong partly to non-Jirahar allies whose consent matters.

## Way-Gates

The Exodus entry and long [[The Glass Sojourn|Glass Sojourn]] impressed stable threshold corridors into the world. Great towers stand at surviving anchor camps, some of which were occupied for years or generations. Their gates follow those routes without paying Astral rent or borrowing a patron's stability.

A gate can transfer a complete caravan, including wagons, animals, households, and cargo, from one intact anchor to another. It cannot open to an arbitrary road, moving ship, market, battlefield, or settlement without an anchor.

## Opening Procedure

- origin and destination gate benches both consent;
- matched scroll-keys identify route and opening;
- trained guides order people, animals, and wagons;
- both chambers are cleared, warded, and provisioned;
- outsiders enter only by Keeper discretion;
- the transfer is recorded in sealed registers.

Preparation takes hours or days. Political refusal, damaged keys, a compromised destination, or suspected imitation closes the route. Whole-caravan capacity makes gates strategically powerful; institutional control and fixed geography make them unsuitable for ordinary trade.

## Failure

A forced opening may return travellers to origin, scatter impressions and cargo within the chamber, admit Astral contamination, or connect to a staged patron corridor. No lawful gate bench opens during an unresolved breach.

## Qiryath

Qiryath's gate has not opened since the Second Destruction. Keepers call it silent, not dead. Any authentic response would trigger a religious and geopolitical crisis.
