# Migdal Melach

**Migdal Melach**, the Salt Tower, controls no kingdom but sits where the Iqashar road meets Tarkhan passage country. Its survival depends on an old flat peace-price associated with Qarayel's word-law.

## Above and Below

A squat white tower, animal yards, wells, and market court stand above extensive cool stables, fodder chambers, salt stores, workshops, hostels, cisterns, schools, and refuges. Broad ramps accommodate wagons and large herds.

## Government

Road Company delegates, animal masters, well engineers, Keepers, resident households, and charter advocates share the council. Tarkhan and Covenant representatives attend the outer market court without seats in internal law.

## Economy

Melach trades animals, salt, leather, tack, medicines, metal repair, caravan provisions, maps, guides, and remittance. It mediates passage-price disputes and ransoms.

## Sacred Role

The tower preserves hospitality rulings from the Scattering. Breaking guest-right inside its charter is treated as an attack on every company using the road.

## Gate

Melach's chamber has the widest caravan approach. Its gate bench is nevertheless cautious: a foreign army disguised as a caravan would make every tower complicit.

## Present Conflict

Qahirid governors demand manifests while rival Tarkhan claimants dispute who may collect the old price. Enforcing either claim could break the other peace.
