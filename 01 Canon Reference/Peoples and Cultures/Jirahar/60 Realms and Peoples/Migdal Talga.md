# Migdal Talga

**Migdal Talga**, the Snow Tower, is the southernmost great anchor in the cold Eclessian marches nearest Grenzburg. Its charter is the youngest and thinnest.

## Above and Below

A single dark tower, snow court, barns, and fortified road gate cover a deep warm settlement cut around geothermal cracks and insulated cisterns. Workshops, winter dormitories, classrooms, granaries, stables, burial galleries, and refuge halls can absorb several displaced companies.

## Government

Resident households share the council with Road Company winter delegates, engineers, Keepers, and charter advocates. Marcher officials maintain a customs office outside the sacred gate.

## Economy

Talga supports cold-road transport, animal care, timber and fur exchange, metal repair, medicine, copying, frontier guides, winter refuge, and the great scroll collection called the Talga Folios.

## Sacred Role

The tower remembers communities expelled into winter. Its hospitality law is unusually strict: no traveller may be turned into lethal cold while space and food remain.

## Gate

Talga's gate remains reliable when surface passes close, making it a strategic temptation to every marcher ruler. Opening for a whole refugee caravan can consume a season's reserves.

## Present Conflict

The current charter protects internal law but gives the host crown an ambiguous emergency clause. Both sides know a winter siege will test it.

## Story Uses

- a Folio mystery exposes a forged charter clause;
- a stranded army asks to be admitted as refugees;
- something has begun walking the geothermal Underways from outside the mapped stone.
