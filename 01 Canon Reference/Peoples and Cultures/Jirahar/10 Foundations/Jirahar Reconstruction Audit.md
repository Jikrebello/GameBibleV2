# Jirahar Reconstruction Audit

## Scope

The pre-reconstruction search found **96 active notes** using the established Jirahar vocabulary. After adding the historical, legal, social, and tower authorities, the post-pass register contains **114 matching active notes**, excluding this audit.

The following path rules and lists partition every result into one disposition.

## Rewritten or New Authority: 35

Every matching note beneath `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/` was rewritten, created, or checked against the reconstructed canon. This includes the overview, foundations, faith, law, Four Ways, economy, government, defence, five living towers, Qiryath, Roads, Underways, and historical events.

The Arqeshite Empire authority and the three controlling Redeemer-era notes were also rewritten or created:

- `The Arqeshite River Empire`
- `The Age of the Redeemer`
- `The Rise of the Redeemer`
- `The Conversion of the Empire`

## Terminology or Authority Updated: 34

- `00 Start Here`: Canon Index, Current Focus Board, Needs Work, and World Egg Setting Primer.
- `01 Canon Reference/Geography`: Gazetteer, Geography Atlas, and Political Map Border Proof Key.
- `01 Canon Reference/History`: AR Chronology, world historical spine, named figures, Real-History Concordance, Age of City-States, Ancient and Early Historic Ages, Proto-Jirahar Tower Towns, Timeline MOC, Present-Day Courts and Crowns, and Political Board.
- `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures`: Cultures MOC, Ethnic Groups Overview, the Ahl-e-Ahd overview and three regional/faith references, Eclessian formation and overview, Thalmyric overview, Sestaran wider-world dossier, Folk overview, and Ättar overview.
- `02 Playable Settings`: two Caleran Caravan Ward references, the Risen from the Glass conversion and full adventure, and The Talga Folios.

## Preserved as In-World Testimony: 7

All matching notes beneath `03 Stories & In-World Texts/` retain their voice, limitation, or bias. The Thalmyric and Sestaran accounts may repeat collective guilt, compressed destruction dates, or the false claim that Jirahar exiles founded the Covenant peoples. Those statements are source perspective, not authorial truth, and are now called out in [[Reliability Register]].

## Intentionally Unchanged: 38

Every remaining active match outside the rules above was reviewed and retained because its use is compatible, incidental, metadata-only, or already subordinate to a reconstructed authority. This category does not grant those notes authority over Jirahar canon.

## Authority Ruling

Authority descends through [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]], [[Historical Spine of the Jirahar]], [[Jirahar Canon Register]], [[The Jirahar Overview]], and the specialist law, tower, and social authorities. Adventures and in-world sources yield when they conflict unless the disagreement is explicitly preserved as testimony.
