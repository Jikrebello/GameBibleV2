## Locked History

- c. 742 BR: the Arqeshite River Empire destroys the First Qiryath and deports its court, priesthood, and skilled households.
- 690 BR: Arqesh falls to internal revolt and the separate commands of Arezan Tiravan and Eryndor III.
- 684 BR: the Decree of Roads and Houses permits selected return under Vasharan supremacy.
- c. 660 BR: the Second Qiryath is consecrated during the Wars of the Inheritance.
- 0 AR: Isfir rises; sacrificial rites continue but the authenticated sign of acceptance never returns.
- 66-73 AR: the Great Tower Revolt.
- 70 AR: Atheria destroys the Second Qiryath.
- 73 AR: the last rebel fortress falls.
- 377-395 AR: Vestian turns older prejudice into imperial policy and destroys or confiscates most surviving eastern towers.
- 1288-1303 AR: the Meshalem catastrophe produces present-day caution toward claimants.

## Authorial Truth

- Isfir is truly the Redeemer.
- Ezrahel bar Qoreth and a controlling council majority knowingly pressed Atherian authorities to execute him. Their guilt is real; hereditary guilt assigned to all Jirahar is false.
- The Great Prophet taught universal moral obligations. The mainstream doctrine condemning every knowing outsider who refuses them to death is a severe post-destruction interpretation, not the Prophet's original command.
- The Way-Gates follow stable threshold corridors impressed by the Exodus. They incur no Astral rent.

## Identity

- Jirahar status passes maternally.
- A child of a Jirahar father and outsider mother requires formal reception.
- Sincere conversion is possible but demanding.
- Marriage to an outsider requires conversion.
- Tower, Road, Sea, and Turning ways are recognized orders of life, not hereditary castes.

## Places

Five living great towers remain: Migdal Qesheth, Dahava, Yamma, Melach, and Talga. Qiryath Reqa is the ruined holy city. Each is materially larger below ground than above.
