# Mesakha Traditions

The **Mesakha**, the Awaited One, is expected to gather the dispersed, restore Qiryath, vindicate the Path, establish truthful judgement, and settle the relation between Jirahar and the nations.

## Keeper Tests

A claimant must demonstrate command of the Scrolls without rehearsed trickery, restore divided households, judge without purchased testimony, refuse patron power, and produce signs connected to Qiryath that cannot be staged by warlock script, contract, Shade, relic fraud, or Idol pressure. No claimant has passed.

## Competing Readings

- **Orthodox Keepers** expect a personal ruler and lawful court.
- **Restorationists** emphasize return, sovereignty, and rebuilding.
- **Mercy jurists** emphasize justice and the near-impossibility of condemning informed outsiders without coercion.
- **Dominion zealots** emphasize subjugation of hostile nations and preparation by force.
- **Hidden Words mystics** allow personal, communal, and cosmic meanings to overlap.

## Qiryath

The Empty City's perpetual charter forbids rebuilding before recognition of the Mesakha. Pilgrims may enter marked courts, mourn, study, and remove nothing. Its silent Way-Gate is treated as the most dangerous possible sign.

## Meshalem

Meshalem the Twice-Turned proclaimed himself in 1288 AR. Communities sold property and began a return before Qahirid authorities forced a public test. His conversion shattered families and credit networks. Since then, presumption against claimants has become nearly absolute.
