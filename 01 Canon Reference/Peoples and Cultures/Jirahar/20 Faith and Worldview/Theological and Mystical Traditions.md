# Theological and Mystical Traditions

The **Millin Setharin**, or Hidden Words, studies patterns within scripture, creation, language, memory, and the Exodus routes. It is an interpretive discipline, not a separate source of magic.

## Disciplines

- **Letter and number study:** compares textual structures without assuming every coincidence is revelation.
- **Road contemplation:** retraces Exodus sequences through prayer, fasting, and memory.
- **Name restraint:** studies the moral danger of naming without adopting Covenant true-name ownership.
- **Dream examination:** treats dreams as mixtures of memory, fear, bodily state, Shade pressure, and occasional insight.
- **Gate discernment:** helps Keepers distinguish a stable Prophet's Road from Astral imitation or patron intrusion.

## Limits

Hidden Words practitioners cannot command time, guarantee prophecy, summon routed souls, or force the Lord to answer. Apparent revelation must survive textual, communal, material, and metaphysical testing. A private vision cannot overturn public law.

## Factions

Some mystics read the silence of sacrifice as judgement on Qiryath. Others call it concealment during exile. A forbidden minority suspects the silence began because the covenant reached fulfilment in Isfir; discovery of that belief can divide a household or provoke expulsion.
