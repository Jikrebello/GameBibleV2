# Relations with Other Religions

## Redeemer Churches

The Jirahar reject Isfir's divine and Mesakha claims. Eclessian and Thalmyric churches proclaim him the Redeemer. Relations range from protected debate and shared charity to coercion and inherited blame. The old-Church, Freeflame, Thalmyric, and Velian communions must not be treated as one institution.

The Velian Church is uniquely painful: it preserves Isfir's Jirahar mother tongue and image-free worship while confessing the claim the Keepers reject.

## Al-Ahdiya

Jirahar recognize shared Scattering memories in Covenant water, witness, and guest law while rejecting Mujalib's revelation and Malak al-Ahd. Covenant governments may protect Jirahar courts, tax them, recruit their skills, pressure conversion, or seize records depending on region and ruler.

## Folk and Menhir Practice

Jirahar law distinguishes respecting a place-bound power from worshipping it as the Lord. This creates narrow practical agreements with Folk communities and recurring accusations that the other side has crossed an invisible line.

## Astratheon Remnants and Patrons

Star worship is remembered as the faith of the empire that killed Isfir and destroyed Qiryath. Patron bargains are rejected as purchased dependence. Keepers nevertheless study both because false gates and staged prophecy often use their methods.
