# The Seven Laws of the Stranger

Jirahar tradition holds that the Great Prophet gave all humanity a minimum law before the Scattering. Outsiders need not enter the full Orakha, but they remain accountable to seven obligations.

## The Seven

1. **Do not worship created powers as the Lord.** Menhir, Idols, patrons, rulers, ancestors, and stars may not receive divine submission.
2. **Do not profane the Lord's name.** False oaths and deliberate sacred fraud fall under this law.
3. **Do not murder.** Private killing, sacrifice of the innocent, and judicial murder are included.
4. **Do not commit forbidden unions or sexual predation.** Coercion, abuse of dependants, and the nearest prohibited kinships are central cases.
5. **Do not steal or kidnap.** Theft of persons, children, names, wages, land, and entrusted goods are debated applications.
6. **Do not consume living flesh or cultivate cruelty for appetite.** The law joins treatment of animals to rejection of predatory ritual.
7. **Establish truthful courts.** A society that refuses evidence, restitution, and judgement cannot claim innocence through private virtue.

## Mainstream Sentence

Keeper orthodoxy teaches that under the Mesakha's restored jurisdiction, every outsider must accept these laws. Knowing and free refusal after lawful instruction is subject to death. The doctrine concerns a future universal court, not present foreign criminal jurisdiction.

No existing Keeper, tower, or caravan may execute an outsider merely for unbelief or non-observance. Present-day vigilantism is murder. Mercy jurists further require competent instruction, freedom from coercion, two lawful witnesses, opportunity to retract, and a court whose jurisdiction cannot presently exist.

Dominion zealots reject those restraints and are consequently among the people most often prosecuted under the third law.
