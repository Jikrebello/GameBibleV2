# The Tarkhan Tribes Overview

The Tarkhan Tribes are the mobile peoples of the inland steppe and high grasslands: horse-lords, oath-brokers, raiders, caravan guards, and seasonal confederates who sit between empire, desert, and frontier. They are not a single nation so much as a political weather system. Clans gather around charismatic khans, sacred horse-lines, winter pastures, trade routes, and old grudges.

## Player-Facing Fantasy

The Tarkhan fantasy is mobility, hard bargaining, and survival in open country. They should feel like the people who know every pass, dry riverbed, salt pan, and grass road. A Tarkhan ally can get a caravan through impossible country. A Tarkhan enemy can make an army starve without ever fighting a pitched battle.

## Society and Power

- **Clans:** Extended kinship blocs with their own herds, banners, songs, and feud obligations.
- **Khans:** War leaders who rule by success, gift-giving, omen-reading, and hostage diplomacy.
- **Oath riders:** Professional envoys and guarantors who carry treaties between camps.
- **Spirit keepers:** Shamans and memory-keepers who preserve migration routes, horse genealogies, and taboo places.
- **Bond markets:** Captives, fosterlings, debt-hostages, and marriage alliances are used to turn violence into enforceable politics.

## Culture and Conflict

The Tarkhan are pulled between independence and opportunity. Empires want them as cavalry, scouts, and buffer peoples. Merchant states want their routes. Religious authorities want their conversion or containment. Rival clans want grazing rights and prestige.

Their core conflict is whether to remain a loose clan-world or become a conquering confederation. A unifier could make them a continental power; a failed unifier could scatter them into mercenary bands, raider hosts, and refugee trains.

## Game Use

- Escort missions across open country where weather, water, and honor matter as much as combat.
- Clan arbitration quests where every solution creates a future feud.
- Horse-line theft, hostage rescue, relic recovery from taboo mounds, and raids against imperial forts.
- A campaign choice between backing a stabilizing khan, preserving clan independence, or letting outside powers fracture the steppe.
