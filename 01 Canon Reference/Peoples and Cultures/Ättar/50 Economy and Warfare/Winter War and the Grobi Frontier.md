# Winter War and the Grobi Frontier

Each winter pushes Grobi bands and hosts north and west from the deep cold belt. Grey-Wolf halls receive the first pressure, but every kindred owes some combination of ships, scouts, food, refuge space, healers, iron, or fighters.

Halls that do not take the keel often devote their strongest season to this war. Lion-Mane crews patrol frozen coasts and estuaries; Hornwall halls move cattle and wagons; Bearshield warriors hold forest forts; Grey-Wolf scouts maintain beacons and migration intelligence.

Winter service earns reputation, marriage prospects, shares of recovered goods, and claims upon allied halls. It also creates amputees, refugees, burned settlements, and debt. The Grobi are driven by hunger and predatory leaders, but their desperation does not make raids harmless.

Architecture follows the war: turf-insulated halls, palisades, bent gates, raised granaries, hidden caches, protected wells, beacon ridges, boathouses above ice damage, refuge paths, and chief-sized sally doors.

Folk and Ättar regularly cooperate against major movements while feuding over pasture, timber, anchorage, and who abandoned whom in previous winters.
