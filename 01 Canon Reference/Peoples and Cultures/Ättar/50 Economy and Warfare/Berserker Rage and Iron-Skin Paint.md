# Berserker Rage and Iron-Skin Paint

Rage is the lesser inheritance of [[Mother Moon's Bargain]]. Strongly moon-blooded Ättar of either sex can trigger it in extremity, but formal berserker training and office are male customs.

## The Rage

Rage drives the heart, lungs, muscles, and senses beyond ordinary limits. It suppresses pain and permits short feats of strength and endurance. For an untrained person it is a last resort: loss of self, attacks upon allies, ruptured muscle, and death when the surge ends are common outcomes. Paternal half-Ättar often inherit a weaker, brief, or unpredictable expression.

## Iron-Skin Paint

Paint-Keepers travel among halls carrying regional recipes of mineral salts, venoms, resins, ash, and pact-reactive pigments. Applied in a hall's unique patterns, the mixture reacts with active rage and hardens the skin to a resistance compared with worked steel.

The paint is poisonous. Rage keeps circulation and heat high enough to prevent immediate collapse. If the rage ends before the Paint-Keeper scrapes, washes, and treats the body, paralysis, organ failure, or death follows. Repeated use scars the skin, heart, liver, and capacity for restraint.

The paint does not cause [[Moontouched|moon-loss]], grant transformation, or make the wearer literally metal. It is dangerous alchemy working through an inherited pact-effect.

## Hall Champions

Berserkers are sworn champions of individual halls rather than an independent caste. They lead breaches, fight transformed enemies, and exercise the right of challenge against their own chief. Paint-Keepers remain itinerant so no ruling line can easily monopolize the only mortal counter to skinwalker power.
