# War of the Empty Halls

Around 1600 BR, after the Ättar had already settled their southern ranges, several winters of hunger drove an unusually large Grobi movement through the northeastern passes. Halls burned faster than messengers could gather a common host. Later songs call the abandoned settlements the Empty Halls because the dead froze where feast benches had stood.

Four surviving coalitions gathered under Lion, Ox, Bear, and Wolf standards. Their rulers accepted [[Mother Moon's Bargain]]: hybrid strength for their chiefs, inherited rage for their people, and one firstborn child from every ruling line in every generation.

The transformed chiefs broke the Grobi host and founded the first durable winter alliance. Their victory saved the ranges while binding political legitimacy to a recurring child-price. Ättar traditions defend the bargain as a necessity of kingship; the children taken by it remained innocent victims of that settlement.

The war did not create four states. Its coalitions became the great kindreds within which later halls continued to feud.
