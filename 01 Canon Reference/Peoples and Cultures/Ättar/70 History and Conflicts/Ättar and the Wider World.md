# Ättar and the Wider World

## Folk and Grobi

The Folk are related neighbors, rivals, trading partners, spouses, and winter allies. Tidewood anchorage, timber, pasture, and old insults produce frequent conflict. Major Grobi movements force cooperation, especially between Grey-Wolf scouts and Gale-Crown or Tidewood warbands.

## Thalmyria and the Saltborn

Western Kedrava bears the deepest history of Ättar raid, settlement, baptism, and naval service. The [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/The Saltborn Coast Overview.md|Saltborn]] are a Thalmyric people with substantial Ättar ancestry, not an Ättar colony. Baptized oath-families serve in the Ash Guard and navy while old-rite halls raid the same coast.

## Sestara

Sermora hires Ättar privateers; Orsena brokers ransom and ship repair; Vardessa trades metal, fur, and foreign goods. Vorthara's Flesh Pits employ free Ättar champions and enslaved or debt-bound captives. A letter of reprisal can turn a raider into a legal ally without changing the dead left behind.

## Covenant and Qazmarid

Ättar crews raid and trade along the western strait, fight Qazmari ships, escort Covenant merchants, and sell captives into foreign markets. Captured Ättar appear in military households and flesh pits. Slaving connects the societies through shared wrongdoing, not cultural friendship.

## Jirahar

Jirahar Sea Fleets buy timber, fur, fish, pilots, and escort while competing for cargo and safe anchorage. Their ransom networks can recover captives whom no state recognizes. Jirahar law condemns Ättar slave-taking, while practical captains continue to bargain with raiders.

## Tarkhan and Nsizwa

Tarkhan horses, steppe goods, and mercenary contacts reach Hornwall markets through intermediaries. Nsizwa routes cross the far southwestern ocean; contact brings trade, suspicion, shipwreck rescue, and contested stories of raids neither side admits.

## Eclessia

Distance limits settlement but not raids, mercenary movement, or captives. Eclessian churches support missions among Ättar halls and dispute Thalmyric claims over baptized oath-families.
