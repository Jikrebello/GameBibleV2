# The Path of the Skinwalker

A skinwalker is the male chief of an old-rite Ättar hall whose paternal ruling line remains bound to [[Mother Moon's Bargain]]. Transformation is both weapon and proof of office.

## The Four Hybrid Forms

- **Lion:** speed, balance, explosive violence, and command of a moving fight.
- **Ox:** load-bearing strength, endurance, horned shock, and resistance to injury.
- **Bear:** crushing force, protection, cold endurance, and close-quarter terror.
- **Wolf:** pursuit, scent, stamina, coordination, and winter movement.

Each skinwalker has one human form and one hybrid form. The hybrid is visibly neither human nor ordinary animal. Clothing, armor, doors, and ships must be designed around the chief's transformed size.

## Transformation

The chief shifts through breath, moon-mark, and inherited pact rather than a written spell. The process is painful, physically demanding, and easier under open night. Daylight does not prevent it. Repeated shifts without food, sleep, and human social grounding increase the risk of [[Moontouched|moon-loss]].

The form magnifies the tendencies of the kindred but does not erase moral agency. A skinwalker remains responsible for violence committed while transformed unless moon-loss is demonstrated.

## Political Use

Chiefs transform at installation, in major duels, against Grobi breaches, and when personal force is required to preserve the hall. Using the form to terrorize dependants is common enough to have produced the lawful [[Berserker Rage and Iron-Skin Paint|berserker challenge]].

Skinwalking ends when a hall sincerely renounces the pact. Existing chiefs may retain unstable access for a time, but no converted successor can renew moon-right.
