# Baptized Ättar Halls

Baptized halls are sincerely Redeemer-faithful communities that remain culturally Ättar. They preserve language, mead halls, fosterage, torcs, compensation law, shipcraft, winter musters, and family songs while renouncing worship of Mother Moon and the taking of firstborn children.

Renunciation ends future pact renewal. A converted hall selects a mortal hall-lord through assembly and oath rather than transformed moon-right. Old-rite rivals call such leaders chair-keepers, not true chiefs.

Inherited rage and lunar sensitivity may persist for generations. Churches disagree over whether emergency rage is an inherited wound that may be disciplined or an active return to the old pact. Skinwalking itself is forbidden because it requires renewed participation in the exchange.

The baptized oath-families of Kedrava and the [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Thalmyric Empire/60 Realms and Peoples/The Saltborn Coast Overview.md|Saltborn]] supplied sailors and household warriors during the Long Reconquest. Their descendants remain prominent in the Ash Guard. Their service does not erase the suspicion directed at families with changeling stories or moon-marked children.
