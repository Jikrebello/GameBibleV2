# Moontouched

**Moontouched**, or **moon-loss**, is the progressive failure of a skinwalker to return completely from the hybrid state. It is most common among old chiefs, habitual transformers, and those who remain hybrid through several nights.

## Course

1. **Pull:** sleep, appetite, temper, and attention begin following lunar phases.
2. **Slippage:** involuntary claws, altered voice, scent fixation, and gaps in human social recognition.
3. **Predatory narrowing:** kin become pack, rival, prey, or threat; judgement contracts around immediate instinct.
4. **Failed return:** the body remains partially hybrid and transformation begins without consent.
5. **Moon-loss:** the chief cannot reliably recognize law, oath, or household and becomes an immediate danger.

The condition does not automatically erase memory, consume the soul, or end in catatonia. Rest, fasting from transformation, familiar voices, moonstone stabilizers, and separation from open moonlight can slow early stages. Late moon-loss is rarely reversible.

Moon-Seers identify signs and advise restraint. Their testimony can justify a berserker challenge, but it can also be manipulated in succession politics. Killing a moon-lost chief may be necessary; pretending every difficult elder is moon-lost is murder wearing medicine.
