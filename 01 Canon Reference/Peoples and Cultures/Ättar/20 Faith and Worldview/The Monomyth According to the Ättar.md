# The Monomyth According to the Ättar

The **Song of the Silver Wolf** claims that the Great Prophet crossed the Glass as Mother Moon's first skinwalker, became a silver wolf, and divided his followers among Lion, Ox, Bear, and Wolf. Some skalds end the song with his promised return during a darkened moon.

Keeper-scribes, Church historians, and many Ättar Moon-Seers reject the song as a late synthesis. Its oldest surviving versions appear after sustained Atherian contact and use court language unknown to early hall poetry. The [[Historical Spine of the Ättar|authorial spine]] places the first pact centuries after the Scattering, during the [[War of the Empty Halls]].

The song remains influential because it gives the child-price a heroic origin and claims that Ättar power descends directly from humanity's liberator. Chiefly lines favor it. Baptized halls condemn it. Some returned children reportedly laugh at it without explaining why.

The source is therefore useful evidence for later Ättar kingship and self-justification, not reliable evidence about the Great Prophet.
