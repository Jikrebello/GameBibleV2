# Ättar Reconstruction Audit

## Scope

The pre-reconstruction search found **79 active notes** using Ättar, Attar, Skinwalker, Moontouched, Saltborn, or the four mapped kindred names. The final post-pass search found **103 active matching notes before this audit**, reflecting nineteen new active culture dossiers and newly explicit cross-vault references.

The following rules assign every original result one disposition.

## Rewritten or Newly Authoritative: 3 original notes, 19 new dossiers

The three former active Ättar notes were archived and replaced. Every note now beneath `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ättar/` was newly written or made authoritative against [[Ättar Canon Register]]. This category includes the public MOC, control layer, historical spine, supernatural rules, society, government, economy, warfare, wider relations, and four great-kindred dossiers.

## Terminology or Authority Updated: 32 original cross-vault notes

These original matching notes received direct changes:

- `00 Start Here`: Current Focus Board.
- `01 Canon Reference/Cosmology`: Mother Moon, The Second Circle, and Names of the Menhir, Masks of the Idols.
- `01 Canon Reference/Geography`: Gazetteer and Political Map Border Proof Key.
- `01 Canon Reference/History`: AR Chronology, Historical Spine, Real-History Concordance, Ancient and Early Historic Ages, Early Folk Clan Worlds, Southern Cold Belt and Grobi Frontier, and Present-Day Political Board.
- `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures`: Cultures MOC, Ethnic Groups Overview, Eclessian formation, Folk wider relations, Jirahar wider relations and Migdal Yamma, Sestaran wider relations, Thalmyric Canon Register, Making of the Thalmyrics, Thalmyric armies, Saronika, Kedrava, and the Saltborn overview.
- `02 Playable Settings`: the Conversion Library, Murder at Lantern Fair conversion, Celion Light, Drowned Bell of Soligrad, Thirtieth Sister, and the Turning Lantern Company.

These corrections control the firstborn bargain, hybrid-only transformation, non-inevitable moon-loss, hall politics, winter war, paternal descent, slavery, and western-Kedravan Saltborn geography.

Three additional notes that did not appear in the original search now state the relationship directly: Covenant slavery, Qazmarid's western-strait policy, and Vorthara's gladiator life path.

## Preserved as In-World Testimony: 3

`World History`, `The Thalmyric Account`, and `The Sestaran Account` retain their limited descriptions of Ättar raiding. They contain no claim about the pact or Saltborn geography and remain governed by [[Reliability Register]].

## Intentionally Unchanged: 41

All remaining original matches were inspected and retained because they refer compatibly to an Ättar person, Saltborn character or district, valid trade or military relationship, established adventure element, creature taboo, archive or migration control, or ordinary historical raid. These notes do not override the culture authorities.

This includes Caleran's later Saltborn population, the Ash Guard, Naris devotion, Sella Two-Oars, adventure characters in Halmenth, Sestaran and Covenant reconstruction audits, Folk migration controls, and creature references to Mother Moon or Ättar belief.
