# Bearshield Holds

The Bearshield Holds occupy central mountain forests, iron valleys, and defended winter country. Their settlements are the most heavily fortified of the Ättar ranges: timber-earth rings, stone footings, deep stores, narrow gates, and halls able to shelter outlying households.

Bear chiefs assume broad, crushing hybrids associated with protection, endurance, and close violence. Bear halls also support the strongest [[Berserker Rage and Iron-Skin Paint|berserker]] traditions and several Paint-Keeper circuits.

Iron, charcoal, weapons, armor, timber, resin, and mountain tolls sustain the holds. Their jarls often claim the right to arbitrate disputes because they can enforce a judgement, a claim the other kindreds resent.

Present tensions include mine-thrall revolts, poisoned berserker veterans, rival claims over Paint-Keeper recipes, and chief-kings seeking Bearshield recognition.
