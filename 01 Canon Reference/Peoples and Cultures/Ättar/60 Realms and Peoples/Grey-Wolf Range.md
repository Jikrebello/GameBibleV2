# Grey-Wolf Range

The Grey-Wolf Range is northeastern taiga, frozen river, permafrost, and the principal Ättar pressure frontier against Grobi migration. Its halls are smaller, more dispersed, and more dependent on beacon chains and seasonal cooperation than the wealthy western settlements.

Wolf chiefs become long-limbed hunting hybrids with exceptional scent, stamina, and coordination. Their scouts read migration signs months before the first attack and frequently guide mixed Folk-Ättar warbands.

The range exports fur, resin, cold iron, dogs, guides, and hard-earned intelligence. It imports grain, salt, livestock, and warriors. Its people judge distant sea wealth by how much of it arrives before winter.

Present tensions center on an unusually broad Grobi movement, abandoned outer halls, Lion-Mane refusal of levy demands, and chiefs showing early moon-loss after remaining transformed through entire patrol seasons.
