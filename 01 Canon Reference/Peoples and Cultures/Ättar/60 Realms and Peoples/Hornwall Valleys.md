# Hornwall Valleys

The Hornwall Valleys are inland pasture, river, and drove country. Ox halls raise cattle and sheep, build wagons and fortifications, work bog iron, and control the routes connecting the coast to the mountain and taiga ranges.

Ox skinwalker chiefs become massive horned hybrids built for endurance, hauling, and shock. Their political prestige rests less on distant raids than on feeding allies, moving winter stores, and keeping roads open.

Valley assemblies host compensation cases, cattle markets, fosterage exchanges, and negotiations with Folk and Tarkhan traders. Wealthy halls own many thralls and use captive labor extensively in fields, earthworks, and mines.

Present tensions concern pasture enclosure, raider demands for supplies, foreign horse markets, and whether the valleys should withhold cattle from halls that neglect the Grobi muster.
