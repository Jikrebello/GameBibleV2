# Lives of Aversia

The forge-principality: iron hills, steel works, and the export of organized violence under seal. If Fiorenzia sells trust and Sermora sells reach, Aversia sells the ending of arguments.

## The Foothill Miner and the Forge-Hand

The Iron Foothills' villages live half-under the ground: ore-cutting by contract gang, charcoal-burning up the valleys, and the forges' eternal appetite. Rougher and freer than Fiorenzia's contado — the Soran pay tolerable wages because trained forge-hands are capital — with the trade's tolls taken in lungs and fingers. The fork: the works or the companies — an Aversian boy chooses between making the steel and carrying it, and the recruiting sergeants of both wait outside the same church.

## The Condottiere Road

The principality's signature life. Entry at the **officer schools** (canon) — where the drill is superb, the contract-law classes are mandatory (an Aversian captain can read an employment clause like a Covenant jurist), and two shadows move among the cadets: the **Militis under-cult** (the vampire "hard tradition" — canon) and the **Red Tally's** talent-spotters, who like their feud-brokers pre-trained. Then the ladder: lance-corporal in a named company, company shares, and — for the gifted or ruthless — a **condotta** of one's own: captain-proprietor of a company that is simultaneously a regiment, a joint-stock venture, and a traveling polity. **The forks that define captains:** which contracts to refuse (fighting Crucible warlocks is priced accordingly; garrisoning against Grobi is honest money; the *third* kind of contract — the kind offered at night, without seals — defines your company forever whichever answer you give); when to winter and where (a wintering company is a guest that can become an occupier — see every Eclessian succession crisis); and the retirement problem, which is the whole tragedy of the road: a captain's company is his estate, and estates of armed men do not pass peacefully.

## The Arms-Factor and the Proof-Master

The commercial roads: the Soran export network's agents (embargo-running is a licensing question — Aversian steel arms every side of every war, and the principality's neutrality is its best-armored product), and the **proof-masters** — the testing-guild whose stamp on a breastplate is worth a tenth of its price, incorruptible by careful design and corrupted, historically, exactly twice, both occasions being required study at the schools.

## Noble, Priest, Criminal

The House of Soran's orbit (canon: family dynamics, rivalries) defines nobility: cadet lines seeded through the officer schools, marriage as armament policy. The church here is chaplaincy writ large — company chaplains are the principality's real parish system, hearing the confessions of men between contracts, and the Aversian funeral rite grew out of the companies: the dead man's contract is read aloud, *fulfilled*, and burned. Crime is craft-adjacent: pattern-theft, proof-stamp forgery (the guild hunts this with company muscle), and the gutter-end of the arms trade — sales the licenses pretend not to see, which is where the Red Tally and worse do their shopping.

## Hooks

A company returns from an Eclessian contract paid in land-deeds nobody can locate; a proof-master's stamp starts appearing on steel he never tested; the third-kind contract arrives at the party's captain's tent, sealed with nothing.
