# The Free City of Vardessa

Vardessa is a landlocked fair-city in the Three Roads Basin, the interior saddle between Fiorenzia, Vorthara, and Aversia. It controls the walled city, fair fields, depots, road gates, pasture, and several nearby villages rather than a large principality.

## Formation

Seasonal markets existed before Atherian conquest. Atheria formalized the roads, measures, fair peace, and municipal charter because armies and taxes needed the same junction as merchants. Vardessa revived the oldest form of that charter during the Decadence and joined the defensive compact in 908.

## Government and Citizenship

**First Burgess Tomas Bellafor** presides over guild benches representing major crafts, carriers, innkeepers, horse dealers, food trades, scribes, and road wards. Recognized guild masters of either sex vote through their bench. The highest benches require rooted citizen ancestry and active mastery, keeping old families powerful without making title alone sufficient.

## Fairs and Society

The great fairs exchange horses, wool, timber, ore, tools, arms, clocks, books, dyes, medicines, art, and distant goods. Jirahar caravans, Tarkhan horse traders, Folk intermediaries, Ättar factors, Covenant merchants, pilgrims, soldiers, entertainers, and spies temporarily multiply the population.

Fair peace protects declared guests, worship, property, and roads for the market season. Beyond it, Vardessan law is narrow and guild-centered. Permanent foreign residents can become indispensable without approaching citizenship.

## Defence

Gate militia, pike guilds, engineers, road towers, wagon forts, and supply depots make occupation expensive. During a Muster, Vardessa supplies militia, roads, depots, encampment, and the neutral war congress.

## Present Tensions

Rooted families resist naturalization; Tarkhan traders challenge fixed measures; Aversian buyers dominate arms fairs; Fiorenzian credit buys guild debt; Vortharan contraband enters as “demonstration equipment”; and every power wants the muster hall neutral in its own favor.
