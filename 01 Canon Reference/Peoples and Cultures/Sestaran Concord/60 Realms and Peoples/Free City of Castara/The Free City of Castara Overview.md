# The Free City of Castara Overview

Castara is a detached city-state on the northeastern Boiling Sea coast, far from the old Sestaran peninsula. Once a minor port, it became the gateway for the 1330s rush toward the Shattered Isles and Emerald Expanse.

## Formation and Accession

Castara's old harbor families governed through a small council while larger powers ignored it. New routes brought shipbuilders, scholars, laborers, soldiers, migrants, and expedition fortunes. Castara joined the Concord as the seventh seal in 1332 in exchange for ships, scouts, and overseas provisions.

## Government and Citizenship

**First Speaker Eliana Corvesi** heads the Merchant Princes. Adults of any sex may enter the citizen roll after seven years' residence combined with civic service or qualifying expedition investment. Only the Founders' Roll may supply the First Speaker and recognized Merchant Princes, encouraging wealthy newcomers to purchase, marry into, or manufacture founding ancestry.

## Society and Faith

Growth has produced new docks, crowded housing, university workshops, foreign quarters, expedition chapels, Freeflame meetings, displaced old neighborhoods, and a large population whose labor does not qualify for citizenship.

## Economy and Defence

Shipyards, navigation, cartography, provisioning, universities, salvage, speculation, and imported curiosities sustain Castara. Claims about Expanse peoples, artifacts, creatures, and “empty” land remain expedition testimony, advertising, rumor, or colonial propaganda unless controlled elsewhere in the vault.

Castara's inland lifeline is the [[Castaran Long Road]], which crosses Ash-Horde grass north of Eclessia toward Vardessa and Aversia. Since 1334 the city has renewed the Ash Road Peace with silver, grain, tools, privileges, and gifts in return for escorts and recognized passage. The road is not Castaran territory, and missing payment ends collective Ash protection.

During a muster Castara supplies ships, scouts, distant provisions, and Boiling Sea intelligence.

## Present Tensions

Founding families versus new fortunes, expedition fraud, Ash Horde extraction on inland routes, treatment of foreign peoples, dangerous imports, worker housing, and whether Castara's boom will enrich the Concord or reroute the world around it.
