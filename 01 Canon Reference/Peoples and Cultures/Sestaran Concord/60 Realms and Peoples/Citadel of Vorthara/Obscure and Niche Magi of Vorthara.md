# Minor Arts of Vorthara

Vorthara attracts practitioners too specialized, disreputable, or poor to hold a ward of their own. These are trades and techniques within established magic families, not separate sources of magic.

## Star-Readers

Small observatories read celestial cycles, compare old Atherian tables, and sell weather or journey forecasts. Their best work combines mathematics, records, and Astral impressions. They cannot command time or guarantee a future. Fraudulent readers thrive beside disciplined ones.

## Memory Workers

These investigators use psychometry, controlled Shadowlands exposure, interviews, and material traces to reconstruct events. Impressions may be contaminated by fear, repetition, Idol pressure, or deliberate staging. Altering a living person's memory requires coercive Shade or Idol techniques and is treated as assault wherever a ward can enforce that rule.

## Glamourists

Performers, courtesans, physicians, spies, and confidence artists use dyes, masks, alchemy, trained suggestion, and limited Astral or Shadow techniques to shape appearance and mood. No glamour grants effortless domination. Consent houses display binding rules against concealed compulsion; predatory houses ignore them until a stronger faction intervenes.

## Threshold Specialists

Independent ward-cutters study doors, locks, thin spaces, and hostile architecture. Many train briefly with the [[Collegium of Doors]] before selling narrow expertise to delvers or smugglers. Their work is valuable precisely because a failed threshold can admit Shell-force, Shades, or something wearing a familiar voice.

## Place in the City

Minor practitioners survive through household patronage, shared workshops, market stalls, and temporary ward protection. A famous specialist may influence a faction; none secretly governs Vorthara or polices fate.
