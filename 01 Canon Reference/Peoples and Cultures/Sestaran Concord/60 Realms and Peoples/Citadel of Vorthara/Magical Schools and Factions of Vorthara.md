# Magical Schools and Factions of Vorthara

Vorthara has factions, not ministries. Each institution controls workshops, housing, clients, guards, roads, and obligations inside a ward. None governs the city as a whole.

## Principal Powers

- [[The Crucible]]: warlock rune scripts, Shell-derived elemental force, battlefield engineering, and counter-siege work.
- [[Collegium of Doors]]: containment, wardcraft, breach response, spatial redirection, and professional liability.
- [[The Nightveil Coven]]: perception, concealment, Shadowlands overlays, residue, and controlled Shade contact.
- [[The Ironbound Guild]]: runic engines, golems, labor constructs, war machines, and maintenance systems.
- [[The Fleshweavers and The Red Covenant]]: surgery, grafting, blood systems, warbeasts, and hidden body procurement.
- [[The Pale Hand]]: corpses, funerary residue, body preservation, controlled undeath, and forbidden soul-route interference.
- [[The Crafters’ Union and The Elixirium]]: instruments, armor, tools, medicines, reagents, and commercial manufacture.

## Power Between Wards

Factions bargain through access, hostage apprentices, supply, debt, favors, shared defences, arena champions, and retaliation kept below the Spire Truce. The Wardens of the Crown Road control no school but can close the ascent or deny heavy cargo access.

Not every practice is lawful within every ward. “Vorthara permits it” usually means a faction is strong enough to protect it and its neighbors have not yet decided the cost is intolerable.
