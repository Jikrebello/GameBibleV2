# The Ironbound Guild

The Ironbound build constructs powered by runic engines, wound springs, pressure, elemental reactions, stored charge, and programmed script sequences. Their ordinary products are pumps, carriers, lifting frames, sentries, mining tools, and workshop automata; war golems are prestigious and expensive exceptions.

Their standard families are [[Ironbound Frames|labor, ward, and war frames]] and [[Script Familiars]]. These are adaptive tools without interior consciousness. Any guild work containing a genuine human soul is not an advanced frame but an illegal soul-prison.

## Construction

A construct core stores instructions and power but does not normally contain a soul. Complex behavior comes from layered scripts, sensory mechanisms, limited decision trees, and trained handlers. Damage, ambiguous commands, contaminated charge, and conflicting scripts produce failure.

Forge-charge, stormglass, and weight-seed from Vorthara's permanent draws make local construction unusually compact. [[Shell Anchors and Extraction]] remains strategically compartmentalized; most members know outputs and handling rather than access geometry.

## Forbidden Work

The Black Forgers attempt to increase autonomy through trapped fragments, imposed mantles, stolen names, or sacrificial binding. These methods are secret Left Hand-adjacent crimes, not the guild's standard technology. Even successful products may be persons under torment rather than machines.

## Place in Vorthara

Ironbound workshops control cranes, freight constructs, bridge mechanisms, and heavy repairs. This infrastructure gives the guild leverage over neighboring wards without making it neutral or governmental. Its arena demonstrations advertise durability while concealing maintenance demands.
