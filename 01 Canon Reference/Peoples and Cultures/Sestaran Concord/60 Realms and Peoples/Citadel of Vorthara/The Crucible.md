# The Crucible

The Crucible is Vorthara's foremost warlock school. It teaches fixed rune scripts that draw elemental force from the Shell for battle, siege, demolition, weather exposure, and industrial work.

## Practice

Students learn inscription, sequencing, fuel preparation, field geometry, interruption, and safe failure before destructive scripts. Wands and gauntlets are tools for prepared script families, not sources of effortless free-form magic. Fire, pressure, cold, lightning, corrosive reactions, smoke, and stone fracture require different training and costs.

The school holds influence over the **Crucible Mouth**, Vorthara's Fire-side permanent draw, but does not exclusively own it. Its forge-charge supports military testing and heavy industry. The city's other three anchors prevent the Crucible from turning one strategic resource into general government.

Lancewright proof benches combine one-handed war spears, fitted gauntlets, physical marks, and filed load limits. Arena proof is one public expression of the craft, not its only purpose; road examination, siege inspection, foundry safety, and mobile counter-script work are equally legitimate traditions.

## Institution

Tuition bonds, foreign military contracts, arena testing, and industrial work fund the school. Its ward contains barracks, proving courts, script libraries, burn hospitals, and reinforced ranges. The Crucible answers to its masters and creditors, not a city council.

The institution contains rival benches with different patrons, safety customs, and technical doctrines. The [[Red Brass Bench]] is one corrupt arena branch, not a synonym for the Crucible as a whole. Independent masters and competing benches are capable of investigating it when protected evidence and witnesses reach them.

## History and Conflict

Crucible predecessors helped shatter Covenant siege golems in 907. That victory supports Vortharan pride and conceals how many defenders died from propagated counter-scripts. Present conflicts concern foreign recruitment, stolen scripts, battlefield liability, and whether the school is training armies that may return as conquerors.
