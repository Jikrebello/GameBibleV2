# The Crafters' Union and the Elixirium

Two neighboring guild federations supply Vorthara with dangerous but practical goods. They cooperate on testing and purchasing while competing for contracts, labor, and ward territory.

## Crafters' Union

The Union joins armorers, lens grinders, lampwrights, script engravers, lockmakers, and salvage refitters. Its signature products are reinforced harnesses fitted with replaceable rune plates: heavy, expensive equipment that can brace a bearer, absorb a limited elemental discharge, or drive a tool. They require fuel, maintenance, trained crews, and frequent plate replacement. They are not invulnerable suits or commonplace relics.

The Union also makes warded lanterns, signal devices, insulated siege tools, sealed cases, and standardized fittings for [[Warlock Rune Magic]]. Intact Atherian mechanisms are studied and repaired cautiously, not reproduced at will.

## The Elixirium

The Elixirium is a compact of apothecaries, distillers, poisoners, physicians, and fire-workers. Reliable goods include antiseptics, painkillers, stimulants, smoke mixtures, lamp fuels, corrosives, and battlefield fire. Claims of instant healing, effortless transformation, or resurrection are fraud, patron work, or Fleshweaver practice sold under a cleaner label.

Its feared green-burning compound clings to wet surfaces and is difficult, not impossible, to extinguish. Production poisons workers and has caused ward fires. Formula theft is a recurring source of bloodshed.

## Testing and Law

Products are tested in enclosed yards, contracted expeditions, and occasionally arenas. The Spire Truce forbids uncontrolled Shell breaches and poisoning common cisterns, but enforcement depends on neighbors with enough strength to collect damages. Both guilds maintain rescue crews because a preventable industrial disaster invites every rival into their ward.

## Present Conflicts

- The Ironbound accuse Union shops of copying construct joints.
- The Crucible disputes who may certify elemental script plates.
- Fleshweavers sell rapid recovery that undercuts honest medicine at a terrible hidden cost.
- Foreign armies want mass production the guilds cannot safely deliver.
