# The Pale Hand

The Pale Hand works with corpses, funerary residue, preservation, anatomy, animated tissue, and undeath. It publicly sells cadaver preparation, dangerous labor, battlefield recovery, and body disposal. Its hidden work reaches toward soul-route predation.

## What Animation Is

Most animated corpses contain no routed person. Scripts, residue, imposed impulses, Shade pressure, or patron force move dead tissue. Memory-like behavior may be physical habit or contamination and must not be treated as proof that the deceased has returned.

## Black Mud

The Hand's black medium preserves tissue and makes bodies receptive to imposed motion. Authorially, its worst supplies connect to Undertow predation and stolen funerary material. Users disagree whether the substance comes from one source or whether “Black Mud” names several preparations sold under a useful terror.

## Higher Undeath

Intelligent undead require a trapped or refusing soul, a predatory occupant, an imposed mantle, or another established metaphysical mechanism. There is no generic phylactery route to effortless immortality. Every durable case has a specific cost, victim, and failure mode.

## Place in Vorthara

The Pale Hand controls mortuaries, sealed workshops, corpse contracts, and labor yards in its ward. Other factions buy its services while denying knowledge of procurement. It has no city office and survives through usefulness, fear, and evidence held against clients.
