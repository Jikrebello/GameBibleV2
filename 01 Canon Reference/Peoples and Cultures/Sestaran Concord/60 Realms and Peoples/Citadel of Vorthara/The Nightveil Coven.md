# The Nightveil Coven

The Nightveil Coven studies perception, concealment, fear, Shadowlands overlays, thin reflections, and the disciplined interpretation of residue.

## Shadow Work

Nightveil practitioners do not freely summon the routed dead. Apparent spirits may be residue, Shades, trapped fragments, patron impersonations, or staged illusions. Controlled contact requires anchors, wards, observers, and procedures for ending the encounter.

Some Shades can inhabit a corpse or constructed vessel, but this does not restore the deceased person. The body becomes an anchor for an outside pressure. Such work is dangerous, restricted within the coven's own ward, and frequently abused for espionage.

## Organization

The coven is a network of teaching houses and client circles rather than a monarchy of shadow lords. Reputation depends on reliable concealment, interpretable evidence, and surviving contact without becoming the thing observed.

## Conflicts

Nightveil sells secrecy and intelligence while insisting it can distinguish observation from manipulation. Rival factions doubt that claim. Its greatest danger is not darkness but evidence deliberately contaminated until no witness can tell discovery from suggestion.
