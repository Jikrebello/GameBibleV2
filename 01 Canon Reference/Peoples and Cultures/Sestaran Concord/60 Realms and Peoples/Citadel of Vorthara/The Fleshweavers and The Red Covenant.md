# The Fleshweavers and the Red Covenant

The Fleshweavers alter living tissue; the Red Covenant manipulates blood, vitality, pain, and shared bodily states. They collaborate where surgery, grafting, warbeasts, enhanced soldiers, and forced adaptation overlap.

## Public Work

Both groups sell trauma surgery, prosthetic grafts, veterinary work, poison treatment, controlled enhancement, and biological countermeasures. Some patients live because no respectable institution would attempt the work.

## Predatory Work

Their hidden economy depends on bodies, captives, rare ancestries, living reagents, coerced test subjects, and failures that cannot be shown. Procurement cells connect portions of both factions to [[The Concord of Weights]] and other Left Hand supply chains. This is organized evil, not merely unconventional medicine.

## Belief

Typhonius and Arachnara are real Menhir whose recombination and network laws can appear around Vortharan fleshcraft. Neither law grants moral permission to coerce, mutilate, or redesign a person. Fleshweaver cults turn their names into stories that justify appetite, multiplication, and freedom from stable identity; where those stories absolve chosen cruelty, the doctrine is a mortal corruption often cultivated by Idol pressure rather than a Menhir command.

## Vortharan Power

The factions control wards, clinics, pits, breeding houses, guards, clients, and arena contracts. They possess no council seat because no council exists. Their leverage comes from products, blackmail, and the number of powerful people whose bodies contain their work.
