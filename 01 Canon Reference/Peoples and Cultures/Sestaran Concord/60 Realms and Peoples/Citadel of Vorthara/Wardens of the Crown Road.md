# Wardens of the Crown Road

The Wardens maintain Vorthara's outer ascent, gates, water stations, warning bells, freight rules, and roads through Crown Gate Pass. They descend from Atherian road and quarantine personnel but owe no surviving empire obedience.

## Authority

The Wardens can close the ascent, quarantine cargo, enforce road peace, ration shared water, and coordinate defence outside the inner wards. They cannot legislate within a faction's territory, appoint school masters, police private belief, or speak for residents in ordinary affairs.

Pass-Keeper **Salvi Corren** holds Vorthara's Concord seal. During an external attack he assembles faction contributions and represents the city at Vardessa. The office ends its emergency powers when the muster dissolves.

## Neutrality

Every major faction depends on the road and therefore tolerates the Wardens. Bribery, favoritism, smuggling, and selective closure remain common. Their neutrality is a maintained balance, not moral purity.
