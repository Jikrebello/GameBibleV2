# The Sermoran Republic Overview

Sermora is a canal metropolis and fortified island republic commanding the central Thalassian routes. Its jurisdiction includes the capital islands, naval stations, pilot villages, dockyards, and protected privateer havens rather than a broad mainland.

## Formation

Island harbours and pilot communities predate Atherian conquest. The capital grew on [[Seravon before 350 BR|Seravon]], where related [[Elmaric Island Peoples before 350 BR|Elmaric]] communities were numerous but did not share one ancient government. Atheria preserved local captains to move fleets and collect customs. During the Decadence those captains kept the revenue and built an independent Admiralty. Sermora joined the first defensive compact in 908.

The harbour chain is the city's oldest wall. In 1102, the second of the Two Winters, Sermora closed it against famine ships from the Eclessian coast; a foreign-born chain-tender lowered it, refugees and harbour workers died in the crush at the gap, and the city hanged him as a traitor. When a rival's fleet with hired Ättar keels and a leased storm-engine besieged the city in 1166, his figure was seen above the harbour wall and the engine broke. At every feast the chain is lowered for one foreign or refugee vessel before it is raised for the procession, and the Admiralty's lawyers have spent two centuries narrowing what counts.

Sermoran money and print shaped the century before 1360. Sermoran and Medori loans carried Caedros Aetherion's Crimson Pilgrimage and the siege of Zehrim (1319–1325), and in 1345 a Sermoran pamphlet printed extracts from the Chancery registers in the Medori vault, called it **the Bank of the Dead** and set Fiorenzia against the Medori.

## Government and Citizenship

**Doge Terenzo Vescari** represents the republic and brokers among the Admiralty, merchant houses, guilds, shipowners, privateer captains, and the **Veiled Office**. Male guild masters and registered ship-share citizens vote; widows retain house votes they inherit. Only Houses of the First Mooring may supply the Doge and senior Admiralty.

The Veiled Office directs foreign intelligence and state secrecy. The **Night Oar** performs deniable retrieval, sabotage, and killing but remains a contested instrument rather than an all-powerful assassin guild.

## Society and Faith

Canal folk, arsenal hands, sailors, pilots, courtesans, craftsmen, servants, captives, foreigners, and resident merchant communities outnumber enfranchised houses. Public faith centers on Saint Naris, ship blessings, confraternities, and harbor processions.

By the third century AR, Sermora was a regional see. Congregations associated with Virella and Aquilan supported interpreters and sent letters of introduction with seafarers. The ties between its churches and other harbours survived changes in political control. [[The Church after Qiryath - From Exile to Legalization]]

Sermoran reed companies join theatre, procession, civic warning, ship signal, and [[Harmonic Shellcraft|harmonic field work]]. Some are private ensembles, some serve fleets or salons, and some travel between Concord cities. Instruments, authored parts, wages, and the right to refuse remain individual claims even where a company owns a booking name or common repertoire.

## Economy and Defence

Shipping, convoy service, insurance, naval stores, luxury trade, pilotage, salvage, and privateering sustain the republic. The Admiralty fleet protects recognized routes; licensed captains raid enemies and occasionally discover after the fact who the republic considers an enemy. During a muster Sermora concentrates fleets and relieves blockades.

## Present Tensions

Castaran routes threaten old trade, Ättar raiding blurs into licensed service, the Veiled Office accumulates secrets no Doge controls, and privateer violence risks wars the Concord will not defend.
