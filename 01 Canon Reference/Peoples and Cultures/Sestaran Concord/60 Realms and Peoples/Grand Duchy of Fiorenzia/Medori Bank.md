# Medori Bank

The Medori Bank is the known world's most extensive private credit network, but it neither owns every debt nor controls every political outcome. Its power comes from correspondent houses, archives, reputation, liquidity, enforcement, and the ability to move obligations between jurisdictions.

## Functions

- deposits, payments, and exchange;
- municipal, princely, Church, and war lending;
- trade and cargo finance;
- insurance and reconstruction credit;
- estate, guild, and household loans;
- custody, authentication, and account clearing;
- distressed-debt purchase and negotiated collection.

## Organization

The family board sets broad risk. Investment offices seek projects and sovereign clients. Correspondent houses maintain foreign access. Advocates and notaries translate instruments between legal systems. The Debt Management Office restructures, collects, sells, or enforces defaulted claims. The [[Debtors’ Corps]] performs labor, security, investigation, and dangerous recovery under debt-service contracts.

## Competing Incentives

One office may finance a government while another insures its rival's cargo and a third buys discounted claims after the war. This is institutional competition, not proof that one hidden hand scripted the conflict. Stability, decisive victory, reconstruction, prolonged debt, and controlled crisis can each profit different books.

## Limits

The Bank depends on courts, local partners, records, roads, confidence, and borrowers capable of payment. Repudiation, destroyed archives, political revolution, hostile saints, contract fraud, rival Jirahar credit, and the Under-Ledger can all defeat it. Its agents therefore prefer leverage to visible rule.
