# Medori Family - Authorial Truth

The Medori descend from a displaced Covenant legal household that entered the Sestaran world during late Atherian rule. They preserved fragments of true-name and metaphysical contract practice beneath ordinary notarial technique.

The family is not collectively loyal to Iqashar, Al-Ahdiya, or the Creditor. Most living Medori understand their inheritance as dangerous family craft rather than theology. Isabella knows the most; Gaius knows enough to fear exposure; many relatives know nothing.

The Bank's uncanny instruments partly derive from this inheritance and partly from what the family acquired through Alessior II's Primarchy. The Under-Ledger has exploited Medori systems but does not secretly own the entire institution.

## The Chancery Registers

What the family acquired through Alessior II's Primarchy is concrete: the master registers of the Chancery of Sendings, taken by Bartolo as surety in 1296. The family understands an unsung assurance letter as a lien, a retaining claim over a named soul's passage that a Patron, the Undertow or an infernal broker could press, and it has never sold one; the registers are leverage held, not stock traded. The Under-Ledger has tried to reach them. Bartolo bought Alessior's election because he understood what the Sufficient Seal would do to the volume of Sending payments; Alessior never understood what Bartolo was. See [[Causality Pass - 09 The Road to 1360]], [[Causality Pass - 10F Bending the Road to 1360]] and [[The Grey Sweat - Authorial Truth]].

## Fiorenzia, 1346–1349

Arrigo Medori took the registers to Orsena in 1346 because he knew what a frightened crowd might do to liens it could not read, and because the family's hold on the Sanctum depended on them. Paying for the Pyre of Masks was his own misjudgement. The Medori did not kill Severin. They profited from his death, and the family has never troubled to deny the rumour that they arranged it, because a city that fears the Medori had a hand in it does not ask what else was in that cellar. What stood in the Pyre, and how Severin died, are recorded in [[Causality Pass - 10F Bending the Road to 1360]].
