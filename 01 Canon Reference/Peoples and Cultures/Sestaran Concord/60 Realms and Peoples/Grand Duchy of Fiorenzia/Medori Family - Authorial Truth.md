# Medori Family - Authorial Truth

The Medori descend from a displaced Covenant legal household that entered the Sestaran world during late Atherian rule. They preserved fragments of true-name and metaphysical contract practice beneath ordinary notarial technique.

The family is not collectively loyal to Iqashar, Al-Ahdiya, or the Creditor. Most living Medori understand their inheritance as dangerous family craft rather than theology. Isabella knows the most; Gaius knows enough to fear exposure; many relatives know nothing.

The Bank's uncanny instruments partly derive from this inheritance and partly from what the family acquired through Alessior VI's Primarchy. The Under-Ledger has exploited Medori systems but does not secretly own the entire institution.

## Secrecy Rule

Public notes may describe unusual clauses, impossible enforcement, sealed family archives, foreign rumors, and suspiciously old techniques. They must not state Covenant ancestry or identify the Creditor connection as fact.
