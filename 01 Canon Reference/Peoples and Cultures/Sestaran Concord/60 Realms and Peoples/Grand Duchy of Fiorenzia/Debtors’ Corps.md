# Debtors' Corps

The Debtors' Corps is Fiorenzia's regulated system of debt-service companies. Bankrupt people may accept a fixed term of paid labor or dangerous service instead of prison and permanent civil disability. Wages are divided between subsistence, dependants, and amortization recorded on a public service schedule.

## Service

Most corpsmen repair roads, clear floods, guard warehouses, escort surveyors, or serve municipal works. Skilled and military debtors may enter armed companies. The best known foreign detachments are called **Blackjacks**, including the [[Blackjack Corps]] employed in Grenzburg.

Not every debtor owes the Medori Bank, but Medori offices purchase and administer enough distressed paper to dominate overseas deployments. Units answer to named provosts and company captains rather than a hidden supreme Collector.

## Law and Abuse

A lawful service instrument states the principal, permitted charges, wage share, term ceiling, dependants' protection, and discharge conditions. Fiorenzian advocates can challenge fraudulent arithmetic. Completed service restores ordinary civil standing, though it does not create Golden Book eligibility.

The system remains coercive. Creditors steer dangerous work toward people with little bargaining power; commanders add disputed costs; foreign charters weaken oversight; and some debtors discover that injury has generated new obligations. Illegal instruments use pain clauses, hostage terms, or name-binding. These are crimes under Fiorenzian law even when compromised officials conceal them.

## Internal Divisions

- **Service officers** believe strict accounts can make a harsh institution survivable.
- **Collectors** treat bodies as recoverable assets and lobby against term limits.
- **Reform advocates** demand independent discharge courts and family protections.
- **Silent Partners** contaminate selected instruments for purposes beyond ordinary profit.

The Corps is feared because lawful service, institutional exploitation, and outright predation can wear the same grey coat.
