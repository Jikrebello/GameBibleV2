# The Principality of Aversia Overview

Aversia controls the southern Iron Foothills: a fortified capital, mining towns, foundry valleys, quarries, forest roads, and military estates. It is the Concord's largest producer of arms, tools, engineers, and professional field companies.

## Formation

Atheria founded Aversia as a mining and military colony securing ore and the peninsula's southern approaches. Imperial officers and industrial guilds became local elites during the Decadence. Aversia joined the compact in 908.

## Government and Citizenship

**Duke Marcus Soran** rules after his mercenary house seized the principality through civil war. **General Aurelia Soran** commands the main army. Men and women can earn citizenship through recognized civic or military service. The old Shield Houses retain senior magistracies, although the Sorans demonstrated that sufficient force can rewrite a charter.

## Society and Faith

Miners, charcoal burners, forge hands, engineers, factors, company families, farmers, prisoners, and migrants sustain the military economy. Old-Church chaplains serve towns and companies; soldiers argue whether a contract can make a war just or only make responsibility legible.

## Economy and Defence

Ore, steel, tools, armor, artillery, fortification, proof-testing, and contract-captain service are the principality's core exports. Citizen militia holds walls; professional companies campaign abroad. During a muster Aversia supplies field troops, artillery, engineers, and fortress officers.

The northeastern freight link reaches detached Castara through Vardessa and the [[Castaran Long Road]]. Aversia calls the annual Ash payment tribute while depending on its results. Aversian arms reach rival Ash banners, and proposed depots repeatedly conflict with pasture and great-horn migration.

## Present Tensions

Soran absolutism, Shield House resentment, unsafe mines, foreign clients, Medori debt, worker organization, Militis vampire influence in officer traditions, and Marcus's ambition to turn temporary military necessity into Concord leadership.
