# Soran Family Dynamics

## Marcus and Aurelia

Marcus trusts Aurelia's loyalty and judgment but resists her preference for permanent readiness. She believes the duke sometimes treats military supply as a bargaining token when a frontier cannot wait for a favorable price.

## Marcus and Valerius

Valerius's contract network gives the house money, intelligence, and foreign obligations. Marcus values all three and fears the last. Their recurring dispute is whether an Aversian company remains a public instrument after a foreign patron has paid for it.

## Aurelia and Valerius

Aurelia commands regulars; Valerius negotiates hired companies. Each believes the other underestimates a different danger: she fears divided loyalty, while he fears an army too expensive to sustain without foreign work.

## Octavia's Position

Octavia moves between civic houses, foreign courts, and company captains. Her intelligence protects the dynasty, but secrecy makes every relative wonder which crisis she has decided they are not ready to know.

## Elena and the Civic Houses

Lady Elena keeps the family connected to old magistrates, mine owners, and guild patrons. She is neither decoration nor spymaster: her influence rests on remembering which compromise allows a proud institution to obey without appearing conquered.

## Shared Boundary

The family closes ranks against an attempt to restore Verati or Carvonius rule by violence. Inside that boundary, succession, military expenditure, company law, marriage, and the limits of Octavia's methods remain open conflicts.
