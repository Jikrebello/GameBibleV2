# The Sestaran Concord Overview

The Sestaran Concord is a defensive league of seven independent city-states connected by trade, finance, specialised craft and a shared concern for their survival. Its members include highland and mountain territories, island harbours, inland fairs and the distant Boiling Sea port of Castara. Their only binding common institution is the Seven-Seal Defensive Muster.

Each member maintains its own ruler or government, citizenship, law and religious arrangements. “Sestaran” is useful as a collective name abroad, while local identities remain more important at home. The league's populations formed from several waves of settlement and mixture; common membership gives them a diplomatic relationship rather than a single ancestry.

## Formation of the cities and league

Sestaran communities grew where ports, rivers, islands and overland routes brought people together. Local Orphaned survivors, southern war-settlers, other Ark-derived migrants, refugees, captives and traders joined in different proportions. Negotiated settlement and intermarriage accompanied armed occupation, slavery, grain seizure and expulsion.

Fiorenzia, Sermora and Vardessa had urban histories before Atherian state conquest. The empire incorporated Fiorenzia and Sermora while retaining useful municipal elites, chartered Vardessa's fairs, founded Aversia as a mining and military colony, and established Orsena as a customs and naval station. At Vorthara, inspectors and quarantine forts surrounded inner wards the empire never securely controlled.

During imperial decline, cities kept revenue, hired forces and negotiated on their own account. The Fall of 905 made their military independence urgent. Vortharan and Caldrian forces defeated a Covenant army at the [[The Battle of the Shattered Engines (907 AR)|Battle of the Shattered Engines]] in 907. Five cities sealed the first defensive compact in 908; Vorthara joined in 909. Castara's accession in 1332 established the present seven seals.

## The seven members

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/60 Realms and Peoples/Grand Duchy of Fiorenzia/The Grand Duchy of Fiorenzia Overview.md|Fiorenzia]] is a highland duchy of vineyards, textiles, scholarship and artistic patronage. Medori finance connects its houses with borrowers and political interests throughout the known world.

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/60 Realms and Peoples/Sermoran Republic/The Sermoran Republic Overview.md|Sermora]] is a canal metropolis with fortified islands, fleets and extensive intelligence networks. Pilotage, shipping, insurance and privateering are central to its wealth.

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/60 Realms and Peoples/Principality of Aversia/The Principality of Aversia Overview.md|Aversia]] supplies ore, steel, arms, tools and siege engineering. Its fortresses and contract-captains connect industrial production with hired warfare.

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/60 Realms and Peoples/Citadel of Vorthara/The Citadel of Vorthara Overview.md|Vorthara]] grew around the Black Spire as a mountain refuge for factions, specialists and dangerous research. Its workshops provide constructs, rune-warlocks, reagents and magical testing. Its seal grants no common government authority over the inner city.

[[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/60 Realms and Peoples/Free City of Castara/The Free City of Castara Overview.md|Castara]] is the league's detached Boiling Sea gateway. Shipyards, migration, exploration and colonial ventures have enlarged a formerly minor port and created new demands for mainland supplies.

[[The Free Port of Orsena|Orsena]] provides neutral transshipment, repair, storage, foreign quarters and prisoner exchange. Its harbour administration is an important service to powers otherwise at war.

[[The Free City of Vardessa|Vardessa]] is landlocked. Seasonal fairs at the Three Roads Basin bring horses, wool, timber, books, clocks, dyes and tools into overland trade. The neutral muster hall gives it a common defensive function within the league.

## Government and citizenship

The [[The Seven-Seal Defensive Muster|Seven-Seal Defensive Muster]] requires fixed contributions when a member suffers external attack. Private wars, piracy, mercenary disputes and colonial ventures fall outside that obligation. The members share neither a treasury nor a general foreign policy.

Within each city-state, political membership is limited by local rules. Citizen owners and guild masters possess advantages unavailable to many residents. Labourers, sailors, migrants, servants and foreigners outnumber citizens in every member. Entry into a licensed occupation can matter as much as formal freedom.

Households support workshops, ships, farms and commercial enterprises. Apprentices, tenants, debtors, prisoners and enslaved people contribute to the wealth advertised by the cities. Civic charity, hospitals and opportunities for skilled work exist alongside purchased office, exclusion and coercive employment.

## Trade and ordinary life

The cities' different specialisms make them dependent on one another. Fiorenzian credit can equip a fleet; Sermoran carriers need Aversian iron and inland supplies; Castaran shipyards draw on mainland suppliers even as their new routes threaten older trading interests. Vardessan fairs distribute goods between the ports and continental markets.

Art, scholarship and religious patronage employ craftspeople as well as celebrated artists. Vineyards, textile work, ship repair, mining, horse dealing and food supply sustain households whose livelihoods receive less attention than banking and diplomacy. [[Economy of the Seven Cities]] follows these connections, while [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/30 Society and Daily Life/Life Paths/Lives of the Concord — The Common Spine.md|Lives of the Concord — The Common Spine]] describes the different paths through urban society.

## Religion and foreign communities

Most inherited public religious institutions descend from the Eclessian old Church. Local governments negotiate appointments and property with ecclesiastical authorities. Fiorenzia funds churches, hospitals and confraternities; Sermoran observance follows sailors' vows, Saint Naris and harbour processions; Aversian chaplains accompany hired companies.

Orsena protects Eclessian and Thalmyric-rite congregations under separate arrangements. Vardessa permits competing preachers at its fairs while protecting access to the roads. Vorthara remains under interdict, with chapels outside the gates serving residents and travellers. Castara contains old-Church, Freeflame and expedition congregations.

Jirahar quarters preserve their own teaching, food, burial and communal institutions. Covenant merchants maintain halls in several ports and fairs. Folk, Ättar, Tarkhan and other travellers bring household practices into a public life where disputes over worship, burial and property are settled locally.

## The Concord in 1360

The league finances, arms and transports major powers while its members compete for tolls, contracts and information. Castara's expansion has widened the field of opportunity and risk. Its annual Ash Road Peace, first agreed in 1334 after a destructive Horde attack, secures registered caravans through Tarkhan territory.

Older members retain their own rivalries and priorities. Their common defence has survived repeated attempts to turn wartime leadership into permanent command. Commerce keeps the cities closely connected even when their governments pursue incompatible interests.
