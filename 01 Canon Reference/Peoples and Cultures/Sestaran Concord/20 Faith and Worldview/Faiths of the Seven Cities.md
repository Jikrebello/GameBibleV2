# Faiths of the Seven Cities

The Concord has no common church. Most inherited public institutions descend from the Eclessian old Church, but city governments negotiate appointments, property, and jurisdiction rather than accepting uniform outside control.

## The Redeemer's Faith

Fiorenzia funds splendid churches, hospitals, confraternities, and art while keeping bishops close to patrician government. Sermora's faith follows sailors' vows, Saint Naris, harbor processions, and merchant foundations. Aversian chaplains travel with companies and argue over whether hired war can be righteous. Castara's crowded port includes old-Church, Freeflame, and expedition chapels.

Orsena protects Eclessian and Thalmyric-rite congregations under separate harbor charters. Vardessa hosts competing preachers during its fairs while forbidding any communion from closing the roads. Vorthara remains under unresolved interdict; chapels outside its gates serve travellers and residents who cross the walls to worship.

## Other Communities

Jirahar quarters maintain their own law, teaching, food, burial, and sanctuary. Covenant merchants maintain halls in Sermora, Orsena, Vardessa, and Castara without receiving political jurisdiction over local subjects. Tarkhan, Folk, Ättar, and foreign sailors keep household and travelling rites that city authorities tolerate until property, burial, or public-order disputes force recognition.

## Moral Boundary

Sestaran piety is worldly without being automatically false. Genuine saints, charity, repentance, and devotion coexist with purchased office, political bishops, relic speculation, and public splendor used as reputation. Vorthara's tolerance does not make body trafficking, soul predation, or false sanctity morally neutral.
