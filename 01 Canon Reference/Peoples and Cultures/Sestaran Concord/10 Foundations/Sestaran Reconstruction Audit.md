# Sestaran Reconstruction Audit

## Scope

The pre-reconstruction search found **173 active notes** using the established Sestaran names. The post-pass register contains **200 active matching notes**, excluding this audit, after adding Orsena, Vardessa, and the new control layer.

The following rules partition every result into one disposition. They are path-based so future maintainers can reproduce the audit rather than trusting an undocumented manual list.

## Rewritten or Newly Authoritative: 43

Every matching note beneath `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Sestaran Concord/` was rewritten, materially condensed, metadata-corrected, or confirmed against the seven-city model. This includes all seven city hubs, Vortharan factions, life paths, the Concord-wide dossiers, and this pass's new authority notes.

## Terminology or Authority Updated: 28

These cross-vault notes received corrections to geography, chronology, rulers, external relations, map logic, public secrets, or navigation:

- `00 Start Here`: World Egg Setting Primer, Needs Work, Current Focus Board, and Canon Index.
- `01 Canon Reference/Geography`: Gazetteer, Geography Atlas, Political Map Border Proof Key, and Photoshop Import Guide.
- `01 Canon Reference/History`: AR Chronology, Named Figures, Real-History Concordance, Invasion and Fall, Present-Day Courts and Crowns, and Present-Day Political Board.
- `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures`: Cultures MOC, Ethnic Groups Overview, and the Ahl-e-Ahd, Jirahar, Tarkhan, Fäskogarfolk, Ättar, Eclessian invasion, Caelmount, and Long Reconquest authorities.
- `02 Playable Settings`: The Bragging Vault conversion, full adventure, and Corsin Marlo; House of Mutable Form.

## Preserved as In-World Testimony: 11

All matching notes beneath `03 Stories & In-World Texts/` retain voice, limitation, or bias. Three comparative histories and one Thalmyric sacred testimony received compatibility corrections; the remaining sources were checked and retained. Their truth value remains governed by [[Reliability Register]].

## Intentionally Unchanged: 118

Every remaining active match outside the three rules above was inspected by search and retained because its Sestaran city, institution, person, trade connection, creature, or adventure use is compatible with the reconstruction. This category is deliberately not evidence that those notes are Sestaran authorities.
