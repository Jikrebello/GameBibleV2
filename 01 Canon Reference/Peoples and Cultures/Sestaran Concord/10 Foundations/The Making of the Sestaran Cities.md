# The Making of the Sestaran Cities

Sestara did not begin as one people or break from Atheria in one revolution. The harbor populations are **Braided peoples**, not a separate branch of the Glass Ark. Their cities have different ages, ancestral mixtures, languages, charters and memories of empire.

## Before Atheria

Across the late Scattering and early Hearth ages, local Orphaned survivors, Ark-derived migrants, island households, river communities, refugees, captives and traders joined differently at each harbor and road junction. Fiorenzia grew where river roads entered fertile highlands. Sermora began as rival island harbors and pilot communities. Vardessa grew around seasonal fairs at the Three Roads Basin. The Black Spire predates all surviving records; Vorthara accumulated refugees, specialists, and forbidden workshops around a structure no empire fully understood.

No single founding migration created all the cities, and modern **Sestaran** identity is chiefly civic and diplomatic rather than proof of one ancient ancestry.

## Atherian Rule

Atheria conquered Fiorenzia and Sermora but preserved municipal elites to collect revenue and move ships. It chartered Vardessa's fairs, established Aversia as a mining and military colony, and built Orsena as a customs and naval station. At Vorthara it maintained quarantine forts, roads, and inspectors without controlling the inner wards.

Imperial citizenship, archives, roads, Old Atherian speech, and the Redeemer's faith left durable common layers. Extraction, slavery, military requisition, and cultural subordination gave every city a different grievance.

## Separation

During the Decadence, Fiorenzian estates withheld revenue, Sermoran captains kept imperial tolls, Vardessa revived its charter, Aversian commanders became local magnates, and Orsena's customs houses stopped forwarding accounts. The Fall made practical autonomy unavoidable. Their defensive league followed invasion rather than shared nationalism.

## Later Members

Vorthara joined after its decisive aid against the Covenant. Castara remained a minor distant port until Boiling Sea expansion transformed it; its accession in 1332 created the present seven seals.

## Modern Identity

Shared notarial habits, trade speech, Old-Church inheritance, mercenary institutions, and fear of conquest make the cities intelligible to one another. They do not erase primary city identity.
