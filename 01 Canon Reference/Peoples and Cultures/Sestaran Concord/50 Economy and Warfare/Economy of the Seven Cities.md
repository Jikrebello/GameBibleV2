# Economy of the Seven Cities

The Concord is an interdependent market without common regulation. Each member protects a different choke point.

- Fiorenzia provides credit, account clearing, highland produce, textiles, art, and patronage.
- Sermora provides shipping, pilotage, insurance, naval stores, luxury imports, and licensed privateering.
- Aversia provides ore, steel, arms, siege engineering, tools, and contract-captain companies.
- Vorthara provides rune-warlocks, constructs, rare reagents, containment, dangerous research, and magical testing.
- Castara provides Boiling Sea shipyards, explorers, new routes, and uncertain Expanse cargoes.
- Orsena provides neutral transshipment, repair, storage, prisoner exchange, foreign quarters, and reliable harbor administration.
- Vardessa provides seasonal fairs, horses, wool, timber, books, clocks, dyes, tools, and overland redistribution.

## Rivalry and Dependence

Fiorenzia can finance a fleet but cannot command the Thalassian. Sermora can blockade a port but needs Aversian iron and Vardessan roads. Aversia can field an army but needs foreign grain and credit. Vorthara sells weapons nobody trusts it to govern. Castara's new wealth threatens old routes while depending on every mainland supplier.

## Labor and Citizenship

Citizen owners, guild masters, tenant farmers, artisans, sailors, apprentices, servants, migrants, debtors, prisoners, and enslaved people support the system. Political exclusion and licensing often matter more than nominal freedom. The cities advertise mobility while rationing the citizenship that makes mobility durable.
