# Sestaran Forces and Private War

The seven Sestaran cities defend themselves through separate militias, fleets, regular companies and hired specialists. They possess no shared standing army outside the forces assembled for the Seven-Seal Defensive Muster. This arrangement protects local control and supports a large market in military labour, while allowing member cities to fight one another through mercenaries, seizures, sabotage and limited sieges.

## Forces by Seal

Fiorenzia hires companies, intelligence networks, and the Debtors' Corps. Sermora maintains an Admiralty fleet alongside licensed privateers. Aversia fields the strongest regular companies, engineers, artillery, and fortress troops. Vortharan factions contribute rune-warlocks, constructs, and irregular specialists. Castara operates escort and expedition fleets. Orsena maintains a compact harbor militia and fixed defences. Vardessa fields disciplined gate and road militia.

## Private War

Members may fight one another through mercenaries, seizures, sabotage, limited sieges, and competing claimants. The Concord does not protect a member from another member. Custom discourages destroying aqueducts, burning archives, or permanently closing fair and sea routes because every city expects to need them later.

## Foreign Service

Aversian companies, Sermoran ships, Vortharan warlocks, Fiorenzian agents, and Castaran navigators serve foreign employers. Their contracts do not become Concord policy. A member attacked by troops hired from another member cannot invoke the defensive muster unless the hiring city openly adopts the war.

## Moral Boundary

Professional force can reduce uncontrolled levies and preserve skilled soldiers. It can also make invasion, assassination, colonial violence, and repression available to anyone with credit. “Only a contract” is an allocation of responsibility, not its disappearance.
