# Sestaran Citizenship and Office Register

Citizenship is a scarce political property, not a synonym for residence. Every city distinguishes citizens from protected residents, guild dependants, foreigners, laborers, sailors, servants, and the unlicensed majority.

| City | Franchise | Senior office |
|---|---|---|
| Fiorenzia | One vote per registered citizen household, exercised by its recognized head of either sex | Golden Book houses; Medori admitted by purchase in 1238 |
| Sermora | Male guild masters and ship-share citizens; widows retain inherited house votes | Houses of the First Mooring elect the Doge and senior Admiralty |
| Aversia | Citizenship earned through recognized civic or military service by men or women | Shield Houses hold magistracies; House Soran seized the princely title |
| Vorthara | No common citizenship or citywide vote; faction and ward membership provide protection | No common office; Pass-Keeper holds only the external seal |
| Castara | Adults of any sex after seven years' residence plus civic service or qualifying expedition investment | Founders' Roll candidates may become First Speaker or Merchant Prince |
| Orsena | One vote per Harbor Book household, exercised by its recognized head of either sex | Three registered generations required for rector and harbor offices |
| Vardessa | Recognized guild masters of either sex vote through guild benches | Highest benches require rooted citizen ancestry and active mastery |

Naturalization is possible everywhere except Vorthara, but difficult enough to preserve old political classes. Adoption, marriage, purchase, service, and fraud are common routes around ancestry rules.
