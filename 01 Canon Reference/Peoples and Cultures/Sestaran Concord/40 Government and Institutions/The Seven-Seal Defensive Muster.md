# The Seven-Seal Defensive Muster

The Muster is the Concord's only binding common institution. It has no authority over domestic law, taxation, succession, religion, commerce, private war, or diplomacy.

## Activation

An open attack by an external sovereign force upon recognized member territory activates the compact automatically. The attacked member sends its broken gate-seal and witness record to Vardessa. The other members must begin their fixed contribution before debating blame or strategy.

Privateers, mercenary companies under foreign contract, faction feuds, punitive trade seizures, offensive expeditions, and colonial ventures do not automatically qualify. These exclusions invite deliberate ambiguity.

## Contributions

| Seal | Required contribution |
|---|---|
| Fiorenzia | Emergency credit, intelligence, and hired field troops |
| Sermora | Fleet concentration, convoy escort, and blockade relief |
| Aversia | Field companies, artillery, engineers, and fortress officers |
| Vorthara | Rune-warlocks, constructs, counter-siege specialists, and pass defence |
| Castara | Ships, scouts, distant supplies, and Boiling Sea intelligence |
| Orsena | Harbor access, repair capacity, diplomacy, prisoner exchange, and intelligence |
| Vardessa | Citizen militia, roads, depots, fair-field encampments, and the muster hall |

## War Congress

Delegates meet at Vardessa only to coordinate the active defence. Each seal has one voice, but quotas do not wait for unanimity. The attacked member commands in its own territory; combined operations require four seals. The congress dissolves after the external force withdraws or a five-seal finding declares the danger ended.

## Failure

A defaulting city owes double its missed quota at the next muster and loses collective protection until restitution. The compact cannot compel a city to stop trading with the enemy unless that trade directly defeats the active defence.
