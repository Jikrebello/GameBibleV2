# Courts of the Folk Lands

This is the creator-facing register for the first six Folk courts. A court is a local ecology of power, not a sovereign state and not a reliable theological category.

## Authorial Rule

Menhir pressure is natural law rather than moral authority. Animals and reflexive organisms may lack agency; sapient courtiers remain responsible for choices made within their compulsions. Patrons seek stability, identity, service, or rent. Shades and residue repeat or imitate. Idol masks deliberately prey upon meaning, appetite, shame, and identity. Folk seers classify all of them by observed custom, so one court can contain several kinds at once.

| Court | Primary country | Public concern | Authorial pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Antler Hall]] | Rootbound and migration country | hunt, fertility, herd movement, blood-price | Sylvorn ecology with recurring Wrath and Gluttony masks |
| [[The Court Beneath the Roots]] | Heartwood and old burial country | boundaries, inheritance, concealed things | place-bound Faewild law, residue, and occasional Greed masks |
| [[The Storm-Cairn Court]] | Gale-Crown highlands | passes, boasting, lightning, reversal | Zephyraxis courtiers, weather organisms, and Pride infiltration |
| [[The Court of Salt and Teeth]] | Tidewood Coast | storms, wrecks, fishing, transformation | Thalassor ecologies, lease-patrons, and hunger masks |
| [[The Ashen Guest Court]] | roads and frontier thresholds | hospitality, disguise, gifts, exchanged children | Lyraxis courtiers, patrons, Shades, and Beloved masks |
| [[The Reed Court of Wolfsmere]] | Wolfsmere | memory, drowned names, grief | thin-space recurrence, trapped residue, and predatory imitation |

## Common Failure

Courts change without issuing doctrine. A seer's true precedent may cease to work after a new member arrives, an old taboo is broken, a road moves, or an Idol learns the local mask. This is why Folk ritual is conservative without being uniform.
