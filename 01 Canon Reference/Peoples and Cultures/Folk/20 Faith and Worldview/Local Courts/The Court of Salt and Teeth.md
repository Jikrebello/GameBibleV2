# The Court of Salt and Teeth

## Folk Account

The Court of Salt and Teeth claims the line where winter forest enters the sea. Its masks include the Net-Mother, the White Seal, the Drowned Ferryman, and the Guest with Shark Teeth. It gives fish, wreckage, weather, and altered bodies without distinguishing rescue from possession.

## Signs and Boundaries

Teeth wash ashore in ordered rows, nets return knotted in unfamiliar patterns, seals watch funerals, and fresh water tastes of salt before a storm.

## Taboo and Offering

Never count the drowned before landfall, deny shore-right after accepting a wreck's cargo, or eat the first catch alone. Offer salt, carved bone, returned wreckage, a boat's name, or blood mixed into the outgoing tide.

## Gifts and Failure

The court may fill nets, hide a vessel, calm a crossing, or lend cold endurance. It may also make prosperity dependent upon wrecks, alter children conceived during the bargain, or call the borrower back to sea.

## Authorial Truth

Thalassor-aligned ecologies mingle with Astral lease-patrons and hunger masks. Some bargains work cleanly because the involved being wants only a bounded exchange; others create generational appetite.

## Story Uses

- A harbor's miraculous catch ends whenever no ship is lost.
- A returned boat name belongs to a living captain.
- A shore-right dispute awakens every wreck claimed by the village.
