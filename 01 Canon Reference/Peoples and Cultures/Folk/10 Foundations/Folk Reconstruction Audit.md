# Folk Reconstruction Audit

## Scope

The controlled pre-pass search identified **84 active files** using direct Folk, Fäskogarfolk, Enaidfae, Chapel-Folk, or Wolfsmere terms. A broader post-pass search using clan, person, and place names identifies **196 active contextual files**. This audit classifies them by reproducible path and authority rules.

## Rewritten or Newly Authoritative

Every note beneath `01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/` was rewritten, created, metadata-corrected, or confirmed against the living Folk umbrella. The imported `Lesser Beings` note and `Early Folk Clan Worlds` were replaced. Ättar files were moved to a separate culture root and received only terminology and metadata corrections.

## Terminology or Authority Updated

Cross-vault authorities received direct corrections where they described the Folk as vanished, treated Enaidfae as a separate ancestry, used the old overview link, or mapped Folk territories as Trium subdivisions. This includes Start Here, chronology, historical spine, early ages, Gazetteer, Geography Atlas, political map controls, Eclessian formation and Sylvan Marches, Grobi, Grenzburg, cultures navigation, and relevant adventure metadata.

## Preserved as In-World Testimony

Comparative histories retain biased descriptions of Folk or Enaidfae raids where those descriptions are compatible with an author's limited perspective. Their claims remain governed by [[Reliability Register]] and do not override [[Folk Canon Register]].

## Intentionally Unchanged

Remaining matches were retained where they refer to a valid clan, person, adventure role, old-rite Enaidfae institution, Chapel-Folk community, Wolfsmere event, or relationship already compatible with the reconstruction. Contextual occurrence does not make those notes an authority on Folk canon.

## Archive

The source snapshot is [[Folk - 2026-07 Pre-Reconstruction Archive Index]]. Its files are obsolete and author-only. [[Folk Reconstruction Migration Manifest]] records the physical migration and title changes.

## Authority Ruling

If an adventure, source text, or intentionally unchanged dossier conflicts with the reconstruction, authority descends through [[Folk Canon Register]], [[The Making of the Folk]], [[Historical Spine of the Folk]], [[Folk Faith and Appeasement]], and the regional hubs.
