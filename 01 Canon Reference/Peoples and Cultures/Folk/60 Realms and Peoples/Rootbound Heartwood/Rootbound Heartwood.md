# Rootbound Heartwood

The Rootbound Heartwood is the deep south-central forest, a country of immense trees, bogs, cold rivers, hidden clearings, and settlements arranged around inherited boundaries rather than roads.

## People and Government

Old-rite Enaidfae identity is strongest here. Hearth-clans form grove confederacies capable of maintaining store villages, craft clearings, judges, and war hosts while leaving large territories apparently empty. Authority depends upon kinship, remembered place-law, and access to seers who understand several overlapping courts.

## Technology and Settlement

Iron is valuable and unevenly distributed. Woodcraft, hidework, textiles, herbal practice, charcoal, traps, winter storage, and landscape concealment are highly developed. Settlements are dispersed around a defended hall and hidden reserve granaries so one raid cannot destroy the community.

## Faith

The [[The Antler Hall|Antler Hall]] and [[The Court Beneath the Roots|Court Beneath the Roots]] are widespread court traditions, but every valley preserves local masks and exceptions. Human sacrifice is lawful in several confederacies during famine, failed kingship, or proven court breach; neighboring clans condemn the practice.

## Economy

The Heartwood exports resin, wax, honey, furs, rare medicines, timber, amber, charcoal, and guides. Foreign extraction is dangerous because apparently unused forest may be a migration path, burial reserve, or court boundary.

## Winter War

Grobi migration cuts through eastern forest corridors. Heartwood scouts excel at ambush, false trails, hidden stores, and moving whole settlements before contact. Their low visible population leads outsiders to underestimate how quickly a confederacy can gather.

## Present Conflicts

- old-rite confederacies disagree over human sacrifice;
- Sestaran and Eclessian timber claims penetrate sacred country;
- displaced clans seek new territory after repeated Grobi winters;
- an Idol mask may be teaching that every boundary requires blood.
