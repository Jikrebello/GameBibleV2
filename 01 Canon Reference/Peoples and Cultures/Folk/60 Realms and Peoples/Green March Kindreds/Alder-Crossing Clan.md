# Alder-Crossing Clan

The Alder-Crossing Clan is a Green March kindred of [[Alderway Vale]]. Its households maintain fisheries, ford marks, refuge halls, and winter paths along the Alderwater. The name describes an obligation as much as an ancestry: keeping a crossing includes guiding strangers through flood, receiving fugitives until their danger is heard, and reopening the salmon channels after icebreak.

## Membership and Authority

The clan contains old-rite households, Chapel-Folk cousins, adopted road families, and several Grenzburger spouses. It is governed through a seasonal moot of hearth heads, ferry keepers, and witnesses to sanctuary law. A chosen **Crossing-Speaker** negotiates with outsiders but cannot sell clan land or dissolve sanctuary rights alone.

For matters of immediate danger, a **Reed-Captain** organizes scouts and refuge escorts. Fishery elders settle channels and harvest turns. Chapel-Folk households maintain a small wayside chapel and participate in the moot without claiming that its old-rite members are parish tenants.

## Rights in Alderway

The clan's recognized customary rights are:

- seasonal fishing and reed harvest on the lower Alderwater;
- free use of three marked crossings and the paths joining them;
- refuge obligations at two winter halls;
- protection of Still-Hearth Glen from iron work, permanent cutting, and armed pursuit;
- consultation before a temporary ducal road becomes a permanent jurisdictional strip.

These rights do not give the clan authority to murder road workers, rob travelers, or close every route west. The clan accepts that Grenzburg's winter supply need is real. Its dispute is over line, cost, passage, and what follows the first wagon.

## Internal Positions

- **The Bend Party** supports a shared river road if the Duke funds floodworks, recognizes passage rights, and forbids settlement inside the sanctuary bounds.
- **The Closed Boughs** want the old military trace abandoned and regard every permanent road as the beginning of eviction.
- **The Chapel Hearths** favor an agreement witnessed by both the clan moot and local clergy, fearing that an unwritten peace will die with its negotiators.
- **Young ferrymen and charcoal families** want wages and market access but oppose outside tolls that would turn them into trespassers at their own crossings.

## Present Crisis

The clan did not authorize [[The Red Garland]] and does not accept its killings as defence of the vale. Some frightened households nevertheless hide from ducal patrols, knowing officials may treat any Folk absence as evidence of conspiracy. The clan's willingness to assist the road depends on whether the road company distinguishes witnesses from predators and rights from obstruction.

## Story Uses

- A Crossing-Speaker must prove that a compact binds households that voted against it.
- Refuge law protects someone whom Grenzburg law wants arrested.
- A new bridge improves winter travel but destroys a spawning channel unless redesigned.
- A road murder is blamed on the clan because the true attacker copied one of its warnings.
