# Green March Kindreds

The Green March is the northern Folk frontier below the Sylvan Marches and around Grenzburg. Parish, ducal, and clan law fade into one another rather than meeting at a surveyed border.

## People and Government

Small clans, mixed marcher families, Chapel-Folk congregations, river leagues, and old-rite kindreds share the region. Speakers negotiate with lords and towns; War-Keepers maintain forest patrols; seers guard surviving court boundaries. Confederacies form around rivers, sanctuaries, and Grobi corridors rather than one ethnicity or crown.

## Technology and Settlement

The Green March has the broadest access to mature iron, crossbows, mills, masonry, coin, and written law. Folk longhouses stand beside chapels, reused Atherian walls, timber ringworks, and marcher market villages. Adoption of foreign tools does not settle who owns the land.

## Faith

Old-rite courts, Chapel-Folk parishes, Freeflame meetings, and Eclessian mission houses all operate here. The [[The Ashen Guest Court|Ashen Guest Court]] thrives along roads and disputed hospitality. Wolfsmere lies within this political world but outside ordinary settlement.

## Economy

Timber, herbs, charcoal, animals, ferries, guides, wage labor, smuggling, and seasonal markets connect the clans to Eclessia. The main conflict is the translation of customary access into permanent written concessions.

## Winter War

The region receives refugees and Grobi bands displaced from deeper southern corridors. Settlements maintain beacon chains, refuge halls, and double stores. Distant volunteers taking the Frost-Road often assemble here before moving farther south or east.

## Present Conflicts

- sanctuary charters versus logging and settlement claims;
- Chapel-Folk recognition and land rights;
- Shattered-Tusk vengeance after broken treaties;
- Ghost-Foot use of dangerous local courts;
- ducal attempts to turn temporary roads into permanent jurisdiction.
