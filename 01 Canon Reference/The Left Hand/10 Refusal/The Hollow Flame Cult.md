# The Hollow Flame Cult

The flagship counter-church of the Adversary — the one heresy both great churches jointly condemn by name, because it is not a doctrinal error but an **inversion of the whole project**.

## Doctrine

The Hollow Flame teaches that the White Flame is a cage: coherence is control, communion is dissolution of the self into a landlord God, and the Weighing is the final audit of a rigged ledger. The true fire, they preach, is **the flame that casts no light** — the will's own heat, owing nothing, illuminating no one, warming only its bearer. Freedom is severance. Their liturgy is precise inversion: **the Quenching** (un-baptism: the candidate's baptismal name written in water and drunk away), the **Unlit Vigil** (a night around a cold brazier, each member feeding it something precious that will never burn), and the **Sacrament of the Closed Hand** (formal renunciation of one debt of love — the cult's true price of admission, paid in relationships).

## Structure

Cells, not hierarchy — refusal doesn't organize well, which is its weakness and its resilience. Ranks by combustion metaphor: **Wicks** (initiates), **Tapers** (cell leaders), **Pyres** (regional masters, perhaps thirty alive), and above them nothing anyone has proven — though every interrogated Taper eventually mentions **the Unburning**, in the tone of someone quoting a superior they have never met. The cult's missionary arm is **the Unlit**: the Adversary's false-prophet apparatus, itinerant preachers who never attack the Church directly but arrive after it fails someone: the widow the assurance sellers fleeced, the veteran the crusade spent, the Folk convert the parish shuns. *They do not lie about the wound. They sell severance as the cure.*

## Signs

Cold braziers in used rooms; congregations that stop attending anything without visible crisis; the water-written name rite's residue (a bowl, a dry pen); marriages and oaths dissolving in clusters; and the tell the Inquisition trusts — **people who have stopped arguing.** The converted don't debate. They've closed the account.

## Methods and Relations

They recruit at the Church's failure-points, which makes every Church abuse their harvest — the Reformation era has been their best century since the Purge. They use the Idol networks as *softening agents* (a community farmed by Envy or debt converts easier) while despising them as feeders; the Idols return the contempt (a refused soul produces nothing). The Undertow gives them professional courtesy rates.

## Hooks

- A Grenzburg winter camp stops praying — no incident, no preacher anyone saw; the trail leads to one Unlit who left three weeks ago.
- A Pyre requests sanctuary from the Church, offering the cult's cell-map — the Adversary's Counsel argues, in closed session, that the offer itself is the attack.
- The Sacrament of the Closed Hand: a companion's estranged parent renounced *them*, formally, years ago — and the party finds the record.
