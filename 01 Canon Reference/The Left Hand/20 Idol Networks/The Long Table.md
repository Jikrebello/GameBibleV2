# The Long Table

The Long Table is Gluttony's principal mortal network. It serves the Devourer's feeding economy through excess, engineered want and the conversion of necessary food into social domination. Its human cells operate independently from the ghoul cities, with which particular members sometimes trade corpses, routes and captives. The Devourer feeds through both ecologies without governing either as a resident monarch.

## Doctrine and Method

The Long Table teaches consumption as communion. Its wealthy members hold feast mysteries and banquets whose rare courses demonstrate their ability to consume what others cannot obtain. Beneath these ceremonies lies a system of famine brokerage. Table factors buy standing crops in abundant years, obstruct competing supplies and sell food during the resulting scarcity. The Devourer feeds upon the excess of the feast and the hunger created outside it.

Some established cells exchange cadavers, grave maps or captive routes with particular ghoul lineages. These temporary arrangements carry no authority over other ghouls, and a buyer may betray, consume or expose a Table factor. The discontinuous [[Underworld, Underways, and Corpse-Roads|corpse-roads]] also prevent the creation of a dependable disposal network.

## Structure and Signs

**Provisioners** control supplies, **Maîtres** organise feasts and scarcities from the same records, and the **Fatted Council** coordinates the largest cells. Each councillor is expected to remain visibly starving in accordance with the inner teaching that true hunger can only be honoured. Investigators look for grain moving against ordinary market conditions, feast clubs sharing members with licensed factors and festivals whose saints have disappeared while their ceremonial eating remains.

## Relations

Individual cells obtain finance from the Under-Ledger and seek disposal or intimidation services from particular ghoul lineages. Apathetic communities make poor customers until deprivation restores immediate desire, and have therefore become targets of engineered scarcity. Church fasts place appetite under a discipline the Long Table opposes; since the Reformation began, its money has supported several theological campaigns against fasting.

## Shadowlands Interface

[[Gluttony Masks and Feast-Beasts|Feastlings]] prepare feeding sites, Gut-Maws consume physical and social goods, and Banquet Tyrants turn whole supply systems into courts. [[Ghouls]] remain independent residue-born predators rather than obedient souls of the dead.
