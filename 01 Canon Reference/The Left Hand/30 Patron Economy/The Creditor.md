# The Creditor

> **SPINE-LEVEL SECRET. Nothing in this file is known, in full, by anyone in-world.**

The patron behind Malak al-Ahd — the Astral rent-lord whose masterpiece is a civilization that pays willingly and calls it worship. The largest single villain in the setting, and the quietest.

## The True Name

**ZAQQUM-SHERAI-VETH-ANNUR-QAI**, abbreviated in the spine as **Sarqinyan** ("the acquiring prince," the form its earliest contracts used). The full name has never existed in one place since the patron itself fractured its custody — a rent-lord knows exactly what a held name costs. **Three fragments** persist: one sealed in the deepest stratum of the Vault of Names (catalogued, uncomprehended — the Vault does not know it holds its own god's leash); one worked into the border-calligraphy of the original Covenant revelation scrolls at Al-Qahur (hidden in plain sight in the holiest object of the faith); one lost with a Banu Mizan library ship sunk in the Thalassian, c. 850 AR. Assembling all three would give a binder the single greatest contract-handle in history. This is the setting's buried end-game quest, and at least two parties below suspect it.

## Nature and Operation

An Egg White anchor-sovereign of the first magnitude: old, patient, solvent. Its Malak al-Ahd operation is the greatest rent scheme ever built — a sincere revelation *engineered* through Mujalib, a contract cosmology that routes a civilization's oaths, names, bound devils, and soul-collateral through systems whose ultimate beneficiary no jurist can see. The genius is that the Covenant *works*: the law is real, the bindings hold, the devils dig. The Creditor learned what crude patrons never do: **honest service is the deepest hook.** It witnessed both instruments at the Fourth Seal and ensured the dispute could never be conclusively audited upward. This does not reveal which instrument was genuine; division itself was the profitable outcome.

## The 4,444 Mantles

The beings remembered as the 4,444 are not one infernal species. Sarqinyan assembled heterogeneous bearers: damned manifestations, native Depth beings, captured court and Drift intelligences, Shell patterns, and constructed enforcers. It imposed standardized devil offices upon them through true-name custody and nested authority.

The office supplies role, limits, appearance, and reporting chain. It does not replace the bearer's continuity. Breaking one mantle may expose a prisoner, a willing predator, a still-damned human soul, or an empty script. None of those outcomes reveals the architecture automatically because custody of each name and mandate is compartmentalized.

Public Covenant memory knows only that the 4,444 were bound, that the oasis works, and that their labor proved Mujalib's revelation. The heterogeneous bearer truth and Sarqinyan's ownership remain creator-facing secrets.

## What It Wants

Nothing dramatic: continued upkeep, widening footprint, and above all **no Restoration** — the Redeemer's end-state would foreclose it entirely, which makes it the quiet financier of half the obstacles the Church will ever meet, always at three removes, always deniable.

## Relations

Senior-most member of [[The Concord of Weights]] and bound by its cartel terms, which it authored. It regards the Idols as weather, the Hollow Flame as a nuisance that spoils debtors, and the Undertow as a competitor poaching freight. It has never once acted in the open. It has never once needed to.

## Hooks (long-arc)

- A Vault archivist finds a catalogue entry that has no accession record and reports it upward; everyone in that chain of report dies of natural causes within a decade, in order.
- Sestaran salvage licenses for a stretch of the Thalassian seabed are quietly bought, decade after decade, by a trust with no beneficiary.
- The Mesakha-claimant test, the Vessing ruling, the Zanthir crusade — wherever the board might *resolve*, gold arrives to keep it unresolved. Someone eventually asks who profits from permanent almost.
