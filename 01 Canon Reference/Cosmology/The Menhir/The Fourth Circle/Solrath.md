### Appearance and Forms:
- **Primary Form:** Solrath is the Menhir-expression of **high summer**: heat-load, wildfire weather, glare, thirst, and the violent acceleration of growth into burnout. When mortals describe him as a towering figure in burnished, blood-red armor, they are imposing anatomy onto a phenomenon. What is actually encountered is *summer made sovereign*—air that feels too dense to breathe, sunlight that seems to press down, and mirages that move like living things.
- **Chariot and Steeds:** The “war chariot” and fire-breathing stallions are the most common mythic translation of Solrath’s motion across the world: a fast-moving heat front and ember-carrying winds that lay down scorched tracks.
- **Weapon:** “Sunstrike” is the folk-name for Solrath’s signature mechanism: concentrated thermal violence—flare-hot gusts, lightning-dry storms, and ignition conditions that turn a single spark into a regional event. 

### Personality and Characterization:
- **Embodiment of Summer Pressure:** Solrath is not a moral warlord; Solrath is **seasonal intensity**. Summer is the period where competition becomes visible: water becomes territory, shade becomes resource, and endurance becomes selection.
- **Conquest as Ecology:** The “war” aspect is the natural law of **contest** under stress: rutting fights, predator surges, territorial expansion, raids between scarce watering points, and the way heat forces conflict between all living things trying to persist.
- **Unyielding Drive:** Solrath does not choose targets. Solrath advances wherever climatic conditions allow summer to fully express itself. Mortals interpret this as aggression because it feels personal to be burned.

### Influence and Worship:
- **Temples and Altars:** Sites attributed to Solrath tend to be **sun-baked breakpoints**: desert mesas, salt flats, burned-over forests that regrow wrong, and old stone circles that become too hot to touch at noon. 
- **Rituals:** Practical seasonal rites dominate: water discipline, controlled burns, endurance trials, and sunward vigils that train communities to survive heat-load. Older blood rites exist in some cultures, but these are human attempts to bargain with a system that does not bargain. 
- **Warrior Cults (Reframed):** Those drawn to Solrath are often mercenary bands, raiders, and frontier militias—not because Solrath “wants war,” but because summer scarcity turns human society into a contest ecology. 
- **Omens:** Heat haze that forms repeating corridor-patterns, unseasonable dry thunder, red dust veils, and the sudden appearance of fire following the wind like a pursuing animal. 

### Symbols and Relics:
- **Symbols:** Burning sun, spear, chariot—reinterpreted as **heat, ignition, and seasonal advance** rather than divine militarism. 
- **Relics:** “Sunstones,” fire-hard glass, and heat-proof metalwork are used as **climate anchors**: objects that help predict and survive extreme summer expression. 

### Additional Elements:
- **Legendary Artifact (Mythic Anchor):** “Sunstrike” is best treated as a mythic name applied to rare anchor-objects (star-metal fragments, vitrified stormglass) that can *concentrate* summer conditions in a small radius—useful for forging and siege, catastrophic if mishandled. 
- **Eldritch Influence (Natural Saturation):** Solrath’s presence manifests as heatwaves, spontaneous ignition conditions, and the way living things become restless, irritable, and territorial under sustained heat-load.
