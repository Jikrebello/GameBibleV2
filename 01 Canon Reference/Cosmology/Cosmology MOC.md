# Cosmology and Powers

The World Egg is a created cosmos whose familiar Material world stands among stranger realms of natural law, hunger, elemental force and unfinished possibility. Heaven and Hell lie beyond the Egg's ordinary middle, while the paths of human souls lead through judgement rather than through the courts and kingdoms that surround mortal life.

Every great power within the Egg is created. Menhir, Idols, Patrons, angels and ancient Giants differ in origin, moral condition and manner of existence. Similar names or appearances do not make them members of one pantheon. This page introduces those distinctions and provides a route into the detailed cosmology.

## The Lord, Creation and the first rebellions

The Lord is the uncreated source of being, truth and coherence. The Redeemer is the Lord's decisive entrance into human history, through whom the road of human souls was restored and the White Flame became active in the Church. No created power belongs beside the Lord as a rival god.

The earliest rebellions shaped the realms within the Egg. Angelic offices and their cohorts entered the yolks, where their different forms of refusal produced the Menhir and Idols. The Adversary belongs to the leftward movement of completed refusal and remains distinct from the seven Idol sovereignties.

## The Material world

The Material Realm is the stable world of mortal bodies, weather, food, disease, work and ordinary history. Most people encounter supernatural powers rarely or through institutions, inherited customs and damaged places. The other realms exert pressure upon Material life without replacing its physical and social causes.

## The Faewild and the Menhir

The Faewild is a realm of concentrated natural and mythic law. Its Menhir sovereignties arose when angelic office-holders, their cohorts and the laws they served were forced into shared continuities. They possess memory, preference and power but lack a complete understanding of moral good. Their bargains can preserve a settlement, demand a terrible price or enforce a natural boundary without becoming divine judgement.

The Circles are an Atherian way of describing observed relationships among these powers. Courts contain many kinds of being and do not form one universal monarchy.

## The Shadowlands and the Idols

The Shadowlands give predatory meaning a landscape and ecology. The seven Idols are guilty sovereignties formed from betrayed angelic commissions. Each endures as one Throne, is organised by a Regnant and appears through many cultural Masks. Human desire supplies images, rituals and feeding structures; it did not create the Idols.

The seven sovereignties corrupt provision, courage, rest, appetite, dignity, love and emulation into Greed, Wrath, Sloth, Gluttony, Pride, Lust and Envy. Their cults, creatures and rival kingdoms differ across history.

## The Egg White and its Patrons

The Egg White, also called the Astral Drift, is unfinished reality within the Egg and between its three yolks. Unanchored places lose stable geometry, names and causal order. Sufficient metaphysical weight can hold a route, settlement or demiplane together.

Patron describes a relationship to such an anchor rather than a single species. Mortal spellcasters, escaped Giants, created patterns and stranger persons may therefore become Patrons by different means while retaining their actual continuities. Egg White cities survive through routes, rents, protection and the constant labour of remaining coherent.

## The Elemental Shell

The Elemental Shell is the continuous outer membrane of created reality. Its four joined quadrants express Water, Earth, Air and Fire as states and pressures rather than native civilisations. Human and other refugees have built settlements within prepared Shell refuges, and Material institutions extract elemental force through anchors and warlock rune magic.

## Human souls, Heaven and the soul-road

Human beings bear complete Image-bearing souls. After bodily death, the soul-road leads through the Vestibule of Weighing and towards judgement. The Boatman and other route servants perform particular offices along this passage.

True angels remain created servants acting under commission. Saints are redeemed human persons who may later manifest under similarly bounded commissions. Neither angels nor saints are independent gods, permanent rulers of the living or powers that institutions can compel.

## Hell, the Depth and the Abyss

Hell is the mapped destination and ecology of completed refusal. Its native character is black, saturated and oppressive, shaped by the leftward meeting of Water and Earth rather than by fire. Fiery infernal imagery usually marks the influence of Wrath, ordinary combustion or later religious conflation.

Damned human souls remain distinct from the offices, mantles, bodies and residues through which they exercise power. The Abyss lies beyond mapped infernal dominions as an anti-place and causal injury. Their wars recur because rulers of fixed dominions and forces of unmaking seek incompatible victories.

## Cosmology through history

Creation, the first rebellions and the Faewild Schism precede ordinary human chronology. Later histories follow the wars of Idol kingdoms, the rivalries of Patron cities, heavenly commissions, infernal dominions, lunar Nythalum and constructed refuges. These are several histories rather than one universal secret war.
