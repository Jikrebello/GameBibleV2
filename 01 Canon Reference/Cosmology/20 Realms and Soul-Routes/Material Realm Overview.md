# Material Realm Overview

The Material Realm is the base plane of mortal existence. It is broadly similar to our own world in physical law, ecology, mortality, social life, and historical texture. People farm, marry, inherit, worship, trade, lie, starve, build cities, bury their dead, and argue about power before they ever encounter the stranger layers of the World Egg.

## What Makes It Familiar

- Bodies are fragile and mortal.
- Food, roads, weather, disease, metal, stone, ships, animals, and seasons matter.
- Most people experience magic as rare, institutional, frightening, expensive, regulated, or distant.
- Political power usually flows through land, law, armies, churches, guilds, banks, kinship, and archives.
- The supernatural is real, but daily life is not a constant carnival of miracles.

## What Makes It Vulnerable

The Material Realm sits at the stable center of multiple pressures:

- **Faewild pressure:** nature becomes mythically literal; old laws of beast, season, migration, hunt, fertility, and threshold awaken.
- **Shadowlands pressure:** predatory meaning architectures turn shame, hunger, envy, appetite, violence, or obsession into social engines.
- **Astral pressure:** drift, patrons, rent, demiplanes, and anchor economies leak into mortal politics.
- **Shell pressure:** elemental force can be drawn, scripted, ruptured, or weaponized by warlocks and other engineered systems.
- **Heaven/Hell pressure:** soul-routing, sainthood, refusal, judgement, corruption, and restoration make mortal choices spiritually consequential.

## Human Scale

Life in the Material Realm remains grounded in human needs even where supernatural powers are active. A failed harvest, disputed inheritance, damaged road or unburied body can carry more immediate consequence than the distant intentions of a greater power. Supernatural events become historically important through the changes they make to such ordinary conditions.
