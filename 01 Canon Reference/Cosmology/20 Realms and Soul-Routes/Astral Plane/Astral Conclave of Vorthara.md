# Astral Conclave of Vorthara

The Astral Conclave is the principal known cross-realm patron market accessible from the Material Realm. It is not Vorthara's government, a permanent council of magi, or a body with authority over the city's wards.

It is a recurring market compact assembled during alignments between the Black Spire and the [[Major Cities and Settlements#Lantern Registry|Lantern Registry]]. Each session exists inside the [[Demiplane Atlas#Vortharan Spire-Annex|Vortharan Spire-Annex]] and ends when the host compact closes its tethers.

## Material Hosts

No faction controls the whole market.

- [[Collegium of Doors]] proves routes, contains arrivals, and certifies exits.
- [[The Nightveil Coven]] identifies Shadow counterfeits, residue, and impersonation risks.
- [[The Ironbound Guild]] supplies non-conscious frames, proof boxes, and mass-handling equipment.
- independent brokers provide translation, surety, names, and clients.
- the Wardens of the Crown Road protect the physical ascent without legislating inside the Annex.

The Spire Truce supplies the minimum political permission. Each Conclave session requires a narrower compact among its actual hosts.

## Who May Trade

Known participants include living human institutions, Driftkin processions, [[Anchor-Lords]], Astral route houses, Ossuary Minds, crafted systems represented by human operators, and agents of extrarealm powers whose continuity is declared.

An Idol mask, devil office, or soul-holding patron may enter only through a registered proxy and remains dangerous. Registration proves route and apparent continuity, not honesty or moral legitimacy.

## Core Market Rules

1. Every arrival declares an anchor and demonstrated return route.
2. Every speaker declares whether it is owner, employee, proxy, copy, or bearer.
3. No unregistered mass may be introduced into the Annex.
4. Name extraction requires the named person's witnessed consent and a stated destruction term.
5. Violence, forced embodiment, soulbinding, and untethered summoning void host protection.
6. Nested demiplanes must remain sealed and separately registered.
7. A trader may refuse inspection and leave; refusal does not authorize continued access.

These are compact rules, not metaphysical guarantees. Powerful clients evade them, and poorer clients face harsher scrutiny.

## Commodities

- route maps, tether phrases, and current forecasts;
- relic substrate, true-silt, weight fragments, and anchor shares;
- demiplane access, storage, shelter, and evacuation capacity;
- copied memory, verified testimony, and name services;
- Proxy Bodies, Current Shepherd contracts, and specialist escorts;
- upkeep labor, novelty, ritual schedules, and repair expertise;
- patron pacts and bounded reality edits.

Human souls are not lawful property. The existence of an illegal market does not make such trade valid.

## Enforcement

Proof boxes isolate disputed matter. Collegium doors can close a trader's route. Host factions seize posted mass or deny future alignment. Current Shepherds may escort a dangerous client out. None of these measures can destroy a soul, defeat a sovereign patron at home, or guarantee justice beyond the Annex.

## Why It Matters

The Conclave makes Vorthara the setting's most practical interface with patrons and demiplanes while preserving the city's fragmented politics. It supplies adventure patrons, strange allies, refuge crises, dangerous commerce, and competing interpretations of personhood without turning Vorthara into a unified wizard state.

## Story Uses

- A fair pact is blocked because its Driftkin guarantor cannot satisfy a human inheritance rule.
- A Proxy Body seeks asylum from its owner despite lacking independent personhood, while the human operator hidden inside the tether remains unknown.
- Two Anchor-Lords bring a border dispute that only Material witness can make dense enough to settle.
- A Conclave closes while visitors remain inside an unregistered pocket.
