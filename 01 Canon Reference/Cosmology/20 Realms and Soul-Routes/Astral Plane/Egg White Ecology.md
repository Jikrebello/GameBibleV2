# Egg White Ecology

This reference routes ecological questions to the completed Astral bestiary. It contributes no catalogue coverage.

## Living and Patterned Continuities

- [[Driftkin]] use drift-rewritten-life and never produce a routable human soul.
- [[Protons of the Drift]] and native route predators use astral-pattern.
- [[Patron Servitors#Current Shepherds|Current Shepherds]] and [[Anchor-Lords]] can be fully accountable Astral persons.
- Proxy Bodies and many patron tools display behavior without becoming independent people.
- [[Great Drift Beings]] require form-specific agency rulings.

## Ecological Pressures

- **Drift** favors mobility, tethers, and repeatable self-description.
- **Mass** creates feeding grounds around relics, names, laws, and inhabited places.
- **Rent** creates symbiosis, labor, predation, parasitism, and patron economies.
- **Routes** behave as habitat. A broken lane can starve one species and create another.
- **Made places** produce edge fauna when upkeep sheds instructions, memories, debris, or contradictions.

Astral creatures do not all bargain, keep ledgers, or serve patrons. Many are ordinary predators, scavengers, parasites, refugees, and route specialists within a strange environment.

## Boundaries with the Yolks

Astral pressure can distort a visiting Faewild, Shadowlands, Heaven, Hell, Shell, or Material being without changing its primary origin or continuity. A mask does not become a Proton; a saint does not become an Anchor-Lord; a Depth animal does not become morally neutral.
