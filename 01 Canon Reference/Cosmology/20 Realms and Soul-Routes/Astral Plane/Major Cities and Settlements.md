# Major Cities and Settlements

The Astral Plane has no fixed continent and no universal government. Navigators organize the known Drift around seven anchor regimes. Each name describes a stable behavior and route complex rather than a sovereign country.

## Ossuary Verge

A crescent accretion of broken demiplanes, remains, divine debris, discarded law, and salvage settlements. The **Driftfall Cataracts** form its eroding outer face, where failed places pour into unstructured Drift. The **Crown-Rime Reef** is an authority-rich flank whose fragments encourage convincing but false claims to rank.

## Cairnmarket of the Claim-Ribs

The largest known mixed market for salvage, relic substrate, route knowledge, demiplane mass, and dangerous provenance. Its stalls move along rib-like anchor ridges. Slow storage currents descending toward the **Siltward Deeps** carry sealed memories and lost cargo back years later.

## Lantern Registry

A route complex of inhabited towers, current lamps, and competing navigation houses. A lit lantern certifies that one named route worked under stated conditions; it does not promise safety now. The [[Astral Conclave of Vorthara]] reaches the Registry through its Spire-Annex during scheduled alignments.

## Index-Shelves of Knots

Layered archives of names, route knots, repeated instructions, and tether histories. The **Mnemonic Sargasso** drifts through its lower shelves as a nursery for [[Protons of the Drift|Mnemonic Kites]]. The **Vow-Hives** occupy abandoned galleries where repeated promises have hardened into comb.

## Knot-Sleeper Basin

A braided loop-field held around a dormant [[Great Drift Beings|Sleeping Wrong]]. Routes repeat with small differences, making the Basin valuable for studying unrealized outcomes and dangerous as a source of false return.

## Root-Glass March

The closest known Astral regime to durable land: terraces of weight, fossil law, buried pockets, and extraction camps pressed against the inner face of Earth. Seasonal routes toward the **Cinder Verge** expose the March to heat-driven demiplanes and failed forge realms without making Fire part of Astral nature.

## Breathwaste

A fast region of current-lanes, whisper storms, stolen addresses, and sudden route collapse against the inner face of Air. **Glass-Spawn Shoals** graze on cracked tethers. Mobile settlements survive by sailing rather than resisting the currents.
