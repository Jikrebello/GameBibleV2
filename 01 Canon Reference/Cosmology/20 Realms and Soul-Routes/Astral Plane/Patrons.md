# 1) The Deathless

**Cosmology name:** _Undying Leaseholders_

**What they are:** Persisting intelligences that defeated Drift by becoming **infrastructure**—lich-principalities, embalmed archmages, vow-mummified saints, or “library-beings” whose minds are indexed into an anchor-core.

**Why they pact into yolk-space:**
- To acquire **metaphysical mass** (relics, saint-anchors, repeated oath-sites).
- To outsource “life-risk” tasks (retrieval, assassination, sabotage) without exposing their brittle cores.
- To secure **named legitimacy** in the Material (records, bloodlines, titles).

**How their power feels:** Cold durability; memory vaulting; refusal to die; “you persist because you are filed.”  

**Common pact terms:** quotas of relics; oath-bound service; surrender of a personal name; nightly cataloguing/record rites.  

**Typical patron personality:** Archivist or Wreck-Baron.
## Power motifs
- **Persistence:** refuse death; stabilize wounds; restore function without “healing warmth.”
- **Memory Vaulting:** store, retrieve, redact, or externalize memories.
- **Cold Authority:** command the dead, the forgotten, and the recordless.
- **Reliquary Craft:** infuse objects with endurance; bind a soul-fragment into an implement.
- **Threshold Immunity:** resist drift, glamours, and identity-shear through filing/registration.

## Signature boons
1. **Filed Soul** — Once per day you may “index” yourself for a short duration: you cannot be lost, misdirected, or erased by distortion; you always know the safest exit.
2. **Deathless Reserve** — When you would fall, you instead enter a controlled stasis (or rise with minimal function). You are not saved for free; you are “paused” as an asset.
3. **Reliquary Hand** — You can bind a minor rite into a token (bone bead, sigil strip, sealed page) that triggers later without casting.

## Signature prices / clauses
1. **Quota of Mass** — deliver relic substrate regularly (oath-stone chips, saint-bone dust, name-amber flakes, battlefield filings).
2. **Cataloguing Compulsion** — you must keep a ledger (written, carved, or spoken into a recording rite). If you fail, your memory becomes disordered until you file again.
3. **Name Surrender** — one personal name is surrendered to the patron. You become harder to target, but also harder to be remembered correctly.

# 2) The Heavenly

**Cosmology name:** _Severed Radiants_ (functional right-pole remnants)

**What they are:** Agents once keyed to the Heaven-gradient who now survive by **function** rather than devotion—boundary wardens, heraldic engines, luminous auditors, “lamps” that enforce crossings.

**Why they pact into yolk-space:**
- To keep thin-spaces from cascading into systemic failure.
- To establish **clean thresholds** and procedural “rights of passage.”
- To suppress rival definitions (Idol creep, Shadowland bleed, uncontrolled demiplane growth).

**How their power feels:** Authority-as-physics; edges harden; truth becomes structural; sanctuary behaves like a clause.  

**Common pact terms:** strict procedure; witness duties; taboo protocols; maintenance at liminal sites.  

**Typical patron personality:** Severed Herald (strongest match).
## Power motifs
- **Boundary Authority:** seal doors, define thresholds, forbid crossing.
- **Revelation-as-Structure:** expose hidden passages, false forms, corrupted rites.
- **Sanctuary Geometry:** create safe zones that behave like clauses, not comfort.
- **Audit Fire:** cleanse corruption by enforcing correct pattern, not “purity rhetoric.”
- **Witness Marks:** brand truths into place (signposts, ward-lines, procedural sigils).

## Signature boons
1. **Warrant of Passage** — You may designate a controlled crossing: allies pass; designated threats cannot.
2. **Audit Sight** — You see “fault lines” in reality: thin spaces, false doors, binding points, concealed routes, and rule-inconsistencies.
3. **Clause-Sanctuary** — You create a temporary sanctuary that enforces a simple law (no violence, no lies, no pursuit, no summoning).

## Signature prices / clauses
1. **Protocol Constraint** — in crises you must follow procedure (mark, warn, witness, seal). Breaking protocol suspends boons until repaired.
2. **Witness Duty** — you must report threshold anomalies; failure causes blindness to edges and exits until atonement.
3. **Containment Mandate** — you must prevent certain events from escalating past set thresholds (bloodshed, demiplane instability, idol bleed). If you knowingly allow escalation, you are “deauthorized.”

# 3) The Shadow

**Cosmology name:** _Funerary Sovereigns_ (custodians of endings)

**What they are:** Not an Idol and not a Menhir: an Egg White class of patron that governs **passage, custody, and the bookkeeping of endings**—souls in transit, names of the dead, rightful destinations. Often anchored to ossuary belts, death-cairns, or “ledger-reefs” of fossilized contracts.

**Why they pact into yolk-space:**
- To prevent “unpaid deaths” (souls snagged, bound, stolen, looped).
- To collect **true names** of the dead and settle metaphysical debts.
- To enforce that death remains a **function**, not a resource for others to hoard.

**How their power feels:** Silence, inevitability, marked thresholds; the dead recognize you; bargains are clean and final.  

**Common pact terms:** deliver certain souls; destroy illicit bindings; keep a funerary rite; never speak a prohibited name.  

**Typical patron personality:** Archivist (death-ledger) or Severed Herald (custodial function).
## Power motifs
- **Custody of the Dead:** protect, guide, retrieve, or release souls in transit.
- **Quiet Binding:** silence, stillness, and “no return” thresholds.
- **Ledger of Endings:** track debts, unresolved deaths, stolen destinies.
- **Grave-Authority:** compel oathbreakers, grave-robbers, and illicit binders.
- **Funerary Doors:** open passage for the dead (and sometimes the living at cost).

## Signature boons
1. **Mark of Custody** — You can designate a soul/ghost as “under protection.” Predators and binders find it harder to seize.
2. **Silence of the Threshold** — You can suppress a space: mute sound, prevent scrying, halt haunt phenomena, still a riot into hush.
3. **Reaper’s Due** — You may “collect” a debt from the dying: last breath becomes fuel to power one effect (seal, ward, or passage).

## Signature prices / clauses
1. **No Unpaid Dead** — you must address deaths you encounter that are improperly bound, stolen, looped, or prevented from passing.
2. **Taboo of the Name** — you may never speak certain names (often your patron’s key entries). Violation triggers ledger backlash (your own name becomes “unreliable”).
3. **Coin of Passage** — you must carry and periodically spend a designated funerary tithe (coins, salt, black thread) to maintain your right of transit.

# 4) The Cosmic

**Cosmology name:** _Drift-Titans_ (sleeping wrong, corpse-anchors, regime-minds)

**What they are:** Vast, impersonal presences whose “mind” is more like a **regime** than a personality. They resist Drift via recursive pattern stability, rent loops, or sheer mass—dead gods, failure-state apotheoses, ossuary-verge colossi.

**Why they pact into yolk-space:**

- To introduce **novel variables** that can break a loop or evolve a stuck state.
- To seed anchor-nodes and observers that increase coherence.
- To harvest specific forms of “fuel” (attention, probability, unmade choices, ritual repetition).

**How their power feels:** Unnatural correspondences; reality stutters; you borrow impossibility and pay with coherence.  

**Common pact terms:** periodic pattern-rites; taboo against disrupting loops; offerings of novelty (first experiences, unspoken names, irreversible choices).  

**Typical patron personality:** Engine-like; sometimes Herald-like in its procedural demands.
## Power motifs
- **Probability Shear:** outcomes bend; “almost happened” becomes real.
- **Loop Effects:** echo, delay, repeat, braid time locally.
- **Unnameable Geometry:** spaces fold; corridors connect; distance becomes negotiable.
- **Coherence Borrowing:** you take order now and pay later (debtwork as power).
- **Novelty Injection:** your presence introduces new variables into stuck systems.

## Signature boons
1. **Echo Step** — Once per rest, you may take an action twice in a “split” moment; one version becomes true, the other becomes a costly shadow.
2. **Unwritten Door** — You can open a passage where there “should not be” one, as long as you can describe the correspondence.
3. **Borrowed Coherence** — Brief immunity to distortion, fear, and confusion; you become “too coherent to fray.”

## Signature prices / clauses
1. **Offering of Novelty** — you must provide firsts: a first kiss, first lie, first murder, first oath, first song—something that has never occurred in that context.
2. **Pattern Compliance** — you must perform periodic rites that repeat exactly; improvisation breaks the loop and incurs penalty.
3. **Coherence Leak** — each major use risks a “tax”: memory blur, name-fray, time-lag, or small identity drift until you stabilize.

# 5) The Fey

**Cosmology name:** _Courts of Unfixed Form_ (lease-courts, glamour economies)

**What they are:** Not Menhir themselves, but **lesser sovereign courts** that cannot fully live in yolk logic—predatory pageants, mask-courts, seasonal-free but not Horsemen, glamour economies that survive by leasing stability from anchors and by trafficking in identity and transformation.

**Why they pact into yolk-space:**
- To acquire **anchoring stories** (oaths, roles, reputations) that stabilize their court.
- To smuggle “unfixed” influence into the Material via agents (rare, targeted interventions).
- To harvest **social mass**: titles, romances, scandals, vows—things that become real when believed.

**How their power feels:** Glamour as leverage; metamorphosis as tool; contracts that read like riddles but enforce like iron.  

**Common pact terms:** perform a role; keep a taboo; carry a court-sign; repay with a name, a face, a season of service.  

**Typical patron personality:** Often Archivist (names/roles) or Wreck-Baron (harvesting story-mass), sometimes Route-Baron if you keep that category in your back pocket.
## Power motifs
- **Metamorphosis:** change bodies, roles, talents; shed constraints.
- **Glamour Contracts:** bargains that enforce via symbolism and taboo.
- **Predatory Play:** misdirection that trains adaptation; social hunting.
- **Mask-Authority:** you can wear phases of self as tools.
- **Story-Mass:** belief and reputation become stabilizing currency.

## Signature boons
1. **Mask of the Phase** — You can assume a role-as-form (hunter, lover, judge, child, beast) with associated advantages; the role leaves residue.
2. **Glamour Toll** — You can demand a small payment (a name, a secret, a promise) to open a safe path, gain entry, or avoid conflict.
3. **Unfixed Escape** — Once per rest, you can slip an identity constraint (grapple, curse, pursuit, social bind) by “molting” into a new phase.

## Signature prices / clauses
1. **Taboo of Behavior** — you must keep a court taboo (never refuse a gift, never eat salt, never speak your birth-name, never strike first, etc.).
2. **Role-Service** — you must perform a role when called (messenger, matchmaker, executioner, herald, jester).
3. **Story Debt** — you must seed reputation for the court: spread a rumor, cause a scandal, arrange a legend, preserve a song.

# 6) The Infernal

**Cosmology name:** _Severed Depths_ (left-pole remnants turned lease-feudalists)

**What they are:** Demonic or infernal beings whose connection to Hell/Abyss is degraded by exile into the Egg White. They persist by becoming **resource feudalists**: hoarding anchor-mass, running extraction, enforcing brutal rent economies.

**Why they pact into yolk-space:**

- To regain leverage against rivals by building agent networks in stable worlds.
- To harvest mass that yolk history concentrates (battlefields, cult-sites, disasters).
- To establish “soft gates” that trend leftward over time.

**How their power feels:** Corrosive bargains; appetite as law; strength purchased with debt; protection that feels like ownership.  

**Common pact terms:** quotas; blood/tribute; sabotage; enforce fear through “demonstrations” (not moral—instrumental).  

**Typical patron personality:** Wreck-Baron (strongest match).
## Power motifs
- **Debt as Weapon:** contracts that bite; leverage that escalates.
- **Corrosive Authority:** domination through ownership; “you are mine” as physics.
- **Disaster Extraction:** turning collapse into resource and advantage.
- **Hunger-Law:** appetites that enforce behavior; controlled predation.
- **Hard Protections:** brutal boons that keep you alive and indebted.

## Signature boons
1. **Claim Mark** — You can mark a target or site as claimed; rivals suffer penalties interfering, and your patron’s “rights” manifest as pressure.
2. **Wreckward** — You gain resistance to catastrophic environments (fire, collapse, warp, panic) and can move through disaster zones effectively.
3. **Debt-Fueled Surge** — You can buy power with immediate debt: burst damage, dominance, or survival—then the bill arrives later.

## Signature prices / clauses
1. **Quota and Levy** — periodic delivery of value (relics, bodies, oaths, labor, fear) with compounding debt on failure.
2. **Audit Rights** — your patron may “inspect” your life: seize infused gear, redirect your next job, or demand a demonstration.
3. **No Free Mercy** — you must not forgive debts owed to your patron’s network; mercy undermines their economy.

# Vorthara’s Astral Conclave: Patron Market Profile

## Why Vorthara matters cosmologically

Vorthara is a rare **dual-occupancy node**: the Black Spire is simultaneously a Material landmark and an Egg White stabilizer. Functionally it behaves as:

- **Recursive Pattern Stability:** repeated rites, regulated contests, consistent adjudication cycles create pattern reinforcement.
- **Self-Paying Rent Loop:** the city’s structured magical economy (rituals, duels, contracts, factional rotation) generates continual upkeep.

This makes Vorthara a **safe-ish interface** where Egg White leaseholders can transact with yolk-born mortals without full exposure to Drift.

## Who comes and why (per archetype)

### Deathless

They come for: **records, bloodlines, relic circulation**, and recruitment of agents who can retrieve mass.  
They maintain: archives, reliquaries, controlled necropolis-vaults, name registries.

### Heavenly

They come for: **threshold supervision**—Vorthara’s spire is a gate-risk.  
They maintain: seal-rites, procedural courts, boundary audits, anomaly response teams.

### Shadow

They come for: **custody disputes**—souls snagged by thin spaces, illicit bindings, funeral rights.  
They maintain: funerary halls, dead-ledger courts, quiet passage treaties.

### Cosmic

They come for: **novelty and leverage**—Vorthara is a concentration of choices, experiments, and strange alignments.  
They maintain: restricted chambers, loop-wards, observation pacts, “do-not-repeat” zones.

### Fey

They come for: **story-mass and roles**—Vorthara is a myth engine.  
They maintain: masked salons, taboo markets, pact-theatres, metamorph rites.

### Infernal

They come for: **contracts, salvage financing, and disaster economics**.  
They maintain: debt offices, claim arbitrations, mercenary hiring pits, “recovery” crews for catastrophes.

## The Conclave’s commodities (what is traded)

- **Names and Titles:** especially those that can anchor places or bind beings.
- **Relics and Substrate:** oath-stone, saint fragments, crown filings, bone-shards.
- **Route Knowledge:** safe lanes, sutures, thin-space maps, tether phrases.
- **Ritual Licenses:** rights to operate certain rites within the Spire’s jurisdiction.
- **Debt Instruments:** promises, labor contracts, service bonds, confession notes.
- **Custody Warrants:** legal-metaphysical permissions regarding the dead and the bound.


## The Conclave’s core rule (what is forbidden)

To keep the Spire stable, Vorthara must forbid acts that break pattern or spike entropy debt. Typical prohibitions:

- unlicensed demiplane forging within the city footprint,

- uncontrolled summoning that creates unaccounted “mass arrivals,”

- unsanctioned name-theft (true-name extraction without registry),

- violence outside regulated arenas or sanctioned duels,

- and any rite that permanently damages the Spire’s recurrence cycle.


## What the Black Spire “charges” as rent

Not money—**pattern compliance**. The Spire is maintained by:

- scheduled rites (calendrical, daily, hourly),

- regulated conflict (duels/arbitration instead of faction war),

- archive deposits (records as stabilizing mass),

- and periodic “tithes of novelty” (controlled experiments to prevent stagnation).


In exchange, it provides:

- stable thresholds,

- protected markets,

- and a “neutral floor” where patron agents can meet mortals without immediate predation by Drift.
