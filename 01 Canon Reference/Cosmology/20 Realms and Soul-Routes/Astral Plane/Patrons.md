# Patrons

A patron is an owner or controller of sufficient metaphysical mass to lease stability, access, power, knowledge, shelter, or reality edits beyond its immediate anchor.

Patron is a role. A living human institution, self-bound human soul, Driftkin refuge, Anchor-Lord, Ossuary Mind, construct network, stranded extrarealm being, or other anchor-owner may act as one. Continuity and moral responsibility must be recorded separately.

## Why Patrons Need Agents

An anchor provides power and stability while also imposing locality. Material agents can:

- carry a patron's tether across yolk boundaries;
- find new anchors and route knowledge;
- perform choices that repetitive Astral patterns cannot;
- negotiate with institutions that recognize human testimony;
- repair, sabotage, or collect upkeep at distant sites;
- introduce novelty into a failing loop.

## What Patrons Offer

- shelter from Drift;
- power or spell-like authority borrowed from an anchor;
- route access and safe arrival;
- demiplane residence or storage;
- information, copied memory, or specialized perception;
- Proxy Bodies, Tether Hounds, Current Shepherds, and other services;
- bounded local edits to scale, weather, sequence, access, or identity.

## What They Charge

Upkeep may be money, labor, memory, ritual repetition, names, salvage, novelty, attention, service, political access, or a share of another anchor. A clear price can still be exploitative. An unfamiliar price can still be fair.

The moral questions are whether the patron has a right to what it leases, whether consent is real, what dependence the arrangement creates, who bears risk, and what happens on failure.

## Failure and Foreclosure

Failure can suspend a boon, close a route, repossess leased matter, expose a dependent realm, or activate a stated security. Predatory patrons hide compounding costs, manufacture dependency, seize persons as collateral, or define every act outside the relationship as theft.

No patron contract can create the Image, own a human soul by moral right, reverse judgement, or make soulbinding good.
