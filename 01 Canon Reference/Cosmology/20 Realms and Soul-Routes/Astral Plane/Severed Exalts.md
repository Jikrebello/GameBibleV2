# Severed Exalts

Severed Exalt is a creator-facing dossier category for a named singular being that no longer occupies its original office and now persists through an Astral anchor. It contributes no reusable creature form.

An Exalt may be a fallen created spirit, stranded court-being, escaped office-bearer, Giant Continuant, human soul-holder, Drift person, or autonomous native pattern. The title says nothing about continuity or morality.

## Required Dossier

Every Severed Exalt must state:

1. original identity and continuity;
2. former commission, office, court, or function;
3. how and why severance occurred;
4. present anchor and governing law;
5. whether the visible body is original, proxy, copy, or manifestation;
6. current moral agency and chosen acts;
7. upkeep and dependents;
8. manifestation and return conditions;
9. permanent defeat, release, or containment.

Calling a being an Exalt may conceal more than it reveals. A fallen angel remains governed by [[Fallen Angels and Broken Commissions]]. A human soul-holder remains subject to the soul-road doctrine. A reconstructed personality is not promoted into personhood by title.
