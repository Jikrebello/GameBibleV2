# Elemental Shell and the Four Planes

The Elemental Shell is the outer membrane of created reality. It encloses the Astral drift and the three grounded reality-modes, defines how outside force can enter, and supplies the raw elemental pressure used by [[Warlock Rune Magic]].

The Shell is one continuous membrane containing four joined **plane-quadrants**, not four detached universes.

## The Joined Quadrants

Viewed through the creator-facing World Egg diagram:

| Position | Plane-quadrant | Governing tendencies |
|---|---|---|
| upper left | Water | flow, pressure, dissolution, cold, brine, carried memory |
| lower left | Earth | mass, enclosure, friction, soil, mineral structure, endurance |
| upper right | Air | motion, breath, distance, sound, charge, signal, dispersal |
| lower right | Fire | heat, radiance, release, acceleration, forge, consumption, transformation |

Water touches Earth and Air. Fire touches Air and Earth. Opposed quadrants can be joined only through a script, artificial anchor, or catastrophic fold. This is why frost, mud, smoke, lightning, magma, and ash occur more readily than stable steam or other opposed expressions.

The four planes are neither moral powers nor populations. Their forces do not want, judge, resent, or consent.

## Natural Conditions

Unanchored Shell is mostly abstract pressure, direction, rate, and boundary. It does not naturally contain continents, cities, courts, or elemental nations. A mortal mind encounters it as impossible gradients: weight without ground, flow without water, heat without fuel, or distance without a path.

Recognizable terrain appears where something imposes a stable frame:

- a permanent extraction anchor;
- an ancient Atherian or Giant platform;
- a failed warlock script still repeating its geometry;
- a thin-space wound that forces Shell pressure into Material shape;
- a temporary expedition lattice built around mortal scale and time.

These constructed littorals can acquire weather, channels, deposits, and pattern ecologies. They remain dependent on the frame that makes them legible.

## Manifestations Are Not Persons

An elemental manifestation is a coherent `shell-pattern`. It may pursue, bargain, imitate speech, protect a post, or adapt to repeated opposition. These behaviors arise from boundary, fuel, instruction, and recursive feedback. They do not produce an interior subject, moral agency, or a soul.

No age or complexity turns a pure Shell pattern into a person. Apparent sovereigns are [[Great Coherences]]: disaster-scale natural computations that cultures may mistake for gods or rulers.

This ruling means ordinary rune magic does not enslave elemental people. It can still be reckless, destructive, or cruel toward the humans and living creatures exposed to it.

## Material Manifestation

Most people encounter elemental force through ordinary matter: rivers, storms, mountains, flame, weather, sea, soil, forge, and breath. A Shell manifestation appears only when a pattern is given enough boundary and repetition to maintain a body.

Common causes include:

- an open seam;
- a damaged extraction vessel;
- a repeating battlefield script;
- industrial waste from a permanent anchor;
- a deliberate binding inside a tool, guardian, or engine;
- incompatible scripts forced through the same conductor.

Destroying the visible body may only scatter the pattern. Permanent defeat requires closing its seam, exhausting or grounding its force, breaking its instruction, or removing the frame that lets it recur.

## Crossing the Shell

Mortals cannot walk into a plane-quadrant through an ordinary portal. A rare **threshold delve** requires a stable anchor, a Material reference frame, Astral ballast, environmental protection, and a maintained return relation. Failure can strand the expedition inside an abstract pressure field or collapse the platform into a Material leak.

Fourteen permanent anchors operate in 1360 AR. They belong to Vorthara, Ironvale, the Qahirid Covenant, and Thalmyria. Their exact access geometries remain strategic secrets recorded in [[Shell Anchors and Extraction]].

## Extraction and War

Anchored platforms recover four strategic products:

- **forge-charge:** stored heat and release used by foundries, artillery, and emergency industry;
- **pressure brine:** stabilized flow and cold used in hydraulics, preservation, cutting, and naval works;
- **stormglass:** charge, vibration, and signal held in prepared glass or wire;
- **weight-seed:** compressed structure used in rapid fortification, mining, foundations, and armor work.

Extraction is not authorially sinful or inherently damaging. It is a technique. Forced labor, unsafe overdraw, concealment, indiscriminate weapons, and exposing civilians to failure remain morally answerable human choices.
