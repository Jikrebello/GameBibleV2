# Elemental Shell and the Four Planes

The Elemental Shell is the outer membrane of created reality. It bounds the World Egg and defines how raw elemental force can enter, resist, or be shaped by the lower realities. It is not just scenery around the world; it is the source-domain that makes elemental war magic possible.

## The Four Shell Quadrants

- **Water:** flow, dissolution, pressure, depth, cold, blood, brine, memory carried in current.
- **Earth:** weight, enclosure, stone, soil, burial, endurance, pressure, mineral body.
- **Air:** breath, distance, sound, storm, motion, signal, height, dispersal.
- **Fire:** heat, transformation, light, burning, forge, hunger, purification, destruction.

## Relationship to the Material Realm

The Material Realm does not usually touch the Shell directly. Most mortals experience elemental force through ordinary matter: rivers, storms, mountains, flame, weather, sea, soil, forge, and breath.

Warlock rune magic is exceptional because it scripts a controlled draw from the Shell into the Material Realm.

## Shell Leakage

Shell leakage happens when elemental force enters the world without proper mediation. It can create:

- weather that behaves like judgement rather than climate,
- fires that burn concepts or names instead of only fuel,
- floods that erase memory or borders,
- stone growths that seal roads, graves, or cities,
- storms that carry voices from impossible distances.

## War and Engineering

Elemental Shell force is the backbone of engineered war magic: siege fire, ice barricades, lightning signals, acid breachwork, battlefield fog, stonework reinforcement, storm screens, and ritual artillery.

The more direct the draw, the greater the risk of Shell burn: bodily damage, instrument failure, local elemental contamination, runaway scripts, or thin-space formation.
