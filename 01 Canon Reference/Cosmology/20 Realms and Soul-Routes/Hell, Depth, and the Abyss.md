# Hell, Depth, and the Abyss

This is the authorial topology for the leftward destination. In-world maps, sermons, cult texts, and contract manuals preserve partial or interested versions.

## Four Depths of Approach

### 1. The Chthonic Breach

The approach appears where the Water and Earth aspects of the Shell meet leftward pressure: weight, saturation, burial, sediment, and choking viscosity. Material thin spaces often resemble flooded crypts, deep wells, mud chambers, or tunnels beneath foundations.

This geometry explains the aesthetic. It does not make ordinary mud, water, burial, or underground life evil.

### 2. The Mapped Mire

Hell's dominant navigable ecology is a black-mud mire containing refusing human souls, manifested damnations, native Depth fauna, imposed works, and temporary dominions. Distances, routes, and landmarks can be mapped at great cost.

Hell does not complete damnation after arrival. A human soul reaches it after judgement has made completed refusal definitive. The mire expresses and sustains that destination; it does not offer a second mortal life in which the verdict can be reversed.

### 3. Dominions and Office Works

Damned rulers, devil mantles, patrons, and other powers build local prisons, courts, armies, roads, and extraction works. These structures overlap, compete, and collapse. None constitutes a universal infernal government.

`Devil` names an imposed office within such architecture. The mantle's continuity is `infrastructure-office`; its bearer retains a separate continuity and identity.

### 4. The Abyss

Beyond mapped Hell lies the Abyss. It is not another province but anti-place: collapsed causality, severed relation, impossible depth, and wounds in creation. Hell can be mapped. The Abyss can only be approached through what it has already damaged.

Abyssal beings use `continuity: abyssal-wound`. Their body recurs until the contradiction permitting it is closed.

## Human Souls

- A human soul cannot be annihilated by a created predator, office, patron, Idol, Menhir, or Abyssal wound-being.
- A soul can be held, obscured, mutilated, deprived of memories or attachments, misdirected, or exploited.
- Such injuries are real and may make mortal rescue extraordinarily difficult.
- Neither concealment nor damage changes the Lord's judgement or converts a human soul into another continuity.
- Breaking an exploitative office can free a damned bearer from that office without reversing damnation.

## Manifestation and Summoning

Infernal entities enter the Material Realm through true names, contracts, physical anchors, damaged soul-routes, leftward thin spaces, or Abyssal contradictions. Mortals can sometimes coerce a manifestation because these routes exploit created law.

Coercion is never safe domestication. A correct circle can hold one mandate while leaving appetite, bearer, reporting chain, and hidden beneficiary untouched.

White Flame can reveal provenance, protect a human soul, repel a manifestation, close an anchor, and restore coherence. It cannot force repentance, erase a soul, or mechanically redeem the damned.

## Distinct Hostile Economies

- [[The Adversary]] recruits and intensifies refusal.
- [[The Idols Overview|Idols]] feed through vice-shaped meaning ecologies.
- [[The Creditor]] and other patrons profit through rent, custody, and dependence.
- [[The Undertow]] intercepts and redirects soul passage.
- Damned, Depth fauna, devil mantles, and Abyssal wounds may be used by any of these without becoming one faction.
