## 1) The Outer Absolutes

### The Lord
- The absolute source of being and authority.
- All-powerful, yet permits free will; all other powers act only within allowance.

### The Adversary
- The absolute opposing pole: exile, negation, corruption, refusal.
- Dwells beyond Hell at the farthest extreme of separation.

These are not peers. They are the two terminal ends of the metaphysical axis.

---

## 2) The Cosmic Axis: Heaven ↔ Abyss

Reality is arranged along a gradient:

- **Far Right:** Heaven, culminating in the Lord’s seat. The Redeemer stands at the Lord’s right hand.
- **Far Left:** Hell, culminating further left in the Abyss where the Adversary is exiled.
- **Middle:** The World Egg—where creation operates, where mortals live, and where most conflict occurs.

This axis is not only “where,” but “how reality behaves”:

- Rightward tendencies: light, ascent, coherence, revelation, alignment.
- Leftward tendencies: depth, pressure, inversion, corruption, descent.

---

## 3) The World Egg

The World Egg is the bounded middle-reality between the outer absolutes.

### The Shell: Elemental Boundary-Realms

The Egg is enclosed by a shell of four elemental realms functioning as a **membrane**: they separate materially stable reality from purely metaphysical extremes, and regulate passage.

- **Air and Fire** resolve at the far-right junction into **Light**, the admissible condition for the rightward approach to Heaven.
- **Water and Earth** resolve at the far-left junction into **Depth**, the admissible condition for the leftward approach to Hell.

These junctions form the two extreme **Gates** within the shell:

- **The Lumen Threshold** (right gate)
- **The Chthonic Breach** (left gate)

---

## 4) The Three Yolks: The Inner Reality System

Inside the Egg are three coexistent “yolks”—three primary modes of reality.

### A) The Faewild (Right Yolk)

- A mirror-realm shaped by **mythic nature**: predation, metamorphosis, fertility, migration, storm, thresholds, ecological law.
- Its powers are natural phenomena given sovereignty—forces that would exist even without mortals.

### B) The Shadowlands (Left Yolk)

- An inverted mirror shaped by **psychic structure and conceptual gravity**: fear, obsession, shame, desire, identity fracture, social pressure.
- Its powers are predatory meaning-architectures—structures that feed through mind and culture, but are not reducible to them.

### C) The Material World (Center Outcome)

- The physical world mortals inhabit: stable, consistent, “real” in the everyday sense.
- It is not isolated; it is the **result** of the overlap of the Faewild and Shadowlands.

### Projection and Overlap

The Material exists where Faewild and Shadowlands **overlay** into a stable composite.

Where this alignment is imperfect, the world produces:

- **Thin spaces**: leaks, bruises, and mismatches where a yolk intrudes or where the Egg White seeps through.

---

## 5) The Egg White: Interstitial Drift

Between and around the yolks—still inside the shell—lies the Egg White: a high-entropy realm of raw metaphysical medium.

It is the primary environment for:

- demiplanes and pocket-realms,
- dead or sleeping god-remains and divine debris,
- patron micro-kingdoms and stability markets,
- lost structures that do not belong cleanly to either yolk.

### Governing Laws of the Egg White

1. **The Law of Drift**  
    Unanchored reality dissolves toward low structure.
2. **The Law of Metaphysical Mass**  
    Weight creates place. Ontological density pins stability (relic cores, god-carcass fragments, fossilized laws, true-name hoards).
3. **The Law of Entropic Rent**  
    Stability is leased. Any ordered pocket accrues entropy debt and must pay upkeep or be repossessed by drift.

---

## 6) How Beings Cross into the World (Layered Manifestation)

Beings from the extremes rarely appear fully in the Material because entry requires **stacking reality layers**:

1. **Egg White:** a preliminary metaphysical body (requires anchor or upkeep).
2. **Yolk Overlay:** interpretive shape-language (Faewild yields mythic-natural forms; Shadowlands yields negative/psychic forms).
3. **Material:** full substance and persistence.

This is why high ritual and rare conditions are usually required for true manifestations.

---

## 7) The Great Power Categories Within the Egg

### The Menhir

- The Faewild’s sovereign laws: nature-principles made godlike.
- Tiered by “circles” (scale and fundamentalness).
- They are forces of nature first; cults and myths are secondary.

### Father Sun and Mother Moon (Second Circle Anchors)

- The dominant luminaries of the second circle, with specialized offspring:
    - **Solar Sons** (aspects of Father Sun’s portfolio)
    - **Midnight Daughters** (aspects of Mother Moon’s portfolio)

- These star children are lesser than the circle powers and distinct from the seasonal Horsemen of later circles.

### The Idols

- The Shadowlands’ sovereign engines: devouring patterns given rule.
- Organized around core predatory structures that feed through identity and society.
- They are not symbolic morals; they are operational metaphysical ecologies.

### Heavenly Servants and Road Mechanisms

- **True angels** are created heavenly spirits with full agency. Loyal angels serve through the Lord's commission; they are not redeemed humans.
- **Manifested saints** are redeemed human souls sent after judgement under bounded commissions. They retain earthly identity and cannot fall after final communion.
- **The Boatman** is a unique true angel appointed as psychopomp and custodian of the soul-road.
- **Road mechanisms** are impersonal infrastructure-office manifestations used to guide, separate, hunt, and ward when passage is endangered.

Authority follows divine commission rather than origin or raw power. Mortals may petition for heavenly aid but cannot summon or command it mechanically.

---

## 8) Death, Judgement, and the Routes of Souls

### The Vestibule of Weighing

When mortals die, their souls are received in a near-material holding demiplane: the Vestibule.

There, disposition is resolved as a convergence of:

- the Redeemer’s authority to reveal truth of a life, and
- the soul’s consent: communion with the Lord or refusal.

### The Boatman

- A unique created angel who serves as custodian and transporter of souls.
- He does not judge; he routes souls after disposition is resolved.

### The Official Roads and the Two Gates

Once disposition is resolved, souls move along the appropriate route:

- Rightward toward the **Lumen Threshold** (the shell’s right gate) and onward to Heaven.
- Leftward toward the **Chthonic Breach** (the shell’s left gate) and onward to Hell, and beyond to the Abyss as the ultimate exile pole.

### The Empty Quarter

In the pre-Redeemer age, the Vestibule also served as a universal holding for the dead. That function has ended; the space remains, largely empty.

---

## 9) Saints, Sinners, and the Human War for Souls

### Saints

- Living saints are mortals sanctified in life by the Lord’s **White Flame**.
- Manifested saints are those redeemed human souls sent from Heaven after judgement under a particular commission.
- Elite agents and exemplars of the Redeemer’s single church.
- Their focus is not only conflict but also building and advancing the church through proclamation, formation, and defense.
- Two major emphases exist within the one body (institutional/juridical vs mystical/liturgical), producing different saintly orders and methods.

**Relics** are valued because they carry rightward metaphysical mass: portable anchors that stabilize, cleanse, and close fractures.

### Sinners

- Mortals who consciously align with refusal and with Adversary or Adversary-adjacent powers.
- They do not form a single unified church; they arise as multiple competing counter-churches and cult ecologies.
- They compete for souls via recruitment, pacts, debt structures, false rites, and corruption campaigns.

### Yolk-Attuned Operatives

- **Verdants:** mortals inducted into Faewild pacts and Menhir-aligned regimes.
- **Hollows:** mortals shaped into Shadowlands hunger-logics and Idol-aligned infrastructures.  
    They matter locally, but generally do not match Saints or Sinners in strategic reach.

---

## 10) Eschatology: The Restoration Doctrine

The Redeemer’s faith holds that at the end of days:

- the yolks will be restored to perfect alignment, removing thin spaces,
- the Egg White’s drift economies and contaminating intermediaries will be purged or rendered inert,
- the gates of Hell will be closed,
- the Adversary will be defeated and destroyed,
- and a renewed eternal era will begin under the Redeemer’s rule in full communion with the Lord.

This belief is not abstract: it threatens every power that depends on misalignment—Idols, drift patrons, and leftward regimes.

# One-Page “Cosmology Diagram in Words”

**FAR RIGHT**  
Heaven → the Lord’s seat (Redeemer at the right hand)

**RIGHT GATE (within the shell)**  
Air + Fire → Light → **Lumen Threshold**

**WORLD EGG (bounded middle)**

- **Shell:** four elemental boundary-realms

- **Inside:**
    - **Faewild (right yolk):** mythic nature laws → Menhir (circles) → Father Sun/Mother Moon → Solar Sons/Midnight Daughters
    - **Shadowlands (left yolk):** psychic inversion/hunger architectures → Idols
    - **Material (center outcome):** stable overlap projection

- **Between/around yolks:** **Egg White** (drift, mass, rent) → demiplanes, patrons, dead god debris
 

**THIN SPACES**  
Projection bruises where yolks misalign or Egg White leaks

**DEATH ROUTE**  
Material → **Vestibule of Weighing** (truth + consent) → Boatman routes → Rightward or Leftward road

**LEFT GATE (within the shell)**  
Water + Earth → Depth → **Chthonic Breach**

**FAR LEFT**  
Hell → beyond: Abyss (Adversary’s exile)
