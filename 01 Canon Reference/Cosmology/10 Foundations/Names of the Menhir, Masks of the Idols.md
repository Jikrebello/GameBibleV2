# Names of the Menhir, Masks of the Idols

**Authorial truth:** naming drift reflects real encounters interpreted through culture. A great power does not have one universally spoken name; it has one continuity and many masks. [[The Great Rebellion and the Yolk Exiles]] controls origin. A cultural genealogy may preserve pressure and relationship while remaining wrong about divine parentage.

## Rule for the Vault

Authorial spine documents use plain creator names such as Father Sun, Mother Moon, Wrath, Greed, Faewild, and Shadowlands. Cultural and in-world documents should use the names their witnesses know. A mask is not automatically an error, but it never overrules the creator-facing concordance.

## The Menhir

The Menhir are former angelic office-holders fused with their cohorts and Faewild natural law. They are intelligent `yolk-sovereignty`, not gods, saints, moral judges, or ordinary fallen angels. Their exactness and caprice are governed by [[Menhir Sovereignty and Bargain]].

- **Father Sun** = **Solzareth**, the deep court-name used in Faewild-facing texts and Atherian solar religion. Folk accounts emphasize exposure and drought; Atherian accounts emphasize order and public visibility. Both encounter the same law.
- **Mother Moon** = **Lunara / Lunara Noctis**, the power behind [[Mother Moon's Bargain]]. Ättar covenant language preserves real terms but mistakes exact administration for kinship.
- **Astaroth, Lyraxis, Orionis, Hecatara, Typhonius,** and **Arachnara** formed the supernatural core of the Atherian Star Pantheon. “Star Child” is an imperial family-title, not literal birth.
- **Sylvorn, Thalassor,** and **Zephyraxis** are the separated world-body sovereignties. **Trium** is their rare temporary phase-lock, not a fourth person.
- **Solrath, Nevaura, Morvalis,** and **Veradia** are the four seasonal sovereignties. War, famine, death, and pestilence are mortal catastrophe titles for summer intensity, winter scarcity, autumnal ending, and spring proliferation.
- **Thanatos** is the decisive Atherian error. The empire encountered [[The Boatman]], a created angel of the soul-road, then inserted him into the Star family. The title remains valid cultural evidence and false cosmology.
- **Kokh, the Blue Eternal** is a Tarkhan composite reading of several sky-aligned pressures rather than another Menhir. Lesser Orionis courtiers mediate the falcon bond described in [[Falconry and Sky-Riding]].

The Second, Third, and Fourth Circles are observed Atherian taxonomies shaped by [[The Faewild Schism]]. They are not governments, ranks, or bloodlines.

## The Idols

The Idols are morally culpable `yolk-sovereignty` and meaning-predators. Unlike the Menhir, they retained moral comprehension and deliberately organized their betrayed commissions into feeding economies. Their many masks are active methods of intrusion.

- **Wrath** = **Furor** in Xolotl blood-cult texts = **the Fury** in Grobi origin traditions.
- **Greed** = **Avarix**, the serpent-mask of Ishkatar.
- **Pride** = **the Sovereign**, patron-mask of Ansharan rule and primordial vampirism.
- **Gluttony** = **the Devourer**, named in ghoul rites and corpse-feast traditions.
- **Sloth** = **the Slumberer**, whose heartland is the Endless Dusk.
- **Envy** = **the Rival**, the voice that says another's good should have been yours.
- **Lust** = **the Beloved**, the counterfeit of faithful attachment.

Fauna, Masks, and Archmasks are separated in [[Idol Manifestation and Feeding]]. Mortal networks are indexed through [[The Atlas of the Left Hand]].

## Archaic Terms

- **The Dreamlands** is an old scholarly name for the Shadowlands. It remains valid in pre-Split source texts and deprecated in creator-facing prose because “dream” understates predation.
- **God**, **goddess**, **child**, **spouse**, and **sibling** are acceptable in in-world religion. Authorial prose treats them as interpretations unless a controlling note establishes an actual relationship.
