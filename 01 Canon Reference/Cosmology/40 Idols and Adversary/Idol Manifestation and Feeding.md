# Idol Manifestation and Feeding

The Shadowlands are a prehuman yolk of predatory meaning, unrealized identity, fear, appetite, and imitation. Human thought can shape routes and local weather within it, but did not create it. The seven Idols became sovereign by fusing with this ecology during the [[The Great Rebellion and the Yolk Exiles|Great Rebellion]].

## Why Mortal Choice Matters

Shadow life can repeat fear, hunger, possession, or comparison forever. Repetition alone is thin food. Human beings can recognize a good and freely turn it toward harm. That chosen distortion possesses metaphysical density unavailable to reflexive Shadow fauna.

The Idols therefore cultivate mortal institutions rather than relying only on isolated temptation. A feud inherited by law, a market designed around dispossession, a household organized around coercion, or a church office used for self-worship can reproduce chosen vice across generations. Scale, inheritance, and repeated choice make the harvest dense.

This does not remove personal culpability or make every participant equally guilty. The Idol feeds most richly where people understand enough to choose, where harms are made durable, and where ordinary goods are used to conceal the predation.

## The Three Manifestation Classes

### Fauna

Fauna are reflexive `shadow-pattern` or `idol-pattern` life. They hunt according to a vice-shaped ecology and possess no or limited moral agency. They can be redirected, starved, trapped, or destroyed as creatures.

### Masks

Masks are projected organs of an Idol or a feeding network. They display cunning, speech, and a role, but depend upon a mortal practice, object, title, dream-route, or dominion condition. A mask is not a shard of a human soul and does not become independent merely because it pleads.

### Archmasks

Archmasks are autonomous Shadow persons with full moral agency. They can improvise, betray their sovereign, establish a local court, or pursue private cruelty. They remain products of a chosen evil ecology and no routine redemption method exists. A named archmask may require a dedicated dossier if an adventure makes its history decisive.

## Material Intrusion Ladder

1. **Suggestion:** dreams, coincidences, compulsive comparisons, appetite, or emotional weather reveal a route.
2. **Sign:** fauna or a minor mask forms around repeated conduct.
3. **Architecture:** a custom, room, relic, office, supply chain, or public ritual begins paying the ecology reliably.
4. **Dominion overlay:** a bounded Material site temporarily obeys part of the Idol's realm logic.
5. **Archmask court:** an autonomous agent uses the overlay to act, recruit, and bargain.

A full Idol never crosses the fifth stage. Its sovereign identity cannot fit Material reality. Claims that an Idol walked bodily through a city describe a mask, a dominion overlay, propaganda, or a catastrophic witness error.

## Moving Heartlands

Each Idol has a recognizable heartland, but none has a fixed continental border. Mortal history changes routes. A war can draw Wrath's marches across Greed's vaults; a famine can let Gluttony's banquet occupy Sloth's still country; public acclaim can open Pride's palace inside Envy's replacement halls. The seven raid one another's harvests, poach cults, and weaponize rival forms.

## Defeat Grammar

Killing a manifestation is useful but rarely sufficient. Permanent local defeat requires three acts:

1. identify the mortal or metaphysical architecture paying for it;
2. end or repair the specific harm that architecture repeats;
3. destroy, sever, expose, or displace the form's anchor according to its dossier.

White Flame can expose false identity and restore coherent boundaries, but it does not replace restitution, truth, consent, duty, or repaired human relations. An Idol cannot be defeated by performing a generic opposite virtue once.

## Boundaries with Other Powers

- Hell is cold, wet, pressure-heavy refusal ecology. Mortal fire-and-brimstone folklore mostly borrows from [[Wrath|Wrath's Red Marches]].
- Veradia retains infection, parasites, spring proliferation, and invasive life. Morvalis retains bodily death, decomposition, grave stillness, and ecological return.
- The Adversary and Idols may exploit the same mortal failure, but they have different ends.
- Patrons rent stability; they are not Shadowland species and need not be evil.
- A local Folk court may contain a mask without being an Idol government.
