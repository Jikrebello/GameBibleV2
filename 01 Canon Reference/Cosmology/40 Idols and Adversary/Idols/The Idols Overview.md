# The Idols Overview

The seven Idols are former angelic orders transformed into `yolk-sovereignty` during the [[The Great Rebellion and the Yolk Exiles|Great Rebellion]]. They are objective predatory powers of the Shadowlands: independent of Hell and the Adversary, guilty of their own rebellion, and sustained by human goods deliberately turned toward vice.

They are not misunderstood gods, neutral archetypes, necessary halves of a balanced cosmos, or products of mortal dreams.

## What an Idol Can Do

An Idol can cultivate dreams, grow fauna, project masks, authorize archmasks, alter a living lineage, organize residue, counterfeit a good, and establish a temporary dominion overlay through a mortal feeding architecture. It cannot create the Image, manufacture or annihilate a human soul, reverse judgement, or enter the Material Realm in sovereign fullness.

The three manifestation classes are controlled in [[Idol Manifestation and Feeding]]. Intelligence and agency must be stated per form. A Shadow creature is not morally innocent or guilty merely because of its origin.

## Courts, Cults, and Institutions

The Idols prefer institutions because chosen vice replicated through law, custom, market, household, army, or congregation provides denser food than automatic Shadow behavior. A mortal network can contain ordinary beneficiaries, coerced participants, sincere reformers, and knowing predators without making the system morally ambiguous at its center.

A mask may infiltrate a local Folk court or human office without making that court an Idol government. Physical resemblance never proves allegiance: drakes remain Menhir court-fauna, disease belongs to Material and Veradian ecologies, and bodily decomposition remains Morvalis's natural law.

## Rivalry

The seven compete for human choice and metaphysical density. Their realms have heartlands but no fixed borders. They poach mortal networks, corrupt one another's symbols, and use rival manifestations as tools. Cooperation is temporary because each economy eventually consumes the conditions another needs.

## Counter-Countenances

Each Idol suppresses a surviving witness to its betrayed heavenly commission. [[The Counter-Countenances of the Idols]] may rarely guide a mortal inside hostile territory. They are not beneficent faces of the Idol, cannot be worshipped, and do not make the sovereign redeemable by ordinary means.

## Creator and Public Language

`Demon` is acceptable creator shorthand and a common church polemic. Active dossiers use fauna, Masks, Archmasks, Shades, and regional names because the umbrella does not identify continuity or defeat conditions. Public teaching may confuse Wrath's fire realm with Hell; authorial notes must not.
