# Envy

**Invidia** is the Idol of Envy: a yolk-sovereignty that turns gratitude, emulation, admiration, and the desire for justice into hatred of another person's good. Envy is not grief at unfairness, ambition, reform, imitation, or recognition of inequality. It feeds when another's flourishing is treated as an injury that can be healed only by diminishment or replacement.

## Original Commission and Corruption

The order that became Invidia was commissioned to witness another's good with joy, to preserve gratitude, and to make excellence communicable through honest emulation. It rebelled by declaring every distinction an accusation. In the Shadowlands it fused with counterfactual identity and became the sovereign of comparison, substitution, and stolen fate.

## Dominion: The Shadowed Glade

The Glade is a dark country of duplicate houses, black pools, empty award halls, orchards whose fruit tastes like another person's memory, and paths that arrive at the life the traveler believes should have been theirs. Every replacement is almost convincing. The missing difference is always a real person who had to be erased.

## Feeding and Mortal Network

Invidia feeds on cultivated resentment, counterfeit sainthood, stolen attribution, grievance used to justify replacement, communities taught that another's good is theft, and reform movements redirected toward symbolic victims. [[The Weeping Courts]] organize comparison through mourning, reputation, patronage, and selective public memory.

## Native and Allied Forms

- [[Envy Masks and Replacement-Beasts|Comparison Leeches]] make every good measurable against a chosen rival.
- [[Envy Masks and Replacement-Beasts|Replacement Doubles]] occupy roles while eroding the original.
- [[Envy Masks and Replacement-Beasts|Stolen-Fate Lords]] are autonomous Archmasks that administer counterfeit lives.
- [[Borrowed Saints]] counterfeit public holiness through stolen signs and reputation.

## Manifestation

Signs include names disappearing from joint work, mirrors showing another person's room, praise causing pain in an unrelated witness, and records changing so a replacement was always present. Overlays form in schools, courts, artistic circles, reform movements, succession disputes, sibling households, and devotional communities built around competitive sanctity.

## Rivalries

Envy feeds Pride by manufacturing audiences and then seeks to replace the admired figure. It supplies Wrath with target lists and Lust with rivals. Greed offers measurable proxies for worth, but possession can satisfy where Envy requires the other's loss.

## Counter-Countenance

**The Joyful Witness** preserves one true name, deed, inheritance, or relationship against replacement. It can make honest praise possible inside the Glade, but never grants the praised good to the speaker.

## Weakness and Permanent Defeat

Envy forms weaken when goods are named truthfully, injustice is repaired without inventing an innocent substitute, and another's excellence can be acknowledged without self-erasure. Permanent defeat requires restoring the original person's name, role, or history, exposing the comparison chosen to feed the ecology, and destroying the copied object, reflection pool, stolen testimony, or public narrative that allows replacement to recur.

## Story Use

- A guild's unknown founder begins appearing in every portrait after a living apprentice's name vanishes from her own inventions.
- A Replacement Double is a kinder parent than the person it displaced, forcing a rescue to confront real family harm without validating erasure.
- A Stolen-Fate Lord offers a defeated people the victorious city's prosperity if they agree that its civilians never existed.
