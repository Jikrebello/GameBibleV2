# The Counter-Countenances of the Idols

A Counter-Countenance is the suppressed witness of an Idol's original heavenly commission. It survives because corruption can betray a created good but cannot make that good have never existed.

It is not a virtuous alter ego, a second person sharing the Idol, a redeemable phase, or a source of balanced boons. It has no cult, accepts no sacrifice, and cannot be summoned. It appears only inside the sovereign's dominion, usually when a mortal freely acts according to the betrayed good despite the realm's pressure.

| Idol | Counter-Countenance | Betrayed commission | Rare aid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greed | **The Last Giver** | provision and stewardship | reveals one thing that must be given away, restored, or put back into use |
| Wrath | **The Sheathed Sword** | courage, protection, and just anger | marks the person who may be defended without retaliation becoming vengeance |
| Sloth | **The Keeper of Rest** | restorative rest and contemplation | distinguishes necessary rest from surrender and preserves one sleeper from predatory stillness |
| Gluttony | **The Bread-Bearer** | nourishment, hospitality, and festivity | provides enough to share, never enough to hoard, and reveals who has been denied a place at the table |
| Pride | **The Unmirrored Crown** | dignity and authority under truth | shows an office without its applause and identifies the person harmed by its misuse |
| Lust | **The Faithful Beloved** | faithful union, beauty, fertility, and delight | restores recognition of the beloved as a person whose consent and good are not possessions |
| Envy | **The Joyful Witness** | gratitude and delight in another's good | preserves another person's name or achievement from replacement and makes honest praise possible |

## Limits

- A Counter-Countenance never grants a repeatable spell, pact, class of servant, or permanent immunity.
- It does not command the Idol or guarantee escape.
- It cannot absolve a mortal from repairing concrete harm.
- It may guide someone the Idol hates and still disappear before an immediate battle.
- Worship directed toward it is captured by the surrounding Idol ecology or goes unanswered.

Its strongest narrative use is not rescue from consequences. It reveals the exact good the Idol has counterfeited, making a truthful choice possible inside a realm designed to conceal that choice.
