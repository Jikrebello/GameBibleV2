## 1) **Cairnmarket of the Claim-Ribs**

**Type:** Mobile ossuary-city / salvage capital  
**Anchor:** A rib-crescent of dead-god bone + embedded fossil contracts (Mass + Name-Lock)  
**What it’s like:** A drifting arc-city built into rib hollows; alleys are carved marrow-tunnels; shopfronts hang from tendon-bridges; the whole place creaks like a ship. Claims are enforced as literal physics: unlicensed salvage burns your hands, names of owners appear on stolen goods.  
**Who’s there:** Wreck-Barons’ factors, relic masons, debt scribes, Driftkin caravans, “bone-architects,” and quiet Funerary emissaries auditing corpse-theft.  
**Adventure use:** Buy demiplane cores; hire extraction crews; get caught in a claim war; discover a rib-segment that _isn’t dead._

---

## 2) **The Lantern Registry**

**Type:** Bureaucratic threshold-city / procedural sanctuary  
**Anchor:** A cluster of ancient lamp-cores that convert observation into coherence (Self-paying loop)  
**What it’s like:** A city of floating lantern towers connected by bridges of written law. Every street corner has a ward-lantern; each lantern registers passage and logs anomalies. You can live safely here—if you accept relentless administration.  
**Who’s there:** Severed Herald auditors, boundary wardens, petitioners seeking safe crossings, archivists verifying identity.  
**Adventure use:** Secure legal passage to restricted lanes; clear your name after a contract breach; uncover a lantern that has begun “appointing” new witnesses.

---

## 3) **The Index-Shelves of Knots**

**Type:** Archive-demiplane city / information empire  
**Anchor:** A map-core fossil and a vast name-lattice (Mass + Name-Lock)  
**What it’s like:** Corridors behave like catalog entries. Doors open only if you can correctly state what lies behind them. Entire neighborhoods are organized by taxonomy: “Cities Built on Bone,” “Names That Cannot Be Spoken,” “Rites That Pay Rent.”  
**Who’s there:** Archivists, oath-scribes, spies, scholars, and warlocks who trade names for power.  
**Adventure use:** Retrieve a true-name; delete a record (dangerous); find a lost route; fight a “correction order” trying to rewrite your identity.

---

## 4) **The Procession of Nine Halls**

**Type:** Nomad-polity / refugee flotilla  
**Anchor:** Recursive pattern stability—nine linked halls that must rotate rites daily (Pattern + Rent)  
**What it’s like:** Nine massive barge-islands of stone and timber, chained together by ritual bridges. Every dawn they rearrange the order of the halls to keep geometry consistent. If they stop moving, they fray.  
**Who’s there:** Driftkin procession clans, oath-midwives, route scouts, and protons that symbiotically “thicken” their hulls.  
**Adventure use:** Escort the Procession through predator-lanes; negotiate passage fees; solve a missing hall (stolen/vanished), which threatens collapse.

---

## 5) **Spire-Annex: The Astral Conclave of Vorthara**

**Type:** Dual-occupancy hub / patron market  
**Anchor:** Black Spire recurrence + rent loop via regulated rites and adjudicated conflict  
**What it’s like:** A “shadow district” of Vorthara that exists only during certain alignments, accessed by staircases that don’t exist every night. Here, pacts are notarized by stability engines.  
**Who’s there:** All patron archetypes, but especially Deathless, Heavenly, Shadow, and Infernal leaseholders; mortal candidates; brokers; auditors.  
**Adventure use:** Patron negotiations; black-market artifacts; threshold disputes; duels as legal arbitration.

# B) Iconic Natural Landmarks (Egg White geology)

## 6) **The Ossuary Verge**

**Type:** Wall-like megastructure / debris barricade  
**Anchor:** Collective mass accretion + salvage rites + fossilized contracts  
**What it’s like:** A crescent wall of crystallized failures and divine debris. Some embedded things are awake enough to rearrange local lanes.  
**Who’s there:** Scavenger kingdoms; cults waiting for “awakening”; Herald auditors; predators that hunt the wall’s upkeep cycles.  
**Adventure use:** Salvage runs; awaken-symptom investigations; “wall-breach” events spilling impossible beings.

---

## 7) **The Driftfall Cataracts**

**Type:** Entropy waterfall / repossession zone  
**Anchor:** None; it is a _mechanism_ of Drift  
**What it’s like:** A slow-motion “waterfall” of dissolving geometry—abandoned demiplanes, broken bridges, discarded laws—pouring into nothing. Things that go over don’t die; they become unstructured.  
**Who’s there:** Predators, debt collectors, desperate engineers trying to siphon usable coherence.  
**Adventure use:** Rescue mission; retrieve a dropped artifact before it loses form; sabotage a rival demiplane by pushing it into Driftfall.

---

## 8) **The Knot-Sleeper Basin**

**Type:** Sleeping Wrong region / loop-field  
**Anchor:** Rent loop paid by repetition (Loop stability)  
**What it’s like:** A basin of braided time. Travelers experience false awakenings and repeated routes. The same bridge can lead to different valleys depending on what you _expect_.  
**Who’s there:** Memory merchants; cosmic warlocks; cults trying to “advance the loop” with novelty offerings.  
**Adventure use:** Break someone out of a loop; acquire a prophecy-like “unlived record”; keep your party from losing names.

---

## 9) **The Crown-Rime Reef**

**Type:** Authority-reef / legitimacy anomaly  
**Anchor:** Crown-shard deposits (high metaphysical mass)  
**What it’s like:** A glittering reef of authority residue. People near it develop “rightful claim delusions”—not madness, but convincing inevitability.  
**Who’s there:** Wreck-Baron claimants; would-be kings; Archivists auditing titles; assassins paid to erase claimants.  
**Adventure use:** Secure a crown filing (powerful demiplane core); stop a legitimacy cult from exporting a false monarch into the Material.

---

# C) Proton Ecologies and “Wild” Egg White Ecosystems

## 10) **The Mnemonic Sargasso**

**Type:** Memory sea / proton nursery  
**Anchor:** Low-grade mass, sustained by constant minor rites and leeching off travelers (soft rent loop)  
**What it’s like:** A floating kelp-field of memory filaments. Boats are thought-rafts; nets catch “remembered voices.”  
**Protons:** Mnemonic kites, memory eels, vow-wasps, lacuna rays.  
**Adventure use:** Harvest memory-salt; retrieve a lost identity; survive a predator bloom that feeds on recollection.

---

## 11) **The Glass-Spawn Shoals**

**Type:** Shard-fish reefs / salvage hazard  
**Anchor:** Divine debris micro-fragments (mass)  
**What it’s like:** Glittering shoals of tiny shard creatures that behave like a school of knives. Useful to crafters—deadly to the careless.  
**Adventure use:** Mine ward-material; fend off scavenger gangs; protect a crafter working in hostile currents.

---

## 12) **The Vow-Hives**

**Type:** Insectile proton biome / contract ecology  
**Anchor:** Self-reinforcing pattern: promises become comb, comb enforces promises  
**What it’s like:** Hives the size of buildings, made of hardened speech. These hives stabilize small pockets of reality but punish contradiction.  
**Adventure use:** Obtain a vow-comb cell to use as a demiplane core; negotiate with hive-keepers; survive a “sting” that compels completion.

# D) Border Ecologies: Where Egg White Meets the Elemental Shell

You described the shell as the **four elemental realms** separating the Material from the immaterial. The Egg White sits _inside the shell but outside the yolks_, so it will press against different “inner faces” of the elemental realms, producing distinct edge biomes.

## 13) **The Cinder Verge (Inner Face of Fire)**

**Type:** Heat-drunk boundary zone  
**Anchor:** Fire’s consistency acts like a stabilizer, but at a cost (rent paid in heat)  
**What it’s like:** Drift becomes viscous and bright. Demiplanes here are easier to keep stable if they can continuously burn something—fuel, memory, offerings.  
**Native phenomena:** “Heat-clauses” that enforce rules as combustion; lantern storms that feed on attention.  
**Who wants it:** Infernal leaseholders and Wreck-Barons—perfect for forge-realms, debt furnaces, and weapon demiplanes.  
**Adventure use:** Steal a furnace-core; free indentured ritual labor; prevent a “burn contract” from spreading into Material.

---

## 14) **The Breathwaste (Inner Face of Air)**

**Type:** Route-dominant boundary zone  
**Anchor:** Air’s motion creates stable currents and lanes (navigation-friendly)  
**What it’s like:** Travel is fast but dangerous; lanes form easily and collapse suddenly. Words carry farther; names echo and can be stolen.  
**Native phenomena:** “Whisper-lanes” that transport secrets; storms of syllables that reorder meaning.  
**Who wants it:** Archivists and Route-Barons (if you ever reintroduce them), plus Herald auditors.  
**Adventure use:** Smuggle a true-name; chase someone across shifting lanes; map a temporary corridor that appears only during alignments.

---

## 15) **The Siltward Deeps (Inner Face of Water)**

**Type:** Pressure-and-memory boundary zone  
**Anchor:** Water accepts form but erodes it (drift is slow, inevitable)  
**What it’s like:** A dark, oceanic regime of viscous metaphysical slurry—excellent for storage, concealment, and slow transformations.  
**Native phenomena:** “Drownmarks” that silence magic; currents that return what was thrown away years later.  
**Who wants it:** Funerary Sovereigns and Deathless (vault realms, soul cisterns, memory repositories).  
**Adventure use:** Retrieve a sealed memory capsule; escort a soul shipment; fight leech-swarms that eat identity.

---

## 16) **The Root-Glass March (Inner Face of Earth)**

**Type:** Stratified stability boundary zone  
**Anchor:** Earth’s “weight” makes place easy to hold (mass synergy)  
**What it’s like:** The closest thing to real land in the Egg White: terraces of stone-like metaphysical matter, fossil contract seams, buried demiplane ruins.  
**Native phenomena:** “Law-veins” mined like ore; caverns where old rules still apply.  
**Who wants it:** Wreck-Barons (mining), Archivists (law fossils), Deathless (fortress-vaults).  
**Adventure use:** Mine a law-vein; prevent an extraction war; explore a buried pocket-realm that still enforces an extinct physics.
