# Solrath

**Solrath** is the Menhir sovereignty of summer intensity, exertion, ripening, open conflict, heat, and the expenditure of stored strength. Mortal traditions call him the Horseman of War.

## Original Commission and Cohort

Solrath's angelic office administered summer heat, maturation, sustained exertion, territorial competition, and the decisive expenditure through which organisms use what spring accumulated.

The office-holder and cohort joined the Rebellion seeking possession of culmination and victory. Fusion produced a sovereignty that understands contest and expenditure but cannot understand peace, civilian immunity, or why strength should remain unused when conditions permit its release.

## The Faewild Schism

Trium attempted to smooth summer into one regulated stage of the world-cycle. Solrath intensified heat, conflict, and maturation until local systems exceeded that regulation. This pressure converged with Father Sun and Astaroth without becoming the same law.

## Domain and Compulsion

Solrath spends reserves. Crops ripen, muscles exert, predators compete, storms release heat, and political tensions become open contests. War is one human expression of this law, not its moral purpose.

## Manifestations and Signs

- stored grain heats as if still ripening;
- rival groups feel that delay itself is defeat;
- old wounds burn before open conflict;
- metal, stone, and soil radiate the memory of noon after sunset;
- a mounted figure appears in heat shimmer where two forces are about to commit;
- fruit, soldiers, and institutions all reach readiness at once.

## Bargain Grammar

- **Permitted gifts:** endurance, rapid maturation, battle vigor, decisive weather, one forced confrontation, or release of stored energy.
- **Recognized prices:** consumed reserves, accepted contest, shortened recovery, burned land, a public challenge, or surrender of something held back for later.
- **Taboo:** hoarding the granted strength, refusing a declared contest after taking its vigor, or asking intensity to distinguish combatant from bystander without a boundary.
- **Failure:** exertion continues into exhaustion, conflict spreads, or the future is consumed to win the present.

## Courts and Ecologies

Heat courts, contest beasts, warlike Hunt Riders, ripening spirits, and fire-adapted fauna converge with Solrath. Human commanders remain responsible for war aims and methods. Solrath's presence proves only that conflict has become ecologically legible.

## Mortal Names and Misreadings

Atheria and Qianglong made Solrath a war god. Some farmers retained older rites recognizing the dangerous abundance of high summer. Father Sun's Solzareth is a separate solar sovereignty despite the similar name.

## Limits and Counterplay

Solrath cannot establish a just war, rightful victor, or honorable target. Counterplay denies open contest, disperses reserves, introduces rest or winter restraint, narrows the field, ends the named maturation, or accepts a smaller loss before escalation.

## Story Uses

- a harvest bargain ripens every field while mobilizing every feud in the district;
- an army's borrowed endurance is consuming the next generation's bodily strength;
- two rulers can prevent war only by refusing the contest that legitimizes both of them;
- a summer court will end a siege today by exhausting the region's winter stores.
