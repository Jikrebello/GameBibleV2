### Appearance and Forms:
- **Primary Form:** Nevaura is the Menhir-expression of **deep winter**: cold-load, dormancy, brittle air, and the world’s withdrawal into survival mode. Mortals describe her as a tall, frost-robed figure with pale-blue skin and eyes devoid of warmth because winter feels like a presence. The truer encounter is environmental: the sensation that the land has stopped spending energy. 
- **Steed:** The spectral reindeer “Frostbite” is the folk-image of winter migration and scarcity routes—herds moving along invisible corridors, breath freezing the air, and the way cold travels faster than a warning. 
- **Weapon:** “Frostmourne” is the mythic translation of Nevaura’s mechanism: **heat extraction**—freezing winds, ice storms, and the slow killing of warmth that turns movement into exhaustion. 

### Personality and Characterization:
- **Embodiment of Scarcity:** Nevaura is not cruelty; Nevaura is the seasonal law that *excess dies*. Winter is the period where stored energy, insulation, and community cohesion determine who persists.
- **Famine as Seasonal Physics:** The “famine” aspect is not a moral curse. It is **resource collapse**: halted growth, reduced forage, frozen ground, and the way the food web tightens until weak links snap.
- **Cold Indifference:** Nevaura’s “detachment” is the impersonal truth that winter does not negotiate. It simply arrives, and organisms either adapt, migrate, or fail. 

### Influence and Worship:
- **Temples and Altars:** Sites attributed to Nevaura are austere by necessity: ice caves, wind-cut ridges, and snowbound stones where breath crystallizes. These are less “temples” than **survival markers** and communal shelters. 
- **Rituals:** Offerings of preserved food and fuel are best understood as **inventory discipline made sacred**—a cultural tool for surviving winter. Charity rites fit the seasonal logic: winter punishes isolation and rewards mutual aid. 
- **Winter Festivals:** Solstice gatherings emphasize endurance, storage, and story—practices that strengthen group survival more than they “appease” a power. 
- **Omens:** Frost forming on the inside of walls, sudden silence in animal calls, and snow that falls in unnatural, evenly spaced bands like a measurement. 

### Symbols and Relics:
- **Symbols:** Snowflake, glaive, reindeer—reframed as **stillness, cutting cold, and migration corridors**. 
- **Relics:** Ice crystals used as preservation anchors, cloaks that resist cold-load, and tools that keep flame alive in thin air—objects that encode winter survival rather than divine favoritism. 

### Additional Elements:
- **Legendary Artifact (Mythic Anchor):** “Frostmourne” is best treated as a category of rare cold-anchors—glacial iron, star-frost, or shell-ice—that can impose local cold stability (useful for preservation and warding, lethal in conflict). 
- **Eldritch Influence (Natural Saturation):** Nevaura’s presence produces temperature collapses, ice creep across surfaces, and the psychological side-effects that follow real scarcity: vigilance, hoarding, communal tension—emergent behaviors under winter law.
