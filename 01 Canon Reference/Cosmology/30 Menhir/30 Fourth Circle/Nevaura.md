# Nevaura

**Nevaura** is the Menhir sovereignty of winter scarcity, dormancy, preservation, rationing, cold selection, and survival by refusing expenditure. Mortal traditions call her the Horseman of Famine.

## Original Commission and Cohort

Nevaura's angelic office administered dormancy, conservation, snow cover, cold preservation, seasonal scarcity, and the restraint through which life survives periods when growth cannot continue.

The office-holder and cohort joined the Rebellion seeking authority over what would be preserved and what would be denied. Fusion produced a sovereignty capable of exact allocation and long patience without understanding mercy, distributive justice, or why every human life should count equally.

## The Faewild Schism

Trium attempted to make winter a regulated recovery state. Nevaura withheld heat, motion, food, and access from the integrated system until its shared circulation failed. The pattern survives wherever preservation for one place produces hunger elsewhere.

## Domain and Compulsion

Nevaura reduces demand until something can survive. She governs hibernation, ration, frozen storage, winter roads, lean bodies, stillness, and the terrible clarity of limited supply.

Famine is a human catastrophe shaped by food, power, war, transport, and choice. Nevaura's pressure can intensify it but does not absolve hoarders or prove the hungry were selected justly.

## Manifestations and Signs

- food spoils nowhere but ceases to nourish some eaters;
- rooms become warmer as their occupants surrender movement or speech;
- snow preserves tracks, bodies, and promises without decay;
- stores divide themselves according to an inhuman measure;
- a white rider appears where a community decides who receives the last ration;
- sleepers do not age while the world spends their absence.

## Bargain Grammar

- **Permitted gifts:** preservation, cold endurance, hidden winter passage, reduced need, protected seed, or survival through dormancy.
- **Recognized prices:** surrendered surplus, silence, immobility, lost warmth, exclusion from one store, or acceptance that something else will not be preserved.
- **Taboo:** wasting what the bargain conserved, breaking a named ration, or demanding preservation without choosing what may be spent.
- **Failure:** dormancy becomes inability to wake, scarcity becomes self-perpetuating, or the preserved thing outlives every relationship that gave it meaning.

## Courts and Ecologies

Winter courts, frost-adapted beasts, hibernation minds, preservation spirits, and some Hunt Riders gather beneath Nevaura pressure. They remain distinct from the Rime Rider and other predatory powers that may exploit cold.

## Limits and Counterplay

Nevaura cannot determine a just ration, make hoarding virtuous, or preserve a soul from judgement. Counterplay restores circulation, shares rather than merely stores, invokes spring proliferation, completes the dormancy term, introduces measured heat, or destroys the local scarcity anchor.

## Story Uses

- a granary never empties because each meal removes the eater's capacity to feel warmth;
- a sleeping refuge survives a century while its guardians become unable to decide when danger has ended;
- a winter road opens only for travelers carrying no surplus for anyone else;
- ending a famine requires breaking a preservation bargain that keeps the seed grain alive.
