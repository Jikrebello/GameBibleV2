# The Faewild Schism

The Faewild Schism was the real post-fusion conflict later remembered as the Great War of the Menhir. It occurred after the yolk exiles had ceased to be angels and before the Giant empires achieved their remembered form.

It was not a second moral rebellion. The new sovereignties could choose, compete, bargain, and fear absorption, but yolk-fusion had already stripped the Menhir of moral comprehension. The Schism was a war of incompatible laws over scope, access, and survival.

## Trium Before the Schism

The office-holders and cohorts now called [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/20 Third Circle/Sylvorn.md|Sylvorn]], [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/20 Third Circle/Thalassor.md|Thalassor]], and [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/20 Third Circle/Zephyraxis.md|Zephyraxis]] entered the Faewild together. Land, sea, and sky initially fused into a stable three-centered sovereignty known as **Trium**.

Trium could coordinate root, watershed, current, atmosphere, migration, coast, weather, and pressure as one world-system. That integration was powerful enough to make the rest of the Faewild subordinate. Routes closed into one topology. Seasons became functions of a single engine. Courts, animals, and local laws were assigned places they had not chosen.

Trium did not understand this as tyranny. It understood total integration as completion.

## Resistance to the Whole World

The other Menhir resisted because Trium's order left too little room for their own domains.

- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Father Sun.md|Father Sun]] and [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Mother Moon.md|Mother Moon]] refused to let exposure and cycle become mere outputs of land-sea-sky administration.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Hecatara.md|Hecatara]] opened routes that Trium's topology had declared impossible.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Lyraxis.md|Lyraxis]] multiplied forms and exceptions faster than the world-body could classify them.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Typhonius.md|Typhonius]] forced incompatible ecologies into stable recombinations.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Arachnara.md|Arachnara]] severed and rewove the distributed networks through which Trium coordinated distant regions.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Astaroth.md|Astaroth]] concentrated collision at the union's structural joins.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/10 Second Circle/Orionis.md|Orionis]] hunted every weak boundary those collisions exposed.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/30 Fourth Circle/Solrath.md|Solrath]], [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/30 Fourth Circle/Nevaura.md|Nevaura]], [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/30 Fourth Circle/Morvalis.md|Morvalis]], and [[01 Canon Reference/Cosmology/30 Menhir/30 Fourth Circle/Veradia.md|Veradia]] drove seasonal extremes beyond the smooth cycle Trium attempted to enforce.

These acts resembled conspiracy, alliance, and betrayal when later told as a family epic. Authorially, they were the coordinated and opportunistic behavior of sovereignties protecting their own expression.

## The Threefold Fracture

Trium's permanent phase-lock failed. Land, sea, and sky separated into three surviving organizing personalities:

- Sylvorn retained land, succession, territorial memory, root, and fertility pressure.
- Thalassor retained sea, depth, pressure, tide, and current.
- Zephyraxis retained atmosphere, altitude, wind, storm, and airborne migration.

None is a fragment awaiting emotional reunion. Each is now a complete yolk-sovereignty organized around one world-body domain.

The Schism did not erase their capacity to synchronize. When all three laws align under extraordinary conditions, **Trium** recurs as a temporary operating state. Modern Trium is an event made from three sovereignties, not a fourth hidden person and not the restored ruler of the Faewild.

## The Stable Blocs

After the fracture, recurring associations settled into patterns later called the Circles. These patterns are real enough to predict convergences but never became institutions.

- Court-facing celestial and mythic laws became the Atherian **Second Circle**.
- Land, sea, sky, and temporary Trium became the **Third Circle**.
- Seasonal extremes became the **Fourth Circle**.

The missing “First Circle” belongs to Atherian theology. Imperial priests imagined an original harmonious divine household because they mistook pre-Schism integration for moral and political order.

## Consequences

- Faewild geography became plural, mobile, and locally sovereign rather than one continuous world-engine.
- Thin places inherited competing laws, allowing bargains and counter-bargains to redirect local pressure.
- Courts became observed ecologies and affiliations rather than departments of a universal realm.
- Trium confluences became rare disaster-scale events capable of organizing a High Hunt.
- Human cultures inherited contradictory but partly accurate accounts of parentage, betrayal, seasonal war, and the splitting of a three-bodied god.

## What the Atherians Preserved

[[The Mythology of The Menhir]] correctly remembers a real war, Trium's fracture, the survival of three bodies, and the later visibility of three Circles. It is wrong about literal birth, marriage, divine virtue, the moral purpose of the war, and the Circles as a governing family.
