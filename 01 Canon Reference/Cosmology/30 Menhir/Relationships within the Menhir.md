# Relationships within the Menhir

This is the authorial pressure register for the Menhir. Parent, child, marriage, feud, court, and alliance are mortal descriptions of interacting laws. They may preserve a real pattern without proving a divine family or political institution.

## Governing Rule

Menhir are intelligent but morally blind natural sovereignties. Their relationships express convergence, competition, dependency, or mutual exclusion. They do not establish moral friendship or enmity.

## Second-Circle Pressures

- **Father Sun and Mother Moon:** fixed exposure meets cycle, concealment, tide, and mutable form. Their rivalry produces alternating dominance rather than a cosmic marriage.
- **Astaroth and Orionis:** collision exposes weakness; pursuit selects what survives it. A battlefield or hunt may attract both without becoming justified.
- **Lyraxis and Typhonius:** adaptation and recombination produce new forms. Lyraxis favors proliferating possibility; Typhonius favors whatever new configuration stabilizes.
- **Hecatara and Arachnara:** routes intersect distributed networks. Hecatara changes adjacency; Arachnara makes distant nodes behave as one system.
- **Mother Moon, Orionis, and Hecatara:** migration, pursuit, and changing roads create many High Hunt traditions.
- **Father Sun, Astaroth, and Solrath:** exposure, collision, and summer intensity produce the strongest mortal war-god interpretations.

## World Bodies

[[Sylvorn]], [[Thalassor]], and [[Zephyraxis]] once operated as permanent Trium. After [[The Faewild Schism]], they became independent sovereignties whose boundaries meet at coast, watershed, storm, erosion, and migration.

When all three phase-lock, [[Trium]] temporarily reappears as a world-scale operating state. This is not reconciliation, resurrection, or a council decision.

## Seasonal Pressures

- [[Veradia]] proliferates; [[Solrath]] intensifies; [[Morvalis]] closes; [[Nevaura]] preserves through scarcity.
- Their sequence is recurrent but not peaceful. Out-of-season intrusion produces plague-blooms, killing heat, premature decay, or lethal frost.
- War, famine, death, and pestilence are human catastrophe names. None of the four judges who deserves the consequence.

## The Atherian Thanatos Error

Atherian lists inserted Thanatos among the Star Children and invented sibling relations around him. Authorial truth identifies the encountered being as [[The Boatman]], a created angel with no Menhir parentage or court alliance.

## Practical Use

Invoking a competing pressure can limit a local manifestation. Moon-cycle may temper solar exposure; Hecatara may route around a territorial closure; Morvalis may end Veradian proliferation. This is ecological counterplay, not an appeal to a morally superior deity.
