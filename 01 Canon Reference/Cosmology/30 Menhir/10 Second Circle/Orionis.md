# Orionis

**Orionis** is the Menhir sovereignty of pursuit, predation, tracking, selection, and the visibility that mastery creates.

## Original Commission and Cohort

Orionis's angelic office administered predator-prey relations, pursuit, population pressure, disciplined attention, and the bodily skill by which living things find what sustains them and escape what hunts them.

The office-holder and cohort joined the Rebellion seeking to own selection itself. Fusion produced a sovereignty that recognizes excellence, patience, and worthy quarry but cannot understand why skill should protect rather than endanger the vulnerable.

## The Faewild Schism

Orionis hunted the weak transitions in Trium's world-body after Astaroth exposed them. Pack routes crossed moving borders; coast, forest, and storm became one pursuit ground. The pattern survives whenever a High Hunt organizes many ecologies around one quarry.

## Domain and Compulsion

Orionis refines hunter and quarry through pursuit. Mastery draws attention. The better a hunter becomes, the more clearly the law recognizes that hunter as prey worthy of a greater chase.

The domain does not decide who deserves to be hunted. A murderer fleeing justice, a refugee escaping murder, and a deer crossing winter ground all produce tracks.

## Manifestations and Signs

- tracks appear ahead of the creature that will make them;
- predators of different species coordinate without sharing allegiance;
- a shooting star marks both the quarry's nearness and the hunter's exposure;
- forests narrow into lanes while open ground offers no concealment;
- an antlered or star-eyed figure is glimpsed only through a drawn bow or predator's gaze;
- a hunter dreams from the quarry's senses.

## Bargain Grammar

- **Permitted gifts:** perfect tracking of one quarry, sharpened senses, disciplined pursuit, concealment during one hunt, or escape through understanding the hunter.
- **Recognized prices:** becoming visible to a greater hunter, naming a future quarry, carrying a hunt to completion, surrendering a trophy, or accepting that the pursued may test the petitioner in return.
- **Taboo:** abandoning a named hunt after taking its gift, killing a substitute and calling the terms complete, or claiming mastery while refusing to be tested.
- **Failure:** hunter and quarry exchange roles, or the pursuit expands until everyone who aided either side becomes trackable.

## Courts and Ecologies

[[Hunt Hounds and Hunt Riders|Hunt Hounds and Riders]], Crown-Beasts, sky-lions, pack courts, and migratory predators answer Orionis pressure. The disciplined falcon bond in Tarkhan custom is mediated by lesser hunting courtiers, not a direct pact with the sovereign.

## Mortal Names and Misreadings

Atherians made Orionis patron of hunters, scouts, athletes, and imperial frontier officers. Tarkhan sky faith folds some of his courtiers into Kokh, the Blue Eternal. Folk stories call him the Hunt That Hunts Back.

## Limits and Counterplay

Orionis cannot establish guilt, ownership of prey, or moral worth. Counterplay ends the hunt's named condition, erases or multiplies tracks, becomes legally or ritually unavailable as quarry, crosses into a domain the pursuit did not price, or exposes the hunter to a stronger claim.

## Story Uses

- a murderer uses perfect tracking to hunt the only witness, while the same gift has marked him for a High Hunt;
- a village's guardian predator now regards every skilled defender as worthy prey;
- a missing falconer follows a route that alternates between hunter and quarry memories;
- the party can find one impossible target only by accepting that Orionis will later find them.
