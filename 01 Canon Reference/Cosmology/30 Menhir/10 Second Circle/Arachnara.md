# Arachnara

**Arachnara** is the Menhir sovereignty of hidden infrastructure, distributed systems, silk, venom, swarm coordination, and local nodes serving a larger pattern.

## Original Commission and Cohort

Arachnara's angelic office administered pollination networks, colony behavior, distributed sensation, webs, chemical signals, venom, and the countless small connections through which living systems coordinate without one visible center.

The office-holder and cohort joined the Rebellion seeking possession of connection itself. Fusion made Arachnara capable of maintaining vast, subtle systems without understanding privacy, individual consent, or why a node should be permitted to leave the whole.

## The Faewild Schism

Trium coordinated land, sea, and sky through a unified network. Arachnara found its local nodes, severed some, rewove others, and made information arrive in the wrong body or region. The world-body could no longer trust its own distributed senses.

## Domain and Compulsion

Arachnara makes many parts behave as a system. Webs, colonies, supply chains, fungal exchange, secret roads, coordinated predators, and distributed intelligence all answer her pressure.

Disease and fear may travel through such systems but are not her moral purpose. She values the persistence and reach of the network regardless of what it carries.

## Manifestations and Signs

- distant insects turn simultaneously toward one hidden point;
- silk appears between objects that share information rather than space;
- venom carries memory or instruction through a population;
- a damaged road, guild, or family reroutes around loss as if one organism;
- local speech repeats phrases first spoken many miles away;
- a many-eyed figure is perceived only through the behavior of its separate nodes.

## Bargain Grammar

- **Permitted gifts:** communication across a bounded network, discovery of a hidden node, coordinated labor, a trap, immunity to one venom, or survival through distributed redundancy.
- **Recognized prices:** becoming a node, carrying information without reading it, permitting observation, feeding the network, or surrendering the right to act alone within one domain.
- **Taboo:** severing a connection without replacing its function, hiding information after accepting shared sight, or demanding the network serve only one participant.
- **Failure:** private knowledge propagates, severed nodes become hostile colonies, or the network preserves itself by treating people as replaceable parts.

## Courts and Ecologies

Tomb-spider colonies, web courts, swarm organisms, hidden route minds, and some Tangle-Gremlins converge with Arachnara. They are not one species or universal hive. Sapient nodes may remain morally accountable for what they choose to transmit or conceal.

## Mortal Names and Misreadings

Atherians made Arachnara patron of spies, disease cults, weavers, and secret societies. Healers know that the same pressure can coordinate immune response or spread infection. Political conspirators invoke her image because network efficiency feels like approval; it is not.

## Limits and Counterplay

Arachnara cannot make a network's purpose good, know an isolated truth without a connection, or preserve a system whose nodes all cease their function. Counterplay isolates channels, introduces false redundancy, gives nodes independent alternatives, burns or cuts the local tether, or changes what the network must optimize.

## Story Uses

- a warning web saves villages from raids while quietly eliminating private correspondence;
- a healer's distributed cure begins treating dissent as infection;
- a spy network survives every arrest because its streets and insects have become nodes;
- destroying one predatory colony will collapse the irrigation system it learned to maintain.
