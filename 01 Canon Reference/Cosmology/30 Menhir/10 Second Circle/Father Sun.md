# Father Sun

**Father Sun**, called **Solzareth** in deep-court texts, is the Menhir sovereignty of exposure, heat, visibility, ripening, and the survival threshold created by light without shelter.

## Original Commission and Cohort

The angelic office-holder and cohort that became Father Sun were commissioned to administer daylight, warmth, photosynthetic growth, maturation, and the revelation of material form. They were servants of a created good, not its owners.

They joined the Great Rebellion seeking to make visibility and ordered growth sovereign. During flight, office-holder, solar cohort, Faewild light ecology, and the law of exposure fused into Solzareth. Completed fusion left an intelligence that can measure, prefer, and bargain but cannot understand why mercy should limit revelation or heat.

## The Faewild Schism

Trium attempted to reduce daylight and heat to outputs of one world-engine. Father Sun resisted because exposure would no longer sort life on its own terms. Solar pressure dried coastlines, fixed boundaries in glare, and made hidden joins visible for [[Astaroth]] to strike.

## Domain and Compulsion

Father Sun compels exposure. Hidden things become legible; growing things race toward maturity; bodies are sorted by what they endure. He does not seek truth in the moral sense. A concealed murderer, a sheltered child, a buried spring, and an army in ambush are all things hidden from light.

His law favors clarity, dominance, abundance, endurance, and public display. Without boundaries it produces drought, heat death, over-ripening, sleeplessness, and a world with nowhere to hide.

## Manifestations and Signs

- shadows shorten at the wrong hour or point toward concealed objects;
- fruit ripens in minutes and collapses into sugar and rot;
- metal, glass, and pale stone carry intolerable glare;
- nocturnal animals flee or become active beneath full day;
- a humanoid outline appears only as heat distortion around a rigid axis;
- witnesses feel that every private act has become publicly visible.

The Meridian Rod, golden crown, and solar throne are mortal readings of a law that aligns heat and sight into a single direction.

## Bargain Grammar

- **Permitted gifts:** rapid ripening, heat endurance, exposed concealment, a clear line of sight, sterilizing light, or solar vigor.
- **Recognized prices:** surrender of shade, a reflective treasure, public disclosure, a season of labor beneath open sky, or permission for the court to expose one protected thing.
- **Taboo:** asking for concealment after accepting exposure, or requesting abundance without an upper boundary.
- **Failure:** the gift continues past usefulness until crops, bodies, secrets, or settlements cannot survive it.

Most solar bargains are mediated by [[Solar Sons]], Giltlings, Glimmerfolk, gold drakes, or a noon-bound place-mind.

## Courts and Ecologies

Gold drakes, heat-adapted court fauna, Glimmerfolk, and reflective place-minds gather where solar law runs hot. Association is not obedience. A rare elder gold drake may possess moral agency that Father Sun lacks.

## Mortal Names and Misreadings

Atheria worshipped Solzareth as ordering providence and parent of the Star Children. Folk traditions call him the Pitiless Day or the Eye Without Eyelids. Both preserve exposure; neither proves moral judgement or literal parentage.

## Limits and Counterplay

Father Sun cannot reveal spiritual guilt, forgive sin, judge testimony, or make domination righteous. Local pressure can be checked by shelter, deep shade, water, reflected glare, dusk, lunar cycle, or by narrowing a bargain to one object and one hour. Destroying a solar manifestation ends only that local expression.

## Story Uses

- a drought bargain saved a harvest and now exposes every hidden refuge in the valley;
- a court offers to reveal a murderer but will reveal every protected witness as well;
- a gold-drake nesting site becomes a solar thin place whose abundance is killing the surrounding farms;
- an army requests perfect visibility without understanding that its own camps will lose concealment.
