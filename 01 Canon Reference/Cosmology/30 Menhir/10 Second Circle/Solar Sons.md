# Solar Sons

**Solar Sons** is an Atherian name for three recurring roles in [[Father Sun|Father Sun's]] court ecology. They are not literal children, independent Menhir, or additional creature forms. Different Glimmerfolk, Giltlings, heat organisms, and bounded solar manifestations may occupy the roles at different sites.

## Aurion, the First Edge

Aurion is the role of dawn: the line at which darkness ceases to govern and forms become legible. Signs include dew withdrawing in a visible front, birds beginning in coordinated waves, and temporary paths that exist only at first light.

## Zenithra, the Unshaded Measure

Zenithra is the role of noon: exposure, shortened shadows, heat pressure, and the sorting of what can endure direct light. A bearer of the role tends to reveal concealment without understanding privacy, shame, or mercy.

## Solcarion, the White Excess

Solcarion is the role of solar overflow: auroral veils at wrong latitudes, washed-out shadows, metallic water, and heat that exceeds ordinary season. The role often appears when a bargain has asked Father Sun for abundance without naming an upper limit.

## Use in Play

A role can serve as omen, messenger, encounter phase, or court office. Defeating its bearer ends the local manifestation, not the role or Father Sun. Bargains follow the controlling solar dossier.
