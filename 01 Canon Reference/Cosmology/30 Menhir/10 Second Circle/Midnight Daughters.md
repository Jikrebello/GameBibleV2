## Star Children of Mother Moon (Lunara)

### Seleneid

**Appearance / Manifestation**
 Seleneid is most visible where land and ocean negotiate. The tide’s edge becomes unnaturally articulate: the waterline looks like a living seam. In the Faewild, Seleneid may be seen as a tall silhouette standing where waves meet sand, its outline rising and falling with the breath of the sea.

**Core Function**
 Seleneid governs tides. This is not “water in general,” but periodic movement: exposure and submersion, feeding windows, spawning timing, and the living geometry of estuaries and tidal flats.

**Omens / Phenomena**
 Common signs include shells shifting overnight into new bands, fish runs arriving with uncanny precision, and fog rolling in exactly at turn tide. In some places, the tide leaves “script”—lines in mud that resemble runes until the next cycle erases them.

**Common Observances**
 Seleneid is acknowledged through **timing with the water’s breath**: creatures feed when the flats expose, spawn when the tide turns, migrate when the pull changes. Coastal markers—knotted ropes, carved stones, driftwood stakes—serve as practical tide-calendars rather than shrines.

### Noxal

**Appearance / Manifestation**
 Noxal is perceived as the world’s cooling veil: the moment brightness ceases to govern and scent, sound, and concealment take over. In the Faewild, Noxal manifests as a moving band of deeper dark—night that seems to have texture, sliding across ground like slow water.

**Core Function**
 Noxal governs dusk/ twilight: radiative cooling, altered visibility, extended sound travel, and the activation of nocturnal ecologies. It is the rule-set under which different hunters thrive and different defenses become viable.

**Omens / Phenomena**
 Signs include sound carrying farther than expected, dew forming rapidly, and predators appearing as moving gaps in starlight rather than shapes. In some regions, Noxal produces “night corridors”—windless routes where temperature drops sharply, creating safe or dangerous pathways depending on the creature.

**Common Observances**
 Noxal is observed through **re-timing**: activity shifting, sheltering styles changing, and routes bending toward cover. Where societies exist, observance is practical—dark travel, quiet movement, minimal fire, and reliance on scent and touch rather than sight.

### Somnira

**Appearance / Manifestation**
 Somnira appears as heaviness in the eyelids and slack in the limbs—the visible edge of metabolic downshift. In the Faewild, Somnira may be seen as pale drifting motes like cold moth-dust, gathering in hollows and sheltered places where life sleeps, hibernates, or waits.

**Core Function**
 Somnira governs **torpor and biological downshift**: sleep cycles, hibernation logic, fatigue-compulsion, the metabolic permission to enter “low-burn” states. This is not a season; it is a recurring biological mechanism that can occur under many conditions (cold, scarcity, exhaustion, migration strain).

**Omens / Phenomena**
 Signs include animals sleeping in exposed places without panic, sudden quieting in forests that are otherwise active, and flocks launching at odd hours as if following an internal drum. In some regions, Somnira causes “shared rest” events—multiple species entering stillness at once, like synchronized breathing.

**Common Observances**
 Somnira is observed through **shelter-making and conserving**: dens prepared, hollow trees claimed, bedding grasses woven, movement reduced. Where societies exist, observance is practical: storing energy, reducing heat-loss, closing routes, and respecting the necessity of pause.
