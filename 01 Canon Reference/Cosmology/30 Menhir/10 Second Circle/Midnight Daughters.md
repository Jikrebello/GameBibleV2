# Midnight Daughters

**Midnight Daughters** is an Atherian name for three recurring roles in [[Mother Moon|Mother Moon's]] court ecology. They are not literal offspring, independent Menhir, or additional creature forms. Court persons, place-minds, and bounded lunar manifestations may wear these roles.

## Seleneid, the Tidal Seam

Seleneid appears where land and water renegotiate their boundary. The tide-line becomes articulate, pools remember earlier shores, and crossings exist only at one phase. The role can grant passage while demanding that something fixed be left mobile.

## Noxal, the Cooling Veil

Noxal is the moment brightness ceases to dominate and scent, concealment, and nocturnal instinct take precedence. It may appear as a moving band of textured darkness rather than a person.

## Somnira, the Lowered Pulse

Somnira expresses sleep, hibernation, bodily downshift, and waiting through an unfavorable cycle. Pale motes gather in sheltered places. A bargain may preserve life through stillness while remaining indifferent to what is lost during absence.

## Use in Play

These roles carry bounded lunar authority. They can mark a cycle, administer an inherited exchange, or test whether a petitioner accepts change. Defeating a bearer ends the local manifestation, not the role or Mother Moon.
