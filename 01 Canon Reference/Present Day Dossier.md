# Present Day Dossier

## The Board Now

The present day is defined by the death of old Atheria and the fight over who gets to inherit its body, roads, saints, archives, and sins.

## Major Powers

- **Eclessia:** fragmented sacred polities with the Church as a unifying but contested spine. Wants reunification and moral legitimacy.
- **Thalmyria:** militarized heir-state claiming imperial continuity. Wants reconquest, border security, and proof that its holy violence is justified.
- **Sestaran Concord:** wealthy city-state network. Wants trade, leverage, archives, art, and profitable instability.
- **Ahl-e-Ahd:** contract-state and imperial rival. Wants enforceable order, strategic depth, and protection of its border regimes.
- **Jirahar:** diaspora prophecy machine. Wants survival, continuity, and truth under pressure from paranoia and real corruption.
- **Fäskogarfolk and frontier peoples:** want land, autonomy, old rights, survival, and control over the terms of assimilation.

## Playable Conflicts

- Reunification versus local autonomy.
- Holy war versus exhaustion.
- Contract law versus mercy.
- Archive truth versus propaganda.
- Relic ownership versus public good.
- Frontier survival versus exploitation.

## Grenzburg as Testbed

Grenzburg is the first practical vertical slice: a contained frontier setting where faction politics, winter scarcity, Grobi migration, Church legitimacy, Bank debt, Folk claims, and artifact economics can all collide in a playable way.
