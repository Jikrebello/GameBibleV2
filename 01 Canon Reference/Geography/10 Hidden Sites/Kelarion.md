# Kelarion

Kelarion was an early Atherian harbor on the eastern Thalassian coast, south of the old Velos sea road. Subsidence and the dragon's manifestation drowned the lower city. Its breakwaters now lie beneath a reef shelf omitted from ordinary sailing charts because currents make the approach appear farther offshore than it is.

## Surviving Geography

- the cut harbor chain lies across the drowned gate;
- the outer refuge beacon survives on a bare reef and is maintained by a small hereditary keeper community;
- flooded warehouses, council chambers, and rescue stairs occupy different depths after collapse;
- the sealed chamber beneath the breakwaters is not a lair in the biological sense but the bounded wound of [[The Shoreless King]].

The site remains absent from the public Gazetteer. Coastal stories preserve several names for the drowned harbor without identifying its exact reef.
