# Orthea

Orthea occupied a side valley between the old Virelos and Velos corridors. Modern roads pass the valley mouth without recording a branch. Travelers who enter without a preserved witness route tend to emerge believing they explored an ordinary landslide.

## Surviving Geography

- the collapsed archive complex fills the upper valley;
- Witness Stones are distributed through different geological layers and cannot be read from one position;
- the Roll of Commands survives in fragments held by separate custodial lines;
- the Empty Street remains an unbuilt corridor marking the destroyed district;
- the sealed interval beneath the archive contains [[The Unwritten Worm]].

Orthea does not appear in the public Gazetteer. Fragmentary Atherian texts refer to a missing campaign valley but do not preserve reliable directions.
