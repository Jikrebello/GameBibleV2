# Castaran Long Road

The **Castaran Long Road** is a chain of tracks, wells, depots, ferry points, and negotiated fires running west from detached Castara across northeastern Tarkhan country, north of Eclessia, toward Vardessa and Aversia. It is a commercial corridor rather than Castaran territory.

## Ash Road Peace

After a destructive attack on the growing route in 1333 AR, Castara concluded the first **Ash Road Peace** in 1334. Each year Castaran agents deliver silver, grain, tools, commercial privileges, and named gifts. In return, the Ash Horde recognizes declared caravans, supplies escorts, maintains agreed water points, and pursues banners that violate the peace.

Castara records the payment as an escort compact. Rivals call it tribute. Both descriptions capture part of its political function.

## Limits

- protection applies only to registered caravans, routes, and travel windows;
- it does not make every Ash clan a Castaran servant;
- a missed payment ends collective Ash protection;
- named outlaw banners remain dangerous even during peace;
- Castaran depots possess only the ground granted by their compact;
- the [[The Seven-Seal Defensive Muster|Seven-Seal Defensive Muster]] does not cover caravans outside recognized member territory.

## Stakeholders

Castara needs a land link to Concord markets. Aversia wants secure ore, tools, arms, and expedition freight without admitting dependence. Vardessa profits from horse fairs and mediation. Ash banners need revenue but resist permanent surveying, forts, and pasture loss.

## Cavora Roadholding

[[Cavora]] lies twelve road-days west of Castara on ground granted by the Grey-Rein Banner. Its roughly 650 permanent residents maintain three wells, a depot, workshops, a market, and two declared approaches. The grant makes Cavora a lawful Castaran charter town without transferring the surrounding grassland to Castaran sovereignty.

Cavora illustrates the road's limits. Its civic officers govern inside the boundary stones. Grey-Rein witnesses retain authority over the grant and adjacent land. Quarrying, new wells, fields, forts, or permanent roads beyond the declared holding require another agreement rather than unilateral Castaran registration.
