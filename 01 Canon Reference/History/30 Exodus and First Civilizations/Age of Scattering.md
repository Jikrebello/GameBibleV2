# Age of Scattering

The Age of Scattering begins after the Prophet's death around c. 2165 BR. One people formed during [[The Glass Sojourn]] divides into Ark-derived successors, while older human communities continue independent reassembly outside the Ark and later populations braid those histories together. This is the hinge between sacred prehistory and recognizable cultures.

## Ark Scattering

- **Proto-Atherians:** settle river valleys, hills, and coastal plains; retain liberation myths while absorbing star cults, civic law, and old Giant knowledge.
- **Proto-Jirahar:** preserve commandment, route-memory, caravan discipline, tower towns, and prophetic expectation.
- **Proto-Ahl-e-Ahd:** remain in desert and oasis networks where oath, water-right, guest-law, and survival contract become sacred.

These are institutional fissions of one camp-formed people, not three nations that entered the Glass separately.

## Orphaned Reassembly

Folk, Ättar, Tarkhan and Nsizwa are principally descended from communities that survive the Giant collapse outside the Ark. Their ancestors include subject villages, local revolts, failing client states, slave-soldier bands, hidden enclaves, mountain and forest refugees, island survivors and people born later among Giant ruins.

Their histories of liberation are local and uneven. Some know the Prophet only as rumor, foreign law, adopted story or not at all. They are neither rejected humanity nor a single related remnant people.

## Braided Peoples

Sestaran harbor populations and Vesari emerge later through distinct mixtures of Ark-derived migrants, Orphaned communities and other local populations. Later Eclessians are a further Braided ethnogenesis of Atherian provincial, Folk, Markish, Vesari and other regional layers. None is a fourth direct branch of the Glass camp.

## What Gets Lost

No Ark-derived people keeps the whole Exodus inheritance intact. Orphaned peoples preserve different truths and errors about the fallen Giant world. Braided peoples inherit several memories that do not always agree. Some lose doctrine but preserve ritual; some preserve law but become hard-hearted; some keep practical survival skills inside false history; some turn Giant remnants into prestige.

## What Gets Made

The Scattering produces:

- ancestor houses,
- clan marks,
- oasis covenants,
- caravan routes,
- first holy roads,
- settlement taboos,
- early calendars,
- memory specialists,
- oral law,
- sacred suspicion of slavery and false gods.

## Inherited Wound

Every human culture descends from people who survived the end of Giant rule, but not every culture descends from the Glass Ark. Direct inheritance, adopted liberation memory and later Braided ancestry must not be collapsed into one claim. No people carries survival without contradiction, sin or loss.
