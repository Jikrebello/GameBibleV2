# Eastern Channels before 350 BR

The eastern channels connected the central Elmaric islands to the older Arkenan–Saronikan ports and the Delkosi–Vashari-facing sea. They held island pilots, near-mainland households, Atherian-speaking settlers and communities with older local roots. By 350 BR their harbours were separate polities and contested positions.

Households around narrow crossings had land, kin, work and rites on both island and facing shore. Grain, timber, pilots, ship service and forts attracted outside claims. Delkosi and Vashari powers, Atherian-speaking ports and other coastal states could make treaties or maintain garrisons while older residents continued to cultivate or sail. A contract with one harbour house did not settle the allegiance of all its neighbours. Many channel families also kept sealed grants from the successor court at Delkos, where Eryndor III's corpse was said to have spoken after his death and to have promised the same anchorages to rival captains. Such grants were still being produced in harbour quarrels three centuries later, and a pilot's household could hold one for a berth that another family farmed under a Vasharan charter.

Caleran's direct presence here was generally thinner than on the Gateway-facing approaches. Paid crews, allied harbours, treaties and isolated stations mattered more than a continuous chain of holdings. Island families could support different patrons on different routes. Relations with the [[Arkenan and Saronikan Cities before the Empire|Arkenan–Saronikan city world]] brought Atherian language and civic practice into selected ports. These channels carried the League of the Eastern Oar's ship money and its chains after 491 BR. Pyrassan hulls were refused at Velanthe c. 480, Elmarai pilot families were held c. 475, the gale came in 466, and Velanthe's walls came down at the Chain Peace of 461.

In 355 BR [[Marelith]] made an exceptional trek east of its ordinary central circuit to support Ardel Veyr, carrying among its fugitives Corial Draven, one of Teren Casvilar's killers. Istran Melvo held the moving city fast in the shallows with Pyrassa's harbour chain, burned it and defeated Ardel's nearby main force in one operation among the central Thalassian anchorages. The [[Black Reef]] grounded along the eastward route. Ardel and the survivors he could gather ran for Pyrassa, whose wardens admitted the burned hulls under the Chain Peace's law of refuge.

At the 350 BR boundary the channels remained useful and politically unsettled. The Elmarai communities, independent island pilots, Atherian-speaking city households and foreign clients would enter imperial expansion from different starting positions.
