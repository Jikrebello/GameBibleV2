# Eastern Channels before 350 BR

The eastern channels connected the central Elmaric islands to the older Arkenan–Saronikan ports and the Delkosi–Vashari-facing sea. They held island pilots, near-mainland households, Atherian-speaking settlers and communities with older local roots. By 350 BR their harbours were separate polities and contested positions, not provinces of the later [[The Maritime Kingdom of Saronika Overview|Saronikan kingdom]]. That kingdom belongs to a subsequent history and lies in the western Thalmyric region despite occupying its eastern maritime edge.

Households around narrow crossings had land, kin, work and rites on both island and facing shore. Grain, timber, pilots, ship service and forts attracted outside claims. Delkosi and Vashari powers, Atherian-speaking ports and other coastal states could make treaties or maintain garrisons while older residents continued to cultivate or sail. A contract with one harbour house did not settle the allegiance of all its neighbours.

Caleran's direct presence here was generally thinner than on the Gateway-facing approaches. Paid crews, allied harbours, treaties and isolated stations mattered more than a continuous chain of holdings. Island families could support different patrons on different routes. Relations with the [[Arkenan and Saronikan Cities before the Empire|Arkenan–Saronikan city world]] brought Atherian language and civic practice into selected ports without turning all connected island peoples into one Atherian branch.

In 355 BR [[Marelith]] made an exceptional trek east of its ordinary central circuit to support Ardel Veyr. Istran Melvo destroyed the moving city and defeated Ardel's nearby main force in one operation among the central Thalassian anchorages. The [[Black Reef]] grounded along the eastward route. This relational placement identifies the battle's connection to the channels; it does not provide an exact anchorage or a second distant campaign.

At the 350 BR boundary the channels remained useful and politically unsettled. The Elmarai communities, independent island pilots, Atherian-speaking city households and foreign clients would enter imperial expansion from different starting positions. Their individual islands and claim lines await a precise chart rather than an invented uniform eastern border.
