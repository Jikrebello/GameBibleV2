# Early Fäskogarfolk Clan Worlds

The early Fäskogarfolk clan worlds form in the southern cold forests after the Scattering. Their homeland is not a mild green wilderness. It is a deep southern frontier of taiga, frost, permafrost, hungry winters, old trees, Menhir pressure, and Grobi danger.

## Southern Formation

Because the known world is southern-hemisphere oriented, the Fäskogarfolk and Ättar become colder the farther south they settle. Their oldest clans are shaped by:

- long winters,
- dark forests,
- frozen passes,
- permafrost graves,
- seasonal hunger,
- clan halls,
- ancestor law,
- moon and animal rites,
- Menhir pact-land,
- Grobi raids and migrations.

## Clan Law

Early clan law is built around survival:

- no one wastes winter stores;
- no outsider owns clan dead;
- no chief may sell pact-land without witness;
- kin-murder stains the whole hearth;
- oath-breakers may be denied fire;
- Grobi-watch duty is sacred labor;
- Menhir bargains require communal memory.

## Menhir and Ancestors

The clans do not treat land as inert property. Forest, stone, grave, animal, moon, and seasonal law press on human life. Some pacts protect the clans. Others bind them into cycles that later Church cultures will see as dangerous, corrupted, or demonic.

## Future Consequence

The Fäskogarfolk are not just "forest tribes." They are the southern cold frontier's answer to the Exodus: a people who remember liberation as the right never to be owned again, especially by cities, churches, banks, or foreign kings.
