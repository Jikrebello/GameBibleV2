# Peoples before Atherian Unification

The societies of the ancient inner sea understood belonging through several overlapping relationships. A person might descend from the people formed in the Glass Desert, belong to an older river or mountain population, speak the language of a maritime city, worship with a local temple household and owe service to a foreign king. None of these facts necessarily decided the others.

This collection follows the peoples whose histories have been established from the Great Unbinding to **350 BR**. It ends with the balance of powers inherited by Aurelius Thalion. Names belonging to later empires and confessional societies are omitted from the ancient account unless a reader follows a link into a later period.

## Atherian-speaking worlds

- [[Southern Leagues, Maritime Atherians and Republican Belonging]] explains how the descendants of southern Glass migration developed a maritime culture and how Atherian identity acquired civic and political meanings.
- [[Enathi]], [[Serathi]] and [[Vardeni]] concern the three related Orphaned peoples of the Aurin basin and uplands.
- [[Arkenan and Saronikan Communities before 350 BR]] follows the mixed ports and islands of the eastern Atherian-speaking world.

The word **Atherian** could describe language and culture, citizenship in an Atherian city or political membership under Caleran. These uses overlapped. They did not imply one ancestry or equal public rights.

## Qahur and the Gateway

- [[The Qahuri]] introduces the river civilisation of the Sacred Basin.
- [[The Three Regions of Ancient Qahur]] distinguishes Tenaret, Qemaret and Sakhret without dividing the Qahuri into separate ancestries.
- [[Gateway Peoples, Qarzeth and the Spared Towns]] follows the ancient Braided coast, the civic population of Qarzeth and the communities which survived its destruction.
- [[The Hadrathi Border People]] describes a related border society whose loyalties remained distinct from those of Qarzeth and Sarqen.

Qahuri identity crossed the boundaries of crowns and foreign occupations. Gateway ancestry likewise survived the fall of its dominant republic. A dynasty, capital or royal title could command these peoples without owning their descent.

## Qiryath and the eastern empires

- [[Jirahar of Road, Tower, Exile and Return]] follows the early Jirahar and the several communities produced by Qiryath's destruction and restoration.
- [[Ubar Canal Peoples and the Arqeshites]] distinguishes the city populations of the river system from the imperial culture centred on Arqesh.
- [[Vashari and the Subjects of Vashar]] separates the highland people from membership in the Crown of Roads and Dawns.
- [[Ortheians and Delkosi]] concerns an Atherian-speaking upland kingdom and the mixed successor culture produced by its eastern conquests.
- [[Tasarian, Ilyessan and Aveshi Coastal Peoples]] introduces the independent civic populations of the eastern sea.

The eastern world contained peoples, cities and imperial systems with different boundaries. A Jirahar exile could serve an Arqeshite office without acquiring Arqeshite ancestry. An Arqeshite could survive the fall of House Ubar. A non-Vashari city could form part of the Vasharan Empire. An Ortheian could speak Atherian while remaining a political enemy of Caleran.

## How belonging was recognised

Ancient communities used descent, marriage, adoption, residence, conversion, manumission, civic grant and service in different combinations. Enslaved people could live within a society while being denied its rights. Freed families might acquire local belonging over generations. Imperial grants could confer land or citizenship while leaving older kinship intact. Public recognition mattered because it governed burial, marriage, inheritance, military duty and access to common stores.

The broad ancestry terms used by this encyclopaedia describe population histories. **Ark-derived** peoples descend principally from the community formed during the Glass Sojourn. **Orphaned** peoples descend principally from human communities which survived the Giant collapse elsewhere. **Braided** peoples arose when previously distinct populations formed a durable society inherited by their descendants. Intermarriage by itself does not create a Braided people.
