# The Erasure of Orthea

Around **520 BR**, the Atherian league centered on [[Orthea]] destroyed a rebellious valley district after a hard campaign. The district had committed killings of its own, but the victors exterminated surrendered households, killed witnesses from allied units, burned tax and burial rolls, and made denial of the campaign's final phase a condition of office.

## The Wound

The rulers attempted more than concealment. They sought to remove people, commands, and consequences from causality itself. New maps joined roads across the district without naming it. Families were forbidden to mourn publicly. Soldiers received replacement service records. Archives copied one another until the absence appeared complete.

The resulting wound manifested as [[The Unwritten Worm]]. The dragon moved through records, streets, and memory, separating every injury from its cause and every name from the person who bore it.

## Reconstruction and Seal

Defecting soldiers, surviving district witnesses, and rival scribes assembled fragments no single authority controlled. They cut accounts into separate stone layers, reconstructed a Roll of Commands, and preserved the destroyed district as an empty line through the valley map.

Once the atrocity had enough boundary to be addressed, they collapsed Orthea's archive-valley around the Worm. The Witness Stones, Roll of Commands, and Empty Street remain the seal.

## Public Memory

Atherian annals preserve legends of a campaign that cannot be placed and a valley omitted from maps. Later historians usually interpret the gaps as copyist error, censorship, or several unrelated wars. The complete reconstruction remains author-only truth.
