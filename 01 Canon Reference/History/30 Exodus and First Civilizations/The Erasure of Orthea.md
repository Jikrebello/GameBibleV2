# The Erasure of Orthea

Around **520 BR**, the Atherian league centred on [[Orthea]] destroyed a rebellious valley district after a hard campaign. The district had committed killings of its own, but the victors exterminated surrendered households, killed witnesses from allied units, burned tax and burial rolls, and made denial of the campaign's final phase a condition of office.

## Cause: the Siege Levy

*Added by the Causality Pass, 14 November 2026; see [[Causality Pass - 02 Gateway Wars and the Late Republic]], item 02.6. This replaces the earlier ruling in [[Atherian Palace and Maritime Histories - Integration Review]] that the final siege of Qarzeth had no causal connection to Orthea.*

Orthea was one of the twelve seats of the Celestial Compact. Within the Compact it led the **Nine-Valley League** of its side valleys, and it kept the Compact's archive: the rolls of members, levies, festivals and arbitrations. During Caleran's final war against Qarzeth, 526–520 BR, the siege levies of grain and men passed through Orthea to its valleys. The league's officers met them by stripping the poorest district hardest. In the last winter of the siege that district, short of men and of seed, killed the collectors who came for its remaining grain.

The league's campaign and the erasure followed. The witnesses killed from "allied units" were Compact soldiers marching home from Qarzeth through the valley, who had seen the final phase. The two catastrophes of 520 are therefore one chain: Caleran's war fed the levy, the levy made the rebellion, and the soldiers of the war were made witnesses and then corpses.

## The Wound

The rulers attempted more than concealment. They sought to remove people, commands, and consequences from causality itself. New maps joined roads across the district without naming it. Families were forbidden to mourn publicly. Soldiers received replacement service records. Archives copied one another until the absence appeared complete.

The resulting wound manifested as [[The Unwritten Worm]]. The dragon moved through records, streets, and memory, separating every injury from its cause and every name from the person who bore it.

## Reconstruction and Seal

Defecting soldiers, surviving district witnesses, and rival scribes assembled fragments no single authority controlled. They cut accounts into separate stone layers, reconstructed a Roll of Commands, and preserved the destroyed district as an empty line through the valley map.

Once the atrocity had enough boundary to be addressed, they collapsed Orthea's archive-valley around the Worm. The Witness Stones, Roll of Commands, and Empty Street remain the seal.

## The Loose Years

Between the erasure and the collapse of the archive-valley came **the loose years**: seven harvests by the count on the Witness Stones, four by Caleran's annals. The two counts disagree and are left to disagree. During them the Worm moved freely through the archive while two great bodies of record were being copied there:

- **the war rolls of Qarzeth's sack**, sent up from the siege for entry in the Compact's archive; and
- **the founders' genealogies** Caleran was then recasting into a common ceremonial order of member cities.

Both came out with their attributions cut. Caleran's rolls of the sack name no officer for the sale of rescued children after the breach. Esharat's ransom registers name Atherian officers whom no Caleran roll places at the siege. Founder lines carry names without cities, and cities without founders. Teshun Ordan's fate is lost from Caleran's rolls of the sack by the same cause. The two sets of records cannot be reconciled, and the cause lies in the archive, not in scribal error; in-world historians blame copyists, censorship or Caleran's pride.

The Worm did not reach the charter rolls of the League of the Eastern Oar, which were kept at Velanthe.

## Aftermath

After the sealing, c. 513 BR, the Compact's rolls were rebuilt from Caleran's own copies, and from then on every Compact dispute went through Caleran's archive; this completed Caleran's hold on the league. The Nine-Valley League dissolved. Its valleys, now assessed directly by Caleran, refused their ship money in 469 BR and joined the rebels in the War of the Closed Chains.

## Public Memory

Atherian annals preserve legends of a campaign that cannot be placed and a valley omitted from maps. Later historians usually interpret the gaps as copyist error, censorship, or several unrelated wars. The complete reconstruction remains author-only truth. Reader articles call the place **the archive valley** and treat its name as uncertain in the copies; they never name the Worm, and present the loose years only as the gap in the records of the sack that historians argue over.

**Name note.** "Orthea" stays in authorial files in this pass. The Causality Pass recommends renaming it **Ambreth** in a dedicated name pass, to end the clash with Ortheia.
