# Black Reef

The Black Reef was the burned and grounded urban mass left by [[Marelith]] after its destruction in 355 BR. It lay along the city's eastward route from the central Thalassian anchorages where Istran Melvo fought Ardel Veyr. It was a hazard to shipping and a place of salvage, mourning and disputed sightings. Its precise chart position remains unresolved. The reef was **not** the older drowned site of [[Caelovar]].

## A city broken from its bearer

Fire broke Marelith's living works during Melvo's linked attack on the mobile city and Ardel's nearby fleet. The mass that became the reef tore from the maimed bearer and grounded. Most of the city's residents were killed or captured; those who escaped by boat or into deep water left households and stores behind. Sailors later blamed wrecks in bad weather on something still moving beneath the blackened structures. Some accounts may preserve encounters with the surviving reefback, while others describe ordinary reefs, storms or deliberate raiding. They do not establish the animal's final fate.

The burning city did not founder as one vessel. Cultivated shell, attached harbours, living buildings and part of its flotilla broke apart as the bearer rolled or fled. Some residents escaped only to be taken from disabled craft or from the surrounding water. Others remained among structures that later grounded. The descriptive name remembers the colour the fire left, while hiding how many different places in the mobile city were destroyed before one mass became a reef.

## Navigation, salvage and memory

The reef's remains offered materials and relics to salvors while making the route dangerous. Its common name describes the blackened wreck, not a formal city or a new island polity. At the 350 BR boundary, nearby coasts still had to deal with survivors, Atherian captives and the destruction of a once mobile harbour.

Salvage promised usable material and evidence of Marelith's living craft, but a burned and unstable structure did not become a safe quarry simply because it no longer moved as a city. The wreck also changed how sailors understood the route. A captain could use its reported position as a warning, while another blamed a later loss on the alleged surviving bearer. These claims belong to the reef's history of danger and remembrance; none fixes its precise coordinates or proves that every wreck near it had one supernatural cause.

Marelith's survivors had different reasons to return or avoid it. A free family might seek a lost relative or property; a person taken by Atherian forces could not choose whether to revisit the wreck. The reef remained a visible consequence of the 355 BR campaign even as the wider Elmaric island world continued beyond it.
