# Black Reef

The Black Reef was the burned and grounded urban mass left by [[Marelith]] after its destruction in 355 BR. It lay along the city's eastward route from the central Thalassian anchorages where Istran Melvo fought Ardel Veyr. It was a hazard to shipping and a place of salvage, mourning and disputed sightings.

## A city broken from its bearer

Fire broke Marelith's living works during Melvo's linked attack on the mobile city and Ardel's nearby fleet, while Pyrassa's harbour chain held the city fast in the shallows. Cultivated shell, attached harbours, living buildings and part of the flotilla broke apart as the maimed bearer rolled or fled, and the mass that became the reef tore away and grounded. Most of the city's residents were killed or captured. Eleven hulls reached Pyrassa with Ardel Veyr, and the survivors aboard them were lodged in the boat sheds that became that city's Burnt Quarter. Some escaped by boat or into deep water, leaving households and stores behind; others were taken from disabled craft or out of the water; others stayed among the structures until they grounded. The name remembers the colour the fire left, and makes one reef of many burned neighbourhoods.

Sailors later blamed wrecks in bad weather on something still moving beneath the blackened structures. Some of their stories may preserve encounters with the surviving reefback; others describe ordinary reefs, storms or deliberate raiding.

## Navigation, salvage and memory

The reef's remains offered materials and relics to salvors while making the route dangerous. In the years that followed, nearby coasts still had to deal with survivors, Atherian captives and the destruction of a once mobile harbour.

Salvage promised usable material and evidence of Marelith's living craft, but the burned structure was unstable. The wreck also changed how sailors understood the route. A captain could use its reported position as a warning, while another blamed a later loss on the alleged surviving bearer.

Marelith's survivors had different reasons to return or avoid it. A free family might seek a lost relative or property; a person taken by Atherian forces could not choose whether to revisit the wreck. Because Marelith had sheltered refused crews and families fleeing the Writing-In as well as raiders, Elmarai pilots passing the reef in bad weather dipped their lanterns to it, as to the drowned refuge they called a second Kelarion.
