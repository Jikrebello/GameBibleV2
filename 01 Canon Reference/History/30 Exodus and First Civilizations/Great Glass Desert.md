# Great Glass Desert

The Great Glass Desert is the refuge and crossing-place of the Exodus: roughly three hundred leagues of ancient, reflective, deadly glass between the old Giant worlds and the southern lands where the Ark-derived peoples become recognizable cultures. It predates the Prophet's plagues and the Giants' recorded attempts to approach it. Its origin, nature, and purpose remain unknown, and every people argues them: the Church calls the Glass judgement before judgement, Covenant teachers the first breach of the first contract, and the wind-speakers say the sky once touched the ground and regretted it.

## Historical Role

During the [[The Great Unbinding|Great Unbinding]], the Lord provides one bounded survivable passage into a place where no imperial road, army, map, or calculation had established one. Giant powers had left failed marginal incursions but no permanent deep-Glass settlement. The Lord sends the principal remnant there to survive the continuing collapse outside.

During [[The Glass Sojourn]], approximately c. 2265–2165 BR, the ordered camp carries and preserves households, animals, seed, tools, medicine, records, law and living crafts. High-sign food, water, shade, orientation, endurance and protection answer impossible needs, while human preparation and labour remain indispensable. The mixed host becomes one people through common institutions. When it is commanded south, the provision thins and then ends at the outward threshold, company by company, so that the last companies leave the Glass hungry and after the Prophet's death.

The passage and long occupations impress real threshold corridors and anchor camps later underlying the Prophet's Roads and lawful Way-Gates.

## Cultural Memory

- [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/The Jirahar Overview.md|The Jirahar Overview]] remember routes, commandments, camps, and the Prophet's failure or martyrdom.
- [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Ahl-e-Ahd/The Ahl-e-Ahd Overview.md|The Ahl-e-Ahd Overview]] remember water, oath, hospitality, provision, and covenant.
- [[The Atherian Empire Overview]] later turns the crossing into heroic civic myth more than binding law.
- Folk, Ättar, Tarkhan and Nsizwa traditions principally remember the orphaned Giant world; any Glass material reaches them through later contact, adoption or mixed descent.
