# Package C — Wells, Open Country and Vathra Canon Integration Review

## Promotion ruling

The author approved Package C for canon integration on 12 September 2026. The promotion fixes the ordinary history of Vathra, the Great Unyoking, ancient Tarkhan formation, ancient Ahdari formation and the defence of Rethmar through 350 BR.

Comparative sources, alternative interpretations and technical collision work remain in the research shelf. The canon articles present the resulting World Egg history without importing real-world culture names or game-setting terminology.

## Fixed history

### Vathra

- Vathra was a lesser Giant crown rather than one of the Five Imperial Horizons.
- Its six principal crown-cities moved physically through the Material sky. City-flight combined crafted lift bodies, Zephyraxis pressure mantles, Hecatara route anchors and human labour. Astaroth-derived structural knowledge contributed to the system without making Astaroth its owner.
- The First Road-Mother was a Titan-Firstborn conceived through a bounded Hecatara manifestation and a human woman. She possessed `titan-continuity` and no human soul. Later Vathran High and Dynastic Giants possessed `giant-continuity` and no human souls.
- Ozhur IV's Closing Ascent, Varakesh's revolt and the human Great Unyoking broke Vathra between approximately 2300 and 2260 BR.
- Seyra Vatan, Dorun of the Draw-Gangs and Meya of Narash coordinated distinct human constituencies. They did not form one universal liberation company.
- Kelvara turned against the royal houses after participating in their crimes. Her aid saved lives and did not undo the captives previously transferred under her authority.

### The six crown-cities

- Urzavad fell and broke across a dry river plain.
- Kel-Orun was deliberately grounded and became a durable human settlement.
- Dazhur discarded a dependent ward and vanished from reliable record within three years.
- Sairat entered a damaged route after its destination anchor was destroyed.
- Molqara lost control of its steering system and continued beyond the dependable northern circuit.
- Yazuran departed and reached no known Material anchor.

The first three outcomes are settled history. Sairat's reported returns, Molqara's current course, Yazuran's destination and the survival of their rulers remain unresolved.

### Tarkhan

- The Tarkhan are principally Orphaned descendants of human communities which survived under, beside and against Vathra outside the Glass refuge.
- Tergai well houses, Elkari grass families, Taleri tether towns and Narashi caravan houses contributed to ancient Tarkhan formation. These are historical regional populations rather than permanent castes or separate species.
- The broad Tarkhan name became common during the Hearth-Crown centuries. Its etymology remains disputed.
- Ancient transport relied on camels, carts, sledges and walking herds. Vathran and chariot horses survived only in small numbers. The First Catch of 905–920 AR remains the mass horse transformation.
- Boran Tall-Hand's Daram Ford episode, the Feast of Copper Wheels, the Savan–Demer client rivalry, Yalun and Marat's different conduct during Arqesh's fall, and Kharuz's leadership at Rethmar are fixed history.

### Ahdari

- The ancient Ahdari were a durable people before Al-Ahdiya. Their ancestry was principally Glass-derived and incorporated older Narashi, oasis, Tarkhan and other Orphaned populations.
- The name became a broad self-designation during the Bronze-Road centuries. Earlier communities commonly named wells, markets and houses.
- Wells, markets, camel circuits, mobile camps and foreign urban quarters all belonged to Ahdari life.
- Name-Keeping Lordward households coexisted with high-place rites, empty memorial objects, dependent Idol Masks and particular Patron bargains. No common pagan church governed the people.
- Harun Savel's massacre at Saqara, the diversion of trade to Mazar and the child sacrifice and executions at Kor Ashal are fixed local histories. Kor Ashal does not characterise every ancient Ahdari community.
- No Ahdari office inherited the Great Prophet's commission.

### Rethmar

- Rethmar was a mixed winter gathering of permanent water workers and visiting Ahdari and Tarkhan households. It was neither a capital nor another name for the Nine Wells of Tergai.
- In 381 BR, Tavisha removed the water Lethren Ostar expected to seize and Kharuz divided his columns. Ostar and his son Pel died during the defeat.
- Northern forces killed and captured members of the army. Sick captives and non-combatant water workers were released; healthy officers were ransomed; other captives were exchanged, admitted to households or enslaved.
- Tavisha governed water and Kharuz commanded a wartime following. The coalition dispersed after the victory.

## Identity and supernatural controls

- Every human in these histories possesses a complete Image-bearing human soul regardless of ancestry, slavery, cult or office.
- Vathran Giant continuity remains distinct from human descent, office, court person, Mask, Patron role, copied pattern and ruin mechanism.
- The First Road-Mother's origin creates no new Menhir species and gives Hecatara no universal title over Vathra or the Tarkhan.
- Kelvara may have received temporal mercy. The history establishes no human redemption, sainthood or Image for a Giant.
- Effective route, weather or well encounters prove only their immediate physical result. They do not establish divine approval, moral innocence or ownership of a people.
- Ancient Ahdari identity is a peoplehood, not Al-Ahdiya. The later Ahl-e-Ahd are an imperial-confessional civilisation containing several ancestries.

## Collision rulings

- Vathra is independent of Nythalum. Its cities travelled within the Material sky and did not pursue the lunar Ascension.
- The Great Unyoking is a regional part of the Great Unbinding. It neither replaces the Prophet's liberation nor causes the whole collapse.
- Dazhur's cast-off ward is separate from the Caravan That Cast Off Its Wards and the Hunt of the Escaped Courts.
- Sairat's damaged route does not explain Bird-Marked Sites, the Glass or a protected Weird.
- Ancient Tarkhan horse use remains exceptional and elite. No text may project the post-905 mounted civilisation into Package C.
- The Ahdari share kin and institutions with Jirahar and Tarkhan neighbours without becoming the same people.
- Rethmar is separate from Tergai and from later Covenant conquest.
