# Authorial Notes from Reader Articles — Ancient East

Rulings, safeguards and exact figures moved out of the public ancient-east history articles during the November 2026 editorial cleanup; their force as canon is unchanged.

## [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria.md|Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria]]

- Haviel's contest: "The flame and rain were genuine signs given for the exposure of the court cult. The fire left no active relic and conferred no continuing power upon the site."
- The gate at the siege: "This second sign answered their rescue. Qoram had chosen to leave the families outside; Zimra and Qoram arranged Tobel's death; Haviel ordered the killings beside the dry channel. None of those acts acquired the authority of the signs." (Reader text now attributes the second sign to the Lower Stair recitation.)
- Received traditions: "Their continued performance is established; details concerning divine signs, private motives and later embellishment remain matters of interpretation."
- Exact figure replaced: Haviel returned when an Arqeshite-backed force threatened Qiryath in **835 BR** (reader text: "around 835 BR").

Causality Pass, 14 November 2026 (01.5, 01.7, inventory OBA-N01, cuts). **The sign of acceptance.** An accepted appointed offering burns without smoke and leaves the altar stones cool; elders of three custodial houses witness by touch and their names are written beside the day. The sign answers the sanctuary's appointed offerings, not persons, councils or kings; reading it as a verdict on city policy is human inference. Haviel's white flame (c. 842–835) was a separate sign outside the city. **The Withholding.** The first withheld offering fell in the siege's first winter, 743 BR, after the council expelled foreign residents to die between the lines; the sign stayed withheld through the fall (742), the exile, and the royal consecration offering (c. 660), and returned at the appointed offering carried by Yaelith's mixed households in 654 BR. That acceptance was authentic; the departing houses' charge of partisan witnesses is in-world dispute only. **The cause of the Withholding is unresolved even authorially.** Whether it answered the expulsion, the envoy's killing or the coming destruction is not decided here and must not be decided by any later file. The reader text gives each answer to an in-world party (Maraiah's party, the burial fraternities, Elior's followers). The *Book of the Unanswered Years* replaces the proposal's "Register of Unanswered Years". Usage: *the Withholding* for 743–654 BR; *the Silence* for the sign's ending at 0 AR (avoids Causality Pass 04's "Second Silence"). **The Reclaiming (c. 1450–1325 BR).** Title-marked Ishkatari salvage metal recalled dormant recovery works ([[Gilded Wardens]], Processional Coils, [[Estate Dead]]) when deeper estate-vaults were opened; they recovered marked metal along the old ceremonial roads, which the Bronze Roads reused as trunk routes. Confined to routes following Ishkatar's ceremonial roads. The Jirahar smiths' provenance custom is reader-level texture. **The Choir at the Eastern Road** is the reader face of OBA-N01 ([[Heavenly Campaigns and the Human Dead#^atlas-oba-n01]]): a genuine true-angel choir closed a road that slave dealers had made into an abduction passage for returning families, c. 700–650 BR; an ordinary caravan carried the last freed captives round it; later reopened stretches killed travellers. **Cuts.** Hador: the Nazirite dedication, the riddle and wager, Delira of the South Gate, the shaving, blinding and pillared hall are removed. Hador's killings on the road, Nareh's and her father's burning, the private war's dead, and the mill workers and prisoners killed with him remain; the orchard villages now hand him to Gedran for the reopening of its road, and "the Braids at Gedran" now names the braided halters they bound him with. Gedran's ruling household no longer dies with him, so Othan fights an undamaged Gedran. Yedran: the Bathsheba–Uriah frame is removed. Yedran withholds relief grain from Uriad's starving post to feast his eldest son's marriage alliance with a river house; Rava is Uriad's widow and her lament's golden bowl now fits. The sister's rape and the half-brother's killing are kept, re-caused through the grain rooms Yedran's sons held in the hungry years; the Amnon–Tamar–Absalom sequence (feigned sickness, delayed revenge at a feast) is removed. The sister remains unnamed.

Causality Pass, 14 November 2026 (01.5, inventory OBA-N13, cuts). **Arezan's wager.** Arezan's decree and first grants began the rebuilding of Qiryath and Daryav completed it, as a bet that the sign would return under Vasharan protection. The royal consecration offering (c. 660) was unanswered; the acceptance of 654 answered the mixed households' offering, and Vasharan inscriptions crediting the crown are false. **The Witness of Dawn** is the dependent Pride Mask already ruled in Package B; it began imitating the royal radiance in the years after the unanswered consecration offering, and Daryam's claim that it was the withheld sign transferred to the king is a counterfeit. It decides nothing in Causality Pass 03's span (350 BR–0 AR). **The ended acclamation (431 BR)** is the reader face of OBA-N13 ([[Heavenly Campaigns and the Human Dead#^atlas-oba-n13]]): at the harvest audience at Kavresh a Witness-Judgement angel struck Orvaz I at the moment public praise of him as the giver of the harvest became accepted divine honour; his perjury of c. 462, the executed leaders and his demand for road-command children for the dawn-house belong to the judgement. The interruption cut the Witness of Dawn's access to the royal cult for about a generation without destroying the Mask or touching Pride's Throne; no Idol succession occurs, and the event is not a reusable test of legitimate sovereignty. Reader text keeps the physicians' sickness against the Jirahar interpreters' messenger. Tiraz III's accession after fighting among the royal kin is left unspecified pending Causality Pass 03's reign-list replacement. **Cuts.** The White-Haired Fosterling (Zal and the Simurgh) is cut; Razana Tiravan remains a physician and negotiator who refused the throne, with no exposure and no bird. The Champion and the Unrecognised Son (Rostam and Sohrab) is cut. Mazhara is no longer punished by the sale of her workers: Daryav co-opts her as keeper of the royal flame, the orchards and their bonded workers pass to the flame's estate, and her sayings are carved in edited form; her students' uncarved text keeps her refusal to call the Witness of Dawn a sign. Rathava (c. 462) recants in the palace garden to save his labourers from sale; his school splits. **Xarun.** The error flagged by Causality Pass 02 ("inherited when Qarzeth's destruction had released Caleran ships") is already corrected in this article (Xarun accedes in 536 as Caleran prepares its final war; Qarzeth falls 520; Tasar revolts 514); only the archived Package B draft still carries the old sentence.

## [[Eryndor III and the Ortheian Eastern Conquest]]

- Collision safeguard (also held in Package B): "Eryndor belongs to an earlier political world than Teren Casvilar and Aurelius Thalion. He was a hereditary king who compelled Atherian cities to serve a royal campaign. Casvilar arose from the institutions of a mature republic, while Thalion converted victory in a civil war into lasting imperial government."
- The Qahuri sign (ruling also held in Package B): "A limited phenomenon associated with Father Sun occurred during the journey… It gave him neither divine parentage nor authority over Qahur." Reader text now attributes the Father Sun association to witnesses.
- "The marriage joined the alliance without removing the different ambitions of the two kings."
- Crowned Countenance at Eryndor's court (also held in Package B): "the royal Pride Mask known as the Crowned Countenance… The dead Ubar kings, Eryndor and the Mask remained separate persons and powers."

Causality Pass, 14 November 2026 (items 01.6, 01.7 and the cuts). **The Voice at Delkos — authorial truth.** Eryndor consented to the White Measure rite at Arqesh in 685 BR; Neresh of Qemaret supplied the masters and Serevan paid. The regimen had barely begun. The masters attempted the final rite at his death in 684 and it failed: his complete human soul went the ordinary human road. The rite closed his true name into an anchor his soul had already left. That empty anchor was filled by the estate-office built around his plans and his attendants' memories, and by the Crowned Countenance, a dependent Pride Mask still anchored in Kassur III's portable images, which Eryndor had kept as regalia. Within months the corpse spoke in his voice. The Voice is a dependent Mask using an office and residue: no Archmask, no accession, no transfer of sovereignty, and no part of Eryndor. Its knowledge of companions' secrets came from the attendants' memories folded into the office. Its double grants follow from the Mask feeding on rival petitioners' praise. The Qemaret masters' testimony of c. 657 was true, whatever Neresh's reasons for procuring it. Eryndor's cause of death stays open (Package B); the mineral regimen is one rumour among several. **Motive.** Pay set the timing of the war; the deportations from Terys made it possible to declare. **Cuts.** Cleitus and Philotas are cut as Plutarch beats; the two killings stay, now caused by the quarrel over bowing to Kassur's images. The officer and companion remain unnamed (protected in Package B). The Alexander Romance incidents are reduced to one linking sentence; the bell's drowned attendants, the Sky-Lion dead, the abandoned prisoners and the missing traveller remain.
