# Reader-Facing Campaign Material — Authorial Control

This ledger preserves campaign prompts and future-story possibilities removed from reader-facing encyclopaedia articles during the corpus editorial programme. Their relocation changes presentation only. It does not promote a possible event, identify a preferred future, or alter the authority of the article from which the material came.

Each section names its original article and preserves the removed wording. Further development should return to the article's controlling canon before using an idea recorded here.
