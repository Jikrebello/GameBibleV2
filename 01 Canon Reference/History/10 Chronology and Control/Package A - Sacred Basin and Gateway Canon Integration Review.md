# Package A — Sacred Basin and Gateway Canon Integration Review

## Promotion ruling

Package A was approved for canon promotion on 7 September 2026. Its Qahuri, Gateway and White Measure histories now form part of the controlled World Egg chronology. The research dossiers remain archived source history and comparative documentation.

Ordinary ancient names, events, relationships and consequences are fixed. Exact coastlines, borders, travel distances, the ancient Qahuri river's proper name, complete royal genealogies and the present condition of the Second Houses remain open.

## Encyclopaedic voice review

The Package A canon received a second editorial review after promotion. Ancient subjects now open with a present-tense definition of the civilisation, institution, event or person under discussion. Completed actions remain in the simple past, as historical prose requires, and use named agents wherever the evidence identifies them. The review does not force historical events into a continuous dramatic present.

Ancient Qahur, the Gateway Republic and the western well societies are described in the terms of their own periods. References to Mujalib, the Covenant, Ahl-e-Ahd political identity and later emirates have been removed from ancient leads and conclusions. Such references remain in articles whose subject genuinely spans those later periods, including the Qahuri culture article, the Covenant historical spine and the present-day Al-Qahur overview.

The visible titles **Qahur and the Gateway, c. 2165–350 BR** and **The Spared Towns and the Kingdom of Sarqen** replace titles that defined their subjects through later Atheria or Samyra. Their filenames and aliases remain unchanged for stable navigation. The three early-era portals have also been consolidated: their old outline summaries and repeated atlas introductions have been replaced by single continuous accounts, and their metadata now uses proto-Ahdari, proto-Jirahar and early maritime identities where later ethnonyms would be anachronistic.

## Fixed Qahuri history

The Sacred Basin's population is principally Orphaned and its river is separate from Ishkatar's lost Serpent's Spine. Tenaret, Qemaret, Sakhret, Isethar and Aseret are established ancient towns located through their relationships to river, roads and cultivated districts.

The canonical dynastic spine comprises the Senareth first crown, the first Twin-Reed division, Khevar II's violent reunification, the mixed Yasharet dynasty, Aramen's conquest and empire, Ithren III's Single Radiance and Zaheret's restoration. Arqeshite and High-Road interventions remain fixed at the degree currently named. Package B controls the future naming and internal history of the High-Road Empire.

The workers' stoppage of 903 BR follows withheld grain, assault by soldiers and the spread of action to boat crews. The Copper Orchard's demand for funerary material is one established cause of the diversion.

## Fixed Gateway history

The Gateway country is a Braided ancient population formed among older shore communities, proto-Ahdari migrants, maritime settlers, captives and freed people. It predates the Covenant and is not an early Covenant state.

Qarzeth, Sarqen, Esharat, Belqara, Hazreth, Tammur, Oresh and Hadrath are established relational locations. Qarzeth's Harbour Judges, Council of Houses and citizen assembly governed an unequal maritime republic whose dependent communities retained different rights.

The three Gateway Wars, Rope Camp revolt and Ordan overseas command are fixed with the named participants promoted into their canonical articles. Qarzeth's civic establishment killed living children. Ilya and Sapha organised resistance and rescue. Caleran ended the sacrifices and also committed murder, rape, enslavement and deliberate urban destruction. The salting affected Qarzeth's central districts rather than the whole Gateway country.

Esharat, Sarqen, Belqara and Hazreth survived. Their league, continuing cultivation and local offices supported royal recovery. The Nerethan dynasty descends from an incoming Delkosi command joined to Hadrima's Esharati and junior Qahuri maternal house.

## White Measure metaphysics

The White Measure is a minority human Qahuri mortuary-economic movement. A successful rite binds the founder's complete human soul to an embalmed corpse and necropolis, opening a private demiplane in the Egg White. The founder can become a Patron by leasing access or power from that realm. Patron is a role; the person's continuity remains `bound-human-soul`.

Meresha, Khaseret and Tamekha successfully completed the rite. Hesenu is a separately bound human attendant. Nemeret's human soul departed normally; his tomb contains an estate-office later parasitised by a dependent Greed Mask.

The rite delays the Boatman and judgement without changing either. Human self-binding remains objectively evil when voluntary. Breaking the true soul-cage resumes the soul-road. Neither the movement's usefulness nor the reality of its Patrons proves its teaching morally true.

## Source and collision controls

The Qahuri sequence uses Egyptian political and material grammar without making one fictional dynasty a renamed historical house. The Gateway sequence uses Phoenician, Carthaginian and Punic structures. Qarzeth's sacrifice and actual salting are World Egg rulings; their occurrence is not presented as proof of every disputed claim about historical Carthage.

The White Measure draws upon Pathfinder's Kalistocratic discipline and funerary ambition. It becomes a minority Qahuri movement rather than a merchant nation. Its successful private realms follow existing Egg White, Patron, lich and soulbinding rules.

Qahur remains separate from Ishkatar. Qarzeth remains separate from Sarqen. The Spared Town League remains separate from the Celestial Compact. The child-rescue network remains separate from the Midwives of the Unnumbered. The Rope Camp remains separate from the Company of the Broken Measure.
