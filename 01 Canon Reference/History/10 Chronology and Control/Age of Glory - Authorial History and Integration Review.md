# Age of Glory — Authorial History and Integration Review

This review records the canonical development of the Atherian Age of Glory from Thalion's settlement around 350 BR to the Redeemer boundary at 0 AR. It follows the authorised imperial-founding pass and completes the historical scope originally assigned to Package 2C. Package 3 retains the Redeemer, Underground, Conversion, later empire and Long Frontier.

Fifteen reader and reference articles establish the political sequence, provincial society, army, economy, learning and religion. Existing Garden articles remain unchanged. Revised Block III material is incorporated through separate historical destinations rather than being compressed into one imperial legend.

## Authority and sources

The controlling chronology fixes the Unification around 350 BR and the Redeemer's death and resurrection at 0 AR. The preceding [[Imperial Founding - Authorial History and Integration Review]] controls the founding generation. The established culture, geographic and cosmological articles govern neighbouring peoples, routes and supernatural identities.

The historical structure draws upon broad patterns of the Roman principate and later imperial centuries: dynastic succession, adoption, civil-war claimants, provincial municipal government, professional armies, slavery, imperial grain systems, roads, public religion and the movement of citizenship beyond the founding city. These are structural grammars rather than a new one-to-one chronology. The fictional houses, events and relationships are World Egg inventions.

Thalion dies naturally in 318 after naming Meren. Meren is the human son of Evara and Velsar and becomes Thalion's political son through adoption. Cassara's rule establishes that imperial command can pass to a woman. The Four Eagles are an actual short civil war, not four permanent successor states.

All birth, reign and relationship intervals fit ordinary human lifespans. Later emperors claim continuity with Thalion without possessing his body, soul or a supernatural hereditary substance.

## Regional placement

The core government remains at Caleran in the Aurin heartland. Raveth is an eastern river province capable of supplying markets and garrisons. Arcelon is an eastern corridor joining a market basin, mountain passages and an inner-sea port. Lacaris is an eastern provincial garrison town. These locations use physical relationships without supplying premature coordinates or modern borders.

Olveth is the already established Vardeni upland settlement. Its shelter-crown deepens that location without creating another people or Menhir. Sarqen remains the enlarged Second Harbour and gateway provincial centre.

The empire's southern edge meets varied Folk societies through forts, treaties, recruitment and war. The western northern dry belt retains camel-and-well cultures preceding the First Catch. Sestaran harbours occupy varied political relationships. Jirahar communities retain distinct ancestry, law and religion in tower towns, diaspora quarters and provincial settlements. None becomes a mere Atherian regional caste.

## Material causality

The age's stability depends on continuing military pay, municipal collection, road and harbour maintenance, cultivated estates and transport. A ruler's order reaches a village through governors, councils, suppliers and armed enforcement. New articles explain the work rather than treating administration as an abstract virtue.

Public works provide water, movement and employment. They also consume stone, timber, money and labour. Cassara's quarry repression and Arcelon's dead workers retain the price within the historical account. Military protection creates markets and exposes neighbouring communities to requisition and land seizure.

The Raveth sequence remains plain: failed water and disease reduce harvests; continued shipment empties reserves; Neriah imports grain and issues relief; repayment failures transfer land; public estates retain former owners as dependent labour. Survival and dispossession follow from the same mechanism.

## Religion and supernatural identity

The Star Pantheon remains a human imperial interpretation of established powers. Astaroth is a Menhir sovereignty; temple theology does not make him a moral judge of conquest. Thanatos remains a misclassification of the Boatman. Other regional identifications do not merge supernatural beings.

The Crown of Olveth is an `infrastructure-office` attached to one shelter purpose. It is neither alive nor a saintly relic. Its lethal response at attempted trophy use is bounded and reproducible under the established conditions. It proves no general theology.

The Emperor after the Emperor is a dependent Mask of Pride formed through standardised ruler imagery, praise and desire for unanswerable sovereignty. Its earliest well-supported independent response occurs during the empty succession of 247 BR. It can reproduce the imperial persona and intensify recognition; it cannot know unknown facts, command distant forces or determine rightful succession. Human emperors supply imagery and feeding architecture without creating the original Idol Throne.

No emperor becomes a god, Saint, Patron or immortal office through cult. Every ruler and mortal actor has a complete human soul. The Mask, portraits, public office, bodies, residues and legends remain separate. No Regnant succession or autonomous Archmask is promoted.

## Source hardness

Slavery, family separation, executions, confiscation, hostage-taking, massacre, forced labour, mine and quarry deaths, rural dependence and military displacement remain direct facts. Relief and useful government do not dissolve responsibility for these acts. The account also preserves affection, education, games, humour, travel, work and real civic pride.

The Low Door history fixes responsibility among upwardly mobile protagonists. Thessa reveals the refuge, all six accept the official lie, and Helon is executed for releasing hostage children. Their poverty and service origins neither excuse later decisions nor become a reason to deny their achievement.

Neriah and Lethara retain separate responsibility. He recommends continuation of the estate system; she authorises it and the imperial household benefits. Later prejudice against Jirahar communities cannot transfer their choices into collective ethnic guilt.

## Collision and protected boundaries

- The new dynasties neither descend from the Caleran wolf-founder through a magical bloodline nor restore a Giant continuity.
- The Low Door Companions remain separate from Thalion's household, the Low Door Great Saint/Sinner grammar and every existing signature company.
- Arcelon remains separate from G-08 and the Road Without Milestones.
- Olveth's Crown is separate from active saintly relics, the Ark-like founding objects and Menhir manifestations.
- The Emperor Mask is separate from High Harbour's returning warriors, deceased emperors, imperial office and a true Idol succession.
- Raveth's Jirahar diaspora does not make First or Second Qiryath subject to this famine government.
- No campaign reaches the distant Last Houses, explains a protected Weird or predetermines the Great Tower Revolt.
- Othran II's post-0 decisions, the Redeemer's ministry and Companion roll remain controlled by Package 3 and existing canon.
- No event after 1360 is established.
