# Protected Questions of the Historical Atlas

This article fixes the limits of the Atlas, not answers behind those limits. A recorded question is not evidence that its proposed explanation is true.

## Two erased people

### The name removed by people

ER-01 was a real human being with a complete soul. After a catastrophe, human authorities removed the name and redistributed followers or practices; they did not exterminate everyone connected with the person. Altered records survive. This history belongs beside the late Long-Frontier figure traditions, but neither the person's name, guilt, judgement nor the nature of the catastrophe is fixed. Suppression may have protected victims or protected the guilty. No current claimant is authenticated.
^atlas-er-01

### The name wounded by an enemy

ER-02 was a different real human. A hostile supernatural attack damaged images, names, testimony and institutional memory, never the soul. Surviving contradictions can establish that an attack occurred without revealing its author. Copied voices, Patron personas, Masks and frauds later offered incompatible identities. None is automatically the original person. Allegiance, judgement, attacker and any authentic later manifestation remain unknown.
^atlas-er-02

The two cases share no event or original institution. Heaven is uncertain about neither. A Saint does not disclose the answer merely because an investigator asks.

## First-world scars and primordial faces

The Ford with Two Owners (I-L01), Three Unfinished Borders (I-L05) and Seven First Faces (I-I01) remain protected research questions. No first-family narrative, mortal witness to the Faewild Schism or human invention of an Idol is inferred from their titles. See [[Creation and the First Rebellions]] and [[The Weird of History — The Unexplained Register]].

## Sovereign changes not established

I-I03, II-I02 and the joint B3-16/B4-18 hypothesis propose true Idol changes of Regnant. None is canonized. B4-17 can support the existence of competing ancient divine names without proving that they were sequential rulers of one Throne. The autonomous survival claimed by ID-R10R/ID-P04R likewise remains unproven. No number of changed masks, images or biographies substitutes for the required distinction of persons.

The Red King's failed revolt is a separate, limited Archmask history. It does not spend a true-succession slot. See [[Idol Kingdoms and the Hunger Wars]].
^atlas-protected-successions

## Commissions not multiplied

HC-N01 and HC-N02 are protected proposals for another Phoenix binding and its choir. This programme establishes neither. The existing Phoenix of the Great Unbinding remains a particular commissioned servant, not a reusable event attached to every early divine intervention.

## Histories known locally but not abroad

The distant mountain court and the Last Houses have real local pasts. Their histories can be fixed authorially without bringing visitors from the core setting before 1360. Far-country knowledge is not automatically available in a Caleran library.

The Country Beneath the Rains has local surface trade. Its first formal embassy to the core world (CBR-12) has not occurred. That embassy, distant first contacts and their results remain future choices.

## The unfinished present

G-17, G-23, G-49, PT-R04, SB-P06, GP-IV-03 and present claims around OBA-N11 have inherited histories but unfinished current disputes. No promised rescue, verdict, authentic return, claimant's victory or institutional survival is guaranteed. The two Fourth Seal instruments and every specifically protected Weird retain their controlling limits.

These restrictions protect particular unknowns. They do not prohibit writing unrelated ordinary histories in the same era or region.
