# Authorial Notes from Reader Articles — Imperial Age

Safeguards and rulings moved out of the public Atherian imperial-founding and Age of Glory articles during the November 2026 editorial cleanup; material already held in the Age of Glory, Imperial Founding and other control reviews was removed without duplication, and each item below keeps its original force.

## [[Atherian Imperial Age]]

- "Their later quarrels over Rome-like legitimacy cannot be projected backward into the first war-settlement leagues." Thalmyric–Eclessian legitimacy disputes are not to be read back into the war-settlement leagues. (The companion "no two ancient biological halves" ruling is already held in the World Historical Atlas ledger.)

## [[Provinces and Municipal Life in Imperial Atheria]]

- "A provincial term had no single uniform length." Do not fix a standard length for Atherian governorships.

## [[The Crown of Olveth]]

- "The response belonged to the made object's continuing purpose. The Crown contained no human soul or Menhir presence." Supplements the Age of Glory review's `infrastructure-office` ruling.

## [[The Companions Who Entered by the Low Door - Imperial History]]

- "No single source preserves every participant's motive." The company's motives remain distributed across Oren's account, workshop marks and household traditions.

## [[Lethren Ostar]]

- Of Tavisha and Kharuz after Rethmar: "Neither founded a northern kingdom."
