# Package D — Folk, Ättar and Southern Cold World Canon Integration Review

Package D establishes the ancient southern cold world as a connected region with several independent human societies, the fallen penal crownland of Nhaldur, a separate Grobi predator lineage and local histories of magic, war and political formation. This review records the material promoted on 13 September 2026 and the distinctions that control later development.

## Promotion ruling

The author approved the Package D canon pass on 13 September 2026 after reviewing the developed histories and their comment-response revisions. The promotion fixes the ordinary history of Nhaldur, Folk and Ättar formation, the War of the Empty Halls, the Deep Voice and ancient Grobi pack life through 350 BR.

The comparative sources, superseded alternatives and comment history remain on the research shelf. Canonical articles present the selected World Egg history without importing the names or claims of the real-world and game sources used for comparison.

## Fixed history

### Nhaldur and the First Rewriting

- Nhaldur was a lesser southern Giant crownland used as a shared penal system by the Five Imperial Horizons and numerous lesser crowns. Six principal ring-fortresses governed scores of mines, camps, road towers, hostage houses and harbour prisons.
- The five Key Houses were hereditary exile dynasties. Their privileged members acted as gaolers while remaining legally confined to the crownland.
- Imported grain, local fish, geothermal heat, sheltered agriculture, mines, roads and skilled prisoner labour sustained the system. Their failure caused the War of Open Keys between approximately 2292 and 2264 BR.
- Vaska Ren, Ilyen Marr and Sorna Vale led distinct parts of the human revolt. No single liberation company or successor government inherited the crownland.
- Crown Gaoler Urdazar Vorzhal and Nashara Nemek concentrated 2,014 registered human captives in the Kharvos lower barracks. A dependent Red Reckoner supplied the regimen as part of Wrath's plan for the military vacuum left by the Giant collapse.
- Wrath directly performed the First Rewriting around 2284 BR. The victims' human souls separated and entered judgement. Wrath rebuilt the vacated flesh as a new self-reproducing living continuity. Giant bodily craft prepared bodies but could not perform the categorical act.

### Folk formation

- Folk ancestors were principally Orphaned southern populations. Escaped lesser Giants, giant-blooded humans and other Nhalduran refugees entered older coast, forest, river and upland communities after the Unbinding.
- Most descendants were human and possessed complete human souls. Particular lines could inherit unusual strength, hardiness or magical aptitude. Rare true Giant births retained Giant continuity and did not form a biological caste.
- Fäskogarfolk is the reconstructed name of an older speech-and-custom continuum. It was never one state, population or pure ancestral race.
- Belathi, Hartheni and Odrani were three broad ancient centres among several local peoples. Their leagues, forest assemblies and gords remained politically independent.
- Local annual All-Things allowed chiefs and free men to settle disputes, compensation, routes and levies across communities. They neither created one Folk state nor gave every inhabitant formal political voice.
- Proto-Atherian and later Atherian relations included conquest, enslavement, hostage-taking, alliance, service, trade, marriage, absorption and resistance. Atherian settlement never made the country empty beforehand.

### Ättar formation and the Empty Halls

- Ättar societies grew from related Orphaned populations in the far-cold valleys and coasts. Nhalduran refugees and Giant-descended lines entered those communities without making the Ättar a Giant people.
- Farms, fisheries, stores, halls, boat routes, compensation law and annual All-Things existed before the War of the Empty Halls. The war changed an established hall world rather than creating it at one moment.
- From 1608 to 1596 BR, Gharuk Eight-Bowls and Varka Red-Ladle attempted to turn the recurring winter raids into a permanent Warm Road. Their Nine-Drum Host depended upon sledges, captured food, breeding hearths and a vulnerable supply route.
- Urzha sold the host's route after Gharuk began feeding her captured brood to his ogres. Human victory also depended upon Hedra Store-Keeper's common stores, boats, gates, scouts and the cooperation of four separate coalitions.
- Savar of Red Shoal, Torvek of Hornwall, Brennar of Stoneden and Eldran of Grey Pass petitioned Mother Moon's Pale Household. Each chief received one office-bound hybrid animal form inherited through the paternal line of that office. The sworn fighters beneath all four standards received the lesser rage, which later spread through intermarriage among a wider Ättar population.
- The courtiers claimed each chief's firstborn child. Returned children possessed the same human souls and apparent ages but discontinuous childhoods altered by Court-time. Hedra opposed the price before the chiefs accepted it.
- The coalitions cut the Warm Road and starved the host. Gharuk's own orcs ate him alive when injury and bodily reversion exposed weakness. Human victors killed wounded Grobi and young at two camps, recovered some human captives and enslaved others after accusing them of service without distinguishing coercion from willing aid. Later halls established harsher watch and storage obligations.

### The Deep Voice

- The Deep Voice developed from Veyrun's prepared speaking galleries and pressure chambers. Escaped callers carried work speech into Odrani and Kreseni country after the ring-fortress fell.
- A working joined meaningful speech, trained breath, bodily stance and a known place. Far calls relayed intelligible words through tested chambers, horns or air paths. Weather workings redirected existing wind, fog or precipitation. The art did not summon lightning.
- Yevra Stone-Ear, Dorovan Rain-Breaker and Ravka Earth-Wake reached the tradition's military height during the War of Seven Gords. Their work retained bodily costs, terrain limits and the possibility of refusal.
- Sarvek survived the Dramovar catastrophe of 1018 BR and founded the Quiet Measure in a high Gale-Crown sanctuary around 1008 BR. It remained a fringe order devoted to Zephyraxis while martial, village and work schools continued.
- Deep Voice and [[Harmonic Shellcraft]] draw upon related elemental resonance. Their histories, institutions, conductors and technical grammars remain separate.

### Grobi life and agency

- All Grobi descend from the First Rewriting. They reproduce sexually, bear live young and form a single lineage whose bodies move through goblin, hobgoblin, orc, ogre and troll stages according to food, inherited floors and bodily strain.
- Grobi are intelligent and capable of suffering, memory, language, planning, tool use, attachment and tactical choice. Their continuity possesses neither the Image nor a human soul or moral conscience.
- Domination, fighting and consumption are their creaturely ends. Pack loyalty, delayed violence and temporary restraint protect food, breeding strength, reputation or dominance; they do not amount to mercy, justice or moral cooperation.
- Humans never become Grobi through hunger, diet, captivity, adoption, blood or pack declaration. Grobi never become human through baptism, instruction or human upbringing.
- Grobi keep human captives as food or for an immediate task before slaughter. They do not admit humans into the lineage or maintain permanent human household slavery.
- Adult goblins approximate the size and strength of human children aged ten to twelve. Orcs stand roughly seven to nine feet and possess at least the strength of five human men. Crude tools, ambush, poison, food-driven growth and regeneration explain the military danger without granting advanced industry.
- Narrow-Bowl and Ash-Marrow hearths, the Eighth Share, drum codes, stage-gland cutting and the White Pot truce are fixed institutions. Winter hosts grow through the warmer seasons and move north when southern forage closes.

## Identity and supernatural controls

- Every human in these histories bears the Image and a complete human soul regardless of ancestry, slavery, descent or cult.
- Human descendants of Giant-blooded lines remain human. A rare child born with Giant continuity is a Giant rather than a human with a larger body.
- Nhalduran Giants, human souls, Grobi continuity, Wrath, dependent Red Reckoners, Menhir persons, the Pale Household and elemental arts retain separate identities.
- Mother Moon's bargain establishes the two gifts and the firstborn price. It grants no moral approval to the chiefs, their offices or later inheritance customs.
- The Quiet Measure's devotion to Zephyraxis neither makes the Menhir the source of every Deep-Voiced working nor turns elemental technique into Menhir sovereignty.
- Effective Grobi ritual and successful predation establish no divine legitimacy. Wrath remains an Idol sovereignty, and a Red Reckoner remains a dependent Mask.

## Collision rulings

- Nhaldur joins the list of lesser Giant crowns. It is not a sixth Imperial Horizon, and its regional collapse neither causes nor replaces the wider Great Unbinding.
- The War of Open Keys is separate from the Great Unyoking in Vathra and the Prophet's liberation of the Glass people.
- [[G-61 - The Hall That Faced the Grobi|The Hall That Faced the Grobi]] records an earlier local crisis about incompatible duties. None of its protagonists becomes one of the four chiefs, and it does not contain the Nine-Drum Host or Moon bargain.
- Kreseni and Vardeni possess old kinship, work and marriage connections. Kreseni are neither the ancestors of every mountain population nor a replacement for the Vardeni within Aurin and Atherian history.
- Tidewood remains one local western Folk history. The Three Beaches, Aulon's dry fleet and Casvilar's southern voyage keep their established places and dates.
- Casvilar's 390–379 BR southern campaigns retain Morveth, Drenoc, Kellovar, Darneth and Velcara. His 385 BR coast voyage gained hostages and gifts without establishing an Ättar province.
- The Great Keel-Taking belongs to approximately 400–540 AR and remains outside the ancient package.
- Grobi full moral agency is superseded wherever it appeared in older bestiary or campaign summaries. Human commanders remain morally responsible for their own surrender, captivity, reprisal and extermination policies.
