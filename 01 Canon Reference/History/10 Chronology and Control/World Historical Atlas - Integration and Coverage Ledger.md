# World Historical Atlas — Integration and Coverage Ledger

This is the canonical integration record for the first whole-world Atlas build. It is not the readable history itself: use [[World Historical Atlas MOC]]. The Garden remains an exploratory source nursery.

## Authority and precedence

1. Existing controlling cosmology, chronology and explicit promotions.
2. Final collision/disposition ruling: identity and placement.
3. Source-fidelity hardening: load-bearing cruelty, guilt and irreversible cost.
4. Human-coherence rulings: actual cause and effect.
5. Narrative rebuild: readable voice, never silent replacement of underlying facts.
6. Superseded drafts: retained as archival sources only.

“Integrate” does not make every cited comparative myth literally true. Each destination declares whether it establishes an event, a cultural memory, a received tradition or a protected question. Myth articles establish their telling communities and ordinary remainders; individual supernatural claims require their own authorial ruling.

## Incorporated reconciliations

- The Giant world remains many-state. The five horizons are not five exhaustive kingdoms. Eighteen lesser polities receive historical roles in [[Lesser Crowns of the Giant Age]].
- The century-long Glass Sojourn ends around 2165 BR. The Second Hearth's former 2210 BR opening is replaced by c. 2120–1780 BR so its emigrants do not leave before the common departure.
- The Long Frontier proper ends in 884 AR. The dated spine's old 612–900 section label is expanded to distinguish that period from renewed war ending in 905.
- “Western empire” is not used as shorthand for Eclessia. Eclessia lies east of Thalmyria in the established map. Local western coasts retain their actual local directions.
- The Drowned Standard's original refusal belongs before the loyal angels' primordial confirmation. Its later human-war origin story is cult propaganda; it is not a later loyal angel falling.
- The old Anthe-at-1002 wording is retrospective Eclessian apologetic, as the controlling chronology already rules. Her actual regency remains 1011–1019.
- PT-R08 enriches EL-R02's warning-city tradition; it does not become an independently promoted Egg White Dead City.
- The pressure-brine court's divers are altered humans with complete souls, alongside independently living Driftkin. No unclassified triton species or native Shell people is smuggled in.
- The hidden Shell quarter occupies Blackwall Compression Yard, one of the existing fourteen anchors. No fifteenth traversable Material installation is added.
- A false deep guide's “single kingdom” remains propaganda, not a claim about Akakor or a real Indigenous people.
- Source composites remain labelled in the research. Generic living-tradition attribution is not canonized: II-L02 uses a world-native local story, and the incomplete Innu comparison in I-L03 does not license invented Tshakapesh atrocities.

### Substantive corrections and source precedence

- **Atherian periodisation:** the old opening implied timeless imperial rule and star worship. The revised entrance follows the controlling sequence from southern separation through the twelve-city Compact and republic to Thalion around 350 BR, then conversion and the Fall. This consolidates existing facts; Package 2's depth writing has not begun.
- **Thalmyric religion in the Atherian entrance:** the statement that Thalmyria had converted to the Eclessian faith was obsolete. The current Thalmyric Church and the 1015 Split control. The revised article identifies the separate successor churches.
- **Successor populations:** the old Atherian description of origin is now read alongside the Two Fissions and later Braided formations. Thalmyrics, Eclessians and Sestarans are not unchanged ancient biological branches. Eclessia remains east of Thalmyria; the largest Atherian material concentration remains in the Thalmyric heartland.
- **Astratheon assertions:** stellar guidance, divine imperial choice and the claim that star gods watch the world are presented as cult interpretations. Actual Menhir encounters remain real at their existing level. Thanatos remains a misclassification of the Boatman, with false theft-and-bargaining myths; no new identity is assigned.
- **Nsizwa triumphal language:** the earlier descriptions of universally unstoppable champions are treated as praise and reputation, consistently with the existing Izivukwa ruling. Awakened bodies and apparitions remain real. Named persons' final continuities remain unassigned. The Mzikhona victory account retains its received status.
- **Jirahar generalisation:** the earlier claim that no foreign state fully trusts any Jirahar is replaced by the already documented range from protected autonomy and cooperation to suspicion and expulsion. It does not establish a new tolerant state or remove an existing persecution.

Other additions to the entrances are explanations drawn from the supporting articles named above. Dates such as Halmenth 411, the compact of 908–909, Castara 1332 and the Ash Road Peace of 1334 are existing dates, not new promotions. Characteristic food, household practice, craft and local places are likewise inherited material.
