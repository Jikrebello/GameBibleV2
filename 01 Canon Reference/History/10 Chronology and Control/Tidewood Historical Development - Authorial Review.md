# Tidewood Historical Development — Authorial Review

This records the first regional depth example after the Atlas build. [[History of the Tidewood Coast]] is the readable history; [[Tidewood Coast]] is its regional entrance. Navigation repairs are separately recorded in [[Dependable Navigation - Review and Repair Ledger]].

## Authority and scale

The post-Giant settlements, Three Beaches relationships, dry-fleet seizure, later voyages, Gannet Head catastrophe, Edris's evacuation and Beam War are now local historical canon. Their dates and consequences are fixed at the stated precision. The living beliefs and contested memories described beside them also exist; that does not authenticate every telling.

The Three Beaches occupy one connected coastal neighbourhood within the existing Tidewood map region. They do not replace the other kindreds, become three regional capitals or define a new country. The Bracken Water is a local estuary stream, not a newly drawn continental river. No distances, coordinates or borders are silently added to the world map.

The ordinary named additions are moderate commitments needed for a readable history. Their existence is not held pending a campaign. Precise ancient headcounts, private genealogies and exact graves remain unassigned where the histories do not need them. Current conflicts remain unresolved after the 1360 baseline.

Tova is Hadrun's adult daughter, a skilled worker and mother of children left in Keldscar; their unknown later fate is a historical loss, not a new protected Weird.

## The Court of Salt and Teeth is not one being

The existing court account already combines Thalassor-facing ecologies, Patron exchanges and hunger Masks. This development fixes only the following local encounters, not every bearer of those folk names.

| Appearance in this history | Authorial identity | What it can and cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Net-Mother at the separate tidal pool | One sapient Water-Wife, `court-law`, acting through a local Thalassor-aligned water pattern | Redirects fish and changes local passage while the pool remains connected to its tidal reach. Prices concern fish, returned matter or local flooding; this is neither moral title to all sailors nor control of drowned human souls. She is not the Guest. |
| White Seal sightings | Some are ordinary seals; others are unresolved local interpretations | No single immortal animal or sovereign is established. A sighting cannot identify who answered a bargain. |
| Guest with Shark Teeth at Gannet Head, 710–712 | Dependent Gluttony Mask, `idol-pattern`, anchored through the feeding stone and the deliberate wreck-to-feast practice | Concentrates the unnatural catch through its feeding arrangement, consumes offerings and punishes withdrawal locally. This does not create fish without food limits everywhere or govern ordinary sea weather. Breaking the feeding apparatus ends this site's extraordinary catch and expels its local manifestation; Gluttony's Throne survives. No Archmask promotion or succession occurs. |
| Drowned Ferryman encountered by Edris in 1002 | A living individual Driftkin, `drift-rewritten-life`, occupying a Patron role at a small Egg White shore refuge | Provides one crossing through a maintained adjacency for people and specified carried goods. Edris's working sailcloth supplies repair and stabilizing material for the refuge. The Ferryman is not a dead human boatman, Hell's ferryman, a Menhir or a Saint. The title describes appearance and local belief. Its later whereabouts are not fixed. |

The Net-Mother's identity is fixed for this pool, not every use of the name across Tidewood. Human children bodily altered by other court bargains remain complete `human-soul` persons unless a separate controlling ruling explicitly establishes another origin; no such change is introduced here. A court can alter a body without acquiring its soul.

The Guest's feeding depended on an invitation-and-supply practice anchored at the inlet. Iven and the participating householders maintained it knowingly as the practice became murder. Their sons and daughters did not become guilty or metaphysically bound merely by descent. The wreckers' apparatus was destroyed locally; its future reconstruction or a new claimant in 1360 is not established.

The Ferryman's exchange was not worship and did not convert Edris into a Patron or give it custody of her children. Its boat carried living people through a short crossing and emerged at a Material landing. Neither an afterlife nor a new Shell gateway was traversed. No existing fourteen-anchor Shell budget changes.

All named mortal figures in these histories have complete human souls. Their bodies, remembered personae and household customs are not alternate continuities. Edris is not promoted into the Great Saints constellation. Brenna's courage is not a sainthood ruling, and Maelin's successful revolt is not a divine commission. No saintly manifestation or angelic campaign is added.

## Sources, retained severity and separate identities

The new detailed episodes are **world-native elaborations of existing canon**, not newly attributed versions of a real people's sacred story. No additional foreign myth is silently assigned to them. The narrative approach uses connected change, momentum and significance without forcing every life into an identical heroic plot.

The execution of surrendered retainers, captive deaths in the fire, Hadrun and Tova's sale, killing of wreck survivors, the drowned sacrifice, Teren's death, lethal sabotage and occupied-storehouse burning are retained in readable accounts. They are not confined to sensitivity notes. Victims have work, relationships and surviving communities; the accounts do not supply automatic reconciliation.

The workshop humour and shared meals are not compensation that makes the crimes acceptable. They establish that these were living societies rather than locations existing only to commit or suffer an atrocity.

## Geography and inherited boundaries

South remains coldward. Tidewood is an ocean-facing Folk region; Ättar ranges lie to the southwest on the established board. The Three Beaches have no invented north–south order beyond their local shore relationships. Keldscar's exposure and Brackenmouth's shelter explain why boats and food move between them. Longer connections to Sestara, the inland Folk, Jirahar fleets and Ättar crews are inherited routes, not evidence of new contact with the Last Houses or distant uncontacted peoples.

Wolfsmere remains a separate 993 event in the Green March history. Edris and her neighbours receive news and experience its consequences; it does not happen again at Tidewood. The wider Folk Wars, War of the Empty Halls, Saltborn formation and Reconquest keep their established participants, places and scales. House Valdren is a purchasing house using Sermoran shipping, not a new ruling Seal or national Sestaran institution.

The 75 Garden roots and their canonical destinations are unchanged. The new comic tradition enriches regional canon outside that fixed nursery inventory. No additional G-ID or GS-ID is created. All current figure choices are pre-1360 biography or present circumstance; no future victory, conversion, succession or supernatural answer is fixed.

### Controls relocated from reader prose

The following boundaries retain their original force. They are consolidated here so that the articles can explain their subjects without repeatedly interrupting the account with design exclusions.

- Tidewood and the Three Beaches establish no new kingdom, surveyed province or coast-wide hereditary command. Local gathering does not speak for every inlet, the whole Folk or the powers encountered at the shore. The Orphaned ancestry and all geographic boundaries above remain controlling.
- Maelin's injury is bodily, not a magical brand. Her employment does not establish a lifelong secret rebellion. Her customary handprint proves neither an authentic relic nor inherited rule. The capture is not a general expulsion of Atherians. The burned labourers died through confinement and the attack, not a necessary supernatural sacrifice.
- Orven establishes no Blackkeel order, inherited command or posthumous return. Neither his gifts nor his enemies' victory establishes moral innocence. Haldren belongs to another house. Hadrun and Tova's fate remains unknown.
- Brenna's warning came from recognised rigging, not a vision. She did not command every rescuer, destroy Tidewood's sea sovereignty or become mistress of the sea. Her renewed float is not her authenticated possession and cannot promise rescue from drowning. A narrative description of aid remains distinct from a sainthood ruling.
- Edris's living passengers were not resurrected dead. The Ferryman acquired neither shore ownership nor their descendants. The Common Loft is not a cult entitled to future passengers. Its contents are renewed working material, not proven original or speaking relics. Edris receives no new commission, sainthood or judgement through the rewrite; Chapel-Folk's regard remains a historical practice.
- The Court's appearances remain separately classified in the table above. Thalassor-aligned ecologies, Patron exchanges and dependent hunger Masks can receive related folk names. Some exchanges end at their agreed price; others sustain recurring predation. Net-Mother bargains offer no general promise of safety or return of drowned relatives. Their description fixes no additional death or encounter.
- The Guest's destruction was local and leaves Gluttony's Throne intact. No inherited guilt attaches to children of participating households. Refusing a compulsory catch is a costly custom, not a protective charm.
- Objects and ruins—including the Burnt Sleeping Ground, the loft, the renewed float and nets carrying Tova's attributed designs—retain their historical associations without authenticating every surviving piece. A donated panel's allocation is a practical decision, not a judgement on a passenger's human worth.
- The launch patch and shared bowl create no compulsory sea tithe. The comic boatwright establishes no magical vessel, sea bargain or lost king. The tale's performance and custom remain canon at the existing stories-told level.
- Valdren remains a purchasing house trading elsewhere after withdrawal, not a fallen national dynasty. No 1360 dispute receives an outcome. All pre-existing continuity, commission, collision and protected-mystery rulings remain unchanged.
