# Real-History Concordance

How the Known World maps onto real history — the people, their motivations, and how each borrowing gets recontextualized so it is load-bearing rather than a knockoff. Companion to [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]].

## The Compass Key

The world is a **mirrored Mediterranean**: north is the desert (the Ahl-e-Ahd's North-Africa/Levant analog), the temperate middle belt holds the old imperial heartland, and south descends into cold forest, taiga, and permafrost (the Folk, the Ättar, and beyond them the Grobi — the pagan Baltic/steppe frontier, frozen). **Thalmyria faces west, Eclessia faces east.** This deliberately flips the real map: the "Byzantine" power sits where Western Rome sat and the "Catholic" world where the East was. The flip is a feature — it prevents one-to-one geographic tracing while keeping every historical dynamic intact.

**Ättar design register:** Viking-age and North Sea hall society, heroic hall poetry, jarls, seasonal raiding, weregild, thralls, and gift economies provide the social scaffold. Changeling traditions and a deliberately transformed skinwalker motif provide the supernatural layer. These comparison labels are creator-facing only; public canon uses halls, taking the keel, Moon-Seers, skinwalker chiefs, and Paint-Keepers.

## Era-by-Era Mapping

### Bronze Age & Exodus → Age of Chains, Exodus, Scattering

The Five Imperial Horizons and their many client crowns are the Bronze Age palace world seen from below: monumental, bureaucratic, divine-king systems where ordinary people are labor inputs. Their fall compresses the Bronze Age Collapse and the biblical Exodus into the [[The Great Unbinding|Great Unbinding]] — prophetic plagues are one part of the systems collapse alongside revolt, Giant civil war, failed escape, divine judgement, and material breakdown. The collapse has a native engine the analogs lack: thinning Giant blood made the old works demand crews, captives, grain and at last children, and the plagues struck along the line where bodies were taken. [[The Glass Sojourn]] extends the Mosaic wilderness grammar into approximately a century of refuge and ethnogenesis: a mixed host becomes one people, and the Great Prophet dies at the outward threshold as its last direct witness to the Giant world. Joshua 5's manna, which ends once the people can eat the land's produce, is bent: the provision ends on its commission's schedule before the host can farm, and the cost is a conquest of farmers. Moses on Nebo is replaced by four incompatible public deaths. The later three-generation sanctuary fails in a quarrel over the seed the rear camps were eating, while responsibility for that failure remains disputed. The Late Bronze Age collapse proper returns much later as the northern Bronze-Road disruption (c. 1450–1325 BR): it keeps its human causes, but its famous part becomes the Reclaiming, when Ishkatar's dead came for their title-marked metal. Lydian coinage is replaced by Ortheian army silver c. 730 BR. The Scattering then uses two fissions rather than one tribal partition: southern war-settlement leagues separate first; the continuing Near-Glass population divides into proto-Jirahar and proto-Ahl-e-Ahd only generations later. Independent Orphaned reassembly and later Braided ethnogenesis proceed alongside both.

**Recontextualization rule:** the Giants must never feel like "fantasy Egypt." Five dominant horizons contain dozens of overlapping states, dynasties, subject cities, and institutions. Their leavings are the setting's Dwemer-ruin equivalent: ancient Glass they did not create, blood-sun craters, sealed Titan bodies, folded courts, and Moon-stranded machine persons. Later cultures inherit different wounds; no one empire or Exodus company causes them all.

### Mixed Maritime Recovery → Proto-Atherian Heroic Age

The proto-Atherian south takes from Late Bronze Age mobility the mixture of raiders, migrants, mercenaries, settlers, captive markets and fortified ports—not one ethnic “Sea People” or one universal collapse. Glass measures and preserved crafts help reconnect several post-Giant recovery horizons, but local pilots, smiths, farmers, laborers and routes make those networks possible. Different regions later break under war, extraction and route failure at different times.

This is also the first of four changing Atherian identities: war-settlement membership, heroic-maritime allegiance, city-civilizational identity and republican-imperial citizenship. The stages inherit from one another without constituting an unchanged bloodline.

The *Iliad*'s war over Helen becomes a war over a speaking image bound to a tide pact; the captive-and-ransom quarrel and the Polyphemus beat are cut, and the homecoming killing of the enslaved women is kept with a native cause.

### Greece → Age of City-States and Age of Stars

The Atherian city-states are the poleis: civic rivalry, leagues, tyrants, colonization, philosophy as civic technology. The Age of Stars is the classical-Hellenistic synthesis — astronomy, archives, law — that Rome inherited and militarized. The Star Pantheon forms here.

The Delian and Latin leagues survive as Caleran's custody of the Celestial Compact's calendar, rolls and ship money: a member whose days were left out of the year lost its own sailors (the Unreckoned Days, c. 652 BR). Kelarion's refused harbour and the Champion of Four Doors are native events with no analog to bend.

### Macedon and the Successors → Ortheia, Eryndor and the eastern kingdoms

Valeron II and Eryndor III supply the Macedonian political grammar omitted from the earlier city-state-to-Rome sequence. Valeron creates a professional royal instrument and compels a league; Eryndor inherits it, destroys a rebellious city, crosses the eastern sea and joins Arezan Tiravan against Arqesh. The war preserves independent Vashari, Arqeshite, Qahuri and Jirahar agency rather than turning the east into an empty field for one conqueror.

Eryndor's early death produces Vasharan, Ortheian, Delkosi, Qahuri and farther-eastern Successor states. Delkos, not Qahur, carries the strongest Ptolemaic court grammar. Ptolemy's seizure of Alexander's body becomes a corpse that governs: a voice in Eryndor's tomb disowns his heirs, which is why they die. Cleitus and Philotas survive only as two killings over bowing to Kassur III's images. The Zannanza affair is cut; Ashkur dies escorting Radiance exiles. House Nerethan descends from a later Delkosi officer family and joins that inheritance to Esharati and junior Qahuri kin. Eryndor's later romances draw from the Alexander tradition across Greek, Syriac, Persian, Arabic, Hebrew and European literature. History no longer narrates them: [[The Five Limits of Eryndor]] keeps the tales, and the campaign articles keep only the people the incidents cost.

**The TES move (Lorkhan/Shor dynamic):** the Star Pantheon is, behind the curtain, **Menhir worship misread**. The "High Augur of Astaroth" already implies it: Astaroth, Father Sun, Mother Moon and the rest are Second Circle Menhir whom the Atherians theologized into a civic pantheon — the same beings the Folk know by other names and opposite moral readings. Atherians worshipped Solzareth/Father Sun as ordering providence; the Ättar know the same being as the pitiless arithmetic of daylight. Same entity, two theologies, both partially right — that is your elf/human Lorkhan split, done with Menhir instead of a dead god. This is authorial canon.

Vashar's Achaemenid frame keeps Cyrus's restoration decree but loses the Shahnameh borrowings: Kaveh's apron, Zal and the Simurgh, and Rostam and Sohrab are cut. Herod Agrippa's death in Acts 12 becomes Orvaz I's at the Kavresh acclamation, after which the royal cult's mirrors stay dark. The "prophet spared, workers sold" template is used once, for Haviel.

### Rome → The Atherian Empire

Unification ends the city-state age (c. 350 BR); the Age of Glory is the Pax Romana. **Aurelius Thalion** is the fixed founder: land concentration, military patronage, provincial wealth, and rival commanders broke the republic; Thalion won the civil wars, seized Caleran, retained republican offices as ceremonial forms, and made supreme command hereditary in practice. The roads, laws, and archives every successor now fights over were built by the republic and centralized by the imperial settlement.

The imperial-founding pass selects Thalion as the **Augustus-like founder**. Teren Casvilar carries the Caesar-like conquest and assassination; Marovan Veyr, Lethren Ostar, Darion Velsar, Samyra Nerethan, Seluc Varroth and Istran Melvo preserve separately identifiable Pompey, Crassus, Antony, Cleopatra, Lepidus and Agrippa grammars. The earlier informal partnership and later public three-command government remain different arrangements. [[Imperial Founding - Authorial History and Integration Review]] records the transformations and precise canonical sequence.

Causality Pass 02 bends the late republic further. The Gracchi become a land bill blocked by an intercalated month and answered by the Sun at Sarven; the Social War is fused with the revolt of the naval league's allies and fought over harbour chains; Sulla dies in an office he cannot lay down; the Delian treasury survives as Caleran's custody of the Compact's calendar, rolls and ship money. Pharsalus becomes an indecisive Velos and Pompey's murder a refused table and death by fever in sanctuary; Julia lives; Cato survives in exile and writes; Crassus's crosses become the sale of rebels north; the Ides lure becomes challengers entered in an arms register; Antony's Parthian campaign is aimed into the Avesh approaches.

The final era review bent two endings that had stayed on their analogues. Vercingetorix's years in a cell and death after the triumph become Drenoc handed to his rival Morveth as part of the river chief's reward and dying in Morveth's hall before the thaw; no Atherian triumph displays him, and the river halls never forgive Morveth. Sextus Pompey's capture and execution by Antony's officer becomes Ardel given up by the Avesh command, which had seen Velsar's columns in its passes four years before and wants no second visit; Velsar's officers execute him in 354 BR.

The queen's dynasty follows the selected incoming-house route: Nerethan of Delkos joins Hadrima of Esharat's spared-town family. Sarqen is a surviving lesser port enlarged over generations, distinct from the destroyed sacrificial capital, and Samyra's legitimacy rests on the spared towns' Table Oath rather than on Cleopatra's grammar alone. Northern war uses defended water, camels and foot-mobile resistance; southern conquest encounters separate Folk coalitions. Neither landscape is a relocated Roman province.

The Age of Glory bends its Roman beats by the second step (Causality Pass 03, November 2026). The Year of the Four Emperors keeps four constituencies and the decisive grain, but the capital's claimant governs through a speaking portrait and the victor's famous act is covering it. The Fidenae collapse becomes an untested hall that kills the founder's heir and a Menhir test that every public work must pass, the Star's share. Caracalla's grant becomes the eastern cities' negotiated price for Elian Serrat, sworn before the emperor's image with a veiled form for Jirahar scribes. Nerva, Commodus and Severus are bent: the vacancy of 151 is held still by divided river commands, the veil and a fever summer; Velen I unveils the portrait and exempts his festival banks from the test; Doran's march is supplied by the Low Door Companions, and his officers enter by a low door instead of swearing before the image. No dated Age of Glory event falls on an analog year. [[Age of Glory - Authorial History and Integration Review]]

### Iron-Age Europe and the Imperial Frontier → The Folk


The Folk draw structural inspiration from the clan, tribal, hillfort, federate, and successor societies encountered beyond and within the Roman frontier: Gaulish confederacies, Gothic war-leadership, Germanic halls, and the Brittonic, Welsh, Cornish, Scottish, and Irish persistence of local law and identity. This produces a political spectrum rather than one generic tribe. Some communities use stone, bronze, and scarce bloomery iron; others field mature iron-working confederacies, fortified towns, treaty systems, and armies capable of negotiating with empires.

The Enaidfae are old-rite Folk confederacies, not a fantasy ancestry or a direct ethnic analogue. Their local courts borrow the structural unease of traditions in which bright and terrible beings share etiquette without becoming morally safe. The powers are appeased because they are dangerous, not loved as benevolent parents. The Chapel-Folk carry the process by which converted frontier peoples remain culturally themselves rather than becoming foreign settlers overnight.

The annual Grobi winter war supplies a setting-native institution with no direct historical duplicate: frontier duty, honor migration along the Frost-Road, refuge architecture, hidden granaries, and seasonal war-kings. It keeps the Folk militarily consequential without unifying them into a nation.

**Recontextualization rule:** public canon uses Folk clans, courts, confederacies, and regional names. It does not use Gaulish, Gothic, Celtic, Germanic, Pictish, or other real-world labels as in-world taxonomy.

### Northern Nomad Empires and Plains Societies → The Tarkhan

The Tarkhan borrow political and military structures from Hunnic, Mongol, Turkic, and Oghuz histories: mobile courts, banner coalitions, kurultai legitimacy, remount wealth, extraction without occupation, contract cavalry, and the danger of hired frontier soldiers becoming rulers. The western dry belt adds ancient camel pastoralism, well law, caravan raiding, and slave markets. The northeastern grassland adds dispersed horse camps, migratory herd hunting, falcon reconnaissance, and the political importance of routes that settled states cannot hold continuously.

Indigenous North American plains histories, particularly Sioux and Apache traditions, inform selected Grass-Tarkhan relationships among mobility, horse adoption, hunting, kin networks, scouting, and resistance to surveyed frontiers. They are not treated as interchangeable with Eurasian nomad histories and do not supply direct names, sacred objects, dress, or flattened “tribal” imagery.

The Four Hordes transform succession-empire precedents into setting-native regional coalitions. White is the raid and reclamation frontier; Iron the employed-warrior-to-institutional-capture trajectory; Moon the ritual and constitutional center; Ash the road-peace and mercenary frontier. The Nine Wells provide an objective dispossession around which sincere present communities still have unequal claims and responsibilities.

The steppe's horse power is the fallen empire's own blessed remount stock (the First Catch), and its history follows that grace's clock: the Long Reach of 951 and the Horse War against the Covenant's hound-guarded wells while the lines were young; the Thinning; then Qarayel's rise and the Partition, bent through the horse-grace: a renewed blessing (1249), extraction timed by its thinning, and herds partitioned by grace-line (1304).

**Recontextualization rule:** public canon uses Well-Tarkhan, Grass-Tarkhan, Hordes, banner-lords, wind-speakers, sky-riding, word-price, and named routes. Hun, Mongol, Oghuz, Sioux, Apache, and related comparison labels remain creator-facing only.

### Christianity → The Redeemer's Faith

Already the vault's strongest mapping (ministry, execution, resurrection, persecution, catacomb church). The conversion-era mapping is embodied by:

- **Cassian the Baptised (vision 292 AR; reign to 316)** — Constantine: a civil-war usurper who sees the White Flame before battle, wins under its sign, and legalizes the faith without immediately outlawing the stars. His motivation remains ambiguous *in-world* while the authorial spine knows the vision was real — and knows the man remained ruthless anyway. His deathbed baptism creates the Cassian Wager. That gap between real grace and unchanged politics is the whole Conversion-era theme.
- **Astrion the Apostate (348–351 AR)** — Julian: a scholar-emperor raised in the new faith who restores the stars, stages a public test of legitimacy which the Red Star answers once, dies young on campaign, and becomes a permanent what-if. Sestaran humanists quietly adore him; the Church uses him as a cautionary tale.
- **Vestian the Pious (377–398 AR; Dissolution 383; compulsion from 385)** — Theodosius, bent: dissolves the augur colleges, wins one civil war against the Star-recognising eastern command (the Frigidus moved before compulsion and decided by a refused answer), and makes the faith compulsory through the Profession, whose lamps fail to kindle (the Second Silence; no analog). Thessalonica's decree follows a war instead of preceding it, and Callinicum becomes the Raveth hearing house. Inherited wound #9. The Vestian persecutions of the Jirahar occur inside this same reign.

**Triangle (Conversion era):** GOOD — sincere bishop-martyrs who remember the catacombs; BAD — court converts weaponizing heresy law for property and revenge; UGLY — the old augur aristocracy, selling their archives and legitimacy to the new order piece by piece.

Causality Pass 04 turns the era into a quarrel over who may authenticate a sign. Plutarch's cessation of oracles becomes the Three Silent Days (0 AR). Nero and Domitian become the Great Purge as a records war (c. 36–45). Decius's edict answers the Kept Lamps (245). Milan becomes the Velos Concord (c. 296), a trade of property for a miracle. Crispus and Fausta become the son and the High Augur (c. 309); the empress lives. Helena's Cross becomes the Nails' route through the governor's case chest to the Pillar. Ephesus becomes an effect of the Second Silence and Raveth. No beat from 245 to 398 keeps its analog's AD year; 292 stays.

The apostolic history follows the shared meals, multilingual relief, mission and disputes over admission in Acts. The Qiryath agreement develops the admission question in the setting's own laws. ### Jewish and Traveller Histories → The Jirahar

The two Qiryath destructions carry the First and Second Temple sequence. Arqesh supplies the earlier river-imperial conquest and deportation. The Babylonian exile and return are bent: an altar, not a decree, settles the return, and the sign answers the mixed households Ezra's counterpart wanted expelled. The Samson and Bathsheba cycles are cut (Hador's and Yedran's dead remain under native causes). Pompey's arbitration of the Hasmonean succession, the daily sacrifice for the emperor and Florus's seizure of the Temple treasury are bent into the Trial before the Stars and the Qiryath charter: no general enters the sanctuary, the decision turns on a refused trial and a scribal record, and the rival empire, Vashar, leases rather than loses. Joseph's famine (Genesis 47) becomes Raveth's integration famine and the loan before the Face, with no seven-year shape. The 61–68 AR Great Tower Revolt follows the sequence of sanctuary exactions, initial defeat of an imperial army, provincial reconquest, factional fighting, siege, sanctuary fire, captive vessels in triumph and a last fortress. The adopted Atherian settlement cancels Qiryath's charter, installs a garrison, and sends a major wave of free refugees beyond direct imperial rule to older diaspora communities. Josephus describes the Roman victory from a position under Flavian patronage. His account of who wanted the Temple preserved is evidence of a claim about responsibility. The Atherian accounts dispute the first ignition and establish the subsequent stripping and demolition. Vestian's persecution centuries later targets the communities that endured or formed after the war; it ends at Qiryath with the Empty Charter (c. 396), which forbids rebuilding *before the Mesakha*, a clause no Roman decree carried. [[Great Tower Revolt and the Second Destruction]] · [Josephus, Jewish War II](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/josephus/war-2.html) · [Jewish War V](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/josephus/war-5.html) · [Jewish War VI](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/josephus/war-6.html) · [Jewish War VII](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/josephus/war-7.html)

The Scrolls and Peshara carry scriptural and legal-scholarly continuity; maternal descent, difficult conversion, food law, host charters, burial, communal courts, and Mesakha expectation carry the covenant-diaspora register. The cessation of authenticated sacrificial acceptance after Isfir's resurrection is objective setting history, interpreted differently by Jirahar and Redeemer churches.

Roma and Irish Traveller histories contribute road households, repair trades, performance, animal dealing, seasonal labor, host suspicion, repeated expulsion, and the ability to remain a people without territorial rule. They do not justify criminal, rootless, biologically exotic, or universally nomadic stereotypes.

The deep tower-citadels borrow the useful fantasy image of a visible stronghold above a much larger excavated settlement. Public canon explains them through generations of ordinary engineering, refuge, cisterns, archives, and Way-Gate custody rather than importing another setting's peoples or places.

**Recontextualization rule:** name the specific elders guilty in Isfir's death while rejecting hereditary guilt. Separate real financial institutions from ethnic conspiracy. Keep all direct historical and fantasy comparisons here; public prose uses Jirahar institutions.

### Egypt, Carthage and Druma → Ancient Qahur and the Gateway country

Ancient Qahur draws its long river chronology from several periods of Egyptian history: regional crowns and reunification, mixed Delta dynasties, violent southern restoration, temple estates, royal mortuary building, an exclusive solar experiment and repeated foreign rule. These elements are redistributed across the Sacred Basin's Orphaned communities and their relationship with Ishkatar's fallen riverworks. No historical Egyptian dynasty or deity appears as an in-world identity. The Ramesside temple estates become the Dead Landlords: mortuary estates and Second Houses, some with waking owners, that bankrupt the Aramen crown by c. 858 BR. Before 0 AR Qahur is three crowns inside the Atherian sphere; Sarqen alone carries the grammar of a royal country placed under a governor after its queen's fall.

The Gateway Republic draws its maritime economy, mixed recruitment and civic government from Phoenician and Carthaginian history. Its wars with Caleran keep Carthage's swift recoveries, an invasion of Italy and the destruction of the city as pressures only. Messana, the Mercenary War's timing, Sardinia, the Barcids, Fabius and Varro, Scipio, Zama and Masinissa are cut or inverted: the first war is fought over festival bread, the invader is beaten by his own gift of grain, the pay revolt becomes a carriers' stoppage at the end, and the salting gains a reason in the Gateway's own oath. Qarzeth's child sacrifice is historically real within the setting and strengthened by a dependent Gluttony Mask. The institution has named local opponents. Caleran destroys the central city while sparing lesser towns, then commits murder, rape and enslavement during the sack. This preserves both the moral urgency of ending sacrifice and the conqueror's culpability, which runs deeper than the sack: Caleran killed Gateway hostage children on its own festival ground in 609 BR, and every case it made against Qarzeth's altars was made from there. The consuls' last demand of 149 BC, that Carthage abandon its city and resettle inland, is bent: Caleran orders Qarzeth's people to carry the Provider's bronze face out of the sanctuary and break it on the shore with their own hands, and the assembly refuses because the city has eaten from the god's granaries for two generations.

The [[The White Measure and the Second Houses|White Measure]] takes its structural premise from Pathfinder's Druma and the Prophecies of Kalistrade: disciplined accumulation, bodily restraint, splendid mortuary preparation and concealed transformation after death. World Egg metaphysics changes the result. Successful founders retain their complete human souls, bind them unlawfully to embalmed bodies and create small Egg White demiplanes. They may act as Patrons through those estates. Failed rites can leave constructed offices or attract Greed Masks. Wealth therefore builds a prison and jurisdiction rather than purchasing heaven.

**Recontextualisation rule:** public canon uses Qahuri dynasties, Gateway institutions and White Measure houses. Egyptian, Carthaginian, Punic, Druman and Kalistocratic labels remain author-facing comparisons.

### Islam → The Ahl-e-Ahd

Mujalib is the Muhammad figure (revelation 541 AR, against 610 AD); the tribal unification is the Rashidun consolidation, fed here by the Bread of the Bound; Wave One is the Arab conquests, with most battles turned into services by breach-courts, Khalid's dismissal turned on the disputed Fourth Seal, and a halt (Tamsar, 611) that has no analog; the Velian States are al-Andalus complete with a Cordoba-style golden age under the Banu Mizan where Atherian learning survives in the conqueror's custody; the Ahdanite Guard is the devshirme/janissary system (already canon: slave-child levies); the Qahirid Resurgence is the Ottoman rise, made possible by the Ruling on Parties rather than by gunpowder; the Fall of Caleran is 1453, taken half by law (see the Long Frontier mapping below).

The present political structure also draws on the Ottoman imperial household, the military-household government of Mamluk Egypt, and the Witnessed-Seal/Sealed-Blood divide as a transformed Sunni/Shia succession dynamic. Iqashar carries the imperial center; Al-Qahur the sacred agricultural basin and military households; Qazmarid the confessional counter-caliphate; Zanthir the commercially powerful frontier gateway; and the Velian client belt the experience of Redeemer-faithful populations living under changing Covenant dynasties.

The empire's ethnic construction draws structurally on Kelesh's layered imperial identity: Ahdari, Iqashari, Qahuri, Qazmari, Zanthiri, and Velian peoples remain distinct beneath an Ahl-e-Ahd civilizational identity. Qadira contributes the patron-driven frontier and trade politics used in Zanthir. Katapesh contributes the market-city structure used for Mazrakan. No Pathfinder proper nouns enter public canon.

After Caleran the Ottoman frame is bent at every turn. 905–1190: the Ottoman Interregnum becomes the Widowed Seals; fratricide law becomes lodging; Suleiman and Mustafa become Suhrab's lodged son; Taifa fragmentation comes from widowed oaths; the Almoravid rescue is deliberately absent; the steppe horse power is the fallen empire's own blessed stock. The present Qahirid court is not an Ottoman "decline". The Guard's officer bench has witnessed accessions since the Emptying of the Benches (1243), the Cull turned lethal after the Pageless Prince (1252), and the Iron Horde holds frontier offices because only its horses stand beside bound things.

**Recontextualization rule:** the contract cosmology is what saves this from knockoff status. The real faith's actual content is replaced wholesale by law-as-cosmos, bound devils, and the Vault of Names. The behind-the-curtain truth that the angel is the Creditor gives the civilization a tragic dimension the historical analogy does not have: a sincere, brilliant legal civilization built around a fraudulent revelation whose institutions and magic still work.

**Triangle (Ahl-e-Ahd, present):** GOOD — jurists who believe the Covenant genuinely restrains tyranny (law protects the weak from the whim of the strong); BAD — the breach-courts and name-custodians for whom every soul is collateral; UGLY — the patron behind Malak al-Ahd, whose rent must keep flowing regardless of who rules.

### Late Rome to 1453 → The Long Frontier (398–905 AR)

Causality Pass 05 keeps the late-antique and early Byzantine pressures and bends every sequence. Gibbon's "triumph of barbarism and religion" becomes the Guttering: the faith did not weaken the state; the state's ownership of the faith emptied its public results, and the court covered the gap with hired Shell-fire and then with counterfeit. The Theodosian-to-Isaurian succession becomes four houses, each raised by a different way of paying the army; there is no Justinianic reconquest and no war of exhaustion on the eve of invasion. The 536 dust veil, the Justinianic plague, the war of 602–628 and the fall of Sasanian Persia become a limited, early Toll War, the Thin Floods and an ox murrain, and Vashar falls by ruling before Atheria is touched; its Ruzhan converts become the officer class that later destroys Atheria. None of these beats falls on the analog years of the dust veil or the plague (535–537, 541–543 AR).

The themes, the thughur and the akritai become the kindled marches, sworn in the Fire and renewed by the emperor in person; the 717 siege becomes the 611 standoff at Tamsar. The papacy's rise (Gregory's grain, the Donation, Pepin) becomes Simeon's examination: no forged donation, only an honest test made cheap by a sunk tax fleet, whose founding verdict may be wrong. The crusade against al-Andalus is a lawyer's adventure undone by its own pretext, and the war of the 860s has no analog. Ottoman gunpowder and Urban's bombard become the Ruling on Parties, a reading of a clause the analog lacks, at a moral cost the source lacks. 1453 keeps no cannon and no Giustiniani: Constantine XI's doomed heroism becomes a trap made of law, and the aid that never comes is withheld for the east's own reason. "Weak emperors" is retired; "devout and strategically incapable" survives only as an attributed schoolbook verdict. ### Byzantium → Thalmyria

Imperial continuity after the heartland's fall draws from Byzantium; liturgy, icons, married parish clergy, monastic bishops, and contemplative restraint draw from Eastern Orthodoxy. The regional texture draws from Greek, Serbian, Bulgarian, Macedonian, Romanian, and related Balkan histories. The return wars use Reconquista Spain as a structural reference: multiple generations of recovery, resettlement, mixed populations, frontier lordship, and a sacred objective achieved before the political process is finished.

Greek fire maps primarily to **warlock rune-script Shell-fire**, a controlled military technology. Weaponized manifestations claimed as Holy Flame are rare, disputed, and theologically dangerous; making every incendiary weapon sacramental would collapse a distinction the setting needs. Caedros I compresses the rebel-restorer pattern with Reconquest founders, while Caleran's recovery inverts the expected fall of an old imperial capital. The victory's cost is structural: demographic exhaustion, permanent militarization, disputed resettlement, war debt, and an urban heartland surrounded by a thinning interior.

The Reconquista frame is bent from the enemy's side: the Covenant south falls apart because its oaths were sworn to a man who died (the Widowed Seals, 929–938), Caleran is surrendered in 957 by an amir in his own name, and Tarkhan riders on the empire's own blessed horses hold Iqashar's army on its northern wells. The keys of their houses carried by exiles from al-Andalus become the Southern Houses' bronze water-tokens (1052): occupation-born families who reject return law go north with their Covenant deeds in cedar boxes and the tokens of fields they will not see again, and Khurzad later enforces those deeds with mercy towers.

Thalmyria's later texture is carried by the First Breaking (a soldier's misreading of the Guttering), the Impounding and Theodosia's Rekindling from the Ember, Caedros III's district lamps, the Unanswered Funeral, the Threshing Floor, the well-peace at Qarath's Well and the Nikandran restoration. The analogs (Leo III, Irene, Theodora, Basil II, Manzikert, Romanos IV, the Komnenoi) supply pressures only. After 1052 no beat should be predictable from them: Caedros III leaves his succession to the Fire, Qarath is lost to a contract cut into a well-curb, and the blinding is an attempt to void a ransom with the enemy's law. Nikandros I's appeal to the West (1227) becomes the Refusal of the Lamp: the price of aid is a test of the Great Lamp, and he refuses it.

### The 1054 Schism → The Great Split (1015 AR)

The filioque supplies the historical pressure, but the setting-native doctrinal weapon is the **Kindling Question**, not the older Procession formula. Veridun teaches that the Flame is uncreated and consents to dwell where sanctity prepares it; Eclessia teaches that the Flame as given to mortals is created grace entrusted to the apostolic body and governed through proof-protocol. Layered beneath it are primacy (Eclessian Lord Primarch versus Patriarch of Veridun), relic custody after the Reconquest, competing claims to Atherian inheritance, and the contested veneration of Caedros I.

Caedros is the empirical test case and the Talos-like dynamic within the no-apotheosis rule: Thalmyria treats him as a saint on the evidence of the Pillar and battle hymns; Eclessia rejects imperial self-authentication. **Authorial ruling:** the hymns kindle according to the righteousness and purpose of the singer, not Caedros's authorship and not institutional recognition. The result neither turns Caedros into a god nor lets either hierarchy own the Flame; it explains why both churches obtain genuine, inconsistent results. Older “Procession of the Flame” language may survive as a secondary scholastic analogy, but it must not be presented as the primary engine of the 1015 Split.

The rupture is a demand to examine the Great Lamp (Simeon's primacy as the see that examines over the see that keeps). Charlemagne's coronation is the discharge of a trust over oaths held since 711, based on a true kindling credited to the wrong person. Verdun is a drawing of lots. Vladimir's choice turns on Veridun refusing to return a lamp. Charlemagne's Saxon wars become Adalmar's register war on the Folk, which ends at a lake that stood up (Wolfsmere, 994).

### Renaissance Italy → The Sestaran Concord

The governing dynamic is Renaissance Italy crossed with pirate republics, free imperial cities, and modern entrepôt microstates: intense local patriotism, narrow citizenship, private war, common fear of conquest, and specialization strong enough to make larger empires dependent.

Direct mappings: Fiorenzia/Medori draws from Florence/Medici; Sermora from Venice, Genoa and Caribbean privateer polities; Aversia from Milan under military dynasties; Vorthara from Kaer Maga's anarchic refuge translated through World Egg magic; Castara from Atlantic boom ports and colonial companies; Orsena from Singapore, Ragusa and neutral free ports; Vardessa from Frankfurt, Nuremberg, Swiss free cities and San Marino. The Seven-Seal Muster carries Swiss and defensive-league logic without creating a federal state.

**Nicanor Vetti** carries the Machiavelli function, **Frate Severin** the Savonarola function, and Alessior II the Borgia function, though the engine of his reign is his treasurer Quarne and the dead paper. The public notes must use native names and institutions rather than these analog labels.

The Golden Book was emptied by the Sweat; the Medori fortune comes from the estates and Sendings of the plague dead and the registers taken in 1296; Severin rises on the 1345 "Bank of the Dead" pamphlet.

**Triangle (Sestara):** GOOD — civic humanists who believe archives and art redeem the fractured inheritance; BAD — the Pale Hand / Fleshweaver end of Vorthara, where knowledge has no brakes; UGLY — the Medori, financing every side of every war including both churches' reunification schemes.

### The Reformation → Eclessia's Present Crisis (1297 AR – now)

The trigger is **routing assurances** — indulgences recontextualized into a world where the Vestibule is real, which sharpens the theology enormously: the reformer's charge is not merely corruption but *fraud against souls*, and the old Church's defense (that sincere intercession genuinely helps) is also not empty, because prayer and relics demonstrably work. Both sides have a real case; that is what makes it a war and not a morality play.

The mapped figures and institutions are now fixed: **Ansgar of Brenholt** is the founding martyr; **Doctor Lucian Ferro** the systematizer; **Duke Rainald of Ironvale** the sheltering prince; **Celestin IX**, the Council of Veridance, and the Order of the Lantern the counter-reform; and [[The Korrheim Accord]] the territorial confessional peace. Bends that carry the chain: Ansgar's Count; Aldersbruck as a test; the Ashenmen's Weiss oath and its fulfilment in 1329; the Great Arrears; the Accord sworn on the Weiss; Sarnwell as the model for the five tests; Vessing's three testimonies, one per party. Augsburg's *cuius regio, eius religio* survives only as the Sanctum canonists' later Latinate rendering, *whose the realm, his the rite*; the formula sworn in the river is the plain speech of the river country, *every roof under its lord's lamp*, and it covers ministries, cantons and chapters as well as dukes.

**Regional inspiration register:** Aurelia carries the Papal States and Latinate church-principality function; Veridancia combines France, Burgundy, universities, and Catholic reform; Caldria combines Iberian crusading frontiers with Habsburg military administration; Ironvale carries Reformation Germany and the early industrial mining belt; Korrheim combines Swiss cantons with the confessional machinery of the Holy Roman Empire; Brauvalis draws on Bavaria, Bohemia, Austria, monasteries, and guild towns; Virelia carries the Polish-Lithuanian elective and magnate system over a Slavic eastern substrate; and the Sylvan Marches carry the Baltic and Livonian woodland-frontier function. These are dynamic registers, not one-to-one identities.

The Grey Sweat carries the demographic role of the Black Death. Its divergence is soul logistics: rite-integrity quarantine, the Emptying of the Benches and the bond of the clean village (see [[The Grey Sweat - Authorial Truth]]).

**Triangle (Eclessia, present):** GOOD — reformers and old-Church saints who each sincerely serve the Redeemer; BAD — princes on both confessions using doctrine as a land-grab; UGLY — the reunification party, for whom the Thalmyric schism *and* the Reformation are both merely obstacles to a restored imperial church, by force if needed.

The Church's reunification mandate is now recursive comedy and tragedy at once: it must reunify with schismatic Thalmyria, reabsorb its own reformed duchies, and police heresy on the frontier — simultaneously, with less money than the Medori and less fire than Veridun. Grenzburg's Bishop/Inquisitor/Reliquarian triangle plugs into this directly. The duchy is officially Sanctum-loyal; its villages combine old-Church observance, Folk practice, and a growing Freeflame presence among migrants.

### The Present → the 1550s–1580s Cold War

The board resembles the 1550s–1580s on the surface; under the Forward Rule each trajectory has a native disruptor listed in [[The Age of Held Proofs - Authorial Control]]. The board is Habsburg–Ottoman–Valois plus confessional stalemate: Thalmyria and the Ahl-e-Ahd in armed frontier peace punctuated by proxy raids (the Hungary/Mediterranean frontier); Eclessia internally tripartite and diplomatically schizophrenic; Sestara financing and informing on everyone (Venice's Ottoman trade despite holy leagues); the New World race through Castara beginning to reroute the world's wealth away from the old frontier entirely — a slow pressure on every power that stays landlocked in old grudges, and excellent long-game material. Under the Forward Rule its outcome is not the Atlantic precedent: whatever the Boiling Sea routes do to the old frontier must turn on a world-native engine such as the Held Breath, patron arbitrage or a Held Proof.

## Standing Recontextualization Rules

1. **Steal the dynamic, not the costume.** Every borrowing must be re-derived from the World Egg's own metaphysics (indulgences → routing assurances; Greek fire → controlled Shell-fire with disputed sacred counterclaims; devshirme → contract-bound levies). If the analog works only because the reader knows the real history, it isn't done yet.
2. **The flip protects you.** Mirrored compass, inverted 1453, Orthodox-west/Catholic-east: keep systematically breaking one-to-one traceability while preserving motivations.
3. **Every era gets a triangle.** Good/Bad/Ugly per the Grenzburg model — and the Ugly party should always be the one whose cooperation is necessary and whose price compounds.
4. **Both sides get a real case.** Reformation, schism, Unsealing, reconquest: each faction's position must be the one its best people would sincerely hold. Villainy lives in method and appetite, not in having the wrong theology.
5. **Filtered analogs everywhere** (per the naming decision): one step sideways on all names; no von Bismarcks, no verbatim liturgical Arabic or Zulu after the rename pass.
6. **Quarry, not script.** Research supplies pressures, institutions and texture, not sequence. A borrowed incident must do work beyond its source having it; incidents that survive only because the analog contains them are cut. Cite sources for texture, never one per beat. If a reader who knows the analog can predict the next beat, bend it with a world-native cause (the Consequence Rule in [[The Divergence Pass — Torque Register]]) or cut it.
7. **Clocks never align.** AR and BR dates for analog events must not coincide with their real AD or BC years, and an analog sequence must not keep its source's spacing between events.
