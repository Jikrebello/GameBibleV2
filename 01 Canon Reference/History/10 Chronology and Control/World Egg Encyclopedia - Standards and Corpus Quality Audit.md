# World Egg Encyclopedia — Standards and Corpus Quality Audit

This audit assesses the canonical encyclopedia as a reader-facing reference work. Canon status answers whether a statement controls the setting. The scores below answer a different question: how well can a reader understand, trust and navigate the article in its present form?

The audit was completed on 5 September 2026 after the Tidewood, culture-entrance and Atherian formation packages. It provides the baseline for the remaining [[World Encyclopedia - Revision and Expansion Programme|revision and expansion programme]].

## Research basis

Britannica's published account of its editorial process emphasises specialist authorship, verification against reputable sources, copy-editing, information architecture, continuous review and transparent identification of sources and revisions. Its public description does not provide an internal sentence-level stylebook. The World Egg therefore uses those practices as an editorial workflow and uses the observed explanatory manner of reference articles as a prose model. [Britannica Education, “How We Work”](https://britannicaeducation.com/about-us/), consulted 5 September 2026.

Wikipedia contributes openly documented standards for a much larger linked encyclopedia. Its useful practices include self-contained leads, proportional emphasis, summary-style parent and child articles, restrained linking, subject-specific layouts and a distinction between canonical publication and editorial maturity. Its neutrality, verifiability and original-research policies require adaptation for an invented world. [Wikipedia, “Core content policies”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Core_content_policies); [“Manual of Style/Lead section”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Lead_section); [“Summary style”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Summary_style); [“Writing better articles”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Writing_better_articles), consulted 5 September 2026.

The resulting World Egg rules are recorded in the [[World Egg Encyclopedia - Editorial Style Guide]].

## Scope and method

The canonical library contained **816 Markdown articles** at the baseline. Of these, **544** were available to readers at public, restricted or secret visibility and **272** were author-only controls. The reader-facing body comprised 443 public, 96 restricted and five secret articles.

The automated survey measured article length, introductory prose, headings, links, list use, corrective-contrast patterns and reader-facing design language. Close reading then sampled every major article family and the recently completed packages. Automated signals identified work for review; they did not determine prose quality by themselves.

The reader-facing body averaged approximately **568 words per article**. It contained no article below 100 words. Its principal structural problem appeared before the first section:

- 306 of 544 reader-facing articles had fewer than forty words of introductory material.
- 57 began directly with a section heading and contained no unheaded introduction.
- 77 opened with a quotation or authority notice, principally the deliberately structured Garden tales and received texts.
- 67 reader-facing files contained a section explicitly labelled as story or campaign use.

The new work performs substantially better. The eleven culture entrances averaged about 994 words, with self-contained leads averaging 107 words. The Age of Glory shelf averaged about 860 words with one short portal lead. The Tidewood package averaged about 720 words; its Court of Salt and Teeth article alone lacked an unheaded introduction.

## Rating rubric

The audit uses the five scores defined in the editorial guide: unusable, outline, serviceable, strong and exemplary. Scores reflect the present article or family, not the value of the underlying setting material.

## Whole-library rating

**Overall reader-facing maturity: 3.5 out of 5.**

The library is already coherent, extensive and unusually well controlled for identity, chronology and metaphysics. A reader can move through a real setting rather than a collection of disconnected prompts. Its average is held below 4 by uneven older shelves: many pages still begin like design notes, use lists where prose should explain relationships, or expose campaign prompts and authorial classifications inside the reference account.

## Topic-specific standards

Uniform tone does not require uniform shape. The article-family rules in the editorial guide now distinguish cultures, polities, historical periods, events, biographies, institutions, supernatural powers, creatures, systems, objects, tales and indexes.

The most important changes are these:

- A biography gives dates, identity and significance before powers or reputation.
- A war explains aims, supply, participants, civilians and consequences before legendary memory.
- A supernatural article first describes the being, place or cult in comprehensible terms, then supplies technical identity controls privately.
- A creature article treats ecology and behaviour as its primary account; encounter tactics become secondary or move to game use.
- A culture article distinguishes people, state, ancestry and religion and represents internal variation.
- A major overview summarises each child article in prose. It does not become a directory.

## Initial correction pass

The first response to this audit makes four bounded corrections:

1. Expand the editorial guide from a regional prose standard into a mature-library standard with leads, summary architecture, article-worthiness, specialised forms, provenance and a multi-pass workflow.
2. Repair the only missing or inadequate leads in the approved Tidewood and Age of Glory model packages.
3. Add self-contained introductions to the nine culture-history entrances that began directly with a section heading.
4. Repair the principal geography, Redeemer and supernatural entry points used to test the new subject-specific rules, and move cartographic design instructions from the public Geography Atlas into an authorial control.

These changes reduce reader-facing pages with no introduction from 57 to 37 and pages with fewer than forty introductory words from 306 to 285. They address high-value navigation points without pretending to complete the remaining reviews through automatic prose generation. Their supporting shelves retain definite ownership under Packages 3–9.

## Corpus remediation in progress

The first value-ordered corpus pass subsequently supplied a stand-alone introduction to every remaining reader-facing article that lacked one. The count of such articles therefore fell from 57 at baseline to zero; 253 articles still have introductions shorter than forty words and require qualitative review within their subject packages. A short introduction may be sufficient for a narrowly bounded article, so length alone does not determine failure.

The same pass removed 148 explicitly labelled campaign, quest, encounter, story-use, game-use and design-rule sections from reader-facing articles. Their wording is preserved in [[Reader-Facing Campaign Material - Authorial Control]], with a link to each source article. No proposed story became history through relocation. Reader-facing articles now contain no section under the audited campaign-use and hook headings.

## Required package gates

Every remaining delivery now includes four editorial gates in addition to its historical work:

- **Lead gate:** every reader-facing page touched by the package receives a stand-alone lead.
- **Control-separation gate:** campaign prompts, encounter instructions and authorial classifications move out of reader prose.
- **Summary gate:** parent and child accounts agree, and the parent retains a substantive summary.
- **Article-worthiness gate:** isolated outlines are expanded, merged into their parent or moved to an appropriate game-use or authorial shelf.

Package 3 owns the Redeemer and later Atherian event portals. Packages 4–7 own the supporting cultural and regional shelves. Package 8 owns cosmology and supernatural histories. Package 9 owns the final creature, magic, organisation and cross-library review. No item returns to an indefinite editorial deferral.

## Reassessment

The library receives another measured audit after Package 4 and a final audit during Package 9. The next assessment should report both the whole-library score and the number of articles at each maturity level. A rising average cannot conceal a remaining score-1 or score-2 reader page.
