# Imperial Founding — Authorial History and Integration Review

This review records the authorised imperial-founding canon pass of 4 September 2026. The user approved the recommended **Alternative A**, with Thalion as the Augustus-like founder, and **Route M**, an incoming maritime dynasty joined to a spared-town family. Fourteen new canonical articles integrate the founding generation and its antecedents. The wider Age of Glory remains the next part of Package 2C.

The historical additions are authorial facts, with partial knowledge and differing memories identified in the reader articles. They are not promotions of every possibility in the comparative research. The Caesar-like Thalion alternative and locally originating royal-house alternative remain unselected research branches.

## Scope and source precedence

Controlling sources are the established chronology, the two fissions, Caleran foundations, the Common Sky settlement, the integrated palace and Gateway histories, and the geography of the Aurin, Folk frontier, northern wells and Qabir–Zanthir gateway. The preceding working files, including uncommitted Package 2B and research additions, were captured before this pass.

The research inheritance is:

- [[The Roman Republic's Last Generations - Conquest, Civil War and Imperial Settlement]] — the researched sequence, source criticism and campaign relationships.
- [[Caesar's World - People, Households and Rivalries]] — separate personalities, household roles and competing testimony.
- [[Atheria's Imperial Founding - Alternative Histories and Canon Review]] — the selected architecture and rejected alternatives, preserved as research history.
- [[Atheria before the Imperial Founding - Palace Remnants, Maritime Rivals and the Queen's Inheritance]] — the preceding comparison, superseded where the canonical palace and gateway histories already select an outcome.
- [[Atherian Palace and Maritime Histories - Integration Review]] — the prior integration, including fictional child sacrifice, destruction, limited salting and the survival of lesser towns.

Ancient testimony and modern research remain cited in those dossiers. The fictional names, exact events and dates below are newly established World Egg history. They are not claimed as discoveries about Rome.

## Actor identities and supernatural limits

All named mortal figures in this package possess complete **human souls**. Citizenship, dynastic descent, public honours, adoption and political deification neither change that continuity nor confer sainthood. No final judgement, saintly manifestation, damned office or returning ancestor is established for this cast.

The two materially effective supernatural episodes are deliberately limited:

1. **Darneth's load test:** a genuine Astaroth correspondence exposes how force will pass through particular siege works, at the price of a destroyed valuable frame. Its result is structural knowledge, not a moral pronouncement or commission. Astaroth remains the established Menhir. The test precedes the engineering effort and surrender; Casvilar chooses the subsequent mutilation himself.
2. **Sarqen's withdrawal:** pilots deal with a local manifestation within Thalassor's sea ecology. Surrendered cargo and the flooding of one disused galley basin enable a particular current for the chosen vessels. The manifestation is not a new sovereignty or a deceased sailor. Officers allocate places to treasure and abandon wounded people; the queen subsequently suppresses that part of the story. No bargain secures the whole campaign or binds the sea permanently.

Idol exploitation remains part of the established imperial religious environment. This pass does not select the research possibility of a new post-assassination Pride apparition. It would duplicate a device already present in High Harbour's returning-warriors cycle. No Mask is identified with a dead founder, no new Archmask or Regnant is created, and no political apotheosis settles an actual afterlife.

The Boatman remains separate from the Thanatos cult. The Common Sky and Astaroth priesthood remain human institutions addressing powers they interpret imperfectly. Varroth's priestly office grants no ownership of a Menhir. No pre-Redeemer saint or White-Flame relic is introduced.

## Authority and surviving memory

The wars, decisions, family connections and named deaths above are fixed history. Particular public justifications remain attributed: Casvilar's claim of heavenly approval, the use of sacrifice accusations against Samyra, and royal omission of the abandoned wounded are beliefs or distortions, not authorial endorsements.

Campaign remains include siege banks, the altered water approach at Darneth, damaged harbour works and a flooded galley basin. Regional traditions preserve lost holdings, ransoms, injuries and household names. Imperial and royal accounts supply another layer of memory. Their survival does not make the complete authorial account available in one source to investigators in 1360.

Samyra's means of suicide, many minor participants and precise modern locations remain uncertain. These are ordinary gaps rather than new protected Weirds. Later lives of survivors remain part of the scheduled imperial history.
