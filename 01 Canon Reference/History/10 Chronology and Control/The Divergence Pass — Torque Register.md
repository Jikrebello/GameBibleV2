# The Divergence Pass — Torque Register

The anti-derivative instrument. Diagnosis: material generated by the world's own physics never reads as borrowed; material imported from history reads borrowed wherever Earth's causal engine came with it. The cure is **torque** — for every imported beat, one consequence only this cosmology could produce, canonized here and made the famous part of the event.

## The Two Standing Rules

**1. The Forward Rule.** Real-history analogs may govern the *past* (familiarity is an entry ramp). From 1360 AR forward, **no event may be predictable from its analog** — the present and future run on world-native engines only (see [[The Age of Held Proofs]]). If a reader who knows the textbook can guess the next beat, the beat is wrong.

**2. The Torque Rule.** Every analog beat carries at least one canonized consequence below. When writing about any of these events, *lead with the torque* — the analog is background, the torque is the story.

## The Register

**Cassian's Vision (292 AR — Constantine).** TORQUE: the site is verifiable. The Aurin at the Stone Bridge of Velos **has never frozen since**, in eleven centuries of hard winters — a standing micro-theophany that passes every relic-court test and belongs to the *crown*, not the Church: history's first fight over whether a miracle can be owned (it continues). And his deathbed baptism is a named scandal — **the Cassian Wager**: one unrepented reign answered with one perfect absolution, cited by every deathbed-delayer since, condemned by every confessor, and unresolved because in this world the Weighing is real and *nobody knows if it worked.*

**Astrion's Restoration (348–351 — Julian).** TORQUE: he didn't merely reopen temples — he staged a public *verification*, demanding the stars answer as the Flame answers. **The stars answered. Once.** What the augurs recorded that night is sealed in the Sanctum's deepest registry, because (per the masks canon — the Star Pantheon IS the Menhir) the Church's problem with Astrion was never that the old religion was false. It's that it wasn't.

**The First Unsealing (568–612 — the Arab conquests).** TORQUE: the conquest was substantially a *legal event*. The armies marched with **portable breach-courts**, and half the northern provinces were never stormed — they were **served**: municipal charters examined, defects found, sovereignty ruled void, terms offered. "The Unsealing was served, not fought" is the Covenant's proudest sentence and the conquered's bitterest, and it is why the Velian States' descendants still keep their pre-conquest charters in fireproof chests — waiting, some say, for a counter-ruling.

**The Fall of Caleran (905) and the Impounded Ember.** TORQUE: the occupiers found the Eternal Ember would not go out — so Idris al-Fattah, contract-minded to the bone, declared it **impounded property in dispute** and posted a guard on it for fifty-two years. The Reconquest found the mother-flame burning, immaculately guarded, with a Covenant ledger recording every day's fuel — **the most famous invoice in history, which Veridun paid in full**, because the alternative was conceding that Covenant law had no force, and the Patriarchate would sooner pay its enemy than imply the Flame had needed him. Enemies who preserved the holy fire under property law: no Earth history contains this event, and it should be told constantly.

**The Choosing at Strazgorod (1061 — the Rus).** TORQUE: the envoys' weeping in the Ember-Cathedral was a **kindling event** — the lamp they carried took the Flame unbidden, before witnesses of both churches. That lamp, **the Virelian Lamp, still burns in Veridun's cathedral**, tended for three centuries as an honored hostage: proof, in Veridun's telling, of whom the east truly chose. At every Virelian succession election, the Patriarchate formally offers to *return the lamp* — the standing seduction behind every "eastern candidate," and the reason the Sanctum's agents attend Virelian elections with unclerical attention.

**The Grey Sweat (1232–1247 — the Black Death).** TORQUE: on Earth plague is biology; here mass death is a **soul-logistics catastrophe**. The Sweat is remembered as *the years the ferry queued*: the Vestibule's intake overwhelmed, burial rites collapsing faster than bodies could be blessed, and the **Undertow's harvest of the century** — their oldest standing routes date from it, bought in bulk from the unburied. Church quarantine doctrine was therefore written about **rite-integrity, not contagion** (a sealed village with a living priest was "clean"; a healthy village with a dead one was not), and the Ash-Walkers were not merely hysterics — where they walked, the records show, the thin places closed. Nobody has ever wanted to study why.

**The First Catch (905–920 — the plains horse-revolution).** TORQUE: the scattered imperial studs were **hymn-blessed remount lines** — war-sanctified stock — and Grey Sister's line carries the blessing still, diluting by generation. Tarkhan horse-genealogy is not sentiment; it is the actuarial tracking of a **fading grace**, which is why the sacred lines are priced above provinces and why a Grey Sister foal given in treaty is the steppe's version of a relic translation.

**The Ashenmen and the Preacher's Water.** TORQUE: Ansgar's ashes went into the Weiss — and Ironvale's reach of the river is now **the Preacher's Water**: oaths sworn on it hold strangely well, the relic courts' one water-test came back *inconclusive* (the file is sealed; inconclusive frightened them more than positive would have), and the Freeflame, whose theology forbids relics, are stuck with a **river** — which cannot be enshrined, sold, or stolen, and which their enemies note is therefore the most Freeflame relic conceivable.

**The Great Split, the Reformation, the crusade cycles.** Already majority-native (Kindling Question, Caedros's conditional hymns, routing assurances, the Hundred Grievances) — the torque holding: keep leading with the doctrine-mechanics, not the 1054/1517 furniture. The Church's *numbering* of crusades stays: bureaucratic numbering is in-world habit, not Earth residue.

**Resolved in later culture passes:** copied governing bodies and signature social vocabulary were replaced with setting-native institutions. Broad translation titles such as Doge remain deliberate conventions where controlled by the relevant culture hub.

## Ripple Notes

The Impounded Ember touches the Patriarchate, Thalmyric daily life, and the Reconquest accounts; the Virelian Lamp touches Virelia's lives file and Courts & Crowns; the Sweat torque touches the Undertow, the Boatman, and Eclessian funeral doctrine; the Stone Bridge touches Velos and relic-court canon. Apply on next edit of each — the register is the source of truth meanwhile.
