## Authorial truth behind the siege cycle

The rescue, coalition dispute, siege, sack and three returning histories described in the public article are established events. The leading commander orders the sanctuary woman's rape and confinement. The challenging captain also holds enslaved people and withdraws partly over his own following and share of stores. The shipmistress's husband dies; she and the escaped woman continue organising rescues. Neither woman is reduced to an occasion for a warrior's anger.

The fighting and local manifestations occur. The pilot enables the fighter to break the bronze and end Astaroth's appearance at the breakwater. The songs' blood from divine armour is not promoted as a wound to the sovereignty. The subsequent murdered prisoners have no part in the companion's death. The bereaved fighter chooses revenge despite the deception used to encourage it.

The father's retrieval secures one body and a pause for burial. The sack follows feigned withdrawal, a tide change and inside assistance given in expectation of terms. Commanders break those terms; the High Giant dies bodily; the palace image is broken during escape. There is no wooden horse, Titan severance or triumph over an Idol Throne.

The refuge-violating officer and the captain pursued by the tide are one human. He is distinct from the coalition commander, the challenging captain, the bereaved fighter and the changing hostess's guest. The shipmistress is another independent participant. These identities are fixed without giving them complete names, genealogies or a future saintly or infernal status.

The pursued captain's captive escapes after the first wreck and joins rescuers. His later hanging of enslaved women includes victims who acted under coercion; restoration of his house begins a continuing feud. The changing hostess's crew retains changed bodies, unequal releases and a companion who stays. The shipmistress settles among an already inhabited shore community and contributes to its first shared vessel. Burning the old ships versus consensually dismantling them remains an unresolved received variant.

## The gateway cult and final war

The ancestral provider is **one dependent Gluttony Mask**. Its bronze face, serving chambers and institutional supply of victims support a local manifestation. It encourages another offering whenever protection fails. Children are killed and bodily offerings consumed. Their complete human souls are neither eaten into the Mask nor reproduced in the image. The rite's ordinary fires create no infernal fire realm; Hell's existing elemental character is unchanged.

The capital's priests, supplying officials and protective civic faction are culpable actors. Some elites offer their own children; others purchase children or use captives. Internal opponents, hiding families, escaped temple workers and defecting towns materially contribute to intervention. Ending child sacrifice is an established **major cause** of the final Atherian war, alongside strategic, commercial and territorial motives. It is not merely a retrospective pretext.

The war policy singles out the capital. Lesser towns accepting the settlement retain their communities. An initial surrender is followed by expanded demands for abandonment, leading to renewed resistance among inhabitants with different positions on the cult. The final siege actually releases children and destroys the sacrificial institution. Atherian murder, rape, enslavement and command responsibility also remain fixed.

The capital is physically burned, razed and heavily salted in selected precincts and residential ground. Salt arrives from coastal stores; cisterns are spoiled and broken. This damage is local and material. It does not permanently sterilise the whole country, explain a desert, alter an elemental realm or become a reliable anti-Idol weapon. The loss of sacrifice, supply and sanctuary ends this established local means of manifestation; Gluttony's Throne survives.

The surviving towns' wells, farms, workshops, ports and households sustain rebuilding. Some assist the intervention and remember liberation; others suffer heavily while escaping planned erasure. The population is never collectively equated with the cult. Descent does not transmit culpability or give the queen a supernatural identity.

## The royal country and outstanding choices

The shared basis of the two dynasty routes is promoted: the country survives through lesser towns; Second Harbour is one of them, enlarged into a royal centre; recovery and hereditary authority develop over c. 520–400 BR; the later queen inherits this country. Her claim rests on the surviving communities and developed kingdom, not on an instantly rebuilt capital or an unchanged republic.

**Route L remains open:** a locally rooted spared-town family gains authority through supply, repairs, marriages and Atherian recognition. **Route M remains open:** an incoming maritime command marries into a powerful spared-town family and develops a hereditary crown. Route M's recommendation is not an authorial choice. Neither founder, marriage, accession incident nor genealogy is fixed through the common account.

Both Thalion architectures remain research. The existing canonical seizure and unification around 350 BR is unchanged. No predecessor's assassination, testamentary heir, territorial triumvirate, queen's romance or Actium-equivalent battle enters canon here. Selection of the imperial architecture must precede those biographies.

- [[90 Archive & Drafts/10 Research/Mythic and Sacred History/Palaces, Sea Migrations and the Long Fall - Mycenaean Research and World Egg Comparison.md|Palaces, Sea Migrations and the Long Fall]] distinguishes archaeological palace history from later heroic literature. The *Iliad* supplies coalition quarrel, coerced captivity, divine intervention and burial; the sack and some return traditions draw on later epic summaries; selected *Odyssey* episodes provide predation, coercive hospitality and a violent homecoming. Capital-only destruction, salting and sacrifice as a major war cause are deliberate World Egg selections.

Source severity is retained through bodily coercion, rape, child sacrifice, torture, cannibal predation, execution, enslavement and irreversible deaths. Three shorter palace encounters remain unpromoted research: the abandoned landing, returning hostage and two brothers' winter. They receive no event in the dated chronology. Their next assessment belongs to the individual heroic-cast development after this integration, where they must supply a distinct local life rather than obligatory preliminaries to High Harbour. - No Wolfsmere event, Tarkhan First Catch, modern Sestaran government, Banu Mizan arrangement or distant first contact is moved into antiquity. Low Door Companions and all imperial-founding candidates remain independent.
- Glass, Bird-Marked Sites, Anshara's final sovereign, divine-generation mysteries, protected successions and all post-1360 outcomes remain unresolved.
