# Package B — Qiryath and Eastern Empires Canon Integration Review

## Promotion ruling

Package B was approved for canon promotion on 11 September 2026. The Jirahar road-house history, First Qiryath, Arqesh, Vashar, Ortheia, Eryndor III, the Wars of the Inheritance and the Eastern Sea Wars now form part of the controlled World Egg chronology. The research dossiers remain archived source history and comparative documentation.

Ordinary ancient names, relationships, campaigns and consequences are fixed at the degree stated in the canonical articles. Exact borders, travel distances, several ancient personal names, the cause of Eryndor's death and the literal interpretation of his romances remain open.

## Fixed historical sequence

Mareh bat Sivan's three camps belong around 1800 BR. The mature Bronze Roads extend from approximately 1750 to 1325, with regional failures between 1450 and 1325. Local protector cycles, the growth of tower settlements and the development of First Qiryath follow across several centuries.

House Ubar takes Arqesh around 958 BR. Kassur I develops the provincial and deportation system. Merod II's existing expansion towards Qahur intensifies after Prince Ashkur is murdered in 814. Neshkar II demands hostages and the royal image from Qiryath in 746 and destroys the city in 742.

Valeron II consolidates Ortheia and imposes a changing hegemony upon several Compact cities between about 735 and 706. Eryndor III succeeds after Valeron's assassination, destroys Olyssar and crosses the eastern sea in 702. He captures Terys after a prolonged siege, enters divided Qahur in 695, allies with Arezan Tiravan in 694 and helps take Arqesh in 690. Kassur III is murdered in 688 and buried by Eryndor.

Eryndor dies at Arqesh in 684. Arezan issues the Decree of Roads and Houses. The Wars of the Inheritance divide the conquests among Arezan, Demeran Kavos, Serevan of Delkos, Neresh of Qemaret and farther eastern commanders. Second Qiryath is consecrated around 660 under Vasharan supremacy.

Serevan's Delkosi kingdom leaves a mixed naval and royal inheritance. House Nerethan descends from a later Delkosi officer family rather than from Eryndor or Serevan. The Eastern Sea Wars of 514–491 end Vashar's attempt to establish permanent rule over the Atherian leagues. Vashar remains an independent imperial peer in the settlement of 350 BR.

## Qahur and the Successor inheritance

Qahur remains an independent Orphaned river civilisation. Merod II's grievance follows an intervention already under way and does not make Ashkur's death the cause of all Arqeshite expansion. Eryndor receives a limited Qahuri title and sign from one crown; other crowns resist or wait. Neresh controls selected crown towns for a generation. Native temple, canal and royal houses absorb or expel the Successor garrisons.

The Single Radiance, Crowned Countenance and Witness of Dawn are separate dependent Pride Masks within established Idol ecology. No true Idol succession occurs. Eryndor is the human son of Valeron II and a mortal queen. His claim of celestial or Qahuri paternity belongs to royal interpretation and later romance.

Samyra's four inheritances remain distinct: the spared Gateway towns, Hadrima's Esharati household, junior Qahuri maternal kin and Delkosi Successor culture. They give her no universal claim to Qahur and do not restore Qarzeth.

## The Far-Road romances

The historical campaign includes an undersea observation bell at Terys, a Sky-Lion reconnaissance frame, a speaking Root-Mind grove, a gate closing one local threat and a failed search for restorative Egg White water. Their witnesses and purposes differ. Later traditions combine and transform them.

The existence of the Ortheian, Qahuri, Arqeshite, Vashari, Jirahar and Atherian romance families is canon. No complete literary version serves as a literal chronicle. The king never ascends into Heaven, acquires prophetic status, imprisons every monster or escapes judgement.

## Identity and supernatural controls

- Eryndor, Arezan, Parena, the Ubar kings and every other mortal actor possess complete human souls.
- Human souls remain distinct from bodies, royal offices, portraits, legendary personae, copied images and Idol Masks.
- The Crowned Countenance's interest in Eryndor is dependent Mask behaviour, not a transfer of guilty sovereignty.
- Astaroth's campaign correspondences and Father Sun's limited sign provide information or passage only. They grant no moral approval or divine title.
- Sky-Lions are living creatures; Root-Minds, court persons, Menhir and angels remain distinct.
- The restorative water delays or repairs embodied decline under local conditions. It cannot cancel judgement or manufacture sainthood.
- No pre-Redeemer conqueror is a saint.

## Historical collisions resolved

Eryndor remains separate from Teren Casvilar and Aurelius Thalion. Arezan remains an equal allied ruler and founder of Vashar. The Gateway Wars begin after the Successor settlement and retain their own causes. The Eastern Sea Wars are a later conflict between Vashar and Atherian leagues. Olyssar is unrelated to the archive catastrophe at Orthea. The Bent-Key Fellowship, Crownless Company, Oar-King and Road Without Milestones remain independent traditions.
