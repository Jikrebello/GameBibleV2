# Authorial Notes from Reader Articles — Peoples Histories

Safeguards, verdicts and design notes moved out of the public peoples' history articles during the November 2026 editorial cleanup; their force as canon is unchanged.

## [[Historical Atlas of Thalmyria]]

- "They [Thalmyrics] are not simply the eastern half of an ancient pure people."

## [[The Long Reconquest (908-1052 AR)]]

- The recovered Wind Stair, Frost Gate and Basalt Draw gave Thalmyria signal, cold-pressure, forge and fortification capacity "without making ordinary armies high-magic."
- Vorthara's 907 intervention created an opportunity for the resistance "without making the city part of the Thalmyric cause."

## [[The Battle of the Shattered Engines (907 AR)]]

- "The victory was neither clean nor holy." (Reader text keeps the defenders killed by the misfiring counter-script and the factions' tests of later-prohibited devices.)
- Shattered Engines enabled the Mountain Rebellion and Long Reconquest "without making Vorthara a Thalmyric ally or subject." [[Sestara and the Wider World]] likewise said Vorthara's role in 907 binds it to Caldrian history "without making it Eclessian."
- Narrator's ruling on the three memories (Caldrian crusading victory, Vortharan paid intervention, Thalmyric useful prelude): "All three accounts contain part of the truth." Reader text now leaves the disagreement open.

## [[Historical Spine of the Covenant]], [[The Covenant from Idris to Suhrab (905-1190 AR)]] and the Reconquest articles

- Added in the Causality Pass (November 2026). The author-only rulings behind the Widowed Seals, the Threshold, lodging, the Long Reach, the Ember deposit, the Pledged Custodian and the two houses of the Vault are held in [[The Covenant after Caleran - Authorial Truth]]. Reader prose gives them only as in-world testimony.
- Safeguard carried there rather than into reader text: custody of a living name never touches soul-routing; only a foundry name-cage closed at death does.

## [[Eclessia During the Invasion]]

- Aratheos and Fareth survive as local leagues "rather than lost kingdoms"; they "explain later regional institutions without replacing the larger history."

## [[Historical Atlas of Eclessia]]

- Partition, the Great Split and the 1104 trust left a world of kingdoms and duchies "rather than a temporary failure to become one state."

## [[The Rise of the Eclessian Duchies and Kingdoms]]

- Corrected founders: Seraphyne was "not the founder of a post-Fall kingdom"; Juran the Stalwart was "not the first king of a fully Eclessian Virelia." "Their corrected roles preserve the regional traditions without making every modern realm spring fully formed from 905 AR." Reader text now gives the inflated offices as the older chronicles' claims.
- "The Vesari princedoms were not a late Eclessian colony."

## [[Historical Spine of the Sestaran Concord]]

- Origins: "no single ancient Sestaran people or proto-Atherian colony precedes them all"; harbour populations are "not an ancient Atherian bloodline"; the harbour founded twice "is not the literal origin of every city."
- Design note: "Mining, shipbuilding and household labour belong in this history as fully as credit."

## [[Historical Atlas of the Velians]]

- Velian identity "is neither an unaltered imperial remnant nor merely a confession applied to people who otherwise remain Thalmyric."
- "Saints, sinners, medicine and translated warnings now have local histories here without making every frontier figure Velian or one translation school the source of all learning."

## [[Historical Atlas of the Nsizwa]]

- Design rationale for border contact: "These relationships prevented the kingdom from becoming an isolated military culture." (The soul ruling, the residue/office/soul possibilities and the Last Houses separation were removed as already held in [[Izivukwa Continuity]] and the World Historical Atlas integration ledger.)

## [[Historical Spine of the Tarkhan]]

- "Tarkhan societies are active Orphaned histories, not a pressure invented to divide Ark descendants."

## [[Historical Spine of the Folk]]

- Folk customs: "autonomy did not make every custom just."
- Menhir courts' gifts and exact prices shaped local customs "without supplying a moral law."
- Enaidfae "remained an old-rite identity rather than a separate human biology."

## [[Historical Spine of the Ättar]]

- Mother Moon's bargain "did not create the human soul or prove every later hall's violence righteous." (Soul ruling also held in Package D.)
- Later raiding, slavery, fosterage, marriage and baptism changed populations "without reducing culture to appearance or blood purity."
- Local Menhir traditions are "not one universal fairy government."
- Placement note: "The winter stories are placed southward where the established geography requires cold, even when an older nursery accidentally called them northern."

## [[Ättar and the Wider World]]

- "Slaving connects the societies [Ättar and the Covenant markets] through shared wrongdoing, not cultural friendship."
