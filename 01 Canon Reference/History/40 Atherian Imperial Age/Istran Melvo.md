# Istran Melvo

Istran Melvo, born in 391 BR, was the naval organiser and commander whose victories secured the sea routes for Aurelius Thalion. His career connected shipyard knowledge with the provisioning, training and political work needed to keep fleets operating over several campaigning seasons.

## From the working shore

Melvo grew up among shipyard households at Pyrassa. Repair work required familiarity with timber, fastenings, weather and the different uses to which a vessel might be put. A sound hull could still fail through careless loading or an exhausted crew.

He entered Thalion's service during the struggles following Casvilar's assassination. The two men were close in age and brought different experience to their association. Melvo could explain why an attractive military proposal would fail at the anchorage, then identify the stores and time needed to make it possible.

He enjoyed discussing a difficult repair with experienced workers and had little patience for an officer who concealed a shortage. Those habits helped him learn from people below his military rank. His demands could also be severe when he believed delay endangered a campaign.

## Building a fleet

The maritime following of Ardel Veyr threatened supplies and offered refuge to the coalition's enemies. Thalion needed a force able to remain at sea long enough to contest those routes. Melvo began with repair, crew training and agreements for provisions at cooperating ports.

His work brought more regular employment to some shipyards and heavy burdens to others. Officials seized timber and compelled labour when contracts and volunteers could not meet the required pace. Families lost income or working animals while public resources went towards the fleet.

Training joined rowers, sailors and fighting troops who often came from different communities. Melvo sought dependable routines for signals, loading and the distribution of food. Skill acquired through repetition gave him an advantage which the acquisition of additional ships alone could not provide.

## The sea-heir's defeat

An agreement with Ardel in 357 BR briefly reduced conflict. Disputes about supplies, property and captured vessels renewed it. In 355 Melvo defeated the sea-heir's main force among the central Thalassian anchorages.

The victory opened routes and destroyed a refuge for displaced households. Some defeated crews were left beyond rescue as the victors concentrated on securing ships and prisoners. Captives subsequently faced pardon, sale or delivery to former enemies. Melvo's work made the campaign effective, including its coercive results.

Seluc Varroth's attempt to claim the surrendered forces failed when Thalion secured their allegiance. Melvo's victory therefore altered the balance within the coalition as well as removing a maritime opponent.

## The royal war

In the final struggle against Velsar and Samyra, Melvo worked to separate the royal coalition from dependable supplies. He negotiated with coastal rulers, threatened reluctant ports and established positions from which vessels could restrict Sarqen's approaches.

The fighting outside the outer anchorage in 352 BR broke part of the royal fleet. Some ships escaped and the war continued on land. Melvo's arrangements allowed Thalion to maintain pressure through the later defections and shortages.

At the settlement of 350, he belonged among the men whose service had made imperial government possible. His reputation rested on vessels that could sail, stores available when promised and crews able to act together. The communities supplying those achievements remembered both employment and compulsory service.
