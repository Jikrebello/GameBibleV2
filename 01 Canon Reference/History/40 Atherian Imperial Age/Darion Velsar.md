# Darion Velsar

Darion Velsar (408–350 BR) was a commander of Teren Casvilar's armies, a member of the three-command government and the principal eastern rival of Aurelius Thalion. His alliance with Samyra Nerethan joined military authority to the resources of the gateway kingdom. Their defeat ended the succession wars.

## Soldier and companion

Velsar's standing grew through service under Casvilar. Soldiers knew his physical confidence, readiness to share food and ability to speak to a tired company without ceremonial distance. He enjoyed music and performance at a gathering and could reward a subordinate with conspicuous generosity.

Those qualities helped hold troops together. They also encouraged dependence on his personal attention. A commander who pleased him might obtain support quickly, while anger or distraction could leave a difficult request unanswered. He was capable of sustained military work when an immediate campaign concentrated his interests.

His wife Miretha Ardel managed property, received officers' families and maintained connections during his absences. Her political activity drew on resources of her own. Their household became an important centre for people whose prospects depended on continued military employment.

## Inheritance and public power

After Casvilar's assassination in 370 BR, Velsar possessed seniority, papers and the loyalty of experienced officers. Thalion's designated inheritance challenged his ability to speak for the dead ruler. Fighting and negotiation eventually produced the public three-command government with Seluc Varroth in 367.

Velsar demanded the death of Belisar Venn, the speaker who had supported Thalion against him. The partners' agreement authorised that murder and many others. Confiscated estates helped pay the armies.

Velsar directed the decisive operations against Mavren Seneth and Corial Draven around Darsil. Victory in 363 increased his military reputation. He then assumed responsibility for eastern commands and royal clients, while Thalion managed much of the veteran settlement.

## Divided households

Miretha and Velsar's brother Tarsen supported resistance to those settlements around Darsil in 362–361. They objected to the expulsion of households which had supplied the armies and sought to preserve Velsar's influence. His encouragement arrived without the forces needed to relieve them. Defeat brought hunger, executions and exile. Miretha continued helping dependants until her death in 360.

Velsar married Evara Thalion in 359. She arranged supplies and negotiations and cared for members of his earlier household. Their children Meren and Sovia were born in 358 and 356. The marriage supported cooperation while the partners still found reasons to compromise.

His failed northern campaign in 358 consumed troops and resources. During the retreat and its aftermath, Velsar seized people and stores from a smaller allied ruler whom he blamed for the defeat. The resulting distrust weakened the network on which future campaigns depended.

## Samyra and the eastern settlement

Samyra's kingdom supplied grain, vessels and credit. Velsar offered military protection and recognition of dynastic claims. Their relationship developed personal attachments alongside those purposes, and their children Talven and Lethia were born in 357 and 354.

At Samyra's request, Velsar had her sister Merath seized and executed in 358. The removal of a rival strengthened the reigning household and alienated people attached to Merath's branch. Royal patronage could carry lethal consequences for relatives outside the favoured line.

The public recognition of titles for Samyra and her children in 354 gave their alliance a wider programme. Some claimed districts remained under other rulers. The ceremony nevertheless alarmed those rulers and supplied Thalion with evidence that Velsar intended a separate centre of authority.

Evara returned to her own household as the relationship broke down. Velsar's surviving children and dependants then belonged to households on opposing sides of an approaching war.

## Defeat

The final struggle began in 353 BR. Istran Melvo restricted the royal coalition's access to supplies and defeated part of its fleet outside Sarqen in 352. Velsar withdrew towards the queen's country as allies deserted.

In 350 he was wounded and faced capture. He took his own life before Sarqen's final submission. Samyra died soon afterwards. Their younger children survived in Evara's custody, sharing a household with children born during Velsar's earlier marriage.

Velsar's reputation remained divided between the commander remembered by comrades and the ruler whose commitments exceeded his capacity to sustain them. The households he left behind preserved both attachments and injuries.
