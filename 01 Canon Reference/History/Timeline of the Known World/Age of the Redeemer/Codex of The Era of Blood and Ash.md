##  **I. Canonized Martyrs of the First Persecution**

### **Saint Velmion of the Muzzled Word**

- **Date of Martyrdom**: ~455 AE
- **Cause**: Public trial and imprisonment beneath Temple of Typhonius
- **Legacy**: Founder of White Flame Doctrine; inspiration for Feast of the Silent Flame
- **Relics**: Muzzle-bit relic fragment held in the Cathedral of Saint Velmion, Caldria

---

### **Saint Calira of the Broken Blade**

- **Date of Martyrdom**: ~438 AE
- **Cause**: Killed during defense of Veridun Minor sanctuary
- **Legacy**: Patron of Redeemer paladins, Order of the Flame-Vowed
- **Relics**: Her fractured sword-pommel preserved in Ironvale's Sanctuary of Ash

---

### **Saint Jareth of the Bound Eye**

- **Date of Martyrdom**: ~440 AE
- **Cause**: Burned while transcribing gospel fragments
- **Legacy**: First chronicler of the Redeemer’s sayings; founder of Ember Lexicons
- **Relics**: Ash-stained writing tablet sealed in Ember Archive, Aurelia

---

### **Saint Solana of the Last Ember**

- **Date of Martyrdom**: ~460 AE
- **Cause**: Died peacefully after protecting a sacred ember
- **Legacy**: Her death marked the first recognition of “natural sainthood”
- **Relics**: Lantern of Solana still burns at Veridancian Cathedral—**never extinguished**

---

##  **II. Confirmed Early Miracle Sites**

### **The Temple That Would Not Burn**

- **Location**: Ruins of Cyrian, western provinces
- **Miracle**: Three failed attempts to raze Redeemer sanctuary; stone remained unscorched
- **Significance**: Symbol of faith’s resilience under celestial persecution
- **Present Site**: Pilgrimage cathedral constructed adjacent to original ruin

---

### **Ashway Bridge**

- **Location**: Near Halmenth
- **Miracle**: Bridge became covert Ember crossing; ritual of carrying lit coal preserved secrecy
- **Significance**: Symbol of flame carried in silence, even under shadow
- **Present Site**: Crossed during annual March of Embers by monks of the Lantern Order

---

### **Velmion’s Vault (The Fireless Trial Site)**

- **Location**: Beneath the former Temple of Typhonius, Caleran
- **Miracle**: Sunlight through overcast skies; posthumous reports of responses to prayer
- **Significance**: Epicenter of White Flame theology
- **Present Site**: Now sealed by the Thalmyric Church; guarded as sacred silence

---

### **The Lantern Cradle**

- **Location**: Hills of Veridancia
- **Miracle**: Lanterns burn without oil when martyrdoms are announced
- **Significance**: Site of early saint commemoration; considered living reliquary
- **Present Site**: Maintained by the Order of the Unfading Light

---

##  **III. Suppressed or Disputed Accounts**

> Entries in this section are preserved despite ecclesiastical controversy and ongoing theological debate.

### **Ashmouth**

- **Location**: Reportedly in southern Eclessia
- **Miracle/Curse**: City where a Redeemer martyr was burned; ash believed to grant visions
- **Disputes**: Accusations of heretical prophecy and unauthorized relic collection
- **Status**: Site barred to clergy since 622 AE under decree of the High Council

---

### **The Fifth Nail**

- **Location**: Unknown
- **Claim**: A fifth nail, used secretly during the Redeemer’s execution, exists and holds hidden power
- **Disputes**: Official doctrine affirms four nails; sectarian disputes suppressed
- **Status**: Considered apocryphal; references restricted in Eclessian seminaries
