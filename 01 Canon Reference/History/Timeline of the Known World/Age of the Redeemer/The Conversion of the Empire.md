## **The Conversion of the Empire (~430–540 AE)**

> _“They tried to silence the flame. But the more they struck, the more it spread.”_  
> —Excerpt from the _Scroll of the Ember Years_

> _Martyrs become saints. The stars burn with vengeance._

#### Overview:

After Isfir’s resurrection and the early spread of the faith, the Empire responded with systemic repression. The Redeemer’s followers were **outlawed**, hunted, and tortured. Temples were burned, sermons banned, and possession of the Gospel fragments was a capital crime.

- **“[[The Ember Underground]]”**: Follow a network of believers smuggling relics and hiding survivors across the Via Celestis.
- **“[[The Fireless Trial]]”**: A Redeemer-worshipping senator is put on public trial—unarmed, unflinching, refusing to speak.
- **“[[The Temple That Would Not Burn]]”**: A hidden Redeemer shrine inside a ruined Star Pantheon temple is set ablaze—three times. Each time, it survives untouched.

#### Overview:

Despite repression, the Redeemer’s faith **grows faster than it can be crushed**. The deaths of key figures (including the Twelve) only make the movement stronger. **Local Exarchs begin converting**, secretly or openly. Imperial soldiers report mutinies, conversions, and miracle sightings.

- **“[[Saintmaker’s Forge]]”**: A persecutor-turned-believer crafts the first canonized saint icons in secret—rumored to glow when in the presence of the faithful.
- **“[[The Forty Flames]]”**: A group of slaves in Ironvale who ignite their chains in fire, leading to a three-day rebellion that ends in massacre—and sainthood.
- **“[[The Apostle’s Bones]]”**: A relic-hunt to retrieve the bones of Saint Calira before they are publicly desecrated by the Imperial Cult of Typhonius.

> _The stars go dark. The empire fractures along lines of faith._

#### Overview:

The Redeemer’s message reaches a **critical mass**. The High Augur declares a sacred war against the “Flamebearers.”  But in the provinces, entire **legions and cities defect**. The **Great Eclipse of 503 AE**—which lasts a full day and night—**is taken by many as a divine sign** of the Star Pantheon’s defeat.

- **“The Burning of Virelos”**: A full Exarchate renounces the Star Gods and is declared heretical—sparking open civil war.
- **“The Heretic Legions”**: Soldiers wear the Chains of Binding into battle. Some say they call fire down from the sky.
- **“The Blood Moon Siege”**: A city of believers is surrounded during a lunar eclipse. They hold out without food for seven days—only to be “saved by flame.”

> _The empire kneels not to the stars, but to fire._

#### Overview:

With the faith dominant in all but name, the emperor at last **converts publicly** after a near-death experience. A palace fire breaks out in Caleran, and he is pulled from the blaze by a chained slave carrying a Redeemer talisman.

The **Star Pantheon is outlawed**, its temples repurposed, desecrated, or quietly abandoned. The **Imperial Creed** is rewritten under the guidance of Redeemer-scholars, and the **Holy Flame becomes the new axis of authority**.

- **“The Crown of Flame”**: The forging of the first holy regalia blessed in Redeemer fire. Who will wear it—Emperor or Priest?
- **“The Seven Temples’ Fall”**: A coordinated operation to shutter the seven major temples of the Star Children before a loyalist uprising can stop it.
- **“The Forgiver”**: An aging martyr returns to Caleran—not to take revenge, but to pardon his torturers on the eve of the empire’s baptism.
