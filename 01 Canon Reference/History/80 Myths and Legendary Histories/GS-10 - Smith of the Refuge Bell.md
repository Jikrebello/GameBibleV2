# Smith of the Refuge Bell

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Smith begins repairing cookware and cart fittings for refugees who cannot pay. Exceptional craft draws military patronage. During a siege the Smith builds a heat weapon intended to clear an empty approach. The first discharge reveals that civilians have been driven into the district. Given a chance to lock the weapon, the Smith instead authorises and personally triggers a second discharge, believing an enemy breakout will kill more refugees. Civilians burn with the fighters. Commanders conceal the second order, but the Smith cannot place the killing upon them alone.

The remaining life is spent dismantling copies, building bells and tools for evacuation, testifying against patrons and teaching that an artefact's rightful owner is the community bearing its danger. The Reader Behind the Sealed Door was once a friend and helped identify a threat, but the Reader's forbidden warning kills members of the Smith's refuge. The Smith refuses both revenge and the claim that knowledge alone excuses the breach.

## Later activity

A later Greater-Saint commission gathers several communities around one threatened route. Attributed tools activate only for their encoded duty—opening a hospice, disabling the heat weapon, sounding evacuation or proving workmanship—not when collectors assemble them. The Saint coordinates angels for one closure against the Reader's warning network and withdraws when refugees reach Material safety.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

The compassionate Smith personally authorises and fires a civilian-killing weapon, followed by military cover-up, retaliatory seizure of workshops and refugee deaths during later dismantling operations.

## The surviving inheritance

Distributed craft customs, dangerous devices, refuge bells and collectors wrongly expecting a nine-object return.

Known institutions organise duty-marked tools into incompatible devotional sets. No authoritative count exists, and assembling any claimed set produces no manifestation.

## What people remember

Flawless merciful smith, war criminal who repented, founder of forge-convents, still-living craftsman or soul divided among tools.

## What remains unknown

The private name, polity, tool count, genuine artefacts, death, commission date and present route.
