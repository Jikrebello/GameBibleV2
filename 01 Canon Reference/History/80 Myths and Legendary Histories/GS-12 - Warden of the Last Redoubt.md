# Warden of the Last Redoubt

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Warden begins as a refugee quartermaster who knows masonry because nobody else will inspect unsafe sleeping vaults. During a siege the quartermaster becomes commander and holds the inner redoubt by sealing an outer ward before every family crosses. The decision prevents immediate capture but leaves civilians and a relief company to be killed.

The Warden refuses celebratory promotion, records the excluded names and spends later years building evacuation depth into provincial forts. Grief hardens into punitive campaigning, and subordinates die in assaults whose real purpose is to prove the Warden did not choose cowardice at the gate. A final confession acknowledges both the original constrained decision and the later voluntary self-punishment imposed upon others. Authorially the body dies; the place, recovery of remains and public account remain genuinely uncertain within the nursery.

## Later activity

One authentic Greater-Saint manifestation is permitted as an authorial possibility and fixed for this research card: during a later Long Rent episode the Warden holds a Material redoubt while civilians evacuate, expressly opening the outer ward first. The commission defeats one link in the Ninth-Bastion Mason's project by denying it the required abandonment. It does not resume command of the Reconquest or prove any imperial claimant.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Sealed civilians, dead relief troops, later wasteful assaults and descendants made to inherit disputed blame.

## The surviving inheritance

Evacuation architecture, a provincial wall feast, contradictory body relics and rival military schools; not a sovereign order.

Several fortresses claim the Warden's hand, while the most credible relic is an ordinary measuring cord repaired by refugee children.

## What people remember

Flawless defender, coward, penitent murderer, secret survivor, Caedros's hidden lieutenant or fabricated provincial saint.

## What remains unknown

The private name, fort, death, remains, church recognition, exact commission date and heirs.
