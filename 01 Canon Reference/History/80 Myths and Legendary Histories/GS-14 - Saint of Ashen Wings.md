# Saint of Ashen Wings

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Saint begins as a mine slave and covert courier. A successful revolt uses prison tunnels and wins freedom for several settlements. Facing immediate reconquest, the rebel leader authorises an experimental passage through a damaged correspondence and uses condemned prisoners as the first test party. Survivors return altered and dangerous; the leader kills some personally and conceals the experiment from the liberated settlements.

The lie protects the new polity long enough to survive but leaves families without bodies or truth. Years later the leader confesses, surrenders office and accepts exile rather than forcing victims to maintain the heroic story. In exile the former commander rescues people from similar experiments and dies closing one Material breach. Redemption does not make the original test lawful or require families to celebrate the penitent.

## Later activity

The Greater Saint later manifests with ash-dark wings during specific heavenly commissions against routes operated by the Preacher of the Second Gospel. The Saint may command angels where the charge grants it, enter hostile custody and destroy constructed gates. Each pursuit ends when the named route, captives or text-delivery is secured. There is no permanent celestial hunt or private vengeance.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Slavery, insurgent killing, non-consensual prisoner experiments, killing of damaged survivors, concealment and abandonment of a vulnerable polity.

## The surviving inheritance

Liberation songs, victim rolls, covert rescue techniques and competing breachward groups; no single surviving order owns the Saint.

a liberation feast is followed the next day by an unofficial reading of experimental victims whom the public Life omits.

## What people remember

Immaculate liberator, unforgivable experimenter, redeemed penitent, hostile shadow being, Patron assassin or founder of every covert order.

## What remains unknown

The private name, culture, polity, exact breach, number of victims, death place, manifestations and institutional heirs.
