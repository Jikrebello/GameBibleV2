# Chain-Bearer of Sarrat

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Chain-Bearer begins as a slave infirmary attendant able to recognise another captive's pain before it is spoken. Owners implant a breach-responsive chain engine that converts surrounding distress into strength and uncontrollable pain. The attendant helps organise a revolt and is removed from the final stand by officials who want the engine for frontier war. Companions die believing they were abandoned.

Later freedom is real but incomplete. The liberated commander kills surrendering overseers, orders new soldiers fitted with imitations of the engine and massacres a neutral household accused of hiding former owners. Offered removal at the cost of losing command, the commander refuses. The final infernal compact is chosen because it promises that the pain will always mean power and that nobody will ever again remove the bearer from the battle.

## Later activity

The Sinner's office creates a recurrence body whenever a specified chain of custody culminates in mass retaliatory killing. Breaking the body interrupts the campaign; preventing the qualifying transfer prevents recurrence. The Witness at the Red Passage later holds one prisoner route against the Sinner without claiming the Chain-Bearer's victims are innocent of every crime.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Slavery, bodily mutilation, companion abandonment, murder of surrendering people, coerced imitation engines and massacre.

## The surviving inheritance

No lawful organisation. Slave revolts remember the healer; Sinner cults remember the engine; states cite the later massacre to discredit all revolt.

Abolitionists still wear broken chain links while recurrence cults secretly preserve the missing joining link.

## What people remember

Betrayed liberator, mindless weapon, responsible mass murderer, patron of revolt, Wrath Mask or infernal jailer.

## What remains unknown

The private name, exact owners, fate of companions, office grantor, recurrence interval and present cult.
