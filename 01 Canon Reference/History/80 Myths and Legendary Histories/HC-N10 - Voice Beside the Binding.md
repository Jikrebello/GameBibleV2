# Voice Beside the Binding

**c. 244–267 AR; later correction c. 404 AR.** This fixes the local human history and the explicitly described commission, not the identity of every later claimant or relic. The title is a received epithet rather than an additional heavenly rank.

## Earthly life

The future Saint was the child of a kiln tender in an inland prison-and-dye district during the Underground Centuries. As a young prisoner clerk under torture, the mortal supplied one false name to stop a beating. The named worker was arrested and died in custody. After release, the clerk recanted publicly, found the victim’s household, surrendered wages for their support and began copying the names of people whom the prison courts removed from official rolls. The family accepted the support but never declared the death forgiven.

Beneath the old civic court, officials maintained a Star-age containment around a dependent Pride Mask. The containment had once used sworn testimony freely given. Persecuting magistrates altered it so that erased prisoners’ names, forced confessions and dying breath sustained the boundary. Custodians demanded that the clerk become its permanent living mouth because the copied name-roll could stabilize it. The clerk refused, exposed the murders and helped move prisoners out through the kiln service ways. Officials sealed the clerk inside the chamber during the final failure. The mortal died as the breach closed; death, usefulness and later veneration did not by themselves establish sainthood.

## Later active history

Around 404 AR, custodians of a new reliquary began issuing commands in the dead clerk’s voice and used them to identify alleged enemies of the converted state. A genuine Saintly manifestation, if the authorial placement survives review, received a single commission: preserve the old containment long enough to evacuate its keepers, state that the relic voice was counterfeit, and repeat the victim’s name the Saint had once betrayed. The Saint did not speak through the binding, inhabit the reliquary or become its keeper. The manifestation ended when the people were out and the false conduit was broken.

## What people remember

murderer turned useful informant, penitent martyr, prison cult invention, bound ghost, Mask ventriloquism or genuine later Saintly correction.

## What remains

an annual reading places the betrayed worker’s name before the martyr’s provisional title. One church preserves the order; another reverses it.

## What remains unknown

gender, city, baptismal history, public recognition, number evacuated, exact Mask, relic custody and present authenticity claims. Within this nursery lead, reception as a redeemed Saint after judgement is fixed; no mortal institution proves it.
