# Unknown Histories — Authorial Accounts

These six histories have fixed central events but incomplete surviving knowledge. Their discoveries require more than one kind of evidence. A ruin, a speaking copy, a vision or a Saint cannot provide the complete account. The dossiers preserve limited contemporary viewpoints, damaged transmission, plausible wrong reconstructions, a 1360 access point and a bounded test.

The Caravan is not the Cut-Loose Wards; the furnace refuge is not the Living Brass city; the Second Sack is not the Unreturned Treasury or one Bread-Jar parcel; the gate Saint belongs to a landlocked fortress and is not the Saint of the Two Ferries. Shared pressures never establish shared persons, relics, polities or events.
