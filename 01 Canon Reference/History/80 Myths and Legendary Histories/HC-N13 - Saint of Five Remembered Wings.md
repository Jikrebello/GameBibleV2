# Saint of Five Remembered Wings

**c. 781–826 AR; later intervention c. 907 AR.** This fixes the local human history and the explicitly described commission, not the identity of every later claimant or relic. The title is a received epithet rather than an additional heavenly rank.

## Earthly life

The future Saint was born into a Long-Frontier client dynasty whose ruler held an infernal inheritance compact. Children of the house were treated as hostages and possible vessels for a hereditary office. The parent beat and mutilated the future Saint, cutting five winglike scars across the shoulders and side after the child refused a custody rite. Servants helped the young heir escape; several were executed for it.

Years later the exile returned with an armed household and overthrew the parent. The heir killed the parent personally, then murdered surrendered relatives and an unarmed ritual custodian in vengeance. The infernal compact influenced fear and opportunity but did not choose the murders. After the victory, the heir refused the inherited office, confessed the killings before victims’ households, surrendered the throne and spent the remaining years securing freedom, burial and property for the house’s former servants and child hostages. Some accepted restitution. Others refused contact and continued to name the penitent as murderer. The future Saint died defending an evacuation from former allies who wanted the dynasty restored.

## Later active history

During the early Fall, an infernal claimant attempted to collect surviving children through the old blood-and-office clause. The manifested Saint received a single commission to testify that neither blood nor mutilation transferred a human soul, guard the children while the actual compact was destroyed and confront the office’s current bearer. The Saint’s five avian signs marked remembered wounds rather than angelic anatomy or hereditary power. The Saint did not return to kill the family again, claim the throne or compel victims to forgive.

## What people remember

infernal bastard made holy by violence, repentant murderer, political fabrication, angel mistaken for a Saint, dynasty founder, victim of slander or continuing secret ruler.

## What remains

several custody courts preserve the clause **“No wound inherits the child.”** It bars blood, mutilation, household office and a parent’s compact from transferring ownership of a child’s soul or person.

## What remains unknown

house, region, gender, exact relations killed, public recognition, form of restitution, later claimant, surviving children and authenticity of all five-wing relics. Within this nursery lead, reception as a redeemed Saint after judgement is fixed; victims remain free to refuse reconciliation.
