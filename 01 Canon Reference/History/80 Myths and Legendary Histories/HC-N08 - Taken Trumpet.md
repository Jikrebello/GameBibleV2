# Taken Trumpet

**c. 910–913 AR.** This fixes the local human history and the explicitly described commission, not the identity of every later claimant or relic. The title is a received epithet rather than an additional heavenly rank.

## Historical aperture

In the first years after Caleran’s fall, an occupation governor held prisoners and negotiated surrenders through a mountain signal house. A Greater Host/Herald Angel was commissioned to announce one protected surrender and name the prisoners covered by it. Human warlocks serving the governor prepared a ward around the signal apertures. The ward could not seize the angel’s whole created spirit, corrupt its will or make it fall. It could trap the active manifestation path and projected commission-body inside a repeating false signal.

The Herald entered because the protected surrender could not otherwise be heard. The ward crushed and stripped the visible pinions, split the beaklike trumpet geometry and repeatedly demanded a proclamation that the prisoners had surrendered their names and property. The Angel refused. A human rescue party breached the lower room but was discovered; most rescuers and several prisoners were killed. Survivors damaged the ward enough for the Herald to complete the true proclamation and withdraw. The occupation then executed additional witnesses. Rebel commanders suppressed the rescue’s failure because they feared that a defeated angel would destroy morale.

## What people remember

captured angel, failed rebel weapon, mutilated Saint, primordial fallen spirit, Patron signal-body, invented morale story or proof that Heaven abandoned the capital.

## What remains

military signal law requires the protected persons in a surrender to be named before property terms. A broken three-note call marks any message suspected of coerced authorship.

## What remains unknown

signal house, governor, prisoners, rescuers, Herald’s title, enemy ward’s ultimate source, later counterfeit identity and whether any metal fragment is a relic.
