# Mason of the Ninth Bastion

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The Mason begins as a quarry measurer responsible for preventing tunnel collapses. After saving a town through an improvised retaining wall, officials repeatedly assign impossible works while honouring noble patrons instead. The Mason records every death and every withheld recognition. Resentment becomes doctrine: only those willing to spend lives understand construction. When sabotage delays a major course, the Mason orders one worker in ten from every surviving crew killed by lot. The condemned include workers who exposed the sabotage and crews who never touched the damaged course.

During the ninth bastion project, the Mason knowingly shortens evacuation time to preserve a geometrically perfect foundation. A workers' quarter collapses and prisoners are sealed inside because reopening the course would delay the campaign. When confronted, the Mason publishes the names as proof of personal honesty but refuses guilt, arguing that the dead finally belong to an enduring work. The later infernal bargain does not corrupt an innocent; it rewards an already chosen account of human beings as structural material.

## Later activity

The Sinner's office now links several incomplete fortresses within the War of the Last Map. Each massacre supplies a temporary causal bridge through which a constructed command-body can enter another bastion. The project seeks one route toward Material cities, not dominion over all Hell. The Warden of the Last Redoubt later opposes one link by evacuating the people whose deaths the design requires.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Decimation by lot, lethal labour, sealed prisoners, civilian districts sacrificed to geometry, execution of saboteurs and later followers repeating the commander's collective punishment.

## The surviving inheritance

a respected engineering school preserves safe measurements alongside a suppressed “necessary loss” appendix; an infernal cadre preserves the appendix alone.

Three ordinary fortresses still contain sealed ninth chambers which engineers refuse to open and Sinner cults try to fill.

## What people remember

Betrayed genius, mass murderer, founder of scientific fortification, infernal captive or Patron architect.

## What remains unknown

The private name, exact forts, bargain partner, number of active links, current command-body and ultimate objective.
