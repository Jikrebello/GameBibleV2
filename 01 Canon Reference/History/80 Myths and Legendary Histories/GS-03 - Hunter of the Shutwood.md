# Hunter of the Shutwood

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The Hunter begins as a hostage-guide exchanged between a forest household and a frontier governor. Exceptional at finding people and detecting false trails, the guide becomes a commander but refuses to explain intelligence sources. During one pursuit a guilty scout is executed after trial and a coerced scout dies under an unlawful sentence. When the third scout refuses a secret interrogation and demands an open hearing, the Hunter kills the innocent scout in a sudden blow, then orders all three deaths entered as one lawful traitor case. Secrecy saves the route network from an immediate infiltrator while poisoning the household that operates it.

The Hunter eventually submits the records, resigns command and personally leads the recovery of families previously condemned as collaborators. The work ends during a Shutwood evacuation in which some genuinely guilty fighters are carried out beside their children and victims. Mercy does not erase trial, restitution or the innocent scout's death.

## Later activity

Much later, the Saint receives a commission within the Long Rent War to find households stranded between foreclosed correspondences. The manifestation can cross only routes necessary to that rescue and withdraws when the last named household reaches a lawful hearing. A separate threshold hunt against the Judge Beneath the Black Eaves occurs when a fear-court uses prophecies to mark refugees as future killers.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

The Hunter personally murders an innocent subordinate, falsifies the case, permits another unlawful execution, sustains hostage politics and collective suspicion, and later rescues guilty people without acquitting them.

## The surviving inheritance

One later regional guide fellowship, several incompatible route manuals and a secret tribunal that falsely claims the Hunter authorised permanent concealment. The fellowship postdates the Hunter and possesses no authenticated institutional descent.

An order of guides leaves one place empty at every route hearing for the scout whom its claimed founder killed.

## What people remember

Flawless ranger, foreign occupier, repentant killer, Menhir guest, Patron route-lord or fabricated founder of the Long Watch.

## What remains unknown

The private name, polity, Menhir involvement, death, exact order inheritance and authenticity of later tribunal claims.
