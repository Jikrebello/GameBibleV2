# Preacher of the Second Gospel

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Preacher begins as a temple copyist who joins the Church because the new congregations feed displaced workers whom both imperial and old-cult authorities abandoned. The copyist becomes an extraordinary preacher and organiser but privately teaches that every real power deserves negotiated worship. A bishop removes the preacher from public teaching after witnesses establish coerced confessions and a secret rite addressed to the White Flame as though it were an appetite to be fed.

The correction is public but not a torture or imperial humiliation. The preacher chooses to interpret it as proof that the Church fears honest revelation. In closed sanctuaries the preacher asks hostile powers for an answer, receives several genuine but contradictory responses and builds the **Second Gospel**: a rival sacramental system in which effectiveness proves truth. The Preacher personally presides over the first captive sacrifice, orders that a child be used in the first possession trial after another teacher refuses, and orders a former companion killed so the original disciplinary record can be seized. Later initiates reproduce each act as proof of fidelity.

## Later activity

After damnation the Preacher bears a heresiarchal office that can speak through authorised copies of the Second Gospel. The Sinner participates in the War of the Closed Doors and Siege of the Martyrs' Names by offering displaced cult workers a coherent counter-church, real minor wonders and false judgement. Later conflicts with the Clerk of a Hundred Wells concern whether law can bind revelation; the Saint of Ashen Wings receives a bounded commission to close one route by which new annotated Gospels are being delivered.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Coerced confession, child possession trials, captive sacrifice, ordered murder and sincere followers damned through chosen participation. The Preacher authors and commands the first acts; Church discipline neither causes nor excuses them.

## The surviving inheritance

Several rival counter-churches, mutually hostile recensions and legitimate charitable practices stolen from the preacher's earliest congregation.

Three mutually exclusive “complete” Second Gospels circulate, while a fourth fragment is used innocently by hospitals unaware of its origin.

## What people remember

Persecuted visionary, obvious fraud, tragically mishandled reformer, first modern Sinner, several preachers conflated, or a text written by later enemies.

## What remains unknown

Name, city, precise responsive powers, final earthly death, office title and which later recension is closest.
