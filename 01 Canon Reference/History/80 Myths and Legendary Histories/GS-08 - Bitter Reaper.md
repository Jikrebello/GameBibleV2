# Bitter Reaper

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Reaper begins carrying corpses from an estate whose owners live above the poison line. Through practical medicine and stolen equipment, the carrier leads workers into the high houses and kills the overseers. Survivors acclaim a liberator. The new regime nevertheless requires every household to accept repeated exposure rites so that nobody will again depend upon elite medicine.

The rites injure children, kill some adults and make survivors dependent upon treatments controlled by the Reaper's clinics. Refusal brings exclusion from water and burial. When the Hundred-Wells Clerk challenges permanent quarantine powers, the Reaper insists that vulnerable people are safer without choice. The final Sinner compact is accepted during an outbreak the Reaper did not cause, exchanging the population's dependency for the commander's continued control.

## Later activity

The damned Reaper now establishes refuge clinics along foreclosed routes. They genuinely protect travellers from local poisons while making departure lethal without continued treatment. This participates in the Long Rent War rather than causing the Grey Sweat. The Hundred-Wells Saint later suspends one inherited dependency seal, but does not cure every patient or destroy every clinic.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Poison labour, killing of overseers, forced exposure, child death, medical dependency, denial of water and burial.

## The surviving inheritance

Clinics divided between real medical descendants, exploitative cult hospitals and states that copied quarantine law without supernatural corruption.

a bitter cordial remains medically useful in small doses and ritually abused by Reaper cults; the recipe proves neither cult nor condemnation.

## What people remember

Liberator-physician, necessary tyrant, plague saint, Sinner, personification of disease or victim of an ungrateful court.

## What remains unknown

The private name, estate, exact medicine, compact party, surviving clinics and any possibility of future repentance.
