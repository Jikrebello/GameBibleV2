# Witness at the Red Passage

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Witness begins as a prison porter hired because a malformed shoulder makes ordinary military service impossible. The porter learns which chains fit which bodies, which guards can be bribed and which prisoners will be killed before trial. During a transfer riot the porter initially shuts one gate to save a smaller group, knowingly leaving another yard to the soldiers. The abandoned prisoners are beaten and several are executed. Inside the escaping group, frightened rescuers then murder bound guards, a captured informer and two prisoners wrongly accused of collaboration. The porter conceals those killings from the escape register because exposing them may destroy the prison network.

The guilt does not produce immediate heroism. The porter spends years carrying letters, food and burial names between prisons and unsuccessfully attempts to restore the murdered prisoners to the record. A later purge drives several households toward a narrow red-painted service passage. Knowing the defence will be fatal, the porter holds that passage while civilians and prisoners escape. The martyr is tortured and killed after capture. The abandoned yard and concealed murders remain part of the received Life rather than a secret excuse for the final sacrifice.

## Later activity

During one later movement in the Siege of the Martyrs' Names, a genuine manifestation holds a soul-road threshold while angels of witness recover names being converted into a Sinner's prisoner registry. The commission protects persons and testimony; it neither releases judged souls nor declares every prisoner innocent. The manifestation later confronts the Chain-Bearer of Sarrat at one infernal transfer and the General of the Glass Court when a perfected martyr-copy begins demanding retaliatory blood.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Abandoned prisoners die; the escape cell murders captives and innocent prisoners; the porter suppresses their names; the Witness is tortured and executed; later admirers murder supposed informers under the Witness's banner. The Saint condemns the later killing as a repetition of the earthly sin rather than violence invented by an unrelated cult.

## The surviving inheritance

No surviving order. There are prison prayers, incompatible red-door processions, a militant cult dissolved after the riot and several organisations falsely claiming succession.

a red-painted prison door kept unhinged in a courthouse, used in an annual reading of names but claimed by no lawful order.

## What people remember

Flawless martyr, repentant coward, invented prison composite, Patron herald, common saint of several prisons or dangerous cult symbol.

## What remains unknown

Personal name, gender, city, number saved, exact torture, relic authenticity, herald identities and present custodianship.
