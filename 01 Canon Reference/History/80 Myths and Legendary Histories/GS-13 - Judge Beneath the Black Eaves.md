# Judge Beneath the Black Eaves

> **What happened — authorial history.** The earthly core and the expressly described later activity are established. Public cult claims, relic pedigrees and the final list of personal names are not thereby authenticated. This person is separate from every other life in the constellation.

## Earthly life

The future Judge begins as a roof runner carrying warrants between courts that no longer recognise one another. Genuine but incomplete visions reveal murders, riots and betrayals before they occur. The runner prevents several crimes and becomes a celebrated examiner. Gradually the Judge tortures people for acts only foreseen, displays mutilated offenders and terrorises whole households because fear appears to reduce visible crime.

The prophecies are neither invented nor inevitable. Several atrocities occur precisely because preventive executions destroy the relationships that might have stopped them. When an assassin finally enters the Black-Eaves courthouse, the Judge orders guards to stand aside so the death can be used as proof that every prediction was fixed. Victims remain dead; the theatrical death does not turn terror into truth.

## Later activity

The damned Judge later offers divided churches accurate visions of the other's future crimes, demanding preventive persecution. A crown office and Envy or Pride Masks imitate the Judge independently. The Shutwood Hunter receives one threshold commission to rescue families marked as future killers, demonstrating that protection need not equal acquittal and possibility is not sentence.

A Saint's work ends with the commission; no institution owns or summons the person. A damned Sinner's office, image, body and copied likeness are not the soul. Defeat of such a manifestation does not reverse judgement.

## What the life cost

Torture, mutilation, execution without committed crime, collective punishment, self-fulfilling massacre and deliberate use of assassination as propaganda.

## The surviving inheritance

Terror procedures, abolition law, a crown-relic dispute and criminal fraternities claiming the Judge's permission.

Courts prohibit conviction for “the future fact,” while secret magistrates still purchase Black-Eaves forecasts.

## What people remember

Perfect prophet, mad judge, Sinner, murdered reformer, crown-bound ghost or several executioners under one title.

## What remains unknown

The private name, town, vision source, assassin, office, crown status and present cult.
