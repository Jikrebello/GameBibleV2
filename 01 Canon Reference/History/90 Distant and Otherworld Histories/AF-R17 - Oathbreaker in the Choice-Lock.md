# The Oathbreaker in the Choice-Lock

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One damned human-soul map-lord, not a native concept-fiend or prophecy incarnate.

## The dominion

Remains bound in a knot-like office coil. Agents arrange treaties, marriages, reconciliations and evacuations hoping someone will freely accept protected surrender and then exploit it after receiving the promised benefit.

## Project

Prove that every covenant is merely delayed predation and thereby reopen the choice-lock.

## Victims and methods

Engineered abuse, false peace, sacrificed generations, murdered mediators and survivors blamed for refusing later reconciliation.

## Separate histories

Unlike the Cold-Plug King, no substitute body supplies release mass. The decisive free betrayal cannot be compelled, and the mechanism neither predicts providence nor makes Heaven uncertain.

## A particular history

### The peace not sold

In a lesser successor-state feud, c. 1190–1220 AR, the Oathbreaker's agent arranged a surrender which genuinely saved a ruler's household. Once the captives were safe, it offered the ruler his enemy's defenceless children and urged him to avenge an earlier murder. The ruler had promised protection freely. Now the agent needed a free betrayal after the benefit had been received.

The ruler refused, although his own brother called that refusal disloyal and killed the mediator. The surviving children were returned under guard; one household had kept its promise while another crime remained unpunished. The choice-lock stayed closed. Later tellers wrongly made the ruler universally merciful. No future peace is predestined to repeat or reverse this failure.

## In 1360

The prisoner and agents remain active, but no present treaty is authorially selected as the final condition.
