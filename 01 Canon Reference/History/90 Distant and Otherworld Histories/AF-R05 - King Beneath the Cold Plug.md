# The King Beneath the Cold Plug

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One damned human-soul whose pressure prison anchors a separable projection office.

## The dominion

Governs agents through reflections in black ice, saturated stone and drowned metal. Every release plan requires another person to occupy one load-bearing route position.

## Project

Persuade rescuers that substitution is mercy and that the proposed victim would eventually have suffered anyway.

## Victims and methods

Expendable cults, crushed route wedges, drowned messengers, murdered witnesses and hostages promised freedom only if they identify replacements.

## Separate histories

Unlike AF-R17, the prison does not require a free betrayal to open; it requires redistributed pressure and a knowingly accepted substitute.

## A particular history

### The substitute under the reservoir

At an abandoned reservoir beneath an inland Star-age town, a projection promised to release prisoners if a damaged retaining wedge was repaired. The rescuers brought stone; agents told them that the last support must instead be a living person. One captain selected a captured enemy mason and lowered him into the flooded shaft.

The mason died, but his companions broke the lifting frame before it could seat him in the required position. The King remained confined. The captain lied that an enemy attack had ruined a successful rescue. Quarry families preserved the broken frame in their funeral procession because the man had been sacrificed by someone who called himself a liberator. The incomplete operation reveals no complete modern release geometry.

## In 1360

Projections remain active, but no canonical cult possesses the complete release geometry and no present countdown is established.
