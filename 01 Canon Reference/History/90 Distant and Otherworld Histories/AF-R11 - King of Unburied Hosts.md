# The King of Unburied Hosts

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One damned human-soul Hollow Prophet controlling residue, copied habits, corpses and command offices.

## The dominion

Rules an empty necropolis carried along a grave-eel manifestation. Corpses perform remembered work, copied voices issue old orders and residue reenacts loyalty. Human souls are not inside this stock.

## Project

Replace every living institution with a version that cannot refuse, repent, improvise or forgive.

## Victims and methods

Mass murder, grave violation, families forced to prepare relatives for reuse, living prisoners walled among corpse machines and dissenters killed so obedient likenesses can replace them.

## Separate histories

[[The General Below]] eternally conscripts genuine persons through a military refusal; this King wants persons removed and controllable simulations installed. The Ossuary Crown debates ancestry and personhood rather than universal obedient death.

## A particular history

### The march without mourners

In a minor reconquered valley, c. 970–1010 AR, a commander prohibited burial while a corpse-working emissary rebuilt the dead into a road gang. A mother recognized her son's repaired coat among the workers and followed it, believing the emissary's claim that he still served. At the camp she found several bodies answering to the same call and a copied voice rehearsing words he had never spoken.

The families smashed the driving fittings and burned the assembled work, including the son's coat. Soldiers killed several mourners and walled surviving prisoners beside the remaining machines. The resistance interrupted one supply line to the King's necropolis. It did not free souls from corpses: the lie had been that the persons were there at all.

## In 1360

The necropolis and King remain active, while several Material corpse techniques imitate fragments without proving direct allegiance.
