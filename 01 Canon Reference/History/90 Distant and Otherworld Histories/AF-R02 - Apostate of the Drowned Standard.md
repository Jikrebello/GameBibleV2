# The Apostate of the Drowned Standard

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One unique fallen created-spirit, not a devil species, former saint or corruptible angelic rank.

## Original commission

During the primordial rebellion, this created spirit was charged with protecting a withdrawal of other created charges and ending the action once they were safe. It refused that limit before the loyal host's confirmation. No later loyal angel fell.

## Refusal

Its refusal to lower the standard belongs to that first rebellion. Much later military cults retold it as the rescue and conscription of human refugees. The cult version is not a second fall: human child levies, hostage killings and mutilated deserters belong to its later predatory campaigns.

## Continuing lie

Ending the campaign would retroactively betray everyone who died during the withdrawal; therefore every surviving charge owes the war another generation.

## The dominion

Commands the Drowned Standard Front, a moving chain of flooded redoubts which annexes every road it successfully protects. Its manifestations are wet banners, pale armor, many-eyed beauty and black water displaced by invisible wings—never flame.

## Manifestation and anchor

The principal body appears wherever the original standard and at least one unlawfully annexed withdrawal marker share a route. The standard is a created relic and true-name handle, not the spirit's body or soul. Lesser banners coordinate offices and mortal troops but contain no angelic fragments.

## Stolen uses of an old charge

It can still recognize a genuine pursuit, preserve a withdrawal road against hostile entry, rally defenders and distinguish named charges from pursuers. It abuses those gifts by refusing to acknowledge when withdrawal is complete and by defining neutrality as delayed attack.

## Victims and methods

Child levies, mutilated deserters, hostage execution, settlements stripped for replacements and genuine rescues converted into permanent recruitment.

## Separate histories

Distinct from [[The General Below]], whose wound is eternal conscription; the Apostate is a created spirit guilty of one chosen refusal and repeated subsequent acts.

## What defeat can end

Breaking the original standard collapses the coordinated front, releases annexed route markers and prevents the present manifestation from reforming there. It neither annihilates the created spirit nor produces repentance. No spell, inherited office or captured banner supplies a routine restoration path; further judgement belongs to this unique person and remains outside mortal control.

## A particular history

### The levy after the safe crossing

During a late Atherian frontier retreat, c. 850–875 AR, a cult carrying one drowned banner protected a convoy from raiders. Once the families reached their home district, its commanders demanded the eldest children as replacements for the dead. They executed a father who pointed out that the rescue had ended, then annexed the ferry that had carried the convoy. A former banner-bearer cut the ferry loose. The freed households escaped across farmland while the stranded rearguard suffered the cult's punishment. Their annual unfastening of a boat's rope remembers a real rescue followed by a chosen betrayal. This local cult neither possessed the complete original standard nor caused the primordial spirit's fall.

## In 1360

The spirit and front remain active in Hell. Material military cults possess incompatible scraps of the original withdrawal order but no present release event is fixed.
