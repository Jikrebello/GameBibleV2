# Nythalum after the Ascension

Nythalum's rulers tried to escape physically into space and reach the Moon. They did not secretly enter the Egg White. Their launch-support harbours, extraction sites, return works and wrecks belong to a Material logistical undertaking whose scale depended on captive labour and allied maritime powers.

## Before the last launches

Engineering houses competed for seats, fuel, material and inherited access. Lunar ancestry claims could legitimate a court without making every ruler an immediate child of Lunara or Lyraxis. Ulmaris and lesser harbours supplied copper, crystal, timber, provisions and captives while assuming that service purchased berths.

Revocation turned that expectation into rebellion. The Moon Harbours of Caelene faced broken schedules, riot and guards defending launches from workers who had built them. The Thief of the Unowned Day cycle remembers a selfish theft which interrupted work but erased a labour gang's promised berths; at least one person died in the panic. No one stole cosmic time.

## The ascent and the abandoned

Some vehicles failed on the ground; others left wrecks across the physical ocean. Support fleets pursued fallen spires rather than entering another realm. The imperial collapse broke supply chains before every installation ceased answering. Later island and continental populations inherited live machinery amid dead institutions.

The catastrophe belongs within the Great Unbinding's converging failures, but not every wreck resulted from the Prophet's direct act. Plagues struck bounded identity-copy, berth and launch systems. Nythalum's choices and the labour it consumed remain its rulers' responsibility.

## One genuine continuant

At least one lunar Continuant preserved an already living Giant through uninterrupted conscious operation. Its body changed; it did not die and return from a backup. It is not human and possesses no human soul. Copies of its old instructions, memories or speech are not additional incarnations of it.

The Continuant maintains a lunar installation after the collapse of the original command structure. Maintenance has become narrower as materials and functioning servants diminish. Its records omit what its own handlers hid before departure; longevity does not supply omniscience. No current rescue, return or conquest is fixed.

## The world below

Return Annexes and other remains in the Emerald Expanse connect later people to Nythalum's material network. Their inhabitants may preserve a method without understanding the regime that made it. A correct repair instruction cannot prove an answering machine is a dead ancestor, and a failed one cannot excuse a living ruler who knowingly sacrifices workers.

This is a distant historical line beside the Egg White flights, not an explanation of them. It leaves the Moon physically distant, the old empire morally culpable and its surviving actors individually distinct.
