# The Exile Behind the Borrowed Temple


The epithet identifies one historical power, not a universal rank.

## The person and the manifestation

autonomous Pride idol-pattern Archmask cut off from one former Material route but active through other Shadow anchors.

a royal human or many-headed draconic persona above the cult; authorially its durable body is a black eel-dragon wearing several conquered crowns. Any authentic flame comes only from a separate Wrath compact.

## Ambition and rule

The Exile is a sovereign performer of return. It appears through several stable royal personae: bereaved empress, armored liberator, beautiful counsellor, mother of victory and many-crowned eel-dragon. These are guises of one autonomous Pride Archmask, not separate souls, dependent Masks or a five-member council. Different cultures remember different personae as the true one.

It cannot simply walk back into the Material world. Each return project requires a stolen or reopened sanctuary, public acclamation, sufficient manifestation mass and a mortal commander who can win territory before the Archmask fully arrives. The commander is promised shared rule and treated as the one person strong enough to understand the Exile. If access succeeds, that exceptional status becomes the first historical fact the Exile erases.

Its dragon armies are constructed war-broods assembled from stolen bodies, Giant-age techniques, shaped residue and animal material. Pure constructed war-broods have no interior agency. Living fleshworks can suffer; neither contains a lesser or fractional human soul. Their dragon form is imperial propaganda: the Exile presents itself as mother of a restored elder majesty without being a True Dragon or creator of life.

## Victims

desecration, stolen bodies used in war-brood manufacture, conquered peoples enslaved, prisoners executed for portal stability and champions discarded once access is secured.

## Limits and inheritance

not a goddess, the Adversary, a true dragon, creator of life or whole Idol Throne. War-broods are constructed patterns; humans retain complete souls.

This power is active in its own ecology in 1360. No present defeat, return, redemption or campaign outcome is fixed.
