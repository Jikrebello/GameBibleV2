# The Furnace General of the Red Lease

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One autonomous Wrath idol-pattern Archmask leasing an infernal office-body and front. It is not native to Hell.

## The dominion

Rules a disciplined expeditionary host wherever a valid Red Lease connects one Hell dominion to Wrath's Red Marches. The host's black armor contains authentic Wrath flame; severing the lease leaves cold armor, surviving offices and ordinary black mire.

## Project

Prove that rage becomes more productive when rationed, drilled and assigned by rank.

## Victims and methods

Punitive decimation, burned prisoners, civilians displayed as reasons for revenge, sacrificial assaults and officers sealed alive into command bodies.

## Separate histories

Not the General Below, which perpetuates command through a specific Abyssal refusal; not the Red Tally, which is a broader Wrath network; not a universal Hell marshal.

## A particular history

### The lease at the burnt quarry

One past lease supplied an infernal mercenary prince during a provincial quarry rebellion, c. 1120–1150 AR. The prince offered the General a route through a captured furnace sanctuary in exchange for soldiers. The General burned surrendered quarry workers to give their relatives a grievance, recruited those relatives against the prince's rivals, and punished anyone who would not keep fighting.

A furnace crew destroyed the leased fittings while the host was deployed. The armor went cold; the Archmask's Wrath supply had been cut. Ordinary soldiers could then break through, but the prisoners already burned did not return. The abandoned quarry remains scarred by that fire. This was an imported Wrath campaign, not a volcanic opening into a fiery Hell, and it is not the same lease still operating in 1360.

## In 1360

At least one Red Lease and host remain active, but neither its infernal counterparty nor any Material campaign is fixed.
