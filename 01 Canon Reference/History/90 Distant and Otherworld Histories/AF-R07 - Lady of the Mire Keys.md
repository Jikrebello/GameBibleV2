# The Lady of the Mire Keys


The epithet identifies one historical power, not a universal rank.

## The person and the manifestation

damned female human-soul ruler carrying several separable gate and sentence offices.

a crowned woman whose lower body extends into a key-ring coil beneath the city; each key opens one cell by closing another.

## Ambition and rule

The Lady is not merely a gaoler. She is the infernal patroness of manufactured criminals and profitable rebellion. She publicly breaks sentences imposed by older courts, hides fugitives inside her city and teaches prisoners how to exploit the precise defects of a hostile mandate. Each liberation humiliates an older authority and makes the rescued person dependent upon her keys, safe houses and criminal standing.

Her court contains escaped prisoners, informers, smugglers, former guards, condemned advocates and damned survivors who genuinely prefer her city to the dominions they fled. Some possess real loyalty and courage. The price is that every sanctuary must help manufacture the next class of outlaw. A person pardoned by the Lady may be required to frame an innocent official, seduce a gaoler, deliver another fugitive or accept that somebody else now occupies the opened cell.

## Victims

judicial murder, prison rape, coerced informing, disappeared families, guards paid in access to captives and victims prosecuted for surviving illegally.

## Limits and inheritance

her sex neither explains nor softens her guilt; no descent from another archfiend is presumed.

This power is active in its own ecology in 1360. No present defeat, return, redemption or campaign outcome is fixed.
