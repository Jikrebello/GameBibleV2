# The False Prince Behind the Rescue

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One autonomous Envy idol-pattern Archmask operating through several false Patron, saintly and infernal personae.

## The dominion

Engineers a bounded danger, performs a real rescue and then uses gratitude to replace names, companions and political memory. The rescued genuinely survive; that truth makes exposure harder.

## Project

Prove that authorship of rescue gives ownership of the rescued person's identity and future loyalty.

## Victims and methods

Companions murdered as impostors, survivors enslaved through rescue debt, institutions purged using fabricated possession evidence and actual rescuers erased from the public account.

## Separate histories

[[Borrowed Saints]] imitate holy persons and [[The Weeping Courts]] consume grief. The Prince's defining operation targets living survivor communities immediately after a materially real rescue, even when no saint is claimed.

## A particular history

### The liberator's replacement family

During one early Reconquest hostage exchange, c. 930–960 AR, the Prince's agents arranged an ambush and then provided a genuinely usable escape. They told a rescued woman that her travelling companions had sold her and presented strangers as the household which had paid her ransom. One true companion was murdered as an impostor.

The woman eventually recognized a nurse's old work song in a voice the rescuers insisted she should fear. She fled with the nurse, leaving property and the public gratitude expected of her behind. Other survivors remained with the false household. This episode preserves a particular deception without identifying the Archmask's first face or declaring every apparently generous rescuer a counterfeit.

## In 1360

Several personae remain active, but no single cult, current rescue or claimed saint is fixed as the original face.
