# The Queen of the Open Scar

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One sovereign abyssal-wound, not an insect spirit, Idol, devil, human soul or True Dragon.

## The dominion

Manifests chasm-ribs and expendable locust-shaped wound bodies. It persuades every side to widen the scar for tactical advantage, then feeds recurrence through famine, displacement and mutually dependent fortifications.

## Victims and methods

Crops and bodies stripped by swarms, prisoners thrown into the opening, occupied settlements sacrificed by defenders and displaced populations forbidden to return because their homes now constitute military depth.

## A particular history

### The farms left outside

At a lesser frontier wound, after the first local fortifications but before their final abandonment, military suppliers sold both sides defenses which required the opening to remain active. An outer farming community was ordered to stay as a warning belt. When swarms stripped the crop and wounded workers, officers refused evacuation because an empty belt would no longer justify supply payments.

Two households opened a service road and escaped. Their neighbours' homes were subsequently demolished by defenders, who used the ruins to widen the military gap. The Queen exploited the chosen prolongation of a real danger; it had not created every supplier's motive. The displaced people's descendants possess no guaranteed route home, and killing a swarm still does not close the wound.

## In 1360

The scar and Queen remain active; destroying swarm bodies provides relief but cannot close the contradiction.
