# The Horned Cartographer

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One damned human map-lord carrying a route office; horns and beast traits are dominion regalia.

## The dominion

Controls a mobile maze made from routes taken for predatory reasons. Its agents enter armies, hunting fraternities, breachward schools and rescue parties, then alter tests so cruelty appears to be the competence needed for survival.

## Project

Prove that only hunters understand the world and that anyone requiring protection has consented to become prey.

## Victims and methods

Captives hunted through corridors, forced bodily alteration, prisoners compelled to kill weaker companions, torture as initiation and massacres committed under defenders' insignia.

## Separate histories

The Auditor controls priced roads and administrative standing. The Cartographer creates situations in which the apparently strongest path is morally predatory and then recruits those who choose it.

## A particular history

### The road that made a hunter

During a High-Noon border ransom, c. 1140–1170 AR, a paid guide accepted a shorter route through the Cartographer's maze. At each junction, the apparent safe passage required abandoning or attacking someone weaker. The guide first surrendered an enemy prisoner, then a wounded companion, and finally found that the recovered hostage was to be offered as the last proof of fitness.

The hostage turned back with the surviving prisoners while the guide pursued the promised exit alone. Their slow return required dismantling the guiding marks. The guide emerged later as an agent wearing the same defenders' insignia. This was the corruption of one rescue, not UH-03's living boundary operation. A forked ransom-road marker now bears a cross-stroke where the shortcut began.

## In 1360

The maze and ruler remain active. One route may intersect the War of the Last Map without making him its commander.
