# The Surgeon of the Wound Arsenal


The epithet identifies one historical power, not a universal rank.

## The person and the manifestation

damned human-soul investigator using stolen office systems and captive wound-beings.

a many-handed human figure seated inside an articulated black eel whose ribs are laboratories and route clamps.

## Ambition and rule

The Surgeon is an elegant infernal intellectual, not a gore-covered technician. He receives rulers, scholars and prospective clients in a quiet anatomical court inside the articulated laboratory-eel. He explains real causal injuries, demonstrates techniques which save otherwise unreachable victims and treats established map-lords as timid bureaucrats frightened of discovery.

His revolutionary project is the **divided instrument method**: testimony clamps, geometrical beacons and separated manifestation anchors which can preserve and redirect an existing Abyssal contradiction with partial repeatability. It is intended to overturn infernal military power, but contains no fire. Its danger is concrete: every controlled redirection damages the roads, names and testimony required to control the next trial.

## Victims

vivisection, identity damage, experimental settlements, assistants erased by failed sequence and captives kept conscious because testimony stabilizes results.

## Limits and inheritance

reports of blue, white or infernal fire are later iconography unless a separate Wrath actor is proven.

This power is active in its own ecology in 1360. No present defeat, return, redemption or campaign outcome is fixed.
