# The Iron Listener

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One damned human-soul ruler bearing listening, custody and route offices.

## The dominion

Controls an iron-ribbed fortress sunk through several strata of black mud. Its listening network consists of offices, informers, architecture and coerced witnesses; the ruler is not omniscient.

## Project

Eliminate betrayal by criminalizing private speech, unreported mercy and any relationship not legible to the fortress.

## Victims and methods

Anticipatory torture, secret detention, family denunciation, execution for predicted treason and districts deliberately buried when their records become unreliable.

## Separate histories

Unlike the Lady, the Listener does not recruit criminals or exploit escape. He wants a society in which crime becomes impossible because nothing unobserved remains.

## A particular history

### The house with no speaking room

After the Listener obtained its sunk fortress, a household of living captive masons built a private alcove for an elderly mother who spoke to an absent son. An informer reported it as a meeting place. The Listener demanded that the surviving daughter repeat every word spoken there; she invented a confession to spare the mother and thereby implicated the missing brother.

The ruler executed a returned prisoner under that brother's name before discovering that it had purchased a false report. It buried the household's gallery to hide the error. One mason escaped through a waste channel and taught later labour gangs to leave listening ribs out of their smallest rooms. Several such rooms survive. Their silence marks deliberate human workmanship, not a region the ruler mysteriously cannot hear.

## In 1360

The fortress is active and sells intelligence to multiple dominions. Some reports are exact, some manufactured and some gaps caused by route failure.
