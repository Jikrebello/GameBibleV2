# The Spore Empress of the Last Granary

These are historical epithets rather than a new universal infernal rank. The power described here is real; claims to cosmic necessity and moral title are not.

## The being behind the title

One autonomous Gluttony idol-pattern Archmask; fungi and decay remain natural life rather than guilty servants.

## The dominion

Occupies relief networks, siege stores and surrender granaries through deliberately cultivated food anchors. Its “wedding” joins a starving settlement to one distribution body whose spores make refusal progressively harder.

## Project

Redefine survival as incorporation until every eater must spread the same anchor to keep receiving food.

## Victims and methods

Poisoned granaries, bodily colonization, coerced marriage rites, children fed contaminated relief and neighboring settlements burned by frightened authorities.

## Separate histories

The Grey Sweat is not its plague; the Long Table does not own every famine feast. This Archmask requires chosen contamination of a named relief system.

## A particular history

### The wedding of the last store

In an isolated late-imperial river granary, c. 860–880 AR, a steward deliberately mixed contaminated relief grain with the last sound stock. The Empress's distributors offered continued food on condition that each household carry a starter portion into its neighbours' stores. Children ate first and became visibly ill; officials called their dependence a sacred marriage.

One household burned its own store and crossed to a rival town, carrying an ill child rather than seed. The receiving authorities burned neighbouring farms in panic, killing people who had never accepted the grain. The family's account accuses both the distributors and that retaliation. The localized outbreak is neither the Grey Sweat nor a claim that ordinary fungal life serves the Empress.

## In 1360

The Empress remains active wherever at least one deliberately maintained granary anchor survives. Ordinary fermentation, mushrooms and rot are not signs of it.
