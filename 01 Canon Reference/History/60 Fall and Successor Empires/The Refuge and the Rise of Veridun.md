# The Refuge and the Rise of Veridun

Veridun became a capital because it first became a refuge. Its volcanic highlands, defensible roads, monasteries, store caves, and surviving Ember-Succession clergy allowed it to receive people and institutions that Caleran could no longer protect.

## The Refuge City

After 905, Veridun absorbed imperial clerks, soldiers, relic custodians, guild families, provincial delegations, and rural refugees. Existing highland communities bore the cost. Caves became granaries, monasteries became hospitals, and private houses became chancelleries.

## The Veil, 906 AR

When a Qahirid force approached Veridun, witnesses reported a woman's figure spreading pale fire above the city before a violent night storm broke the siege. Thalmyria remembers this as the Mother's Veil. The event saved the refuge without settling whether the apparition was Amaryah, another saint, or an image granted for the city's need.

## Caedros and the Mountain League

Caedros Aetherion was a minor-house commander, not the heir of a lost imperial dynasty. He joined refuge councils, surviving soldiers, monasteries, village militias, and dispossessed houses into a mountain league. His legitimacy came from service, victory, and later coronation.

The resistance preserved population and roads before attempting reconquest. It evacuated exposed villages, denied passes, recovered archives, and offered amnesty to occupation troops who surrendered without carrying bound contracts into the mountains.

## Virelos, 921 AR

The capture of Virelos gave the rebellion a defensible assembly and coronation city. Veridun remained the administrative refuge; Virelos became the place where resistance accepted an emperor.
