# The Reclamation of Caleran

The Caleran campaign of 950-962 AR recovered the old Atherian capital and opened the central lowlands. It was the sacred midpoint of the Long Reconquest.

## The Descent

Highland armies moved along repaired Atherian roads while coastal and local resistance disrupted occupation supply. Weaponized Holy Flame first appears reliably in this campaign, carried through hymns, sanctified lamps, and bearers whose moral condition made the result impossible to standardize.

## Caleran, 957 AR

The city fell after a combined siege, negotiated surrenders, and revolt within occupied districts. Thalmyric troops found the Eternal Ember still burning under Covenant guard, accompanied by fifty-two years of fuel and custody records.

Veridun paid the invoice in full. The payment acknowledged neither ownership nor gratitude; it acknowledged that the occupation had preserved what it could not extinguish.

Caedros I entered the sacred forum and died at the Pillar of Concordance as the White Flame descended. No body remained. Thalmyria glorified him within a decade; Eclessian relic courts reject the proof.

## Completion, 957-962 AR

Caedros II secured the city, coast road, and immediate river districts. He rebuilt walls, protected surrendered quarters, and made Caleran the Patriarchal and pilgrimage center. The imperial court remained at Veridun because the return war was not over and the refuge institutions could not simply be uprooted.
