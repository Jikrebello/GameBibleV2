# The War of the Burning Banners

The Burning Banners were the wars and settlements by which independent refuge armies accepted Aetherion command before the Caleran campaign.

## The Banner Leagues

Every refuge valley possessed a commander, saint, grievance, and version of Atherian law. Some banners wanted immediate return; others preferred defensible independence. Monasteries controlled grain and legitimacy; noble houses controlled cavalry; city leagues controlled workshops and roads.

## War and Settlement

Caedros combined negotiated precedence, marriage, hostages, limited campaigns, and the promise of return land. Defeated banners were more often incorporated than destroyed because the empire needed their soldiers and local knowledge.

The settlement established common musters, road taxes, campaign courts, and a return registry. Provincial autonomy survived beneath imperial command, creating the structure Thalmyria still uses.

## Moral Memory

Imperial histories call the war unification. Local songs remember forced oaths, seized grain, and rival resistance leaders who were erased after accepting peace. Both memories are true.

## Outcome

By 949 AR, the highland roads and supply depots could support a major descent toward Caleran. The empire had become capable of reconquest and permanently dependent on military administration.
