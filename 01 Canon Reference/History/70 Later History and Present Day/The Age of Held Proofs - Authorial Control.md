# The Age of Held Proofs — Authorial Control

This control preserves the private structural model behind the reader-facing [[The Age of Held Proofs]]. It defines how present-day powers manage decisive evidence without turning that evidence into a predetermined future.

## The Structure

The 1360 board has sometimes been described as a semi-cold war, but cold war is an Earth structure built around deterrence by weapons. This world's present has a structure Earth never had: **deterrence by unopened proof**.

Because this cosmology is testable — sainthood has protocols, kindling has witnesses and contracts have forensics — every major power possesses at least one object or case whose public test would either vindicate it completely or shatter it completely. Because each holder knows the result is not certain, nobody tests. The world's peace is not the absence of war; it is a mutual deterrence of verification.

## The Held Proofs

- **The Unburnt Psalter** (Sanctum vault): test it and the arch-heretic is a saint, or the tests themselves are exposed as riggable. Either result breaks the old Church's spine.
- **The Vessing apparition** (pending, eight years): confirmation inflames Veridun and the Freeflame; condemnation breaks the marches' piety; everyone remembers Bruckmoor.
- **The Saint-Emperor's hymns** (Thalmyria): field-testable any day, with results maddeningly conditional. Both churches have quietly decided this is worse than either clean answer.
- **The two succession instruments** (the Covenant): the Fourth Seal and the Counter-Instrument, each capable of settling the Sealed-Blood schism and each legally impossible to open because examination would itself breach the instrument.
- **The Virelian Lamp** (Veridun): still burning and formally offered home at every Virelian election. Accepting it would prove what the east chose, which is why no Virelian king dares.
- **The Empty City's dead gate** (Qiryath Reqa): if it flickers, the Mesakha question stops being theoretical for every power simultaneously.
- **Wolfsmere's sleep** (the Lakekeepers): the one proof no one wants opened, held shut by an annual price that is rising.
- **The Creditor's three name-fragments:** the assembled true name would be the greatest contract-handle in history. Its assembly is the one event the Concord of Weights, the Vault of Names and the Church would unite to prevent, each for different reasons.

## Why this is the engine

Every faction's strategy in 1360 is legible as **proof-management**: acquiring rivals' proofs, protecting its own, funding permanent-almost wherever a test threatens and building deniable test conditions. Wars are dangerous not only for territory but because battlefields are uncontrolled test environments — a siege can settle a doctrine by accident.

This is what the Restoration Doctrine means politically: the end of days is, among other things, the day everything gets tested. Every institution that would fail its test is therefore, knowingly or not, on the Adversary's side of the schedule.

## Writing rule

When plotting anything set in the present, identify which held proof the story walks toward. Every power in the room should be more afraid of certainty than of the enemy. That fear is the setting's signature.

[[Unknown Histories - Authorial Accounts]] may establish central crimes without making every witness omniscient. The Forward Rule still holds: no control chooses the next emperor, resolves Vessing, opens the distant embassy or rewards the first person who believes the right legend.
