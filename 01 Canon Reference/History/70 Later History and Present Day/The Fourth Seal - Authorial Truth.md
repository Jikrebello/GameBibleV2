# The Fourth Seal - Authorial Truth

Which succession instrument began genuine remains deliberately unresolved.

[[The Creditor]], wearing the mask of Malak al-Ahd, witnessed both instruments and manipulated their preservation, legal framing, and institutional custody so neither could be conclusively privileged. It encouraged the loophole that enabled Bir Qassim and has profited from duplicated allegiance, fear, pilgrimage, rebellion, and metaphysical rent ever since.

The Creditor's interference does not make both traditions equally true, nor prove that every believer or institution knowingly serves it. The original human dispute may have had an innocent claimant, a forger, two partial authorities, or a succession Mujalib himself failed to resolve.

## Secrecy Rule

Public notes may describe contradictory evidence, impossible examination, dreams, accusations, and the spiritual fruits of the schism. They must not identify the Creditor as Malak al-Ahd or state that it engineered the permanent deadlock.
