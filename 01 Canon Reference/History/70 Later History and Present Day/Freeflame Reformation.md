# Freeflame Reformation

The Freeflame Reformation is Eclessia's continuing religious fracture over who may mediate the White Flame, whether sanctity can be administered, and whether the Church's routing assurances ever possessed the authority claimed for them.

## Ansgar's Protest

In 1297 AR, Ansgar of Brenholt published the *Hundred Grievances* against the assurance trade. He argued that prayer, relics, and intercession can genuinely aid souls, but no office can sell a guaranteed route through the Vestibule. Tried at Aldersbruck in 1305, he refused to recant and was burned. His surviving Psalter became a suppressed relic of the movement.

## Wars of Reform

The Ashenmen Risings of 1306-1329 joined religious protest to peasant grievance, guild ambition, noble land seizure, and resistance to Church courts. The wars destroyed Ironvale's ducal house and allowed its guild ministries to become a government.

Doctor Lucian Ferro later systematized the movement as the **Order of the Open Flame**. Its central claim is that the Lord's Flame kindles wherever real sanctity gathers and cannot be monopolized by a hierarchy. Freeflame congregations are governed by tested elders, emphasize scripture and communal hymn-casting, reject the assurance trade, and sharply limit relic devotion.

## Genuine Goods and Real Errors

Freeflame reform exposed fraud, widened literacy, disciplined corrupt clergy, and restored responsibility to local congregations. It also enabled princes to seize Church lands, produced communities whose moral rigor becomes cruelty, and tempted believers to treat every inherited practice as corruption. The old Church contains genuine saints and valid sacraments; the reformers' indictment does not make the institution spiritually empty.

## Counter-Reform

The Council of Veridance abolished assurance sales, codified relic tests, and founded the Order of the Lantern to teach, investigate, and recover contested regions. The reform prevented institutional collapse but did not restore trust.

## The Frozen Settlement

The [[The Korrheim Accord|Korrheim Accord]] ended the open wars in 1343 AR by allowing recognized rulers to determine territorial confession. It excluded unaffiliated radicals and left conscience, mixed families, and border parishes unresolved.

## Present-Day Fault Lines

- Ironvale is the strongest Freeflame polity.
- Veridancia trains both old-Church reformers and some of their most effective opponents.
- Virelia's elective crown could change the confessional balance with one succession.
- Grenzburg is formally old Church but exposed to migrants, pamphlets, and reform-minded laborers.
- The Hollow Flame exploits every abuse while offering severance rather than reform.
