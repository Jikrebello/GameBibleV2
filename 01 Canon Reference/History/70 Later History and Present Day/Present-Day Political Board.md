# Present-Day Political Board

The present day is a fractured inheritance war over Atheria's body, the Redeemer's meaning, relic authority, archives, borderlands, and the future of soul-order.

## Central Conflict

The empire is dead, but its roads, churches, ruins, saints, debts, enemies, and myths still govern the living.

## Major Powers

### Eclessia

Western Christian-coded, fragmented, church-bound, feudal, reform-haunted. Wants unity, legitimacy, and proof that its institutional inheritance is the true public face of the Redeemer's Church.

### Thalmyria

Mystical, militarized, imperial, and ascetic, with authority expressed through icons, holy fire, monastic witness, and continuity with old Atheria. Wants reconquest, security, and proof that its suffering has preserved the true flame.

### Sestaran Concord

Archive-market civilization. Wants leverage, trade, art, secrets, magical schools, banking influence, and profitable ambiguity.

### Ahl-e-Ahd

Contract-state civilization. Wants enforceable order, strategic depth, name custody, bound infrastructure, and legal-metaphysical dominance.

### Jirahar

Exilic prophetic people. Want survival, memory, ritual continuity, protection from persecution, and eventual vindication.

### Fäskogarfolk and Frontier Peoples

Land-and-pact peoples. Want autonomy, sacred territories, old rights, and survival against conversion, settlement, and extraction.

## Recurring Board Questions

- Who owns Atheria's legacy?
- Who speaks for the Redeemer in public history?
- Who controls relics, archives, and proof?
- Who pays for war?
- Which borders are political, and which are metaphysical?
- Can restoration happen without conquest?
