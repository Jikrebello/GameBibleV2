# The Age of Held Proofs

The reframe that de-Earths the present day. The 1360 board has been described as a "semi-cold war" — but cold war is an Earth structure (deterrence by weapons). This world's present has a structure Earth never had: **deterrence by unopened proof.**

## The Structure

Because this cosmology is *testable* — sainthood has protocols, kindling has witnesses, contracts have forensics — every major power has come to possess at least one object or case whose **public test would either vindicate it completely or shatter it completely.** And because each holder knows the odds are not certain, nobody tests. The world's peace is not the absence of war; it is a **mutual deterrence of verification**.

## The Held Proofs (the register)

- **The Unburnt Psalter** (Sanctum vault): test it and the arch-heretic is a saint, or the tests themselves are exposed as riggable. Either result breaks the old Church's spine.
- **The Vessing apparition** (pending, eight years): confirmation inflames Veridun and the Freeflame; condemnation breaks the marches' piety; and everyone remembers Bruckmoor.
- **The Saint-Emperor's hymns** (Thalmyria): field-testable any day, results maddeningly conditional (per the kindling ruling) — which both churches have quietly decided is worse than either clean answer.
- **The two succession instruments** (the Covenant): the Fourth Seal and the Counter-Instrument, each capable of settling the Sealed-Blood schism, each legally impossible to open — breach-by-examination.
- **The Virelian Lamp** (Veridun): still burning; formally offered home at every Virelian election; accepting it would prove what the east chose, which is why no Virelian king dares.
- **The Empty City's dead gate** (Qiryath Reqa): if it flickers, the Mesakha question stops being theoretical for every power simultaneously.
- **Wolfsmere's sleep** (the Lakekeepers): the one proof no one wants opened, held shut by an annual price that is rising.
- **The Creditor's three name-fragments**: the assembled true name would be the greatest contract-handle in history — and the assembly is the one event the Concord of Weights, the Vault of Names, and the Church would unite to prevent, each for different reasons.

## Why This Is the Engine

Every faction's strategy in 1360 is legible as **proof-management**: acquiring rivals' proofs, protecting one's own, funding permanent-almost wherever a test threatens (the Creditor's specialty), and building deniable test-conditions (much of Vorthara's quiet client work). Wars are dangerous not for territory but because **battlefields are uncontrolled test environments** — a siege can settle a doctrine by accident. This is what the Restoration Doctrine means politically: the end of days is, among other things, the day *everything gets tested*, which is why every institution that would fail its test is, knowingly or not, on the Adversary's side of the schedule.

## Writing Rule

When plotting anything set in the present: identify which held proof the story walks toward, and remember that **every power in the room is more afraid of certainty than of the enemy.** That fear is the setting's signature, and no Earth history can supply it — which is the point.

## Atlas narrative

The following account distinguishes established history from the received stories named within it.

## Reform after catastrophe

The Reformation began in 1297 when Ansgar of Brenholt challenged the assurance trade. Its context included real abuses, fiscal need, fear for the dead and a Church which had developed increasingly elaborate claims about access to sacred aid. Doctor Lucian Ferro gave the movement an enduring ecclesiology; Duke Rainald's protection made that thought politically consequential.

Ansgar's burning in 1305 did not end the dispute. The Ashenmen Risings of 1306–1329 exposed grievances which neither doctrine nor princely protection fully controlled. Reform could inspire costly faithfulness and permit new coercion. No side inherited moral legitimacy simply because an older institution had sinned.

## The settlement that settles too little

The Council of Veridance, 1337–1352, abolished assurance sales and codified the five tests. The Order of the Lantern arose within that correction. The Korrheim Accord of 1343 protected particular territorial confessions while excluding unaffiliated radicals and leaving the reunification mandate unresolved.

The resulting world is full of people for whom a settlement is real but insufficient. A family can be protected by its ruler's rite and unwelcome across the next bridge. A genuine spring can heal without settling the claims of every shrine. An object can survive a test while its custodians conceal a crime unrelated to the response.

## Present inheritances

Caedros IV's frontier reputation, campaigns and crown belong to the established recent history. Vasilia's death in 1357 and Prince Nikandros's death in 1358 intensify the current succession problem without deciding it. Marzuq III's Covenant is layered, guard-brokered and challenged by the Qazmarid counter-caliphate. Sestara finances rivals while Castara's sea routes broaden the world its merchants imagine they understand.

The Tarkhan partitions after Qarayel's death in 1303 produce rival successor hordes, not a verdict upon the worth of the preceding society. Castara's annual Ash Road payment began in 1334. Its expeditions make distant exploitation and discovery possible; they do not establish contact with every remote culture already known to the author.

## Small histories now open

The Company with No Charter has rumours and living participants, not a completed destiny. The Nameless Castellan's dispute has evidence and claimants, not a chosen authentic ruler. The two workhouse tags place families before a real question in 1360; no investigation has yet decided the ending.

Likewise, the rebuilt Garden's stories are things people tell, not instructions that players must discover a particular truth. Some customs preserve a genuine event. Some legends combine several people. Some feared relics are false, while useful tools may retain a duty nobody can fully explain. These differences give the world room to exist independently of any one future narrative.

## What can be known

[[Unknown Histories - Authorial Accounts]] records events which no living actor knows completely. The author can know the central crimes without making every witness omniscient. Investigators require independent kinds of evidence; a Saint, archive or copied ancestor cannot narrate the whole past on demand.

The Forward Rule holds at 1360. The historical wiki describes the pressures, peoples, inheritances and available evidence. It does not choose the next emperor, resolve Vessing, open the distant embassy or reward the first person who believes the right legend.
