# Age of Chains

The Age of Chains is the era of Giant dominion. Humanity lives under empires that claim divine authority through size, terror, ancient science, Menhir power, Idol corruption, and celestial inheritance.

## Shape of the Age

The Giant empires are not one nation. They are rival god-kingdoms, each teaching humanity a different form of bondage:

- **Qianglong:** order turned into caste, plague, famine, war, and death.
- **Ishkatar:** river wealth, serpent kings, sacrifice, greed, and monumental debt.
- **Xolotl:** blood astronomy, ritual violence, volcanic power, and the Blood Sun.
- **Nythalum:** moonward technology, body-transcendence, machine-priesthoods, and cosmic escape.

## Human Memory

The Age of Chains leaves humanity with:

- terror of false gods,
- suspicion of giant ruins,
- inherited slave routes,
- sacred hatred of human sacrifice,
- stolen engineering traditions,
- forbidden arts buried under later religious law,
- scattered memories of the Lord beneath layers of Giant myth.

## Cultural Seeds

Many later cultures define themselves by how they remember the Chains:

- the [[The Jirahar Overview|Jirahar]] preserve the liberation memory most directly;
- the [[The Ahl-e-Ahd Overview|Ahl-e-Ahd]] turn bondage and deliverance into legal memory;
- the [[The Atherian Empire Overview|Atherian Empire]] later converts Giant astronomy and monuments into civic glory;
- the [[The Fäskogarfolk Overview|Fäskogarfolk]] remember bondage as the reason no foreign king may own clan land;
- Sestaran harbor peoples inherit the practical lesson that memory, archives, and ledgers are survival weapons.

## Inherited Wound

The world learns that power can impersonate divinity. Every later empire is judged against the Giant shadow, especially when it builds monuments, rewrites memory, or demands obedience in sacred language.
