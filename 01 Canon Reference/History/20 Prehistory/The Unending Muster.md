# The Unending Muster

The Unending Muster was a late Qianglong military-continuity project built where the claimed domains of War and Death overlapped. It attempted to make service itself survive soldiers, officers, dynasties, and defeat.

## Original Purpose

The complex received bodies, armor, campaign records, drill patterns, oath rolls, command seals, and residue from Qianglong armies. Its engines sorted those materials into reusable formations. A dead unit could be reconstructed as posture, equipment habit, route memory, and obedience even though its human souls had already left.

The project did not resurrect soldiers. It made armies out of what service had impressed upon bodies and objects. Qianglong called this continuity. In practice it converted people into replaceable military material and treated discharge, burial, surrender, and death as administrative errors.

## The Command Error

War-Caste architects required every reconstructed formation to answer a valid living command. Death-Caste administrators attempted to remove that limit by creating offices that could recursively certify one another. The result could preserve orders but could not establish a morally or historically legitimate commander.

As Qianglong's dynastic wars consumed the frontier, the Muster began retaining contradictory campaigns. Units marched toward extinct borders, guarded officers rebuilt from incompatible records, and recalled every casualty as a reason to demand another body.

## The Wound Beneath

The repeated refusal to release service opened an Abyssal contradiction beneath the architecture. That wound is [[The General Below]]. It is not the soul of a general, a collective of trapped soldiers, or a Qianglong god. It is eternal conscription given sovereign appetite: the claim that service can never be fulfilled and that death creates no release.

The complex and the dragon are distinct. The Muster can recur without fully materializing the General. The General uses the Muster as hierarchy, body supply, and a path into the Material Realm.

## First Closure

The ancient complex was disabled from within during Qianglong's collapse. Surviving laborers, coerced auxiliaries, defecting keepers, or rival castes broke its command continuity and sealed the deepest galleries. The exact actors and sequence remain to be locked when the dungeon's historical evidence is designed.

The closure depended on three broad conditions:

- no accepted living commander;
- separation of the principal muster relays;
- physical burial of the deepest command architecture.

These conditions explain why isolated residue and engines can wake without immediately releasing the General Below.

## Present Recurrence

In 1360 AR, thaw, damaged frontier works, and an abundance of war dead allow outer commands to repeat beneath Grenzburg. The first recurrence is independent of the Grobi siege, Emerald Drake, Medori Bank, Heart-Stone, Pale-Skins, and Sir Gerhold Brandt.

Gerhold later chooses to accept the vacant command and use the residue army as Grenzburg's permanent garrison. His act supplies the living command relationship the ancient closure denied and accelerates the General Below's materialization.

## Authorial Rulings

- Human souls are not trapped in Muster soldiers.
- Spoken memories and familiar tactics are residue or archived command, not restored persons.
- Destroying bodies alone cannot end a system that continues printing formations.
- No apparent military necessity makes eternal service lawful.
- Gerhold does not create the original wound, but he is responsible for knowingly accepting it.
- The General Below must be stopped in every completed Grenzburg campaign.
