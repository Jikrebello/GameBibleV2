# The Fall of Anshara

The fall of Anshara received no direct prophetic plague cycle. It was not one battle or slave rising, but the collapse of an empire that had made sovereignty biological. Giant rulers granted, withheld, bred, and cursed blood as proof that every life beneath them existed by court permission. When that hierarchy broke, its courts consumed one another literally as well as politically.

## Before the Collapse

Anshara was the Court of Courts: a plateau empire of satrapies, paradise-gardens, processional roads, blood archives, and genuinely Giant ruling houses. Their Giant ancestry predated the mature Ansharan blood system; some houses may have preserved remote Idol-derived Titanic descent, but court claims of immediate and exclusive birth from the Idol of Pride called the Sovereign remain unverified political theology. Later Pride patronage and Giant blood craft produced primordial vampirism as a distinct inherited technology and condition. The Giant rulers then used their own altered blood to extend life, dominate memory, preserve servants in office, and create the first specialized **human** vampire lines. Giant ancestry, Giant vampirism, and the retained human souls of those mortal lines are related histories, not one continuity.

The later Regalis, Nocturnis, Sanguine, Eclipsis, and Militis did not begin as equal nations. They emerged from gifts, punishments, breeding programs, military houses, ritual offices, and rebel lineages whose inherited powers specialized as Giant blood diluted.

## The Drinking Wars

The first fracture came when mortal vampire households ceased accepting that their blood belonged to Giant patrons. Some joined human uprisings. Some sought freedom for their own households while preserving predation over ordinary mortals. Others tried to replace their makers.

Giant satraps answered revolt by drinking rebellious lineages, absorbing memory and strength. Rival courts then attacked weakened neighbors to seize blood archives and cultivated heirs. Mortal vampires fed upon Giant descendants and one another. Paradise-gardens closed their boundaries, trapping households inside perfect estates that starved or repeated themselves.

This period is called the **Drinking Wars** in the Sanguine Memory. No surviving account offers a complete chronology. The phrase **Anshara drank itself** is literal authorial truth: the court system's final resource was the life and blood of the court itself.

## The Last Audience

Grand Chamberlain [[Azravan the Last Cup]] controlled a supreme continuity palace outside the unidentified capital. As the Drinking Wars reached the imperial center, he summoned surviving nobles, blood-priests, guards, officials, and servants to a final audience. He promised to preserve the court until the sovereign returned.

Azravan murdered and drank the assembly. He distilled selected blood-memories into [[The Five Memory Cups]] and sealed the [[Palace of the Last Audience]]. This preserved evidence and technique, not lawful continuity and not the victims' whole souls.

The Last Audience was one atrocity within Anshara's fall, not the cause of the entire collapse. Other courts escaped, folded themselves away, were destroyed by rebels, or consumed their own households independently.

## What Remains Unresolved

- the location and final destruction of the Court of Courts;
- the identity and fate of Anshara's last sovereign;
- whether any supreme Giant ruler escaped the empire;
- which modern bloodline preserves the least altered account;
- how much later human history was deliberately purged by vampires and how much simply vanished during the Scattering.

No discovery at the Palace of the Last Audience settles these questions. Azravan's claim that he speaks as the sovereign is false.

## Historical Consequences

- surviving vampire lineages scattered among post-Exodus peoples and later courts;
- Ansharan ruins became unusually dangerous because hierarchy, blood, and architecture remained connected;
- mortal histories omitted the fifth Giant empire while vampire traditions preserved self-serving fragments;
- modern vampires inherited real memories of oppression alongside the Pride-shaped desire to inherit the oppressor's rank;
- the Redeemer's later restoration made Pride's blood-lien answerable in a way Ansharan rulers had believed impossible.
