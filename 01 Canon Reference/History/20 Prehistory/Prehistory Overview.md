# Prehistory Overview

## The Five Imperial Horizons

Qianglong, Ishkatar, Xolotl, Nythalum, and Anshara are the five dominant **Imperial Horizons** through which later history organizes the Giant Age. They were not the only Giant states, did not all reach their height simultaneously, and did not maintain fixed borders throughout their existence. Titan-Firstborn domains, High Giant houses, cadet kingdoms, satrapies, marriage states, client crowns, temple estates, city leagues, and human subject polities rose between and within them. Marriage, conquest, disputed inheritance, and changing patronage could move a kingdom between horizons without replacing its people or local history.

The five therefore name enduring imperial traditions and archaeological worlds rather than five exhaustive, uninterrupted countries. Their individual dossiers control their distinct collapses.

### **1. The Celestial Empire of Qianglong**
- **Geography:** A sprawling empire located in lush valleys surrounded by towering mountains and serene rivers. The landscape was marked by grand cities, terraced farmlands, and sacred peaks that were home to the empire's rulers.
- **Ruling Giants:** The empire was divided into four castes of Giants, each aligned with one of the Seasonal Menhir: Solrath (War), Nevaura (Famine), Morvalis (bodily Death), and Veradia (Pestilence and proliferating life). Some early houses may genuinely have descended from Menhir-associated Titan-Firstborn; later marriage, alteration, selective breeding, and office-law formed the imperial castes, whose ruling position changed through grand civil wars.
- **Culture and Religion:** Qianglong theology worshipped the Horsemen as continuously present divine parents and treated natural correspondence as political legitimacy. Authorially, genuine remote ancestry did not mean that the Menhir authorized caste rule or judged between the castes. Their amoral domains answered compatible rites while Giant institutions supplied the coercion and claim of cosmic balance.
- **Technology and Achievements:** The Qianglong were master engineers, responsible for constructing vast canals, irrigation systems, and towering fortresses. Their grand cities were centers of culture, philosophy, and martial prowess. The Giants were also skilled in necromancy, medicine, and agriculture, using their knowledge to maintain their power.
- **Continuity Project:** Qianglong attempted necromantic caste recurrence. Bodies, titles, and trained residue were cycled through sealed vaults so the ruling order could return without possessing human souls or accepting human mortality.
- **Unending Muster:** A late War-and-Death project attempted to make military service survive every soldier and commander. Its command architecture became the historical basis of [[The Unending Muster]] and the Abyssal wound called [[The General Below]].
- **Decline:** The empire fell into decline due to internal strife and a final catastrophic civil war known as the War of the Four Horsemen. The ruling Giants retreated into hidden underground vaults, leaving the lesser castes to tear each other apart. The once-great cities of Qianglong were abandoned, their secrets lost to time.

### **2. The Serpent Kingdom of Ishkatar**
- **Geography:** Located along the fertile banks of the Serpent’s Spine, a great river that wound through a vast desert. The kingdom’s cities were built along the river, with towering pyramids and monumental statues rising above the landscape.
- **Ruling Giants:** The Naga Giants were serpentine rulers with humanoid upper bodies and massive coiling lower bodies. Their oldest houses possessed genuine Titanic ancestry, including remote Avarix-associated lines. Court theology exaggerated this into the claim that every Pharaoh-Serpent was Avarix's direct and undiluted child; later Idol patronage and bodily alteration further shaped the dynasties.
- **Culture and Religion:** Ishkatar was a deeply religious society centered around the worship of the Naga Giants. Human sacrifices and elaborate rituals were performed to appease these deities and ensure the continuation of the kingdom. The Naga Giants were seen as the intermediaries between the mortal world and the divine, with their temples and pyramids serving as both religious and political centers.
- **Technology and Achievements:** The Naga Giants were master architects and engineers, responsible for the construction of massive pyramids, obelisks, and advanced irrigation systems that controlled the Serpent’s Spine. The kingdom was known for its vast wealth, with treasures of gold, jewels, and artifacts accumulated over centuries.
- **Continuity Project:** Ishkatar attempted persistence through title and property. Estates, offices, guardians, and ownership claims were designed to outlive every body and reconstruct the ruler from what remained theirs.
- **Decline:** The decline of Ishkatar was triggered by the Great Exodus and subsequent slave rebellions. The Naga Giants, consumed by greed and paranoia, turned on each other, leading to civil war. The final blow came when one of the Giants unleashed a destructive ritual that dried up the Serpent’s Spine, turning the fertile land into a barren wasteland. The civilization was reduced to ruins, its wealth buried beneath the sands.

### **3. The Blood Empire of Xolotl**
- **Geography:** Situated in a dense jungle region surrounded by towering mountains and vast swamps. The empire’s cities were built atop massive ziggurats that rose above the jungle canopy, connected by a network of stone causeways and canals.
- **Ruling Giants:** The Obsidian Giants had skin like volcanic glass and eyes burning with crimson light. Their dynasties carried ancient Idol-derived Giant ancestry, while later Wrath patronage, sacrifice, volcanic rites, selective breeding, and blood craft produced the more uniform imperial caste. Their claim that every ruler was Furor's immediate child collapsed those distinct stages into one sacred genealogy.
- **Culture and Religion:** Xolotl was a civilization steeped in blood and ritual sacrifice. The Obsidian Giants were worshipped as gods of war and death, and the empire’s religion centered around the appeasement of these deities through the offering of human hearts and blood. The people of Xolotl believed that the world was sustained by the blood of the sacrificed, and that the Obsidian Giants were the protectors of the world.
- **Technology and Achievements:** The Obsidian Giants were known for their advanced knowledge of astronomy, mathematics, and medicine. They wielded volcanic energy to create powerful weapons and tools. The empire’s most feared achievement was the creation of the Blood Sun, an artificial sun powered by blood sacrifices that hung in the sky above Xolotlan.
- **Continuity Project:** Xolotl attempted sacrificial blood continuity. The Blood Sun and its rites stored lineage-memory, authority, and vitality by consuming human life, but could not create or preserve the Image.
- **Decline:** The decline of Xolotl was precipitated by the Great Exodus and a series of slave rebellions. As the Obsidian Giants turned on each other in a bid for power, the empire began to crumble. The final catastrophe occurred when the Blood Sun exploded in a massive eruption of volcanic energy, obliterating the capital city and triggering volcanic eruptions across the empire. The once-great Blood Empire was reduced to a fiery wasteland, its glory consumed by its own insatiable thirst for blood.

### **4. The Forgotten Ones of Nythalum**
- **Geography:** A once-great island civilization located far to the west, surrounded by turbulent seas and dense fog. The land was rich and fertile, with sprawling cities built on cliffs and shores that eventually fell into the sea.
- **Ruling Giants:** Nythalum's rulers belonged to ancient Giant lineages that predated the mature lunar cult. Their later claims of immediate descent from Mother Moon, Lyraxis, or both were false or imprecise compressions of older ancestry, patronage, captured court-law, and engineered celestial symbolism.
- **Culture and Religion:** The Nythalians venerated the moon and stars, believing that their ultimate destiny lay in the heavens. They developed advanced technologies, including spires that served as physical vessels aimed at the physical moon. Their society was a blend of mysticism and science, but its Ascension Project was neither Egg White flight nor passage into another realm.
- **Technology and Achievements:** Nythalum was the most technologically advanced of the giant civilizations. They built towering spires that could launch into space, complex machines powered by the minds of their rulers, and a civilization that blended magic and technology. The most ambitious project was their attempt to settle on the moon, which ultimately led to their downfall.
- **Continuity Project:** Nythalum attempted mechanical persistence. A genuinely uninterrupted transfer can preserve an existing `giant-continuity`; a Reconstruction assembled after interruption is only a `constructed-pattern`. Neither process creates a human soul.
- **Decline:** The downfall of Nythalum came when the physical lunar launch ended in disaster. Most spires exploded or fell, killing the majority of the population and sinking the island. A few genuine Giant persons reached the physical Moon through uninterrupted bodies or Continuant machinery; many more surviving systems are Reconstructions or non-conscious machines that merely claim ancestral identity. Later returns must distinguish those categories rather than calling every machine the ruler it remembers.

### **5. The Imperial Court of Anshara**
- **Geography:** A plateau empire of satrap-courts, paradise-gardens, and processional roads — the Court of Courts, where the other empires' envoys knelt.
- **Ruling Giants:** Anshara's ruling houses possessed genuine Giant ancestry before the mature court blood system arose. Some may have preserved remote Idol-derived Titanic descent, but claims of immediate and exclusive birth from the **Idol of Pride ("The Sovereign")** were political theology. Later Pride patronage and Giant blood craft produced primordial Giant vampirism and were then used to create the distinct human vampire lines (see [[Vampire Origins]]).
- **Culture and Religion:** Rule as liturgy: rank, radiance, tribute, and the gift-and-curse of the Giants' blood, by which favored mortals were made the first vampires and enemies were cursed through their lineages.
- **Continuity Project:** Anshara attempted persistence through blood-memory and primordial vampirism. It could retain human souls within vampire bodies, transmit biological and mnemonic patterns, and prolong some embodied Giant continuities. A copied memory or inherited office was not thereby the Giant person, and none of these systems could create the Image.
- **Decline:** Anshara received no direct prophetic plague cycle — **it drank itself**. The other four horizons broke through the wider providential convergence controlled by [[The Great Unbinding]], while Anshara's bloodlines turned on the Court, Giant courts consumed rivals and households, and paradise-gardens closed over the wreckage. [[The Fall of Anshara]] controls the authorial account; the final sovereign and capital remain unresolved.

### Summary

These five Imperial Horizons—**Qianglong, Ishkatar, Xolotl, Nythalum,** and half-remembered **Anshara**—each played a pivotal role in the early history of the world without exhausting the Giant Age's kingdoms and peoples. They were defined by their unique cultures, religious practices, technological achievements, and ultimate downfalls, leaving behind legacies of grandeur, horror, and mystery that continue to echo through the ages. Their ruins and those of the smaller states around them now serve as haunted reminders of the power and ambition that once shaped the world, and their stories are woven into the myths and legends of the southern hemisphere.
