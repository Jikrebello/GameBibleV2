# World Egg Setting Primer

## 1) One-page “How the world works” cheat sheet

### The Axis (objective cosmology)

- **Rightward terminal:** Heaven, culminating in **the Lord** (all-good, self-sustaining coherence). The **Redeemer** stands at the Lord’s right hand.
    
- **Leftward terminal:** Hell, and beyond it the **Abyss** (terminal severance/refusal). The **Adversary** is exiled at the far-left pole.
    
- **Key idea:** These are not “peer gods.” They are **terminal poles**: coherence vs refusal.
    

### The World Egg (where stories happen)

Think of reality as a layered egg:

**[Shell / Membrane Realms]**

- The boundary of created reality; defines what can enter and how.
    
- The left hand side top quadrant is the elemental plane of water, the left bottom side is the elemental plane of earth.
    
- The right top side is the elemental plane of air, the bottom right is the elemental plane of fire.
    

**[Yolks = reality-modes]**

- **Faewild (right yolk):** mythic-nature laws; **Menhir** are sovereign regime-principles.
    
- **Shadowlands (left yolk):** predatory meaning architectures; **Idols** are sovereign engines.
    
- **Material (center outcome):** the mortal world; stable overlap projection.
    

**[Astral Plane = drift medium]**

- High-entropy interstitial space in between the shell/membranes and the 3 in 1 yolks of "grounded" reality. It is mostly empty entropy, but also holds host to demiplanes, patron micro-kingdoms, dead-god debris, and instability markets.
    

### The three governing “physics” laws of the Astral Plane

1. **Drift:** Unanchored structures dissolve toward low order in Astral Plane.
    
2. **Metaphysical mass:** “Weight creates place.” Relics, god-debris, and true-name hoards pin stability.
    
3. **Entropic rent:** “Stability is leased.” Order accrues upkeep debt; if the rent isn’t paid, stability fails (repossession by drift).
    

### Thin spaces (your main weirdness generator)

- Thin spaces happen when yolk overlap misaligns or Astral plane leaks.
    
- Result: myths can become locally literal; rules shift by location.
    

### Manifestation rule (why rituals matter)

To “really” enter the world, beings generally need:

1. (If coming from outside physical reality) passing through one of the elemental planes of the shell.
    
2. an Astral Plane body/anchor,
    
3. a yolk overlay (Faewild or Shadowlands shaping),
    
4. Material persistence.  
    This makes major supernatural events expensive, localized, and contested.

## 2) One-page “Who matters” cheat sheet

### Power categories (defined by function)

- **The Lord:** terminal coherence source (not a peer within the Egg).
    
- **White Flame:** the Lord’s coherence operating in created reality; cancels corruption and can “sterilize” discord, sewing up thin spaces and centering each plane towards their original cosmological orientation.
    
- **The Redeemer:** reorders judgement and soul-path around truth + consent.
    
- **Menhir (Faewild):** sovereign “laws of nature” made godlike; cult is secondary to regime.
    
- **Idols (Shadowlands):** predatory meaning engines feeding on identity, obsession, shame, envy, appetite, pressure.
    
- **Patrons (Astral Plane):** anchor-owners who lease stability in the Astral plane via pacts; profit from rent and intermediation and grant power via entropy to agents in the material world.
    
- **Infrastructure beings:** entities required by the system (e.g., the Boatman for the material plane, elementals within the shell's planes).
    

### Death and the routing war

- Souls pass through the **Vestibule of Weighing **a sub plane of the material where mortal souls are judged/ justified (truth-of-life + consent).
    
- The **Boatman** routes souls rightwards (Lumen threshold) or leftwards (Chthonic breach → Hell/Abyss risk).
    
- Strategic point: factions fight over **soul routing**, not just territory.
    

### The Restoration Doctrine (why everyone is threatened)

- End-state doctrine: yolks align, thin spaces vanish, intermediaries lose power, Hell closes, Adversary defeated.
    
- Threatened parties: Idols, drift patrons, leftward infrastructures, anyone monetizing misalignment.

## 3) Institutions as “truth engines” (how the setting stays arguable)

### Knowledge tiers (in-world epistemics)

- **Public doctrine:** what common people are taught.
    
- **Scholar mythography:** comparative, contradictory, catalogued.
    
- **Occult doctrine:** actionable, dangerous.
    
- **Propaganda:** legitimacy laundering.
    
- **Field reports:** partial ground truth.
    
- **Heresy:** internally coherent counter-cosmology (often partly correct).
    

Rule: disputes persist because truth has value; institutions manufacture it.

### Your contradiction engines (why there’s no single answer)

- **Thin-space locality:** some places make myths literally true.
    
- **Entropic rent:** power always has an accounting tail.
    
- **Role-warping via contracts:** identities change under binding (records conflict because reality changes).
    
- **Archive warfare:** control the “source of truth” to control legitimacy.

## 4) Present-era geopolitical board (campaign stage)

**Time:** ~80 years after the great split between Eclessia and Thalmyria.  
**Main collision zone (“Tamriel board”):** Eclessia, Thalmyria, Sestaran Concord and the Ahl-e-Ahd.  
**Weirdness dial:** ~10–15% stranger than standard Elder Scrolls.

### Eclessia (fragmented sacred politics)

- Many duchies/kingdoms comprised of older ethnic groups of Atherians and Fäskogarfolk and newer ethnic groups comprised of their mixture into a broadly Eclessian ethnic and cultural group; Church is the cultural spine.
    
- Situated geographically east of Thalmyria, largely underdeveloped/ rural outside of main population centers/ natural resource points.
    
- Church declares reunification as a divine mandate.
    

### Thalmyria (militarized heir-state)

- Claims continuity; reconquest framed as sacred duty.
    
- Situated centrally, with Eclessia and Sestara to the east, and  the Ahl-e-Ahd to the north/ north west.
    
- Known for “weaponized Holy Flame” usage to combat the Ahl-e-Ahd binding magick.
    

### Sestaran Concord (amplifier and marketplace)

- Competitive city-states; trade, finance, archives, philosophy.
    
- The best place for secrets to circulate and be weaponized.
    

### Ahl-e-Ahd (contract-state)

- Cosmology: reality governed through contracts; “law-as-cosmos.”
    
- Behind the curtain: their “angel” is an Astral Plane patron masquerading as divine messenger of the Lord.
    
- Uses devils (enslaved Astral plane natives) as bound assets for civil engineering and war.
    

### Jirahar (diaspora prophecy machine)

- Legalistic/ritualistic monotheists; await Al-Muntazar.
    
- Corruption model: some leadership capture is real; emergent worship exists; false patronage occurs; paranoia is politically useful.

## 5) Two major systems added (what changed in the world bible)

### A) Ahl-e-Ahd contract enforcement pipeline (bureaucratic horror apparatus)

Core idea: devils are not a species; they are “job mantles” imposed by binding.

Key components:

- **Drafting authority:** Quill-Line + licensed Contract Masters (civil vs military).
    
- **Seal logic:** precision text + ontological collateral + rent routing.
    
- **Vault of Names:** compartmentalized true-name custody (glyph/tongue/debt keepers).
    
- **Audit & breach courts:** loophole mastery becomes doctrine; breach-hunting is power.
    
- **Enforcement arm:** compliance operators (janissary analogue) who execute bindings in the field.
    

### B) Church proof-of-sainthood protocol (saints as hymn-authors)

Core idea: clergy “cast” by reciting saint-authored hymns/psalms; saints are authorization sources.

Five tests:

1. **White Flame resonance** (stabilization / thin-space sealing).
    
2. **Hymn-mint test** (prayer works reliably when channeled by ordinary clergy).
    
3. **Idol-resistance** (coherence under predatory meaning pressure).
    
4. **Relic anchor test** (portable stabilization posthumously).
    
5. **Anti-contract immunity** (resistance to being “re-typed” by contract/name tricks).
    

Outcome: sainthood becomes contestable, forgeable, politically explosive, and operationally meaningful.

## 6) Behind-the-curtain truths currently set

- The Lord remains all-good, powerful, self-sustaining; Redeemer is the Son; White Flame is sent into the world.
    
- Ahl-e-Ahd “angel” is an Astral Plane patron using contract-tech and rent extraction.
    
- Devils = any significant powers bound by true names; binding imposes a mantle that reshapes identity.
    
- Jirahar contain real corruption and also weaponized paranoia; “not 100% provable” by design.

## 7) Fast “at-the-table” diagnostics

When you’re unsure what a weird event means, ask:

1. Which layer is leaking (Faewild / Shadowlands / Astral Plane)?
    
2. What is the rent stream (who is paying upkeep, and how)?
    
3. Is a true-name involved (custody, leakage, forgery)?
    
4. Which institution benefits if a certain interpretation is believed?
    
5. What anomaly persists (relic behavior, thin-space symptom, contradictory record)?
    

If you can answer those five, the setting stays coherent even when truth stays arguable.
