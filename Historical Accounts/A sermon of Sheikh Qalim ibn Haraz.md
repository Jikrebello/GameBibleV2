## **“The Broken Contract and the Sword of the Faithful”**

_A sermon of Sheikh Qalim ibn Haraz, Voice of the Contract and Orator of the Covenant Court_  
Delivered in the Great Hall of Flame, Al-Qahur, on the Eve of the Jihad

> _“To all who breathe beneath the Covenant, hear this: We go not to conquer—we go to correct.”_

---

**Praise be to the One Who Binds.**  
To He who formed the First Word, carved the clauses of the cosmos into the sky, and gave to us—His stewards—the sacred right to seal what has been made.

We gather now in the heart of His miracle—the Oasis of Flames. Not born of rainfall or rivers, but summoned by pact. **Four thousand four hundred and forty-four devils**, made to bow, to dig, to sweat and cry until water flowed where none dared dream. That is the proof of covenant. That is the power of law.

And yet—**look west**.

There lies Atheria. Once mighty. Once iron-clad in their laws and cities. But now? What do they worship?

Not the One Who Binds. Not the Word. Not the Law.  
But **a man**—born of sand and sin. A man they say ascended. A man who dared to offer _love_ where there should be **terms**, who taught _sacrifice_ where there should be **service**, who gave _flame_ without **conditions**.

They have **broken the Great Contract**.

---

**Oaths were made. They were not kept.**

- The Jirahar, our wayward cousins, were hunted and cast out for refusing to bow to flame-idolatry.
- The Tower-Towns were destroyed, their contract-vaults violated.
- Pilgrims were slaughtered. Scholars arrested. Traders bound in chains.

And Atheria **did nothing**.  
Because it was not an empire of law. It was an empire of arrogance.

Well now, it is our turn to write the next clause.

---

**The Caliph and the Supreme Arbiter have signed the Grand Scroll of Redress.**  
This is not war born of greed or vengeance. It is the fulfillment of the final clause of the Covenant:

> _“When the world forgets the Law, let the swords of the faithful inscribe it anew.”_

This jihad—**Al-Jihad al-Mu‘ahad**—is not conquest. It is a legal execution. The clause has been breached. The penalty is binding.

**The siege golems have been awakened.**  
**The Ahdanite Guard stands ready.**  
**The contracts have been written in ash and sealed in fire.**

---

Do not weep for the cities that will fall.  
They have built temples atop perjury.  
Do not pity the priests of flame.  
They have lit the altars of lies.  
Do not fear the Redeemer.  
He is not a god. He is an _unsigned page._

---

**But remember this, O faithful:**

We are not barbarians. We do not raze needlessly. We **offer terms**. The law is always offered.

- Surrender, and your cities may stand.
- Accept the Covenant, and your names shall be preserved in ink.
- Breach again, and your name shall be struck from every scroll.

We do not kill for sport. We bind for service.  
Even devils may serve in the contract.  
And so may kings.

---

So go, sons and daughters of the Ahd.

Go forth in fire and form, in ink and blade.  
Let the West learn the language of righteousness once more.  
Let the Flame be **contained**, and the Law **fulfilled**.  
Let every signature be sealed in glory.

**This is not war.**  
**This is correction.**  
**This is the Redemption of the Word.**

---

> _“By the Angel of the Covenant, I place this jihad beneath clause and command.  
> Let no oath be broken in the field.  
> Let no soul be struck down unless offered contract.  
> And let every sword be not drawn in wrath, but in remembrance.”_
