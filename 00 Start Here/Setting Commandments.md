# Setting Commandments

These are the load-bearing rules for keeping the World Egg setting coherent across lore, fiction, and game design.

## 1. The Material Realm Comes First

The Material Realm is the ordinary base plane where most human life happens. It should feel recognizably human: weather, hunger, birth, labor, class, law, war, disease, trade, agriculture, family, faith, memory, and death all matter before the stranger planes intrude.

The other layers are not a replacement for ordinary reality. They pressure it, rupture it, overlay it, tempt it, and sometimes weaponize it.

## 2. The Cosmology Is Objectively Real

The Lord, the Redeemer, White Flame, soul-routing, corruption, Idols, the Adversary, Hell, the Abyss, and Restoration are not merely subjective cultural interpretations. People may misunderstand them, politicize them, forge signs, or use partial truths, but the metaphysical stakes are real.

## 3. Mystery Belongs In-World, Not in the Author's Spine

Characters, churches, archives, cults, banks, scholars, and nations may disagree. The authorial bible should know what is true, what is false, and what is deliberately unresolved.

## 4. Moral Contrast Must Remain Sharp

The world can contain complex people inside compromised institutions without dissolving into moral sludge. Mercy, sanctity, predation, enslavement, corruption, falsehood, repentance, and refusal should remain morally meaningful.

## 5. Institutions Are Truth Engines

Churches, banks, courts, archives, contract offices, relic cults, noble houses, war colleges, and oral traditions do not merely record truth. They manufacture public reality, enforce memory, and decide what counts as legitimate.

## 6. Magic Creates Society

Every major magic system should create institutions, laws, taboos, professions, military doctrines, markets, heresies, and abuses. Magic is never just a visual effect.

## 7. Supernatural Power Has a Cost Structure

White Flame demands sanctity and coherence. Contracts demand names, consent, loopholes, or coercion. Warlock scripts demand training, instruments, and elemental risk. Patron pacts demand rent. Menhir pacts demand obedience to living law. Idol power demands psychic food.

## 8. Cultures Need Both Thesis and Texture

Each culture should have a clear answer to reality, but also ordinary life: food, songs, jokes, mourning, marriage, class resentment, hypocrisy, saints, villains, reformers, cowards, and sincere believers.

## 9. History Must Leave Wounds

Every present conflict should trace back to a real inherited wound: conquest, exile, schism, betrayal, persecution, debt, lost land, relic theft, archive warfare, or failed restoration.

## 10. Grenzburg Is the Test

If the setting works, Grenzburg should naturally produce quests from its existing pressures: winter, migration, debt, relics, Church legitimacy, Folk land claims, war magic, and buried metaphysical wounds.
