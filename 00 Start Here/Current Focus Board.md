# Current Focus Board

## First Creative Priority

Make Grenzburg the first practical vertical slice: a playable frontier duchy with factions, characters, seasonal pressure, quests, and enough world context to prove the IP works as a game setting.

## Next Three Workstreams

1. **Navigation:** keep MOCs, Properties, and Bases current as notes are added.
2. **Canon cleanup:** resolve notes marked `needs-review`, especially archived versions and old phase material.
3. **Game usability:** turn lore into places, conflicts, factions, NPCs, mechanics, and quest hooks.

## Open Questions to Resolve Later

- Exact present-day year and dating system.
- Final map boundaries for Eclessia, Thalmyria, Sestara, Ahl-e-Ahd, Jirahar, and frontier peoples.
- Which in-world historical accounts are true, biased, forged, or partially correct.
