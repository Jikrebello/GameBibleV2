## 🔥 The Twelve Companions of the Redeemer

> _"They were called not because they were worthy, but because they were willing."_  
> —_Commentaries of Jareth, Book I_

---

### 1. **Saint Jareth of the Bound Eye**

- Formerly a blind imperial scribe and forger.
- Gained partial sight from Isfir. Chronicled his teachings.
- Burned alive while copying the Gospel of Fire.

---

### 2. **Saint Calira of the Broken Blade**

- Former legionnaire and noble exile.
- Healed by Isfir; became protector of pilgrims.
- Died in battle defending the first shrine in Veridun Minor.

---

### 3. **Saint Thalos the Laughing Monk**

- Wandering satirist who mocked temples and stars.
- “Turned holy by laughter.”
- Crucified upside down for refusing to denounce the Redeemer.

---

### 4. **Saint Myra of the Hollow Womb**

- Noblewoman marked as cursed after many miscarriages.
- Claimed the Holy Flame filled her in Isfir’s presence.
- Killed during a riot against Redeemer-worshippers in Aurelia.

---

### 5. **Saint Erom the Flamebound**

- Former murderer seeking penance.
- Confessed publicly and became a healer of prisoners.
- Died saving orphans from a fire set by imperial loyalists.

---

### 6. **Saint Naris the Waterspoken**

- A dockworker and recovering drunk in the city of Halmenth.
- Isfir calmed a seizure he had during a sermon, and afterward, Naris spoke only in prayer and poetry.
- Baptized hundreds in secret across the coast.
- Drowned after refusing to stop a public ritual at the River Celion.

---

### 7. **Saint Virella the Twin-Veiled**

- A traveling herbalist accused of being a witch.
- Believed to be possessed by two spirits—one light, one shadow.
- Isfir’s touch “split the veil,” and she became a seer and healer.
- Patron saint of mystics and those with mental illness.
- Allegedly died by slow poisoning from an imperial inquisitor.

---

### 8. **Saint Dorn of the Stone Road**

- An ex-gladiator turned caravan guard.
- Protected Isfir during multiple attacks on the road to Caleran.
- Built the first secret shrines along the **Via Celestis**.
- Beheaded in public; his blood stained the road, where wildflowers now grow.

---

### 9. **Saint Elsin of the Wordless Flame**

- A mute shepherd from the Fäskogar borderlands.
- Claimed the Redeemer gave him the gift of “fire speech”—unspoken messages that could calm beasts and inspire crowds.
- Said to have led a crowd of prisoners to freedom by walking past guards unnoticed.
- Died in silence, smiling, surrounded by converts.

---

### 10. **Saint Menos the Chain-Breaker**

- A slave chained to a golem-forge in Ironvale.
- Freed by Isfir and carried his message back to the mines.
- Led the first worker uprisings in the Empire tied to the Redeemer’s message.
- Executed by being bound in molten chains—now symbol of martyrdom for laborers.

---

### 11. **Saint Solana of the Last Ember**

- A baker’s daughter who witnessed the Binding at Caleran.
- Took a single ember from the altar and preserved it in a sealed jar.
- That flame supposedly kindled the first Holy Flame ever seen after the Redeemer’s death.
- Died in her sleep surrounded by pilgrims; her body did not decay for seven days.

---

### 12. **Saint Tyros the Doubter**

- A former astrologer-priest who first sought to disprove Isfir’s teachings.
- Confronted the Redeemer in debate—and wept at his feet by the end.
- His conversion inspired several priestly orders to defect to the new faith.
- Last of the Twelve to die, beheaded in Caleran during the Great Purge.
