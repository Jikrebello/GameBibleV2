# Humane Terms

## Role in the Arc

This is Zafir ibn Samad's quest 2 of four. It is playable in Open Autumn and Early Winter. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Zafir ibn Samad duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A Grenzburg workshop uses a copied Iqashari restraint to prevent indebted laborers leaving during winter preparation. The owner insists its hours, meals, and appeal clause make it humane.

Free coerced workers and force Zafir to judge his own former argument when spoken by someone else.

## Player Actions

Interview workers beyond the owner's hearing, locate the cord anchor in the underways, obtain a civil injunction or physically sever control, and protect the workshop's essential production through voluntary staffing.

## Opposition and Pressure

The owner threatens closure and lost wages; guards enforce a facially orderly instrument; some workers fear freedom without winter shelter.

## Information Paths

Worker testimony, copied clauses bearing Zafir's annotations, and the anchor's command log prove coercion.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Void the restraint while funding transition, impose temporary court custody, or preserve it through winter. Zafir's language and actions, not merely player choice, set his trajectory.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the injunction fails, physical severance remains possible but creates criminal exposure. If the shop closes, a cooperative can continue reduced production.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.
