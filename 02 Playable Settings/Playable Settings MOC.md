# Playable Settings

The playable library turns the wider World Egg into places where characters can travel, choose allegiances and change local outcomes. It currently contains two developed settings: Grenzburg, a frontier region built for an open-world role-playing game, and Caleran, a great-city sandbox. A separate quest library holds independent adventures and conversions that may be placed where their histories and geography permit.

Campaign material establishes starting conditions, inhabitants and pressures. Player choices and events after the opening situation remain outside the historical canon unless separately recorded.

## Grenzburg

Grenzburg lies on Eclessia's southern frontier where ducal government, Folk customary country, debt, winter migration and Grobi warfare meet. Its base-game world contains a twin-walled city and seven exterior regions extending from settled lowlands into Frostfenn. The main campaign follows one region from autumn through winter and into a postwar summer.

The setting includes a player-authored protagonist, eight companions with independent campaigns, five major faction lines and a twenty-one-chapter critical path. Its articles separate the physical world, campaign state and production controls so that the region can remain coherent across different choices.

## Caleran

Caleran is the recovered old capital of Atheria and the principal sacred city of Thalmyria. Its living metropolis surrounds the Eternal Ember, imperial institutions, pilgrimage districts, river trade and ruins belonging to several earlier cities. The setting supports campaigns beginning with street and district concerns and descending into buried waterworks, ossuary roads, Glass courts and the remains of the Deep Hill City.

## Independent adventures

The quest library contains adventures whose opening situations can exist within the wider setting without fixing their endings. Completed adventure packages develop their locations, casts, dangers and possible consequences. Conversion notes preserve the structural lessons taken from published modules while changing their worlds, characters and plots.
