# Lenka Vrana

## Core Concept

Lenka Vrana is a Kedravan laundress in Lantern Ward, thirty-four, and the only person in Grenzburg whom Bernard Valeon's caches actually cost.

Her refusal is precise: **she will not take food from his hand.**

## Josef

Her father, **Josef Vrana**, was on the second sled.

He is one of the five permanently injured at [[The Froststep Evacuation]] — nerve pain in both hands, a chest that fails every winter, and forty years of carrying work ended in one night above the lower switchback. He stayed in Kedrava, where the compensation reaches him and where the roads are ones he knows. Lenka and her sister came west for wages three years ago and send money home.

She has never met Bernard. She knew his name before she knew his face, because for two years it was the name in her father's house.

## The Refusal

Bernard's care language is food. Lenka's refusal is fluent in it, and that is the whole design: the one weapon she has is the exact shape of his kindness.

She accepts everything else. She uses the shelters. She takes the network's help. She lets the Yard's porters carry her baskets and she is civil to Brina and fond of Hannel. She simply never takes a bowl, a portion, a cup, or a loaf from Bernard, and never eats anything he has plated.

In practice it looks like nothing. She waits for another server. She goes without. She brings her own. Once, at a Long Table supper, she takes soup — from the ladle, held by somebody else, six feet from where Bernard is standing — and eats it looking at nothing in particular.

## Behaviour Rules — Fixed

`lenka_state` does not move. Not through direction, preparation, approval, restitution, White Flame, or any outcome.

- Never a quest; never gates content; never appears in a preparation table.
- Never eloquent about it. She has no speech and no thesis.
- Never performs anger. She is not owed a scene of confrontation and does not want one.
- If Bernard tries charm, it fails without ceremony — she answers the question he actually asked and moves on.
- No player option unlocks her. No dialogue tree persuades her. There is no version of this campaign in which she forgives him, and none in which she denounces him.

## The One Exchange

Act II or Act III window, at the laundry or a shelter line. A few lines, once, never reprised. If Bernard asks directly — and the player can prompt him to, or not:

> **Lenka:** My father was on the second sled.
>
> *(She goes back to the basket. Bernard does not leave.)*
>
> **Lenka:** You don't have to go. I'm not going to eat from your hand.

That is all of it. She does not say *ever*, because she does not need to, and she does not say what her father's winters are like, because he is not hers to hand over.

## Appearances

- **[[The Long Winter Table]]** — the non-eating beat at one of the four shelters. Unremarked. A player who never notices has lost nothing yet.
- **The exchange** — as above.
- **[[Beds Before Banners]] / [[The Open Hospice]]** — present among the Lantern Ward arrivals; accepts a bed, a blanket, and a carer, and not his hands.
- **[[Grace]]** — at the open table, **eating**, served by somebody else. That is as far as the world ever moves her, and it is far enough to notice.

## Resolution

In the resolved ending, Josef's compensation continues and the restitution schedule reaches him through the record like any other claimant's. Nothing about that is Bernard's to be thanked for, and nobody thanks him.

Lenka nods at the tally board on her way past. That is the entire transaction.
