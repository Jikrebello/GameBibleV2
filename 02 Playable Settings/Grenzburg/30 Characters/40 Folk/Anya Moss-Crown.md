# Anya Moss-Crown

## Core Concept

A senior Moss-Crown hearth speaker whose authority rests in one kindred and its accepted dependents.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Anya will make costly practical agreements but refuses permanent surrender of sanctuary. She regards Runa as a trusted guide, not a subordinate diplomat.

## Narrative Function

She holds one compact seat, validates Root-Witness findings, and may renew or extinguish the Moss-Crown fire.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
