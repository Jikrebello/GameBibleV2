# Runa Moss-Crown

## Core Concept

A Moss-Crown path-speaker and field guide who can move among Grenzburg officials without pretending to rule her kindred.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Runa knows roads, sanctuary boundaries, and the cost of failed agreements. She favors temporary reciprocal compacts and opposes both ducal occupation and Jorund's civilian war.

## Narrative Function

She guides every line stage but cannot bind Anya, River-Clans, Chapel-Folk, or Ghost-Foot delegates.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
