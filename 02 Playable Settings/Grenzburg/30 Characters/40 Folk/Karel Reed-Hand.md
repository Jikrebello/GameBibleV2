# Karel Reed-Hand

## Core Concept

Karel is a twenty-year-old Chapel-Folk repairer from Longlake Ward who learns mechanisms spatially and records them with charcoal, knots, and laid cord.

He repairs:

- refuge bells;
- stove catches;
- ferry gates;
- road-shelter shutters;
- hand winches and ordinary latches.

## Personality

Karel is patient with objects and impatient with explanations that begin after the work has already been decided.

He does not speak like a miniature Zafir. He thinks by touching distance, walking a route, and asking where a person stands when the mechanism fails.

He enjoys:

- drawing plans on floors too large for paper;
- making elegant tools from ugly scraps;
- teaching children bell codes;
- refusing a grand title for an ordinary repair;
- discovering that a scholar has forgotten the return journey.

## Relationship with Zafir

Karel voluntarily studies with Zafir because Zafir can explain relations Karel has only felt through load and movement.

The terms are explicit:

- no assignment or debt;
- no magical binding;
- Karel owns his notes and tools;
- he may revise or refuse an instruction;
- he may end the apprenticeship;
- neither man speaks for Chapel-Folk generally.

Karel is not Samira's replacement, local analogue, forgiveness device, or proof that Zafir is safe.

## Campaign States

Karel may become:

- a continuing learner;
- an equal collaborator;
- an independent field mechanic;
- a critic who leaves Zafir;
- an Open-Cord participant who later withdraws.

He cannot die unseen. Injury, departure, or disagreement remains visible and authored.

## Voice

Karel uses physical comparisons.

- “Your line gets them across. Mine gets the boatmen back.”
- “I understand the ring. I do not agree to it.”
- “Stop saying elegant. The stove still smokes.”
- “Draw it where a wet man can read it.”
