# Thorn-Seer Mael

## Core Concept

A Lakekeeper-line seer who guards the restricted truth of Wolfsmere and understands the annual costs of its boundaries.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Mael neither worships the lake nor treats its demands as moral. He accepts prudent observance where refusal would harm innocents and rejects identity prices, sacrifice, and public curiosity.

## Narrative Function

He controls no compact seat by himself but determines what Wolfsmere knowledge can be shared safely.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
