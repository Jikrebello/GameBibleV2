# Elof Bent-Oak

## Core Concept

The reader and refuge keeper of Bent-Oak, responsible for shelter, worship, and road testimony among one Chapel-Folk community.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Elof defends sanctuary for noncombatants and will identify active killers who abuse it. He seeks recognized land and worship without claiming authority over all Chapel-Folk.

## Narrative Function

He holds one compact seat and can anchor the strongest refuge clause.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
