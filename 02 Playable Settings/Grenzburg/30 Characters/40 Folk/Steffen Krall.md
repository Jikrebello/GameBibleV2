# Steffen Krall

## Core Concept

Steffen Krall is a nineteen-year-old crane-hand from Hammer Ward who fights unlicensed pit nights in **Meran-style** — spear and proof gauntlet, arena flourishes, the guard he learned off bout bills and one touring exhibition he saw when he was eleven.

He is the fear Tavio's campaign never staged: somebody younger, wearing his style, winning his crowd, on gear his name sold.

## The Gear

His kit is the campaign's own loose thread come home.

The centrepiece is the private demonstration gauntlet that left the Old Market auction during [[The Signed Measure]] — the one the quest notes "returns through the field inspection chain in Act III." This is that return. Around it are knockoff Red Brass rings from the recalled endorsement batches, bought cheap from a Hammer Ward fence precisely *because* they carry a Meran signature that the sellers still think means quality.

Tavio's name made this trusted. Tavio's legend is now a safety hazard with a fan club.

## Character Rules

- **He is genuinely good.** Not a dupe, not a poseur, not a cautionary example — gifted, fast, and improving between fights. The scenes must sell his gift, or the piece collapses into a lecture.
- **He is not humble,** and he is not cruel. He is nineteen and winning.
- **His counter-argument has teeth.** An old champion preaching limits to the young reads exactly like a man closing the door behind himself, and Steffen says so, in public, to applause. Nothing in the quest may make him simply wrong.
- **The gear is his ladder out of the crane yards.** A recall costs him the only route he has found. His refusal is self-interested and reasonable-shaped, never a seminar.

## Voice

Cocky-warm. Quotes Tavio's own bout bills back at him, accurately, because he has read them for eight years:

> “You said a halt is part of the bout.”
>
> *(beat)*
>
> “You never called one.”

He is delighted by Tavio's attention and completely unmoved by Tavio's authority, and he does not experience those as contradictory.

## Appearances

- **[[Games Without a Crown]]** — seeded. He enters the fair as a fair competitor, wins the safe spear-vault demonstration, and *glows* when Tavio praises him. Worship fully visible. The player likes him before the gear does. No hint of the pits.
- **[[Meran-Style]]** — the Act III operation. The pit night, the needling, the refusal, the public challenge Tavio must decline, and the fair ladder he may or may not take.
- **[[The Road Examiner]]** — summer. On a resolved path he is fighting filed and clean under examiner rules, and is still a little insufferable about it.

## State

`steffen_state` persists to summer: **examiner-apprentice**, **licensed fighter**, **injured**, or **estranged.**

The replacement fear does not resolve by his removal. It resolves — when it resolves — by succession Tavio can bear to watch.
