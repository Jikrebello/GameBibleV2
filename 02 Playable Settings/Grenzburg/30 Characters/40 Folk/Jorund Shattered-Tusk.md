# Jorund Shattered-Tusk

## Core Concept

A war leader who turns valid grievances over occupation, extraction, and broken refuge into a program of civilian killing.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Jorund knowingly targets families to make the frontier ungovernable. Some fighters follow him; other households reject his method without becoming ducal loyalists.

## Narrative Function

He can be defeated, captured, killed in combat, escape wounded, or retain a reduced war band. He never becomes the moral voice of the land.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
