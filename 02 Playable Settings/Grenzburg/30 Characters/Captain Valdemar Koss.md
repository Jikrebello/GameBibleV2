# Captain Valdemar Koss

## Core Concept

Koss is the Bank's commander of the Grenzburg Blackjacks and the player's direct contractual superior. A former free-company captain bought out of his own debt, he believes contract discipline is stronger than inherited oath, local sentiment, or religious scruple.

## Public Face

Efficient, protective of his personnel, and contemptuous of ceremonial waste. Koss secures good equipment, pays on time when the Bank permits it, and defends Blackjacks from anyone attempting to use them without compensation.

## Private Truth

He protects the Corps as valuable inventory. He will spend lives ruthlessly when the expected return justifies the loss, and he interprets affection as a weakness creditors can price.

## Campaign Function

- Conducts the player's Bank induction and explains that service changes repayment terms rather than ownership.
- Issues explicit Bank faction assignments and controls equipment and debt pressure; he does not attach a rider to every ducal mission.
- Rewards recoveries with debt relief, equipment, and access.
- Escalates collection pressure when Bank Leverage rises.
- Opposes Gerhold's traditional garrison and Pierre's claim that public danger outranks collateral.

## Rebuild Boundary

Koss has no fixed survival, command, or political outcome in Wave 1. His faction role must be rebuilt around selective Bank pressure and the possible futures in [[Factions Overview]].
