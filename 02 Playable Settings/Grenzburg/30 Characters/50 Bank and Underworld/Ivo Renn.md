# Ivo Renn

## Core Concept

A victim archivist who co-founded the Low Lantern network, then built the Black Bell after protected officials murdered witnesses named in his files.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Ivo is careful with evidence and still claims a right no private person can hold cleanly: deliberate punishment beyond public appeal. Grief makes the path understandable, not morally neutral.

## Narrative Function

He leads the tribunal branch and can accept strict limits, convert the Bell into an evidence network, or normalize hidden execution.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
