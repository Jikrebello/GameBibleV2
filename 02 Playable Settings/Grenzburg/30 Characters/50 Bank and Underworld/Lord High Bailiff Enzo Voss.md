# Lord High Bailiff Enzo Voss

## Core Concept

Grenzburg's corrupt high bailiff, who sells warrants, protects favored crimes, and turns administrative scarcity into patronage.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Enzo protects Oskar Vey and buys underworld intelligence, but he does not command the Low Lanterns or Black Bell. His corruption is civic and human, not a hidden Bank or occult conspiracy.

## Narrative Function

Evidence can expose, constrain, remove, or preserve him. His fate intersects several lines without making him their universal villain.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
