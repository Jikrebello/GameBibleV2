# Quartermaster Brina Kest

## Core Concept

A veteran debtor and quartermaster who knows every issued coat, missing ration, injury extension, and person Koss has written off.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Brina organizes ordinary Blackjacks around practical governance rather than romantic rebellion. She distrusts Lucia's reform limits and knows a free company must still discipline theft, desertion, and unsafe work.

## Narrative Function

She leads the work stoppage and can become elected quartermaster of a free company, cooperative officer, ducal logistics sergeant, or displaced organizer.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
