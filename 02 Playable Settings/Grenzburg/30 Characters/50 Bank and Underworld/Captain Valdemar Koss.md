# Captain Valdemar Koss

## Core Concept

The Bank-appointed commander of Grenzburg's Blackjacks and the principal human antagonist of their faction line.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Koss understands the difference between discipline and exploitation and chooses exploitation because owned specialists make him powerful. Fear of returning to debt explains his obsession without excusing it. He has no occult patron or secret Left Hand function.

## Narrative Function

He inflates equipment accounts, abandons recoverable units, provokes mutiny, and can be removed, imprisoned, killed in immediate conflict, or retain command through player choice.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
