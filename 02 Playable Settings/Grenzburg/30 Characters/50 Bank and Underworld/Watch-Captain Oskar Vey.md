# Watch-Captain Oskar Vey

## Core Concept

A decorated Watch captain who murders witnesses to preserve a protection economy involving officers, bailiffs, and favored households.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Vey knows the people he kills and chooses each death. Emergency commendations and Enzo's protection make ordinary arrest difficult but do not make him invulnerable.

## Narrative Function

His case opens the underworld fork. He can be arrested, killed during lethal resistance, executed by Black Bell, escape, or remain protected through player failure.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
