# Mara Senn

## Core Concept

A smuggler and route organizer who co-founded the Low Lanterns to move food, fugitives, and testimony through Lantern Ward.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Mara accepts theft and vice as funding but opposes trafficking, refuge sales, and private execution. Her concern about the Black Bell is moral and practical.

## Narrative Function

She leads the night-guild branch and may become a licensed guild officer, tolerated smuggler, prisoner, or displaced route keeper.
