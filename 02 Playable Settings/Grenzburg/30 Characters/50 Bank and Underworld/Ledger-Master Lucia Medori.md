# Ledger-Master Lucia Medori

## Core Concept

The Medori ledger-master responsible for Grenzburg logistics, credit, and debtor-service policy. She sincerely believes humane long-term credit is both profitable and socially better.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Lucia corrects wasteful cruelty, funds roads, and protects solvent households. She does not voluntarily abolish Bank or family power and initially defends ownership that is transparent, reviewable, and productive.

## Narrative Function

She can support a regulated free company, preserve reformed Medori control, negotiate compensation, or oppose liberation she considers confiscation.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
