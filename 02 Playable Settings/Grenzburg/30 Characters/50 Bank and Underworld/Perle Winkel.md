# Perle Winkel

## Core Concept

Perle Winkel is a Blackjack mender and salvage-hand in her mid-fifties, a debtor of long standing, and the most boring person in the Yard. She is also the person Elara Velescar's charm can actually wound, and the only reason anyone remembers where anything went.

She exists to be woundable. Everyone else in Elara's orbit is too dignified, too articulate, or too socially protected for a joke to land on. Perle is comic, adoring, harmless, and present.

## Manner

Perle talks the way water finds a drain: constantly, without malice, and by whatever route is available. Her subjects are her pigeons, her nephew's letters from the Fiorenzian coast, the state of everyone's knees, and the soup bone of 1349, which was famous.

She is not witless. Her digressions arrive at true observations — reliably, and always one clause after the last listener has escaped. This is her structural joke and her structural function: she is the Yard's institutional memory, and nobody can bear listening long enough to consult it.

> “—which is not the strap that wants watching, it's the ring above it, because that ring came off the Kestrel job and the Kestrel job had the bad batch, the ones with the seam in them, nobody wanted those but they went in the common box regardless because Vey said a ring is a ring, and I said, Oskar, a ring is a ring until it is two rings, and he laughed, he does laugh, whatever they say about him, and—”
>
> *(Everyone has gone. The ring fails in spring, on the stairs.)*

## Her Adoration

Perle loves Elara without irony, without expectation, and without accuracy. She retells Elara's exploits wrong — grander, sillier, with the wrong ruin and the wrong number of vermin — and she retells them constantly, to people who have already heard.

She keeps a ribbon from the Ropehouse expedition pinned to her apron. It was one of the ribbons Elara tied to the packs for the crew she assembled. Perle was not on that crew.

This is the seeded wound: in [[A Splendid Little Expedition]] Perle volunteers, and Elara curates her out **with a compliment** — warm, public, entirely kind, and final. The expedition's unfilled *record* function was hers to take; she remembers everything that has ever passed through the Yard's common box, and nobody thought of her, including the player.

## What She Actually Holds

Perle can supply, without notes:

- which debtor owned which object in which season;
- what was in the common box the week anything was lost;
- who was on which crew, and who asked to be and wasn't;
- the provenance of half the Yard's salvage, because she mended it.

In Act III her memory is materially useful to the Velescar coalition — burial-goods provenance, an object's route through the Yard, who signed for what. She provides it. She is decent, and she provides it, which is worse for Elara than spite would be.

## The Wound

The wound occurs in [[Uninvited]], is authored-fixed, and happens in every route including the autonomous one. Needled at the Reclamation Feast — the scandal named within earshot, a delegate asked whether the grave lady picked him — Elara makes one perfectly shaped joke at Perle's expense, loudly, lovingly, at the worst possible moment, with Perle attached to her arm.

The room laughs. **The player is offered the chance to laugh, and the option is the charmingly written, socially natural one.** This is never announced as a test. `player_laughed_at_perle` changes Perle's later manner toward the player and one line of the summer coda.

Perle understands in stages. That is the cruelty: not the instant, but the four seconds.

## After

`perle_state` moves **adoring → wounded → distant-kind**. There is no restored value. **No repair scene exists in any outcome, in any playthrough.** She sits outside the vault's ordinary conflict-and-repair loop by design; this is one of the campaign's two intentional exceptions to repairability (see [[Elara Companion Campaign]]). Do not add one.

She does not become cold, bitter, or pointed. She becomes **brief.**

The voice change is how the player hears the damage:

> **Before:** “—and I said to her, my lady, that's the Kestrel ring, I'd know it in the dark, I've had my hands on every ring in that box since before the fever year, and she said—”
>
> **After:** “Green box. Third shelf. It was in the green box.”

Complete sentences. Short ones. Correct ones. The ribbon is off the apron and nobody mentions it.

## Appearances

- **[[A Splendid Little Expedition]]** — volunteers; curated out with a compliment; the record function goes unfilled.
- **Winter, Yard ambient** — brings Elara mended gloves. Wrong size. Right love.
- **[[A Better Wager]]**, *Without the Powder* — optional line: Elara mentions Perle's adoration and, for one beat, lets the player see that it makes her feel fraudulent.
- **[[Uninvited]]** — the joke.
- **Act III coalition work** — supplies her memory, briefly, accurately.
- **[[The First Honest Find]]** — attends the occasion. Kind, remote, ribbon gone. If the player laughed, one line acknowledges it, without accusation.

## Voice Rules

- Run-on, associative, self-interrupting, never bitter, never self-pitying.
- Always eventually correct, always one clause too late.
- Comic without being stupid: she is boring, not foolish, and the distinction is the whole character.
- **After the wound:** short declaratives only. Never sarcasm. Never a speech about dignity.
- She never analyzes Elara, never diagnoses the joke, never forgives anyone out loud. She is not the campaign's aphorist — that licence belongs to [[Ashira bath Melan]] alone.
