# Neda Volk

Neda Volk is a thirty-six-year-old Blackjack recovery hand based at [[Blackjack Yard]]. She recovers people, equipment, and identifiable property after failed assignments. She is not a healer or commander. Her job begins where a heroic account usually ends: confirming who came back, returning what belongs to somebody, and naming what did not.

## Present Role

Neda works under [[Quartermaster Brina Kest|Quartermaster Brina Kest]] and rotates between:

- casualty retrieval;
- safe recovery of abandoned packs and marked company property;
- quiet interviews with frightened survivors;
- preparation of personal effects for return;
- escorting debtors who are not ready to enter the Yard alone.

She has no connection to any companion's life before Grenzburg. Her importance comes from having seen all eight companions at their least composed after they joined the same company.

## Character

Neda is broad-shouldered, close-cropped, patient, and economical with comfort. She dislikes the phrase “nothing we could do” when nobody has yet listed what was attempted.

She shows care by completing practical work without forcing disclosure. She will sit beside somebody in silence, clean mud from a dead person's buckle, or put a meal within reach. She will not turn any of those acts into permission to ask what happened.

Her dry humor appears when the danger has passed:

> “You may have the dramatic chair. It has the loose leg.”

## Elara

Elara initially introduces Neda as though public recognition can repay invisible work. Neda appreciates accurate credit and objects when Elara makes her into an emblem of neglected workers.

Their useful tension is simple: Elara can make a room notice Neda; Neda decides whether she wanted the room.

## Ashira

Ashira and Neda share custody of difficult recovery scenes under explicit boundaries. Neda tells Ashira which questions are necessary now, which can wait, and which belong to nobody present.

Neda trusts Ashira's ability to distinguish residue, repetition, and a living person in danger. She does not trust Ashira's instinct that understanding a likeness grants the right to retain it.

## Use

Neda supplies present-day Blackjack continuity across the Elara-Ashira pair:

- she can provide testimony about current work;
- she may lead temporary recovery teams;
- she can hold private effects without treating them as evidence;
- she may give one preparation assist in each woman's campaign;
- she never determines either woman's confession, consent, custody, or moral outcome.
