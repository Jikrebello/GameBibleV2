# Elara Velescar

## Companion Contract

A poor Virelian sabre-right noblewoman who robbed Grey Sweat graves to keep House Velescar solvent, then learned that her magnate patron had preserved the profit and sold her liabilities. Elara Velescar is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. She can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

Lord Marek Vardek financed the excavations, chose the plague plots, and bought the recovered heirlooms through cutouts. When a burial clerk traced the work, Vardek delivered Elara and the laborers to judgement while retaining the cabinet that proved his direction. Restitution, burial costs, and criminal surety became a Medori account.

Elara converted the dead and the poor into resources for a house whose dignity mattered more to her than their consent. Vardek betrayed her, but he did not choose her first trespass.

## Person Before Problem

Courtly, curious, and delighted by difficult doors. Elara likes competent company, wagers she can afford, bad inns with good stories, and the moment a plan becomes an adventure. Her manners are real rather than a mask, but so is her habit of treating lineage as an emergency that excuses taking from people with less power.

## Combat and World Use

A mobile Slayer who uses paired blades, grappling knives, weak-point attacks, and social access. She reads heraldry, funerary property, noble custom, and the difference between expensive display and actual wealth.

Elara Velescar contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. She is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens in **Early Autumn**. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** She exposes Vardek, relinquishes the Velescar title, transfers viable land to its workers, and establishes a burial-repair fund from recoverable family assets. She remains an adventurer without pretending that motion itself is restitution.

**Compromised:** She defeats Vardek and funds partial restitution while preserving the title and enough hidden property to rebuild the house. She is loyal to the player but still believes rank deserves a private reserve.

**Broken or departed:** She accepts Vardek's settlement, destroys or buries the cabinet, and restores House Velescar through the same patronage economy. A warned confrontation gives the player one final chance to expose the bargain; refusal or complicity can end her service.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Elara opens through daring, respect for the socially powerless, truthful handling of spoils, and refusing inherited privilege when it harms the living. She strains under needless humiliation, grave desecration, cowardice dressed as prudence, or using her confession as leverage.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **adventure between equals**. A male player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **Equal Footing**, letting either partner turn the other's weak-point opening into a second finisher. Sworn bond grants **Witness at My Back**, extending an exposed weak point when either partner creates it through traversal or the environment.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Zafir ibn Samad]] challenges Elara Velescar's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
