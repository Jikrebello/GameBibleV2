# Ylva Winter-Track

## Immediate Read

Ylva is already tying down the loose load while everyone else is deciding whose responsibility it was.

She is a severe-looking Grey-Wolf Hunter with a dry mouth, a long memory for bad knots, and an instinctive refusal to let danger become dramatic when it can become work. She notices wet gloves, bad footing, uneven breathing, and the one person nodding along without understanding the route.

She is not cold. She is economical.

## Character Thesis

Ylva establishes worth through competence and autonomy. To be trusted with difficult work is to be free; to be protected without consent is to be placed back under somebody else's judgement.

At her best, she makes survival practical and gives frightened people actions they can actually perform. At her worst, she treats visible need as surrender, regards care as a potential leash, and spends her own body before allowing a team to discuss the cost.

Her resolved arc does not make her domestic, verbally effusive, or less capable. She learns that reporting an injury, accepting relief, and respecting another person's chosen risk are forms of team competence.

## Identity

- **Age:** thirty-one in 1360 AR.
- **Origin:** the Grey-Wolf Ättar.
- **Earned name:** `Winter-Track`, a pathfinder name rather than an office or family title.
- **Current standing:** Medori surety debtor and Blackjack rescue scout.
- **Public style:** laconic field professional, difficult to impress and easy to rely on.
- **Private contradiction:** she wants other people to possess agency while treating her own need as evidence that agency may be removed.
- **Present ambition:** to establish a living route service whose competence belongs to trained teams rather than one heroic guide.

## Appearance

Ylva is tall, pale, and severe-featured, with straight pale-blonde hair normally bound beneath a weather hood. Her clothes use grey, white, charcoal, and faded blue:

- layered wool and waxed cloth;
- light scale at shoulders and ribs;
- a cold-weather longbow with a wrapped grip;
- quivers divided by arrow purpose;
- rope, signal cloth, lamp caps, chalk, wedges, needles, and fletching tools.

She wears no ornament that can catch a line. A tiny carved spoon inside her cooking kit is the exception, and she becomes unreasonably defensive if anyone calls it pretty.

## Grey-Wolf Formation

Grey-Wolf women hunt for households, tend beacons, maintain refuge routes, and perform the winter work that keeps formal war-scouts alive. They cannot hold the male war-scout office.

Ylva left because she wanted dangerous competence recognized as a profession rather than tolerated as household necessity. She does not hate her people, reject all custom, or imagine every foreign institution is freer. She becomes especially angry when outsiders use Grey-Wolf restrictions to patronize her while ignoring their own.

No relative, rival, claimant, or Grey-Wolf representative arrives in Grenzburg. Her formation appears through practice, memory, language, and the choices she makes now.

## The White-Sled Rescue

One winter before the game, Ylva scouted a Medori convoy near Snowblind Rise. A whiteout and road collapse trapped freight crews and passengers.

Ylva cut secured cargo loose and converted sleds, cloth, timber, and lamp oil into rescue litters, windbreaks, and signals. Saving the people was right.

Medori pursued the material loss. Rather than let the crew contest shared liability, Ylva signed the entire surety herself. She denied every survivor and worker the right to decide whether they would stand beside her. Medori converted the claim into Blackjack service.

The Bank's claim is exploitative. Ylva's signature remains an expression of her flaw.

## Appetite

Ylva enjoys:

- the clean release of a good bow;
- competitive route finding;
- cold air sharp enough to expose bad breathing;
- repairing an object so neatly that the owner cannot find the break;
- cooking heavy food for people who have stopped pretending they are not hungry;
- practical riddles, especially when the answer is a tool;
- small children who ask direct questions;
- sleeping near a door after someone else has formally taken the watch.

She dislikes ceremonial helplessness, decorative hardship, unsolicited assistance, wet bedding, people who borrow knives and return them dirty, and the phrase “for your own good.”

## Humor and Embarrassment

Ylva's humor is dry, literal, and often delayed by several seconds. She enjoys allowing a boastful person to discover that she has been teasing them for the last mile.

She is embarrassed by:

- losing a physical contest after advertising certainty;
- being carried;
- crying from pain or exhaustion;
- not knowing a domestic skill someone assumes all women know;
- receiving a gift designed for beauty rather than utility;
- discovering that other people prepared for her needs without discussing her as a problem.

Embarrassment makes her inspect equipment that does not need inspecting.

## Care

Ylva shows care by:

- correcting a strap before it tears;
- placing food within reach without announcing concern;
- walking the exposed side of a path;
- giving a frightened person one useful instruction;
- teaching a skill rather than demonstrating superiority;
- remembering exactly when someone should be relieved.

Her care becomes controlling when she withholds information so others cannot choose to share danger.

## Fear, Shadow, and Preserving Change

Ylva fears becoming a protected body whose judgment no longer counts.

Her shadow appears when she:

- conceals injury because disclosure might remove her from work;
- assumes a less experienced person has no right to choose risk;
- treats tenderness as the beginning of custody;
- quietly despises people who ask for help sooner than she would;
- makes her own death an efficient solution nobody else approved.

Her resolved self remains a frontier Hunter. The change is procedural and intimate: she reports limits, names the cost, permits dissent, trains substitutes, and can leave the last watch because somebody competent has arrived.

## Voice

Ylva uses compressed practical language. Distance, weather, and effort are her ordinary measures.

She says:

- “Wind changed.”
- “Eat before you decide.”
- “That knot is a confession.”
- “You may be brave after the ice is sounded.”

Care often appears as instruction. Anger makes her quieter. Fear makes her volunteer for something. A direct admission from Ylva should be short enough that another speaker may initially miss its importance.

Her voice obeys [[Companion Dialogue and Scene Standard]]. She does not deliver speeches about shared burden while somebody still needs carrying.

## Present-Day Grenzburg

### Blackjack Route Board

Ylva keeps the route board accurate and has a low-grade feud with debtors who record “passable” without specifying for whom. [[Quartermaster Brina Kest|Brina Kest]] insists every watch has a named relief; Ylva initially treats this as mistrust and later recognizes it as company law worth keeping.

### Marcher Service

[[Sergeant Liese of the Marcher Vanguard|Liese]] respects Ylva's field judgment and refuses to let specialist prestige erase chain-of-reporting obligations. Their strongest scenes concern disagreement between two competent women, not one proving women can be competent.

### Ashfield Lodge

Hunters at Ashfield test her local knowledge and teach her what Grey-Wolf reading does not cover. She gives them severe-weather practice in return. The lodge becomes a place where being corrected need not mean being confined.

### Folk Routes

Ylva respects local route authority when it is real and resents romantic vagueness. [[Runa Moss-Crown]] recognizes her rescue ethics and challenges her habit of treating the rescuer as outside the circle of lives being protected.

## Company Routine

At camp Ylva:

- checks the next day's weather before removing boots;
- cooks if nobody else has begun and complains if thanked too elaborately;
- repairs arrows while listening to conversations she appears to ignore;
- takes the worst sleeping position without asking;
- conducts quiet inspections of people returning from cold exposure;
- occasionally proposes a shooting game and becomes much less laconic if losing.

She may be recruited and dismissed normally. Repeatedly taking her only for blizzards or rescues makes her feel like equipment; leaving her out of danger because she is a woman produces immediate conflict.

## Combat and Fieldcraft

Ylva remains a standard [[Hunter]] using the Rescue Scout doctrine.

Her longbow is her only authored weapon. She prioritizes:

1. reading threats and retreat routes;
2. identifying endangered allies;
3. establishing concealment or a rally mark;
4. interrupting pursuit;
5. opening extraction;
6. repositioning before becoming a front-line combatant.

Her field-arrow progression remains:

- All-Weather Flare Arrow;
- Reinforced Blunt-Head;
- Line Arrow;
- Smoke Arrow;
- Controlled Blast Arrow;
- Winter-Track Fieldcraft.

The arrows create advantages, not critical-route permissions. Their recipes are friendship-independent and account-wide at their approved milestones.

## Approval and Moral Limits

Ylva approves of:

- competence honestly reported;
- informed people choosing risk;
- abandoning cargo to save life;
- practical preparation;
- leaders who accept correction;
- receiving relief when the relief is ready.

She opposes:

- patronizing protection;
- concealed danger imposed on a team;
- glory bought with avoidable casualties;
- using women, children, or civilians as proof of an enemy's weakness;
- punishing rescuers through predatory liability;
- treating self-destruction as the purest form of service.

Approval and temperament remain separate controls.

## Companion Campaign Direction

[[Ylva Companion Campaign]] retains its eleven-piece architecture. [[Companion Emotional Rewrite Audit]] preserves the major premises while requiring more variety.

Her campaign must include:

- skill used for pleasure and competition;
- an injury she cannot convert immediately into another task;
- a person whose different competence Ylva initially underrates;
- a command decision in which accepting relief improves rather than diminishes the result;
- a moment where refusing patronization is entirely right;
- a final route where sacrifice remains available but is not presented as the best proof of love.

No person from her homeland arrives to decide her worth.

## Relationships

Ylva is heterosexual. A male player may pursue romance; a female player may form the exclusive sworn bond.

Romance concerns care that is freely received rather than entered as debt. Sworn friendship concerns alternating lead, watch, and relief between two women whose competence does not require hierarchy.

Relationship recipes and progression remain mechanically equal. Friendship alone can produce her strongest resolution.

## Principal Foil

[[Tavio Meran]] exceeds limits because mastery and public excellence tempt him. Ylva exceeds them because she treats herself as expendable.

They recognize one another's physical intelligence. Their arc must include enjoyment, not only argument: contests, training, shared rescue, and the disorienting discovery that being beaten by the other can be fun.

## Faction Outlook

- **Ducal/Marcher:** favors trained field authority, named relief, and stop rights over heroic improvisation without record.
- **Blackjacks/Medori:** supports Brina's shared liability and rejects Koss's use of individual sacrifice as an operating model.
- **Church:** respects shelters, food, and practical care; distrusts pity used to remove agency.
- **Folk:** respects particular route custodians and rejects both universal Folk claims and outsider dismissal.
- **Underworld:** values escape routes protecting the vulnerable; opposes Black Bell punishment and smugglers who abandon clients when pursuit begins.

## Seasonal Movement

- **Autumn:** proves herself, recruits teams reluctantly, and begins confronting how quickly usefulness becomes self-erasure.
- **Winter:** becomes essential to urban and ice evacuation while learning that distant teams may make informed decisions without her.
- **Spring:** builds duplicate routes and faces the last-marker temptation.
- **Summer:** becomes an independent pathfinder, supervised scout, solitary contractor, or memorialized sacrifice according to play.

She cannot die autonomously.
