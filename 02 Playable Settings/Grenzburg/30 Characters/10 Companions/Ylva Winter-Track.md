# Ylva Winter-Track

## Companion Contract

Ylva Winter-Track is a thirty-one-year-old Grey-Wolf rescue pathfinder, Medori debtor, and optional full companion. She joins the Blackjacks during [[The Southbound Muster]] and remains a standard [[Hunter]] rather than introducing a new vocation.

She is a specialist in finding a living route through weather, pursuit, panic, and collapsing ground. Her story is not about learning that sacrifice is noble. Ylva already knows how to spend herself for other people. It asks whether she can accept that those people have the right to share the decision, the cost, the care, and the credit.

## Appearance

Ylva is tall, pale, and severe-featured, with straight pale-blonde hair normally bound beneath a weather hood. Her field clothing uses grey, white, charcoal, and faded blue rather than heraldic colours:

- layered wool and waxed cloth;
- light scale at the shoulders and ribs;
- a cold-weather longbow with a wrapped grip;
- practical quivers divided by arrow purpose;
- rope, signal cloth, lamp caps, chalk, wedges, and fletching tools;
- repaired gloves with the first two fingers cut for the string.

Her bow is her only authored weapon. A knife at her belt is a camp tool, not a second combat style.

## Voice and Presence

Ylva is laconic without being joyless. Her humour is dry, exact, and usually delivered while repairing something another person did not realize was broken. She dislikes boasting, crowded rooms, decorative danger, and questions asked after a decision has already been made.

Her warmth appears through action:

- putting hot food into the hand of the person who forgot to eat;
- replacing wet cord before anyone asks;
- shifting camp so the exhausted person receives the lee side;
- remembering who is frightened of deep water;
- teaching signals patiently;
- pretending not to notice when someone needs a second attempt.

She is comfortable with silence and visibly awkward when thanked.

## Grey-Wolf Formation

Grey-Wolf women hunt for households, tend beacons, maintain refuge stores, read winter routes, and organize recovery after storms. Formal war-scout office remains male. Ylva learned nearly every practical duty of that office without being eligible to hold its rank.

She did not leave to wage a campaign against her homeland. She took foreign route contracts because those employers would name her competence as a profession and pay for it. `Winter-Track` is an earned pathfinder name recognizing repeated safe returns through winter country. It is not a family title, clan office, or claim to speak for all Grey-Wolves.

No relative, former crew member, claimant, or homeland representative follows her to Grenzburg. Her past is present through habit, memory, and the contract she signed.

## The White-Sled Rescue

One winter before the game, Ylva served as a Medori convoy scout near Snowblind Rise. A whiteout and road collapse trapped freight sleds and passenger wagons above an exposed drop.

Ylva cut loose secured cargo and converted sled timber, sailcloth, lamp oil, and packing felt into rescue litters, windbreaks, signals, and warming stations. The people survived because she spent property without waiting for permission.

Medori pursued the material loss. The convoy crew had grounds to contest the claim collectively, but Ylva signed the full surety herself before they could act. She believed she was protecting them. In doing so, she denied them the right to testify, share liability, or stand beside her.

[[The White-Sled Rescue]] controls the full event. The rescue was right. The Bank's claim was exploitative. Ylva's self-isolation was still a chosen mistake.

## Central Lie and Need

> If I can still stand, the burden is mine.

Ylva mistakes endurance for sole authority. She reports an injury only when it prevents movement, accepts every unclaimed watch, and treats her own body as reserve equipment.

She needs to understand:

- a team has the right to know the real risk;
- asking for relief is part of maintaining a route;
- another person may knowingly choose danger without being protected from their own agency;
- shared liability is not cowardice;
- care may be received without becoming debt;
- being the last person on the road is not the same as bringing everyone home.

## Everyday Life

At camp Ylva:

- checks the weather before sitting down;
- dries everyone else's bowstrings before her own;
- makes dense travel bread and very thin soup;
- repairs boots with more patience than conversation;
- sleeps near exits and wakes at changes in wind;
- teaches practical knots to children and refuses to call it a lesson;
- tracks who has skipped a watch or meal;
- quietly takes over work when she should instead ask why it was missed.

She enjoys target games, route riddles, bird calls, strong cheese, and hearing someone else correctly read a trail she taught them.

## Combat

[[Ylva Rescue Hunter Package]] controls her authored Hunter behaviour.

Her field priorities are:

1. read threats and retreat routes;
2. identify an endangered ally;
3. establish a signal or concealment point;
4. interrupt pursuit of the wounded or encumbered;
5. open an extraction lane;
6. reposition before becoming a front-line combatant.

She differs from [[Dismas Raben]] without changing the base vocation. Dismas is an ambush tracker and quarry reader. Ylva is a rescue scout and team coordinator. Dismas remains a Hunter pending his dedicated vocation reassessment.

## Approval

Ylva approves of:

- reporting danger before it becomes a spectacle;
- abandoning replaceable property to save people;
- distributing watches and liability openly;
- respecting local route knowledge;
- giving civilians enough information to choose risk;
- withdrawing in good order;
- accepting help before collapse;
- crediting every person who held a route.

She disapproves of:

- concealed injuries;
- using civilians as unbriefed bait;
- wasting lives for symbolic ground;
- treating rescue workers as expendable;
- hoarding maps or signals for prestige;
- forcing a person to accept protection without hearing their choice;
- abandoning the slowest traveler to preserve cargo;
- romanticizing a needless last stand.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**. A permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one repair opportunity.

## Faction Views

- **Ducal and marcher:** respects bounded service, staffed roads, and accountable evacuation command; rejects officers who preserve a fort by spending its people.
- **Blackjacks and Medori:** trusts [[Quartermaster Brina Kest]] over [[Captain Valdemar Koss]]; supports collective debtor relief and rejects sole-liability waivers extracted during crisis.
- **Church:** values shelters, kitchens, burial details, and practical pastoral work; rejects jurisdiction claimed over people because aid was provided.
- **Folk:** defers to particular households and custodians on their own paths; rejects both universal Folk claims and total-war attacks on travelers.
- **Underworld:** accepts Low Lantern routes used to move threatened people; rejects stolen refuge stores, false signals, and Black Bell executions.

## Outcomes

**Resolved:** Ylva leaves the last position with the final group, accepts relief, settles or clears her debt, and becomes an independent pathfinder using openly shared contracts.

**Compromised:** She remains an excellent ducal or Blackjack route scout whose staffing, rest, and liability still require external supervision.

**Broken or departed:** She rejects shared routes and takes increasingly solitary contracts where self-destruction is mistaken for usefulness.

**Heroic but unresolved sacrifice:** after every safer alternative is shown, the player may explicitly accept Ylva's decision to hold the final marker. She saves lives and dies believing that her own life was always the expendable part. This is moving, but it is not her strongest resolution.

No outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ylva cannot die autonomously.

## Principal Foil

[[Tavio Meran]] is Ylva's principal foil. Tavio exceeds limits for mastery, acclaim, and proof. Ylva exceeds them to make herself expendable. Both must learn that another person's right to call a halt is part of working together.

[[Dismas Raben]] supplies a secondary contrast: he once escaped responsibility while Ylva takes responsibility until no one else can carry it.
