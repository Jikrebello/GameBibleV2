# Dismas Raben

## Companion Contract

A Korrheim scout turned highway robber who triggered a prepared rockfall before identifying his target, crushed a civilian coach, and abandoned the survivors when soldiers approached. Dismas Raben is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. He can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

Dismas was convicted for armed robbery and road destruction because the dead coach could not speak and one survivor disappeared. He chose a purchased service sentence over long imprisonment. He has never admitted that he heard people alive beneath the stone and left them.

The robbery was chosen, the premature rockfall was reckless, and abandoning trapped civilians was cowardice. Trauma is consequence, not supernatural punishment or exoneration.

## Person Before Problem

A dry road captain who reads weather, spoor, and frightened travelers better than he reads rooms. He tells excellent stories without making himself the hero. Competence makes escape easy for him, and he has built a life around leaving before anyone can require an answer.

## Combat and World Use

A longbow Hunter focused on tracking, crafted arrows, monster reading, and route control. He adds scouting options but is never required for critical traversal.

Dismas Raben contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. He is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens in **Black Road**. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** He confesses publicly and accepts permanent marcher scout service under civil oversight. Victims owe no forgiveness; his future is useful and constrained rather than cleansed.

**Compromised:** He admits the deaths privately and serves under Pierre with a sealed record. Roads benefit, but the public version remains incomplete.

**Broken or departed:** He silences the survivor, frames his old gang, or flees the reckoning. After warning and one repair chance, he departs rather than remain accountable.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Dismas opens through keeping watch, returning for people, honest risk, and refusing glamour around banditry. He strains at abandoning civilians, coercing witnesses, or using legal technicality to erase known harm.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **no escape route**. A female player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **No Escape Route**, preserving a shared pursuit or withdrawal lane when either partner chooses to remain for the other. Sworn bond grants **No Blind Turn**, preventing full road surprise and keeping the first marked escape route visible through weather or darkness.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Ilyana Veshkar]] challenges Dismas Raben's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
