# Bernard Valeon

## Companion Contract

A hospice-raised Radiant Path knight whose compulsive theft of valueless pilgrim keepsakes prevented identification of a murder victim and allowed the killer to escape. Bernard Valeon is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. He can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

Bernard stole a pilgrim's marked token during a relapse. When the pilgrim was murdered, the missing heirloom delayed identification and sent the search down the wrong road. His order paid victims and investigators. Bernard voluntarily entered Blackjack service to repay that sum, but money alone cannot repair the failure.

Illness explains the pressure to steal, not the decision to hide relapse or the harm caused. The murderer bears the murder; Bernard bears the obstruction and concealment.

## Person Before Problem

Open-hearted, attentive, and excellent with the sick, children, and exhausted people. He can laugh at himself without turning guilt into a performance. His stealing is compulsive rather than a charming quirk, and he uses routines, witnesses, and returned objects as practical treatment.

## Combat and World Use

He begins as a great-weapon fighter with faint White-Flame embers. Quest three unlocks Paladin; quest four grants its capstone. Sacred power follows moral coherence and genuine restitution, not rank or self-belief.

Bernard Valeon contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. He is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens in **Black Road**. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** He becomes a lay hospice knight outside his old order, living with treatment, safeguards, public restitution, and a faith no longer used as immunity.

**Compromised:** He returns to supervised order service and does real good, but permits the institution to keep his case private to protect its reputation.

**Broken or departed:** He hides evidence, steals again during crisis, or accepts easy absolution without restitution. A warned repair quest can keep him; refusal may close White-Flame abilities and end service.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Bernard opens through mercy joined to truth, care for the overlooked, returned property, and justice that distinguishes illness from impunity. He strains at humiliation, theft for sport, institutional cover-up, and executions without need.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **care without rescue**. A female player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **Kept in the Light**, letting reciprocal healing restore recoverable health and moral-coherence pressure to both partners. Sworn bond grants **Open Hand**, causing either oath-partner's heal or revive to restore a smaller amount of recoverable health to the whole field party.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Ashira bath Melan]] challenges Bernard Valeon's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
