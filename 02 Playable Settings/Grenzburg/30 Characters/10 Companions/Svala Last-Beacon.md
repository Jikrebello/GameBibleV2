# Svala Last-Beacon

## Companion Contract

A Grey-Wolf Rimewing rescue fighter who killed a yielded hall champion while using forbidden rage discipline and entered foreign debt service to keep the broken weregild from consuming her fellowship. Svala Last-Beacon is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. She can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

Svala challenged a lawful champion who had barred women from the winter ring by custom. Her challenge was irregular but the bout was accepted. When he yielded, rage training narrowed her hearing and she struck again. The Rimewing Sisters could not pay the full weregild without losing rescue stores, so Svala accepted a Medori service purchase.

The champion's exclusion and pride were wrong; his yield still bound her. She killed him after the contest had morally and socially ended.

## Person Before Problem

A warm hearth-keeper with a booming laugh, careful hands, and no patience for guests going unfed. She keeps lamps, remembers birthdays, and turns camp repair into company. Her danger is not a hidden beast but trained rage she once prized more than another person's surrender.

## Combat and World Use

A heavy Fighter using shield, axe, guard counters, carries, and rescue control. Her rage state is a dangerous emergency tool whose resolved version gains a reliable recall cue and surrender recognition.

Svala Last-Beacon contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. She is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens at the **Southbound Muster** during Open Autumn, making Svala the final member of the roster to become available. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** She accepts chosen exile, pays continuing weregild through service, and becomes a free protector of Grenzburg's winter roads without claiming restoration to her hall.

**Compromised:** She wins conditional hall pardon and continues Rimewing methods under public safeguards, but retains a belief that exceptional rescuers deserve exceptional license.

**Broken or departed:** She silences witnesses or embraces rage as necessity. After warning and repair, she can leave to gather fighters who value victory over yielded lives.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Svala opens through hospitality, courageous rescue, keeping terms with enemies, and making room for ordinary weakness. She strains at cruelty to captives, contempt for custom without understanding, or exploiting surrender.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **shared hearth**. A male player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **Shared Hearth**, restoring recoverable health and guard when either partner carries or revives the other. Sworn bond grants **Beacon Stand**, giving both oath-partners a brief unbreakable guard and clear retreat lane whenever either revives any ally.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Tavio Meran]] challenges Svala Last-Beacon's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
