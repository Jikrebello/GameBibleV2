# Ilyana Veshkar

## Companion Contract

A decorated Vlastravan breach-warlock who concealed the deaths caused by her unauthorized Shell overdraw, then deserted rather than repeat the same sacrifice. Ilyana Veshkar is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. She can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

Ilyana collapsed a fortress breach while a relief platoon was still inside. Her commander, Colonel Varko Milen, wrote a clean victory report; she accepted the medal and silence. Years later she refused an order built on the same expendable arithmetic and deserted with issued war-wands. The equipment claim and broken-service surety were sold to Medori.

The first overdraw was her choice, and accepting the false report converted dead soldiers into a decoration. Later desertion proves she could refuse, not that the earlier silence disappears.

## Person Before Problem

Earthy, practical, and unexpectedly patient with frightened recruits. Ilyana makes fatalistic jokes about officers, sleeps near exits, and treats clean equipment as a form of respect. She can command without swagger, but mistakes emotional containment for honesty.

## Combat and World Use

A paired-wand Warlock specializing in breach control, elemental weaving, field geometry, and emergency counter-scripts. Her personal unlocks favor safer casting around allies rather than larger explosions.

Ilyana Veshkar contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. She is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens in **Black Road**. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** She returns the medal, names every dead soldier publicly, and commands Blackjack specialists under a doctrine that forbids treating trapped people as expendable inputs.

**Compromised:** She exposes Milen but preserves the official victory account to protect surviving comrades and keeps the medal as evidence. Her doctrine is safer but still permits command secrecy.

**Broken or departed:** She accepts Milen's restored commission or repeats the clean-breach method during the Muster. After a warned confrontation she may depart to command a force that values victory above rescue.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Ilyana responds to rescue under pressure, candid risk, disciplined spell use, and refusing easy sacrifices. She strains when the player conceals casualties, treats subordinates as ammunition, or demands confession without sharing danger.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **comrades who choose life**. A male player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **Chosen Life**, converting one lethal elemental field per major engagement into a stable corridor toward the player. Sworn bond grants **Relief Held**, stabilizing a defensive lane whenever either oath-partner begins a rescue or revive under elemental pressure.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Dismas Raben]] challenges Ilyana Veshkar's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
