# Sergeant Liese of the Marcher Vanguard

## Core Concept

Liese is a third-generation marcher scout who knows the hamlets, Folk paths, winter approaches, and practical limits of ducal authority. She is not a recruitable companion; she is one of the three trust gates that proves the player can serve Grenzburg rather than merely complete contracts.

## Trust Gate

Liese trusts the player when all three autumn villages remain viable and the player refuses to treat settlers, Chapel-Folk, or Folk guides as disposable route assets. Her trust certifies the player's field judgement to Pierre and unlocks the ducal claim at the Barrow.

## Campaign Use

- Provides the ducal brief for village stabilization and frontier reconnaissance.
- Measures outcomes by who can still live on the land after the mission.
- Challenges Koss when a specific Bank demand sacrifices roads or people for recoverable property.
- Leads the Marcher Vanguard during the winter assault and spring resettlement.
