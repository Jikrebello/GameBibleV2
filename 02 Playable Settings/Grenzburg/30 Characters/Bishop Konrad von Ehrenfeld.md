### **Bishop Konrad & The Church of the Redeemer in Grenzburg**

**Role in the Rule of Three:**
The Church is the ultimate **Ugly** faction. It is not a monolith of faith, but a sovereign power disguised as a ministry. It trades in legitimacy, fear, and hope, offering the Duchy spiritual salvation in exchange for temporal control. Its internal schism—between Charity, Zealotry, and cold Politics—threatens to tear it apart and consume Grenzburg in the process.

**Core Concept:** A frontier diocese is a unique beast: far from the oversight of the Arch-Synod, surrounded by heathens, and flush with the wealth of ruins it claims as holy sites. Bishop Konrad runs a **spiritual corporation**. His product is divine favor, his currency is sin and relic, and his bottom line is the acquisition of land, souls, and political sway. He must balance the three faces of his Church to keep it profitable and powerful.

---

## **The Sovereign: Bishop Konrad**

**Concept:** A disgraced political operator from the Eclessian heartlands, exiled to this "savage outpost." He has turned punishment into opportunity, building a miniature papacy where his word is law. He is not deeply pious; he is a **theocrat**, believing Church rule is the only stable, moral governance possible.
*   **Public Face:** The imposing, regal Bishop. Robes of fine wool, a heavy amethyst ring. Holds lavish, theatrical masses. The image of unwavering authority and divine mandate.
*   **Private Truth:** He is a gambler playing a long con. He fears the zealous purity of Ignatius as much as the secular greed of the Bank. His faith is in **institutional power**; if the Church becomes weak, the entire moral order collapses, and men like the Medori will rule over soul-less ledgers.
*   **Goal:** To make the Diocese of Grenzburg so wealthy, powerful, and spiritually essential that it becomes the de facto authority in the Marches, with the Duke as its loyal secular arm.
*   **Method:** Play the sub-factions against each other. Let Clemens' charity build popular support, let Ignatius' zeal terrify enemies, and let Marcellina's deals secure the assets. He is the conductor of a dangerous orchestra.

## **Internal Factions**

### **1. The Hospitallers (The "Good" within the Ugly)**
**Led by: Brother Clemens**
*   **Core Concept:** The conscience of the Church. They embody the Redeemer's mercy in a land that has none. They believe faith is proven through selfless acts, not conquest or coin.
*   **Goal:** To provide unconditional charity, create a true sanctuary for all (even Grobi), and prove that compassion is the most powerful evangelizing tool.
*   **Resources:** The hospital itself (a sprawling, damp, but vital hall). Knowledge of herbs and healing. The immense, grinding labor of its brothers and sisters. The genuine love of the common people.
*   **Conflict with Church Leadership:** They are a **financial drain and a political liability**. Clemens heals Folk raiders and Grobi prisoners, undermining the "us vs. heathens" narrative. Konrad tolerates them because they provide the Church's "human face," but Marcellina seeks to divert their funds, and Ignatius considers them heretical for aiding the enemy.

### **2. The Ashen Band (The "Bad" within the Ugly)**
**Led by: Deacon Ignatius**
*   **Core Concept:** The militant arm. They believe the frontier is a holy furnace meant to burn away the unfaithful. Theirs is a theology of purity through violence.
*   **Goal:** To purge Grenzburg of Folk influence, reject all dealings with Grobi, claim the ancient ruins through right of conquest, and establish a theocratic state ruled by the "pure of faith."
*   **Resources:** A fanatical militia of 50-70 armed devotees. Secret donations from reactionary nobles in southern Eclessia. A powerful, fear-based messaging that resonates deeply during winter despair.
*   **Conflict with Church Leadership:** They are **uncontrollable and provocative**. Ignatius operates as a zealot, not a soldier of the Bishop. His unsanctioned raids threaten to start wars Konrad cannot afford. Yet, his fervor is useful for intimidating the Duke and keeping the laity in line, so Konrad must reign him in without breaking him.

### **3. The Chancery (The "Ugly" within the Ugly)**
**Led by: Sister Marcellina**
*   **Core Concept:** The administrative and intelligence heart. The Church as a bureaucratic empire. Faith is a strategic asset; sin is a transactional commodity.
*   **Goal:** To maximize the Church's temporal power, wealth, and land holdings. To ensure the Church is the indispensable power-broker in every deal in the Marches.
*   **Resources:** The relic vaults. The deed and tithe archives. A network of informants (confessors, clerks, servants). Mastery of canon law and contractual loopholes. Control over the interpretation of "miracles."
*   **Conflict with Church Leadership:** They are **amoral and autonomous**. Marcellina's goals align with Konrad's (power), but her methods—blackmail, extortion, fraud—create vulnerabilities and enemies. She knows where every body is buried, including Konrad's. He depends on her but fears the day her ledger becomes a weapon against him.

## **The Church's Seasonal Rhythm & Political Strategy**

*   **Summer (Acquisition):** Marcellina's season. Surveying new land claims near ruins, negotiating tithes from guilds, buying up debt from the Bank to hold over key figures. The Chancery works.
*   **Autumn (Judgment):** Ignatius's season. Preaching fire and brimstone to the crowds flooding in. Conducting "purges" of suspected Folk-sympathizers or heretics to "cleanse" the city before winter. The Ashen Band flexes its muscle.
*   **Winter (Dependency):** Clemens's season (by necessity). The hospital overflows. The Church becomes the primary source of alms and thin soup. Konrad presents himself as the guardian of the poor, building loyalties that will last past the thaw.
*   **Spring (Consolidation):** Konrad's season. Holding courts to settle disputes (with heavy Church fees). Sending reports (heavily edited) to the Arch-Synod, boasting of conversions and revenue. Integrating the year's gains into the Church's permanent portfolio.

## **Relationship with the Duchy & The Duke**

The Church's relationship with Duke Alaric is a **cold symbiosis**.
*   **What the Church Provides:**
    1.  **Legitimacy:** His rule is "blessed."
    2.  **Social Control:** Sermons can calm or inflame the populace.
    3.  **Crisis Charity:** The hospice and alms stave off total collapse in winter.
*   **What the Church Demands:**
    1.  **Land Grants:** Title to any newly discovered ruin sites.
    2.  **Tax Exemptions:** For Church holdings and businesses.
    3.  **Legal Privilege:** Church courts for clergy, veto over certain laws (e.g., "blasphemy").
    4.  **A Share of Power:** A seat at every table, a voice in every decision.

The Duke uses Prelate Caestus as his point of contact, but Konrad, Ignatius, and Marcellina all work around him to pressure Alaric directly.

## **Relationship with the Players (Seconded Blackjacks)**

The player arrives as a Bank-owned Blackjack carrying a ducal warrant; this relationship may pressure either side of that divided authority.

The Church views the Blackjacks as **useful sinners**.
*   **Brother Clemens (Hospitallers):** Might hire you to protect a medical supply caravan from bandits (or the Ashen Band), or to find a rare healing herb in Folk territory. Pays in kindness, safe lodging, and genuine blessings.
*   **Deacon Ignatius (Ashen Band):** Will hire you for deniable wetwork: assassinating a Folk shaman, sabotaging a Grobi treaty, or stealing an artifact from a "heretical" collector. Pays in silver, zealot fervor, and promises of divine favor you don't want.
*   **Sister Marcellina (Chancery):** Your most likely employer. Tasks involve economic and political espionage: retrieving a blackmail ledger, forging a deed, auditing a rival's books for hidden wealth, or "convincing" a debtor to transfer their debt to the Church. Pays well, in coin and in secrets that could be valuable or damning.

**The Faustian Bargain:** Any deal with the Church advances its power. Retrieve a relic for Clemens? It goes in Konrad's vault, increasing his prestige. Sabotage a rival for Marcellina? You make the Church the region's prime power-broker. Help Ignatius? You bring the Duchy closer to a holy war.

---

## **Failure Modes for the Church**

1.  **Schism:** The three factions break into open conflict. The Hospitallers shelter Folk, the Ashen Band besieges the hospital, and the Chancery backs the Duke in crushing both. The Church's power is shattered.
2.  **Secular Crackdown:** The Duke, backed by the Bank, finally calls their bluff. He nationalizes Church lands, arrests Marcellina for fraud, and exiles Ignatius. The Church is reduced to a purely spiritual role under tight control.
3.  **Zealot Takeover:** Ignatius succeeds in a coup, murdering Konrad and declaring a theocracy. Grenzburg becomes a paranoid, puritanical fortress that attacks the Folk and Grobi indiscriminately, leading to its inevitable military and economic ruin.
4.  **The God That Fails:** A major "relic" is proven to be a fake, or a prayed-for miracle (like holding back the Grobi) fails spectacularly. The people's faith collapses, and with it, the Church's political capital evaporates overnight.
