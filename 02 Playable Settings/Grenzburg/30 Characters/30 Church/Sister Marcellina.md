# Sister Marcellina

## Core Concept

A chancery intelligence officer who has prevented real killings through informants, sealed reports, and careful evidence.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Marcellina normalizes surveillance, blackmail, and coercion because they often work. She is a compromised protector, not an amoral caricature and not the secret ruler of Grenzburg.

## Narrative Function

Her network can be purged and bounded, preserved, exposed, or broken. She protects witnesses even when doing so reduces her own leverage.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
