# Bishop Konrad von Ehrenfeld

## Core Concept

Grenzburg's bishop, a sincere institutionalist who believes strong doctrine, courts, and hierarchy protect real spiritual goods.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Konrad is pious and politically ambitious. His error is identifying the Church's true spiritual authority with broad institutional jurisdiction. He can accept bounds without becoming secretly unbelieving.

## Narrative Function

He remains bishop in the strongest result and enacts, resists, or circumvents the jurisdiction settlement according to the player's line.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
