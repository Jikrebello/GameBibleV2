# Deacon Ignatius

## Core Concept

A severe deacon responsible for militant protection and investigation during frontier emergencies.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Ignatius correctly recognizes some supernatural dangers and wrongly expands suspicion into collective guilt. He is recoverable before atrocity if evidence, discipline, and pastoral authority constrain him.

## Narrative Function

He can become a bounded protector, resign under penance, remain a coercive officer, or cross a final line and face secular judgement.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
