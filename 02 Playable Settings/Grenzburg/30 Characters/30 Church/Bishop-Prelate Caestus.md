# Bishop-Prelate Caestus

## Core Concept

The diocese's pastoral prelate, responsible for hospitals, formation, burial, and relief under Konrad.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Caestus is not naive. He understands scarcity and doctrine, but insists that institutions prove sacred authority through truthful service rather than acquired jurisdiction.

## Narrative Function

He leads Bread and Burial, the winter mercy operation, and may gain authority over hospitals and formation.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
