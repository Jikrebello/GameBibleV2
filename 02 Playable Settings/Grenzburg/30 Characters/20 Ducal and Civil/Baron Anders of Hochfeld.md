# Baron Anders of Hochfeld

Baron Anders is an eastern Eclessian landholder whose household maintains a winter residence in Crown Heights and contributes cavalry remounts, grain guarantees, and junior officers to Grenzburg. He is not a second sovereign inside the duchy.

Anders sincerely believes hereditary households preserve long memory and can finance public service across bad years. He also expects appointments, entry exceptions, and requisition protection in return. His political method is respectable patronage rather than secret treason.

In the Ducal line he represents nobles who will accept a professional marcher charter if it preserves lawful command and honors real service. He resists village petitions that can delay mobilization and supports permanent emergency discretion when fear rises. The player may win his support, expose a patronage promotion, leave him a loyal critic, or reduce his court influence. None of those outcomes becomes future canon.
