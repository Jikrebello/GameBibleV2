# Duke Alaric von Grenzburg

## Core Concept

The reigning duke and public driver of Grenzburg's campaign. Alaric believes a sovereign must keep the frontier alive without surrendering it piecemeal to institutions that can help it.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

He and Pierre are loyal rivals. Alaric values political flexibility; Pierre fears flexibility becoming permanent exception. Alaric can accept a bounded marcher charter without ceasing to rule.

## Narrative Function

He sets strategic priorities and receives the military-authority settlement. He may become a constitutional frontier duke, emergency ruler, patronage sovereign, or weakened survivor; none is canonical.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
