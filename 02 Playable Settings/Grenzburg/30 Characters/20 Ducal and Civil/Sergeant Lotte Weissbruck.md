# Sergeant Lotte Weissbruck

Lotte is a Grenzburger garrison sergeant responsible for gate relief, reserve drills, and the practical handover between city soldiers and field patrols. She is local, not a companion, romance option, or outsider debtor.

She believes professional service is a craft: written watches, maintained gear, honest casualty rolls, and officers who eat after their people. Her blind spot is impatience with local exceptions. She can mistake an improvised village solution for indiscipline until it succeeds in front of her.

Lotte provides recurring continuity through Tannward, Fort Tannbruck survivors, the winter wall, and spring training. She supports Pierre's civil-service charter, challenges Blackjacks who want privilege without responsibility, and can recognize Brina Kest's free company as professional service. Her survival and promotion remain player-dependent.
