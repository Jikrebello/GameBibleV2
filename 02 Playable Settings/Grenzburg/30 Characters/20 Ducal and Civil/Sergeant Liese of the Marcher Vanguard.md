# Sergeant Liese of the Marcher Vanguard

## Core Concept

A third-generation marcher scout and the player's field counterpart, trusted by village levies, soldiers, and particular Folk contacts.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Liese does not speak for the Folk and does not conceal a universal secret network. She carries practical testimony upward and challenges orders that treat settlements as blank map resources.

## Narrative Function

She introduces field warrants, gathers petitions, and can become senior scout within any professional or ducal outcome.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
