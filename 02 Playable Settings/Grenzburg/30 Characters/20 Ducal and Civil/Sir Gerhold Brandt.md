# Sir Gerhold Brandt

Gerhold is Grenzburg's senior field marshal and the commander most identified with the winter defense. He is disciplined, personally brave, protective of soldiers, and convinced that a frontier survives only when service outlives the person serving.

That conviction contains the fault that destroys him. [[The Deep Muster]] begins waking independently through thaw, damaged seals, ancient infrastructure, and accumulated war dead. Gerhold neither causes the awakening nor serves a hidden faction. Once he learns that its command patterns can animate a tireless garrison, he knowingly accepts the Muster as Grenzburg's replacement army.

He understands that animated troops are corpse residue and command patterns, not trapped souls. His evil choice is therefore not accidental soul imprisonment. It is the conversion of bodies, death, and compulsory service into a permanent military instrument, followed by violence against anyone who refuses it.

Gerhold cannot return to ordinary command after that choice. During [[ACT III THE UNENDING MUSTER|Gerhold's Choice]] he is killed in battle or defeated and executed after due custody. There is no redemptive ending after he accepts the Muster. Earlier respect, faction service, and saved soldiers make the confrontation more tragic and may change who survives it, but not its moral result.
