# Castellan Pierre Hohenwall

## Core Concept

Grenzburg's professional castellan, responsible for fortification, field assignments, and disciplined service under law.

## Conviction and Blind Spot

Pierre is not a spotless procedure machine. He can mistake written regularity for justice and may accept harsh temporary measures. His defining virtue is willingness to place his own command under a workable civil rule.

## Narrative Function

He owns the professional-service track, Tannbruck coordination, and the charter test. He never plots a coup against Alaric.

## Outcome Range

This character may gain authority, accept limits, lose office, face judgement, withdraw, or die through explicit quest outcomes. No personal outcome becomes future-setting canon. Ordinary followers and beneficiaries remain distinct from the leader's choices.
