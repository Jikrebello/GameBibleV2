# Marta Etten

## Core Concept

The surviving passenger from the civilian coach crushed by Dismas Raben's robbery rockfall. She escaped later and spent years assembling enough proof to name him.

## Narrative Function

Marta wants public truth, material repair, and protection from intimidation. She owes Dismas neither reconciliation nor participation in his healing.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Marta Etten has interests independent of [[Dismas Raben]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
