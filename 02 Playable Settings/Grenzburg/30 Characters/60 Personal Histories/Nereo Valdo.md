# Nereo Valdo

Nereo Valdo is the Salt-Reed Company's frame-drum player, pulse line, and former road manager. He is thirty-eight, broad-handed, practical, and more interested in whether performers eat and arrive safely than in a patron's promises.

Nereo maintained bookings, wagons, lodging, wages, and instrument insurance until Ilyana began bypassing him. His refusal of the Gilded Wake season was partly professional: the offer hid the length of confinement and made no adequate provision for individual withdrawal.

## Position

- He will not perform under Ilyana's direction merely because lives are at stake.
- He may freely choose to help once a viable plan exists without his labor.
- He wants the common road accounts separated into individual shares.
- He believes Ilyana can change but refuses to become the person who proves it for her.

Nereo remains outside the playable worldspace. His refusal, road accounts, property instructions, and any voluntary technical aid arrive through authenticated recordings, notation, correspondence, and local agents. The physical Eel Bridge version of [[No Song by Command]] is a superseded structural placeholder pending Ilyana's paired rewrite.
