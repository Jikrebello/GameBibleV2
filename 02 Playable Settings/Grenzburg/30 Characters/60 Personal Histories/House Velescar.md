# House Velescar

House Velescar is a poor Virelian sabre-right house whose legal dignity outlasted its money, buildings, and ability to meet the obligations attached to noble display. Its history explains [[Elara Velescar]] without converting the house into a person that could demand her crimes.

## Holding

Before Elara's conviction, the Velescar holding contained:

- one decaying manor with an older chapel foundation;
- two grain villages;
- a minor river landing used by larger Vardek interests;
- sixty-three bound households recorded through estate rolls;
- a small birch wood and exhausted pasture;
- sabre-right standing in its district noble moot;
- substantial riding, river-work, and grain debt.

The holding is never a playable off-map region. It enters Grenzburg through records, goods, correspondence, remote testimony, authenticated household instructions, and material claims. No former Velescar resident travels to Grenzburg for the base-game campaign.

## Social Truth

The households feared absorption by a larger Vardek demesne estate because its labor demands were harsher and its right of movement narrower. Elara used that real danger to describe preservation of House Velescar as preservation of everyone beneath it.

The equation was false. The households were not asked whether the title justified grave robbery, new fines, or increased secrecy. Some wanted land; some wanted cash and freedom to leave; some valued familiar obligations more than an uncertain sale. The final settlement must preserve those differences.

## Decline

Velescar income failed through poor harvests, damaged river works, inherited riding debt, and the costs of maintaining noble participation. Elara inherited at twenty-three. She could have sold the landing, reduced the household, petitioned for a structured surrender of the title, or accepted Vardek absorption. Every path carried loss.

She instead accepted Vardek's antiquities patronage because it promised enough money to preserve both the holding and the adventure-shaped version of herself.

## Grave Economy

The house mark entered six Grey Sweat expeditions through Elara's survey cuts, tools, and private correspondence. Local factors recruited labor and converted coercion into apparently voluntary account records. [[Lord Marek Vardek]] financed the work and purchased goods through cutouts.

When burial tracing exposed the chain, Vardek surrendered Elara and vulnerable workers while retaining profitable material and the records that proved his control.

## Present Legal State

At the start of Grenzburg:

- Elara's title is suspended but not extinguished;
- Medori owns her personal restitution and service surety;
- Vardek interests hold options against the landing and estate debt;
- households remain bound under interim stewards;
- grave claimants have incomplete inventories;
- the decisive land rolls, title seal, and burial provenance are hidden in [[Old Tithe Vault]].

No single document morally owns the households. The records establish what can be transferred, released, compensated, or concealed.

## Outcome Range

**Relinquished:** Elara surrenders the title, viable land transfers to the sixty-three households through locally chosen parcels or commons, the landing becomes a shared revenue asset, and recoverable wealth funds burial repair.

**Reduced:** households receive freedom or partial compensation while Elara preserves a smaller title, reserve, and estate agency.

**Restored through patronage:** claims disappear, private wealth returns to the house, and Vardek or successor creditors regain influence. This is Elara's broken outcome.

No result becomes future-setting canon.
