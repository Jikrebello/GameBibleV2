# Oda Reed-Way

## Core Concept

Reader Oda Reed-Way is a forty-year-old Chapel-Folk household reader, winter-school teacher, and refuge-route instructor attached to Bent Oak Chapel in Longlake Ward. She organizes the recovery in [[The Wagon in the Reeds]] and the journey in [[Little Hands, Long Road]].

## Office and Authority

Oda can lead ordinary prayers, teach letters and road custom, maintain a school roll, and coordinate children under refuge law. She is neither ordained clergy nor a universal Folk representative. Her authority belongs to the participating households and the specific journey.

She values Bernard because he treats children's questions as real questions. She also refuses to let his warmth replace planning. The trip carries counted food, written guardians' consent, route bells, bandages, and two named shelter plans.

## The Seven Children

Oda supervises seven children aged nine to thirteen. Three receive principal authored beats:

- **Mika Reed-Kin, eleven:** quietly hides food for a younger foster sibling;
- **Ansel Pike, twelve:** reads tracks accurately but overestimates his ability to manage danger;
- **Tova Ash-Bent, nine:** becomes separated at the bear set piece and is physically saved by Bernard.

All seven travel as temporary quest NPCs outside the companion limit. They remain individually countable but use grouped movement and shelter behavior to avoid fragile escort design.
