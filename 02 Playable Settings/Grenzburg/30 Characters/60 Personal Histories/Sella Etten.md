# Sella Etten

## Core Concept

The surviving passenger from the coach crushed at Stonefall Cut. Pinned from midday until the soldiers came; dug out by the men whose approach made [[Dismas Raben]] run.

She never learned his name. She is not looking for it.

*(Renamed from Marta Etten to resolve a collision with Marta Vell. The old outline's arriving-claimant version is superseded — see below.)*

## Where She Is

Korrheim, one province from the road, in a house she rebuilt with a settlement from the coach line and eleven years of work. She keeps goats and a cart share, she is not poor, and she is not waiting for anything.

**She never enters the base-game worldspace,** and no version of this campaign brings her to Grenzburg. Everything she is reaches the player as two documents.

The previous treatment — Sella arriving with a civil charge naming Dismas, under threat from underworld brokers, requiring protection to a hearing — is retired. It let the world corner him into accountability, which is the one thing his campaign cannot afford; see [[Dismas Companion Campaign]].

## The Claim

Years ago she filed a civil claim in a Korrheim registry against *persons unknown*, because that is who the record said had done it. It has sat there since, unactioned, retrievable through [[Claims-Factor Marco Bellandi]] once somebody thinks to look — which nobody has, in eleven years, until Dismas does it himself.

The claim is quoted whole in [[The Stonefall Papers]]. It is written in the register of expenses, because that is the register the form asked for, and it is unbearable for exactly that reason.

## Her Response

`sella_etten_response` is a fixed instruction, selected by the filing Dismas produces in [[The Name on the Charge]] — never by approval, direction, dialogue, or persuasion:

| State | Condition |
|---|---|
| `record-accepted-no-contact` | a complete, truthful, unprompted filing that names the deaths. She acknowledges the corrected record and asks for nothing further. |
| `redirected-to-road-works` | she directs any restitution owed to her toward Korrheim road-safety works, and says why in one line. |
| `no-response` | an incomplete filing, a filing made under pressure, or an abandoned inquiry. Silence, permanently, which the campaign does not interpret for the player. |

**None is forgiveness.** The player cannot contact her, persuade her, or obtain a scene with her. She is not owed a reunion and does not want one.

## The Cruelest Available Truth

**She may not know that a person caused it.**

The record she was given said the road fell down. Eleven years of her life are built on an accident, and Dismas's filing hands her a man.

Whether that is repair or injury is the question underneath the Act II finale, and the campaign refuses to answer it: the truth is owed to the record regardless of whether it is a kindness to her, and Dismas has to decide whose need the confession serves *before* he can learn which it was.
