# Hospice of the Lantern Road

The Hospice of the Lantern Road is a Long Watch refuge compound on a cold Kedravan mountain route. It is the childhood home of [[Bernard Valeon]] and remains outside Grenzburg's playable map.

## Form

The compound combines a road chapel, long kitchen, two patient halls, foster dormitory, stable, bakehouse, winter store, beacon platform, and married lay households behind a defensible wall. It serves evacuees, pilgrims, injured soldiers, prisoners in exchange, and children whose families cannot be traced immediately.

## Household Rule

Long Watch poverty means vowed members possess little private property while the household remains obligated to feed, equip, and shelter them. Married members may maintain families; spouses and children are not made destitute to demonstrate holiness. Personal letters, clothing, and declared devotional objects may be retained.

The distinction failed emotionally for Bernard long before it failed institutionally. He could see a full store and still experience it as something that might be withdrawn without warning.

## Present Day

[[Hospice-Mother Radena Vosk]] oversees the household. The hospice replaced the stores lost during [[The Froststep Evacuation]] and retains no secret scandal surrounding Bernard. Some members favor reinstatement after restitution; others consider independent service healthier. Neither view controls his ending.
