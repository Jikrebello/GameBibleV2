# Patron-Captain Caterina Morante

## Core Concept

Caterina Morante is a prosperous Sermoran privateer captain and patron who first entered the Salt-Reed engagement in good faith, then knowingly enforced it after learning Ilyana had forged the other performers' consent.

## Presentation

Morante is forty-six, immaculate without being ornamental, and accustomed to speaking as though every room already accepts the practical necessity of her proposal. She wears a captain's dark coat with prize-court ribbons, carries no theatrical weapon, and remembers exactly which useful thing each person wants.

She is charming because she listens. She is dangerous because she treats information about another person's need as leverage.

## Motive and Culpability

Morante believes fleets, crews, patrons, and performers survive by honoring commitments after comfort ends. That principle becomes a chosen lie when she knows the commitment was never made.

She wants:

- control of the Salt-Reed repertoire during the disputed season;
- recovery of instrument and cancellation losses;
- recognition that the lawful first prize generated value;
- an example discouraging other contracted specialists from leaving during danger.

She is not responsible for Ilyana's original forgery. Once the truth emerged, Morante chose captivity, property seizure, threats, and continued battle service over release.

## Adventure Resilience

Morante may be:

- exposed and arrested under Grenzburg or Sermoran process;
- killed during justified resistance;
- forced to release the claims;
- permitted to retain a bounded property claim;
- driven from Grenzburg;
- or allowed to escape.

No later Ilyana chapter requires her survival. Her factors, documents, property holders, and the former troupe's unresolved relations carry the campaign forward without plot armour.

## Moral Boundary

Morante can lawfully defend herself, contest actual property, and demand a hearing. She cannot make forged consent real, command performers as people, or claim that wartime usefulness cancels refusal.
