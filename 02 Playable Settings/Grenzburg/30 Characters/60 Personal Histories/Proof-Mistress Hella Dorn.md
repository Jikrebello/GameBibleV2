# Proof-Mistress Hella Dorn

## Role

Hella Dorn is the Great Forge's independent load examiner and Grenzburg's principal [[Lancewright]] trainer. She certifies physical limits, names stop authorities, and treats a successful Yield as stronger evidence of mastery than a spectacular overproof.

## Character

Hella is blunt, dry, and more patient with ignorance than with concealed uncertainty. She has no personal hatred of [[Tavio Meran]], but refuses to let charm substitute for a filed measure.

Her rule is:

> If nobody is allowed to stop the test, it is not proof. It is a wager made with other people's bodies.

## Functions

Hella can:

- identify the Fifth Ring's over-capacity architecture;
- provide neutral control samples;
- train the player after [[The Fair Defeat]];
- establish worker stop authority;
- certify a mobile vent frame;
- testify without requiring one political faction;
- continue her role whether Maro or Seraf survives.

She does not decide Tavio's confession, apology, or final choice.
