# Celia Orsani

Celia Orsani is the Salt-Reed Company's bowed-viol player, mending-line performer, and instrument repairer. She is twenty-nine, observant, patient with damaged objects, and unwilling to confuse patience with acquiescence.

Celia's mending refrains saved Ilyana after several reckless performances. Ilyana converted that reliable care into an assumption that Celia would follow the company anywhere.

## Position

- She refused the privateer season and never transferred ownership of her viol.
- She wants her instrument, repair tools, unpaid share, and right to leave.
- She is willing to speak with Ilyana sooner than Mirella, but not to soften facts for her.
- She may support future emergency work under clear individual terms.

Celia remains outside the playable worldspace. Her property, repair ciphers, refusal, and any later response can enter Grenzburg; she cannot. The physical rescue version of [[The Road Without a Conductor]] remains only an audited structural source pending Ilyana's paired rewrite.
