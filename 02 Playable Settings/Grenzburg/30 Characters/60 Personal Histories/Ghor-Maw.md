# Ghor-Maw

Ghor-Maw is the war-chief who turns a hard southern migration into the Bloody-Sun conquest of Grenzburg. Hunger, displaced routes, and competition among Grobi bands explain why a host can form; they do not require Ghor to target civilians, hold dependents close to his army, promise plunder, or demand one crown over groups that do not owe him obedience.

## Public Character

Ghor presents himself as the chief willing to seize what weaker leaders ask permission to survive. He rewards visible courage, distributes captured status, and converts every concession into proof that a harder demand would also have worked. His appeal is material before it is grand: food, walls, winter stores, and a victory large enough to make rival bands follow him.

## Military Method

Ghor coordinates otherwise competing forces through the Southern Horn, captured human signals, route intelligence, reserve beasts, and ration priority. He studies roads and bridge mechanisms rather than relying upon supernatural command. His probes at Tannbruck are reconnaissance for a campaign he has already chosen.

The host remains internally plural:

- Uru's dependents are labor, leverage, and food pressure rather than willing assault troops;
- Grakka's Stone-Tooth fighters seek survival and status without sharing every Bloody-Sun aim;
- Skiv's trader-captains profit through double dealing;
- other bands can scatter, surrender locally, or withdraw when coordination breaks.

## Moral Ruling

Ghor has full moral agency. Conditions may make migration necessary; they do not make conquest, hostage-taking, civilian attack, or slavery necessary. He cannot be negotiated into a good ruler of Grenzburg and will not surrender once his final command collapses.

## Campaign Arc

- In Act I, his scouts map the Upper Grenz approaches and exploit deserter routes without causing the bandit or Folk conflicts.
- In Act II, he takes Tannbruck, breaches Grenzburg by force, employs the Cinder Knives opportunistically, and commits his host to a final assault after the Southern Horn falls.
- In [[Ghor at the Gate]], he is killed and the organized siege breaks in every completed campaign.
- His death does not erase Uru, Grakka, Skiv, prisoners, dependents, splinter bands, or migration pressure.

## Combat Identity

Ghor is a physical war-chief: heavy polearm, captured shield work, bodyguard rotations, horn signals, retreat lanes, and personal endurance. He has no patron, prophecy, spell of invulnerability, connection to the Emerald Drake, or role in the Deep Muster.
