# Sandro Tessari

## Core Concept

Sandro Tessari is a crane-hand at the Ledger Quays, a Virelian emigrant in his thirties, and the only person in Grenzburg whom Elara Velescar personally harmed.

He is not a claimant, a quest, or an obstacle. He is a fixed point. Everything the campaign otherwise offers Elara — refusal from strangers, restitution to people she never wronged, forgiveness she can earn through competence — becomes cheap in his presence, which is why he exists.

## Aldi Tessari

His mother, **Aldi Tessari**, dug for House Velescar.

She was recruited into one of the six Grey Sweat expeditions through an arrangement she could not safely refuse — food debt against a season's work, papers she could not read, a lady who was kind to her in front of others. She handled burial lime for eighteen months. It took the skin off her hands in sheets and it took her lungs slowly, over years, in Grenzburg, where she and Sandro came after the house fell and the work dried up.

She died two winters before the game opens. Her name is on the Velescar labour rolls, and the campaign's finale can recover the tube it is written in.

## The Inversion

Elara remembers names after one introduction. It is her gift and her currency; the bio is explicit about it.

**She does not know Sandro's face.** She never did. The diggers' children were not people she performed for — they were outside the room where the performing happened, and her memory only holds what the room contained.

He knows hers on sight. He was fourteen.

## Behaviour Rules — Fixed

These do not vary by playthrough, preparation, direction, approval, or outcome. `sandro_state` does not move.

- He refuses her coin.
- He refuses her introductions, her advocacy, and her offers of work.
- He refuses to testify against Vardek. Not from fear — he simply will not do it *for her*, and no other framing is offered to him.
- **He refuses to perform anger or forgiveness.** He is not a grieving son on the verge of tears and not a man nursing a grudge; he is a crane-hand with a shift to finish.
- He never becomes a quest, never gates content, never blocks a route, and never appears in a preparation table.

## The Exchange

One fixed conversation, placed between Acts II and III at the Quays — or during the quay traffic of [[The Winter Company]] if the player reaches it first. A few lines, total. It happens once and is never reprised.

> **Sandro:** I know you.
>
> *(Elara begins the introduction. Her hand is already moving.)*
>
> **Sandro:** No. You don't have to do anything.
>
> *(He goes back to the hook. Then, without turning:)*
>
> **Sandro:** I wanted you to know that I know.

Nothing sharper. He does not name his mother, the lime, the house, or the graves in this scene. He is the anti-aphorist: the one person in Elara's campaign whose refusal is not eloquent, not shaped for use, and not available for anyone's improvement.

Elara's ceremonial-politeness tell may fire. Nobody stops her; nobody is impressed.

## Appearances

## The Rolls

In No Lady of Velescar's burial gallery, one recovered tube among the labour and burial rolls carries **Aldi Tessari's** name and hand-mark.

If the player has met Sandro, that ledger line has a face, and the campaign's central metaphor closes: the paper Elara spends the whole game recovering is made of people, and one of them is a man she has walked past for three seasons.

The recognition is available and never forced. No dialogue announces it.

## Resolution

In the resolved ending, restitution reaches Sandro through the settlement like any other claimant. He signs for it, or doesn't, per the household terms.

He nods, and walks off.

That is the entire payoff, and it is deliberate: **the best available ending does not move him either.** No outcome earns his gratitude, his testimony, his anger, or his absolution. He is what harm looks like once the harm is finished — a person with his own afternoon.
