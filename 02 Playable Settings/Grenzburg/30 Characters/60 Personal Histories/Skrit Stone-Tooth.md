# Skrit Stone-Tooth

Skrit is a Stone-Tooth scout, broker, and opportunistic interpreter who knows several Grobi migration corridors south of Tannbruck. He is not one of the eight companions and receives no romance, sworn bond, loyalty arc, or redemptive transformation into a human moral outlook.

Skrit understands restraint, exchange, fear, appetite, and reputation. He may trade routes, identify Ghor's recruiters, carry ransom terms, betray an agreement for a better advantage, or honor a narrow promise because future exchange matters. Every arrangement is band-specific and action-specific.

Cooperation never grants Skrit authority over all Grobi, proves predation virtuous, or makes him safe around vulnerable people without terms and supervision. He can survive, die, flee, become a recurring broker, or become an enemy according to later regional quests. The active campaign assumes none of those outcomes.
