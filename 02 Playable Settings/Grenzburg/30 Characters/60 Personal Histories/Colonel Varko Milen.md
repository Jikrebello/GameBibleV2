# Colonel Varko Milen

## Core Concept

A decorated Vlastravan commander who authored the false report covering Ilyana Veshkar's fatal breach. He believes the lie preserved a fortress and still treats unnamed soldiers as a command resource.

## Narrative Function

He brings genuine winter artillery expertise to Grenzburg, making exposure materially costly. He may accept a private correction, defend the public lie, or withdraw; he is never an occult conspirator.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Colonel Varko Milen has interests independent of [[Ilyana Veshkar]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
