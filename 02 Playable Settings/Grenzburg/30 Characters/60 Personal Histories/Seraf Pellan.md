# Seraf Pellan

## Role

Seraf Pellan is a travelling proof-broker who supplied the [[Red Brass Bench]] with over-capacity rings, substituted ore certificates, and unsigned fittings. He knows how to make a dangerous device appear to belong to a lawful batch.

## Motive

Seraf is not devoted to Tavio, Maro, or arena glory. He believes every filed limit creates a market for someone willing to exceed it discreetly. War in Grenzburg offers him a new market among frightened officers and hurried foundries.

## Methods

- duplicate batch stamps;
- split shipments;
- bought signatures;
- forged control samples;
- pressure on injured workers;
- hired escorts and ordinary violence;
- destroying one register while preserving a more valuable copy.

He has no supernatural immunity and no secret patron. He may be exposed, arrested, killed during justified resistance, or escape without breaking later stages.

## Narrative Use

Seraf provides continuity between unsafe fittings without becoming a universal mastermind. If removed early, Red Brass quartermasters, copied records, and field failures carry the evidence forward.
