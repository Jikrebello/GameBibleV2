# Lord Marek Vardek

## Core Concept

A Virelian magnate who financed Elara's plague-grave robberies, retained the profitable objects, and sold the workers and junior noblewoman to judgement when discovery approached.

## Narrative Function

He arrives as a respectable relief patron with real grain warrants. He uses genuine public benefit as protection for chosen exploitation and offers restoration at the price of silence.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Lord Marek Vardek has interests independent of [[Elara Velescar]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
