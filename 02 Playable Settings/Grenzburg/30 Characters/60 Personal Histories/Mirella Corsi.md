# Mirella Corsi

Mirella Corsi is the Salt-Reed Company's singer, principal composer, and scorekeeper. She is thirty-four, deliberate, dryly funny, and the least susceptible to Ilyana's claim that enthusiasm can repair a decision already made.

Mirella co-founded the company. Her annotations turned improvised performances into repeatable repertoire, and several works publicly credited to the Salt-Reeds are primarily hers.

## Position

- She refused the Gilded Wake engagement before Ilyana forged approval.
- She does not seek revenge or reunion.
- She wants correct authorship, return of her scores, release from every service claim, and freedom to decide whether she ever performs with Ilyana again.
- She can voluntarily assist during immediate danger without forgiving what happened.

Mirella's strongest personal outcome is an independent composing and teaching practice. Friendship with Ilyana remains possible but is never owed.

Mirella remains outside the playable worldspace. Her authorship, refusal, evidence, and any later answer reach Grenzburg through scores, sworn testimony, correspondence, and escrow instructions.
