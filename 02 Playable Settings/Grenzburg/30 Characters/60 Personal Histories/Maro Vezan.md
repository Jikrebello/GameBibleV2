# Maro Vezan

## Role

Maro Vezan is [[Tavio Meran]]'s former coach and the senior public face of the [[Red Brass Bench]]. He is a patient technical teacher, a gifted reader of fighters, and a man who believes rules should protect ordinary competitors but may be exceeded by the rare people capable of surviving more.

That belief makes him dangerous precisely because it contains real knowledge.

## The Fifth Ring

Maro conceived the Fifth Ring, arranged its installation through [[Seraf Pellan]], and told Tavio that carrying the excess load would make the result his responsibility. Tavio accepted that argument because it sounded like courage.

After [[Lio Varcen]] was maimed, Maro:

- removed the private load sheet;
- instructed witnesses to describe an unpredictable fracture;
- protected the Bench's senior officials;
- encouraged Tavio to accept the title;
- later prepared to offer Tavio as the sole culprit if broader exposure became likely.

## Methods

Maro prefers persuasion, professional loyalty, selective truth, and reputation pressure. He resorts to sabotage or hired violence only after records and protected witnesses place the Bench in real danger.

He may be exposed, detained abroad, removed from the Bench, or remain at large. No later Tavio chapter requires his cooperation or freedom.

Maro remains outside the playable worldspace. He exerts pressure through Red Brass records, local affiliates, equipment, correspondence, and remote institutional action. Active Tavio quests confront his local network and written choices rather than his body.

## View of Tavio

Maro genuinely loves Tavio as a student and still treats that love as a claim on his decisions. His most effective appeal is not denial:

> We made you capable of choosing. Do not pretend the choice began with the ring.

He is correct about Tavio's agency and wrong that this absolves the institution that prepared and concealed the fraud.
