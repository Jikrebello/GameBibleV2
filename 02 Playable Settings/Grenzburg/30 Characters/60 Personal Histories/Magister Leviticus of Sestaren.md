# Magister Leviticus of Sestaren

Leviticus is a Sermoran-trained antiquarian and licensed ruin assessor working from the Delvers' Arcade. He identifies Atherian civil works, prices salvage, teaches field notation, and irritates nearly everyone by distinguishing what an object is from what its owner hopes it is.

He believes dangerous knowledge should remain usable under competent custody. That position makes him valuable and sometimes overconfident, especially when ordinary laborers bear the first risk of his tests. He can support delver, Warlock Hall, and artifact-economy secondary quests without leading a principal faction.

Leviticus is not the winter betrayer, does not steal the Heart-Stone, and has no Pale-Skin connection. Those roles belong only to archived campaign history. In the rebuilt game he may become a trusted assessor, censured scholar, rival contractor, or casualty through later secondary content; no outcome is fixed here.
