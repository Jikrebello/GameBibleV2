# Yrsa Wolf-Brand

## Core Concept

The dead champion's sister and a lawful hall advocate. She rejects both the champion's exclusionary custom and attempts to pretend his surrender did not matter.

## Narrative Function

Yrsa seeks named responsibility and durable limits, not Svala's humiliation. Her verdict can recognize exile, conditional hall pardon, or unresolved blood claim.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Yrsa Wolf-Brand has interests independent of [[Svala Last-Beacon]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
