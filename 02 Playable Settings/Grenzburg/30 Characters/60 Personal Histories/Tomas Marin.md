# Tomas Marin

Tomas Marin is the Salt-Reed Company's horn player, public signaler, and elemental-cadence specialist. He is twenty-six, eager for difficult work, proud of clean technical performance, and furious that Ilyana used his appetite for adventure as evidence that he had agreed.

Tomas enjoyed the lawful prize action. He still refused the season that followed. Both facts remain true.

## Position

- He wants personal credit for the weapon cadences he developed.
- He rejects the claim that enjoying one dangerous success consents to every later operation.
- He may be the first former member to perform beside Ilyana again and the last to call it reconciliation.
- He treats battle compulsion as lethal technique, not a joke or social shortcut.

Tomas remains outside the playable worldspace. His horn, cadence plates, authorship claim, recorded aid, and refusal may reach Grenzburg through ordinary transport and authenticated correspondence.
