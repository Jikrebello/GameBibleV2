# Hospice-Mother Radena Vosk

## Core Concept

Radena Vosk is the sixty-one-year-old senior lay steward of the [[Hospice of the Lantern Road]] and the principal caregiver of [[Bernard Valeon|Bernard's]] childhood. **Hospice-Mother** is an administrative and affectionate title, not monastic rank or ordination.

## Life and Authority

Radena married young, was widowed during a road-fever winter, and raised one surviving daughter alongside successive cohorts of fostered children. She entered Long Watch service as a cook and store clerk, later becoming keeper of the hospice household, intake slate, foster placements, and winter pantry.

She taught Bernard letters through casualty rolls and gave him his wooden-beaded prayer rope when he entered adult service. The rope was a personal gift, openly entered among the few devotional objects permitted under Long Watch poverty.

## Relationship with Bernard

Radena loves Bernard without calling him innocent. She was not present at Froststep and does not presume to speak for Petra or the other injured evacuees. Her letters consistently distinguish:

- hunger from fasting;
- an urge from an act;
- confession from repair;
- useful supervision from permanent childhood;
- freely received property from secret possession.

She refuses to travel to Grenzburg merely to deliver absolution. During [[Embers Without Excuse]], [[Claims-Factor Marco Bellandi]] authenticates a current letter in which she tells Bernard that returning to the order is possible but must not become another place to hide his choices.

## Agency and Limits

Radena can confirm Bernard's upbringing, the prayer rope's origin, and Long Watch household practice. She cannot forgive Froststep on behalf of the injured, command Grenzburg's Church, clear Medori debt, or decide Bernard's destination.
