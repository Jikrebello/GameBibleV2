# Grenzburg Campaign Branch Matrix

This is the controlling branch reference beneath [[Three-Act Overview]]. Each required beat has a player-controlled trigger, two-employer conflict, fail-forward state, and measurable output.

## State Vocabulary

- **DT:** Ducal Trust, 0-4.
- **BL:** Bank Leverage, 0-4.
- **Concessions:** Church, Bank, Folk, and External tracks, each 0-4.
- **Material state:** food weeks, fuel weeks, garrison strength, refugee load, disease risk, Grobi cohesion, and Heart-Stone custody.
- **Companion state:** recruited, loyal, unresolved, departed, or broken.

## Alternate Ending Locks

- **Bank's Iron Peace:** Bank concession or BL reaches 4; the Bank controls recovery or settlement finance.
- **Church's Ember Peace:** Church concession reaches 4 and relic jurisdiction decides the crisis.
- **Folk's Old Peace:** Folk concession reaches 4, the sanctuary survives, and expansion is surrendered.
- **External Dependency:** no local coalition controls the spring settlement and foreign aid becomes permanent authority.
- **Hollow Peace:** the player accepts the final bargain or attempts to retain both Stones' power.

These are complete playable outcomes. The Duke's Peace is the authorial continuation used by later setting material.
