# Act III: The Unending Muster

## Act Identity

Act III begins with return, burial, work, and land reclamation, then escalates through disciplined residue formations into a regional dead march and the final mega-dungeon. Its horror is order: service that continues after every rightful cause and treats bodies, equipment, roads, and settlements as replaceable military material.

## Anchor Campaign

Cold-Iron is always destroyed in MQ-16. MQ-17 opens Watch Hill, River Arsenal, Tuskway, and Fen Ossuary operations. Three destroyed anchors make the finale possible; all five remove every regional boss power. Remaining anchors close only when the player knowingly starts the Dead March.

## Gerhold Contract

The recurrence begins independently. Gerhold later understands its nature and accepts command because he wants a garrison incapable of hunger, desertion, age, or discharge. His winter heroism remains true; his later choice remains evil. He is killed in battle or defeated, judged, and executed. There is no pardon or redemptive command ending.

## Residue Contract

Muster soldiers are corpse residue, equipment memory, and command patterns. Familiar words and gestures do not contain routed human souls. Compassion governs burial and treatment of remains; it does not require pretending the formation is the person.

## Final Dragon Contract

The General Below is a sovereign Abyssal true dragon embodying eternal conscription. Its battlefield avatar uses corpse residue, black mud, eel-like coils, a lamprey mouth, and crab-armored replacement limbs. It is neither a drake nor a biological species. Every completed campaign stops it and destroys the deepest altar.

## Open-World Windows

Early and Open Spring support reclamation, companions, factions, anchors, and transformed side content. MQ-20 gives the exact point-of-no-return warning. Summer follows victory without a formal credits cutoff.
