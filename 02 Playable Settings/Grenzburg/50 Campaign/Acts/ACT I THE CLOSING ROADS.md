# Act I: The Closing Roads

## Act Identity

Act I is the grounded frontier tour. Human bandits and deserters dominate settled roads; particular hostile Folk war-bands contest passage, occupation sites, and expansion. Predators, ordinary ruins, and early Grobi scouts add texture without announcing a universal conspiracy.

The act teaches the region by repeatedly sending the player out from Grenzburg to solve understandable problems. Faction and companion content opens between main chapters, letting the player establish their own route through autumn.

## Threat Discipline

- Bandit groups have specific leaders, profits, and methods.
- Deserters may have real grievances and remain responsible for chosen crimes.
- Shattered-Tusk and total-war followers do not represent all Folk.
- Anya, Runa, Chapel-Folk, River-Clans, Ghost-Foot households, and opponents of civilian murder remain distinct.
- Ghor's probes foreshadow a larger campaign without secretly causing every autumn threat.
- Qianglong Muster signs do not appear as an organized enemy in Act I.

## Open-World Windows

Early Autumn opens after the warrant. Open Autumn begins with the Southbound Muster and contains the broadest faction, recruitment, trainer, exploration, and local-story space. The Last Convoy warning starts Late Autumn; Tannbruck's fall resolves exposed work through authored outcomes rather than erasing it.

## Fixed and Variable

Tannbruck falls. The player cannot solve the regional geometry by winning one defense. Who escapes, what is denied, which roads remain usable, who trusts the Duke or Folk kindreds, and what reaches Grenzburg determine the winter that follows.
