# Prologue: The Black Road

## Purpose

The prologue teaches the game through an immediate human emergency. The player learns who they are, what a Blackjack is, how companions behave, and why Grenzburg needs outsiders before receiving a lecture about the world.

## Chapters

1. [[The Black Road Main Quest|The Black Road]]: toll-bandits sabotage the debtor convoy; unrelated Grobi scouts exploit the wreck. Rescue, pursuit, supply protection, traversal, party commands, and first social choices occur inside one crisis.
2. [[Warrant at the Gate]]: the surviving convoy crosses Funnel Camp, meets the campaign's field authorities, enters the city, and receives a ducal warrant without escaping Medori debt.

## Onboarding Order

1. movement, rescue, and grounded combat;
2. the player plus Ilyana, Bernard, and Dismas as an improvised party;
3. bandits, road conditions, and ordinary civilians;
4. Grobi as a separate opportunistic threat;
5. debt, Blackjack command, and ducal authority;
6. the city and wider faction world after the player has a practical reason to care.

## Output

The convoy always reaches Grenzburg. Survivors, cargo, bandits, Grobi warning, Funnel Camp, and the player's first visible tendencies vary. All eight surface districts open after the warrant; selected interiors and traversal layers remain content-gated.
