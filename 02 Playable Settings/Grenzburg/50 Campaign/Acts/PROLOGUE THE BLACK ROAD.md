# Prologue: The Black Road

The prologue introduces the player through action rather than explanation. A Medori debtor convoy travels south toward Grenzburg as autumn traffic chokes the northern road. Something grounded and legible turns the journey into a rescue and survival operation. The exact hostile incident is reserved for Wave 5, but it must arise from road conditions, human violence, animals, Grobi opportunism, or a credible combination rather than the final supernatural plot.

## Fixed Functions

The prologue teaches:

- movement, camera, interaction, looting, and basic survival;
- the selected starting vocation in a practical encounter;
- party commands and the first differences among companions;
- rescue, stabilization, and carrying or escorting vulnerable people;
- a social check with a visible governing skill and risk band;
- debt, Blackjack status, and the distinction between Koss's authority and ducal need;
- field discovery and a small optional detour;
- that failure changes survivors, supplies, injuries, and reception at the gate.

## Information Budget

The player needs to understand only:

- they owe a debt;
- the Bank has converted that debt into dangerous service;
- Grenzburg needs specialists because its roads are closing;
- the convoy contains other people in comparable service for different reasons;
- Grobi and bandits are immediate regional dangers, not interchangeable species labels.

The prologue does not explain the World Egg, all nine cultures, the Left Hand, Menhir history, Qianglong, the Deep Muster, or the final dragon.

## Chapter 1: The Black Road

The opening crisis must have at least three simultaneous but readable priorities: survive the attack or collapse, save specific people, and prevent a material loss or secondary disaster. The player can excel at all three through attention and skill, but early failure never demands a reload.

At least one companion fights beside the player. At least one future companion or recurring debtor is seen under pressure. Their behavior establishes personality before biography.

## Chapter 2: Warrant at the Gate

Arrival resolves convoy consequences and shifts into a short playable admission sequence. The player receives a ducal field warrant through the Blackjack secondment, encounters Koss's debt authority, gains a base or bunk, and chooses what to investigate first inside Grenzburg.

The chapter ends by opening the city rather than by immediately forcing another briefing. Players may visit services, speak to companions, find local work, and orient themselves before beginning [[ACT I THE CLOSING ROADS|The Closing Roads]].

## Prohibited Opening Moves

- no prisoner-cart imitation without a distinct debtor convoy purpose;
- no long cinematic lore lecture;
- no full faction council;
- no prophecy, secret bloodline, or unique immunity;
- no final villain cameo;
- no companion roll call;
- no false choice whose only result is the same off-screen rescue;
- no requirement that the player already know the setting's vocabulary.
