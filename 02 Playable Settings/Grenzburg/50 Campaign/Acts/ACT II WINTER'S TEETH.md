# Act II: Winter's Teeth

## Act Identity

Act II is a three-strand winter campaign: a city physically under siege, a mandatory hunt through transformed Southwood, and shock operations against Ghor's coalition. The city supplies dense political and underworld play while dangerous winter expeditions keep the exterior relevant.

## City Breach Contract

The Grobi cross the frozen river and break the damaged western defenses by military action. The player fights through Lantern Ward, rescues residents, restores Millwheel pressure, contains fires, and physically closes the breach. The later Cinder Knives did not cause this attack; they try to reuse its damage for Ghor.

## Drake Contract

The Emerald Drake is animal court-fauna displaced by logging, refugee fuel-cutting, and Grobi war fires. It cannot speak or consent. The hunt is mandatory because its territory has closed a vital route. Custody of its law-gem is a consequential institutional choice, not a key to the final dragon.

## Grobi Contract

Ghor knowingly chooses conquest. Uru's dependents, Grakka's Stone-Tooth fighters, Skiv's traders, prisoners, and scattered bands remain distinct. Protecting noncombatants or accepting bounded withdrawal does not make predation harmless; defeating raiders does not authorize collective punishment.

## Open-World Windows

Early Winter begins after the Tannbruck intake. Deep Winter opens around the hunt and urban investigation. Ghor's final assault displays exact quest transformations before advancing to spring.
