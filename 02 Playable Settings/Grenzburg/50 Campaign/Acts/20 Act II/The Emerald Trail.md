# The Emerald Trail

## Chapter Function

This chapter teaches the Emerald Drake through signs, ecology, and territory before the boss hunt. It proves why the animal moved, makes preparation concrete, and lets the player learn Southwood rather than following a glowing trail directly to the lair.

## Trigger and Commitment

Ashfield Lodge sends word that the Drake's silence belt now crosses the winter supply trail. The quest can be pursued before or after MQ-10. Its field investigations are open and may be left between sites; entering the final range survey is a closed operation.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Two sleds vanished without signs of robbery. A third returned with sleeping draft animals, pollen in sealed boxes, and wheels cut by roots that had grown across a used track. The Southwood route cannot remain open while the territorial order covers it.

## Beginning: Signs at Ashfield

The player meets surviving hunters and reconstructs the changed range. If AO-6 was completed, the mapped edge and rescued hunters provide a safe starting camp. Otherwise, the party must find Hunter's Last Mark during poor visibility.

The first survey teaches five Emerald signs:

- animal paths ending at exact boundaries;
- circular sleep sites;
- silence that begins before visual change;
- pollen moving against wind;
- shed scale flakes near ordered root growth.

## Middle: Why the Range Moved

Three sites establish the cause:

- **Charcoal Hollow:** refugee fuel-cutting crossed old hunter warnings during the autumn intake.
- **Split Pine Ravine:** ducal-authorized logging widened a route without understanding that the heart-oak line was territorial.
- **First Snow Cairn:** Grobi war fires burned the last continuous root corridor south of the range.

No one actor planned the displacement. Officials and cutters remain responsible for ignored warnings; Ghor's fighters remain responsible for deliberate fire; none created or controlled the Drake.

At each site the player can secure one preparation:

1. smoke hoods and sealed breathing cloth;
2. clean-wind bellows or warlock gust scripts;
3. cross-cut escape paths marked from outside the law field;
4. controlled fire pots that burn pollen without igniting the wood;
5. a heart-oak route reconstructed from hunter and Folk testimony.

Three preparations are sufficient to start the hunt. All five produce the strongest route and protect more assistants.

## End: Mark the Heart-Oak

The final survey enters the outer silence belt to locate the Drake's winter return path. Court-fauna reactions, moving game boundaries, and one direct pass by the Drake force the party to use its preparations and withdraw rather than fight prematurely.

The player marks the heart-oak root vault from a distant ridge. Dismas or any trained tracker establishes the return interval; without them, repeated observation provides the same fact at greater exposure.

Ashfield Lodge prepares the hunt. The player may continue Deep Winter content before accepting [[A Drake in Winter]].

## Encounters and Opposition

- environmental tracking in snow and ordered woodland;
- ordinary winter predators displaced toward settlements;
- pollen sleep zones and false paths;
- rescue of one late cutting party;
- non-boss Drake pass requiring concealment, wind, or retreat.

## Information Paths

Cut stumps, work orders, refugee testimony, burned roots, Grobi fire gear, hunters' marks, and Folk boundary memory establish the compounded cause. Missing one site never changes the Drake into a mystery mastermind; it reduces preparation and public accountability.

## Choices and State Changes

- `drake_hunt_preparations` records each of five preparations.
- `drake_displacement_truth` becomes full, partial-human, partial-war-fire, or misunderstood.
- `heart_oak_route` becomes mapped, hazardous, or brute-force.

## Fail-Forward Results

- A sleeping party wakes at a hunter cache with time lost and one preparation damaged.
- A rescued cutter may be wounded or lost, but their tools still identify the cut line.
- If the Drake destroys an observation blind, scale traces reveal its return path.
- Failed public proof lets one responsible official deny fault, affecting summer restoration rather than hunt access.

## Companion Intersections

Dismas reads travel and prey. Ashira correctly identifies the territory as non-conscious law rather than spirit dialogue. Ilyana and Tavio create controlled wind or fire tools. Svala, Bernard, Elara, and Zafir provide rescue, traversal, or bounded-form solutions.

## Faction Advantages

Folk work supplies the cleanest boundary memory. Ducal and Warlock work supplies engineering. Church work supplies disciplined evacuation. Blackjacks supply field gear. Underworld routes can obtain scarce breathing cloth. No faction is required.

## Cutoff and Unlocks

Three preparations and the marked heart-oak unlock [[A Drake in Winter]]. Beginning the hunt resolves any unfinished preparation site through a named weaker version and closes no unrelated Deep Winter content.
