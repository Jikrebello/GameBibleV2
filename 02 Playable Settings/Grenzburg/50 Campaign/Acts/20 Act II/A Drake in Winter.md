# A Drake in Winter

## Chapter Function

This is the mandatory large-monster hunt and Act II's principal wilderness spectacle. Preparation changes traversal, assistants, boss phases, and aftermath. The Drake remains an animal throughout; its death creates a custody problem because territorial law persists without a mind.

## Trigger and Commitment

At least three hunt preparations and a heart-oak route are required. Accepting Ashfield Lodge's departure warning starts a closed hunt that ends only through retreat or the Drake's death. Retreat preserves progress but raises territorial pressure and may cost assistants.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The silence belt has reached the sled route and will close the last reliable Southwood supply crossing within days. Driving the Drake away is no longer stable: the damaged root network leaves no intact neighboring territory, and previous deterrence only displaced attacks.

## Beginning: Cross the Silence

The party and a small hunt team enter through the mapped route. Preparation alters the approach:

- breathing gear prevents pollen attrition;
- clean wind reveals false trails;
- cross-cut paths create retreat and flanking lanes;
- controlled fire clears sleep growth without a forest burn;
- the heart-oak route bypasses one outer territorial loop.

Without a preparation, the same obstacle remains solvable through slower observation, consumables, combat, or exposure cost.

The player rescues or abandons one separated hunter team and disables three root knots that let the Drake fold paths back toward its vault.

## Middle: The Ordered Range

The Drake attacks across Split Pine Ravine and the winter canopy. Its phases express ecology:

1. **Territorial passes:** it strikes from screened routes and retreats into silence.
2. **Pollen court:** wounded movement releases sleep pollen and directs animals along exact lanes.
3. **Root-vault defense:** it uses the heart-oak chamber, scale armor, and law-gem pulses to impose stillness and false distance.

Climbing the Drake is possible but not mandatory. Fighter creates guarded openings; Slayer can climb and cut scale seams; Hunter controls range and reads passes; Warlock counters pollen and roots; Medium fields expose repeated movement. An unlocked Lancewright can Brace or Break marked scales, but every starting vocation has a complete route.

## End: The Empty Court

The Drake dies permanently. It does not speak, thank the player, reveal prophecy, or become the General Below's key. Its death leaves:

- a law-gem still imposing territorial relations;
- a damaged heart-oak network;
- suppressed predators and growth beginning to return;
- valuable but regulated scale and gland materials.

The player secures the site, rescues remaining hunters, and chooses custody according to [[Emerald Law-Gem Custody Matrix]]:

- Folk land-keepers reseat it;
- ducal Warlock Hall engineers build the Green Lattice;
- Church custodians create field sanctuaries;
- the player's unit installs the Green Ward at Blackjack Yard.

The choice is explained before confirmation, including the Act III aid and Southwood cost. No custody outcome becomes setting canon.

## Encounters and Opposition

- silence-belt traversal and root-knot objectives;
- displaced animal encounters;
- ravine and canopy Drake phases;
- heart-oak vault boss fight;
- post-kill ecological containment.

## Information Paths

The Drake's routes are learned from physical signs, preparation sites, and repeated behavior. The law-gem is identified through Folk testimony, Ashira's ontology, Warlock Hall measurement, or hunter records. Every route reaches the same core truth: animal mind, persistent territorial instrument.

## Choices and State Changes

- `emerald_drake_dead` becomes true.
- `emerald_law_gem_custody` records folk, ducal, church, or blackjack-yard.
- `southwood_winter_state` records surviving hunters, route damage, returning predators, and heart-oak restoration.

## Fail-Forward Results

- A failed outer approach costs supplies and moves the party to a marked retreat camp.
- Lost assistants reduce post-fight rescue and material recovery, not boss access.
- Destroyed preparation tools remove their advantage from later phases.
- If the player damages the heart-oak excessively, every custody option must spend more effort on containment and summer restoration worsens.
- A party defeat produces rescue by the remaining hunt team and a more wounded but more alert Drake on re-entry.

## Companion Intersections

Dismas and Ashira receive primary ecological reactions. Tavio and Ilyana support tools; Svala and Bernard protect hunters; Elara exploits climb routes; Zafir can direct covenanted nonhuman forms but cannot bind the Drake or its gem. No companion is mandatory.

## Faction Advantages

Faction progress supplies preparations, assistants, and custody credibility. The custody decision does not commit the player to a faction line or decide its political ending.

## Cutoff and Unlocks

Completion reopens Southwood in a dangerous changed winter state, enables the selected Act III gem aid, and unlocks MQ-14 once [[Knives Behind the Walls]] is also complete. It does not advance the season.
