# Embers Without Excuse

## Role in the Arc

This is Bernard Valeon's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Bernard Valeon duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Vaust takes refuge among winter dead-bearers and prepares to kill the witness who can identify him. At the same time, false relic ash is causing panic in the Cathedral Close.

Protect the witness, expose the murderer, and test whether Bernard can receive White-Flame strength without treating it as a declaration of innocence.

## Player Actions

Guard burial routes, distinguish genuine residue from staged signs, confront Vaust, and return the original stolen token during a quiet rite at the cloister.

## Opposition and Pressure

Vaust manipulates Bernard's shame and the Ashen Commandery wants a public spectacle. Bernard's old order offers restoration if the case stays private.

## Information Paths

The witness, Vaust's route book, and the recovered token prove identity; Cathedral examination and a hospital ledger disprove the relic panic.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Accept lay responsibility and public restitution, return to the order under secrecy, or pursue Vaust at the expense of the witness. A genuine White-Flame ember answers only the first two coherent paths and never erases the crime.

## Fail-Forward Results

If Vaust escapes again, he is wounded and loses his disguise network. If the witness is harmed, written testimony preserves prosecution but Bernard's resolution becomes harder.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If left until the Muster Crisis, the confrontation occurs under emergency conditions: one evidence path closes, the relationship cannot commit until the issue is faced, and the final quest begins from a compromised state.

## Rewards and Return Value

Unlocks Paladin, permits romance or sworn-bond commitment, and establishes that grace strengthens restitution rather than replacing it.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
