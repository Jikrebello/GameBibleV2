# The Pilgrim's Mark

## Role in the Arc

This is Bernard Valeon's quest 2 of four. It is playable in Open Autumn and Early Winter. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Bernard Valeon duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A marked token matching Bernard's old theft appears on a body recovered at Old Coach Burn. The killer, Gerren Vaust, may be using the winter refugee stream to enter Grenzburg.

Identify the murdered pilgrim correctly and prevent another killing without allowing Bernard to turn the hunt into self-punishment.

## Player Actions

Search the wreck, restore the shrine register, question coach survivors, and track Vaust's false pilgrim route through the Lowlands.

## Opposition and Pressure

Vaust is an ordinary murderer who exploits hospitality and changing names. Bernard becomes reckless whenever he thinks capture could erase his old failure.

## Information Paths

The token's maker mark, Willow Graveyard burial copy, and a survivor's description converge on Vaust. Each also restores a victim's name.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Take Vaust alive for testimony, kill him if he creates immediate lethal danger, or lose him while saving a new victim. Bernard cannot offer his own life as payment.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the trail goes cold, Vaust attempts entry at Long Field Shrine. If he escapes, his route and identity remain fixed for quest three.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.
