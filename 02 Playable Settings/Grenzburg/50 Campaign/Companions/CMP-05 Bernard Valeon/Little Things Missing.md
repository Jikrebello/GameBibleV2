# Little Things Missing

## Role in the Arc

This is Bernard Valeon's quest 1 of four. It is playable in Early Autumn and Open Autumn and advances the arc without requiring Bernard Valeon in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Pilgrim keepsakes vanish from the Red Stag while Bernard volunteers at Mercy Hospital. The objects later appear arranged in his Blackjack locker.

Find whether Bernard relapsed, prevent Koss from turning the theft into indefinite debt, and return objects before their owners leave Grenzburg.

## Player Actions

Interview owners without public spectacle, reconstruct Bernard's route, inspect a fence who copied his habit, and establish a practical safeguard plan.

## Opposition and Pressure

Bernard did take two objects; an inn thief took the rest after recognizing an easy scapegoat. Koss benefits from treating every item as a new charge.

## Information Paths

Bernard remembers tactile details, the fence has duplicate wrappings, and a hospital sister records his unsupervised interval. Any two separate the relapse from the framing.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Require public return, quiet return with named witnesses, or let Koss prosecute all losses. Bernard must acknowledge his two thefts.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the fence escapes, the copied objects surface in Lantern Market. If owners depart, West Refuge Hall forwards returns but trust falls.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the winter transition, the initiating evidence is secured by another party and the quest returns in Early Winter with higher institutional leverage and lower trust; it does not simply vanish.
