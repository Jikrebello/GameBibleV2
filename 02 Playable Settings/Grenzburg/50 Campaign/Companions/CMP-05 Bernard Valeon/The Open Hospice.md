# The Open Hospice

## Role in the Arc

This is Bernard Valeon's quest 4 of four. It is playable in Early Spring and Muster Crisis. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Bernard Valeon duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The dead march forces Mercy Hospital to choose between evacuation and remaining open for people who cannot move. Bernard's old order offers command, funds, and secrecy in exchange for institutional custody.

Create a defensible hospice that treats wounded soldiers, debtors, Folk, and captives without becoming a suicidal symbol or a denominational prize.

## Player Actions

Secure routes, move medicines, expose stolen ration chits, organize lay defenders, and hold a final triage during a residue attack.

## Opposition and Pressure

Scarcity, frightened clergy, and opportunistic theft threaten the hospital. No enemy mastermind created the crisis.

## Information Paths

Patient rolls, camp counts, and route scouts reveal who can move and what the hospital can sustain. Bernard's safeguards determine whether a missing cache is quickly found.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Found an open lay hospice, return under order authority, or abandon the institution for the final march. An explicit late sacrifice is possible only if the hospital cannot otherwise evacuate and the player confirms it.

## Fail-Forward Results

If supplies are lost, the hospice narrows services rather than vanishing. If the building falls, a field hospice survives in Paupers' Cloister.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.
