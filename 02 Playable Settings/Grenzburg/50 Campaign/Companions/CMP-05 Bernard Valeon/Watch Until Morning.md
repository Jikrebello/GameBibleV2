# Watch Until Morning

## Quest Function

This is Bernard's sworn-bond episode for a male player. It is a completely platonic night of shared labor, declared provision, honest watch, and chosen brotherhood.

There is no villain, ambush, hidden theft, supernatural test, or contrived emergency. The episode grants the sworn-bond move upgrade and one physical safeguard for [[The Open Hospice]]. It cannot determine Bernard's confession, White-Flame coherence, or final moral outcome.

## Hook

The small road shelter beside [[Grenzburg Worldspace Location Register#Longlake Country|Lake Road Cairn]] lost a roof beam during the thaw. Its stove draws badly, rain reaches the sleeping bench, and its store chest has not been witnessed since winter.

Bernard has arranged a replacement beam from a dismantled Longlake shed. He asks the player to help him carry it to the shelter, complete the repairs, and keep the first night watch after reopening.

He is careful about what the invitation means. This is not a confession hearing, a holy vigil, or repayment for the player's support. He wants to do useful work beside a man he trusts and then speak without an audience.

## Party and Materials

The activity is one-on-one. The replacement beam, roof pegs, stove clay, lamp oil, two blankets, and a small declared food store are listed on a public tally tied to the load.

Other companions may wish them a dry night before departure but do not join the route or its central conversation.

Every vocation can complete the work:

- ordinary carrying, bracing, climbing, and tool use form the principal route;
- Fighter and Paladin improve heavy handling; an unlocked Lancewright can add a bounded Brace;
- Slayer and Free Running improve roof access;
- Hunter identifies dry footing, usable thatch, and chimney draw;
- Warlock can dry or warm material without replacing carpentry;
- Medium and Binder tools can hold a marked position but cannot perform the whole repair.

Bernard is a capable second worker. The player's vocation changes method and pace, not access or emotional outcome.

## Beginning: The Beam

The pair collect the beam at Reedmile turnoff and carry it along the Lake Road. It can be moved:

- by shoulder with regular rest changes;
- on a simple drag sledge;
- by borrowing a handcart if the route state permits;
- through a vocation-specific handling advantage.

The mechanical purpose is to establish shared burden rather than test strength. If the load is dropped or damaged, the pair splice it with an iron fishplate at the shelter. The repair takes longer but the quest continues.

Bernard reports every carried provision aloud before departure. If a previous accountability incident remains unresolved, he names it without waiting for the player to discover it.

## Middle: A Shelter for Whoever Arrives

The shelter requires four ordinary jobs:

1. replace or splice the roof beam;
2. close the rain gap without blocking the smoke vent;
3. clean and reseal the stove throat;
4. inventory the store chest and hang the tally where a traveler can read it.

The player and Bernard choose the order. Weather, failing light, and prior road conditions may prevent a perfect repair, but there is no false choice between moral goods.

Travelers can arrive while the work continues:

- a Chapel-Folk fisher heading home;
- two discharged laborers sharing a handcart;
- a ducal message rider changing a wet wrapping.

They are ordinary shelter users, not disguised enemies or quest judges. The pair can offer space, food under the posted rule, advice, or quiet. Refusing entry without danger leaves the shelter technically repaired but contradicts the service being claimed and creates a warned bond conflict.

## Declared Stores

Bernard opens the old chest in the player's presence. The contents are mundane: damp kindling, salt, two lamp stubs, a cracked cup, and one blanket that should have been replaced.

The pair place the new stores inside and write:

- what entered;
- what was discarded;
- what the travelers used;
- what remains for the next road party.

Bernard feels the familiar urge to put one ration somewhere only he knows. He names it before touching the food. The player may acknowledge the admission, help choose a visible emergency compartment, or ask Bernard to finish the tally himself.

The correct practice is not having no reserve. It is a sufficient reserve whose existence, purpose, and custodian are known.

## Night Watch

Once the roof holds and the stove draws, the travelers sleep or continue onward. Bernard and the player divide the night into two watches.

The scene proceeds through three quiet conversations:

### What Shame Hides

Bernard explains that confession after discovery still feels safer than naming an urge before anyone can prove it. The player may speak about his own failings, remain private, or distinguish support from surveillance.

### What One Man Can Ask

They discuss the point at which help becomes command. Bernard does not ask the player to monitor every pocket or certify his goodness. He asks for one promise: when either man sees the other using shame, pride, fear, or duty to disappear from the truth, he will call him back plainly.

### The Watch That Is Shared

Each man takes a watch while the other sleeps. The resting character is not attacked, robbed, or secretly tested. Trust is demonstrated by sleep itself and by the ordinary fact that the waking man remains.

## Dawn: Sworn Brothers

At first light, Bernard explains a Long Watch lay custom used by two unordained road servants who freely accept mutual responsibility. The custom grants neither clerical office nor military rank.

The pair cut two service cords from the repair rope. Each names:

- one burden he will help carry;
- one truth he expects the other to speak;
- the duty to return for the other when return remains possible;
- the duty to tell the truth when return was impossible.

They knot the cords together around the new shelter beam and explicitly call one another sworn brothers. The cords remain on the beam as a public sign of service, while Bernard's prayer rope remains unchanged.

The player may accept, ask to make the promise in plainer words, or decline without losing ordinary friendship.

## Bond Upgrade: No One Alone: Two Bearers

The upgraded move retains Bernard's interposition and safe rescue window. One revive, carry, extraction, or protected interaction may withstand a single ordinary interruption. When the action completes, the player and rescued target gain brief Staying Power.

Boss grabs, authored catastrophic hazards, and total defeat retain their intended answers. The move requires Bernard in the active party.

## Second Bearer Practiced

`bernard_second_bearer_practiced` records that Bernard and the player have deliberately shared a physical burden, declared stores, and completed a watch without converting care into control.

During [[The Open Hospice]], it prevents one exhaustion-driven physical failure: a dropped load, missed carrier call, failed brace, or momentary collapse. It is the same safeguard supplied by `bernard_table_received`; the two states are mutually exclusive and can never stack.

It cannot expose a hidden reserve, make a triage decision, restore moral coherence, preserve a store category by itself, or prevent Bernard's explicit sacrifice if the player deliberately accepts it.

## Shelter Outcomes

`cairnside_shelter_state` records:

- **open-and-witnessed:** roof, stove, tally, and stores are sound;
- **open-under-repair:** the shelter is usable but needs a later work crew;
- **weather-tight-only:** the pair preserve immediate refuge but the stove or stores remain incomplete;
- **closed:** damage or player choice leaves the shelter safely barred for later repair;
- **unrepaired:** the episode reaches its cutoff.

Imperfect carpentry does not invalidate the bond. Deliberate concealment, abandonment, or using the rite to excuse harm creates a warned relationship conflict.

## Cutoff

Beginning [[The Deep Muster Main Quest|The Deep Muster]] closes the repair window. A Longlake work party later makes Cairnside minimally usable. Bernard does not punish the player or end the sworn bond, but the private watch, move upgrade, and Second Bearer safeguard are lost.

## Outputs and Rewards

- `bernard_bond_episode`: complete, declined, or missed;
- `bernard_bond_move_upgrade`: two-bearers or none;
- `bernard_second_bearer_practiced`: true or false;
- `cairnside_shelter_state`: open-and-witnessed, open-under-repair, weather-tight-only, closed, or unrepaired.
