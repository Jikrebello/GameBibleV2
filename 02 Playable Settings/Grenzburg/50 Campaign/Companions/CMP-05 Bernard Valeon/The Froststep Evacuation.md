# The Froststep Evacuation

The Froststep Evacuation was a Long Watch civilian withdrawal in Kedrava during the winter of 1356 AR. It is the controlling account of [[Bernard Valeon|Bernard Valeon's]] dismissal and restitution debt.

## Situation

An early freeze, a mountain slide, and reports of raiders made three high-road hamlets untenable. The [[Hospice of the Lantern Road]] prepared three relief sleds to move forty-three people toward the lower refuge houses before a second storm closed Froststep Road.

Bernard had spent the preceding month helping receive stores. Fear of another empty winter had already led him to make four private caches:

- two sacks of hard bread and dried beans beneath a disused bakehouse floor;
- lamp oil and four blankets inside a road-wall niche;
- bandages and warming salve in a locked stable box;
- small food portions hidden among his issued bedding.

He did not intend to sell the stores or deny one named person. He knowingly removed communal goods, concealed the inventory mismatch, and treated his imagined future need as superior to the declared evacuation.

## How the Concealment Reached the Sled

The chain is short, and it matters, because the harm was arithmetic before it was weather.

Bernard was helping receive stores that month, and the count he helped keep was the count the loading plan used. The goods he removed were subtracted from the shelves and **not** from the tally. So the manifest promised oil, salve, food, and covering that had already left the shelf; the sleds were loaded against that manifest, in the dark, at speed; and the second sled went out carrying a written surplus and a physical shortfall.

Nobody caught it in time because there was nothing to catch — the paper was consistent with itself. The concealment did not merely hide a theft. It falsified the number three people used to decide what forty-three people would have on a mountain.

## The Understocked Sled

The second sled lost a runner and spent a night above the lower switchback. Its people had too little oil, salve, food, and dry covering.

No one died. Five evacuees suffered lasting cold injury:

- [[Petra Ilev]], relief driver — two fingers of her left hand, and severe cold pain in one foot;
- **Josef Vrana**, carrier — nerve damage to both hands and a chest that fails every winter; forty years of carrying work ended in a night. His daughter [[Lenka Vrana]] works in Grenzburg;
- three others carry reduced mobility, nerve pain, or recurrent winter illness.

When [[Matej Solin|Watch-Captain Matej Solin]] ordered an urgent store search, Bernard denied knowing where the missing supplies were. He confessed after the injured sled reached the hospice and then led the order to all four caches. The recovered goods helped later evacuees but could not reverse the injuries.

## Judgement

The Long Watch investigation found:

- the evacuation plan and store count were reasonable for the information available;
- no commander ordered Bernard to hide supplies or falsify the count;
- his childhood deprivation and recognized compulsion reduced neither ownership nor the need for practical care;
- his initial denial worsened the emergency search;
- his voluntary confession, full recovery of the caches, and prior service mattered without erasing the harm.

The order dismissed Bernard from vowed service, compensated the five injured people, and funded replacement care. To restore the hospice stores immediately, it sold Bernard's restitution bond to Medori. Bernard signed the bond rather than contesting the injuries or the order's authority.

## Competing Memories

Bernard remembers the moment of confession as the first honest thing he did after the shortage. Petra remembers that honesty began after her hand was already black with cold. Matej remembers both facts and refuses to choose one as the whole account.

[[Hospice-Mother Radena Vosk]] was not present at Froststep. Her later letter told Bernard that confession opens repair but does not convert consequence into persecution.

## Present Consequences

- Medori owns Bernard's remaining restitution bond.
- The Long Watch considers reinstatement legally possible after sustained repair; it is neither promised nor secretly barred.
- Petra has her own work, loyalties, and judgement. She may accept complete restitution without reconciliation, redirect her share to winter-road care, or close contact.
- Visible White Flame cannot revise the event's material facts.
