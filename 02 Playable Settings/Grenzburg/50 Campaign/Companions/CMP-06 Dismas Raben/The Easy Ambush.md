# The Easy Ambush

## Role in the Arc

This is Dismas Raben's quest 2 of four. It is playable in Open Autumn and Early Winter. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Dismas Raben duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A marcher patrol asks Dismas to design an ambush at Split Pine Ravine against raiders carrying stolen winter medicine. A civilian wood train enters the kill zone early.

Recover the medicine without repeating his decision to trigger a prepared hazard before confirming the target.

## Player Actions

Scout the ravine, mark civilian traffic, set reversible traps, pursue the raiders, and choose whether to sacrifice tactical advantage to clear the wood train.

## Opposition and Pressure

Raiders exploit mixed traffic and Dismas' old instincts favor the cleanest shot. A marcher lieutenant values results and promises to falsify any civilian losses.

## Information Paths

Hunter sign distinguishes wagon weights, a camp witness knows the raider tack, and lookout timing reveals the civilian train.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Abort the ambush and pursue, spring only controlled sections, or accept civilian risk for decisive capture.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the raid escapes, tracking leads to Burnt Antler Camp with stronger defenses. If civilians are struck, survivors remain rescueable and later testify.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

At the spring transition, unresolved danger produces a named casualty, loss, or policy change and the reckoning quest inherits it.

Tests whether he returns for people when escape would still be easier.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
