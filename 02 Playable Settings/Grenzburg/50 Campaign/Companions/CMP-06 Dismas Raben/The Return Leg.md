# The Return Leg

## Quest Function

Dismas's romance episode for a female player. **No villain, no ambush, no theft, no emergency, no test.**

Its entire subject is announced in the title, and he announces it too, which is the point: the man who never says he is coming back says it at the start.

## Hook

He proposes two days on the Ash Road — a walk, not a job — to a view above Hollow Ash he has been saving. And then, before they leave the gate, entirely unprompted and visibly costing him something:

> **Dismas:** We come back Thursday. I am saying it now so it is said.
>
> *(A beat, while he decides whether to make it a joke.)*
>
> **Dismas:** Do not make a ceremony of it.

## The Road

Ordinary travel, ordinary weather, no encounter deck. Roadside tea that is bad in a specific way he defends. A ferry he refuses to pay for on principle and then pays for. An argument about whether a shortcut is a shortcut if it takes longer.

Nothing tests either of them. The player may lead, follow, or set the pace, and nothing records it as a posture.

## The Middle: Every Exit

At the camp above Hollow Ash he does the only intimate thing his character permits, and it is stranger and more revealing than a confession.

**He shows her the exits.**

All of them, one after another, from where they are sitting: the fen track behind the ridge, the ferry at Reed Landing that runs at dawn, the drover's road that reaches Kedrava in nine days, the two places on this hillside where a man could sit for a week and not be found. He knows all of it by heart. He has always known it. He knows it in every room he has ever slept in, including the Yard, including hers.

> **Dismas:** That is what is in my head. All the time. It is not about you and it has never once been about you.

Telling her where the doors are **is** the vow. He is not promising the doors will close, and she is not asked to close them — a woman who tried would be doing the one thing he cannot survive, and the quest gives her no option to try.

The player may answer with a promise, with her own version of the same map, with humour, with a boundary, or with nothing. There is no correct response and no social check.

## The Question

Plainly, without arrangement:

- he wants this, and he is bad at wanting things out loud;
- he will keep the map, because it is how he is built;
- he will tell her when he is going and where, which he has never done for anyone;
- **she may leave, and he will not treat it as proof of anything;**
- refusal now does not damage the friendship or the campaign.

On commitment, the evening becomes physically affectionate and fades to black. The player may also keep it tender without sex, or ask for more time.

## Morning

They walk back. They are at the Yard gate on Thursday.

He does not remark on it. Brina does, once, and he says something dismissive about the ferry timetable.

## Rewards

- **Second Journey: Chosen Twice** — after either partner disengages, the other's next reposition is protected, and the protection can be spent a second time if both have withdrawn and returned to the line in the same encounter;
- `dismas_return_leg_practiced` — one Act III safeguard, equal and exclusive with the bond's, preventing one extraction or exhaustion failure at Three Horn;
- romance-specific summer dialogue.

The permanent perk **No Escape Route** is earned only after a resolved or compromised [[The Road That Answers]].

## Cutoff

Beginning [[The Deep Muster Main Quest|The Deep Muster]] closes the episode. He does not punish the player and does not end the relationship; the road, the upgrade, and the safeguard are lost.
