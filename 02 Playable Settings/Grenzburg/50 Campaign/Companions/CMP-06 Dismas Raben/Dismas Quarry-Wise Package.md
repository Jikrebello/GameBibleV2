# Dismas Quarry-Wise Package

Dismas remains a standard [[Hunter]]. His doctrine is **Quarry-Wise**, and the name is an inversion rather than a boast.

## The Inversion

[[Ylva Winter-Track|Ylva]] hunts. Dismas is the escaped quarry who learned the hunter's habits from the wrong side of them — eleven years of being the thing on the other end of a tracker's attention, and a professional education assembled entirely out of near-misses.

He does not close on a target. **He collapses a pursuer's options.**

This resolves the reassessment reserved in his character file. He stays Hunter; the doctrine is doctrine-distinct from Rescue Scout at every point:

| | Ylva — Rescue Scout | Dismas — Quarry-Wise |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | toward the endangered | behind the party |
| First read | who needs extraction | who is following, and how many |
| Terrain use | rally marks, extraction lines | dead ends closed, sight lines broken |
| Signature | opens a route out | removes the route in |
| Failure register | somebody is left behind | somebody is caught |

## Field Functions

- **counter-scouting:** reads pursuit numbers, pace, and discipline before contact;
- **reversible traps:** every device he sets can be disarmed by anyone, including in the dark, including under fire — the mechanical expression of a man who once could not un-fire something;
- **pursuit disruption:** breaks a following formation's line of sight, footing, or nerve;
- **weak-route identification:** unsafe fastenings, false walls, bored sentries, and decorative fences surface as interactable;
- **rapid withdrawal:** guarded disengagement for the party, not only himself;
- **route marks:** the missing queen's mark, readable by the player once **Follow the Queen** is earned.

## Progression

| Stage | Technique |
|---|---|
| introduction | **Read the Hinge** — unsafe fastenings and inattentive guards become visible interactions |
| Act I finale | **Reversible Line** — his traps carry a disarm interaction available to any character |
| friendship | **Follow the Queen** — his old route marks become readable across the worldspace |
| Act II finale | Quarry-Wise custom-vocation unlock |
| Act III operation | **Somebody Else's Road** — trained guides act on their own initiative during escorts |
| Act III finale | **No One Behind** capstone; Quarry-Wise blueprint |

## No One Behind

The capstone is a rearguard function, not a damage spike: while the party withdraws, Dismas holds the last position and every ally's disengagement is protected once. It is named for the thing he did not do at Stonefall Cut, and no in-game text says so.

## Boundaries

- No critical route depends on his package. Every gate he opens is openable by ordinary tools, climbing, or force.
- His haunting is memory, not a supernatural entity ([[Companion Constitution]] guardrail); no technique interacts with residue, imprints, or the Muster in a privileged way.
- Reversible traps are **always** reversible. There is no upgrade, situation, or outcome in which he sets something that cannot be undone. This is a character rule wearing a mechanic's clothes; do not balance it away.
