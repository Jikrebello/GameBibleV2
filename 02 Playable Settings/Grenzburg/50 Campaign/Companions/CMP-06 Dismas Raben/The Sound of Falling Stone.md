# The Sound of Falling Stone

## Role in the Arc

This is Dismas Raben's quest 1 of four. It is playable in Early Autumn and Open Autumn. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Dismas Raben duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A fresh quarry slide produces the same whistle-and-collapse pattern that freezes Dismas during the Black Road aftermath. Workers are trapped beneath an ordinary construction accident.

Rescue the workers and force Dismas to distinguish present danger from the coach he abandoned.

## Player Actions

Read the unstable face, choose rescue lanes, fetch lifting gear, and follow Dismas when he bolts toward Old Coach Burn after hearing survivors under rock.

## Opposition and Pressure

Aftershocks, poor quarry maintenance, and frightened laborers create pressure. Dismas withholds why the sound makes him count voices.

## Information Paths

Tool marks show the slide is accidental; an old coach fitting at the Burn matches his former gang; Dismas can identify a route no official report records.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Let him lead the rescue under witness, remove him from command but keep him present, or allow him to flee. He must admit there were living people in the old coach.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed lift injures rather than instantly kills workers and opens a narrower crawl route. If Dismas flees, his trail goes to Split Elm Inn and the confession occurs there.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the winter transition, the initiating evidence is secured by another party and the quest returns in Early Winter with higher institutional leverage and lower trust; it does not simply vanish.

## Rewards and Return Value

Names the concealed crime through action rather than a camp monologue.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
