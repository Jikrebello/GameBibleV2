# The Name on the Charge

## Role in the Arc

This is Dismas Raben's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Dismas Raben duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Marta Etten, sole known survivor of the crushed coach, reaches Grenzburg with a civil charge naming Dismas. Underworld brokers offer to make her disappear before the hearing.

Keep Marta alive, establish the full event, and prevent Dismas from converting confession into a demand for forgiveness.

## Player Actions

Protect Marta through the underways, recover the old road report, confront hired intimidators, and hold a public or sealed hearing at Guild Court.

## Opposition and Pressure

A former gang contact wants the case blamed entirely on an absent cofounder. Dismas considers flight. Marta wants truth and material repair, not reconciliation.

## Information Paths

Marta's memory, quarry blasting marks, and Dismas' route notation establish sequence. The lesser conviction record proves what the court did not know.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Public confession and service sentence, sealed admission under marcher oversight, or suppression of the charge. Relationship commitment follows a path where the player names what they will and will not carry for him.

## Fail-Forward Results

If Marta is abducted, she leaves route signs and can be recovered. If records burn, her testimony plus Dismas' knowledge still establishes the case.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If left until the Muster Crisis, the confrontation occurs under emergency conditions: one evidence path closes, the relationship cannot commit until the issue is faced, and the final quest begins from a compromised state.

## Rewards and Return Value

Unlocks commitment and fixes whether his future service rests on public accountability, private usefulness, or escape.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
