# Companion Blind Dialogue Test Pair 04

Use these lines without names, vocation labels, culture, or backstory context. A reader should attribute at least ten of twelve lines correctly to each companion.

## Test Lines

1. “Wind changed.”
2. “Again. Slower is not smaller.”
3. “Eat before you decide.”
4. “First champion of carrying the left end while complaining in tune.”
5. “That knot is a confession.”
6. “The barrel has won by technical fall.”
7. “Not there. The water is lying.”
8. “Anybody can break the wall. We are keeping the roof.”
9. “Name the injury. Weather later.”
10. “A halt is part of the bout. Anyone can continue.”
11. “Seven minutes to shelter. I have known for eleven.”
12. “I require a witness. If my footwork is terrible, it deserves a proper audience.”
13. “You could ask.” “Fair.”
14. “No crowd today. Tell me if that makes this easier or worse.”
15. “Wake me if the wind turns.”
16. “You may laugh after you lift your end.”
17. “You said the smoke would flatten. I wrote unlikely. It was likely. I was wrong.”
18. “The discharge was an accident. The ring was not.”
19. “You may be brave after the ice is sounded.”
20. “The judges admired your range. They have awarded you this turnip.”
21. “Put me down. Not there.”
22. “My name made this easier to trust. You decide whether I stay for the refit.”
23. “There is no wind? Then I am improving.”
24. “No.”

## Rejection Rules

Revise a set if:

- Ylva is identifiable only through weather, cold, or sacrifice;
- Tavio is identifiable only through sport, crowds, or encouragement;
- either speaker sounds sexually performative before an interest signal;
- Tavio turns every task into a game or Ylva turns every task into rescue logistics;
- more than four lines invoke their defining wrongs;
- vulnerability arrives as a polished lesson instead of behavior, refusal, or work.
