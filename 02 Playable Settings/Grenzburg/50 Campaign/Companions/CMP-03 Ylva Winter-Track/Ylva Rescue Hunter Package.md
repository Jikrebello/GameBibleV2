# Ylva Rescue Hunter Package

Ylva uses the standard [[Hunter]] vocation. This package is companion authorship, AI priority, and progression. It does not create a variant vocation, second weapon set, or exclusive critical traversal ability.

## Field Role

Ylva is a rescue scout and team coordinator. She excels when a fight has a civilian, wounded ally, carried object, unstable route, weather front, or retreat lane.

Her priority stack is:

1. identify the immediate danger and the credible exit;
2. identify who cannot reach that exit unaided;
3. place a signal, smoke point, or covering angle;
4. interrupt pursuit rather than chase damage;
5. open a line for the player or temporary allies;
6. relocate before melee pressure fixes her in place.

She is not a front-line tank. She may defend herself at close range through evasive bow movement and blunt interruption, but her authored behaviour seeks distance and crossfire.

## Signature Families

### Trail Read

Ylva marks the safest visible retreat, a concealed approach, or the creature most likely to pursue a wounded target. The mark improves companion path choice and communicates tactical intent to the player.

It does not reveal routes she has not observed or replace required investigation.

### Covering Shot

A fast interruption against an enemy attacking a wounded, carrying, reviving, or mechanism-operating ally. It prioritizes stagger and pursuit denial over raw damage.

### Winter Draw

A weather-stable aiming discipline that reduces disruption from wind, snow, rain, and unstable footing. It does not erase severe-weather visibility or make impossible shots routine.

### Rally Mark

Ylva establishes a visible rally position with cloth, flare, or terrain. Allies moving toward it receive clearer routeing and limited resistance to panic and ordinary stagger.

### Line Cast

Ylva places a pilot cord across a credible gap or current. A proper rescue line must then be hauled, secured, and tested. The arrow cannot carry a person, replace a bridge, or bypass a critical route.

### Smoke Withdrawal

Ylva creates bounded concealment, reads the wind, and shifts the party out of a losing angle. Ordinary ranged attackers lose clean sight; prepared area attacks, close pursuit, and creatures using other senses remain dangerous.

## Field Fletching

Every recipe uses ordinary materials, remains subject to local weapons and fire law, unlocks account-wide through core campaign stages, and upgrades Ylva automatically.

### All-Weather Flare Arrow

An upgrade to the existing Flare family:

- burns visibly through snow, fog, and rain;
- establishes a rally or rescue marker;
- lights a limited area;
- disrupts light-sensitive threats;
- does not become an explosive or supernatural beacon.

Typical materials: sealed flare compound, waxed wrapping, bright mineral salts, and a broad stable shaft.

### Reinforced Blunt-Head

An upgrade to the existing Blunt-head family:

- pressures armour and guard;
- enables nonlethal stops where force remains proportionate;
- breaks weak ice and fragile obstructions;
- jars simple latches or mechanisms;
- cannot silently open secure locks or demolish structural stone.

Typical materials: forged cap, weighted collar, shock-binding, and a short heavy shaft.

### Line Arrow

A heavy arrow carrying a pilot cord:

- crosses water, gaps, or unstable surfaces;
- lets a proper rope be hauled and secured;
- supports rescue, ferrying, and route marking;
- cannot bear a person directly;
- never replaces the shared traversal baseline.

Typical materials: reinforced shaft, barbed or ringed head, low-drag pilot cord, and swivel.

### Smoke Arrow

A bounded concealment and wind-reading tool:

- breaks ordinary ranged sight;
- identifies wind direction;
- marks safe withdrawal;
- can expose currents through cracks;
- does not blind creatures immune to smoke or conceal sound.

Typical materials: clay or thin-metal capsule, cool smoke compound, waxed fuse, and stable shaft.

### Controlled Blast Arrow

An upgrade to the existing Blast family:

- uses a smaller shaped charge;
- breaks ice, timber, fragile obstruction, or a selected armour point;
- reduces spread and fire risk;
- remains loud, hazardous, and legally restricted;
- cannot replace full demolition, siege equipment, or a Lancewright Break.

Typical materials: licensed blast charge, shaped cap, shock collar, and reinforced shaft.

## Winter-Track Fieldcraft

This account-wide augment improves:

- severe-weather aiming;
- field-arrow material economy;
- movement while guiding, carrying for, or protecting another character;
- route-sign readability during evacuation.

It grants no new access key and uses no relationship requirement. Exact values remain provisional.

## Vocation Boundary

Ylva does not:

- use a sword-and-shield package;
- fight as a front-line heavy;
- create magical arrows without materials;
- command animals;
- make critical traversal Hunter-only;
- turn a pilot line into a grappling teleport;
- gain stronger recipes from romance or bond.

[[Dismas Raben]] remains a Hunter for now, focused on ambush, quarry reading, and irregular approach. His vocation is explicitly due for reassessment during CMP-06.
