# The Rearguard Road

## Quest Function

Ylva's Act I finale is a player-and-Ylva rescue expedition. It tests whether she will use the shared system when she becomes the casualty, and whether the player can treat cargo, evidence, and route prestige as secondary to living people.

Other active companions are dismissed at the staging point. Prepared relief workers may appear as temporary NPCs outside the companion limit.

## Trigger

An early freeze breaks loose a timber jam above River Teeth. Water and logs cut the Three-Log road while bandits exploit the confusion to seize abandoned carts.

Two groups remain isolated:

- six bridge workers and three household members above the damaged crossing;
- five charcoal workers sheltering beneath River Teeth with one injured carter.

Ylva's first plan uses herself as the moving link between both groups. With two preparation assets, the player can insist upon a credible duo operation backed by a named relief point.

## Beginning: Name the Expendable Things

At the relief cut, Ylva identifies:

- two carts of military fittings;
- winter flour;
- a box of private merchant glass;
- spare bridge chain;
- signal oil;
- three rescue litters.

The player marks what may be abandoned before departure. No item is secretly a moral trick:

- flour directly supports winter survival;
- bridge chain improves the route;
- military fittings help Tannbruck;
- merchant glass is valuable but replaceable;
- oil and litters are immediate rescue tools.

Prepared routes can preserve more. The moral requirement is that no property receive priority over a person in immediate danger.

## Approaches

The duo may reach Three-Log by:

- the damaged bridge deck, fastest but exposed to rolling timber;
- the Black Fir bypass, safer if surveyed;
- the river shelf, allowing a line to the lower workers;
- a timber lift track, offering height and a bandit flank.

Every starting vocation can complete each critical route through shared climbing, swimming, carrying, and ordinary interaction. Vocation tools alter danger and pace:

- Fighter holds a moving barrier;
- Slayer reaches a release point quickly;
- Hunter establishes earlier cover;
- Warlock controls heat, water, or brittle timber within bounded scripts;
- Medium distracts non-conscious residue at an old road niche;
- unlocked advanced vocations provide equivalent tactical advantages.

## Middle: Three-Log Bridge

The upper group has stabilized one span with a cargo cart. Moving the cart saves its load but risks the bridge. Leaving it pins the structure but blocks the litter route.

Solutions include:

- unload and redistribute the cargo;
- cut the cart loose after adding a second brace;
- lower people to the river shelf;
- repair the bridge chain;
- construct a line ferry;
- guide the group over one at a time.

Bandits fire from the timber lift when the rescue begins. Ylva prioritizes covering shots and the slowest evacuee. The player may suppress the bandits, reach their flank, build protection, or continue extraction under fire.

Defeated bandits do not reveal a mastermind. Their leader chose an apparently undefended road.

## River Teeth

Below the bridge, the charcoal workers are threatened by rising water and a second timber release. One worker cannot climb unaided.

The duo can:

- carry the injured worker along the shelf;
- haul a proper rescue rope from a prepared pilot line;
- clear a fragile obstruction and open a dry pocket;
- signal a relief team to receive evacuees;
- sacrifice a cart to build a temporary breakwater.

Ylva is struck by falling timber while setting the final line. Her bow arm remains functional, but she cannot safely climb and coordinate both groups.

## The Honest Distress Call

Ylva initially says the injury is "within route tolerance." The player has enough evidence to know it is not.

The decisive action is not a speech check. Ylva must choose whether to:

- issue the shared distress signal and name what she cannot do;
- ask the player to issue it while she reports the facts;
- continue until physical failure;
- order the player to evacuate everyone else and leave her.

Earlier direction and player response shape her choice. A prepared relief roster answers the signal. Without one, local workers still respond, but one material objective is lost while they assemble.

## End: Who Leaves Last

The final crossing requires two positions: one controls the line and one confirms the route. Ylva cannot occupy both.

Resolved play has Ylva:

- assign one position to the player or a relief worker;
- leave when her replacement arrives;
- credit the local teams;
- refuse sole liability for abandoned cargo.

The player may preserve cargo after everyone is secure. Pursuing retreating bandits before the last evacuation risks injuries and lost stores.

## Fail-Forward

- **Bridge fails:** the upper group reaches a river shelf, adding a water extraction.
- **Bandits retain the lift:** the rescue proceeds under ranged pressure and the cargo is harder to recover.
- **Line breaks:** all people remain on stable ground while a second route opens at greater time and material cost.
- **Ylva collapses:** the player completes a carry or signals relief; her concealment becomes public.
- **Late distress call:** one temporary NPC suffers a lasting but nonfatal injury.
- **Duo defeat:** a prepared relief team recovers both characters; people live according to assets, while cargo and route states worsen.

No single failed check kills an evacuee or Ylva.

## Rewards

- **Line Arrow**, account-wide.
- **Rescue Line Command:** Ylva more reliably establishes extraction interactions during combat.
- Act I direction and readiness states.
- access to [[No Quarry Today]] and Act II campaign progression.
