# Ylva Banter and Reactivity

## Voice Key

- Begins with the physical fact.
- Uses distance, wind, weight, food, and time as ordinary language.
- Gives care as an instruction and affection as remembered preparation.
- Lets a dry joke remain undiscovered.
- Grows quieter under anger or fear.
- Inspects equipment when embarrassed.
- Does not narrate sacrifice while work remains.

## Calibration Scenes

### Joy — Target game

Ylva's arrow splits the cord holding Tavio's ridiculous prize.

**Ylva:** “Wind.”

**Tavio:** “There is no wind.”

She collects the prize.

**Ylva:** “Then I am improving.”

### Competence — River crossing

Ylva watches three sticks pass the same eddy.

**Ylva:** “Not there. The water is lying.”

She walks upstream, ties the line low, and hands the player the first crossing rather than demonstrating it herself.

### Embarrassment — Being carried

After an injury, the player lifts her.

**Ylva:** “Put me down.”

The ground shifts again.

**Ylva:** “Not there.”

She spends the rest of the route criticizing the player's foot placement with suspicious precision.

### Anger — Patronizing protection

An officer orders her behind the line because “the women have done enough.”

**Ylva:** “Name the injury. Weather later.”

He cannot.

**Ylva:** “Then name the rule. We can disobey something real.”

### Care — Cold hands

Ylva takes a companion's wet gloves, replaces them with dry ones, and stuffs the wet pair inside her own coat.

**Companion:** “You could ask.”

**Ylva:** “Your hands said yes.”

Later she apologizes for the assumption without returning the wet gloves early.

### Shame — The concealed wound

The player finds blood frozen inside her sleeve.

Ylva looks at the route ahead.

**Ylva:** “Seven minutes to shelter.”

**Player:** “How long have you known?”

**Ylva:** “Eleven.”

The shame lies in the four minutes she stole from the team's decision.

### Apology — Relief called late

Ylva replaces the route board herself.

**Ylva:** “I waited because I could still stand.”

She writes the actual injury time beside her name.

**Ylva:** “That was not my decision alone. I made it alone.”

### Quiet — Somebody else takes watch

Ylva wakes, sees the player already at the fire, and starts reaching for her boots.

The player points to the relief mark.

She lies back down.

**Ylva:** “Wake me if the wind turns.”

She is asleep before receiving an answer.

## Foil Arc — Tavio

### Autumn

They compete over route time and target placement. Tavio praises her publicly; Ylva assumes he is displaying her. He later credits her method without summoning her to receive the applause.

### Winter

Tavio calls a halt after seeing Ylva conceal an injury. She accuses him of enjoying command. During a later breach he obeys her halt immediately, in front of his team.

### Spring

Each is asked to take the heroic final position. They design a relief rotation instead. Their mature rivalry continues through wagers whose rules include stopping.

## Faction Reactivity

- Supports trained marcher authority with named relief and stop rights.
- Favors Brina's shared liability over Koss's individual expendability.
- Respects practical Church shelter and rejects pity as jurisdiction.
- Defers to specific Folk route holders where authority is demonstrated.
- Supports Low Lantern extraction routes only when handoffs and supplies are real.

## Temperament Use

- Grey trust accumulates through field proof.
- Tomboy similarity may intensify unhealthy self-sufficiency.
- Jock chemistry must not make every exchange a contest.
- Goofball play remains welcome until a boundary is stated.

## Failure Modes

Reject any Ylva line that:

- turns laconic speech into absence of personality;
- treats femininity as incompetence she escaped;
- makes every scene another lesson about shared burden;
- confuses self-neglect with strength;
- uses weather metaphors when a plain word is stronger.
