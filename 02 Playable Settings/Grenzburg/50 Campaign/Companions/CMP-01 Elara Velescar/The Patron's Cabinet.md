# The Patron's Cabinet

## Role in the Arc

This is Elara Velescar's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring and advances the arc without requiring Elara Velescar in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Lord Marek Vardek arrives with a Virelian relief delegation and a locked travelling cabinet containing both incriminating rolls and desperately needed grain warrants.

Expose his betrayal without casually destroying relief for Grenzburg or letting him purchase silence with food.

## Player Actions

Enter the delegation's guarded rooms by invitation, burglary, ducal seizure, or a Chancery evidence order; authenticate the cabinet; keep Vardek from removing witnesses by river.

## Opposition and Pressure

Vardek relies on respectable retainers, bought testimony, and the fact that his warrants genuinely feed refugees. He offers Elara absolution, restored title, and the player a share if she accepts sole blame.

## Information Paths

The earlier customs roll, Seven Mounds correspondence, and a living Virelian burial clerk each prove different parts. The cabinet itself joins money, orders, and disposal of liability.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Publish everything and force emergency replacement of the warrants, trade selected property for immediate relief while preserving the criminal record, or accept Vardek's settlement. This is the relationship commitment point after the reckoning.

## Fail-Forward Results

If Vardek escapes, certified copies still expose him but he keeps movable wealth. If the cabinet is destroyed, witness testimony can convict Elara and the factor but not reach his whole network.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

If left until the Muster Crisis, the confrontation occurs under emergency conditions: one evidence path closes, the relationship cannot commit until the issue is faced, and the final quest begins from a compromised state.

Unlocks the romance or sworn-bond commitment and fixes whether Elara faces the final quest as a reformer, house-restorer, or accomplice.
