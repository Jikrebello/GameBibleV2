# Two Names on the Rope

## Quest Function

This is Elara's sworn-bond episode for a female player. It is a completely platonic two-person delve about mutual competence, reciprocal rescue, honest ownership, and the certainty that each woman will come back for the other.

It grants the sworn-bond move upgrade and one material safeguard for [[No Lady of Velescar]]. It does not forgive Elara's grave-robbing, supervise her conscience, or determine her final moral outcome.

## Hook

Highlake workers have reopened the entrance of **Windcut Mine**, a small abandoned extraction passage beneath an exposed ridge. A spring collapse revealed an older survey branch, but the workers will not enter until its ownership and safety are recorded.

Elara has already done the work that her younger self would have skipped. She has obtained a written two-person survey permission naming:

- the precise branch the pair may enter;
- the Highlake workers as custodians of the mine and fixed material;
- the surveyors' right to record and report portable finds;
- the requirement that nothing leaves before the public claim board sees it;
- an agreed emergency exit and return time.

She asks the player to sign beside her. Neither woman is witness, patron, servant, or guarantor for the other. They enter as equal surveyors.

## Party and Access

The activity is one-on-one. Elara brings rope, matched anchor plates, chalk, a counterweight bag, lamps, and the signed survey copy. Other companions remain available before departure and after the public return.

Every player vocation can complete the route:

- ordinary climbing, carrying, bracing, and rope interaction form the main path;
- Slayer and Free Running improve exposed crossings;
- Fighter and Paladin can hold or reposition heavy counterweights;
- Hunter reads disturbed stone and old survey marks;
- Warlock and an unlocked Lancewright can clear ice or test stress without opening a unique route;
- Medium imprints can indicate pressure movement but cannot solve the paired system alone;
- Binder forms can hold a marked station but cannot replace the player's judgement.

Elara can demonstrate each rope signal once. Missing the demonstration does not create an unwarned failure.

## Beginning: The Written Line

At the Highlake Claim Board, the pair inspect the permission with a clerk and one worker witness. The player may ask to narrow the survey, add an injury clause, or confirm what happens to an unexpected object. Elara accepts every clarification without treating caution as mistrust.

At Windcut, two route features establish the delving language:

1. **Paired anchors:** one surveyor places and tests the leading anchor while the other controls the standing line.
2. **Rope signals:** one pull means hold, two means feed line, three means return, and a continuous drag means the speaker cannot secure herself.
3. **Counterweights:** doors and old ore stages move only while weight is balanced across separated platforms.

The route contains no hostile occupant. Its danger comes from spring water, old timber, narrow drops, loose scree, and machinery built for crews larger than two.

## Middle: The Split Passage

The survey branch divides around a stone rib. Elara takes the upper inspection ledge while the player crosses the lower ore stage. Each can see only the other's rope and lamp glow.

A delayed roof shift breaks the central walkway, jams the counterweight, and separates them. The player lands on a sloping platform above a flooded shaft. Elara is pinned by her safety line on the opposite ledge with loose stone continuing to fall.

The collapse creates two linked rescue problems:

- the player must stop the lower platform from sliding;
- Elara must release or redirect her trapped line;
- the jammed counterweight must move before either can cross;
- both must communicate despite broken sight lines and running water.

The player can brace the platform, transfer weight to a tool bundle, climb to a secondary ring, use a vocation advantage, or signal Elara to act first. Elara does not wait passively. She cuts away loose rigging, anchors the player's line from her side, and uses her pick to expose the counterweight catch.

The decisive exchange is reciprocal:

1. the player creates enough stability for Elara to free herself;
2. Elara then crosses the dangerous upper gap and arrests the player's sliding platform;
3. both work the counterweight from opposite sides to rebuild a return line.

No single failed check kills either character. Failure can:

- destroy a set of climbing tools;
- close the deeper survey route;
- injure Elara, the player, or both;
- force an immediate retreat;
- leave the return route usable only after Highlake workers shore it.

## Discovery

Beyond the split is a modest survey niche containing an old seam map, three tagged mineral samples, and a collapsed tool chest. Nothing is a legendary treasure.

The meaningful discovery is that the old surveyors recorded two names on every hazardous line: the person who entered and the person responsible for bringing them back. Elara recognizes the practice as more honest than the expedition books she once kept, where laborers appeared as costs rather than named partners.

The pair may:

- document the niche and leave everything in place;
- carry the tagged samples under the written permission;
- abandon the recovery because of injury or route damage;
- mark the deeper branch for a properly staffed future crew.

All are valid. Concealing an object contradicts the bond and triggers a warned relationship conflict before the quest can close.

## End: The Public Board

The pair return to Highlake and report before washing off the mine dust or dividing any credit. The clerk records both names as equal surveyors. If the route was lost, the board records the loss without reducing either woman to an assistant.

Outside the board, Elara proposes a platonic pact:

- every claim will name the people who made it possible;
- either woman may call the other back from appetite, pride, or fear;
- neither will abandon the other for proof, treasure, rank, or reputation;
- when one goes missing, the other returns or makes the reason she could not return known.

The player may accept, ask for plainer words, or decline without ending ordinary friendship. On acceptance, the pair knot two short survey cords to the same iron ring and take them apart again. Each keeps one cord as a practical record of friendship, accountability, and return.

## Bond Upgrade: Back-to-Back: Held Line

The upgrade preserves Elara's contextual interception. While the player rescues, revives, climbs, or operates a mechanism, the action withstands one ordinary interruption. Elara exposes the intercepted attacker and creates a safe disengagement after completion.

Boss grabs, authored catastrophic hazards, and total defeat retain their intended answers. The move requires Elara in the active party.

## Two Names Line Practiced

`elara_two_names_line_practiced` records the paired-anchor discipline and reciprocal rescue.

During the clearing-chamber collapse in [[No Lady of Velescar]], it can preserve one additional person or material recovery. This is the same safeguard supplied by `elara_raven_line_practiced`; the two states are mutually exclusive and can never stack.

The safeguard does not decide which recovery Elara values first and cannot convert entitled conduct into a resolved outcome.

## Outcome States

`windcut_return_route_state` records:

- **secure:** the full route is surveyed and left safely usable;
- **worker-shoring-required:** both return, but Highlake must repair the line;
- **closed-by-injury:** the pair withdraw and seal the route after an injury;
- **lost:** equipment and access are lost, but both women return;
- **unopened:** the episode reaches its cutoff without being played.

Relationship quality does not depend on perfect material success. Deliberate concealment, abandonment, or treating the other woman as expendable creates a warned conflict rather than an accidental bond failure.

## Cutoff

Beginning [[The Deep Muster Main Quest|The Deep Muster]] closes the survey window. Highlake workers seal Windcut until after the campaign crisis. Elara does not punish the player or end the sworn bond, but the private delve, move upgrade, and Two Names Line safeguard are lost.
