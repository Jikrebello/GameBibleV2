# The Sixth Field Book

## Quest Function

This is Elara's short Act I operation. It converts the evidence recovered in [[Names Beneath Lime]] into practical preparation for [[A House Fed by Bones]] while giving the normal field party a stylish urban counter-theft.

The operation asks Elara to recover a record because workers and custodians need it, not because the book is hers.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The Customs manifest identifies a confiscated chest consigned to a bailiff's sale in [[Old Market]]. Inside is the sixth and final field book from Elara's Virelian expeditions.

The book contains:

- the names and family liabilities of the original excavation crew;
- corrections to Vasko's dangerous shoring diagrams;
- a copied survey of [[Seven Mounds]];
- Vardek payment marks connecting buyers to the expedition chain.

The sale begins at midday. A Vardek runner named **Pavel Nesk** has instructions to buy the whole lot, destroy the worker roll, and deliver the survey to the southbound field factor.

## Party and Staging

Elara is required, but ordinary party management remains available. The quest stages from [[Blackjack Yard]] and permits preparation at the market before the sale begins.

Other companions may provide methods:

- Zafir identifies which documents the bailiff may lawfully withdraw as evidence;
- Tavio recognizes a false-bottomed display cage and its load trigger;
- Dismas identifies the buyer's two planned exits.

None replaces Elara during the book's identification or the final custody decision.

## Principal Cast

- **Elara Velescar:** knows the book's shorthand, but initially calls it "my field book" rather than the workers' record.
- **Bailiff Oren Halvik:** administers the sale and will stop it for credible evidence, not rank or a private accusation.
- **Pavel Nesk:** a disposable Vardek auction runner who prefers a clean purchase but has hired roof hands for theft.
- **Danara Veln:** can authenticate a copied burial mark if the player previously protected her channel.
- **Daria Keln:** named in Nesk's sealed delivery instruction as the reserve factor if Vasko is unavailable.

## Pressure State: Sale Order 3-0

The state advances through long delay, a public failed accusation, an escaped courier, or retreat after the sale opens.

- **3:** inspection and approach selection;
- **2:** the chest enters the public lot;
- **1:** Nesk acquires or steals it and moves toward Delvers' Arcade;
- **0:** the worker roll burns and the survey leaves Grenzburg.

The state never kills a witness or ends the campaign. It determines how much of the book remains and whether the preparation asset is earned.

## Beginning: The Confiscated Lot

The party examines the sale preview in the Great Market. Three independent signs identify the chest:

1. Elara's six-notch survey cut beneath the carrying handle;
2. lime and funerary cloth matching the Black Lime warehouse;
3. a lot number copied from the recovered Customs manifest.

The display contains unrelated tools and personal effects from the dead intermediary. Taking the whole chest without inventory creates new claimants and weakens Elara's accountability. The objective is the field book and its attached worker roll.

## Approaches

### Evidence Petition

Present the manifest, a burial mark, or Customs testimony at the Hall of Measures. A successful petition withdraws the book from sale but leaves Nesk free to attempt theft. Failure consumes time and moves the confrontation into the public arcade rather than closing the quest.

### Honest Purchase

Bid for the lot using personal funds, a lawful Blackjack advance, or a guild surety. Nesk drives the price upward before ordering his roof hands to steal the chest if he loses. The money is genuinely spent and is not secretly refunded.

### Display Swap

Enter through the stall roofs, underside service lane, or a forged porter rotation. Copy the book and replace it with a weighted blank. Poor execution raises market alarm and lets Nesk identify Elara, but the chase continues.

### Follow the Buyer

Let Nesk acquire the lot, mark his carriers, and intercept them after they leave the protected sale. This preserves market law at the cost of giving the opposition its strongest initial position.

## Middle: The Counter-Theft

Whichever approach the player chooses, Nesk discovers that the book has been identified. He orders a switch:

- one carrier takes the public chest through Delvers' Arcade;
- one roof hand carries the extracted field book across the Market Roofline;
- a courier leaves with Daria's southbound instruction.

The party cannot cover every route without preparation or companion expertise. Recovering the book is the primary objective. Capturing the courier identifies Daria; recovering the chest preserves unrelated property; taking Nesk alive supplies testimony.

## End: Above the Arcade

The pursuit crosses awnings, rain channels, suspended guild signs, and the Delvers' Arcade roof. Every vocation can follow by stairs, interior galleries, ordinary climbing, or market scaffolds. Slayer movement, Free Running, and Elara's route callouts provide faster lines without becoming required.

Nesk fights only when the roof hand is cornered. If defeated, he may surrender, fall through an awning into guild custody, or escape through a prepared hay cart. His outcome does not control Vasko or Daria.

The field book can be:

- placed in civil evidence while Elara keeps a certified working copy;
- entrusted to Danara with copies for the workers;
- held at the Blackjack Yard under named-property procedure;
- concealed by Elara as private Velescar property.

The first three secure the preparation asset. Concealment preserves the survey but moves `elara_direction` toward entitled and withholds living-custodian support.

## Information Redundancy

- Seven Mounds appears in the survey, Nesk's delivery instruction, or a captured roof hand's route.
- Worker names survive in the original roll, the Customs packing copy, or Danara's comparison.
- Daria is identified through the sealed instruction, payment shorthand, or Nesk's testimony.
- Shoring corrections can be copied from the book, reconstructed at Ropehouse Ruin, or learned from Kata later.

## Fail-Forward Results

- If the book burns, one charred survey leaf and the Customs copy still point to Seven Mounds.
- If Nesk escapes with the book, Daria receives full warning and the Act I preparation asset is lost.
- If the market arrests the party, Elara must admit why the book matters; the case converts to a public evidence hearing and costs time.
- If unrelated property is stolen, owners file claims at the Yard and Elara receives a repair scene rather than a campaign failure.

## Outputs

- `elara_sixth_fieldbook_secured`: original, certified-copy, partial, concealed, or lost;
- `elara_worker_roll_state`: complete, reconstructed, partial, or destroyed;
- `elara_seven_mounds_survey_state`: corrected, incomplete, enemy-held, or unknown;
- `auction_runner_state`: captured, injured, escaped, or released;
- `daria_keln_identified`: true or false.

## Autonomous Resolution

If the operation remains incomplete at [[The Last Convoy]], Nesk's delivery reaches the field factor. Elara can still locate Seven Mounds through the introductory case, but she loses this readiness asset and the workers begin under tighter control.

## Rewards and Unlocks

- the Act I preparation asset `elara_sixth_fieldbook_secured`;
- the full worker roster as an investigation and rescue reference;
- Elara's hub appraisal service can now distinguish provenance records from sale value;
- a small guild or Customs standing change based on the chosen method.
