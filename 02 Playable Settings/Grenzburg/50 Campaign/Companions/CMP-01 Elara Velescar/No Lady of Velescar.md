# No Lady of Velescar

## Role in the Arc

This is Elara Velescar's quest 4 of four. It is playable in Early Spring and Muster Crisis and advances the arc without requiring Elara Velescar in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The final Velescar settlement reaches Grenzburg: land, tenant obligations, recovered heirlooms, and burial claims must be assigned before the Muster Crisis closes ordinary courts.

Choose a future that pays living and dead claimants without turning Elara into either a saint by paperwork or a permanent hostage to inherited shame.

## Player Actions

Hear worker and family petitions, secure transport for returned goods, defend the hearing when Vardek loyalists attempt destruction, and enact the selected disposition.

## Opposition and Pressure

Remaining house retainers demand continuity; some workers want land while others need cash; Medori wants a sale that clears its account; opportunists exploit the crisis.

## Information Paths

Petitions, estate rolls, and Paupers' Cloister burial records independently establish who is owed. Elara's conduct in the first three quests determines which claimants trust her.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Relinquish the house and distribute viable assets, preserve a reduced house under binding obligations, or abandon the settlement and take liquid wealth into the Muster.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the hearing is disrupted, the player can save people or records first; the lost half narrows but does not erase the settlement. Ignored petitions default to a compromised court sale in summer.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

Committing to the Deep Muster closes ordinary resolution. The companion reaches a specified compromised summer state unless the quest has already produced a broken or departed outcome.

Completes Elara's resolved, compromised, or broken state and supplies her late-campaign intervention: a route through noble custody, a funded civilian evacuation, or private wealth.
