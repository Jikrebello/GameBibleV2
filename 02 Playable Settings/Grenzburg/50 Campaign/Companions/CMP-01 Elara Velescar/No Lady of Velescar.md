# No Lady of Velescar

## Premise

During the Muster disruption, local Vardek agents move to empty an old tithe vault beneath Ledger Quays. It contains three recoveries:

1. **household land records** establishing use, obligations, and viable transfers;
2. **burial claims** connecting grave property to named restitution;
3. **title and wealth instruments** capable of restoring a reduced House Velescar.

The player and Elara enter as a duo. [[Claims-Factor Marco Bellandi]] remains above ground to administer whatever legally emerges. He does not accompany the delve or advise Elara's moral choice.

## Approaches

- **Bellandi stair:** lawful, observed, and heavily contested;
- **Low Lantern underways:** discreet, socially costly, and vulnerable to flooding;
- **exterior flood channel:** physically dangerous but independent of faction access.

All vocations can complete all routes using shared movement and supplied tools. Elara provides faster structural reads and contextual shortcuts.

## Beginning: The Emptying House

The chosen route reveals paid loyalists, hired salvage hands, and early Muster residue converging for unrelated reasons. The agents want documents and wealth. Residue follows old rank and movement patterns. Floodwater obeys neither.

The coalition's preparedness determines whether current delegates hold exits, receive material, and distinguish originals from attractive substitutes.

Elara begins in high spirits:

> “Three doors, one collapsing quay, and a title nobody sensible should want. At last, an honest afternoon.”

The joke stops when the first land bundle lists households as movable obligations.

## Middle: Three Galleries

### Land Gallery

Rotating shelves and counterweighted gates separate use records from ornamental estate maps. Recovering every impressive map first floods the working rolls. Living-claims preparation identifies the plain bundles.

### Burial Gallery

Sealed tubes hang over a lime cistern. Provenance lets the party release them in safe order. Without it, physical clues, names, and object matching still work under pressure.

### Dry Cage

Title seal, liquid instruments, and portable wealth are suspended above an old river mechanism. Local commander **Advocate Sera Vann** offers Elara a clean bargain: take the title bundle and permit the agents to destroy disputed household copies.

Sera is a current hired professional, not a former acquaintance. She may be captured, killed in justified resistance, or escape.

## Collapse

Opening any two galleries activates failed riverworks while residue enters through the service halls. The duo must:

- route water;
- move rescued or captured workers;
- defend coalition runners;
- choose which recovery uses the safest hoist;
- prevent title agents from substituting packets.

Three assets make all principal recoveries achievable. Four add a safeguard. A completed romance or bond episode adds one equal, non-stacking rescue:

- Raven Line preserves one person or object through guarded movement;
- Two Names Line does the same through reciprocal rope work.

No relationship state changes Elara's settlement.

## End: Hall of Petitions

The hearing is concise. Delegates speak first if the coalition survived. Bellandi reports:

- which instructions are authentic;
- which assets satisfy restitution;
- what remains legally owed;
- what Elara may lawfully retain.

Then Elara chooses.

### Resolved

She relinquishes title, transfers viable land and authority according to household instructions, funds burial repair, and retains only freely granted personal property. She remains an adventurer.

She does not declare herself “no one.” She says:

> “Elara will do. It has had less practice than Lady Velescar, but better company.”

### Compromised

She cancels binding obligations and transfers meaningful rights but preserves a reduced title, patron role, or private reserve.

### Broken or departed

She suppresses claims, takes concealed wealth, or accepts restoration on Vardek's terms. A warned confrontation and one repair opportunity precede departure.

Legal debt may be satisfied in any moral outcome if assets lawfully cover it. A resolved Elara may remain legally owned or under review if the recovery was lost. The states never collapse into one another.

## Failure and Autonomy

- Lost land records leave divided-copy settlement at higher cost.
- Damaged burial tubes preserve names but lose some objects.
- Lost wealth may keep the legal debt open without preventing moral relinquishment.
- A captured delegate remains rescuable during the collapse.
- Defeat triggers an exterior recovery; agents take one bundle and the hearing proceeds with what remains.
- Missing the finale fixes the compromised autonomous settlement.

## Rewards

Resolved or compromised outcomes grant:

- **The Last Claim** capstone;
- Delver-Duelist blueprint and parts;
- the appropriate permanent relationship perk if committed and eligible:
  - romance: **Equal Footing**;
  - bond: **Witness at My Back**.

Broken outcomes withhold the capstone and blueprint. Friendship can achieve the strongest result.
