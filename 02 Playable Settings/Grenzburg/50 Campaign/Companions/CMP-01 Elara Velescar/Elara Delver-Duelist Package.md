# Elara Delver-Duelist Package

Elara is a [[Slayer]] by combat role, but she does not use the player's paired-blade and grappling-knife loadout. Her **Delver-Duelist** package is a companion-specific discipline built from court fencing, practical excavation, climbing, prepared missiles, and the bad habits of a woman who once expected every locked place to become hers.

This package does not create a ninth authored vocation and does not broaden the normal Slayer's equipment permissions.

## Field Role

Elara is a mobile weak-point opener and opportunistic finisher. She works between close and middle range, makes guarded enemies vulnerable, interrupts dangerous actions, and creates safe movement through confined ruins.

She is strongest when she can:

1. read a target or structure;
2. mark an opening with rapier or dart;
3. evade the first answer;
4. break guard, armour, or footing with the pick;
5. exploit the exposed point;
6. withdraw before prolonged frontal pressure.

She is not a shield line, sustained area caster, healer, or universal lock-and-trap solution.

## Equipment

| Element | Function |
|---|---|
| **Velescar rapier** | precise thrusts, parries, feints, and weak-point finishers |
| **compact delver's pick** | guard damage, armour cracking, prying, masonry testing, and climbing purchase |
| **throwing knives** | interruption, pressure at retreating targets, and line cutting |
| **prepared darts** | bounded living-target afflictions selected before danger |
| **rope, hooks, wedges, powder-smoke** | contextual traversal, escape, and environmental interaction |
| **light field armour** | mobility beneath a tailored coat, reinforced gloves, boots, and shoulder protection |

The pick is a short one-handed excavation and climbing tool, not a miner's two-handed pickaxe. Rope gear uses physical anchors and load limits. Elara cannot cross empty space or dismantle arbitrary architecture because a contextual prompt exists.

## Core Actions

### Measured Thrust

Elara tests reach with a feint and places a rapier mark on a valid weak point. A marked point is easier for either member of the duo to exploit but does not create damage where the target has no vulnerable structure.

### Faultfinder

The delver's pick strikes guard, armour joins, carapace seams, shields, doors, and authored break points. Against living unarmoured targets it remains a dangerous weapon, but its special value comes from structure rather than implausible raw damage.

### Courtesy Returned

A timed rapier deflection carries Elara past the attacker's lead side and opens a counter. Heavy blows, area attacks, and attacks she cannot physically redirect require evasion instead.

### Three Needles

Elara prepares one dart family at a safe point:

- **numbing preparation:** slows a living target's movement and recovery;
- **bleeding preparation:** worsens an already opened wound;
- **monsterbane preparation:** irritates a selected natural creature family and helps reveal its movement or weak tissue.

Preparations use real material, carried ammunition, and creature knowledge. Corpse residue, constructs, slimes, and creatures without compatible anatomy ignore inappropriate darts.

### Over the Lintel

Elara uses a low obstacle, climb, rope, or descending line to change angle and attack. It supplies shortcuts and combat entries but never gates critical traversal for another player vocation.

### A Lady's Exit

Elara breaks line of sight with prepared powder-smoke, relocates, and can draw the player out of immediate focus. Wind, water, enclosed fire, and creatures that do not rely on ordinary sight change its value.

## Companion AI

- Prefer marked, casting, climbing, or heavily guarded targets over the nearest target.
- Do not spend darts on immune creatures once immunity is known.
- Use Faultfinder on authored armour and structure rather than repeatedly striking flesh.
- Reserve A Lady's Exit for dangerous focus, revive pressure, or a commanded withdrawal.
- Avoid standing inside the player's heavy attack line or persistent spell geometry.
- Call out valuable openings without claiming that every crack is a secret passage.

## Custom-Vocation Transfer

Completing [[A House Fed by Bones]] records Elara's equipment parts as account unlocks. They remain unavailable in an authored Slayer loadout but can be used once [[Custom Vocation Workshop]] is open.

Completing [[No Lady of Velescar]] with Elara resolved or compromised adds a coherent **Delver-Duelist** blueprint. The blueprint uses one-handed finesse weapon, off-hand tool, prepared missiles, light armour, and a compatible mastered growth profile. It cannot also claim paired blades or another occupied off-hand system.

## Moral and Presentation Limits

- Poison is neither treated as uniquely evil nor made consequence-free; target, purpose, dosage, and bystander risk matter.
- Grave goods remain evidence or property with living claimants, not generic loot quality.
- Her makeup, court posture, and elegant fencing are sincere parts of her identity, not a disguise that disappears when she becomes morally better.
- Her resolved arc makes her more careful about ownership, not less delighted by dangerous discovery.
