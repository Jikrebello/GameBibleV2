# Names Beneath Lime

## Role in the Arc

This is Elara Velescar's quest 1 of four. It is playable in Early Autumn and Open Autumn and advances the arc without requiring Elara Velescar in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A customs seizure contains lime-caked Virelian grave goods marked with Elara's old survey cuts. A porter who opened the crate has been arrested as the thief.

Clear the porter before the goods are dispersed, identify the dead whose property was moved, and decide whether Elara will make a first public admission.

## Player Actions

Inspect the customs crate, follow the porter's route through Quarry Steps, compare the pieces with burial copies at Paupers' Cloister, and confront the Vardek factor who arranged their winter sale.

## Opposition and Pressure

The factor has bribed warehouse guards and tries to burn the packing roll. Elara first wants the goods secured quietly because a public record will attach her name.

## Information Paths

The lime recipe and cut marks identify the original plots; the porter saw the factor's seal; a cloister copyist can match two names to Virelian burial appeals. Any two establish the chain.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Return the named goods publicly, return them quietly while clearing the porter, or seize them as evidence. Elara must state her own part rather than letting the player invent an innocent version.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the roll burns, surviving labels and the porter still lead to the cloister, but one family remains unidentified and restitution costs rise. If the factor escapes, he warns Vardek.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the winter transition, the initiating evidence is secured by another party and the quest returns in Early Winter with higher institutional leverage and lower trust; it does not simply vanish.

## Rewards and Return Value

A public admission opens harder but stronger evidence routes later. Quiet repair protects approval in the short term but gives Vardek room to frame her.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
