# Elara Banter and Reactivity

This register controls Elara's voice around the other companions, Grenzburg's factions, locations, and campaign crises. [[Elara Companion Campaign]] controls the eleven-piece authored sequence and ten-piece playthrough ceiling. This note gives writing direction and representative exchanges; final performance scripts may vary wording without changing relationship movement.

## Voice Rules

- Elara is witty because she enjoys company, not because she must puncture every sincere moment.
- Her court manners are genuine. Growth changes what she believes rank permits, not whether she likes elegance.
- She uses flirtation only with an eligible player who has reciprocated interest.
- She does not reduce companions to their crimes in ambient banter.
- Early class assumptions should be legible but repairable; she notices servants before learning consistently to address them as decision-makers.
- In danger she becomes concise: target, structure, exit, person at risk.
- Grave jokes are rare and usually directed at her former self, never at named dead or grieving families.

## Ilyana Veshkar

**Early - the room already planned**

> **Elara:** You have assigned us doors without asking which ones we can open.
>
> **Ilyana:** I chose by posture.
>
> **Elara:** Mine says front door.
>
> **Ilyana:** Yours says window, with expensive objections.

**After Five Cases - shared seals**

> **Elara:** Shared seals are dangerous things.
>
> **Ilyana:** You had a title seal.
>
> **Elara:** I had villages beneath mine. You had friends.
>
> **Ilyana:** That was meant to sound like a smaller crime. It did not.

**Winter - useful property**

> **Ilyana:** If the instrument saves the ward, can we argue custody afterward?
>
> **Elara:** We may use it with its owner's answer now.
>
> **Ilyana:** You make daring sound administrative.
>
> **Elara:** You make theft sound like tempo.

**Late - open road**

> **Elara:** An expedition with no house and a company with no proprietor.
>
> **Ilyana:** That sounds structurally irresponsible.
>
> **Elara:** Exhilarating, isn't it?
>
> **Ilyana:** Ask me again when we have provisions.

## Svala Last-Beacon

**Early - powder and weather**

> **Svala:** Snow does not care about powder.
>
> **Elara:** Precisely why I must. Someone should maintain standards.
>
> **Svala:** Maintain your gloves first. That seam is opening.

**After Elara quest one - the dead**

> **Svala:** In my hall, taking from a grave makes the whole hearth answer.
>
> **Elara:** In mine, it made one lady answer and several workers disappear into a ledger.
>
> **Svala:** Then your hall knew how to hide shame. Not how to carry it.

**Winter - hospitality**

> **Elara:** You have fed every person in this camp except yourself.
>
> **Svala:** I ate while stirring.
>
> **Elara:** That is tasting. Sit down before I invoke noble privilege.
>
> **Svala:** Try it. I need kindling.

**Late - chosen names**

> **Svala:** No Lady of Velescar, then?
>
> **Elara:** Elara will do. Lady, when the dress deserves it.
>
> **Svala:** Good. Last-Beacon is work, not blood. A name should know its job.

## Ashira bath Melan

This is Elara's principal foil relationship: two delighted investigators learning that accurate knowledge still carries provenance, consent, and use obligations.

**Early - scholarship**

> **Ashira:** Your dating is wrong by at least sixty years.
>
> **Elara:** My dating put us beside the door.
>
> **Ashira:** Your confidence put us beside the door. The inscription says drain.
>
> **Elara:** A drain with excellent proportions.

**After Elara quest two - useful records**

> **Elara:** I called the field book knowledge, as though knowledge could not be stolen.
>
> **Ashira:** Knowledge is not innocent because it is accurate.
>
> **Elara:** You have been waiting to say that to me.
>
> **Ashira:** I have been waiting to hear it myself.

**Winter - categories**

> **Ashira:** That is residue, not a person.
>
> **Elara:** And therefore not harmless.
>
> **Ashira:** Correct.
>
> **Elara:** We may be becoming unbearable in stereo.

**Late - ethical delving**

> **Ashira:** Record, witness, remove only what cannot remain safely.
>
> **Elara:** And ask who still claims the place before congratulating ourselves.
>
> **Ashira:** Irritatingly complete.
>
> **Elara:** I learned from an irritant.

## Bernard Valeon

**Early - the missing spoon**

> **Elara:** Bernard, the spoon in your sleeve is attempting escape.
>
> **Bernard:** I was going to return it.
>
> **Elara:** Good. I will accompany you and admire the miracle.

**After Elara quest one - kinds of theft**

> **Bernard:** You planned yours. Mine arrives before the planning.
>
> **Elara:** And we both became very clever after the taking.
>
> **Bernard:** Clever is useful before, too.
>
> **Elara:** An upsetting doctrine. Keep it.

**Winter - burial duty**

> **Bernard:** The soul is gone. The body still belongs to love and truth.
>
> **Elara:** I once thought that was sentiment dressed as law.
>
> **Bernard:** And now?
>
> **Elara:** Now I know law was the cheaper garment.

**Late - safeguards**

> **Elara:** You asked me to watch your hands.
>
> **Bernard:** You asked me to watch your justifications.
>
> **Elara:** Mine are faster.
>
> **Bernard:** Mine fit in a pocket.

## Dismas Raben

**Early - exits**

> **Elara:** You found three exits before noticing the painted ceiling.
>
> **Dismas:** The ceiling will still be painted if we need one of them.
>
> **Elara:** This is why nobody invites scouts to galleries.

**After Elara quest two - risk**

> **Dismas:** You went back for the diggers.
>
> **Elara:** You sound surprised.
>
> **Dismas:** I know what it costs to hear people under stone and keep moving.
>
> **Elara:** Then do not let me call the cost adventure.

**Winter - route planning**

> **Elara:** The short route has only one drawback.
>
> **Dismas:** It is an ambush.
>
> **Elara:** I was going to say poor scenery.
>
> **Dismas:** You may admire it while I find the shooters.

**Late - returning**

> **Dismas:** You still enter places you should not.
>
> **Elara:** I ask better people first.
>
> **Dismas:** And if they say no?
>
> **Elara:** I suffer character development. Quietly.

## Zafir ibn Samad

This remains a strong secondary relationship. Their inherited and procedural forms of ownership let each recognize the other's favored excuse with unusual precision.

**Early - orderly ownership**

> **Zafir:** A fixed term and right of petition are better than arbitrary bondage.
>
> **Elara:** A clean grave inventory is better than a sack in the dark.
>
> **Zafir:** You intend an accusation.
>
> **Elara:** I intend company.

**After Elara quest two - favored excuses**

> **Zafir:** You protected households by refusing them the choice of what protection cost.
>
> **Elara:** You protected debtors by writing how they might ask an owner to stop.
>
> **Zafir:** We are unpleasantly well matched.

**Winter - competence**

> **Elara:** The third seal transfers the cabinet without transferring its contents.
>
> **Zafir:** Correct. The drafter believed nouns obeyed furniture.
>
> **Elara:** Shall we disappoint him?
>
> **Zafir:** With precision.

**Late - property and freedom**

> **Zafir:** You can transfer the land without declaring yourself forgiven.
>
> **Elara:** You can cut the cords without pretending the law taught you to.
>
> **Zafir:** Then we do the work and spare one another absolution.
>
> **Elara:** At last, a beautiful procedure.

### Named Leader Reactions

- **Alaric:** she likes his decisiveness and watches for the moment need becomes entitlement.
- **Pierre:** she calls him dull before learning that recorded limits protect people after brave rulers leave.
- **Liese:** she respects service without aristocratic theater.
- **Brina:** immediate practical trust, sharpened by shared debtor status.
- **Koss:** open dislike constrained by the danger of giving him a pretext.
- **Lucia:** polite intellectual sympathy mixed with recognition of her own former excuses.
- **Caestus:** respect without flirtation or irreverent needling; he serves the dead and poor in ways she failed to.
- **Konrad:** accepts his sincerity and resists his jurisdictional conclusions.
- **Runa:** early fascination becomes respect once Elara stops treating Folk memory as discoverable treasure.
- **Jorund:** recognizes genuine grievance and condemns chosen civilian murder.
- **Mara:** enjoys her routes and dislikes how easily enjoyment can conceal who pays for them.
- **Ivo:** values his archive; rejects the claim that correct evidence grants private execution.

## Grenzburg Routine

Elara's home schedule changes with season and personal progress. It makes her findable without trapping every conversation inside the Blackjack Yard.

### Ordinary Autumn

- **Morning:** practices rapier footwork against a chalked Yard wall, repairs darts, and checks the public assignment board.
- **Midday:** alternates between the Delvers' Arcade, Customs Hall viewing tables, Old Market appraisers, and the Yard's equipment bench.
- **Evening:** eats with ordinary Blackjacks before moving to the roof to sketch routes, clean her face, and repair clothes.
- **Night:** sleeps at the Yard unless an unlocked underworld contact has arranged one authored reconnaissance scene.

### Winter Siege

- **Morning:** helps mark safe roof crossings and inspects fire-damaged masonry without pretending to be a wallwright.
- **Midday:** carries burial lists between Customs, Chancery House, and Paupers' Cloister after quest one; before it, she avoids the cloister.
- **Evening:** repairs equipment near the warmest Yard stove and offers short conversations about refugee privilege, Vardek, or the Drake preparations.
- **Night:** takes one roof watch during high siege states and otherwise remains available at the Yard.

### Spring and Summer

- **Spring:** visits reclaimed sites, claimant hearings, and archive stores according to her quest states. She never enters Old Tithe Vault as a casual idle location.
- **Resolved summer:** divides time among the Delvers' Arcade, returned-burial work, the Provenance Board, and expedition planning at the Yard.
- **Compromised summer:** receives estate agents at the River Exchange, manages a reserved share through the Provenance Board, and keeps a more formal room in Ledger Quays while retaining her Yard bed.
- **Broken summer:** withdraws from ordinary routines after the warned confrontation and appears at her departure, restored-house scene, or changed [[The First Honest Find]] encounter.

When Elara is selected for the active party, her schedule yields at the next valid hub. Quest, siege, injury, and relationship scenes override ambient routine without creating duplicate versions of her.

## Location Observations

- **Crown Heights:** identifies which noble houses are solvent by maintenance rather than display.
- **Cathedral Close:** becomes quieter around burial lists and notices unnamed labor before relic ornament.
- **Old Market:** enjoys appraisers, bad antiquities, performers, and public bargaining.
- **Ledger Quays:** reads cargo laundering and becomes visibly tense around sealed household accounts.
- **Lantern Ward:** initially overdresses for the Yard, then makes no apology for continuing to do so.
- **Grenzburg Underways:** gives structural callouts, marks return routes, and refuses to call every sealed niche treasure.
- **Wolfsmere:** asks permission more often after being corrected; never receives a convenient revelation of restricted truth.
- **Southwood:** becomes most animated around barrows, scarred masonry, and dangerous climbs.
- **Highlake:** respects miners who understand stone better than titled surveyors.
- **Tannbruck:** reads the fort's hierarchy through kitchens, repair crews, and who sleeps nearest the gate.
- **Frostfenn:** jokes less, checks equipment more, and treats every safe return as an achievement.
- **Deep Muster:** identifies rank and property language in the command architecture and rejects its claim to honorable continuity.

## Embedded Opportunity Register

These ten authored opportunities make Elara present outside her formal campaign. They may add readiness, direction evidence, or a later line; none is mandatory.

1. **Roads Worth Saving:** Elara distinguishes Vardek spoil cuts, ordinary bandit handling, and Folk boundary marks. She refuses to make separate enemies into one convenient conspiracy.
2. **The City in Siege:** she opens a roof-and-crane route through damaged Ledger Quays and can preserve either trapped residents or a pursuit line.
3. **The Land Reclaimed:** she identifies which recovered rolls, tools, and grave goods establish living claims rather than military salvage.
4. **Bread and Burial:** she helps restore names while accepting that useful burial work does not acquit her.
5. **Lucia's Long Book:** she separates grain logistics from debtor valuation and recognizes Lucia's reform language as a more polished version of an old temptation.
6. **The Names They Buried:** she matches protected-crime records to burial provenance without turning the archive into private leverage.
7. **Finder's Share:** in a future local story, Elara distinguishes discoverer, worker, landholder, and living-custodian claims instead of declaring a found object ownerless.
8. **Ropehouse Ruin:** she reads load marks and practices shoring whose safety margin belongs to the workers using it.
9. **Named Property Board:** at the Blackjack Yard, she establishes a visible holding process for seized items with identifiable owners.
10. **Abandoned Pack:** a bounded systemic encounter changes with her direction: accountable Elara seeks the owner, mixed Elara inventories before deciding, and entitled Elara calls useful property abandoned too quickly.

## Campaign Scenes

1. **First camp:** Elara wagers she can identify everyone's former station from boots and packing; she is wrong about at least one person.
2. **After the introduction:** The Wager opens interest without committing it.
3. **Tannbruck fall:** she chooses a rescue or denial task and later names who paid for the option the player selected.
4. **After Act I:** [[A Better Wager]] and Without the Powder establish friendship before intimacy.
5. **Winter camps:** she catches herself referring to refugees as a burden and deliberately restarts the sentence.
6. **City breach:** she opens a roof route and uses powder-smoke to cover people crossing it.
7. **Drake hunt:** awe never turns the animal into a speaking moral claimant; she insists custody of its remains still needs a living decision.
8. **After The Patron's Cabinet:** relationship commitment or friendship refusal occurs without another companion present.
9. **Raven Shelf:** a committed male-player romance gains its own ascent, rescue, private camp, and romantic memory rather than borrowing Elara's moral climax.
10. **Windcut Mine:** a committed female-player bond gains its own equal survey, reciprocal rescue, public claim, and platonic pact of guaranteed return.
11. **Gerhold:** she recognizes the same grammar as House Velescar carried to its extreme: service that authority refuses to release.
12. **Before the Deep Muster:** she offers one honest wager about both returning and specifies that neither life belongs to the other.
13. **Summer:** [[The First Honest Find]] and ambient schedules reflect resolved, compromised, or broken disposition of the house, plus only the relationship episode actually completed.

## Outcome Variants

**Resolved Elara** jokes more freely because rank no longer needs defending. Her banter distinguishes continuing duty from permanent shame.

**Compromised Elara** remains warm and loyal but becomes defensive around private reserves, estate agents, and claims that relinquishment was possible.

**Broken Elara** stops ordinary party banter after the final warned confrontation. If encountered before departure, her manners become formally perfect and emotionally closed.

## Summer Dialogue Samples

### Resolved

> **Elara:** I have three expedition offers. One has permission, one has a map, and one has excellent wine.
>
> **Player response:** Which one are we taking?
>
> **Elara:** The one with permission. Then we steal the wine from the third offer by paying for it.

### Compromised

> **Elara:** The agents call it a modest reserve. Kata calls it the room where I keep the part I could not surrender.
>
> **Player response:** Is she wrong?
>
> **Elara:** No. That is why I still open her letters.

### Broken Before Departure

> **Elara:** House Velescar survived. You need not look as though I have announced a death.
>
> **Player response:** I am looking at the price.
>
> **Elara:** Then we have nothing left to discuss politely.
