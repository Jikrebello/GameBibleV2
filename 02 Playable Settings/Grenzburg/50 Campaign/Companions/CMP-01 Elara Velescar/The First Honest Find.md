# The First Honest Find

## Quest Function

This is Elara's summer coda. It proves what her settled outcome looks like in practice by giving her another ruin, useful objects, paid labor, local custodians, and a chance to be delighted.

The coda reflects the Act III outcome. It cannot turn a compromised or broken Elara into the resolved version after judgement has closed.

## Premise

Spring repairs expose a sealed survey loft inside Ropehouse Ruin in the Highlake Uplands. The ruin once served Atherian road crews who measured rope loads, bridge spans, and mountain weather.

The loft is not a tomb. Its likely contents are:

- brass measuring frames;
- road and snowfall tablets;
- named work rotations;
- a compact Atherian load warden;
- tools useful to Highlake bridge and mine crews.

Highlake ropeworkers and one Ghost-Foot route household both claim a legitimate interest. Elara proposes an expedition whose terms depend upon who she became.

## Outcome-Dependent Agreement

### Resolved Elara

Elara arrives with a written plain-language agreement:

- workers receive stated wages whether or not treasure exists;
- the route household controls access and receives copies of every map;
- practical tools remain in Highlake;
- named historical records enter local custody;
- Elara receives expedition expenses, a fair discoverer's share of unclaimed movable goods, and credited copies.

She asks both custodians to correct the terms before anyone enters.

### Compromised Elara

The agreement pays people and records provenance but reserves Elara's first selection, display credit, and control of publication. The local parties knowingly accept because the work is useful, while their unease remains visible.

The player cannot retroactively erase this compromise through one dialogue choice.

### Broken or Departed Elara

Elara reaches the ruin under the reduced or restored Velescar title with a household-backed crew. If she has departed the Blackjacks, the player encounters the expedition already operating.

Immediate safety decisions remain playable, but her title settlement and controlling claim do not reverse. A final letter or conversation records whether she still recognizes the player as friend, former intimate, rival, or witness.

## Party and Staging

When Elara remains available, she is required and the ordinary field party remains available. Temporary workers wait at secured stations outside the companion limit. In a departed state, the player may bring the ordinary field party to the site and encounter her expedition already operating.

Only Elara participates in the final coda conversation. No relationship is required.

## Vocation Access

Every player vocation can stabilize the outer shell, cross the load floor, and answer the warden through ordinary equipment, counterweights, declared loads, maintenance access, or physical shutdown. Vocation abilities create faster or safer methods without owning a required route.

## Beginning: Terms at the Ropehouse

The player may review the agreement, inspect the exposed wall, speak with workers, and identify three interests:

- safe reuse of the rope-testing frame;
- preservation of names and mountain-route records;
- access to movable brass parts.

The coda does not hide a secretly evil claimant. Its tension comes from how a settled Elara orders legitimate interests.

## Middle: The Survey Loft

Entry requires:

1. stabilizing a roof beam through shoring, force, counterweight, or a safer upper approach;
2. crossing an old load-testing floor whose marks explain its function;
3. disabling or satisfying a dormant Atherian load warden.

The warden is a non-conscious industrial engine. It tests declared weight against platform capacity and reacts to overload. The player may:

- distribute weight across marked lanes;
- reset the counterweights;
- physically disable the drive;
- use Warlock or Lancewright counter-work after the relevant vocation is available;
- take the upper maintenance path.

Failure damages tools, injures no one without a visible rescue window, and may close one recovery.

## End: What Comes Down

The loft contains three recoveries:

- **The Crew Tablets:** names, shifts, injuries, and route notes;
- **The Span Frame:** practical measuring equipment useful to Highlake reconstruction;
- **The Brass Sky Rule:** a valuable portable instrument with both display and technical value.

Resolved Elara follows the agreement without asking the player for permission to backslide. Compromised Elara uses her reserved first selection. Broken Elara directs the crew through title authority.

The player still determines immediate handling when collapse, weather, or the load warden makes all three recoveries difficult. That choice affects Ropehouse Ruin, not Elara's already settled moral outcome.

## Relationship Variants

- A romance completed through [[Raven Shelf at Dawn]] recalls the Highlake camp, Nika's rescue, and choosing the next journey together without treating either person's future as property. A committed romance that missed the episode remains real but does not invent those memories or rewards in summer.
- A sworn bond completed through [[Two Names on the Rope]] adds the site to the two women's shared record of honest claims and guaranteed returns. A committed bond that missed the episode remains real but does not retroactively create the Windcut pact.
- A friend receives the same expedition access and moral resolution without reduced content.
- A former or refused relationship retains specific history rather than defaulting to neutral dialogue.

## Persistent Result

On resolved or compromised paths where Elara remains connected to the Blackjacks, the **Provenance Board** opens at the Yard. Named antiquities display known custodians, evidence uses, lawful buyers, and unresolved claimants before sale or donation.

The board improves information. It does not automatically identify every owner or prevent the player from making a harmful choice.

Ropehouse Ruin retains:

- a stabilized workshop, controlled ruin, stripped shell, or collapsed loft;
- Highlake or title-backed custody;
- visible crew tablets or their copied record;
- a seasonal Elara visit when she remains in Grenzburg.
