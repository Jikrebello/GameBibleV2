# Yield Heard Twice

## Role in the Arc

This is Svala Last-Beacon's quest 2 of four. It is playable in Early Winter and Deep Winter and advances the arc without requiring Svala Last-Beacon in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

During a food riot, an armed deserter yields to Svala after striking a child. Her rage response begins before she fully hears him.

Keep civilians alive, take the deserter into custody, and determine whether Svala can accept an external recall before the siege worsens.

## Player Actions

Defend the granary, separate looters from armed organizers, pursue the deserter onto the wall circuit, and test a chosen recall signal in a second combat crisis.

## Opposition and Pressure

The deserter uses civilians as cover before yielding; soldiers encourage Svala to finish him; rage makes target changes and commands harder to register.

## Information Paths

Witnesses saw the first yield, shield dents establish when force stopped, and the deserter's own route exposes the planned theft.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Choose who holds Svala's recall authority and what happens if she ignores it. Executing a captive after surrender cannot be reframed as efficient justice.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the deserter escapes, his abandoned stores reveal the next riot cell. If Svala strikes him, he survives maimed and the trial becomes part of her reckoning.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the spring transition, unresolved danger produces a named casualty, loss, or policy change and the reckoning quest inherits it.

## Rewards and Return Value

Turns rage from flavor into a controlled narrative risk and prepares her formal trial.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
