# The Unpaid Champion

## Role in the Arc

This is Svala Last-Beacon's quest 1 of four. It is playable in Open Autumn and Late Autumn and advances the arc without requiring Svala Last-Beacon in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

An Ättar winter delegation reaches Fort Tannbruck carrying the dead champion's broken shield and an unpaid weregild claim. Svala is recognized during the Southbound Muster.

Prevent Koss from converting the claim into permanent service and hear what Svala actually did before the delegation departs south.

## Player Actions

Translate the claim with Astrid Snow-Rope, recover a stolen payment chest on the military road, and stage a protected hearing at Muster Green.

## Opposition and Pressure

Debt collectors want the claim folded into Svala's Bank account; hostile soldiers mock hall law; the champion's sister Yrsa refuses any settlement built on a lie.

## Information Paths

The broken shield bears the yield mark, Astrid witnessed the end, and Svala's Rimewing cord records emergency payments after the killing.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Acknowledge killing after yield and preserve the claim, dispute only the amount, or let Medori buy the claimant out. Svala's own confession is required for a resolved route.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the chest is lost, service can replace coin. If the delegation is attacked, survivors continue the hearing at West Refuge Hall with worse trust.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the winter transition, the initiating evidence is secured by another party and the quest returns in Early Winter with higher institutional leverage and lower trust; it does not simply vanish.

## Rewards and Return Value

Recruits Svala and establishes that lawful grievance and lawful surrender remain real even when custom was unjust.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
