# The Rimewing Trial

## Role in the Arc

This is Svala Last-Beacon's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring and advances the arc without requiring Svala Last-Beacon in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The Rimewing Sisters convene a travelling trial at Tuskway Redoubt after evidence surfaces that their forbidden rage school trained several rescue fighters.

Judge Svala's conduct without sacrificing the rescue fellowship as a scapegoat or excusing its secret discipline.

## Player Actions

Escort witnesses through the uplands, recover the rage instructor's hidden lesson cords, survive an avalanche rescue, and present the case before Yrsa Wolf-Brand.

## Opposition and Pressure

Some Sisters want Svala to bear everything; others demand the player destroy records because exposure could end a service that saves lives. Weather and ordinary predators threaten the hearing independently.

## Information Paths

Astrid's witness account, lesson cords, and the champion's shield establish personal and institutional responsibility. Any two hold the trial; all three expose the secret curriculum.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Accept exile and reform, seek conditional pardon, or protect the school. Relationship commitment follows the verdict, with exact promises named.

## Fail-Forward Results

If a witness is lost in the avalanche, recovered gear preserves testimony but weakens trust. If cords burn, surviving trainees can still confess at personal cost.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If left until the Muster Crisis, the confrontation occurs under emergency conditions: one evidence path closes, the relationship cannot commit until the issue is faced, and the final quest begins from a compromised state.

## Rewards and Return Value

Unlocks romance or sworn bond and decides whether Rimewing rescue work can continue under visible limits.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
