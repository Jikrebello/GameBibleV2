# Companion Grounded Writing Standard

This note controls the social reality, independence, restraint, and long-form appeal of Grenzburg's companions. It supersedes any older assumption that all eight must behave like an always-on dramatic ensemble.

## Audience Contract

Companions are professional Blackjack colleagues before they are confidants. The player may come to love, trust, oppose, avoid, romance, or swear fellowship with them. None begins by demanding intimacy, performing a marketable personality, or treating the player as a universal confessor.

The desired test is simple: each companion should remain credible company during work, travel, meals, boredom, defeat, and silence.

## Craft Reference

Author-only reference territory is used for craft rather than imitation:

- Fallout 4 supplies ordinary availability, useful field presence, routines, and affinity earned through action.
- Cyberpunk and GTA supply material lives, class, loyalty, family, crime, and relationships that exist beyond the protagonist.
- Fallout: New Vegas and Pillars of Eternity supply conviction, moral ugliness, intellectual independence, and characters who do not require approval to remain compelling.
- The Witcher supplies professional familiarity, selective affection, restrained exposition, and the sense that adults existed before the player arrived.
- Constant sass, ensemble therapy, compulsory flirting, universal emotional fluency, and sexuality used as a substitute for character are negative controls.

Reference characters never authorize copied plots, lines, mannerisms, or identities.

## Adult-Life Requirement

Every companion bible must establish:

1. current Blackjack occupation and useful field role;
2. an ordinary weekly routine;
3. at least three present-day relationships not dependent on the player;
4. material wants involving money, tools, shelter, status, faith, craft, food, or future work;
5. convictions they will defend even when the player disapproves;
6. manners toward strangers, superiors, workers, children, enemies, and intimates;
7. what they keep private and what earns disclosure;
8. humor, anger, attraction, care, and silence as different modes rather than one voice trick;
9. behavior when unselected and untracked;
10. one pursuit that would continue if the player never recruited them.

## Professional Baseline

- Colleagues begin civil and task-focused.
- Bluntness is not contempt. Open disrespect requires a specific offence, rivalry, betrayal, or extreme pressure.
- Rank, culture, age, danger, hospitality, and location affect speech.
- Disagreement need not become intimacy. A character may refuse, leave, work in silence, or remain unconvinced.
- No companion must like every other companion. All can normally share a field assignment without turning it into a performance of hostility.
- A companion comments only on events they witnessed or learned through a credible report.

## Speech and Silence

Characters enter scenes wanting something immediate. Theme emerges from work, appetite, error, loyalty, fear, or refusal.

Do not use:

- symmetrical moral debates;
- group therapy scenes;
- polished insight from every speaker;
- routine snark as chemistry;
- a quip after death, confession, prayer, or irreversible loss;
- dialogue that announces the companion's archetype, central lie, or required lesson;
- flirtation as ambient proof of charisma;
- the player as a compulsory therapist;
- a serious disagreement resolved because both speakers summarize the design document correctly.

Across ambient material, target approximately:

| Material | Share |
|---|---:|
| work, surroundings, tactics, craft, and current events | 50% |
| appetite, humor, irritation, pleasure, and ordinary relationships | 25% |
| trust, friendship, attraction, and factional opinion | 15% |
| original wrong and direct backstory explanation | no more than 10% |

## Romance and Sworn Fellowship

- Romantic subtext remains inactive until the player selects an explicit authored interest signal.
- Before that signal, there is no flirting, sexual teasing, jealousy, or suggestive party banter.
- After commitment, romance remains primarily private rather than becoming an ensemble spectacle.
- Sworn bonds remain explicitly platonic and use distinct activities, language, and rewards.
- Refusing a private conversation or declining interest causes no approval loss and no repeated solicitation.
- Relationship status never supplies the strongest moral resolution.

## Banter Setting

The player controls ambient chatter without changing content access:

| Setting | Rule |
|---|---|
| Low | essential quest, tactical, and player-prompted dialogue only |
| Normal | no more than two ambient exchanges during roughly twenty minutes of uninterrupted travel |
| High | no more than four contextual exchanges during the same span, with no repeated filler |

Deaths, funerals, confessions, major failures, stealth sequences, solemn discoveries, and explicit requests for quiet impose a silence lock at every setting.

## Campaign Composition

Each eleven-piece campaign keeps its authoring budget while hiding the template from the player.

- No more than four pieces primarily prosecute the original wrong.
- At least six contain an external objective worth playing without the personal arc.
- At least three primarily show work, pleasure, loyalty, competence, or ordinary life.
- Scale and form vary: expedition, job, investigation, social event, field incident, correspondence, workshop, game, meal, or quiet activity.
- Only act finales require the fixed duo. A duo finale need not end in confession, moral summary, or relationship escalation.
- The journal presents titles and immediate stakes, never architecture labels or an eleven-step emotional ladder.

## Stable Colleague Rule

Ignoring a personal campaign leaves a functional colleague, not a deteriorating patient.

- Untracked companions continue visible assignments and routines.
- Seasonal incidents change or pass, but the central personal decision remains unresolved.
- Later engagement uses changed re-entry appropriate to the current world.
- Vocation and capstone paths receive credible changed routes rather than disappearing solely through inattention.
- A never-engaged companion reaches summer employed, recruitable, and recognizably unresolved.
- No ignored companion dies, departs, becomes a villain, requires supervision, or achieves a grand transformation unseen.

## Acceptance Tests

Every companion must pass:

- **company test:** three engaging scenes without romance, confession, backstory revelation, or moral instruction;
- **external-stake test:** each formal quest has a playable situation beyond correcting the companion;
- **silence test:** serious material can end without explanation or a joke;
- **respect test:** hostility has a concrete cause and consequence;
- **absence test:** the companion remains useful and socially situated when ignored;
- **interest test:** no romantic language appears before the player signals it;
- **voice test:** lines remain identifiable through priorities and rhythm rather than catchphrases or archetype labels.
