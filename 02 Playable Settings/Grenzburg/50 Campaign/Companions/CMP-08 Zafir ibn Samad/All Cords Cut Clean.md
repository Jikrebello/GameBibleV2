# All Cords Cut Clean

## Premise

The Unending Muster captures the Open-Cord Circle's deployed relations and feeds them into a Qianglong **Name-Rank Relay**. The relay mistakes voluntary roles for military ranks and attempts to make them permanent.

The Circle's participants are separated among five signal stations. Their personal release keys still work, but using them independently will break Zafir's perfect rescue sequence.

A master ring inside the relay offers him one flawless emergency command over the whole team.

## Staging

With two Act III assets, the tracked docket identifies the Relay and the five participant signals. Accepting the warning forms the fixed player-plus-Zafir duo.

Temporary rescued volunteers may move through local scenes, but no other full companion enters the finale.

The warning names:

- every participant's current state;
- the five preparation assets;
- the relationship safeguard, if any;
- autonomous resolution at commitment to [[The Deep Muster Main Quest]].

## Preparation

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `zafir_open_cord_circle_formed` | provides practiced voluntary roles |
| `zafir_muster_command_sample` | identifies false rank before it closes |
| `zafir_distributed_release_keys` | lets every participant cut a station relation |
| `zafir_fenn_anchor_route` | opens a low-attention approach |
| `zafir_person_form_separation` | preserves non-conscious forms while destroying person-control |

Two permit entry. Three make all volunteers, keys, and the Relay objective achievable. Four or five protect one objective from a failed station.

The romance or bond safeguard preserves one volunteer or key after one failure. It never chooses the override.

## Approaches

- **Fenn service road:** fastest, exposed to residue patrols.
- **Drainage gallery:** cold water and structural hazards, low command attention.
- **Signal ridge:** vertical approach that reveals all five stations before entry.

Every vocation has ordinary ladders, doors, tools, and combat routes. Special movement or magic supplies shortcuts.

## The Five Stations

Each station corresponds to one Circle role:

1. carrier;
2. route keeper;
3. mechanism worker;
4. field carer;
5. Karel's revision station.

At each, the volunteer may:

- follow Zafir's signal;
- use a personal key early;
- alter the release;
- withdraw from the rescue pattern to save somebody nearby.

An early release changes later geometry but does not morally fail the volunteer.

Signals travel through shutters, bells, tension cords, and visible lamps. There is no telepathic command.

### Pause Point: Volunteer Signals Found

The quest may pause once all surviving signals are identified.

## The Relay Interior

The duo crosses:

- a rank intake gallery that assigns false office by carried equipment;
- a replacement hall where non-conscious residue imitates officers;
- a cord drum that converts voluntary load into permanent command;
- the master-ring chamber.

Threats are residue, ward engines, structural movement, and imposed rank. They are not trapped souls.

## The Master Ring

The Relay offers one bounded command over every relation copied from the Circle:

> HOLD POSITION UNTIL RELEASED BY MASTER.

Using it produces the cleanest simultaneous extraction. It also removes each participant's right to decide during the most important moment.

Zafir may:

- destroy the ring before use and trust independent keys;
- use it within a previously accepted emergency term, then destroy it;
- impose it without present consent;
- preserve it for future emergencies.

Prior blanket consent does not cover a materially changed danger. Participants who can signal refusal must be heard.

## Boss: The Rank Warden

A non-conscious corpse-and-brass command form guards the master drum.

It:

- assigns forced positions;
- replaces broken armour from the drum;
- redirects attacks through rank lines;
- reforms while the master relation remains.

Distributed releases strip its powers. The player may fight, break anchors, redirect loads, or collapse the drum supports. No Binder-only action is mandatory.

## Final Decision

If the ring is destroyed, the Circle's releases occur at different times. The player and Zafir must adapt:

- carry a volunteer who left position;
- open a route Karel revised;
- abandon one material objective;
- trust a carer to delay for a patient;
- cut the final relation without central timing.

The best moral outcome is harder, not ceremonial.

## Outcomes

**No Master Ring:** all surviving participants retain release, living-person control is destroyed, and Zafir accepts a plan changed by others.

**Bounded Override:** one previously accepted command saves lives, the ring is destroyed afterward, and Zafir remains under review.

**Master's Rescue:** Zafir imposes command and saves the team efficiently, producing a compromised or broken direction according to prior conduct.

**Relay Preserved:** an authority retains the command engine for war use.

Karel may remain, claim peer standing, or leave. His choice is recorded separately.

## Fail-Forward

- A lost station moves its volunteer into a rescue route.
- A broken key forces physical severance.
- A volunteer may be injured, never killed unseen.
- Defeat lets the Muster relocate one participant and begins a shorter pursuit before final resolution.
- A partial Relay collapse destroys evidence and equipment while preserving the campaign decision.

## Rewards

Resolved or compromised completion unlocks **No Master Ring** and the **Open-Cord Jurist** blueprint. Eligible romance grants **Still Worth Knowing**; eligible bond grants **Peer's Objection**.

Legal debt, personal direction, Karel's future, relationship, and destination remain independent.
