# All Cords Cut Clean

## Role in the Arc

This is Zafir ibn Samad's quest 4 of four. It is playable in Early Spring and Muster Crisis. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Zafir ibn Samad duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A client caravan brings several surviving living-person instruments into Grenzburg as supposed protection against the dead march. One anchor is tied to the Deep Muster approach and can command refugees as labor.

Destroy the remaining restraints without discarding freely covenanted beings or non-conscious forms needed for defense.

## Player Actions

Identify every living bearer, escort them out of command range, enter the surface anchor works, and perform a distributed severance while residue troops attack.

## Opposition and Pressure

Clients argue emergency necessity, some restrained people fear retaliation at home, and Zafir may be tempted to use command once to reach the device.

## Information Paths

Client rolls, bearer testimony, and cord resonance map all anchors. Any two prevent someone being left bound.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Cut all person-restraints and prepare return to Iqashar, preserve court emergency powers, or seize the network. Explicit sacrifice can destroy the final anchor only if safer routes have failed.

## Fail-Forward Results

If severance is partial, freed bearers identify the remaining line and the finale proceeds with a command hazard. If records burn, testimony preserves future claims but slows reform.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

Committing to the Deep Muster closes ordinary resolution. The companion reaches a specified compromised summer state unless the quest has already produced a broken or departed outcome.

## Rewards and Return Value

Completes Zafir's freedom and makes his best ending a return to judgement and repair, not escape from his homeland.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
