# Zafir Cord Jurist Package

## Role

Zafir is a control, protection, and relation-management companion using the advanced [[Binder]] vocation.

He does not summon souls. He directs:

- non-conscious bound forms;
- prepared mechanisms;
- elemental relations;
- freely covenanted beings under revocable terms.

His moment-to-moment fantasy is not “master of servants.” It is seeing the relation holding a danger together, giving allies safe control of it, and cutting it at the right instant.

## Native Kit

- binding rings worn across both hands;
- true-name cords stored on a segmented belt;
- light field armour;
- chalk, seal wax, witness tags, and small mechanical releases;
- no shield or conventional melee weapon.

Pressure bursts and snapped cords provide close defence. They are not a substitute martial kit.

## Signature Families

### Relation Read

Reveals the active anchor behind a hostile form, trap, pull, or command. It identifies function rather than moral status.

### Bounded Form

Directs a non-conscious elemental or constructed form inside a visible relation. The form ends when its anchor breaks or the prepared duration expires.

### Release at Hand

Places one ordinary release under an ally's control. The ally chooses when to trigger it.

### Counterclaim

Interrupts an enemy command or compulsion by exposing its unsupported relation. It cannot cancel ordinary persuasion, loyalty, fear, or law by declaration.

### Shared Covenant

Distributes one bounded effect among voluntary participants. Each retains an exit and receives an explicit tell before load transfers.

### No Master Ring

Capstone. Zafir breaks the central override in a multi-part relation, strengthening distributed releases while permanently sacrificing his own master control for that cast.

## Combat Rhythm

1. identify the anchor;
2. mark a safe boundary;
3. give an ally or form a release;
4. exploit the enemy's attempted command;
5. cut the relation before accumulated load becomes domination or backlash.

## Failure

- a broken ring increases Arcane Burn;
- an unrecognized anchor redirects a form;
- too many active relations reduce clarity and response speed;
- a withdrawn participant immediately weakens or ends the relevant covenant;
- an unwilling person cannot become a valid advanced-vocation resource.

## Companion AI

Zafir prioritizes:

1. hostile control affecting the player;
2. endangered allies with a prepared release;
3. enemy anchors;
4. non-conscious forms appropriate to the encounter;
5. damage only after the field is legible.

He announces important relations in short concrete language: “Left ring. Break it.” “Your key.” “That one may leave.”

## Moral and Legal Boundary

Game systems reject human souls, coerced persons, slavery renamed as covenant, and consent that cannot be withdrawn.

Using a lethal simple command remains lethal force. A legal seal proves procedure, not moral validity.
