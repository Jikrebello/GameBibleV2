# The Open-Cord Circle

## Purpose

The Open-Cord Circle is a temporary voluntary field team created during [[Every Hand May Let Go]] to test participant-controlled Binder support against the Unending Muster.

It is not a guild, cult, companion faction, or permanent government.

## Membership

The initial circle consists of:

- [[Karel Reed-Hand]];
- one Blackjack carrier;
- one Chapel-Folk route keeper;
- one Hammer Ward mechanism worker;
- one field carer.

Exact members may change with world state. Every participant:

- volunteers after receiving a plain-language explanation;
- holds a personal release key;
- may revise a route or load;
- may withdraw after deployment begins;
- keeps ordinary civil and faction identity;
- receives independent witness of any injury or equipment loss.

## Field Rule

> No ring closes another hand.

The phrase means no central operator may remove a participant's own release. It does not forbid teamwork, emergency calls, or bounded authority accepted for a specific act.

## Failure and Continuity

A volunteer may leave without becoming a coward, traitor, or quest failure. The plan must contract around the remaining participants.

The Circle may become:

- an ethical teaching model;
- a supervised technical crew;
- a one-campaign emergency team that dissolves;
- evidence of Zafir's renewed mastery if he retains an override.

No outcome becomes future-setting canon.
