# The Fifth Crossing

## Premise

Autumn floodwater cuts Eel Bridge's lower approaches while bandits hold three ferry families on a gravel island.

Zafir calculates four safe outward crossings for a rescue crew. Every one depends on boats, ropes, or counterweights that will be left on the wrong bank after the captives are collected.

Karel and the ferry households identify the missing requirement: rescuers and ferrymen need a fifth crossing home.

## Beginning

The normal party reaches Eel Bridge while a damaged ferry bumps against its chain. Zafir turns the bank into a clean diagram using rings, stakes, and four colored cords.

The local ferry keeper asks, “Which one brings my sons back?”

Zafir initially answers that return follows from successful extraction. It does not.

The player may:

- let Karel redraw the model;
- walk the banks with ferry households;
- inspect the current and damaged chain;
- scout the island and bandit sightlines.

### Pause Point: Four Crossings Marked

The quest may pause before deployment. Flood and captive states remain fixed.

## The Load Claim

Before the rescue begins, deckhand **Aron Reed-Step** tests Zafir's counterweighted platform. Karel points out a rust bloom beneath the eastern collar and asks for the load to be halved. Zafir measures the visible pin, publicly states that the assembly remains within proof, and authorizes the full test.

The hidden back plate tears. Aron falls against the ferry rail and suffers a compound forearm fracture. The platform is lost downstream.

The injury is not random punishment and cannot be prevented by guessing a secret dialogue answer. Two concrete warnings exist: Karel's rust observation and a groaning return in the unloaded chain. A player who orders a strip inspection, reduces the load, or tests with sacks prevents the fracture. Otherwise the error belongs to Zafir's confident claim.

Zafir's first instinct is to explain why the visible proof passed. Aron tells him to hold the splint instead. Until Aron is stabilized and the ferry household names a replacement lead, Zafir cannot retreat into recalculation or delegate the consequence to Karel.

## The Five Routes

Three original outward methods remain valid, and the damaged fourth can be rebuilt at lower load:

1. repair the public ferry chain;
2. run a high pilot rope from Eel Bridge;
3. use a reed skiff through the back channel;
4. create a short bounded Binder pull on a repaired light cargo platform.

The fifth route combines household knowledge with Karel's physical model: a downstream recovery line that the first returning boat can raise for the others.

Using the fifth route requires no special vocation. Tracking, magic, climbing, or strength changes exposure and timing.

## Rescue

Bandits threaten the island from a timber blind. They want ferry tolls and captives, not Zafir's research.

The party may:

- suppress or flank the blind;
- negotiate while placing the return line;
- send a small team by back channel;
- create noise at one crossing and use another.

If the player obeys Zafir's original plan, the captives are reached but the rescue team becomes stranded and must fight for time while a household improvises the fifth crossing.

If Zafir accepts revision, Karel or a ferry keeper may alter ring placement after deployment without requesting his permission.

Aron cannot return to deck work during this quest. Saving every captive does not erase his injury, lost wages, anger, or Zafir's responsibility for the test.

## End

The operation ends when the last rescuer and household member reaches a safe bank, not when the captives first leave the island.

Zafir may:

- credit Karel and the ferry households in the permanent crossing pattern;
- call their work a field correction to his design;
- preserve only his four crossings in the official record.

The record also names who authorized the failed load test and who bears Aron's treatment and wage replacement. Credit for the fifth route cannot be used to blur liability for the fourth.

This changes direction and later Karel trust. It does not rewrite who physically built the route.

## Fail-Forward

- A broken ferry becomes a rope extraction.
- A lost skiff strands one group until the recovery line is raised.
- Escaped bandits create a later road camp.
- A flooded plan washes away, but Karel's cord model and household testimony preserve the pattern.
- An injured ferryman survives and becomes the winter changed-reentry focus.

## Rewards

`zafir_fifth_crossing_pattern` becomes an Act I preparation asset. Longlake ferry reliability and household trust persist.
