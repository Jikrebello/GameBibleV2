# Every Hand May Let Go

## Premise

The Muster's command patterns can seize ordinary defensive mechanisms and non-conscious bound forms. Zafir proposes a five-person voluntary field circle whose control is distributed rather than held by one master ring.

The first deployment protects a returning salvage team near the Old River Arsenal.

## Recruitment

The initial participants are:

- [[Karel Reed-Hand]];
- Blackjack carrier Neda Volk;
- Chapel-Folk route keeper Olof Reed-Kin;
- Hammer Ward mechanism worker Tessa Morn;
- field carer Jannik Vale.

World-state substitutions are allowed. Every person receives:

- a plain-language explanation;
- a personal release key;
- a named load limit;
- the right to revise;
- the right to withdraw after deployment.

The player may not fill the Circle with prisoners, debtors ordered by Koss, or bound forms disguised as volunteers.

## Training

The normal party helps the Circle rehearse:

1. passing a directional shield;
2. redirecting one non-conscious bound carrier;
3. dropping load when a participant exits;
4. completing the route with three, four, or five members.

Zafir's first design assumes all five remain. Karel refuses the final repetition until the reduced versions are practiced.

### Pause Point: Circle Terms Accepted

The quest may pause after every participant holds a key.

## Deployment

At the Old River Arsenal approach, residue activates a rank carrier and drives salvage constructs toward the returning workers.

During the defence:

- Tessa may revise her ring placement;
- Jannik may leave to treat a casualty;
- Olof may abandon the planned lane when thaw water rises;
- Neda may refuse a load transfer after injury;
- Karel may challenge Zafir's central fallback.

Each choice changes the active arrangement. None automatically fails the operation.

The player protects, carries, destroys anchors, or secures the route according to vocation. Shared Covenant provides an advantage, never required access.

## The Override Offer

A ducal engineer offers Zafir a master emergency ring “only for this test.” It would preserve output when volunteers withdraw.

Zafir may:

- refuse it;
- accept it under external seal but never use it;
- use it with prior participant consent;
- use it against a withdrawal;
- keep it afterward.

Consent to one bounded emergency action does not create permanent command.

## End

The Circle's after-action review is led by a participant other than Zafir.

The strongest result records:

- what each person changed;
- who withdrew and why;
- whether the plan still worked;
- which keys were used;
- what must be removed before the next deployment.

Karel may remain a learner, claim peer standing, or leave. The player cannot order him to stay.

## Fail-Forward

- A volunteer withdrawal contracts the plan.
- A broken key forces ordinary physical escape.
- A failed defence wounds or scatters the salvage team and creates a rescue, not instant death.
- An override use preserves one material objective while changing direction and trust.
- Defeat leaves a reduced Circle available for the finale.

## Rewards

Completion unlocks **Shared Covenant**, `zafir_open_cord_circle_formed`, and the distributed-key Act III preparation asset.
