# The Ninth Lever

## Premise

Nine-Lever Chainworks copied a family of Zafir's old living-person restraints into its safety interlocks. Eight operators are keyed to eight stations. A ninth master lever can stop the whole works, but it also locks every operator in place so one supervisor can restart without reassembly.

During a load failure, nine workers are trapped inside moving chains, counterweights, and suspended iron. The duo must save them before the load floor tears loose.

## Staging

With two Act I assets, Zafir receives a Hammer Ward stop petition signed in charcoal thumb marks. Accepting the warning forms the player-plus-Zafir duo.

The warning names:

- nine individually tracked workers;
- a structural failure clock;
- the choice between central and personal release;
- permanent losses if the finale resolves autonomously.

Karel remains outside the full party. His countermodel, if earned, enters as preparation and a shouted route from the public gallery.

## The Workers

Each station has a visible worker and physical problem:

1. furnace feed;
2. quench gate;
3. high chain;
4. stamp table;
5. counterweight pit;
6. coil rack;
7. water wheel;
8. loading crane;
9. inspection bridge.

A failed rescue wounds, moves, or isolates a worker before death. Nobody dies off-screen at a clock tick.

## Beginning: Stop Means Stay

Chainmaster **Arn Brenner** pulls the ninth lever when the first chain snaps. The machinery slows. The workers' identity catches close around wrists and ankles.

Zafir recognizes his architecture.

> “It is functioning exactly as designed.”

One worker answers:

> “Then design faster.”

The duo can inspect the public control floor, enter through the water channel, or climb the maintenance frame. All routes reach the same legible load problem.

### Pause Point: Workers Located

Once all nine stations are marked, the quest may pause. The structural clock does not move during unrelated free play.

## Middle: Three Load Zones

### Heat Floor

Free furnace, quench, and stamp workers while controlling steam and hot chain. Ordinary valves and shields provide the baseline. Magic, mobility, or heavy protection reduces exposure.

### Hanging Floor

Cross moving loads to reach high-chain, coil, and crane workers. Breaking the wrong restraint drops weight onto the lower floor. Observation reveals two safe sequences.

### Drive Floor

Reach the pit, water wheel, and inspection bridge. The master lever can hold this floor temporarily at the cost of tightening every remaining restraint.

The player may use a temporary central stop. The moral question is not whether emergency authority can ever exist; it is who still holds release when the emergency ends.

## The Ninth Release

At the load floor, Zafir can derive:

- **Personal keys:** each worker receives one prepared release and may exit or hold voluntarily.
- **Supervised master stop:** Zafir or a civil inspector retains central authority with external limits.
- **Private master ring:** Zafir retains the most efficient control.
- **Destruction:** sever all identity paths, sacrificing precise restart and part of the chainworks.

Three assets make personal keys and full rescue simultaneously achievable. Four or five preserve one worker or major machine objective after a failed sequence.

## Opposition

Brenner knowingly bought copied restraints because they reduced shutdown time. He may surrender, flee with a pattern plate, or resist beside hired guards.

He is culpable without being the cause of the mechanical break. Killing, arresting, or losing him does not determine the release choice.

## Finale

The failure drops a suspended load and tears the inspection bridge loose. Zafir must maintain one relation while the player completes a vocation-neutral rescue, then trust the relevant worker to use the release.

If Zafir overrides a worker who chooses to leave, the material rescue may still succeed while direction moves toward `mastering`.

## Fail-Forward

- A missed station becomes an injury and alternate lower-floor rescue.
- A broken key forces physical severance.
- Destroyed machinery preserves lives but closes production through winter.
- Defeat lets Great Forge crews stop the outer wheel while one objective is lost.
- Brenner escaping preserves a later technical-fence encounter, not a recurring mastermind.

## Outcomes and Rewards

Record every worker, chainworks condition, key distribution, Karel state, master-stop state, and Zafir direction.

Personal completion unlocks **Release at Hand** and opens [[A Bird for No Door]]. The quest never creates a legal right to bind workers.
