# The Inverted Surety

## Function

The inverted surety is a bounded Iqashari restraint relation created when [[Samira bint Nahal]] reversed one of [[Zafir ibn Samad]]'s own living-person instruments.

It has three required parts:

1. a court witness and surviving judgement;
2. Zafir's identity seal;
3. a locally held execution-end.

The execution-end reaches Grenzburg through ordinary custody and is held at Chain House under Medori and civil witness. Samira does not operate it remotely.

## Recorded Effects

The instrument may perform only the effects recorded in the judgement:

- arrest one marked motion;
- impose a short bounded kneel;
- close one prepared Binder relation;
- prevent Zafir from crossing a named threshold while the end remains properly anchored.

It cannot:

- transmit thought, speech, affection, pain reports, or memory;
- allow real-time conversation with Samira;
- improvise new commands;
- move Zafir like a puppet;
- reach another person without a valid identity seal;
- operate after its witness, seal, or local anchor is destroyed.

## Why It Matters

The instrument is technically impressive and morally compromised.

Samira knowingly used coercive force against Zafir. That does not make Zafir's prior ownership just. Zafir's victimhood under the inverted relation does not erase the people he restrained.

The campaign therefore separates:

- personal severance;
- destruction of living-person technique;
- Samira's authorship;
- legal debt;
- moral direction.

No single technical act resolves all four.

## Safe Handling

- Every transfer requires two named witnesses.
- The execution-end remains visible in play.
- Testing occurs only against non-conscious forms or Zafir's freely revocable participation.
- A severance method derived from the instrument must not reproduce its identity-control path.
- Bellandi may authenticate custody but cannot declare the mechanism moral.

## Final States

- `secured`: execution-end held under valid local custody;
- `damaged`: one recorded effect remains unreliable;
- `severed`: Zafir's personal relation is ended;
- `destroyed`: execution-end and living-person pattern are rendered unusable;
- `retained`: an authority preserves the instrument under supervision;
- `copied`: a culpable actor escapes with part of the design.
