# The Route That Wasn't There

## Premise

After the western breach, Zafir marks a casualty and supply route from Lantern Ward to the Ledger Quays aid station. A secondary collapse makes the map obsolete within minutes.

Residents, Karel, porters, and cooks create another route through buildings no official plan treats as connected.

The operation is not about proving planning useless. Zafir's prepared rings make heavy movement possible. The crisis asks whether users may move those rings without his permission.

## Beginning

The normal party escorts the first casualty cart along Zafir's marked lane. A Grobi bombardment drops a cooper's wall and fills the street with burning hoops.

The official alternatives are:

- a stair too narrow for stretchers;
- a frozen quay under enemy sight;
- an underways gate locked from the far side.

Karel points through a laundry window, across a kitchen, over a dye-yard bridge, and down a cooper's cargo lift.

Zafir says the rings are not calibrated for that route.

The first patient does not have time for a seminar.

### Pause Point: Official Route Failed

The operation may pause after the new route is identified. Casualties remain stable until resumption.

## Building the Unwritten Route

The party completes three linked tasks:

1. protect residents while they open private rooms and clear domestic obstacles;
2. move or recalibrate Zafir's load rings;
3. hold two Grobi pressure points while casualties and supplies pass.

Each space has present owners:

- a cook decides which wall may be broken;
- laundresses control the balcony weight;
- a dyer knows the safe boards above the vats;
- a cooper releases the lift.

The player may ask, pay, invoke emergency authority, or force access. Strongest results preserve consent and document repair obligations after the siege.

## Revision

Karel moves one ring before Zafir approves it.

- If Zafir accepts field authority, Karel records why and the route improves.
- If Zafir demands central approval, the route remains usable but a casualty group waits under fire.
- If the player gives every resident unrestricted magical control, a ring overloads; consent does not replace competence or preparation.

The best method combines training, distributed authority, and visible limits.

Every starting vocation can clear domestic obstacles, protect residents, carry or escort casualties, and hold the two pressure points. Movement skills, magic, heavy protection, and Zafir's rings alter speed and exposure without becoming access keys.

## End

The last transfer carries wounded people one way and hot food back the other. The route works in both directions.

Zafir can publish it as:

- a resident-designed siege route with his support relations;
- a joint field revision;
- his emergency plan;
- an unsafe improvisation to be closed.

The physical route persists according to the actual work, while credit affects direction and Karel.

## Fail-Forward

- A broken ring forces hand carrying and removes one later safeguard.
- A lost room opens the underways alternative.
- Civilian injury changes ownership and trust without deleting the route.
- A defeated party wakes at the aid station after residents finish one transfer and lose another.
- Grobi pressure creates a later street hold, not a secret companion antagonist.

## Rewards

`zafir_unwritten_siege_route` and `karel_field_revision_recorded` become Act II preparation assets. The route changes winter ambient movement.
