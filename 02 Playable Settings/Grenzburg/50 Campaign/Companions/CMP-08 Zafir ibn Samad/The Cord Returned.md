# The Cord Returned

## Role in the Arc

This is Zafir ibn Samad's quest 1 of four. It is playable in Early Autumn and Open Autumn and advances the arc without requiring Zafir ibn Samad in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A sealed Iqashari packet reaches the Chain House containing the end of Zafir's inverted cord and a message in Samira bint Nahal's hand. Pulling it tight can immobilize him.

Keep the cord from Koss, understand its custody, and hear the relation between Zafir and the copyist who wrote the inversion.

## Player Actions

Inspect the cord with Warlock and legal experts, survive a remote command test, trace who opened the packet, and choose temporary custody.

## Opposition and Pressure

Koss wants a reliable control device; Lucia treats the court appointment as real but reviewable; Zafir describes Samira only as a criminal until confronted with her enslaved status.

## Information Paths

The custody order, copyist marks, and cord response establish authorship and legal history. Any two explain the present control; all three expose Zafir's prior instruments.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Place the cord with Zafir under safeguards, divided custody, or Medori. Destroying this end weakens but does not free him because the inversion has other anchors.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the packet is stolen, the command pulse reveals the thief's route. If Zafir is immobilized, the party can break line-of-command and continue while he recovers.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the winter transition, the initiating evidence is secured by another party and the quest returns in Early Winter with higher institutional leverage and lower trust; it does not simply vanish.

## Rewards and Return Value

Names both his victimization and his culpability without treating either as cancellation of the other.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
