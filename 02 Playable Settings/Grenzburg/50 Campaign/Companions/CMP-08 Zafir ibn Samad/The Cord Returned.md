# The Cord Returned

## Premise

An ordinary courier brings the locally executable end of [[The Inverted Surety]] to Chain House under an Iqashari judgement and Medori custody order. Before the witnesses finish transfer, a seal-cutting crew steals the case.

The crew wants a saleable restraint, not Zafir's history. The execution-end can perform only its recorded effects, but one effect arrests Zafir's marked motion. The thieves test it during escape.

## Opening

Zafir attends because Bellandi requires the subject present for identity verification. [[Karel Reed-Hand]] is repairing the Chain House refuge bell.

When the seal opens, Zafir forgets to be afraid for several breaths. He traces the reversed notation in the air and says:

> “She found the dependent loop. I published three corrections and never saw it.”

He calls it “Samira's hand” only if pressed. Otherwise he moves directly to function.

The theft begins when a false witness drops a smoke jar and sends the case through a document chute.

## Investigation

The normal party reconstructs the route through:

- wax from a forged witness seal;
- a cord groove inside the document chute;
- Karel's floor-sized charcoal plan;
- a clerk who heard the refuge bell answer from the wrong stair;
- crane hooks marked for a Ledger Quays transfer.

Every conclusion has at least two sources. A failed check raises thief readiness or damages the cord; it cannot lose the trail.

Zafir wants to direct Karel from the plan. Karel may agree, revise the route, or refuse to enter an unsafe shaft. The player can establish the apprenticeship's first real term: Karel's “no” changes the plan.

## Pursuit

The operation crosses:

1. the Chain House record stairs;
2. a hanging bridge above the customs yard;
3. a quay crane carrying the sealed case toward a barge;
4. an old ring-room where the thieves attempt a sale demonstration.

The execution-end can arrest Zafir for one short interval. During that interval, the player and other companions remain active. Zafir can describe the relation but cannot solve his own immobilization.

All vocations can reach the case through public stairs, crane controls, ordinary climbing, or a quay interception. Specialist movement shortens routes.

## Confrontation

Debt-broker **Rulfe Senn** offers the case in exchange for being allowed to leave. He did not create the surety and cannot expand it.

The player may:

- recover the case and arrest the crew;
- trade ordinary money or evidence for immediate custody;
- pursue Senn after securing the case;
- destroy the execution-end at the risk of losing proof and safe severance data.

Zafir's first instinct is to preserve the object because it is brilliant.

Karel asks:

> “Brilliant for whom?”

Zafir may answer with Samira's name, the court, himself, or only the mechanism. The answer affects direction but does not replace the physical choice.

## Custody

The case may end under:

- Bellandi and civil dual witness;
- Chain House civil custody;
- Blackjack custody with a public record;
- damaged or destroyed evidence.

Koss cannot privately keep it. If the player tries, Brina and Bellandi create an immediate warned conflict.

## Fail-Forward

- A lost crane chase moves the sale to the ring-room.
- A damaged cord retains one bounded effect and enough notation for later work.
- An escaped Senn becomes a minor fence rather than the campaign mastermind.
- A wounded Karel withdraws from the pursuit and later decides whether study continues.
- Destruction protects Zafir immediately but removes the Act I preparation asset.

## Outcomes

Record custody, condition, Karel's voluntary apprenticeship, chain-house staff injuries, and whether Samira's authorship is recognized in the official inventory.

Completion unlocks **Relation Read** and [[The Fifth Crossing]].
