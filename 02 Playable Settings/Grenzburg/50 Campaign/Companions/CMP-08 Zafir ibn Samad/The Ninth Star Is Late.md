# The Ninth Star Is Late

## Premise

Zafir invites a committed female player to restore an obsolete rooftop sighting instrument. The ninth reference star is expected to clear the roofline before midnight.

The sky is clouded.

There is no attack, saboteur, captive, legal dispute, or secret technical necessity.

## The Instrument

The pair carry a lens, balance weight, and brass sight arm through Crown Heights service roofs. Ordinary stairs and ladders provide full access.

The player may:

- repair the instrument exactly;
- improvise an ugly but functional support;
- turn the task into a race;
- ignore the mechanism and watch the city;
- ask why this instrument matters.

Zafir eventually admits it does not.

> “I had constructed a reason to ask you here. The reason has failed. I am relieved you have not.”

## Private Scene

The conversation concerns:

- being valued beyond usefulness;
- what Zafir notices when he is not assessing risk;
- whether continuity can be chosen without becoming ownership;
- the player's own temperament and prior boundaries.

Affection is explicitly invited, never inferred from commitment. The player may choose touch, a kiss, distance, or quiet company. Mature intimacy fades to black.

The star may appear late, remain hidden, or be abandoned entirely. None is a relationship verdict.

## Reward

Completion grants **Chosen Return: Open Sky** and `zafir_open_sky_practiced`. The latter preserves one volunteer or release key in [[All Cords Cut Clean]]. It never decides Zafir's command choice.
