# The Door Opens Both Ways

## Premise

In summer, a Longlake refuge needs a door latch operable from either side by a wet, frightened person.

The task is ordinary. Its cast and procedure reflect Zafir's settled outcome without offering a late redemption choice.

## Destination Variants

### Ethical Teacher

Zafir teaches a mixed group using models they may alter. Karel may assist as a peer, teach another station, or work elsewhere.

### Supervised Technician

A civil or Blackjack witness holds the master tools. Zafir's work is useful, exact, and visibly bounded by somebody else's stop authority.

### Returning Defendant

The scene is a departure repair before Zafir travels beyond the playable map to answer Iqashari process. His account state records whether the journey is chosen, required, or negotiated.

### Renewed Master

Zafir installs an elegant central latch and explains why ordinary users should not alter it. Karel has left or openly disputes the work.

### Departed Controller

Zafir is absent. Karel, a Circle volunteer, or a refuge worker replaces the inaccessible mechanism he left behind.

## Karel

Karel's state is independent:

- learner;
- peer;
- independent mechanic;
- departed critic;
- former Circle volunteer.

No version awards Karel to Zafir for good conduct. No version punishes Karel for leaving.

## Relationships

Romance reflects [[The Ninth Star Is Late]], **Still Worth Knowing**, and the repaired rooftop instrument where earned.

Bond reflects [[The Night of Small Repairs]], **Peer's Objection**, and the two ordinary repair keys where earned.

Friendship reflects the reed bird and a complete destination scene. Missing exclusive episodes are not recreated.

## Closing Beat

The player tests the door from one side. A refuge resident tests it from the other.

If the latch fails, they revise it.

The reed bird then attempts the same door, succeeds or fails according to its earlier state, and continues down the lane without opening anything important.
