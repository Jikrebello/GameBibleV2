# Zafir Field Familiarity and Relationships

## Approval

Display only:

- **Guarded**
- **Open**
- **Trusted**
- **Strained**

Exact progress remains hidden. Idling, repeated jobs, and repeating the same mechanism cannot farm familiarity.

Zafir notices whether the player:

- asks who may revise a plan;
- preserves useful evidence without publishing a person;
- understands a difficult explanation without treating expertise as magic;
- lets an informed person refuse an efficient solution;
- admits when improvisation outperforms a model;
- destroys coercive capability even when it remains useful.

He does not automatically approve because a player sounds intelligent, lawful, skeptical, religious, or rebellious.

## Field Familiarity

Distinct authored beats count:

1. a dangerous relation identified in the field;
2. a person refusing a seemingly beneficial plan;
3. an ordinary repair shared with Karel;
4. a social or faction choice about expiry and exit;
5. a main-campaign use of Binder limits;
6. a quiet company scene in which Zafir is wanted without being needed.

## Campaign Gates

**The Cord Returned** opens after recruitment and one meaningful outing.

**The Fifth Crossing** opens after the introduction and one field problem involving local knowledge.

**The Ninth Lever** requires two of five Act I assets and closes at [[The Last Convoy]].

**A Bird for No Door** follows a personally completed Act I finale or its changed aftermath. It remains friendship content.

**The Route That Wasn't There** opens during the winter siege.

**The Copyist's Answer** requires two of five Act II assets and closes at [[Ghor at the Gate]]. Personal completion is required for Binder and either relationship path.

**Every Hand May Let Go** opens in Act III.

**All Cords Cut Clean** requires two of five Act III assets and closes at [[The Deep Muster Main Quest]].

## Commitment

Commitment requires:

- Trusted approval;
- personal completion of [[The Copyist's Answer]];
- no unresolved major violation involving slavery, coerced persons, or destruction of Samira's refusal;
- an available global romance or bond slot.

A female player may commit to romance. A male player may commit to a sworn bond. The two episodes are separate authored events and never alternate branches inside one quest.

Missing the eligible episode does not cancel commitment. It forfeits the move upgrade and Act III safeguard.

## Romance

[[The Ninth Star Is Late]] asks whether Zafir can invite a woman because he wants her rather than because her skills complete an observation.

**Duo move - Chosen Return:** Zafir places a bounded relation that draws one displaced ally back toward a prepared safe point after the ally chooses to trigger it.

**Upgrade - Chosen Return: Open Sky:** the return clears one ordinary movement impairment and leaves the arrival point unbound.

**Permanent perk - Still Worth Knowing:** once per major encounter, completing an interaction, observation, or rescue that grants no direct damage benefit briefly strengthens the player's next support or control action.

`zafir_open_sky_practiced` preserves one volunteer or release key during the Act III finale.

## Sworn Bond

[[The Night of Small Repairs]] establishes two men as peers with separate keys, alternating leadership, and an explicit right to object.

**Duo move - Two Keys:** player and Zafir each open one side of a prepared relation, reducing the risk and Arcane Burn of the release.

**Upgrade - Two Keys: Night Circuit:** a timely player cancel, guard, or route change lets Zafir safely redirect the unused half.

**Permanent perk - Peer's Objection:** once per major encounter, acting on a companion warning before an overcommitted action completes refunds part of its Stamina or resource cost and improves the safer alternative.

`zafir_night_circuit_practiced` provides the same finale safeguard as the romance state. They cannot stack.

## Friendship

Friendship includes:

- [[A Bird for No Door]];
- full campaign progression;
- Karel's independent scenes;
- Zafir's strongest moral outcome;
- resolved or compromised destinations.

Friendship is not a lesser version of romance or bond. The reed bird, private jokes, revision habits, and summer teaching scene remain unique.

## Conflict and Departure

Major conflict occurs if the player:

- gives Zafir command over an unwilling person;
- destroys Samira's refusal while preserving Zafir's notes;
- compels an Open-Cord volunteer to remain;
- treats Karel as Zafir's property;
- praises an emergency override as permanent government.

Departure requires a warned confrontation and a repair opportunity. Zafir cannot die autonomously.

## Temperament

Every player temperament receives complete writing:

- a Nerd player may share technical delight but also compete for interpretive authority;
- a Goofball player can reveal social harm Zafir has not modeled without becoming anti-intellectual;
- a Rebel player may protect exit while refusing necessary maintenance;
- a Jock player tests whether embodied practice receives equal authority;
- female tendencies alter intimacy, rhythm, and disclosure rather than competence;
- Grey receives a complete baseline grounded in the player's actual choices.

Temperament never gates relationship eligibility or moral resolution.
