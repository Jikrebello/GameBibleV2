# Companion Archetype Chemistry Matrix

This note controls 64 primary player-companion dynamics: eight possible player tendencies for each of Grenzburg's eight companions. [[Player Temperament and Chemistry]] controls recognition and locking. [[Companion Dialogue and Scene Standard]] controls execution.

Each card defines a source of recognition and a recurring pressure. It does not predetermine approval, attraction, friendship, romance, sworn bond, or moral outcome.

## Elara Velescar — Cheerleader

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Elara's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | organizer and operator | the player acts without needing the room's permission or applause | Elara may package the player's competence into her own public story; the player may mistake all social work for vanity |
| Goth | host and dissenter | the player notices exclusions Elara has made invisible | Elara risks treating strangeness as an invited ornament; the player risks treating every welcome as assimilation |
| Cheerleader | co-captains | each can make a frightened group feel chosen and capable | shared leadership becomes a contest over whose room, language, and reputation matter |
| Bohemian | patron and provocateur | the player makes Elara's carefully arranged occasions surprising | Elara may convert invention into a social asset; the player may enjoy her audience while scorning its maintenance |
| Jock | visible captains | public courage, standards, and the ability to rally a team | they can perform the perfect partnership without admitting what happens when one of them loses status |
| Nerd | translator and specialist | expertise gives substance to the projects Elara can mobilize | Elara may turn the player into a useful curiosity; the player may dismiss her social intelligence as decoration |
| Rebel | insider and outsider | the player sees which doors Elara opens and which rules deserve breaking | each is tempted to convert the other: she domesticates refusal, while the player treats all belonging as capture |
| Goofball | manager and heart | the player can release a room Elara has overmanaged | Elara may become parent, scheduler, and defender; the player must make affection reliable rather than merely charming |
| Grey | unclassified equal | the player responds to the immediate person rather than her social role | Elara cannot anticipate the correct performance and must ask more direct questions |

## Ylva Winter-Track — Tomboy

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Ylva's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | field peers | competence proved under weather, injury, and awkward work | mutual respect hardens into a contest over who needs less and yields last |
| Goth | warrior and witness | the player notices fear without trying to make Ylva publicly softer | Ylva may treat observation as intrusion; the player may romanticize her endurance |
| Cheerleader | operator and coordinator | the player can secure people, credit, and resources Ylva cannot obtain by effort alone | Ylva suspects management is patronage; the player may display her as exemplary frontier womanhood |
| Bohemian | route and horizon | the player gives Ylva reasons to enjoy a journey rather than merely survive it | Ylva treats improvisation as unreliability; the player treats necessary routine as a cage |
| Jock | competitive partners | each recognizes embodied discipline and courage immediately | every rescue, shot, and burden risks becoming evidence of rank or gender |
| Nerd | strategy and execution | the player makes weather, logistics, and risk intelligible without denying her judgment | either can reduce the other to planner or instrument |
| Rebel | two sovereignties | neither accepts ownership disguised as protection | both may leave rather than admit dependence or negotiate a durable commitment |
| Goofball | competence and play | the player makes rest and failure survivable without pity | Ylva may become the permanent responsible adult; humor must stop when she names a boundary |
| Grey | practical trust | the player is judged by preparation, follow-through, and what they do when plans fail | the relationship develops through accumulated field proof rather than an immediate social category |

## Ashira bath Melan — Goth

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Ashira's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | warrior and witness | the player acts cleanly around things other people fear to name | Ashira may interpret silence as consent to examine; the player may treat private grief as another obstacle to clear |
| Goth | two keepers of the strange | neither asks the other to translate discomfort for ordinary approval | recognition can become a sealed world built on contempt, co-rumination, and competitive darkness |
| Cheerleader | dissenter and host | the player can protect a strange person publicly rather than privately tolerating her | Ashira tests every welcome for assimilation; the player may confuse access with understanding |
| Bohemian | dark romantic and free spirit | the player treats Ashira's interests as material for possibility rather than pathology | each may aestheticize the other's pain and avoid the mundane work that makes intimacy safe |
| Jock | shadow and daylight | embodied confidence and public standing do not make the player flee from grief | the player may exoticize or rescue her; Ashira may use access to the admired public figure |
| Nerd | hidden-knowledge pair | the player takes forbidden phenomena seriously and asks exact questions | they can analyze emotion instead of experiencing it and build superiority around being the only informed people |
| Rebel | anti-normality alliance | the player acts on truths Ashira can articulate but institutions avoid | opposition becomes their only positive bond, making recklessness and cruelty feel like authenticity |
| Goofball | darkness and laughter | the player can make a severe room breathable without demanding cheerfulness | humor may violate privacy; Ashira may treat warmth as stupidity or require the player to regulate every silence |
| Grey | selective recognition | the player neither recoils nor performs fascination | Ashira must reveal preferences and needs without relying on shared identity or productive contrast |

## Ilyana Veshkar — Bohemian

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Ilyana's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | flight and footing | the player can turn an inspiration into action without owning it | Ilyana may use competence as invisible maintenance; the player may dismiss art as indulgence |
| Goth | possibility and tragic truth | the player finds meaning in what Ilyana would rather transform and move beyond | they may romanticize pain together or quarrel over whether honesty requires dwelling |
| Cheerleader | artist and impresario | the player can give communal form, audience, and continuity to invention | Ilyana fears becoming programming for somebody else's room; the player may confuse applause with artistic consent |
| Bohemian | co-creators | each recognizes reinvention, sensual attention, and permission to be strange | inspiration becomes envy, drift, missed promises, or one person quietly doing all maintenance |
| Jock | flight and grounding | the player brings reliable presence and disciplined practice to her experiments | Ilyana risks becoming a muse; the player risks turning stability into authority over her life |
| Nerd | imagination and system | the player can preserve, test, and realize possibilities she generates | the player may pathologize intuition; Ilyana may treat planning as proof of spiritual smallness |
| Rebel | freedom doubled | neither asks permission to become someone new | intensity substitutes for durability, and each may disappear when the relationship becomes ordinary |
| Goofball | shared improvisation | play can remain affectionate without status competition | joy becomes permanent adolescence unless both learn appointments, repair, and follow-through |
| Grey | curious collaborator | the player answers each invitation on its own terms | Ilyana cannot assume admiration, rebellion, or practicality and must listen for an actual preference |

## Bernard Valeon — Goofball

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Bernard's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | shelter and morale | the player acts where Bernard's warmth needs practical completion | the player may become his keeper; Bernard must show a sphere of competence and stop joking when asked |
| Goth | darkness and laughter | the player recognizes the sadness beneath a joke without demanding confession | Bernard may trespass on privacy for a laugh; the player may make solemnity into moral superiority |
| Cheerleader | organizer and humanizer | the player gives his kindness reach, protection, and public legitimacy | the player may manage him like a charming liability; Bernard may rely on charm instead of reliability |
| Bohemian | affectionate absurdity | the player welcomes ritual, invention, sensual pleasure, and imperfection | both can make responsibility the enemy of joy and leave somebody else to count the stores |
| Jock | captain and morale keeper | the player respects that courage may look like making the frightened laugh | Bernard may hide behind the admired protector; the player may tolerate him only while he entertains the team |
| Nerd | explanation and play | the player takes his odd observations seriously and makes confusion manageable | the player may reduce him to amiable noise; Bernard may turn expertise into a joke when it threatens him |
| Rebel | sanctuary and escape hatch | both distrust authority that humiliates the poor or vulnerable | rebellion and humor can become twin evasions whenever repair requires staying in the room |
| Goofball | two makers of room | neither requires constant dignity before offering affection | the friendship fails if no one can stop performing, keep an appointment, or survive an unfunny silence |
| Grey | ordinary hospitality | the player accepts or refuses care without fitting Bernard's expected bit | Bernard must risk plain speech instead of selecting the joke he thinks this kind of person wants |

## Dismas Raben — Rebel

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Dismas's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | two sovereignties | the player has the competence to leave and sometimes chooses to stay | neither admits dependence first, and useful discipline can be mistaken for ownership |
| Goth | anti-normality alliance | the player is unimpressed by respectable stories and unafraid of his edges | shared contempt can become their only substance and make tenderness feel false |
| Cheerleader | insider and outsider | the player knows how belonging can create leverage rather than only obedience | Dismas calls compromise capture; the player may turn his refusal into a fashionable asset |
| Bohemian | freedom doubled | the player makes escape feel like reinvention rather than flight | neither wants to maintain the life created after the door is broken open |
| Jock | captain and dissenter | the player proves duty can be chosen and materially protective | Dismas baits rank; the player may interpret all refusal as cowardice or lack of team spirit |
| Nerd | system and breach | the player can identify the mechanism Dismas knows must be bypassed | condescension runs both ways: clever plans can become control, while instinct becomes an excuse to ignore consequences |
| Rebel | outlaw mirrors | each recognizes the dignity of refusal without explanation | they compete over who is least owned and may abandon a cause as soon as it develops rules |
| Goofball | irreverent accomplices | humor strips fraudulent authority of its dignity | jokes and escape routes can prevent either man from naming who will remain for the cleanup |
| Grey | proof by return | the player is not immediately legible as authority, accomplice, or reformer | Dismas tests the relationship through small disappearances and notices whether the player still leaves him a way back |

## Zafir ibn Samad — Nerd

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Zafir's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | model and execution | the player tests whether an elegant proposal works in bodies, weather, and time | Zafir may treat her as an instrument; she may dismiss thought until she needs it |
| Goth | hidden-knowledge pair | the player respects exact study of taboo systems and notices what procedure conceals | analysis becomes intimacy's substitute and shared information becomes a private hierarchy |
| Cheerleader | specialist and translator | the player can make difficult truth socially actionable without simplifying it into nonsense | Zafir may use her as a mouthpiece; she may present him as a valuable curiosity |
| Bohemian | possibility and form | the player asks what else a system could become while Zafir asks how it would endure | he may pathologize improvisation; she may treat continuity as small-minded control |
| Jock | strategist and captain | the player values preparation because other bodies must rely on it | rank may overrule evidence, while Zafir may act as though correct analysis entitles him to command |
| Nerd | two exact minds | each experiences the relief of being corrected by someone worth the trouble | being right becomes a status contest and ordinary people become an audience to their model |
| Rebel | law and refusal | the player exposes where a beautiful system protects domination | Zafir may try to regulate freedom; the player may reject every safeguard because some rules were corrupt |
| Goofball | intelligence made breathable | the player makes expertise socially accessible and notices when explanation is fear | jokes may humiliate real study; Zafir may treat warmth as an inefficient delivery system |
| Grey | patient inquiry | the player asks what matters now rather than rewarding or attacking expertise as identity | Zafir must offer knowledge without first knowing what category of respect it will purchase |

## Tavio Meran — Jock

| Player tendency | Relationship register | What catches Tavio's attention | Recurring pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomboy | competitive equals | competence remains attractive even when it defeats him | every rescue and training bout risks becoming a referendum on leadership or gender |
| Goth | public hero and private shadow | the player sees fear and shame that applause cannot absorb | Tavio may seek absolution through fascination; the player may treat his public warmth as shallowness |
| Cheerleader | visible captains | the player understands crowds, standards, morale, and symbolic victory | the partnership becomes a brand that cannot admit injury, disagreement, or private failure |
| Bohemian | grounding and expansion | the player opens forms of delight beyond rank and sanctioned contest | Tavio may cast her as inspirational ornament; she may treat discipline as a cage she need not help maintain |
| Jock | teammates and rivals | each recognizes work, courage, and fair challenge immediately | dignity begins to depend on winning, protecting, or recovering faster than the other |
| Nerd | captain and strategist | the player can explain the structure beneath physical mastery | Tavio may use expertise as support staff; the player may treat his body as the dumb end of a plan |
| Rebel | team and refusal | the player exposes rigged rules while respecting genuine courage | Tavio may defend an institution because the contest was real to him; the player may dismiss every code as submission |
| Goofball | captain and social solvent | the player can release a crowd from performance without mocking effort | Tavio may mistake laughter for disloyalty; the player may avoid the disciplined work that makes play possible |
| Grey | an honest second | the player neither flatters the champion nor reflexively challenges him | Tavio must ask what the player values instead of turning every silence into applause or rivalry |

## Use in Formal Content

Each companion eventually receives:

- four full chemistry scenes: early recognition, friendship, Act II relationship or close-friend threshold, and summer;
- smaller primary variants in selected operations and finales;
- one grey version for every archetype-aware scene;
- no more than one secondary-tendency modification within a scene;
- no archetype-specific permanent reward.
