# Companion Archetype Chemistry Matrix

This note is an optional hook bank, not a sixty-four-card coverage requirement. [[Player Temperament and Chemistry]] may alter an exchange only when the player's established manner would materially change recognition, risk, trust, information, or method.

The complete Grey response is always controlling. A writer may add no variant at all.

## Permitted Uses

- a practical player notices a tool, route, injury, or workload the companion respects;
- a socially confident player handles a room in a way the companion admires or distrusts;
- a rebellious player challenges an actual authority or unjust procedure;
- an intellectual player understands a real technical distinction;
- an artistic player recognizes craft, beauty, or performance without automatically understanding the person;
- a comic player changes tension without erasing consequence;
- a severe player tolerates discomfort without turning cruelty into authenticity;
- an athletic player recognizes bodily discipline, fear, or fatigue.

## Prohibited Uses

- automatic approval or attraction;
- a complete alternate personality for the companion;
- dialogue that names the tendency's philosophy;
- eight mandatory variants for every companion;
- choosing the morally correct response by archetype;
- making a mixed or Grey player feel unfinished;
- repeating the same chemistry in every scene;
- using temperament where concrete history already explains the reaction better.

| [[Elara Velescar]] | room management, public credit, risk appetite, or class friction | flatter her social intelligence |
| [[Ilyana Veshkar]] | rehearsal, authorship, civic performance, or tolerance for repetition | produce whimsical banter |
| [[Ylva Winter-Track]] | field judgement, bodily risk, practical help, or patronization | praise competence |
| [[Ashira bath Melan]] | privacy, evidence, grief, forbidden knowledge, or silence | make darkness seductive |
| [[Bernard Valeon]] | hospitality, faith, responsibility, humor, or receiving aid | reward niceness |
| [[Dismas Raben]] | authority, escape, maintenance, testimony, or chosen return | celebrate lawbreaking |
| [[Tavio Meran]] | competition, public standards, defeat, training, or stop authority | turn every exchange into a challenge |
| [[Zafir ibn Samad]] | proof, law, error, revision, or participant control | invite another explanation |

## Authoring Rule

For each substantial scene:

1. write and approve the Grey version;
2. identify whether temperament would change anything material;
3. add at most two high-value variants unless the quest itself is built around social identity;
4. let a secondary tendency modify no more than one beat;
5. permit the companion to misread the player.

There is no minimum variant count and no completion target of sixty-four dynamics.
