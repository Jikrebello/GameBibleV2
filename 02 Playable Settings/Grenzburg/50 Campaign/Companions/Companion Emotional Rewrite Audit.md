# Companion Emotional Rewrite Audit

This note controls emotional readiness across all 88 authored companion pieces. Paired rewrites have cleared Elara, Ashira, Bernard, and Zafir. The remaining pairs retain design dispositions.

## Disposition Meanings

- **preserve:** the premise and emotional function survive; dialogue and scene texture still require the new standard.
- **reframe:** retain useful locations, mechanics, or dilemmas, but change the emotional engine, scene emphasis, or supporting cast.
- **replace:** retire the current premise or fill the reserved slot with a fresh adventure.

No piece reaches implementation readiness until `dialogue_status: approved` and its disposition is cleared during the relevant paired campaign pass.
