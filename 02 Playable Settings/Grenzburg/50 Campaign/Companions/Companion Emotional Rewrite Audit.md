# Companion Emotional Rewrite Audit

This audit supersedes the paired-rewrite queue. All eighty-eight slots are linked. Grounded work proceeds one companion at a time, with foil checks only after each individual campaign can stand alone.

## Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| pending | not yet checked against the grounded standard |
| control-applied | metadata and shared rules are attached; prose still uses prior framing |
| recalibrated | adult-life, quest-purpose, autonomy, and relationship treatment have been rewritten |
| approved | the campaign passes company, external-stake, respect, silence, interest, and absence tests |

## Piece-Level Rule

All quest notes must carry `grounded_rewrite_status`, `external_stakes`, `banter_priority`, `player_interest_gate`, and `missed_arc_treatment`.

An approved campaign contains no more than four original-wrong-centred pieces, at least six world-facing pieces, and at least three ordinary-life or professional pieces. Relationship variants never carry required moral progression.

## Reference Boundary

Positive and negative game references remain author-only craft prompts. They are not public comparisons and do not authorize imitation.
