# Bernard Rewrite Spec

Execution spec for actioning [[Companion Critical Review - Elara and Bernard]], Bernard scope. Companion document to [[Elara Rewrite Spec]] — shared prose rules and verification method are restated here only where they differ.

## Locked Decisions

1. **Secondary archetype: Jock** — Bernard becomes Goofball-primary / Jock-secondary ("Hidden Heavyweight" per the blended reference). Payload: the stress behaviour (louder, then abruptly focused and physically decisive — sharpens his existing humor-vanishes tell) and the two shadow notes his campaign never plays: **resentment of responsibility** and **feigned incompetence as evasion**.
2. **Structure: reframe Beds Before Banners.** Slot, name, and cast stay; the engine is replaced — rank tablet cut, indispensability drill in. The Froststep anniversary becomes an authored winter reactive scene, not a formal piece. His summer coda is renamed to break the twin-title pattern with Elara's.
3. **Both unfairness exceptions adopted, fused at the finale.** The Open Hospice triage wave exceeds medicine at any preparation level, and the child who goes without carries a permanent minor cold-injury discovered in summer. Flagged in-doc as intentional; do not regress.
4. **Local victim: yes — Lenka Vrana.** Kedravan laundress whose father was one of the five injured on the second sled. Petra stays remote and untouched.

## The Re-Architecture in One View

Bernard's bio promises eight things his campaign "must include." Mapped to where they will actually live after the rewrite:

| Bio promise | Where it lands | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pleasure of his company before the first relapse is solved | Little Things Missing opening | exists — protect |
| 2. Children who like him without becoming instruments | Wagon in the Reeds, Little Hands | exists — protect |
| 3. A gift he cannot earn, steal, or repay | A Blade Freely Given | exists — add the covert-repayment wrinkle |
| 4. **Humor failing and the relationship surviving** | **The silence-after-the-joke scene (new, Open Hospice triage)** | **promised in the bio, currently staged nowhere — exactly Elara's missing beat 3 pattern** |
| 5. Petra's independent judgement | Embers Without Excuse | exists — upgraded from enum to letter |
| 6. White Flame kindling without erasing accountability | Embers + **the misreading aftermath (new)** | half-exists — the dramatized half is new |
| 7. An open institution whose stores stay honest when he is tired and afraid | Beds Before Banners (drill reframe) + Open Hospice | exists — re-engined |
| 8. Resolved / supervised / concealed / sacrificial outcomes | Open Hospice | exists — hinge rebuilt |

Plus four scenes the review demands that the bio never promised: **differential grace** (the world refuses to punish him), **resentment** (the Hidden Heavyweight shadow), **feigned incompetence**, and the **beloved-theft with a face**. All four are placed below.

**Campaign shape statement** (goes into the campaign doc's Dramatic Promise): Bernard's silhouette is a **spiral, not an escalation** — relapse, repair, relapse smaller. The governing scale rule: **the war gets bigger and the object gets smaller.** Act I's decisive object is a food portion among children; Act II's is a salve packet in a siege; Act III's is one dose with a name on it. Institutions grow around him; the drama keeps shrinking toward a single hidden thing in a single pocket. This is the direct answer to the review's "warehouse-scale finales for a spoon-scale flaw."

# Part 1 — New Canon Elements

## 1.1 The letters (de-enum Petra, Radena, Matej)

Full prose, player-readable, replacing the summarized "Remote Packet" in [[Embers Without Excuse]]. One in-world text note; the packet scene quotes them whole.

**Petra's letter** (~200 words). Design rules:

- **Dictated** — she lost two fingers; the letter opens with the scribe line per Long Watch custom ("set down by the hand of…"). The fact that the handwriting is not hers is the first thing Bernard sees, and nobody comments on it.
- **One letter, fixed across all playthroughs.** Her three-way instruction (accept / redirect / close contact) remains a separate formal line item selected by the restitution record. The letter is her personhood; it does not vary with Bernard's performance — that is the design point, stated in a do-not-regress note.
- Voice: unsentimental, specific, businesslike, tired rather than cruel. Concrete-object memory, not principle — e.g., the stove he hid the salve beneath was one she cleaned as a girl; she knew its bricks. One devastating mundane line about the fingers (buttons relearned; laces not yet). States what she does with winters now. No absolution, no curse, no aphorism. She is allowed one flat joke — she is Kedravan, not a symbol.

**Radena's letter** (~150 words). Hospice-mother brisk and warm. Keeps the substance of "stop repaying childhood shelter by trying to possess every future need" but in her diction, less polished. Two textures: the hospice goose from his road stories is still alive and still hates the bell; and the boundary line — the floor was relaid last spring and she had his old hiding places taken apart, "so you may stop keeping the map." Tender and final.

**Matej's paragraph.** Dry certification voice; both facts standing, refusing to choose — his canon stance rendered as prose, three sentences, inside the packet.

## 1.2 Lenka Vrana (the local unresolvable victim)

**File:** new character note. **Role:** the one person in Grenzburg his warmth cannot reach, aimed precisely at his care language.

- Kedravan laundress, thirties, works Lantern Ward and the winter shelters. Her father, **Josef Vrana**, was one of the five injured on the second sled — nerve pain, recurring winter illness; the family emigrated for work while he stayed. Josef gets named in [[The Froststep Evacuation]]'s injury record (currently only Petra is named of the five — fix that regardless).
- **The refusal:** she will not take food from Bernard's hand. Not dramatic — she waits for another server, or goes without, or brings her own. She accepts the shelter, the network, the Yard's help; just never his, and never from him. His care language is food; her refusal is fluent in it.
- **Behavior rules (fixed):** never a quest; never blocks content; never eloquent about it; never performs anger; `lenka_state` does not move. If Bernard tries charm, it fails without a scene. If he asks directly (one authored exchange, Act II–III window), she answers flat and short — voice key: declarative, unshaped, no metaphor: *"My father was on the second sled. You don't have to leave. I'm not going to eat from your hand."* Nothing sharper. She is Bernard's Sandro.
- **Placements (no new locations):** background at a Long Winter Table shelter (the visible non-eating beat — she takes soup only once a different server holds the ladle); the one fixed exchange; present at West Refuge during Act III; in the coda she is at the open table *eating* — served by someone else — which is as far as the world ever moves her.
- In the resolved ending, restitution reaches Josef through the record like anyone else. Lenka nods at the tally board and leaves. The campaign's fixed point.

## 1.3 The differential-grace scene (the world refuses to punish him)

Authored reactive scene, Act I–II hub, triggered off a Things Not Mine return circuit. Design:

- Bernard returns a taken object to an Old Market stallholder before witnesses. The stallholder — because Bernard is beloved and the return is funny — publicly waves it off, refuses repayment, turns it into a story. The crowd enjoys it. Grace lands on him *because* he is charming.
- **Hannel is present.** She was confined and lost standing over his clasp for less. Her line, in her register (angry-flat teen, never aphoristic): *"Nobody laughed when it was me."*
- Bernard's playable choice: accept the grace (easy, warm, everyone's happier) or insist on his consequence in public against the room's goodwill — which reads as ungracious and costs him socially. The scene must make accepting feel kind and insisting feel awkward; that's the trap working.
- Output: `bernard_grace_scene` (accepted / insisted / deflected), feeding direction. No lecture anywhere in it — the scene never explains itself.

This scene is the campaign's thesis inverted: his accountability quests all assume the world wants to hold him to account; the truth is the world keeps refusing to, and he has to *volunteer*. Falstaff without the rejection — nobody in Grenzburg will say "I know thee not," so he must say it to himself.

## 1.4 The Hannel incident ("The Mended Sleeve" — the beloved-owner theft)

One new authored incident in the finite pool ([[Bernard Compulsion and Accountability System]]), Act II–III window. The system's current law quietly protects Bernard: the pool excludes anyone with a claim on his heart, so every theft is emotionally safe. Add the one exception the Dostoevsky comparison demands:

- The object is Hannel's — small, hers, loved (her mother's thimble, or the button-hook she mends with; execution picks one). The girl who was once blamed for him now actually loses something to him.
- Standard tell sequence applies; if completed, the return circuit is unlike any other: Hannel's response is *not gracious and not a boundary-seminar* — she has to decide whether to do to him what was done to her (report it, let it be public) or protect him the way nobody protected her. Either way she says some version of: *"Now it's real."*
- Repair is possible per the vault's normal loop — but the relationship state caps at `repaired-guarded`, never back to before. (Bernard's *permanent* loss lives at the finale, 2.3; Hannel's is lasting strain, not severance.)
- This incident is authored, one-shot, cannot be farmed, and does not fire if the player has kept Bernard's self-report state at its best tier — earned trust genuinely prevents it, which makes it the one incident in the pool that rewards the accountability play retroactively.

## 1.5 The Froststep anniversary (winter reactive scene, not a formal piece)

Authored scene in [[Bernard Banter and Reactivity]] + registered in the Reactive Scene Register. First hard freeze of Deep Winter:

- No crisis, no quest. The Yard preps for cold. Bernard is *visibly wrong all day* — over-counts the stores twice, walks the kitchen at night, packs and unpacks his kit, snaps the prayer rope beads through his fingers. The compulsion spiking seasonally, shown somatically, never named clinically.
- One short scene with the player: he is found checking the store board by lamplight. He doesn't confess anything — there is nothing to confess yet. He says something plain about the cold and the sled. If the player stays, he finishes the count aloud, hands the player the chalk, and goes to bed. If they don't, he finishes it alone and is fine in the morning. No state moves. The scene exists so winter itself is his antagonist once, on screen, for free.

Single-word, theologically loaded exactly right for him — unearned receiving *and* the table prayer. Elara's coda keeps its name; the twin pattern breaks on his side.

- [[The Froststep Evacuation]]: add the two-sentence causal chain the review flagged — his concealment falsified the store count, so the second sled was loaded against a manifest that promised goods no longer there. Name Josef Vrana among the five.

# Part 2 — Structural Surgery

## 2.1 Little Hands, Long Road: wire the flaw into the played finale

The review's sharpest structural finding: the autonomous route ("carries an undeclared food reserve and delays naming the danger") is *more dramatic than the played one*. Invert by giving the played route the same live wire:

- **Bernard carries a small undeclared portion in every route.** Authored, like the intro relapse — the sin precedes player agency; the live choice is concealment.
- If `bernard_hunger_named_early` was earned: he names it at the cairn count — a small shame in front of the children, honest count restored, cost minor but nonzero. **Mika watches an adult name it.** This retroactively makes the prep asset the emotional device it was always trying to be.
- If not earned: the midday count is wrong because of him. The shelter decision — push past Reedmile or wait out the weather — gets made on bad numbers, and a specific avoidable cost follows (the group moves late into the marsh; the separation happens at a worse spot; a child goes hungry a night). His supper confession then costs something real, in front of children who trusted the count.
- **The Mika mirror now interacts with Bernard's own pocket.** If Bernard concealed and Mika's hidden portions surface, Bernard must handle the child's theft while carrying his own — and the scene forks on whether he confesses first (*"Mine first."*) or manages the child hypocritically. If Bernard declared at the cairn, Mika may self-report unprompted, having seen it done. The current version — Bernard modeling perfect pedagogy at a child who is Literally Him — is replaced by a mirror that can distort.
- Bear, hero beat, readiness/stress tracks, fail-forward: unchanged.

## 2.2 Beds Before Banners: the indispensability drill (reframe, locked decision 2)

Slot, title, cast (Hannel, Neda, Olof, Sister Hanne), and prep outputs stay. Engine replaced:

- **Cut the rank tablet entirely.** No undercroft mechanism, no residue combat in this piece. (The finale's assault supplies Act III combat; this operation's job is human.)
- **New premise:** before Mercy Hospital and the sponsors will back a mixed hospice, Brina and Sister Hanne require a demonstration: West Refuge Hall must run a full admission-and-care drill **without Bernard touching it.** "If you are the hospice, it dies when you are tired." The sponsors attend as observers; their eventual roles get bounded by what the drill proves — which is how the piece still outputs `bernard_west_refuge_neutral_claim` and feeds `bernard_mixed_carer_pledge`.
- **Beat 1 — sabotage by helpfulness.** Bernard cheerfully undermines the drill's premise: only he knows where the good kettle is; the stove "only draws for me"; his tally handwriting "can't be taught." This is **feigned incompetence as evasion** (Hidden Heavyweight shadow) — playing helpless at delegation to stay central. The player watches failures trace to Bernard-shaped holes.
- **Beat 2 — the resentment scene.** Mid-drill, exhausted, benched, he snaps at someone who didn't deserve it (a carer asking a reasonable question; execution picks the target for maximum unfairness). One flash of the thing under the warmth: *he is tired of being the one who carries, and has never once been allowed to say so, least of all by himself.* Then he must repair *that* — which is harder for him than confession, because there's no theft to return, just an apology with nothing funny in it.
- **Beat 3 — live fire.** The current first-admission test survives as the drill's ending: real arrivals mid-drill (wounded marchers; **Mika with the feverish younger cousin and the food bag he won't surrender**; the guarded Folk prisoner Olof recognizes). The hall runs it. Bernard's assigned station is one job, and the hardest playable thing in the piece is him doing *only* that job.
- **Residue:** the hall can live without him. Whether that is a loss is left with him, unanswered — the piece ends before he decides.
- Outputs otherwise as current; autonomous route: without the player, Bernard "helps" the drill into dependence and a sponsor claims the hall — same asset loss as now, better cause.

## 2.3 The Open Hospice: the hinge with a face (locked decision 3)

The finale keeps its admission rule, routes, pressure tracks, assault beats, destination architecture, and sacrifice rule. The middle is rebuilt around the fused exception:

- **The shortage is real at any preparation.** The triage wave exceeds warming-salve and medicine, full stop. Preparation changes *who* bears it and *how badly* — never *whether*. In-doc flag: "This finale intentionally breaks the three-assets-all-objectives guarantee. Do not regress." (Mirror clause to Elara's cabinet.)
- **The theft is for a beloved, and it is already done.** Before the count, Bernard has set aside the last full salve portion — *for Mika's feverish cousin*. Mika did not ask him to. The concealment, not the taking, is the live choice — same architecture as his intro. The dark mirror closes: the man who once watched a child hide food for a sibling now does the adult version, in an institution he built, against an admission rule he wrote.
- **The stranger with the claim:** the salve's rightful next allocation under his own rule is a guarded captive's child (Grobi dependent — the admission rule's hardest test case, already in the quest's arrival list). His love breaks his law. The thesis in one gesture.
- **The parallel line.** Elara's controlling excuse was "I did what the house required" / *the house required nothing*. Bernard's finale earns the twin: he did what love required — **and the child required nothing.** Mika never asked. The line (or its dramatized equivalent) belongs to the resolved path's naming scene; Mika's own reaction — betrayed by being made the reason — is written, not skipped.
- **Outcome mapping stays reachable:** resolved = he names it *before* discovery, when the captive child's need is called (truth chosen at the hinge); compromised = discovered, then owned; broken = maintained or blame-shifted. Discovery vectors: the witnessed count, Hanne's tally, Zafir's weight-check assist, or the allocation call itself.
- **The permanent cost (unwarned):** the child who went without carries a lasting minor cold-injury — a stiff hand, a winter limp. Not revealed in the finale. It surfaces in the summer coda, quietly, after judgement has closed: Froststep's five, echoed by one, discovered the way Bernard's own harms have always been discovered — later, when nothing can be done. No repair scene exists.
- **The silence after the joke** (bio promise 4) is staged here, in the triage wave: Bernard tries the invented-kitchen-office bit on a terrified captive child who doesn't share his language. Silence. Nobody laughs, nothing lands, and he has to stay in the room as himself, without the tool. The relationship with the room survives; the joke does not. Two minutes, no dialogue lesson.

## 2.4 Embers Without Excuse: letters in, misreading staged, disclaimers out

- **The Remote Packet becomes prose.** Bellandi opens it; Petra's letter, Radena's letter, and Matej's paragraph are read in full on screen (1.1). The scene direction stays anti-climactic by design — the docs' instinct ("no reunion, no persuasion") was right; only the texture was missing.
- **New aftermath scene — the misreading.** The White Flame kindling was public. Back at Mercy: a carer hangs a votive by his bunk; a mother asks him to bless her child; **Deacon Ignatius** (existing character — read his file first) preaches that the debt is plainly annulled by grace; Koss's ledger of Bernard's costs becomes *unpopular*. Bernard must insist on his own guilt against a room that has decided to absolve him — the only version of the White Flame theme that needs the player present. Output: `white_flame_public_reading` (vindicated / contested / quiet), ambient consequences through spring.
- **Disclaimer cut:** metaphysics guardrails survive once in the bio (load-bearing) and once here, plainly. The other four-plus repetitions across his files are deleted in the prose pass. The dramatized scene now does their work.

## 2.5 A Blade Freely Given: one wrinkle

Keep the piece whole — the review endorsed its core. Add the friction it lacks: within days of receiving the sword, Bernard tries to repay covertly — extra unasked labor for the player, a "found" item left in their kit, over-giving disguised as ordinariness — and is caught at it. One short scene: the player catches him, and he has to *un-repay*, which he finds physically difficult. Receiving stays hard after the ceremony; the gift episode stops being frictionless. Output folds into existing states; no new reward.

## 2.6 The Long Winter Table: protect, place, and pass

His best operation; structure untouched. Three additions: Lenka's non-eating beat at one shelter (1.2); the resentment thread *seeded* (he waves off the fourth night's exhaustion with a joke — one beat the drill piece later pays off); prose pass. The folding-table-into-litters image is protected verbatim.
