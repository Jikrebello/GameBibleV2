# Companion Blind Dialogue Test Pair 03

Use these lines without names, vocation labels, culture, or backstory context. A reader should attribute at least ten of twelve lines correctly to each companion.

## Test Lines

1. “The stew has surrendered. We should give the room one brave colour.”
2. “That gate is lawful. The hinge is less devout.”
3. “Again, but leave somewhere for her to answer this time.”
4. “I did not say it was safe. I said the guards do not like it.”
5. “Not a curse. Onion. A more democratic enemy.”
6. “Captain Polished-Buckle has discovered weather.”
7. “Keep it ugly. Pretty slips on ice.”
8. “Lie to the paper if you like. The cart already knows.”
9. “A voice is not required to sound like mine.”
10. “Then it is not an escape. You sold them the first mile.”
11. “The rafters understood. I will ask the people tomorrow.”
12. “No, I am only passing through slowly. The bedroll is unrelated.”
13. “Tonight the free spirit meets the broom and discovers continuity.”
14. “Waste of a good queen. Better route marker, though.”
15. “That room has been beige for too long.”
16. “I will be there tomorrow. Do not make a ceremony of it.”
17. “Play the second note exactly. You may hate it after the wounded are through.”
18. “The fence is decorative. The consequence on the other side is not.”
19. “No audience. Just you. That is more frightening, actually.”
20. “Three voices. I counted them because counting was easier than turning around.”
21. “There. It survived improvement and still has a tune.”
22. “No story. What day is the hearing?”
23. “Keep it, cut it, or make it embarrassing. I do not own the next performance.”
24. “I will take second watch. You may notice. You may not announce it.”

## Rejection Rules

Revise a set if:

- Ilyana is identifiable only through music or colourful clothes;
- Dismas is identifiable only through crime, escape, or charm;
- either speaker turns disagreement into polished mutual insight;
- more than four lines directly invoke the old company or rockfall;
- playfulness could be exchanged between them without changing rhythm;
- Ilyana performs every silence or Dismas abandons every commitment.
