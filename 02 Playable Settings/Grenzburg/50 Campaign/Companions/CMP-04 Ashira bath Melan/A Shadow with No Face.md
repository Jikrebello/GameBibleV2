# A Shadow with No Face

## Premise

The player finds Ashira conducting an ordinary Yard exercise. A hound-turn shade carries a wooden spoon through an obstacle course while Neda insists this is the first useful thing Medium practice has done all week.

The scene reflects, rather than changes, the campaign outcome.

## Variants

- **Ethical teacher:** Ashira teaches nonhuman patterns under explicit entry, stop, and privacy rules. She enjoys the work and corrects herself once without ceremony.
- **Supervised practitioner:** Brina and divided custodians hold the roster. Ashira is useful, restrained, and not yet convinced.
- **Isolated keeper:** Ashira maintains sealed media alone and receives only operational contact.
- **Departed predator:** the player encounters evidence of a faceless pattern used elsewhere; Ashira is absent.

Romance, bond, friendship, and Grey/temperament profiles alter greetings, private jokes, and where the player stands during the exercise. They do not overwrite the settled moral and legal states.

## Closing Beat

In resolved play, the spoon reaches the final hoop and drops.

> Neda: “It died.”
>
> Ashira: “It was never alive.”
>
> “Still dropped the spoon.”
>
> Ashira looks at the player. “This is why belonging is dangerous.”
