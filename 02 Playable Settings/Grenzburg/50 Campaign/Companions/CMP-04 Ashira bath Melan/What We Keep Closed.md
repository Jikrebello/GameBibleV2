# What We Keep Closed

## Premise

The winter breach scattered locked boxes of private Blackjack effects through three damaged Yard rooms. Ashira asks a committed female player to help recover and secure them before the company reopens.

There is no villain. The danger is unstable structure, water, and the temptation to inspect what might help identify an owner.

## Structure

The pair:

- brace a collapsed screened alcove;
- recover sealed letters without reading them;
- identify ownership through exterior marks and Neda's register;
- choose when a life-saving identification justifies opening one box;
- build a divided-custody cabinet no single person can search alone.

They find one absurd object: a carved puppet leg labelled **NOT EVIDENCE**. Ashira laughs, then checks whether the player noticed.

## Sworn Confidence

At the repaired screen they define:

- what may be repeated in danger;
- what requires permission;
- how either woman calls the other back from possession or concealment;
- the right to say “closed” without giving a reason.

The rite uses two plain cabinet keys and spoken names. It contains no romance, rings, physical courtship, fade to black, marriage imagery, or claim over future partners.

Ashira says:

> “I will keep what you entrust. I will not call the keeping understanding.”

## Reward

Completion sets `ashira_closed_circle_practiced`, provides the equal Act III safeguard, and upgrades **Closed Circle** to **Door Kept**. Failure or leaving early preserves the committed bond but forfeits the upgrade.
