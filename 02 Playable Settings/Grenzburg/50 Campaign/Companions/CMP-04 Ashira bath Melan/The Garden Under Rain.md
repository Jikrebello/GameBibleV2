# The Garden Under Rain

## Premise

Ashira invites a committed male player to the **Rain Vault**, a blue-glass water garden exposed by spring thaw. It is dangerous because channels are broken and glass terraces are slick, not because a villain waits inside.

## Experience

The pair:

- align rain cups to open the first terrace;
- cross a chamber where reflections repeat one movement late;
- shelter while a runoff surge changes the return route;
- observe silver cave insects moving through blue light;
- choose whether to preserve, sketch, or leave a fragile glass bloom.

Ashira asks before every shared anchor and becomes irritated when the player asks permission merely as performance.

At the inner terrace:

> “I did not bring you here to be understood.”
>
> She lets the rain fill the pause.
>
> “I brought you because I wanted you here.”

The player may reciprocate, slow the scene, or reaffirm commitment without sex. Physical affection follows explicit consent and may fade to black.

## Failure and Reward

Missteps cost injury, equipment, or access. No single check kills either character. Leaving early preserves the relationship but forfeits the upgrade.

Completion sets `ashira_rain_vault_practiced`, supplies one Act III safeguard, and upgrades **Shared Shadow** to **Rain Between Us**.
