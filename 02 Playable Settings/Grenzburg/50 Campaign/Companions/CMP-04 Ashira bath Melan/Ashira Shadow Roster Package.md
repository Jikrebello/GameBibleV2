# Ashira Shadow Roster Package

Ashira is a [[Medium]]. This is a companion package, not a ninth vocation.

## Ontology

A shade is a bounded, non-conscious combat computation shaped from movement, pressure, sound, and residual pattern. It is not a soul, ghost, person, memory subject, or moral witness.

Recognizable human likeness creates a separate ethical problem. A shape can violate identity, privacy, and grief without containing the person it resembles.

## Combat Rhythm

Ashira:

1. reads repetition;
2. marks a threat or space;
3. deploys one suitable nonhuman pattern;
4. creates a field that changes movement or visibility;
5. dismisses the pattern before it becomes contaminated by unrelated commands.

She is strongest when the party acts on information. She is not a passive summon dispenser.

## Legitimate Roster

- **Hound Turn:** flanking movement and pursuit interruption;
- **Moth Screen:** bounded visual interference;
- **Crab Brace:** directional stagger resistance;
- **Hart Break:** lateral escape and knock-aside;
- **Ward-Step:** construct-derived repetition that tests a dangerous tile or lane;
- **Reed Coil:** environmental movement that binds a small area.

These forms never speak, recognize people, plead, or persist as characters.

## Progression

### Pattern Known

After [[The Faces She Kept]], Ashira identifies repeated attack lines and distinguishes living motion from residue or scripted echo. The player still must act on the information.

### Hush Field

After [[A Useful Terror]], Ashira creates a bounded field that dampens ordinary sound, reduces visual acquisition, and reveals movement crossing its edge. It does not make the party invisible.

### Witness Circle

After [[When the Screen Comes Down]], allies inside a marked field see the origin of repeated attacks and gain bounded resistance to panic caused by sensory repetition.

### No Human Shadow

After a resolved or compromised [[No Human Shadow]], Ashira's capstone separates every legitimate nonhuman pattern from human-likeness media. She can dismiss contaminated copies and briefly combine two nonhuman relations without implying consciousness.

The same outcome unlocks the account-wide **Shadow Witness** custom-vocation blueprint.

## Human Roster

The prohibited roster contains recognizable human appearance and movement derived from the Qesheth terror action. It has five media branches:

- approach;
- guard;
- pursuit;
- strike;
- retreat.

Its apparent tactical adaptation is computation. Its use remains culpable because the likeness was retained and deployed without permission.

Campaign choices may destroy, divide, seal, or weaponize it. Legal custody does not make deployment ethical.

## Limits

Medium practice cannot:

- capture or command a soul;
- summon a dead person's consciousness;
- recover reliable testimony from residue;
- manufacture consent;
- preserve a person by copying their movement;
- turn repeated speech into evidence without corroboration;
- maintain unlimited shades.

## All-Vocation Support

Ashira's quest methods never require the player to be a Medium. Any vocation can:

- observe repeated timing;
- carry a shutter or lamp;
- break a projector or anchor;
- follow a living rescue signal;
- protect a witness;
- use prepared routes.

Medium knowledge adds interpretation and efficiency, not exclusive critical access.
