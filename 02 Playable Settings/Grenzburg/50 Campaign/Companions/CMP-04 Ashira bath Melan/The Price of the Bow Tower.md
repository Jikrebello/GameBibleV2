# The Price of the Bow Tower

## Role in the Arc

This is Ashira bath Melan's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Ashira bath Melan duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Keeper Othara bath Qesin arrives with a Qesheth delegation to renew the judgement that shields the tower from collective sanctions. Medori offers to erase Ashira's liability if the tower licenses human-imprint methods.

Protect innocent tower households without turning Ashira's culpability into a tradeable weapons franchise.

## Player Actions

Authenticate the original judgement, expose a forged casualty schedule, demonstrate safe nonhuman techniques, and negotiate before ducal, Church, and Bank observers.

## Opposition and Pressure

A Medori advocate exploits genuine fear; some Qesheth scholars want Ashira permanently cast out; Koss wants field access to the roster. No secret occult power directs them.

## Information Paths

Othara carries the tower roll, Ashira has the original liability cord, and Chancery copies reveal the forged schedule.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Maintain personal liability with a viable service plan, transfer custody under strict restrictions, or license the method. Relationship commitment follows Ashira's decision to surrender or preserve the named roster.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the forgery stands, the tower pays more but is not destroyed. If the demonstration fails, public Medium restrictions tighten while the player can still preserve the personal judgement.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

If left until the Muster Crisis, the confrontation occurs under emergency conditions: one evidence path closes, the relationship cannot commit until the issue is faced, and the final quest begins from a compromised state.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
