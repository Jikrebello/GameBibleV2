# The Puppet Dies Twice

## Premise

Ashira asks the player to attend a cheap Old Market puppet show whose villain is killed in Act I, returns as his own ghost in Act II, and is killed again because the puppeteer forgot the first ending.

There is no villain, investigation, attack, stolen object, or moral test.

## Evening

The pair:

- buy black cherries and bitter tea;
- judge improvised epitaphs written for doomed puppets;
- help a child repair a paper jaw;
- watch Ashira laugh at a grotesquely mistimed death;
- carry the small stage back to the Yard through light rain.

Ashira attempts to describe the show as “structurally indefensible.” The player may agree, tease her, defend it, or simply quote the worst line.

At the Yard she admits:

> “I like it here.”
>
> The player can answer.
>
> “Do not make me repeat it. The second death was already indulgent.”

## Temperament

All eight profiles and Grey receive distinct exchanges. None scores automatic approval. A Goth player gets competitive taste; a Goofball can make her laugh and also overplay it; a Cheerleader may include the Yard without displaying Ashira; a Nerd can ruin the joke by diagramming it; Grey shares the walk and lets the moment stand.

## Outputs and Reward

Set `ashira_friendship_episode`, `yard_puppet_state`, and `ashira_private_joke_state`. Improve Hush Field coordination modestly. The scene remains available whether or not the player later chooses romance, bond, or ordinary friendship.
