# Ashira Companion Campaign

This note controls Ashira's eleven authored pieces, ten-piece playthrough ceiling, three deadlines, preparation, autonomy, changed re-entry, progression, and outcomes. [[Ashira bath Melan]] controls character. [[Ashira Field Familiarity and Relationships]] controls approval and intimacy. [[Ashira Shadow Roster Package]] controls combat.

## Dramatic Promise

Ashira can look directly at the thing everybody else has renamed, hidden, or sentimentalized. She offers the player mysteries, disturbing beauty, exact danger, and the rare relief of being believed without being tidied up.

Her danger is possession. Because she can recognize a pattern, a grief, or a likeness, she begins to believe she understands who may see it and how it may be used.

The campaign moves through:

1. the allure of her unnerving competence;
2. a company learning that she will believe an uncanny report;
3. the effectiveness of a human face used as terror;
4. pleasure in darkness that belongs to nobody's suffering;
5. a siege mystery where truth cannot impersonate consolation;
6. public need that requires disclosure without appropriation;
7. chosen romance or sworn confidence;
8. the Muster reproducing likeness at industrial scale;
9. destruction, divided custody, or renewed possession of the roster.

The human imprints are never souls. Ontological accuracy does not grant ethical permission.

## Direction and Legal State

`ashira_direction` changes only through named decisions:

- **witnessing:** she recognizes accurately, asks before use, accepts closed boundaries, and destroys useful likeness when required;
- **contained:** she performs valuable work under external limits but has not accepted those limits internally;
- **possessive:** she retains, exposes, or deploys another person's likeness or grief because she believes her understanding outranks refusal.

`ashira_legal_debt_state` follows [[Companion Debt and Remote Claims Standard]]. [[Claims-Factor Marco Bellandi]] may authenticate Qesheth judgements, place remittances, restructure liability, and close the account. Legal closure neither destroys the roster nor proves ethical practice.

No Qesheth Keeper, affected family, raider relation, or former rival appears in Grenzburg. Remote rulings, silence, rejected contact, and settlement instructions remain real.

### Zero-participation route

Ashira becomes a contained survivor:

- she saves the Causeway captives but uses one recognizable face after the affected group refuses;
- she stops the copied winter method by accepting Brina's externally enforced limits;
- she seals the human roster under divided Blackjack, Church, and civil custody.

She remains a supervised Blackjack Medium. She cannot die unseen.

With witnessing direction and at least three assets, an autonomous finale can achieve moral success while losing evidence, progression, and relationship rewards. Possessive direction worsens privacy, trust, and roster outcomes without random death.

[[Elara Velescar]] may protect a public witness in autumn, include Ashira without exposing a confidence in winter, and preserve one provenance chain in spring. She cannot decide whether Ashira uses or relinquishes a face.

## Changed Re-entry

**The Drowned Answer:** missing Act I opens a winter aftermath at a flooded Causeway annex. Captives are already recovered or lost according to autonomy; the player can repair privacy harm and recover a nonhuman substitute, not replay the rescue.

**After the Screen:** missing Act II opens a spring restitution encounter around copied Yard notes. It can restore company trust and contain a method. It cannot reopen romance, bond, or the missed progression reward.

Missing Act III fixes roster custody and summer destination.

## Embedded Opportunities

Ashira has ten:

1. Act I road disappearances: distinguish trail repetition from pursuit.
2. City breach: identify a living person beneath repeated cries.
3. Dead March: classify command residue without offering false soul rescue.
4. Church case: protect a private identification.
5. Underworld case: refuse to turn a confidence into currency.
6. Local haunting: expose fraud without humiliating grief.
7. Rain Vault exploration: recognize blue-glass repeats.
8. Yard screened practice: let a debtor revoke participation.
9. Neda recovery scene: return private effects unopened.
10. Systemic abandoned camp: distinguish one-shot human trace from reusable combat pattern.

## Outcomes

**Resolved:** Ashira destroys human likeness as equipment, preserves legitimate nonhuman Medium practice, publishes narrow safety doctrine, and teaches by consent.

**Compromised:** the roster remains sealed under divided custody while Ashira works under supervision.

**Broken/departed:** Ashira preserves a private copy, deploys the roster during the finale, or treats company refusal as ignorance. Departure follows a warned conflict and repair opportunity.

No result becomes future-setting canon.
