# Ashira Field Familiarity and Relationships

## Approval

Ashira uses the shared qualitative states:

- **Guarded:** professional access, little voluntary disclosure;
- **Open:** she invites the player into ordinary tastes and minor confidences;
- **Trusted:** she accepts challenge without assuming correction or exposure;
- **Strained:** she expects the player to publish, simplify, or exploit what she says.

Approval is not assent. Agreeing with every severe judgement can increase Ashira's sense of possession while failing to build trust.

## Field Familiarity

Only authored, distinct experiences count:

- believing an uncanny report while still testing it;
- protecting private evidence;
- naming a death accurately;
- respecting a closed room, unoffered story, or refusal;
- sharing a discovery that is strange without being tragic;
- completing a dangerous rescue;
- seeing Ashira embarrassed by ordinary pleasure without using it against her.

Idling, repeated jobs, and farming the same reaction never count.

## Relationship Separation

Ashira is heterosexual.

Relationship commitment also requires an explicit authored player interest signal. Before that signal, Ashira's attention, severity, and private trust are not written as flirtation; there is no jealousy, sexual teasing, or romance-coded party banter.

### Romance

Available to a male player. Commitment begins after [[When the Screen Comes Down]]. [[The Garden Under Rain]] is a romance-only gothic excursion and contains chosen desire, physical affection, and fade to black.

**Duo move: Shared Shadow**

Ashira folds the player into a moving shadow field. The player becomes harder to track while crossing danger; an attack made on leaving the field exposes its source.

**Episode upgrade: Shared Shadow - Rain Between Us**

The field now preserves one continuing action through ordinary visual interruption and grants a brief safe reposition when the pair separate deliberately.

**Permanent perk: Seen Without Light**

Once per major encounter, acting on a threat Ashira has revealed grants brief resistance to fear and concealment. It occupies no augment slot.

### Sworn Bond

Available to a female player. Commitment begins after [[When the Screen Comes Down]]. [[What We Keep Closed]] is explicitly platonic and establishes a sworn confidence with named boundaries.

**Duo move: Closed Circle**

Ashira creates a bounded field around a rescue, revive, private search, or mechanism interaction. One ordinary intruder is exposed and interrupted.

**Episode upgrade: Closed Circle - Door Kept**

The protected action survives one ordinary interruption, and a named private object cannot be taken during the interval.

**Permanent perk: Confidence Kept**

Once per major encounter, responding to an ally's warning without breaking their position reveals nearby hidden movement and grants brief Staying Power. It occupies no augment slot.

The paths are mutually exclusive and consume the global romance or bond slot. Rewards are distinct but equivalent. Neither path is needed for the strongest moral outcome.

## Friendship

[[The Puppet Dies Twice]] is universal. It is not a lesser relationship piece and grants:

- Ashira's camp puppet routine;
- the private joke set used in later scenes;
- a modest Hush Field coordination improvement;
- access to her strongest moral resolution when all other conditions are met.

## Conflict and Repair

Major violations include:

- publishing a confidence after explicit refusal;
- using a recognizable human imprint against civilians or captives;
- treating a private effect as loot;
- falsely telling bereaved people that a repetition is a soul;
- demanding disclosure as proof of trust.

Ashira confronts the player with one concrete object, name, or action. Permanent departure requires a visible warning and one repair opportunity. An apology must include changed custody or conduct; elegant agreement is insufficient.

## Optional Temperament Hooks

Grey is the controlling baseline. Use these only when a variation makes a particular scene sharper:

- **Cheerleader:** Ashira distrusts easy inclusion and is moved when welcome survives privacy.
- **Tomboy:** practical trust comes quickly; emotional need may be dismissed as ornamental.
- **Goth:** immediate recognition can become rivalry over who owns the darkest reading.
- **Bohemian:** shared oddity delights her; porous boundaries alarm her.
- **Jock:** direct protection is legible, public praise less so.
- **Nerd:** classification becomes intimacy until either person forgets the living subject.
- **Rebel:** secrecy and refusal align; opposition can become reflexive cruelty.
- **Goofball:** humor disarms her and can also become a way around serious consent.
- **Grey:** full baseline scenes use observation, silence, and the player's recorded actions without assuming a type.
