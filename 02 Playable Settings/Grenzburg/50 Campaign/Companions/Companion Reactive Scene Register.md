# Companion Reactive Scene Register

This register controls ordinary visibility, schedules, embedded work, ambient dialogue, and main-story contributions. It supplements the eighty-eight formal pieces without turning daily life into another intimacy meter.

## Shared Rules

- Every companion has a named assignment or routine in each campaign window.
- Recruitment does not trigger a biography dump.
- A companion may go a full journey or several visits without requesting private attention.
- Only selected companions use ambient banter.
- Low, Normal, and High frequency follow [[Companion Dialogue and Scene Standard]].
- Serious events impose silence locks.
- The complete Grey response exists before any optional temperament variant.
- Companions react only to witnessed conduct or credible reports.
- Untracked campaigns show stable work rather than deterioration.
- Romance remains unvoiced until the player selects an interest signal.

## Main-Campaign Contributions

Each companion retains one optional contribution per act. Contributions offer a route, observation, rescue, technical method, or visible assignment. None becomes a critical-path key, compulsory moral commentary, or substitute for their personal campaign.

## Embedded Opportunity Budget

Each companion retains ten authored opportunities. At least half concern work, places, ordinary company life, or current relationships. No more than two directly explain the original wrong. Repeatable jobs and recycled banter do not count.

## Player-Prompted Conversation

At hubs and camps the player may ask about current work, local opinions, equipment, travel, food, faith, or recent events. Private questions appear only after relevant shared history. Refusal to ask or continue has no approval cost.

## Pair Scenes

The twenty-eight possible pairings remain available as a source pool, not a completion quota. A pair scene requires a concrete shared object such as a task, meal, repair, wager, patient, route, or public decision. Foil validation occurs after the individuals work independently.

## Summer

Engaged codas reflect personal outcomes. Unengaged codas use stable-colleague states from [[Companion Autonomy and Act Convergence]]. Postgame seasons change work and observations without opening automatic personal arcs.
