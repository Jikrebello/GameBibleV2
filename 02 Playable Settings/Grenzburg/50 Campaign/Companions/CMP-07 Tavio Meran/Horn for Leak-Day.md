# Horn for Leak-Day

## Quest Function

Tavio's Act III finale is a player-and-Tavio industrial disaster adventure at the Great Forge. It tests whether he sounds the evacuation horn before a difficult containment becomes the final arena of his life.

The Great Forge leak is caused by damaged Shell-fire equipment interacting with a physical Muster cadence. It is not a soul, patron, idol, or universal conspiracy.

## Hook

The Great Forge's replacement line begins repeating load commands after a captured plate is installed. Three banks synchronize beyond their filed relation, igniting an expanding Shell-fire leak through the floor channels.

Hella orders the upper floor cleared. Foundry owners and military officers argue that abandoning the line will cripple the spring campaign.

Tavio sees a technically possible mobile containment. The window to try it overlaps the safest evacuation interval.

## Preparation

| Asset | Concrete advantage |
|---|---|
| Field inspection ledger | identifies which banks and fittings must be isolated |
| Leak-horn drill | starts evacuation before smoke blocks the route |
| Mobile vent frame | permits one bounded containment attempt |
| Muster cadence reading | reveals the repeating physical command |
| Named worker consent roster | separates volunteers from people ordered into danger |

Two assets permit Tavio's planned entry. Three make workers, forge, evidence, and controlled vent achievable. Four or five preserve one objective after a major failure.

One completed relationship episode supplies a non-stacking safeguard:

- `tavio_courtship_measure_practiced`; or
- `tavio_seconds_halt_practiced`.

## Beginning: Sound or Silence

Accepting at the Great Forge forms the player-Tavio duo. Hella, workers, carers, and engineers are temporary NPCs.

The first choice is not whether to abandon the forge forever. It is whether to sound the first evacuation horn before attempting containment.

- **Sound now:** safer evacuation, less time to preserve production.
- **Stage the first lane:** bounded delay with named workers.
- **Remain silent:** more technical time, unacceptable undisclosed risk.

Tavio's direction and the player's instruction determine his initial response.

## Phase One: Upper Forge

The duo crosses:

- buckling proof rails;
- elemental flare jets;
- panicked apprentices;
- jammed load gates;
- non-conscious animated forge tools;
- collapsing catwalks.

Objectives:

1. open two evacuation lanes;
2. isolate the public fuel store;
3. rescue trapped workers;
4. preserve or abandon the control ledger;
5. reach the lower proof floor.

Every vocation can solve the route through shared movement, mechanisms, protection, ranged action, or combat.

### Pause Point: Upper Forge Evacuated

The quest may pause once all living people have a route to the Yard and river steps.

## Phase Two: The Repeating Measure

In the lower floor, physical Muster plates repeat a replacement command through three forge banks. The system treats every failed part as something to be remade and returned to service.

The duo must:

- identify the original bank;
- Break two physical command carriers;
- Brace a vent corridor;
- Cast damaged plates away from the workers;
- Yield contaminated marks before they jump.

Ordinary Warlock counter-scripts, physical destruction, cooling, or mechanical isolation provide alternatives to Lancewright-specific actions.

## Phase Three: Mobile Vent

If available, the mobile frame can redirect the remaining load toward the River Vent. It requires:

- one person at the proof gauntlet;
- one at the manual stops;
- a clear evacuation lane;
- an agreed final threshold.

Tavio wants to hold until the forge can reopen immediately. Hella's filed measure permits only enough proof to prevent a district fire.

The player may:

- call the halt at the filed threshold;
- accept one bounded extension after confirming evacuation;
- order immediate Yield;
- encourage Tavio to chase complete preservation;
- use the relationship safeguard after a material failure.

## Climax: The Horn

The final leak presents three readable warnings:

1. proof rings turn white;
2. the gauntlet begins carrying two cadences;
3. the River Vent reverses.

At that point, continued containment cannot preserve both the line and the people.

Tavio must sound the horn himself for the strongest result. The player cannot press a dialogue option that physically does it for him.

If Tavio refuses:

- the player may sound it;
- Hella may order evacuation;
- the frame may fail;
- an explicit sacrifice may become available only after every living evacuation route is shown.

## Outcome

### Resolved

Tavio sounds the horn before catastrophe, Yields the loaded proof, accepts loss of production or reputation, and files the complete incident. With earlier institutional truth, he accepts a permanent arena ban and qualifies as an independent road examiner.

### Compromised

The forge is saved through external command, Tavio conceals part of the load, or he requires permanent supervision. He becomes a supervised tester or attached foundry examiner.

### Broken or Departed

Tavio treats the forge as his final arena, suppresses the horn, sacrifices workers for the line, or uses the disaster to prove unbounded mastery.

### Sacrifice

A late sacrifice is available only if:

- both evacuation routes have failed;
- living workers remain trapped;
- the player has seen a viable alternative fail through prior choices;
- Tavio offers it explicitly;
- the player deliberately accepts.

It is never an autonomous or low-approval death.

## Fail-Forward

- Upper line lost: production moves to smaller workshops.
- River Vent destroyed: Hammer Ward suffers a longer closure.
- Workers injured: named casualties affect summer and faction states.
- Control ledger lost: Hella reconstructs the cause with less institutional leverage.
- Duo defeat: rescue crews extract survivors after the automatic horn sounds; the forge is heavily damaged.
- Tavio survives a failed overproof: broken outcome remains available rather than granting martyrdom.

## Rewards

Resolved or compromised completion grants:

- **Horn Before Ruin** Lancewright capstone;
- **Road Examiner** custom-vocation blueprint;
- Tavio's settled companion capstone;
- romance perk **No Wager Between Us** or bond perk **Second's Word** when eligible;
- stateful summer destination.

Friendship alone can produce the resolved outcome and all non-relationship progression.
