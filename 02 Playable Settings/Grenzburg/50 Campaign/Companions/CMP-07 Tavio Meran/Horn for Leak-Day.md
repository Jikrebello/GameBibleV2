# Horn for Leak-Day

## Role in the Arc

This is Tavio Meran's quest 4 of four. It is playable in Early Spring and Muster Crisis and advances the arc without requiring Tavio Meran in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Shell-fire leaks from a Deep Muster surface engine and threatens the Great Forge's defense production. An evacuation horn would save workers but concede the foundry during the crisis.

Use Rune Fist discipline to contain force, sound retreat at the right moment, and decide Tavio's post-arena craft.

## Player Actions

Train leak teams, place venting gauntlets, escort specialists to a surface conduit, and alternate controlled counters with worker evacuation.

## Opposition and Pressure

Muster engines repeat bounded commands; foundry owners demand continued production; Tavio sees a chance for one impossible public victory.

## Information Paths

Warlock Hall readings, forge pressure gauges, and surface frost patterns reveal the discharge cycle.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Sound the horn and preserve people, attempt a bounded hold with full consent, or chase glory beyond the evacuation threshold. Explicit sacrifice is possible only after the player knows the retreat route.

## Fail-Forward Results

If containment fails, the forge closes and production moves to Last Hearth workshops. If the horn sounds late, injuries reduce summer capacity but do not erase the line.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

Committing to the Deep Muster closes ordinary resolution. The companion reaches a specified compromised summer state unless the quest has already produced a broken or departed outcome.

Completes Tavio's leak-warden, compromised teacher, departure, or sacrifice outcome.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
