# The Fair Defeat

## Role in the Arc

This is Tavio Meran's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring and advances the arc without requiring Tavio Meran in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Lio Varcen arrives with a recovered fighting brace and publicly challenges Tavio to the fair match he was denied. The Crucible offers Tavio restored status if he quietly accepts sole blame afterward.

Give Lio a real reckoning without turning an injured victim into a prop for Tavio's redemption.

## Player Actions

Negotiate match rules, investigate sabotage aimed at Lio's brace, train or withdraw, and conduct a duel whose outcome depends on play rather than a scripted victory.

## Opposition and Pressure

Crucible agents want a clean spectacle; gamblers threaten Lio; Tavio is tempted to throw the bout, which Lio considers another theft of agency.

## Information Paths

Brace tool marks, bookmaker messages, and an agent's travel warrant prove interference.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Fight honestly and accept the result, expose the school before the bout, or take the sole-blame bargain. Relationship commitment follows the first honest reckoning, not whether Tavio wins.

## Fail-Forward Results

If sabotage succeeds, Lio may fight with altered rules or postpone while still testifying. A lost duel advances the story and can improve trust if honored.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If left until the Muster Crisis, the confrontation occurs under emergency conditions: one evidence path closes, the relationship cannot commit until the issue is faced, and the final quest begins from a compromised state.

## Rewards and Return Value

Unlocks commitment and fixes whether Tavio values fair defeat above managed reputation.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
