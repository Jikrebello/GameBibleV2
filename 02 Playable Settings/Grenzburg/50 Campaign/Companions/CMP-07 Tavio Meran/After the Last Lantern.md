# After the Last Lantern

## Quest Function

This is Tavio's male-player sworn-bond episode. It is a completely platonic night of cleaning the fairground, keeping watch, and establishing brotherhood between trusted seconds.

There is no romance, courtship, fade to black, marriage imagery, or alternate romantic branch.

## Beginning: After the Crowd

Tavio asks the player to remain after the fair's last lantern is lowered. The work is ordinary:

- collect broken cups;
- return borrowed benches;
- count unclaimed prizes;
- fold stop-flags;
- walk lost children or tired workers to the correct camp;
- extinguish cooking fires.

The episode does not reveal a hidden crime. Nothing attacks because the crowd has left.

## The Work of a Second

As they clean, the two men discuss what a second actually owes:

- speak before danger becomes spectacle;
- name a hidden weakness;
- stop the bout when the fighter cannot;
- accept anger without withdrawing the halt;
- never throw a result to protect pride;
- remain after the crowd leaves.

The player may challenge Tavio, confess a parallel fear, keep the conversation practical, or use humour. The bond does not require matching personalities.

## Night Watch

The pair take the final watch over the emptied fairground and nearby refugee tents. Small ambient events test attention without becoming a crisis:

- a loose awning in the wind;
- a cooking coal thought extinguished;
- an exhausted worker carrying too much;
- a drunk contestant looking for his absurd prize.

Calling an early stop, sharing a load, or asking for help receives the same respect as solving it alone.

## Brotherhood

Before dawn, each gives the other a plain brass second's token stamped with an open hand. They explicitly become sworn brothers and trusted seconds.

The promise is:

> I will call the halt when you cannot. I will hear it when you do.

The token is not a ring, courtship object, sacred ordination, or claim of ownership.

## Rewards

- **Fair Return: Second's Halt**, letting either sworn brother cancel one overcommitted action into guarded Yield;
- permanent non-slot perk **Second's Word**, rewarding a timely cancel, guard, or retreat after a companion warning;
- `tavio_seconds_halt_practiced`, one Act III safeguard;
- bond-specific summer dialogue.

Missing the episode does not end an existing bond, but loses these upgrades.
