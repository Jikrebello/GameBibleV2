# The Road Takes the Measure

## Quest Function

Tavio's summer coda demonstrates his settled conduct. It does not reopen Lio's decision, the arena judgement, the Great Forge horn, or a missed relationship episode.

## Resolved: Independent Road Examiner

Tavio invites the player on his first independent inspection between Grenzburg and Tannbruck. He:

- files a realistic load;
- asks the road crew who holds stop authority;
- rejects a ceremonial overproof;
- accepts an ugly but safe repair;
- hosts a small game after the work is complete.

He keeps no arena title. His delight in performance survives as teaching, fair games, and the pleasure of a difficult measure honestly met.

If Lio remains in contact, a letter reports his own work or refusal without granting forgiveness by default.

## Compromised: Supervised Tester

Tavio works under Hella, Brina, or ducal supervision. He performs useful proof and follows the halt, but still looks toward the named authority before trusting his own judgement.

The player may encourage the work, challenge his dependence, or accept that incomplete accountability can still protect people.

## Broken or Departed: Circuit Notice

The player encounters a touring bill, abandoned testing ground, or distant public challenge. Tavio has returned to spectacle, and the filed details are thinner than the promises.

If he remained in Grenzburg under strain, the scene becomes a final warned conversation rather than an automatic reconciliation.

## Relationship Variants

- **Romance:** a private exchange about building a life that still contains risk without making love a wager.
- **Sworn bond:** the brothers inspect one another's stop-flags before the road opens.
- **Friendship:** the player receives an absurd road-safety prize.
- **No close relationship:** Tavio remains warm or distant according to approval and outcome.

## Play

The coda includes one bounded inspection and one social return:

- identify a damaged brace;
- ask workers for the real load history;
- choose repair, restriction, or closure;
- observe whether Tavio calls the stop himself;
- finish with a game, meal, or departure.

The coda shows the outcome rather than grading it again.

## Rewards

- Tavio's permanent summer routine;
- postgame road-inspection encounters where appropriate;
- relationship-specific ambient dialogue;
- final `tavio_summer_destination`.
