# Tavio Lancewright Package

## Role

Tavio is a mobile technical striker and party setup companion. He marks armour, limbs, terrain, and mechanisms; allies load those marks; he then chooses rupture, protection, displacement, or Yield.

His AI should feel daring without repeatedly exposing the player to involuntary overproof.

## Native Equipment

- one-handed war spear;
- heavy proof gauntlet;
- medium arena armour;
- three visible brass proof rings;
- stop-flags and repair wire.

## Combat Priorities

1. mark a tactically useful part or surface;
2. call the intended release in plain language;
3. load through spear attacks or a gauntlet catch;
4. invite allied loading only when the relation is stable;
5. release before the warning state becomes unsafe;
6. Yield when an ally, worker, or player call makes continued proof unacceptable.

## Signature Families

| Family | Companion function |
|---|---|
| **First Measure** | places a fast mark on armour, limb, shield, or physical cover |
| **Red-Brass Break** | ruptures a loaded mark for armour damage and stagger |
| **Second's Brace** | converts a ground mark into directional protection |
| **Cast the Line** | moves debris or pulls a large target off balance |
| **Vaulting Answer** | lunges, vaults, or descends through marked geometry |
| **Yield Called** | vents all load, covers disengagement, and cancels an unsafe action |

## Companion AI Limits

Tavio never:

- places a heavy mark on a civilian, captive, or friendly creature;
- spends a player resource;
- forces an ally to load a mark;
- holds an overproof state without an audible and visible warning;
- uses Cast as unrestricted telekinesis;
- treats a retreat command as an approval loss event.

At measured direction he Yields proactively. At managed direction he sometimes waits for an external call. At overproof direction he takes longer risks, but the player always receives a readable halt window.

## Party Synergies

- **Fighter:** loads armour and ground marks through guard pressure.
- **Slayer:** exploits exposed parts after Break.
- **Hunter:** loads distant marks and chooses precise body parts.
- **Warlock:** supplies compatible elemental relations.
- **Medium:** uses non-conscious imprints to load or distract without interacting with souls.
- **Paladin:** protects the Yield interval.
- **Binder:** directs valid bound forms into marked geometry.
- **Bard:** times coordinated loading and safe withdrawal.
- **Lancewright player:** shares proof geometry without exceeding the three-mark ceiling per practitioner.

## Relationship Moves

- **Fair Challenge / Answer Freely:** romance path.
- **Fair Return / Second's Halt:** sworn-bond path.

Both are coordination tools, not damage multipliers that excuse unsafe proof.
