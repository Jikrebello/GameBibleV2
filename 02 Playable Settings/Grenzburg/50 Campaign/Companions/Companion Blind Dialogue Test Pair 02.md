# Companion Blind Dialogue Test Pair 02

Use these lines without names, vocation labels, culture, or backstory context. A reader should attribute at least ten of twelve lines correctly to each man.

## Test Lines

1. “That is not soup. It is a rumor being spread by hot water.”
2. “The red mark is visible, yes. Unfortunately it indicates the safe valve.”
3. “Do not be heroic. Heroic is heavy. Be narrow.”
4. “You have improved my design by refusing to use it. I dislike the efficiency of the lesson.”
5. “I am not a priest. I can pray, and I can stay.”
6. “The hinge has three complaints. Only one concerns the door.”
7. “I had a plan. It has failed twice, so now we are old friends.”
8. “No. Explain it where the person carrying the load can interrupt you.”
9. “You may count the bread. You may not count his shame as part of the ration.”
10. “The rule may be amended. My loss may not be renamed consensus.”
11. “Give me the ugly end. I have the face for public service.”
12. “I do not require gratitude. I require you to test the release.”
13. “If I begin the goose story, stop me. There is no goose. There is only cowardice with feathers.”
14. “An exit nobody can reach is decoration.”
15. “Prayer, company, or quiet?”
16. “The instrument is useless. I have become unexpectedly attached to this feature.”
17. “I found another blanket. Before you ask: yes, I am telling you before sitting on it.”
18. “Your field correction is now the plan. Kindly enjoy that sentence once.”
19. “The saint in this story has acquired a mule because the children demanded one.”
20. “The latch works perfectly for a dry man standing indoors. We have solved the wrong person.”
21. “No joke. Not for this.”
22. “I invited you because I wanted you present. The telescope was administrative camouflage.”
23. “You needn't forgive me to tell me where the blankets go.”
24. “If every participant must remain, you have built a trap with excellent minutes.”

## Rejection Rules

Revise a set if:

- Bernard is identifiable only through food, prayer, or theft;
- Zafir is identifiable only through law, contracts, or technical jargon;
- either man sounds like he is completing a symmetrical moral debate;
- more than four lines per man directly invoke his core wrong;
- the humor could be exchanged without changing rhythm;
- the line explains an emotion that concrete scene business could show.
