# A Breach Too Clean

## Role in the Arc

This is Ilyana Veshkar's quest 2 of four. It is playable in Open Autumn and Early Winter and advances the arc without requiring Ilyana Veshkar in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A ducal engineer asks Ilyana to demonstrate the celebrated breach method on the Broken Tollworks before winter. The geometry is identical to the operation that killed her platoon.

Clear a bandit-held civil ruin without repeating the unsafe overdraw or allowing Ilyana's refusal to be framed as cowardice.

## Player Actions

Survey three approaches, evacuate quarry workers, recover a second conductor from Warlock Hall, and choose a slow counter-breach, close assault, or carefully bounded script.

## Opposition and Pressure

Bandits have hostages in a load chamber. An ambitious warlock examiner pressures Ilyana to prove the medal was deserved, while Koss values speed over safety.

## Information Paths

Worker testimony locates the hostages; vibration dust reveals occupied voids; old toll plans show a service culvert. Any two reveal why the clean line is unacceptable.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Choose time, risk to the party, or damage to the ruin, but not trapped civilians. Letting Ilyana cast the full overdraw drives her arc toward broken even if it wins quickly.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed approach moves hostages and raises alarms. A partial collapse closes one route but exposes the culvert. The stronghold remains finishable.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

At the spring transition, unresolved danger produces a named casualty, loss, or policy change and the reckoning quest inherits it.

Proves whether Ilyana has learned a different method or merely fears the old one.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
