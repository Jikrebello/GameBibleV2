# Duet Without a Conductor

## Quest Function

This is Ilyana's female-player sworn-bond episode. It establishes an exceptional, completely platonic friendship through an equal composition that neither woman conducts.

## Beginning

Ilyana brings two small answer-reeds to Bridgehold's disused signal chamber. Each reed can play only half the intended interval.

She explains the rule:

- either woman may begin;
- either may stop;
- the other must listen rather than fill the silence;
- neither part is complete or subordinate.

The player does not need a musical skill. Timing choices shape dialogue and sound, not success or failure.

## Middle

The pair test the duet across:

- the signal chamber's short echo;
- the open Great Bridge;
- separate stair landings where neither can see the other.

They discuss:

- Ilyana's fear of leaving an empty measure;
- the player's right to call her back without becoming her commander;
- how sworn friendship differs from romance, family, contract, and ordinary approval;
- the duty to speak when the other turns fear into control or flight.

There is no interruption, villain, hidden collapse, flirtation, marriage imagery, or fade to black.

## End

Back in the signal chamber, the pair compose a final line by alternating offers. They tie the two answer-reeds together for storage but each keeps her own reed.

They explicitly name the bond:

- equal voices;
- honest interruption;
- no ownership;
- guaranteed answer when the other calls for truth.

## Reward

**Call and Answer: Equal Voices** adds brief group Staying Power when the coordinated counter succeeds.

`ilyana_equal_cadence_practiced` supplies one Act III safeguard equal to the romance bonus. Missing the episode does not end the bond but forfeits the upgrade and safeguard.
