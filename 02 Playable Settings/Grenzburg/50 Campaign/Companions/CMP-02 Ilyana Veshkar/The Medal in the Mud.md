# The Medal in the Mud

## Role in the Arc

This is Ilyana Veshkar's quest 1 of four. It is playable in Early Autumn and Open Autumn and advances the arc without requiring Ilyana Veshkar in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A looter at Old Coach Burn finds Ilyana's Vlastravan medal in baggage she threw from the debtor convoy. A military courier recognizes it and calls her a hero.

Recover the medal before Koss turns it into leverage and learn why Ilyana cannot bear to keep or destroy it.

## Player Actions

Search the wreck, pursue the looter to Split Elm Inn, protect the courier from bandits, and compare the medal citation with a mud-stained field roll in the same dispatch case.

## Opposition and Pressure

Koss wants the citation for her personnel file, the looter wants ransom, and Ilyana urges a quiet disposal. Road bandits exploit the search but know nothing of the old battle.

## Information Paths

The field roll lists the relief platoon; heat damage on the medal matches an overdraw; the courier carries a later commendation signed by Varko Milen.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Return, surrender, or retain the medal as evidence. Ilyana must tell the player that the celebrated breach killed her own relief platoon.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the medal is lost, the citation and field roll preserve the case but Milen can call them copies. If the courier dies, his sealed pouch still reaches Warlock Hall with reduced context.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

## Seasonal Cutoff

At the winter transition, the initiating evidence is secured by another party and the quest returns in Early Winter with higher institutional leverage and lower trust; it does not simply vanish.

## Rewards and Return Value

Names the hidden crime and establishes the difference between battlefield necessity and falsified sacrifice.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
