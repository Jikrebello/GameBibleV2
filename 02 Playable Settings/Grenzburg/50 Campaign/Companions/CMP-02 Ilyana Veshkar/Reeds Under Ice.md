# Reeds Under Ice

## Quest Function

This normal-party operation brings [[Mirella Corsi]] and [[Tomas Marin]] physically into Grenzburg, recovers individually owned instruments, and prepares the winter finale without forcing the Salt-Reeds to reform.

## Hook

A late Sermoran freight lighter reaches the river boom just before hard freeze. It carries Mirella, Tomas, two instrument cases, and a Morante factor named **Luca Sern**.

Grobi river scouts strike the boom while local looters break onto the immobilized freight deck. The lighter shears loose and grounds against the frozen channel below North Quay.

Luca has a facially valid claim over disputed common property. He does not own Mirella, Tomas, their personal instruments, or their labor.

## Beginning: The Broken Boom

From Customs Hall, the party can identify:

- Mirella and three sailors trapped in the stern cabin;
- Tomas on the forward ice with a damaged horn case;
- common Salt-Reed costume and score trunks in the hold;
- Grobi scouts approaching from the west-bank ice;
- looters entering through the quay side;
- the lighter drifting toward a weakened boom chain.

The party chooses an initial route:

- down the crane ladders;
- across marked ice from Ferry Yard;
- through a warehouse loading tunnel;
- or along the River Boom wall walk.

Every route supports every vocation.

## Middle: Four Kinds of Rescue

The operation contains four simultaneous objectives:

1. free Mirella and the sailors before the stern floods;
2. secure Tomas and the horn case;
3. prevent the boom chain from dragging a quay crane down;
4. keep looters and Grobi from removing the instrument cases.

Other companions provide advantages:

- Bernard stabilizes cold casualties;
- Tavio braces or vents the boom mechanism;
- Elara identifies substituted case marks;
- Dismas reads the safest ice route.

None decides property or consent.

### Pause Point

After all living survivors reach a stable section of ice or quay, the quest may pause before the property confrontation.

## The Individual Cases

Each case is resolved separately:

- Mirella's scores and voice reeds;
- Tomas's horn and cadence plates;
- Celia's viol if previously lost;
- Nereo's drum if previously lost;
- Ilyana's current Blackjack flute;
- disputed common costumes and arrangements.

Ilyana may return personal property immediately, ask Customs to hold it, support Luca's claim, or attempt to keep everything together "until the crisis ends."

Mirella and Tomas will assist with rescue signals but do not agree to re-form the company.

## End: The Ice Gives

The lighter rolls as the boom chain parts. The player chooses how to distribute people, cases, ropes, and counterweight while fighting the final Grobi push.

Three preparation or route successes make all people and instruments recoverable. Otherwise the player may lose property, suffer injuries, or let the lighter crush part of the quay without losing the performers off-screen.

## Fail-Forward

- Damaged instrument: its owner remains present but cannot perform a full line in the finale.
- Lost common trunk: authorship evidence survives in Mirella's memory and Customs copies.
- Escaped Luca: Morante's claim remains active at The Free Refrain.
- Captured Luca: another lawful custodian may argue the property claim; no antagonist gains plot armour.
- Quay damage: one winter route closes and the finale uses a longer casualty lane.
- Party defeat: the boom watch saves survivors after instruments and cargo scatter.

## Rewards

- `salt_reed_roster_in_grenzburg`: all four former members are alive and physically present by winter.
- `ilyana_instruments_returned` when individual instruments reach their owners.
- **Second Bore:** Ilyana prepares simultaneous field and Lead lines more efficiently, foreshadowing two-refrain mastery.
