# The Gilded Wake Season

The Gilded Wake Season is the event that broke the [[The Salt-Reed Company|Salt-Reed Company]] and placed [[Ilyana Veshkar]] in Medori service.

## The Offer

[[Patron-Captain Caterina Morante]] offered the company an exclusive privateer season aboard her flotilla:

- exceptional pay;
- a promised tour of prize ports;
- ownership of a new keyed double flute at completion;
- public credit in Sermoran victory ceremonies;
- dangerous battle service and restricted freedom to leave while at sea.

Mirella, Nereo, Celia, and Tomas refused. Ilyana believed the season would make the company secure and famous. She used the common seal, forged their individual approvals, and concealed the refusal while controlling the booking purse and instrument cases.

## The Lawful Prize

Morante initially believed the delegation valid. The company completed one lawful action against a vessel named by a current letter of marque. Its fog, signals, weapon cadences, and surrender call helped take the ship without civilian massacre.

This success strengthened Ilyana's belief that the others would eventually thank her.

## The Refusal

The next operations required widening violence, longer confinement, and battle compulsion against crews whose status was uncertain. The four performers refused further service and revealed the forged approvals.

Morante then became knowingly culpable. Rather than release them, she:

- held their instruments as performance security;
- threatened personal claims against anyone leaving;
- treated the successful prize as retroactive consent;
- used Ilyana's forgery to divide the company;
- demanded continued battle work until the season's contracted value was met.

Ilyana did not magically compel her companions. She continued to argue, schedule, withhold funds, and invoke the company's needs until their escape made the arrangement impossible.

## Collapse and Debt

The troupe separated during the ensuing port dispute. Instrument damage, cancelled work, prize-share litigation, forgery restitution, and broken-service claims exceeded Ilyana's resources.

A Sermoran court distinguished the wrongs:

- the first prize remained lawful;
- Ilyana bore responsibility for forgery and coercive company control;
- Morante bore responsibility for knowingly enforcing invalid consent after discovery;
- the other four performers were not collectively liable for service they had refused.

Medori purchased Ilyana's personal restitution and criminal surety. That debt ultimately placed her in the [[Blackjack Corps]].

## Canon Boundary

Privateering is not declared universally evil by this event. Ilyana and Morante are culpable for their specific choices. Neither artistic brilliance nor a useful military outcome changes what consent existed.
