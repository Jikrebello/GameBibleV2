# The Last Common Measure

## Quest Function

Ilyana's Act III duo finale destroys a Muster cadence engine at the River Boom Towers. It proves whether the civic music built across her campaign can function when she is isolated, exhausted, or gone.

The player and Ilyana can complete the assault alone. Current district crews provide optional remote support from positions they already own. No former Salt-Reed member enters Grenzburg or performs in the finale.

## Hook

A Qianglong command assembly occupies the [[Grenzburg City Anchor Register|River Boom Towers]]. Bronze wind pipes, rank drums, residue carriers, and boom chains turn the river crossing into a repeating mobilization signal.

The pattern:

- directs corpse residue toward the western wards;
- prevents civilian boats from using the river;
- recalls broken residue into replacement formations;
- amplifies command signals moving north from the Deep Muster.

It is non-conscious machinery and residue, not a choir or imprisoned intelligence.

## Preparation

| Asset | Concrete advantage |
|---|---|
| Field cases distributed | one district line survives the loss of its tower |
| Muster cadence sampled | identifies the false close and command reset |
| Boom route known | opens the service tunnel and civilian evacuation path |
| Common score credited | lets local crews revise counter-lines without waiting for permission |
| Crew destinations secured | every supporting team has a retreat point and relief shift |

Two assets make the assault viable. Three make civilians, crews, records, and boom machinery recoverable. Four or five protect one objective from one operational failure.

The completed romance or bond episode supplies one equivalent extra safeguard outside the threshold.

## Beginning: Five Independent Answers

At Blackjack Yard, Ilyana presents a complete duo plan. She then sends one bounded request to each field-case crew. A crew may accept, alter, or refuse without further persuasion.

Possible roles are:

- Longlake carries the civilian withdrawal call;
- Mercy controls the safe interval for casualty boats;
- Tannward marks incoming siege fire;
- Hammer Ward counters the engine's heat pulse;
- Blackjack runners maintain the fallback rally signal.

Refused roles become additional duo tasks. Nobody is brought to a pre-battle meeting for a moral discussion.

## Approach Routes

- **Wall walk:** direct combat across exposed tower platforms.
- **River service tunnel:** flooded traversal and mechanism access.
- **Boom chain gallery:** moving counterweights and residue labor forms.
- **Boat approach:** fast and exposed if river services survived.

All routes remain viable for every vocation.

### Pause Point: Boom Towers Reached

The quest may pause once the duo establishes a foothold and receives the crews' final signals.

## Middle: Five Repetitions

The assembly cycles through:

1. **Call:** residue forms rise and take rank.
2. **Arm:** black mud and stored pieces replace damaged bodies.
3. **March:** the western gate opens and the column advances.
4. **Return:** destroyed forms are drawn back by command pattern.
5. **Close:** the engine resets through the river boom.

The duo can interrupt phases through physical destruction, reversed counterweights, route separation, elemental detuning, harmonic counterphrase, or cutting the boom after evacuation.

Each supporting district line suppresses one pressure or preserves one route. If a team withdraws, the quest becomes harder without treating the withdrawal as betrayal.

## Cadence Core

Inside the east tower, the assembly produces apparent speech from repeated military orders. It is computation, not a negotiator.

A collapse severs Ilyana from every visible crew. She can either trust the published close, seize the five field lines through an emergency master phrase, or abandon counterattack support to keep the evacuation rhythm intact.

The strongest route is not the cleanest music. It lets independent crews finish differently while the duo exposes the core.

### Pause Point: Cadence Core Exposed

The quest may pause before final destruction and the later account settlement.

## End: Last Common Measure

Victory requires:

- Ilyana's field refrain;
- a physical break or detune performed by the player;
- one safe evacuation interval;
- destruction or permanent silencing of the command reset.

The title names the common close shared by Grenzburg's five field crews. It belongs to the people who keep using it.

After the tower is secure, Bellandi meets Ilyana privately at the Iron Ledger. He has authenticated the Salt-Reed instructions and can administer the legal settlement without producing any former companion in the room. He places three separate items on the desk: personal-service releases, individually owned work, and disputed common property.

Ilyana signs, refuses, or alters the documents. Bellandi records the act and moves to the next account. No victim forgives her, no crowd applauds, and the player cannot negotiate a remote person's answer.

## Settlements

### Resolved

Ilyana releases every personal-service claim, returns individual property and credit, and submits disputed common work to neutral partition. She may remain ambitious and publicly successful. She simply cannot build that success on other people's compulsory continuity.

### Compromised

She releases people but retains disputed repertoire, the company name, or a civic post governed by external stop rules.

### Broken or Departed

She conceals releases, claims emergency performance as ownership proof, uses compulsion against a non-hostile participant, or accepts renewed remote patronage on the old terms.

Morante remains a remote legal and sequel state only. Her arrest, death, or escape elsewhere never blocks this settlement.

## Fail-Forward

- Destroying the boom ends the signal but closes river transit.
- A supporting crew may be injured or displaced, never killed by one hidden check.
- Lost field cases weaken the counter-cadence without erasing local testimony or legal instructions.
- A failed first assault allows one residue column into a western district before recovery.
- A relationship safeguard preserves one person or material objective; it cannot choose the settlement.

## Rewards

- **Master Counter-Cadence:** Bard capstone against repeating hostile signals, roars, and conducted workings.
- **Sermoran Field Bard Blueprint** and mastered custom-vocation parts.
- Romance perk **One Listener** or bond perk **Equal Voices** on eligible resolved or compromised paths.
- final civic-work, legal, tower, and summer states.
