# The Last Common Measure

## Quest Function

Ilyana's Act III duo finale joins her artistic settlement to a real Muster threat without making her troupe dispute the cause of the undead crisis.

The player and Ilyana can complete the mission alone. Former Salt-Reed members may voluntarily improve it as temporary NPCs.

## Hook

A Qianglong command assembly has occupied the [[Grenzburg City Anchor Register|River Boom Towers]]. Bronze wind pipes, rank drums, residue carriers, and the boom chains turn the river crossing into a repeating mobilization signal.

The pattern:

- directs corpse residue toward the western wards;
- prevents civilian boats from using the river;
- recalls destroyed residue into replacement formations;
- amplifies command signals moving north from the Deep Muster.

It is non-conscious machinery and residue, not a choir of trapped souls.

Ilyana can counter its cadence. Physical destruction, Warlock detuning, mechanism failure, and route control remain equally real parts of victory.

## Preparation

| Asset | Concrete advantage |
|---|---|
| Releases protected | no emergency can reactivate the old service claims |
| Muster cadence sampled | identifies the false close and command reset |
| Boom route known | opens the service tunnel and civilian evacuation path |
| Common score partitioned | assigns optional counter-lines without assuming ownership |
| Destinations secured | performers can withdraw without losing shelter or work |

Two assets make the assault viable. Three make civilians, performers, records, and boom machinery all recoverable. Four or five protect one objective from one operational failure.

The completed romance or bond episode supplies one equivalent extra safeguard outside the threshold.

## Beginning: Invitations

At Blackjack Yard, Ilyana presents a complete plan that works with only the player and her flute.

She then asks each former member whether they wish to take one bounded role:

- Mirella: identify and break the command phrase;
- Nereo: hold a withdrawal pulse;
- Celia: maintain recoverable-health support;
- Tomas: counter the elemental cadence.

Every refusal is accepted without negotiation. An accepted role includes a stopping signal and retreat point.

The player cannot use social checks to force participation.

## Approach Routes

- **Wall walk:** direct combat across the tower platforms.
- **River service tunnel:** flooded traversal and mechanism access.
- **Boom chain gallery:** moving counterweights, residue labor forms, and close control.
- **Boat approach:** exposed but fast if river services survived.

All routes remain viable for every vocation.

### Pause Point: Boom Towers Reached

The quest may pause once the duo establishes a foothold and the optional performers occupy or decline their positions.

## Middle: Five Repetitions

The assembly cycles through five command phases:

1. **Call:** residue forms rise and take rank.
2. **Arm:** black mud and stored pieces replace damaged bodies.
3. **March:** the western gate opens and the column advances.
4. **Return:** destroyed forms are dragged back by command pattern.
5. **Close:** the engine resets through the river boom.

The duo can interrupt each phase through:

- physical destruction;
- reversed counterweights;
- route separation;
- elemental detuning;
- harmonic counterphrase;
- cutting the boom after evacuation.

Optional performers each suppress one phase but may be injured or withdraw. Their absence increases player tasks rather than closing the quest.

## Cadence Core

Inside the east tower, the command assembly adapts apparent speech from repeated military orders. It is computation, not a conscious negotiator.

Ilyana must resist treating the player's silence or the old troupe's presence as permission to assign a final formation. The duo exposes the core while civilians evacuate beneath the west platform.

### Pause Point: Cadence Core Exposed

The quest may pause before the final destruction and settlement sequence.

## End: Last Common Measure

The final counter requires:

- Ilyana's field refrain;
- a physical break or detune performed by the player;
- one safe evacuation interval;
- destruction or permanent silencing of the command reset.

If former performers volunteer, their independent lines create the Last Common Measure. The name describes the last piece all five may share under the old company history. It does not promise another performance.

After victory, Chancery House records:

- each service release;
- instrument ownership;
- authored scores and divided credit;
- disputed common repertoire;
- the company name;
- restitution and remaining property claims.

## Moral Settlement

### Resolved

Ilyana releases every service and repertoire claim she cannot prove as solely hers, returns property and credit, and accepts that the Salt-Reeds may part without forgiving or re-forming. She chooses an open-road future.

### Compromised

She releases people but retains disputed common repertoire, public credit, or a supervised civic charter. Useful service continues under external boundaries.

### Broken or Departed

She conceals releases, claims the company name through emergency service, compels a non-hostile performer, destroys authorship records, or accepts Morante's replacement patronage.

## Morante Adaptation

- If Morante is present, she may argue, resist arrest, fight, release claims, or escape.
- If arrested, a factor carries the genuine property issue.
- If dead, her estate papers remain but no replacement mastermind appears.
- If exposed, the settlement proceeds with fewer human opponents.
- If escaped, she may retain bounded sequel potential without changing Ilyana's decision.

## Fail-Forward

- Tower loss: the boom is destroyed to end the signal, closing river transit.
- Performer injury: every former member survives but their summer destination changes.
- Lost archive: testimony resolves personal release while music or credit is permanently lost.
- Failed first assault: the duo is recovered after a residue column reaches one western district.
- Relationship safeguard: preserves one person or material objective once; it cannot choose release for Ilyana.

## Rewards

- **Master Counter-Cadence:** Bard capstone against repeating hostile signals, roars, and conducted workings.
- **Sermoran Field Bard Blueprint** and mastered custom-vocation parts.
- Romance perk **One Listener** or bond perk **Equal Voices** on resolved or compromised committed paths.
- final arc, troupe, Morante, tower, and summer states.
