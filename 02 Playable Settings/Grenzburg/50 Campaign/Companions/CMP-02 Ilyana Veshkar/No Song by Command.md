# No Song by Command

## Quest Function

Ilyana's Act I duo finale turns professional refusal into a gameplay constraint. The player and Ilyana must rescue **Tessa Rill**, the Eel Bridge households, and the ferry route without requiring Tessa to continue a performance that is injuring her.

Tessa is a current Grenzburg colleague introduced through [[The Road Without a Conductor]]. She has no old grievance to settle and no interest in becoming Ilyana's moral examiner.

## Hook

The **Reedknife Band**, ordinary human road raiders, has occupied [[Eel Bridge]]. They force Tessa to maintain concealment fog through a prepared signal reed while they move stolen food wagons across the ferry slip.

Band leader **Heda Rusk** holds ferrymen beneath the bridge and has weakened the downstream brace. Tessa has played long enough that blood is visible inside the mouthpiece. She marks guard movements with tiny timing errors that only a working Bard or an attentive observer would notice.

## Preparation

| Asset | Advantage |
|---|---|
| Cases secured | identifies the prepared fog reed and its safe release phrase |
| Road circuit shared | lets Tessa communicate through the public code rather than Ilyana's private cues |
| Eel Bridge route known | opens the culvert and reed-bank approaches |
| Ownership recorded | lets the watch seize stolen wagons without claiming Tessa's equipment |
| Voluntary signal support | supplies ferrymen able to carry the evacuation signal after Tessa stops |

Two assets permit the rescue. Three make Tessa, households, reed, bridge, and bandit capture achievable. Four or five protect one objective from one logistical failure.

## Beginning: The False Quiet

From the approach, the duo can hear and see:

- fog pulses at exact wagon intervals;
- hammers weakening the downstream brace;
- a deliberately ugly grace note each time a guard passes Tessa;
- ferry poles laid across the undercroft door;
- a second signal whistle answering from the reed bank.

Approaches include the main-road buyer bluff, reed-bank crawl, spring culvert, ferry cable, and ordinary climb along the bridge inspection posts. Every vocation has a complete route.

### Pause Point: Reconnaissance Complete

The duo may pause after marking Tessa, the ferrymen, wagons, brace, and patrol signal.

## Middle: Break the Leverage

The duo must separate three controls:

1. the guard holding the ferrymen;
2. the weakened brace and lamp-oil charge;
3. Heda's outside patrol signal.

Possible methods include stealth, false musical replies, simultaneous mechanism releases, a public-road warning, direct assault, or turning the stolen wagons into cover.

The local **Bridge Alarm 0-3** advances play:

- **0:** routine occupation;
- **1:** outside patrol recalled;
- **2:** Heda begins withdrawing the wagons;
- **3:** the brace is lit and captives are moved onto the span.

## Tessa Stops

When the duo reaches her, Tessa pulls the reed from her mouth and cannot safely resume. Ilyana can see that another thirty seconds of fog would simplify the evacuation.

Tessa says only: "You have the bridge. I have no breath. Decide with both facts."

The player can help Ilyana build a route without the fog, ask what Tessa can safely do, endorse an order, or remain silent. Ilyana's response follows her established direction.

Valid alternatives include smoke pots, cut reed screens, a wagon barricade, the culvert, the public signal crew, or Ilyana taking the dangerous refrain herself. Tessa may call one ordinary warning after reaching cover. She never has to prove goodwill by playing through injury.

### Pause Point: Tessa Freed

The quest may pause after Tessa reaches the ferry house and the withdrawal route is chosen.

## End: Eel Bridge Break

Heda retreats across the loaded span while the downstream brace begins to fail. The duo manages:

- households leaving the undercroft;
- food wagons blocking the bridge;
- the burning brace;
- Tessa's signal reed;
- Heda's escape route.

The player can hold the bridge, cut one span after evacuation, redirect wagons into the weir yard, or abandon property to save people. Strong preparation permits every material objective, but never removes the need to choose a method under pressure.

## Outcomes

- **Accountable:** Ilyana accepts Tessa's stop, protects the evacuation, and returns the reed.
- **Costly:** the stop is respected while the bridge, food, or bandit capture is lost.
- **Procedural:** Ilyana obeys only because the player or written code compels it.
- **Entitled:** Ilyana orders an injured non-hostile colleague to continue, creating a warned major conflict.

Tessa's future opinion depends on the work done, not a forgiveness scene.

## Fail-Forward

- A bridge collapse preserves the households through ferries but worsens Longlake transit.
- Heda may reinforce a later road camp if she escapes.
- A lost signal reed weakens one winter preparation path.
- Tessa may be injured but is never killed by a single failed check.
- Duo defeat ends with local ferrymen recovering them after the bandits escape with wagons.

## Rewards

- **Countervoice:** Ilyana gains a stronger interrupt after reading a hostile cadence.
- **Voluntary Lead:** companion AI never assigns a non-hostile temporary NPC as Lead without an authored agreement.
- Act I direction, bridge, household, and changed-reentry states.
