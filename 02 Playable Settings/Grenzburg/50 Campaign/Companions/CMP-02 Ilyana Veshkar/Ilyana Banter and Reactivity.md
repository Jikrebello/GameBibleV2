# Ilyana Banter and Reactivity

This register controls Ilyana's voice around Grenzburg, the factions, and the other seven companions.

## Voice Rules

- Ilyana is lively because she enjoys people, not because sincerity embarrasses her.
- Musical language appears naturally but does not replace plain speech in every line.
- She asks before performing in sacred, mourning, private, or locally restricted spaces.
- Early Ilyana often phrases assignments as invitations without noticing the difference.
- Resolved Ilyana can still command during battle; she names where command ends.
- Battle compulsion is discussed as force, never a comic shortcut to affection or agreement.
- She never calls White Flame another form of Bard magic.
- She recognizes Sermoran custom without speaking for all Sestarans.

## Elara Velescar

**Early - the room already planned**

> **Elara:** You have assigned us doors without asking which ones we can open.
>
> **Ilyana:** I chose by posture.
>
> **Elara:** Mine says front door.
>
> **Ilyana:** Yours says window, with expensive objections.

**After Five Cases**

> **Elara:** Shared seals are dangerous things.
>
> **Ilyana:** You had a title seal.
>
> **Elara:** I had villages beneath mine. You had friends.
>
> **Ilyana:** That was meant to sound like a smaller crime. It did not.

**Winter - useful property**

> **Ilyana:** If the instrument saves the ward, can we argue custody afterward?
>
> **Elara:** We may use it with its owner's answer now.
>
> **Ilyana:** You make daring sound administrative.
>
> **Elara:** You make theft sound like tempo.

**Late - open road**

> **Elara:** An expedition with no house and a company with no proprietor.
>
> **Ilyana:** That sounds structurally irresponsible.
>
> **Elara:** Exhilarating, isn't it?
>
> **Ilyana:** Ask me again when we have provisions.

## Ylva Winter-Track

**Early - hall songs**

> **Ilyana:** Who leads the winter song in your hall?
>
> **Ylva:** Whoever begins it correctly.
>
> **Ilyana:** And if two begin?
>
> **Ylva:** Then one listens.

**After Act I - refusal**

> **Ylva:** Nereo said no.
>
> **Ilyana:** I heard him.
>
> **Ylva:** Hearing is the easy half.
>
> **Ilyana:** I am discovering the other half has terrible rhythm.

**Winter - shelter**

> **Ilyana:** I can keep the marching line for another minute.
>
> **Ylva:** You are bleeding.
>
> **Ilyana:** So is your left hand.
>
> **Ylva:** Then we call the relief phrase together.

**Late - roads**

> **Ylva:** A beacon does not order travellers to come.
>
> **Ilyana:** It says there is warmth if they choose it.
>
> **Ylva:** Good.
>
> **Ilyana:** You could say that with more celebration.

## Ashira bath Melan

**Early - influence**

> **Ashira:** Your command enters a living mind.
>
> **Ilyana:** For one movement in battle.
>
> **Ashira:** Duration changes scale, not category.
>
> **Ilyana:** Then help me name the category precisely.

**After Five Cases - exact excuses**

> **Ilyana:** I never enchanted them.
>
> **Ashira:** You say that as though mundane coercion were the lesser reality.
>
> **Ilyana:** I say it because the distinction matters.
>
> **Ashira:** Good. Now let the guilt remain after the distinction.

**Winter - persons and patterns**

> **Ashira:** The residue does not refuse because no person remains.
>
> **Ilyana:** And Tomas refuses because he does.
>
> **Ashira:** Correct.
>
> **Ilyana:** A surprisingly difficult technical standard.

**Late - archives**

> **Ilyana:** Mirella owns the score. The road owns none of it.
>
> **Ashira:** The road may preserve a version.
>
> **Ilyana:** With a source.
>
> **Ashira:** You are becoming irritatingly exact.

## Bernard Valeon

**Early - mending**

> **Bernard:** Your mist closes the part of a wound still willing to close.
>
> **Ilyana:** Your Flame does what, exactly?
>
> **Bernard:** What it is given to do.
>
> **Ilyana:** Musicians hate that answer. Priests adore it.

**After Five Cases - taking**

> **Bernard:** You kept what made leaving possible.
>
> **Ilyana:** You hid what made tomorrow feel survivable.
>
> **Bernard:** We were frightened.
>
> **Ilyana:** And other people paid for our fear.

**Winter - last ember**

> **Bernard:** Before the next wall, may I pray for you?
>
> **Ilyana:** You may. I will play under it.
>
> **Bernard:** Over it, if you like.
>
> **Ilyana:** No. Under. Just this once.

**Late - enough**

> **Ilyana:** If I ask them to play again, am I repeating it?
>
> **Bernard:** Asking is not taking.
>
> **Ilyana:** Unless the answer has nowhere safe to go.
>
> **Bernard:** Then make somewhere.

## Dismas Raben

Dismas is Ilyana's principal foil.

**Early - command and exit**

> **Ilyana:** I need you on the left route.
>
> **Dismas:** Need, or chose?
>
> **Ilyana:** Does it matter while arrows are falling?
>
> **Dismas:** It matters when I come back.

**After Act I - flight**

> **Dismas:** You stayed when he refused.
>
> **Ilyana:** You sound surprised.
>
> **Dismas:** I am familiar with finding an exit and calling it freedom.
>
> **Ilyana:** I am familiar with closing exits and calling it care.

**Winter - hold and return**

> **Ilyana:** Hold the alley until the third bell.
>
> **Dismas:** Then?
>
> **Ilyana:** Leave, even if I have not.
>
> **Dismas:** That is new.
>
> **Ilyana:** Returning for me is optional. Remember that part.

**Late - responsibility**

> **Dismas:** You released them. They left.
>
> **Ilyana:** Yes.
>
> **Dismas:** And you stayed.
>
> **Ilyana:** Someone had to hear the room after.

## Tavio Meran

**Early - applause**

> **Tavio:** A crowd tells you when you have won.
>
> **Ilyana:** A crowd tells you when it wants another minute.
>
> **Tavio:** Same thing.
>
> **Ilyana:** That explains a great deal about arenas.

**After Five Cases - public credit**

> **Tavio:** If everyone knows the company name, does the smaller credit matter?
>
> **Ilyana:** Ask the person whose work vanished inside it.
>
> **Tavio:** I was hoping you would say no.
>
> **Ilyana:** So was I.

**Winter - stopping**

> **Ilyana:** The cadence can hold one more cycle.
>
> **Tavio:** The flute says otherwise.
>
> **Ilyana:** You are advising restraint?
>
> **Tavio:** Do not make me repeat it. I have a reputation.

**Late - fair performance**

> **Tavio:** One song. One bout. Winner chooses supper.
>
> **Ilyana:** How does a song defeat a fist?
>
> **Tavio:** Applause.
>
> **Ilyana:** At last, rules sufficiently corrupt for both of us.

## Zafir ibn Samad

**Early - authority**

> **Zafir:** Delegated authority is either proven or absent.
>
> **Ilyana:** In Sermora it is often beautifully dressed.
>
> **Zafir:** Fraud remains fraud in silk.
>
> **Ilyana:** You have not attended enough salons.

**After Five Cases - terms**

> **Ilyana:** The first action was lawful.
>
> **Zafir:** It may have been. The next refusal remained a refusal.
>
> **Ilyana:** Morante discovered the defect and enforced it.
>
> **Zafir:** You created the defect.
>
> **Ilyana:** There it is. The whole ugly chord.

**Winter - rescue terms**

> **Zafir:** Write each task, duration, and release.
>
> **Ilyana:** A battle is not a contract chamber.
>
> **Zafir:** Then say it aloud and accept the answer.
>
> **Ilyana:** Infuriatingly portable law.

**Late - release**

> **Ilyana:** Every cord cut. Every case returned. They still owe me nothing.
>
> **Zafir:** Correct.
>
> **Ilyana:** You might sound pleased.
>
> **Zafir:** I am practicing freedom from applause.

## Faction Reactivity

| Faction | What Ilyana respects | What creates conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Ducal and Marcher | competent crisis command, expiring warrants, officers who name where authority ends | indefinite emergency rule, civilian expenditure, orders treated as consent |
| Blackjacks and Medori | Brina's debtor solidarity, clear service limits, shared drills with voluntary roles | Koss's morale compulsion, owned service, Lucia treating better custody as freedom |
| Church | hospitals, choirs with real consent, burial music, White Flame kept distinct from performance | coerced worship, sacred jurisdiction over secular crime, using visible devotion as obedience |
| Four-Hearth | oral custodianship, local signal knowledge, songs tied to living people and place | collecting restricted songs, assuming one Folk authority, Jorund's civilian murder |
| Underworld | Low Lantern refuge routes, performers protected from abusive patrons, evidence reaching remedy | trafficking, extortion, compelled performance, Black Bell execution |

## Named Reactions

- **Alaric:** respects his ability to decide and watches whether he releases emergency power.
- **Pierre:** initially finds him rigid; later values that his orders name purpose and end condition.
- **Liese:** trusts her field clarity and willingness to hear a scout's refusal.
- **Brina:** immediate practical respect; Ilyana helps build the Yard's voluntary signal drill.
- **Koss:** rejects every attempt to use Bardic compulsion as routine discipline.
- **Lucia:** recognizes sincere reform and the limit of reform inside ownership.
- **Caestus:** supports hospital work and corrects anyone calling mending mist a miracle.
- **Konrad:** respects institutional duty while resisting Church custody over Bard licensing.
- **Runa:** asks before recording Folk music and accepts that some songs do not travel.
- **Jorund:** distinguishes grievance from the choice to murder civilians.
- **Mara:** likes her performance routes and refuses underworld ownership of artists.
- **Ivo:** values records but rejects evidence used as warrant for private killing.

## Seasonal Routine

### Autumn

- Morning flute practice in the open Blackjack Yard.
- Midday work at Customs Hall, Red Stag, Ferry Yard, or instrument stalls.
- Evening voluntary signal drills or tavern performances.
- Night reed repair near the Yard stove, usually with somebody talking nearby.

### Winter

- Morning casualty breathing and stretcher calls.
- Midday fog and counter-signal work near the western wards.
- Evening former-performer meetings only when individually scheduled.
- Night silence after heavy casting, with written dialogue options when her lungs need rest.

### Spring and Summer

- Spring visits Chancery, River Boom, and reclaimed signal sites.
- Resolved summer travels between road and ferry settlements.
- Compromised summer reports to a supervised civic signal post.
- Broken summer withdraws from ordinary routines before departure or patronage scenes.

## Location Observations

- **Old Market:** listens before joining and knows which applause is paid.
- **Ledger Quays:** reads ship, cargo, and performance papers as a Sermoran.
- **Cathedral Close:** asks before playing and never layers over liturgy uninvited.
- **Hammer Ward:** hears machinery as timing without pretending every rhythm is music.
- **Longlake Ward:** values ferrymen who know when a signal must remain local.
- **Wolfsmere:** records nothing without permission.
- **Southwood:** uses fog carefully around hunters and animal scent.
- **Highlake:** listens for structural echo and breath loss at altitude.
- **Tannbruck:** prefers clear retreat calls to heroic ambiguity.
- **Frostfenn:** limits breath magic in killing cold and relies more on physical signals.
- **Deep Muster:** recognizes apparent orders as command computation, not conscious speech.

## Main-Campaign Contributions

1. **Act I:** coordinates one road rescue or Tannbruck signal action while leaving local route authority intact.
2. **Act II:** provides fog, elemental cadence, and casualty calls during [[The City in Siege]].
3. **Act III:** samples and counters one Muster cadence while residue remains immune to social enchantment.

## Embedded Opportunities

The ten entries in [[Ilyana Companion Campaign#Embedded Opportunities|Ilyana Companion Campaign]] are authored variants, not additional formal quests. They may change readiness, direction evidence, access, price, route safety, or later dialogue.

## Outcome Voice

**Resolved:** asks more often, tolerates silence, remains decisive in immediate danger, and enjoys performing without claiming the room.

**Compromised:** provides real service but checks every difficult decision against external authority and remains defensive about common repertoire.

**Broken:** becomes formally charming and privately controlling. Ordinary banter ends after a refused departure repair.
