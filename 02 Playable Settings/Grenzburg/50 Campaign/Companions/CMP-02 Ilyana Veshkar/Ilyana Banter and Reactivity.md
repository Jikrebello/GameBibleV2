# Ilyana Banter and Reactivity

## Voice Key

- Speaks through sense, rhythm, colour, and possibility.
- Invites people into experiments before checking who will close afterward.
- Changes tempo when frightened.
- Uses performance to avoid an answer she cannot control.
- Becomes physically still when genuinely listening.
- Treats repetition as death until she learns its protective uses.
- Never sounds like a contract lecturer merely because consent is central to her arc.

## Calibration Scenes

### Joy — A room altered

Ilyana ties three coloured scarves across the grey Blackjack dining room.

**Brina:** “Why?”

**Ilyana:** “Because the stew has surrendered.”

By supper, debtors are adding scraps of their own. Ilyana does not correct the arrangement.

### Competence — Casualty cadence

The corridor is full of smoke and conflicting shouts.

Ilyana plays four low notes. Bearers repeat the pattern against the walls; every fourth beat marks a clear lane.

**Ilyana:** “Do not follow me. Follow the answer when the second note turns.”

The system keeps working after she moves away.

### Embarrassment — No applause

Ilyana finishes an improvised song. Nobody reacts.

She looks at the ceiling.

**Ilyana:** “The rafters understood.”

Later she asks one listener, without joking, where the song lost them.

### Anger — A borrowed refrain claimed

A patron calls a dockworkers' refrain his commission.

Ilyana takes the stage, names all six workers, and plays the melody without the patron's ornamental ending.

**Ilyana:** “There. It survived improvement and still has a tune.”

### Care — A person afraid to sing

Ilyana does not ask the frightened witness to perform.

She taps a cup and lets the witness answer by moving one coin for yes and two for no.

**Ilyana:** “A voice is not required to sound like mine.”

### Shame — The forged approval

Someone praises her for keeping the Salt-Reed Company together.

**Ilyana:** “I held the name above the noise until it came back in one piece.”

She turns the flute case so the old signatures face down.

**Ilyana:** “The people had already answered.”

### Apology — A score returned

Ilyana hands over a clean copy with the composer's name first and her variations marked separately.

**Composer:** “You changed the ending.”

**Ilyana:** “Yes. Keep it, cut it, or make it embarrassing. I do not own the next performance.”

### Quiet — One unfinished phrase

At camp, Ilyana plays three notes and stops.

The player waits.

She starts again, stops at the same place, and puts the flute down.

No explanation follows. The unfinished phrase may return several scenes later.

## Company-Life Voice Samples

At the laundry line, Ilyana rearranges three faded scarves against the brick.

**Ilyana:** “There. Now it is not washing. It is a declaration with soap.”

When a flute key sticks during supper:

**Ilyana:** “Not a curse. Onion. A more democratic enemy.”

Brina reminds her that she volunteered to close the rehearsal room.

**Ilyana:** “Yes. Tonight the free spirit meets the broom and discovers continuity.”

During an ugly but effective marching cadence:

**Ilyana:** “Keep it ugly. Pretty slips on ice.”

## Foil Arc — Dismas

### Autumn

Ilyana turns an illegal route into a procession; Dismas is furious because spectacle risks the people using it. She points out that nobody will defend a route they experience only as shame.

Both are partly right and pleased by the argument.

### Winter

Dismas creates an escape and intends to leave. Ilyana turns the destination into a working shelter but forgets the fuel roster. They must return together for the boring part.

### Spring

They build something neither may remain to lead. Ilyana writes an opening with room for revision; Dismas returns on the appointed day without being pursued.

## Faction Reactivity

- Supports revocable emergency roles and resists becoming official propaganda.
- Values Brina's debtor culture and opposes ownership of specialist talent.
- Respects music, mourning, and sanctuary without imposed confession.
- Credits living Folk traditions and refuses to turn them into decorative source material.
- Admires Low Lantern invention while rejecting Black Bell spectacle and romantic danger.

## Temperament Use

- Grey players receive curiosity without assumed admiration or rebellion.
- Bohemian similarity must include envy, maintenance, and follow-through.
- Rebel chemistry cannot substitute intensity for commitment.
- Nerd contrast gives form without making the player Ilyana's permanent administrator.

## Failure Modes

Reject any Ilyana line that:

- sounds whimsical in every emotional mode;
- treats metaphor as a substitute for concrete desire;
- makes all practical people spiritually smaller;
- converts every consent problem into legal vocabulary;
- requires former troupe members to arrive merely to explain her.
