# Ilyana Field Familiarity and Relationships

This note controls Ilyana's approval, familiarity, commitment, departure, and relationship rewards. [[Ilyana Companion Campaign]] controls formal progression and autonomy.

## Approval

Approval is visible as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**.

Ilyana values:

- plans with a credible refusal route;
- decisive rescue and clear retreat signals;
- asking local experts before assuming command;
- naming artistic, technical, and cultural sources;
- accepting a no without punishment or sulking leverage;
- admitting when urgency became an excuse;
- using battle compulsion as accountable force rather than entertainment.

She resents:

- slavery and hostage service;
- treating debt as consent;
- using friendship to extract free labor;
- false credit, seized instruments, and erased authorship;
- commanders who flee consequences;
- refusing every difficult decision and calling it freedom;
- using her own wrong as permission to humiliate her indefinitely.

Idle time, repeated jobs, and recycled performances do not farm familiarity.

## Campaign Release

- [[Five Cases at Customs]] opens after one meaningful outing.
- Act finales require two of five preparation assets.
- [[The Free Refrain]] personally unlocks Bard and may open relationship commitment after an explicit player interest signal.
- Missing The Free Refrain moves the Bard unlock to the authored **Refrain After Snow** spring re-entry. It closes the time-specific relationship opportunity, not the vocation.
- Friendship remains capable of reaching the resolved outcome.

Only [[No Song by Command]], [[The Free Refrain]], and [[The Last Common Measure]] are fixed duo quests.

## Major Conflict

A warned conflict begins if the player:

- orders Ilyana to compel a non-hostile companion or former performer;
- conceals forged releases after learning what they are;
- returns an instrument to Morante as leverage over its performer;
- repeatedly treats a lawful first prize as consent to later service;
- demands reunion as the price of restitution;
- encourages Ilyana to keep the company name through destroyed claims.

Repair requires release, return, corrected credit, public or affected-party truth, protected testimony, or rejection of the coercive order. Refusing repair marks Ilyana Strained.

## Relationship Rhythm

Ilyana is heterosexual. A male player may pursue romance. A female player may pursue a sworn bond. Every player may complete [[A Tune with No Owner]].

Two familiarity scenes exist, but only the second can become an interest signal:

1. **The Empty Chair:** after Five Cases, Ilyana admits that she still counts five positions whenever she enters a room.
2. **Your Part, If You Want It:** after A Tune with No Owner, she offers the player a simple response line. The player may explicitly mark romantic interest, sworn-bond interest, friendship, or no private interest.

Before that authored selection there is no flirting, jealousy, sexual teasing, or romance-coded banter. Commitment requires Trusted approval, no active major conflict, personal completion of [[The Free Refrain]], the appropriate explicit signal, and an available global relationship slot.

## Romance

A male player may commit to romance. Ilyana asks for a relationship in which invitation remains real even after love makes the answer feel obvious.

Commitment grants **Private Refrain**. [[One Private Song]] upgrades it and records `ilyana_private_cadence_practiced`.

### Private Refrain

Ilyana concentrates both available Lead lines on the player for a short interval. Active support and weapon cadence follow through movement outside the ordinary field.

### Private Refrain: Held Note

After One Private Song, the move also preserves one current support effect through a brief stagger, separation, or forced reposition. It cannot ignore defeat or boss-scripted removal.

### One Listener

After a resolved or compromised [[The Last Common Measure]], romance grants **One Listener**, a permanent non-slot perk.

Once per major encounter, the first allied support effect received while the player is critically wounded clears one ordinary control effect and lingers briefly after leaving its area. It functions without Ilyana present and does not restore lasting Health.

## Sworn Bond

A female player may commit to a strictly platonic sworn bond. Ilyana and the player name each other as equal voices who may interrupt, refuse, and call the other back without claiming command.

Commitment grants **Call and Answer**. [[Duet Without a Conductor]] upgrades it and records `ilyana_equal_cadence_practiced`.

### Call and Answer

When the player successfully interrupts or breaks a hostile action, Ilyana refreshes the active refrain and prepares a countervoice against the next valid spell, roar, or signal aimed at either partner.

### Call and Answer: Equal Voices

After Duet Without a Conductor, the response also grants nearby allies brief Staying Power. It cannot suppress an uncounterable boss phase.

### Equal Voices

After a resolved or compromised The Last Common Measure, bond grants **Equal Voices**, a permanent non-slot perk.

Once per major encounter, the first successful interrupt while the player has allied support reveals the target's relevant weakness and grants nearby allies brief Staying Power. It functions without Ilyana present.

## Equivalence and Persistence

Romance and bond each provide:

- one companion duo move;
- one episode upgrade;
- one Act III safeguard;
- one permanent non-slot perk;
- distinct summer dialogue.

The safeguards cannot stack. Neither relationship decides Ilyana's direction.

Permanent perks remain learned after a warned departure or player-chosen sacrifice. Duo moves require Ilyana in the party. Romance and bond do not prevent disagreement, cure entitlement, or create automatic consent.

## Outcome and Destination

- **Resolved:** open-road performer, with relationship-specific summer invitations that remain optional.
- **Compromised:** bounded civic signal-Bard whose stop rules and shared credit remain externally documented; the relationship acknowledges unfinished work.
- **Broken:** departure, Morante patronage, or isolated control of disputed repertoire.

No destination becomes future-setting canon.
