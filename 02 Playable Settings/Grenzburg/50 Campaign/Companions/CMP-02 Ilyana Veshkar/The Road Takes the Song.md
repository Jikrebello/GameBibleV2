# The Road Takes the Song

## Quest Function

This summer coda demonstrates Ilyana's settled conduct. It does not reopen her moral judgement or automatically reunite the Salt-Reed Company.

## Resolved Route

Ilyana prepares to travel from Grenzburg through Three Mills and the Longlake ferry circuit as an open-road Bard.

At each stop she:

- asks which songs may travel;
- records the source of a new verse;
- agrees payment before performance;
- offers support without claiming local signal authority;
- accepts that no former Salt-Reed member is required to join.

The player may accompany the first leg. A romance adds a private invitation without making the player's presence compulsory. A sworn bond adds an equal route-call and promised answer. Friendship receives a complete farewell-and-return scene.

## Compromised Route

Ilyana serves as a civic signal-Bard under a witnessed Grenzburg charter. The player inspects one summer drill where:

- roles are voluntary;
- stores and instruments are individually recorded;
- Ilyana still looks to the supervisor before trusting her own restraint.

The scene recognizes real service and unfinished independence.

## Broken or Departed Route

The coda becomes a changed encounter:

- a formal departure under Morante patronage;
- an isolated performance using disputed repertoire;
- or an empty Yard space with letters from the former performers.

The player cannot restore the missed Act III settlement in summer.

## Salt-Reed States

- Former members may attend as individuals.
- One or more may perform a new piece under fresh terms.
- They may exchange civil letters without friendship.
- They may refuse all contact.
- The old company does not re-form merely because Ilyana resolved.

## End

Ilyana learns a short road verse from summer workers. She asks who wrote it before adding a line.

The player can answer the final cadence, listen, or let the road carry it beyond hearing.

## Reward

**Road Refrain:** a postgame travel preparation granting a modest first-encounter Stamina and recoverable-health support after deliberate campsite or settlement performance. It does not function as passive infinite healing.
