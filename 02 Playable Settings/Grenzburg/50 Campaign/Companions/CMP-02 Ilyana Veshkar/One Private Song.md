# One Private Song

## Quest Function

This is Ilyana's male-player romance episode. It is not a rescue, performance job, or altered version of her sworn bond.

## Beginning

Ilyana asks the player to take a small ferry beyond the last occupied Longlake landing. She has written something that:

> has no crowd to win, no officer to signal, and no one to impress except the person invited.

The player may steer, sit at the bow, or let Ilyana handle the boat. There is no attack, storm, stranded traveller, or hidden task.

## Middle

At Quiet Reed Landing, Ilyana asks what the player hears when no group needs directing. Dialogue can address:

- the player's silence and chosen forms of expression;
- whether Ilyana fears a relationship she cannot manage like a company;
- how invitation remains meaningful after commitment;
- which parts of their separate lives remain separate.

She plays one private composition. The performance has no public version, purchasable score, or mechanical quality check.

The player can listen, move closer, ask for the final line again, or end the romantic path without humiliation. Ending romance preserves friendship.

## End

On a continuing romance, the pair return to the sheltered ferry cabin. Physical intimacy is explicit in intention and fades to black.

At dawn Ilyana teaches the player the Held Note: a way to keep one support line alive across distance without treating closeness as control.

## Reward

**Private Refrain: Held Note** preserves one active support effect through a brief stagger, separation, or forced reposition.

`ilyana_private_cadence_practiced` supplies one Act III safeguard. Missing this episode does not end the romance but forfeits its upgrade and safeguard.
