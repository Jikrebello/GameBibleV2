# Names Behind the Wall

## Role in the Arc

This is Ilyana Veshkar's quest 3 of four. It is playable in Deep Winter and Early Spring. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Ilyana Veshkar duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Colonel Varko Milen arrives with winter artillery aid and demands Ilyana's return for desertion. A damaged fire-control plate in the Old River Arsenal contains the missing platoon names copied by a dead clerk.

Recover the names and expose the false report without losing artillery Grenzburg needs for the siege aftermath.

## Player Actions

Escort Milen's inspection, search the arsenal's sealed plotting room, stabilize war-script residue, and bring testimony before the Hall of Petitions.

## Opposition and Pressure

Milen is not an occult villain; he is a decorated commander who still believes the deaths were acceptable and the lie preserved morale. He threatens to withdraw technical crews if publicly humiliated.

## Information Paths

The plate records the platoon, an arsenal clerk kept correspondence, and Ilyana can reproduce the precise overdraw signature. Two sources prove death; all three prove concealment.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Accept a private correction with aid retained, force full public truth and improvise replacement crews, or let Ilyana take sole blame. Relationship commitment occurs after she chooses whether names matter more than her image.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the plate shatters, rubbings and script echoes retain partial proof. If Milen leaves early, local Warlock Hall apprentices can operate fewer guns at greater material cost.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.
