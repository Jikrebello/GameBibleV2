# The Free Refrain

## Quest Function

Ilyana's Act II duo finale defends a winter casualty corridor with a temporary civic ensemble. Personal completion unlocks [[Bard]]. The external objective stands on its own: patients, residents, and relief teams need a route from the damaged western wards to Mercy Hospital.

No former Salt-Reed member appears. Their authenticated winter instruction forbids Ilyana from using the jointly owned **Wake Refrain**. The refusal is fixed and cannot be negotiated by the player.

## Hook

After [[The City in Siege]], Grobi horn teams prepare a second push toward Debtors' Row. Fire, smoke, broken pumps, and blocked streets isolate West Refuge Hall from Tann Gate's casualty lane.

Ilyana proposes a moving corridor built from new material:

- fog against missiles and smoke;
- a plain walking cadence for stretcher teams;
- elemental support for defenders;
- a counterphrase against Grobi horns;
- fixed retreat calls between the Yard and Mercy Hospital.

The performers are current workers with other jobs and reasons to leave when their agreed task ends.

## Civic Ensemble

- **Tessa Rill:** holds one bounded fog shift if fit and willing.
- **Oren Fask:** carries the horn counterphrase from a protected post.
- **Sella Morn:** controls the stretcher pace and may stop music that interferes with triage.
- **Nella Crane:** a Blackjack runner who maintains ordinary rope and bell signals if magic fails.

Substitutes come from Yard bell teams, Church stretcher callers, Warlock counter-tuners, and Longlake ferrymen. The quest never requires all four named people.

## Preparation

| Asset | Concrete advantage |
|---|---|
| Winter signal code | keeps movement working after the ensemble splits |
| Casualty lane mapped | opens a protected route through Debtors' Row |
| Counter-tuning prepared | suppresses one Grobi horn phase |
| Local roles recorded | names each task, stop signal, and replacement |
| Refuge acoustics known | provides a fallback position inside West Refuge Hall |

Two assets permit the plan. Three make patients, crews, refuge, and counterattack achievable. Four or five protect one objective from one logistical failure.

## Beginning: A Job with Edges

At Blackjack Yard, Ilyana rehearses each role separately. Every participant may accept, alter, or refuse a task. The player can solve staffing gaps through practical contacts, but no social check secures an entire group.

The work is specific:

- Tessa will play until the red cloth is raised or her breathing fails;
- Oren will not leave the tower platform;
- Sella can cancel any cadence that disrupts casualty care;
- Nella controls the withdrawal bell regardless of Ilyana's preference.

Ilyana is visibly irritated by the fragmented rehearsal. She remains civil and does the work.

### Pause Point: Local Roles Recorded

The quest may pause after roles and fallback signals are set. Resuming forms the player-Ilyana duo; participating workers remain temporary NPCs.

## Middle: The Moving Corridor

The duo crosses three crisis spaces:

1. **Debtors' Row:** rescue residents behind smoke and collapsed awnings.
2. **West Refuge Hall:** stabilize patients and relocate the performance position.
3. **Tann Gate Casualty Lane:** counter the Grobi horn push and reopen movement to Mercy Hospital.

Threats include Grobi shield and horn teams, burning structures, blocked stretchers, frightened crowds, and attempts to isolate Ilyana. Ordinary bells, ropes, and runners remain usable if harmonic support fails.

### Pause Point: West Refuge Hall Secured

The quest may pause after the refuge is stable and the final route is selected.

## The Split Measure

A tower collapse cuts line of sight between every post. Ilyana cannot hear whether two workers have stopped. Her first instinct is to pull everyone back onto one phrase.

The actual solution is operational:

- trust each stop condition and continue with the remaining parts;
- send Nella's ordinary signal rather than a magical command;
- take over one vacant line personally;
- simplify the refrain so independent posts can finish it;
- or impose emergency orders and accept the human cost.

On the strongest path, the corridor sounds rougher after the split and works better. The player learns the second line through action, establishing the Bard unlock without a rhythm minigame.

## End: Shift Change

The corridor holds long enough for the last casualty sled. There is no grand company reunion. Sella closes her register, Oren asks who relieves him, and Tessa leaves if her shift is finished.

Bellandi separately confirms that the Salt-Reed refusal remains in force. Ilyana may acknowledge the local co-authors, claim the new refrain as hers, or leave its custody with the crews that use it. This affects her direction but does not delay patient care.

## Relationship Availability

The quest does not produce romance from general approval. If the player previously chose an explicit authored interest signal and Ilyana is Trusted, a private follow-up can establish:

- male-player romance commitment, opening [[One Private Song]];
- female-player sworn bond commitment, opening [[Duet Without a Conductor]].

Without that signal, the follow-up is professional or friendly and no relationship prompt appears.

## Fail-Forward

- Refusing or exhausted workers are replaced by less efficient ordinary signals.
- A collapsed corridor moves patients through the underways with greater injury and district cost.
- If Ilyana is silenced, the route continues through bells, ropes, and player action.
- Party defeat lets Blackjack and Church teams recover survivors after Grobi retake the lane.
- Missing the deadline opens changed spring re-entry. Bard is delayed, not lost solely through inattention.

## Outputs and Rewards

- personal and account-wide [[Bard]] unlock on personal completion or its credible changed re-entry;
- second Lead and two compatible refrains for Ilyana;
- a persistent casualty-corridor and civic-ensemble state;
- relationship episodes only after the explicit interest gate.
