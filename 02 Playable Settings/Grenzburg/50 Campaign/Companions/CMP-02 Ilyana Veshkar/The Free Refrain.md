# The Free Refrain

## Quest Function

Ilyana's Act II finale is a winter casualty defence in which the old Salt-Reed ensemble may help only through individual, specific, revocable consent.

Personal completion unlocks [[Bard]]. The lesson is demonstrated in play: a strong company is not one voice controlling the others.

## Hook

After [[The City in Siege]], the repaired western defence remains unstable. Grobi horn teams prepare a second push toward Debtors' Row while fire, smoke, and blocked streets isolate West Refuge Hall from Tann Gate's casualty lane.

Ilyana proposes a moving harmonic corridor:

- fog against missiles and smoke;
- mending lines for walking wounded;
- elemental cadence for defenders;
- counterphrase against Grobi horns;
- retreat signals between the Yard and Mercy Hospital.

All four former Salt-Reeds are present. None has agreed to perform.

## Preparation

| Asset | Concrete advantage |
|---|---|
| Instruments returned | each performer can choose a full technical role |
| Casualty lane mapped | opens a protected movement route |
| Counter-tuning prepared | suppresses one horn phase without overstrain |
| Performer pledges recorded | makes every accepted task and stopping condition explicit |
| Refuge acoustics known | supplies a second performance position and safe fallback |

Two assets permit the plan. Three make casualties, performers, refuge, and counterattack achievable. Four or five protect one objective from one logistical failure.

## Beginning: Ask Each Voice

At Blackjack Yard, Ilyana must speak to each former member separately:

- Mirella may maintain the civilian breath count.
- Nereo may carry the retreat pulse.
- Celia may provide mending support.
- Tomas may counter the Grobi horns.

Each may:

- accept the exact task;
- accept with a stopping condition;
- offer a different task;
- refuse.

The player cannot pass one social check to secure the whole company. Morante, Luca, or another claim custodian may invoke the disputed engagement. That can settle physical custody, never personal service.

If a performer refuses, the plan substitutes:

- Yard bell teams;
- Church stretcher calls;
- Warlock counter-tuning;
- smoke ropes and ordinary guides;
- player and Ilyana taking a harder route.

### Pause Point: Individual Roles Recorded

The quest may pause after roles and refusals are named. Resuming forms the player-Ilyana duo. Participating performers and carers are temporary NPCs.

## Middle: The Moving Corridor

The duo crosses three crisis spaces:

1. **Debtors' Row:** rescue residents behind smoke and collapsed awnings.
2. **West Refuge Hall:** stabilize patients and choose the corridor's next position.
3. **Tann Gate Casualty Lane:** counter the Grobi horn push and reopen movement to Mercy Hospital.

Threats include:

- Grobi shield and horn teams;
- burning structures;
- panicked crowds;
- blocked stretchers;
- enemy attempts to isolate Ilyana;
- accumulating breath and instrument strain.

No performer is invulnerable. The player can reposition temporary NPCs, release them from their task, or continue without them.

### Pause Point: West Refuge Hall Secured

The quest may pause after the refuge is stable and the final corridor selected.

## The Free Refrain

At the decisive breach, Ilyana's first arrangement fails when a tower collapse splits the ensemble. Her old instinct is to command everyone back into position.

The player can:

- direct Ilyana to ask for new consent;
- help her build a plan around refusals;
- support emergency authority over property only;
- encourage her to order service;
- or let her choose according to direction.

On a strong path, Ilyana plays the field refrain while each willing performer chooses when and how to answer. The player learns the second line through action, establishing the Bard unlock without requiring a rhythm minigame.

## End: Claim and Release

After the corridor holds, Morante or the current claim representative attempts to preserve the engagement because it proved useful.

Possible outcomes:

- release every personal service claim;
- separate genuine property claims for later hearing;
- expose and arrest Morante;
- kill Morante during justified resistance;
- let her escape;
- accept a temporary company charter;
- conceal the invalid consent.

Morante's state never blocks later chapters.

## Relationship Commitment

Trusted players with no major conflict may choose:

- male-player romance commitment, opening [[One Private Song]];
- female-player sworn bond, opening [[Duet Without a Conductor]];
- continued friendship;
- refusal.

The two relationship episodes are separate and mutually exclusive.

## Fail-Forward

- Refusing performers do not make the quest fail; substitutes increase route danger.
- Lost instrument: that performer may still guide, carry, or call ordinary signals.
- Collapsed corridor: patients move through the underways with greater injury and district cost.
- Ilyana silenced or exhausted: ordinary bells, ropes, and player action continue the rescue.
- Party defeat: Blackjack and Church teams recover survivors after the Grobi take the lane.
- Missed deadline: autonomous resolution closes Bard and relationship unlocks for the playthrough.

## Outputs and Rewards

- personal and account-wide [[Bard]] unlock;
- second Lead and two compatible refrains for Ilyana;
- romance duo move **Private Refrain** or bond duo move **Call and Answer** after commitment;
- individual performer and Morante states;
- Act II direction and changed re-entry.
