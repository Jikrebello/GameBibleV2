# Companion Constitution

This note controls Grenzburg's roster, field availability, approval, personal campaigns, relationships, and ending treatment. [[Companion Grounded Writing Standard]] is the primary social and tonal authority. [[Companion Campaign Architecture]], [[Companion Autonomy and Act Convergence]], and [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]] control campaign execution.

## Colleague Contract

- Recruitment grants field availability, not instant friendship.
- Dismissal carries no approval loss.
- Every companion has a home base, current assignment, and relationships outside the player.
- Personal campaigns are substantial opt-in stories rather than mandatory payment for usable party members.
- A player may dislike or ignore any companion without losing the critical campaign.
- The strongest personal outcome depends on conduct inside the relevant campaign, never romance or player temperament.

## Archetype Boundary

Archetypes are private authoring tools. They identify habitual desires and failure pressures but do not dictate every line, quest, pairing, or choice. [[Companion Archetype Constitution]] may diagnose a scene; it may not force a chemistry variant where the character would be more credible without one.

At least ninety percent of ambient material concerns present work, the world, ordinary appetite, relationships, tactics, faith, craft, and current events rather than the original wrong.

## Recruitment and Party

The ordinary party is the player plus three companions. Party changes occur at the Blackjack Yard, hubs, camps, and stabilized travel nodes. Companions not selected continue their assignments and may be encountered in the world.

No recruitment conversation delivers a complete biography. Private history emerges through credible need, shared work, records, and chosen disclosure.

## Personal Campaigns

Each companion retains eleven authored pieces and ten maximum playable pieces because romance and sworn bond are mutually exclusive. The eighty-eight-piece budget is an authoring ledger, not a player-facing pattern.

- Introductory cases and act operations use the ordinary party.
- Three act finales use the player and owner as the only full party members.
- Friendship is available to every player.
- Romance and sworn bond are separate optional episodes.
- Summer codas show the result of engaged campaigns or stable-colleague continuity.
- Preparation creates methods and safeguards, not approval currency.

## Approval

Approval remains qualitative: Guarded, Open, Trusted, or Strained.

- Approval follows witnessed conduct and credible reports.
- Repeated jobs, idling, equipment gifts, and chatter settings cannot farm approval.
- Ordinary disagreement does not cause departure.
- A serious violation produces one specific confrontation and a credible repair opportunity where repair remains possible.
- Permanent departure requires a known moral boundary, explicit warning, and authored consequence.

## Relationships

- The player may commit to one heterosexual romance and one same-sex sworn bond per playthrough.
- A male player may romance women; a female player may romance men.
- Same-sex paths are explicitly platonic sworn friendships.
- Romantic presentation remains dormant until the player makes an explicit interest choice.
- Early interest may overlap; commitment names what closes.
- Romance and bond rewards remain distinct, equivalent, campaign-specific, and non-slot.
- Mature romance fades to black.
- Friendship alone reaches every strongest moral and vocation outcome.

## Professional Conflict

Foils exist to reveal different methods and convictions, not to maintain camp hostility. Companions may argue, refuse one another's method, or lose trust. They normally remain capable colleagues. Seasonal pair scenes occur only when a concrete job, person, or decision gives the disagreement a reason to exist.

## Past Claims and the Lio Exception

[[Companion Debt and Remote Claims Standard]] keeps former casts outside Grenzburg by default. Past people retain agency through authenticated correspondence, testimony, property, and settlement terms rather than arriving to forgive, condemn, or complete a companion.

**The Lio Clause** is the sole active exception. [[Lio Varcen]] may enter the base-game worldspace under his own power and for his own purposes, with independent lodging, medical care, protection, a second, a clerk, and a credible exit route. He may refuse the bout, meeting, apology, or reconciliation without becoming a failure state. His presence is valid only while the decisive action remains his and Tavio's separate choices rather than a scene built to award [[Tavio Meran]] redemption.

No other companion campaign inherits this exception without a later explicit canon amendment.

## Outcomes and Absence

Engaged campaigns may resolve, compromise, break, depart, or permit a fully warned sacrifice according to their own authority. Untracked campaigns follow [[Companion Autonomy and Act Convergence]] and do not enter those moral outcome tiers merely because the player was absent.

No political, relationship, legal, or personal outcome becomes future-setting canon.

## Metaphysical Guardrails

- Medium imprints are never souls.
- Binder practice cannot legitimize ownership or coercion of persons.
- White Flame is neither a battery nor proof of innocence.
- Bard and Lancewright practice obey bounded Shellcraft.
- Human accountability is not replaced by patrons, destiny, corruption, or metaphysical compulsion.
