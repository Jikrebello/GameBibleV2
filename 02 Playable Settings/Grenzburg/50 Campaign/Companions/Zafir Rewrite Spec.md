# Zafir Rewrite Spec

Execution spec for actioning [[Companion Critical Review - Ylva and Zafir]], Zafir scope. Companion to the six prior specs — shared prose rules and verification inherited. **This is the roster's lightest spec.** His campaign is the reference implementation — theme as mechanics ([[The Copyist's Answer]]'s severance constraint), the mechanically real temptation (the Master Ring), and the model local countervoice (Karel) — so this pass adds texture and faces and protects nearly everything. His `cleared` status is broadly earned; touched files flip to a working status during the pass and re-clear at Phase 5.

## Locked Decisions

1. **The grateful freed debtor is built** — his local face, inverted valence: she served a full term under an instrument bearing his authorship seal, never met him, and is *grateful*. She seeks him out; she means it; her state never moves; the resolved outcome does not convert her to anger.
2. **Secondary archetype: Goofball** — Zafir becomes Nerd-primary / Goofball-secondary ("Eccentric Genius"): expertise through play and absurdity; conflict, precision versus spontaneity; shadow, irresponsible cleverness. The Bernard blend-inversion is registered: Goofball-with-hidden-weight and Nerd-with-hidden-play, each the other reversed.
3. **The public error is staged with a named cost.** One authored Act II scene: a diligent model, publicly posted and confidently asserted, is wrong anyway — and a named worker is hurt because they trusted his certainty. The injury heals; the trust doesn't fully.
4. **Karel's want: to beat Zafir once** — fairly, on record — and retire the trophy.

**Campaign shape statement** (added to the campaign doc's Dramatic Promise): **the keys multiply.** Act I: one master lever against nine personal releases. (The deliberate sibling of Bernard's shrinking object and Dismas's open door.)

# Part 1 — New Canon Elements

## 1.1 Yusra — the grateful freed debtor

**File:** new character note. **Role:** the fourth local face in the roster's pattern, valence inverted — where Sandro refuses, Lenka won't eat, and Mavra asks for nothing, **Yusra approaches.**

- Working name **Yusra** (surname at execution, registry-checked; Iqashari/Ahl-e-Ahd flavor per Samira bint Nahal's pattern). Thirties-forties. Served a seven-year term under a standardized fixed-term instrument bearing Zafir's authorship seal — his clauses, though they never met; she knows his notation the way you know the handwriting of weather. Freed at term-end on the exact day the clause named; emigrated; works in Grenzburg as a scribe — **because the instrument's maintenance duties taught her letters.** The cage taught her the trade she lives on. Her file states this without comment.
- **The approach:** she recognizes his seal-work at the Yard or Chain House and seeks him out to thank him. The buckles didn't cut. The petitions were answered — she filed two and both were ruled on within the clause's deadline. The food schedule held through a drought year. The term ended on the day it said. *She means all of it,* and her gratitude is the cage-world testifying that his work made it livable — "reduced harm mattered to those forced to live inside the system," given a face who agrees, which is worse than hatred.
- **The detail that lands it:** she still keeps her release day as a festival — celebrates it annually, like a birthday. Her freedom's anniversary is his clause's execution date. One beat, once, uncommented.
- **Behavior rules:** never a quest; never blocks content; not a lesson — the resolved outcome does **not** radicalize her, and no player option "awakens" her (the seminar version is the failure mode; her file forbids it). She returns two or three times across the acts (once bringing him a copied text as a gift — a gift he cannot bear and must accept, the Bernard-adjacent beat done in his register). `yusra_state` does not move.
- Margin note in her file, per the review: the Never Let Me Go comparison — she is a Hailsham alumna; the kindness of the institution is the institution's best defense, standing in his doorway, smiling.

## 1.2 Samira's edges — no letter

Locked by the review and held: **do not write her a letter.** Her refusal-to-be-present is the characterization. Two textures only:

- **The transcript.** She testified at the Iqashari judgement — public record he has read a hundred times. Quote three lines in [[The Inverted Surety]], surfaced during [[The Copyist's Answer]]'s staging: her diction under oath — dry, exact, entirely about the instruments, never about him. Register (final wording at scene pass): *"The term limits functioned. I was fed to schedule. The schedule was not mine."* Personhood through the record she could not prevent existing.
- **The caught-annotating beat.** One scene, between Acts I and II, after the execution-end is secured: the player finds Zafir at night with the inversion notation copied out, annotating it — professional admiration in the margins, *studying her without leave*, the old act in miniature. His shame-tell fires (qualifications accumulating until the original sentence disappears), and then he stops, closes the book, and surrenders the copy to the evidence custody where it belongs. One beat; no repair quest; direction notes it. This is his caught-studying equivalent, and its budget is one.

## 1.3 The Posted Load — the public error

New authored scene (quest-adjacent or embedded, Act II window, siege context — staging at execution; candidates: Chain House works or the Yard's winter load board):

- Zafir posts a load model — checked, diligent, confidently asserted, publicly relied on. **The model is wrong anyway.** No arrogance-in-the-moment to blame, no skipped step to find afterward: the diligence was real and insufficient, which is the humbler horror and the design guard against a neat moral.
- A named worker (registry-checked; one line of prior presence so the name lands) is hurt following the posted instruction — an injury that heals across the campaign while the trust doesn't fully. Bounded: no death, no permanent maiming; the Bernard-scar register at smaller scale, *visible* scale.
- **The recovery is the scene:** not technical correction — he must stand in the room as the man who was wrong, and the quest architecture must deny him every repair that converts to acquittal (he may fix the model; fixing the model fixes nothing). The worker's response is theirs and is not gratitude, not fury, and not seminar — working register: they check his next three postings against their own count, visibly, and he has to let them.
- One aftermath beat later in the campaign: the worker uses one of his postings again, without comment. That is the entire arc of the repair, and nobody names it.

## 1.4 Karel's want

Added to [[Karel Reed-Hand]] and threaded: **he wants to beat Zafir at exactly one thing — fairly, on record — and retire the trophy.**

- Seeded in [[A Bird for No Door]] (he proposes the contest category during the latch course, or claims one station as "his event"); recurring as a running thread — attempts, near-misses, rule disputes conducted with total seriousness by both parties; Zafir keeps a small written record of the standings *because Karel demanded one*.
- The day he wins (placement: Act III window or the coda — executor's choice, coda recommended): Zafir's genuine, documented delight at being beaten well — his bible's "lights up when corrected by someone who has done the work" made ceremonial. Karel retires the trophy (the object at execution — something small and absurd, in the gold-turnip register) and never competes in that category again, which Zafir finds incomprehensible and correct.
- Design guard: the want stays non-curricular — no beat converts it into a lesson about pedagogy or consent; it is an appetite, and its whole function is that Karel has one.

## 1.5 The co-drafting scene — the paternalism twins

The cross-campaign scene the review found sitting in an autonomy table ("Zafir may formalize shared liability" for Ylva's Act III): built as one authored ensemble scene, owned by this spec, referenced by Ylva's when it executes ⟨PAIR-ADJACENT⟩.

- **Staging:** the Yard or route board, spring; Zafir and Ylva drafting the shared-liability instrument for her route teams.
- **The engine:** he produces three drafts of exit clauses; she strikes every clause that speaks *for* the signers; each catches the other doing the old thing — he over-providing (protections nobody asked for, the humane-instrument reflex), she pre-deciding what signers can bear (the sole-signature reflex). Two reformed paternalists, each fluent in the other's disease.
- Neither wins the exchange (the Label Board rule); the instrument that results is shorter than either draft; the player may witness, contribute one clause, or hold the pen.
- Registered in the Reactive Scene Register; summarized in both banter files' companion-objects (his Ylva line — "field evidence defeating a beautiful model" — gains its scene; her Zafir line likewise).
