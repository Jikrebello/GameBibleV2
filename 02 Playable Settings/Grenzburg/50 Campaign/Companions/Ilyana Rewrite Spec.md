# Ilyana Rewrite Spec

Execution spec for actioning [[Companion Critical Review - Tavio and Ilyana]], Ilyana scope. Companion document to the Elara, Bernard, and Tavio specs — shared prose rules and verification inherited; differences only are restated. This is the Ilyana half of the pending Ilyana-Dismas paired pass; pair-cohesion items are marked ⟨PAIR⟩, and one cross-campaign coordination point with Elara's spec is marked ⟨CROSS⟩.

## Locked Decisions

1. **Full remote, postal score.** All four Salt-Reed arrivals are cut — Celia's rescue, Nereo at Eel Bridge, the Mirella/Tomas shipwreck, the assembled winter roster. Each ex-member answers Ilyana's request for one final line by letter, or refuses. [[The Last Common Measure]] is performed by Ilyana and local volunteers with authored silences where refused lines would be. Her bio's claim ("None physically enters the base-game worldspace") becomes true.
2. **Secondary archetype: Cheerleader** — Ilyana becomes Bohemian-primary / Cheerleader-secondary ("Creative Connector"). Creed material: the distortion *"If everyone is having an experience through me, I cannot be abandoned"* and the shadow line *"Friends become cast members."* Differentiation from Elara stays bright: Elara manages the existing room; Ilyana changes its weather.
3. **The player-instrumentalization thread: full.** Three to four authored beats across the acts where her shadow fires at the player — charming-scale, never quest-blocking, with the player's own refusal loop as the mechanic.
4. **The guaranteed silence.** No preparation state, direction, or approval total produces a finale with zero refused lines. Minimum one (Nereo default; states may shift who). Do-not-regress flag. Restitution cannot purchase reunion — mechanically enforced.

**Campaign shape statement** (replaces the "tests four truths" template block): **two companies in opposite motion.** A new ensemble assembles around her in Grenzburg, beat by beat, while the old one dissolves by post, letter by letter — and the campaign's question is whether she can refuse to own the first while releasing the second. They converge on one stage at the finale: locals performing, absentees present as lines and silences. Her resolved ending holds both: a company she teaches but does not found, and a piece that contains four people she will never see again.

# Part 1 — New Canon Elements

## 1.1 Immi — the local mirror-musician

**File:** new character note. **Role:** the next Salt-Reed — the person Ilyana begins absorbing *in play*, whose refusal is beat 2, and whose final ask is the second-company temptation made flesh.

- Working name **Immi** (surname at execution after the name-registry check; nothing near Ivo Renn, Mika, Nika, Heda×2, Marta×2). Seventeen to nineteen, Grenzburg-born, Bridgehold family — **a bell-ringer**: her instrument is the city's signal infrastructure, the warning couplets and refuge bells the campaign already collects in [[A Tune with No Owner]]. An instrument tied to place, duty, and civic trust — everything Ilyana's road-freedom is not. That contrast is the design: she is not a Mirella stand-in (Mirella was the peer-professional; Immi is raw, local, and *rooted*).
- **Her want: the road.** She dreams of a portable instrument and a leaving. Ilyana is her proof it exists. This is what makes the absorption effortless — the kid volunteers for it.
- **The absorption arc (the Salt-Reed machine, restarted on camera):**
  1. *Seeded* — A Tune with No Owner: she is the Bridgehold sentry's kid who knows the warning couplet; one exchange, pure charm.
  2. *The object* — The Second Flute (new A1-O): Ilyana lends her spare flute for one performance. It is never returned and never asked for. "Borrowed" quietly becomes "hers" — the absorption object, planted exactly like Perle's ribbon and the missing queen.
  3. *The language* — Acts I–II ambient: "we" arrives before agreement; Ilyana introduces her as "my second"; roles are assigned before they are asked for. The player watches the Gilded Wake grammar reassemble in miniature.
  4. *The refusal* — No Song by Command retrofit (2.3): freed from the bandits, Immi refuses to ring under Ilyana's command, for a reason the player has witnessed and Ilyana starts rewriting as fear. Beat 2, finally local and live.
  5. *The ask* — Act III: Immi, now trained, asks Ilyana to found the new company and lead it. The resolved requirement ("does not found another company during the game") finally has its living temptation: the kids *want* the cage; it would be so easy, and so kind-looking, to build it.
- **Voice key:** quick, literal, city-cadenced; asks practical questions about the road that expose its romance ("Where do you wash?"); never seminar. Her refusal reason must be concrete and hers — family bell-duty, her own judgment about a signal, or a promise she made — *not* a boundary thesis.
- `immi_state` persists to summer: road-apprentice, Grenzburg bell-warden, ensemble leader, or estranged — per Their Version and finale outcomes.

## 1.2 The four letters and the margins — the troupe's remote apparatus

The Petra standard, times four, but *short* — they are musicians, not correspondents. Each answer to Ilyana's final-line request (the request itself is an authored Act III beat: four letters she drafts, with explicit no-obligation terms, sent through Bellandi with the release packets):

- **Mirella** (~80 words): precise, professional, composer's diction — and one warm sentence visibly crossed out but legible. The manuscript texture *is* the emotion; she almost said something kind and decided Ilyana had not earned reading it easily.
- **Celia** (~50 words): brief, practical; sends her line *with repair instructions for Ilyana's flute* — care expressed through maintenance, refusal of everything else.
- **Tomas** (no words): his answer is cadence plates — pure notation, no letter at all. The best beat of the four: he will share music and nothing else.
- **Nereo** (one sentence): the default guaranteed silence. His refusal, in his own hand, must not be seminar — one flat line, e.g. register: *"Not this one."* Exact wording at scene pass.
- **Mirella's margins** (separate artifact, seeded from Act I): the scores in the customs cases carry her marginalia — alternating hands on shared staves, crossed-out bars, one dry annotation per act surfacing as the player encounters the papers. This is where the band becomes lovable before mournable (1.4) and where Mirella exists as a person across the whole campaign rather than at its end.

Also required: **purge the arrival framing from the four personal-history files** (Celia, Nereo, Mirella, Tomas — read first; their notes currently support physical presence in Grenzburg) and from Ilyana's asset tables (`ilyana_instruments_returned` and `salt_reed_roster_in_grenzburg` die; replacements in 2.4).

## 1.3 Morante's letter — the dark mirror voiced

One letter (~150 words, in-world text, quoted whole), arriving late Act II or early Act III: the replacement-patronage offer that currently exists only as a broken-outcome bullet. Voice: magnificent, generous, and nauseating because accurate — she praises Ilyana in Ilyana's own logic. Register line (final wording at scene pass): *"You understood what the timid never will: that greatness sometimes signs for its friends."* The letter must be genuinely flattering on first read; its horror is recognition. The symmetry the review found unstated — Ilyana forged consent before success; Morante manufactured it after — should be *legible in the letter without being named by anyone*. Luca Sern survives as her locally hired agent (no past contact with Ilyana; remote-compliant).

## 1.4 The good-night story

One authored telling — campfire or over the opened score cases in Five Cases — of a specific good Salt-Reed night: concrete, joyful, no foreshadowing poison. (Execution picks the night; requirement: it must feature all four, each doing the thing they were irreplaceable at, and one detail so specific it has to be true.) Then the knife, later and quiet: Mirella's margin on that same song either corroborates a detail warmly — which is worse — or remembers it differently, and the gap between the two memories is the wound. The player must be given the band before the ledger; this story is where they get it.

## 1.5 The handwriting scene

The compliance register, weaponized exactly once (then evicted per the prose rules): Marta Vell requires authentication that the four approvals are forged, and the procedure requires Ilyana to **write the four names again** at a customs desk, before witnesses, for hand comparison. Her hand knows Mirella's signature better than its own. No dialogue explains the scene; the writing is the confession. This becomes Five Cases' new climax (2.2), displacing the crane-net set piece.

## 1.6 Marta Vell, differentiated

She leaves the Bellandi-clone register by gaining visible opinions she rules against: she says what she thinks *and* applies the rule anyway — "I think the captain's claim is vulgar. It is also facially valid." (register; wording at scene pass). One texture object: she keeps a list of things she has lawfully released to people she despised. The incorruptibility reads differently when it visibly costs her.

# Part 2 — Structural Surgery

## 2.1 The instrumentalization thread (locked decision 3)

Three to four authored beats, charming-scale, never quest-blocking:

1. **Act I (Yard, ambient or The Second Flute):** Ilyana publicly announces the player will judge/perform something — small, warm, deniable. First refusal-loop exposure: go along (she escalates next time), correct privately, correct publicly, or name it ("ask me first").
2. **Act II (The Same Four Notes or Free Refrain staging):** she commits the player to a role by speaking *for* them to a third party — the player finds out from the third party.
3. **Act II–III (social scene):** she introduces the player with an invented title inside her story of the current work — the Gilded Wake grammar ("speaking for others") aimed at the person holding the controller.
4. **Payoff (relationship episode, light touch):** in One Private Song or Duet, one line acknowledges the thread's history — the question "can she love something she doesn't conduct" now has receipts.

Design guards: each beat costs nothing mechanical; her response to refusal evolves with `ilyana_direction` (consensual: she catches herself sooner; entitled: the escalation continues); the thread is never named in dialogue as a pattern — the player assembles it or doesn't.

## 2.2 Five Cases at Customs — de-clone retrofit

Keep: the cases, the false lining, the forged approvals in her hand, Morante's claim-by-paper, separate custody per owner, the direction taxonomy.

Change:

- **The climax is the handwriting scene** (1.5), not a crane-net rescue. The pursuit shrinks to one movement; the de-clone from Names Beneath Lime is structural, not cosmetic.
- **Pell One-Thumb is replaced** by a different species of small person: a young dock clerk who *mis-shelved* the fifth case and lied about it — an error-concealer, not a thief. The rhyme with Ilyana (both hid a false record rather than face a small shame) is left entirely unnarrated. The smugglers survive as buyers exploiting the mis-shelving; different pursuit geometry (warehouse interior, not crane walk).
- Marta Vell texture per 1.6; the good-night story seeded over the opened cases (1.4); Mirella's first margin surfaces here.
- The forged-lining discovery stays the introduction's engine — it is the best prop in the roster and is protected.

## 2.3 The Act I pieces — replacement and retrofit

**The Road Without a Conductor is replaced** (its function — Celia's arrival — is dead, and its title collides with Duet Without a Conductor). New A1-O, working title **The Second Flute** (alternates: *Spare Parts*, *First Chair*): an autumn Yard-and-Bridgehold operation in which Ilyana coordinates local performers — Immi central — into a debtor-supper performance and signal-teaching exchange with the Bridgehold bell-folk. Function per the audit brief: test whether she can coordinate without claiming authorship. The absorption object is planted here (the lent flute); instrumentalization beat 1 fires here; the operation's material spine is ordinary (a performance that also teaches the emergency couplets — art as infrastructure, her Creative Connector light expression). Output: re-sourced Act I prep (`ilyana_voluntary_signal_support` fits naturally) + `immi_flute_state`.

**No Song by Command is retrofitted, not replaced.** The bandit occupation of Eel Bridge, Bridge Alarm structure, and rescue architecture all survive. The coerced signaler is now **Immi** — taken because she knows the bell codes, forced to ring false all-clears (which also connects cleanly to the signal-spoofing texture in Ylva's world ⟨PAIR⟩ — one line of cross-reference, no dependency). Freed, her refusal lands: *"I will not play because you have decided I must"* transfers from an imported Nereo to a local whose reason the player witnessed — and the quest's decisive material stays identical (the rescue must be replanned around a refusal; the engine the review called genuinely novel is untouched). Ilyana's temptation to rewrite the refusal as fear now has a specific, checkable falsehood in it. Nereo's drum and roster references are purged; the quest's title survives unchanged.

## 2.4 The Act II pieces

**Reeds Under Ice is replaced.** New A2-O, working title **The Same Four Notes** (alternates: *Maintenance*, *The Plain Version*): the siege-corridor signal system needs weeks of what Ilyana fears most — repetition. Daily rehearsals with bearer crews; relief signalers trained (Immi central); the cadence kept *identical* because bearers trained on four notes hesitate at a fifth, and hesitation kills. Beats: teaching-as-repetition; the authored near-miss (one night she embellishes and a stretcher crew freezes — no death, one visible cost); the discipline of playing it plain; the handoff. Built directly on her banter file's best competence beat — "the system keeps working after she moves away" — which becomes the operation's win-state. Her temptation is *novelty*: "improving" the working thing. Instrumentalization beat 2 fires in staging. Outputs: `ilyana_cadence_holders_trained` (replacing the dead `ilyana_instruments_returned` as an Act II asset) + existing lane/tuning assets re-sourced here as appropriate.

**The Free Refrain is retrofitted.** The ask-each-voice structure — the roster's best consent mechanics — survives intact, re-based onto people with *witnessed* history: Immi and the cadence-holders the player watched her train. The four separate conversations remain (accept / accept-with-stopping-condition / counter-offer / refuse), and the refusals now weigh something because these relationships were built on camera. Ilyana's command instinct gains its fresh variant: not "I led you" but **"I taught you"** — the teacher's claim, which feels even more like love and is even less like consent. Morante's claim thread runs through Luca Sern (locally hired); the emergency-authority-over-property-only option survives. All former-troupe presence is purged; the Bard unlock and commitment gating stand.

## 2.5 The Act III pieces

**Every Case Its Owner is replaced.** New A3-O, working title **Their Version** (alternates: *Second Voices*, *The Changed Ending*): the proto-troupe prepares the spring reclamation performance — and Ilyana cannot be its centre, because the release-packet registration (the legal thread, compressed from the old quest into one same-day movement) requires her at Chancery during the performance. The paperwork she owes pulls her from the music; the irony is left unnarrated. The ensemble performs without her — **and changes her arrangement, and it is better.** She returns to hear the changed ending. Her own banter line is now aimed at her: "You changed the ending." / "Keep it, cut it, or make it embarrassing. I do not own the next performance." — whether she can *receive* that line is the direction beat. Immi's final ask (found a company, lead us) lands in this piece's residue. Outputs: `ilyana_releases_protected` and `ilyana_common_score_partitioned` re-sourced here; `ensemble_state`.

⟨CROSS⟩ **Coordination with Elara's spec:** the spring performance's natural venue is the Reclamation Feast from [[Elara Rewrite Spec]]'s *Uninvited*. Soft-couple only: same event window, shared ambient scenery if both specs execute, zero hard dependency in either direction. One sentence in each quest doc acknowledging the other's event; nothing more.

**The Last Common Measure is rebuilt.** Keep: River Boom towers, the cadence engine, five command phases, the Rank Warden equivalent, physical/harmonic/mechanical interruption plurality, the settlement recording.

Change:

- **The performers are Ilyana, the player, and the locals** (Immi, cadence-holders, volunteers). Former members participate as *lines*: the assembled-by-post score, each sent line carried by a local performer, each refusal held as an authored rest.
- **The silences are mechanical as well as musical:** a refused line's window is a gap in the counter-cadence the engine exploits — the duo must *hold* those windows with action (defense, traversal, mechanism work). Holding a rest under fire is the hardest performance beat; the absence is playable.
- **The guaranteed silence** (locked decision 4): minimum one refusal in every playthrough; Nereo default; do-not-regress flag in-doc. The finale can never be completed into perfect consent.
- **The name reframes:** "the last piece all five may share" now means by presence *or* absence — the piece contains the refusals as composed rests, and the score's final copy (a summer artifact) shows them notated. The four letters are read before the assault (staging beat), not after — the player fights the finale knowing exactly who answered and who didn't.
- Morante's letter reaches its decision point here (accept her patronage = broken path's document); the settlement recording at Chancery stands.

**The coda ([[The Road Takes the Song]]) keeps its name** — with Tavio's coda renamed, the collision set is reduced, and hers is thematically load-bearing (the road taking the song *is* her lesson). Add: the notated-rests score as a visible object; Immi's summer state; the ensemble's first performance of something Ilyana had no hand in.
