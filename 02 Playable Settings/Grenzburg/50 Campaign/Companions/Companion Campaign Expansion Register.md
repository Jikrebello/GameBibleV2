# Companion Campaign Expansion Register

This register controls exactly eighty-eight linked companion pieces. The eleven-piece budget is an authoring ledger hidden from the player. [[Companion Grounded Writing Standard]] controls variation, external stakes, and stable colleague treatment.

Current count: **88 linked / 0 reserved / 88 authored**. Because romance and sworn bond are mutually exclusive and only one of each may be committed across the roster, no more than **74 pieces** are playable in one run.

## Composition Authority

Every linked campaign authority identifies:

- the four-or-fewer pieces that directly centre the original wrong;
- six or more pieces with world-facing external stakes;
- three or more pieces centred on work, pleasure, loyalty, competence, or ordinary life;
- one stable-colleague summer condition;
- changed re-entry for expired seasonal incidents;
- one explicit player-interest gate before romance.

## Embedded Opportunities

Each companion retains ten embedded opportunities distributed across main campaign, factions, local stories, exploration, company life, and the systemic world. These are optional context, not a second hidden affinity ladder.
