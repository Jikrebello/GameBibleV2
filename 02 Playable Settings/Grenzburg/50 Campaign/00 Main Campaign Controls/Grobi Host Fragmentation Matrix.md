# Grobi Host Fragmentation Matrix

Ghor turns migration into conquest. His culpability does not erase the distinct interests of dependents, traders, rival chiefs, and fighters who may abandon him. The player cannot join Grobi as one faction and cannot negotiate Ghor out of his chosen war.

| Thread | Starting state | Player leverage | Siege effect | Aftermath effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uru's dependent train | held close to the war-host as labor, food pressure, and political hostages | secure a marked withdrawal corridor and prove it will not become an ambush | removes desperate camp followers and one reinforcement wave | fewer starving raiders; a recoverable migration route remains |
| Grakka's Stone-Tooth fighters | allied for food and status but unwilling to die for Ghor's crown | defeat their champion force, expose Ghor's ration priority, or grant bounded withdrawal | removes tunnel fighters or turns one approach into a three-sided fight | Grakka survives as a dangerous independent chief or dies in the operation |
| Skiv's trader-captains | profit from both movement and siege while selling Ghor route knowledge | seize the flotilla, purchase one truthful chart, or expose his double sales | reveals the human strike cell and weakens Ghor's resupply | Skiv becomes a watched intermediary, fugitive profiteer, or casualty |
| Southern command horn | coordinates otherwise competing bands | destroy the horn and its relay crews | Ghor loses synchronized assault phases | the host cannot reform as one army after his death |

`ghor_host_cohesion` begins `massed`. Each successfully separated thread moves it through `strained` and `fractured`; destroying the horn sets the maximum final state to `fractured`. Ghor's death and defeat of the assault set it to `routed`.
