# Emerald Law-Gem Custody Matrix

The dead Emerald Drake's law-gem is a non-conscious territorial instrument. It contains no soul, personality, command voice, or moral claim. [[A Drake in Winter]] requires one custody decision after the hunt.

| Custody | Immediate treatment | Act III aid | Lasting cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Folk land-keepers | reseat the gem in a repaired heart-oak boundary under Moss-Crown witness | pathfinders, root-stakes, and one secured remote route during the Dead March | least direct siege power; ducal engineers cannot study or deploy it |
| Ducal Warlock Hall | mount it in a regulated Green Lattice without claiming it as a Shell source | suppress one replacement surge during an anchor assault and strengthen the Upper Grenz front | militarizes a natural office and leaves Southwood dependent on ordinary restoration |
| Church custodians | house it as created natural order, explicitly not a saint's relic | coherent field sanctuaries prevent one casualty downgrade during the Dead March | removes it from its territory and gives the diocese visible custody prestige |
| Blackjack Yard | install it as the Green Ward under the player's unit custody | before each closed Act III operation choose Still Footing, Clear Breath, or Ordered Camp as a non-stacking party preparation | no institutional reinforcement arrives; Southwood receives no supernatural restoration |

The Green Ward visibly grows emerald glass, root-like tracery, and ordered patches of frost through the Blackjack Yard. Its effects alter preparation and appearance, not the player's mind or morality. Numerical bonuses remain provisional.
