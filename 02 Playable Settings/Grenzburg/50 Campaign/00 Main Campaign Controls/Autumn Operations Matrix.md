# Autumn Operations Matrix

[[Roads Worth Saving]] opens six operations. Three completed operations unlock [[The Last Convoy]]. All six remain available until the player accepts the Late Autumn transition warning.

| ID | Operation | Core location | Pressure | Required play | Later advantage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AO-1 | Water for Three Mills | [[Three Mills Reach]] | bandits seize the shared leat while mill households accuse one another | repair gates, clear the mill race, identify the raiders' inland cache | more flour and one additional material slot in the Last Convoy |
| AO-2 | The Lake Road Still Runs | [[Lakewatch]] and [[Eel Bridge]] | road-raiders and a damaged ferry isolate fish stores | reopen the bridge or ferry, rescue carriers, settle passage for one winter convoy | lake food and an alternate northern withdrawal route |
| AO-3 | Timber on the Grenz | [[Timberfalls]] and [[Fellwater Camps]] | deserters extort rafters while loose timber threatens the riverworks | free workers, clear the boom, choose fuel or structural timber priority | winter fuel or faster wall and bridge repairs |
| AO-4 | Carriers of Highlake | [[Highlake]] and [[Tuskway Redoubt]] | Shattered-Tusk fighters demand abandonment of a marcher outpost | escort carriers, hold or evacuate the outpost, distinguish fighters from nearby households | upland scouts and a safer eastern withdrawal |
| AO-5 | Leave to Cross | [[Grey-Withy Crossing]] and [[Outer Road Stone]] | hostile Folk fighters exploit a genuine boundary dispute | learn the local claim, confront the killers, negotiate or force bounded passage | Wolfsmere warnings and Folk routes without surrendering ducal road safety |
| AO-6 | The Quiet Antlers | [[Ashfield Lodge]] and [[Hunter's Last Mark]] | missing hunters and an unnaturally ordered animal range | track the party, rescue survivors, withdraw from the silence belt with evidence | early Drake preparation and a safe Southwood staging camp |

No operation resolves a companion or faction line. Their outputs reduce later costs but never replace the baseline main-story solution.
