# Muster Anchor Matrix

Five regional anchors let contradictory Qianglong formations act as one campaign. Each uses `active`, `damaged`, or `destroyed`. Only destruction severs its function and counts toward the threshold.

| Anchor | Location | Function | Regional pressure while active | Final power removed when destroyed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold-Iron Muster Shaft | [[Cold-Iron Deeps]] | draws iron, bodies, and work-patterns into replacement armor | Highlake dead continue mining and armoring formations | crab-like armor replacement |
| Watch-Hill Command Crypt | [[Fort Tannbruck]] beneath Watch Hill | enrolls recent battlefield residue under obsolete units | Tannbruck's recent defenders rise as reinforcements | recent-dead reinforcement waves |
| River Arsenal Replacement Forge | [[Old River Arsenal]] | rebuilds siege equipment and conducts dead artillery crews | Upper Grenz settlements suffer repeating bombardment | siege volleys and pressure breath |
| Tuskway Banner Vault | beneath [[Tuskway Redoubt]] | imposes rank and synchronized movement across the eastern road | living defenders receive false orders and movement constraints | imposed-rank and formation-control effects |
| Fen Ossuary | beyond [[White Mud Causeway]] | pumps black mire and burial material toward the Deep Muster | Fenn Road floods with black mud, eels, and rebuilding residue | black-mud regeneration and eel-burrow movement |

[[The Open Thaw]] destroys the Cold-Iron anchor. [[The Land Reclaimed]] opens the other four in any order. Three destroyed anchors open [[The Unending Muster Main Quest|The Unending Muster]]; all five remain available until [[The Dead March]] begins.

Uncleared anchors do not survive the altar's destruction as functioning Muster sites. They instead increase pre-finale casualties, retain their boss power, and leave a more contaminated summer ruin.

Gerhold's living acceptance at [[Three Horn Depot]] and the deepest altar are separate anchors. Gerhold is severed in [[Gerhold's Choice]]; the altar is destroyed in [[The Deep Muster Main Quest|The Deep Muster]].
