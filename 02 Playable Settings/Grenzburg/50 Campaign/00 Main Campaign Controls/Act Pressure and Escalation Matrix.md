# Act Pressure and Escalation Matrix

Each act owns a distinct crisis. Earlier problems may continue through faction, companion, and local content, but they cannot blur the main campaign's escalation.

| Block | Dominant pressure | Principal enemies | Supernatural ceiling | Core regions | Required set piece |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue | survival and owned service | toll-bandits; opportunistic Grobi scouts | none beyond ordinary setting practice | northern Lowlands and Funnel Camp | sabotaged convoy crossing |
| Act I | roads closing under human and local war | bandits, deserters, Shattered-Tusk road-raiders, hostile Folk war-bands | ordinary residue, old civil ruins, dangerous fauna, early Drake signs | Lowlands, Longlake, Upper Grenz, public Highlake; optional Wolfsmere and Southwood | fighting withdrawal from Tannbruck |
| Act II | invasion, siege, and displaced territorial fauna | Ghor's Grobi host, human siege profiteers, Emerald Drake | Menhir court-fauna and bounded law-gem effects | Grenzburg city, Southwood, Tuskway, winter corridors | Grobi breach into Lantern Ward; Ghor's final siege |
| Act III | organized death and compulsory service | Qianglong residue formations, recurrence engines, Gerhold, General Below | Abyssal true-dragon manifestation | Highlake, Upper Grenz, Frostfenn, Deep Muster | dead march and altar-destruction finale |
| Summer | consequence and rebuilding | scarcity, grief, institutional capture, unresolved local danger | no new escalation | surviving city and frontier | first reconstruction road |

## Through-Lines

| Thread | Early form | Later consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Folk conflict | particular road-raiders contest roads and settlement | Four-Hearth work distinguishes rights-bearing households from Jorund's civilian-murder doctrine |
| Grobi pressure | scouts exploit the Black Road and map Tannbruck | Ghor converts migration into conquest; surviving bands and dependents remain after his death |
| Death and burial | ordinary residue and named dead appear in local contexts | the Muster converts residue, equipment, and command records into organized formations |
| Blackjack service | debt determines the player's starting vulnerability | proven service creates the final claim that may free the player or strengthen collective relief |
| Material survival | roads, mills, fuel, bridges, and stores determine autumn capacity | the same assets determine winter defense, spring casualties, and summer reconstruction |

## Prohibitions

- No universal mastermind links the acts.
- No organized Muster formation appears before spring.
- No speaking or morally negotiating Emerald Drake appears in winter.
- No hostile Folk war-band speaks for all Folk.
- No Act I bandit or Folk outcome secretly causes Ghor, the Drake, or the Deep Muster.
