# Grenzburg Chapter and Window Matrix

This note controls the order in which the main story opens the game. [[Grenzburg Campaign Spine]] controls story truth; [[Season and World-State Constitution]] controls persistence and cutoffs. The matrix prevents the critical path from becoming a rail and prevents side content from floating outside campaign time.

## Gate Vocabulary

- **Open gate:** the chapter becomes available while exploration and parallel quests remain unrestricted.
- **Operation gate:** accepting the mission begins a bounded operation, after which the player returns to an open window.
- **Season gate:** accepting the mission advances the world after an explicit cutoff warning.
- **Finale gate:** accepting the mission begins the end sequence after an explicit point-of-no-return warning.

## Open-Window Rhythm

The intended rhythm is main mission, changed hub, several available parallel missions, then player-controlled recommitment. Chapters 2, 4, 6, 9, 11, 12, 16, 17, and 18 create the broadest exploration windows.

Main quest urgency is expressed through condition and stakes rather than false minute-by-minute deadlines. Closed operations may use real clocks after the player accepts them. Outside those operations, the world waits at a credible campaign posture.

## Side-Content Unlock Principles

- Faction admission begins in Early Autumn; no line delivers all of its leaders in one scene.
- The Black Road introduces [[Bernard Valeon]], [[Dismas Raben]], and [[Ilyana Veshkar]]. Early Autumn opens [[Elara Velescar]], [[Tavio Meran]], [[Ashira bath Melan]], and [[Zafir ibn Samad]]. [[Svala Last-Beacon]] recruits at the Southbound Muster, so every companion is available by the end of Open Autumn.
- All five faction lines can reach quest six before any exclusive internal commitment. *Knives Behind the Walls* applies pressure to the underworld fork but does not silently choose it for the player.
- Companion quest three opens an explicit romance or sworn-bond commitment; quest four resolves the personal arc before the finale gate or produces a named compromised summer state.
- At least one alternate starting-vocation trainer and one representative quest from each gameplay mode appear before chapter 5.
- All six starting vocations can be unlocked by the end of chapter 6 without requiring permanent faction loyalty.
- No advanced vocation unlocks before the player's core combat language is established.
- The first marquee supernatural quest uses familiar local stakes and limited terminology.
- Winter does not delete the exterior world; it changes routes, dangers, services, and quest forms.
- Spring revisits locations from autumn so reclamation has memory.
- Summer provides new scenes and selected quests rather than only resolving old markers.

## Failure Transformation Standard

Wave 5 must give every chapter:

- an explicit trigger;
- at least one fail-forward state;
- required and optional locations;
- critical NPC survival rules;
- world-state outputs;
- companion and faction intersections;
- the content unlocked on completion;
- the content transformed at commitment.

No chapter may rely on an unnamed traitor, forced player stupidity, off-screen companion action that could have been prevented, or a hidden timer.
