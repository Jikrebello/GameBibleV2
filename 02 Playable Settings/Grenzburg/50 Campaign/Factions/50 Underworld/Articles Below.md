# Articles Below

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 7. It belongs to the Alignment movement and is available during Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Mara proposes articles for a night guild that can smuggle refugees, contraband, and information under bounded civil license.

Choose the Low Lantern branch and define what legal recognition costs in secrecy and independence.

## Player Actions

Draft prohibited trades, register route keepers through aliases, test an inspection, and stop a member selling a refuge door.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Officials want names; runners fear exposure; profitable vice funds genuine rescue.

## Information Paths

Route accounts, safehouse testimony, and the sale attempt show what must be governed.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Legalized night guild, looser tolerated network, or withdrawal from formal recognition.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed inspection closes one route and exposes one keeper, who remains rescueable.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Exclusive branch:** starting this quest closes The First Toll, A Bell Behind the Walls, and The Hidden Tribunal.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## Rewards and Return Value

Locks the Low Lantern branch and sets its governance.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.
