# The Names They Buried

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 5. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Deep Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Ivo reveals an archive of murders and assaults protected by rank. Some entries are well proved; others rest on grief, rumor, or one terrified statement.

Preserve victims' testimony without converting a mixed archive directly into a death list.

## Player Actions

Authenticate sample cases, protect archivists, recover a missing corroboration box, and decide who may access the names.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Marcellina wants evidence for leverage, Enzo wants destruction, bereaved families demand action, and Black Bell recruits want immediate killings.

## Information Paths

Court copies, burial records, and independent witnesses grade each case rather than treating every name equally.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Build a civil evidence channel, keep a protected private archive, or authorize punishment from the list.

## Fail-Forward Results

A burned box leaves partial cases that can be rebuilt. Exposed witnesses move through the Night House at material cost.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

Establishes the archive as testimony requiring judgement, not a magical truth ledger.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.
