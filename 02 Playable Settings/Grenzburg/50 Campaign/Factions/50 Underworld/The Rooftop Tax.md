# The Rooftop Tax

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 3. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Open Autumn and Early Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A crew charges every roof runner a violent toll and claims to be collecting for the Low Lanterns. Mara denies authorizing it but has tolerated their intelligence.

End predation without automatically handing the city's hidden travel network to corrupt Watch officers.

## Player Actions

Map the roof route, protect couriers, confront the crew, and recover its payment book.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

The crew provides useful siege warnings and extorts poor runners. Enzo's clerk buys information from them.

## Information Paths

Runner testimony, payment marks, and Enzo's messages prove the operation.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Fold runners into accountable route service, destroy the crew, or preserve tolls under limits.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the leader escapes, the book exposes safehouses and forces relocations. Failed rooftop pursuit shifts the confrontation to Bridge Market.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
