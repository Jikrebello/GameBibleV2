# The Watchman Who Walked

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 6. It belongs to the Fracture movement and is available during Deep Winter and Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Oskar Vey murders a second protected witness and walks out of High Court under an emergency commendation. Ivo rings a black handbell and proposes killing him.

Confront a plainly protected killer and decide whether failure of court justifies a permanent private jurisdiction.

## Player Actions

Reconstruct the murder, survive Watch retaliation, confront Vey on the wall circuit, and attempt arrest, immediate defense, or execution.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Vey knowingly kills witnesses and has official protectors. Enzo suppresses evidence. Mara fears a killing will destroy every safe route; Ivo believes another trial means another body.

## Information Paths

Blood route, watch roster, and the victim's hidden statement independently establish guilt and protection.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Arrest with overwhelming proof, kill during immediate lethal resistance, or authorize a planned Black Bell execution.

## Fail-Forward Results

If Vey escapes the wall, he fortifies a Watch house for a later assault. If evidence is seized, copies survive with different custodians.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Fork warning:** beginning either branch quest seven closes the other branch. The UI names lost quests, leaders, services, and settlement terms before commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
