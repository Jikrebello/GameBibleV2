# A Bell Behind the Walls

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 8. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Black Bell members add names during the Muster, claiming emergency collaboration. Ivo discovers one is a personal vendetta and another is a genuine imminent betrayer.

Prevent the tribunal becoming a weapon for private appetite while responding to immediate danger.

## Player Actions

Authenticate cases, enter a wall passage, stop the betrayer, and expose the false accuser.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Time pressure rewards shortcuts; armed Bell cells believe review is weakness; Chancery files contain both truth and coercive leverage.

## Information Paths

Multiple witnesses, physical plans, and motive checks distinguish the two names.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Enforce strict evidence and unpaid service, tolerate emergency single-source action, or dissolve the Bell.

## Fail-Forward Results

A missed betrayer damages a defense position but remains confrontable. A wrongful target can be rescued before sentence.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Black Bell branch remains locked.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
