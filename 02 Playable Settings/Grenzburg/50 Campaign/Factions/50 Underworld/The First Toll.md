# The First Toll

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 7. It belongs to the Alignment movement and is available during Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Ivo asks the player to approve the Black Bell's first planned killing: an official protected from trial but not currently posing immediate lethal danger.

Choose the Black Bell branch with full knowledge that this creates private punishment, not merely self-defense.

## Player Actions

Recheck the evidence, hear surviving victims, infiltrate the target's protection, and choose execution, abduction for trial, or refusal.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

The target is guilty; official protection is real; the absence of immediate attack makes planned killing morally distinct from defense.

## Information Paths

Three independent case sources meet Ivo's stated standard, while one hidden witness offers a civil path.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Authorize the toll, divert to public trial, or break with Ivo.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the target escapes, the Bell gains a pursuit quest state. If captured, protectors attempt extraction.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Exclusive branch:** starting this quest closes Articles Below, The Store Beneath the Siege, and The Licensed Night.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## Rewards and Return Value

Locks the Black Bell branch and fixes whether its founding act is killing or coerced trial.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.

At the next season gate, unresolved work transforms into a named institutional response, casualty, closure, or harder route and remains legible in the journal.
