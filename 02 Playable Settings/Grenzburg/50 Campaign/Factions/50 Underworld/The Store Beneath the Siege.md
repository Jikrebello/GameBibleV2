# The Store Beneath the Siege

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 8. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A hidden food store can sustain displaced families during the dead march, but half was accumulated through theft from public reserves.

Use the Low Lantern network in crisis while deciding restitution and control.

## Player Actions

Defend the store, verify origins, move food through threatened routes, and choose public, guild, or mixed distribution.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Residue incursions, hungry crowds, and guild members protecting private shares create pressure.

## Information Paths

Crate marks, granary deficits, and route ledgers distinguish stolen public food from donated contraband.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return and redistribute, preserve guild emergency custody, or let members divide the store.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the store burns, salvage and public reserve replacement preserve partial relief.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Low Lantern branch remains locked.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
