# The Hidden Tribunal

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 9. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis and Summer Aftermath.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

After victory, the Black Bell must decide whether to disband, submit evidence to civil courts, or continue as a hidden tribunal.

Set the underworld term while refusing to call even the strongest private execution system morally clean.

## Player Actions

Hear a final protected-crime case, secure witnesses, confront a member who demands payment, and enact the tribunal's limits or dissolution.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Victims distrust courts, officials resist exposure, and Bell members have tasted unanswerable authority.

## Information Paths

The archive, live testimony, and Guild Court response reveal whether civil channels can carry the case.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Strict unpaid hidden tribunal, evidence network without executions, civil integration, or violent splintering.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed settlement creates a named splinter and visible summer murders rather than an undefined bad ending.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Final Black Bell settlement. No result becomes setting canon.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
