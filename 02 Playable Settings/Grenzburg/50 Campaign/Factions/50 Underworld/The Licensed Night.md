# The Licensed Night

## Line Position

This is Grenzburg Underworld quest 9. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis and Summer Aftermath.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The first licensed night route opens after the crisis and immediately faces a case involving a fugitive, stolen medicine, and a corrupt officer.

Prove what the guild has become and set the underworld term for summer.

## Player Actions

Run the case, hold a guild hearing, coordinate or refuse civil review, and enact sanctions.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Members resist oversight; officials seek total control; victims need immediate help.

## Information Paths

Route records, medical stock, and witness accounts allow a fair decision.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Licensed night guild, tolerated shadow network, official absorption, or collapse into ordinary gangs.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed hearing creates a splinter route whose activity remains visible in summer.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Final Low Lantern settlement. No result becomes setting canon.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
