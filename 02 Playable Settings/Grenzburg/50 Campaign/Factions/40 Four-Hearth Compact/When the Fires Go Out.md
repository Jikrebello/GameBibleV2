# When the Fires Go Out

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 9. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis and Summer Aftermath.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Compact fires lit for the crisis must be extinguished, renewed, or claimed as permanent authority after victory.

Show whether the temporary institution can end honorably and leave durable rights behind.

## Player Actions

Visit each hearth, settle a breach, return borrowed tokens, and renew only the clauses communities still accept.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Victors want permanent control, injured households demand compensation, and opportunists claim to speak for absent kin.

## Information Paths

Hearth records, local witnesses, and visible fulfillment establish which terms earned renewal.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Renew seasonal rights, harden a permanent compact, preserve an informal network, or allow dissolution.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed renewal leaves bilateral agreements rather than erasing every relationship.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Final faction settlement. No result becomes setting canon.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## World-State Outputs

- `settlement_folk_rights`
- `four_hearth_line_outcome`
- `summer_folk_presence`

## Rewards and Return Value

Changes summer routes, refuges, warnings, settlement relations, and what remains secret.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If the player passes the named finale gate without resolving this stage, the selected or default institutional alignment produces a specified compromised summer settlement.
