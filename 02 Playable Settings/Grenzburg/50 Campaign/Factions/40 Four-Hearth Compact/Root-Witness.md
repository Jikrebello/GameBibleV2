# Root-Witness

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 2. It belongs to the Admission movement and is available during Open Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A ducal road crew and Moss-Crown household dispute whether a widened track crosses a living boundary. Both possess plausible markers.

Establish the boundary and a usable road without treating Menhir law as proof of justice.

## Player Actions

Survey old stones, hear household memory, test flood changes, and hold a place-law hearing with independent safety measures.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A foreman moved a marker for convenience; one Moss-Crown elder exaggerates the protected zone to control tolls; the grove itself presents dangerous but non-moral environmental pressure.

## Information Paths

Tree scars, floodpost records, and three-generation testimony reconstruct the line.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Restore the boundary with a road bend, permit a seasonal crossing, or force the straight road.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed hearing closes Grey-Withy Crossing until a harder outer route is cleared.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
