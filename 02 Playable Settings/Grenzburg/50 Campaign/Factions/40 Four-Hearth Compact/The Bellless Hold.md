# The Bellless Hold

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 5. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Early Winter and Deep Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Occupation-era mechanisms in Bellless Hold reactivate, and Jorund uses the danger to demand attacks on nearby settler households.

Clear a major ruin, recover testimony of old wrongs, and refuse inherited civilian guilt.

## Player Actions

Enter by ford or roof, disable ward engines, rescue trapped delvers, and recover the hold roll beneath place-law pressure.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Atherian mechanisms, winter terrain, and Jorund's fighters create distinct threats. Settler families outside the hold are not occupiers by ancestry alone.

## Information Paths

Hold inscriptions, delver testimony, and Moss-Crown memory establish the occupation history and present innocence.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return the roll to compact elders under restriction, publish selected wrongs, or let Jorund seize it as a war banner.

## Fail-Forward Results

If engines remain active, the Hold becomes a restricted return dungeon. If Jorund escapes with copies, his recruitment grows.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
