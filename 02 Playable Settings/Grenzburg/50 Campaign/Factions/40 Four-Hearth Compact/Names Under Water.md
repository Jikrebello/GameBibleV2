# Names Under Water

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 7. It belongs to the Alignment movement and is available during Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Thaw reveals drowned memorial rolls connecting Wolfsmere warnings, River-Clan losses, and a secret that Mael refuses to make public.

Recover names and decide who may know the restricted Wolfsmere truth before the compact is written.

## Player Actions

Navigate flooded paths, recover roll cases, evade place hazards, and hold a closed elder hearing.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Treasure seekers want the records; ducal scholars demand public access; some elders want all evidence destroyed. The boundary remains sacred but not morally infallible.

## Information Paths

Drowned rolls, Mael's account, and River-Clan memorials overlap without any single source controlling truth.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Restrict the truth to player and compact elders, share a bounded warning with the Duke, or publish it.

## Fail-Forward Results

Lost cases leave names reconstructable from two communities at higher trust cost. Boundary damage closes a waystone link until repaired.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Lock warning:** elder restriction preserves the strongest compact; public disclosure closes deep Wolfsmere cooperation; total concealment closes formal ducal recognition. Other faction lines remain open.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
