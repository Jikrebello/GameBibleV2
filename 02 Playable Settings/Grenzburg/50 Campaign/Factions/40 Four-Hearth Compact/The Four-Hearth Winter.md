# The Four-Hearth Winter

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 8. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Early Spring and Muster Crisis.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The four delegations must write a compact before the dead march makes every local promise an armed improvisation.

Define renewable rights and reciprocal duties without creating a permanent Folk state.

## Player Actions

Assemble delegates, resolve one attempted killing, test warning and refuge obligations during a Muster incursion, and write term-specific hearth clauses.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Jorund remnants, ducal hardliners, and exhausted households threaten the meeting. Delegates disagree sincerely about secrecy and duration.

## Information Paths

Prior quest states, seat tokens, and the live incursion demonstrate who can fulfill each obligation.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Renewable seasonal compact, permanent territorial charter, informal private network, or failed assembly.

## Fail-Forward Results

If a delegate leaves, a three-hearth compact remains possible with reduced reach. If the moot is attacked, surviving testimony preserves the clauses.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Confirms the quest-seven internal alignment and displays each right, duty, duration, and disclosure.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## World-State Outputs

- `four_hearth_compact_form`
- `folk_rights_term`
- `compact_warning_network`

## Rewards and Return Value

Sets the Folk-rights term for the composite settlement.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If the player passes the named finale gate without resolving this stage, the selected or default institutional alignment produces a specified compromised summer settlement.
