# Bent Oak, Living

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 4. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Late Autumn and Early Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Elof's Chapel-Folk refuge admits families accused of aiding raiders. Ignatius demands names before the city will supply food.

Preserve sanctuary for noncombatants while distinguishing refuge from sheltering active killers.

## Player Actions

Audit the refuge through consent, track an armed fugitive who slipped away, secure food, and defend the chapel from retaliation.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

One fugitive committed murder; most residents did not. Church militants and grieving villagers collapse the distinction.

## Information Paths

Refuge rolls, weapon traces, and child witnesses identify the fugitive without exposing every household.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Maintain bounded sanctuary, submit all names, or close the refuge.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the fugitive escapes, he attacks a supply route and leaves a clear trail. Lost food transforms into a winter delivery.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

Tests reciprocal refuge and its limits through ordinary wrongdoing.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.
