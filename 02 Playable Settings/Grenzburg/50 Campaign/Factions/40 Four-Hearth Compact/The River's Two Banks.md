# The River's Two Banks

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 3. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Open Autumn and Late Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

River-Clan ferrymen and Lakewatch fishers each claim winter passage priority after part of the Sunken Causeway collapses.

Keep food and evacuation moving while recognizing that use rights differ from ownership of the whole lake.

## Player Actions

Recover causeway markers, escort both crews, clear a natural predator, and negotiate a tide-and-ice schedule.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Smugglers provoke fights to preserve hidden crossings; both communities fear losing winter livelihood.

## Information Paths

Atherian level marks, ferry logs, and fish-spawning patterns establish safe and customary use.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Create shared schedule, ducal priority, or clan-controlled passage with public refuge obligations.

## Fail-Forward Results

If boats are lost, a temporary ice-cable route opens at greater risk. Failed talks produce tolls, not total closure.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
