# Tusk Broken Twice

## Line Position

This is Four-Hearth Compact quest 6. It belongs to the Fracture movement and is available during Deep Winter and Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Jorund plans to collapse Tuskway under a civilian convoy to force the Duke from the uplands. Several fighters and households want out but fear collective punishment.

Split total-war followers from people who reject murder without declaring every dissenter loyal to Grenzburg.

## Player Actions

Warn the convoy, enter the redoubt, disarm charges, offer protected withdrawal, and confront Jorund.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Jorund knowingly targets civilians. Ducal officers want to arrest entire families. Ghost-Foot delegates will close their stair if fighting spills inward.

## Information Paths

Charge placement, deserter testimony, and convoy timing prove intent.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Capture or kill committed attackers, protect withdrawing households, or permit collective retaliation.

## Fail-Forward Results

A partial collapse diverts survivors through Ghost-Foot Stair under strict terms. Jorund may escape wounded into spring.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment; all five faction lines remain available through this quest.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
