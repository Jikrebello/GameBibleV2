# The Power Returned

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 9. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis and Summer Aftermath.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

After the General Below is stopped, emergency warrants remain in force unless the authority holding them formally returns, renews, or expands them.

Make the line's summer result visible and prevent victory from silently normalizing crisis government.

## Player Actions

Collect outstanding warrants, resolve an armed refusal, conduct the first post-crisis petition, and witness Alaric enact the chosen charter.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Officers fear demobilization, courtiers want loyal appointments, and damaged settlements still need force.

## Information Paths

Warrant copies, garrison rolls, and petition records identify every live power.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return powers under the strongest charter, renew bounded ducal discretion, retain martial administration, or leave unresolved authority to harden offscreen.

## Fail-Forward Results

If an officer refuses, capture, negotiation, or defeat all recover the command seal; violence changes casualties and legitimacy, not the existence of an ending.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Final faction settlement. No result becomes setting canon.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## Rewards and Return Value

Provides patrols, petition scenes, uniforms, and reconstruction behavior across summer.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If the player passes the named finale gate without resolving this stage, the selected or default institutional alignment produces a specified compromised summer settlement.
