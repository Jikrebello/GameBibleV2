# The Officers' Garden

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 7. It belongs to the Alignment movement and is available during Deep Winter and Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Senior officers use a memorial supper to propose making winter command powers permanent after the siege. Their plan would stabilize supply and place petitions under military permission.

Choose the Ducal line's governing direction before spring reclamation.

## Player Actions

Gather positions from veterans and village speakers, expose a patronage promotion, prevent a staged granary alarm, and answer the officers in the Garden.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

The permanent-command faction contains sincere defenders and ambitious clients. Alaric and Pierre disagree loyally about how much flexibility survival requires.

## Information Paths

Promotion rolls, alarm orders, and Liese's petitions reveal who manufactured the crisis and who argues in good faith.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Back a civil-service charter, preserve broad ducal emergency discretion, or endorse permanent military administration.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the staged alarm succeeds, stores are saved but the officers gain public support. The choice remains, with different costs.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Lock warning:** civil-service alignment closes permanent martial government; permanent command closes full village petitions; ducal discretion closes the strongest fixed-expiry charter. Other faction lines remain open.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
