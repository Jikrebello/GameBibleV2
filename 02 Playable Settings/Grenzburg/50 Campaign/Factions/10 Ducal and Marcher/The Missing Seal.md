# The Missing Seal

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 2. It belongs to the Admission movement and is available during Early Autumn and Open Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The field seal used to authorize road requisitions vanishes before Pierre can recall it. Valid-looking orders begin stripping village carts.

Recover the seal and repair the harm before distrust closes lawful supply routes.

## Player Actions

Compare orders at High Court, question seal-cutters in Old Market, follow a runner into the underways, and choose whether to expose the officer who left the seal unsecured.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A procurement clerk sold access to cover gambling losses; an unaffiliated theft crew now uses the seal. Neither is a secret faction mastermind.

## Information Paths

Wax flaws, clerk schedules, and cartmen's descriptions provide three independent routes to the underways cache.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return the seal quietly to preserve confidence, publish the breach and compensate villages, or use it once to set a trap.

## Fail-Forward Results

A burned seal can be invalidated by public notice; stolen goods become a recovery route. A fleeing clerk leaves debt records.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

Unlocks legal evidence access and changes village willingness to honor later warrants.
