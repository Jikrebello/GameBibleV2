# Law Behind the Walls

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 6. It belongs to the Fracture movement and is available during Early Winter and Deep Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Military police hold civilians in Bridgehold without charges after sabotage. One detainee knows the saboteur; others are leverage.

Find the attacker while deciding whether emergency detention answers to a court during siege.

## Player Actions

Inspect the blast site, interview detainees separately, protect a witness, and force or avoid High Court review.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A real saboteur hides among frightened people. A captain uses the case to normalize indefinite detention, while Pierre worries a public release could trigger another strike.

## Information Paths

Blast residue, bridge rosters, and witness route knowledge independently narrow the suspect.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Submit military detention to rapid civil review, preserve closed command review, or release everyone without safeguards.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the suspect escapes, a planted fuse reveals the next target. If a detainee is harmed, testimony survives but legitimacy drops.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment; all five faction lines remain available through this quest.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

Completing quest six leaves every other faction line open and prepares the internal military lock.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.
