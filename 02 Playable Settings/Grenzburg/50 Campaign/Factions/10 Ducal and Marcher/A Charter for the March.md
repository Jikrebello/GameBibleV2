# A Charter for the March

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 8. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Early Spring and Muster Crisis.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Spring councils must define who commands rebuilt outposts, who may requisition property, and where villagers appeal before the dead march consumes ordinary politics.

Write a workable marcher charter and prove it at a reclaimed fort site.

## Player Actions

Draft clauses, collect four regional petitions, test command during a residue attack, and revise the document after its first failure.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Officers, nobles, village speakers, and exhausted soldiers each defend real interests and exploitable privileges.

## Information Paths

Past quest outputs, petition rolls, and the Tannbruck field test show which clauses protect or obstruct survival.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Choose professional civil service, ducal emergency office, military administration, or withdraw from authorship.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed field test kills no clause by itself; it exposes a flaw and forces a costly revision before the Muster.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

This confirms the quest-seven internal alignment. The exact settlement term is displayed before enactment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
