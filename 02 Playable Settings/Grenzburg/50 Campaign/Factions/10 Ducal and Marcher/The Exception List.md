# The Exception List

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 4. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Late Autumn and Early Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

An emergency entry list excludes several families whose carts were requisitioned for Tannbruck. Enzo offers to sell exceptions while officers insist the gate is full.

Decide who enters without pretending capacity is infinite or allowing emergency policy to become a market in lives.

## Player Actions

Audit shelter capacity, find falsified household counts, escort exposed families, and hold a rapid petition session before lockdown.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Enzo's clerks, panicked gate crowds, and an officer protecting relatives distort the list. The shortage itself is real.

## Information Paths

Camp census, requisition receipts, and granary shelter plans identify lawful claims and actual space.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Publish criteria and remove patronage, preserve officer discretion, or accept paid exceptions to fund extra tents.

## Fail-Forward Results

If no space is created, families receive guarded routes to West Refuge Hall rather than disappearing. Riot damage reduces gate service.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
