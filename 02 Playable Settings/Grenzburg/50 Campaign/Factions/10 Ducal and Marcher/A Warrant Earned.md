# A Warrant Earned

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 1. It belongs to the Admission movement and is available during Early Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A provisional ducal warrant will expire unless the player resolves a road seizure without letting soldiers, guild guards, or refugees invent authority on the spot.

Open the Marcher line and prove specialist judgement through a concrete public service rather than noble favor.

## Player Actions

Inspect the seizure at Tann Gate, recover the lost dispatch from Broken Tollworks, protect displaced travelers, and present an action report before Liese and Pierre.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A junior officer has detained an entire convoy after one forged pass. Smugglers exploit the confusion, while frightened soldiers fear being blamed for releasing an infiltrator.

## Information Paths

Travelers carry matching parish rolls; the dispatch names the actual suspect; tollhouse wax proves the pass plate was stolen. Any two identify the guilty party.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Release the convoy under escort, maintain bounded detention while searching, or endorse collective confinement.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the suspect escapes, the stolen plate leads to the Tollworks. If the convoy scatters, named families reach Grenzburg by separate routes with higher losses.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
