# Outpost Arithmetic

## Line Position

This is Ducal and Marcher Service quest 3. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Open Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Pierre orders an outpost reopened at Tuskway Redoubt, but its supply plan assumes seasonal households will carry freight without pay.

Make the outpost defensible without turning nearby people into invisible military labor.

## Player Actions

Survey the redoubt, test routes, negotiate carriers in Highlake, clear an ordinary predator nest, and revise the garrison table.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

An officer favors compulsory carts; mine owners demand protection first; Ghost-Foot observers will close the stair if soldiers exceed the old footprint.

## Information Paths

Old garrison tallies, carrier testimony, and physical route capacity reveal the false arithmetic.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Fund a smaller professional post, impose a larger levy, or make a renewable service compact with named limits.

## Fail-Forward Results

A missed supply run leaves the post undermanned but recoverable. Failed talks shift freight to a longer public road and increase cost.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

No exclusive commitment.
