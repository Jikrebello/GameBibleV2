## Explicit Alignment Locks

### Ducal and Marcher

Quest seven chooses civil service, broad ducal discretion, or permanent military administration. Civil service closes martial rule; martial rule closes full village petitions; ducal discretion closes fixed automatic expiry.

### Blackjacks and Medori

Quest seven chooses Brina's free-company track, Lucia's reformed Bank track, ducal absorption, or Koss's command. Quest eight may shape cooperative ownership inside the Brina track. One form cannot inherit another form's exclusive headquarters authority.

### Church

Quest seven chooses bounded sacred jurisdiction, broad episcopal court, or strong secular restriction. The bounded result preserves independent doctrine and hospitals while returning secular crimes to civil courts.

### Four-Hearth

Quest seven sets the scope of Wolfsmere truth. Public disclosure closes deep cooperation; complete concealment closes formal ducal recognition; elder restriction preserves the strongest seasonal compact.

### Underworld

Quest six opens the fork. Beginning [[Articles Below]] closes the Black Bell branch. Beginning [[The First Toll]] closes the Low Lantern branch. The warning names all six affected quests and their route, evidence, and summer services.

## Composite Settlement Rule

The line settlements contribute independent terms rather than a single faction victory. [[Accounts of the Living]] must read all five terms, casualties, settlement survival, player freedom, companion outcomes, and unresolved leaders before presenting the immediate political result.

No strongest term requires another strongest term, and no combination becomes future-setting canon in this wave.
