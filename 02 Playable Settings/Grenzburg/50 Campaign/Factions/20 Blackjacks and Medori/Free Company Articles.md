# Free Company Articles

## Line Position

This is Blackjacks and Medori quest 9. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis and Summer Aftermath.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

After the crisis, the company needs enforceable articles governing pay, refusal, casualties, salvage, and civilian jurisdiction.

Make the chosen Blackjack future operate in summer rather than ending at a speech.

## Player Actions

Draft articles, resolve a first disciplinary case, assign logistics custody, and defend the headquarters from a final Koss loyalist action if needed.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Veterans disagree over profit shares, Lucia protects credit, ducal officers seek command, and predatory collectors seek old privileges.

## Information Paths

Member votes, service rolls, and the first case reveal whether the selected form can govern real conflict.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Enact free-company articles, cooperative bylaws, ducal corps rules, Medori regulations, or renewed private command.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed vote produces a smaller breakaway organization and visible vacancies rather than deleting the faction.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Final faction settlement. No result becomes setting canon.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
