# Lucia's Long Book

## Line Position

This is Blackjacks and Medori quest 4. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Late Autumn and Early Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Lucia asks the player to recover a long-term risk book stolen during grain unloading. It proves her humane-credit model is profitable and also lists households she will never release because they remain valuable.

Recover winter logistics while deciding what parts of reform deserve protection and what must become public.

## Player Actions

Search Grain Stairs, question customs clerks, track the thief through warehouse roofs, and read or return the book.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

The thief plans to sell routes to raiders; Lucia genuinely needs the book to move grain; Koss wants the debtor-value pages.

## Information Paths

Cargo tallies, roof scuffs, and customs interviews locate the thief. Separate book sections prove logistics and ownership policy.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return sealed, copy the debtor pages, or publish everything at the cost of disrupted credit.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the thief escapes with route pages, convoy ambushes increase but duplicated customs schedules preserve deliveries.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
