# Collections Day

## Line Position

This is Blackjacks and Medori quest 5. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Late Autumn and Early Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Koss sends collectors to seize mill animals after a bad harvest, even though doing so will stop winter flour. Lucia offers refinancing tied to future land control.

Keep food production alive and decide what a creditor may take from a functioning community.

## Player Actions

Audit grain and mill capacity, defend collectors from unrelated bandits, negotiate with households, and secure an enforceable payment plan.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Millers hide some stocks; collectors use violence; Lucia wants a viable claim; the Duke needs flour now.

## Information Paths

Millstones, granary tallies, and household rolls establish what can actually be paid without collapse.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Moratorium with oversight, land-backed refinance, selective seizure, or Koss' full collection.

## Fail-Forward Results

If animals are seized, the player can recover or replace them through a harder delivery. Hidden grain reduces trust but does not erase the village claim.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
