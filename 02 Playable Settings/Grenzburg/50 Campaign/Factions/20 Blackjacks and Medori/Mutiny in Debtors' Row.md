# Mutiny in Debtors' Row

## Line Position

This is Blackjacks and Medori quest 7. It belongs to the Alignment movement and is available during Deep Winter and Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Koss orders arrests after debtors refuse an execution-risk assignment. Brina organizes a work stoppage; Lucia offers amnesty if Koss retains operational command.

Choose who speaks for the company and whether owned specialists may refuse manifestly abusive orders.

## Player Actions

Protect assemblies, prevent weapons from reaching provocateurs, expose a staged attack, and negotiate or fight for control of the Yard.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Koss deliberately escalates. Lucia fears collapse of winter logistics. Some debtors want immediate violent revenge rather than governance.

## Information Paths

Order copies, armory counts, and witnesses identify the staged attack and distinguish organizers from provocateurs.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Back Brina's free-company track, Lucia's reformed Bank track, ducal absorption, or Koss' continued control.

## Fail-Forward Results

If violence erupts, the Yard remains capturable and survivors regroup in Lantern Market. Casualties alter legitimacy and later command.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Lock warning:** free-company, Medori-reform, ducal-absorption, and Koss-control tracks become mutually exclusive. Other faction lines stay open.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
