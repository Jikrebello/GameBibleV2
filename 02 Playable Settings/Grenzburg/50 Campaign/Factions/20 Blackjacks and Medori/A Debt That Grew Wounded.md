# A Debt That Grew Wounded

## Line Position

This is Blackjacks and Medori quest 3. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Open Autumn and Late Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A wounded Blackjack wakes to find treatment, missed shifts, and prosthetic rental have doubled her service term. Koss orders collection before she can petition.

Protect a specific debtor while deciding who bears the cost of injuries received on company work.

## Player Actions

Trace the mission report, interview healers, recover the damaged gear, and escort the petition through hostile collectors.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

The injury is real, treatment costly, and the company still needs replacement labor. Koss chooses compounding debt as discipline; Lucia offers capped extension rather than freedom.

## Information Paths

Mission orders, hospital treatment roll, and gear salvage prove the wound occurred in service.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Company injury pool, capped personal liability, or full compounding enforcement.

## Fail-Forward Results

A failed petition converts to a work stoppage and rescue from an unsafe reassignment. The debtor remains alive and named.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.
