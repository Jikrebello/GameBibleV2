# Purchase of Freedom

## Line Position

This is Blackjacks and Medori quest 8. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Early Spring and Muster Crisis.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The player and company can now settle their legal service before the Deep Muster, but every route assigns costs to someone.

End or redefine personal debt without making freedom a late epilogue reward only.

## Player Actions

Assemble service records, value surviving equipment, hear debtor preferences, and enact a collective or individual settlement.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Medori investors demand value, the Duke wants specialists, Brina resists trading one owner for another, and some debtors prefer stable Bank service.

## Information Paths

Quest records, injury rolls, and Lucia's long book determine lawful and equitable claims.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Chartered freedom, cooperative buyout, ducal absorption, regulated Medori service, or continued Koss control.

## Fail-Forward Results

If funds fall short, future paid contracts or ducal indemnity can bridge the gap with visible obligations. No route forces the player back into ownership after a valid release.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Confirms the quest-seven internal alignment. Exact company governance and continuing obligations are displayed.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## World-State Outputs

- `player_debt_freedom`
- `blackjack_legal_form`
- `medori_compensation`

## Rewards and Return Value

Can free the player before the main finale and sets the debt term for the political settlement.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.

## Seasonal Cutoff

If the player passes the named finale gate without resolving this stage, the selected or default institutional alignment produces a specified compromised summer settlement.
