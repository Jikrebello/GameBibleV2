# Faction Questlines MOC

This is the operational register for forty-eight authored faction quest notes. Four principal lines contain nine quests each. The underworld contains six shared quests and two exclusive three-quest branches, so forty-five faction quests are playable in one run.

## Lines

- [[Ducal and Marcher Service]]
- [[Blackjacks and Medori]]
- [[Church of Grenzburg]]
- [[Four-Hearth Compact]]
- [[Grenzburg Underworld]], branching to [[Low Lanterns]] or [[Black Bell]]

## Lock Rule

All five lines remain available through quest six. Quest seven begins an explicit internal alignment. Underworld quest six opens the exclusive branch warning; beginning either branch closes the other three quests. Cross-faction best terms can coexist if the player has earned them.
