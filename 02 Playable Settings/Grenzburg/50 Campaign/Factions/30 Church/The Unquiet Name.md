# The Unquiet Name

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 2. It belongs to the Admission movement and is available during Early Autumn and Open Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Residue repeatedly follows the wrong grave marker because a Grey Sweat copyist transposed two names. Families accuse each other of sacrilege.

Correct the burial record and settle the residue without inventing a trapped soul.

## Player Actions

Inspect residue behavior, compare parish copies, recover a washed-out marker, and perform a proper renaming rite.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A local notable wants the poorer family removed; frightened mourners interfere; ordinary residue lashes out at repeated disturbance.

## Information Paths

Chancery copy, coffin plate, and Long Field prayer roll establish the transposition.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Correct publicly with restitution, correct quietly, or destroy the marker and leave one family without proof.

## Fail-Forward Results

If residue dispersal fails, it reforms at the accurate name and remains containable. A damaged record can be reconstructed from two sources.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
