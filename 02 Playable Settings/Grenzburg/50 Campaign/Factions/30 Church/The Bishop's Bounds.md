# The Bishop's Bounds

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 8. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Early Spring and Muster Crisis.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Konrad must issue diocesan bounds before spring courts resume. Ignatius, Caestus, and Marcellina each propose a different enforcement structure.

Turn the jurisdiction choice into named offices, appeals, and safeguards.

## Player Actions

Review cases, appoint or reject officers, recover a destroyed appeal roll, and test the bounds during a real residue emergency.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure
