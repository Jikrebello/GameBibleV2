# Mercy in Winter

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 6. It belongs to the Fracture movement and is available during Deep Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A fever ward loses heat during bombardment while an evacuation team is trapped outside. Caestus asks the player to hold the hospital long enough for both groups to survive.

Stage a quiet, costly, unambiguously genuine White-Flame miracle inside practical rescue work.

## Player Actions

Repair heat, move patients, defend the cloister route, and choose where limited people and fuel go.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Weather, siege damage, disease, and panic are the opposition. No cult or fraud causes the crisis.

## Information Paths

Hospital charts, wall damage, and camp signals make needs legible.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Ask Bernard or another coherent servant to bear the rite, rely only on material rescue, or prioritize one ward. The White Flame answers genuine self-giving but does not replace labor.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the rite cannot occur, hard rescue still saves many at greater injury. If it occurs, the bearer suffers exhaustion and no institution gains automatic jurisdiction.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment; all five faction lines remain available through this quest.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
