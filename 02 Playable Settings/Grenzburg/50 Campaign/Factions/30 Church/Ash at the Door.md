# Ash at the Door

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 3. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Open Autumn and Late Autumn.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Ignatius marks several Chapel-Folk homes with ash after a real Menhir-linked danger is found nearby. He has evidence for one house and suspicion for the rest.

Stop a genuine threat without letting valid spiritual concern become collective punishment.

## Player Actions

Examine ash marks, track the actual dangerous object, protect households from a crowd, and confront Ignatius before his search becomes a purge.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A place-bound hazard is real; one household concealed it to avoid seizure; Ignatius expands guilt by association.

## Information Paths

Object residue, household interviews, and Runa's boundary knowledge isolate the danger.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Remove the object under bounded Church authority, grant ducal evidence oversight, or endorse broad search powers.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the object moves, its environmental signs lead to the next home. Crowd damage becomes restitution work.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

## Rewards and Return Value

Establishes Ignatius as severe and corrigible rather than secretly monstrous.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.
