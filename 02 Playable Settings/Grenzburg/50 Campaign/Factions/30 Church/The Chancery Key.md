# The Chancery Key

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 4. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Late Autumn and Early Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Marcellina loses a cipher key linking confessor reports to a network that has prevented several killings and coerced dozens of unrelated people.

Recover the key while deciding which intelligence can remain and which confessional misuse must end.

## Player Actions

Trace the courier route, rescue an exposed informant, enter a Chancery cache, and separate imminent-threat evidence from leverage files.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Criminals hunt the key; Marcellina believes coercion is justified by lives saved; Konrad fears public scandal.

## Information Paths

Courier marks, cache index, and protected-person testimony distinguish useful warnings from political blackmail.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return a purged system, preserve full surveillance under rules, or expose everything and end active protection.

## Fail-Forward Results

If criminals copy the key, named informants can be relocated. Destroyed files save privacy but remove later shortcuts.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

Makes surveillance a real protection problem rather than simple villainy or automatic necessity.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.
