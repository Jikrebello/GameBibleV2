# Jurisdiction of Fire

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 7. It belongs to the Alignment movement and is available during Deep Winter and Early Spring.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

After a genuine miracle and a real spiritual crime, Konrad claims Church trial authority over connected bribery, assault, and property offenses.

Define which truths justify sacred jurisdiction and which secular harms still belong to civil court.

## Player Actions

Build parallel case files, protect an accused cleric, examine the spiritual offense, and conduct a joint hearing.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Konrad sincerely fears civil judges cannot understand the sacred danger. Secular officials try to use jurisdictional limits to dismiss all doctrine.

## Information Paths

Confession, physical evidence, and rite examination separate spiritual culpability from ordinary crimes.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Back bounded jurisdiction, broad episcopal court, ducal supremacy over all matters, or an unstable joint arrangement.

## Fail-Forward Results

If hearings split, each court reaches a partial result and later settlement must reconcile conflict.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

**Lock warning:** bounded jurisdiction closes episcopal temporal rule; broad Church court closes full civil jurisdiction; total ducal control closes the strongest independent spiritual protections. Other faction lines remain open.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
