# A Relic Proven False

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 5. It belongs to the Service movement and is available during Early Winter and Deep Winter.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A popular winter relic produces heat and healings that appear miraculous. A delver claims it is an Atherian device and is attacked for fraud.

Prove what the object is without implying that a false relic makes the faith false or that sincere healings excuse deception.

## Player Actions

Test the object, trace its sale, protect witnesses, and present findings before Church and civic audiences.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

A relic custodian knowingly exaggerated provenance; desperate patients fear losing hope; Ignatius wants the delver punished for scandal.

## Information Paths

Warlock Hall measurements, sale records, and saint-register absence prove false provenance. Patient outcomes show ordinary heat aided care.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Expose and repurpose the device, preserve devotion with corrected teaching, or maintain the fraud for morale.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the device breaks, its plates still prove origin. Public disorder changes trust but the investigation continues.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

No exclusive commitment.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.
