# The Keys Returned

## Line Position

This is Church of Grenzburg quest 9. It belongs to the Settlement movement and is available during Muster Crisis and Summer Aftermath.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Church officers still hold cell, archive, and property keys granted during siege. The chosen settlement requires returning or legitimizing each one.

Make spiritual authority and secular restraint visible in summer institutions.

## Player Actions

Inventory keys, resolve Ignatius' last refusal or restoration, establish hospital governance, and enact the first postwar case.

## Opposition and Institutional Pressure

Konrad fears institutional humiliation; civil officers seek revenge; Marcellina wants protections for exposed witnesses.

## Information Paths

Key rolls, prisoner files, and hospital charters identify every power.

At least two routes establish every required fact. Social skill, cultural familiarity, background knowledge, and prior faction work change cost or interpretation rather than deciding whether the story continues.

## Choice or Mastery Point

Return secular keys under bounded diocese, retain episcopal jurisdiction, impose secular control, or leave an unresolved dual system.

## Fail-Forward Results

A missing key leads to a final recovery and one transformed case, not an undefined outcome.

Failure changes casualties, evidence, access, services, institutional stance, or the next mission. It never deletes the faction line without an authored result.

## Lock-In Consequences

Final faction settlement. No result becomes setting canon.

The warning screen names affected quests, leaders, services, and political-settlement terms before any exclusive commitment.

Changes courts, hospitals, patrols, sermons, and White-Flame support in summer.

Rewards include a faction rank or stance change, service access, equipment or training, and a visible world-state consequence. Exact numerical values remain provisional.

If the player passes the named finale gate without resolving this stage, the selected or default institutional alignment produces a specified compromised summer settlement.
