# **The Skeleton of Grenzburg: A Three-Act Overview**

## **Core Loop:** Every major decision involves navigating the **Rule of Three**: choosing between the **Duchy's Needs (Good)**, the **Immediate Threat (Bad)**, and the **Costly Aid of an Ugly Faction**. Each choice strengthens one faction, weakens another, and alters the resources and alliances available for the next crisis.

---

## **ACT I: AUTUMN (The Setup)**

**Goal:** Survive the Funnel, discover the unnatural Grobi threat, secure the **Heart-Stone**.
**Central Dilemma:** How do you balance saving people, obeying orders, and navigating factional greed?

### **Key Beats & Cascading Decisions:**

1.  **Prologue Quests:** Initial introductions to the Bank, Church, and Military. Your first reactions here tag you as leaning toward **Law, Faith, or Pragmatism**.

2.  **Three Village Missions:** Each settlement presents a microcosm of the Duchy's problems.
    *   **Lakewatch:** Choose between **Humanitarian Aid**, **Martial Law**, or **Folk Diplomacy**.
    *   **Highlake:** Choose between **Guild Profits**, **Folk Spirits**, or **Scientific Curiosity**.
    *   **Timberfalls:** Choose between **Cartel Power**, **Settler Lives**, or **Folk Vengeance**.
    *   **Outcome:** Your choices in each determine your **reputation** with the key power players (Strun, Katarina, Moss-Crown, Shattered-Tusk) and which village leaders (Jaromir, Berti, Gunther) will trust or resent you later.

3.  **Three Crises:** The scaled-up consequences of your village choices.
    *   **Crisis Choice:** Determines which faction you **build trust** with: **Sergeant Liese (Military), Magister Leviticus (Civil), or Prelate Caestus (Spiritual)**.
    *   **Mechanic:** Earning trust with all three is the only path to unlock the **Duke's Path** for the climax.

4.  **Barrow Climax: Who Gets the Heart-Stone?**
    *   **Default (Low Effort):** Goes to the faction whose mission you focused on (Bank or Church).
    *   **Unlocked (High Effort - Duke's Path):** Requires earning the three trusts above. The Duke intervenes, claiming it for the Duchy.
    *   **Wildcard (Folk Path):** Requires deep alliance with the Ghost-Foot Clan.
    *   **Consequence:** This decision determines **who holds the city's greatest weapon/liability** for Act II and defines your primary patron or enemy.

**Act I Output:** A city locked down for winter, a powerful artifact in the hands of a faction, and the player's primary alliance established.

---

## **ACT II: WINTER (The Siege)**

**Goal:** Survive the dual threats of the **Grobi Horde** and the **Emerald Drake**, prevent internal collapse.
**Central Dilemma:** How do you allocate dwindling resources and trust when the walls are cracking?

### **Key Beats & Cascading Decisions:**

1.  **Siege Mechanics:** The **Heart-Stone's location** (determined in Act I) now provides a **Faction Buff** and a **Faction Flaw** during the siege, shaping the feel of every battle.
    *   e.g., Bank = Efficient but Ruthless; Church = Zealous but Paranoid; Duke = Strategic but Burdensome.

2.  **Internal Siege Missions:** You deal with sabotage, crime, and intrigue inside the walls.
    *   **Decisions:** Do you enforce **Bank law**, **Church dogma**, or **Ducal justice**? Each choice deepens your alignment and reveals more about the **Pale-Skin infiltration**.

3.  **The Drake Hunt:**
    *   A major challenge that requires spending political or military capital.
    *   **Key Decision:** What do you do with the **Drake's Heart Gem**? Keep it, give it to a faction, or use it? This becomes your primary **counter-weapon** to the Heart-Stone in Act III.

4.  **Ghor's Assault & Revelation:**
    *   The siege's military climax. Defeating Ghor reveals the **Pale-Skins' true plan**: using the siege's death-energy to power their ritual.
    *   **Consequence:** You understand the stakes are higher than survival; it's about preventing an apocalypse.

5.  **The Theft Cliffhanger:**
    *   The Heart-Stone is stolen. **Who stole it is determined by who held it:**
        *   **Bank:** Stolen by the **Silent Partners** (internal corruption).
        *   **Church:** Stolen by **Ignatius** (religious fanaticism).
        *   **Folk:** Stolen by the **Pale-Skins** (outsider enemy).
        *   **Duke:** Stolen by a **Council Traitor** (deepest betrayal).
    *   This defines the **primary antagonist faction for Act III** and the nature of the investigation.

**Act II Output:** The Grobi horde is broken, but the Pale-Skins have won—they now possess the Heart-Stone. The city is physically saved but politically and spiritually shattered. You possess the Drake's Gem (the key to victory) and know the final battle is underground.

---

## **ACT III: SPRING (The Reckoning)**

**Goal:** Uncover the traitor, descend into the **Dragon's Tomb**, shatter the Pale-Skins' ritual.
**Central Dilemma:** Who do you trust to help you save the world, given everyone is compromised?

### **Key Beats & Cascading Decisions:**

1.  **The Investigation:** Based on the thief from Act II, you follow a unique **thread of clues** through the corrupt city.
    *   **Decision:** Your investigation method (covert, brutal, diplomatic) determines which **NPC allies** you have left to call upon for the final descent.

2.  **Assembling the Company:** You recruit your final dungeon party from remaining allies.
    *   **Mechanic:** Your **reputation with each faction** (earned across Acts I & II) determines who will answer your call and what their **price** is.
    *   **The Duke's Path:** If you've been consistently loyal to the Duchy's *idea* (not just a faction), you can unite fragments of all groups under the Ducal banner.

3.  **The Descent & Final Ritual:** The dungeon crawl through the **Dragon's Tomb**.
    *   **Challenge:** Combats are infused with the psychic corruption of the Heart-Stone. Your allies' special abilities (gained from their factions) are key to overcoming obstacles.
    *   **Climax:** Confrontation with the **Hollow-Kings**. They offer a **Faustian Bargain** for power, tailored to your journey's temptations (wealth, absolution, belonging, transcendence).

4.  **The Destruction of the Stone:**
    *   To win, you must sacrifice something of great value, usually the **Drake's Heart Gem** or a **trusted ally**.
    *   **Choice:** The method of destruction reflects your core allegiance (a financial transaction for the Bank, a sacrament for the Church, a natural balance for the Folk, a sovereign order for the Duke).

5.  **Epilogue - The New Peace:**
    *   The ending state of Grenzburg is a direct result of **who held the Stone at the end of Act I** and **which faction you sided with most consistently**.
    *   **Duke's Peace:** Reformed, sovereign state.
    *   **Bank's Peace:** Efficient, mercantile oligarchy.
    *   **Church's Peace:** Pious, communal theocracy.
    *   **Folk's Peace:** Decentralized, naturalistic frontier.
    *   **Your Reward:** Your place in this new world—from Warden to outcast—is defined by your choices.

---

## **The Skeletal Flowchart:**

**Act I (Autumn):** **Choose Village Approach** -> **Solve Crises to Build Trust** -> **Decide Stone's Holder** -> *Output: City Locked, Faction Empowered.*

**Act II (Winter):** **Manage Siege w/ Faction Buff/Flaw** -> **Secure Drake's Gem** -> **Learn True Threat** -> **Suffer Theft (based on Holder)** -> *Output: Horde Broken, Stone Lost, Gem Acquired.*

**Act III (Spring):** **Investigate Theft (path unique to Holder)** -> **Recruit Allies (based on Reputation)** -> **Descend & Destroy Stone (sacrificing Gem or Ally)** -> **Determine New World Order (based on Faction Alignment)**.

**Golden Thread (The Duke's Path):** The most difficult, "canon" path requires consistently choosing the **Duchy's long-term health** over any single faction's short-term gain in Acts I & II, uniting the state's pillars (Marshal, Steward, Chaplain) to secure the Stone and weather the siege, ultimately reforging a stronger, more independent Grenzburg. It's the path of the true nation-builder, demanding sacrifice, integrity, and a relentless focus on the greater good.
