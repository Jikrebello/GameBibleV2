# Player Statistics and Resolution

Grenzburg uses a compact set of combat and social statistics. Their meanings are canon; their exact curves, coefficients, and caps remain provisional.

## Combat Resources

| Statistic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Health** | Capacity to survive injury before defeat. Recoverable and lasting loss are distinguished through [[Health Recovery Afflictions and Encumbrance]]. |
| **Stamina** | Capacity for sustained exertion, weapon skills, climbing, sprinting, spell preparation, and resistance to exhaustion. |

## Offensive Statistics

| Statistic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Physical force applied through weapons, unarmed attacks, carrying, throwing, grappling, and bodily techniques. |
| **Magick** | Power and coherence applied through supernatural techniques. The interface uses `Magick`; ordinary setting prose continues to use `magic`. |
| **Stagger Power** | Ability to interrupt, unbalance, launch, pin, or knock down a target. It is distinct from raw injury. |

## Defensive Statistics

| Statistic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Physical Defence** | Resistance to bodily injury from weapons, impacts, creatures, and material hazards. |
| **Magick Defence** | Resistance to elemental, occult, sacred, and other supernatural harm without implying immunity to their moral or narrative consequences. |
| **Staying Power** | Resistance to interruption, forced movement, knockdown, loss of footing, and broken preparation. |

Stagger Power opposes Staying Power at the level of combat intent. The exact resolution formula belongs to later balancing.

## Social Statistics

| Statistic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Charisma** | Ability to press, persuade, misdirect, perform, command attention, and shape another person's immediate response. |
| **Wisdom** | Perception of motive, omission, emotion, habit, danger, and practical consequence. |
| **Intelligence** | Reasoning, recall, comparison, technical understanding, and useful application of cultural or background knowledge. |
| **Luck** | The second chance around an uncertain result: fortunate timing, an overlooked opening, a softened failure, or an unusually productive success. |

Charisma is not beauty, Wisdom is not moral goodness, Intelligence is not education alone, and Luck does not replace preparation.

## Growth Rules

- Character level is global.
- Vocation mastery is separate.
- The vocation active when the player gains a character level supplies that level's combat growth profile.
- Authored profiles favor particular statistics but never reduce the player to one viable number.
- Social focus creates passive priorities independent of combat vocation.
- Books, quests, teachers, relics, injuries, vows, and world states may create bounded permanent changes.
- A custom vocation uses one authored growth profile or a rounded-down blend of two mastered profiles.

Exact early-level acceleration, late-level tapering, caps, and percentage modifiers remain in [[Provisional Player Balance Appendix]].

## Resolution Principles

1. A statistic means the same thing in exploration, combat, and authored checks.
2. Preparation and circumstances may matter as much as raw value.
3. Failure changes cost, timing, danger, information, or position.
4. No hidden formula may contradict the displayed qualitative risk.
5. Knowledge can reveal a method without guaranteeing success at that method.
6. Luck modifies a result; it never silently substitutes for every other capability.
