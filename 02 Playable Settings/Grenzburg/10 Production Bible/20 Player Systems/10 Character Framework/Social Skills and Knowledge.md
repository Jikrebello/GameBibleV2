# Social Skills and Knowledge

Social play rewards preparation, attention, lived experience, and a chosen approach. It does not reduce every conversation to one universal speech statistic.

## Four Social Skills

| Skill | Governing statistic | What it does | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coercion** | Charisma | applies fear, rank, leverage, urgency, or credible consequence | make an impossible threat credible or erase later resentment |
| **Diplomacy** | Charisma | finds acceptable language, shared interest, reassurance, compromise, or public face | make incompatible interests disappear |
| **Deception** | Charisma | lies, misdirects, performs a role, conceals intention, or creates a false inference | rewrite evidence already known to the listener |
| **Insight** | Wisdom | reads omission, emotion, body language, practiced evasion, and likely motive | read minds or prove guilt by instinct alone |

Intelligence governs reasoned solutions and the useful application of cultural or background knowledge. Luck follows uncertain Charisma, Wisdom, or Intelligence results as a second-stage adjustment.

## Cultural Knowledge

The nine culture tags are Eclessian, Thalmyric, Sestaran, Jirahar, Ahl-e-Ahd, Folk, Ättar, Tarkhan, and Nsizwa.

A tag covers broad institutions, etiquette, history, material culture, religious registers, and common misunderstandings. Specific content must still use the correct realm, city, clan, tower, tribe, confession, or class. Cultural knowledge is neither ethnic essence nor automatic agreement.

## Active Check Sequence

1. The dialogue option identifies its skill or knowledge source.
2. The interface shows visible advantages or penalties.
3. The interface shows **Favored**, **Even**, **Risky**, or **Dire**.
4. The primary hidden resolution uses Charisma, Wisdom, or Intelligence as appropriate.
5. Luck then softens a failure or improves a success when its second-stage resolution succeeds.
6. The conversation moves into an authored consequence.

Luck cannot transform nonsense into truth. It may cause the right witness to arrive, make a guard dismiss rather than arrest, expose an additional detail, preserve dignity after failure, or turn a normal success into a better bargain.

## Passive Recognition

Passive checks may expose:

- a suspicious route or concealed entrance;
- rare materials or useful salvage;
- a contradiction in testimony;
- an optional objective;
- a cultural insult the player is about to commit;
- a safer method suggested by prior work;
- a clue that remains visible in the journal even if not immediately understood.

Passive failure never deletes the only route forward. Investigation supplies redundant evidence.

## Failure Standard

A failed check may change price, trust, alertness, timing, evidence, witness cooperation, faction interpretation, or available compromise. Repeating the same line does not reroll the same situation. The player may change circumstances, obtain evidence, use another approach, accept the result, or pursue a non-social route.
