# Health Recovery Afflictions and Encumbrance

Harm and recovery should support dangerous travel without making every injury a medical simulation. The player reads immediate recoverable harm, lasting loss, exhaustion, conditions, carried weight, and the value of reaching shelter.

## Health

Damage first reduces Health and establishes a recoverable portion of the loss. Quick curatives, brief aid, and some abilities restore only that recoverable portion. Lasting loss requires slower treatment, rest, substantial medicine, sacred aid, or return to a suitable service.

This distinction keeps food, camp, healers, hospitals, and safe routes relevant. Exact ratios and recovery speed remain provisional.

## Stamina and Exhaustion

Stamina supports sprinting, climbing, grabbing, heavy attacks, weapon skills, spell preparation, resisting creature movement, and other sustained exertion.

When exhausted, the player loses pace, footing, or readiness before recovering. Repeated emergency stimulants may restore function while creating later cost. Vocation techniques alter how Stamina is spent but do not abolish bodily limits.

## Affliction Families

| Affliction | Readable effect and setting interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Burning** | ongoing heat injury until extinguished, smothered, or treated |
| **Fear** | broken focus, retreat, hesitation, or lost aggression; courage and discipline resist it |
| **Torpor** | slowed action and response produced by cold, poison, occult pressure, or creature effect |
| **Frozen** | immobilizing ice and severe vulnerability to physical breakage without implying stopped time |
| **Blinded** | reduced sight, targeting, and situational awareness |
| **Melting** | corrosive physical damage from acid and related earth-water-fire expressions |
| **Stunned** | interrupted action, lost footing, or brief incapacity |
| **Shocked** | electrical disruption that may conduct through nearby bodies, water, or metal |
| **Petrification** | progressive mineralization that becomes lethal when completed and shattered |
| **Silence** | disruption of spoken, conducted, or prepared supernatural technique; it does not erase thought |
| **Cursed** | a bounded hostile pattern that lowers capability or resistance according to its actual source |
| **Sleep** | forced unconsciousness broken by harm, aid, time, or resistance |
| **Poison** | bodily harm over time with source-specific antidotes and resistance |

Affliction labels are gameplay families. Each creature, script, substance, or relic must still explain its actual cause.

## Consumable Families

- **Immediate curatives** restore only recoverable Health with a short field action.
- **Slow treatment** restores recoverable Health over time and requires vulnerable application.
- **Stamina supplies** restore exertion capacity and may be chained at later cost.
- **Antidotes** treat named affliction families; stronger preparations may briefly improve resistance.
- **Weapon preparations** apply bounded elemental or material effects.
- **Tonics** temporarily improve carrying, resistance, recovery, or another stated capability.

Consumables belong to material culture: herbs, distilled medicine, food, powder, salts, alchemy, script charge, relic preparation, and trade. They are not generic bottles of unexplained magic.

## Encumbrance

The controlled qualitative tiers are:

1. Very Light;
2. Light;
3. Average;
4. Heavy;
5. Very Heavy;
6. Over-encumbered.

Increasing load affects acceleration, Stamina use, climbing, swimming, recovery, noise, and some movement techniques. Strength, equipment, pack organization, companions, transport, and selected augments may shift thresholds.

Over-encumbered movement exists for short recovery and inventory decisions; it is not an efficient travel state. Wagons, boats, sleds, stores, and settlements remain meaningful because one person cannot carry a campaign's supplies.

## Enchantments and Equipment Powers

Weapons and armour may carry passive protections or bounded active effects. Active equipment powers use charges, rest, prepared batteries, or another stated source rather than ordinary Stamina alone. Their exact slot and recharge rules remain provisional in [[Equipment and Skill Loadouts]].

## Cosmological Limits

- Healing does not casually recall routed souls.
- Corpse residue is not a wounded person waiting for a potion.
- Petrification, curse, and silence require specific causes rather than one universal magic substance.
- White-Flame restoration, warlock counter-scripts, medicine, and ordinary rest solve different problems.
