# Shared Movement and Physical Interaction

Grenzburg's traversal begins with a capable human body in a difficult landscape. Every vocation can travel the critical route, enter required dungeons, and engage large creatures. Vocation movement creates speed, safety, positioning, and discovery rather than hard progression locks.

## Shared Kit

Every player character can:

- walk, run, sprint, crouch, and swim;
- jump, vault low obstacles, mantle reachable ledges, and climb authored surfaces;
- climb ladders, ropes, rubble, trees, scaffolds, and ordinary rock holds;
- slide down suitable slopes and use momentum without universal acrobatic wall-running;
- grab, carry, drag, brace, and throw suitable objects;
- hold, shove, restrain, or knock out appropriate human-sized targets;
- grab and climb large creatures;
- perform a non-lethal stealth knockout or a lethal takedown when position and target permit;
- help or be helped by companions at contextual obstacles.

Movement consumes Stamina according to exertion, load, weather, injury, and vocation advantages. Exact costs remain provisional.

## Large-Creature Climbing

Every vocation may mount a large creature, move across its body, strike exposed parts, hold against its motion, and interact with attached objects or wounds.

The shared rules prevent monster climbing from becoming a Slayer-only game. Specialists gain advantages:

- [[Slayer]] reaches targets faster, clings longer, and has the deepest mounted attack language.
- [[Hunter]] reads anatomy and exposed parts, creating better approach and weak-point choices.
- [[Fighter]] braces securely and can hold attention or defend while attached.
- [[Warlock]] uses short wand releases and elemental conditions from dangerous positions.
- [[Medium]] directs shades while repositioning and marks a priority body part or target.
- [[Lancewright]] marks physical surfaces, lunges through proof geometry, vaults with the spear, and Yields captured force.

## Vocation Movement Signatures

| Vocation | Distinct advantage |
|---|---|
| [[Fighter]] | braced advances, shield movement, controlled falls, and secure footing |
| [[Slayer]] | grappling knives, rapid climbing, wall-assisted movement, aerial redirection, and long evasions |
| [[Hunter]] | terrain reading, stable movement while aiming, evasive bow movement, and efficient pursuit |
| [[Warlock]] | brief air-assisted jumps and controlled descent through a prepared wand response, never sustained flight |
| [[Medium]] | short shadow-steps through open gaps and broken apertures, never sealed walls or locked doors |
| [[Lancewright]] | mark-directed lunges, spear vaults, aerial descents, and large-monster repositioning |
| [[Paladin]] | armoured interposition, ally boosts, and stable descents under load |
| [[Binder]] | temporary cord anchors and assistance from an eligible bound form |

## Worldspace Rule

Every critical route documented by [[Grenzburg Regional Geography]] must have a vocation-neutral path. Movement signatures may provide:

- a shorter ascent;
- a safer descent;
- a stealth entry;
- better opening position;
- hidden salvage or lore;
- an alternate large-creature solution;
- reduced travel risk.

They may not be the only way to reach a critical objective. [[Road Keys and Menhir Paths]] remain a separate travel system and do not depend on vocation.

## Grounded Limits

- There is no universal double jump or unrestricted wall run.
- Warlock lift and Medium shadow-step are bounded techniques, not flight or intangibility.
- Grappling requires a viable anchor and cannot pull arbitrary architecture apart.
- Carrying and climbing respond to [[Health Recovery Afflictions and Encumbrance|encumbrance]], injury, weather, and Stamina.
- Large creatures can shake, scrape, submerge, burn, freeze, crush, or throw climbers.
