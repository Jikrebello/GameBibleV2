# Proficiencies and Field Skills

Five trainable proficiencies govern practical actions shared across vocations. They are learned through instruction and improved through use. Background knowledge may identify a method or context; proficiency determines whether the player can perform the work cleanly under pressure.

## Controlled Proficiencies

| Proficiency | Field use | Typical failure consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Locksmithing** | opens locks, disarms mechanical traps, reads tampering, preserves a mechanism, or creates a quiet entry | noise, delay, broken tools, damaged contents, partial trigger, or need for another route |
| **Pickpocketing** | lifts small objects, plants evidence or tools, interferes with carried equipment, and performs close sleight of hand | suspicion, moved patrols, confrontation, false identification, or loss of access |
| **Sneaking** | controls visibility and noise, uses cover, performs knockouts or lethal takedowns, and moves through watched spaces | alert escalation, changed positions, pursuit, or fortified resistance |
| **Free Running** | improves sprint transitions, vaults, climbing speed, controlled falls, slides, and difficult urban movement | lost time, Stamina loss, injury, noise, or a longer route |
| **Tracking** | follows people and creatures, reads direction and age, identifies false trails, and predicts movement through terrain | delayed pursuit, incomplete identification, ambush risk, or reliance on another clue |

## Training Model

1. A trainer opens the first usable rank and demonstrates safe practice.
2. Field use develops familiarity.
3. Later ranks require both demonstrated use and a trainer, text, tool, or authored challenge.
4. A signature training quest tests judgement as well as execution.
5. Final rank counts, thresholds, and practice rates remain provisional.

No proficiency is raised with ordinary vocation mastery currency alone.

## Knowledge and Proficiency

The systems cooperate without collapsing into one another.

- Underworld knowledge may recognize the maker of a lock; Locksmithing opens it.
- Military knowledge may identify a sentry pattern; Sneaking crosses it.
- Wilderness knowledge may identify spoor; Tracking follows it through snow.
- Merchant/Craft knowledge may recognize a trapped strongbox; Locksmithing preserves its contents.
- Drifter/Vagabond knowledge may know where roof-runners cross; Free Running makes the crossing reliable.

## Stealth Outcomes

Every character can attempt a contextual stealth knockout or lethal takedown. Sneaking improves access, concealment, recovery, and effectiveness.

- Unarmed stealth takedowns are non-lethal unless equipment or a chosen action makes them lethal.
- A lethal takedown kills ordinary vulnerable targets and heavily damages larger or prepared foes; it is not a universal boss deletion.
- Non-lethal play remains possible but carries practical burdens: witnesses awaken, prisoners require custody, and enemies may return to the conflict.
- Companions and factions react to the conduct, not merely to whether the animation was labeled stealth.
