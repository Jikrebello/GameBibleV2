# Vocation Progression and Mastery

Grenzburg separates the growth of the person from mastery of a combat discipline. This lets the player change vocation without becoming a beginner in every human capacity while preserving the identity of authored kits.

## Two Growth Tracks

### Character Level

Character level records broad growth across the campaign. The vocation active when the level is gained supplies that level's combat-stat profile.

### Vocation Mastery

Vocation mastery records familiarity with one authored combat language. A portion of relevant experience earned while the vocation is active advances its mastery.

Mastery opens:

- ordinary core-skill improvements;
- active skill families;
- augments;
- advanced versions of known techniques;
- access to master instruction;
- account-wide parts for later custom vocation construction.

Exact rank counts and mastery costs remain provisional. The archived source's nine-rank structure remains a useful reference, not a locked production value.

## Field Lock

The player equips one authored or custom vocation at a time. An authored vocation uses only its native equipment, skills, and signature mechanic.

Vocation changes occur at safe reorganization points such as the Blackjack Yard, appropriate trainers, secure inns, long camps, and other explicitly supported hubs. The player cannot change vocation during immediate danger or inside a restricted encounter.

## Opening Other Starting Vocations

- Character creation grants one starting vocation.
- The city and early frontier introduce the other four through [[Vocation Training Network]].
- At least one alternate trainer is available before main chapter 5.
- All five starting vocations can be opened by the end of main chapter 6 without requiring faction allegiance.
- A basic opening may require instruction, demonstration, equipment, and a modest cost. Signature techniques require deeper mastery or authored training.

## Trainers and Masters

Ordinary mastery teaches routine development. Trainers and sources are required for:

- opening a new vocation;
- opening or testing a proficiency rank;
- signature techniques;
- late Warlock master scripts;
- unusual augments;
- advanced vocations;
- methods whose moral or physical danger cannot responsibly be learned through repetition alone.

Teachers can be institutions, named masters, manuals, relic disciplines, recovered scripts, or companion instruction. This wave fixes routes and institutions; named trainer stories come later.

## Advanced Vocations

- [[Paladin]] opens during Bernard's Act II finale and receives its final sacred doctrine during his Act III finale.
- [[Binder]] opens during Zafir's Act II finale and receives its final doctrine during his Act III finale.
- [[Bard]] opens during Ilyana's Act II finale and receives its master counter-cadence during her Act III finale.
- [[Lancewright]] opens during Tavio's Act II finale and receives its mass-Yield capstone during his Act III finale.

No advanced vocation is required to finish the critical path.

## Custom Vocation Gate

After two authored vocations are fully mastered, [[Custom Vocation Workshop]] becomes available in the main menu and at safe vocation-change points. It is a metagame system rather than an institution in the fiction.

## Account Progression

Once unlocked, mastered weapons, armour categories, skills, augments, advanced-vocation parts, and saved custom blueprints become account-wide starting material for future new games.

Paladin, Binder, Bard, and Lancewright require their companion quests for the first account unlock. After that first completion, the earned vocation and its unlocked parts may be selected at supported change points in later new games. This is metagame access; it does not rewrite the new protagonist's prior relationship with Bernard, Zafir, Ilyana, or Tavio.

This account access does not rewrite the current campaign's history. A later character may begin with a custom discipline because the player earned the option, not because Grenzburg suddenly contains a universal custom-vocation academy.

## Superseded Rule

The former active rule allowing reversible pure or two-vocation hybrid profiles is retired. Authored vocations remain complete field-locked kits. Cross-vocation recombination happens only through a custom vocation.
