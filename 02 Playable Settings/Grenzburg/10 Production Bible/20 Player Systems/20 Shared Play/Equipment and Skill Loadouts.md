# Equipment and Skill Loadouts

Equipment expresses a vocation's native combat language. Authored vocations remain field-locked to their own weapons, armour, active skills, and signature mechanics. Free recombination belongs to [[Custom Vocation Workshop]].

## Authored Vocation Rule

| Vocation | Native equipment |
|---|---|
| [[Fighter]] | one-handed sword, war axe, or mace; shield; heavy armour |
| [[Slayer]] | paired daggers or short swords; grappling knives; light armour |
| [[Hunter]] | longbow; light field armour |
| [[Warlock]] | paired war-wands; robes or light armour |
| [[Medium]] | medium's rod; robes or light armour |
| [[Rune Fist]] | paired inscribed gauntlets; medium armour |
| [[Paladin]] | great weapon; heavy armour; sacred implements appropriate to the technique |
| [[Binder]] | inscribed rings and true-name cords; robes or light armour |

Unlocking Fighter does not let an authored Warlock equip a shield. Mastered parts become available for a custom vocation once the builder is unlocked.

## Core, Active, and Augment Layers

- **Core skills** define basic attacks, guard or evasion, movement interaction, and the signature mechanic of the equipped vocation.
- **Active skills** are selected techniques used through the native weapon or implement.
- **Augments** are learned passive principles. Their exact cross-vocation use belongs to the custom builder rather than authored vocation loadouts.
- **Equipment powers** belong to a particular weapon, armour piece, relic, or instrument and function only while that item is equipped.

Each vocation supports a bounded active loadout. The number of slots, alternate loadout switching, controller mapping, and combat swap timing remain provisional.

## Weapon Construction and Enchantment

Weapons may differ through material, balance, edge or head, grip, fittings, workmanship, provenance, and bounded enchantment. These features can change weight, stagger, reach, durability, resistance, or one special behavior.

Passive enchantments remain active while equipped. Active enchantments consume a stated charge, prepared battery, rest-limited use, relic condition, or other concrete source. A sword that releases fire must contain or conduct a real effect; it does not acquire an unexplained daily spell because the loot table wanted one.

## Armour

Armour proficiency represents movement, maintenance, fitting, endurance, and safe use rather than permission to recognize an item. Wearing an unauthorised kit is reserved for custom vocation construction and later production rules.

Armour can provide:

- physical and Magick Defence;
- Staying Power;
- affliction resistance;
- insulation or grounding;
- altered encumbrance;
- passive or charged equipment effects;
- social recognition when its livery or construction is legible.

## Field Inventory

The player carries a bounded quick-access set of curatives, antidotes, ammunition, preparations, and equipment powers. The full inventory supports planning outside immediate danger.

The source proposal for cycling quick-slot families and two skill layouts is preserved in [[Provisional Player Balance Appendix]]. It is not yet a locked input scheme.

## Loot and Mastery

Equipment can support a build without replacing mastery. A rare weapon may open a new tactic, but it does not teach the underlying vocation or bypass its moral and metaphysical requirements.

- A relic cannot make an incoherent character a functioning [[Paladin]].
- A stolen cord cannot teach responsible [[Binder]] practice.
- War-wands do not make an untrained bearer a [[Warlock]].
- A Medium's rod does not contain dead people's souls.
