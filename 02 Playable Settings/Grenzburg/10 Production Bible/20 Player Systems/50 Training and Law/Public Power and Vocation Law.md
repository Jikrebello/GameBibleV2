# Public Power and Vocation Law

## Controlling Principle

Vocation names are player-facing umbrellas. Grenzburg's inhabitants judge visible instruments, licenses, affiliations, conduct, and consequences. They do not identify a menu class floating above the protagonist.

The law distinguishes carrying, training, threatening, reckless use, trespass, assault, desecration, and homicide. Equipping an unusual vocation is not itself a universal crime state.

## The Blackjack Warrant

The player's Blackjack service gives a narrow emergency warrant to carry specialist equipment, cross selected closures, investigate assigned threats, and use proportionate force in ducal operations. It does not provide:

- immunity from ordinary crimes;
- permission to seize property without cause;
- automatic entry to private, sacred, or Folk-restricted places;
- a warlock license for every possible script;
- moral permission to imitate the dead;
- authority to bind persons;
- protection from faction, companion, or public judgement.

Specific assignments can widen the warrant for a time. The interface states that authority before the player commits the relevant act.

## Instruments and Evidence

The world reacts to what can be seen:

- heavy war gear in a courtroom;
- drawn paired blades on a roof;
- a nocked hunting arrow in a market;
- active war-wand inscriptions near timber buildings;
- a shade using a recognizable human silhouette;
- glowing counter-runes on fitted gauntlets;
- White Flame displayed while making a threat;
- a conscious being held inside visible Binder cords.

Holstered, sheathed, dismissed, cooled, or properly sealed equipment reduces alarm. It does not erase evidence of earlier harm.

## Jurisdiction

- **Ducal law** controls walls, roads, military closures, public peace, and the emergency warrant.
- **Civic and guild authority** controls markets, workshops, fire rules, trade instruments, and many damages.
- **Church jurisdiction** controls its sacred sites, offices, relic custody, and ecclesiastical discipline; it does not automatically own every supernatural case.
- **Folk place-law and kindred custom** govern valid sanctuaries, seasonal roads, burial places, and local compacts where those claims are established.
- **Bank service discipline** controls Blackjack employment and equipment accounts; it cannot legalize crimes against outsiders merely by recording them as work.

The same act may create several consequences without turning every quest into a hearing. The relevant authority depends on place, victim, office, and proof.

## Narrative Enforcement

Law should produce authored play rather than constant nuisance interruption.

- Ordinary weapon carrying receives contextual reactions, not repeated stops.
- Restricted spaces state their rules through guards, signs, companions, or prior dialogue.
- Serious witnessed offences can create pursuit, fines, confiscation attempts, loss of access, testimony, faction consequences, or a dedicated resolution quest.
- Unwitnessed acts may still leave bodies, damage, missing goods, magical residue, or betrayed confidences.
- The Medium's legal danger appears through specific people and situations; simply selecting the vocation does not summon a guard encounter loop.
- The player receives a clear warning before a publicly catastrophic or jurisdiction-changing use where the character would understand the risk.

## Moral Boundary

Law and morality overlap imperfectly. A licensed action can remain evil; an illegal rescue can remain good. The player may accept legal consequence for right action, exploit legal protection for wrong action, expose corrupt enforcement, or discover that an initially distrusted restriction protects a real good.

No reputation bar decides objective truth. Facts, intention, means, foreseeable harm, and chosen response remain central.
