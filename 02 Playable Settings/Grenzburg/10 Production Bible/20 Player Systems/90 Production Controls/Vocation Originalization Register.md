# Vocation Originalization Register

## Purpose

The archived [[Game Lore Bible]] contains useful combat functions and direct source-derived names, especially from Dragon's Dogma. Those functions may inform private design, but no uncleared name, description, animation identity, combination, or progression sequence may enter public-facing or implementation-ready material.

This register is mandatory. `It is only a placeholder` is not clearance.

## Source Record

- [Dragon's Dogma Fighter skill reference](https://dragonsdogma.fandom.com/wiki/Fighter)
- [Dragon's Dogma Hundred Kisses reference](https://dragonsdogma.fandom.com/wiki/Hundred_Kisses)
- Archived internal source: [[Game Lore Bible]]

These links document comparison targets; they are not implementation specifications.

## Clearance States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `placeholder` | direct or close source language remains; author-only use only |
| `review` | function is substantially reframed, but presentation and full comparison remain |
| `clear` | name, function bundle, world basis, presentation, and progression are independently expressed |
| `retired` | source function will not be used |

No vocation or skill family with `placeholder` status may be marked public, game-ready, or implementation-ready.

## Known Placeholder Families

### Fighter

`Blink Strike`, `Burst Strike`, `Skyward Lash`, `Heavenward Lash`, `Tusk Toss`, `Antler Toss`, `Flesh Skewer`, `Soul Skewer`, `Compass Slash`, `Full Moon Slash`, `Downthrust`, `Broad Cut`, `Clairvoyance`, `Arc of Might`, `Shield Drum`, `Shield Summons`, `Cymbal Onslaught`, `Shield Charge`, `Stone Will`, and related augment names require replacement or retirement.

### Slayer

`Scarlet Kisses`, `Hundred Kisses`, `Biting Wind`, `Cutting Wind`, `Instant Reset`, `Masterful Kill`, `Dazzle Blast`, `Gale Harness`, `Powder Charge`, and all inherited dagger and grappling upgrades require replacement or substantial recomposition.

### Hunter

`Deathly Arrow`, `Elevenfold Flurry`, `Gamble Draw`, `Cloudburst Volley`, `Downpour Volley`, `Keen Sight`, `Eagle Sight`, `Tempest Arrows`, `Bow Trance`, `Rebound Shot`, and the inherited progression order require replacement or substantial recomposition.

### Warlock

The twelve single-script names are provisional. Mixed labels including `Laser Beam`, `Gatling Gun`, `Dragon's Egg`, `Electric Fence`, and `Mortar Shell` are production shorthand and cannot survive. All four master-spell names and their source descriptions require complete original treatment.

### Medium

`Miasma`, `Lassitude`, `Silentium`, `Exequy`, `Petrification`, `Funnel Trail`, `Sacrificial Bolt`, `Perdition`, `Sopor`, and inherited augment labels require review. Generic dictionary terms may ultimately remain only where their final function and presentation are independently designed.

### Bard

The design may retain general functions such as area healing, elemental boons, counter-performance, sustained support, and a secondary resource. It may not reuse distinctive Dragon's Dogma, Dragon's Dogma Online, Dungeons & Dragons, or Pathfinder skill names, tooltip language, exact progression order, animation identity, or full kit grouping. `Harbor Fog`, `Common Breath`, `Mending Mist`, `Countervoice`, and other active labels remain author-only working names until prototype review.

## Originalization Test

A skill family becomes `clear` only when it passes all of the following:

1. **World basis:** its function follows a Grenzburg vocation, instrument, material, or metaphysical rule.
2. **Combat purpose:** it solves a defined player problem rather than filling a copied slot.
3. **Input and animation:** its timing, body use, camera need, and contact are independently designed.
4. **Progression:** its upgrade path expresses the vocation's own mastery rather than mirroring a source list.
5. **Language:** its final name and description do not retain distinctive source phrasing.
6. **Combination:** its surrounding kit produces a different decision rhythm, even if one isolated action is familiar.
7. **Public comparison:** a reasonable player does not read the complete package as a renamed source vocation.

## Description Rule

Author notes describe intended function in concise original prose. They do not paste or lightly paraphrase source tooltips. Final public text will be written only after the mechanics and world expression are cleared.

## Release Gate

Before a vocation becomes implementation-ready:

- every active and passive family has a register entry;
- placeholder names are gone from public notes, user interface, data, and dialogue;
- source-comparison review is complete;
- final animations and effects have their own silhouette and timing;
- the vocation still passes its lore safeguards;
- the dossier's `originalization_status` is `clear`.
