# Player Systems MOC

This is the doorway to Grenzburg's narrative player systems. [[Player Character Constitution]] controls the protagonist and the non-negotiable rules. The notes below explain what those rules mean for character creation, field play, progression, training, and the world.

Exact damage, frame data, input mappings, probability curves, slot budgets, and final numerical balance are not canon here. They remain in [[Provisional Player Balance Appendix]] until production testing.

## Fixed Rules

1. Combat is third-person action combat.
2. The player equips one authored or custom vocation at a time.
3. Authored vocations retain their native equipment and skill language.
4. Global character level and vocation mastery are separate.
5. The active vocation supplies level-up growth.
6. Every vocation can complete every critical route.
7. Custom vocations are a metagame feature, not an in-world school.
8. Medium shades are non-conscious imprints, never captured souls.
9. White-Flame techniques require moral coherence.
10. Human souls and coerced persons cannot become responsible Binder assets.

## Source Boundary

The imported [[Game Lore Bible]] is archived source material. Its statistics, proficiencies, movement intentions, and vocation functions informed this system. Its later peoples, cosmology, creatures, companions, and world history did not enter active canon.
