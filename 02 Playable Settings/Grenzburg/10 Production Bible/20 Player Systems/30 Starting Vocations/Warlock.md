# Warlock

## Player Fantasy

The Warlock is a mobile battlefield engineer who writes elemental instructions under pressure. Two war-wands let the player prepare a shape with one hand, qualify or bind it with the other, and decide how close to overload they are willing to work.

The vocation rewards preparation, positioning, elemental knowledge, deliberate combinations, and the nerve to use dangerous power without losing control of it.

## In the World

`Warlock` is a player-facing umbrella for several regulated schools descended from pre-imperial rune craft. Grenzburg's public route runs through the [[Hammer Ward#Anchor Locations|Warlock Hall]], where scripts are taught as repeatable engineering rather than spontaneous sorcery. Marcher artillery crews, mine technicians, Vortharan schools, shipboard weather crews, and private masters use different terminologies and licensing traditions.

All responsible practice follows [[Warlock Rune Magic]] and [[Elemental Shell and the Four Planes]]. A warlock does not create an element from imagination. They inscribe and conduct a bounded instruction through the Elemental Shell.

## Native Kit

| Element | Warlock rule |
|---|---|
| Weapons | paired war-wands |
| Armour | robes or light armour |
| Primary statistics | Stamina, Magick, Magick Defence |
| Signature interaction | script binding and Arcane Burn management |
| Movement advantage | brief wand-assisted fall control and directed recoil steps |

War-wands are paired because one establishes the working and the other checks, qualifies, or closes it. A broken or mismatched pair can still release force, but it makes every script less stable.

## Script Grammar

The starter kit uses four elemental families and three delivery shapes.

| Family | Material relation | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | heat, ignition, expansion | pressure, denial, burning, launch |
| Water and ice | cooling, adhesion, crystallization | slowing, footing, cover, pinning |
| Air and lightning | charge, pressure, conduction | interruption, chaining, displacement |
| Earth and acid | mineral suspension, erosion, reaction | armour damage, blindness, persistent ground control |

| Shape | Battlefield promise |
|---|---|
| Spray | short or middle-range continuous projection and close control |
| Missile | aimed release against a target or point |
| Field | a bounded wall, cloud, slick, spike, or persistent area |

Each family begins with one script in each shape. Later mastery improves the script, changes its material behavior, or lets it be bound to a second script of the same shape.

## Core Combat Loop

1. Read terrain, materials, weather, and enemy resistances.
2. Establish a safe angle or create space with basic wand force.
3. Select an elemental family and delivery shape.
4. Cast a single script or bind two compatible scripts of the same shape.
5. Exploit the physical result rather than treating damage as the only purpose.
6. Vent Arcane Burn through basic attacks, grounding, movement, or withdrawal.

Two-script bindings produce one coherent mixed effect. They do not stack every property of both elements without cost. The final function is authored and must remain physically legible.

## Arcane Burn

Every conducted script leaves charge, heat, vibration, or contradictory instruction in the wands and caster. This accumulated strain is **Arcane Burn**.

- Low Burn permits safe preparation.
- Working Burn strengthens basic wand releases and makes rapid bindings possible.
- High Burn begins to harm Health and Stamina and makes scripts physically unstable.
- Overdraw releases an uncontrolled local backlash, empties the caster's Stamina, and may inflict an elemental affliction.

Basic wand attacks bleed accumulated Burn into simple force. Rest, grounding tools, safe water, prepared mineral beds, and trained breathing also reduce it. Magick Defence reflects the caster's ability to survive and regulate exposure; it does not make overdraw harmless.

## Master Spells

A master spell binds all three shapes of one family into a dangerous battlefield working. It is not an ordinary final-rank purchase.

- one master spell may be earned through a late-game quest capstone for each elemental family;
- the quest must secure a lost proof, master, apparatus, or safe method appropriate to that family;
- using a master spell creates severe Burn, terrain danger, and public consequences;
- master spells cannot open the Shell, create permanent anchors, or improvise effects beyond their fixed script;
- the working titles `Helios Cataclysm`, `Absolute Zero`, `Pandora's Abyss`, and `Divine Maelstrom` require replacement.

## Mastery Shape

- **Opening:** basic wand force, one script from each family, Burn reading, and emergency venting.
- **Practiced:** improved single scripts, terrain interactions, quick shape selection, and first mixed bindings.
- **Veteran:** broad two-script library, counterscripts, cast interruption, environmental conduction, and controlled high-Burn play.
- **Master:** advanced binding doctrine, superior grounding, and eligibility to earn master spells through their quests.

## Augment Themes

Burn capacity, Burn venting, elemental resistance, casting composure, script preparation, affliction strength, Magick Defence, Magick, and safe environmental interaction.

Imported labels such as `Smoulder`, `Injection`, `Anisotropy`, `Acclimatization`, and `Magnitude` remain placeholders.

## Training and Reputation

The public training path begins at the Warlock Hall. Upper Grenz military sites teach siege counters and safe demolition, while [[Old River Arsenal]] and [[Cold-Iron Deeps]] can support advanced field instruction. The vocation is respected where licensed, feared near crowded timber districts, and treated harshly when its users conceal unsafe scripts.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- Elemental scripts are tools; the wielder remains responsible for foreseeable fire, collapse, poisoning, and civilian harm.
- A successful script is not evidence of divine favor or moral permission.
- Reckless overdraw can scar the caster, bystanders, architecture, and local Shell conditions.
- Warlocks cannot summon persons from the Shell because pure Shell patterns are not persons.
- Patron bargains, White Flame, Menhir acts, and contract magic are different systems and cannot be reproduced by clever elemental syntax.
