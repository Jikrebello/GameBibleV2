# Slayer

## Player Fantasy

The Slayer is a mobile close-range specialist who controls approach, vertical position, and the moment of commitment. Paired blades punish weak points while grappling knives turn enemies, ledges, loose objects, and the battlefield itself into movement tools.

## In the World

`Slayer` is a player-facing umbrella. Delvers, scouts, climbers, bounty hunters, roof-runners, infiltrators, monster killers, criminals, and deniable soldiers may share its techniques without sharing a profession or moral code.

Grenzburg's training routes run through the [[Lantern Ward#Anchor Locations|Blackjack Yard]], the [[Old Market#Anchor Locations|Delvers' Arcade]], quarry climbers, Lantern Ward contacts, and the [[Grenzburg Underways]]. Learning the vocation does not make the player an assassin; using it for murder remains a choice.

## Combat Grammar

1. Read the target and route.
2. Create or exploit an angle.
3. Close rapidly, often through a grapple.
4. Concentrate attacks on one weak point.
5. Reset, evade, or redirect before retaliation.
6. use powder, dazzle, pull, or terrain when direct pressure becomes unsafe.

Slayer survives through position and timing. It is deliberately poor at standing inside prolonged frontal pressure.

## Core Skill Families

- **Paired carve:** rapid alternating blade chain, including aerial use.
- **Twofold heavy:** concentrated paired stab followed by a turning cut at higher mastery.
- **Evasive roll:** immediate physical reposition and recovery.
- **Controlled fall:** reduces landing harm and wake-up time.
- **Grappling shot:** chooses between drawing the player to an anchor and drawing a viable target or object toward the player.

The grapple requires a physical anchor, line, and load the equipment can bear. It does not target empty air or arbitrarily dismantle architecture.

## Mastery Shape

- **Opening:** paired blade chain, roll, grapple approach, focused flurry.
- **Practiced:** aerial control, target pulling, dazzle, powder, climbing attacks, and quick recovery.
- **Veteran:** multi-target line work, mounted weak-point sequences, large-creature destabilization, and terrain dislodgement.
- **Master:** one signature counter and one advanced grappling doctrine earned from a specialist route.

## Augment Themes

Sprint efficiency, fall safety, climb speed, maximum Stamina, exhaustion recovery, aerial Strength, clinging endurance, mobility under load, and danger recognition at low Health.

## Training and Reputation

The same movements may look heroic on a Drake, practical in a ruin, or criminal on a roof. Guards and ordinary people judge tools through context. The Blackjack warrant explains why the player may carry grappling knives; it does not excuse burglary or assassination.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- A lethal stealth option remains deliberate killing.
- Explosives endanger bystanders and structures.
- Grappling a person can maim or kill even when the skill is framed as control.
- The vocation does not grant supernatural invisibility or a universal instant kill.
