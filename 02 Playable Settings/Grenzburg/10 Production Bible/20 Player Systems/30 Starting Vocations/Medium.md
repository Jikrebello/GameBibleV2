# Medium

## Player Fantasy

The Medium is a battlefield director who marks priorities, shapes hostile space, and commands a small persistent roster of shadow imprints. It rewards preparation and attention: the player chooses which defeated forms are worth recording, when to commit them, and when to sacrifice or replace them.

The vocation should feel unsettling without asking the player to command imprisoned people.

## In the World

`Medium` is an imprecise player-facing umbrella for practices that most Eclessian authorities condemn as corpse imitation, illicit shadow craft, or both. Grenzburg's Blackjacks shelter a narrow field discipline because it is useful against monsters and residue. That protection prevents routine guard harassment during ordinary play; it does not create public acceptance or excuse desecration.

Training occurs through clandestine contacts around the [[Lantern Ward#Anchor Locations|Blackjack Yard]] and the [[Grenzburg Underways]]. Cultural reactions appear in dialogue, prices, trust, testimony, access, and quest choices rather than a constant arrest interruption.

## What a Shade Is

A combat shade is a **non-conscious behavioral imprint** cast through the Shadowlands and held as a bounded pattern in a prepared rod. It can reproduce silhouette, movement preference, attack rhythm, and limited tactical responses from a defeated form.

A shade is not:

- the creature or person returned to life;
- a human soul delayed from its route;
- a memory-complete personality;
- a speaking witness to the dead person's experience;
- proof that corpse residue contains a person.

The original soul has routed according to [[Soul Routing and the Vestibule]]. The Medium captures a temporary combat relation, not the self that once enacted it.

The rod stores the roster while shades are dismissed. It is a working instrument rather than a prison or phylactery.

## Shade Roster

The player maintains a limited persistent roster between encounters.

- **Lesser imprint:** small form or simple behavior; low capacity cost.
- **Greater imprint:** human-sized or tactically complex form; moderate capacity cost.
- **Massive imprint:** large creature relation; high capacity cost and restricted deployment space.

The roster records a combat template, provenance, and broad behavior. It does not preserve an individual's name or identity. A new imprint may replace an old one, and some techniques expend an imprint entirely.

Persons cannot be intentionally killed to farm superior shades without moral, legal, and companion consequences. Mechanical usefulness does not neutralize the murder that produced the body.

## Core Combat Loop

1. Bring forth a selected roster around the player.
2. Mark a priority target or threatened zone with the rod.
3. Use shadow lashes and fields to shape enemy movement.
4. Let different imprints apply their limited behaviors.
5. Reposition, dismiss, or expend a shade before it is destroyed.
6. Record a new eligible imprint after combat when the remains and conditions permit.

Shades do not solve exploration, testify, perform ordinary labor, converse, or make independent moral decisions.

## Core Functions

- **Shadow lash:** a repeatable ranged strike from the rod.
- **Priority mark:** directs shades toward one target or position.
- **Bring forth:** deploys the selected roster from the rod.
- **Dismiss:** recalls surviving imprints and protects them from ordinary travel hazards.
- **Record imprint:** takes a bounded combat relation from suitable fresh residue.
- **Field shaping:** creates shadowed areas that inflict pressure, fear, sleep, silence, impaired sight, or slowed movement through non-personal effects.
- **Roster expenditure:** sacrifices one or more imprints to power a decisive release.

## Mastery Shape

- **Opening:** lash, priority mark, one lesser imprint, bring forth, dismiss, and one simple field.
- **Practiced:** larger roster, improved commands, controlled recording, multiple fields, and safer shadow movement.
- **Veteran:** greater and massive imprints, roster substitution, coordinated behaviors, stronger zone control, and deliberate expenditure.
- **Master:** a signature roster doctrine, reliable resistance to personality bleed, and one rare imprint-binding technique taught by an illicit master.

## Augment Themes

Roster capacity, imprint durability, field duration, marking speed, stealth, affliction pressure, Magick, Stamina, and resistance to Shadowlands feedback.

Imported labels such as `Reanimation`, `Morbidity`, `Reinforcement`, `Retribution`, and `Restoration` remain working names only.

## Training and Reputation

The Blackjack route teaches evidentiary handling, residue distinction, emergency dismissal, and rules against soul claims. Underways teachers may know stronger techniques while holding worse ethics. A player who publicly flaunts shades in a chapel, uses a known person's likeness to terrorize family, or harvests people as resources should meet specific narrative consequences.

The game does not convert those consequences into routine guard interruption every time the vocation is equipped.

## Ashira's Field Doctrine

[[Ashira bath Melan]] demonstrates a companion-specific ethical and tactical route through the vocation. Her campaign progression adds [[Ashira Shadow Roster Package|Pattern Known]], Hush Field, Witness Circle, and the No Human Shadow capstone without redefining the player Medium.

Her strongest doctrine keeps legitimate nonhuman combat imprints while refusing to store or deploy recognizable human likeness as equipment. This is a moral and custodial discipline, not proof that human imprints contain souls. The [[Ashira Companion Campaign]] determines her conduct and access to the Shadow Witness custom-vocation blueprint.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- **Personality bleed:** repeated use of one imprint can make its movement priorities feel natural to the caster.
- **Imitative confidence:** a useful likeness tempts the Medium to speak as if they know the dead person's mind.
- **Shadow attention:** repeated projection may make local shadow phenomena easier to organize around the caster.
- **Desecration:** the absence of an imprisoned soul does not make every use of remains respectful or lawful.
- **Instrumental murder:** killing to acquire an imprint remains murder.

No Medium technique routes souls, raises a person, creates a conscious servant, or turns the Shadowlands into a universal explanation for undead phenomena.
