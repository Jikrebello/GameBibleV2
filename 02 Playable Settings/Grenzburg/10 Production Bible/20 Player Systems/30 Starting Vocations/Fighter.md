# Fighter

## Player Fantasy

The Fighter is an armoured line-holder who makes danger face them on chosen terms. It rewards reading attacks, controlling space, defending companions, breaking enemy balance, and turning a perfect guard into decisive violence.

## In the World

`Fighter` is a player-facing umbrella, not an international order. Marcher sergeants, caravan guards, noble retainers, shield guilds, town watches, duelists, mercenaries, and veteran militia may all teach part of its language.

Grenzburg's clearest local expressions come through the [[Tannward#Anchor Locations|Marcher Barracks]], garrison veterans, Fort Tannbruck survivors, and practical weapon masters attached to the ducal service. None speaks for every culture's armed tradition.

The three main-hand families share the vocation's skill grammar while differing in reach, edge, armour pressure, stagger, and available equipment powers.

## Combat Grammar

1. Establish a defended position.
2. Draw or intercept enemy commitment.
3. Deflect, absorb, or step through the attack.
4. Break balance with shield, body, or weapon.
5. Convert the opening into a pin, launch, heavy strike, or protected advance.
6. Choose whether to remain the line or move it.

The Fighter is durable because it acts correctly under pressure, not because armour permits the player to ignore every attack.

## Core Skill Families

- **Weapon chain:** reliable one-handed sequences with distinct sword, axe, and mace contact.
- **Committed thrust or blow:** a direct heavy attack that becomes a downward strike in the air.
- **Guard:** broad damage prevention with a perfect timing window for deflection and stagger.
- **Moving guard:** a short protected advance that clears light bodies and closes a threatened gap.
- **Controlled fall:** a trained landing that reduces injury and recovery time.

## Mastery Shape

- **Opening:** guard, weapon chain, direct heavy, one approach skill, one shield interruption.
- **Practiced:** anti-air, crowd response, reliable counters, protected movement, and weapon-family distinctions.
- **Veteran:** pins, mobile guarding, large-creature bracing, stronger ally protection, and heavy commitment skills.
- **Master:** a signature counter doctrine and the highest charged blow, earned through a weapon master rather than ordinary repetition alone.

## Augment Themes

Health, carry threshold, shield Stamina, perfect-guard timing, recoverable Health, Staying Power, Strength, stagger, and physical-skill efficiency.

The archived names `Sinew`, `Prescience`, `Vehemence`, and related terms remain working labels only.

## Training and Reputation

Fighter training is respectable and widely legible. The player may open it through Tannward service without swearing permanent loyalty to the Duke. Advanced local techniques may come from marcher veterans, fortress survivors, or weapon masters with different views of protection and obedience.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- Holding a line can protect the weak or preserve an unjust order.
- Attention-control techniques do not compel minds; they exploit noise, threat, formation, and battlefield priority.
- `Soul Skewer` is a placeholder name and does not manipulate a soul.
- Durability does not make execution, torture, or collective punishment morally neutral.
