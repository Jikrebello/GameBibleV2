# Hunter

## Player Fantasy

The Hunter is a mobile longbow specialist who wins by understanding distance, anatomy, materials, weather, and terrain. Its close-range bow strikes and evasions preserve agency when crowded, but the vocation is built to recover the line rather than become a dagger class.

## In the World

`Hunter` is a player-facing umbrella for gamekeepers, frontier scouts, monster hunters, caravan marksmen, poachers, militia archers, highland stalkers, and professional trackers. These people do not share one law or guild.

Grenzburg's strongest routes run through [[Ashfield Lodge]], Highlake pathfinders, Longlake fishers, road scouts, and local hunting circles. Folk instruction belongs to the kindred or place offering it; it never implies a universal Folk hunting order.

## Native Kit

| Element | Hunter rule |
|---|---|
| Weapon | longbow only |
| Armour | light field armour |
| Primary statistics | Stamina, Strength, precision, mobile defence |
| Signature interaction | craft and select limited special arrows from real materials |
| Movement advantage | terrain reading, pursuit efficiency, stable mobile aim, and evasive bow movement |

Ordinary ammunition is abstracted for play. Special arrows consume gathered, purchased, or prepared materials and remain limited.

## Combat Grammar

1. Read tracks, behavior, cover, armour, and weak points.
2. Establish a useful range and route of retreat.
3. Choose an ordinary or prepared arrow for the problem.
4. Interrupt, bind, pierce, burst, or reposition the target.
5. Use bow strikes and evasive movement when crowded.
6. recover range or create a companion opening.

## Core Skill Families

- **Loose arrow:** mobile ordinary shot with improved nocking through mastery.
- **Bow strike:** close blunt or sweeping use of the longbow without pretending it is a sword.
- **Aim discipline:** deliberate range and weak-point control.
- **Evasive bow movement:** cartwheel, step, or low recovery while maintaining orientation.
- **Field arrowcraft:** rapid preparation from materials already carried or gathered.

## Special Arrow Families

| Arrow | World Egg basis | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| **Magick-infused** | purchased or prepared warlock charge | split physical and elemental pressure |
| **Blast** | powder package and reinforced shaft | explosion, knockback, structure, and fire risk |
| **Blunt-head** | weighted or rounded head | armour pressure and conditional non-lethal knockout |
| **Sapper** | predatory alchemical or creature-derived preparation | drains a target before a second impact releases stored harm |
| **Petrifying** | rare restricted mineralizing compound or relic preparation | progressive mineralization; expensive and legally sensitive |
| **Flare** | bright chemical or charged head | illumination, surprise, signaling, and harm to light-sensitive threats |

Special arrows must name their supply and legality in the quest, vendor, recipe, or item. `Magick-infused` is a gameplay family rather than evidence of generic enchanting.

## Rescue Scout Doctrine

[[Ylva Winter-Track]] supplies a rescue-scout expression of Hunter through [[Ylva Rescue Hunter Package]]. It remains the same vocation and obeys the same longbow-only equipment boundary.

The doctrine prioritizes:

- reading an extraction route before committing damage;
- locating the wounded, encumbered, or cut off;
- interrupting pursuit;
- placing signals and bounded concealment;
- establishing a proper rescue line;
- moving out of front-line pressure after the route opens.

It provides tactical advantages and clearer rescue interactions. No critical path, climb, gap, or evacuation requires Ylva, Hunter, or a special arrow.

### Winter-Track Field Fletching

Ylva's campaign adds five practical, account-wide recipes:

| Recipe | Relationship to existing family | Use and limit |
|---|---|---|
| **All-Weather Flare Arrow** | Flare upgrade | visible through snow, fog, and rain; establishes a rally marker and disrupts light-sensitive threats |
| **Reinforced Blunt-Head** | Blunt-head upgrade | armour pressure, nonlethal stops, weak ice, simple latches, and fragile obstruction |
| **Line Arrow** | new practical field tool | carries a pilot cord so a proper rope can be hauled and secured; it never carries a person directly |
| **Smoke Arrow** | new practical field tool | bounded concealment, wind reading, and ordinary ranged-sight interruption |
| **Controlled Blast Arrow** | Blast upgrade | smaller shaped charge for ice, timber, obstruction, or armour with reduced spread and fire risk |

All five use named material supplies and remain subject to local fire, weapon, hunting, and explosives law. They unlock through Ylva's core companion campaign regardless of friendship, romance, or sworn bond.

Completing [[The Last Marker]] unlocks **Winter-Track Fieldcraft**, an account-wide augment improving severe-weather aiming, field-arrow economy, and movement while protecting or guiding another character. Exact values remain provisional.

## Mastery Shape

- **Opening:** mobile shot, close bow strike, one charged shot, ordinary tracking, and simple arrowcraft.
- **Practiced:** area fire, target binding, evasive aiming, creature reading, and broader materials.
- **Veteran:** difficult shots, environmental ricochet, large-creature anatomy, pursuit in severe weather, and rare preparations.
- **Master:** one signature hunt doctrine and a dangerous specialist arrow route earned through authored instruction.

## Augment Themes

Gathering speed, difficult-terrain movement, night endurance, recoil control, opening damage, material yield, range, moving accuracy, and arrowcraft efficiency.

## Training and Reputation

Hunters are ordinary enough to be trusted and mobile enough to be suspected. Poaching, trespass, sanctuary violation, and use of restricted preparations remain questions of law and conduct. Ashfield access does not grant hunting rights in Wolfsmere's sacred inner boundary.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- Tracking reveals a route, not guilt.
- Monster knowledge does not make every creature evil or every trophy lawful.
- Petrifying, sapper, poison, and blast preparations create serious moral and legal responsibility.
- A longbow-only authored kit keeps the vocation distinct; sidearms belong to custom construction.

## Custom Vocation Parts

Mastery contributes the longbow, light field armour, special-arrow families, aiming and terrain techniques, tracking augments, and the Hunter growth profile to [[Custom Vocation Workshop]].
