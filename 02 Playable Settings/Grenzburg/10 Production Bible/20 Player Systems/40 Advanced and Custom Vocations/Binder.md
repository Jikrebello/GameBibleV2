# Binder

## Player Fantasy

The Binder is a preparation-heavy field controller who defines terms, anchors positions, calls bounded forms, and breaks hostile compulsions through exact language. It rewards foresight, true-name knowledge, spatial planning, and the discipline to release what cannot rightly be held.

Its strongest play does not come from owning more servants. It comes from understanding which relations can be made, which must be refused, and where a hostile binding can be inverted.

## In the World

`Binder` is a gameplay umbrella for one field discipline that [[Zafir the Bound Chainmaster|Zafir]] can teach after his third personal quest. Its instruments draw on Ahl-e-Ahd legal metaphysics, but the player form is shaped by Zafir's confrontation with coercion and the inversion of his own craft.

The vocation's existence does not vindicate Covenant slavery, the Creditor's architecture, patron ownership, or the proposition that formally stated predation becomes moral. [[Contract Magic and True Names]] controls the metaphysics; real consent and objective moral limits control responsible use.

The first account unlock must occur through Zafir's story. After that first completion, Binder may be selected at supported vocation-change points in future new games as an earned metagame option; the new campaign does not pretend Zafir has already taught that protagonist in its fiction.

## Native Kit

| Element | Binder rule |
|---|---|
| Instruments | binding rings and true-name cords |
| Armour | robes or light armour |
| Primary statistics | Stamina, Magick, Magick Defence, Intelligence |
| Signature interaction | prepare terms, position forms, expose breach, release or invert |
| Movement advantage | prepared recall points and short line-draw repositioning |

Rings establish bounded locations or relations. Cords identify the permitted connection and make its limits visible. Neither instrument grants ownership of a person.

## Permitted Assets

The player may direct:

- non-conscious elemental or script forms whose behavior is genuinely bounded;
- non-conscious constructed relations that possess no self;
- freely covenanted beings capable of understanding the relevant terms and withdrawing under the agreed conditions;
- temporary manifestations already authorized by a responsible source.

The player may not bind:

- human souls or corpse residue presented as a person;
- coerced people or beings whose refusal is punished by continued captivity;
- children, dependents, captives, or impaired persons through counterfeit consent;
- predatory patrons under terms that merely move the cost onto another victim;
- a conscious being whose nature or language prevents meaningful assent.

An enemy can be contained during immediate defence or lawful capture without becoming a summonable property asset.

## Combat Grammar

1. Prepare one or more ring positions before or during contact.
2. Identify the name, category, behavior, or breach that makes a relation addressable.
3. Manifest a permitted form or redirect an existing hostile relation.
4. Use cords to set range, priority, duration, and release condition.
5. Exploit a broken term, false name, or hostile overreach.
6. Release the form cleanly before damaged terms reverse onto the caster.

## Skill Families

| Family | Intended function |
|---|---|
| Ring setting | establish bounded summon, ward, or return positions |
| Cord direction | set target, route, duration, and distance for a permitted form |
| Manifested form | call one prepared non-conscious or freely covenanted asset |
| Recall and release | withdraw a form safely and terminate the relation |
| Breach mark | expose an opponent that violates its own stated or inherent terms |
| Inversion | turn a coercive or malformed binding against its controlling structure |
| Name shelter | protect an ally from false naming, imposed mantle, or hostile designation |
| Counterclaim | interrupt a manifested power by proving the wrong relation rather than winning a legal debate |
| Shared covenant | temporary voluntary cooperation with a conscious being, carrying obligations for both sides |

## Consent Presentation

The interface distinguishes assets with no consciousness from conscious covenant partners. A conscious partner's dossier shows the agreed service, duration, prohibited commands, release condition, and current willingness. The player cannot bypass these limits through a higher social statistic.

Mechanical power may be lost when the player honors a right to withdraw. That cost is part of the vocation's moral and tactical identity.

## Mastery Shape

- **Unlock:** Zafir's third quest grants safe rings, release forms, one non-conscious asset, and basic inversion.
- **Practiced:** multiple ring arrangements, better cord directions, breach marks, and a wider prepared roster.
- **Veteran:** hostile-binding counters, voluntary covenant partners, complex releases, and name shelter.
- **Capstone:** Zafir's fourth quest resolves his own inverted bond and teaches one master inversion or covenant technique.

## Training and Reputation

After unlock, practice can improve ring placement and prepared forms. Higher instruction remains tied to Zafir, recovered name traditions, and encounters where consent or coercion is materially testable. Covenant-trained observers may regard Grenzburg's form as naive, heretical, or threatening because it rejects profitable coercive assumptions.

Eclessian authorities distinguish little between responsible and predatory binding at first glance. Player conduct and evidence determine whether that suspicion can be answered.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- Exact terms do not convert an evil purpose into a good one.
- Apparent consent under captivity, desperation, fraud, or unequal threat may be invalid.
- False names can redirect force onto the caster, an ally, or an unintended bearer.
- Broken releases create breach, backlash, hostile persistence, or public danger.
- A conscious covenant partner remains a person with standing, not equipment.
- The vocation cannot seize souls from their route or make slavery metaphysically clean.

## Custom Vocation Parts

Once unlocked and mastered, Binder contributes rings, cords, prepared forms, breach marks, inversion parts, and the Binder growth profile to [[Custom Vocation Workshop]]. Every part retains its consent, release, and anti-soulbinding restrictions.
