# Paladin

## Player Fantasy

The Paladin is a great-weapon protector whose committed blows, interpositions, challenges, and sanctifying fire turn personal moral courage into battlefield shelter. It is powerful when the player accepts risk for a real good and unreliable when sacred language is used to excuse cruelty.

It is not an upgraded Fighter. The Fighter controls a line through trained arms and defence; the Paladin gives up the shield, commits to slower weapons, and stakes supernatural coherence on what the player is actually doing.

## In the World

`Paladin` is a gameplay umbrella for several Eclessian and marcher traditions of armed White-Flame service. It does not designate one universal knightly order. [[Bernard Valeon]] teaches the player a personal field form during his third quest after the player has confronted the difference between holy purpose, public office, and self-excusing piety.

Bernard's fourth quest supplies the vocation's capstone. Neither unlock requires the player to make the Church politically supreme, and neither establishes that private feeling is always purer than institution.

The first account unlock must occur through Bernard's story. After that first completion, Paladin may be selected at supported vocation-change points in future new games as an earned metagame option; the new campaign does not pretend Bernard has already taught that protagonist in its fiction.

Great-weapon handling is learned physical knowledge. Once earned, it remains available even if the player later loses access to sacred techniques.

## White-Flame Coherence

White Flame is not a neutral fuel and not a reward for selecting flattering dialogue. Its techniques work through real alignment between act, intention, truth, and restorative purpose.

- ordinary moral mistakes do not cause constant power flicker;
- one isolated failure does not erase a vocation;
- sustained grave conduct, violated vows, deliberate sacrilege, or using holy authority to serve known evil can dim or close sacred techniques;
- the character record and narrative scenes clearly communicate a threatened or broken coherence;
- sincere confession without restitution is insufficient where restitution remains possible;
- restoration follows concrete truth-telling, repair, relinquishment, protection, and accepted consequence appropriate to the wrong.

The [[Virtue and Sin Tendencies]] system supplies evidence about repeated conduct. It does not mechanically redefine the Lord or turn sacred truth into a morality meter.

## Combat Grammar

1. Identify the person, route, or space that must be protected.
2. Commit with a challenge, interposition, or heavy approach.
3. Absorb or break the enemy's decisive action.
4. Create a sanctified interval in which allies can recover or advance.
5. Punish corruption, residue, or predatory supernatural force with a prepared blow.
6. Choose whether to remain with the endangered or pursue the defeated threat.

## Mastery Shape

- **Unlock:** Bernard's third quest grants the physical and sacred opening kit.
- **Practiced:** interposition, heavy-weapon distinctions, protective challenges, and reliable anti-corruption work.
- **Veteran:** burden-bearing, stronger sanctification, group protection, and techniques shaped by the player's vows.
- **Capstone:** Bernard's fourth quest resolves the vocation's master proof and one signature White-Flame technique.

## Training and Reputation

After the unlock, ordinary physical mastery can be practiced with great-weapon veterans. Sacred rank gates require Bernard, relic custodians, saints' testimony, or another credible White-Flame teacher. No trainer can sell restored coherence as a fee or legal indulgence.

People may trust the visible flame too quickly, distrust the player's Blackjack chain, or expect political loyalty that the vocation does not require. False public expectations are story material, not proof that the Flame itself is false.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- Sacred power cannot excuse cruelty, collective punishment, theft, torture, or false witness.
- The White Flame is especially effective against corruption and destabilizing false forms; it is not an unlimited damage type against ordinary people.
- A Paladin may be mistaken about facts and still sincere. Once the truth is known, chosen refusal matters.
- Losing sacred access does not delete learned great-weapon movement, armour proficiency, or ordinary combat skills.
- Restitution must repair what can still be repaired and accept consequence for what cannot.

## Custom Vocation Parts

Once unlocked and mastered, Paladin contributes great weapons, heavy armour, interposition, protective augments, and White-Flame parts to [[Custom Vocation Workshop]]. Sacred parts retain their coherence requirements in every custom build.
