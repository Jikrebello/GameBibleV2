# Lancewright

## Player Fantasy

The Lancewright is a mobile spear fighter and battlefield proof-worker. It places bounded runic marks with a one-handed war spear, loads them through coordinated force, and releases them through a heavy proof gauntlet as rupture, protection, displacement, or a safe withdrawal.

Its rhythm is not that of a distant caster. The Lancewright enters the dangerous middle distance, creates a measured opportunity, invites the party to load it, then decides whether the agreed proof has been reached.

## In the World

Lancewright practice grew from Vortharan Crucible testing, siege workshops, arena proof schools, and mobile warlock inspection teams. Its marks use the same elemental Shell logic as [[Warlock Rune Magic]], but compress the script into a bounded physical circuit:

1. the spear inscribes a temporary relation;
2. impact, movement, or a gauntlet catch loads that relation;
3. the gauntlet conducts a declared release;
4. the remaining force is yielded or vented.

The discipline cannot make an unsafe load moral merely because the practitioner survives it. Certified limits protect opponents, assistants, spectators, and workers as much as the Lancewright.

[[Tavio Meran]] teaches the player a field form during a personally completed [[The Fair Defeat]]. He does not invent or own the discipline.

## Design Research Basis

The production synthesis draws from:

- Capcom's [Spirit Lancer developer discussion](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/interview/2016/vol04.html) and the archived [Spirit Lancer skill set](https://maxhubnet0x01.github.io/ddon-fan-wiki/vocations/spirit_lancer/) for flowing spear movement and support rhythm;
- the archived [Alchemist mechanics](https://maxhubnet0x01.github.io/ddon-fan-wiki/vocations/alchemist/) for ally-loaded marks, selective releases, and battlefield manipulation;
- the documented [Mystic Spearhand vocation](https://game8.co/games/Dragons-Dogma-2/archives/Mystic-Spearhand) for charged control, gap-closing, aerial repositioning, and polearm flow.

These references guide combat purpose only. Final names, animations, timings, resource presentation, and exact interactions must remain distinct and pass the [[Vocation Originalization Register]].

## Native Kit

| Element | Lancewright rule |
|---|---|
| Primary weapon | one-handed war spear |
| Secondary tool | heavy off-hand proof gauntlet |
| Armour | medium armour |
| Primary statistics | Strength, Magick, Stagger Power, Staying Power |
| Signature resource | up to three temporary Proof Marks |
| Shared risk | Arcane Burn and overproof recoil |
| Movement advantage | mark-directed lunges, spear vaults, aerial descents, and large-monster repositioning |

The spear and gauntlet form one native kit. A shield, second spear, paired gauntlets, or free telekinetic focus is not part of the authored vocation.

## Combat Grammar

1. Read the target, surface, route, and elemental risk.
2. Inscribe one to three Proof Marks on valid enemy parts or physical surfaces.
3. Load a mark through spear attacks, allied strikes, enemy force, or a timed gauntlet catch.
4. Choose the release that answers the present problem.
5. Reposition through the marked geometry.
6. Yield unused load before Arcane Burn or equipment strain becomes uncontrolled.

The strongest play alternates technical preparation with decisive movement. Holding every mark for the largest possible spectacle is intentionally dangerous.

## Proof Marks

Proof Marks are temporary runic relations, not physical stakes, creatures, souls, or permanent enchantments.

- A novice maintains one mark reliably.
- A trained Lancewright maintains two.
- A master maintains three, with at most one heavy overproof load.
- Marks may be placed on enemy limbs, armour plates, shields, doors, beams, floors, walls, siege parts, and compatible debris.
- Marks cannot be placed on empty air, an abstract destination, a soul, a memory, or a social relationship.
- Allied attacks may load a mark only while the marked surface remains physically reachable.

Repeated loading beyond certification creates visible warning: gauntlet ringing, colour shift, hot proof rings, script skipping, and involuntary spear vibration.

## Four Releases

| Release | Function | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Break** | elemental rupture, armour damage, weak-point exposure, or heavy stagger | must follow the mark's element and physical placement |
| **Brace** | converts a ground or target mark into brief directional protection and stagger resistance | not full invulnerability and does not follow the party indefinitely |
| **Cast** | launches small marked targets or debris and pulls larger targets off balance | cannot freely levitate, carry, or puppet living beings |
| **Yield** | abandons loaded marks, vents Arcane Burn, and creates a guarded disengagement | sacrifices stored force and offensive tempo |

Yield is a core mastery action, not a failed attack. The vocation becomes safer and more capable as the player learns when not to release.

## Gauntlet Catches

A timed proof-gauntlet guard may capture a bounded portion of:

- a physical impact;
- an elemental projectile;
- a compatible warlock script;
- a monster's charged collision;
- a failing mechanism's directional force.

Captured force must be assigned to an existing mark, vented, or yielded within a short interval. It cannot become permanent stored power, restore Stamina, heal injury, or be carried between distant encounters.

## Mobility

The vocation's movement remains an advantage rather than a critical-path key.

- **Line Lunge:** close toward a marked target or surface.
- **Spear Vault:** clear a low obstacle or enemy line.
- **Falling Proof:** descend onto a marked part with controlled stagger.
- **Great-Body Traverse:** shift between valid marks while climbing a large creature.
- **Cast Step:** use a small release to redirect momentum.

Every critical route remains traversable by all starting vocations through the shared movement kit.

## Failure and Arcane Burn

Overproof may cause:

- recoil and loss of guard;
- Arcane Burn;
- broken spear fittings or gauntlet rings;
- an uncontrolled elemental release;
- a mark jumping to an adjacent compatible surface;
- injury to nearby allies or workers.

Warnings are always legible before a voluntary overproof. Enemy sabotage may shorten the warning interval, but never removes every sign.

## Hard Limits

Lancewright cannot:

- heal or restore recoverable Health;
- steal Stamina or life;
- create full invulnerability;
- manipulate souls, corpse residue, memory, or identity;
- perform unrestricted telekinesis;
- maintain marks across the whole region;
- make an unsafe test legitimate through consent that was coerced or uninformed.

It may disrupt non-conscious Muster commands only by interacting with their physical script carriers.

## Mastery Arc

| Rank | Capability |
|---|---|
| Initiate | one mark, Break, basic Yield, spear lunge |
| Proven | two marks, Brace, gauntlet catches, spear vault |
| Examiner | Cast, ally loading, controlled aerial descent |
| Master | three marks, selective releases, heavy-load warning extension |
| Capstone | **Horn Before Ruin**, a disciplined mass Yield that protects an evacuation lane while surrendering all loaded marks |

Personally completing [[The Fair Defeat]] grants the current-playthrough and account-wide unlock. An autonomous resolution or later aftermath cannot substitute for that instruction.

Personally completing [[Horn for Leak-Day]] grants the capstone and the **Road Examiner** saved custom-vocation blueprint.

## Custom Vocation Access

After the relevant account unlocks, the custom builder may use:

- one-handed war spear proficiency;
- proof gauntlet proficiency;
- mark placement;
- one selected release family;
- one mark-directed movement family;
- Lancewright growth profiles and augments.

The custom budget cannot remove the three-mark ceiling, Arcane Burn, physical-surface requirement, or the need to Yield.

## Legal and Social Treatment

- Vortharan proof schools treat the vocation as respected technical combat craft.
- Grenzburg's Great Forge and Warlock Hall require declared test limits and a named stop authority.
- Ducal service values bridge, breach, and siege inspection.
- Craftsmen distrust unsigned fittings and unrecorded overproof.
- Folk custodians judge marked land and structures by local permission, not arena custom.
- The Church treats the discipline as ordinary Shellcraft unless it is falsely presented as sacred power.

## Identity Hooks

At least four post-unlock uses must remain in authored content:

1. identify a concealed over-capacity fitting without dismantling it;
2. call a safe halt during a military or industrial load test;
3. build a temporary Brace across an evacuation lane;
4. read whether a physical script carrier can be safely Broken, Cast, or Yielded.

Every use has a credible workaround for other vocations.
