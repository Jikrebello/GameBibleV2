# Postgame Reconstruction Cycle

This note controls Grenzburg after [[Accounts of the Living]] and [[The First Summer Road]]. The postgame is a deliberate seasonal sandbox, not a second advancing historical campaign.

## Unlock

The cycle unlocks after the first reconstruction corridor is selected in [[The First Summer Road]]. The game initially remains in postwar summer.

At supported homes, the Blackjack Yard, and designated settlement rest points, the player may choose **Advance the Reconstruction Season**.

## Sequential Rule

Seasons advance only in this order:

1. summer;
2. autumn;
3. winter;
4. spring;
5. summer again.

There is no direct season selector. The player may advance repeatedly, with confirmation at every step, to reach a preferred season.

Advancement is always voluntary. Travel, resting, jobs, crafting, and exploration do not move the season automatically.

## What Changes

Each postwar season may alter:

- weather, ice, mud, water level, daylight, and traversal;
- ferries, carts, sleds, Road Keys, and route closures;
- harvest, fuel, fish, ore, prices, and available services;
- ordinary wildlife, migration, renewable hunts, and regional encounter decks;
- patrols, workers, traders, refugees, pilgrims, and ambient populations;
- reconstruction visuals, memorial practice, and repeatable job tables;
- seasonal dialogue at surviving settlements and companion destinations.

## What Never Resets

Seasonal advancement cannot reverse:

- named deaths, injuries, departures, or companion outcomes;
- settlement destruction, survival, or political control;
- faction settlements and player debt status;
- named dungeon clearance, collapse, sealing, or occupation;
- the deaths of Ghor, the Emerald Drake, Gerhold, or the defeat of the General Below;
- law-gem custody, Muster-anchor results, or campaign casualties;
- completed one-shot quests and discoveries.

Only sites already marked renewable may repopulate.

## Canonical Time Boundary

The repeating cycle represents the playable postwar condition. It does not accumulate canonical decades, age the cast, produce new heirs, or advance the wider setting's official chronology. A later historical sequel requires its own continuity ruling.

## Companion Boundary

Companions remain at the destinations established by their Act III outcomes and [[Accounts of the Living]]. They receive seasonal ambient dialogue and schedule variants but no new postgame personal arcs, relationship decisions, departures, or moral resolutions.

## Systemic Content

[[World Simulation and Discovery Constitution]] controls postwar encounter decks. Events may reflect reconstruction, remnants, trade, weather, and unresolved local violence. They may not recreate campaign armies or pretend the crisis never ended.
