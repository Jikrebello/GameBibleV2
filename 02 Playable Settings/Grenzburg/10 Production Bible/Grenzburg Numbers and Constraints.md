# Grenzburg Numbers and Constraints

These are design anchors, not perfect simulation. Use them to keep quests grounded in material pressure.

## Population and Shelter

| Category | Number |
|---|---:|
| City permanent population | ~18,000 |
| Winter population inside walls | ~32,500; variable range 31,000-35,000 |
| People in the outer winter camps at peak | ~3,000-5,500 before admission, removal, or dispersal |
| Exterior permanent population | ~17,050 across the seven regions |
| Lakewatch, Highlake, and Timberfalls combined | 4,800 |
| People permanently south of Fort Tannbruck | fewer than 2,000 |
| Maximum emergency shelter before disease spirals | ~38,000 |

## Food, Fuel, and Time

| Constraint | Anchor |
|---|---|
| Winter closure | 14-17 weeks from Gates Close to First Thaw |
| Stored grain at clean start | 20 weeks for the permanent city; 11-13 weeks at winter population |
| Fish and smoked stores from Lakewatch | 15-20% of winter protein if secured |
| Timberfalls fuel contribution | 30-40% of emergency heating fuel |
| Highlake ore output | enough to maintain arms, hinges, nails, and wall hardware, not enough for major expansion during siege |
| Disease threshold | sustained crowding above 35,000 plus bad water for two weeks |

## Travel and Map Anchors

| Route | Time |
|---|---|
| Northern map edge to the Funnel | 1 day by cart |
| Funnel to Grenzburg | half a day in normal traffic |
| Grenzburg to Lakewatch | 2 days by road, 3 in bad mud |
| Grenzburg to Timberfalls | 2 days upriver road |
| Grenzburg to Highlake | 2-3 days hard travel |
| Grenzburg to Fort Tannbruck | 3-4 days |
| Grenzburg to Barrow of the First Chieftain | 4-5 days, more if avoiding patrol roads |
| Grenzburg to Wolfsmere | 4 days by safe roads, 2-3 by Folk paths |
| Grenzburg to Fenn Road Exchange | 4-5 days |
| Grenzburg to Last Hearth | 6 days in safe autumn weather |
| Grenzburg to Deep Muster surface basin | 7-8 days before spring damage |
| Grenzburg to Aurelium | 5-7 weeks by road |

The compressed playable crossing from the northern map edge to the Deep Muster approach is about ninety uninterrupted minutes. Safe nodes sit roughly ten minutes apart near Grenzburg, fifteen to twenty minutes apart across the middle frontier, and up to twenty-five minutes apart in the deep south.

| Annual ducal revenue in a good year | ~18,000-22,000 silver-equivalent |
| Debt danger line | when payroll depends on Bank advances for two consecutive seasons |

## Faction Manpower Levers

- **Duke:** controls gates, courts, ration tokens, charters, and militia call-up.
- **Church:** controls hospitals, funerals, public legitimacy, relic processions, and sanctuary space.
- **Bank:** controls payroll timing, imported grain, debt courts, blackjacks, and expedition finance.
- **Folk:** control forest paths, Grobi warning, hidden winter stores, and sacred-site permission.
- **Grobi:** control whether the migration stays cohesive or fractures into dozens of raids.

## Design Rule

Whenever a quest materially changes the crisis, update at least one grounded state: food, fuel, garrison strength, refugee load, debt exposure, morale, disease risk, road access, settlement survival, or Grobi cohesion. Not every personal or exploratory quest needs to move a campaign-wide number.
