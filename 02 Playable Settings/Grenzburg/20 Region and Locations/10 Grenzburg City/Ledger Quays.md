# Ledger Quays

Ledger Quays follows the west bank north of [[Bridgehold]]. Warehouses, customs yards, cranes, counting-houses, ferries, and foreign lodgings make it the district where distant power becomes grain, payroll, cargo, and enforceable debt.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 2,200, with a large daily workforce from neighboring wards.
- **Primary authority:** river customs, merchant houses, dock guilds, and the Medori Bank's secured compounds.
- **Visual identity:** stone quays, timber cranes, slate warehouses, painted cargo marks, river fog, and narrow counting-house fronts.
- **Living conflict:** credit and organized transport can save Grenzburg while monopolies, seizures, hidden risk, and debt ownership convert necessity into control.

## Anchor Locations

- **Iron Ledger Counting-House:** Bank authority, debt records, secure archives, and high finance.
- **Customs Hall:** manifests, searches, confiscation, and jurisdiction.
- **Grain Stairs:** food arrival, dock labor, shortages, and public pressure.
- **North Quay Warehouses:** storage, burglary, fire, and contraband.
- **River Exchange:** shipping, insurance, couriers, and foreign factors.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** imported grain, debtor arrivals, freight disputes, and river congestion dominate.
- **Winter:** ice restricts traffic while guarded stores, payroll, black markets, and river raids raise the stakes.
- **Spring:** thaw wreckage, disputed cargo, reopened accounts, and exposed fraud return with the boats.
- **Summer:** trade privileges, public credit, confiscated property, and dock ownership reflect the political outcome.

## Return Reasons

Debt progression, Blackjack work, trade services, heists, customs investigations, river travel, and urban faction conflict repeatedly return the player here.
