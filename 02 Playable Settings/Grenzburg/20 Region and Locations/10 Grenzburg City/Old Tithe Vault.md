# Old Tithe Vault

Old Tithe Vault is a disused bonded-storage complex beneath the North Quay Warehouses. It joins later ducal riverworks to older bridge foundations and a flood channel that empties below the west-bank quay.

The vault is the principal dungeon of [[No Lady of Velescar]]. It is not a secret city, Shell site, permanent anchor, or supernatural treasury.

## History

Grenzburg once stored seized toll goods and disputed estate records here while river cases were heard. Repeated flooding and the construction of the Iron Ledger Counting-House made the chambers obsolete. Legal access remained fragmented among customs, the Bank, and old quay leases.

Vardek cutouts rented a sealed section during the winter relief delegation. They moved the decisive Velescar cache into Grenzburg so estate options, title instruments, and burial claims could be cleared through the same commercial network handling the relief warrants.

## Access Routes

- **Bonded stair:** a documented route from the North Quay Warehouses, opened through Medori or customs authority.
- **Bridge-vault passage:** an underworld route reached through the Bridge Vaults and old inspection niches.
- **Flood channel:** a low exterior approach available when river level, grates, and pumps permit entry.

Every player vocation can use all three routes with ordinary prepared equipment. Elara shortens searches, identifies false cabinets, and opens optional recoveries.

## Interior Structure

### Intake Hall

Weighing stones, hoists, flooded floor marks, and the crew's first barricade establish the site's material logic. A damaged sluice determines later water pressure.

### Land-Roll Gallery

Shelves contain estate maps, household obligations, sale options, and the papers needed to transfer or conceal viable land. Vardek loyalists prepare controlled fires rather than leaving an unexplained self-destruct mechanism.

### Burial-Provenance Room

Sealed cases connect grave goods to names, plots, intermediaries, and buyers. Broken containers expose residue-bearing remains and create a respectful recovery problem rather than loot.

### Title Cistern

The Velescar seal, movable wealth, and creditor instruments sit in a dry strongroom surrounded by an old overflow cistern. Opening it without managing water can save the wealth while destroying other records.

### Clearing Chamber

The three record families meet at a former hearing table. Vasko or the replacement senior retainer makes the final stand here while flood, fire, or early Muster residue closes unused routes.

## Opposition

Human loyalists and commercial recovery hands are the principal opposition. They want records, wealth, silence, or payment rather than service to a supernatural power.

Independent hazards include:

- rising water and failed sluices;
- burning oil and paper;
- collapsing shelves and hoists;
- traps built from ordinary counterweights and powder;
- corpse residue entering through an older military foundation during the Muster crisis.

The residue follows old route and guard patterns. It is not sent by Vardek and contains no trapped soul.

## Persistent States

- **Full recovery:** land, burial, and title caches survive.
- **Partial recovery:** one or two record families survive, narrowing settlement options.
- **Records saved, wealth lost:** strong restitution remains possible without private reserve.
- **Wealth saved, claims lost:** the house can be restored only through concealment and harm.
- **Vault destroyed:** testimony survives, but physical settlement defaults to creditor control or partial summer remedy.

The vault never repopulates as a generic dungeon. Its summer state is secured archive, flooded ruin, sealed crime scene, or stripped private store.
