# Open Lamp Hospice

The Open Lamp Hospice is an outcome-dependent medical and refuge institution established through [[The Open Hospice]]. It develops from [[Longlake Ward#Anchor Locations|West Refuge Hall]] rather than occupying a new unplaced district site.

## Founding Rule

The hospice serves wounded soldiers, debtors, Folk households, captives, travellers, and civilians according to medical and shelter need. It does not require conversion, faction allegiance, debt transfer, or surrender of secular claims.

Every reserve is declared by purpose, count, release condition, and at least two witnesses. Transparent storage is not treated as distrust of every worker; it is the institution's response to scarcity, faction pressure, and Bernard's own history.

## Outcome Forms

### Open lay hospice

Bernard remains in Grenzburg while mixed lay staff, Chapel-Folk stewards, and recognized clinicians maintain the hall. Church clergy provide sacraments without owning admission. This is a possible resolved or compromised destination.

### Long Watch chapter

If Bernard accepts reinstatement, the hall may become a cooperative Long Watch station. It remains subject to ducal civil law and cannot claim Thalmyric jurisdiction over Grenzburg.

### Entrusted road house

On a travelling-romance future, Bernard appoints the established staff to continue the hospice while he takes road service beside the player. He does not abandon patients to preserve a romantic ending.

### Failed or captured institution

Broken stores, narrow Church custody, Medori control, or destruction may reduce the hospice to a field service in [[Cathedral Close#Anchor Locations|Paupers' Cloister]]. The name can survive as aspiration without pretending the full institution exists.

### Autonomous Blackjack service

On Bernard's default unattended route, Open Lamp is never founded. [[Quartermaster Brina Kest|Brina Kest]] places the viable care work inside the [[Blackjack Yard]] infirmary under an open company inventory. Bernard remains useful and caring but has not developed independent accountability. [[The First Open Table]] presents that state at the Yard rather than creating this location retroactively.
