# Cathedral Close

Cathedral Close lies between Crown Heights and Tannward. Its cathedral, hospital, chancery, cloisters, schools, cemeteries, and militant commandery place sincere ministry beside institutional power.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 2,000, excluding pilgrims and hospital patients.
- **Primary authority:** the diocesan hierarchy, cathedral chapter, hospital officers, and recognized religious houses.
- **Visual identity:** pale stone, steep red and slate roofs, covered walks, crowded shrines, hospital yards, bells, and burial gardens.
- **Living conflict:** the Church performs indispensable healing, worship, burial, and sanctuary while its offices compete over jurisdiction, property, reform, and coercion.

## Anchor Locations

- **Cathedral of the Redeemer:** public worship, great rites, legitimacy, and sanctuary.
- **Mercy Hospital:** triage, plague pressure, healing, and difficult allocation.
- **Chancery House:** ecclesiastical records, licences, trials, and policy.
- **Ashen Commandery:** militant discipline, frontier zeal, and internal Church conflict.
- **Paupers' Cloister:** alms, lodging, burial lists, and witnesses overlooked elsewhere.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** blessings, supply appeals, pilgrims, and displaced clergy fill the Close.
- **Winter:** hospital overflow, sanctuary disputes, funerals, ration kitchens, and armed zeal make the district vital and volatile.
- **Spring:** burial integrity, missing records, reform accusations, and returned parishioners force public accounting.
- **Summer:** restored ministry, schism, constrained authority, or expanded jurisdiction appears through services and public space.

## Return Reasons

Healing, burial, Church faction work, relic and residue expertise, Bernard's future arc, public rites, and civilian crises repeatedly use the Close.
