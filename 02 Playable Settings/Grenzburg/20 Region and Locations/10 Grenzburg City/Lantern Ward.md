# Lantern Ward

Lantern Ward presses against the west wall between the quays, Lake Road, and Hammer Ward. Cheap lodging, debtor compounds, night markets, tenements, bathhouses, small shrines, criminal routes, and seasonal shelter make it the player's first home rather than a generic slum.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 1,900, rising toward 6,800 in winter.
- **Primary authority:** ward watch, lodging owners, mutual-aid circles, Blackjack officers, creditors, and competing underworld networks.
- **Visual identity:** close timber streets, hanging lamps, painted doors, patched roofs, public braziers, quarry steps, and glimpses of the west wall.
- **Living conflict:** outsiders and debtors build real community under pressure while creditors, criminals, officials, and desperate neighbors compete to own their labor, silence, and movement.

## Anchor Locations

- **Blackjack Yard:** player base, debt service, equipment, and party reorganization.
- **Lantern Market:** cheap goods, rumors, food, and illicit exchange.
- **Debtors' Row:** households, solidarity, intimidation, and personal stakes.
- **Night House:** underworld meetings, surveillance, smuggling, and the later faction fork.
- **Quarry Steps:** dense housing and a principal route into [[Grenzburg Underways]].

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** new debtors, workers, refugees, and recruiters make the district crowded but mobile.
- **Winter:** extreme shelter pressure, fires, crime, mutual aid, ration searches, and curfew transform every public space.
- **Spring:** missing people, unpaid service, exposed crimes, returned survivors, and contested housing demand resolution.
- **Summer:** freedom, continued Blackjack service, criminal control, reform, or abandonment changes the player's home and its neighbors.

## Return Reasons

The persistent base, companion routines, debt progression, underworld line, local quests, low-cost services, and transformed residents keep Lantern Ward central in every act.
