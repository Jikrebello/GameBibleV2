# Outer Winter Camps

The outer camps are seasonal communities around North Gate, Lake Gate, Tann Gate, and the protected river meadows. They contain coach yards, animal pens, temporary markets, military assembly, refugee shelters, quarantine ground, and burial space. They are not one administratively unified district.

## Principal Areas

- **The Funnel:** the northern coaching and arrival camp, permanent at its core and enormous in autumn.
- **Lake Field:** fish carts, Chapel-Folk shelters, western delegations, and sled staging.
- **Tann Field:** military assembly, southern convoy reception, field hospitals, and withdrawal camps.
- **River Meadow:** drovers, timber rafts, quarantine, and temporary warehouses above flood level.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** several thousand travelers, laborers, petitioners, animals, and refugees gather outside the gates.
- **Winter:** admitted households crowd the wards; excluded groups disperse, fortify, evacuate, or die according to prior decisions.
- **Spring:** dismantled shelters, graves, claims, abandoned goods, and returning families create an aftermath landscape.
- **Summer:** surviving camp communities may become legal suburbs, cleared commons, memorial fields, or renewed seasonal ground.
