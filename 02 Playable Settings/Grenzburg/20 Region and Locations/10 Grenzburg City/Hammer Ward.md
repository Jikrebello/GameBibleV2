# Hammer Ward

Hammer Ward occupies the west bank's southern mill races and industrial ground. Foundries, workshops, regulated warlock practice, mills, yards, workers' houses, and repair stores make it Grenzburg's loudest district.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 2,400.
- **Primary authority:** craft guilds, ward officers, industrial owners, labor associations, and licensed warlock instructors.
- **Visual identity:** brick furnace stacks, timber galleries, mill channels, sparks, hoists, painted hazard marks, and crowded workers' courts.
- **Living conflict:** skilled production keeps walls, tools, heat, and weapons functioning while dangerous work and emergency demand tempt owners and rulers to spend workers as another resource.

## Anchor Locations

- **Warlock Hall:** rune training, counterscripts, licensing, and elemental safety.
- **Great Forge:** arms, repair, heavy craft, and vocation support.
- **Bellfounders' Yard:** civic and sacred bells, artillery metal, and contested contracts.
- **Millwheel Works:** pumps, machinery, water power, and sabotage.
- **Labor Hall:** wages, mutual aid, grievances, and collective action.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** commissions, repairs, apprentices, and fuel stockpiles strain the district.
- **Winter:** continuous war production, accidents, conscription, fuel shortage, and labor conflict alter services and streets.
- **Spring:** damaged works, salvage, funerals, unpaid promises, and reconstruction contracts reshape power.
- **Summer:** reopened guilds, worker protections, private monopolies, or military control become visible in ordinary production.

## Return Reasons

Crafting, Warlock and post-unlock Lancewright training, equipment repair, labour stories, proof disputes, faction resources, and reconstruction keep Hammer Ward relevant.
