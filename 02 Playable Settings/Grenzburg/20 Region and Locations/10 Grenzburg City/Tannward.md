# Tannward

Tannward occupies the east bank's southern approach. Barracks, store yards, smiths, animal pens, wall stairs, and the road to Timberfalls make it the hinge between city and frontier.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 2,500, rising sharply with soldiers and displaced households.
- **Primary authority:** the Castellan's officers, Marcher Vanguard, gate watch, and military storekeepers.
- **Visual identity:** heavier stone, packed yards, drill fields, timber galleries, smoke, wagons, and the south wall above the river road.
- **Living conflict:** professional defence is necessary, but requisition, curfew, weapon control, and triage can turn service into domination.

## Anchor Locations

- **Tann Gate:** principal southern checkpoint and campaign departure point.
- **Marcher Barracks:** training, service, command, and Gerhold's institutional base.
- **South Granary:** military and emergency stores distinct from the Crown reserve.
- **Wallwright Yard:** repair crews, siege craft, carpentry, and engineering.
- **Muster Green:** assembly, refugees, public discipline, and military ceremony.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** convoys, recruits, repairs, and news from Tannbruck dominate the ward.
- **Winter:** the gate becomes a battle space; wounded, prisoners, raids, fires, and scarce stores transform every yard.
- **Spring:** returning patrols, deserters, claims, ruined equipment, and Gerhold's choices change the ward's loyalties.
- **Summer:** demobilization, memorials, reconstruction, or permanent emergency rule determines its atmosphere.

## Return Reasons

Main-story deployments, ducal faction work, combat training, siege services, wall access, regional convoys, and later military reckoning repeatedly use Tannward.
