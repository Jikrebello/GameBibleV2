# Blackjack Yard

Blackjack Yard is a walled coaching compound in [[Lantern Ward]] converted into the Grenzburg company's barracks, stores, drill court, infirmary, debt office, and player headquarters. It is functional rather than prestigious: former wagon sheds become bunks and workshops, while the old stable court supports training and party organization.

## Play Functions

- change vocation and active party at safe points;
- maintain storage, crafting benches, trophies, and companion routines;
- receive Koss's orders, Brina Kest's company work, and selective Medori business;
- visibly improve or deteriorate through supplies, faction progress, the city breach, and Emerald law-gem custody;
- anchor relationship, company, memorial, and summer scenes.

The Yard never becomes sovereign ground beyond Grenzburg law. Bank ownership of contracts does not create a private prison, and ducal secondment does not erase those contracts by itself.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** crowded intake, improvised bunks, drill, equipment shortages, and new companion routines.
- **Winter:** casualty station, breached walls nearby, emergency stores, fire damage, and possible Green Ward support.
- **Spring:** recovery teams, body registers, Muster equipment quarantine, and company political conflict.
- **Summer:** free-company hall, cooperative yard, ducal corps post, Medori compound, or reduced barracks according to faction and service outcomes.
