# Bridgehold

Bridgehold is the fortified Great Bridge and the inhabited works at both ends. It is Grenzburg's only permanent wagon crossing, a market, a customs line, a military redoubt, and the place where the east and west cities are forced to deal with one another.

## Physical Form

Stone piers carry a broad road between two gate keeps. Shops and booths occupy protected recesses without narrowing the military lane. Lower stairs descend to ferries, inspection slips, chain mechanisms, and old bridge vaults. Both banks can seal their own gate, but neither can use the bridge normally while the other refuses passage.

## Play Uses

- public entry inspections, warrants, searches, and riots;
- bridge-market stories and cross-bank labor;
- rooftop, pier, and under-bridge pursuit;
- river-chain sabotage or defence;
- winter barricades and the final defence of a city route.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** freight jams, refugee inspections, and temporary stalls crowd the bridge.
- **Winter:** weapon checks, ration searches, siege barriers, and restricted ferries make passage political.
- **Spring:** ice damage, flood debris, structural inspection, and missing cargo expose the lower works.
- **Summer:** repairs, memorials, ownership of stalls, and the presence of guards reflect the settlement.
