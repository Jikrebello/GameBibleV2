# Crown Heights

Crown Heights occupies the east-bank bluff above [[Bridgehold]]. Grenzmark Keep, courts, reserve stores, service houses, and narrow noble streets make ducal government physically visible without turning the district into a palace compound.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 1,700.
- **Primary authority:** Duke Alaric, ducal household officers, high courts, and the Castellan's security staff.
- **Visual identity:** dark stone retaining walls, steep slate roofs, enclosed gardens, petition stairs, banners, and river overlooks.
- **Living conflict:** Grenzburg needs decisive government, but emergency rule can turn petition, ration, and military necessity into privilege protected by walls.

## Anchor Locations

- **Grenzmark Keep:** strategy, audience, council, and political settlement.
- **Hall of Petitions:** visible grievances and public standing.
- **High Court House:** warrants, trials, evidence, and jurisdiction.
- **Crown Granary:** protected reserve distinct from ordinary market stores.
- **Officers' Garden:** elite households, private meetings, and later memorials.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** petitioners and officers crowd the approaches while the Duke attempts to direct a frontier he cannot fully supply.
- **Winter:** access tightens, rooms become command space, and casualties or scarcity determine whether the Heights reads as refuge or insulation.
- **Spring:** investigations, claims, funerals, and Gerhold's institutional shadow enter spaces built for orderly rule.
- **Summer:** banners, guards, petition access, memorials, and repairs reveal the political settlement without fixing it in wider canon.

## Return Reasons

Ducal service, warrants, trials, public petitions, faction settlement, high-level companion scenes, and campaign gates repeatedly return the player here.
