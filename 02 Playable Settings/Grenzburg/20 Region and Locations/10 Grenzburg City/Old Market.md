# Old Market

Old Market is the east bank's commercial and civic middle: broad enough for grain carts and public gatherings, but surrounded by lanes built before either modern wall circuit. It is where official policy becomes price, rumor, work, and argument.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 3,000.
- **Primary authority:** civic market officers, craft guilds, the High Bailiff's agents, and ordinary watch companies.
- **Visual identity:** timber guildhouses over stone arcades, cloth awnings, public wells, inns, measure posts, and crowded rooflines.
- **Living conflict:** regulation protects buyers and workers while licences, monopolies, and patronage let powerful households decide who may trade or delve.

## Anchor Locations

- **Great Market:** ordinary trade, prices, rumors, and public events.
- **Hall of Measures:** weights, charters, guild hearings, and labor disputes.
- **Delvers' Arcade:** expedition contracts, salvage appraisal, trainers, and equipment.
- **Red Stag Inn:** travelers, companions, music, gossip, and informal negotiation.
- **Guild Court:** apprenticeship, craft politics, arbitration, and collective action.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** harvest traffic, recruitment, refugee labor, and expedition supply crowd the streets.
- **Winter:** ration stalls replace luxury trade, prices become political, and rooftops and back rooms carry clandestine work.
- **Spring:** salvage, lost-property claims, returned traders, and public accusations transform the market.
- **Summer:** reopened routes and reconstruction determine which guilds, delvers, and merchants prosper.

## Return Reasons

Core shopping, trainers, delver chains, guild and underworld work, public scenes, companion routines, and price changes keep Old Market useful throughout the game.
