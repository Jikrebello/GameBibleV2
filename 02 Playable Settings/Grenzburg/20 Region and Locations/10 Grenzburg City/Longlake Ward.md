# Longlake Ward

Longlake Ward fills the west-bank road fan between Lake Gate, the quays, and Hammer Ward. Fishers, Chapel-Folk households, carriers, guides, foster kin, and recent arrivals give it Grenzburg's strongest everyday connection to the western frontier.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 2,300.
- **Primary authority:** ward officers, fish and carrier associations, Chapel-Folk elders, parish clergy, and refuge stewards.
- **Visual identity:** smoking sheds, low timber courts, fish scales underfoot, sled yards, road shrines, clan marks, and long communal roofs.
- **Living conflict:** the ward translates between city and frontier, but both sides may demand loyalty while denying its people full trust, land, or jurisdiction.

## Anchor Locations

- **Fish Market:** Lakewatch trade, winter protein, ordinary work, and prices.
- **Bent Oak Chapel:** Chapel-Folk worship, mediation, and inherited identity.
- **Lake Gate:** western traffic, searches, refuge, and road control.
- **Ferry Yard:** carriers, sleds, lake-road guides, and travel services.
- **West Refuge Hall:** displaced households, clan delegates, and sanctuary disputes.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** fish carts, foster households, interpreters, and western refugees fill the ward.
- **Winter:** shelter pressure, frozen stores, sled traffic, suspicion, and Folk negotiations make it politically central.
- **Spring:** returning families, contested property, thawed fish stores, and road reopening expose broken promises.
- **Summer:** recognized rights, assimilation pressure, thriving trade, or displacement changes who remains visible.

## Return Reasons

Folk faction work, Chapel-Folk stories, Lakewatch travel, refuge consequences, food logistics, guides, and companion scenes repeatedly use the ward.
