# Grenzburg City Districts Overview

Grenzburg is a twin-walled river city built around the last dependable bridge over the north-flowing River Grenz. Its older, higher east bank holds crown, Church, court, and garrison. Its later west bank holds quays, industry, migrants, debtors, and the roads into Longlake country. The division is historical and practical rather than absolute: laborers work east, nobles invest west, and every district contains households that do not fit its public reputation.

Both halves lie inside permanent walls. [[Bridgehold]] is the only permanent crossing and can be defended from either bank. The player may enter every surface district after [[PROLOGUE THE BLACK ROAD|Warrant at the Gate]]; quests and standing govern particular interiors, roofs, walls, and underways rather than closing whole neighborhoods.

The winter peak counts people lodged within both walls. [[Outer Winter Camps]] may hold several thousand more before closure, exclusion, evacuation, or admission changes their state.

## Cross-City Layers

- [[Bridgehold]]: fortified bridge, bridge market, inspection points, river chains, and the city's central chokepoint.
- [[Grenzburg Wall Circuit]]: four land gates, towers, artillery positions, stores, and siege traversal.
- [[Grenzburg Underways]]: drains, cellars, old bridge vaults, quarry galleries, and criminal routes.
- [[Outer Winter Camps]]: the Funnel, gate fields, military camps, refugee shelters, and burial space.

Rooftops and riverworks are access overlays rather than ninth and tenth districts. [[Grenzburg City Adjacency and Access]] controls how these layers connect.

## City Use

- **Autumn:** every bank receives traffic, stores, petitioners, and displaced households at a different rate.
- **Winter:** the city becomes the campaign's densest worldspace; crowds, closures, shortages, patrols, and faction presence alter routes and services.
- **Spring:** burial, clearance, damage claims, reopened underways, and returning households expose what emergency rule concealed.
- **Summer:** reconstruction, memorials, faction control, prices, and public space reflect the completed campaign without selecting one political canon.
