# Grenzburg Wall Circuit

Grenzburg's two wall circuits meet the River Grenz at fortified boom towers and meet one another only through [[Bridgehold]]. The east wall is older, higher, and more regular. The west wall encloses later working districts and follows mills, flood ground, and reused foundations.

## Principal Works

- North Gate and the northern road towers;
- Tann Gate and southern military works;
- Lake Gate and western road towers;
- Mill Gate and west-bank service road;
- upstream and downstream river boom towers;
- inner fallback lines around Crown Heights and Bridgehold;
- magazines, wallwright stores, cisterns, signal stations, and casualty stairs.

## Play Grammar

The circuit is a connected traversal space rather than a painted boundary. Warrants, ladders, roof routes, damaged stairs, siege towers, and underways change access. Act II uses it for scouting, sabotage, defence, pursuit, civilian evacuation, and the material cost of holding a city.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** repair gangs, inspections, militia drill, and incoming carts dominate.
- **Winter:** siege damage, watch rotations, bodies, fire, and contested towers change routes.
- **Spring:** breaches, salvage, claims, unsafe stone, and burial work replace battle readiness.
- **Summer:** repaired or neglected sections display faction strength and reconstruction choices.
