# Grenzburg Underways

The Grenzburg Underways are discontinuous drains, cellars, old quarries, river culverts, bridge vaults, military galleries, and buried Atherian civil works. They are not a second city, a soul-road, a Jirahar Underway, or one continuous criminal tunnel.

## Access Families

- **Riverworks:** drains, chain chambers, flood sluices, and mill races.
- **Foundation works:** bridge vaults, wall galleries, granary culverts, and collapsed gate passages.
- **Quarry works:** extraction galleries beneath Lantern and Hammer wards.
- **Cellar chains:** privately joined basements, smuggler holes, shelters, and blocked wells.
- **Atherian remnants:** road inspection rooms and civic machinery with no automatic relation to the Deep Muster.

The [[Old Tithe Vault]] is a private bonded branch below Ledger Quays, reached through a warehouse stair, the Bridge Vaults, or an exterior flood channel. It is not a central junction and does not turn the underways into one continuous network.

## Play Rules

Every surface district has at least one known or discoverable entry, but underground adjacency follows old engineering rather than district borders. Named routes persist after discovery. Flood, siege collapse, faction control, and repairs may close one route while opening another.

Threats begin with people, bad air, water, collapse, vermin, and residue. A hidden space does not automatically justify a cosmic explanation.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** smugglers expand routes beneath crowded gates and store yards.
- **Winter:** dry drains become valuable while broken pipes, fires, and patrols alter control.
- **Spring:** floodwater closes low passages and exposes buried rooms elsewhere.
- **Summer:** repairs may formalize useful routes or leave them to criminal and delver control.
