# Timberfalls

Timberfalls is a river, lumber, milling, and transport settlement of about 1,800 people where the upper Grenz descends through a navigable series of falls and slips. Its fuel and structural timber are essential to the city, but its households live with the cost of clear-cutting, dangerous work, and military exposure.

## Services

Carriages, ox-carts, seasonal riverboats, sleds, timber craft, repairs, lodging, labor contacts, river guides, and onward military transport to Tannbruck.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** timber drives, convoy preparation, labor conflict, and fort supply dominate.
- **Winter:** occupation, raid, fire, or evacuation changes what remains accessible.
- **Spring:** salvage, log jams, damaged slopes, bodies, and returning households create reclamation play.
- **Summer:** reconstruction and forest policy determine whether the town recovers or is exhausted.
