# Fort Tannbruck

Fort Tannbruck is Grenzburg's principal forward stronghold at the Tann Crossing, where the upper River Grenz road meets the military spur toward the Tuskway uplands. The name refers to the fir-built bridge and palisade that preceded the present stone-and-timber fort.

The permanent garrison and service settlement contains about 650 people in ordinary season. Autumn muster, workers, traders, displaced households, and field forces can raise the population toward 1,800 before the fixed withdrawal.

## Strategic Role

Tannbruck watches the most practical route by which an organized southern host can move siege stores toward Grenzburg. It also protects seasonal traffic among Timberfalls, upland holdings, southern camps, and the near march. Holding it keeps warning and supply space between the city and the frontier. Losing it does not open an effortless highway, but it removes Grenzburg's best forward screen.

## Fixed Plan

The fort is a lived-in military settlement rather than an isolated castle. Its production plan contains:

- **Tann Bridge:** fir-and-stone crossing protected by river towers and a removable central span;
- **Inner Fort:** stone keep, command hall, magazine, signal tower, and final fallback yard;
- **Lower Bailey:** barracks, stable, smithy, infirmary, chapel, store yards, and kitchens;
- **Watch Hill:** detached outer work controlling the Tuskway spur;
- **Service Row:** carters, smith families, laundries, traders, guides, and civilian lodging;
- **Autumn Camp:** muster, refugee, animal, and labor ground outside the lower palisade;
- **Old Route Gallery:** Atherian inspection work beneath the bridge road, separate from [[Old River Arsenal]] and [[The Deep Muster]];
- **Withdrawal Road:** forested north-bank route toward Timberfalls with two defensible delay points.

Marcher regulars, militia rotations, carters, smiths, laundresses, healers, traders, guides, families, and displaced people give the place social life.

## Campaign Role

Tannbruck is introduced as a recurring Act I hub. The player solves local problems, uses its services, learns its defenders, and prepares the road. Its fall at the end of [[ACT I THE CLOSING ROADS]] is fixed. The survival of its people, records, animals, arms, and supplies is not.

The fort does not fall because of a hidden Pale-Skin conspiracy, Heart-Stone effect, or Bank sabotage. It falls because autumn closes the regional position while Ghor commits an organized Grobi coalition that Grenzburg cannot defeat so far from its walls.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** populated service hub, field headquarters, repairs, local quests, and visible preparation.
- **Winter:** occupied, burned, bypassed, stripped, or partly held ruin according to evacuation and denial results; it never remains a functioning ducal fort.
- **Spring:** reclamation space containing remains, survivors, stores, evidence, Grobi remnants, and first coordinated residue signs.
- **Summer:** rebuilt fort, memorial and watch post, abandoned ruin, or reduced crossing according to political and material outcomes.

## Persistence

The fall is fixed. Named defenders, civilians, animals, records, weapons, food, bridge sections, and Old Route access are recorded separately so prior work changes every later version of the space.
