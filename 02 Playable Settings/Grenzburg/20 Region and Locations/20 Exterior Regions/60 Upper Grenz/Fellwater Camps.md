# Fellwater Camps

Fellwater Camps are a chain of lumber, charcoal, and rafting camps between Timberfalls and Tannbruck. They produce essential fuel and bridge timber while exposing workers to flood, predation, coercive labor, and military requisition.

The Timberfalls operation in [[Roads Worth Saving]] addresses a real camp crisis without making every logging problem part of Ghor's plan. Later states affect winter fuel, spring return, and the material available for Tannbruck reconstruction.
