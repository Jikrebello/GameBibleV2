# Upper Grenz

The Upper Grenz follows the river south through falls, timber country, military roads, and the strategic crossing at [[Fort Tannbruck]]. It is the frontier's central supply spine and the region most visibly transformed by the three acts.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 4,000, including 1,800 in [[Timberfalls]].
- **Principal traversal problem:** river crossings, logging damage, steep valley roads, military checkpoints, and seasonal destruction.
- **Visual identity:** cold river gorge, falls, timber rafts, saw yards, cleared slopes, stone-and-timber forts, smoke, and wagon roads under forest walls.
- **Faction presence:** ducal troops, timber interests, workers, Chapel-Folk settlements, carriers, Grobi scouts, and later residue formations.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[Timberfalls]]: industrial settlement and river transport hub.
- [[Fort Tannbruck]]: recurring Act I hub, fixed fall, winter ruin, and spring reclamation space.
- [[Old River Arsenal]]: buried Atherian military-river complex independent of the Deep Muster.
- **Fellwater Camps:** medium logging and labor landscape.
- **Tann Crossing Farms:** medium vulnerable settlement belt around the fort road.

## Campaign Use

The player repeatedly travels this region before Tannbruck falls. Winter makes it occupied and dangerous. Spring reclamation returns the player through recognizable ruins. Act III uses its roads as one branch of the dead march without claiming the Muster caused its earlier conflicts.

## Return Reasons

Fort consequences, timber and fuel, river transport, faction work, reclamation, regional defence, and access to Frostfenn keep the Upper Grenz central.
