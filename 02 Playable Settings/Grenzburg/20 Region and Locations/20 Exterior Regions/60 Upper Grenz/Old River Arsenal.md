# Old River Arsenal

The Old River Arsenal is an Atherian military store, repair yard, lock, and fortified river gallery cut into the gorge between Timberfalls and Tannbruck. It supported ordinary imperial movement and is not a branch of the Deep Muster.

## Production Role

The Arsenal combines river traversal, war material, salvage law, fort logistics, and a reusable route-changing dungeon. Its machinery can affect transport without becoming a permanent Shell anchor.

## Access and Shape

- river landing below a broken defensive tower;
- towpath gate exposed at low water;
- military gallery from the Tann road;
- spring breach through a collapsed spillway.

## Encounter Families

Human salvagers or occupiers, flood, machinery, stored hazards, [[Atherian Ward and Trial Engines]], residue, vermin, and river predators.

## Persistence

Opening, sealing, stripping, or repairing the Arsenal changes Upper Grenz transport, Tannbruck salvage, and summer reconstruction. It does not reset after clearance.
