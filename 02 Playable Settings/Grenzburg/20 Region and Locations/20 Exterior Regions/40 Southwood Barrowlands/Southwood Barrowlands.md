# Southwood Barrowlands

The Southwood is an old-growth belt of damaged clearings, hunter trails, barrow fields, hidden valleys, and war-magic scars southwest of the upper river. It is the region where practical monster hunting and buried history first become landscape rather than isolated quest premises.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 700 in scattered clearings, forest-edge households, and service camps.
- **Principal traversal problem:** dense forest, broken sight lines, scarred ground, territorial predators, and routes made dangerous by weather.
- **Visual identity:** dark conifers, white birch breaks, tilted barrows, red-brown Ashfield scars, frozen ravines, hunter marks, and drake-damaged canopy.
- **Faction presence:** hunters, delvers, Moss-Crown and Ash-Spear traffic, ducal scouts, charcoal workers, and occasional warlock surveyors.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[Ashfield Lodge]]: trailhead, hunter service, and Road-Key custodian.
- [[Emerald Drake Range]]: mandatory winter hunt landscape and lair.
- [[Barrow of the First Chieftain]]: hidden optional high-level site with no main-story role.
- **Ashfield Scar:** medium war-magic footprint with material hazards and mixed-script residue.
- [[Seven Mounds]]: medium barrow cluster used by [[A House Fed by Bones]] for a coerced excavation, worker rescue, and burial-provenance conflict.

## Campaign Use

Autumn introduces hunting and scarred terrain. Winter transforms the Drake's range into a deliberate expedition. Spring exposes disturbed barrows and ecological aftermath. Summer shows whether the forest recovers, is exploited, or remains dangerous in new ways.

## Return Reasons

Monster hunts, materials, barrow delving, warlock investigation, Folk boundaries, advanced traversal, and the optional Heart-Stone adventure make Southwood a major optional pillar.
