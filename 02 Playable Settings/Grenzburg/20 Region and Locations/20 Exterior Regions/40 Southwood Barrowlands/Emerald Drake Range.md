# Emerald Drake Range

The Emerald Drake's range is a chain of forested ravines, wind-cut shelves, scarred feeding grounds, abandoned hunter shelters, and a cliff-root lair on Southwood's upland edge. The range is a hunt landscape rather than one boss chamber.

## Cause

The Drake is dangerous court-fauna displaced by three compounding pressures: ducal logging, refugee fuel-cutting, and Grobi war fires. Damage to the heart-oak network prevents stable redirection into a neighboring territory. It is not speaking, conspiratorial, patron-bound, Pale-Skin controlled, or responsible for the Grobi siege.

## Production Role

The range supports investigation, signs, preparations, alternate approaches, large-creature combat, wounded ecology, and a mandatory winter slaying whose casualties and aftermath remain variable.

## Access and Shape

- Ashfield hunter trail and ordinary winter ascent;
- fixed Road-Key passage from [[Ashfield Lodge]] to Drake Ridge after both stones are stabilized;
- ravine climb and grappling shortcuts;
- frozen stream approach exposed only in deep winter.

The **Heart-Oak Root Vault** is the final lair chamber beneath a damaged heart-oak. It is a natural court-fauna nesting and territorial site rather than a speaking shrine, Menhir court, Shell anchor, or buried human temple.

## Encounter Families

[[01 Canon Reference/Creatures/20 Faewild and Menhir/10 Drakes/Emerald Drakes.md|Emerald Drakes]], ordinary predators, displaced prey, dangerous plants, weather, falls, wounded terrain, and opportunistic human hunters. Not every encounter belongs to the Drake.

## Persistence

The Emerald Drake is slain in the critical path. The range remains explorable afterward with changed predators, materials, scavengers, regrowth, claims, and ecological consequences.

[[The Emerald Trail]] controls investigation and preparation. [[A Drake in Winter]] controls the silence-belt approach, ravine and canopy phases, heart-oak vault, death, and law-gem custody. Custody changes restoration and Act III support without making the gem a required final weapon.
