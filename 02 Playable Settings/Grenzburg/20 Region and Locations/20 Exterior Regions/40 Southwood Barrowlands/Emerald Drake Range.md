# Emerald Drake Range

The Emerald Drake's range is a chain of forested ravines, wind-cut shelves, scarred feeding grounds, abandoned hunter shelters, and a cliff-root lair on Southwood's upland edge. The range is a hunt landscape rather than one boss chamber.

## Cause

The Drake is dangerous court-fauna displaced or provoked by regional ecological change. Its exact local trigger belongs to the hunt block. It is not speaking, conspiratorial, patron-bound, Pale-Skin controlled, or responsible for the Grobi siege.

## Production Role

The range supports investigation, signs, preparations, alternate approaches, large-creature combat, wounded ecology, and a mandatory winter slaying whose casualties and aftermath remain variable.

## Access and Shape

- Ashfield hunter trail and ordinary winter ascent;
- fixed Road-Key passage from [[Ashfield Lodge]] to Drake Ridge after both stones are stabilized;
- ravine climb and grappling shortcuts;
- frozen stream approach exposed only in deep winter.

## Encounter Families

[[Emerald Drakes]], ordinary predators, displaced prey, dangerous plants, weather, falls, wounded terrain, and opportunistic human hunters. Not every encounter belongs to the Drake.

## Persistence

The Emerald Drake is slain in the critical path. The range remains explorable afterward with changed predators, materials, scavengers, regrowth, claims, and ecological consequences.
