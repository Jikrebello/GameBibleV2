# Seven Mounds

Seven Mounds is a dispersed burial and refuge complex in the Southwood Barrowlands, one difficult day beyond [[Ashfield Lodge]]. The name describes seven separate rises around a birch-ringed hollow, not one continuous underground dungeon.

## Cultural Status

The mounds were built, reopened, and repurposed by different local households over centuries. Some contain named dead; one served as winter refuge; one is an empty memorial marker; one has already collapsed. No universal Folk government owns them, but nearby households retain burial claims, warnings, and duties that delvers cannot erase by calling the site ancient.

Ashfield hunters maintain approach marks and report disturbance. Moss-Crown and other Southwood travelers can identify some boundaries, but no single speaker can waive every claim.

## Surface Layout

- **Hunter's Mark:** final warning and emergency cache before the hollow.
- **Seven-Rise Hollow:** central low ground from which every mound can be seen in winter.
- **Spoil Trenches:** old subsidence and new clandestine excavation cuts.
- **Birch Ring:** living boundary whose breaks reveal recent carts and powder movement.
- **Foreman's Camp:** temporary platforms, coercive work stores, tool shelter, and the local excavation crew's escape preparations.

## The Opened Mound

[[A House Fed by Bones]] uses the fourth mound, a burial chamber later connected to an emergency refuge passage.

Its playable layers are:

1. a cut entrance held by armed delvers;
2. a shored descent with deliberately weakened supports;
3. a names chamber containing damaged markers and non-conscious defensive residue;
4. a lower goods niche already stripped of some objects;
5. a refuge passage that can become a rescue route;
6. a powder fracture capable of collapsing the chamber and opening an alternate escape.

## Approaches

- **Hunter trail:** best observation and safest footing, but watched by Vasko's sentries.
- **Spoil trench:** direct access to workers and tools, exposed to collapse and crossfire.
- **Vertical crown:** rope descent through an old ventilation and offering shaft, requiring physical anchors available to every vocation through prepared gear.

Elara can identify faster routes and weak shoring. She is never the only way to enter or leave.

## Threats

- armed professional delvers and labor overseers;
- unstable soil, timber failure, powder, cold water, and confined smoke;
- ordinary ruin vermin;
- rattle-bone residue repeating a defensive path after grave markers are broken;
- panicked or injured workers whose rescue changes the encounter rather than adding an enemy faction.

The residue contains no trapped person. The remains retain burial claims even when the movement does not contain a soul.

## Persistent States

- **Preserved:** disturbance is contained, names remain, and local witnesses supervise repair.
- **Scarred:** people survive but one chamber and part of its record are lost.
- **Collapsed:** the opened mound is sealed by failure or deliberate containment; future recovery is difficult.
- **Looted:** portable goods leave without adequate record, strengthening fences and later claims.

Named people, grave damage, and recovered objects never reset with renewable Southwood encounters.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** carts and footprints remain readable; rain makes trenches unstable.
- **Winter:** frozen approaches are faster but the hollow traps wind and the refuge passage ices.
- **Spring:** runoff exposes spoil and carries small objects downslope; collapse pressure rises.
- **Summer:** repair, sanctioned study, abandonment, or scavenging reflects the quest outcome.
