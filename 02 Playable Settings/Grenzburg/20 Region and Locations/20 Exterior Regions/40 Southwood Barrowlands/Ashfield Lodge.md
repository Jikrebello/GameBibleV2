# Ashfield Lodge

Ashfield Lodge is a fortified hunter and survey station at the last dependable cart road before the deep Southwood. About 120 permanent residents support hunting, delving, charcoal traffic, scar monitoring, and rescue.

## Services

Rest, hunt preparation, monster materials, tracking instruction, field repairs, guides, delver notices, and route-specific Road Keys for Drake Ridge. Heavy carts stop here.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** hunters, charcoal crews, and delvers gather before road closure.
- **Winter:** the Lodge becomes the Drake expedition's trailhead and a refuge under pressure.
- **Spring:** wounded ecology, exposed barrows, missing hunters, and thawed scars alter its work.
- **Summer:** conservation, extraction, abandonment, or regulated delving changes the lodge's future.
