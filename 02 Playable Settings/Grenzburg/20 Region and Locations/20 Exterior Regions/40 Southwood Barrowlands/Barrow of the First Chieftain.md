# Barrow of the First Chieftain

The Barrow of the First Chieftain is a sealed high-level site hidden among Southwood's oldest mound fields. It contains the Heart-Stone known from the retired campaign treatment, but neither the stone nor its custodians cause the siege, Drake, Gerhold's fall, Deep Muster, or General Below.

## Canon Boundary

- The barrow and Heart-Stone exist in local canon.
- The site is absent from the initial ducal survey map.
- No critical-path objective enters it.
- No active faction owns its outcome.
- Its former theft, betrayal, and Dragon's Tomb plot are obsolete.

## Production Use

Reserve the barrow for a later optional adventure about place, custody, history, and the cost of disturbing a sealed power. Until then it remains closed, discoverable only through high-level exploration or future quest logic.
