# Sunken Causeway

The Sunken Causeway is an Atherian raised road, lockhouse, ferry station, and flood-control work partly drowned beneath Long Lake. It is material infrastructure rather than a Way-Gate or Menhir road.

## Production Role

The site makes water level, weather, local labor, and historical engineering into dungeon structure. Different seasons expose different entrances and chambers rather than applying a cosmetic lake texture.

## Access and Shape

- boat entry through the broken lockhouse in open water;
- winter descent through stable ice shafts;
- spring access when flood exposes maintenance stairs;
- raised-road approach through reed islands and collapsed arches.

## Encounter Families

Cold water, drowning, unstable masonry, natural lake predators, [[Natural Oozes and Slimes]], [[Ruin Vermin and Swarms]], residue, and non-conscious civic ward engines.

## Persistence

The player may stabilize, drain, seal, or leave sections flooded. The result changes ferries, fishing, smuggling, and spring access without selecting one future canon.
