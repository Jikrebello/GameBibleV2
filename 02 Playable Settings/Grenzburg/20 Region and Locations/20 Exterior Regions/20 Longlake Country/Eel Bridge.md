# Eel Bridge

Eel Bridge is a low timber-and-stone crossing over a reed channel feeding Long Lake. Net racks, a ferry slip, eel weirs, and flood posts make it both road infrastructure and household livelihood.

The Lakewatch operation in [[Roads Worth Saving]] can hold, repair, or lose the crossing while protecting civilians and food traffic. Its winter ice route and spring flood state depend on that outcome rather than on a permanent enemy camp.
