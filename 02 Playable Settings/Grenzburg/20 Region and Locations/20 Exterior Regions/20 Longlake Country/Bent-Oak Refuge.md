# Bent-Oak Refuge

Bent-Oak Refuge is a Chapel-Folk communal hall and protected store on the western lake road. It serves as a teaching destination in [[Little Hands, Long Road]] and as ordinary shelter infrastructure before and after that quest.

## Site

The refuge includes a long timber hall, raised dry store, animal lean-to, rain cistern, road bell, smoke vent, and a small enclosed yard. Its door mark promises one night's emergency shelter before questions of district, confession, or payment are settled.

The promise is enforceable through participating households rather than a universal Folk government. Persistent abuse may close ordinary hospitality after the immediate danger has passed.

## Seasonal Use

- **Autumn:** school journeys, fish carts, foster transfers, and winter-store preparation.
- **Winter:** crowded shelter, sled exchange, ration count, and emergency triage.
- **Spring:** flood refuge, damaged-floor repair, and return-household disputes.
- **Summer:** road school, weddings, work meals, and public compact meetings.

## Quest State

The wounded bear in [[Little Hands, Long Road]] is an ordinary animal injured by an abandoned snare. Its survival or death does not sanctify or curse the refuge. Child injuries, lost supplies, and household trust remain persistent local dialogue states.
