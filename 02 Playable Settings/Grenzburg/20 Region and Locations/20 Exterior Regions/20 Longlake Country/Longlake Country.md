# Longlake Country

Longlake Country spreads west of Grenzburg around Long Lake, marsh roads, fisheries, refuge halls, Chapel-Folk settlements, and old imperial spurs. It is settled enough to be legible and culturally mixed enough that every road carries overlapping obligations.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 3,600, including 1,600 in [[Lakewatch]].
- **Principal traversal problem:** water, marsh causeways, seasonal ice, ferry dependence, and roads that change with thaw.
- **Visual identity:** broad cold water, reed beds, smoking sheds, low wooded islands, raised roads, fish racks, chapel roofs, and refuge markers.
- **Faction presence:** Lakewatch households, Chapel-Folk elders, ducal carriers, Church missions, Moss-Crown visitors, smugglers, and winter officials.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[Lakewatch]]: the principal fishery and western service hub.
- [[Sunken Causeway]]: flooded Atherian road-and-water infrastructure.
- [[Bent-Oak Refuge]]: medium communal hall and protected store on the western road.
- **Netter's Isle:** medium ferry, fishery, and smuggling site exposed differently by water and ice.

## Campaign Use

Autumn food and shelter work introduces western politics. Winter turns the lake into both road and battlefield. Spring exposes drowned infrastructure and disputed stores. Summer reveals whether lake communities remain connected to Grenzburg or turn toward local autonomy.

## Return Reasons

Fish supply, ferry and sled services, Chapel-Folk stories, Folk introductions, water hunts, smuggling, and the route into Wolfsmere sustain repeated use.

## Boundary

Birchcross remains wider local canon but lies outside the base-game player map. Its adventure, cast, and outcome are not imported here.
