# Lakewatch

Lakewatch is a fishing, smoking, boatbuilding, and refuge settlement of about 1,600 people on Long Lake's eastern shore. Its food stores matter to Grenzburg, but it is a living community rather than a supply depot with houses attached.

## Services

Ferries, winter sleds, fish and preserved food, boats, marsh guides, basic crafting, lodging, Road Keys for the outer Wolfsmere route, and Chapel-Folk mediation.

## Place Character

Raised timber walks, smokehouses, net yards, low stone storehouses, ice ramps, communal halls, and a weathered lake watchtower create a practical waterside hub.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** smokehouses and refuge stores work at full capacity while the lake road crowds.
- **Winter:** the frozen lake opens fast routes and new dangers; stored fish becomes political.
- **Spring:** unsafe ice, flood, lost boats, and exposed causeway stones remake access.
- **Summer:** fishing rights, rebuilt fleets, and Grenzburg's settlement determine prosperity.
