# Bone-Reed Pool

Bone-Reed Pool is a black-water fen basin where pale mineral reeds grow through a buried Qianglong signal platform. Before the Unending Muster it is an ominous navigation hazard. During Act III, recovered field plates let the platform project human likeness across its rank lanes.

[[No Human Shadow]] uses the pool and its attached command screen. The projections are non-conscious computations. No copied face contains the person it resembles.
