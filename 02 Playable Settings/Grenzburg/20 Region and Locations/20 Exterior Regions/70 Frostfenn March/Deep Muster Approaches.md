# Deep Muster Approaches

The Deep Muster Approaches are the surface roads, depots, intake yards, signal cuts, and sealed descents through which the buried Qianglong complex once received armies. Permafrost, forest, fen, and later settlement concealed them as separate ruins until spring recurrence begins aligning them again.

## Three Ancient Roads

- **Fenn Road:** western mobilization route through migration country and White Mud Causeway.
- **Upper Grenz Road:** central heavy route toward Tannbruck, river transport, and Grenzburg.
- **Tuskway Road:** eastern officer and warband route through Highlake passes.

These branches make the dead march a regional event. The Muster did not cause the existing modern roads, Grobi migration, or Ghor's campaign; it exploits overlapping terrain after awakening.

## Surface Entries

- **Intake Gate:** main military descent, inaccessible until the final operation.
- **Three Horn Depot:** outer command and supply work revealed in spring.
- **Last Hearth Shaft:** damaged maintenance route discovered through local route work.
- **Fen Wound:** unstable emergency breach that offers a dangerous alternate finale entry.

[[The Unending Muster Main Quest|The Unending Muster]] secures the first surface access and Last Hearth forward base. [[The Dead March]] establishes final assault strength. [[The Deep Muster Main Quest|The Deep Muster]] then supports three complete entries: the direct Intake Gate, quieter Last Hearth Shaft, and unstable Fen Wound. Every vocation can complete each route.

## Road-Key Limit

The final Menhir path ends at an exterior approach stone. It never enters the Qianglong complex, carries an army, bypasses the final operation, or remains reliable once the General Below distorts the area.
