# Fenn Road Exchange

Fenn Road Exchange is a seasonal palisade, raised market, refuge yard, and witness ground at the last broad meeting of southern routes. Fewer than 180 people remain year-round, but traders, hunters, Folk delegations, Grobi dependants, scouts, and smugglers may increase that number severalfold.

## Services

Seasonal sled transport, shelter, repair, food at frontier prices, guides, route news, translators, prisoner exchange, and negotiated passage. It is not a neutral state or a Grobi embassy.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** migration, warning, trade, and passage disputes define the Exchange.
- **Winter:** the market disperses or fortifies; remaining services depend on prior safety work.
- **Spring:** survivors, captives, lost property, residue signs, and reopened paths return.
- **Summer:** the Exchange may become a recognized frontier institution or remain a precarious seasonal truce.
