# Last Hearth

Last Hearth is the final dependable refuge before the far southern fen. A ringwork, store cellar, signal mast, communal hall, and route stones shelter about seventy permanent keepers and their dependants.

## Services

Rest, deep-march equipment, guides, route repair, Road Keys for the final fixed paths, weather warning, and emergency burial. No wagon or public transport continues beyond it.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** hunters, scouts, and migrating households exchange warnings before closure.
- **Winter:** Last Hearth becomes isolated and may be held, evacuated, or lost.
- **Spring:** it anchors Qianglong investigation and the first safe route toward the Muster.
- **Summer:** survivors either restore the watch or abandon the deep road after final closure work.
