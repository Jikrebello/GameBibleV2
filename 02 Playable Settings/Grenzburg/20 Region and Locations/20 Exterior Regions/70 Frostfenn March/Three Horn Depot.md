# Three Horn Depot

Three Horn Depot is a ruined Qianglong migration store and outer command work beside three converging cold-road cuts. Grobi travelers have reused its surface shelters without knowing the full lower function; that use does not make Ghor or Grobi responsible for the Muster.

In spring, [[Sir Gerhold Brandt]] brings copied rank tablets, diverted remains, command equipment, and living loyalists here. [[Gerhold's Choice]] resolves his accepted command. The depot is separate from the five regional anchors and does not substitute for the deepest altar.
