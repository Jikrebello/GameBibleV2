# White Mud Causeway

White Mud Causeway is a broken raised road across false-firm fen south of Last Hearth. Timber repairs, ancient stone islands, thaw channels, and black-mud contamination make it a changing traversal and defense landscape rather than one corridor encounter.

The Fen Ossuary lies beyond the public road. While active it pumps black mire and rebuilding residue toward the Deep Muster. The causeway supports its optional anchor operation, the Last Hearth expedition, and the Fenn Road front in [[The Dead March]].
