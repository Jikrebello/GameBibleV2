# Frostfenn March

Frostfenn is the deep southern march: frozen wetlands, black spruce, permafrost shelves, sparse refuge points, migration corridors, and ancient roads whose destinations have been forgotten. It is not an empty evil waste. Folk watchers, traders, Grobi dependants, hunters, and isolated households use it differently and at great cost.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 450, with large seasonal movement that never becomes a stable town population.
- **Principal traversal problem:** distance, cold, frozen water, false firm ground, sparse shelter, migration traffic, and routes that become military corridors.
- **Visual identity:** low black forest, white fen, wind-polished ice, raised timber roads, abandoned towers, aurora, deep mud at thaw, and Qianglong stones emerging from permafrost.
- **Faction presence:** local route keepers, Fenn traders, scattered Folk households, Herd-Matron Uru's dependants, Ghor's splinters, hunters, and dormant Qianglong recurrences.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[Fenn Road Exchange]]: seasonal trade, intelligence, and last public service point.
- [[Last Hearth]]: final dependable shelter and Road-Key custody.
- [[Deep Muster Approaches]]: surface mobilization roads, depots, and sealed entries.
- [[The Deep Muster]]: final mega-dungeon beneath the far southern fen.
- **Three Horn Depot:** medium Qianglong outer work awakening in spring.
- **White Mud Causeway:** medium route and dead-march defence landscape.

## Campaign Use

Autumn presents migration and survival rather than the finale. Winter makes travel exceptional and separates Ghor's war coalition from dependants seeking corridors. Spring exposes Qianglong works and organized residue. The Muster Crisis turns the region into the origin of three northbound dead roads.

## Return Reasons

Grobi negotiations, extreme hunts, rare materials, route restoration, Qianglong investigation, main-story operations, and summer closure work justify returning after each opening.
