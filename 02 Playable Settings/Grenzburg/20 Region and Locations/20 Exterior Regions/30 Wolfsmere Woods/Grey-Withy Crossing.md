# Grey-Withy Crossing

Grey-Withy Crossing is an outer-wood ford, meeting ground, and fixed waystone at the edge of Moss-Crown territory. It is a place of negotiated passage, not a border post for a universal Folk state.

In [[Roads Worth Saving]], the player can address a specific hostile war-band while preserving the distinction between the valid boundary, Moss-Crown households, and total-war followers. The inner sacred boundary remains beyond convenient waystone travel.
