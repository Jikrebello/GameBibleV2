# Bellless Hold

Bellless Hold is a ruined Eclessian occupation fort built to watch the approaches to Wolfsmere after the Folk Wars. Its chapel tower was deliberately stripped of its bell when the garrison discovered that sound carried wrongly toward the lake.

## Production Role

The Hold combines human occupation history, culpable violence, present material danger, anchored residue, and place-law without declaring either culture innocent in every dispute or making the ruin a generic haunted castle.

## Access and Shape

- forest road through a collapsed outer bailey;
- root-covered postern recognized by local guides;
- dry moat and supply culvert;
- roof and watchtower route for skilled traversal.

## Encounter Families

Human intruders or relic seekers, forest predators, [[Anchored Hauntings]], [[Oathwood Wardens]], dangerous local-court manifestations, collapse, and old weapons. The dead are not trapped souls awaiting routine negotiation.

## Persistence

The Hold may be resealed, memorialized, reclaimed as a watch, or left under local custody. Any solution must address remains, present safety, and the fort's claim over surrounding land separately.
