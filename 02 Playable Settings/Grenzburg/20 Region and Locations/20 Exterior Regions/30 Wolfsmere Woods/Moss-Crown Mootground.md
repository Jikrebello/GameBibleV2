# Moss-Crown Mootground

The Moss-Crown Mootground is a cluster of longhouses, guest shelters, store pits, craft yards, and witness trees around a protected assembly clearing. It is not a Folk capital. It serves Moss-Crown territory and hosts outsiders only under named local responsibility.

## Services

Conditional rest, herbal supplies, hunting craft, guides, Folk knowledge, local-court interpretation, and Road Keys for fixed deep-wood paths. Services depend on conduct and specific relationships rather than one universal Folk reputation bar.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** treaty, store, hunting, and route disputes fill the clearing.
- **Winter:** households consolidate, scouts rotate, and paths narrow under snow and danger.
- **Spring:** boundary damage, graves, returned foster kin, and court signs demand settlement.
- **Summer:** the moot recognizes or rejects new roads, sanctuary terms, and political arrangements.
