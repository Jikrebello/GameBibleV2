# Outer Road Stone

Outer Road Stone marks the first customary permission boundary on the public Wolfsmere approach. Its cut marks record seasonal passage, warning, shelter obligation, and routes that must not be widened without a local hearing.

Moving or ignoring the stone does not make every Folk claim valid; neither does a ducal road erase this one. [[Roads Worth Saving]] uses it as physical evidence in the dispute leading toward Grey-Withy Crossing.
