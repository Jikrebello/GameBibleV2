# Wolfsmere Woods

Wolfsmere Woods begin beyond the western lake roads and deepen toward the Lake That Remembers. This is living Folk country, not an empty supernatural forest. Households, hunting grounds, memorial paths, groves, winter stores, and customary boundaries exist beside places whose laws are genuinely dangerous.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 1,500 in dispersed households, halls, and seasonal communities.
- **Principal traversal problem:** path recognition, local permission, false adjacency, wet ground, and the cost of crossing a boundary incorrectly.
- **Visual identity:** old conifer and birch forest, black water, root roads, mossed standing stones, longhouses, memorial cords, and distant pale lake light.
- **Faction presence:** Moss-Crown households, Lakekeeper-line seers, Grey-Withy traders, Chapel-Folk cousins, local courts, hunters, and occasional ducal or Church intruders.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[Moss-Crown Mootground]]: conditional service hub and political meeting place.
- [[Bellless Hold]]: ruined Eclessian occupation fort under place-law pressure.
- **Grey-Withy Crossing:** medium neutral exchange and route-custodian station.
- **Drowned Roll Paths:** medium memorial landscape approaching Wolfsmere's taboo boundary.

## Sacred Boundary

No boat, bell, or iron crosses Wolfsmere's inner law. No convenience waystone stands at the lake. The nearest fixed Menhir path ends in the outer woods, leaving the final approach on foot under local guidance.

## Campaign Use

Wolfsmere carries the game's deepest optional Folk history, Menhir route play, occupation wounds, and place-bound mysteries. It need not explain the Grobi siege, Emerald Drake, or Deep Muster to matter.

## Return Reasons

Folk standing, route access, rare medicines, hunts, memorial rites, local-court stories, companion content, and changing forest law support long-term exploration.
