# Broken Tollworks

The Broken Tollworks are a collapsed Atherian road station, bridge inspection complex, drainage gallery, and later marcher tollhouse beside the Black Road. Ordinary abandonment, quarry damage, flood, and criminal occupation explain its present condition.

## Production Role

This is the first substantial delve that teaches observation, alternate entry, human opposition, physical hazards, simple residue, and one legible Atherian civic engine without introducing the setting's final metaphysics.

## Access and Shape

- public ruined toll yard from the Black Road;
- drainage culvert from the river meadow;
- quarry ledge and collapsed roof route;
- lower inspection galleries beneath the road.

## Encounter Families

Bandits or deserters, guard animals, flood and collapse, [[Ruin Vermin and Swarms]], limited [[Corpse Residue]], and one [[Atherian Ward and Trial Engines|Atherian road engine]].

## Persistence

Clearing the Tollworks permanently changes road safety and later occupation. It may become a watch post, shelter, criminal cache, or abandoned ruin; it does not reset to its original population.
