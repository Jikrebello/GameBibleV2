# Black Road Quarry

Black Road Quarry is an active civil stone cutting above the northern road. Benches, waste slopes, crane tracks, lime sheds, and a disused haul tunnel provide ordinary labor space and concealed approaches without making the site an ancient supernatural ruin.

The Broken Axle Tollers use its abandoned benches to stage the convoy sabotage in [[The Black Road Main Quest|The Black Road]]. Afterward it can reopen, remain dangerous, or support road repair according to surviving workers and recovered tools.
