# Grenz Lowlands

The Grenz Lowlands are the warmer farms, mills, flood meadows, coach roads, and river land north of Grenzburg. This is the player's first landscape and the frontier's least exotic region. Its dangers arise first from people, traffic, weather, animals, failed infrastructure, and opportunity.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 4,200 across farms, mills, roadside settlements, and [[The Funnel]].
- **Principal traversal problem:** congestion, washed crossings, tolls, blocked carts, and river channels rather than wilderness navigation.
- **Visual identity:** open fields, willow banks, mills, low ridges, coach yards, stacked hay, roadside chapels, and Grenzburg's walls on the southern horizon.
- **Faction presence:** ducal road officers, Bank carriers, Blackjacks, Church shelters, guild agents, bandits, and ordinary local households.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[The Funnel]]: permanent coaching settlement and autumn arrival camp.
- [[Broken Tollworks]]: grounded early delve through abandoned civil infrastructure.
- **Three Mills Reach:** medium water-and-labor site where competing mill races threaten farms.
- **Black Road Quarry:** medium extraction site, ambush ground, and prologue-adjacent landmark.

## Campaign Use

The prologue and first road work establish ordinary stakes here. Later returns show convoy losses, siege isolation, spring flood, released prisoners, and whether northern trade can reach the city.

## Return Reasons

Transport, road jobs, farms, low-level hunts, refugee consequences, river access, and summer trade keep the region active after onboarding.
