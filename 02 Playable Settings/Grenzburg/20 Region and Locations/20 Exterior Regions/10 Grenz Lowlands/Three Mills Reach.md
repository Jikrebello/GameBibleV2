# Three Mills Reach

Three Mills Reach is a small hamlet built around three watermills sharing one managed leat. Its flour, saw work, and bridge make it more important than its population suggests, while competing mill households and downstream farmers dispute water even without an outside enemy.

[[The Closing Roads]] rescues travelers and restores the approach before the player enters [[Broken Tollworks]]. [[Roads Worth Saving]] may later settle labor, flood-control, and grain movement. Bandit clearance does not resolve the hamlet's ordinary rivalries.
