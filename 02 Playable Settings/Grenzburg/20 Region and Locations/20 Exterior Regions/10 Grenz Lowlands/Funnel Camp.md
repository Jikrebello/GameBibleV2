# Funnel Camp

Funnel Camp is the overflow ground north and east of [[The Funnel]], laid out in wagon lanes around drainage ditches, cook sheds, animal lines, a roadside chapel, and a ducal inspection post. It is the first place where the player sees the scale of the closing roads.

In [[Warrant at the Gate]], convoy survivors arrive amid a medicine-wagon theft, crowd panic, and competing priorities for shelter. The camp's condition reflects Black Road survivors and cargo rather than resetting to one tutorial state.

Autumn crowds it with travelers and refugees; winter empties exposed lanes and fortifies warehouses; spring floods low ground and fills it with claimants; summer restores commerce or leaves memorial plots according to losses.
