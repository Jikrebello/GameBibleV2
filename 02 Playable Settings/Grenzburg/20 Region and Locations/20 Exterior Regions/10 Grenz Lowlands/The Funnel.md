# The Funnel

The Funnel is the permanent coaching, toll, animal, and warehouse settlement where the northern road narrows toward Grenzburg. About 900 people live here year-round; as many as 3,000 travelers, workers, petitioners, and refugees may crowd its yards in late autumn.

## Services

Carriages, ox-carts, river freight, repairs, basic lodging, stables, food, road news, and low-level Blackjack work remain available while the northern road holds.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** arrival camp, tutorial aftermath, recruitment, and traffic crisis.
- **Winter:** abandoned yards, guarded warehouses, stranded households, and northern raids.
- **Spring:** flood clearance, recovered carts, claims, and road reopening.
- **Summer:** renewed commerce or reduced frontier traffic according to city survival.
