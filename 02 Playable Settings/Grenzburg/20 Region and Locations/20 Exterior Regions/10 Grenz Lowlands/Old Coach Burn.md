# Old Coach Burn

Old Coach Burn is a shallow stream crossing beside the charred frame of a long-abandoned coaching house. A timber bridge, steep drainage banks, a roadside shrine, and the nearby quarry road create the three-way terrain used in the opening convoy attack.

[[The Black Road Main Quest|The Black Road]] permanently records wrecks, graves, recovered cargo, and survivors here. Later seasons show clearance, a memorial, renewed bandit use, or public road repair rather than resetting the ambush.
