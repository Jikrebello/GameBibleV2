# Highlake Uplands

Highlake Uplands rise east of the Upper Grenz into cold tarns, mines, exposed passes, Ghost-Foot crags, and the Tuskway military corridor. The region combines productive settlement with vertical travel and a frontier that can see danger coming without always being able to stop it.

## Profile

- **Permanent population:** about 2,600, including 1,400 in [[Highlake]].
- **Principal traversal problem:** elevation, narrow ledges, mine roads, avalanche, exposed weather, and defended passes.
- **Visual identity:** black rock, pale grass, cold tarns, mine gantries, rope lifts, cairns, low clouds, and immense views toward the south.
- **Faction presence:** miners, mine owners, Ghost-Foot households, ducal scouts, warlock surveyors, hunters, smugglers, and Ghor's eastern pressure.

## Major and Supporting Sites

- [[Highlake]]: mining hub, upland market, and public transport terminus.
- [[Cold-Iron Deeps]]: vertical mine complex with layered human and ancient use.
- [[Ropehouse Ruin]]: a damaged lift-and-rope station used for mixed-crew delving and custody practice.
- [[Rain Vault]]: a blue-glass weather chamber whose beauty and danger survive together.
- **Ghost-Foot Stair:** medium cliff route, settlement approach, and Road-Key terminus.
- **Tuskway Redoubt:** medium abandoned marcher work overlooking the invasion corridor.

## Campaign Use

Autumn establishes mines, passes, and local claims. Winter makes the Tuskway a military front. Spring exposes collapsed works and the first Qianglong route evidence. Summer determines access, ownership, and whether the uplands remain inhabited.

## Return Reasons

Mining, advanced materials, vertical traversal, monster hunting, Ghost-Foot politics, warlock work, military reconnaissance, and Deep Muster foreshadowing sustain the region.
