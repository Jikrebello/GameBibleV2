# Ropehouse Ruin

Ropehouse Ruin is a stepped Atherian freight station on Highlake's lower slopes. Later households reused its winches, lofts, rain stair, and stone rope channels. The name comes from the surviving hauling gallery, not a known ancient title.

Its accessible outer works support [[A Splendid Little Expedition]]. A sealed survey loft opens only after spring stabilization and becomes the site of [[The First Honest Find]].

The ruin has living claimants: route workers, local custodians, and crews who maintain the modern descent. Discovery never creates ownership by itself.
