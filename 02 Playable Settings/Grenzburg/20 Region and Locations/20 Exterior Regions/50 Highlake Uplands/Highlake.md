# Highlake

Highlake is a mining and pass settlement of about 1,400 people beside a cold upland tarn. Mine yards, rope lifts, smithies, terrace houses, pack tracks, and a fortified storehouse make it a working mountain town rather than an isolated quest village.

## Services

Carriage and sled terminus, mining supplies, smithing, advanced materials, climbing gear, guides, lodging, upland hunts, and Road Keys for Ghost-Foot heights and the High Tuskway.

## Seasonal Role

- **Autumn:** ore convoys, pass closure, mine disputes, and winter provisioning dominate.
- **Winter:** the town becomes an exposed military and supply position under Tuskway pressure.
- **Spring:** collapse, meltwater, reopened shafts, and ancient route signs reshape work.
- **Summer:** mine ownership, Ghost-Foot relations, and restored transport determine recovery.
