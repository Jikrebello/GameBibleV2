# Cold-Iron Deeps

Cold-Iron Deeps is a vertical mining complex beneath Highlake: active upper workings, abandoned shafts, flooded galleries, older Atherian extraction rooms, and natural caverns cut by cold water.

## Production Role

The Deeps support mining politics, rescue, vertical traversal, industrial danger, materials, and older evidence without treating every buried chamber as part of the Deep Muster.

## Access and Shape

- regulated main incline from Highlake mine yards;
- rope-lift maintenance shaft;
- Ghost-Foot cliff passage entering abandoned levels;
- spring meltwater outlet.

## Encounter Families

Collapse, gas, water, labor violence, ordinary predators, [[Burrowers of Road and Deep]], [[Natural Oozes and Slimes]], residue, and limited Atherian works. Qianglong signs appear only in a separate lower route discovered in spring.

## Persistence

Rescue, ownership, safety work, and closure determine which levels reopen and what Highlake can produce. Cleared dangers remain resolved unless a later world-state event introduces a new population for a stated reason.

In [[The Open Thaw]], a separate spring lower route contains the Cold-Iron Muster Shaft. Its destruction is fixed; miner survival and the damage done to ordinary workings vary. The anchor does not make the rest of the mine Qianglong infrastructure.
