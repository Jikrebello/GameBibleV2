# Tuskway Redoubt

Tuskway Redoubt is an abandoned marcher fort overlooking the eastern invasion corridor. Its lower works reuse older Atherian road stone; a sealed Qianglong Banner Vault lies beneath those later foundations without making the whole redoubt a Muster construction.

The site supports the Highlake operation in [[Roads Worth Saving]], the lower approach to [[Break the Southern Horn]], faction and companion conflicts over carrier service, and the spring Tuskway anchor operation under [[The Land Reclaimed]]. Each use must respect prior occupation and damage.
