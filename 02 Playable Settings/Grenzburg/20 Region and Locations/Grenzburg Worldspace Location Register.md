# Grenzburg Worldspace Location Register

This register controls content placement below the regional-hub level. A name in this table reserves a physical site and broad purpose. [[Main Campaign MOC]] now assigns critical-path ownership; later waves assign remaining side content, room layouts, and implementation budgets.

## Allocation Summary

| Scale | Count | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| City district anchors | 40 | Five reusable anchors in each of eight districts; controlled by [[Grenzburg City Anchor Register]] |
| Cross-city anchors | 12 | Bridge, walls, underways, riverworks, roofs, and camps; controlled by [[Grenzburg City Anchor Register]] |
| Exterior major spaces | 9 | Regional signature spaces and campaign-scale sites |
| Exterior medium sites | 14 | Two authored quest or dungeon spaces per region |
| Exterior minor landmarks | 49 | Seven navigation, encounter, story, or job anchors per region |

Named dungeons retain a cleared, occupied, damaged, sealed, or transformed state. Only sites explicitly marked **renewable** may repopulate for hunts, camps, or jobs.

## Content Placement Rules

1. A critical-path chapter may claim a reserved site, but cannot silently move it to another region.
2. A faction or companion quest records its site's seasonal window and persistent result before blocking begins.
3. Minor landmarks receive dedicated notes only when their quest, named cast, or state complexity justifies one.
4. Renewable sites reset only their designated hunt, camp, or job layer. Named people, discoveries, structures, and moral outcomes persist.
5. New sites require a map ID and must preserve each region's traversal identity and content budget.
