# The Black Road

The Black Road enters the base-game map from warmer northern Eclessia, reaches [[The Funnel]], and passes through Grenzburg's North Gate. It is the march's broadest civil road and the player's prologue route.

## Physical Contract

- Northern map edge to the Funnel: one in-world day by ordinary cart.
- Funnel to Grenzburg: half a day under normal traffic.
- Compressed play: frequent safe nodes and services about ten minutes apart.
- Surface: raised gravel, drainage ditches, milestone shrines, toll ruins, mill spurs, and flood bypasses.

The road is legitimate infrastructure rather than a sovereign strip extending beyond surveyed approaches. Farms and hamlets retain their ordinary rights beside it.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** carts, refugees, harvest freight, deserters, and patrols create congestion.
- **Winter:** the northern section remains the city's strongest land lifeline until raids or gate closure interrupt it.
- **Spring:** washouts and floodwater create temporary bypasses through the Lowlands.
- **Summer:** repairs, memorials, new toll policy, and convoy confidence reflect campaign outcomes.

## Production Use

Use the Black Road for convoy work, bandit pressure, escorts, traffic events, grounded investigations, and visible changes in public order. [[Broken Tollworks]] supplies its major early ruin; the route should not acquire a second interchangeable toll dungeon.
