# Fenn Road

The Fenn Road descends from the Southwood and western migration country through [[Fenn Road Exchange]] to [[Last Hearth]]. It is a chain of witnessed paths, raised fen traces, old store points, and winter shelters rather than one uniformly built ducal road.

## Physical Contract

- Grenzburg to Fenn Road Exchange: four to five in-world days by the viable approaches.
- Grenzburg to Last Hearth: about six days in safe autumn weather.
- South of the exchange, ordinary carts cease to be practical.
- Sled services and the WK-6 and WK-7 Menhir pairs operate only while custodians, endpoints, and local compacts hold.

The corridor carries dependents, traders, raiders, scouts, and splinter bands. It cannot be read as a single hostile invasion road.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** migration intelligence and closing shelters matter more than road speed.
- **Winter:** prepared sled paths may improve movement while war and hunger make every stop dangerous.
- **Spring:** white mud, flood, rotten ice, and the damaged causeway create severe closures.
- **Summer:** survival, local agreements, and reclamation decide which shelters return.

## Production Use

Use the Fenn Road for survival journeys, migration encounters, negotiated passage, scarce safe nodes, western dead-march pressure, and the final approach to the Deep Muster basin.
