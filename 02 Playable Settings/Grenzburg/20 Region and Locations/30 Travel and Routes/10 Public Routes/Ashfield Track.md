# Ashfield Track

The Ashfield Track leaves the west-bank mill roads, crosses the southern edge of Longlake country, and reaches [[Ashfield Lodge]] after roughly three in-world days from Grenzburg. Beyond the lodge it divides toward barrow country, the Drake Ridge trail, and the Fenn Road.

## Physical Contract

The northern grade can carry hired carts in good weather. Its southern branches are hunter and refuge tracks rather than public wagon roads. Charcoal clearings, marked cairns, bounded war-magic scars, and deep snow make local knowledge materially useful.

Every critical destination remains reachable on foot. Tracking, climbing, companions, and the [[Road Keys and Menhir Paths|Drake Ridge Menhir link]] provide safer or faster options, never mandatory progression.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** usable by carts to the lodge; smoke and hunting traffic mark the route.
- **Winter:** the main trail narrows into a prepared expedition corridor for the Drake hunt.
- **Spring:** meltwater exposes barrow stones while making low crossings dangerous.
- **Summer:** hunting pressure, sanctuary rulings, and reconstruction determine access.

## Production Use

Use the track for hunts, tracking stories, war-scar recovery, barrow warnings, weather survival, and the transition from settled country into a serious wilderness pillar.
