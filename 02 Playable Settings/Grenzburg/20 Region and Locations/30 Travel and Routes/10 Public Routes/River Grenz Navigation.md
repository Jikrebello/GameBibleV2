# River Grenz Navigation

The River Grenz flows north from the Frostfenn and Upper Grenz, passes Timberfalls and between Grenzburg's walls, then enters the warmer Lowlands. It carries timber, fuel, grain transfers, ferries, military stores, and information.

## Physical Contract

- Riverboats serve the Lowlands, Grenzburg, and Timberfalls while water, ice, and control permit.
- [[Bridgehold]] and paired river-boom towers regulate passage through the city.
- Only the fortified bridge carries wagons between banks; civilian ferries supplement it outside siege closure.
- Rapids, falls, upstream hauling, and seasonal current prevent the river from functioning as effortless fast travel.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** high freight demand and freeze preparation crowd the landings.
- **Winter:** ice and river chains sharply contract service; local sled routes may use inspected ice.
- **Spring:** dangerous current and flood damage close some landings while moving heavy salvage.
- **Summer:** repaired quays and settlement survival determine the restored network.

## Production Use

Use the river for freight defense, salvage, rescue, smuggling, boom control, ferry consequences, and visible changes to fuel and food supply. Water travel follows discovered operating links rather than arbitrary map selection.
