# Long Lake Ferries

Lakewatch's ferry network joins fishing settlements, smokehouses, seasonal islands, and the road landings of [[Longlake Country]]. It is distinct from sacred Wolfsmere: Long Lake supports ordinary boats and winter crossings, while Wolfsmere's separate prohibitions remain intact.

## Physical Contract

- Ferries operate between known landings in fair water.
- Cargo, weather, daylight, and boat survival govern capacity.
- Inspected sled routes may replace boats after a stable freeze.
- Spring breakup closes both boat and ice travel until marked channels reopen.

The network carries fish stores and people more effectively than wagons around the marsh shore, but cannot move armies or unlimited freight without preparation.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** heavy fish and refuge traffic precedes freeze.
- **Winter:** safe ice opens direct sled lines while thin sections become lethal.
- **Spring:** breakup is the network's hardest closure.
- **Summer:** repaired boats, surviving crews, and local confidence restore service.

## Production Use

Use the ferries for rescue, weather, smuggling, missing boats, island stories, food supply, and routes whose visible state changes with Lakewatch's survival.
