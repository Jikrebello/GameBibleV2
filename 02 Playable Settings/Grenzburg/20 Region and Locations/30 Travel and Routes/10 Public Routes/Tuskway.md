# Tuskway

The Tuskway climbs from Grenzburg's eastern road fan through [[Highlake Uplands]], meets the Tannbruck spur, and continues as a harsher ancient military corridor toward the eastern Frostfenn. Broad sections can move organized warbands; its crags and side paths support Ghost-Foot movement and ambush.

## Physical Contract

- Grenzburg to Highlake: two to three days of hard travel.
- Carriages reach Highlake while bridges and lower grades remain controlled.
- Beyond Highlake, pack movement, sleds, and military carts depend upon weather and pass work.
- [[Road Keys and Menhir Paths|High Tuskway and Ghost-Foot waystones]] serve remote heights rather than replacing the road.

Ghor's coalition uses this broad axis because it can carry organized forces. That does not make every Grobi movement on the Tuskway part of Ghor's army.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** ore traffic and military reconnaissance compete for road space.
- **Winter:** avalanche, coalition control, and exposed heights make service conditional.
- **Spring:** thaw destabilizes slopes while reopening military movement.
- **Summer:** fortification, mining policy, and Ghost-Foot relations determine access.

## Production Use

Use the Tuskway for vertical travel, military intelligence, mine logistics, Grobi war movement, pass defense, and the eastern branch of the Act III dead march.
