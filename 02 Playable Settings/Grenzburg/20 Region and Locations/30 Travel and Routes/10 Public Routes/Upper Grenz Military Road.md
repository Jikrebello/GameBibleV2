# Upper Grenz Military Road

The Upper Grenz road leaves Tann Gate, follows the River Grenz upstream and south to [[Timberfalls]], and reaches [[Fort Tannbruck]] at the frontier crossing. It is Grenzburg's principal military and fuel corridor.

## Physical Contract

- Grenzburg to Timberfalls: two in-world days.
- Grenzburg to Tannbruck: three to four days under ordinary autumn conditions.
- Military carts and civilian freight share the northern section.
- River bridges, rafting landings, fortified farms, and the Tann crossing create recurring state checks.

The road remains physically present after Tannbruck falls. Enemy control, wreckage, destroyed bridges, or weather can make sections unusable without erasing the route.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** heavy timber, fuel, garrison, and evacuation traffic.
- **Winter:** service ends at the safe line; fallen Tannbruck cuts ordinary through-movement.
- **Spring:** the route becomes the central reclamation axis and one dead-march front.
- **Summer:** repaired bridges, settlement return, and military policy reflect player outcomes.

## Production Use

Use the road for supply defense, withdrawals, engineering, patrols, worker rescue, fixed military loss, and later return through changed ground. [[Old River Arsenal]] is its major independent military-industrial space.
