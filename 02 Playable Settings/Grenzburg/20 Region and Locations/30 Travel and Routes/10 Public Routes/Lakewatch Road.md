# Lakewatch Road

The Lakewatch Road leaves Grenzburg through Lake Gate, crosses the western floodplain, and reaches [[Lakewatch]] in two in-world days. It is the public spine of [[Longlake Country]] and the last maintained wagon road before Wolfsmere and Southwood tracks divide.

## Physical Contract

- Normal travel: two days; three in bad mud.
- Surface: causeway, plank bridges, reed ditches, refuge houses, fish carts, and raised winter markers.
- Public carriage runs after discovery while Eel Bridge and the marsh approaches remain intact.
- Winter sleds replace carts where ice is inspected and marked.

The road does not extend ducal sovereignty into every customary Folk holding. Side paths require local knowledge or permission where they leave surveyed approaches.

## Seasonal States

- **Autumn:** fish stores and refuge traffic make it crowded but reliable.
- **Winter:** snow and safe ice shorten some sections; siege patrols can close others.
- **Spring:** flood and mud produce the route's most severe ordinary closures.
- **Summer:** bridge repair and Lakewatch's survival determine restored service.

## Production Use

Use the road for refuge obligations, ferries, food supply, weather rescue, smuggling, and friction between public access and local duty. [[Sunken Causeway]] is its major infrastructure site.
