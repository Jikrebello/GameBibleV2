# Grenzburg Travel and Road-Key Network

Grenzburg uses physical discovery, public transport, and seven fixed Menhir links. There is no persistent player mount and no arbitrary map-click fast travel.

## Scale Contract

- **Compressed game crossing:** about ninety uninterrupted minutes from the northern map edge to the Deep Muster approach.
- **Near-city safe-node spacing:** about ten minutes on foot.
- **Middle-frontier spacing:** fifteen to twenty minutes.
- **Deep-south spacing:** up to twenty-five minutes.
- Encounters, detours, weather, carrying, investigation, and combat add time; these figures describe clean traversal.

The compressed worldspace represents larger in-world distances:

| Route | In-world time |
|---|---|
| Northern map edge to the Funnel | 1 day by cart |
| Funnel to Grenzburg gate | half a day in normal traffic |
| Grenzburg to Lakewatch | 2 days; 3 in mud |
| Grenzburg to Wolfsmere outer woods | 4 days by safe roads; 2–3 by recognized Folk paths |
| Grenzburg to Ashfield Lodge | 3 days |
| Grenzburg to First Chieftain barrow country | 4–5 days |
| Grenzburg to Highlake | 2–3 days hard travel |
| Grenzburg to Timberfalls | 2 days |
| Grenzburg to Fort Tannbruck | 3–4 days |
| Grenzburg to Fenn Road Exchange | 4–5 days |
| Grenzburg to Last Hearth | 6 days in safe autumn weather |
| Grenzburg to Deep Muster surface basin | 7–8 days before spring damage |

Public transport follows a route rather than erasing it. A service becomes available only after its destination is known and the link is physically operating.

## Fixed Menhir Links

| ID | Origin | Destination | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| WK-1 | Lakewatch outer stone | Grey-Withy Crossing | bypasses marsh road into Wolfsmere's outer woods |
| WK-2 | Grey-Withy Crossing | Moss-Crown Mootground | deep-wood return route after local recognition |
| WK-3 | Ashfield Lodge | Drake Ridge | reaches the winter hunt trailhead, not the lair |
| WK-4 | Highlake | Ghost-Foot Stair | reaches the high western crags |
| WK-5 | Highlake | High Tuskway Post | reaches the eastern military heights |
| WK-6 | Fenn Road Exchange | Last Hearth | crosses the cartless deep-fen section |
| WK-7 | Last Hearth | Muster Approach Stone | Act III link to the exterior basin, never inside the dungeon |

Every link connects only its named pair. Wolfsmere's sacred inner boundary has no stone. No link terminates inside a settlement that ordinary carts can already reach more sensibly unless that settlement is the route's custody point.

## On-Foot Rule

All critical paths remain passable on foot. Climbing, grappling, tracking, cultural knowledge, companions, and restored infrastructure expose shortcuts or safer alternatives. No vocation is mandatory for regional access or critical-path completion.

## Seasonal Travel

- **Autumn:** broadest public service, greatest congestion, and closing high roads.
- **Winter:** riverboats and ferries contract; sled and ice routes open; siege control disables exposed services.
- **Spring:** flood, mud, broken bridges, and unsafe ice close the greatest number of ordinary links while exposing new physical routes.
- **Summer:** repair and political outcomes determine which services return permanently.
