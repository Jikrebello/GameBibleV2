# Grenzburg Seasonal Worldspace Matrix

This matrix controls authored environmental change. Every region changes through traversal, inhabitants, hostility, services, and visible consequence. A season swap is never only weather and texture.

## Grenzburg City

| Season | Gates and traversal | Crowds and inhabitants | Patrols and danger | Services and prices | Interiors and public space |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Autumn | all surface districts open; freight congests bridge and gates | convoys, petitioners, laborers, and refugees swell Lantern and Longlake wards | ordinary watch, theft, fire, and crowd danger increase | broadest stock; rising food, lodging, and fuel prices | camps grow; warehouses and halls convert to storage and shelter |
| Winter | curfews, bridge checks, siege closures, damaged walls, and restricted ferries alter routes | 31,000–35,000 people crowd both walls; hospitals and refuge halls overflow | siege action, sabotage, disease, ration violence, and underworld control intensify | scarce food and fuel; faction-controlled services and black markets matter | granaries, hospitals, barracks, cellars, rooftops, and wall spaces become main play areas |
| Spring | flood closes low underways while breaches and clearance works open temporary routes | soldiers, returnees, widows, prisoners, and displaced households contest space | collapse, unexploded scripts, residue, revenge, and public disorder replace siege fronts | salvage and labor recover first; food and housing remain unstable | claims courts, burial yards, damaged institutions, and reopened records dominate |
| Summer | repaired or neglected gates, bridge works, and legal access reflect outcomes | memorials, resettlement, departures, and new households change ambient life | surviving gangs, splinters, predators, and political enforcement replace campaign armies | reconstruction priorities and faction control determine prices and services | headquarters, public notices, markets, and player housing show the completed settlement |

Each of the eight district dossiers applies this city-wide state to its own five anchors.

## Persistence and Repopulation

- Named dungeons retain their cleared, occupied, sealed, damaged, or reclaimed state.
- Settlement damage and service access persist across seasons.
- Repeatable content may repopulate only designated hunts, road camps, nests, escorts, and bounties.
- A later population must have a stated route, motive, and world-state trigger; nothing simply respawns because the player left the cell.
