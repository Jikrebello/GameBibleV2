# Road Keys and Menhir Paths

Grenzburg's waystones are fixed local threshold compacts aligned to Hecatara's route law. They shorten one named path between two prepared stones. They are not Jirahar Way-Gates, generic teleportation, Shell scripts, patron leases, or proof that Menhir law is morally good.

## Road Keys

A **Road Key** is a link-specific carved token prepared by that path's recognized local custodians. The token carries both endpoint marks and a bounded crossing permission. It is consumed when the path closes behind the traveler.

- One crossing consumes one Road Key for that exact link.
- Keys for one pair cannot activate another pair.
- Custodians maintain limited replenishing stock rather than a one-time global treasure supply.
- Keys may be purchased, granted for service, recovered from route stores, or withheld when a compact is broken.
- Preparing a key is custody of an existing local compact, not the manufacture of a new Menhir or the harvesting of a person.

## Capacity

One activation carries:

- the player;
- up to three active companions;
- worn and normally carried equipment.

It cannot carry mounts, carts, large cargo, refugee groups, formed troops, a siege engine, or an army. Quest escorts who exceed the party capacity must use physical routes.

## Activation Rules

- Both endpoints must be physically discovered.
- The player must stabilize or receive recognition for the link.
- The party must stand at the origin stone with a valid key.
- Combat, active pursuit, hostile occupation, structural damage, or a broken compact prevents activation.
- The route moves the party immediately in world terms and never permits an arbitrary destination.
- Using a path does not confer ownership, moral approval, or authority over surrounding land.

## Custody

Each link has its own custodians: a settlement, crossing household, local court witness, Chapel-Folk reader, or frontier refuge. No universal Folk council controls the network. Eclessian use may be recognized through local treaty without converting the path into ducal infrastructure.

## Counterplay and Failure

Damaged endpoint marks, counterfeit keys, concealed passengers, an unacknowledged pursuit, or attempts to overload a path may consume the key without passage, exchange endpoints, close the route temporarily, or expose an unwanted crossing. Failures remain legible through damaged markings, changed path signs, and custodian warning.
