# Church of Grenzburg

The Grenzburg diocese is simultaneously a church, hospital network, burial authority, teaching institution, archive, and claimant to coercive jurisdiction. Its quest line lets the player serve genuine sacred goods while deciding where spiritual authority must stop.

## Leadership

[[Bishop Konrad von Ehrenfeld]] is a sincere institutionalist who believes a weak Church leaves souls and the poor undefended. [[Bishop-Prelate Caestus]] leads pastoral work and measures authority by whether the sick, dead, and frightened are actually served. [[Deacon Ignatius]] begins severe but remains recoverable before atrocity. [[Sister Marcellina]] protects vulnerable people through evidence networks while normalizing surveillance that can outlive its emergency.

Their disagreement is not faith against unbelief. All four know spiritual danger is real. They dispute what that truth permits the institution to search, judge, conceal, and punish.

## People and Services

Parish readers, hospital sisters, burial clerks, chaplains, copyists, almoners, commandery brothers, gravediggers, and ordinary congregations make up the line's human body. They provide hospital care, burial support, sanctuary, restricted archives, relic testing, spiritual counsel, and eventually bounded White-Flame aid.

A quiet genuine miracle occurs in [[Mercy in Winter]]. It confirms divine reality without proving that Konrad's preferred court structure is just.

## Crisis and Settlement

The line can contribute hospital capacity, burial integrity, civilian morale, and truthful sacred protection during the Muster. Its strongest settlement keeps Konrad as bishop, gives Caestus authority over hospitals and formation, returns secular crimes to ducal courts, and gives genuinely spiritual cases a fixed appeal process.

Broad episcopal court, strong ducal restriction, and unstable dual jurisdiction remain playable. No result becomes future-setting canon.

## Moral Boundary

The Church teaches true things and performs indispensable works. Neither truth nor miracle grants it authority over unrelated secular crimes, coerced confession, indefinite surveillance, or punishment without appeal.
