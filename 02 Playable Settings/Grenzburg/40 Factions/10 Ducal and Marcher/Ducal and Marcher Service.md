# Ducal and Marcher Service

The ducal line joins two institutions that need one another and should never become identical: Grenzmark civil government and the Marcher Vanguard. Its playable fantasy is public service under pressure. The player patrols roads, opens outposts, carries warrants, investigates officers, protects stores, and decides whether emergency command remains a temporary shield or becomes Grenzburg's normal government.

## Leadership Tension

[[Duke Alaric von Grenzburg]] and [[Castellan Pierre Hohenwall]] are loyal rivals. Alaric believes a ruler must be able to act before every petition is heard; Pierre believes emergency action survives only if records, expiry dates, and ordinary courts follow it. Neither is a hidden traitor. [[Sergeant Liese of the Marcher Vanguard]] is the field counterpart who sees how their principles land on frightened soldiers and villages.

The player never replaces them. Rank rises from **Probationer** to **Warranted Specialist**, **March Officer**, and finally **Charter Witness**: trusted enough to shape the settlement, never supreme commander or duke.

## People and Daily Work

The line includes road patrols, fort engineers, warrant clerks, granary officers, scouts, wagon-masters, healers, wallwright crews, and village petitioners. Some abuse requisition or detention; many are exhausted professionals trying to keep roads open without becoming occupiers.

The service can provide lawful warrants, patrol support, military stores, outpost beds, route intelligence, and coordinated evacuation. Access depends on named quests and world states rather than a universal reputation number.

## Crisis and Settlement

The line can contribute organized evacuation, trained scouts, repaired defenses, lawful command, and disciplined units during the Unending Muster. Its strongest settlement keeps the Duke sovereign while placing the marcher service under civil law: villages gain petitions, emergency powers expire on fixed dates, and officers answer ordinary courts for secular crimes.

Broader ducal office and permanent martial administration remain playable outcomes. No result becomes future-setting canon.

## Moral Boundary

Grenzburg genuinely needs force, coordination, and public authority. That need never makes collective punishment, indefinite detention, confiscation without remedy, or government without petitions morally neutral.
