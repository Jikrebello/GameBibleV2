# Low Lanterns

The Low Lanterns are Mara Senn's route-and-refuge answer to corrupt authority. They move people, food, contraband, and testimony through doors that official power cannot reliably reach. They do not claim the right to execute offenders.

## Structure

[[Mara Senn]] coordinates independent route keepers rather than ruling a rigid guild. A keeper knows only the adjoining doors needed for a journey. Innkeepers, dockhands, carters, fences, rooftop guides, householders, and sympathetic clerks participate for different reasons and may refuse a cargo.

The network prohibits trafficking, the sale of refuge locations, coercive vice, and paid killing. It tolerates theft, fencing, smuggling, false papers, and bribery when members accept the risk. These rules constrain the Lanterns without turning them into hidden saints.

## Branch Quests

1. [[Articles Below]] defines prohibited trades, aliases, inspections, and the price of civil recognition.
2. [[The Store Beneath the Siege]] decides who controls emergency caches and whether stolen public grain is restored.
3. [[The Licensed Night]] establishes a licensed guild, tolerated network, civil evidence office, or failed compact.

Starting [[Articles Below]] closes the Black Bell branch. The choice is displayed before commitment.

## Services and Cost

Low Lantern support can provide underways access, safehouses, fences, covert evacuation, hidden stores, and protected testimony. Formal license gives routes legal durability but exposes keepers to inspection and political pressure. Refusing license preserves secrecy while leaving beneficiaries dependent on favors and selective enforcement.

## Best Available Result

A bounded night guild registers roles through protected aliases, accepts civil inspection of stores and complaints, bans trafficking and private execution, and retains emergency refuge discretion. It is useful and imperfect, not the moral equivalent of open public justice.
