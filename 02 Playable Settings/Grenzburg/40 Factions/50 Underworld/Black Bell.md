# Black Bell

The Black Bell is Ivo Renn's answer to crimes that public officers repeatedly conceal. It combines an evidence archive, covert witness protection, abduction, and selective killing into a private tribunal. Its cases may identify genuine murderers; that does not give it rightful jurisdiction.

## Structure

[[Ivo Renn]] requires records rather than rumor. Bell readers separate collection, corroboration, judgement, and action so that no single member can name and kill a target. The best version demands multiple independent witnesses, preserved contrary evidence, unpaid service, and an absolute ban on punishing families.

Those safeguards reduce error and venality. They do not turn secret execution into clean justice. The Bell has no public appeal, no lawful prison, and no reliable way to repair a wrongful death.

## Branch Quests

1. [[The First Toll]] decides whether Vey is exposed, captured, killed, or protected and defines what evidence can authorize violence.
2. [[A Bell Behind the Walls]] tests the rules against a false accusation and a betrayer during the Muster.
3. [[The Hidden Tribunal]] establishes a strict Bell, evidence network without executions, splintered cells, or collapse.

Starting [[The First Toll]] closes the Low Lantern branch. The choice is displayed before commitment.

## Services and Cost

Black Bell support can expose protected crimes, secure endangered witnesses, identify infiltrators, and perform covert sabotage. Its agents are less capable at mass refuge and food movement than the Low Lanterns. Empowering it normalizes fear, disappearance, and a second jurisdiction beneath Grenzburg's courts.

## Best Available Result

The strict tribunal requires corroboration, contrary review, no payment, no inherited guilt, and service aimed only at grave violence protected by corrupt office. Even this result remains morally compromised. Converting the archive into a civil evidence office is cleaner but leaves some killers beyond immediate reach.
