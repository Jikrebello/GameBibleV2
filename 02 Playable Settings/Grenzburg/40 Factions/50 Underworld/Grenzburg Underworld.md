# Grenzburg Underworld

Grenzburg's underworld is a set of routes, favors, fences, refuge doors, and evidence stores concentrated in Lantern Ward and the underways. It is not governed by Enzo Voss, a thieves' king, or one citywide guild at the start of play.

## Shared Origin

[[Mara Senn]] and [[Ivo Renn]] built the Low Lantern network together. Mara organized movement: food around confiscation, fugitives around corrupt warrants, stolen goods toward fences, and messages through closed districts. Ivo organized memory: names, payments, protected crimes, missing prisoners, and officials who made evidence disappear.

[[Watch-Captain Oskar Vey]] used public office to protect a sequence of murders. When courts repeatedly buried the proof, Ivo concluded that an archive without punishment merely catalogued victims. That conclusion produced the [[Black Bell]]. [[Lord High Bailiff Enzo Voss]] profits from corrupt warrants and suppressed cases, but he protects disorder rather than commanding it.

## Shared Work

The first six quests establish night routes, refugee bread, rooftop access, erased prisoners, protected testimony, and the truth about Vey:

1. [[A Door After Dark]]
2. [[Bread Through Stone]]
3. [[The Rooftop Tax]]
4. [[No One in the Cell]]
5. [[The Names They Buried]]
6. [[The Watchman Who Walked]]

During this shared phase the player may work with runners, fences, innkeepers, clerks, gravediggers, dockhands, and households without endorsing a private court. Standing rises from **Unknown** to **Door-Known** and **Route-Keeper**.

## The Fork

After quest six, the line divides:

- [[Low Lanterns]] attempts to turn routes and refuge into a bounded night guild under civil license.
- [[Black Bell]] attempts to turn Ivo's evidence discipline into a hidden tribunal able to punish protected killers.

Beginning either branch's quest seven closes the other three-quest branch only. It does not close the four other principal faction lines. The warning identifies lost routes, evidence uses, leaders, and summer services.

## Crisis and Settlement

The shared network can move civilians, hide stores, sabotage hostile positions, and report movement during the Muster. Its final political term may be a licensed night guild, tolerated network, hidden tribunal, civil evidence office, or splintered gangs. No outcome becomes future-setting canon.

## Moral Boundary

Smuggling food past a corrupt seizure and defending a fugitive can be good acts. Extortion, trafficking, paid murder, retaliation against families, and permanent private execution remain evil. Failed public courts explain the Black Bell's appeal without making it clean.
