### **The Medori Bank ("The Iron Ledger") in Grenzburg**

**Role in the Rule of Three:**
The Bank is the **Ugly** faction par excellence. It is sovereignty through finance, an empire built on compound interest. It owns the future of Grenzburg by owning its past debts. Its internal conflict is between **Growth, Harvest, and Experimentation**—three approaches to turning a volatile frontier into a perpetual profit engine.

**Core Concept:** The Medori Bank is not a supporter of the Duchy; it is its **majority shareholder**. Grenzburg is a high-risk, high-reward investment portfolio. The Duke is the CEO, the factions are competing departments, and the people are the labor stock. The Bank’s local branch (the "Iron Ledger") manages this asset, balancing the need for the city to survive (so debt can be repaid) with the desire to maximize extraction (to please distant partners).

---

## **The Portfolio Manager: Factor Silas Medori**

**Concept:** A young, ambitious scion of a minor branch of the Medori family. Grenzburg is his proving ground—a place to show the Partners in Sestaren that he can manage chaos and produce profit. He is not evil; he is **financially rational**. Emotions are volatility; morality is a liability.
*   **Public Face:** Impeccably dressed in somber Sestaren finery. Unflappable, precise, and polite. Holds meetings in a stark, soundproofed office above the counting-house. The embodiment of cold capital.
*   **Private Truth:** He is under immense pressure. The "Silent Partners" expect exponential returns. He must navigate the violent idiocy of frontier politics while making the numbers work. He secretly fears that Koss’s brutality will spark a revolt that wrecks the asset, and that Lucia’s reforms are too slow to satisfy his quarterly reports. He is a man trying to perform alchemy: turning blood and ruin into clean, reportable profit.
*   **Goal:** To increase the total value of the "Grenzburg Portfolio" by at least 20% year-over-year, and to be recalled to Sestaren as a hero, leaving a self-sustaining, profit-generating entity behind.
*   **Method:** Balance the three internal divisions, pitting them against each other to find the optimal equilibrium of risk and reward. Use the Duke’s desperation as leverage. Treat information as the most valuable currency.

## **Internal Divisions**

### **1. The Venture Capitalists (The "Good" within the Ugly)**
**Led by: Ledger-Master Lucia Medori**
*   **Core Concept:** The builders. They believe in **value creation**. A stable, prosperous, growing Grenzburg has a higher long-term valuation than a burned-out husk. They are the closest thing the Bank has to a conscience, though it’s a conscience of fiduciary duty, not morality.
*   **Goal:** To develop infrastructure (roads, mills, granaries) that increases the Duchy’s productive capacity. To reform the Blackjack system into a skilled, motivated contractor corps, not just scared debt-slaves. To build a lasting, profitable economic ecosystem.
*   **Resources:** Project financing funds. Engineering consultants from Sestaren. A network of reliable local suppliers and craftsmen. The patience for 5-10 year returns.
*   **Conflict with Bank Leadership:** They are a **drag on short-term earnings**. Their projects have high upfront costs and slow returns. Silas needs their success for the long-term health of the asset, but Koss and the Silent Partners deride them as "soft" and "sentimental." Lucia views Koss as a parasite killing the host.

### **2. The Collection Division (The "Bad" within the Ugly)**
**Led by: Captain Valdemar Koss**
*   **Core Concept:** The harvesters. They believe in **liquidation and arbitrage**. People are amortizing assets; their labor and fear are commodities to be squeezed until depleted. The frontier is a perfect environment for high-yield, high-risk enforcement strategies.
*   **Goal:** Maximize short-term cash flow. Drive up debt yields through punitive fees and compound interest. Push Blackjacks into ever-riskier delves for immediate artifact payouts. Create new debtors through manufactured crises or predatory loans.
*   **Resources:** The Blackjacks. A network of informants and loan-sharks. A reputation for horrific, calculated violence. The ability to call due massive debts at a moment's notice to cripple any individual or guild.
*   **Conflict with Bank Leadership:** They are **existentially reckless**. Koss’s methods generate incredible profit *now*, but they foment hatred and risk a systemic collapse (e.g., a debtor uprising). Silas uses him as a threat and a tool, but fears Koss will trigger a crash that ruins Silas’s career. Koss views Silas as a coward and Lucia as a fool.

### **3. The Silent Partners (The "Ugly" within the Ugly)**
**Represented by: Encrypted ledgers, dead-drop messengers, and the unspoken pressure on Silas.**
*   **Core Concept:** The experimentalists. The senior partners in Sestaren see Grenzburg as a **laboratory**. A place to test economic theories, weapon systems, and social control mechanisms far from the prying eyes of civilized law.
*   **Goal:** To generate unique, non-replicable data and extreme returns. To use the chaos of the frontier to run experiments on conflict economics, debt slavery, and resource extraction that would be illegal or immoral at home.
*   **Resources:** Vast, untraceable capital. Contacts with arms dealers, alchemists, and other forbidden specialists. The ultimate power to hire, fire, or disappear Silas.
*   **Conflict with Bank Leadership:** They are **inscrutable and demand the impossible**. Their orders often contradict each other: "Increase stability" and "Double artifact yields." They are the source of the "double-funding" (arming both sides of a conflict) to study war as a market force. Silas, Lucia, and Koss are all, to varying degrees, their terrified test subjects.

## **The Bank's Seasonal Strategy & The Debt Cycle**

*   **Summer (Investment & Leverage):** Lucia’s season. Disbursing loans for seasonal operations, financing expeditions. The Bank’s tentacles extend deepest into the wilderness.
*   **Autumn (The Squeeze):** Koss’s season. Calling in short-term debts before winter lockdown. Repossessing assets from those who failed. The Blackjacks are most active, both defending the city and seizing property for the Bank.
*   **Winter (Controlled Depreciation):** Silas’s season. The asset is under stress. The Bank must carefully meter out liquidity (food, fuel loans) to keep the city alive—but just barely. Interest accrues on survival itself. The "books" are adjusted to write off expected losses.
*   **Spring (Audit & Acquisition):** All divisions act. Lucia assesses infrastructure damage. Koss hunts those who fled or hid during winter. Silas presents the annual report to the Silent Partners and negotiates with the Duke for new collateral (often land or mining rights) to cover the Duchy’s ever-growing debt.

## **Relationship with the Duchy & The Duke**

The Bank’s relationship with Duke Alaric is that of a **secured creditor to a struggling business owner**.
*   **What the Bank Provides:**
    1.  **Liquidity:** The coin to pay soldiers, repair walls, and buy grain.
    2.  **Expertise:** Logistical and engineering knowledge.
    3.  **Deniable Assets:** The Blackjacks.
*   **What the Bank Demands:**
    1.  **Collateral:** First liens on tax revenue, mining outputs, and eventually, land titles.
    2.  **Policy Influence:** Veto over laws that might impair debt collection or Bank profits.
    3.  **Access:** Unrestricted ability to operate and enforce contracts within the Duchy.
    4.  **A Share of Sovereignty:** The right to mint coins, run the treasury, or garrison troops is the ultimate goal.

The Duke uses Magister Leviticus as his buffer, but Silas and Koss constantly work to bypass him, dealing directly with guild masters or scared nobles.

## **Relationship with the Players (Seconded Blackjacks)**

The player arrives as a Bank-owned Blackjack carrying a ducal warrant; this relationship may pressure either side of that divided authority.

The Blackjacks are the Bank’s **premium, high-risk financial instruments**. You are not people; you are collateralized debt obligations.
*   **Ledger-Master Lucia Medori (Ventures):** Might hire you as security for a infrastructure project in dangerous territory, or to retrieve a specific piece of low-value but critical technical data (e.g., survey maps). Pays with official Bank scrip, better rations, and perhaps a note in your file about "cooperative behavior."
*   **Captain Valdemar Koss (Collections):** Your direct, brutal boss. Assigns you artifact delves, debt collections, sabotage of rival operations, and "personnel management" (intimidating or disappearing problematic debtors). Pays in coin, debt relief, and the grim respect of a slaughterman.
*   **The Silent Partners (Experiments):** You will never meet them. Their selected tasks come through local officers with little context and extreme urgency. Refusal is possible, but it can freeze equipment, worsen debt terms, or shift collection pressure toward named collateral.

**The Faustian Bargain:** Every transaction with the Bank increases your debt or deepens its control. Take a loan for better gear? The interest is 100% and the collateral is your friend’s life. Do a job for Lucia? You strengthen the Bank’s legitimate hold on the Duchy. Do a job for the Silent Partners? You are actively participating in an experiment that may doom the entire region for the sake of a Sestaren profit model.

The rebuilt division of authority is recorded in [[Player Character Constitution]] and the [[Crisis Charter of Secondment]].

## Possible Futures

The rebuilt faction line must distinguish ordinary credit from predatory sovereignty and support several costed outcomes: reform, continued extraction, ducal limitation, debtor rupture, internal capture, or expulsion. No Bank or political future is setting canon in Wave 1.

## **Failure Modes for the Bank**

1.  **Debtor Revolt:** The Blackjacks, guilds, and commoners unite under a charismatic leader and repudiate all debts. They seize the counting-house, burn the ledgers, and expel or kill Medori personnel. The asset is wiped out.
2.  **Sovereign Default:** The Duke, in a final act of defiance, declares a "Debt Jubilee" with the backing of the Church or a rival kingdom. The Bank's legal claims are nullified by martial force. Its financial weaponry fails against raw political power.
3.  **Experiment Catastrophe:** One of the Silent Partners' tests goes horribly wrong—an unleashed ruin-curse, a Grobi horde armed too well, a Folk unification. The resulting disaster destroys Grenzburg entirely, vaporizing the Bank's investment.
4.  **Internal Hostile Takeover:** Koss, with the secret backing of the more ruthless Silent Partners, stages a coup. He murders Silas, sidelines Lucia, and transforms Grenzburg into a pure extraction camp, bleeding it dry in a few violent years until it collapses, leaving the Bank with a massive, total loss.
