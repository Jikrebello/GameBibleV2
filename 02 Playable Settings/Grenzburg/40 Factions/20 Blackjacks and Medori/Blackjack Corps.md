# Blackjack Corps

The Blackjack Corps is the armed debtor company in which the player serves. It is not the Medori Bank with weapons. Its members recover cargo, escort exposed routes, clear ruins, support sieges, and perform dangerous specialist work that ordinary patrols cannot absorb.

## Command and Membership

[[Captain Valdemar Koss]] holds operational command and deliberately uses debt, injury, and lost equipment to lengthen service. [[Quartermaster Brina Kest]] controls the yard's practical life: beds, repairs, casualty rolls, stores, and the quiet agreements by which debtors keep one another alive. [[Ledger-Master Lucia Medori]] can alter accounts and policy but does not command a field squad.

Blackjacks include deserters, failed apprentices, foreign specialists, ruined householders, injured veterans, convicted smugglers, and people who borrowed to save someone else. Their debt histories differ; their legal vulnerability does not. They are neither a criminal brotherhood nor a uniformly noble resistance.

## Work and Services

The Blackjack Yard supports party organization, vocation changes at a safe point, specialist equipment, salvage assessment, medical stabilization, recovery crews, and field intelligence. Rank rises from **Asset** through **Proven Hand** and **Company Voice** to **Settled Member**.

Company work intersects every companion because all eight are debtors, but the strongest personal intersections belong to [[Elara Velescar]], [[Ilyana Veshkar]], [[Ashira bath Melan]], and [[Zafir ibn Samad]].

## Shared Quest Line

The Corps and Bank share one nine-quest political line, beginning with [[Terms of Service]] and ending with [[Free Company Articles]]. The company-facing hinge quests are [[The Equipment Account]], [[A Debt That Grew Wounded]], [[The Blackjacks Koss Lost]], and [[Mutiny in Debtors' Row]]. [[Blackjacks and Medori]] controls the full order.

## Possible Futures

- **Chartered free company:** existing terms are fixed and reviewable; future members are paid contractors; Brina protects company governance.
- **Debtor cooperative:** members own stores and work allocation collectively, while external credit remains regulated.
- **Ducal corps:** public service ends private ownership but risks turning debtors into a permanent emergency arm.
- **Regulated Medori company:** conditions improve and Koss can be removed, but creditor ownership survives.
- **Koss control:** the company remains an efficient predatory asset.

Personal debt may end at the Act III climax even when the institution remains unresolved. No company future becomes setting canon.

## Moral Boundary

Dangerous work can be necessary and freely chosen. Injury charges, inherited liability, concealed account growth, and ownership of a person's labor through unpayable debt remain wrong even when the company saves lives.
