# Blackjacks and Medori

This hub controls the single faction line shared by two unequal institutions. [[The Medori Bank]] owns the contracts, money, warehouses, and external routes. The [[Blackjack Corps]] owns the lived profession, yard culture, field expertise, and immediate danger. Treating either as a decorative wing of the other destroys the line's central conflict.

## Human Triangle

- [[Captain Valdemar Koss]] wants a disciplined asset whose casualties and debts strengthen his command.
- [[Ledger-Master Lucia Medori]] wants humane, predictable ownership and struggles to recognize the contradiction in those words.
- [[Quartermaster Brina Kest]] wants debtors to govern dangerous work and retain the value they create.

The first six quests remain compatible with every other faction line. Quest seven displays the exact institutional futures before commitment. Personal freedom can be won before the Deep Muster, but freedom for one specialist does not by itself settle the Corps.

## Settlement Range

The company may become a chartered free company, debtor cooperative, ducal corps, regulated Medori company, or remain under Koss's predatory command. Every result changes logistics, stores, training, crisis support, and the visible summer yard. None becomes future-setting canon.
