# The Medori Bank

The Medori Bank's Grenzburg branch is a creditor, warehouse network, insurer, and long-distance logistics house based at the Iron Ledger Counting-House. It owns the Blackjack contracts but does not share the Corps' culture, risks, or chain of command.

## Local Office

[[Ledger-Master Lucia Medori]] directs the branch. She sincerely believes predictable credit is better than hunger, arbitrary noble seizure, or informal bondage, and she can prove that Medori grain and equipment keep Grenzburg functioning. Her limit is institutional: she will reform terms long before she will admit that ownership itself is unjust.

The branch employs ledger clerks, weighers, warehouse crews, appraisers, translators, factors, river agents, claims examiners, and guards. Many never see a Blackjack mission. Some protect ordinary savings or keep mills supplied; others turn wounds and failed harvests into leverage.

## Services and Leverage

Medori can move imported grain, insure cargo, advance equipment, assess salvage, exchange foreign money, carry messages through commercial routes, and finance reconstruction. These services respond to evidence and political settlement rather than disappearing merely because the player opposes Lucia.

The Bank pressures the protagonist selectively through their contract, major equipment losses, and the faction line. It does not attach a secret rider to every ducal quest and is not an occult conspiracy.

## Shared Quest Line

The Bank-facing hinge quests are [[Lucia's Long Book]], [[Collections Day]], [[Purchase of Freedom]], and [[Free Company Articles]]. They test whether reform merely prices coercion more neatly or actually gives debtors standing, review, and exit. [[Blackjacks and Medori]] controls the complete nine-quest sequence.

## Possible Futures

Lucia may support a chartered free company, negotiate with a debtor cooperative, retain a regulated Medori company, or lose the Corps to ducal absorption. Koss may retain command if the player protects him. Credit, warehouses, and ordinary clients survive every ending in altered form; no branch allows the player to erase all finance without material consequence.

## Moral Boundary

Credit and logistics are real goods. They do not make ownership of persons, concealed compounding, inherited liability, or injury used to prolong compulsory service morally neutral.
