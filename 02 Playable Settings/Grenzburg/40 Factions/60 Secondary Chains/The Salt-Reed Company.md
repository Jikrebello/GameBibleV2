# The Salt-Reed Company

The Salt-Reed Company was a five-person Sermoran travelling ensemble specializing in theatre, civic music, shipboard signals, and harmonic battlefield support.

## Members

- [[Ilyana Veshkar]]: keyed double flute, booking leader, and public face.
- [[Mirella Corsi]]: voice, principal composer, and scorekeeper.
- [[Nereo Valdo]]: frame drum, pulse line, and road management.
- [[Celia Orsani]]: bowed viol, mending line, and instrument repair.
- [[Tomas Marin]]: horn, public signals, and elemental cadence.

Each member owns their personal instrument, authored work, wages, and right to refuse. The company's common case, shared arrangements, booking name, and jointly written repertoire require all five principals or a valid delegated agreement.

## Reputation

Before [[The Gilded Wake Season]], the Salt-Reeds were known for:

- canal processions and small theatres;
- precise fog and harbor-signal work;
- adapting local songs while naming their source;
- a performance style that moved between court elegance and dockside improvisation;
- refusing to imitate White-Flame liturgy as entertainment.

Ilyana's charisma brought the company work. Mirella's scores and Nereo's management kept it solvent. None of that gave Ilyana unilateral ownership.

## Present State

The company is broken, not dead. All four former companions survive and reach Grenzburg by winter through separate routes. They may cooperate during immediate danger, forgive in part, reject Ilyana, or create new individual futures.

The strongest settlement does not automatically reunite the Salt-Reed Company. It establishes:

- individual ownership of instruments and wages;
- correct authorship and performance credit;
- release from the Gilded Wake engagement;
- restitution without compelled friendship;
- permission for any future collaboration to begin again from an explicit yes.
