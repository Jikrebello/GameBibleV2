# Frontier Industry Chains

Industry is a family of secondary quest chains, not a sixth joinable faction or a single cartel governing the frontier.

| Chain | Home | Work | Central pressure | Main-line intersections |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timber and rafting | [[Timberfalls]], Fellwater Camps, Hammer Ward | rescues, labor disputes, river work, fuel | winter output against worker safety and sustainable cutting | Ducal, Bank, Folk |
| Mining and cold iron | [[Highlake]], [[Cold-Iron Deeps]], Great Forge | delves, ventilation, ore transport, wage claims | military demand against dangerous extraction and Ghost-Foot limits | Ducal, Bank, Folk |
| Foundry and warlock craft | Hammer Ward, Warlock Hall, Great Forge | training, leak control, sabotage, production | necessary defense against unsafe scripts and proprietary control | Bank, Church, Ducal |
| Hunting and materials | [[Ashfield Lodge]], Southwood | hunts, carcass recovery, ecology, craft | livelihood against overharvest and drake displacement | Folk, Ducal |
| Lake food and transport | [[Lakewatch]], Longlake Ward | ferries, smokehouses, fisheries, rescue | winter stores against passage conflict and smuggling | Folk, Underworld, Ducal |

Each chain receives named local leaders when its quests are blocked. No industry leader automatically speaks for every worker or becomes a candidate to rule Grenzburg. Outputs feed settlement survival, services, prices, and faction resources.
