# Red Brass Bench

## Scope

The Red Brass Bench is one Vortharan arena branch within [[The Crucible]]. It trains Lancewrights, certifies fittings, arranges public bouts, and controls a profitable chain of coaches, proof-brokers, bookmakers, and touring champions.

It is not the whole Crucible. Other benches oppose its methods, compete with it, or genuinely believe that filed limits are part of mastery.

## Public Reputation

The Bench is known for:

- brilliant technical fighters;
- brightly staged contests;
- aggressive touring contracts;
- exacting proof demonstrations;
- champions who make dangerous techniques look effortless.

Its motto, usually translated as **"Proof Is What Remains,"** has been used both as a call to honest craft and as an excuse to treat survival as validation.

## Hidden Practice

Under [[Maro Vezan]], the Bench:

- signed blank equipment endorsements;
- substituted over-capacity fittings after inspection;
- maintained separate public and private load books;
- paid injured fighters through silence agreements;
- used [[Seraf Pellan]] to move unsafe fittings through independent workshops;
- described preventable injuries as the natural cost of elite competition.

The Bench did not force [[Tavio Meran]] to use the Fifth Ring. It made the fraud available, normalized it, and covered his willing choice.

## Why a Grenzburg Proof Is Dangerous

The Bench's exposure is not reputational. It is contractual, and it travels.

Crucible certification is portable: a fitting proved unsafe by an independent examiner in one member city voids the warranty that carries it everywhere else. If [[Proof-Mistress Hella Dorn|Hella Dorn]] files a substitution finding at the Great Forge, every Red Brass warranty from Vorthara to the border marches becomes contestable at once — refunds, recalls, and cancelled touring contracts — and the branch's certification licence goes before a Crucible panel that contains its competitors.

That is what prices their aggression, and bounds it. The Bench will spend agents, bribes, hired disruptors, and a great deal of correspondence to stop one examiner's paperwork. It will not send soldiers into a war zone, because the exposure is a market problem and armies do not fix market problems.

## Present Objective

The Red Brass Bench wants:

1. Tavio's championship treated as an individual scandal.
2. [[Lio Varcen]]'s testimony discredited or privately settled.
3. unsafe fittings recalled without exposing the endorsement chain;
4. Grenzburg's foundries opened as a wartime market;
5. Tavio returned as a supervised public face if he can still draw a crowd.

## Internal Division

- **Vezan loyalists:** preserve the Bench and sacrifice expendable brokers or fighters.
- **Young technicians:** know something is wrong but fear losing their training and livelihood.
- **Touring veterans:** divide between honest proof traditionalists and spectacle-first pragmatists.
- **Independent Crucible examiners:** may investigate if given filed measures, physical samples, and protected witnesses.

## Player-Dependent States

- **Exposed:** records and witnesses establish a branch-level safety conspiracy.
- **Partially exposed:** Tavio's cheating is public, but the endorsement network survives.
- **Protected:** Tavio accepts sole blame and the branch continues under cosmetic reform.
- **Scattered:** Maro or Seraf escapes with records and unsafe inventory.

No state condemns every Crucible school or Vortharan arena practice.
