# Rimewing Sisters

The Rimewing Sisters are a tolerated Grey-Wolf rescue fellowship that keeps winter beacons, recovers bodies, escorts travelers, and reaches avalanche or ice casualties whom ordinary hall fighters cannot. They are not a matriarchal government, universal Ättar institution, or secret army.

Their public discipline emphasizes shield rescue, cold endurance, rope work, and carrying the wounded. A hidden cadre taught rage narrowing as an emergency survival method. That training can produce extraordinary persistence, but it also suppresses surrender recognition, changed commands, and target discrimination. Grey-Wolf halls forbid it because a rescuer who cannot hear a yield can become the danger.

[[Svala Last-Beacon]] killed a yielded champion while using that discipline. [[Astrid Snow-Rope]] and [[Yrsa Wolf-Brand]] force the fellowship to decide whether useful rescue work can survive public safeguards. The strongest outcome preserves beacons and rescue teams while ending secret rage license, creating external recall rules, and keeping weregild claims visible.
