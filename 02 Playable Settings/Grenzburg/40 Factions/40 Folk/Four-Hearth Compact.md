# Four-Hearth Compact

The Four-Hearth Compact is a temporary Grenzburg alliance, not a Folk state and not a claim that four delegates rule every kindred. The line asks the player to earn separate trust, carry promises accurately, and build limited cooperation among communities that retain their own courts and refusals.

## The Four Seats

- **Moss-Crown and Wolfsmere:** [[Runa Moss-Crown]] handles field negotiation, [[Anya Moss-Crown]] speaks for household survival, and [[Thorn-Seer Mael]] guards restricted Lakekeeper truth.
- **River-Clan households:** ferry, fishing, drowned-name, and passage obligations are voiced by local speakers rather than a permanent chief.
- **Chapel-Folk refuges:** [[Elof Bent-Oak]] represents shelter, burial, worship, and the limits of sanctuary at Bent-Oak and connected road houses.
- **Wolfsmere and Ghost-Foot delegates:** hunters, path custodians, and upland households share a crisis seat without surrendering local authority.

[[Jorund Shattered-Tusk]] organizes a total-war following that targets civilians. His grievance against occupation is real; his chosen method is evil. The line must separate him from households that reject murder rather than treating all Folk resistance as one army.

## People and Services

The compact's practical body consists of ferrymen, refuge keepers, hunters, healers, kin-speakers, path custodians, memorial readers, and households able to hide or move the displaced. Services include guides, ferries, refuge, seasonal paths, warnings, remote waystone custody, and local scouts. Each service remains tied to a named community and promise.

## Crisis and Settlement

The compact can provide winter routes, refuge, ferries, civilian shelter, scouts, and raid restraint during the Muster. Its strongest settlement grants renewable seasonal sanctuary, passage, land-and-worship, and boundary rights in return for warning, refuge, and restraint. Wolfsmere's deepest truth remains restricted to the player and compact elders.

Permanent compact, bilateral agreements, and dissolution remain playable. No result becomes future-setting canon.

Local memory and valid land claims matter. They do not make every ancestral pact just, excuse civilian murder, or create a universal Folk government by implication.
