# The Grobi in Grenzburg

Grobi are enemies, migrants, raiders, dependents, traders, captives, and negotiable regional actors. They are not a joinable faction line, and no band speaks for the species.

[[Ghor-Maw]] turns south-to-north winter movement into chosen conquest through the Bloody-Sun Host. He promises food, transformation, and status in exchange for war submission. His organized siege has military causes and is defeated in Act II; no occult, artifact, financial, or ancient-command mastermind controls him.

The host takes [[Fort Tannbruck]], advances through the Upper Grenz and Tuskway corridors, crosses the frozen river during [[The City in Siege]], and later concentrates against Grenzburg's west and south walls. The Southern Horn coordinates groups that otherwise obey different chiefs. Its destruction and Ghor's death break the organized host without erasing its members.

Uru leads a dependent herd whose vulnerability does not make predation harmless. Grakka, Skiv, and [[Skrit Stone-Tooth]] support band-specific trade, intelligence, ransom, betrayal, and temporary cooperation. Agreements must name the actual group, protected people, payment, prohibited conduct, and consequence of breach.

Grobi do not use a campaign-wide reputation bar. Quest outcomes record named host, band, captive, migration, and corridor states. The player may split Ghor's coalition, protect noncombatants, exchange prisoners, redirect a herd, kill committed raiders, or create future enemies without joining a Grobi government that does not exist.

Post-siege states are controlled by [[Grobi Host Fragmentation Matrix]]: Uru's dependents may retain a withdrawal corridor; Grakka may survive as an independent dangerous chief or die; Skiv may remain a watched intermediary, flee as a profiteer, or be killed; Bloody-Sun bands scatter, surrender locally, or retreat. No single outcome becomes a general peace with Grobi.
