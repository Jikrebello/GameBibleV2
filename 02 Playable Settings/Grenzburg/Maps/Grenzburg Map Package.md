## Map Rules

- The creator map is authoritative where prose and an older image disagree.
- The initial player map omits waystones, hidden dungeons, the Heart-Stone barrow, and the Deep Muster.
- Discovery adds information; it does not redraw geography.
- Seasonal overlays show route state, not a second contradictory map.
- Birchcross and Alderway Vale are wider-canon locations outside this atlas.
