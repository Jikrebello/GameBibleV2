# The Echo Seam

**Source:** *Lost Mine of Phandelver* | **Tier:** starter F | **Region:** Castaran Long Road | **Band:** levels 1-5 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Centuries before the adventure, dwarven and gnomish miners entered an agreement with nearby human spellcasters to work Wave Echo Cave and its Forge of Spells. An assault destroyed the settlement, buried the mine, and killed or scattered the people who knew its location. The Rockseeker brothers rediscover the cave in the present day, but the drow schemer Nezznar, called the Black Spider, learns of their discovery and uses the Cragmaw goblins and Redbrand gang to seize the map and remove rival claimants.

### Beginning

Gundren Rockseeker hires the heroes to escort a supply wagon to the frontier settlement of Phandalin while he and Sildar Hallwinter ride ahead. The heroes find their abandoned horses and are attacked by Cragmaw goblins. Following the attackers leads to Cragmaw Hideout, where Sildar can be rescued. Gundren and the map have already been sent to Cragmaw Castle.

### Middle

Phandalin is being terrorized by the Redbrands, an armed gang led by the wizard Glasstaff from a hideout beneath Tresendar Manor. Breaking the gang's control gives the heroes a usable home base and reveals its connection to the Black Spider. Regional leads can then be pursued in flexible order: Agatha at Conyberry, the necromancer at Old Owl Well, the ruined settlement of Thundertree, the orcs at Wyvern Tor, and Cragmaw Castle. These sites provide allies, information, treasure, and routes toward Wave Echo Cave.

### End

The heroes enter Wave Echo Cave, where one Rockseeker brother is dead and another is held captive. Undead, oozes, old guardians, rival agents, and the Black Spider occupy different portions of the mine. Defeating Nezznar, rescuing the survivor, and dealing with the Forge of Spells determine whether the mine can support Phandalin again.

## What the Translation Preserves

- the missing patron and recoverable road companion;
- an ambush that points toward a wider criminal organization;
- a frontier settlement whose daily life is constrained by an armed gang;
- a flexible middle built from several regional leads;
- three related surveyors with different fates;
- a lost mine containing an ancient production system;
- a hidden organizer whose agents appear before the organizer does;
- a finale altered by information, allies, and routes gained earlier.

## What the Translation Replaces

- Goblins and Redbrands become culturally specific human criminal groups: mixed claim-jumpers and one rogue Aversian contract company. Grobi and Giltlings have no role in this story.
- Phandalin becomes [[Cavora]], a Castaran charter town occupying a bounded grant inside Ash-Horde road country rather than sovereign Castaran territory.
- The Forge of Spells becomes [[The Rehearsal Forge]], an Atherian industrial workshop that tempers one bounded elemental script into compatible material.
- The Black Spider becomes [[Magister Orsino Vellaro]], an expelled Castaran metallurgist whose real professional disgrace develops into deliberate murder, coercion, and armed monopoly.
- The mine's supernatural occupants follow the current bestiary ontology. Residue is not a trapped soul; ward engines and Crystal Forms are not conscious beings.

## Exact World Egg Placement

[[Cavora]] stands twelve road-days west of Castara on the [[Castaran Long Road]]. It occupies wells, a depot, a market yard, and declared approaches granted by the Grey-Rein Banner under the Ash Road Peace. The grant is real and limited. It does not transfer the surrounding grass, migration routes, or undeclared tracks to Castaran authority.

The Marevi survey siblings have rediscovered [[Echo Seam Mine]], an Atherian mine-workshop built over a naturally resonant crystal deposit. It uses a lesser, non-traversable Shell tap and is not one of the world's fourteen active permanent anchors. Its Rehearsal Forge is valuable because it can prepare finite elemental releases in metal or ceramic. Repeated use has contaminated the lattice with fragments of earlier scripts.

Orsino Vellaro hires [[The Broken Stakes]] to seize the route plate and pays [[The Copper Mantles]] to keep Cavora frightened and competing claimants away. The player party arrives while escorting Marevi equipment, finds the ambush, rescues [[Temur Grey-Rein]], and becomes the first mobile force able to connect the road crime, town occupation, regional sites, and mine.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** find Dario Marevi, free Cavora from armed extortion, and reach the Echo Seam before Orsino activates it.
- **Conflict:** rescue and public safety come first, but the mine could transform Cavora's future. Castaran institutions, local workers, an Ash landholding banner, and armed commercial interests all want influence over what follows.
- **Antagonist:** Orsino knowingly repeats the concealment that caused his expulsion. He uses other people as test material while insisting that only he is brave enough to finish the work.
- **Escalation:** road violence gives way to occupation, open regional investigation, and finally technical Shell danger beneath the mine.
- **Consequences:** Cavora is freed or subordinated; Dario and Nella live or die; the false Ash marks are exposed or provoke retaliation; and the Forge is regulated, sealed, monopolized, destroyed, or copied.
