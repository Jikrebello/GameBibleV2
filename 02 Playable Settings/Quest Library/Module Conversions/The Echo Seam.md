# The Echo Seam

**Source:** *Lost Mine of Phandelver* | **Tier:** starter F | **Region:** Castaran Road | **Band:** levels 1-5 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A prospector discovers the route to a long-lost mine and is abducted before he can secure it. A hidden organizer uses local raiders and town thugs to search for the mine while keeping rivals divided.

### Beginning

An ambush on the road reveals the patron's disappearance and leads to a raider hideout. The party reaches a troubled frontier settlement where an armed gang dominates ordinary life.

### Middle

Several regional leads can be pursued in flexible order. Allies, prisoners, ruins, and rival bands reveal the mine's location and the identity of the organizer behind the violence.

### End

The party enters the mine, confronts competing occupants and the organizer, rescues survivors, and determines who controls the dormant magical forge.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The missing patron, road ambush, recoverable frontier town, open middle, lost mine, and final rival.
- A starter campaign that teaches social, wilderness, and dungeon play.

### Replace

- The generic ducal frontier and unnecessary Grobi insertion. The Castaran Road has its own prospectors, caravan guards, claim gangs, and expedition politics.
- Ownership as the only climax. Rescue and freeing the settlement remain primary goods.

## Exact World Egg Placement

A Castaran surveyor disappears while carrying the route to the Echo Seam, an Atherian mine abandoned when its rune-forge began repeating spoken spellwork through the rock. A contract-captain's gang controls the nearby road settlement, while an expelled University metallurgist coordinates the search from hiding.

The forge can temper a rehearsed elemental script into prepared metal, but each production run leaves an audible echo that contaminates later work. It is valuable, finite, and dangerous rather than an unlimited industrial miracle.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** find the missing surveyor and break the claim gang's hold on the road settlement.
- **Conflict:** several local groups need the mine but disagree over labor, safety, and ownership.
- **Antagonist:** an expelled Castaran scholar using hired violence to monopolize the forge.
- **Consequences:** the town is freed or subordinated; the surveyor lives or dies; the forge is regulated, destroyed, or secretly worked.
