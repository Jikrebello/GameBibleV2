# The Echo Seam

**Source:** *Lost Mine of Phandelver* | **Tier:** starter F | **Region:** Highlake-style silver frontier | **Band:** levels 1-5 analog

**The original:** a missing patron draws adventurers into a frontier town, gang trouble, regional leads, and a race to recover a lost magical mine.

**The conversion:** a charter surveyor vanishes while reopening the Echo Seam. A Grobi band holds one approach, wildcat delvers intimidate the town, and a guild claims an expired title. Beneath the galleries lies a Shell-forge able to temper rune-script directly into metal.

**The torque:** the working forge makes ownership more important than rescue. Guild, delver, ducal, and destruction outcomes alter wages, output, deaths, and regional militarization.

**Hook:** the starter campaign teaches town, wilderness, faction, and dungeon play through one disputed workplace.
