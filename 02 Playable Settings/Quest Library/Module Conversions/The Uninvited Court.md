# The Uninvited Court

**Source:** *Realm of the Fellnight Queen* • **Tier:** W→3 • **Region:** Folk frontier / Sylvan Marches • **Band:** level 7 analog

**The original:** a spiteful fey queen, excluded from an ancient pact, breaks free of her demiplane to crash a wedding and reclaim the forest.

**The conversion:** a Bright-Fire handfasting between a settler's son and a Folk speaker's daughter — the frontier's first such match in a generation — is interrupted when the mists rise: a **banished Faewild power**, cut from her court's rolls in some old realignment of the Menhir's circles, has found the seam the wedding's mixed vows opened. She doesn't want the couple. She wants *standing* — a seat at a court that erased her — and a mortal wedding's witnessed vows are, to Faewild law, a court in session.

**The torque:** the invasion is a legal filing. She can be fought (her mist-realm bleeding through, thin-space rules) or *ruled against* — if the wedding party can constitute itself as a court under grove-law and hear her claim, which means learning why she was banished, which is the part everyone regrets.

**Hook:** the Menhir canon's courts taken at their word.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A banished fey ruler exploits a broken pact and invades the mortal realm during a communal celebration.

### Beginning

Ominous signs interrupt a wedding and reveal that the boundary is failing.

### Middle

The party enters the invader's realm, gathers the history of the exclusion, and finds allies.

### End

They confront the ruler as the two realms overlap and determine the pact's future.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the interrupted wedding, invading court, realm crossing, old exclusion, and public finale.

### Replace

Replace the claim as merely a legal filing; grove-law matters, but pride, revenge, and threatened people drive the invasion.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Grenzburg, Folk frontier. **Cultures:** Folk, Chapel-Folk. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** defend a mixed Chapel-Folk handfasting when its witnessed vows open a venue for a banished Faewild power demanding restored standing.
- **Conflict:** the intruder can be fought in a bleeding mist-realm or heard and ruled against under grove-law, but understanding the old banishment may implicate current allies.
- **Antagonist:** the banished ruler, whose grievance may be intelligible but whose invasion and coercion remain chosen wrongs.
- **Consequences:** the marriage and guests survive or fail, the old banishment is upheld or revised, and the frontier gains a precedent for mixed vows or another generation of mistrust.
