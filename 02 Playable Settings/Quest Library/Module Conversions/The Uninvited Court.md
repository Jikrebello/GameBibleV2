# The Uninvited Court

**Source:** *Realm of the Fellnight Queen* • **Tier:** W→3 • **Region:** Folk frontier / Sylvan Marches • **Band:** level 7 analog

**The original:** a spiteful fey queen, excluded from an ancient pact, breaks free of her demiplane to crash a wedding and reclaim the forest.

**The conversion:** a Bright-Fire handfasting between a settler's son and a Folk speaker's daughter — the frontier's first such match in a generation — is interrupted when the mists rise: a **banished Faewild power**, cut from her court's rolls in some old realignment of the Menhir's circles, has found the seam the wedding's mixed vows opened. She doesn't want the couple. She wants *standing* — a seat at a court that erased her — and a mortal wedding's witnessed vows are, to Faewild law, a court in session.

**The torque:** the invasion is a legal filing. She can be fought (her mist-realm bleeding through, thin-space rules) or *ruled against* — if the wedding party can constitute itself as a court under grove-law and hear her claim, which means learning why she was banished, which is the part everyone regrets.

**Hook:** the Menhir canon's courts taken at their word.
