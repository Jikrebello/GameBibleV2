# The Bright-Fire Carnival

**Source:** E1 *Carnival of Tears* | **Tier:** W->3 | **Region:** Ferren Hollow, Grenzburg Folk frontier | **Band:** level 5 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Falcon's Hollow has profited by cutting Darkmoon Vale while its fey inhabitants watch in anger. Their queen, Syntira, seeks to frighten the town into ending its desecration, but the Cold Rider twists that grievance toward indiscriminate slaughter. Carnival master Namdrin Quinn is trapped in the plot because the Rider holds his dead wife's soul and has purchased his noninterference with a promise to restore her.

### Beginning

The party attends a genuine carnival on the eve of winter. Its games, rides, and performers establish the grounds before the horror begins. Small incidents test whether the characters protect vulnerable fairgoers; sufficiently compassionate characters earn Syntira's warning and aid. Namdrin behaves strangely but will not explain his bargain.

### Middle

After the fireworks, corrupted fey seize the carnival. Familiar attractions become isolated murder-sites, allowing the party to move through an event-based rescue gauntlet in an order of its choosing. The characters learn that destroying the witch-ice shard can free Namdrin from his oath and save his wife's soul, turning a passive and compromised ringmaster into an ally.

### End

After enough attractions are disrupted, the Cold Rider enters openly and challenges the party. Defeating it ends the coordinated massacre, but the number of survivors, Namdrin's fate, Syntira's standing, and the future conflict between town and forest depend on what the characters protected and uncovered during the carnival.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- A joyful fair whose layout and attractions become the map of a later horror.
- A free first phase that lets players meet people and notice small wrongness before the turn.
- An event-based rescue gauntlet with several simultaneous crises.
- A compromised local figure who can be freed from coercion and become an ally.
- A final winter predator that appears once its servants and set-pieces begin to fail.

### Replace

- Replace undifferentiated evil fey with a specific dispute between Ferren Hollow's timber interests and a burned Folk offering grove.
- Replace the distant northern witch connection with the [[The Rime Rider|Rime Rider]], an exiled winter-hunt power that attaches itself to genuine grievance and widens it into predation.
- Remove the Left Hand, banks, and universal contract procedure. The supernatural logic belongs to local grove-law and Faewild seasonality.
- Keep wrongdoing morally distinct: the cartel's desecration is real, Sisrin's first demand is bounded but morally contestable, and the Rider's attempted massacre is knowingly evil. Sylvorn's law establishes consequence rather than justice.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The carnival stands on the Two-Fires green outside [[Ferren Hollow]] during Bright-Fire. The town lies on Grenzburg's Folk frontier, where Chapel-Folk households, seasonal cutters, clear-cut crews, and the [[The Frontier Industry Lords|Timber-Cartel]] live beside land governed by older Folk and Faewild obligations.

Last spring, Clear-Cut Karl's crew burned an offering grove to meet a quota. [[Sisrin the Alder-Maid]], the grove's surviving law, brought the [[The Cloven|Cloven]] carnival to demand restitution from those responsible. The Rime Rider entered as a supposed ally, corrupted part of the carnival into the [[The Rimebound|Rimebound]], and widened a targeted reckoning into an attack on the whole town.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** enjoy and investigate three days of Bright-Fire festivities, then save Ferren Hollow when the carnival's attractions turn murderous.
- **Conflict:** the party must stop an indiscriminate winter hunt without denying the real desecration that gave it entry.
- **Antagonist:** the Rime Rider, aided by Rimebound carnival creatures and by guilty timber leaders who would rather endanger the town than admit what they burned.
- **Consequences:** Ferren Hollow may survive with the grove restored and the guilty exposed, survive while leaving the original wrong unsettled, or lose people and border peace to a massacre falsely wearing the face of justice.
