# The Founder's Lamp

**Source:** *Crypt of the Everflame* | **Tier:** R->W starter | **Region:** Caldria | **Band:** level 1 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A village marks adulthood by sending young people to recover ceremonial flame from its founder's crypt. Elders normally prepare harmless tests along the route, but this year's rite has been overtaken by real danger.

### Beginning

The participants receive supplies and enter the crypt expecting a controlled trial. Early hazards blur the line between staged challenges and evidence that something has gone wrong.

### Middle

The party explores deeper, finds casualties and hostile occupants, and learns that an intruder disturbed the crypt while seeking an old relic.

### End

The party confronts the intruder and the awakened dead, restores or retrieves the ceremonial flame, and returns as proven adults for reasons no elder intended.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The communal coming-of-age rite, staged tests becoming real, founder's crypt, and earned homecoming.
- A tutorial that begins with trust rather than employment.

### Replace

- A magical municipal charter and Vortharan collector. The Caldrian community's rite is culturally sufficient.
- A residue-warden doing routine custodial work, which makes the elders' staged intrusion implausible.

## Exact World Egg Placement

In a Caldrian hill town, youths carry a lamp from the tomb of the settlement's Freeflame founder to renew the chapel hearth. Elders seed the upper crypt with tests based on local history. This year an icon thief has entered from a collapsed lower gallery, killed one of the elders preparing the rite, and broken a seal over older Conversion-era dead.

The lamp is a genuine White Flame relic, but a modest one: it burns cleanly in foul air and reveals deliberate falsehood when held between speaker and hearer. Its worth is spiritual and communal, not industrial.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** complete the rite after discovering that the prepared crypt has become a real crime scene.
- **Conflict:** rescue a wounded elder, recover the lamp, and avoid condemning awakened dead that can still be laid to rest.
- **Antagonist:** an icon thief seeking proof against the founder's Freeflame allegiance.
- **Consequences:** the youths return with truth, a comforting lie, or evidence that reopens a local confessional wound.
