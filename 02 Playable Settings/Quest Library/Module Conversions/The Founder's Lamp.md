# The Founder's Lamp

**Source:** *Crypt of the Everflame* • **Tier:** R→W starter • **Region:** any marches town • **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** the town's coming-of-age rite sends youths to the founder's crypt to light a torch from his everflame; this year, the crypt is occupied.

**The conversion:** a marches town's founding charter requires its young adults to walk to the founder's barrow each Kindlenight and carry home fire from the **founder's lamp** — a hearth-lamp that has never gone out, tended by the crypt's residue-warden (a wall-warden the town politely calls "the Sexton"). This year the delvers-of-age find the Sexton smashed, the lamp missing, and fresh bodies staged to look like rattle-bone work.

**The torque:** the theft is mundane (a wildcat crew hired by a collector who knows what a never-extinguished flame is worth to Vorthara), but the town's charter is *ritually load-bearing* — no lamp, no Kindlenight relight, and every hearth in town knows what a dead flame means.

**Hook:** the perfect tutorial: Tier One dungeon, real stakes, and the lesson that some relics are municipal.
