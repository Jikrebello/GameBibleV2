# The Weeping Vale

**Source:** I6 *Ravenloft* | **Tier:** W campaign | **Region:** Sylvan Marches | **Band:** levels 5-7 analog

**The original:** travelers are trapped in a mist-bound valley ruled by a vampire lord whose obsession, castle, and history shape every route to escape.

**The conversion:** a Regalis elder holds invitation-jurisdiction over a whole valley because every household and ruling family once accepted his protection. The fog is the boundary of that consent. His obsession with a dead noblewoman persists through generations of coerced resemblance and Diadem-backed Pride escrow.

**The torque:** the claim is real without being righteous. Relics and history reveal which acceptances can be revoked. Destroying him is possible; routing his soul through the Weighing is harder and removes protections that kept worse things outside.

**Hook:** gothic imprisonment becomes a valley-sized contract nobody alive remembers signing.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Travelers enter a mist-bound valley ruled by a vampire whose obsession and castle dominate every path.

### Beginning

The party is trapped, meets the oppressed communities, and receives signs pointing toward the castle.

### Middle

Open exploration finds allies, relics, history, and routes that weaken the ruler's advantages.

### End

The party confronts the vampire in his castle and escapes or inherits the valley's consequences.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the trapped valley, mobile villain, open relic hunt, personal obsession, and castle climax.

### Replace

Replace the whole vale as a contract and consent puzzle; Regalis jurisdiction can explain the boundary while pride, murder, and domination remain the evil.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Folk frontier. **Cultures:** Folk, Eclessian. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** enter a fog-bound Folk valley whose households once accepted a Regalis elder's protection and now cannot revoke his invitation-jurisdiction.
- **Conflict:** his claim is metaphysically real but morally corrupt, and ending it also removes protections that kept other threats outside the vale.
- **Antagonist:** the Regalis elder sustaining generations of coerced resemblance to a dead noblewoman.
- **Consequences:** the elder is destroyed, routed, or left in power; households regain or lose standing; and the valley becomes free, exposed, or bound under revised protections.
