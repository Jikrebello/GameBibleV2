# Risen from the Glass

**Source:** *Risen from the Sands* • **Tier:** R→W one-shot • **Region:** Great Glass Desert margin / Covenant caravan roads • **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** a sandstorm exposes a pharaoh's tomb; wake the mummy or loot around him.

**The conversion:** a glass-storm exposes an **Ishkatar-era tomb** at the desert's edge — serpent-friezes, gold the Covenant's law says belongs to nobody living, and an interred priest of Avarix whose residue has had two thousand years to concentrate. Caravan-crews, a Jirahar land-ark, and a breach-court assessor all arrive within days.

**The torque:** under Covenant law the tomb's contents are *unclaimed estate* — the assessor is there to probate a serpent-cult, and his paperwork is genuinely the party's best weapon against what wakes.

**Hook:** the classic tomb one-shot, with a lawyer.
