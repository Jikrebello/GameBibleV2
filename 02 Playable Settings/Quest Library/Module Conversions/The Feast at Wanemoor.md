# The Feast at Wanemoor

**Source:** *Feast of Ravenmoor* • **Tier:** W one-shot (folk horror) • **Region:** remote marches village • **Band:** level 3 analog

**The original:** a missing tax collector, a charming isolated village, a harvest festival, and the old thing the village feeds.

**The conversion:** a Wanefall feast in a fen-village that the tithe-rolls list and nobody visits. The missing man is a Bank assessor; the village is warm, generous, strange in the small ways (no Souls-month graves older than a generation; the chapel's saint unlisted in any calendar; children who don't know the Kindlenight verse). What they keep in the fen is theirs — **a Long Table congregation gone feral**, feeding appetite-made-local for so long the Idol's mask has grown a parish.

**The torque:** the village genuinely prospers and genuinely loves its children — the horror is a working covenant, and breaking it means famine the party will watch. The old-thing can be starved, fed, or (the discernment option) *audited*: its terms were never written, and appetite argued with is appetite weakened.

**Hook:** folk horror where the monster's best defense is the harvest ledger.
