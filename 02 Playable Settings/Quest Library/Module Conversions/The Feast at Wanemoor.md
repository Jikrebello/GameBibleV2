# The Feast at Wanemoor

**Source:** *Feast of Ravenmoor* | **Tier:** W folk horror | **Region:** Wanemoor | **Band:** level 3 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

The remote Varisian village of Ravenmoor has gone years without paying taxes because of a clerical oversight. Tax collector Elias Kyle is sent from Magnimar to recover the arrears and never returns. A minority of villagers belongs to a hidden cult whose sacrifices have been associated with the village's continued harvests. Most residents know nothing of the cult's full nature.

### Beginning

The party is sent to find Kyle and the missing collection. Ravenmoor is isolated, humid, and without an inn. An encounter with a child's escaped pet stirge introduces unsettling but not necessarily evil local customs. Mayor Andretti Kriegler welcomes the party, claims Kyle stole the taxes and fled, and invites the investigators to the Founders' Festival and to sleep at his manor.

### Middle

The village can be investigated in open order before and during the festival. Games, meals, local stories, a neglected temple, the mayor's manor, and inconsistent testimony gradually expose a hidden inner circle. A young woman chosen as Feast Queen may be sacrificed or inducted. Her family attempts to lure away a visitor so that an outsider can take her place.

### End

The investigation leads to an abandoned farm used by the cult. The party crosses traps, guardians, hidden evidence, and a field maze while the cult prepares a pre-dawn sacrifice. Defeating the mayor does not end the danger: the parasitic horror gestating within him emerges as the final threat.

## What the Translation Preserves

- the missing official and simple personal reason to investigate;
- isolated hospitality that feels sincere because much of it is sincere;
- one strange animal custom that tests the party's assumptions;
- open village investigation and a playable harvest festival;
- a secret minority cult rather than universal village guilt;
- a loving family that chooses to sacrifice a stranger instead of its child;
- an abandoned farm, field maze, sacrificial climax, and body-horror turn;
- consequences for villagers who were innocent, frightened, complicit, or leading the murders.

## What the Translation Replaces

- Tax collection becomes a search for missing diocesan almoner [[Brother Matthias Veit]], whose report showed a full poor-box during a failed harvest.
- Ravenmoor becomes [[Merewick]], an isolated Brauvalian fen village reached by the [[Wanemoor District|Reedway]] and shallow boats.
- Ghlaunder, faceless stalkers, and blightspawn become one local Gluttony architecture: [[The Reed Mother]], [[The First Bowl]], its Mother-Root, and the living seed carried by [[Reeve Gerwin Holt]].
- Pet stirges become ordinary domesticated blood-kites, small Material fen animals that need no new creature family.
- Corn and generic swamp horror become reed agriculture, peat ground, eel channels, smokehouses, raised paths, and [[Hollow Acre]].
- The cult is not a branch of [[The Long Table]]. Its evil grew locally through repeated human choice.

## Exact World Egg Placement

[[Merewick]] lies in the reed fens of [[Wanemoor District]], eastern [[The Duchy of Brauvalis Overview|Brauvalis]]. The maintained Reedway is half a day from [[Wanenbruck]] in dry weather but can become a two-day boat route after flood. Weak parish visitation and difficult travel explain the village's isolation without turning it into an ungoverned foreign culture.

The adventure occurs during Reaping Week, roughly three weeks before Souls-month. Brother Matthias reached Merewick six weeks earlier and sent one report: the chapel poor-box was full although the district harvest had failed and named households were hungry. The inner cult seized him before his second message left. He remains alive because Reeve Gerwin intends to make him the principal offering at the Reed-Crown Feast.

The Reed Mother is a fixed local **Gut-Maw Mask of Gluttony**. It is not a Menhir, natural plant, saint, patron, or full Idol. Each sacrifice lets it convert the victim's vitality and Merewick's future seed, fish-spawn, and soil fertility into immediate abundance. The village receives real food now by consuming its own future and a murdered person.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** find Brother Matthias before the Reed-Crown procession reaches Hollow Acre.
- **Conflict:** expose the sacrificial custom without treating every villager as equally informed or culpable, then keep the survivors alive after ending the abundance on which they were taught to depend.
- **Antagonists:** the Reed Mother and the First Bowl Keepers led by Gerwin Holt. The Mask is a predatory pattern; the human keepers possess full moral agency.
- **Escalation:** suspicious hospitality becomes festival investigation, family abduction, pursuit through an abandoned farm and reed maze, then a sacrificial and manifestation crisis before dawn.
- **Consequences:** Matthias, Elsa, and other captives live or die; cult leaders escape or face judgement; the Mask is ended or survives through an anchor; and Merewick enters winter with relief, hunger, evacuation, or renewed sacrifice.
