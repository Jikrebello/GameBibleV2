# Entombed with the Court

**Source:** J1 *Entombed with the Pharaohs* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** deep desert (Anshara) • **Band:** level 6 analog

**The original:** a pyramid heist with treacherous rival adventurers and a sealed god-king.

**The conversion:** a Sestaran consortium quietly funds two delver crews — the party and a rival — into a newly-located **Ansharan satellite tomb** (a satrap's court, not the Court of Courts itself). Inside: paradise-garden friezes, court-protocol traps that punish *precedence errors*, and the satrap's household, embalmed in throne-wax, some of it recently disturbed... from inside.

**The torque:** the Sanguine bloodline claims everything Ansharan as family estate — and the rival crew's real employer is a Regalis intermediary, which the party learns exactly when it's least convenient.

**Hook:** the vampire canon's archaeology, with competitors.
