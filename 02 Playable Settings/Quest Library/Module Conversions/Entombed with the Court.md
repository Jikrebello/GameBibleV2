# Entombed with the Court

**Source:** J1 *Entombed with the Pharaohs* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** deep [[Great Glass Desert]] and Ansharan ruins | **Band:** level 6 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Four ancient pharaohs ruled together, joined their destinies, and built a single pyramid in which they would continue their reign after death. The pyramid was hidden in a separate desert demiplane called Ramlock's Hallow. It can be recalled to the Material Plane only by wearing the rulers' true golden funeral mask at the correct desert site.

A previous expedition found the tomb and was destroyed. Its sole survivor, Raegos, learned that four concealed encircled hieroglyphs form a progressive curse: anyone who sees all four is transformed into an undead guardian of the pyramid.

### Beginning: Auction, Survivor, and Rival Crew

1. The party arrives in Sothis under Pathfinder Society sponsorship and attends an antiquities auction in the Malhitu Bazaar.
2. A competing expedition, **Her Majestrix's Expeditionary**, is also searching for the pyramid. Its members are Xaven, Scepter, Hrokon, and Lonicera.
3. Lonicera infiltrates the party disguised as the translator Neferet Anu. She steals small personal possessions, reports the party's plans, and prepares evidence that can later frame them for theft.
4. Auction lots contain clues and useful relics connected to the Four Pharaohs. The party's secret contact, the Mithral Scarab, takes them to Raegos.
5. Raegos explains the four-symbol curse and identifies the true funeral mask as the device needed to make the missing pyramid reappear.

### Middle: The Mask Heist and Hidden Pyramid

1. The party and rivals race to acquire the true mask. A public museum holds a replica; the real object is kept in a private and heavily guarded reliquary.
2. The mask can be obtained through bargaining, stealth, or force. The rival crew may reach it first, frame the party with Lonicera's stolen objects, or follow whoever succeeds.
3. Both crews cross roughly fifty miles of desert to the pyramid site. Wearing the mask recalls the enormous pyramid from Ramlock's Hallow and gives the bearer a short lead.
4. Once the explorers enter, the entrance closes and both parties are trapped. The rivals can exploit cleared rooms, ambush the party, negotiate a truce, or divide the tomb between them.
5. The tomb is divided among the four pharaohs. Its chambers contain traps, undead retainers, a golem, treasure, architectural puzzles, and the four concealed curse-runes.
6. Escape instructions are hidden in the Cerulean Pharaoh's sarcophagus, behind a powerful mummified dragon. This forces the competing crews to address the tomb rather than simply steal portable treasure and leave.

### End: Four Pharaohs and a Timed Escape

1. The four pharaohs awaken and must be defeated. When the last falls, the true funeral mask shatters.
2. The pyramid begins returning to Ramlock's Hallow, leaving the survivors about thirty minutes to escape.
3. The party must use the exit knowledge recovered from the Cerulean tomb or find an alternative shaft while rooms collapse, seals fail, and remaining guardians converge.
4. The rival crew may cooperate, betray the party, abandon wounded members, or survive to become recurring competitors.
5. Characters transformed by the four-symbol curse regain free will after the rulers' destruction, but remain physically mummified and gravely diminished in the original rules.

## Conversion Diagnosis

### Preserve

- An antiquities auction that lets clues, tools, and rivalries appear before the dungeon.
- A survivor who can warn the party but cannot safely return.
- A genuine race for the key that reveals the tomb.
- A competent rival expedition with changing relationships rather than disposable bandits.
- An infiltrator embedded before the journey.
- Two hostile crews trapped together and forced to decide when cooperation becomes necessary.
- A progressive tomb curse that can turn allies into guardians.
- Four distinct funerary precincts, a central ruling household, and a timed escape.

### Replace

- Egyptian royal imagery belongs to the source adventure, not to Anshara. The conversion needs Ansharan satrapal courts, paradise-gardens, throne-wax, Giant pride, and vampire lineage claims.
- The original curse is mechanically memorable but causally arbitrary. The Ansharan tomb should transform intruders because it recognizes, ranks, and absorbs them into its household.
- The rival crew's constant cheating can feel scheduled by the module. Its members need separate motives, limits, and a believable patron.
- A sponsor funding competing crews needs internal logic. The modern conflict should come from rival principals inside one antiquities venture, not from everyone agreeing to an obviously wasteful arrangement.
- The tomb's mixed monsters need a shared origin and purpose. Every inhabitant should be part of the court, its menagerie, or the ecosystem that grew after its sealing.
- Treasure alone is too thin a stake. The expedition must decide the fate of conscious servants, evidence about vampiric origins, and a court-engine that could restore an ancient claim.
- The ending needs consequences for the rival crew and the Sanguine and Regalis claimants, not merely an escape roll.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**The conversion:** A Fiorenzian antiquities venture locates the processional road to an Ansharan satellite court sealed inside a folded paradise-garden. The party and the rival [[The Gilded Step|Gilded Step]] race to recover the **Processional Face** that can recall it. Once inside, the court's protocol assigns every intruder a rank and gradually seals them in throne-wax as members of its dead household.

**The torque:** the party's curator wants a documented recovery; a hidden Regalis investor wants proof that his bloodline inherits the court; the Sanguine claim the entire complex as ancestral memory. The court's human servants are still conscious enough to be freed, but opening the archives also gives modern vampires access to primordial blood rites.

**Hook:** two crews enter looking for a tomb and discover that the tomb has been waiting for a court.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** join a Fiorenzian antiquities venture racing to recall and enter a folded Ansharan court before a rival expedition claims it.
- **Conflict:** the dead court assigns every intruder a permanent household rank while modern sponsors compete over archives, blood rites, and ownership of the find.
- **Antagonist:** Satrap Varesh and the court protocol he still embodies, with the Gilded Step acting as a dangerous human rival rather than a second final villain.
- **Consequences:** trapped servants may be freed or sealed forever, the Processional Face changes hands, and primordial blood knowledge is destroyed, controlled, or released to modern claimants.
