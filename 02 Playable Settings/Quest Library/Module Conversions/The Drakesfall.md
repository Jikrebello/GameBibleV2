# The Drakesfall

**Source:** J2 *Guardians of Dragonfall* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** remote highlands (Thalmyric or Marches) • **Band:** level 5 analog

**The original:** dragons go to a sacred graveyard to die; its guardians need help when the site is violated.

**The conversion:** the **Drakesfall** — where the courts' beasts have gone to die since before men — is a valley floored with law-gems slowly returning to the Faewild. Its hereditary Folk wardens have been slaughtered by a Vorthara-funded harvesting crew, and the valley's accumulated law is destabilizing: seasons stacking wrong, game walking in circles, a dead silver drake's moon-law leaking into local nights.

**The torque:** heart-gems in bulk are a Concord-of-Weights-grade prize; the harvesting crew's paper is *legal* (a Marches lord sold rights he arguably held); and the last warden's heir is twelve.

**Hook:** the drake canon's holiest site, priced by the gem.
