# The Latch Jobs

**Source:** *Keys from the Golden Vault* | **Tier:** F ladder | **Region:** Sestaran Concord | **Band:** levels 1-11 analog

**The original:** thirteen heists give crews a brief, map, planning time, target, complications, and escape across varied scenarios.

**The conversion:** Matteo the Latch's successors broker jobs against a Medori outer vault, prize-court evidence room, Vortharan reagent store, salon ledger, artifact auction, and prison-ship manifest. Every client offers a persuasive reason and omits who profits from the altered ledger.

**The torque:** each job has three victories: acquire the target, escape attribution, and fence or disclose it without becoming a cutout. Alarm, heat, crew debt, and leverage persist across the ladder.

**Hook:** a heist campaign where the getaway ends only when the books settle.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A patron organization offers a series of unrelated heists against difficult, well-defended targets.

### Beginning

Each job begins with a mission, limited intelligence, and an opportunity to plan.

### Middle

The crew gathers information, chooses an approach, executes the theft, and adapts when the plan fails.

### End

Escape and delivery determine reward, exposure, and the target's response.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the mission-plan-execute-escape rhythm, varied targets, and player choice.

### Replace

Replace generic Sestaran identity; each job must belong to a specific city culture, patron, target, and local law.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Sestaran Concord. **Cultures:** Fiorenzian, Vortharan, Vardessan. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** take a ladder of Sestaran thefts against vaults, evidence rooms, reagent stores, salons, auctions, and prison shipping.
- **Conflict:** every client has a plausible reason and conceals who benefits when the stolen record or object changes hands.
- **Antagonist:** each job's security chief or rival crew, followed by the patron attempting to turn the party into an expendable cutout.
- **Consequences:** targets are acquired or lost, the crew escapes or accumulates heat, and evidence can be fenced, disclosed, returned, or used to expose the client.
