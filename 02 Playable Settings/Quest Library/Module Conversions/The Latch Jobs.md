# The Latch Jobs

**Source:** *Keys from the Golden Vault* | **Tier:** F ladder | **Region:** Sestaran Concord | **Band:** levels 1-11 analog

**The original:** thirteen heists give crews a brief, map, planning time, target, complications, and escape across varied scenarios.

**The conversion:** Matteo the Latch's successors broker jobs against a Medori outer vault, prize-court evidence room, Vortharan reagent store, salon ledger, artifact auction, and prison-ship manifest. Every client offers a persuasive reason and omits who profits from the altered ledger.

**The torque:** each job has three victories: acquire the target, escape attribution, and fence or disclose it without becoming a cutout. Alarm, heat, crew debt, and leverage persist across the ladder.

**Hook:** a heist campaign where the getaway ends only when the books settle.
