# The God of the Open Hand

**Source:** *Masks of the Living God* • **Tier:** W (infiltration) • **Region:** any Accord town or Sestaran port • **Band:** level 3 analog

**The original:** infiltrate the temple-fortress of Razmir, a mortal con-man worshipped as a living god, and break his grip on a town.

**The conversion:** a new faith arrives in an Accord-frozen town where both old confessions have exhausted each other: **the Open Hand** — no tithes, no relics, instant healing (staged), a masked living prophet, and contracts of membership that read gentle and compound like the Under-Ledger wrote them (someone did). The town's official churches can't act without violating the Accord; the party can.

**The torque:** the con is *real fraud wrapped around one real thing* — the prophet's inner circle includes a single genuine Weeping Courts Weeper whose fed miracles even the con-man doesn't understand. Exposing the fraud is easy; the last miracle argues back.

**Hook:** the Age of Held Proofs at village scale — everyone would rather believe than test.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Agents infiltrate the fortified temple of a fraudulent living god to learn its plans and break its local hold.

### Beginning

The party enters under cover and submits to recruitment, discipline, and scrutiny.

### Middle

They gather evidence, win allies, and endure the cult's escalating demands without exposing themselves.

### End

The party steals the needed proof, frees prisoners, or openly breaks the temple's leadership.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve deep-cover infiltration, communal pressure, staged miracles, and risk of exposure.

### Replace

Replace a generic Accord town and automatic Under-Ledger contract; use a Brauvalian confessional vacuum and one concrete fraud with one genuine corrupt miracle.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Brauvalis. **Cultures:** Brauvalian. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** investigate a generous new faith healing an Accord-frozen town before its gentle membership agreements mature into control.
- **Conflict:** the public miracles are mostly staged fraud, but one genuine Weeper is producing real supernatural effects that neither the prophet nor the established churches understand.
- **Antagonist:** the masked false prophet and Under-Ledger-backed inner circle, with the Weeper serving as a dangerous exploited power rather than proof of the fraud's truth.
- **Consequences:** the town is freed, captured, or left religiously shattered; the Weeper is rescued, destroyed, or inherited; and the Accord churches gain or lose legitimacy through their response.
