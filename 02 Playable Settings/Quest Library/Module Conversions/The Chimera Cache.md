# The Chimera Cache

**Source:** LB2 *Treasure of Chimera Cove* | **Tier:** F | **Region:** Thalassian coast | **Band:** level 7 analog

**The original:** follow the fallen baron's trail to a pirate cove and recover a hidden weapon-cache before it can decide a border war.

**The conversion:** records from [[The Last Baron of Brauvalis]] point to a privateer cache concealed in a tide-locked cove. Its treasure is the missing half of the baron's bargain: gunpowder, payroll silver, seal-registers, and letters proving which respectable courts financed the transfer. A contract-captain already holds the outer caves and intends to auction the evidence separately from the arms.

**The torque:** returning the weapons may save Brauvalis and restart the war; publishing the letters may preserve peace and collapse several governments; keeping both gives the party the means to become exactly the unaccountable force everyone feared.

**Hook:** the espionage sequel becomes a maritime evidence heist with a navy packed inside it.
