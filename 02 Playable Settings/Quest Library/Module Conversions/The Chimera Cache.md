# The Chimera Cache

**Source:** LB2 *Treasure of Chimera Cove* | **Tier:** F | **Region:** Thalassian coast | **Band:** level 7 analog

**The original:** follow the fallen baron's trail to a pirate cove and recover a hidden weapon-cache before it can decide a border war.

**The conversion:** records from [[The Last Baron of Brauvalis]] point to a privateer cache concealed in a tide-locked cove. Its treasure is the missing half of the baron's bargain: gunpowder, payroll silver, seal-registers, and letters proving which respectable courts financed the transfer. A contract-captain already holds the outer caves and intends to auction the evidence separately from the arms.

**The torque:** returning the weapons may save Brauvalis and restart the war; publishing the letters may preserve peace and collapse several governments; keeping both gives the party the means to become exactly the unaccountable force everyone feared.

**Hook:** the espionage sequel becomes a maritime evidence heist with a navy packed inside it.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A defeated baron concealed arms and evidence in a pirate cove before his fall.

### Beginning

Clues from the border crisis send the party along the coast while rivals follow.

### Middle

The party navigates a tide-bound cove, pirate defenses, and competing treasure hunters.

### End

The cache is recovered before hostile forces can use it to decide the border conflict.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the sequel trail, maritime infiltration, rival claimants, and strategic cache.

### Replace

Replace generic treasure; the cache must contain arms and letters that matter to Brauvalis and Castaran sponsors.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** follow the fallen baron's records into a tide-locked cove and seize a strategic privateer cache before its current holder auctions it.
- **Conflict:** the arms can decide the Brauvalian border while the accompanying letters can expose the respectable Castaran and Eclessian sponsors who prolonged it.
- **Antagonist:** the contract-captain occupying the cove and the buyers prepared to separate useful weapons from dangerous truth.
- **Consequences:** Brauvalis is armed or abandoned, a war is restarted or checked, and the evidence is published, suppressed, sold, or used as private leverage.
