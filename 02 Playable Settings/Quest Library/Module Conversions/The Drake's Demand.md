# The Drake's Demand

**Source:** *The Dragon's Demand* • **Tier:** F (mini-campaign) • **Region:** any marches town • **Band:** levels 1–7 analog

**The original:** a town under a dragon's tribute-demand; a dead wizard's tomb; a monster-hunting mini-campaign.

**The conversion:** a gold drake has descended on the commons of a market town and — in the manner of its sun-court livery — *demanded tribute in gleam*: coin, bells, the reliquary's fittings, hoarded and sunned on the moot-hill. The town's dead founding-magus (a Vorthara washout who retired rich, canonically suspicious) left a sealed vault everyone now wants opened, because his journals claimed he *bought* the town's charter-peace from the drake's court once before.

**The torque:** the drake/dragon confusion is the plot — the Church's rural clergy preach apocalypse, a Verena-style hunter arrives for glory, and the actual solution is in the dead magus's paperwork: the peace was a *lease*, and it expired. Renew, renegotiate, or slay — and the slaying option prices in what lapsed drake-law does to a valley (see the Drakesbane's vale).

**Hook:** the bestiary canon as a negotiation the town keeps trying to solve with a sword.
