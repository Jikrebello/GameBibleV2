# The Drake's Demand

**Source:** *The Dragon's Demand* | **Tier:** F mini-campaign | **Region:** Brauvalis | **Band:** levels 1-7 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A dangerous dragon settles near a small town and demands tribute. The crisis is connected to a dead wizard's estate, buried ruins, and several escalating monster threats around the settlement.

### Beginning

The party becomes involved in local troubles and investigates the wizard's abandoned home. Early discoveries establish the dragon's interest and point toward a wider search for the means to resist it.

### Middle

The party travels between town, wilderness sites, and old ruins, gathering allies and tools while the dragon's deadline approaches. Its agents and other monsters pressure the community.

### End

The party reaches the dragon's lair with the knowledge and resources earned through the campaign. The town's survival depends on confronting the demand rather than endlessly paying it.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- A town-scale campaign, escalating tribute, wizard's estate, regional monster hunts, and decisive lair confrontation.
- Preparation that materially changes the finale.

### Replace

- The drake as a misunderstood contractual party. A gold drake can be intelligent without turning predation into a lease dispute.
- The Vortharan expatriate and paperwork solution, which do not belong in every magical conflict.

## Exact World Egg Placement

A mature gold drake claims an old sun-court nesting ground in Brauvalis and demands polished metal, bells, and relic fittings because hoarded gleam strengthens its territorial law. It has already burned one farm and will take more. Its demand is intelligible but unjust.

The late magus of the nearby market town fought the same drake as a juvenile and concealed several tools, observations, and failures in his estate. The party's middle campaign is about learning the beast, protecting communities, and assembling a credible defense.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** save a Brauvalian market town from a drake whose tribute grows with every concession.
- **Conflict:** use the dead magus's legacy without repeating his reckless experiments.
- **Antagonist:** a proud, territorial gold drake that treats mortal settlement as trespass.
- **Consequences:** the drake is slain, driven away, confined by restored boundary law, or appeased at an unacceptable continuing cost.
