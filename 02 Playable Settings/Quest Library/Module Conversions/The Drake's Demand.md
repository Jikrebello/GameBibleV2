# The Drake's Demand

**Source:** *Pathfinder Module: The Dragon's Demand* (PZO9542, Mike Shel) | **Tier:** F mini-campaign | **World Egg region:** Hellerbruck, central Brauvalis | **Band:** levels 1-7 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Belhaim was founded by Tula Belhaim, a renowned dragonslayer credited with killing the black dragon Aeteperax. In the present, the reclusive wizard Balthus Hunclay investigates creatures associated with the Dominion of the Black and maintains a dangerous estate. A young adult green dragon has adopted Aeteperax's name and reputation. It wants Hunclay's research and has gathered kobolds and several unusual lieutenants.

### Beginning

The heroes have just arrived in Belhaim when the founder's last castle tower, known as the Witch Tower, collapses. Dead kobolds are found in the ruins, while a charred kobold lies at Hunclay's door. The heroes explore beneath the tower, find Hunclay dead, and follow the kobold incursion to its lair. The kobolds' service introduces the name Aeteperax and the possibility that the founder's old enemy has returned.

### Middle

The local baroness asks the heroes to make Hunclay's manor safe without looting it because his property belongs to an heir and will be auctioned. The manor contains traps, summoned otherworldly creatures, servants, and evidence of Hunclay's dangerous research. A hidden cave holds additional property, including a book the dragon wants. A month of downtime gives the heroes room to know Belhaim and pursue smaller local problems before the estate auction.

When the dragon's agent fails to acquire the books at auction, the green dragon projects the image of the supposedly dead black dragon and issues a tribute beyond the town's ability to pay.

### End

The heroes recover Tula Belhaim's dragon-hunting arms from her tomb and travel to an abandoned monastery near the tribute site and lair. There they can deal with the dragon's lieutenants, rescue captives, disable a device that brings alien servants under the dragon's control, and learn the dragon's true identity or name. Founder weapons, disabled machinery, allies, and knowledge all make the otherwise overwhelming final confrontation survivable.

The heroes ultimately enter the lair and confront the green dragon impersonating Aeteperax. The town's fate depends on whether they prepared well enough to reject the demand.

## What the Translation Preserves

- A dramatic tower collapse that places the party inside the crisis rather than briefing them afterward.
- A divided small servant people whose members can remain loyal, defect, or withdraw.
- The murdered magus as the absent character whose choices and research structure the investigation.
- A dangerous estate that must be secured under a no-looting mandate.
- A hidden cache, month of town life, and public auction before the central demand.
- A founder's tomb, occupied monastery, captured ally, multiple lieutenants, and a threshold device.
- Preparation that removes powers and encounter phases from a superior final enemy.
- A town detailed enough that its survival matters independently of the reward.

## What the Translation Replaces

- The impersonating green dragon becomes [[Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon]], the same gold drake Dame Sabine honestly believed she killed.
- The Dominion of the Black, alien summons, and Dark Window become a Sun-Court menagerie, molt research, the [[The Meridian Mirror|Meridian Mirror]], and the Noon Window.
- Kobolds become [[Giltlings]], divided between fearful service, opportunism, and resistance.
- Belhaim becomes [[Hellerbruck]], a central Brauvalian river, quarry, brewing, pottery, and bell-founding town.
- Hunclay becomes [[Magister Othmar Veyl]], a murdered drake scholar whose captive research was useful and morally compromised.
- Tula becomes [[Dame Sabine Heller]], a founder who made an honest error rather than constructing a deliberate civic fraud.
- The impossible monetary tribute becomes the seizure of bells, regalia, shrine fittings, the Meridian Mirror, and twelve living attendants.
- A simple dragon identity trick becomes the return of a rare sapient elder drake whose real grievance does not excuse chosen tyranny.

## Exact World Egg Placement

Hellerbruck lies on the River Weiss in central [[The Duchy of Brauvalis Overview|Brauvalis]], away from eastern [[Wanemoor District]]. Its confessional compromise, guild economy, river traffic, brewing, ceramics, quarrying, and bell-founding make the demanded objects part of ordinary public life rather than interchangeable fantasy treasure.

The town occupies an old gold-drake nesting court. Sabine wounded Sovarath in 1117 AR and found it apparently dead beneath a cave fall. Sovarath recovered in distant sun-cliffs. Centuries of heart-gem memory eventually produced the rare elder threshold: language, self-awareness, and moral responsibility without transforming it into a true dragon or patron.

Othmar proved that the founding trophy scales and modern molts came from the same creature. The Bright-Tail Host murdered him before his warning reached Baroness Margarethe Heller. Its tunnelling caused the tower collapse that opens the campaign.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** save Hellerbruck from the elder gold drake its founder mistakenly believed dead.
- **Conflict:** acknowledge a real historical wrong without accepting collective punishment, desecration, or slavery as restitution.
- **Antagonist:** Sovarath, an intelligent elder that deliberately turns territorial grievance into a mortal court founded on fear.
- **Preparation:** recover the founder's arms, learn the first heat-name, master or destroy the Mirror, close the Noon Window, rescue Mara Auen, split the Giltlings, and neutralize the lieutenants.
- **Consequences:** Sovarath is slain, driven away, contained, appeased through a continuing Sun-Tithe, or allowed to establish a Court of Noon.
