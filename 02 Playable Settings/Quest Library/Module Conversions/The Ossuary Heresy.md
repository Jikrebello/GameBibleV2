# The Ossuary Heresy

**Source:** *The Godsmouth Heresy* • **Tier:** W starter • **Region:** Caleran, Martyrs' Steps and the ossuary roads • **Band:** level 1 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Corpses vanish from a respected city ossuary. Its custodians quietly recruit outsiders to investigate, fearing public panic and damage to their institution.

### Beginning

The party is admitted to closed burial galleries, verifies that bodies have been removed from within, and follows the route into older chambers below the active crypt.

### Middle

Exploration reveals undead, scavengers, and an ancient temple beneath the ossuary. The thefts are the work of a former member of the burial faith who is creating undead servants.

### End

The party confronts the heretic, stops the body thefts, and returns with evidence that the city's sacred burial ground rests over a far older and dangerous complex.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The quiet commission, body-count discrepancy, layered burial dungeon, and apostate antagonist.
- Recovery of the dead as a moral obligation, not merely evidence management.

### Replace

- The Undertow/Fleshweaver marketplace. It overloads a starter dungeon with two secret networks and turns desecration into commerce by default.
- The demand that families never learn. Caleran's court may fear scandal, but truth and restitution remain possible outcomes.

## Exact World Egg Placement

In Caleran, a dismissed ossuary deacon has been stealing unclaimed and pauper dead to prove that White Flame rites preserve enough spiritual imprint to animate obedient guardians. Beneath the Martyrs' Steps he has found an Atherian funerary workshop whose old mechanisms amplify his blasphemous experiments.

He is wrong about the souls: the dead have routed onward. What moves are residues forced into familiar gestures. That does not make the desecration harmless; it makes his claim to have recalled willing saints both false and wicked.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** discover who is removing bodies before the next public interment exposes the breach.
- **Conflict:** protect the living, recover the dead, and decide how much of the court's negligence becomes public.
- **Antagonist:** a former deacon seeking vindication through forbidden residue-work.
- **Consequences:** families receive restitution or concealment; the under-ossuary is sealed, studied, or exploited; Caleran's burial court is reformed or protected.
