# The Broken Tower

**Source:** *Master of the Fallen Fortress* • **Tier:** R starter • **Region:** any frontier (Velian border / Grenzburg approaches) • **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** a siege-cracked tower, vertical dungeon-crawl, a captive to rescue, troglodytes in residence.

**The conversion:** an earthquake-split Velian border tower (three ownerships of dead in its history, per lives-canon) has been colonized by harridans below and a Ghost-Foot exile above — who holds a Bank surveyor hostage against a grievance the Bank genuinely owes him. Floor-by-floor vertical delve, Tier One residue throughout.

**The torque:** the salvage-law question — the tower's charter lapsed with the earthquake, and whoever *clears* it can claim it, which the party's employer knew and didn't mention.

**Hook:** the delver trade's cleanest tutorial: iron for bones, silver for shades, paperwork for everything else.
