# The Severin Panels

**Source:** U1 *Gallery of Evil* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** Fiorenzia • **Band:** level 4 analog

**The original:** a celebrated artist's new works are killing their owners; the paintings are doorways.

**The conversion:** fourteen years after the Pyre of Masks, a dead master's *confiscated drawer* (per Fiorenzia lives-canon) surfaces at auction — panels painted in Severin's four years, when the Flame was in the city and something else was painting back. Owners of the sold panels begin dying in compositions.

**The torque:** the art-appraisers' guild (the city's real police) must decide whether the panels are haunted residue, a Weeping Courts forgery-op harvesting fear — or genuine, which would make them *relics of a maybe-saint* and legally unburnable. All three theories have evidence. The Latch-job option (steal them all back into the drawer) is on the table.

**Hook:** Fiorenzia pays genius lavishly and remembers hanging it; the panels remember too.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A celebrated artist's paintings kill or trap their owners through supernatural scenes.

### Beginning

The first death and auction trail lead the party to dispersed works and frightened collectors.

### Middle

Each recovered panel reveals the artist's history and the mechanism connecting art to victims.

### End

The party confronts the force behind the paintings and decides whether to destroy, contain, or preserve them.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the art mystery, dispersed victims, visual clues, and gallery climax.

### Replace

Replace generic Sestaran policing; Fiorenzian appraisers, patrons, and post-Pyre memory must determine authority.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** recover a dead Fiorenzian master's confiscated panels as their new owners begin dying in compositions copied from the art.
- **Conflict:** the works may be residue, deliberate Weeping Courts instruments, or relics of a possible saint, and each classification grants a different authority custody.
- **Antagonist:** the force painting back through the panels and the collectors willing to profit while its nature remains unresolved.
- **Consequences:** owners live or die, the panels are burned, stolen, sanctified, or contained, and the master's reputation becomes sainthood, scandal, or a criminal market.
