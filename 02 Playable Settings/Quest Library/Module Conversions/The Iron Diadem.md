# The Iron Diadem

**Source:** *Tomb of the Iron Medusa* | **Tier:** F/W | **Region:** a remote Thalmyric border range | **Band:** level 14 analog

**The original:** the last heir of a disgraced noble family hires explorers to enter its trap-laden necropolis and recover evidence that might clear the family name.

**The conversion:** a claimant to an extinguished Thalmyric house needs the sealed family crypt opened before a court rules its line attainted forever. The crypt was designed as a legal argument in stone: ancestor effigies testify, traps punish contradictory claims, and the iron funerary crown records every oath by which the family rose.

**The torque:** the family was framed for one treason and guilty of a worse one. Its founders admitted the Diadem into imperial court life in exchange for a hereditary aura of command. Clearing the modern heir requires publishing evidence that may reactivate the family's supernatural claim over old dependents.

**Hook:** a high-tier tomb where vindication and restoration are dangerously different verdicts.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A noble family's tomb hides a dangerous relic, ancestral secrets, and a rival seeking the same prize.

### Beginning

The party enters the estate and identifies the route into the sealed family complex.

### Middle

Portraits, guardians, and family history reveal the cost of the relic and the truth of the lineage.

### End

The rival and the tomb's final guardian converge at the diadem, forcing a choice over inheritance.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the aristocratic tomb, family mystery, rival delvers, and dangerous crown.

### Replace

Replace generic Thalmyric placement; make it a Veridun succession relic whose claim matters to a named court family.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** open a sealed Thalmyric family crypt before its modern claimant is declared attainted forever.
- **Conflict:** the crypt proves the family was framed for one treason while revealing that its founders knowingly accepted a worse supernatural source of authority.
- **Antagonist:** the rival claimant exploiting the deadline and the Iron Diadem's inherited command-presence within the tomb.
- **Consequences:** the heir is vindicated, condemned, or bound to the old power, while publication or concealment of the evidence changes former dependents' rights and Veridun's court politics.
