# The Iron Diadem

**Source:** *Tomb of the Iron Medusa* | **Tier:** F/W | **Region:** a remote Thalmyric border range | **Band:** level 14 analog

**The original:** the last heir of a disgraced noble family hires explorers to enter its trap-laden necropolis and recover evidence that might clear the family name.

**The conversion:** a claimant to an extinguished Thalmyric house needs the sealed family crypt opened before a court rules its line attainted forever. The crypt was designed as a legal argument in stone: ancestor effigies testify, traps punish contradictory claims, and the iron funerary crown records every oath by which the family rose.

**The torque:** the family was framed for one treason and guilty of a worse one. Its founders admitted the Diadem into imperial court life in exchange for a hereditary aura of command. Clearing the modern heir requires publishing evidence that may reactivate the family's supernatural claim over old dependents.

**Hook:** a high-tier tomb where vindication and restoration are dangerously different verdicts.
