# Breach-Week

**Source:** *Academy of Secrets* • **Tier:** F/W • **Region:** Vorthara • **Band:** level 13 analog (scale down freely)

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

The Acadamae of Korvosa holds the annual **Breaching Festival**, a lethal public contest in which entrants collect magical key-lights from seven school halls and use them to enter the supposedly impregnable Hall of Wards. No legitimate contestant has returned victorious in more than 150 years. The festival is secretly a soul trap created by the Acadamae's founder, **Volshyenek Ornelos**, who is actually the exiled infernal duke **Lorthact**. Contestants who breach the Hall are diverted into the infernal demiplane **Belzeragna**; their bodies appear dead in the Hall while their souls remain imprisoned.

Headmaster **Toff Ornelos** knows that his supposedly immortal ancestor still controls the family and school, but does not know Volshyenek is Lorthact or that the festival harvests souls. Hoping to produce a winner, improve his prestige, and weaken his ancestor's influence, Toff breaks tradition and invites the player characters as powerful outside ringers.

### Beginning: The Unexpected Invitation

1. The party receives a last-minute invitation and meets Toff the day before the festival. He offers a vast accumulated prize and permits the party to cooperate, although only the first person to emerge officially wins.
2. A student, **Seska Imintar**, attempts an unauthorized high-level summoning and loses control of a retriever. The party can save her and contain the creature. This demonstrates the Acadamae's culture of dangerous ambition but is only loosely connected to the main conspiracy.
3. The party can explore the campus and meet four rival contestants: frightened freelancer **Fatmire**, guild thief **Illia Ean**, brutal opportunist **Knur**, and accomplished wizard **Maganrad**. Fatmire withdraws; the others enter the competition.

### Middle: Festival and Infernal Prison

1. The public festival begins. Each contestant must secure a key-light from one of seven lesser halls, whose challenges are lethal magical traps built around the schools of magic. Rival contestants die, disappear, or reach the Hall before the party.
2. After collecting enough key-lights, the party enters the Hall of Wards and is transported to Belzeragna rather than the building's real interior.
3. The party explores a distorted copy of the academy containing previous contestants, damned souls, torturers, devils, and the remains of recent rivals. Some prisoners can be freed by recovering personal objects or destroying the instruments binding them.
4. **Chyvvom**, Lorthact's contract-devil lieutenant, offers power or release under terms designed to keep the party trapped. The party can bargain, exploit the contracts, or fight him.

### End: Escape, Rupture, and Scandal

1. Defeating or outmaneuvering Chyvvom allows the party to force a return to the Material Plane. The route was not designed to open outward, so their escape ruptures Belzeragna's connection to the Hall.
2. Devils spill across the festival grounds and attack students, faculty, and spectators. The party must move around the campus containing several outbreaks before the school is overwhelmed.
3. After the fighting, the party possesses evidence that the Breaching Festival imprisoned generations of contestants. Toff denies knowledge of the infernal truth, which is credible, but he remains responsible for sustaining a lethal institution he did not understand.
4. The party may expose the scandal, preserve the Acadamae while removing Toff, cooperate in a cover-up, fight the headmaster, or use the revelation to claim influence over the school. Lorthact remains at large for a future campaign.

## Conversion Diagnosis

### What Is Worth Keeping

- A famous public competition that becomes a real emergency.
- A preparatory evening with rivals and warning signs.
- Modular challenges across distinct magical schools.
- A final defense that leads somewhere the institution denies exists.
- Earlier contestants found alive, changed, or recoverable inside.
- Escape causing a campus-wide disaster in front of witnesses.
- A compromised head who is culpable without being the true mastermind.

### What Needs Rebuilding

- The original soul-harvest conspiracy depends on a remote villain the party cannot meaningfully confront.
- Requiring one key-light per character makes the contest scale awkwardly with party size.
- The opening summoning accident demonstrates tone but does not affect the later plot.
- Toff's prestige motive is too weak for the danger he knowingly sustains.
- The infernal rupture happens because the ending requires a public battle, not because contestants made a legible choice.
- The aftermath leans heavily on scandal management instead of resolving what happened to the trapped contestants and the dangerous institution itself.

## World Egg Conversion Seed

**The conversion:** Vorthara's schools hold **Breach-Week**, a sanctioned trial in which crews breach staged defenses for prizes, licenses, sponsorships, and recruitment. The hosting [[Collegium of Doors]] possesses an adaptive ward-engine that has learned from every contestant ever sent through it. Failed challengers were not simply killed: their magical patterns and memories remain trapped inside the engine's folded annex. This year the party becomes the first crew capable of reaching the intelligence at its center, [[The Examiner]].

**The torque:** Vorthara built a test that improves by consuming failure and mistook increasing lethality for proof of excellence. Breaking the Examiner can free its captives, but every defense it has stored will lose containment at once. The final problem is not who signs for the damage; it is whether the party saves the people outside, the people trapped within, the institution that enabled it, or only themselves.

**Hook:** the mill-town of the uncanny opens its gates for a sporting holiday and discovers that its favorite examination has been studying the city in return.
