# Breach-Week

**Source:** *Academy of Secrets* • **Tier:** F/W • **Region:** Vorthara • **Band:** level 13 analog (scale down freely)

**The original:** the Acadamae's yearly Breaching festival invites outsiders to penetrate its wards; this year the summoning-school's secrets get loose.

**The conversion:** Vorthara's schools hold **Breach-Week** — the sanctioned trial where outside crews attempt the wards of a chosen faction-house for prizes, licenses, and recruitment (arena canon, formalized). The party enters as contestants; mid-trial, the hosting school's *actual* containment fails — a Pale Hand client-working, off the books — and the game becomes real inside a campus that cannot admit anything is wrong until the festival crowds are gone.

**The torque:** liability-court logic runs the horror: every ward the party broke *as contestants* is now legally their breach, and the school's counsel offers to trade indemnity for silence, cleanup, and one small retrieval nobody must ever audit.

**Hook:** the mill-town of the uncanny, on its one open-house week.
