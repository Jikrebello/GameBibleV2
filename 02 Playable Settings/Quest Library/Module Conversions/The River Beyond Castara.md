# The River Beyond Castara

**Source:** W2 *River into Darkness* | **Tier:** F | **Region:** Emerald Expanse | **Band:** level 4 analog

**The original:** escort a merchant expedition upriver to a remote outpost, then discover why local peoples are attacking and what the company considers worth the losses.

**The conversion:** a Castaran consortium pays extravagantly for guards to relieve an upriver factor-house. Its staff say Expanse communities attack without warning. In fact, the company has driven pilings into a buried Giant servitor conduit, and every shipment sends a signal farther inland. The attacks are attempts to stop a mechanism the locals remember through prohibition but cannot safely explain near the site.

**The torque:** the expedition's mundane abuses are real, and so is the older danger. Destroying the outpost protects the river but abandons debt-bound workers; defending it preserves lives now and activates the conduit later. The useful solution requires evacuating people while denying every sponsor the discovery it paid for.

**Hook:** a river expedition in which the map's blank space is someone else's warning label.
