# The River Beyond Castara

**Source:** W2 *River into Darkness* | **Tier:** F | **Region:** Emerald Expanse | **Band:** level 4 analog

**The original:** escort a merchant expedition upriver to a remote outpost, then discover why local peoples are attacking and what the company considers worth the losses.

**The conversion:** a Castaran consortium pays extravagantly for guards to relieve an upriver factor-house. Its staff say Expanse communities attack without warning. In fact, the company has driven pilings into a buried Giant servitor conduit, and every shipment sends a signal farther inland. The attacks are attempts to stop a mechanism the locals remember through prohibition but cannot safely explain near the site.

**The torque:** the expedition's mundane abuses are real, and so is the older danger. Destroying the outpost protects the river but abandons debt-bound workers; defending it preserves lives now and activates the conduit later. The useful solution requires evacuating people while denying every sponsor the discovery it paid for.

**Hook:** a river expedition in which the map's blank space is someone else's warning label.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

An expedition follows a dangerous river into an unknown interior in search of a lost route and valuable site.

### Beginning

The crew assembles, enters the river country, and encounters the first signs of rival or hostile interest.

### Middle

Travel hazards, settlements, betrayal, and dwindling supplies turn exploration into survival.

### End

The expedition reaches its goal and must escape or establish a route with what remains of the crew.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the expedition vessel, attrition, river encounters, rival interest, and return journey.

### Replace

Replace generic colonial discovery; Castaran motives, local sovereignty, and the cost of opening the route must be explicit.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Castara, Emerald Expanse. **Cultures:** Castaran. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** relieve a Castaran factor-house far upriver and discover why Expanse communities are attacking it.
- **Conflict:** the company's abuses are real, but its pilings have also awakened a Giant servitor conduit that threatens workers and local settlements alike.
- **Antagonist:** the expedition leadership that refuses evacuation and the ancient conduit its extraction work is activating.
- **Consequences:** debt-bound workers are rescued or abandoned, the outpost is closed or defended, and knowledge of the conduit is buried, sold, or transmitted farther inland.
