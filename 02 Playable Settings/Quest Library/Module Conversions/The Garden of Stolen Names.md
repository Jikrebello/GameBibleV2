# The Garden of Stolen Names

**Source:** *Broken Chains* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** a Covenant trade city | **Band:** level 6 analog

**The original:** search an underground slave operation for a missing abolitionist and uncover the slavers' deeper monstrous allegiance.

**The conversion:** a lawful redemption-house asks the party to find an agent who vanished beneath the old water channels. The hidden auction does not sell bodies openly; it strips captives of names, kinship claims, and legal personhood, then sells the resulting labor-contracts as abandoned obligations. The trade is illegal even under Covenant law, but several respected jurists have certified its paperwork.

**The torque:** freeing the captives physically is only half the rescue. Their stolen names remain lodged in a binder's archive, and destroying it may erase the only proof that they were ever free. The deepest contracts bear the Creditor's architecture, though no public court will use that name.

**Hook:** an abolitionist dungeon where identity, evidence, and escape must all survive.
