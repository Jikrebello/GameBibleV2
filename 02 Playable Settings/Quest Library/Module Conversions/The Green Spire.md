# The Green Spire

**Source:** *The Emerald Spire Superdungeon* | **Tier:** F/W/3 campaign | **Region:** Emerald Expanse | **Band:** levels 1-13 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A strange green tower descends far deeper than its visible ruin suggests. Successive occupants have claimed separate levels, while the structure's ancient purpose lies at the bottom.

### Beginning

The party reaches a fortified settlement near the Spire, meets rival patrons and delvers, and begins with upper levels shaped by recent occupants.

### Middle

Each descent reveals a distinct environment, faction, and fragment of history. Alliances and shortcuts accumulate while deeper levels expose the Spire as a connected machine rather than unrelated dungeons.

### End

The lowest levels reveal the structure's origin and controlling intelligence. The party's relationships with surface powers determine who can exploit, seal, or defend what they find.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The nearby expedition settlement, many distinct levels, recurring factions, and deep technological reveal.
- A campaign in which vertical descent also moves backward through occupation history.

### Replace

- The grab bag of every secret network and mystery in the setting. A megadungeon needs focus, not a sample of the whole bible.
- Generic Grobi and Undertow insertions where local Expanse peoples and expedition failures should carry the middle levels.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The Green Spire is a Giant survey-engine embedded in the Emerald Expanse. Its upper crown has been occupied by Castaran expeditions, marooned privateers, escaped laborers, and local communities pushed into its defensible galleries. Its lower shaft records climate, bloodlines, migration, and obedience from the Age of Chains.

The Spire's through-line is **classification**. Each level preserves or resists a different category the engine imposed on living peoples. The deepest intelligence is not the Heart-Stone's sibling and does not run a financial ledger; it is a machine built to decide what kinds of life could be ruled, remade, or erased.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** establish a Castaran expedition base and descend before a rival claim controls the only stable route.
- **Conflict:** every opened level creates discoveries, displaced occupants, and new claimants.
- **Antagonist:** the surviving classification engine and people who would restart it for power.
- **Consequences:** the Spire becomes a protected archive, colonial weapon, independent refuge, or sealed wound.
