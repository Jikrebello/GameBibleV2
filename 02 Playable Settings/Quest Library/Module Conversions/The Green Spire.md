# The Green Spire

**Source:** *The Emerald Spire Superdungeon* • **Tier:** F/W/3 (the campaign-length megadungeon) • **Region:** the Emerald Expanse • **Band:** levels 1–13 analog

**The original:** sixteen levels of ancient green-crystal spire, each floor a different faction or era, descending to the machine at the root.

**The conversion:** the Expanse's defining site — **a Giant-era spire of green glass** rising from the jungle where the Viscaran charts simply stop, its shaft descending level by level through squatter history: pirate caches in the crown, a failed colony's fort, a Grobi-analog native warren, Fleshweaver poachers, an Undertow way-station (the dead travel far out here), a bird-marked gallery (swept, waiting), and below that, floors no chart admits. At the root: an **engine of the Age of Chains**, sibling to the Heart-Stone, still holding whatever ledger the Giants left running.

**The torque:** the spire is the New World arc's spine and the Held Proofs register's overseas entry — every faction that funds an expedition level is buying a stake in what the root proves. Per register rules, the deepest floors touch the sealed bird-mark answer: author sign-off required past floor twelve.

**Hook:** the superdungeon as colonial history in vertical section.
