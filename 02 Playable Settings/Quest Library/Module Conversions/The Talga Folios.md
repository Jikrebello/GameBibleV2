# The Talga Folios

**Source:** *Candlekeep Mysteries* | **Tier:** R/W anthology | **Region:** Migdal Talga | **Band:** levels 1-16 analog

**The original:** seventeen stand-alone mysteries begin with unusual books in a great library and lead into varied contained adventures.

**The conversion:** disputed books arrive at [[Migdal Talga]]'s deep scroll-floor and sealed stacks: a pilgrimage account changing its witnesses, a ledger containing an extra generation, a map whose roads exist only when cited, or a sermon remembering being heard.

**The torque:** the question is who gains standing if a text is admitted, who must never read it, and whether destroying dangerous proof also destroys the rights it establishes. Reliability, readership, and custody persist between mysteries.

**Hook:** the book anthology becomes a campaign about evidence capable of acting on readers.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A great library's unusual books each open a separate contained mystery.

### Beginning

A reader, donation, or catalog error activates the volume that begins each case.

### Middle

Research in the library points outward into a distinct site, community, or pocket reality.

### End

Resolving the case returns the book with new provenance and consequences for the collection.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the book-triggered anthology, varied genres, recurring library, and archival aftermath.

### Replace

Replace generic dangerous texts; every folio must matter to Jirahar memory, law, descent, pilgrimage, or disputed testimony.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Migdal Talga. **Cultures:** Jirahar. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** investigate dangerous and self-altering texts admitted to Migdal Talga's sealed stacks.
- **Conflict:** each text can establish real rights or history while changing readers, routes, or testimony, so destruction and preservation both carry human costs.
- **Antagonist:** the specific intelligence, fraud, or metaphysical pressure activated through each folio, together with claimants seeking a favorable reading.
- **Consequences:** a text is admitted, restricted, corrected, or destroyed, and its readers, custodians, and beneficiaries carry the ruling into later mysteries.
