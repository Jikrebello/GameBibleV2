# The Tithe-House

**Source:** *Death House* | **Tier:** W horror one-shot | **Region:** Aurelium | **Band:** levels 1-3 analog

**The original:** a welcoming townhouse traps visitors inside a murderous household cult's history, ending in a basement rite demanding sacrifice.

**The conversion:** an Aurelium household devoted itself to a living saint so completely that nobody admitted when she died. Meals, confessions, gifts, punishments, and rites continued from habit. The Devoted architecture still distributes approval and shame although no Beloved remains.

**The torque:** there is no mastermind. Every room recruits a replacement household role, while the empty altar demands sacrifice because that is what it learned. Escape requires ending the pattern without installing a new object of devotion.

**Hook:** domestic horror becomes the Beloved's machinery running after its operator is gone.
