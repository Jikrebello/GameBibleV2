# The Star over Dustfeld

**Source:** *Doom Comes to Dustpawn* • **Tier:** W→3 • **Region:** remote hills (Marches or steppe edge) • **Band:** level 8 analog

**The original:** a falling star lands near a quiet town; the crashed vessel's occupants begin replacing the locals.

**The conversion:** a light crosses the sky and grounds in the hills above Dustfeld — and per canon, everyone knows what falling metal means: **Nythalum's returns** (the moon-stranded minds' probes, Prehistory canon). The town's strangeness starts small: perfect memories, mended tools, neighbors who stop dreaming. The machine in the crater is *cataloguing* — and its patient, courteous custodians need local shapes to finish the survey their masters launched two thousand years ago.

**The torque:** the probe's masters predate the Exodus; its mission brief is sane, methodical, and horrifying only in scale — and Vorthara, the University, and the Hollow-Kings' agents converge on the crater with three incompatible definitions of *salvage*.

**Hook:** the setting's one science-horror vein, tapped exactly once — which is what keeps it Tier Three.
