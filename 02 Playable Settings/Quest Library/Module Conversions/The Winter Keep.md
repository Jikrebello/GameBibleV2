# The Winter Keep

**Source:** *Daughters of Fury* • **Tier:** W (siege one-shot) • **Region:** Grenzburg approaches / any Grobi frontier • **Band:** level 3 analog

**The original:** defend an isolated keep against a warband while its mixed-heritage defenders confront what they are to each other.

**The conversion:** a way-fort on the Tuskway must hold three days against a Bloody-Sun vanguard until the thaw-riders come — garrison: a half-strength Blackjack squad, Chapel-Folk refugees, two Ghost-Foot hostages nobody trusts, and the fort's chatelaine, whose mother was Folk and whose command nobody contests twice. The warband's herald offers terms each dawn, and the terms get *stranger* — the band is running from something down-corridor, not toward the fort.

**The torque:** the siege is Tier Two; what the Grobi are fleeing is the hook into Tier Three (Pale-Skin pressure per canon) — and the fort's survival math runs on the Numbers doc: arrows, bread, trust, in that order of scarcity.

**Hook:** the Grobi canon's tragedy in one defensible square: the enemy is starving, herded, and at the gate.
