# The Winter Keep

**Source:** *Daughters of Fury* | **Tier:** W siege adventure | **Region:** Bearshield and the Grobi frontier | **Band:** levels 3-6 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A border community is destabilized by old vengeance, revolutionary violence, and an infernal threat tied to a family debt. The adventure expands beyond a single siege through several connected settlements and confrontations.

### Beginning

The party reaches a tense town as violence erupts, learns that several factions are pursuing the same family, and must protect civilians while deciding whom to trust.

### Middle

The conflict moves through roads, ruins, and divided communities. The party uncovers the history binding the antagonists together and the supernatural force exploiting that grievance.

### End

The party confronts the principal attackers and the power behind them, deciding whether inherited guilt produces vengeance, restitution, or another cycle of violence.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The multi-part frontier crisis, family grievance, divided defenders, escalating supernatural exploitation, and hard-won reconciliation.
- A defensible hall as one major act, not the entire imported plot.

### Replace

- Blackjacks, Chapel-Folk, and Grenzburg's Tuskway. Bearshield must be distinctly Ättar.
- Pale-Skins as an automatic secret cause. Grobi aggression can be chosen and evil without always being remote-controlled.

## Exact World Egg Placement

Winter Keep is a Bearshield hall-fort above the permafrost road. A Stone-Tooth chief demands the return of a ritual jaw-relic taken by the keep's former lord during a punitive raid. The present hall-speaker inherited both the keep and the concealed crime. A Bloody-Sun warband joins the attack for plunder, while a Grobi herd-matron seeks only safe passage for dependents before the deep freeze.

The supernatural pressure comes from the stolen jaw itself: it is changing the dead lord's memorial presence into a predatory hall-residue that rewards pride, feud, and refusal of restitution. The Grobi are not absolved; each band chooses what it does at the walls.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** hold Winter Keep through the first great storm while uncovering why three Grobi forces have converged.
- **Conflict:** defend families from genuine raiders, negotiate with non-allied enemies, and answer the keep's founding crime.
- **Antagonist:** the Bloody-Sun war-chief, the hall's lying former retainers, and the weaponized residue of the dead lord.
- **Consequences:** Bearshield honor is restored through costly truth, preserved through denial, or broken in massacre; the Stone-Tooth and herd-matron leave as enemies, temporary allies, or dead.
