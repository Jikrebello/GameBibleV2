# The Court That Drank Itself

**Source:** S1 *Tomb of Horrors* | **Tier:** 3 | **Region:** deep Great Glass Desert | **Band:** levels 10-14 analog

**The original:** a legendary death-trap tomb tests observation and restraint more than combat while protecting an ancient soul-remnant.

**The conversion:** an Ansharan court-magus built a tomb-palace that treats intruders as presumptuous petitioners. Entrances are consent gates, traps are forfeiture clauses, and false treasure is an admission of motive. The final remnant is a chamberlain enforcing protocol for a sovereign dead before the Exodus.

**The torque:** the tomb is not a fair challenge. It punishes entitlement and the assumption that ancient builders wished to be understood. Surviving expands knowledge of Giant-era legal magic; looting may restore the chamberlain's standing.

**Hook:** the adversarial tomb becomes a dead empire's last contemptuous audience.
