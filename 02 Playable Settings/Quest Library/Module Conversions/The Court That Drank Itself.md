# The Court That Drank Itself

**Source:** S1 *Tomb of Horrors* | **Tier:** 3 | **Region:** deep Great Glass Desert | **Band:** levels 10-14 analog | **Status:** fully blocked

**Completed translation:** [[The Court That Drank Itself — Full Adventure]]

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

The legendary wizard Acererak creates a hidden tomb filled with treasure, false routes, monsters, and lethal traps. The site is not designed as a fair burial or a challenge that wants to be solved. Its wealth and reputation lure accomplished adventurers into an adversarial construction intended to kill the proud, careless, and unlucky while protecting Acererak's final remnant.

The original adventure provides little conventional story beyond the tomb and its maker. Its drama comes from expert players examining physical detail, testing assumptions, conserving resources, and recognizing that apparent success may itself be another trap.

### Beginning

Explorers locate a forbidding burial mound with three possible entrances. Two approaches are false and dangerous. The true entrance opens into a long, decorated passage whose imagery and cryptic warning contain partial guidance about dangers deeper inside.

The opening establishes the module's core rules: observation matters more than force, visible routes can be bait, and information is useful without being complete or benevolent.

### Middle

The tomb presents deceptive doors, lethal corridors, pits, teleportation effects, a chapel, a black mouth that annihilates entrants, a many-armed guardian, and a massive stone juggernaut able to crush intruders. Routes loop, strip equipment, separate explorers, or make unsafe choices appear ceremonial and inviting.

A false crypt offers an apparent climactic encounter and can persuade survivors that Acererak has been defeated. Careful explorers can discover that the tomb continues beyond it into a laboratory and treasure chambers.

### End

Beyond the false conclusion lies treasure intended as another lure and the concealed true crypt. Acererak's demilich remnant is both the final guardian and the deadliest trap: extraordinarily difficult to harm, capable of destroying or consuming characters who treat it as an ordinary combat encounter.

Survival and treasure recovery depend upon recognizing the false ending, finding the hidden route, understanding the remnant's narrow vulnerabilities, and knowing when not to engage.

## What the Translation Preserves

- a legendary expert-level death-palace rather than a routine ruin;
- two false approaches and one hidden practical entrance;
- a decorated clue passage that warns without supplying a walkthrough;
- deceptive portals, rank corridors, an annihilation mouth, a many-armed guardian, and a crushing moving set piece;
- a convincing false sovereign, false crypt, and valuable treasure bait;
- a hidden lower works and true final chamber;
- a central horror that is almost unbeatable until its protections are dismantled;
- player success based on observation, experimentation, restraint, and return-route planning.

## What the Translation Replaces

- Acererak becomes [[Azravan the Last Cup]], Grand Chamberlain of Anshara and a surviving primordial Vampire Giant.
- the isolated tomb becomes the [[Palace of the Last Audience]], an Ansharan continuity site beneath [[Bitter Crown Basin]].
- treasure rumors become the disappearance of the [[Six-Lantern Expedition]], giving the party a rescue purpose beyond loot.
- false mound entrances become the Gate of Conquest, Gate of Tribute, and Servants' Dust Door.
- arbitrary tournament-era lethality becomes lethal but legible court machinery with visible signs and coherent functions.
- the many-armed gargoyle becomes a [[Many-Handed Tribute Wardens|Many-Handed Tribute Warden]].
- the stone juggernaut becomes the moving Throne Procession.
- the false lich and crypt become a counterfeit sovereign, ceremonial burial, and marked regalia.
- the demilich's narrow immunities become Azravan's five court-wide protections sustained by [[The Five Memory Cups]].

## Exact World Egg Placement

The public expedition gateway is [[Qasr Ilyun]] on Al-Qahur's Glass frontier. The secret palace lies eight days farther along the abandoned Black Salt Track in Bitter Crown Basin.

The palace was a supreme imperial continuity site, not Anshara's capital. Its evidence confirms the Drinking Wars and Azravan's massacre without identifying the Court of Courts or the last sovereign's fate.

The Qahuri and Jirahar are the modern people undertaking the rescue. They are not descendants cast as interchangeable desert locals, and neither culture exists merely to explain the ruin.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** follow a returned expedition lantern into an Ansharan death-palace and recover six people scattered through its lethal audience route.
- **Conflict:** the palace assigns rank and punishes presumption while its false ending tries to send capable intruders away carrying marked regalia.
- **Antagonist:** Azravan, who murdered the Last Court, preserved stolen blood-memories in five vessels, and falsely claims to embody imperial continuity.
- **Player work:** read signs, test mechanisms, rescue survivors, secure a return route, recognize the false crypt, neutralize five Cups, destroy Azravan, and choose what evidence survives.
- **Consequences:** the threat ends, burns with its witnesses, remains behind a false victory, escapes into the present, or waits for another audience.

## Translation Rulings

### Lethal but Legible

The translated palace can kill characters. Every lethal mechanism must first provide at least two sensory clues, serve a recognizable Ansharan function, and allow observation or experimentation to reveal behavior. A failed skill check alone never causes unavoidable death.

### Court Hierarchy, Not Contract Magic

Entrances, marks, gifts, titles, and punishments express Giant hierarchy through architecture and blood-responsive craft. They are not Covenant contracts and cannot be defeated through legal wording alone.

### Memory Is Not Soul

The Five Cups contain blood-borne impressions rather than whole imprisoned people. Selected testimony can be separated before the vessels are destroyed, but preserving history does not require preserving functional domination rites.

### Destruction Is the Only Lasting Victory

Azravan knowingly chose mass murder and remains committed to it. He can offer exact temporary bargains, but cannot be restored. Permanent victory requires destruction of all Five Cups and his body.

### Independent Ansharan Adventure

This adventure requires no clue, artifact, cast member, faction, or outcome from [[Entombed with the Court]]. It uses no rival expedition, Regalis patron, Sanguine claimant, office-seal collection, or folded paradise-garden.
