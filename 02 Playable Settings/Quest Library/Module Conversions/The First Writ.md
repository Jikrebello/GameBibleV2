# The First Writ

**Source:** *The Delian Tomb* | **Tier:** R->W tutorial | **Region:** any marcher district | **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** a compact five-room rescue teaches investigation, combat, traps, secrets, and a final confrontation through a kidnapped villager and old tomb.

**The conversion:** the party's first collection-writ concerns a hostage taken by road bandits into a barrow. The warrant authorizes rescue and recovery, not disturbance of the dead. Tracks, sentry, threshold, confrontation, and hidden testament teach the campaign's basic verbs.

**The torque:** behind the secret door is worked stone older than the barrow. The lesson is that professional delvers record dangerous boundaries and leave some doors closed. Hostage survival and lawful recovery are the only required wins.

**Hook:** a first session teaching both competence and archaeological restraint.
