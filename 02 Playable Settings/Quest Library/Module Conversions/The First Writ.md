# The First Writ

**Source:** *The Delian Tomb* | **Tier:** R tutorial | **Region:** Birchcross near Grenzburg | **Band:** level 1 analog

## Source Version

This conversion uses the compact *Running the Game* version of *The Delian Tomb*: a first-session rescue built around a short trail and five-room teaching dungeon. It does not translate the later, expanded Draw Steel adventure that now carries the same title.

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A long-forgotten knightly order lies in an old tomb now occupied by goblins and their stronger leader. In the familiar playable framing, the occupiers abduct a local blacksmith's daughter and carry her to the tomb. The site is deliberately small enough to teach a new group tracking, sentries, room entry, traps, clues, a leader fight, and optional exploration in one evening.

### Beginning

The heroes learn that the young woman has been taken and follow the raiders' trail into the nearby wild. A patrol outside the tomb can be fought, avoided, followed, captured, or allowed to return. What happens to that patrol changes the defenders' preparation later.

### Middle

Sentries and goblins occupy the entrance and offering hall. Carvings preserve the old order's martial history and oath. A pressure-triggered blade guards the passage to the leader's chamber, teaching players to observe and manipulate the environment rather than treating every hazard as a mandatory injury.

### End

The heroes confront the bugbear leader and rescue the captive. The old oath and a riddle reveal a hidden crypt beyond the apparent ending. Disturbing its graves awakens undead defenders and exposes an optional reward, but neither the secret room nor its danger is required to complete the rescue.

## What to Preserve

- A direct human reason to act before any reward or faction calculation.
- A trail whose patrol can return later if bypassed.
- A compact occupied memorial site with escalating rooms.
- A visible, comprehensible pressure trap with multiple solutions.
- A captive who can still be saved when the final confrontation begins badly.
- An oath clue that rewards attention to the location's history.
- A hidden fifth area whose danger and discovery remain optional.
- A complete adventure that fits one long session or two substantial sessions.

## What to Replace

- Generic goblins and a bugbear become eleven human deserters from Grenzburg's first autumn muster. No Grobi identity is used as tutorial shorthand for evil.
- The blacksmith's daughter becomes [[Klara Mettler]], a miller's apprentice who leaves deliberate clues and acts during her rescue.
- The generic village becomes [[Birchcross]], a named Chapel-Folk road hamlet within the [[Green March Kindreds]].
- The knightly tomb becomes [[First Shelter Watch-Barrow]], an Atherian relay reused by a mixed Chapel-Folk and Eclessian frontier fraternity after the Folk Wars.
- The Delian oath becomes [[The First-Shelter Writ]], a local rule that places the rescue and shelter of persons before claims over stores, rank, or border.
- The hidden undead are [[Corpse Residue]], not imprisoned souls. The deeper reward is knowledge of the [[Birchcross Imperial Stair]], not a generic magic weapon.
- Any collection or debt hook is removed. A local reader asks available people to save a neighbor.

## Exact World Egg Placement

[[Birchcross]] stands one day's travel west of Grenzburg on the road toward Lakewatch. It contains roughly 150 people in thirty-four households, most of them Chapel-Folk with Bent Oak kinship or neighboring marcher ties. The adventure begins during the first autumn muster of 1360 AR.

The [[The Nine-Mile Deserters|Nine-Mile Deserters]] raid Mettler Mill after leaving their levy column. Their wages were late, but all eleven knowingly choose robbery, murder, kidnapping, ransom, and the intended killing of their witness. [[Sergeant Volker Rass]] demands twenty sacks of meal, three pack mules, and smithing tools at moonrise. He plans to take the supplies, kill Klara, and escape north on an abandoned imperial spur.

[[Reader Signe Bent-Oak]] asks the party to act. She supplies road knowledge, the fraternity's old shelter token, and local recognition that the rescuers enter the Watch-Barrow for life rather than plunder. This recognition may prevent later accusations, but it is not the subject of play.

The barrow is built around a late-imperial relay whose first buried landing still contains an Atherian route mosaic, brass road tablet, and dormant road warden. A sealed bronze shutter blocks the deeper route. The stair is material infrastructure rather than a Shell anchor, Way-Gate, soul-route, or metaphysical threshold.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** rescue Klara before Rass collects Birchcross's supplies and kills the only witness who can identify his whole band.
- **Conflict:** every delay improves the deserters' position, but careful observation reveals safe approaches, the blade passage, Klara's condition, and the barrow's concealed history.
- **Antagonist:** Volker Rass and ten committed deserters who have chosen predation as their route out of military failure.
- **Escalation:** mill aftermath, redundant trail clues, returning patrol, three barrow approaches, fortified refuge hall, hostage confrontation, then an optional crypt and imperial stair.
- **Consequences:** Klara lives or dies; supplies are protected or lost; Rass is stopped or escapes; the barrow is respected or violated; and the imperial stair is recorded, disturbed, or resealed.
