# The First Writ

**Source:** *The Delian Tomb* | **Tier:** R->W tutorial | **Region:** Grenzburg | **Band:** level 1 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A small band of raiders has abducted a local and withdrawn into the tomb of an old knightly order. The adventure is deliberately compact: tracks, sentries, a guarded threshold, a simple trap, a leader, and an optional secret room.

### Beginning

The party learns of the abduction, follows the trail, and deals with a patrol outside the tomb. This teaches investigation and establishes that delay endangers the captive.

### Middle

The party enters the tomb, reads its martial imagery, survives its defenses, and fights through the raiders. The old order's oath supplies the clue to the hidden chamber.

### End

The captive is rescued during the confrontation with the raider chief. Curious or respectful characters can discover the sealed resting place beyond, where disturbing the dead creates an optional final danger.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The five-room tutorial structure, hostage clock, readable clues, and optional secret.
- A rescue that can succeed without clearing or looting the whole site.

### Replace

- The old collection-writ premise. The party needs no financial procedure to care about an abducted neighbor.
- Generic raiders with a small deserter band displaced by Grenzburg's winter mobilization.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The hostage is taken from a road hamlet outside Grenzburg to an old Atherian watch-barrow reused by an Eclessian marcher fraternity. Duke Alaric's reeve asks whoever is available to act before the deserters move on. A short written warrant protects the rescuers from being mistaken for tomb-robbers, but it is permission, not the adventure's subject.

The secret chamber contains a sealed Atherian stair dropping below the later barrow. It is cold, intact, and outside the mission. Opening it is not required for victory and creates the first durable campaign hook.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** rescue a kidnapped miller's daughter before a deserter captain trades her to a larger outlaw company.
- **Conflict:** speed favors rescue; caution reveals the site's hazards and history.
- **Antagonist:** a frightened, violent deserter captain trying to buy entry into a safer band.
- **Consequences:** the captive lives or dies; the deserters scatter or surrender; the buried Atherian stair is recorded, concealed, or opened.
