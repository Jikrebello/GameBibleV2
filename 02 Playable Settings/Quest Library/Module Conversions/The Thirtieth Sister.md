# The Thirtieth Sister

**Source:** U1 *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh* | **Tier:** W/F opener | **Region:** western Kedrava's Saltborn coast | **Band:** levels 1-3 analog

**The original:** a supposedly haunted coast-house conceals smugglers using staged effects, and their trail leads to a shipboard operation.

**The conversion:** wreckers use lights, chains, and planted residue to secure a coast-house. Their cargo vessel is one of the Thirty Sisters, missing from formation records and answering a bell nobody aboard rang. The smugglers know their haunting is fake and fear the genuine one traveling with them.

**The torque:** exposing smugglers is simple; deciding whether to sail, burn, or return the Sister is not. Cargo value, drowned deaths, customs corruption, and formation integrity move separately.

**Hook:** the fake haunting works because it accidentally hides a real maritime weird.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A supposedly haunted coastal house conceals smugglers whose operation leads to a ship offshore.

### Beginning

The party investigates staged hauntings and uncovers the mundane occupants.

### Middle

Evidence and prisoners reveal the signaling system, cargo route, and vessel.

### End

A shipboard confrontation breaks the smuggling ring and exposes its buyers.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the fake haunting, coastal investigation, smugglers, signal code, and ship finale.

### Replace

Replace a detached supernatural twist; the missing Sister's bell should complicate but not replace the smuggling plot.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** western Kedrava. **Cultures:** Saltborn, Thalmyric. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** expose a wrecking and smuggling operation aboard a vessel that may truly be the missing Thirtieth Sister.
- **Conflict:** the criminals' staged haunting conceals a genuine bell and formation anomaly they fear as much as their pursuers do.
- **Antagonist:** the smuggling ring and the unresolved maritime intelligence or residue traveling with the ship.
- **Consequences:** the ring is exposed or escapes, drowned deaths and customs corruption come to light or remain hidden, and the Sister is sailed, burned, returned, or left outside formation.
