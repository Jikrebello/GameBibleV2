# No Word from Claim Seventeen

**Source:** *No Response from Deepmar* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** the Shattered Isles / Emerald Expanse • **Band:** level 4 analog

**The original:** a penal mining colony on an isolated island goes silent; investigate.

**The conversion:** Claim Seventeen — a Merchant-Prince crystal-mining camp on a Shattered Isles atoll, crewed half by wage-men and half by passage-debtors — misses three supply calls. The relief ship finds the docks tidy, the ledgers current to a Tuesday, and no one. The mine's lowest gallery is worked stone. The veterans' rule (*work elsewhere*) was in the foreman's log, underlined, four weeks before the silence.

**The torque:** the University's charter requires the site surveyed, the Prince's insurers require it *not* be declared bird-marked (the payout language), and the debtors' families require bodies or manumission papers — three employers, one island, and the answer under the atoll doesn't map to any of their forms.

**Hook:** the Expanse frontier's signature dread: the Gold Rush digging into the Age of Chains.
