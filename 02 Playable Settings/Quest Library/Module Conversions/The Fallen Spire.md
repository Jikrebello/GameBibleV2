# The Fallen Spire

**Source:** S3 *Expedition to the Barrier Peaks* | **Tier:** 3 | **Region:** Ash-Horde frontier | **Band:** levels 8-12 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Unfamiliar monsters emerge from a remote mountain and threaten the surrounding country. The mountain is actually a stranded vessel whose crew is gone and whose decks preserve alien creatures, machines, and access systems.

### Beginning

Authorities send the party to stop the creatures. Tracks and attacks lead to a smooth, unnatural entrance where ordinary dungeon assumptions immediately fail.

### Middle

The party learns to use colored access cards and unfamiliar devices while moving through habitat, service, and command decks. Released specimens and malfunctioning machines make the vessel a changing environment.

### End

The deepest accessible decks reveal the vessel's nature and permit the party to contain its ecology, recover dangerous devices, or leave systems active for future expeditions.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The monster outbreak, unfamiliar access logic, exploratory decks, dangerous devices, and alien ecology.
- Players learning by observation rather than receiving an explanatory lecture.

### Replace

- A second recent Nythalum crash. [[The Star over Dustfeld]] already fills that role.
- Automatic Vortharan and Hollow-King involvement. The local Ash Horde and Castaran road expedition own the first response.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The Fallen Spire is an ancient Nythalum launch-support vessel buried nose-down in the escarpment above the Ash-Horde frontier. Dustfeld's falling probe transmits coordinates that wake it, opening sealed decks and releasing preserved lunar organisms into the grasslands.

The vessel arrived before the Exodus and has been inert ever since. Its access seals express crew function, not cargo ownership, and its custodians judge living entrants against incomplete Nythalum personnel patterns. Tarkhan sky-priests regard its opening as the answer to the Dustfeld omen; Castaran scholars arrive because they decoded the coordinates.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** stop an outbreak from a newly awakened ancient vessel and learn how to close it again.
- **Conflict:** exploration yields tools and proof while every opened deck releases further hazards.
- **Antagonist:** the Spire's damaged command intelligence and the expedition leader who intends to keep it awake.
- **Consequences:** the vessel is resealed, occupied, stripped, or becomes the center of a frontier war.

## Linked Adventure

[[The Star over Dustfeld]] can reveal the coordinates and first access seal, but the Fallen Spire also begins independently when its doors open and unknown beasts attack Ash-Horde camps.
