# The Fallen Spire

**Source:** S3 *Expedition to the Barrier Peaks* | **Tier:** 3 | **Region:** Grobi Wastes or Emerald Expanse | **Band:** levels 8-12 analog

**The original:** monsters emerging from a mountain lead explorers into a crashed spacecraft with access cards, machines, and alien ecology.

**The conversion:** a Nythalum return-vessel has struck the Material Realm. Its crew are gone, custodians are mind-wiped, and lunar specimens have entered local food chains. Access seals are cargo permissions, and machines attack according to manifest status rather than morality.

**The torque:** everything inside predates the Exodus but arrived recently. The University wants proof, Vorthara wants systems, and the Hollow-Kings want memory architecture. Every deck opened raises salvage and contamination.

**Hook:** science fantasy becomes physical evidence that Nythalum has sent something back.
