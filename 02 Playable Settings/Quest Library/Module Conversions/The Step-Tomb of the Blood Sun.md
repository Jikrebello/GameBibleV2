# The Step-Tomb of the Blood Sun

**Source:** C1 *The Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Emerald Expanse | **Band:** levels 5-7 analog

**The original:** explorers fall into the poisonous lower chambers of an ancient stepped shrine and climb through traps and guardians to escape.

**The conversion:** a Xolotl step-tomb collapses beneath a Castaran survey party. It is flooded, air-poor, and bird-marked on Viscaran charts as a site found unnaturally empty. The chambers retain offerings and mechanisms but almost none of the people or dead expected there.

**The torque:** escape remains immediate; the larger question is what swept the site. Removing objects raises expedition yield while degrading the sequence of evidence that the sweepers distinguished between memory, life, and ritual function.

**Hook:** archaeological survival becomes forensics for a civilization-scale disappearance.
