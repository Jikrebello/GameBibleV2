# The Step-Tomb of the Blood Sun

**Source:** C1 *The Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Emerald Expanse | **Band:** levels 5-7 analog

**The original:** explorers fall into the poisonous lower chambers of an ancient stepped shrine and climb through traps and guardians to escape.

**The conversion:** a Xolotl step-tomb collapses beneath a Castaran survey party. It is flooded, air-poor, and bird-marked on Viscaran charts as a site found unnaturally empty. The chambers retain offerings and mechanisms but almost none of the people or dead expected there.

**The torque:** escape remains immediate; the larger question is what swept the site. Removing objects raises expedition yield while degrading the sequence of evidence that the sweepers distinguished between memory, life, and ritual function.

**Hook:** archaeological survival becomes forensics for a civilization-scale disappearance.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Explorers fall into the poisonous lower levels of an ancient stepped shrine and must climb out.

### Beginning

A collapse cuts off the planned entrance and establishes immediate survival pressure.

### Middle

The party ascends through traps, guardians, failing air, and evidence of the vanished culture.

### End

They emerge through the upper sanctuary with whatever people and evidence survived.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the forced lower entry, upward escape, environmental clock, and archaeological clues.

### Replace

Replace generic Expanse mystery; define the Castaran expedition and preserve the Xolotl site's own religious logic.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** escape a collapsed, flooded Xolotl step-tomb while preserving evidence of why its people and dead vanished.
- **Conflict:** air and water force quick choices, while every removed offering raises expedition yield and destroys part of the sequence needed to understand the ancient sweep.
- **Antagonist:** the tomb's surviving mechanisms, unstable environment, and any expedition member who values portable treasure over rescue and evidence.
- **Consequences:** the survey party lives or dies, the site remains legible or is stripped, and the bird-mark warning gains proof, profit, or another unexplained silence.
