# The Celion Light

**Source:** *The Secrets of Skyhorn Lighthouse* | **Tier:** W coastal | **Region:** Halmenth and western Kedrava | **Band:** level 5 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Hostile sea-creatures seize a lighthouse as part of an assault on the coast. The keepers are killed or captured, the beacon fails, and shipping is endangered.

### Beginning

The party learns that the lighthouse has gone dark and reaches the isolated tower through dangerous coastal conditions. Evidence outside establishes that this is an attack, not an accident.

### Middle

The party climbs through occupied rooms, rescues survivors, restores mechanisms, and discovers the attackers' larger plan.

### End

The assault culminates at the beacon and sea-caves. Relighting the lamp and defeating the leader prevents immediate wrecks and warns the coast of the wider threat.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The urgent dark beacon, vertical assault, captive keepers, and impending coastal attack.
- A practical maritime problem ordinary people understand immediately.

### Replace

- Wreckers, harridans, drowned walkers, and soul-routing all in one tower. The combination obscures the clean siege premise.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The Celion Light marks Halmenth's western approach during Saint Naris's feast, when fishing craft and pilgrim ships crowd the bar. A disciplined raiding band from a hostile island captain takes the tower to darken the channel before his ships enter. Saltborn pilots, Thalmyric marines, and keeper families all have immediate stakes.

The supernatural element is limited to the lighthouse lens: an old sun-glass optic that magnifies flame unnaturally and can blind ships if reversed. The attackers intend to turn it seaward after drawing vessels into the wrong channel.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** retake the lighthouse before the feast convoy reaches the reef passage.
- **Conflict:** climb quickly enough to save ships while rescuing keepers and preventing the lens from being weaponized.
- **Antagonist:** a privateer captain's landing party, aided by one coerced local pilot.
- **Consequences:** shipping survives or founders; the pilot is rescued or condemned; Halmenth fortifies the coast or retaliates against the wrong island.
