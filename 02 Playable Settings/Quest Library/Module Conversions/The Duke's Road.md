# The Duke's Road

**Source:** W1 *Conquest of Bloodsworn Vale* • **Tier:** F (mini-ladder) • **Region:** Sylvan Marches / Grenzburg hinterland • **Band:** levels 5–7 analog

**The original:** open a trade route through a hostile valley — part wilderness clearing, part fort-building, part fey diplomacy.

**The conversion:** the Duke charters the party to open a winter-viable road through a Folk-claimed vale — bandits, residue-sites, a Grey-Withy toll dispute, and a Faewild-touched glen the road *must not cut through* (though the surveyor's line says otherwise). Success is measured in the Numbers doc: weeks of supply the new road adds.

**The torque:** every obstacle can be cleared, bought, or routed around, and the three methods build three different roads — the vale remembers which one you built, and so do the clans.

**Hook:** the quest where the map itself is the reward, and the political cost of the straight line is the lesson.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A ruler must reopen a trade route through a dangerous valley and recruits a capable company.

### Beginning

The party surveys the route, establishes a camp, and meets the valley's first claimants.

### Middle

Wilderness threats, settlers, rival interests, and local powers contest how the road is built.

### End

The completed or abandoned route permanently changes trade, settlement, and control of the valley.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the route-building campaign, persistent base, multiple obstacles, and consequential path choices.

### Replace

Replace Numbers-doc shorthand and a universal Folk claim; use named clans, grove rights, and Grenzburg's actual winter needs.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Grenzburg, Folk frontier. **Cultures:** Grenzburger, Folk. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** open a winter road through a Folk-claimed vale before Grenzburg's supplies fail.
- **Conflict:** bandits, old residue, a toll dispute, and a Faewild-touched glen can be fought, bought off, or avoided, but each method creates a different road and political future.
- **Antagonist:** the leaders exploiting the closed route and the survey interests insisting on a line through land that cannot safely bear it.
- **Consequences:** Grenzburg gains or loses winter supply, Folk relations harden or improve, and the finished road becomes a shared corridor, an occupied cut, or a recurring hazard.
