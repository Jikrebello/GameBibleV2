# The Duke's Road

**Source:** W1 *Conquest of Bloodsworn Vale* • **Tier:** F (mini-ladder) • **Region:** Sylvan Marches / Grenzburg hinterland • **Band:** levels 5–7 analog

**The original:** open a trade route through a hostile valley — part wilderness clearing, part fort-building, part fey diplomacy.

**The conversion:** the Duke charters the party to open a winter-viable road through a Folk-claimed vale — bandits, residue-sites, a Grey-Withy toll dispute, and a Faewild-touched glen the road *must not cut through* (though the surveyor's line says otherwise). Success is measured in the Numbers doc: weeks of supply the new road adds.

**The torque:** every obstacle can be cleared, bought, or routed around, and the three methods build three different roads — the vale remembers which one you built, and so do the clans.

**Hook:** the quest where the map itself is the reward, and the political cost of the straight line is the lesson.
