# The Fane of the Braided Flame

**Source:** T1-4 *The Temple of Elemental Evil* | **Tier:** W campaign with Tier 3 capstone | **Region:** Velian border | **Band:** levels 1-10 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

An elemental cult once threatened the surrounding country and was defeated. Years later, agents return to nearby settlements while rival elemental factions reoccupy the ruined temple and compete for control of the imprisoned power beneath it.

### Beginning

The party arrives in a seemingly peaceful village shadowed by the old war. Banditry and suspicious newcomers lead to a fortified outpost whose occupants are testing whether the cult can return openly.

### Middle

Evidence points from the outpost to the ruined temple. The party navigates several rival factions, prisoners, defectors, and levels of the complex, learning that the factions weaken one another as often as they cooperate.

### End

The deepest delves reveal the power behind the revival. The party can exploit the factional struggle, seal the elemental nodes, or allow one faction to dominate before the final confrontation.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The village investigation, ruined outpost, factional megadungeon, and escalating return of a defeated evil.
- Four elemental traditions that can be played against one another.

### Replace

- The stacked Left Hand conspiracy. It makes several exceptional evils appear everywhere at once and obscures the adventure's elemental identity.
- Abstract supply-chain language with concrete Velian history, warlock practice, and local collaborators.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The Fane stands on the Velian border where a pre-imperial Shell vent was formalized into four runic choirs: ember, flood, stone, and gale. Atherian war colleges later used it to train battle-scripters. During the wars after the Fall, a magister attempted to braid all four scripts into a single obedient weapon. The result burned villages, changed a river's course, and buried the complex.

Now four descendant circles have returned. Each believes the disaster came from another choir's corruption and that only its own script can safely command the Fane. Their crimes are chosen, not merely technical errors: abduction, sacrifice, coerced testing, and attacks on Chapel-Folk settlements.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** investigate raids around Chapel-Ford and the Commandery, then prevent the four circles from restoring the Braided Script.
- **Conflict:** rival warlock schools possess pieces of a weapon none can safely control.
- **Antagonist:** four circle-masters and the imprinted battle-intelligence formed by the original failed braid.
- **Consequences:** sealing, purifying, or claiming each node changes weather, roads, and military power along the Velian border.
