# The Fane of the Braided Flame

**Source:** T1-4 *The Temple of Elemental Evil* | **Tier:** W campaign with Tier 3 capstone | **Region:** old Shell-leak corridor | **Band:** levels 1-10 analog

**The original:** four elemental cults rebuild inside a defeated temple, competing while deeper powers use their rivalry to restore the complex.

**The conversion:** ember, flood, stone, and gale cults occupy four nodes of a ruined Shell-working. Above them the Weeping Courts recruit; the Under-Ledger finances excavation; beneath them a patron anchor converts sacrifice and spell failure into Astral rent.

**The torque:** no faction understands the complete architecture. Victory comes from breaking supply-chain interdependence rather than clearing four armies. Closing a node changes local weather, warlock access, cult recruitment, and anchor stability.

**Hook:** the archetypal megatemple becomes the Left Hand Atlas made playable.
