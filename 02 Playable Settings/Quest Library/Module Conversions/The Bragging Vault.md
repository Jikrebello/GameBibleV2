# The Bragging Vault

**Source:** S2 *White Plume Mountain* | **Tier:** F/W | **Region:** Vortharan geothermal district | **Band:** levels 5-8 analog

**The original:** a boastful wizard steals three celebrated weapons and invites recovery teams into a bizarre three-branch volcanic gauntlet.

**The conversion:** a rogue magus steals three pit-champion weapons and issues a public challenge. One arm consumes victory, one binds crowds to its wielder, and one remembers defeated owners. The vault is an audition for those able to retrieve them without becoming their next advertisement.

**The torque:** completion is a job interview. The magus offers a new arena partnership while the Pale Hand reveals it holds the mountain's mortgage and title to anything dying below. Custody, reputation, and debt move independently.

**Hook:** the funhouse dungeon becomes a spectacularly unethical recruitment exercise.
