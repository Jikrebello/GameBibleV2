# The Gallows Court

**Source:** U2 *Hangman's Noose* • **Tier:** W one-shot (horror) • **Region:** Brauvalis or Grenzburg • **Band:** level 3 analog

**The original:** the anniversary of a wrongful hanging traps the descendants of the responsible parties in the courthouse with the vengeful dead.

**The conversion:** thirty years after a rushed treason trial hanged an innocent man on feast-day evidence, the courthouse seals itself at the year's mind with the jurors' heirs inside — and the residue holds *court*: the trial replays, and the haunting's rules are procedural (objections work; perjury feeds it). The keener at the bench is not the hanged man — per residue canon, he routed long ago — it is the town's own thirty-year guilt, wearing his shape.

**The torque:** the haunting can be fought (sexton-work, hard) or *satisfied* — retry the case honestly, with the descendants as witnesses, and let the verdict land where it lands. The second option acquits the dead and convicts a living name.

**Hook:** soul-routing doctrine as courtroom horror: the ghost isn't him, and somehow that's worse.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A wrongful execution leaves a courthouse haunted on the anniversary of the crime.

### Beginning

The responsible families or their heirs become trapped when the haunting seals the building.

### Middle

The dead replay testimony and punishment while the party reconstructs the failed trial.

### End

The haunting ends through truthful judgment, spiritual release, or violent suppression.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the closed courthouse, anniversary clock, reconstructed case, and moral verdict.

### Replace

Replace procedure as a clever gimmick; the Brauvalian confessional wound and human perjury should drive the haunting.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** survive a courthouse haunting by retrying the thirty-year-old treason case that created it.
- **Conflict:** force can suppress the residue, but only honest procedure acquits the innocent dead and identifies the living beneficiary of the false verdict.
- **Antagonist:** the town's accumulated guilt wearing the victim's shape, strengthened by perjury and by those who still profit from the conviction.
- **Consequences:** the dead man's name is cleared or further stained, a living family is exposed or protected, and the courthouse becomes usable, condemned, or annually dangerous.
