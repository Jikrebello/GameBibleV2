# The Gilded City of Avarix

**Source:** *City of Golden Death* | **Tier:** W->3 | **Region:** an isolated lake-isle or desert basin | **Band:** level 5 analog

**The original:** heroes cross a cursed island and race rival cultists through a ruined treasure-city before its defenses drown everything in molten gold.

**The conversion:** masked adherents of Avarix open a necropolis built around a pre-Conversion treasury rite. The streets are plated, the dead are appraisers, and each district assigns a price to those entering it. The cult intends to restart the central mint and issue coin whose ownership returns spiritually to Avarix whenever it changes hands.

**The torque:** the golden flood is not merely a death trap; it is the city's final audit, liquefying every unregistered object and person into one hoard. The Under-Ledger wants samples, the Church wants the mint unmade, and every expedition sponsor insists the ordinary treasure remain recoverable.

**Hook:** a treasure race through a city where taking possession is how the dungeon notices you.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Rival expeditions race across a cursed island to reach a ruined golden city and seize its central treasure.

### Beginning

The party secures passage and enters the deadly isolation zone ahead of or behind its rivals.

### Middle

They cross dangerous districts while traps, cultists, and the environment reduce every expedition.

### End

The central defense activates, forcing a final struggle and escape before the city destroys intruders.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the expedition race, cursed city, rival team, treasure temptation, and catastrophic escape.

### Replace

Replace an unspecified lake-isle; place the city on Al-Qahur's Glass frontier and root its wealth in Avarix's pre-Covenant cult.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Al-Qahur Glass frontier. **Cultures:** Qahuri, Covenant. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** enter an opened necropolis and stop Avarix's masked cult from restarting a mint that spiritually reclaims every coin it issues.
- **Conflict:** the city's defenses price intruders and liquefy the unregistered, while expedition sponsors demand that ordinary treasure remain recoverable.
- **Antagonist:** the Avarix cult and the dead appraisers operating the central mint's predatory economy.
- **Consequences:** the mint is destroyed, contained, or restarted; the cult is broken or enriched; and any recovered coin may carry the city's claim into the wider world.
