# The Warren Under the Mission

**Source:** D1 *Crown of the Kobold King* + D1.5 *Revenge* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** Grenzburg / Sylvan Marches • **Band:** levels 2–5 analog

**The original:** kobolds under a ruined monastery abduct the town's children; their "king" plays at royalty; a sequel brings his avenger.

**The conversion:** goblins of a starving splinter-band have laired in the undercroft of a burned Elsin-tradition mission and taken Chapel-Folk children — not for the pot (yet) but because their **crowned goblin**, wearing a dead prior's circlet, has decided he is a king and kings hold hostages. The children's parents are Chapel-Folk: the Duke's law, the Church's fury, and the clans' contempt all collide over whose rescue this is.

**The torque:** goblin "kingship" is metamorphic dread — the crowned goblin is *eating like a king*, and the Grobi ladder means the longer the rescue takes, the bigger he gets. The sequel writes itself: whatever survives the rescue remembers it.

**Hook:** Mother Averil's fence-law is invoked by one desperate parent — feed them at the fence — and it *almost* works.
