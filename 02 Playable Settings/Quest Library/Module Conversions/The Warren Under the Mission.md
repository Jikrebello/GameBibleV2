# The Warren Under the Mission

**Source:** D1 *Crown of the Kobold King* + D1.5 *Revenge* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** Grenzburg / Sylvan Marches • **Band:** levels 2–5 analog

**The original:** kobolds under a ruined monastery abduct the town's children; their "king" plays at royalty; a sequel brings his avenger.

**The conversion:** goblins of a starving splinter-band have laired in the undercroft of a burned Elsin-tradition mission and taken Chapel-Folk children — not for the pot (yet) but because their **crowned goblin**, wearing a dead prior's circlet, has decided he is a king and kings hold hostages. The children's parents are Chapel-Folk: the Duke's law, the Church's fury, and the clans' contempt all collide over whose rescue this is.

**The torque:** goblin "kingship" is metamorphic dread — the crowned goblin is *eating like a king*, and the Grobi ladder means the longer the rescue takes, the bigger he gets. The sequel writes itself: whatever survives the rescue remembers it.

**Hook:** Mother Averil's fence-law is invoked by one desperate parent — feed them at the fence — and it *almost* works.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Subterranean raiders abduct children and carry them beneath a ruined monastery; survivors later seek revenge.

### Beginning

The party follows the abductors through forest and burned mission grounds.

### Middle

They descend through a dynamic warren, locate captives, and meet the self-crowned chief.

### End

The rescue ends with the chief's fall or escape, creating the sequel's revenge threat.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the child rescue, ruined sacred site, living warren, crowned leader, and returning consequences.

### Replace

Replace kobold logic copied onto Grobi; use a specific starving splinter-band and make metamorphic growth follow established Grobi canon.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Grenzburg, Folk frontier. **Cultures:** Chapel-Folk, Grobi. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** rescue Chapel-Folk children from a Grobi splinter-band occupying a burned mission before its crowned chief grows into a more dangerous form.
- **Conflict:** Duke, Church, and Folk authorities dispute whose rescue it is while every delay strengthens the chief and changes what his starving band will do with the hostages.
- **Antagonist:** the crowned Grobi chief who chose hostage-taking and predation as the basis of kingship.
- **Consequences:** the children live or die, the splinter-band scatters, submits, or returns for revenge, and the rescuers' methods shape local Grobi and Chapel-Folk relations.
