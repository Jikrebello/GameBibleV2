# The Bailiff's Ram

**Source:** *A Wild Sheep Chase* | **Tier:** comic R | **Region:** road near Vorthara | **Band:** levels 4-5 analog

**The original:** a transformed animal asks the party to recover a powerful wand from a bitter apprentice and his transformed guards.

**The conversion:** a ram interrupts lunch carrying a notarized speech-scroll. He is a Vorthara washout whose tuition bond allowed the school to polymorph delinquent collateral. The recovery bailiff has transformed other debtors into stronger collection agents.

**The torque:** restoration requires theft, repayment, proof of unconscionable terms, or turning the clause against its drafter. Each solution changes who owes tuition, who retains a body, and whether the bailiff keeps his license.

**Hook:** a talking ram introduces predatory magical finance without an apocalypse.
