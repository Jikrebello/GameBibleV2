# Into the Old Wood

**Source:** TC1 *Into the Haunted Forest* • **Tier:** R starter • **Region:** Sylvan Marches / any Folk frontier • **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** retrieve three treasures from a forest guarded by its ancient warden-spirits.

**The conversion:** a Marches lordling's inheritance requires three heirlooms his grandfather "left" in the Old Wood — which is to say, forfeited to the wood under grove-law for a broken oath. The Folk won't stop the party; the wood's own residue and the terms of the old forfeit will.

**The torque:** each heirloom can be *taken* (fight the wood) or *redeemed* (learn what the grandfather broke and pay it) — the same dungeon, two moral prices, and the Folk watching which one the settled people choose.

**Hook:** hearth-law taught by dungeon design.
