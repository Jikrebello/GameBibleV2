# Into the Old Wood

**Source:** TC1 *Into the Haunted Forest* | **Tier:** R starter | **Region:** [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/60 Realms and Peoples/Sylvan Marches/The Sylvan Marches Overview.md|Sylvan Marches]] | **Band:** level 1 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Narven, the last forest king of Arthfell, possessed five elemental relics known as the **Panoply of Narven**: the Breastplate of Sacred Fire, Wand of Earth's Ire, Codex of the Firmament, Vial of Pure Water, and Spirit-Staff of Narven.

The druid Willowroot recovered the Panoply and returned its pieces to sites matching their elemental natures. He marked their locations through riddles carved at his grove. Goblins later attacked Willowroot and his fey allies. He summoned a Wooden Protector, but the defenders were killed and the relics remained scattered. A goblin survivor eventually returned with a new adventuring company to recover them.

### Beginning: Dagger, Brawl, and Imposed Debt

1. The party travels through Arthfell Forest without a defined initial purpose and finds a murdered hobgoblin being eaten by wild dogs.
2. A hidden magical dagger named **Brightflame** lies near the body. Finding it is necessary for the intended plot but depends on a search check.
3. At the Travelers Stop Inn, the **Company of the Black Banner** recognizes Brightflame and claims it. The Black Banners killed the hobgoblin but lost the dagger during the encounter.
4. A tavern brawl follows. A knocked-over lantern destroys Professor Krane's supposedly priceless funerary shroud.
5. Sheriff Blunnde arrests or confines both groups. During the night, a gnome sorcerer steals Brightflame using his owl familiar. The party can clear itself of that theft by finding the owl and hidden dagger.
6. Krane and the sheriff nevertheless hold the party partly responsible for the destroyed shroud. To avoid decades of debt or arrest, the party must recover all five pieces of Narven's Panoply. The sheriff gives the same offer and an hour's lead to the Black Banners.

### Middle: Willowroot's Riddles and the Relic Hunt

1. The party follows Krane's directions to Willowroot's abandoned grove. An assassin vine guards standing stones carved with riddles describing the relic sites.
2. The Breastplate of Sacred Fire is hidden at the grove itself and can be recovered after dealing with the vine.
3. The other relics lie at separate forest encounters. One is held in a bear's cave, one requires bargaining with a nixie, and one rests high in a great tree guarded by a giant bombardier beetle.
4. The party can visit the sites in flexible order, but the race discourages rest and exposes inexperienced characters to several dangerous encounters in quick succession.
5. The Black Banners remain ahead or nearby, interfere with the hunt, and may leave traps or allow the party to absorb the guardians' attacks.

### End: The Spirit of the Wood

1. The Spirit-Staff lies at the abandoned Spirit of the Wood Inn in the forest's center.
2. A Wooden Protector created during Willowroot's final defense guards the site and attacks those seeking the staff.
3. The Black Banners wait for the party to engage the guardian and then enter the fight to seize the recovered Panoply.
4. The goblin member of the rival group betrays both sides and tries to take all five relics for himself, creating a battle among the party, the Black Banners, the goblin, and the Wooden Protector.
5. If the party returns with the relics, the sheriff clears its debt and releases Brightflame. The forest and the fate of its guardians receive little further resolution.

## Conversion Diagnosis

### Preserve

- A starter adventure built around learning one bounded wilderness.
- A roadside warning, a memorable inn, and a rival adventuring crew.
- Riddles that turn the forest into a location-based hunt.
- Relics placed according to the history and nature of their sites.
- Different kinds of encounters: animal behavior, negotiation, traversal, and a final guardian.
- Rivals who can race, interfere, betray one another, or be rescued.
- A final confrontation at an abandoned inn where settlement once tried to manage the wood.

### Replace

- The original depends on the party finding one hidden dagger, accepting an inevitable brawl, being blamed for another person's loss, and submitting to a coercive debt. The translated hook should be voluntary and intelligible.
- Five weak elemental objects produce repetition without deepening the story. The three heirlooms in the conversion seed can each embody one specific broken promise.
- Willowroot's decision to scatter the relics is not sufficiently explained. The Old Wood should hold them because a witnessed forfeit transferred them under pact-law.
- Most guardians exist to be fought despite supposedly protecting a legitimate restoration. Each site needs a peaceful, costly solution and a violent, expedient one.
- The rival company behaves badly because the scenario requires a race. Its members need a patron, motives, and limits.
- The final Wooden Protector is visually strong but morally empty. Its World Egg counterpart should preserve the person and wrong that created it.
- Recovery should not automatically mean justice. The heir must decide whether to repair the compact or merely regain the symbols of authority.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**The conversion:** [[Lord Lucan Harrowfen]] cannot be acclaimed as a Marcher lord without the sword, cup, and seal of his house. His grandfather said the heirlooms were lost in the Old Wood. In truth, the wood took them as forfeit after he closed a shared road, dammed free water, and killed a Folk witness who entered his lodge under hearth-law.

**The torque:** each heirloom can be seized by defeating its guardian or redeemed by repairing the breach it remembers. Lucan's cousin has sent [[The Briar Lances]] to claim one relic and invalidate his inheritance. Folk witnesses will not stop either crew. They are watching whether settled authority can keep a promise once keeping it becomes expensive.

**Hook:** a forest treasure hunt in which every shortcut teaches the player what the previous lord did wrong.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** recover the sword, cup, and seal Lord Lucan needs for acclamation from an Old Wood that holds them as payment for his grandfather's crimes.
- **Conflict:** each heirloom can be taken by force or redeemed by repairing the specific broken promise its guardian remembers, while a rival recovery crew closes in.
- **Antagonist:** the Briar Lances and the violent residue of Harrowfen's inherited breach; the wood itself is an exacting witness, not an evil mastermind.
- **Consequences:** Lucan is acclaimed, disqualified, or forced into restitution, and the marcher court either proves or breaks its ability to keep faith with Folk law.
