# The Saint of the Morning Quarter

**Source:** *Dawn of the Scarlet Sun* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** any Latinate city (Aurelium / Veridancia) • **Band:** level 5 analog

**The original:** murders in a temple district trace to a false cult and the seductive fiend behind it.

**The conversion:** a district's beloved new healing-shrine works — attendance surges, the parish empties — and then devotees start dying beautifully. The "saint" is a **Devoted Adored** run by a Beloved handler-cell, and the murders are the coldness-doses escalating. The relic courts smell Bruckmoor and move slow; the killer moves fast.

**The torque:** per Devoted canon, the victims defend the shrine — the party's real opponent is four hundred sincere witnesses, and the discernment-tradition's test (*jealousy in the doctrine*) is the actual investigation mechanic.

**Hook:** Prelate-tier clergy quietly hire outsiders because any Church raid becomes a massacre of the faithful.
