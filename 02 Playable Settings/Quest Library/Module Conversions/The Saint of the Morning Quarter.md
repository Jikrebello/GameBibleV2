# The Saint of the Morning Quarter

**Source:** *Dawn of the Scarlet Sun* • **Tier:** W • **Region:** any Latinate city (Aurelium / Veridancia) • **Band:** level 5 analog

**The original:** murders in a temple district trace to a false cult and the seductive fiend behind it.

**The conversion:** a district's beloved new healing-shrine works — attendance surges, the parish empties — and then devotees start dying beautifully. The "saint" is a **Devoted Adored** run by a Beloved handler-cell, and the murders are the coldness-doses escalating. The relic courts smell Bruckmoor and move slow; the killer moves fast.

**The torque:** per Devoted canon, the victims defend the shrine — the party's real opponent is four hundred sincere witnesses, and the discernment-tradition's test (*jealousy in the doctrine*) is the actual investigation mechanic.

**Hook:** Prelate-tier clergy quietly hire outsiders because any Church raid becomes a massacre of the faithful.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A murderer targets a city district, leaving ritual signs that point toward a hidden cult and imminent sacrifice.

### Beginning

The party investigates victims and scenes while public fear rises.

### Middle

Witnesses, chases, and cult sites reveal the killer's pattern and next target.

### End

The party reaches the final rite in time to save the intended victim and expose the cult.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the urban investigation, escalating murders, time pressure, and rescue climax.

### Replace

Replace a generic city saint; Aurelium's neighborhood devotion, parish rivalry, and real sainthood claims must control the case.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** investigate beautiful deaths among devotees of a new healing shrine before the killer escalates its next coldness-dose.
- **Conflict:** hundreds of sincere beneficiaries defend the shrine, and the genuine relief they received makes ordinary accusation ineffective.
- **Antagonist:** a Devoted Adored directed by a Beloved handler-cell that converts dependence, jealousy, and staged sanctity into murder.
- **Consequences:** future victims live or die, the handler-cell is exposed or relocated, and the district's faith is purified, shattered, or captured by another false object of devotion.
