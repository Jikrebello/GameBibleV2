# The Drowned Bell of Soligrad

**Source:** *From Shore to Sea* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Soligrad, western Kedrava's Saltborn coast | **Band:** level 6-8 analog

**The original:** strange changes in an isolated fishing village lead to an offshore island, a drowned ancient complex, and a captive sea intelligence manipulating generations of villagers.

**The conversion:** Soligrad's children are increasingly born able to hear the formation-bells of the Thirty Sisters beneath ordinary surf. The trail leads to a tidal island holding a drowned Atherian observatory and an imprisoned Undertow pilot that has bred sympathetic listeners into the village for generations. It needs those listeners to complete a channel through the old machinery and escape.

**The torque:** the villagers are not impostors and cannot be cured into different ancestry. They are Saltborn families whose inherited adaptation has been deliberately cultivated. Killing the pilot ends the immediate call but may strand souls already moving through its channel; freeing it turns the coast into an Undertow harbor.

**Hook:** coastal exploration where the village's secret is lineage, not guilt.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Changes among an isolated fishing people lead to an offshore ruin and a trapped intelligence manipulating the community.

### Beginning

The party investigates disappearances, altered behavior, and strange coastal signs in the village.

### Middle

A voyage to the island exposes ruins, aquatic threats, and the history of the manipulation.

### End

The party reaches the imprisoned intelligence and chooses whether to destroy, contain, or release it.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the village mystery, sea journey, submerged site, and morally consequential captive.

### Replace

Replace generic Undertow machinery; Saltborn lineage and the Thirty Sisters must remain central and culturally specific.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** follow the formation-bells heard by Soligrad's children to a drowned Atherian observatory and the intelligence imprisoned beneath it.
- **Conflict:** stopping the call may strand souls already moving through its channel, while freeing the prisoner makes the Saltborn coast an Undertow harbor.
- **Antagonist:** the captive Undertow pilot that cultivated generations of listeners as the living mechanism of its escape.
- **Consequences:** the children retain or lose the call, the shoreline remains closed or becomes a passage, and Soligrad must face the deliberate origin of an inherited adaptation.
