# The Drowned Bell of Soligrad

**Source:** *From Shore to Sea* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Soligrad, western Kedrava's Saltborn coast | **Band:** level 6-8 analog

**The original:** strange changes in an isolated fishing village lead to an offshore island, a drowned ancient complex, and a captive sea intelligence manipulating generations of villagers.

**The conversion:** Soligrad's children are increasingly born able to hear the formation-bells of the Thirty Sisters beneath ordinary surf. The trail leads to a tidal island holding a drowned Atherian observatory and an imprisoned Undertow pilot that has bred sympathetic listeners into the village for generations. It needs those listeners to complete a channel through the old machinery and escape.

**The torque:** the villagers are not impostors and cannot be cured into different ancestry. They are Saltborn families whose inherited adaptation has been deliberately cultivated. Killing the pilot ends the immediate call but may strand souls already moving through its channel; freeing it turns the coast into an Undertow harbor.

**Hook:** coastal exploration where the village's secret is lineage, not guilt.
