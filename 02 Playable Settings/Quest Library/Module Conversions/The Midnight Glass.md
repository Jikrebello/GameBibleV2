# The Midnight Glass

**Source:** *The Midnight Mirror* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** a Brauvalis manor-town or Vortharan client estate | **Band:** level 4 analog

**The original:** disease, disappearances, and communal tension lead investigators through a noble manor into a prison demiplane hidden inside a mirror.

**The conversion:** servants vanish from a manor district while a wasting chill spreads through households that have never met. The lord's family keeps a black mirror used by an ancestor to imprison Shadowlands refugees after a failed revolt. The present heir made a private exchange with the prisoners' leader, gained political advantage from it, and now refuses the payment date.

**The torque:** the mirror-prison has held victims and predators together so long that neither category remains simple, but the original imprisonment was still a crime and the present bargain is still binding. Breaking the glass releases everything; honoring it exchanges one guilty man for a claimant who has spent centuries learning revenge.

**Hook:** a manor mystery whose locked room contains a small country of accumulated grievance.
