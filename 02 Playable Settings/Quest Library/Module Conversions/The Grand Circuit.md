# The Grand Circuit

**Source:** *The Ruby Phoenix Tournament* | **Tier:** F/W | **Region:** Vorthara arena circuit | **Band:** level 11 analog

**The original:** a famous team tournament mixes spectacular bouts and side challenges while a conspiracy develops behind the competition.

**The conversion:** once each decade, Vorthara's arena schools host the Grand Circuit: team bouts, pursuit courses, monster trials, and public demonstrations whose results set mercenary rates across the known world. The prize is first choice from a sealed vault of surrendered champion-arms, each carrying a liability history.

**The torque:** performance is contract negotiation. Crowd favor changes sponsorship, defeated rivals can become final allies, and every school is advertising a military product. Behind the contests, the Pale Hand intends to use the massed oaths of competitors and spectators to transfer ownership of one champion's future victories.

**Hook:** the world's greatest sporting event is also its largest live weapons market.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A famous international tournament draws elite teams and conceals a larger threat behind the competition.

### Beginning

The party qualifies, meets rivals, and learns the arena's customs and stakes.

### Middle

Successive matches build relationships while intrigue outside the ring points to sabotage or exploitation.

### End

The final bouts and hidden crisis determine both the champion and the tournament's future.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** compete in Vorthara's decennial arena circuit, where victories set mercenary rates and determine first choice from a sealed champion-arm vault.
- **Conflict:** every bout also negotiates sponsorship and reputation while a hidden patron uses the massed oaths of competitors and spectators to claim future victories.
- **Antagonist:** the Pale Hand agent engineering the oath-transfer, supported by any school officials willing to ignore the danger for prestige.
- **Consequences:** champions, sponsors, and surrendered arms change hands; the transfer succeeds or fails; and the party leaves as celebrated professionals, owned talent, or enemies of the circuit.
