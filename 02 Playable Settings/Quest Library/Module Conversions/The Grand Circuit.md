# The Grand Circuit

**Source:** *The Ruby Phoenix Tournament* | **Tier:** F/W | **Region:** Vorthara arena circuit | **Band:** level 11 analog

**The original:** a famous team tournament mixes spectacular bouts and side challenges while a conspiracy develops behind the competition.

**The conversion:** once each decade, Vorthara's arena schools host the Grand Circuit: team bouts, pursuit courses, monster trials, and public demonstrations whose results set mercenary rates across the known world. The prize is first choice from a sealed vault of surrendered champion-arms, each carrying a liability history.

**The torque:** performance is contract negotiation. Crowd favor changes sponsorship, defeated rivals can become final allies, and every school is advertising a military product. Behind the contests, the Pale Hand intends to use the massed oaths of competitors and spectators to transfer ownership of one champion's future victories.

**Hook:** the world's greatest sporting event is also its largest live weapons market.
