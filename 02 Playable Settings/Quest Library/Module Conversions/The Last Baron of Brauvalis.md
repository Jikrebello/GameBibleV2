# The Last Baron of Brauvalis

**Source:** LB1 *Tower of the Last Baron* | **Tier:** F infiltration | **Region:** Brauvalis | **Band:** level 5 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A strategically placed baron abandons neutrality and prepares to deliver his fortified town to an enemy power. A friendly army approaches, but open battle would kill the town's people and destroy the asset both sides want.

### Beginning

The party enters the mobilizing town before it closes, surveys its factions, and builds contacts among dissidents, soldiers, merchants, and officials.

### Middle

The party gathers intelligence and chooses an infiltration route into the keep. Patrols, schedules, divided loyalties, and the approaching army create a flexible stealth problem.

### End

The party confronts, captures, persuades, or kills the baron before the transfer is completed. The result determines whether the town surrenders, resists, or falls into battle.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The living town sandbox, timetable, infiltration, conflicted population, and imminent siege.
- A ruler with understandable pressures who still chooses treachery and coercion.

### Replace

- Vague "foreign-backed claimant" language with a named confessional and political settlement rooted in the Korrheim Accord.
- Accounting as the emotional ending. Food and solvency matter, but the climax is sovereignty and the prevention of war.

## Exact World Egg Placement

Baron Othric Vale has agreed to yield the river-fort of Harthwatch to a hardline confessional league that promises debt relief, troops, and hereditary recognition. The transfer violates the Korrheim settlement and would let the league close a major Brauvalian crossing. Othric's people have real grievances against distant dukes, but he answers them by silencing dissent and inviting a war onto their roofs.

Evidence in Harthwatch points onward to a privateer cache in [[The Chimera Cache]]. Either adventure remains usable alone; surviving agents, letters, and missing arms provide the bridge.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** enter Harthwatch and prevent its transfer before the relief army reaches the walls.
- **Conflict:** stop the baron without turning the town into a battlefield or treating every loyal soldier as an enemy.
- **Antagonist:** Baron Othric and the foreign military mission already inside his keep.
- **Consequences:** Harthwatch returns to ducal authority, wins negotiated autonomy, joins the league, or is shattered in a siege.
