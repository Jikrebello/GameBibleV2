# The Last Baron of Brauvalis

**Source:** LB1 *Tower of the Last Baron* | **Tier:** F | **Region:** Brauvalis | **Band:** level 5 analog

**The original:** infiltrate a fortified border town, build local contacts, and prevent its isolated baron from delivering a strategic advantage to an enemy power.

**The conversion:** an Accord baron plans to flip his river-fort, toll chain, and confession register to a foreign-backed claimant before either Eclessian bloc can react openly. The party enters during mobilization and must learn whether to blackmail, depose, abduct, or persuade him while soldiers, dissidents, clergy, and smugglers pursue incompatible futures for the town.

**The torque:** the baron is harsh but not a cartoon traitor: his demesne is bankrupt, both confessions have used it as a buffer, and the offered privateer money will feed it. Stopping the transfer without replacing that money leaves the population loyal to whoever pays next.

**Hook:** espionage where regime change is the beginning of the accounting.
