# The Horror Beneath Blackspire

**Source:** *Carrion Hill* | **Tier:** W urban horror | **Region:** Castara | **Band:** level 5 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A hidden cult summons a destructive otherworldly creature beneath a crowded city. The rite goes wrong, the creature escapes, and the surviving cultists scatter. Their continued lives sustain its strength.

### Beginning

The party witnesses catastrophic destruction and receives an urgent charge from the city authorities. Initial evidence identifies several surviving cultists who can be pursued in different orders.

### Middle

Each hunt explores a different district and reveals another part of the failed rite. Defeating or capturing a cultist weakens the creature, while delay permits more deaths and structural collapse.

### End

The party confronts the diminished horror amid the city's oldest buried works. Success depends on what they learned and whom they stopped before the final pursuit.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The public disaster, nonlinear hunt, weakening structure, and final urban confrontation.
- Human conspirators whose choices caused the supernatural crisis.

### Replace

- The Vortharan licensing and investment metaphor. Blackspire belongs to Castara and should feel like an expedition city, not a magical corporation.
- A generic patron anchor with a specific pre-Exodus organism recovered from the Expanse.

## Exact World Egg Placement

The University of Castara receives a sealed stone chrysalis from Blackspire, a bird-marked ruin in the Emerald Expanse. A clandestine circle of lecturers opens it beneath the old anatomy court, hoping to prove that the vanished civilization mastered living memory. The thing inside takes form from remembered bodies and moves unseen between impacts.

Three surviving scholars each carry a mnemonic graft required for the creature to remain coherent. They flee into Castara's docks, specimen quarter, and undercity. Finding them weakens the horror, but they are witnesses and culpable people, not hit points in human shape.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** stop an invisible, body-copying relic from crossing Castara to the crowded departure quays.
- **Conflict:** hunt the responsible scholars while rescuing districts in the creature's path.
- **Antagonist:** the Blackspire organism and the expedition circle that opened it despite explicit warnings.
- **Consequences:** Castara may close Blackspire claims, conceal the disaster, or accelerate the race to control the ruin.
