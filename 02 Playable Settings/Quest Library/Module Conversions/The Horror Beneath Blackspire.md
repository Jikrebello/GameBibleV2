# The Horror Beneath Blackspire

**Source:** *Carrion Hill* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Vorthara leak district or an old barrow-town | **Band:** level 5 analog

**The original:** an urban horror erupts from ancient vaults; the party hunts several surviving cultists in any order, weakening the creature before confronting it.

**The conversion:** something invisible is demolishing a Vortharan district one building at a time. It is an unfinished patron anchor given appetite and mass by a failed summoning syndicate. Three surviving investors each retain a controlling share encoded in flesh, ledger, or true name. Every share recovered strips one capacity from the horror before the final pursuit beneath the streets.

**The torque:** the modular hunt remains intact, but the cult becomes a failed limited partnership. The city authorities want the horror stopped without revealing that licensed pact-brokers financed it; the Pale Hand wants the anchor intact; trapped residents need routes opened now, not after the evidence is secured.

**Hook:** urban cosmic horror structured as the liquidation of a supernatural enterprise.
