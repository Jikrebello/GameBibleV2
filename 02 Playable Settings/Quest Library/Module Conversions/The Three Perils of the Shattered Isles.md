# The Three Perils of the Shattered Isles

**Source:** *Plunder & Peril* | **Tier:** F campaign | **Region:** Shattered Isles | **Band:** levels 4-7 analog

**The original:** three linked privateering adventures combine rival crews, dangerous islands, and a race for a legendary pirate treasure.

**The conversion:** a prize-court offers the party a provisional letter of marque and three chances to make it permanent: recover a vanished surveyor's chart, seize a hostile contract-captain's tally ship, and reach a pre-Concord cache before three rival crews. The cache is not loose gold but old bearer titles to anchorages, salvage grounds, and passage debts across the Isles.

**The torque:** whoever wins becomes a political fact. Keeping the titles creates a new privateer house; selling them redraws Merchant-Prince influence; destroying them frees settlements whose debts are otherwise perfectly enforceable. Each of the three adventures can stand alone, but earlier rivals and mercies return in the final race.

**Hook:** piracy as the violent birth of a legal institution.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Three linked privateering adventures pit a crew against rivals, dangerous islands, and a legendary cache.

### Beginning

The party earns or steals a vessel and enters competition with recurring crews.

### Middle

Two independent expeditions build rivalries, maps, and resources for the final voyage.

### End

The crews race through the last island site and decide who controls the prize.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the three-part maritime ladder, recurring rivals, ship growth, and final treasure race.

### Replace

Replace a single generic prize court; each peril should involve a different island authority and Sestaran city sponsor.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Shattered Isles. **Cultures:** Castaran, Fiorenzian, Vardessan. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** earn a permanent letter of marque through three linked island trials involving a lost chart, a hostile tally ship, and a race to an old title-cache.
- **Conflict:** rival Castaran, Fiorenzian, and Vardessan crews recur across the trials, and the final treasure grants enforceable control over settlements rather than simple wealth.
- **Antagonist:** the rival contract-captains and island powers prepared to kill for the same titles.
- **Consequences:** a new privateer house forms, existing Merchant-Princes divide the rights, or indebted settlements are freed by destroying the cache.
