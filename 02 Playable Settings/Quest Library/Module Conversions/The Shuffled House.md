# The Shuffled House

**Source:** *The Harrowing* • **Tier:** W→3 • **Region:** Astral (entry anywhere Sestaran) • **Band:** level 9 analog

**The original:** a legendary fortune-deck pulls the party into a storybook demiplane ruled by its walking archetypes.

**The conversion:** a Sestaran fortune-deck — the card-game every port plays — has one true deck among its million printings: a **patron's micro-kingdom** folded into fifty-four painted rooms (Egg-White canon: demiplanes, rent, anchors). The party follows a vanished mark inside and finds the deck's archetypes — the Anchor, the Debtor, the Unpaid Bride, the Empty Seat — living the same hands over and over, because the demiplane's patron died and **the rent is in arrears**: drift is eating the outer cards.

**The torque:** the Concord of Weights has noticed the vacancy. Escape requires playing the house rules; *saving* the card-people requires someone taking over the lease — and the terms are on the table when they leave.

**Hook:** the Astral canon as a fairy tale with a mortgage.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A magical fortune deck pulls the party into a demiplane where its illustrated archetypes rule separate realms.

### Beginning

A missing person or reading draws the party through the deck.

### Middle

The party crosses card-realms, allies with archetypes, and restores the broken story sequence.

### End

The final hand determines escape and whether the deck's inhabitants remain trapped.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the deck-world, archetypal factions, nonlinear travel, and story-shaped escape.

### Replace

Replace generic Sestaran entry and automatic Astral rent; make the deck a Vardessan salon artifact with one specific dead patron.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** follow a vanished person into a true Sestaran fortune-deck whose painted rooms form a failing demiplane.
- **Conflict:** escape requires playing the dead patron's house rules, but saving the card-people requires stabilizing or replacing the power that sustained their world.
- **Antagonist:** the abandoned deck-realm's compulsive rules and any Concord of Weights claimant seeking to acquire its vacant anchor.
- **Consequences:** the party escapes or is assigned a permanent role, the inhabitants are saved or erased by drift, and ownership of the micro-kingdom is refused, transferred, or assumed.
