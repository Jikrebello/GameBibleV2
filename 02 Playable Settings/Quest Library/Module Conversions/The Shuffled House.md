# The Shuffled House

**Source:** *The Harrowing* • **Tier:** W→3 • **Region:** Astral (entry anywhere Sestaran) • **Band:** level 9 analog

**The original:** a legendary fortune-deck pulls the party into a storybook demiplane ruled by its walking archetypes.

**The conversion:** a Sestaran fortune-deck — the card-game every port plays — has one true deck among its million printings: a **patron's micro-kingdom** folded into fifty-four painted rooms (Egg-White canon: demiplanes, rent, anchors). The party follows a vanished mark inside and finds the deck's archetypes — the Anchor, the Debtor, the Unpaid Bride, the Empty Seat — living the same hands over and over, because the demiplane's patron died and **the rent is in arrears**: drift is eating the outer cards.

**The torque:** the Concord of Weights has noticed the vacancy. Escape requires playing the house rules; *saving* the card-people requires someone taking over the lease — and the terms are on the table when they leave.

**Hook:** the Astral canon as a fairy tale with a mortgage.
