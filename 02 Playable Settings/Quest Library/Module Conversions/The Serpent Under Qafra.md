# The Serpent Under Qafra

**Source:** N1 *Against the Cult of the Reptile God* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Covenant caravan country | **Band:** levels 1-3 analog

**The original:** residents of an isolated town disappear or behave strangely because a hidden cult beneath it replaces civic trust with supernatural obedience.

**The conversion:** Qafra's cistern carries dreams in which households sign small agreements. The counterparty is an Ishkatar remnant wearing Avarix's oldest mask. Each contract redirects water, inheritance, and testimony toward the buried serpent court; the breach-court refuses jurisdiction over a pre-Covenant party.

**The torque:** victims consented under fraud. Killing the remnant stops new contracts but leaves existing terms enforceable unless the party develops a lawful theory for annulment.

**Hook:** the hidden-town cult becomes a jurisdictional horror beneath the water supply.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A hidden cult beneath an isolated settlement enthralls residents and replaces civic trust with obedience.

### Beginning

Strange behavior and disappearances draw the party into a divided town.

### Middle

Investigation identifies free allies, controlled households, and the underground route to the cult.

### End

The party confronts the cult leader and supernatural master, then helps the town recover.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the compromised town, investigation, free holdouts, underground cult, and rescue.

### Replace

Replace dream-contracts as the whole method; combine fraud, fear, poison, and genuine serpent influence under Qahuri conditions.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** trace dream-agreements carried through Qafra's cistern before they transfer the town's water, inheritance, and testimony to a buried court.
- **Conflict:** the victims consented under fraud, but the local breach-court claims no jurisdiction over the pre-Covenant counterparty.
- **Antagonist:** an Ishkatar remnant wearing Avarix's oldest mask and the cult agents translating its predation into household agreements.
- **Consequences:** new contracts stop or spread, existing victims are released or remain bound, and Qafra's water and family order pass back to the town or into the serpent court.
