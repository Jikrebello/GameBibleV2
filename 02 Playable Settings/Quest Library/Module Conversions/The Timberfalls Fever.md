# The Timberfalls Fever

**Source:** D0 *Hollow's Last Hope* • **Tier:** R→W starter • **Region:** Grenzburg (Timberfalls) • **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** a logging town's plague can only be cured with herbs from a dangerous forest and a rare fungus from an abandoned dwarven monastery.

**The conversion:** blackscour fever tears through the Timberfalls lumber camps during the Funnel. The chirurgeon needs three things: ironbloom from the ruined Elsin-mission cellars, marsh-herb from Fenn Road country (Grobi moving through it), and — the hard part — elfroot's analog from inside the Moss-Crown grove the Clear-Cut crews violated. The Folk will grant grove-right for a price the cartel won't like.

**The torque:** the cure is easy; the *permissions* are the adventure. Every ingredient sits in someone's jurisdiction, and the fever worsens each day, so every delayed permission has a visible human cost.

**Hook:** the Blackjacks are sent because sick loggers can't pay their debts.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A deadly disease strikes a logging town and a healer needs three rare ingredients from nearby wilderness.

### Beginning

The party learns the cure and the worsening timetable.

### Middle

Separate journeys recover ingredients from forest hazards, an abandoned ruin, and dangerous inhabitants.

### End

The completed remedy is brought home in time to save some or all of the sick.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the three-ingredient structure, rising death clock, wilderness variety, and visible cure.

### Replace

Replace Bank debt as the reason to help and Numbers-doc language; Timberfalls families, healers, cartel crews, and Folk permissions are sufficient.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Timberfalls, Grenzburg. **Cultures:** Grenzburger, Folk. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** gather three ingredients for blackscour fever before Timberfalls' lumber camps pass the point where a cure can save them.
- **Conflict:** every ingredient lies under a different local authority, and the hardest cure requires restitution for a grove the clear-cut crews violated.
- **Antagonist:** the fever and the cartel leaders who obstruct Folk permission to avoid admitting responsibility; Grobi movement through the marsh is a hazard, not the cause.
- **Consequences:** patients live or die by delay, the grove is restored or exploited again, and relations among Timberfalls, the Folk, and the road's Grobi bands improve or harden.
