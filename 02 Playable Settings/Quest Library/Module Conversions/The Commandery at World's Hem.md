# The Commandery at World's Hem

**Source:** B2 *The Keep on the Borderlands* | **Tier:** F/R hub | **Region:** Grenzburg and the Folk frontier | **Band:** levels 1-3 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

A fortified keep protects settled lands near a ravine whose caves are occupied by several hostile groups. The keep provides safety, rumors, supplies, rivals, and authority without dictating a single route through the wilderness.

### Beginning

New arrivals establish themselves at the keep, meet its residents, gather rumors, and choose which nearby problem to investigate first.

### Middle

The party explores the wilderness and faction-held caves. The inhabitants raid one another, respond to losses, and can be fought, deceived, divided, or bargained with.

### End

There is no single mandatory finale. The frontier changes according to which groups are destroyed, displaced, reconciled, or left dominant, and the party's relationship with the keep evolves with it.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

- The defensible home base, rumor-driven sandbox, nearby ravine, competing occupants, and freedom of approach.
- Consequences that persist between expeditions.

### Replace

- Caldria and the assumption that all cave inhabitants form one enemy civilization.
- A universal Folk or Vortharan authority. The commandery answers to a specific Grenzburg marcher charter and negotiates with named clans.

## Exact World Egg Placement

World's Hem is a small marcher commandery near Dornwatch, built where a winter road crosses Folk hunting ground. Nearby caves shelter a Bloody-Sun raiding lodge, a starving herd-matron's dependents, Stone-Tooth brokers, human fugitives, and one sealed gallery all parties fear. They are neighbors and enemies, not one army.

The commandery's Aveline chaplain, Elsin-tradition missioner, ducal castellan, and Folk road-speaker disagree over extermination, ransom, sanctuary, and passage. Their authority overlaps only at this road and fort.

## Native Adventure Spine

- **Premise:** keep the winter road open while learning why several unrelated groups have converged on the ravine.
- **Conflict:** every cleared cave changes raids, food, prisoners, and alliances elsewhere.
- **Antagonist:** specific violent leaders, including a Bloody-Sun war-chief and human trafficker; hunger or Grobi identity alone is not villainy.
- **Consequences:** the commandery becomes a stable mixed frontier post, a crusading fort, an abandoned road, or the cause of a larger migration war.
