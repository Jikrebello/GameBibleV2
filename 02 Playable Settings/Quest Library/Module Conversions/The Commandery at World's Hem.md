# The Commandery at World's Hem

**Source:** B2 *The Keep on the Borderlands* | **Tier:** F/R hub | **Region:** Caldria | **Band:** levels 1-3 analog

**The original:** a fortified border settlement serves as a safe hub while expeditions explore a nearby ravine occupied by competing monster factions.

**The conversion:** an Aveline commandery guards a migration road above a Grobi splinter-warren. Bloody-Sun raiders, a starving matron-band, Stone-Tooth brokers, and human scavengers occupy separate caves. One gallery has been walled shut by every Grobi faction in turn.

**The torque:** the Aveline chaplain and an Elsin missioner disagree over conversion, extermination, and ransom. Every cave changes food stores, refugee flow, Grobi cohesion, and doctrinal authority. The sealed gallery is Pale-Skin work reserved for a later tier.

**Hook:** the reusable frontier sandbox becomes a political ecology with a defensible home.
