# Murder at Lantern Fair

**Source:** *Murder's Mark* | **Tier:** R->W | **Region:** any Eclessian market town | **Band:** level 1 analog

**The original:** a traveling carnival is blamed for a sequence of murders, forcing outsiders to investigate amid prejudice, planted evidence, and a rising mob.

**The conversion:** a mixed Saltborn, Sestaran, and Folk fair-company arrives for Lantern Week. A local notable is murdered with a performer's distinctive tool, then a second body appears during the fair's busiest night. The killers are warehouse interests using the panic to break the fair's trading charter and seize its portable credit chest.

**The torque:** this is deliberately mundane by setting standards. Residue tests, witness times, wagon inventories, and who profits from a riot matter more than occult revelation. If the party waits for a monster, ordinary people will burn the fair themselves.

**Hook:** a starter investigation about proving guilt before familiarity becomes evidence.
