# The Accord's Hollow Keep

**Source:** *Fangwood Keep* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Korrheim-Brauvalis confessional border | **Band:** level 4 analog

## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Fangwood Keep stands in the Marideth River valley between Nirmathas and Molthune. The two nations have repeatedly taken and retaken it because the fortress controls an important border crossing.

Lieutenant Pavo Vos served at the keep roughly ten years before the adventure and learned that it had been built over the surviving chambers of an ancient elven wizard named Tessarael. Obsessed with the secrets below, he eventually takes his specialist platoon, Vos's Strikers, across the border without authorization and captures the keep. He tells his soldiers that they are searching for a buried weapons cache.

After weeks of digging, casualties, and lies, the Strikers rebel. Vos and his companion Elessia betray their own troops, killing or imprisoning them. Elessia then hires hobgoblin mercenaries to replace the dead soldiers and continue excavating. Her real objective is Tessarael's Book of Infinite Spells, which powers the damaged arcane engines below the fortress.

### Beginning: Infiltrating Fangwood Keep

1. The party may be hired by either Nirmathas or Molthune. Both employers want Vos removed, although their political reasons and desired custody differ.
2. The keep stands on a cleared hill, making a daylight approach easy to detect. The party is warned that fighting the whole garrison at once is likely to fail.
3. The keep can be entered through gates, walls, damaged fortifications, or secret defender passages known to former commanders.
4. Guards move logically, respond to alarms, reinforce threatened positions, and change routine by time of day.
5. Inside the hidden passages, the party can find Akre Belring, a surviving Striker who escaped the hobgoblin takeover. He explains Vos's lies, points out peepholes and concealed armories, and may join the party.
6. Captured hobgoblins can be bribed or persuaded to reveal troop positions. They are mercenaries rather than fanatics and are already unhappy with the job.

### Middle: The Divided Garrison

1. The party moves through the keep's gatehouse, yard, kitchen, barracks, armory, library, ramparts, and command quarters while trying to avoid a general alarm.
2. Two captive cooks can be rescued from the violent bugbear Tralg. They know the keep's ordinary layout but little about the chambers below.
3. A goblin snake named Aseri guards the stairs and offers information about the underground riddles if the party kills hobgoblin commander Fethi Ken-Shakkas.
4. Fethi is intelligent and negotiable. She offers to withdraw every surviving hobgoblin for payment, or may settle an insult through a nonlethal duel. If treated honorably, she keeps her agreement.
5. Vos remains in the command quarters with loyal guards. Elessia quietly enchants and deceives him while spending much of her time underground.
6. Vos fights when confronted but surrenders if beaten. In return for his life, he reveals why he seized the keep and warns the party about Elessia and the arcane chambers.

### End: Tessarael's Arcane Engine

1. The party descends into the wizard's buried complex, where damaged engines have produced gravity distortions and sporadic portals.
2. Creatures pulled from other planes have become trapped below, including a murderous redcap, a transformed phase spider, and a barrow hound that may stop fighting if approached peacefully.
3. The chambers contain mirrors retaining an image of Tessarael, dead vegetation growing against gravity, animated objects, oozes, undead, and other products of the malfunctioning engines.
4. Tessarael originally tried to use a living machine and her spellbook to return to her distant homeland. A miscalculation instead hurled her into an unknown region of the Dark Tapestry.
5. Elessia reaches the final engine first. She uses dead bugbear servants and magic to protect herself while trying to extract the Book of Infinite Spells.
6. Removing the book shuts down the portals and gravity effects without collapsing the dungeon. Elessia fights to the death, while Vos may already be captured, dead, or escaped.
7. The party returns the keep to its employer. Vos and Elessia receive trial if captured, but the published ending gives limited attention to the long-term custody of the fortress or the dangerous artifact.

## Conversion Diagnosis

### What Is Worth Keeping

- A compact fortress whose defenders move, communicate, and react to alarms.
- Several viable infiltration routes, including secret passages built for defenders after occupation.
- A loyal survivor hiding inside the walls who can become guide and ally.
- A commander driven by an old discovery and willing to betray his own soldiers to reach it.
- A more ruthless companion who manipulates that obsession for her own objective.
- Mercenaries with clear motives who can be bribed, respected, divided, or fought.
- Captives who know practical information rather than delivering the whole plot.
- A surface military problem that opens into an older hidden complex.
- A final choice over a strategic keep rather than a dungeon that ceases to matter when its boss dies.

### What Needs Rebuilding

- The original national border war should become a post-Accord confessional border where renewed fighting could undo a fragile peace.
- Hobgoblin mercenaries should not be imported merely to fill enemy rooms. A Korrheimer contract-company can preserve the negotiable military faction without confusing Grobi canon.
- The underground creatures and planar accidents are colorful but disconnected. Every supernatural encounter should arise from one repeated massacre and one thin-space law.
- The Freeflame and old Church occupants need legitimate grievances and ordinary soldiers worth saving. Neither side should exist as disposable ideological scenery.
- The rogue commander must remain culpable without becoming a puppet. Grief and manipulation explain his choices but do not excuse mutiny, murder, or manufactured orders.
- The primary villain needs a concrete use for the phenomenon: portable soldiers and witnesses made from stolen memory.
- Detecting an after-image must rely on behavior, dates, wounds, names, and improvisation rather than one convenient spell.
- Capturing the fort cannot be the only objective. The party must prevent approaching relief columns from turning a contained crisis into a new reform war.
- The confession register must matter as testimony and supernatural anchor, not as another excuse to make every scene about paperwork.

## World Egg Conversion Seed

**The conversion:** [[Hollow Keep]] is a joint Accord watch-fort controlling the Dreywater road between Korrheim and Brauvalis. It stops answering both sides. Freeflame auxiliaries hold the walls, old-Church soldiers barricade the chapel wing, the [[Grey Pike Company]] controls the gatehouse, and surviving [[Accord Wardens]] hide in passages beneath them.

**The torque:** Captain [[Othmar Vale]] reopened [[The Hollow Muster]], a soul-route thin space left by a pre-Accord parley massacre. [[Magistra Sabine Krail]] promised it could restore his dead brother, then used its confession register to create [[Hollow Sentries]] that copy current soldiers and continue dead men's final orders. Taking the keep is easier than learning who is alive before two relief columns arrive.

**Hook:** every uniform is genuine, every side has orders, and some of the soldiers remember dying twenty-two years ago.
