# The Accord's Hollow Keep

**Source:** *Fangwood Keep* | **Tier:** W | **Region:** Korrheim-Brauvalis confessional border | **Band:** level 4 analog

**The original:** enter a disputed border fortress, navigate several armed occupants, and decide who will control a strategically valuable keep.

**The conversion:** an Accord watch-fort has stopped answering either side. Freeflame irregulars hold the walls, old-Church soldiers occupy the chapel wing, and the surviving garrison has sealed the undercroft. Each claims the other broke the peace. Beneath them, a thin space is repeating the fort's bloodiest night and replacing living sentries with convincing after-images.

**The torque:** taking the keep is easy compared with proving who still lives, who lawfully commands, and whether exposing the breach will collapse the local Accord. The winning faction gains a border road, a confession register, and a supernatural liability.

**Hook:** a military infiltration in which every uniform may be genuine and every memory may belong to the fort.
