# The Undergrove

**Source:** *The Sunless Citadel* | **Tier:** starter W | **Region:** southern barrow-forest | **Band:** levels 1-3 analog

**The original:** a sunken fortress contains competing inhabitants and a corrupted tree whose miraculous fruit draws nearby families into danger.

**The conversion:** an Enaidfae shrine collapsed beneath a barrow. At its root, a damaged drake heart-gem grew into a law-tree. Its fruit cures one condition by imposing another law: silence, rootedness, seasonal sleep, or obedience to a forgotten boundary.

**The torque:** the Folk want the gem re-seated, the Bank wants harvest rights, and families want cures. The tree is enforcing ecological law without the context that made it livable. Fruit supply, cured villagers, and Faewild pressure are the practical stakes.

**Hook:** the first dungeon teaches that miracles can be accurate and catastrophically misplaced.
