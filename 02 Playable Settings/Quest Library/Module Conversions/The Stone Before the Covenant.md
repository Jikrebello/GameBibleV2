# The Stone Before the Covenant

**Source:** J4 *The Pact Stone Pyramid* | **Tier:** W->3 | **Region:** Great Glass Desert | **Band:** level 7 analog

**The original:** rival expeditions race to excavate a lost pyramid whose sealed chambers preserve the secret of an ancient dynasty's power.

**The conversion:** a sandfall exposes a black contract monolith beneath an Ishkatar ruin. A breach-court expedition, a Jirahar memory caravan, and a private Covenant magnate all claim first right to enter. Every door is a clause, every trap a forfeiture, and the central seal uses contractual syntax older than Malak al-Ahd.

**The torque:** the site does not automatically prove any faction's preferred history. It proves that something used enforceable metaphysical contract architecture before the Covenant's public founding. The authorial possibility is a Creditor instrument recovered and renamed by later jurists; what evidence survives the expedition determines whether anyone in-world can make that case.

**Hook:** an archaeological race where opening the tomb may invalidate an empire's claim to have invented its own law.
## Original Adventure: Spark Notes

### Background

Rival expeditions race to excavate a newly exposed pyramid preserving an ancient dynasty's secret.

### Beginning

A sandfall reveals the site and competing sponsors establish camps.

### Middle

Teams sabotage, negotiate, and explore as the pyramid's defenses test their claims.

### End

The central chamber reveals evidence that can reorder present political history.

## Conversion Ruling

### Preserve

Preserve the rival camps, excavation race, layered pyramid, and destabilizing proof.

### Replace

Replace generic contract-shaped traps; use mixed Ansharan ritual and early metaphysical law, with only some genuinely contractual mechanisms.

## Exact World Egg Placement

**Region:** Al-Qahur Glass frontier. **Cultures:** Qahuri, Jirahar. The frontmatter placement is controlling; later blocking must use named local offices and factions rather than an umbrella government.

## Translated Adventure Core

- **Premise:** enter a newly exposed Ishkatar contract monument before three modern claimant groups seize or destroy its pre-Covenant evidence.
- **Conflict:** doors and traps enforce ancient clauses, while no discovery automatically proves the historical story preferred by the Covenant, Jirahar, or private sponsor.
- **Antagonist:** the magnate prepared to monopolize the find and the monument's ancient seal-system, which treats modern explorers as parties to its terms.
- **Consequences:** the evidence survives, disappears, or becomes propaganda, and custody of the stone changes what the world can prove about the origins of contract magic.
