# The Briar Lances

The **Briar Lances** are a small Marcher recovery company hired by Sabine Harrowfen to secure at least one of her family's forfeited heirlooms. They know woods, ruins, and bounty work. They do not understand the Old Wood's compact when they enter it.

## Members

### Corin Ash, Captain

Corin is a former Ranger auxiliary who prefers a completed contract to a glorious fight. He accepted Sabine's account that Lucan was sending untested outsiders to steal family property.

**Limit:** he will not abandon a crew member or burn occupied shelter.

### Ysabet Crow, Scout

Ysabet reads trails, weather, and ordinary animal behavior better than anyone in either expedition. She distrusts Folk explanations but changes her mind when evidence repeats reliably.

**Limit:** she refuses orders that require killing an animal guarding young.

### Toma Pike, Axeman

Toma comes from a Coldwater mill family and believes restoring the stream will destroy his kin's livelihood. He reacts to supernatural resistance by cutting and burning harder.

**Limit:** he will accept an alternative that preserves paid work for the mill families; abstract moral condemnation only entrenches him.

### Pell Rowan, Chirurgeon

Pell treats wounds and collects forest medicines. He is the first Lance willing to trade supplies or shelter with the party.

**Limit:** he will not leave a conscious person to become part of an Oathwood Warden.

## Rival Trust

Track Rival Trust from 0-3.

- Rescue a Lance, share a camp under true names, or honor an agreed division: +1.
- Steal after making terms, abandon a wounded rival, or use the wood to punish them: -1.
- Reveal Othmar's confession and offer a material answer for Coldwater workers: +1.

At Trust 0, the Lances let the party engage guardians and then attempt to seize relics. At 1, they honor temporary bargains. At 2, they refuse to attack during Lucan's compact rite. At 3, they oppose any order to burn Hartrest Lodge or kill witnesses.

## After the Adventure

The Briar Lances can become a useful recurring mirror for a player crew. They take practical jobs, ask what the pay omits, and remember whether the party treated competition as permission for cruelty.

Under Sabine they become household scouts. Under Lucan they may maintain the shared road. If betrayed by both claimants, they drift toward Blackbriar's bounty economy.
