# Rector Ilyra Sorn

## Role

Ilyra is the present rector of the [[Collegium of Doors]] and public host of Breach-Week. She is an accomplished spatial mage, an effective teacher, and a poor custodian of inherited danger.

## What She Wants

The Collegium has not produced a Breach-Week winner in living memory and risks losing its prestige, students, and Council influence. Ilyra invites the party because she wants someone strong enough to defeat the Hall and prove her institution still matters.

## What She Knows

Ilyra knows the Hall's dimensions are false, that its inner threshold diverts entrants into a sealed annex, and that previous rectors suppressed investigation. She believes contestants die during copying. She does not know coherent minds remain trapped or that the Examiner can project into campus systems.

## Her Fault

She had enough evidence to stop the festival and chose reputation over uncertainty. She did not create the Examiner and is not secretly controlled by it. Her guilt is ordinary ambition reinforced by institutional habit.

## Arc

- **Beginning:** polished, candid about danger, evasive about the Hall.
- **Middle:** prioritizes continuation after the opening projection and loses control of the event.
- **End:** may confess and dismantle her own authority, preserve the institution through partial concealment, fight to retain control, or accept stewardship of the Annex.

## Use in Play

Ilyra should remain useful after exposure. She understands the Hall's geometry, can coordinate campus defenses, and may personally save civilians during the breach. Redemption requires public responsibility and surrender of office, not merely helping in the final fight.
