# The Accord's Hollow Keep — Full Adventure

## Adventure at a Glance

**Premise:** A joint confessional watch-fort has gone silent on the Korrheim-Brauvalis border. Freeflame and old-Church troops both claim lawful command, hidden survivors say their comrades have been replaced, and two relief columns are marching toward each other.

**Beginning:** The party receives authority to enter [[Hollow Keep]], reopen the Dreywater road, and determine what happened without allowing either relief force to seize the fort. It must cross exposed ground, choose an infiltration route, and identify which of four armed groups might cooperate.

**Middle:** Inside, the party navigates a dynamic garrison: Freeflame auxiliaries on the walls, old-Church soldiers in the chapel, hired pikes at the gate, and Accord Wardens hiding in defender passages. Some sentries are [[Hollow Sentries]] wearing memories copied from the living. Captain [[Othmar Vale]] admits that he reopened the sealed undercroft to recover his dead brother.

**End:** Beneath the keep, [[Magistra Sabine Krail]] is extracting the confession register that anchors [[The Hollow Muster]]. The party must defeat or expose her, recover soldiers trapped in the repetition, and either heal, seal, destroy, or surrender the phenomenon before the relief columns arrive.

**Tier:** W. The adventure supports a level 4 analog when each armed faction can be negotiated with, the keep's alarm changes deployments rather than summoning endless reinforcements, and the Hollow Sentries can be identified or released through investigation.

**Expected length:** 5-7 substantial sessions: commission and approach, two sessions of fortress infiltration, command confrontation, two sessions below, and the relief/ownership conclusion.

## Who, What, Where, Why, and How

- **Who:** the player crew; Captain Othmar Vale; Magistra Sabine Krail; Freeflame Sergeant Greta Voss; Canon-Militant Lucien Harrow; Accord Corporal Mara Eltz; Grey Pike captain Rika Tarn; trapped soldiers; and the remembered dead of the Hollow Muster.
- **What:** secure the keep, distinguish living people from after-images, stop Sabine removing the Muster Register, prevent the relief columns from fighting, and determine the fort's future custody.
- **Where:** Hollow Keep above the Dreywater crossing, on the confessional settlement line between the Confederation of Korrheim and the Duchy of Brauvalis.
- **Why the party acts:** a joint Accord commission needs outsiders neither garrison can dismiss as enemy troops; one border government hires the party but the other offers cooperation; or the party reaches the blocked crossing before the relief forces and becomes the only neutral body present.
- **Why the crisis began:** Othmar broke the undercroft seal after Sabine claimed the Hollow Muster could restore his brother Hannes, killed during the Wars of Reform. Sabine copied current sentries into the old repetition and forged contradictory orders to keep the garrison divided while she worked.
- **How it escalates:** alarms consolidate armed factions, accusations cause executions, each bell pulse replaces another sentry, and the approaching columns interpret smoke, closed gates, or partisan banners as proof of hostile seizure.

## Moral and Metaphysical Ruling

The old Church and Freeflame movement contain sincere believers, genuine goods, serious errors, and people willing to exploit the crisis. Their theological dispute is not resolved by declaring both sides equally right or equally corrupt.

The Korrheim Accord stopped a war and provides a real public good. It also buried crimes that its signatories considered too dangerous to assign. Peace built over concealed murder remains vulnerable to the truth, but exposing the truth need not require restarting the war.

The Hollow Sentries are not resurrected soldiers. The souls of those killed during the Night of Broken Bells were not stored as a convenient army. The thin space repeats memory, office, fear, wounds, and final intention. Its echoes deserve truthful handling because they bear human testimony, but they are not whole persons who can be kept as soldiers indefinitely.

Sabine's project is objectively evil. She copies living minds without consent, imprisons bodies in repeated actions, and intends to turn unresolved deaths into obedient infiltrators. Othmar's grief explains why he listened to her. It does not absolve his mutiny, forged orders, or the deaths caused by reopening the Muster.

## The Historical Truth

Hollow Keep existed before the [[The Korrheim Accord|Korrheim Accord]]. During the Wars of Reform it changed hands repeatedly and was divided between a Freeflame field congregation and an old-Church marcher company under a temporary truce.

In 1338 AR, commanders arranged a prisoner exchange and winter ceasefire. Three signals were issued through the same chapel bell:

1. **Admit the parley under guard.**
2. **Hold every assigned post.**
3. **No quarter; the enemy has broken faith.**

The third order was forged by a Brauvalian noble agent who feared peace would expose his sale of provisions to both armies. Each side heard enough movement to believe the other had betrayed it. Fighting filled the courtyard, chapel, infirmary, and undercroft. Eighty-one soldiers, prisoners, servants, and camp followers died before dawn.

The chaplain's confession register recorded every person's rite and assigned station because both confessions demanded separate burial. The register, bell, contradictory orders, and concentrated deaths formed a soul-route thin space. Survivors reported seeing sentries continue their patrols after their bodies were carried away.

When the Accord was signed five years later, delegates sealed the undercroft and rebuilt the site as a joint watch-fort. Publicly acknowledging the massacre would have required accusing one of the first signatory houses of engineering a false breach. The keep became a monument to peace while the reason it needed peace remained underground.

## Why the Muster Woke Now

Othmar Vale served at Hollow Keep in 1352 AR and discovered his brother Hannes's name in a damaged casualty list. Hannes had officially died elsewhere, leaving Othmar convinced that his family had been denied the truth.

Sabine Krail, formerly an Order of the Lantern investigator, approached him with records of after-images beneath the keep. She correctly argued that the official history was false. She falsely promised that the repeated Hannes could be restored as the man he had been.

Othmar returned as a relief commander during a routine border alarm. He used his authority to isolate the garrison, opened the defender passages, and broke the undercroft seal. When his mixed company refused further excavation after the first disappearances, he sent one forged message to the Freeflame auxiliaries and another to the old-Church chapel guard, persuading each that the other intended seizure.

Sabine then hired the [[Grey Pike Company]] as neutral muscle and began using the confession register to pattern Hollow Sentries after current soldiers. She intends to leave before either relief column arrives.

## The Four Armed Groups

### Freeflame Wall Company

Fourteen living auxiliaries and several Hollow Sentries hold the ramparts, north tower, and signal platform. Greta believes the chapel company killed missing sentries and intends to hand the keep to Brauvalis.

They offer observation, archers, and control of the signal fire. They demand that the Freeflame banner remain visible until Korrheim relief acknowledges them.

### Old-Church Chapel Guard

Twelve living soldiers, two clergy, three servants, and at least one Hollow Sentry occupy the chapel wing, library, and west tower. Lucien believes the Freeflame company stole the confession register and summoned irregular reinforcements.

They offer healing, access to the modern records, and the only consecrated route below. They demand that the chapel remain outside Freeflame command.

### Grey Pike Company

Nine contract soldiers hold the gatehouse, kitchen, and main yard. They were hired to protect Sabine's excavation, not fight a confessional war. Rika will negotiate withdrawal, neutrality, or active assistance depending on payment, proof of supernatural substitution, and how the party treats her company.

### Hidden Accord Wardens

Mara leads six surviving wardens through the secret defender passages. Five more living wardens are trapped below, repeating fragments of the 1338 watch. Mara knows the keep better than anyone but has begun doubting every face, including her own.

## Adventure States

- **Keep Alert (0-4):** 0 is routine suspicion; 4 is general battle. It changes patrols, barred routes, and willingness to talk.
- **Muster Pressure (0-4):** rises when the cracked bell sounds, a living person is copied, or the register is moved. At 4, the keep begins repeating the Night of Broken Bells openly.
- **Living Recovered (0-4):** hidden wardens, wall-company victims, chapel victims, and the undercroft work party.
- **Accord Stability (-2 to +2):** tracks whether the crisis appears to be confessional aggression or a jointly witnessed crime.
- **Relief Clock (0-4):** each major delay advances the columns. At 4, both forces can see the keep and one another.
- **Sabine's Preparation (0-3):** copied exit identity, freed register, and secured escape route. At 3, she can leave through a sentry double unless stopped.

## Running the Keep as a Living Fortress

The keep is not a sequence of rooms waiting to fight. Every loud scene changes it.

- At **Alert 1**, nearby patrols investigate and commanders send runners.
- At **Alert 2**, factions bar internal doors and move civilians to protected rooms.
- At **Alert 3**, Greta controls the wall signals, Lucien fortifies the chapel corridor, and Rika occupies the central yard.
- At **Alert 4**, open fighting begins unless the party has secured a truce or exposed the Hollow Sentries.

Secret passages let the party bypass positions, observe conversations, move captives, and reach commanders. They do not make consequences disappear: factions notice missing troops, opened doors, and changed signals.

## Identifying a Hollow Sentry

No single sign is conclusive. Two or more establish a reliable case.

- It gives the correct password but uses the challenge sequence from 1338.
- It remembers a room's old purpose or doorway rather than its current layout.
- It repeats a patrol exactly, including pauses after obstructions have moved.
- Its equipment is genuine in appearance but cannot be separated from its role for long.
- It recognizes names in the old confession register and ignores later ranks.
- It cannot explain what it did between two memorized moments.
- A wound appears only when the person hears a bell, smells lamp oil, or crosses the place of death.
- The living original can sometimes be found below, entranced but physically present.

Destroying an echo does not kill the original. Accusing a living soldier of being an echo can still get that person executed by frightened comrades.

## Act I — Two Seals on One Order

### Beat 1: The Joint Commission

At a road shrine outside bow range, the party meets **Brauvalian Marshal Edric Dorn** and **Korrheimer Delegate Ilse Falk** under a temporary flag. Each has received a message from Hollow Keep claiming the other confession attempted a coup. Neither message bears a verifiable witness mark.

They agree to authorize a small neutral entry because marching either column forward would look like invasion. The commission requires the party to:

- reopen the Dreywater crossing;
- identify the lawful living commander;
- account for missing wardens;
- prevent removal of the confession register;
- signal both columns before either enters bow range.

Dorn privately asks the party to preserve the chapel. Falk privately asks it to keep Freeflame auxiliaries from being disarmed before testimony. These requests create pressure without changing the public mission.

### Beat 2: The Silent Road

The road to the keep contains three clues:

- a dispatch rider found alive but walking the same hundred paces between two trees;
- two contradictory sealed messages written in Othmar's hand;
- a sentry visible on the battlement in precisely the same posture at dawn and noon.

Freeing the rider requires interrupting the route, speaking his present assignment, or removing the old watch token Sabine placed in his coat. He remembers a woman reading names below the chapel.

### Beat 3: Choose an Approach

- **Front gate:** negotiate with the Grey Pikes and enter under parley; the fastest route to civilians and Rika.
- **Broken south wall:** climb under observation from both factions; access to the Freeflame wall company.
- **Chapel culvert:** enter near the old-Church infirmary; risks immediate detention but reaches records quickly.
- **Defender passage:** find the four-circle Accord mark under the north retaining wall; reaches Mara without alerting the main garrison.
- **Night tower crossing:** use ropes from a ravine spur; preserves secrecy but advances the Relief Clock through preparation.

### Act I Output

The party knows the messages conflict, has seen the repetition affect a living person, and has chosen which faction encounters it first.

## Act II — Four Garrisons in One Keep

### Zone 1: The Gatehouse and Grey Pikes

Rika Tarn receives a parley if the party shows either official seal. She admits Sabine hired the company for excavation security and that the assignment changed after men began vanishing.

She offers three possible arrangements:

- withdraw for the unpaid half of her fee and safe conduct;
- hold the gate neutrally if shown proof that Sabine violated the contract;
- join the party below in exchange for first claim on ordinary salvage, excluding relics and registers.

Insult, betrayal, or an attempt to kill isolated pikes makes the company consolidate and fight professionally. Respecting terms can remove the largest mobile force without combat.

### Zone 2: Kitchen and Service Yard

Three cooks, two stable hands, and Brother Ansel are confined here. Their guards rotate, but one returns every forty minutes without eating, speaking, or reacting to changed orders.

The civilians know:

- Othmar opened the undercroft eleven days ago;
- Sabine brought old name lists and blank uniform tabs;
- some missing soldiers returned without recognizing spouses or debts;
- the chapel bell sounds softly even when its rope is tied;
- Grey Pike casualties have been lower because Sabine did not use their names in the register.

Ansel can distinguish the modern register from the 1338 Muster Register and reveals that confession is only one column among name, station, kin, and burial instruction.

### Zone 3: Freeflame Ramparts

Greta Voss controls the north and east walls. She does not surrender command to someone carrying only a Brauvalian seal, but allows investigation if the party brings the rescued rider, a living Accord Warden, or proof that Othmar wrote both messages.

Her immediate crisis is **Sentry Pell**, accused of being an after-image after appearing on two towers in the same hour. Both are visible: one living and terrified, one calmly maintaining that it has never left its post.

The party can use the encounter to prove substitution publicly. Killing both out of caution reduces Accord Stability and turns Greta against outside judgment.

### Zone 4: Old-Church Chapel

Lucien Harrow holds the west wing. His people have found blood beneath the altar and believe Freeflame troops tunneled into consecrated ground.

The blood belongs to a living warden dragged through a hidden stair. The modern register proves that several people seen on the walls are officially missing, while one chapel soldier present at prayers was buried in Brauvalis twenty-two years earlier.

Lucien cooperates if shown that the phenomenon is a soul-route wound rather than Freeflame conjuration. He can provide White Flame stabilization, but insists it will not be used to validate Sabine's extraction.

### Zone 5: The Defender Passages

Mara Eltz and six wardens occupy narrow corridors between the public rooms. Peepholes reveal faction routines and at least one sentry entering a room twice without crossing the connecting hall.

Mara gives the party:

- a complete passage map;
- names of five wardens missing below;
- the location of Othmar's command quarters;
- a bloodstained copy of his excavation order;
- the fact that Othmar killed Warden-Captain Rohe when Rohe tried to light both relief beacons.

She joins a quiet plan but refuses another frontal assault after watching echoes replace casualties during the first one.

### Act II Output

The party can reach every faction leader, has a repeatable method for identifying Hollow Sentries, and knows Othmar and Sabine deliberately opened the sealed undercroft.

## Act III — The Commander Who Wanted His Brother

### Zone 6: Command Tower

Othmar occupies the command tower with two loyal soldiers and a Hollow Sentry bearing Hannes Vale's face. He has stopped pretending the fort is functioning normally but still believes Sabine is close to making Hannes whole.

Hannes can answer questions about childhood only where those memories overlap Othmar's testimony or objects Sabine used during patterning. Asked about a private event Sabine never learned, he defaults to his final watch report from 1338.

Othmar can be reached through:

- proof that living originals remain below after their echoes appear;
- Hannes admitting that he remembers dying but not living between memories;
- Warden-Captain Rohe's death and the forged orders;
- Sabine's notes describing Othmar as an access condition;
- force followed by an offer of trial rather than execution.

He surrenders if Hannes asks him to stop and the party promises to recover the five wardens. He fights if the party mocks Hannes, threatens to burn the register unread, or appears to be handing the fort to one confession without investigation.

### Othmar's Evidence

Whether captured, persuaded, or defeated, Othmar provides:

- the undercroft key;
- the original seal-breaking tools;
- Sabine's route to the lower archive;
- a list of objects she used to pattern current soldiers;
- his confession that he sent both false messages;
- warning that Sabine has prepared an image wearing her own face.

### Surface Truce

Before descending, the party can arrange a temporary command:

- Greta holds the walls and agrees not to raise a partisan banner.
- Lucien protects civilians and treats wounded of either rite.
- Rika holds or opens the gate according to contract.
- Mara controls the passages and verifies living messengers.

Each leader included raises Accord Stability. Leaving the factions unresolved allows Sabine's echoes to trigger battle while the party is below.

### Act III Output

The military conspiracy is known, the surface can be stabilized, and the party has a direct route into the Hollow Muster.

## Act IV — The Night Beneath the Keep

### Zone 7: Stair of Returning Steps

The undercroft stair descends farther than the keep's hill permits. Every seventh landing resembles the first, but changes according to which faction leads the party.

Travelers escape the repetition by keeping one shared order, naming their destination in present tense, or marking each landing with an object tied to a living memory. Splitting by confession sends groups to different versions of the 1338 night.

### Zone 8: Infirmary of Unfinished Wounds

Living soldiers lie among remembered casualties. Their bodies repeat treatment movements while Hollow Sentries rise, report for duty, and leave wearing their current faces.

The first recovery group includes two Accord Wardens and Sentry Pell's living original. The party must interrupt the copied task without making the body reenact the wound that placed the old memory there.

White Flame, Freeflame communal hymn, present-day roll call, destroying pattern objects, or physically carrying a victim beyond the room can work. Recovery increases Living Recovered and gives direct testimony against Sabine.

### Zone 9: Hall of Last Orders

Three corridors embody the contradictory commands from the Night of Broken Bells. Each leads to the same sealed yard but imposes different behavior:

- **Admit parley:** doors open, but weapons and suspicion become physically heavy.
- **Hold every post:** anyone who stops moving toward an assigned station becomes rooted in place.
- **No quarter:** every approaching figure appears momentarily in an enemy uniform.

The party can traverse one order, carry evidence between them, or overwrite all three with a present command witnessed by members of both confessions.

### Zone 10: The Muster Yard Below

An impossible copy of the keep's courtyard lies beneath the chapel. Rain hangs motionless above it. Eighty-one figures wait at posts, doors, litters, and the parley table.

They reenact the massacre when the cracked bell sounds. Observation reveals that no side attacked first: the forged third command reached both groups through different runners, and each reacted to the other's defensive movement.

Hannes stands at the signal stair. His final act was trying to ring a general cease-watch after realizing both orders were false. He died before reaching the rope.

The party may speak to echoes between repetitions, but they can answer only from memories available before death. This scene supplies truth, not resurrection.

### Zone 11: Confession Gallery

The gallery holds burial plaques, personal effects, and the damaged confession register. Sabine has cut current names into blank margins, creating bridges between living soldiers and old stations.

Removing a current name releases that living victim but alerts Sabine. Restoring erased kin and burial instructions weakens the whole repetition. Tearing pages quickly destroys copied identities but loses testimony about the massacre.

The remaining three wardens and two chapel soldiers form the second recovery group here.

### Zone 12: The Cracked Bell

The original chapel bell hangs in a chamber directly below its modern replacement. It rings without moving whenever a command is accepted by enough people in the keep.

The bell can be:

- silenced temporarily by binding its clapper with the seals of both current commanders;
- broken physically, causing the undercroft to collapse toward ordinary geometry;
- reconsecrated through old-Church rite and Freeflame response together;
- rung once for cease-watch after the forged orders are read aloud and rejected;
- left intact as a controlled warning mechanism, an unstable and politically tempting choice.

### Act IV Output

The party has recovered the living, witnessed the original massacre, and reached the mechanism Sabine intends to carry away.

## Act V — The Woman in Every Uniform

### Zone 13: Register Chamber

Sabine works in a circular archive where the Muster Register is bound to the bell by strips of uniform cloth and name tabs. Her prepared double stands among several Hollow Sentries, allowing her to change apparent position whenever an echo crosses a doorway.

She offers terms according to the surface situation:

1. Give her the register and she will withdraw every Hollow Sentry before the columns arrive.
2. Let her copy one party member as a portable anchor and she will release all trapped soldiers.
3. Publicly blame Othmar alone and she will provide proof acceptable to both factions.
4. Help her perfect the system and no living garrison will ever need die holding the border again.

Every offer preserves her ability to repeat and replace people without consent.

## Defeating Sabine and the Muster

### Expose the Original

Sabine's double knows her prepared story but not events after the party entered the keep. Questions about recent faction agreements, injuries, or the changed register reveal which body improvises. Destroying the pattern objects stops her from exchanging places with the echo.

### Turn the Garrison Against Her

If the party secured cooperation from two or more surface factions, living representatives can descend or answer through the bell shaft. Their present voices invalidate Sabine's claim that the fort has no lawful living command.

### Ring Cease-Watch

Recover the forged orders, bring witnesses from both confessions, name the dead without separating them by rite, and let Hannes complete his final movement to the bell. The resulting peal ends the repeated command. Hollow Sentries lose their posts and the register becomes an ordinary damaged historical object.

### Break the Bell and Burn the Register

Destroying both anchors ends the phenomenon quickly. Living originals can still be rescued if already found, but the testimony and many personal identities carried by the echoes are lost. The relief forces see the keep partially collapse.

### Seal the Undercroft Again

Sabine can be defeated while the Muster remains. Joint seals and a stable surface truce contain it, buying time for a later ecclesiastical and Freeflame commission. This avoids immediate destruction but repeats the Accord's original temptation to postpone truth.

### Let One Confession Claim It

Either side can use its soldiers and rites to suppress the other half of the repetition. This stabilizes the keep temporarily and produces an official history in which the chosen side was innocent. The excluded memories later return as hostile echoes.

## Act VI — The Armies at the Dreywater

### The Relief Clock Expires

At Relief 4, Korrheimer auxiliaries approach from the heights while a Brauvalian column forms across the bridge. Each reads the keep through visible signals:

- partisan banner: presumed seizure;
- closed gate and smoke: presumed battle;
- both relief beacons: request for joint parley;
- no sentries visible: suspected supernatural loss;
- mixed living witnesses on the wall: strongest evidence of restored command.

The party needs a messenger, signal, or public display credible to both forces. A single confession's commander cannot settle the matter alone.

### What Must Be Proven

The strongest account establishes four facts:

- Othmar forged the immediate orders and opened the seal.
- Sabine copied living soldiers and attempted to remove the register.
- the 1338 massacre began through a forged no-quarter command rather than confessional attack;
- current soldiers of both rites resisted or survived the conspiracy.

Evidence can include Othmar's confession, rescued originals, Sabine's notes, the modern and old registers, Hannes's completed cease-watch, and testimony from Grey Pike contractors.

## Ending States

### The Joint Watch Restored

Sabine is stopped, Living Recovered is 3+, Accord Stability is +1 or better, and both relief columns receive the truth. The Muster is healed or jointly sealed. Hollow Keep returns to a mixed garrison under a new commander, and the Night of Broken Bells enters the public record. This is the strongest continuation.

### Korrheim Holds the Walls

Greta controls the signal tower when relief arrives and the Freeflame company can prove old-Church orders permitted seizure. Korrheim occupies the fort pending arbitration. The road remains open northward, but Brauvalis treats the outcome as a strategic defeat.

### Brauvalis Holds the Chapel

Lucien preserves the register and opens the gate to Brauvalian troops. Church investigators take custody of the undercroft. They release some echoes through proper burial but restrict the massacre record to prevent scandal.

### The Grey Pike Settlement

Rika holds the gate while both confessional commands are compromised. The joint commission temporarily contracts the Grey Pikes as neutral custodians. It is practical, embarrassing, expensive, and vulnerable to whoever pays the next quarter.

### The Keep Is Broken

The party destroys the bell, register, and lower structure. Hollow Keep partially collapses and can no longer command the road without rebuilding. The supernatural threat ends, but each state accuses the other of arranging the demolition.

### The Peace of Silence

The party reseals the Muster and reports only Othmar's mutiny. War is avoided and the present garrison survives. The truth remains buried, Sabine's methods may survive in her notes, and the next breach will find the old wound even less stable.

### The Hollow Company Escapes

Sabine leaves with the register, her double, or a copied party member. The keep can be retaken, but sentries bearing impossible uniforms begin appearing at bridgehouses and arsenals across Eclessia.

### Brother Returned

Othmar leaves with Hannes's echo bound to an object or living pattern. The echo can perform love, duty, and memory within its limits, but cannot become the dead man. Othmar's refusal to accept that boundary feeds a later tragedy.

## What the Adventure Changes

- The Night of Broken Bells becomes a documented wound of the Wars of Reform.
- The Korrheim Accord gains either a successful joint precedent or a fresh territorial crisis.
- Hollow Sentries become a known soul-route threat rather than folklore about doubled guards.
- The Order of the Lantern must answer for Sabine Krail's methods and dismissal.
- Othmar Vale becomes prisoner, penitent witness, fugitive, or keeper of an impossible brother.
- The Grey Pike Company becomes a trusted neutral force, compromised contractor, or recurring border rival.
- Hollow Keep remains a usable stronghold, disputed base, sealed supernatural site, or ruin controlling the Dreywater road.

## Encounter and Threat Register

- Dynamic soldiers and patrols: living Freeflame, old-Church, Accord, and Grey Pike forces.
- [[Hollow Sentries]]: copied sentries, repeated casualties, command-bound patrols, and Sabine's double.
- Muster hound: a Hollow Sentry patterned from a dead war dog and handler, used as a pursuit encounter rather than a separate species.
- Repeating architecture: stairs, orders, wounds, and doors governed by [[The Hollow Muster]].
- [[Othmar Vale]]: culpable commander who can surrender, testify, or fight.
- [[Magistra Sabine Krail]]: unique memory engineer and final antagonist.
