# Hollow Keep

Hollow Keep is a compact two-story watch-fort controlling the Dreywater bridge and the most reliable wagon road between a Korrheimer canton and northern Brauvalis. Its formal post-1343 title is **Accord Watchhouse Seven**, but nobody outside government schedules uses it.

The name “Hollow” predates the present garrison. Soldiers attribute it to wind in the hill, old tunnels, or the feeling that someone else has just left a room.

## Strategic Value

The keep controls:

- the Dreywater stone bridge;
- a road suitable for artillery and merchant wagons;
- two signal lines into Korrheim and Brauvalis;
- a customs yard and winter storehouse;
- the nearest defensible crossing for several leagues.

Holding it does not win a war. It determines which side can begin one without first building a bridge.

## Physical Structure

- **Outer wall:** partly rebuilt after the Wars of Reform, with a climbable southern break retained as an emergency sally route.
- **Gatehouse:** heavy doors, murder passage, toll office, and barracks for contract auxiliaries.
- **Main yard:** stables, kitchen access, well, wagon space, and the only route large enough for relief formation.
- **North and east ramparts:** signal tower and Freeflame auxiliary stations.
- **West chapel wing:** chapel, infirmary, clerk's room, library, and old-Church quarters.
- **Command tower:** officer rooms, dispatch loft, and sightlines over both roads.
- **Defender passages:** narrow corridors, peepholes, concealed armories, and exits intended for a loyal remnant after occupation.
- **Sealed undercroft:** the impossible chambers of [[The Hollow Muster]].

## Accord Settlement

After [[The Korrheim Accord]], neither neighboring government trusted the other with exclusive control. The fort therefore uses a mixed structure:

- a small core of [[Accord Wardens]];
- rotating Freeflame and old-Church auxiliaries;
- separate worship provisions;
- shared road, beacon, and rescue duties;
- a rule that neither confessional banner may fly from the command tower.

The arrangement is awkward but has prevented open fighting for seventeen years.

## The Buried Crime

The keep stands over the site of the Night of Broken Bells in 1338 AR, when a forged no-quarter order turned a prisoner exchange into a massacre. The confession register, cracked bell, and repeated final duties formed a soul-route thin space.

The Accord sealed the undercroft without publicly assigning blame. See [[The Hollow Muster]].

## Story Use

Hollow Keep can remain a joint frontier base, become a partisan occupation, serve as a player stronghold, house a joint supernatural commission, or collapse into a ruin whose bridge remains strategically valuable.
