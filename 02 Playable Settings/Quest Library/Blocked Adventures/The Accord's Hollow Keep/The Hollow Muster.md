# The Hollow Muster

The Hollow Muster is a soul-route thin space beneath [[Hollow Keep]]. It repeats the Night of Broken Bells, a failed 1338 AR parley in which contradictory commands caused Freeflame and old-Church soldiers to massacre one another.

It is a historical wound behaving as local law, not a portal machine or general method of resurrection.

## Four Anchors

- **The cracked bell:** accepted and transmitted all three commands.
- **The confession register:** connects names, rites, stations, kin, and burial instructions.
- **The final orders:** admit parley, hold every post, and the forged command of no quarter.
- **The buried geometry:** courtyard, infirmary, chapel, and passages where the deaths occurred.

Damaging one anchor changes the repetition. Resolving or destroying all four ends it.

## What It Can Copy

The Muster preserves:

- final intentions;
- trained habits;
- spoken orders;
- witnessed memories;
- familiar faces and uniforms;
- wounds and fear associated with specific places;
- incomplete relationships contained in available testimony.

It does not preserve an entire soul, a lifetime of inaccessible memory, or genuine growth after death.

## Present-Day Replacement

[[Magistra Sabine Krail]] discovered that adding a living name and personal pattern object to the old register lets the Muster fit a dead station around a current person.

The result has two parts:

- the living original becomes entranced below, repeating the duty or wound;
- a [[Hollow Sentries|Hollow Sentry]] appears above with enough memory to pass routine inspection.

The copy fails under improvisation because it has only the memories Sabine supplied and those observed by the thin space.

## Escalation

- **Pressure 1:** old sounds, repeated footsteps, incorrect dates.
- **Pressure 2:** isolated sentry doubles and short spatial loops.
- **Pressure 3:** multiple living replacements, rooms adopting their 1338 arrangement, bell heard without movement.
- **Pressure 4:** the massacre repeats across the whole keep and everyone present risks assignment to an old role.

## Resolution

The cleanest resolution is witnessed cease-watch: recover the forged orders, bring living representatives of both confessions, name the dead without separating their worth by rite, and complete Hannes Vale's interrupted signal.

Other resolutions include breaking the bell and register, reconsecrating the wound jointly, sealing it under dual custody, or allowing one confession to suppress the other. The last option produces temporary control rather than healing.

## Canon Boundary

The Hollow Muster establishes that concentrated death, contradictory duty, repeated history, and identity records can create a soul-route thin space. It does not make every battlefield capable of producing perfect duplicates or provide a reproducible army without the same rare conditions.
