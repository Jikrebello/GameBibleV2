# Othmar Vale

Captain Othmar Vale is the acting commander who reopened the sealed undercroft of [[Hollow Keep]]. He is an experienced Brauvalian marcher officer, an old-Church communicant, and the elder brother of Hannes Vale, whose true death during the Night of Broken Bells was hidden from their family.

## Grievance

Othmar discovered Hannes's name in a damaged casualty list while stationed at the keep in 1352 AR. Official records placed Hannes's death elsewhere and described it as an honorable defense. The discrepancy convinced Othmar that the Accord rested on a deliberate lie.

He was right about the lie and wrong about what should follow from it.

## Fall

[[Magistra Sabine Krail]] promised that [[The Hollow Muster]] preserved enough of Hannes to restore him. Othmar used a routine relief command to return to the keep, broke the undercroft seal, and concealed the first disappearances.

When wardens resisted, he:

- forged incompatible orders to isolate the Freeflame and chapel companies;
- killed Warden-Captain Rohe during an attempted beacon signal;
- hired the [[Grey Pike Company]] outside normal authority;
- allowed Sabine to copy current soldiers;
- continued after it became clear that Hannes could not remember a whole life.

## Character

Othmar is disciplined, ashamed, exhausted, and still dangerous. He does not enjoy cruelty and no longer believes his own claim that the situation is controlled. His remaining defense is that stopping now would make every death since the breach meaningless.

He is protective of the Hannes echo and responds violently to mockery. Honest recognition that the echo bears real testimony but is not his whole brother can reach him.

## Surrender Conditions

Othmar can surrender when:

- a living original is found below its apparent double;
- Hannes admits the limits of his memory;
- Sabine's notes prove she treated Othmar as access rather than partner;
- trial and recovery are offered instead of summary execution;
- the party commits to recovering the remaining wardens.

He remains accountable even in the strongest redemption outcome. His testimony can prevent war, but does not erase Rohe's death or the forged orders.

## Continuation

Othmar can become a prisoner and historical witness, a penitent frontier officer under guard, a fugitive carrying Hannes's echo, or an antagonist who decides every imperfect copy merely needs more memory taken from the living.
