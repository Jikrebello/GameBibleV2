# Grey Pike Company

The Grey Pike Company is a small Korrheimer contract-company specializing in gate security, convoy defense, and holding contested structures until larger forces arrive. Its captain is **Rika Tarn**, a veteran of canton feuds who treats a precisely limited contract as protection against becoming someone else's disposable army.

## Hollow Keep Contract

[[Magistra Sabine Krail]] hired the company to protect an antiquarian excavation at [[Hollow Keep]]. The written terms cover:

- gatehouse and yard security;
- prevention of unauthorized descent;
- protection of Sabine and her equipment;
- withdrawal if regular armies approach;
- no participation in confessional enforcement.

Sabine concealed the replacement of living soldiers and paid half the fee with silver stolen from the chapel strongbox. Both facts constitute breach in Rika's judgment.

## Company Character

The Grey Pikes are mercenaries, not neutral saints. They take property allowed by contract, charge desperate clients heavily, and may leave a losing employer once terms permit. They also despise commanders who lie about the enemy or use contracted troops to conceal murder.

Rika expects respect, direct speech, and safe conduct for wounded. She can be bought, but cannot be bought twice in the same matter without destroying the company name.

## Hollow Keep Roster

- **Rika Tarn:** captain and negotiator.
- **Bors Feld:** senior pike, suspicious of all clergy.
- **Meira Hoss:** crossbow leader and company reader.
- **Toman Rusk:** cook and stretcher-bearer who first notices repeated sentries.
- five additional pikes and one wounded digger.

## Adventure Outcomes

- withdraw after receiving lawful payment or proof of breach;
- hold the gate neutrally while the party descends;
- join the rescue in exchange for ordinary salvage;
- fight if attacked, cheated, or treated as confessionally disposable;
- become temporary custodians if every formal command is compromised.
