# Chief Keeper Milena Shore-Wake

Milena Shore-Wake is chief keeper of [[Celion Light Station]] and the senior local ally in [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]]. She is a Saltborn technician, household head, rescue officer, and lay custodian of the station's Naris chapel. None of those duties makes her ornamental. When the lower station falls, she keeps the raiders from turning its light upon the convoy.

## Keeper's Life

Milena entered station service as an apprentice at fourteen and has worked the Celion bearing for thirty-two years. Her byname comes from a winter rescue in which she followed the change in shore-break after the beacon vanished behind sleet and found a broken fishing boat by sound.

She commands a mixed household of trained keepers, apprentices, boat hands, and relatives. Halmenth's harbor authority confirms her office; the Church blesses the lamp and chapel; neither tells her how to read a dirty lens, a dragging counterweight, or a current that has moved since last winter.

Milena believes a lighthouse is a promise made by people on shore to people they cannot see. Saint Naris gives that promise a sacred language. Maintenance gives it a working wick.

## During the Seizure

The Black-Cord landing comes through the flood caves during a keeper watch change. Milena gets herself and lamp-tender Ana Salt-Braid into the lantern chamber and bars the iron door. From there she:

- extinguishes the main lamp before the raiders can redirect it;
- holds the manual brake against their outer-shutter adjustments;
- uses the emergency bell until its lower rope is cut;
- prepares the destruction lever that will drop the optic into its sand trough if the door fails.

She knows members of her household are below and hears the raiders threaten them. She still refuses to cast the false light. Her refusal is not indifference to the captives; it is the judgement that surrendering the beacon would add an entire convoy to them.

## Character

Milena is exacting, unsentimental under pressure, and warmer once the work is done. She trusts demonstrated competence quickly and excuses carelessness slowly. She is angry with [[Petar Reef-Braid]] before she knows the whole story and remains angry afterward, but she will use his skill to save the convoy before arguing about judgement.

She does not romanticize the old Atherian machinery. The sun-glass is difficult, valuable equipment. If breaking it is the only way to prevent a false bearing, she breaks it.

## Adventure Uses

- explains the station layout, signals, and destruction lever;
- restores the lamp while the party holds the gallery;
- aligns the true bearing with Petar or the chart-room record;
- leads an improvised living signal if the optic is broken;
- provides an honest account of Petar's attempted warning without concealing his earlier access.

## Continuation

Milena can oversee the rebuilt station, train temporary pilot-boat crews while it remains blind, testify in Petar's case, and become Halmenth's practical authority whenever a later story concerns beacons, wrecks, or missing ships.
