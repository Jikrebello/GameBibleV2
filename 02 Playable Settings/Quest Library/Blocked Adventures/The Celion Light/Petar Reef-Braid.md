# Petar Reef-Braid

Petar Reef-Braid is a licensed Saltborn pilot, the compromised insider in [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]], and the man whose knowledge makes the seizure of [[Celion Light Station]] possible. He is guilty of a willing betrayal and then coerced into a much greater crime he tries to prevent.

## Before the Raid

Petar grew up on the Celion bar and earned his byname by reading the pale lines where cross-currents braid over the reef. His elder brother died when a coast-defence cutter foundered. The household's service payment remained delayed while its boat stayed under levy, leaving Petar to support his widowed sister and her son Luka.

The grievance is genuine but not singular. Petar also wanted money, resented harbor officers who still called upon his skill, and enjoyed believing he could sell one secret without becoming the kind of man who sells a coast.

He gave a Qazmari intermediary the station watch changes, supply day, tide-door routine, emergency marks, and the tide route into [[Black Fig Cove]]. He believed the buyers intended a smuggling passage and the theft of naval stores. He asked too few questions because the answer might have cost him the payment.

## When He Refused

Petar learned the true target only after the Black-Cord landing: the Lantern Week convoy, including pilgrim ferries and local families. He introduced errors into the false-bearing calculation and attempted to warn Chief Keeper Milena. [[Captain Nadira bint Safwan]] caught the attempt, placed Luka among the cave prisoners, and forced Petar to continue under a signal-master's watch.

At the signal gallery, Petar is still resisting in ways small enough to survive: a wrong shutter interval, a slipped counterweight mark, a plausible recalculation. Those delays are why Milena can keep the lamp dark until help arrives.

## What He Knows

- every safe and false route across the Teeth;
- how to set [[The Celion Sun-Glass]] from the signal gallery;
- the hidden keeper stair to the caves;
- Nadira's skiff plan and the *Veiled Heron's* waiting position;
- which parts of the seizure came directly from the information he sold.

## Character

Petar is skilled, tired, defensive, and capable of courage after cowardice. He does not confess cleanly while Luka is in danger. He admits the facts needed to stop the raid, minimizes the sale if pressed, and only tells the whole story once the nephew is safe or the written annotations make denial useless.

He should not be flattened into either traitor or victim. Without him, Nadira could not have taken the station so cleanly. Without his later resistance, the false light would already be burning when the party arrives.

## Adventure Uses

- Petar can restore the true bearing faster than anyone except Milena.
- He can deliberately complete a harmless false alignment to deceive the signal crew.
- His testimony identifies the Black-Cord approach and Nadira's sponsor.
- His presence makes the party decide whether immediate competence and later justice can be held apart.

## Judgement

Petar can confess and make restitution, submit to public trial, remain protected by a lie that he was only a captive, die stopping the raid, or flee. Rescue does not automatically absolve him. Punishment does not require denying that he finally chose to resist.
