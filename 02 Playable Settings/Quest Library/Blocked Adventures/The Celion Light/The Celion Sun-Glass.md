# The Celion Sun-Glass

The Celion sun-glass is the fixed Atherian optic at the heart of [[Celion Light Station]] and the central environmental mechanic of [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]]. It is a machine for making one maintained flame visible across miles of dark water. Misaligned, it becomes a convincing lie about where safe water lies.

## Construction

The device combines:

- a thick segmented lens of pale gold Atherian glass;
- a lamp cradle built around a steady oil flame;
- mirrored outer shutters that shape the bearing;
- counterweights that rotate the light at a fixed interval;
- colored emergency screens;
- a manual brake in the lantern chamber;
- an emergency lever that drops the optic into a sand trough to prevent seizure or fire.

The glasswork magnifies heat and light beyond ordinary lenses but does not create either from nothing. It needs fuel, cleaning, calibration, and trained keepers. It is too large and fragile to serve as portable treasure.

## Operating Requirements

A correct signal needs three things:

1. a working flame and intact optic in the lantern chamber;
2. the rotation brake and counterweights released at the proper interval;
3. the outer shutters aligned to the current Celion channel from the signal gallery.

[[Chief Keeper Milena Shore-Wake]] can operate the chamber. [[Petar Reef-Braid]] can align the bearing from memory. The chart room contains enough recorded measurements for another skilled pilot to do so more slowly.

## Beacon States

- **Dark:** no usable bearing; safest against enemy control but dangerous to approaching ships.
- **False:** the lamp works while shutters cast the beam toward the Teeth.
- **True:** three white sweeps mark the current safe channel.
- **Destroyed:** the optic has cracked, burned, or dropped into the sand trough and cannot be restored during the adventure.

## Hazards

- Direct exposure to the focused beam causes temporary blindness and can permanently injure an unprotected observer.
- A stopped rotation concentrates heat upon one shutter, frame, or person.
- Oil spilled in the gallery can turn the tower stair into a chimney.
- Broken lens segments remain hot and razor-sharp.
- Forcing gears under load can drop a counterweight through multiple floors.

These hazards are physical. No will, curse, soul, patron, idol, or Undertow presence inhabits the glass.

## Adventure Uses

- align the true channel and save the convoy directly;
- cast a false readiness signal to deceive the Black-Cord crews;
- blind an approaching skiff at the risk of damaging the station;
- keep the lamp dark while living pilots guide the ships;
- destroy the optic to deny it to the raiders;
- improvise around its loss with fires, bells, boats, and direct pilotage.

## Repair and Value

Routine damage can be repaired by Halmenth's keepers and Kedravan metalworkers. A cracked primary lens requires an imperial workshop, Sestaran glass expertise, or an optic salvaged from another Atherian beacon. The delay would leave Halmenth dependent upon pilot boats and daylight convoys for weeks or months.

The device's value is infrastructural rather than saleable. Breaking it may save lives tonight and endanger shipping for a season, which is why [[Chief Keeper Milena Shore-Wake|Milena]] treats destruction as a real sacrifice rather than a forbidden outcome.
