# Celion Light Station

Celion Light Station is the outer beacon of Halmenth and the principal location of [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]]. It stands on **Saint Naris's Tooth**, a basalt reef-islet six miles west of the Celion estuary, where the safe channel bends between the mainland shoals and a reef called the Teeth.

The station is a practical installation first. Without its bearing, a local pilot can pass the reef in daylight and fair weather, but an arriving convoy at night must shorten sail, scatter, or trust imperfect soundings. During Lantern Week, when the first concentrated traffic of the season reaches Halmenth, a dark or false beacon can kill hundreds.

## Saint Naris's Tooth

The Tooth is a steep wedge of black rock, low enough for winter spray to cross its eastern quay and high enough for the lantern to clear the outer haze. The station occupies its eastern and central ridge. Its western face drops into [[Black Fig Cove]], a natural cleft invisible from Halmenth's ordinary approach.

The islet has no spring. Keepers collect rainwater in an Atherian cistern and receive fuel, food, replacement glass, and letters by supply boat. A narrow kitchen garden survives behind the chapel wall during mild seasons, more stubbornly than productively.

## The Keeper Household

The station is maintained by a resident household rather than an isolated hermit. At full strength it contains a chief keeper, two trained lamp-tenders, two apprentices, a boat-keeper, and temporary relatives or coast-watch sailors. Service passes through trained families but is confirmed by Halmenth's harbor authority and blessed through the Naris chapel.

[[Chief Keeper Milena Shore-Wake]] commands the present household. Keeper authority covers the lamp, station stores, rescue work, and the immediate safety of the Tooth. It does not make Milena a general magistrate or priest.

## The Four Levels

### Tide Door and Stores

The eastern service quay enters through a heavy tide door. Wet stores, lamp fuel, repair tools, rescue lines, and the rain cistern occupy the lowest level. A concealed keeper stair descends from behind the repair bench into the flood caves.

### Keeper Hall and Naris Chapel

The household sleeps and eats on the second level. The chart room records reef bearings, wrecks, current marks, and every change to the channel. A storm-resistant chapel contains the coast's register of unreturned ships and a plain mosaic of Saint Naris walking into dark water.

### Signal Gallery

The third level contains the rotation gears, counterweights, fog bell, emergency screens, and controls for the outer mirror shutters. A trained pilot can change the bearing here without entering the lantern chamber, which is why the station can be weaponized.

### Lantern Chamber

The upper chamber houses [[The Celion Sun-Glass]], its maintained flame, the manual rotation brake, and the emergency destruction lever. An iron door and exterior maintenance walk let the chamber be held even when the lower station falls.

## Signals

- **Three white sweeps:** the true Celion channel is open.
- **White-red-white:** shoal or wreck in the inner passage; wait for a pilot.
- **Continuous red:** harbor closed by storm, fire, or military order.
- **Dark with three bell groups:** the lamp is disabled but keepers remain alive.

The Black-Cord seizure begins by preventing every one of these signals, making the absence itself Halmenth's alarm.

## Adventure Condition

At the beginning of [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]], [[The Black-Cord Company]] holds the lower three levels. Milena and Ana Salt-Braid are barricaded in the lantern chamber; other keepers are dead, hidden, or imprisoned in the caves. [[Petar Reef-Braid]] is forced to align the shutters while Captain Nadira prepares the false channel.

## Aftermath

The station can emerge intact, damaged, or blind. Replacing ordinary shutters and gears takes weeks. Replacing the Atherian sun-glass requires imperial workshops, Sestaran glass expertise, or salvage from another beacon and can leave Halmenth dependent upon living pilot boats for a season.
