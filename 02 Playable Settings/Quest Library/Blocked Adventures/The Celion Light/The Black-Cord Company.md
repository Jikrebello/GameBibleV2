# The Black-Cord Company

The Black-Cord Company is the Qazmari privateer band that seizes [[Celion Light Station]] in [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]]. It is a ship-company centered upon the *Veiled Heron*, not a branch of a universal Qazmari navy and not a representative body for the Qazmari people.

## Name and Membership

Members bind a length of black cord around one wrist when they take a company share. The cord recalls Qazmari mourning cloth and the company's promise to ransom or recover its own. It also lets mixed ship-kin recognize one another in boarding smoke.

The company recruits from Qazmari ports, orchard valleys, fortress households, and sailors adopted into ship-kin through service. Most follow the Sealed Blood. A few Witnessed-Seal or foreign sailors serve under recognized private agreements. Their common identity is the ship and its share, not a claim that every Qazmari belongs to one maritime culture.

## Ordinary Work

The *Veiled Heron* escorts cargo, carries messages between western-strait ports, ransoms captured crews, and takes prizes under privateering leave. Its members have fought Ättar raiders, Qahirid rivals, smugglers, and Thalmyric patrols. Some of that work is lawful defence. Some is predation made respectable by a sponsor's seal.

Captain [[Captain Nadira bint Safwan|Nadira bint Safwan]] turned several successful seizures into the company's current reputation. Shares are paid reliably, wounded members remain on the roll, and the company does not abandon its own captives cheaply. Those genuine internal goods coexist with its willingness to enslave and ransom outsiders.

## The Celion Landing Party

Nadira takes twenty-eight people ashore:

- eight station guards;
- six signal saboteurs and deck-fighters;
- eight cave guards and skiff crews;
- four reef divers;
- Nadira and her signal-master.

The *Veiled Heron's* sailing crew remains beyond the reef. The landing party knows it is wrecking civilian vessels and preparing irons for survivors. Individual members may surrender, hesitate, or protect a captive, but the operation itself is not an innocent misunderstanding.

## Command

- **Captain Nadira:** sets the objective, shares, and withdrawal point.
- **Signal-master Harun Veyz:** oversees [[Petar Reef-Braid]] and the false alignment.
- **Four boat leaders:** command guards, divers, cave crews, and skiffs; each can withdraw a section if Nadira is cut off.
- **The ship council:** senior share-holders aboard the *Veiled Heron* can leave without Nadira if the coast response makes recovery impossible.

The company is disciplined but not fanatical. Removing signals, boats, and confidence in extraction breaks it more effectively than killing every member.

## Aftermath

If destroyed, surviving Black-Cord sailors fragment into other western-strait crews. If Nadira escapes with no prizes, the company loses standing and may seek one decisive recovery. If it takes ships or captives through the Black Channel, it becomes wealthy enough to finance a later ransom or pursuit story.

Proof that Nadira exceeded her privateering leave gives Qazmarid reason to disown her without making the western-strait rivalry disappear. Proof that her sponsor encouraged the civilian wrecking can instead turn a local raid into a diplomatic crisis.
