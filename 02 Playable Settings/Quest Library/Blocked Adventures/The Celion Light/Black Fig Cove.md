# Black Fig Cove

Black Fig Cove is a flooded cleft beneath the western face of Saint Naris's Tooth and the lower adventure site of [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]]. From open water it appears to be a shadow below an overhang. At favorable tide, shallow boats can pass through a low basalt arch into a chamber large enough to hide four skiffs and the inspection cutter *Naris's Gull*.

## The Name

Saltborn keepers call it **the Soot Cleft**. Black Fig is the [[The Black-Cord Company|Black-Cord Company's]] recognition name, chosen for the dark, pinched opening and for the orchard-country from which several of its sailors come. Recovering the phrase from a raider reveals that the company knows an approach absent from public harbor charts.

## Cave Structure

- **The sea arch:** passable by skiff at ordinary tide and dangerous for a larger hull.
- **The boat pool:** a deep central basin with rings cut into Atherian masonry.
- **The prison ledge:** the highest dry shelf, used during the raid to hold Luka, two keepers, and the twelve-person cutter crew.
- **The keeper stair:** a concealed passage rising to the stores of [[Celion Light Station]].
- **The storm gate:** an old shutter that can release water from a surge pocket across the boat pool.
- **The signal niche:** a screened lamp and horn position with line of sight to the *Veiled Heron* beyond the reef.

## Ordinary Use

The keepers use the cave to store storm spars, recover wreckage, and shelter a rescue boat when the eastern quay is impossible. Its existence is not a state secret, but the exact tide route is restricted to station households and senior Halmenth pilots. [[Petar Reef-Braid]] sold that route to Captain Nadira.

## During the Raid

[[Captain Nadira bint Safwan]] uses the cove as her command post, prison, and launch point. Black-Cord crews repair four boarding skiffs here and prepare hooks, nets, tow lines, and empty irons for the approaching convoy. The *Veiled Heron* remains outside because it cannot safely enter.

The cove creates a practical tradeoff. Flooding or blocking the arch traps the raiders but may destroy the best boats available for convoy rescue. Preserving the boats gives the party reach while leaving Nadira a route to open water.

## Aftermath

If the cove's role becomes public, Halmenth can gate it, post a second keeper watch, or retain it as a controlled rescue harbor. Sealing it completely makes the lighthouse easier to defend and harder to supply in winter storms.
