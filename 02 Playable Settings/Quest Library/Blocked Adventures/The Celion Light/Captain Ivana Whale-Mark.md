# Captain Ivana Whale-Mark

Ivana Whale-Mark is a Saltborn pilot-boat captain, master of the *Little Wake*, and the party's route to Saint Naris's Tooth in [[The Celion Light — Full Adventure]]. She fills the adventure's essential captain role: the local professional willing to sail when the harbor has good reasons to be afraid.

## Background

Ivana belongs to a baptized Saltborn service family whose boats have carried pilots, lamp fuel, messages, and rescue lines around the Celion mouth for generations. The Whale-Mark byname began with an Ättar forebear and now belongs to a thoroughly Kedravan household whose church gifts are carved with whale-tail knots.

She owns the *Little Wake* jointly with two sisters and a cousin. The boat is a shallow, broad-beamed pilot cutter built to cross the inner reef, not chase privateers in open water. Ivana earns most of her living putting foreign captains through the Celion passage and spends the dangerous part of it answering wreck bells.

## The Dark-Beacon Alarm

When Celion Light misses its sweeps, Ivana begins unloading cargo and spare rigging before anyone hires her. The navy's nearest suitable patrol craft cannot arrive before the convoy, and heavier harbor boats cannot safely cross the reef without the beacon. The *Little Wake* can.

Ivana needs a boarding crew because a pilot boat's deckhands cannot retake a tower while also keeping their vessel off rock. She offers transport, reef knowledge, and a way home. She expects the party to handle the occupied station.

## Character

Ivana is dry, decisive, and uninterested in dramatic speeches while a tide is moving. She gives instructions once and assumes adults understand that the sea will enforce them. She has no wish to die, but she will burn or ground the *Little Wake* as a signal if that is the only way to turn the convoy.

Her first loyalty is to people in the water, including enemies who have dropped their weapons. She will abandon a chase to pull a raider from the reef and then bind that raider for trial without seeing a contradiction.

## Adventure Uses

- identifies the false outer buoy and safe alternative approaches;
- holds the *Little Wake* under the Tooth while the party lands;
- draws off a Black-Cord skiff or recovers swimmers;
- pilots the inspection cutter or a captured skiff during the convoy finale;
- provides the clearest voice against chasing Nadira while civilians remain at risk.

## Continuation

Ivana can become a recurring coastal patron, navigator, transport ally, or witness to the party's conduct under rescue pressure. If the *Little Wake* is lost saving the convoy, Halmenth can replace it through public shares without making that sacrifice costless to her family.
