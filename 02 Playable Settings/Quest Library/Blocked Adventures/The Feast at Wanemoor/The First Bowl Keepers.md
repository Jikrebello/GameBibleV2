# The First Bowl Keepers

The First Bowl Keepers are Merewick's inner sacrificial cult. Twenty-four adults know that the [[The Reed-Crown Feast|Feast]] ends with a human victim at [[Hollow Acre]] and that [[The Reed Mother]] is more than village symbolism.

## Membership

Membership passes through selected household heads rather than whole families. A Keeper's spouse or adult child may know nothing. This preserves secrecy and prevents investigators from treating blood relation as proof.

The inner offices are:

- **First Keeper:** [[Reeve Gerwin Holt]], host and living-seed bearer;
- **Bowl Keeper:** Widow Alke Sorn, carrier and store controller;
- **Root Tenders:** four adults maintaining Hollow Acre and captive bleeding;
- **Path Keepers:** six adults controlling maze cuts, ferries, and procession knots;
- **Table Keepers:** twelve adults who manage public food, stories, and selection.

## Doctrine

The Keepers teach that Merewick survives because one person is made to carry everyone else's hunger. They call the victim honored, remove the name from ordinary records, and describe the resulting abundance as proof of consent.

They know captives resist. The language of honor is therefore concealment rather than misunderstanding.

## Methods

- control hidden feast stores and household allotments;
- rehearse one account of every disappearance;
- select isolated strangers, dissenters, or Feast Daughters;
- keep most villagers away from Hollow Acre after dark;
- use Reedbound households for surveillance and abduction;
- move the First Bowl separately from the victim;
- threaten starvation rather than explain the Mask's future cost.

## Limits and Divisions

Several Keepers joined to protect family access to food. Others value authority, the cult's intimacy, or the power to define who belongs. Two Root Tenders know Gerwin concealed the worsening seed failures and may surrender when shown that the feast no longer secures a future.

Defection can save lives and provide evidence. It does not erase participation in prior abductions or murders.

## Relationship to Wider Evil

The Keepers are not a cell of [[The Long Table]]. They receive no outside orders, finance, or theology. Their repeated mortal choices independently created a Gluttony architecture.
