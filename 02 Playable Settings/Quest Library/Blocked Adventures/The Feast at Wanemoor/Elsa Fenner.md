# Elsa Fenner

Elsa Fenner is a seventeen-year-old Merewick resident selected as Feast Daughter during [[The Reed-Crown Feast]]. Publicly, she will carry first seed to the chapel and spend a year learning household duties. The Reedbound adults in her family know she may instead disappear if no outsider is available.

## Family Plan

Elsa's parents, Rudolf and Anke, intend to substitute a visitor. They ask Elsa to draw one party member away while relatives wait with nets, sleep draught, sickles, and trained blood-kites.

Elsa initially cooperates because she has been taught that one death is unavoidable. She wants to live and loves her family. Neither fact gives her or them the right to choose another victim.

## Warning Signs

Elsa's public seed tale omits every prior Feast Daughter said to have left Merewick. She wears a Reed Crown containing one green-glazed bead. She knows the safest procession route to [[Hollow Acre]] and has heard [[Brother Matthias Veit]] coughing beneath the old farm.

At Village Trust 2, she warns a party member not to sleep alone. At Trust 3, she reveals the substitution plan before the ambush. If treated only as a liar or cultist, she follows her parents until their plan fails.

## Possible Choices

Elsa may:

- expose the family plan and guide the party;
- freeze during the ambush and surrender afterward;
- flee toward the chapel or fen;
- become the fallback sacrifice if Matthias is rescued;
- accept induction into the First Bowl Keepers out of fear;
- testify against her parents while still pleading for their lives.

No response makes her responsible for the older murders. Her participation in luring a victim remains an act requiring truth and restitution if she carries it through.
