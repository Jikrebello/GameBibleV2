# The Open Lanterns

The Open Lanterns are thirteen Merewick adults who share food, keep disappearance names, and try to restore outside contact. They are a mutual-aid circle and resistance network, not a rival secret theology.

## Origin

Midwife and chapel keeper **Marta Lenz** began comparing baptism, marriage, and departure claims after two young adults supposedly left without collecting wages. Ferryman **Jost Rell** added channel logs that contradicted the village account. Freeflame and old-Church households joined for practical reasons despite confessional differences.

## What They Know

The Lanterns know:

- at least nineteen disappearances follow Reed-Crown years;
- Holt House and the smokehouses control more food than public marks show;
- Hollow Acre receives night traffic before the final procession;
- several Reedbound families are terrified of Feast Daughter selection;
- [[Brother Matthias Veit]] discovered the same distribution problem.

They do not know Matthias is alive or that the Reed Mother is an Idol Mask when play begins.

## Methods

- leave one chapel lamp visibly unshuttered when someone needs shelter;
- copy names into psalm margins and midwife records;
- move food to omitted households;
- preserve ferry and peat-cart logs;
- hide letters in outgoing reed bundles;
- keep an evacuation punt beyond the ordinary landing.

## In the Adventure

At Village Trust 2, Lanterns share names and shelter witnesses. At 3, they keep the chapel open during the cult mobilization. At 4, they organize ordinary defenders to hold the ferry, stores, or prisoners while the party enters Hollow Acre.

The Lanterns need outside help but are not passive. Their resistance establishes that local isolation did not erase knowledge of good, duty, or alternatives.
