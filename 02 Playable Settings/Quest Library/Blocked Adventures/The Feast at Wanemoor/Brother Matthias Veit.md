# Brother Matthias Veit

Brother Matthias Veit is a diocesan almoner sent into [[Wanemoor District]] to distribute salt, medicine, and grain grants while reviewing parish poor relief. He is the missing official whose search begins [[The Feast at Wanemoor — Full Adventure]].

## Work in Merewick

Matthias reaches [[Merewick]] six weeks before the party. He finds:

- a full chapel poor-box during a failed district harvest;
- feast stores larger than the public household allotments;
- seven hungry households omitted from distributions;
- a repeated pattern of Reed-Crowned young people supposedly leaving without clothes or wages;
- no ferry record matching several claimed departures.

His first letter reaches his sister Hanne. He hides a second list in the chapel poor-box and tries to send a fuller account. [[Reeve Gerwin Holt]] intercepts him before it leaves.

## Captivity

The First Bowl Keepers hold Matthias in the root cellar at [[Hollow Acre]]. They bleed him in controlled amounts to strengthen the Mother-Root while preserving him for the final sacrifice. He remains lucid but weak when the adventure begins.

Matthias has learned that Gerwin carries a root-seed and that the First Bowl is moved separately from the procession. He has not identified the Mask as a Gut-Maw and initially calls it a false field spirit.

## Character

Matthias is patient, observant, and practical. He cares more about named hungry households than proving that his office was right. Captivity has frightened him without making him passive. He scratches distribution marks into the cellar beam and sabotages one root channel with lamp salt.

If rescued, he insists that ending the rite and preserving winter food are one duty, not competing moral options. He supports judgement for specific crimes and rejects collective punishment of Merewick.

If he dies, his body, hidden list, beam marks, and notes preserve the investigation. His death is a loss, not a required key that ends the adventure.
