# Merewick Winter Margin

Merewick Winter Margin tracks whether ending the Reed Mother's false abundance is followed by enough practical work to keep the village alive. It separates moral necessity from logistical consequence: destroying the sacrificial system is right, and the winter still has to be survived.

## Scale

Begin at **1** and track from 0 to 3.

- **3 - Winter secured:** stores, routes, and relief cover the vulnerable season with strict but adequate rationing.
- **2 - Hard ration:** hunger and illness rise, but organized relief prevents general displacement or death.
- **1 - Breaking stores:** households evacuate, sell tools, slaughter breeding stock, and suffer avoidable illness.
- **0 - Winter loss:** exposure, hunger, or chaotic evacuation kills residents unless extraordinary outside aid arrives.

## Raising Margin

Gain one, to a maximum of 3, for each completed support:

- preserve the public granary and common smokehouses;
- recover the hidden Hollow Acre stores without fire, taint, or armed seizure;
- send a relief signal and keep either the Reedway or ferry open.

Additional creative support can replace one of these: securing a monastery barge, organizing an evacuation before flood, exchanging recovered cult silver for grain, or obtaining district seed stock.

## Losing Margin

Reduce Margin for:

- broad fire through Hollow Acre or Merewick's stores;
- cult sabotage of smokehouses or ferry;
- allowing an armed faction to seize all food as leverage;
- planting Mother-fed seed that fails after consuming labor and time;
- delaying relief until winter water closes both routes.

## What Survives the Mask

Existing grain, smoked food, roots, oil, and livestock remain real after the Reed Mother is destroyed. Mother-fed seed and spawning beds have been depleted and cannot be trusted for spring. The urgent problem is therefore both winter calories and replacement seed.

The First Bowl silver and Holt House goods can support relief, but seizure must preserve evidence and household necessities. Punishing innocent families through confiscation simply recreates exclusion under a different authority.

## Independence from Wanenbruck's Adventure

Relief can come through the district bailiff, parishes, Freeflame poor funds, monasteries, guild stores, or private kin. It does not require any selected outcome from [[The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure]]. If Wanenbruck is itself in crisis, the source and travel time change without invalidating this mechanic.
