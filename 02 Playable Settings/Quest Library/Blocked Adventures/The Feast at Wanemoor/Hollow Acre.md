# Hollow Acre

Hollow Acre is an abandoned dye farm and raised peat field beyond Merewick's ordinary work paths. The First Bowl Keepers cultivate the Mother-Root, hold captives, store food, and perform the concealed final procession there.

## The Dye Farm

The sagging house and barn are presented as a memorial to families lost during the Three Wet Years. In practice they contain:

- weak floors above flooded dye vats;
- a root cellar used for prisoners and hidden stores;
- cult ledgers and victim tokens;
- trained blood-kites in reed cages;
- a natural rot-heap feeding on dye waste and slaughter refuse;
- strangler vines cultivated across the rear approach;
- a loft route into the reed maze.

The plants and animals are Material life. Cult feeding and neglect make them dangerous without turning them into worshippers or extensions of the Reed Mother's mind.

## The Reed Maze

Cut reeds, drainage channels, and raised strips form a maze between farm and ritual island. The layout follows practical field logic beneath cult alterations:

- water flows away from the raised center;
- ordinary evacuation cuts lead toward the ferry;
- procession knots point inward;
- root growth thickens along routes where victim names were removed;
- imitation voices favor paths that isolate or delay.

The First Bowl Keepers use cross-cuts for ambush and withdrawal. Feastlings arrange food and equipment into serving roles near the inner ring.

## The Dry Island

The center holds a stone table, drainage ring, Mother-Root, and socket for [[The First Bowl]]. The bound victim is placed where root channels can carry blood into the field before first light.

The Mother-Root and concealed winter stores lie dangerously close. Broad fire can destroy the visible Mask but also kill captives, spoil food, and ignite dry peat beneath the maze.

## Entrances and Exits

- memorial path through the front yard;
- shallow punt landing behind the smoke reeds;
- loft bridge into the outer maze;
- drainage crawl from an abandoned eel channel;
- procession path opened after the public feast.

Failure at one entrance raises Cult Alarm or moves captives. It does not erase the other routes.
