# Merewick

Merewick is an isolated fen village in eastern [[Wanemoor District]], reached by a raised road called the Reedway and by shallow channels from [[Wanenbruck]]. About 180 people live in 43 households spread across peat islands, reed beds, rye strips, smokehouses, and eel-water.

The maintained journey from Wanenbruck takes half a day in dry weather. Flooded culverts and reed-choked channels can turn it into two days by punt. This difficult access weakens visitation and trade without placing Merewick outside Brauvalian authority or Eclessian culture.

## Land and Work

- **Raised rye and bean strips:** narrow fields banked above ordinary fen water.
- **Reed beds:** roofing, baskets, mats, fuel, boat caulking, and sale to Wanenbruck.
- **Channels and eel pots:** fish, eels, lamp oil, and shallow transport.
- **Peat cuts:** winter fuel and a dangerous source of collapse and preserved remains.
- **Pigs and blood-kites:** the livestock most tolerant of wet ground and parasites.
- **Smokehouses:** fish, pork, mushrooms, beans, and roots preserved for winter.

Merewick imports salt, metal tools, cloth, and emergency grain. One broken road or missed relief barge can therefore change an austere winter into hunger.

## Authority and Faith

A village reeve keeps land custom, work obligations, and district correspondence. Household heads meet at the common shed during flood, fire, and harvest decisions. Serious crime remains subject to the district bailiff and Brauvalian courts.

The Reed Chapel is old-Church in law but lacks a resident priest. A chapel keeper maintains the altar, poor-box, and burial register between visits. Several Freeflame households keep protected psalm books and share the same building by local toleration. Both confessions recognize the Redeemer, White Flame, murder's wrong, and the duty to feed the poor.

## Known Places

- **Reed Chapel:** worship, poor relief, names, and public shelter.
- **Holt House:** reeve's dwelling, guest rooms, and feast records.
- **Common Smokehouses:** village winter stores and distribution marks.
- **North Ferry:** shallow route toward Wanenbruck when the Reedway floods.
- **Reed-Crown Ground:** public games, table, and harvest procession.
- **Hollow Acre:** abandoned dye farm and memorial field beyond the ordinary village paths.

## The Reed-Crown Feast

Merewick holds an annual [[The Reed-Crown Feast|Reed-Crown Feast]] during Reaping Week, roughly three weeks before Souls-month. Public customs include raised-way races, eel-pot judging, seed stories, reed-torch leaping, a communal table, and naming a Feast Daughter to carry seed to the chapel.

These observances are not automatically evil. Creator-facing truth about the concealed final procession belongs to [[The Feast at Wanemoor — Full Adventure]]. Public canon does not select whether the village, its leadership, or its feast changes afterward.

## Story Uses

Merewick supports flood rescues, winter relief, missing ferries, disputed peat finds, confessional cooperation, household feuds, medicinal animal customs, smuggling, failed roads, and horror arising when a real community good is made to conceal predation.
