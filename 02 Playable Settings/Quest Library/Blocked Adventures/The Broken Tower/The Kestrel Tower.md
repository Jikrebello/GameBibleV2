# The Kestrel Tower

The Kestrel Tower is an earthquake-split Atherian-Eclessian frontier fort near Dornwatch, and the delving site of [[The Broken Tower]]. It is a textbook Tier One dungeon: a long-sealed ruin with three garrisons' worth of dead in its history, cracked open by a quake, now holding old residue, a vermin flock, a squatter, and somebody's lost strongbox. It is exactly the kind of place the delver trade runs on.

## History — Three Layers of Dead

The tower changed hands three times across two centuries of border war, and each garrison buried its dead in place before the next took the walls. When the last war moved on, the fort was sealed, its charter filed with the Bank, and its death-soaked stone left to drain quietly into stillness. It nearly finished draining.

Two seasons ago the ground shifted. A quake split the tower, cracked its sealed floors open for the first time in living memory, and stirred the settled residue back to motion — as quakes do to old death-soaked stone. Within a month the vermin came; not long after, so did the exile.

## The Delve — Five Floors

The tower rises in floors rather than spreading in halls, which makes it the cleanest possible teaching dungeon: climb, clear, take the stair up.

- **Floor One, the Cracked Gate:** the quake-split entrance; grave-rats, corpse-beetles, a rattle-bones or two. Iron for bones. A lost purse in the rubble.
- **Floor Two, the Roost:** open to the sky, colonized by a [[Harridans]] flock; the delve's big vermin fight. Beyond it, the old armory — iron and a little silver.
- **Floor Three, the Cold Floor:** the officers' interments; barrow-shades in the dark. Silver for shades. The interment-stones name the tower's three owners.
- **Floor Four, Torvald's Line:** lived-in; a barred door, cook-smoke, snares from fort debris. The first place to talk instead of fight.
- **Floor Five, the Top:** broken and open to the wind, where [[Torvald Ghost-Foot]] keeps the surveyor and makes his stand.

## The Residue

The tower's dead are [[The Mundane Layer — Residue, Vermin, and the Delver Trade|Tier One residue]] — shed echoes of three garrisons, animated because the quake broke the rites and years that had drained them. They are not the people who died here; those souls routed long ago. Putting them down is sexton-work: iron, silver, a torch, and a consecration after if a priest is passing. No theology required, which is the whole point of the site as a tutorial.

## The Ownership Question

The fort's old title is disputed between the ducal march, the Bank file that last surveyed it, and Folk descendants whose holdings were cut by later boundaries. That dispute does not need resolution during the rescue. Until masons stabilize the quake damage and clergy settle the disturbed dead, no responsible authority should award occupation.

## Environmental Features

- quake-cracked floors and unstable rubble; falling hazards on a botched climb;
- the open roost and open top, exposed to weather and to noise carrying;
- fort debris everywhere — usable as barricade, snare, and improvised silver;
- interment-stones that quietly narrate the tower's three-owner history;
- narrow stairs that make the vertical delve a series of controllable choke-points.

## Aftermath Uses

The Kestrel Tower can become a stabilized ducal watchpost, a jointly administered border site, a sealed ruin, or a later contested holding. Those are follow-on stories rather than rewards for completing the rescue.
