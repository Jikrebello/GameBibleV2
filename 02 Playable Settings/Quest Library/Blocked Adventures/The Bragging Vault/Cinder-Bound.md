# Cinder-Bound

The Cinder-Bound are fire-spirits bound into service in Vorthara's geothermal district — the worked heat of the vents given just enough shape and will to hold a valve, mind a forge, or guard a threshold. They are not summoned genies and not free elementals. They are labor: fire under contract, sealed by the Crafters' Union valve-crews who run the district's heat. In [[The Bragging Vault]] a set of Cinder-Bound is assigned as the [[The Ashplume Vault|Ashplume]] gauntlet's **exit toll** — the guardians that light when a party tries to carry prizes out of the vent.

## Nature

A Cinder-Bound is a coherent knot of vent-fire held to a task by a binding-seal. Away from its heat-source and its seal it gutters; fed and bound, it persists for years. It has appetite (fuel, motion, spectacle) and something like pride in its post, but no lineage, ecology, or soul. It is closer to a very willful machine than to a person, though the line blurs in old bindings that have "kept their post" long enough to develop preferences.

They are native hazard, not monster: the district is full of them, mostly minding forges and steam-tolls without incident.

## The Toll

Marlo set the Ashplume Cinder-Bound as a scaling exit challenge, kept from the original gauntlet's logic: the more prizes a party removes, the harder the vent fights to keep its show. Carrying one arm out may pass unremarked; carrying two or three lights the toll, and the Cinder-Bound bar the exits until paid.

The toll is not only blood. It can be met by:

- **coin** — the binding includes a fuel-and-fee schedule the Union honors;
- **wager** — Cinder-Bound love a contest and will bargain passage against one;
- **spectacle** — a display worth broadcasting satisfies a toll built to feed a show;
- **manumission** — proof that a Cinder-Bound's binding-seal has expired frees it, and a freed spirit has no reason to hold a gate.

## Signs

- vent-heat that moves with intention, gathering at thresholds rather than rising freely;
- a valve, forge, or gate that "watches" and reacts to prizes leaving more than to people arriving;
- a Crafters' Union seal-plate nearby, dated, sometimes lapsed;
- fire that bargains — pausing for a wager or a fee where a wild flame would only burn.

## Behavior and Tactics

- hold thresholds and choke-points rather than pursue;
- escalate with the value carried out, not with the intruders' hostility;
- fight with heat, backdraft, and the vent's own geysers and flues;
- pause instantly for a credible wager, fee, or show, because the binding rewards these;
- turn on the binder, not the party, if released mid-toll by a proven expired seal.

## Binding and Manumission

The district's valve-crews keep every Cinder-Bound's seal and schedule. A binding has a term; past it, holding the spirit is theft of labor, and the Union will not defend it. A sharp party that recovers the Ashplume seals — often stored in the outer pipe-galleries — can turn a lethal toll into a manumission, freeing the Cinder-Bound and walking out through an open gate.

Freeing them is not pure mercy: a released Cinder-Bound is loose fire with a grievance, and where it goes next is the district's problem.

## Moral and Legal Status

Bound service in Vorthara is ordinary and lightly examined. A Cinder-Bound minding a forge raises no eyebrows. A Cinder-Bound set to kill people leaving a lethal audition is the same instrument pointed at a worse end — the wrong is the binder's, not the fire's. Destroying one ends a hazard; freeing one ends a servitude and starts a small fire somewhere else.

## Quest Uses

- an expired seal turns a boss-toll into a bargaining scene;
- a freed Cinder-Bound becomes a volatile guide back through a vent it knows;
- a valve-crew hires the party to recover a Cinder-Bound that "walked off its post" after manumission;
- a rival uses lapsed seals to steal a competitor's bound labor;
- a Cinder-Bound that has kept one gate for decades refuses freedom, because the post is all the self it has.
