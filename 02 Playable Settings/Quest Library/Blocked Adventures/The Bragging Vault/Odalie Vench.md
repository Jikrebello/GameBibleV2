# Odalie Vench

Odalie Vench is a Pale Hand **under-factor** — a lending-and-collections officer of Vorthara's necromantic faction — and the second antagonist of [[The Bragging Vault]]. She did not build the vault and she does not run the show. She wrote a loan clause, and the clause harvests the dead for her. When anyone declares victory in the [[The Ashplume Vault|Ashplume Vault]], Vench walks in with a registrar and files.

## The Clause

The [[The Pale Hand]] lent [[Corsin Marlo]] the money to reopen and rebuild Ashplume, secured against the vent. The Pale Hand's standard **sub-floor clause** grants it title to any body that dies below the mortgaged level. It is a clean, legal, and monstrous instrument, written to catch desperate ventures at the moment they cost someone their life.

Vench administers dozens of such loans across the geothermal district. Ashplume is her best-performing note, because it was built to kill contestants and then broadcast the killing as sport, and every fallen team pays the loan in bodies.

## The Harvest

The teams that ran the gauntlet before the party and did not come out did not disappear. Vench raised them as [[Vault-Claimed]] — the guards, the champions in the dark, the set-pieces the party now fights. She folded the labor cost of staffing the dungeon into the mortgage. From her ledger it is elegant: the failed applicants guard the audition that will produce the next ones.

She conceals the raised-contestant register, because a sub-floor claim built on deaths caused by an undisclosed, lender-built hazard will not survive the arena bond-court.

## Character

Vench is calm, precise, and entirely without theater — the opposite of the Vaunt, which is part of why they grate on each other. She does not gloat and she does not hate the people she claims. She regards a death below her floor the way a miller regards grain: it is simply what the arrangement produces. That composure is the horror of her, not a mask over it.

She would genuinely prefer the party sign, win, and leave quietly, so the vault keeps running and the harvest keeps yielding. She resents Marlo recruiting away the labor she is trying to grow.

## Negotiation

Vench can be moved by leverage, never by appeal:

- Proof of the Mortgage and the raised-contestant register threaten the whole clause, and she will trade release of specific Vault-Claimed to keep the instrument intact elsewhere.
- She can be turned against Marlo, whose overdue payments and public launch endanger her quiet note.
- She will accept a bond-court settlement that voids Ashplume's harvest if it preserves the sub-floor clause as Pale Hand doctrine — a bad bargain for the party to accept uncut.

She refuses any outcome that brands the sub-floor clause itself as unlawful, because that is the Pale Hand's business model and not hers to surrender.

## The Standing Danger

The clause is the point. Even a total victory at Ashplume — arms recovered, contestants freed, Marlo ruined — leaves Vench free to move the same instrument to the next over-leveraged vent in the district. Ending Ashplume's harvest is a rescue. Ending the clause is a campaign.

## Continuation

Vench can become a discredited factor pulled back to Vorthara for review, a lasting and patient enemy of a party that voided her note, a reluctant witness against the sub-floor clause if the bond-court squeezes her, or a Reaper's rising star if the party lets the harvest stand.
