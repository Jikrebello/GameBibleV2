# The Ashplume Vault

The Ashplume Vault is a live fumarole in Vorthara's geothermal district, reopened under a new lease and rebuilt into a broadcast gauntlet by the impresario [[Corsin Marlo]]. It is the setting of [[The Bragging Vault]]: three prize-branches, a set of signature hazards, and a scrying rig that turns the whole run into a saleable spectacle. It is beautiful in its billowing white plume from a distance and unmistakably industrial once you are inside its heat.

## The District

Ashplume sits among the vents and fumaroles that heat the Flesh Pits and drive Vorthara's arcane forges. The approach is a working landscape of pipe-galleries, steam-tolls, and Crafters' Union valve-crews. The vent was dark for years before Marlo took the lease; its sudden reopening, and the loan that funded it, are district gossip.

## The Boasting Door

The vault opens through a bronze door cast in Marlo's smiling likeness. It recites his boast aloud, scores the party's entrance like a bout, and will not open until they give it a good enough show. Scrying-eyes in the door's crown begin the broadcast; from here the run is watched and sold.

## Layout

- **The Junction:** a heat-shimmering hall under three banners, splitting into the three prize-branches. The promise is honest — any order.
- **Branch A, the Mud Gallery:** a floor of boiling mud crossed by hung brass disks between timed geysers, ending in a vault of conjured darkness where a [[Vault-Claimed]] champion keeps [[The Trophy-Arms|Cairn]].
- **Branch B, the Boiling Pool:** a near-boiling geothermal pool holding a sunk blown-glass bubble, guarded by a Fleshweaver crab that will not crack the glass. [[The Trophy-Arms|Ovation]] waits inside.
- **Branch C, the Stepped Menagerie:** an inverted stepped structure rising through the heat, each tier stocked with arena-stock beasts, topped by the champion **Gorreth** and [[The Trophy-Arms|Forfeit]].
- **The Glazed Run:** a frictionless volcanic-glass corridor studded with steam-pits, connecting the branches.
- **The Against-Current Flumes:** steam-tubes that carry water and riders upward, used by the scout **Wisp Aldery** to appear, grade, and vanish.
- **The Green Room:** the warm, lamplit chamber the geometry funnels winners into, where the offer and the claim are made.

## Function

Ashplume is three things at once, and the party rarely sees all three at first:

- a **prize gauntlet**, as advertised on the handbills;
- a **talent audition**, scried and broadcast so Marlo can scout whoever wins;
- a **body-farm**, because the [[The Pale Hand]]'s sub-floor mortgage grants title to everyone who dies below the floor.

The hazards are engineered for spectacle, not defense. The vent does not want to kill efficiently; it wants to kill *well*, on camera, in a way that sells.

## Environmental Features

- timed geysers and boiling mud that punish poor pace, not poor luck;
- a glass bubble whose only protection against the boil is not breaking it;
- frictionless glass and against-gravity flumes as arena sports the audience bets on;
- scrying-eyes throughout, feeding the broadcast and the scout's grading tally;
- [[Cinder-Bound]] worked into the heat, set as an exit toll that lights when prizes leave;
- Crafters' Union valve-seals in the outer galleries — some expired, a lever for freeing the Cinder-Bound.

## The Two Hidden Ledgers

- **The audition rig** proves the run is scouted, not sporting — the scrying feeds, Wisp Aldery's grading tally, and the lapsed broker's-license paper that ties the "theft" to Marlo.
- **The mortgage register** proves the harvest — the Pale Hand loan seal, the sub-floor clause, and the raised-contestant roll showing the guards are the previous teams.

Either ledger, recovered, changes the finale. Both together carve the Vaunt and the Pale Hand apart.

## Aftermath Uses

Ashplume can become a suppressed and re-leased vent, an impounded evidence site under the bond-court, the founding venue of the party's own arena stable, a freed workshop staffed by released contestants, or a dark fumarole the Pale Hand quietly re-mortgages to the next hopeful.
