# Valcori Passage and Extraction Company

The **Valcori Passage and Extraction Company** finances ships, frontier crossings, company claims, and wage advances from Castara. Merchant Prince Serena Valcori built her position by combining transport with extraction: a worker who cannot afford passage can sign against future claim wages, and a claim that needs labor receives a ready crew.

## Legitimate Function

Valcori provides services without which many expeditions could not leave Castara:

- passage shares and equipment advances;
- claim registration and assay;
- supply ships and storm insurance brokerage;
- wage crews and specialist recruitment;
- sale of extracted crystal to Vorthara and the University;
- return passage for workers who complete terms.

Many workers knowingly accept difficult bargains and return richer than they left. The company is not a disguised supernatural contract cult.

## Structural Abuse

Valcori controls the price of passage, camp supplies, and extracted material. A worker can be paid fairly at every line and still discover that the company sets every line.

Managers receive bonuses for quota recovery and low declared loss. Hazard reports threaten a claim's insurance and every supervisor's advancement. The system therefore rewards people who avoid asking whether worked stone, missing hours, or bird glyphs require closure.

## Claim Seventeen

Serena Valcori did not know a Nythalum Return Annex lay beneath Morrow Atoll. Her office did receive a report mentioning worked stone and a clear geometric crystal sample. It promised double quota credit and praised rapid reopening without asking for a ruin survey.

Assayer Leone Baret removed the bird mark before transmission. The company can therefore deny informed approval while remaining responsible for incentives designed not to learn inconvenient facts.

## Serena Valcori

Serena is decisive, charismatic, and capable of honoring obligations once they are made undeniable. She prefers compensation and improved procedure to admissions that establish legal precedent.

Her desired outcome is a bounded accident finding, rescued workers returned to revised contracts, and Claim Seventeen reopened under University wards. Public bird-mark classification threatens several other insured ventures and makes her resist the strongest truthful report.

## Aftermath Paths

- **Reform:** discharge Claim Seventeen debts, establish ruin-stop authority independent of quotas, and fund survivor care.
- **Containment:** compensate only proven survivors and reopen through a subsidiary.
- **Exposure:** lose claims, insurance standing, and Merchant Council influence.
- **Expansion:** acquire Nythalum memory technology and become more powerful than before the disaster.
