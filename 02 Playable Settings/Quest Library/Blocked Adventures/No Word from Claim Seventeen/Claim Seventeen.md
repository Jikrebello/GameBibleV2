# Claim Seventeen

Claim Seventeen is a crystal extraction camp on Morrow Atoll near the eastern edge of the Shattered Isles passage. Its formal designation is **Valcori Morrow Claim 17**, but crews, insurers, and family letters all shorten it to the number.

## Surface Geography

Morrow Atoll is a broken volcanic ring around a deep storm lagoon. Claim Seventeen occupies the only reliable landing shelf. The atoll provides shelter during ordinary weather and becomes a trap when hurricane currents close the channel.

Important surface sites are:

- the dock and signal tower;
- the palisaded work camp;
- the assay house and stores;
- the modern spiral mine;
- Bird Ridge above the volcanic shelf;
- Iria Stone's western lava tubes;
- the swept seabird rookery;
- three rain cisterns connected to ancient drainage.

## Claim Economy

The claim extracts pale song-crystal used for navigation, rune scripting, ward resonance, and University instruments. Its forty-three residents include wage workers, specialists, passage-debtors, and company staff.

Passage-debtors receive food, bunk space, medical service, and credited wages against their crossing. The terms are legal by Castaran standards. They become abusive when quotas, company pricing, and concealed hazards make repayment impossible or uninformed.

## Gallery Seventeen

The lowest gallery intersected worked Nythalum stone. Foreman Marta Serrin closed it briefly. Assayer Leone Baret removed the bird glyph from the outgoing report, and extraction resumed after Valcori's office praised the clear crystal sample.

The breach awakened a Return Annex governed by [[The Curator Below]]. Four weeks later the camp disappeared.

## The Silence

Claim Seventeen missed three scheduled supply calls. Its surviving ledgers end on Tuesday. Hushwrights returned tools, meals, cards, and books to the last positions they could reconstruct from stolen memories.

The tidiness is not aesthetic. The annex understands an inspection-ready site as one free of unindexed life, unexplained damage, and objects outside assigned positions.

## Bird-Mark Boundary

The local annex bears a bird-shaped inspection glyph matching the old chart symbol. This establishes a link for Claim Seventeen only. It does not resolve every Bird-Marked Site in the Emerald Expanse.
