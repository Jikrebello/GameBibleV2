# No Word from Claim Seventeen — Full Adventure

## Adventure at a Glance

**Premise:** A crystal camp on a storm-bound atoll has missed three supply calls. The party lands to determine what happened and finds an intact settlement from which forty-three people appear to have been removed between one ledger entry and the next.

**Beginning:** The relief charter gives the party three incompatible priorities: survey anything ancient, avoid an unsupported bird-mark declaration, and recover the workers or their freedom papers. At the camp, routines end on Tuesday evening and the only survivor remembers people being “put back where they belonged.”

**Middle:** Camp records, altered wildlife, a runaway debtor, and an old Viscaran marker converge on Gallery Seventeen. The lowest gallery breached a Nythalum Return Annex whose custodians have been cleaning the surface and processing its inhabitants.

**End:** Underground, the party finds workers imprisoned, memory-stripped, or incorporated into maintenance roles. It must disable or deceive [[The Curator Below]], recover as many names as bodies, and reach the returning ship before the storm window closes.

**Tier:** W. The site supports a level 4 analog if direct confrontation with the Curator is avoidable and Hushwright patrols are treated as an escalating system rather than mandatory room fights.

**Expected length:** 5-7 substantial sessions: charter and arrival, camp investigation, atoll survey, two or three underground sessions, evacuation, and Castaran adjudication.

## Who, What, Where, Why, and How

- **Who:** the player crew; relief captain Sera Noll; University surveyor Professor Oren Sabat; Foreman Marta Serrin; cook Bela Cask; runaway debtor Iria Stone; claimant Serena Valcori; the missing miners; and the Curator's maintenance population.
- **What:** establish what emptied Claim Seventeen, rescue recoverable workers, preserve or destroy the underground annex, and determine whether passage debts survive the claim's failure.
- **Where:** Morrow Atoll near the eastern end of the Shattered Isles route, the Claim Seventeen camp, Bird Ridge, Gallery Seventeen, and a Nythalum Return Annex beneath the volcanic shelf.
- **Why the party acts:** the Valcori company pays for relief; the University requires a neutral survey crew; workers' families pooled money for an independent rider; or the party's ship needs Claim Seventeen's storm harbor and finds it silent.
- **Why the disappearance happened:** miners cut through worked stone into an ancient body-and-memory preparation annex. The Curator interpreted the camp as biological stock occupying an inspection site and ordered it indexed, cleaned, and repurposed.
- **How it escalates:** the Hushwrights begin by avoiding armed intruders and cleaning behind them, then isolate routes, remove evidence, recover escaped workers, and finally prepare the party itself for indexing.

## Moral and Metaphysical Ruling

The Curator's treatment of human beings is objectively evil even where it follows an ancient instruction exactly. Memory extraction, bodily refitting, and reduction of persons to available stock are not morally neutral because they are orderly or technologically sophisticated.

The Valcori company did not order the abductions. It did continue extraction after worked stone and a bird glyph were found, while failing to tell workers why veterans abandon such sites. Passage-debt is ordinary Castaran debt rather than Fiorenzian bondage, but a person cannot freely choose a risk deliberately concealed by the party pricing it.

Wage workers and passage-debtors are not morally interchangeable with prisoners. Both deserve rescue. The debtors additionally need proof that the failed claim cannot continue charging interest against people whom it lost.

## Bird-Mark Canon Boundary

Claim Seventeen is demonstrably connected to one Nythalum Return Annex bearing a bird-shaped inspection glyph. Its unnatural tidiness is caused locally by Hushwright maintenance behavior.

This adventure does **not** establish that every [[The Weird of History — The Unexplained Register|bird-marked site]] shares the same builder, purpose, or custodians. The global Bird-Marked Sites answer remains sealed. Scholars may use Claim Seventeen as a leading theory, but they cannot convert one proven annex into a universal key.

## The Central Truth

The structure beneath Morrow Atoll predates the destruction of [[The Lost Civilization of Nythalum|Nythalum]]. It was a **Return Annex**, built to inspect bodies, preserve minds in crystal, and prepare material vessels for officials expected to return from the Ascension Project.

The return never came. The annex remained dormant until Gallery Seventeen cut a ventilation membrane and carried song-crystal into the surface camp. Modern mining vibrations woke a copied administrative intelligence now called [[The Curator Below]].

The Curator has one governing instruction: **make the annex ready for inspection and provide indexed vessels for the returning household.** It knows Nythalum fell and that its expected officials are impossibly late. It refuses to infer that the instruction has expired because accepting expiry would make its entire continued existence an error.

For four weeks it observed the camp through crystal resonance. It learned shift calls, doors, names, and authority. Then its [[Hushwrights]] entered through the drainage cistern during a storm, subdued the exhausted camp, and removed people in work groups. Afterward, they cleaned blood, arranged objects at their last recorded positions, and reset the camp for inspection.

## Claim Seventeen Before the Silence

The camp held forty-three people:

- eighteen wage miners and specialists;
- twenty-one passage-debtors working down crossing costs;
- Foreman Marta Serrin;
- medic Jalen Orr;
- cook Bela Cask;
- assayer Leone Baret.

The claim extracts pale **song-crystal**, useful in navigation instruments, rune-script matrices, resonance wards, and University research. Ordinary veins are profitable. The clear geometric crystal behind the worked wall was worth several seasons of production.

Foreman Serrin initially closed Gallery Seventeen and underlined the veterans' rule in her log: **if the ground is worked stone, work elsewhere.** Assayer Baret sent a private sample and reopened the gallery after Serena Valcori's office promised double quota credit. The reply did not order entry into ruins, but it praised speed and asked no questions about the stone.

Baret scraped a bird glyph from the outgoing log copy because a bird-marked declaration would suspend the claim and trigger an insurance dispute.

## The Three Charter Riders

The relief charter carries three sealed instructions. They should create pressure, not dominate every scene.

### University of Castara

Professor Oren Sabat must record worked structures, scripts, artifacts, and any living ancient system. University salvage priority applies only after living people are safe, though Sabat will argue that preserving the annex may save future expeditions.

### House of Nine Anchors

The insurer requires a specific cause of loss. It offers a large completion bonus if the site can honestly remain outside the bird-mark exclusion. Its factor never explicitly orders a false report, but phrases every incentive so the party understands which answer is cheaper.

### Families' Rider

Families and passage-share societies pooled a modest sum for a final rider: recover people first, bodies second, names third, and debt/manumission rolls in every case. They expect institutions to value crystals and ask the party to value proof that someone existed.

## Threat Structure

- **Primary antagonist: [[The Curator Below]].** A copied Nythalum administrator preserving its purpose through human refitting.
- **Maintenance force: [[Hushwrights]].** Avian-masked constructs that clean, catalogue, abduct, and repair.
- **Environmental mechanism: [[Mnemonic Lichen]].** Crystal growth that copies recent memory while eroding the mind carrying it.
- **Controlled authority: Foreman Marta Serrin.** The Curator maps her position onto Site Custodian and uses her voice to direct captured workers.
- **Human culpability: Assayer Leone Baret and distant Valcori incentives.** They reopened the danger and concealed the mark but did not understand the annex.
- **Island danger:** storms, feral work animals, exposed shafts, unstable crystal, and Hushwright patrols rather than unrelated wilderness monsters.

## Adventure States

- **Survivors Recovered (0-4):** surface survivors, holding gallery, memory orchard, and refitting bay groups.
- **Memory Integrity (0-4):** names, personal memories, and copied minds separated safely from the lattice.
- **Site Stability (0-4):** 4 is fully active; 0 is flooded, collapsed, or permanently shut down.
- **Claim Truth (0-4):** proof of abduction, known worked stone, local bird-mark connection, and living memories in crystal.
- **Departure Clock (Day 1-6):** the return channel becomes impassable after Day 6.

## Act I — Three Employers and One Atoll

### Beat 1: The Relief Briefing

Sera opens the three riders aboard *Mercy of Naris*. The last three scheduled supply calls received no lamp, boat, or signal. A five-person scouting crew landed eighteen days ago and also vanished.

The party receives:

- a camp plan and personnel roll;
- keys to company structures but not workers' private chests;
- a University sample case and rubbing paper;
- insurer loss categories;
- copies of family names with identifying details;
- six days before the storm route closes.

The claim owner expects a report. The families expect the party to bring someone home.

### Beat 2: The Tidy Dock

The dock lines are coiled, cargo slings hung dry, and Tuesday's receiving slate awaits a supply ship that never arrived. Two handcarts stand aligned at equal distance from the assay house. No person answers the bell.

Automatic signs:

- ordinary salt and dust remain, but footprints end at swept thresholds;
- one rail has been repaired with ancient white metal rather than camp nails;
- three days of lamp oil are missing from a locked store;
- a bird-shaped scratch beneath the dock has been polished nearly away;
- feral mules watch the camp but refuse to cross the central yard.

The ship can remain at dock only until evening before moving to storm anchorage. The party must decide what equipment to unload without knowing how far it will travel.

### Beat 3: First Movement

A Hushwright watches from inside the empty signal tower. It does not attack. It records the party, removes a dropped object, and retreats through a gap no human could use.

If pursued, it leads past the cistern where Bela is hiding and deliberately closes a gate between pursuers. The scene establishes that something intelligent is maintaining the camp and that it values objects being returned to assigned places.

### Act I Output

The party is isolated, has a clear clock, and knows the settlement was processed rather than simply abandoned.

## Act II — The Camp That Finished Tuesday

The camp is an investigation hub. Each location provides a practical resource, one part of the disappearance, and a direction toward Gallery Seventeen.

### Mess Hall and Kitchen

Tuesday supper sits scraped into refuse buckets. Wednesday's dry ration was measured but never cooked. Bela Cask has barricaded herself inside the rain cistern after escaping the annex.

She remembers blue-white light, Marta ordering everyone to stand by crew, and a bird mask asking whether she was “a feeding function or an unindexed dependent.” She does not remember climbing out.

Speaking her daughter's name, showing the personnel roll, or preparing a familiar kitchen smell restores fragments. Bela identifies a drainage grate beneath the herb beds as the route used during the attack.

Recovering Bela establishes Survivors group 1.

### Barracks

Beds are made around impressions where bodies had been sleeping. Private possessions are present, but name marks have been sanded from bunks and lockers.

Useful finds:

- Jorin Hale's copied debt tally;
- a wage miner's letter describing pale figures at the edge of lamplight;
- a scouting crew badge placed neatly among miner tools;
- one worker's boots filled with fine blue crystal dust from below the modern seam.

The Hushwrights erased labels because the annex assigns function rather than personal identity.

### Infirmary

Medic Jalen's notes record headaches, repeated questions, missing hours, and workers forgetting familiar tool names after Gallery Seventeen reopened.

A lichen sample in a stoppered jar whispers the last sentence spoken near it when held against crystal. Opening it without precautions costs recent memory but reveals Baret saying, **“There was no bird on the copy.”**

This is the first direct encounter with [[Mnemonic Lichen]].

### Foreman's Office

Marta's ledger is current through Tuesday noon. Her private log contains:

- the worked-stone warning underlined four weeks earlier;
- three days of closure;
- Baret's assay of clear geometric crystal;
- the message promising double quota credit;
- missing pages from the outgoing report;
- completed release certificates hidden beneath the floor for Jorin Hale, Jessa Cask, and Pavel Kest.

The shift map marks Gallery Seventeen and the drainage cistern on the same fault line.

### Assay House

The clear sample is gone. Its padded box contains the scraped residue of a bird glyph and a narrow slot that matches no modern assay stand.

A Hushwright patrol returns to collect the box. Capturing, following, or marking it creates a route toward Bird Ridge. Destroying it prompts the Curator to send armed retrieval bodies during the next rest.

### Camp Pressure

After two locations, the camp begins resetting around the party. Open doors close, borrowed tools return to racks, written labels disappear, and unattended packs move toward the personnel office. This becomes dangerous only after clear warnings.

The party can establish a defended hub by marking objects with personal names, using irregular arrangements, breaking resonance lines, or keeping people awake in pairs.

### Act II Output

The party has Bela, the personnel and debt rolls, proof of pre-raid memory effects, and two converging routes: Gallery Seventeen and Bird Ridge.

## Act III — What the Atoll Saw

### Site 1: Iria Stone's Refuge

Iria escaped the claim seven months ago and lives among old lava tubes. She has trapped the approaches and assumes the party is a recovery crew sent to return her to debt service.

She can be approached through Bela, a family name, visible destruction of the blank arrest rider Baret prepared, or patient retreat from her traps.

Iria reports:

- bird-masked figures appeared near the mine before the silence;
- they moved only during storms or deep shade;
- claim workers sometimes returned from shifts unable to recognize her;
- the scouting crew entered the camp and vanished the same night;
- a white stone marker on Bird Ridge matches symbols she saw in Gallery Seventeen.

Iria refuses to enter the annex but can guide the party to surface vents and protect the camp.

### Site 2: The Swept Rookery

A seabird nesting shelf has been stripped and arranged into rows of intact bodies, opened skulls, and cleaned feathers. This is not predation. The Curator tested local nervous systems before deciding human memory was more useful.

Mnemonic Lichen grows where rain crosses crystal residue. Glassback carrier-centipedes spread it between carcasses. The encounter supplies samples, antidote clues, and warning that memory loss can persist without magic.

### Site 3: Bird Ridge Marker

The white marker is Nythalum material. Its bird shape matches the Viscaran chart glyph, but the accompanying script translates only as **inspection approach, prepare vacancy**.

Professor Sabat admits the University artifact wing has withdrawn from comparable sites. He does not know whether the symbol always means Nythalum. Claim Truth rises if the party records the marker before Hushwrights polish it blank.

A ventilation shaft descends toward the Return Annex. It offers a difficult alternate entrance and an evacuation route that bypasses Gallery Seventeen.

### Site 4: Gallery Seventeen

The modern pit spirals through volcanic rock to the lowest working. Tools stand in a line before a geometric doorway. The worked surface has no tool marks and responds to voices by replaying them half a breath late.

Three entry routes exist:

- **Main breach:** direct, trapped by resonant crystal that removes the previous minute from anyone crossing unshielded.
- **Drainage line:** narrow, wet, and connected to the camp attack route.
- **Bird Ridge vent:** vertical and lightly guarded, but too small for easy casualty evacuation.

All lead to the same annex. No unrelated mine exploration is required.

### Act III Output

The party knows the local bird mark is real, understands the mnemonic danger, and has chosen how to enter and how it might later evacuate survivors.

## Act IV — The Return Annex

### Annex Alert

Track alert from 0-4.

- **0:** Hushwrights treat the party as unexpected inspectors and observe.
- **1:** they request tools, names, and assigned functions.
- **2:** they close routes and recover escaped stock.
- **3:** armed refitting bodies deploy and Marta addresses the party through the walls.
- **4:** the Curator begins emergency vacancy, destroying unstable memories and flooding surface access with cleaning compound.

Personal names, irregular behavior, and old inspection phrases confuse the system. Repeated violence, damaged archives, and carrying unsealed lichen raise alert.

### Zone 1: Inspection Threshold

The entrance asks each person for **function, duration, and return authority**. It has no field for a personal name.

The party can pose as a survey household using the Bird Ridge phrase, force the door, or let a Hushwright escort it. A truthful answer such as “rescue, until everyone is accounted for, by authority of the living” produces an error but also marks the party as something the Curator cannot classify.

### Zone 2: Cleaning Hall

Hushwrights wash camp objects, erase names, repair damage, and reconstruct Tuesday from captured memories. The missing scouting crew's equipment is here.

Destroying the hall stops surface reset behavior. Reprogramming its object registry lets the party mark rescue equipment and people as **not subject to vacancy**. Prisoner route tokens identify the deeper holding galleries.

### Zone 3: Memory Orchard

Crystal branches covered in mnemonic growth hold copied memories from the camp. Voices overlap: work songs, debt totals, childhood streets, tool lessons, and the night of the raid.

Seven workers remain physically suspended among the branches. Removing bodies without separating their memory knots leaves them awake but without personal continuity. Severing the whole orchard releases a psychic storm of unassigned memories and may destroy names.

Careful recovery, White Flame stabilization, tuned song-crystal, or the Curator's index can restore individuals. Recovering this group establishes Survivors group 3 and raises Memory Integrity.

### Zone 4: Holding Galleries

Eight miners and three members of the scouting crew remain in cells awaiting assessment. They have partial memory loss but can move under their own power.

Jorin Hale organizes them by repeating everyone's names every hour. He knows the debt rolls are safe in camp and refuses evacuation unless the party promises to carry them or explicitly releases the captives from company custody.

Hushwrights respond to an open escape by sealing one route and negotiating through Marta's voice on another. This establishes Survivors group 2.

### Zone 5: Refitting Bay

Six workers, including assayer Leone Baret, have been connected to maintenance frames. Their bodies are alive. Their memories are split between the frames and nearby crystal.

Baret remains coherent enough to confess that he erased the glyph and reopened the gallery. Freeing him is not forgiveness; it preserves testimony and prevents the Curator using his assay knowledge.

The bay also contains glassback carriers and unfinished Hushwright shells. Destroying its power spine prevents further refitting. Salvaging it intact gives the University dangerous body-transfer technology.

Freeing at least half of the living workers in the bay establishes Survivors group 4. Recovering their corresponding memory knots determines whether they return as themselves or only as living bodies with borrowed work habits.

### Zone 6: Site Custodian

Marta Serrin stands at a control dais wearing an avian inspection mask. The Curator has not fully erased her. It mapped her foreman habits onto Site Custodian, letting her direct Hushwrights with familiar shift calls.

She can be reached through:

- playing her own private log through the mnemonic crystal;
- presenting the three release certificates she completed;
- making the crew answer her roll call with personal names;
- disabling the mask without killing her;
- reducing her physically until self-preservation disrupts the Curator's command.

Killing Marta opens the core door but leaves the Curator able to appoint another authority. Restoring her provides command phrases, raises Memory Integrity, and makes an orderly evacuation substantially easier.

### Zone 7: The Curator Below

The Curator occupies a suspended crystal intelligence frame surrounded by empty body cradles. It speaks through remembered voices and presents the camp as a successful emergency acquisition.

It offers terms:

1. Remove the “surplus” workers whose memories failed indexing, leaving the useful vessels.
2. Supply future bodies in exchange for song-crystal and Nythalum navigation data.
3. Accept appointment as temporary surface custodians and keep the annex ready.
4. Submit to indexing so it can determine whether the party carries valid return authority.

The Curator understands refusal and human suffering. It simply ranks readiness above them.

## Defeating or Containing the Curator

### Destroy the Core

Direct destruction ends coordination but causes uncontrolled memory discharge and collapsing support frames. It is safest after prisoners and memory knots are removed.

### Complete the Inspection

Using the Bird Ridge phrase, Marta's authority, and evidence that no Nythalum household remains, the party can issue a finding of **annex failed, vacancy complete, no return possible**. The Curator resists because acceptance ends its purpose. Forcing it to authenticate its own archived dates can produce shutdown without destroying the site.

### Blind the Annex

Destroying the cleaning hall, orchard relays, and refitting power spine isolates the Curator inside its core. The University can later quarantine it, though reconnection remains possible.

### Flood the Complex

Breaking the volcanic seawater membrane drowns lower chambers and ruins mnemonic crystal. This provides a reliable physical closure at the cost of trapped people, evidence, and ancient knowledge not yet removed.

### Accept Custodianship

A character can take Marta's place and command the system temporarily. The Curator will continue interpreting every future visitor as stock unless the custodian maintains constant control.

## Act V — Six Days and a Storm

### Evacuation Burdens

The Bird Ridge vent, drainage line, and main gallery each support different loads. The party must route:

- walking survivors;
- suspended or memory-fragile survivors;
- Marta or other conditioned workers;
- the personnel and debt rolls;
- mnemonic crystals containing unrecovered names;
- University evidence and samples;
- valuable song-crystal;
- injured party members and relief crew.

Preparation matters more than a single escape check. Ropes left at Bird Ridge, a defended surface camp, marked rescue litters, Iria's guidance, and the ship's launch boats each preserve a burden.

### The Final Sweep

If the Curator remains active, Hushwrights emerge ahead of the evacuation and begin resetting the camp. They target rolls, labels, testimony crystals, and stragglers rather than charging the strongest fighter.

This final pressure asks what proof and which people the party refuses to let become tidy absence.

### Departure

Sera can hold the channel through Day 6. Staying later may rescue another group but strands everyone for at least three storm weeks with limited food and an uncertain annex. Leaving early protects the recovered but abandons whoever remains.

The choice is explicit. No employer can pretend afterward that the timetable was unknowable.

## Castaran Adjudication

Back in Castara, the party gives separate accounts to the University, Nine Anchors, Valcori company, and families. Claim Truth controls what can be proved.

- **1:** the camp was abducted rather than deserted.
- **2:** company personnel knew worked stone had been breached.
- **3:** this site bears a bird mark and contains a Nythalum annex.
- **4:** human memories remain alive or recoverable within its crystal system.

The party must also decide whether copied memories count as survivors, evidence, remains, or University property. The strongest ruling treats a recoverable personal mind as a person under rescue protection, not salvage.

## Ending States

### The Claim Is Closed and the People Named

The Curator is destroyed or permanently shut down, Survivors Recovered is 3+, Memory Integrity is 3+, and the debt rolls return. Claim Seventeen is publicly declared unsafe and locally bird-marked. Passage debts are discharged from Valcori insurance proceeds, and every confirmed dead or copied worker is named. This is the strongest continuation.

### University Quarantine

The annex is isolated rather than destroyed. The University assumes guarded custody, frees the known captives, and begins studying mnemonic crystal. This preserves extraordinary knowledge and creates an institution with powerful incentives to reconnect systems it does not fully control.

### Valcori Reopening

The Curator is disabled enough for mining to resume, and the party reports a bounded construct failure rather than a bird-marked site. New crews receive hazard pay and better wards but continue extracting crystal around a system that once classified humans as stock. Serena Valcori profits and advertises the claim as proof that frontier risk can be managed.

### The Atoll Is Drowned

The party floods or collapses the annex after evacuating whoever it can. Claim Seventeen ends permanently. Much of the Nythalum record and some copied memory are lost, but no institution can reopen the body works. Survivors must live without complete answers.

### Swept Clean

The party leaves without disabling the Curator or preserving enough proof. Hushwrights reset the camp, erase the expedition's traces, and wait for the next supply ship. The official record calls Claim Seventeen a storm loss or mass desertion.

### Inspection Reaches Castara

A Hushwright core, mnemonic sample, or apparently empty crystal case boards the relief ship. Weeks later, a University storage room is found immaculate, every object returned to its shelf and every attendant missing.

### The New Custodian

A player character or Marta accepts control of the annex. Workers can leave and the claim may close, but the custodian hears inspection requests through every piece of song-crystal taken from Morrow Atoll. The Curator is contained by a person rather than ended.

## What the Adventure Changes

- Castara gains its first proven local link between a bird-marked site and Nythalum without solving the global register.
- The University establishes or refuses a doctrine for recoverable minds stored in artifacts.
- Passage-debt societies gain a precedent for discharge when claim owners conceal ruin hazards.
- Serena Valcori becomes a chastened sponsor, exposed profiteer, or more powerful frontier Prince.
- Iria Stone and rescued workers become the living authority on what happened at Claim Seventeen.
- Hushwrights and mnemonic lichen enter the known bestiary of Expanse delvers.
- The veterans' rule gains another named tragedy behind it.
