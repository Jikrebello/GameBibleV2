# The Duke's Road Company

The Duke's Road Company is a temporary mixed workforce chartered by Alaric von Grenzmark to open a western relief corridor from Grenzburg toward Dornwatch. [[Road-Captain Hanne Eber]] commands the company in the field. [[Master Surveyor Oskar Pell]] controls line, grades, and work estimates but not military discipline or local diplomacy.

## Composition

The company combines paid Grenzburger laborers, marcher guards, masons, bridgewrights, charcoal workers, carters, Chapel-Folk guides, and a small number of people working off criminal fines. It is not a penal battalion and has no right to seize local labor. Wages are modest but real; families receive death payments only if names and circumstances are properly recorded.

## Charter

The sixty-day field charter authorizes the company to survey, clear, bridge, quarry, and defend a road corridor. It permits temporary camps and emergency requisition with receipts. It does not grant title to every piece of land touched by a wheel, nor does it extinguish Folk sanctuary, fishery, or refuge rights.

The charter expires when winter weather makes the upper vale impassable. Failure does not abolish the project, but it ends this season's labor and exposes Grenzburg to reliance on a single supply road.

## Internal Pressures

- guards want a defensible ridge route;
- merchants and concession investors want the cheap Straight Cut;
- workers favor whichever line does not kill them before payday;
- local hires want written protection against later trespass charges;
- engineers fear a negotiated route will become impossible to maintain if every repair requires a new moot.

## In Play

Road Progress represents completed, supplied, and maintainable work, not raw miles cut. Optional successes add labor, stone, timber, local guides, or safe working days. Casualties, disease, sabotage, and bad route choices reduce the company's ability to convert time into progress.
