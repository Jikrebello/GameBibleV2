# Magister Caspar Rauk

Caspar Rauk is a rogue siege-warlock occupying [[Cinder Crown]] above [[Alderway Vale]]. He trained first in an Eclessian marcher arsenal and later studied Vortharan Fleshweaver instrumentation without becoming a member of that House. He was dismissed after using live prisoners to test heat-bearing scripts and falsifying the results as battlefield casualties.

## Creed and Crime

Rauk believes sovereignty belongs to whoever can impose a stable system upon danger. He calls the vale ungoverned, the Folk claim sentimental, and Grenzburg's charter timid. His intended proof is simple: let the Duke spend blood opening the corridor, destroy the fort at the moment of success, then offer surviving traffic protection under his own weapons.

His grievance against timid patrons contains facts but no justification. He knowingly uses murder, coerces animals through pain, bargains with predatory Guests, and places workers and refugees inside an elemental kill zone.

## Resources

- a dozen deserters, failed apprentices, and hired climbers;
- the Red Garland's road intelligence and mutilated trophies;
- three to five ruby drakes fitted with pain-and-bearing collars;
- old Atherian signal shafts and cisterns within Cinder Crown;
- the Cinder Seed under Fort Alderwatch;
- prepared heat scripts, false survey marks, and shell-fire beacons.

## Method

Rauk does not control every problem in the vale. He watches ordinary failures and adds pressure where it is useful. His agents poison no well that camp negligence has already fouled. They instead destroy the replacement pump. He did not create the spider colony or the old residue field; he places false trail signs near them. This makes the road seem cursed and hides the narrower pattern of his attacks.

## In Confrontation

Rauk fights as an engineer. He divides space with heat lines, drops shutters, vents furnaces, and forces intruders to choose between pursuit and preventing a discharge. He abandons followers readily but tries to preserve notebooks, law-gems, and the master key to the Cinder Seed.

He may offer to finish the road under his protection. The offer is materially possible and morally corrupt: it creates a private warlock lordship founded on terror. Capture, death, escape, or exposure all remain possible outcomes.
