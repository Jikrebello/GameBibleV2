# Fort Alderwatch

Fort Alderwatch is the road company's stockaded base on a gravel rise above the Alderwater. It is a work camp made defensible, not a permanent castle: two timber towers, an earth bank, crowded barracks, forge, infirmary, storehouse, survey office, animal pen, and an unfinished stone gatehouse.

## Population and Stores

At full strength the fort houses about 170 people: 96 road workers, 24 engineers and tradespeople, 28 guards and scouts, 12 carters and animal hands, and 10 cooks, healers, clerks, and dependants. Another 30-50 workers occupy satellite camps when the road is active.

The fort begins with twenty days of ordinary food, forty days of hard reserve, two weeks of clean lamp oil, six wagon teams, and enough dressed timber to repair either the walls or a major bridge. Those choices matter after losses.

## Key Areas

- **West Gate:** faces the completed road toward Grenzburg and receives supplies.
- **Survey House:** contains route maps, work ledgers, sanctuary testimony, and Oskar Pell's revised straight-line calculations.
- **Forge Yard:** principal heat source and therefore the first attraction for ruby drakes.
- **River Tower:** watches the ford and the low camps; its signal mast can relay to Grenzburg in clear weather.
- **Old Stone Cellar:** remnant of an Atherian road post, cool enough to shelter people during a fire attack.
- **Powder Shed:** stores quarry charges and warlock-prepared ignition rods under separate locks.
- **Sickhouse:** where fouled water, thorn poison, and drake burns can overwhelm the company together.

## Defending the Fort

The stockade stops bandits and animals but was not built for aerial fire. Water barrels, wet hides, cleared roofs, dispersed animals, and a functioning river pump determine whether the fort survives the drake assault. The Cinder Seed is hidden in the unfinished gate foundation, where it can burn both wall and bridge approach.

Fort Condition begins at 3 in the full adventure. Damage changes available support and ending costs; it never prevents the party from reaching Cinder Crown.
