# The Cinder Seed

The Cinder Seed is a delayed Shell-fire siege script planted beneath the unfinished gate foundation of [[Fort Alderwatch]]. It is a network rather than a single bomb: a buried receiver lattice, four heat-bearing roots under wall and bridge, and a master relay in [[Cinder Crown]].

## Purpose

When fully discharged, the Seed flash-heats the gate foundation, ignites the stockade, cracks the river tower, and drops burning fill into the bridge approach. Its intended result is not a mysterious disappearance but a visible military lesson: Rauk can destroy the road whenever he chooses.

The script draws from prepared furnace stones and the Shell. It does not contain a spirit, soul, patron, or trapped elemental. Its metaphysical danger is the use of a powerful engineered script near ordinary people and unstable construction.

## Cinder Clock

The clock begins at **5** when Rauk activates the Seed after the drake assault.

- **5:** buried roots warm; dogs and sleepers avoid the gate.
- **4:** nails sweat rust and the foundation dries despite rain.
- **3:** smoke appears in the gatehouse; the fort can begin evacuation.
- **2:** isolated flare-lines ignite; one section can be severed only at risk.
- **1:** the gate and bridge become unsafe; defenders must withdraw.
- **0:** the Seed discharges.

Delay, retreat, or a failed intervention advances the clock. Destroying the relay, grounding two roots, substituting cooled law-gems, or forcing Rauk to break the pattern can halt or soften it.

## Ways to Stop It

- seize Rauk's master key and shut the relay in sequence;
- flood the gate foundation after opening the old cellar drains;
- ground the roots with prepared iron and White Flame warding;
- isolate the gate and allow a controlled partial burn;
- evacuate the fort and sever the bridge, limiting casualties even if the Seed succeeds.

Failure creates [[The Duke's Road — Full Adventure#Cinderfall|Cinderfall]], not an automatic end to play.
