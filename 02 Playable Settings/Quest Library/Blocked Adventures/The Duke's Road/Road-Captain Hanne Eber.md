# Road-Captain Hanne Eber

Hanne Eber is the field commander of [[The Duke's Road Company]] and commandant of [[Fort Alderwatch]]. She is a professional marcher officer under Castellan [[Castellan Pierre Hohenwall|Pierre Hohenwall]], chosen because she can manage builders, escorts, stores, and frightened civilians without pretending they are one military unit.

## Character

Hanne is direct, dry, and visibly tired. She writes every death to the family herself and refuses to describe workers as replaceable. She believes the western road is necessary for Grenzburg's survival, but she has no authority to turn necessity into ownership of the entire vale.

Her weakness is institutional loyalty under a deadline. When forced to choose quickly, she protects the project before the relationship that makes it sustainable. She initially treats Oskar Pell's route as an engineering question because Pierre approved the survey process, not because she despises Folk claims.

## What She Wants

- finish a defensible supply line before winter closes the vale;
- keep enough workers alive that the road is not built by conscription and graves;
- identify the force murdering her crews;
- return a result to Pierre that the Duke can actually maintain.

## What She Knows

Hanne knows the road is half finished, the budget cannot cover every ideal solution, and the Alder-Crossing claim predates the current survey. She does not know Caspar Rauk survived his dismissal or that the Cinder Seed is under the gate. She recognizes Rauk's siege notation once shown a complete collar plate.

## In Play

Hanne gives priorities rather than forcing an order. She rewards concrete protection, honest reporting, and solutions the company can sustain. She can command the fort during the drake assault, lead an evacuation after Cinderfall, or hold the project together while the party crosses the mountain. If she dies, Quartermaster **Matthes Dorn** preserves logistics but lacks her authority over guards and engineers.
