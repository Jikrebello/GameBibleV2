# Cinder Crown

Cinder Crown is an abandoned Atherian signal and quarry redoubt on the northern rim of [[Alderway Vale]]. Its black-stone crown overlooks the central shelf, Fort Alderwatch, and the three possible road lines. [[Magister Caspar Rauk]] has rebuilt its furnaces and turned its signal shafts into a siege-warlock laboratory.

## Approaches

- **Quarry Stair:** fastest route, watched by deserters and exposed to rolling stone and heat lines.
- **Ash Goats' Path:** narrow southern traverse discovered by hunters; bypasses the lower gate but enters near the drake pens.
- **Old Signal Shaft:** a climb through the collapsed beacon flue; dangerous smoke and masonry, but access to Rauk's script gallery.
- **Water Cut:** a cold cistern channel exposed if the party secured old Atherian plans or Alder-Crossing guides.

## Levels

1. **Lower Works:** gate, barracks, store caverns, prisoners, and the counterweight controlling the quarry bridge.
2. **Drake Yard:** heated nesting ledges, restraint frames, collar workshop, and an escape opening onto the cliff.
3. **Script Gallery:** copied road maps, Red Garland tokens, signal mirrors, and the relay that drives the Cinder Seed.
4. **Crown Furnace:** Rauk's command chamber around an old beacon brazier. Disabling it safely ends the remote countdown; destroying it violently may trigger a partial discharge.

## Finale Logic

The redoubt is a machine as much as a dungeon. Cooling the cistern, freeing drakes, turning signal mirrors, destroying collar keys, or cutting the quarry lift changes later encounters. Rauk should not wait untouched in the last room if alarms rise; he uses the site, retreats upward, and attempts to preserve the Seed relay.
