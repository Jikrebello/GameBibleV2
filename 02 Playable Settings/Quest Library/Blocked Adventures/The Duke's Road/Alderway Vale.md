# Alderway Vale

Alderway Vale is a forested western corridor north of [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/30 Society and Daily Life/The Folk of the Frontier and the Wolfsmere Lineage.md|Wolfsmere]] and west-northwest of Grenzburg. The vale climbs from wet alder bottoms and fisheries toward a broken northern ridge, then opens onto old roads leading toward Dornwatch and the Brauvalian supply network. Duke Alaric has begun a relief road through it, but the project remains unfinished in 1360 AR.

## The Land

The lower vale is braided by the Alderwater, a cold river that changes channel after spring thaw. Alder woods, reed pools, salmon runs, and gravel islands support scattered Folk households. The central shelf carries old charcoal clearings and the remains of an Atherian military trace. The north ridge is drier, stonier, and defensible, but steep enough to exhaust wagon teams.

Three lines through the vale are physically possible:

- the direct central trace passes through [[Still-Hearth Glen]], the oldest local sanctuary;
- a river-bend way follows firm gravel around the glen but requires bridges, retaining works, and negotiated passage;
- a northern ridge line avoids the sanctuary and the floodplain at the cost of hard grades and a longer construction season.

None is yet the settled public road. A future *Duke's Road* production map should show the project and [[Fort Alderwatch]], not a completed alignment. Alderway remains outside Grenzburg's base-game atlas.

## People and Rights

The [[Alder-Crossing Clan]] holds seasonal fishing, passage, refuge, and sanctuary rights in the lower and central vale. Its rights overlap Chapel-Folk farmsteads, Grenzburger charcoal camps, and older ducal claims to the ruined Atherian trace. The clan speaks for Alderway, not for every Green March kindred.

Temporary passage has long been allowed during famine, Grobi flight, and winter rescue. Permanent banks, toll stations, logging strips, and armed patrols require separate agreement. This distinction is the heart of the present road dispute.

## The Unfinished Road

The Duke's western road company operates from Fort Alderwatch. Its immediate purpose is legitimate: Grenzburg needs a second supply corridor that cannot be cut with the River Grenz road. Its danger is also real. A road can become a relief line, a settlement wedge, an occupied strip, or all three in sequence.

Workers have opened several miles of grade, repaired the old ford, and established quarry and timber camps. Murders, desertion, poor sanitation, dangerous wildlife, local resistance, and unexplained fires have slowed the work. Public accounts do not yet agree on which dangers share one cause.

## Story Uses

- A caravan must cross before the road's jurisdiction and toll rights are settled.
- Spring flood exposes an older causeway, grave field, or forgotten refuge cache.
- A completed section attracts settlers who mistake a right of passage for ownership.
- Alder-Crossing fishers and ducal engineers must cooperate when the Alderwater changes course.
- Fort Alderwatch becomes a rescue station, military post, ruin, or contested market according to later play.
