# The Red Garland

The Red Garland is a murderous host of thirteen [[Blood-Briar Guests]] led by the figure road workers call the **Garland King**. They occupy an abandoned treaty camp in western [[Alderway Vale]] and trade information, ears, and fear to [[Magister Caspar Rauk]].

## Origin

The host formed around a broken hospitality three winters ago. A charcoal factor accepted shelter at the treaty camp, murdered his Folk guide, and burned the body to avoid payment. Blood-Briar Guests gathered where the welcome was inverted. They killed the factor and then chose to keep reenacting the betrayal against unrelated travelers.

Rauk did not create them. He offered heated thorn-beds, metal splinters, and victims in exchange for patrol routes and terror. The bargain made their hunting more organized and gave him deniable eyes along the road.

## Conduct

The Garland marks workers with small thorn wounds, uses familiar voices to split search parties, and removes ears as proof that the road has "heard" its warning. It sometimes leaves a survivor carrying a garland of red briar. The survivor is not mystically guilty, but others often treat the mark as infection or collaboration.

The Garland may parley under a strict threshold. It bargains truthfully about immediate exchanges and maliciously about consequences. It cannot grant Alder-Crossing land rights and has no legitimate authority to negotiate the road.

## Breaking the Host

The host weakens if denied the abandoned treaty hearth, if its bargain-token from Rauk is destroyed, or if the original guide's remains receive witnessed hospitality and burial. These acts remove advantages; they do not force forgiveness or turn the Guests harmless. The Garland King and surviving hunters may still need to be killed, driven out, or bound away from mortal roads.
