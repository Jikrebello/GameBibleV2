# Master Surveyor Oskar Pell

Oskar Pell is the chief surveyor of the Duke's western relief road. He is a competent engineer, a minor investor in two charcoal concessions, and the strongest advocate for the Straight Cut through [[Still-Hearth Glen]].

## Position

Oskar's measurements are honest: the sanctuary shelf is firm, direct, and much cheaper than either alternative. His moral failure is the belief that a sufficiently useful calculation settles whether the line may be taken. He records Alder-Crossing testimony as "seasonal custom" while treating abandoned Atherian milestones as permanent title.

He is not Caspar Rauk's accomplice and did not arrange the worker murders. Rauk copies survey marks and lets suspicion fall on him because Oskar's conduct makes that suspicion plausible.

## What He Wants

- complete Road Progress before the sixty-day charter expires;
- preserve his professional reputation and concession shares;
- prove that the road can be maintained at a price Grenzburg can afford;
- avoid public responsibility for having dismissed sanctuary warnings.

## Pressure Points

Oskar can be forced to release his full maps, exposed for concealing the river-bend cost estimate, persuaded to design the Shared Way, or allowed to drive the Straight Cut. He responds to measurements, funding, and clear authority more readily than moral accusation. Seeing the glen react to bloodshed shakes him, but does not substitute for restitution.

If given skilled labor, recovered stone, and proof of reliable Alder-Crossing maintenance, he can reduce the Shared Way's delay. If publicly humiliated without replacing his expertise, work slows and less scrupulous foremen copy his worst assumptions.
