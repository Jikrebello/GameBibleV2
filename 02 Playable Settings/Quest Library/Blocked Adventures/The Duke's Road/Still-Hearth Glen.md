# Still-Hearth Glen

Still-Hearth Glen is a sheltered alder bowl around a cold spring in central [[Alderway Vale]]. For generations the [[Alder-Crossing Clan]] has used it as a refuge where pursuit, iron work, blood-feud, and permanent cutting must stop. A low ring of smoke-dark stones marks the common hearth. No resident power requires human sacrifice or predatory service there; its protection rests on remembered pact, repeated hospitality, and the place's Menhir pressure.

## Sanctuary Law

A person reaching the hearth may receive one night and one hearing even if pursued for a real crime. Protection is temporary, not acquittal. The clan must hear the pursuer on the following day and arrange surrender, compensation, escort, or exile. Anyone who draws blood at the stones loses sanctuary and owes restitution to both injured party and place.

The glen also shelters families during flood, deep freeze, and Grobi movement. Chapel-Folk use the outer shelf for prayer and share its refuge obligation without treating the hearth as an idol.

## Why the Road Matters

The old straight military trace crosses the spring basin at its firmest point. Reopening it would require cutting the hearth ring, driving iron piles through the spring shelf, and maintaining an armed road post inside the sanctuary. Oskar Pell argues that the glen's gravel makes it the only economical line. He is correct about cost and wrong that cost erases a valid right.

The Shared Way bends east around the spring and rejoins the trace after two bridges and a retaining wall. The North Ridge avoids the glen entirely. Both preserve sanctuary, though only a negotiated Shared Way also settles ordinary Alder-Crossing passage and fishery rights.

## Dangers

The glen is not a monster lair. Violence and reckless cutting can nevertheless produce path loops, cold pockets, panicked animals, and a sense of being unable to cross the same stream twice. These are place-bound consequences of violating the pact, not attacks directed by [[Magister Caspar Rauk]].
