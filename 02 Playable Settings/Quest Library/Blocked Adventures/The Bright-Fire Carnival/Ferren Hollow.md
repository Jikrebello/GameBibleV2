# Ferren Hollow

Ferren Hollow is a logging town on the Folk border of the Sylvan Marches, and the setting of [[The Bright-Fire Carnival]]. It lives by the axe, sits a day's float from deeper Folk country, and hosts a Bright-Fire fair every spring on the Two-Fires green between the town and the wood. It is prosperous, nervous, and quietly guilty — the kind of frontier town that profits from a wrong it would rather not name.

## The Town

A [[The Frontier Industry Lords|Timber-Cartel]] town in all but charter: the mill, the log-float, the charcoal kilns, and the winter stockpiles set the rhythm, and Guild Master Holger Strun's crews are the real power on the ground. The town warden, Mayor Adelheid Ports, governs at the cartel's sufferance and keeps the Folk-border trade quiet because quiet trade is profitable trade.

Two calendars run at once, as everywhere on the frontier: the Church's Greenveil and the Folk's Bright-Fire fall close together in spring, and Ferren Hollow keeps both — the priests fret, the young people attend both, and the market-days are scheduled twice.

## The Two-Fires Green

The fairground outside the town, named for the two Bright-Fire bonfires between which the cattle are driven and the courting couples walk. It is neutral-ish ground between the town and the wood, which is exactly why the carnival chose it: close enough to gather the whole town, close enough to the grove whose debt it has come to collect.

During the fair it holds the carnival — the Laughing House, the Sweetheart Float, the Wonder Menagerie, the Fortune Tent, the games, and the great fireworks rig — and on the third night it becomes the gauntlet.

## The Stump-Ring

In the wood beyond the green stands the burned offering grove: a ring of blackened stumps that still smells of last spring's fire. This was one of the groves the Season-Cutters left uncut and paid into each Bright-Fire, until "Clear-Cut" Karl's Firebrands burned it to hit a quota. It is the source of the whole adventure — the [[The Grove-Debt|grove-debt]] made visible — and [[Sisrin the Alder-Maid]] is the part of it that survived.

A party that visits the Stump-Ring learns more than any amount of fair-gossip: the burning was deliberate, the offering was owed, and the grove remembers.

## Powers and Players

- **The Timber-Cartel:** Strun's shadow-government, split between Old Tomas's Season-Cutters (who left the offering groves), "Clear-Cut" Karl's Firebrands (who burned this one), and Strun himself (who set the quota and denies it).
- **Mayor Adelheid Ports:** town warden, keeper of the quiet, burier of the grove-debt rumor.
- **The Folk across the border:** Folk who know exactly what an unpaid offering grove means and are watching to see whether the town pays or the fair burns.
- **The carnival:** [[The Cloven]] under Sisrin, and the cold thing riding them.

## The Festival Clock

The fair runs three days. Each day the carnival's warmth thins and the frost creeps further in from the wood. The third night's fireworks are the lure's climax and the massacre's trigger. Ferren Hollow's fate is set less by the fey than by whether its own guilty parties pay a debt before that last shell bursts.

## Aftermath Uses

Ferren Hollow can become a town that paid and made a real Folk-border peace, a town saved by force that still owes the grove, a cartel scandal-site where the burning was finally admitted, a colder and smaller place the Rider fed on, or a flashpoint where the offering-grove custom becomes the next frontier war.
