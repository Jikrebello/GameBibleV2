# The Rime Rider

The Rime Rider is the principal antagonist of [[The Bright-Fire Carnival]]: an ancient winter-hunter exiled from seasonal retinues and wearing the mask of the year's winter half. It did not suffer the wrong the carnival answers. It fastened itself to the grievance and means to turn Sisrin's bounded but cruel collection into a harvest of a whole town's warmth.

## What It Is

The Rider is not a Seasonal sovereign and not the grove's law. It is a culpable predator that follows aggrieved land until a bounded response can be widened into a hunt. It rides the cold half of the year because that is when territorial-memory claims press hardest, and it wears frost and antlered dark because those are the shapes a wronged forest expects an ally to wear. Underneath, it feeds on stolen warmth and the terror of being hunted. A whole town sustains it longer than a few targeted victims.

## Method

The Rider's craft is the [[The Grove-Debt|grove-debt]] turned against everyone but its rightful target:

- it approaches the wronged party — here [[Sisrin the Alder-Maid]] — as a guarantor and ally;
- it takes a bargain in the wronged party's name, binding them by Faewild law;
- it **reclassifies** the debt, widening it from the guilty few to the profitable many;
- it sets a public moment — the third-night bonfires — as the collection's due date;
- it uses the guilty party's refusal to repair the wrong as cover, so that by the third night its massacre can be presented as collective consequence.

Every hour "Clear-Cut" Karl and the Timber-Cartel refuse to admit and repair the real wrong gives the Rider more leverage. It is counting on their pride precisely because it has watched such men before.

## Character

The Rider is patient, courteous, and predatory. It does not need to hate Ferren Hollow; it prefers the larger harvest. It speaks in the language of fairness and owed things, mixing true accusations with a corrupt conclusion. It regards Sisrin's restraint as a quaint inefficiency and moral distinctions as obstacles to yield.

## Weaknesses and Levers

- **It does not own the grievance outright.** Prove the debt is not its to inflate, and its pretext collapses to the targeted claim.
- **Its bargain has terms.** The guarantee it took from Sisrin has a voidable overreach — a filing at a Folk grove-court, a thin-space rule, or its own widened claim used against it.
- **Repair strips it.** If the guilty confess and perform a settlement the grove recognizes before the third night, Sisrin's claim closes and the Rider must either withdraw or attack openly as the aggressor it is.
- **Exposure unmakes it.** A winter-hunt predator caught wearing a wronged party's face, collecting for yield, loses the legitimacy it hides inside.

## In the Fight

If forced to open violence, the Rider fights with cold, with the [[The Rimebound]] it has made from Sisrin's Cloven, and with the turned attractions as terrain. Defeating it by force collapses the town-wide harvest but does not repair the grove or settle Sisrin's claim. Killing the Rider is never the whole solution; it is the part that saves the innocent from its chosen predation.

## Continuation

The Rider can become a destroyed aggressor unmasked before the Folk and the town, a driven-off predator that keeps the warmth it already stole and nurses a grudge, or a cold power loose in the Sylvan Marches with a taste for aggrieved groves and a trail of grievances to corrupt.
