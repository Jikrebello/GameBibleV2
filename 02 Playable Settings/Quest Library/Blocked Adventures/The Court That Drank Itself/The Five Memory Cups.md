# The Five Memory Cups

The Five Memory Cups are Giant-scale blood vessels through which [[Azravan the Last Cup]] preserved and organized memories taken from Anshara's final audience. They contain blood-borne impressions, habits, knowledge, and voices. They do not contain whole souls, and destroying them does not condemn the people whose memories were stolen.

## The Vessels

| Cup | Memory held | Palace power while active | Physical vulnerability exposed when neutralized |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crown Cup** | nobles, heralds, and precedence masters | Azravan sees through official surfaces and commands accepted titles | his brow-seal opens and court-wide commands lose force |
| **Spear Cup** | guards, executioners, and war officers | coordinates Tribute Wardens and weapon systems | his armor-like skin no longer turns ordinary force |
| **Veil Cup** | spies, courtiers, and secret keepers | alters routes, disguises doors, and sustains the false audience | his true position and hidden passages remain visible |
| **Garden Cup** | physicians, breeders, gardeners, and household stewards | drains preserved vitality to heal Azravan and animate throne-wax | wounds cease closing and living blood no longer restores him at range |
| **Ash Cup** | funerary officers, archivists, and the last dead | rebuilds Azravan's body and stores his continuity pattern | destruction of his body becomes permanent once all other Cups are also gone |

## Neutralizing a Cup

Each Cup can be handled through several disciplines:

- **physical destruction:** break vessel and support channels while surviving the released blood-memory;
- **[[White Flame Doctrine|White Flame]]:** separate stolen human memory from the vessel, release what can be released, then destroy the empty shell;
- **warlock counter-script:** ground the blood-responsive circuit and burn its pattern out through an instrument;
- **memory separation:** identify enough speakers to divide the composite authority, making the Cup unable to answer as one office;
- **environmental collapse:** bury, melt, or drop the vessel beyond the palace's ability to reconnect it.

Merely carrying a Cup away extends the palace. Azravan can reconstruct through the Ash Cup or speak through another vessel once blood reaches it.

## Memory Evidence

A carefully neutralized Cup can yield selected testimony before destruction. The evidence establishes competing courts drinking rivals, mortal vampire revolts, forced blood investiture, and Azravan's massacre. It does not reveal a single complete witness or settle the last sovereign's fate.

Preserving every usable blood rite is not required to preserve history. Names, images, and non-operational testimony can survive while the mechanisms of domination are destroyed.

## Finale Rule

Track **Memory Cups from 5 to 0**. Azravan loses the listed power and vulnerability protection as each is neutralized. At least the Ash Cup must be destroyed before his body falls, or he reconstructs after the party leaves. Destroying every Cup before confronting him wakes a weakened but mobile Giant who attempts to escape the palace.
