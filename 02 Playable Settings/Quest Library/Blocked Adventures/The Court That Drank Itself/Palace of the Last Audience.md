# Palace of the Last Audience

The Palace of the Last Audience is a buried Ansharan continuity palace beneath [[Bitter Crown Basin]]. It was built to receive the empire's highest officers during catastrophe, preserve the court's memory, and proclaim an unbroken hierarchy even if every ordinary capital fell.

It was not the Court of Courts and does not reveal where Anshara's capital stood. Its architecture imitates imperial audience at a smaller scale: every route assigns rank, every chamber presumes service, and every visible treasure is also a test of whether an entrant accepts the palace's account of ownership.

## Construction

The palace occupies four descending bands:

1. **The Buried Forecourt:** three entrances, only one of which was intended for servants who needed to survive their work.
2. **The Petition Circuit:** the Litany Gallery, rank-gates, waiting halls, tribute court, and moving throne procession.
3. **The False Audience:** rank labyrinth, counterfeit sovereign, ceremonial crypt, and portable regalia intended to dismiss successful intruders.
4. **The Last Cup Works:** blood distillery, memory-vessel chambers, servant maintenance routes, the Dry Throne, and Azravan's true resting place.

## The Three Entrances

- **Gate of Conquest:** a monumental breach framed by kneeling enemies. It accepts armed entrants, then closes them inside a crushing victory corridor.
- **Gate of Tribute:** a gilded receiving aperture. It opens for valuables or blood and marks donors as property to be inventoried.
- **Servants' Dust Door:** a concealed maintenance entrance below the processional paving. Its worn handholds, drain channel, and lack of titles reveal that laborers used it repeatedly.

The Dust Door is the intended safe entry, but the other gates contain useful clues and recoverable evidence. None requires a single successful check to understand.

## Palace Logic

The [[Last Audience Protocol]] governs doors, hazards, guardians, and false conclusions. It is court hierarchy encoded in architecture, blood-responsive materials, and Giant craft. It is not Covenant contract magic and does not become lawful because its mechanisms are consistent.

[[Azravan the Last Cup]] perceives the palace through the [[The Five Memory Cups|Five Memory Cups]]. He can speak through mosaics, move marked captives, and strengthen a chamber only as Court Attention rises. The palace can continue after his body falls while any Cup remains intact.

## What the Palace Proves

Its records establish that Ansharan courts consumed one another during the empire's fall, that mortal vampire lines participated as rebels and opportunists, and that Azravan deliberately drank the assembled Last Court. They do not identify the final sovereign's fate or prove that Azravan inherited the throne.
