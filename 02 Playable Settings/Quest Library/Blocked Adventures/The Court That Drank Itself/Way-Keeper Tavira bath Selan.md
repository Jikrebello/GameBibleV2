# Way-Keeper Tavira bath Selan

Tavira bath Selan is a Jirahar Way-Keeper attached to the road companies using [[Qasr Ilyun]]. She organizes the rescue of the [[Six-Lantern Expedition]] after its pack camel returns alone. Her younger sister, epigrapher Shimara bath Selan, is among the missing.

## Office and Character

Tavira records dangerous roads, witnesses lost travelers, arbitrates shared caravan obligations, and decides when a route should be closed rather than merely priced more highly. She is deliberate, unsentimental about equipment, and unwilling to call missing people dead for administrative convenience.

Her personal fear is that concern for Shimara will make every other life look secondary. She therefore writes the names of all nine expedition members on the rescue warrant before she writes her sister's.

## What She Provides

- the returned camel, extinguished lantern, and bloodless equipment;
- the Six-Lantern route log through the first sixteen days;
- water, desert veils, spare line, lamp oil, and payment for a capable rescue party;
- authority to speak for the Jirahar families of the missing;
- introductions to Qahuri officials and caravan houses without claiming control over them.

Tavira can accompany the party if a table wants a guide and witness. By default she remains at Qasr Ilyun to organize relief, protect the expedition's records, and prevent treasure-seekers from following the rescue crew.

## Limits

She does not possess an Ansharan key, secret testimony from another adventure, or a ritual that neutralizes the palace. Her best guidance is practical: mark the return, test before trusting, and do not let an ancient ruler define what a living person is.
