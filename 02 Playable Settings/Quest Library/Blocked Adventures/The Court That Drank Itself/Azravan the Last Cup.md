# Azravan the Last Cup

Azravan was Grand Chamberlain of Anshara and master of the [[Palace of the Last Audience]]. He is a primordial Vampire Giant: a towering court-bred descendant of the Sovereign whose blood can dominate, preserve, and consume memory as well as life.

## The Last Audience

As Anshara collapsed into rebellions and court-on-court predation, Azravan summoned surviving nobles, blood-priests, guards, officials, and servants to the continuity palace. He promised an audience that would preserve imperial order until the sovereign returned.

He sealed the doors and drank the assembly. The most useful blood-memories were distilled into [[The Five Memory Cups]]. The rest he consumed directly. He calls this act preservation because their knowledge remains available to him. It was mass murder committed to make one official indistinguishable from the court he served.

## False Sovereignty

Azravan addresses himself as the Last Cup, Keeper of the Empty Throne, and Voice of the Court Entire. He is not Anshara's emperor. He possesses memories of commands, ceremonies, rivalries, and state secrets, but memory is not lawful succession and consumption is not consent.

He cannot answer where the final sovereign died. When pressed, several contradictory voices answer through him. This is evidence that the Cups preserve partial imprints, not a complete imperial person.

## Primordial Vampirism

Azravan's body is dry, black-red, and apparently mummified until blood or accepted deference reaches the palace. His powers include:

- draining vitality and recent memory through blood;
- imposing physical deference on anyone who accepts a court title;
- rebuilding damaged flesh from the Ash Cup;
- commanding tribute wardens through the Spear Cup;
- seeing and speaking through court surfaces while the Crown Cup remains;
- turning routes and appearances through the Veil Cup;
- drawing vigor from the palace's preserved life through the Garden Cup.

He is not a modern Regalis, Sanguine, or other specialized bloodline. Those lines inherit fragments of a broader Ansharan condition.

## Conduct

Azravan is patient because the palace makes waiting lethal for others. He offers accurate short-term bargains: a captive released, a door opened, a hazard suspended. Every price advances his sovereignty through blood, title, memory, or a living replacement.

No bargain makes his rule good. He will not repent, release the Last Court voluntarily, or accept ordinary judgement. He uses the language of preservation to deny that any victim possessed a life beyond office.

## Defeat

Azravan is nearly unassailable while all five Cups remain. Each neutralized vessel removes one court-wide power and exposes a corresponding physical vulnerability. Destroying his body alone lets the Ash Cup rebuild him. Destroying all Cups alone wakes his diminished body and releases it from the palace.

Permanent victory requires destroying both the body and every Cup. [[White Flame Doctrine|White Flame]], physical ruin, warlock counter-scripts, memory separation, and palace collapse can contribute. Restoration of Azravan is not possible; preserving victims' names and memory fragments does not require preserving him.
