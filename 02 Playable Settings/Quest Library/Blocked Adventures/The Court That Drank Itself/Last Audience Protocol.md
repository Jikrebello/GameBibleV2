# Last Audience Protocol

The Last Audience Protocol is the organizing logic of the [[Palace of the Last Audience]]. It combines Giant-scale architecture, blood-responsive stone, court training, preserved instructions, and Ansharan hierarchy. It is not a sentient law, a fair test, or Covenant contract magic.

## Core Rules

1. **Entrances assign motive.** Conquest, tribute, and service routes presume different ranks and expose entrants to different machinery.
2. **Visible honor is dangerous.** Raised paths, titled doors, thrones, crowns, and processional positions receive the palace's strongest attention.
3. **Labor routes tell the truth.** Drains, handholds, lamp niches, worn thresholds, and undecorated passages reveal how mortal servants survived.
4. **Gifts establish subordination.** Blood or treasure placed in an official receptacle becomes tribute. The palace then treats the donor as a lesser member of its hierarchy.
5. **False conclusions dismiss the worthy.** The palace offers a counterfeit sovereign and valuable regalia to convince capable intruders that they have reached the end.
6. **The court preserves offices, not persons.** Names, relationships, and consent are irrelevant to it unless living entrants insist upon them.

## Lethal but Legible Standard

Every lethal mechanism must show at least two signs before commitment:

- wear, damage, remains, air movement, heat, sound, repeated geometry, mismatched inscriptions, or survivor marks;
- a coherent function within Ansharan audience, punishment, tribute, or maintenance;
- at least one response based on observation, testing, alternate routing, interruption, or accepting material cost.

A single failed skill check never triggers unavoidable death. A character can still die after ignoring evidence, committing bodily to an untested route, or choosing speed over a known safeguard.

## Court Attention

Court Attention tracks how fully the palace recognizes the intruders.

- **0 - Dust:** dormant mechanisms respond only when physically activated.
- **1 - Petitioners:** mosaics watch, doors sort the group, and distant voices repeat titles.
- **2 - Household Notice:** Wax-Bound Courtiers mobilize and Azravan can speak through official surfaces.
- **3 - Grand Audience:** routes shift through the Veil Cup, captives are relocated, and guardians coordinate.
- **4 - Court Assembled:** Azravan can project authority throughout the palace and begins preparing a living heir or replacement household.

Accepted titles, blood tribute, secret theft, unnecessary destruction of servants, and prolonged retreat raise Attention. Destroying a Memory Cup does not lower Attention, but removes the power through which Azravan exploits it.

## Court Marks

The palace marks recognized entrants with faint red-gold lines at the wrist, brow, or throat. A mark grants passage appropriate to its assigned rank and exposes the bearer to matching commands. Refusing a title before the mark completes, crossing through servant routes, or separating the mark with [[White Flame Doctrine|White Flame]] or counter-script prevents escalation.

Marks disappear when Azravan and the Five Cups are destroyed. They are not metaphysical contracts and do not survive through legal wording.
