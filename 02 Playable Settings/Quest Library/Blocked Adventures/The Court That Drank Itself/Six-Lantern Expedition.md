# Six-Lantern Expedition

The Six-Lantern Expedition is a nine-person Qahuri-Jirahar scholarly mission formed at [[Qasr Ilyun]] to survey the abandoned Black Salt Track. It takes its name from six brass route lanterns placed behind an expedition in country where wind erases ordinary marks.

The group disappeared after locating [[Bitter Crown Basin]]. It expected to return after twenty-four days. On day twenty-nine, one pack camel reached Qasr Ilyun carrying an extinguished lantern, an unopened water skin, and tools wiped clean of blood.

## Expedition Principles

Bashir and Shimara agreed to shared custody of inscriptions, public recording of human names, and division of ordinary salvage under Qasr Ilyun's breach court. The expedition carried no vampire patron, concealed rival crew, or inherited Ansharan key.

Its members made intelligent mistakes under pressure. They recognized the two monumental gates as dangerous, found the Dust Door, and penetrated deeply. Court Attention separated them after Bashir offered blood to save an injured porter and the palace treated that act as acceptance of rank.

## Rescue Use

Each recoverable member contributes something distinct if found:

- Bashir reconstructs the palace's water and blood channels;
- Shimara corrects the Last Audience litany;
- Rahil reveals the safest return markings;
- Mazen can counter one Memory Cup relay;
- Tiratha preserves victims' identities and treats blood loss;
- Korin knows the lower servant route and the distillery guard pattern.

No one survivor is mandatory. Their combined knowledge rewards rescue without turning them into six interchangeable keys.
