# Bitter Crown Basin

Bitter Crown Basin is a glass-scoured depression eight hard days beyond [[Qasr Ilyun]] along the abandoned Black Salt Track. Black escarpments enclose a floor of white salt, wind-polished glass, and isolated stone ridges. From the eastern approach those ridges resemble the points of a buried crown.

## The Journey

The Black Salt Track was once an Ansharan processional road. Modern caravans abandoned it because its final wells turned bitter and its cut-stone markers direct travelers toward dead basins. A prepared expedition can reach Bitter Crown in eight days from Qasr Ilyun. Heat, glass wind, and the absence of grazing make the return more dangerous than the outward journey.

Three landmarks govern navigation:

- **The Kneeling Mile:** paired stumps of Giant statues, all facing west toward the basin.
- **The Bitter Wells:** three stone-lined wells whose upper water is saline; a drinkable seep survives behind the northern lining.
- **The Crown Ridge:** a low black ridge concealing the forecourt of the [[Palace of the Last Audience]].

## The Six-Lantern Camp

The [[Six-Lantern Expedition]] established a nine-person camp in the ridge's lee. Its tents remain pegged, food chests shut, and water carefully rationed. The absence of disorder shows that the explorers entered deliberately rather than fleeing an attack.

Six brass expedition lanterns form a line between camp and ridge. Five are cold. The sixth was tied to a pack camel that returned to Qasr Ilyun. Their spacing records the expedition's safe approach and warns against a band of glass that liquefies under midday heat.

## Environmental Dangers

- midday glass glare can blind travelers who cross without veils;
- cold desert nights split heated stone and expose old voids;
- wind erases ordinary tracks within an hour but leaves blood and lamp oil visible;
- the bitter wells cause sickness rather than supernatural corruption;
- Court Attention can make the basin echo voices from the palace, but the weather itself does not serve Azravan.

## Canon Boundary

Bitter Crown Basin and its exact route are creator-facing secrets. Public geography establishes Qasr Ilyun and the Black Salt Track as a dead ancient road, not the palace at its end. No player outcome at the palace is assumed elsewhere.
