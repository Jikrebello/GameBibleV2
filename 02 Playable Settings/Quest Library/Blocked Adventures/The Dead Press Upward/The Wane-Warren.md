# The Wane-Warren

The Wane-Warren is a lesser ghoul settlement below [[Wanemoor District]]. It exploits the broken [[The Seal of the Shut Mouth|Seal of the Shut Mouth]] during [[The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure]]. It is an organized predatory faction, not a displaced community entitled to the bodies above it.

## Origin

The warren occupies natural caverns and abandoned cuttings below the Last Lamp ridge. It descends from several hunting lineages that found old funerary routes after the abbey sealed the main Maw-Road. For generations they survived by raiding battle burials, animals, isolated graves, and one another.

The Seal denied them regular access to Wanenbruck. Its age did not turn their frustrated predation into a lawful claim.

## Present Leadership

Three elder feeders govern through control of routes and meat:

- **Old Sere:** holds the deepest nursery and wants steady access to plague galleries.
- **Crooked Veil:** directs surface scouts and understands human grave-watch.
- **Malt-Tongue:** consumes memories from recent dead and advises which households can be frightened into silence.

[[Mournfang]] belongs to an older grave-runner lineage but does not obey this council. It intends to take the Maw-Road and subordinate or abandon the present warren.

## What the Warren Wants

- access to the Grey Sweat galleries;
- living and newly dead captives;
- destruction of burial records and name-tablets;
- a defended route into Wanenbruck's cemetery;
- Edrik's grave-script after he is weakened.

The warren does not seek political recognition, a corpse treaty, or evacuation. It attacks opportunities and retreats from strength. There is no peaceful settlement branch in this adventure.

## Methods

Wane-Warren hunters use corpse chutes, side cuts, darkness, false cries for help, and collapses. They avoid defended rooms unless bodies or records make the attack worthwhile. They prefer to drag casualties below rather than remain to fight over ground.

They drive ordinary residue bodies upward because the rising empties streets, exhausts grave-watch, and conceals ghoul movement. Edrik interprets this as obedience. The warren regards his directed dead as moving cover.

## Signs

- graves opened from the foot rather than the head;
- cleanly removed shroud pins beside gnawed bone;
- claw scoring above human shoulder height in corpse chutes;
- drag marks that stop at apparently solid walls;
- grave goods sorted away from remains;
- recent dead missing while older dry bone is left behind.

## Opposition Pattern

- **Scouts** watch retreats and carry warnings through narrow cuts.
- **Feeders** attack fallen or isolated targets and withdraw with bodies.
- **Grave-Runners** pursue, trip, and drag victims into prepared lanes.
- **Elders** collapse routes, spread ghoul-fever, and target rite-keepers.

Closing the Wane-Warren Cut keeps most of the faction out of the Seal chamber. Leaving it open lets the warren enter during Edrik's defeat and threaten the records needed for restoration.

## Moral Ruling

The warren contains memory, loyalty, fear, hierarchy, and deliberate action. Those qualities make its members morally legible; they do not excuse consuming people, stealing the dead, spreading ghoul-fever, or damaging soul routes. Complexity is not absolution.
