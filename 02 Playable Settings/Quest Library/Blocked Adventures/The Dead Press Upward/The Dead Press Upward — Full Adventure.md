# The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure

## Adventure at a Glance

**Premise:** During Souls-month, the graves above [[Wanenbruck]] open from below and the dead press into town. The attack begins as an ordinary residue rising, but the bodies are being driven through old plague galleries by [[The Wane-Warren|a ghoul warren]]. Beneath the abandoned [[Abbey of the Last Lamp]], [[Magister Edrik Morn]] has broken an ancient seal and is using the resulting soul-route scar to command corpse residue. He calls himself the Second Boatman.

**Beginning:** [[Sexton Adelheid Kern]] reaches Wanenbruck minutes ahead of the first dead. The party must hold three threatened districts, keep civilians alive, and learn that the attackers came from graves emptied underneath rather than from a surface desecration.

**Middle:** The trail leads through the cemetery and into the abbey by one of four approaches. The descent exposes a concealed mine trespass, Grey Sweat pauper galleries, the Wane-Warren's feeding route, and the burial records needed to repair the breach. The elder ghoul [[Mournfang]] stalks the galleries and tries to seize the route for itself.

**End:** At [[The Seal of the Shut Mouth]], the party confronts Edrik while the warren attacks from below and residue dead rise around the chamber. Killing Edrik stops his direction of the horde. Saving Wanemoor from another rising requires a separate decision about the seal, the violated dead, and the mine that broke into them.

**Tier:** W undead crisis, level 6 analog. Most enemies are ordinary residue dead or local predators. Edrik and the Maw-Road are the bounded metaphysical exception.

**Expected length:** 3-4 substantial sessions: the defense of Wanenbruck, the cemetery investigation, the abbey and pauper galleries, then the seal chamber and aftermath.

## Who, What, Where, Why, and How

- **Who:** the player party; Sexton Adelheid Kern; Mine-Mistress [[Helene Bruch]]; Magister Edrik Morn; Mournfang; frightened townspeople; trapped miners; and the Wane-Warren.
- **What:** survive the first rising, trace the dead to their source, stop Edrik, prevent the ghouls from claiming the breach, and decide whether Wanemoor repairs or buries its old crime.
- **Where:** Wanenbruck, its cemetery, Bruch's eastern mine, the Abbey of the Last Lamp, the Grey Sweat pauper galleries, and the Seal of the Shut Mouth.
- **Why the party acts:** the dead attack while the party is in town; Adelheid asks known delvers or local defenders for help; the duke's road bailiff offers emergency pay; or someone known to the party is trapped in one of the threatened districts.
- **Why the crisis exists:** mine leaders crossed a marked burial boundary and concealed what they found. Edrik used their access to break the seal in a deliberate attempt to inherit the Boatman's office. The Wane-Warren followed the opening upward.
- **How it escalates:** Edrik learns from every corpse he directs, the warren widens the Maw-Road, the town loses defensible ground, and disturbed names and remains make a complete resealing harder.

## Moral and Metaphysical Ruling

The ordinary dead in this adventure are residue-animate bodies, not imprisoned people. Their souls have already gone toward the Vestibule. Destroying a rattle-bone or barrow-shade is necessary sexton-work, not harm to the deceased.

The breach is more serious because ghouls consume corpses before burial residue has settled and deliberately tear at the roads by which the dead are mourned, named, and released. They are intelligent predators. Their hunger, memories, discipline, and loyalty to one another do not make that predation morally neutral.

Edrik cannot become the Boatman. The Boatman's office is received under the Redeemer's authority and concerns truth, judgement, and passage. Edrik commands remains, residue, and a damaged threshold. His ability to make a corpse walk proves only that he has learned to exploit what a person leaves behind.

Helene Bruch and Wanenbruck's mine council are culpable because they knowingly crossed protected burial ground, concealed deaths and subsidence, and valued the mine over the pauper galleries. They did not hire Edrik to raise the dead and did not understand the Seal. Their crime is grave without being identical to Edrik's necromancy or the warren's predation.

Old-Church and Freeflame households disagree about authority, images, and the proper rite of repair. Both recognize that poor people do not lose their names or burial rights because a mine becomes profitable. The adventure does not require either confession to become secretly monstrous for the crisis to matter.

## The Central Truth

The Abbey of the Last Lamp was founded in 1028 AR over a much older funerary cutting. Early Brauvalian brothers discovered that ghouls had used the cutting as a road into surface grave fields. They built the Seal of the Shut Mouth across that route and maintained a consecrated drainage of residue through named interment, lamp vigils, and an annual closing rite.

During the Grey Sweat, Wanenbruck buried more than four hundred paupers and strangers in new galleries around the abbey. The brothers recorded each name they knew on clay tablets and left blank marks for the unknown. The abbey later lost population, property, and office. Its surviving burial duties passed to Wanenbruck's sextons, while its outer land was leased to mines and timber yards.

Three years ago, Bruch Mine found a rich iron seam beneath the abbey ridge. Survey markers and an old warning stone showed that the east adit crossed consecrated ground. Helene halted work briefly, then accepted a guild scholar's claim that the marked galleries had collapsed centuries earlier. Two workers disappeared after hearing movement behind a mortared wall. Helene recorded them as deserters and ordered the wall closed.

Edrik Morn supplied the false survey. Formerly a diocesan antiquarian and death-law scholar, he had become convinced that the Boatman was merely the last successful mortal to master funerary infrastructure. He used the mine adit to enter the galleries, recovered fragments of the abbey's closing rite, and cut the Seal's central name-stone.

The backlash crushed and burned away Edrik's lower body but did not let him die. He fixed himself into a pale iron bier-chair whose jointed pallbearer legs move by grave-script and residue. From the Seal chamber he can see through nearby animated eyes and address intruders through dead mouths. He interprets his survival and command as confirmation that he is the Second Boatman.

The broken Seal reopened the **Maw-Road**, a steep underworld passage used by the Wane-Warren. The ghouls entered the plague galleries to feed. Edrik learned that directing residue bodies upward concealed his experiments and supplied him with more remains. The warren learned that each rising emptied Wanenbruck's streets and graveyard. They cooperate only in the sense that two predators can exploit the same wound. Each intends to own it when the other weakens.

Mournfang is not the warren's envoy. It is an elder grave-runner and residue-worker that wants the Maw-Road for its own hunting lineage. It studies the party, lets them spend strength against Edrik, and attacks when it can take the broken Seal or the recovered name-tablets.

## Wanenbruck in Numbers

- **Population:** approximately 1,700 in the town and another 900 in nearby hamlets.
- **Defenders:** 24 gate and road watch, 46 guild militia fit for immediate service, and roughly 80 civilians able to hold barricades if armed.
- **First rising:** 70-90 residue dead in scattered groups, 8 Grave-Runners, and corrupted grave-rats and carrion birds.
- **Trapped below:** six living miners at the start, plus two bodies whose deaths were concealed three years ago.
- **Pauper galleries:** 417 recorded interments, 53 unnamed marks, and recent evidence of at least 60 bodies consumed or displaced.
- **Wane-Warren force:** about 30 scavenger ghouls use the opened route; only a portion can enter the abbey at once.

These figures establish stakes rather than fixed encounter groups. The chosen game system should convert them into waves, hazards, background pressure, and a manageable number of decisive fights.

## Adventure States

### Upward Pressure: 0 to 4

Begin at **1** after the first cemetery alarm.

- **0 - Quieted:** Edrik's direction is interrupted and the Maw-Road is contained.
- **1 - First rising:** isolated bodies move; the warren scouts the galleries.
- **2 - Directed dead:** Edrik speaks through corpses and Grave-Runners hunt ahead of the waves.
- **3 - Open galleries:** another town district is attacked and the warren reaches the lower abbey.
- **4 - The road yawns:** Edrik animates every available remain, the Wane-Warren commits its hunters, and Wanenbruck faces a second general assault.

Advance Pressure after a long rest, a full retreat from the abbey, destruction of a name-tablet cache, or a failed attempt to manipulate the Seal. Reduce it by disabling Edrik's observation points, closing a secondary breach, or completing a burial release for one gallery.

### Town Holds: 3 to 0

Begin at **3**. During Act I, each abandoned or badly failed defense front reduces it by one. Upward Pressure 4 causes another loss unless the party prepared defenders before descending.

- **3 - Holding:** all districts remain connected and the wounded can reach the parish.
- **2 - Bloodied:** one district is lost or burned; casualties and panic strain the shelters.
- **1 - Divided:** the bridge or central road falls and families begin fleeing into the woods.
- **0 - Evacuating:** Wanenbruck is partly overrun. The party can still stop Edrik, but the finale decides whether there is a town to return to.

### Burial Integrity: 0 to 3

Begin at **0**. Gain one each for:

1. recovering the Grey Sweat burial register or an authenticated copy;
2. recovering the clay name-tablets and gathering the displaced remains;
3. preserving Adelheid, the abbey rite-book, or enough witnesses from both confessions to complete a lawful closing.

At **3**, the party can restore the Seal and release the galleries properly. At **2**, it can close the Maw-Road with a lasting but incomplete repair. At **0-1**, only destructive closure or temporary suppression is available.

## Running the Dead Without Repetition

Every undead scene must do at least one additional job: threaten a named group, change a route, reveal evidence, damage a burial resource, or show how Edrik and the ghouls differ.

- **Residue dead** repeat physical habits: carrying timber, queuing for bread, knocking on a former home, or following an old funeral path. They are dangerous but not tactical.
- **Grave-Runners** circle, isolate, knock victims down, and drag bodies toward openings. They are fast and deliberately frightening.
- **Wane-Warren ghouls** use darkness, crawlspaces, and retreat routes. They target bodies, wounded people, tablets, and rite-keepers before treasure.
- **Edrik's directed dead** coordinate unnaturally and speak in his voice. Destroying the grave-script token or blocking his sight returns them to ordinary residue behavior.
- **Mournfang** observes, tests a weakness, and withdraws until the Seal chamber unless trapped earlier.

## Act I - Wanenbruck Under the Dead

### Beat 1: The Sexton's Warning

Adelheid reaches the lower market with torn sleeves, one broken cemetery key, and a handbell she has rung raw. Graves opened behind her. The first bodies are following the funeral road toward town, while something low and fast moves along the drainage ditch.

She gives the party three immediate facts:

- the graves were opened from below;
- the cemetery wards held until someone broke the eastern boundary stone;
- the dead include Grey Sweat burials that should lie in sealed abbey galleries, not the modern yard.

The watch can bar only one district without help. The party may divide, direct militia, create defenses, or fight personally at each front.

### Front A: Cemetery Road

The largest body of residue dead approaches the north gate along the old funeral road. Families are still outside the wall tending graves for Souls-month. The challenge is to recover the living while holding a narrow gate that defenders are afraid to close on their kin.

Clues carried by the dead include abbey shroud pins, Grey Sweat clay dust, and fresh mine scoring on old bones. A corpse speaking in Edrik's voice calls the defenders "passengers without a ferryman."

Failure costs Town Holds 1 and lets Edrik place grave-script inside the wall. The survivors retreat through the tannery lane, preserving the adventure.

### Front B: Sawmill Bridge

Grave-Runners and residue-animated draft animals attack workers crossing the Weiss feeder stream. The bridge controls access between the militia store and parish shelter. Burning it stops the immediate rush but divides the town.

The Grave-Runners attempt to drag fallen people into the mill race rather than hold ground. Captured or examined remains show that several were miners missing during the last fortnight.

Failure costs Town Holds 1. Success preserves the town's fastest route and gives the party a captured grave-script collar that can later blind one of Edrik's observation points.

### Front C: Parish Cellar

Poorhouse burials beneath Saint Orra's parish rise inside the shelter. Old-Church families, Freeflame households, and mine widows are trapped together while Pastor Lukas and Elder Marta argue over whether to evacuate or finish a common warding prayer.

The dead repeat the action of receiving plague bread and are drawn toward the old abbey dole chest. Inside it is a copied list of pauper names transferred to the Last Lamp galleries.

Failure costs Town Holds 1 but the civilians escape through the vestry garden. Success secures the shelter and gives an early route to Burial Integrity.

### The First Night's Accounting

After the three fronts, Adelheid identifies bodies from the old register. Helene Bruch arrives with armed mine workers and insists that the east cemetery has suffered natural subsidence. Foreman Odo contradicts her: the missing miners were working beneath the abbey ridge.

The party can expose Helene immediately, compel her help, or postpone judgement. She provides the mine map if confronted with mine scoring, Odo's testimony, or the copied poorhouse list. If she refuses, Odo steals a copy and brings it to the party.

### Act I Output

The party has preserved as much of Wanenbruck as its choices allow, knows the rising came through the old galleries, and has a route to the cemetery and abbey ridge.

## Act II - The Open Graves

### The Eastern Cemetery

The opened graves form a descending pattern toward the broken boundary stone. Bodies nearest the town climbed out. Bodies nearest the abbey were dragged down and partly consumed. This establishes two forces: Edrik pushes residue upward while ghouls feed and pull remains below.

Adelheid can restore one surface ward if protected. Doing so prevents another cemetery wave and reveals the old closing phrase: **"Name the dead; shut the mouth; leave no hunger a road."**

### Evidence of the Concealment

The investigation can establish all of the following without depending on a single check:

- the boundary warning stone was cut with Bruch Mine tools;
- the mine ledger lists the east adit as closed but continues issuing lamp oil to it;
- the two supposed deserters left boots and pay tokens in a sealed tool room;
- Edrik's survey bears a diocesan antiquarian seal revoked seven years ago;
- Helene ordered the first discovered bones moved without a sexton.

Missing one clue changes who can be held publicly responsible, not whether the party can continue.

### Four Ways Below

- **Abbey gate:** the most direct and defensible route. It passes through the ruined cloister and lets the party secure a retreat, but Edrik sees them early.
- **Cemetery sink:** a steep descent through collapsed graves into the upper pauper gallery. It reaches the tablets quickly but risks damaging remains and Burial Integrity.
- **East mine adit:** the easiest route for supplies and survivors. It reaches the trapped miners first, but contains Helene's evidence and a Grave-Runner pack.
- **Ruined cloister roof:** a concealed approach through the old bell loft and keeper stair. It can disable Edrik's first observation point but requires a dangerous climb over unstable masonry.

All four routes eventually meet in the abbey undercroft. Securing a second route creates a reliable evacuation path and prevents Town Holds from falling if the party must withdraw once.

### Act II Output

The party understands the living trespass, knows Edrik entered through the mine, and chooses what it values first: retreat, trapped workers, burial records, or surprise.

## Act III - The Abbey and Pauper Galleries

### Zone 1: Cloister of the Last Lamp

The roofless cloister contains ordinary barrow-shades repeating the abbey's final procession. They become violent when anyone crosses the route backward or carries an unshielded flame through the rain. Observing the procession reveals the correct sequence for approaching the Seal.

The keeper's room holds half of the closing rite and a list showing that burial office passed lawfully to Wanenbruck's sextons. This gives Adelheid standing to repair the Seal despite the abbey's dissolution.

### Zone 2: The East Adit

Broken carts, fresh shoring, and cut grave markers show the mine trespass plainly. Six miners are trapped behind a collapse. Two Grave-Runners use the voices of injured workers to draw rescuers into a charge lane.

Saving the miners provides labor for moving remains or collapsing the Maw-Road. Leaving them does not block the finale, but Helene loses any chance to make restitution and the town's anger sharpens.

### Zone 3: The Grey Sweat Gallery

Rows of wall niches hold clay name-tablets, shroud pins, and mingled bones. The Wane-Warren has opened some niches carefully and smashed others in haste. Ghouls attack from corpse chutes and target anyone carrying tablets.

The original burial register lies in a stone lectern whose lock opens when the names of three plague nurses are spoken. A copied register can prove identity, but only the original records the 53 unnamed marks and the rite used to release them together.

Recovering the register grants Burial Integrity 1. Preserving the tablets and gathering the displaced remains grants another.

### Zone 4: The Wane-Warren Cut

The gallery becomes raw stone scored by ghoul claws. Feeding shelves, stripped grave goods, and drag marks show that the warren is an organized raiding population, not a natural sanitation force. Its scouts attack, withdraw, collapse narrow passages, and attempt to isolate the rite-keeper.

The party can close a side cut with mine labor or consecrated mortar. Doing so reduces Upward Pressure by 1 and keeps most of the warren out of the finale. Failure opens an additional attack route but does not halt the descent.

### Zone 5: Mournfang's Watch

Mournfang has arranged dead miners and ghouls to resemble casualties from a fight that never happened. It wants the party to believe Edrik controls the warren. Careful examination reveals that the dead face the Seal rather than Wanenbruck and that a map has been carved into one miner's back.

Mournfang attacks if the party carries both register and tablets, if it can isolate a wounded character, or if its deception is exposed. Otherwise it withdraws toward the Seal and attacks after Edrik is weakened. Driving it off now prevents its strongest finale intervention; killing it is possible but not required.

### Zone 6: Hall of Unnamed Marks

Fifty-three blank clay plaques stand around a dry lamp basin. Edrik's voice claims these dead belong to him because nobody named them. The rite-book states the opposite: the unknown were commended together precisely so that loss of a name could not become loss of personhood.

Lighting the basin and speaking the communal clause breaks Edrik's control over one entire residue wave. It also establishes the adventure's central answer to his doctrine before the final fight.

### Act III Output

The party has the truth of the galleries, the means to judge Helene's concealment, and as much Burial Integrity as it preserved. It knows the ghouls and Edrik are competing for the same breach.

## Act IV - The Shut Mouth

### The Seal Chamber

The chamber is a circular funerary quarry cut across the Maw-Road. The cracked Seal stands at its southern edge. Three stone gutters once carried lamp oil, wash water, and burial ash through separate channels; Edrik has filled them with grave-script and corpse fragments.

Edrik's bier-chair hangs above the central pit on chains and jointed pallbearer legs. He sees through six corpse masks fixed to the walls. Each intact mask lets him direct another group, anticipate a repeated tactic, or speak through a dead body. Destroying, covering, or reconsecrating masks limits him and returns those corpses to ordinary residue behavior.

The encounter has three simultaneous pressures:

- Edrik attacks through grave-script, directed dead, and the moving bier-chair;
- Wane-Warren ghouls climb through the cracked Seal and target remains, records, and wounded people;
- the Seal deteriorates whenever grave-script spills into its name channels or a body falls into the Maw-Road.

Mournfang enters when Edrik is badly weakened, when the party begins a repair, or when it can seize the register. It attacks both sides but prioritizes ownership of the route, not alliance.

### Reaching Edrik

Edrik can be forced to acknowledge his failure before defeat:

- ask him to name one soul he has actually judged rather than one corpse he has moved;
- use the unnamed communal clause, proving that forgotten people are not unowned freight;
- show that his controlled bodies revert when an observation mask breaks;
- invite him to release his own dying body and face the judgement he claims to administer.

He does not repent by default. The confrontation strips away his claim and may cause one lost action, a collapse of directed control, or a final desperate attempt to enter the Maw-Road. Killing him is morally permissible and often necessary. Capturing him alive requires dismantling the bier-chair while maintaining his failing body.

### Closing Options

#### Restore the Seal

Requires Burial Integrity 3. The party names the recorded dead, commends the unknown together, restores the three channels, and holds the chamber while Adelheid or another qualified witness completes the closing. The mine adit must be surrendered as burial ground. This is the only complete restoration.

#### Mortar the Mouth

Requires Burial Integrity 2 and mine labor, engineering, or equivalent magic. The breach is rebuilt with stone, iron pins, and consecrated mortar. It should hold for generations, but displaced remains still require later recovery and the annual closing rite must be restored.

#### Collapse the Maw-Road

Possible at any Integrity. The trapped miners, recovered charges, warlock scripts, or structural sabotage bring the lower abbey down. The route closes physically, but some galleries and remains are lost. Wanenbruck survives at the price of an unresolved burial wound and the loss of the rich seam.

#### Burn the Scar

White Flame, sanctified fire, or controlled Shell-rune heat can cauterize Edrik's grave-script while destroying the bier-chair and nearby remains. This prevents immediate reuse but cannot replace naming and reinterment. At Integrity 0-1 it is a harsh emergency ending; at 2 it can support a lasting closure.

### If the Party Fails

At Upward Pressure 4 with Edrik active, the Seal tears wide enough for the Wane-Warren to enter the upper galleries in force. The party can still evacuate Wanenbruck, rescue named groups, or pursue Edrik and Mournfang after one of them carries the grave-script elsewhere. Failure changes the campaign; it does not erase play.

## End States

### The Mouth Shut

Edrik is stopped, the Seal is properly restored, the Wane-Warren is severed from the cemetery, and the pauper dead are reinterred or returned to named niches. Wanenbruck loses the east seam and must rebuild openly. Helene faces judgement, restitution, or a life spent repairing what she concealed.

### The Abbey Falls

The Maw-Road and lower galleries collapse. Wanenbruck is saved from immediate attack, but many remains and records are lost. The abbey ridge becomes forbidden ground, and future stories concern recovery, memorial, and whatever found another route below.

### The Town Survives

Edrik dies and the first rising ends, but the party leaves the violated galleries, mine structure, or burial government largely unchanged. Trade resumes quickly. So do quiet subsidence, missing grave goods, and political pressure to call the crisis finished.

### The Dead Road Opens

The warren gains the galleries, Edrik or Mournfang escapes with grave-script, or Wanenbruck must evacuate. Survivors retreat toward Brauvalian abbey towns while the party chooses between protecting them and hunting the force that now controls the road.

No ending is the canonical player outcome.

## Judgement and Aftermath

Stopping the rising does not automatically decide the living cases.

- **Helene Bruch:** public trial, ducal inquest, guild restitution, concealed guilt, death below, or leadership in the repair are all possible. Saving miners counts in her favor; falsifying their deaths remains evidence against her.
- **The mine council:** individual records distinguish those who knew about bones, those who knew about missing workers, and those deceived by Edrik. Collective anger should not replace particular judgement.
- **The confessions:** a joint repair strengthens Wanemoor's local settlement. A monopolized rite may save the Seal while reopening old political wounds.
- **The poor dead:** returning their names, compensating descendants where possible, and establishing a funded grave-watch determine whether Wanenbruck has learned anything beyond fear.

## Follow-Up Hooks

- Mournfang escapes with one fragment of Edrik's bier-script and begins testing it on battlefield residue.
- One of the 53 unnamed plaques bears a symbol linking the buried stranger to another conversion adventure.
- The ducal court sends an investigator whose real concern is how many other abbey leases crossed plague galleries.
- Merewick asks Wanenbruck for winter aid soon after the mine closes, connecting the pressure of [[The Feast at Wanemoor]] without requiring either adventure's outcome.
- A recovered letter shows that Edrik consulted another scholar who rejected his Boatman thesis but kept the practical notes.

## Game-System Conversion Notes

- Build Act I as three compact defense problems rather than one long wave fight.
- Use residue dead as hazardous groups, objectives, or clocks so the party does not repeat the same combat for an entire session.
- Give Grave-Runners high mobility, knockdown, dragging, and disease pressure, but do not make every hit a campaign-ending infection.
- Let Edrik learn only from corpse masks the party leaves intact. His counterplay should be visible and interruptible.
- Mournfang must have a clear withdrawal method if intended as a recurring villain.
- Reward rescue, evidence, route control, and burial repair as strongly as enemy defeat.
