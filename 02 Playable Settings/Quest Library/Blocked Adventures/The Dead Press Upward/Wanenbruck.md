# Wanenbruck

Wanenbruck is the district seat of [[Wanemoor District]] and the threatened town in [[The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure]]. It stands where an old stone bridge crosses a Weiss feeder stream below the Last Lamp ridge. Mine carts arrive from the east, timber slides down from the north, and shallow fen barges use the lower water when the season allows.

## First Impression

Wanenbruck smells of wet bark, charcoal, iron water, brewing mash, and peat smoke. It has roughly 1,700 residents behind an old ditch and practical timber wall. The town is large enough to possess guild factions and poor enough that every closure becomes personal.

The abbey's ruined bell tower is visible on the eastern ridge. Local people use it to judge weather and rarely climb to it. The modern cemetery lies between town and ridge along the same funeral road used for nine generations.

## Districts

### Lower Market

The bridge, weigh shed, weekly market, and bailiff's office occupy the center. The market square becomes the defenders' command point during the rising. Its stone well and two large warehouses make it the strongest fallback position.

### Sawmill Bank

The northern bank contains mills, log booms, worker yards, and the militia store. The sawmill bridge links it to the rest of town. Losing the bridge divides defenders and makes the parish shelter difficult to supply.

### Saint Orra's Parish

The old-Church parish, poorhouse, Freeflame meeting hall, and common burial-fund office stand within two streets of one another. During the attack, both congregations shelter families in the parish complex because it has cellars, a kitchen, and the largest enclosed yard.

### East Gate and Mine Road

Mine offices, smithies, charcoal yards, and labor lodging line the road toward Bruch Mine. This quarter knows the missing workers were not deserters, although few people possess proof.

### Cemetery Road

The north gate opens onto the funeral road, grave fields, and Last Lamp ridge. The road becomes the first attack lane because residue bodies repeat the route by which they were once carried to burial.

## Authority and Defenders

The district bailiff handles ducal revenue and serious criminal cases but has only a small permanent watch. In crisis, authority depends upon cooperation among the bailiff, guild council, parish, Freeflame elders, and senior sexton.

At the beginning of the adventure Wanenbruck can assemble:

- 24 watch and road guards;
- 46 guild militia with serviceable weapons;
- about 80 civilians able to hold barricades or carry the wounded;
- two priests, one Freeflame elder, three trained sextons, and a dozen grave-watch volunteers.

These people can hold ground if directed. They cannot pursue the source and defend every district simultaneously.

## The Mine and the Dead

Bruch Mine is Wanenbruck's largest single employer but not its only livelihood. Its eastern adit crossed an old burial boundary beneath [[Abbey of the Last Lamp|the Abbey of the Last Lamp]]. Mine-Mistress [[Helene Bruch]] concealed the trespass because closure threatened wages, guild credit, and her family's office.

When graves open, Wanenbruck's people initially blame grave robbers, Freeflame disrespect, old-Church negligence, mine foreigners, or ghouls according to prior grievance. Physical evidence gradually separates the crimes: the mine made the opening, [[Magister Edrik Morn]] broke the Seal, and [[The Wane-Warren]] chose to exploit it.

## What Changes During Play

The `Town Holds` state determines Wanenbruck's condition:

- At 3, the market, bridge, and parish remain connected.
- At 2, one quarter burns or is abandoned and the shelter fills with wounded.
- At 1, the town divides into defended islands and families flee into wet woodland.
- At 0, organized evacuation begins along the Charcoal Road.

The town should remain populated by particular people after the opening battle. Survivors argue about responsibility, care for casualties, organize relief, and decide whether to support the descent. They are not a reward screen waiting for the party to return.

## Quest Uses

- Defend three fronts while choosing which local leaders receive militia support.
- Investigate missing workers without allowing panic to become a lynching.
- Recruit miners to rescue their fellows or collapse the Maw-Road.
- Decide whether evidence is preserved for ducal judgement or traded for immediate cooperation.
- Rebuild burial, mine, and confessional arrangements after the crisis.
