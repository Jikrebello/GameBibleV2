# Mournfang

Mournfang is an elder ghoul grave-runner, residue-worker, and independent antagonist in [[The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure]]. It preserves the original adventure's intelligent undead predator: a creature with its own purpose that may survive the principal villain.

## Origin

Mournfang was once a human funerary scholar, although it offers several incompatible names and origins when mocking prey. Ghoul-fever, deliberate feeding, and generations below ground have left memories without an honest identity. It has learned enough residue craft to read strong habits from bodies and to arrange corpses into misleading scenes.

Its long spine and forelimbs permit it to run on all fours like a [[Grave-Runners|Grave-Runner]], but it can stand, speak, write, and use tools. Bone splints fixed beneath its hide support old injuries. Strips of burial cloth mark kills whose memories proved useful.

## Relationship to the Wane-Warren

Mournfang belongs to an older hunting lineage and considers the current [[The Wane-Warren|Wane-Warren]] elders timid. It does not command their full population and will sacrifice their hunters without hesitation.

The broken Maw-Road offers it three prizes:

- regular access to Wanenbruck's dead;
- Edrik's method for directing residue;
- the Grey Sweat register and tablets, through which it can identify descendants, graves, and remembered routes.

## Behavior

Mournfang studies before attacking. It uses corpses, scratched maps, and repeated voices to make opponents draw false conclusions. It targets evidence carriers, injured people, and isolated rear guards. It avoids a fair fight unless escape is secure.

It never offers a genuine settlement in this adventure. Speech is used to delay, divide, frighten, or learn. The party may hear it through a wall, find a false scene it arranged, or receive truthful information whose purpose is to move them against Edrik.

## Tactics

- move through corpse chutes too narrow for an armored human;
- imitate a missing miner after consuming memory residue from the body;
- release Grave-Runners into a retreat lane;
- extinguish lights before attacking the person carrying records;
- enter the Seal chamber only after Edrik or the party is weakened;
- steal a register, script fragment, or body rather than fight to destruction.

## Limits

Mournfang cannot read a soul or recover a complete person from remains. Its corpse memories are fragments of habit, sensation, and recent fear. It lies about this because humans are easier to manipulate when they believe the dead are speaking whole.

White Flame, strong consecrated boundaries, and restored Seal channels restrict its movement. It is physically powerful but not immune to ordinary weapons and preparation.

## Recurring Use

If Mournfang escapes, it may return as:

- a battlefield scavenger testing Edrik's script;
- the hidden purchaser of plague registers;
- a predator targeting one Wanenbruck family whose ancestor it remembers;
- a rival to another ghoul warren;
- evidence that closing one road did not destroy the people who used it.
