# Helene Bruch

Helene Bruch is mine-mistress of Wanenbruck's eastern workings and a guild councillor. Her concealment gives [[Magister Edrik Morn]] access to [[Abbey of the Last Lamp|the Last Lamp galleries]], but she neither commissions nor knowingly assists his necromancy.

## Background

Helene inherited a failing shallow mine and rebuilt it around a new iron seam beneath the eastern ridge. The mine employs more than 140 people directly and supports smiths, charcoal burners, carriers, and widowed households through its burial fund.

She has spent ten years being told that every closure will ruin Wanenbruck and that every successful season proves her harder decisions correct. This pressure is real. It also became the excuse through which she stopped allowing some facts to change her course.

## The Trespass

Surveyors found a consecrated boundary stone where the east adit approached the abbey. Helene suspended work and sought an expert opinion. Edrik provided a false diocesan survey claiming the marked galleries had collapsed and burial custody no longer applied below the safe depth.

When miners broke into worked stone and found bones, Helene ordered them moved to a side niche without a sexton. Two workers then disappeared behind a mortared wall. She recorded them as deserters, compensated their households privately, and closed the section on paper while continuing controlled extraction nearby.

Helene knew she was concealing a burial violation and workplace deaths. She did not know the wall covered the Seal approach or that Edrik was entering at night.

## Public Face

Helene is direct, capable, and accustomed to making decisions while other councillors discuss liability. During the rising she arrives with armed workers, lamp oil, picks, rope, and the confidence of someone who expects usefulness to postpone judgement.

She first blames subsidence because admitting the mine connection may cause the militia to abandon trapped workers or burn the mine offices. Evidence and Odo Krell's testimony force her to change position.

## Goals

- rescue the six miners trapped in the east adit;
- prevent the dead from destroying Wanenbruck;
- keep the mine workforce from being treated as accomplices;
- preserve some future livelihood if possible;
- avoid public exposure of the false deserter record.

## Resources

- mine maps, tools, shoring, carts, rope, and demolition knowledge;
- disciplined workers able to clear or collapse routes;
- council influence and access to guild records;
- Edrik's false survey and payment record;
- knowledge of where the original burial stone was cut into paving.

## Judgement

Helene is neither a secret innocent nor the adventure's ultimate villain. Her choices made Edrik's crime possible and denied two families the truth. Her later courage can save lives and repair damage, but it cannot make the records honest retroactively.

Possible outcomes include:

- public trial and loss of office;
- ducal inquest with restitution to families and burial funds;
- leadership in surrendering and rebuilding the east seam;
- concealed guilt in exchange for immediate cooperation;
- death during rescue or closure;
- refusal and attempted destruction of evidence.
