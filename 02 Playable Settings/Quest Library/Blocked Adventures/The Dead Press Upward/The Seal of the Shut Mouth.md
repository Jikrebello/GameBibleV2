# The Seal of the Shut Mouth

The Seal of the Shut Mouth is a consecrated stone barrier beneath [[Abbey of the Last Lamp|the Abbey of the Last Lamp]]. It closes the **Maw-Road**, a natural and excavated passage once used by ghouls to reach surface cemeteries. Its breaking causes [[The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure]].

## Purpose

The Seal does not imprison a god, patron, Idol, Held Proof, or human soul. It denies an intelligent predatory population a physical and metaphysical feeding route. Its builders combined masonry, burial order, White Flame blessing, and yearly maintenance because none was sufficient alone.

The Seal's working principle is simple:

1. every interred person is named or explicitly commended among the unknown;
2. residue is allowed to drain rather than being hoarded or commanded;
3. the physical road is narrowed and barred;
4. living custodians inspect and renew the boundary;
5. no hunger is granted customary use merely because it has fed there before.

## Construction

The barrier consists of a fitted stone mouth across the lower quarry, a central name-stone, three shallow channels, and an iron inspection gate.

- **Lamp-oil channel:** carries maintained sacred flame across the upper joint.
- **Wash-water channel:** clears grave soil, decay, and residue from the threshold.
- **Ash channel:** receives a small part of each lawful burial fire or hearth remembrance.
- **Name-stone:** holds the communal clause for known and unknown dead.
- **Inspection gate:** permits repair without opening a passage large enough for a ghoul.

The mechanism is local sacred engineering. It works because its material, communal, and spiritual functions agree.

## The Breaking

[[Magister Edrik Morn]] cut through the name-stone and reversed the channels. He expected the released residue to reveal the Boatman's method. The backlash destroyed his lower body, opened the Maw-Road, and made surrounding corpse residue responsive to his grave-script.

Edrik calls this inheritance. In truth, the broken Seal gives him observation and leverage over remains. It does not show him a soul, permit judgement, or alter the authority of the Vestibule.

## Adventure Functions

The Seal produces four visible problems:

- residue dead rise through the opened burial galleries;
- ghouls can travel upward and fray recent burials;
- Edrik can direct corpses marked by his script;
- damaged names and displaced remains make complete repair harder.

The party can weaken Edrik by restoring channel flow, covering or breaking corpse-masks, speaking the communal clause, and removing bodies from the Maw-Road.

## Repair States

### True Restoration

Requires the original register, preserved or recovered name-tablets and remains, a lawful rite-keeper, physical rebuilding, and surrender of the mine adit as burial ground. It closes the road and restores ordinary residue drainage.

### Lasting Mortar

Uses authenticated records, consecrated stonework, and living witnesses but lacks some names or remains. It should hold for generations if Wanenbruck restores annual inspection.

### Destructive Closure

Collapsing or burning the route prevents immediate passage but loses graves and does not repair every burial duty. It is a legitimate emergency act, not an equivalent form of restoration.

### Temporary Suppression

Killing Edrik, clearing nearby dead, or blessing the chamber quiets the rising without fixing the road. The breach will become usable again.

## Limits

- The Seal cannot compel a soul or determine judgement.
- It cannot make an unburied crime disappear by closing stone over it.
- Consecration without masonry leaves a physical ghoul route.
- Masonry without names and residue release creates another pressure chamber.
- No private owner can acquire the Seal through mine, grave, or salvage title.
