# Sexton Adelheid Kern

Adelheid Kern is Wanenbruck's senior sexton, the first person to understand that the cemetery has opened from below, and the principal patron of [[The Dead Press Upward — Full Adventure]]. She is a lay burial officer rather than an ordained priest.

## Background

Adelheid comes from a family of potters and entered cemetery service after helping make burial plaques during the Grey Sweat memorial renewal. Twenty-three years of work have taught her soil, drainage, family quarrels, grave theft, residue behavior, and the difference between a frightened mourner's story and a disturbed ward.

Her office is confirmed through Saint Orra's parish, but Wanemoor custom requires the senior sexton to serve every lawful burial in the common ground. Freeflame families therefore trust her more than they trust some old-Church clergy.

## During the First Rising

Adelheid is checking Souls-month boundary marks when the eastern stone splits and the first graves collapse inward. She sees bodies climb from openings farther west while something drags fresher remains downward near the ridge. That observation lets her distinguish residue dead from ghouls before anyone else.

She runs to Wanenbruck ringing the cemetery handbell. Her warning gives the town minutes rather than none.

## Character

Adelheid is exact, dry-spoken, and unwilling to turn fear into theatrical mysticism. She names a dead person when she knows the name and says "the body" when the person is no longer present. Careless language matters to her because Edrik's entire doctrine depends upon confusing those things.

She believes good burial is both mercy and public work. A pauper's grave must drain, be recorded, and be defended exactly as a duke's must. She has little patience for officials who call this sentiment after using cemetery land as cheap ground.

## Private Burden

Adelheid inherited a note from her predecessor describing faint knocking below the eastern cemetery twenty years ago. She inspected the modern graves, found no opening, and filed it as old-mine subsidence. She did not take a bribe or knowingly conceal danger, but the omission weighs on her once the abbey galleries are found.

## Resources

- cemetery keys, maps, and current burial register;
- authority to identify and move remains;
- trained grave-watch volunteers;
- practical knowledge of residue behavior;
- part of the Last Lamp annual closing phrase;
- trust across Wanenbruck's confessional divide.

## Quest Use

- warns the town and explains that the dead opened from below;
- can stabilize one cemetery ward while the party defends her;
- authenticates the Grey Sweat register and name-tablets;
- completes the true restoration if Burial Integrity reaches 3;
- insists that emergency destruction and complete repair be named honestly;
- serves as a witness in any later judgement of Helene and the mine council.

If Adelheid dies, the adventure continues. The party must recover the rite-book and assemble Pastor Lukas, Elder Marta, and surviving grave-watch witnesses to replace her authority.
