# The Bailiff's Ram — Full Adventure

## Adventure at a Glance

**Premise:** A ram carrying a notarized speech-scroll interrupts the party's lunch and claims to be a Vortharan student transformed for defaulting on his tuition bond. Before he finishes, a collection crew arrives with intelligent hounds and a bear that all used to be debtors.

**Beginning:** The party must decide whether to surrender [[Tavian Sorel]], fight or negotiate with recovery foreman Borso Caldrin, and believe enough of Tavian's self-serving account to investigate the [[House of Mutable Form]].

**Middle:** Evidence on the road and at [[Bent Bough Lodge]] reveals that Tavian stole credit from [[Nera Voss]], but Nera has gone far beyond recovering one defaulted ram. She uses the unstable [[Reversion Crook]] to turn other debtors into [[Collateral Beasts]] and work off their balances through dangerous collections.

**End:** The party can steal the Crook, release the transformed agents, force a settlement, repay or redirect Tavian's debt, or confront Nera directly. If pressed, she turns a canopy bed into a wooden imitation wyrm and may become a [[Form-Ruins|Form-Ruin]] when the Crook finally fails.

**Tier:** Comic R. The adventure supports a level 4-5 analog when transformed debtors can be persuaded or released, combat is brisk and environmental, and the furniture-wyrm is a dangerous joke rather than a true drake.

**Expected length:** 2-4 substantial sessions: lunch and collection, road investigation, lodge infiltration, climax and restoration.

## Who, What, Where, Why, and How

- **Who:** the player crew; Tavian Sorel in ram form; Bailiff Nera Voss; recovery foreman Borso Caldrin; transformed clerk Mira Tern; the lodge's other collateral agents; and representatives of the House of Mutable Form.
- **What:** decide whether Tavian should be returned, recover or stabilize the Reversion Crook, restore people trapped in collection forms, and settle what legitimate debts and wrongs remain.
- **Where:** a roadside eating-house below Vorthara, the highland collection road, and Bent Bough Lodge, a transformation laboratory grown through four hornbeam trees.
- **Why the party acts:** Tavian asks for rescue; the collection crew attacks or attempts seizure in front of them; an injured transformed debtor begs for release; or the unstable Crook threatens everyone who has been altered with it.
- **Why Tavian became a ram:** his tuition bond authorized the House to assign a reversible productive form after willful default. Nera chose a ram template and registered him as transport collateral.
- **How it escalates:** Tavian omits his exploitation of Nera, Nera conceals the Crook's failures, transformed agents choose sides, and each further use makes the next body less predictable.

## Moral and Metaphysical Ruling

Tavian owes tuition, service, credit, and compensation. He claimed Nera's research, used her labor, fled a service obligation he knowingly signed, and now promises rewards he cannot pay.

He does not owe anyone ownership of his body. A clause allowing an institution to transform a debtor into laboring collateral is evil even when signed and enforceable. Its metaphysical effect proves that identities and obligations can be bound; it does not make the bargain just.

Nera's grievance is genuine. Her later conduct is still her own. She has transformed poorer debtors into dangerous collection forms, withheld safe reversal, and treated bodily autonomy as a privilege earned through performance.

The transformed agents remain people. Some have used their forms to intimidate and injure others. Some prefer aspects of their altered bodies. None should lose the right to restoration because their current silhouette resembles a monster.

The adventure's comedy comes from Vortharan confidence colliding with badly controlled magic: notarized livestock, a dog demanding receipts, a bear trapped in an outhouse, and an airborne bed. Their personhood remains real underneath it.

## The Central Truth

Tavian and Nera entered the House of Mutable Form in the same apprentice cohort. Tavian was charismatic, well connected, and unusually gifted at adaptive body templates. Nera was a scholarship student with stronger technical discipline.

Their most important project was a **reversion index**, a method for storing a person's original body pattern separately from a temporary working form. Nera built the index. Tavian presented it to patrons under his name and secured an extended tuition line. When Nera protested, Tavian called her his proof-assistant and claimed the arrangement was customary.

The House eventually denied Tavian mastery because he could not reproduce the work without her. His tuition balance converted into compulsory service. Tavian instead left Vorthara, performed unlicensed transformations, and defaulted.

Nera completed her qualification but received neither the patronage nor credit Tavian had taken. The House offered her a recovery license and access to the [[Reversion Crook]], built from their joint research. Recovering Tavian would clear her own remaining tuition balance and establish her authorship.

She found him, served notice, and transformed him into a ram under the bond's body-collateral clause. Tavian later escaped [[Bent Bough Lodge]] with a speech-scroll.

By then Nera had expanded the system. Debtors unable to pay were offered collection work in stronger forms. The Crook's reversion index began accumulating errors, but Nera concealed them because admitting instability would invalidate every active transformation and make the House responsible for restoration.

## The House of Mutable Form

The [[House of Mutable Form]] is a minor Vortharan teaching-house specializing in reversible bodies for dangerous labor, surgery, performance, infiltration, and arena use. It is licensed, profitable, academically serious, and morally compromised.

Its standard tuition bond requires years of post-study work. The disputed clause permits a defaulted debtor to be assigned a **productive form suited to restitution**, provided the form is certified, reversible, medically monitored, and not deployed outside the listed hazard class.

Nera's practices violate the final three safeguards. The House would prefer to discipline one bailiff quietly rather than admit the clause itself invited the abuse.

## Adventure States

- **Crook Strain (0-4):** starts at 2. Every major transformation or rushed reversal raises it. At 4, the next use creates a Form-Ruin and breaks the index.
- **Collateral Trust (-2 to +2):** whether transformed debtors fight for Nera, remain neutral, or help the party.
- **Tavian's Honesty (0-3):** rises when Tavian admits his unpaid debt, Nera's authorship, and his unlicensed work without being forced.
- **Proof of Misuse (0-3):** unlicensed templates, concealed strain log, and debtor testimony/form register.
- **House Exposure (0-3):** how many outside witnesses or copied records make a quiet cover-up impossible.

## The Four Main Solutions

The adventure supports several approaches from the outset.

### Theft

Steal the Reversion Crook and its form register from the lodge. The Crook alone cannot safely identify every original body, so stealing both matters.

### Repayment

Pay or secure Tavian's tuition balance. This ends the House's immediate claim over him but does not restore other debtors or address unsafe transformations.

### Proof

Recover evidence that Nera exceeded the bond's health, template, and hazard limits. Under Vortharan liability custom, unsafe collateral must be restored before collection resumes.

### Turn the Clause

The House classifies a transformed debtor as maintained collateral while in an assigned form. That makes the House responsible for damage caused by an unsafe instrument and prohibits deploying the collateral where its value is knowingly threatened. Every combat collection Nera ordered becomes evidence that the House breached its own clause first.

This argument can force restoration quickly. It does not make body-collateral morally acceptable; it uses the institution's own language to rescue people before the larger fight.

## Act I — Lunch, Interrupted

### Beat 1: The Notarized Ram

The party is eating at **The Last Ladle**, a highland road-house whose proprietor has posted the ordinary Vortharan notices: no loose elementals, no unpaid familiars, and transformed customers pay for damaged furniture according to current mass.

A white ram crashes through the herb stall, leaps onto the party's table, and drops a waxed scroll into the drink of whoever looks most capable of reading it.

The scroll is a standard **speech deposition**, witnessed in advance so the subject can make intelligible statements while transformed. Reading its activation line lets everyone nearby understand Tavian for one hour.

Tavian introduces himself as a respected transmuter imprisoned by a vindictive bailiff. He asks the party to recover a stolen restoration artifact and promises a substantial reward.

Automatic doubts:

- the scroll identifies him as **Tavian Sorel, non-master student, status incomplete**;
- a brass tag under his wool bears the House of Mutable Form seal;
- he calls Nera his former assistant before admitting they entered the same cohort;
- he knows exactly which menu items contain clover.

### Beat 2: Borso's Collection Crew

Before Tavian completes his account, Borso Caldrin enters with three collared hounds and a large cloaked figure. The hounds have alert human eyes; one places a folded receipt carefully beside the innkeeper before growling.

Borso presents a recovery notice and asks for the ram. He is willing to:

- pay a modest handling fee;
- promise a House favor;
- let the party accompany Tavian to the lodge;
- explain that Tavian defaulted and stole academic credit;
- threaten force if the party hides or removes marked collateral.

He does not know the Crook is near failure. Mira Tern, the receipt-bearing hound, does.

### Beat 3: Collection, Fight, or Chase

If talks fail, the hounds maneuver to seize Tavian while Borso and the cloaked bear contain the party. The agents fight intelligently and seek capture, not slaughter.

The inn provides slapstick terrain with real consequences:

- spilled stew and rolling stools;
- Tavian climbing shelves while continuing his argument;
- the bear becoming entangled in a serving hatch;
- Mira protecting the receipt box from every side;
- frightened customers who know the legal hazard signs but not where to stand.

Defeated Collateral Beasts revert only if their name tab is removed with the alignment key; ordinary injury does not conveniently cure them.

If Borso captures Tavian, the crew departs immediately and the party can follow in a road chase. If the party wins, Mira or another captured agent can reveal the transformed debtors' situation.

### Act I Output

The party has Tavian or a trail to him, has met intelligent collection forms, and knows the dispute includes both a real debt and an unsafe transformation system.

## Act II — The Road of Better Explanations

The party can take one or two investigative stops before reaching Bent Bough Lodge. Each helps a different solution and keeps the adventure moving.

### Stop 1: The Recovery Post

A brass kiosk beside the road stores public bond notices. Its clerk refuses to adjudicate anything but provides certified summaries for a fee or a sufficiently entertaining account of the inn fight.

The summary establishes:

- Tavian's actual student status and service default;
- the productive-form clause;
- required medical monitoring and certified templates;
- Nera's recovery license;
- no authorization for hound, ape, bear, or combat collection forms.

This provides Proof of Misuse 1.

### Stop 2: The Discarded Form

Beside a stream, the party finds **Pell Arno**, a former collateral agent whose attempted reversal left him human except for a hound's voice and sense of smell. Nera released him from service but struck the failure from her log.

Pell describes the lodge, the strain symptoms, and the spare alignment key hidden by Sella. Helping him reach the city adds House Exposure and gives the party a witness.

### Stop 3: Tavian's Full Account

Pressure, kindness, or evidence can force Tavian to admit one omitted truth at a time:

1. He was never awarded mastery.
2. Nera built the reversion index he presented.
3. He fled compulsory service and performed unlicensed transformations.

Each admission raises Tavian's Honesty. At 3, he voluntarily offers future earnings and public authorship correction as part of a settlement.

### Stop 4: The Fast Track

Following the collection crew without delay reaches the lodge before Nera recalls the yard agents or chains the register to the Crook. The party gains tactical surprise but less institutional leverage.

### Act II Output

The party understands that neither Tavian's rescue story nor Nera's recovery notice is sufficient by itself and has chosen speed, proof, or witness support.

## Act III — Bent Bough Lodge

### The Compound

[[Bent Bough Lodge]] is grown through four hornbeam trees on a steep shelf above the road. Root ramps link three platforms:

- **Garden Platform:** experimental fruit, open work space, and yard agents.
- **Middle House:** kitchen, sitting room, laboratory, register room, and stair roots.
- **Canopy Room:** Nera's bedroom, private notes, and the Crook's charging rig.

Two debtor huts and an oversized outhouse stand below. Copper braces, body-pattern ribbons, and warning chimes make the lodge look half woodland retreat and half industrial clinic.

### Zone 1: The Dice Yard

Daro, Sella, and Hobb occupy powerful great-ape forms and play with oversized dice beside their weapons. They are bored, underpaid in debt credit, and expecting Borso.

They can be bypassed or turned through:

- Tavian admitting what he did to Nera;
- proof that their forms are unlicensed;
- a credible restoration plan;
- winning or cheating entertainingly at their dice game;
- finding Sella's alignment key;
- promising not to treat Daro's preferred strength as a disease to erase.

At Collateral Trust +1, they stand aside. At +2, they help restrain Nera or evacuate unstable debtors.

### Zone 2: The Outhouse Bear

Oren Bale was sent to intimidate a caravan and returned with the Crook already misaligning his proportions. He has become stuck in the lodge's reinforced outhouse and is furious that everyone assumes the noise is an ambush.

Freeing him quietly creates an ally and provides Proof of Misuse 2: the body tag lists a certified mule form, not a bear.

### Zone 3: Laboratory and Form Register

The middle platform contains body diagrams, pattern ribbons, specimen molds, and the form register pairing each debtor's current template with an original identity index.

The party finds:

- eight active debtors and two failed reversals;
- a concealed strain log showing the Crook has exceeded its service limit;
- Tavian and Nera's original project notes, with Nera's handwriting beneath Tavian's submitted cover page;
- combat deployments outside every licensed hazard class;
- a scheduled inspection that Nera has postponed twice.

The log provides Proof of Misuse 3. The research proves Nera's authorship and Tavian's theft.

Removing the form register makes safe mass restoration harder. Copying it or carrying the full case preserves every identity.

### Zone 4: The Kitchen Conversation

If the lodge has not reached full alarm, Nera speaks from the middle house rather than attacking. She asks the party to return Tavian and offers to release one transformed agent immediately as proof of good faith.

Her argument:

- Tavian stole her future and fled obligations poorer students cannot escape.
- Every active agent accepted collection work instead of harsher enforcement.
- Their strength makes them safer than ordinary collectors.
- The House will erase them from the profession if the Crook's strain becomes public.
- Tavian will exploit anyone who restores him without conditions.

Most of this is true. It does not justify withholding bodies or concealing failures.

### Zone 5: Nera's Canopy Room

The [[Reversion Crook]] rests in a copper charging frame chained to a heavy canopy bed. Nera keeps its alignment ring around her neck and the master register key beneath the mattress.

The party can reach it by:

- climbing the exterior hornbeam;
- drawing Nera into negotiation below;
- using Sella's spare key through the laboratory stair;
- returning Tavian as a distraction;
- staging a lodge inspection with recovered notices;
- openly challenging Nera and letting the agents choose whether to defend her.

### Act III Output

The party has the people, evidence, artifact, or leverage needed to resolve the matter. If it chooses violence, the lodge itself becomes Nera's final weapon.

## Act IV — The Bed-Wyrm

### Nera's Escalation

Nera does not begin with suicidal violence. She escalates when the party attempts to take the Crook and register together, turns every debtor against her, or appears ready to restore Tavian without acknowledging her work.

She uses the Crook's object-form setting on the canopy bed. The frame tears free of its chains and crashes through the roof as a wooden imitation wyrm with curtain wings, mattress hide, brass-claw fittings, and a breath of nails and splinters.

The **bed-wyrm is not a true drake**:

- it has no heart-gem or Menhir court;
- it has no soul or animal ecology;
- it follows a crude attack template;
- fire, severing the charging ribbons, or breaking Nera's control returns it to ruined furniture;
- it cannot survive beyond the Crook's operating range.

Characters can fight it, climb it, cut the pattern ribbons, steal the Crook from its rider, lure it into the hornbeams, or persuade collateral agents to pull down the charging frame.

### The Final Misfire

When Crook Strain reaches 4, the next use combines every partially indexed form still in the instrument. A user attempting self-transformation becomes a [[Form-Ruins|Form-Ruin]]: one person trapped inside several incomplete bodies and object patterns.

Nera may attempt this out of desperation, intending to become an arena predator strong enough to win. The party can prevent the use by disarming her, showing the strain log, gaining Collateral Trust +2, or offering a settlement that publicly recognizes her authorship.

If the misfire occurs, the Form-Ruin is frightened, dangerous, and still Nera. It must be contained without assuming destruction is the only answer.

## Ways to Resolve the Conflict

### Steal and Restore

Take the Crook, register, and alignment keys. Safe reversal requires time and raises strain only when rushed. The party can restore two people immediately, then bring the system to Vorthara under guard.

### Force House Liability

Present all three Proofs of Misuse and preserve at least one debtor witness. The House must suspend collection, restore every maintained collateral body, and place Nera under review. House agents may arrive in the aftermath rather than interrupt the climax.

### Repay and Negotiate

Pay Tavian's balance or secure a patron guarantee. Nera can release him while retaining her license. Other debtors require separate release unless the party negotiates a collective settlement.

### Collective Refusal

At Collateral Trust +2, the agents refuse further collection and seize the lodge. Without operators, the House faces the cost of recovering its instrument and restoring everyone. The party can support, restrain, or mediate the resulting occupation.

### Arena Challenge

Vorthara permits institutional disputes to be settled through sponsored arena terms. The party can issue a challenge over whether the body-collateral clause produces safer recovery. Nera must either restore agents for testimony or field them as proof of safety before a public audience.

This defers final judgment but guarantees House Exposure 3 and creates a follow-up adventure.

### Destroy the Crook

Breaking the instrument ends further transformation but strands every active form until another specialist reconstructs the register. Destroying it after copying the indices is defensible; destroying it impulsively harms the debtors it was supposedly meant to free.

## Restoration Procedure

Each safe restoration needs:

- the person's name and original index from the form register;
- an alignment key;
- the Crook below Strain 4;
- a stable physical space;
- the subject's present consent.

One careful procedure can restore a person completely or preserve chosen safe modifications. A rushed group reversal risks swapped features, lost speech, or a Form-Ruin.

Tavian's restoration is the least technically difficult and the most politically contested. Restoring him does not cancel his debt or erase Nera's authorship claim.

## Ending States

### Every Body Returned

Proof of Misuse is 3, the register survives, Collateral Trust is positive, and the Crook remains usable long enough for controlled restoration. The House voids the body-collateral clause in settlement, Nera loses her recovery license, and Tavian accepts service plus public correction of authorship. This is the strongest continuation.

### Restitution Without Ownership

Tavian is restored after accepting tuition service, naming Nera as co-author, and funding compensation from future work. Nera surrenders the Crook and helps restore the others. She remains accountable but becomes the technical witness who dismantles the system she abused.

### Tavian Gets Everything

The party restores Tavian, returns his notes, and leaves before helping the other debtors. He resumes calling himself Magister, minimizes Nera's contribution, and describes the party as assistants. The House continues collection while hunting him again.

### The Bailiff Collects

The party returns Tavian or accepts Nera's offer. She pays, clears Borso's service credit, and restores one cooperative agent. Tavian remains a ram and the lodge continues operating until the Crook fails somewhere less contained.

### The Collateral Company

The transformed agents seize the lodge, register, and Crook. They restore those who wish it and retain useful modifications for others. Vorthara declares them stolen institutional property; they declare themselves a recovery company specializing in recovering people from institutions.

### Broken Crook, Fixed Forms

The Crook is destroyed or exhausted after the immediate crisis. Nera and Tavian survive, but several debtors remain in animal bodies. The group must travel to Vorthara, bargain with Fleshweavers, or find another reversion specialist.

### The Form-Ruin

Nera or another user survives the catastrophic misfire and escapes into the highlands. The result speaks in several voices, remembers pieces of multiple debtors, and seeks components that make its body feel briefly coherent.

### House Settlement

The party returns everything quietly in exchange for full restoration and payment. The House dismisses Nera, credits Tavian's debt against recovered property, and rewrites the same clause with improved safety language. The immediate people are helped; the underlying institution survives.

## What the Adventure Changes

- Vorthara gains a precedent over whether a tuition bond can claim bodily form.
- The House of Mutable Form becomes discredited, reformed, emboldened, or occupied by its former collateral agents.
- Tavian becomes a chastened practitioner, recurring fraudulent magister, or unusually articulate ram.
- Nera becomes prisoner, whistleblower, fugitive form-engineer, or Form-Ruin.
- The transformed debtors become restored witnesses, a new company, or a rescue network for other bonded apprentices.
- Collateral Beasts and Form-Ruins enter the known bestiary of Vortharan magical industry.
- The Reversion Crook becomes evidence, dangerous loot, destroyed research, or a player tool with strict limits.
