# Reversion Crook

The Reversion Crook is a hooked blackwood instrument built by the [[House of Mutable Form]] from Tavian Sorel and Nera Voss's reversion-index research. It imposes stored temporary forms and restores a subject from those forms using a separately preserved original body pattern.

It is a specialized industrial artifact, not an unrestricted instrument of true creation.

## Components

- **Crook:** projects and removes registered form patterns.
- **Alignment ring:** selects identity, template, and direction of change.
- **Form register:** stores names, original indices, current form, consent instrument, and service status.
- **Pattern ribbons:** maintain large or object-based forms within operating range.
- **Charging frame:** releases accumulated strain between uses.

## Valid Uses

- apply a certified temporary body;
- reverse that body when the original index is intact;
- preserve selected safe modifications with present consent;
- animate a simple object according to a short behavior template;
- diagnose which parts of a transformation no longer match its index.

It cannot create a true drake, supply a soul to furniture, recreate a dead person from appearance, or restore someone whose original index has been destroyed without outside reconstruction.

## Strain

Every use leaves conflicting body instructions in the Crook. Proper cleaning and charging remove them. Rapid use, combat forms, damaged ribbons, and rushed reversal accumulate strain.

- **0:** aligned and inspected.
- **1:** cosmetic bleed between templates.
- **2:** speech, scale, or instinct persists after reversal.
- **3:** active forms begin exchanging traits.
- **4:** the next use layers several forms into a [[Form-Ruins|Form-Ruin]] and breaks the index.

## Restoration Rule

Safe restoration needs the subject's name, original index, alignment key, stable space, and present consent. Contractual permission recorded years earlier is not a substitute for present consent during reversion or preservation of modifications.

## Object Forms

The Crook can animate large objects temporarily. The bed-wyrm at Bent Bough Lodge is a wooden attack pattern powered through the charging frame. It resembles a drake because Nera used an arena sketch; it has no heart-gem, lineage, or independent life.

## Story Use

The Crook can become dangerous player equipment, institutional evidence, a restoration tool, stolen identity technology, or the seed of a broader Vortharan body-market scandal.
