# Form-Ruins

A Form-Ruin is a living person caught inside several incompatible transformation instructions after an artifact, ritual, or flesh-script fails. Limbs, senses, instincts, voices, and object patterns compete to define one body.

The person remains present. “Ruined” describes the failed magical structure, not their moral worth.

## Causes

- repeated transformations without clearing prior templates;
- damaged or missing original body indices;
- rushed reversal under combat conditions;
- multiple practitioners issuing incompatible form commands;
- fleshweaving layered over contract-bound transformation;
- catastrophic strain in an artifact such as the [[Reversion Crook]].

## Signs

- anatomy changing in response to nearby animals, objects, or spoken templates;
- several voices using different pieces of one memory;
- clothing or furniture material incorporated into living tissue temporarily;
- body parts reverting at different rates;
- instinctive behavior that the person recognizes but cannot consistently stop;
- repeated requests for one stabilizing name or shape.

## Encounter Role

Form-Ruins are volatile rescue encounters, tragic antagonists, magical-industry evidence, and body-horror threats. They should not default to enemies that must be killed.

Their danger comes from pain, panic, environmental damage, and abilities firing without coherent control.

## Behavior

- seek familiar objects or people that reinforce identity;
- attack restraints or practitioners associated with the failed change;
- borrow movement from whichever template currently dominates;
- repeat contractual or ritual language embedded in the transformation;
- become briefly lucid when addressed by name and present intention;
- absorb nearby pattern magic if it promises coherence.

## Stabilization

Useful methods include:

- isolate and remove one form instruction at a time;
- recover the original index without assuming it must be the final chosen body;
- establish a present self-description spoken by the subject;
- use White Flame to stabilize personhood before physical reconstruction;
- separate contract seals from flesh-pattern commands;
- transfer excess templates into prepared inert material;
- bring the subject to a qualified restoration team rather than improvising further changes.

## Canon Boundary

Form-Ruins are transformation failures, not a naturally breeding creature family, a new people, or evidence that bodies are unreal. They remain Material persons harmed by unstable magic.

## Quest Uses

- a Form-Ruin remembers details belonging to several vanished debtors;
- a restoration clinic hides failed patients before inspection;
- an artifact tries to “repair” everyone nearby using the same damaged index;
- a subject prefers one acquired form but needs the others removed;
- Fleshweavers offer a reliable cure whose price is permanent institutional custody.
