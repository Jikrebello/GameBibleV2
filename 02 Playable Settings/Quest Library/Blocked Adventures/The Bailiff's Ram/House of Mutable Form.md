# House of Mutable Form

The House of Mutable Form is a dissident teaching-house within Vorthara's broader Fleshweaver inheritance. Its founders rejected the Red Covenant's most predatory body-market while retaining disciplined study of living form, reversal, and controlled transformation. That ancestry gives the House expertise; it does not make the House innocent of coercion or bad teaching.

Its authority is private and local. House seals, recovery officers, and student covenants bind members and named partner schools, not Vorthara as a whole.

The House of Mutable Form is a minor Vortharan teaching-house specializing in reversible bodily transformation. Its graduates design temporary forms for hazardous labor, surgery, performance, infiltration, rescue, and arena use.

It lacks the scale of the Fleshweavers and presents itself as their cleaner, reversible, commercially responsible counterpart.

## Curriculum

- body-pattern indexing;
- temporary animal and labor forms;
- preservation of speech, memory, and fine control;
- surgical transformation and recovery;
- object-form animation;
- transformation liability and consent instruments;
- emergency reversion.

## Tuition Road

Entry uses the ordinary Vortharan tuition bond: the House fronts training costs against years of post-mastery service. Washouts retain the debt without the earning power promised to successful graduates.

The House's most notorious term permits assignment of a **productive form suited to restitution** after willful default. Written safeguards require:

- a certified reversible template;
- continuing medical monitoring;
- use within a declared hazard class;
- a preserved original identity index;
- restoration after the credited service period.

The clause remains bodily coercion even when every safeguard is observed.

## Institutional Position

House masters defend the clause as preferable to prison, arena sale, or inherited debt. Critics note that the House defines the alternatives, licenses the forms, prices the service, and decides whether restoration was earned.

The scandal at [[Bent Bough Lodge]] threatens the whole model because [[Nera Voss]] violated safeguards using an instrument built and maintained by the House.

## Magic

The House combines flesh-pattern craft, artifact engineering, identity indexing, and contract seals. It does not create true animal souls or drake lineages. Temporary object-beasts are animated patterns, not living species.

## Story Use

- apprentices trying to escape inherited service;
- workers who want to retain chosen modifications;
- restoration specialists asked to reverse war magic;
- stolen body indices used for impersonation;
- institutional hearings settled in the arena;
- a house that can genuinely heal catastrophic injuries while treating consent as negotiable.
