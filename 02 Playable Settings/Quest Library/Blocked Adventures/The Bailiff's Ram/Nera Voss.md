# Nera Voss

Nera Voss is a licensed Vortharan recovery bailiff and the principal antagonist of [[The Bailiff's Ram]]. She co-developed the reversion index that powers the [[Reversion Crook]], received little credit for it, and now uses the instrument to turn defaulted debtors into collection agents.

## Grievance

Nera entered the [[House of Mutable Form]] on a scholarship bond. She performed the exacting index work while Tavian Sorel handled patrons and presentations. Tavian claimed the project publicly and secured money with it.

The House eventually recognized enough of Nera's contribution to qualify her, but not enough to give her the patronage Tavian had already consumed. It offered recovery work as the quickest way to clear her own tuition.

## Crossing the Line

Nera's first transformation of Tavian used a certified clause and stable ram form. She later began offering other debtors collection work in hound, ape, bear, and combat bodies.

She concealed that:

- the forms exceeded her license;
- refusal often meant harsher enforcement rather than a real alternative;
- the Crook's index was accumulating errors;
- two attempted reversals had already failed;
- transformed agents had no independent guarantee of restoration.

## Character

Nera is controlled, bitter, technically formidable, and capable of excellent explanations for choices she already knows are wrong. She values competence and despises Tavian's charm because it succeeded where her work did not.

She is not needlessly cruel to the collateral agents. Her deeper failure is believing that good maintenance makes ownership humane.

## Negotiation

Nera can accept a settlement that includes:

- public recognition of her authorship;
- Tavian's continuing tuition service;
- preservation of the form register;
- supervised restoration rather than destruction of the Crook;
- testimony that distinguishes her original work from later abuse.

She refuses any outcome in which Tavian is restored, praised, and allowed to describe her as a jealous assistant.

## Failure

When cornered, Nera uses object-form magic to animate her canopy bed as a wooden wyrm. If she then uses the overloaded Crook on herself, she becomes a [[Form-Ruins|Form-Ruin]] rather than the powerful arena predator she intended.

## Continuation

Nera can become a prisoner, technical witness against body-collateral, fugitive form-engineer, reluctant restoration specialist, or recurring Form-Ruin seeking coherence.
