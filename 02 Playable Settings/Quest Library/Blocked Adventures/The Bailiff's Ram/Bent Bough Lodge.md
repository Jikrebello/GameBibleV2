# Bent Bough Lodge

Bent Bough Lodge is a transformation recovery station on the highland road below Vorthara. Four hornbeam trees have been shaped into linked working platforms using old House of Mutable Form demonstrations.

It is beautiful at a distance and unmistakably industrial up close.

## Layout

- **Garden Platform:** experimental plants, open exercise space, and body-form testing.
- **Middle House:** laboratory, kitchen, sitting room, register room, and root stair.
- **Canopy Room:** bailiff's bedroom, private records, and artifact charging frame.
- **Debtor Huts:** reinforced sleeping quarters sized for several assigned forms.
- **Outhouse:** rebuilt twice, currently too small for a bear.
- **Lower Yard:** carts, recovery cages, pattern-ribbon drying lines, and the road gate.

## Function

The lodge receives transformed debtors between assignments. Staff monitor body stability, issue collection routes, record debt credit, and perform scheduled reversals.

Under [[Nera Voss]], monitoring became irregular and assignments increasingly dangerous. The lodge now contains more combat-capable bodies than a recovery station is licensed to maintain.

## Environmental Features

- root ramps that can be raised or redirected;
- copper charging braces linking the canopy to the laboratory;
- warning chimes reacting to unstable transformation magic;
- branches strong enough to support great-ape forms;
- specimen fruit capable of briefly changing hair, voice, or scent;
- body-pattern ribbons that can interrupt or redirect the bed-wyrm.

## Aftermath Uses

The lodge can become a supervised restoration clinic, collateral-agent cooperative, player base near Vorthara, seized House evidence site, or abandoned leak-zone filled with half-finished forms.
