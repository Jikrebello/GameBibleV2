# Tavian Sorel

Tavian Sorel is a talented Vortharan transfiguration washout currently trapped in the form of a white ram. He speaks through notarized deposition scrolls, complains with great precision, and introduces himself as “Magister” despite never receiving mastery.

## Education

Tavian entered the [[House of Mutable Form]] with exceptional intuitive talent and enough social confidence to make patrons believe every promising idea was already his.

His cohort-mate [[Nera Voss]] designed the reversion index that made their joint project valuable. Tavian presented it under his name, described Nera as a proof-assistant, and received an extended tuition line. He could not reproduce her work during final examination and was denied mastery.

## Default

Tavian's bond converted into compulsory post-study service. He fled Vorthara, performed unlicensed temporary transformations, and continued styling himself Magister until Nera recovered him under the bond's body-collateral clause.

She assigned him a certified ram form. He escaped [[Bent Bough Lodge]] two years later carrying a speech-scroll.

## Character

Tavian is vain, funny, articulate, selfish under pressure, and genuinely horrified by what Nera has done to other debtors. He wants to believe his own exploitation was ordinary academic custom while hers is uniquely monstrous.

He is worth rescuing without being allowed to author the final account.

## What He Offers

- exact knowledge of the lodge's original layout;
- practical transfiguration theory despite lacking mastery;
- promises of money he does not possess;
- information about Nera's habits and pride;
- public correction of Nera's authorship if pushed toward honesty;
- future service and transformations after restoration.

## Growth

Tavian's most restorative continuation requires three admissions:

1. He never mastered.
2. Nera built the reversion index.
3. His debt and unlicensed work remain real even though bodily collection is wrong.

Restoration without these admissions gives him back a body without changing the behavior that lost it.
