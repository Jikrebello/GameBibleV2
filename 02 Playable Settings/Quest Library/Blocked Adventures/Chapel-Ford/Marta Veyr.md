# Marta Veyr

## Role

Marta is the public benefactor behind the House of the Kind Lantern and the trained Weeping Courts operative who manufactured Chapel-Ford's apparition.

## Public Face

A widowed merchant who came south after losing her family to fever. She speaks plainly, remembers household needs, pays workers promptly, and attends funerals after everyone important has left. Many villagers have good reason to love her.

## Belief

Marta believes neglected communities will never receive justice from institutions that already overlooked them. She offers counterfeit recognition first, then uses the resulting devotion to make it supernaturally real. In her view, every great shrine began because somebody insisted on being heard.

## The Evil

She knows the shrine feeds the Rival through comparison, resentment, and dependency. She deliberately teaches villagers that another community's blessing is evidence of their own deprivation. Her charity makes this manipulation durable; it does not make it good.

## Present Crisis

The Ford Apparition has outgrown her control and selected Miri as a permanent living witness. Marta could stop the ritual by destroying six years of work. Instead, she convinces herself that stabilizing the apparition through Miri will protect everyone.

## Resolution

- **Confession:** possible when she sees that Miri is being erased rather than elevated.
- **Capture:** makes her a valuable source on other Courts.
- **Escape:** creates a recurring antagonist who knows the party's private griefs.
- **Death:** ends her performances but does not automatically destroy the Borrowed Saint.

Marta's redemption, if attempted, requires dismantling the shrine, returning stolen letters and memories, and accepting judgement. Good intentions or useful charity alone are insufficient.
