# Edda Bent-Oak

## Role

Edda is Chapel-Ford's senior elder, treaty voice, and keeper of the local Bent-Oak genealogy. She commissions the search for her granddaughter Miri.

## Character

Edda is practical, devout, and tired of outsiders treating Chapel-Folk as either exemplary converts or pitiful cultural casualties. She preserves inherited fire customs, attends the Redeemer's chapel, and refuses to apologize for doing both.

## Relationship to the Apparition

Edda distrusted Marta's first appearance but accepted Kind Lantern seed and bridge money after two failed winters. When villagers reported comfort and no obvious harm, she chose silence. Her compromise helped the shrine become normal.

## What She Wants

- Miri returned as herself, not as a symbol.
- The village's winter stores and bridge preserved.
- No inquisitorial spectacle or retaliatory raid by unconverted kin.
- The truth spoken in terms Chapel-Ford can survive.

## Adventure Arc

Edda begins by asking for discretion. Evidence and the party's conduct determine whether she supports public confession, quiet destruction, or defense of the shrine. In the Honest Chapel ending, she places the Kind Lantern's material assets under communal stewardship and refuses every attempt to name Miri a saint.
