# Crypt of Dame Sabine

The Crypt of Dame Sabine lies beneath Hellerbruck's small founder chapel. It contains [[Dame Sabine Heller|Sabine's]] remains, her consecrated boar-spear, sun-notched shield, sealed final journal, and memorial stones for the retainers who fought beside her.

## Why It Is Dangerous

Repeated public pageants, simplified founder recitations, and the handling of blood-marked arms have left strong residue in the crypt. The dead are not imprisoned whole souls. Memory, role, and expectation cling to the regalia and answer intruders as thieves or challengers.

The strongest identity is **Gernot Vale**, Sabine's companion in the first battle. Later Heller chroniclers omitted his name after a dispute with his descendants. The omission turned his residue from guard into accuser.

## Chambers

- **The Twelve Steps:** each bears one charter duty and reveals that Hellerbruck was founded as shared obligation rather than personal possession.
- **Companions' Wall:** damaged names and replaced plaques expose Gernot's omission.
- **Trophy Chapel:** founding scales and court metal warm as visitors approach the sealed arms.
- **Sabine's Bier:** journal, spear, shield, and rubbing of the original nesting boundary.
- **Quarry Stair:** a blocked service passage descends toward the old court and can later provide an approach to [[The Open-Noon Lair]].

## Resolution

The guardians can be settled by restoring names, reading Sabine's final doubt aloud, returning a removed memorial token, or demonstrating that the arms will defend the town rather than enrich the bearer. Force can disperse the residue but destroys testimony and may weaken the arms.

Sabine's journal proves both parts of the moral truth: she fought a dangerous predator in legitimate defense and honestly believed it dead, but later discovered evidence that should have prompted investigation. The boundary rubbing shows that Hellerbruck can protect the surviving upper court without abandoning the inhabited lower town.

## Founder Arms

The boar-spear remembers the path of the first chest wound. The shield's sun-notches preserve one rhythm of Sovarath's juvenile call. Together they pierce a layer of the elder's territorial shelter and contribute to the First Heat-Name.

They are loaned public objects, not ordinary loot. Their fate after the adventure depends on the town's outcome.
