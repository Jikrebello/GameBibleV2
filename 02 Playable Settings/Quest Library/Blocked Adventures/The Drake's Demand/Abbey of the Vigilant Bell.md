# Abbey of the Vigilant Bell

The Abbey of the Vigilant Bell is an abandoned old-Church watcher house on the ridge above [[Hellerbruck]]. Its original duty was to observe a natural Sun-Court threshold and ring warning when heat, glare, or court-fauna crossed into settled land.

It was never a temple to Father Sun. Its bell and mirrored shutters were instruments of watchfulness shaped by Eclessian prayer and practical knowledge.

## Abandonment

Quarry shifts weakened the abbey road and a lightning fire damaged the dormitory in 1308 AR. The last brothers transferred their warning records to Hellerbruck, but later custodians treated the Noon Window as a dead mechanism. [[Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon]] reopened it through Helka Brand and Ilyra-of-the-White-Eye.

The abbey now serves as tribute station, prison, and crossing point between the mortal ridge and Sovarath's improvised court.

## Approaches

- **Bell road:** direct enough for carts and visible to every watcher.
- **Quarry stair:** damaged but defensible, discovered through local rescue or old plans.
- **Cliff walk:** Lukas Auen's narrow patrol route.
- **Ash flue:** a Giltling route from the old kitchen, suitable for scouts and escape.

## Key Areas

### Outer Bell Yard

The Bright-Tail loyalists sort stolen metal, practice household marks, and prepare carrying frames. Their records identify the twelve intended attendants.

### Pilgrim Hall

Lukas maintains false maps and diverts attacks away from some homes while serving under threat. Evidence here can expose him, turn him, or cause a frightened flight toward Mara.

### Herb Cloister

Mara Auen is held among plants that open and close according to the Noon Window's cycle. She has learned its rhythm and sends signals through the old watering channel.

### Brass Refectory

Helka Brand prepares glare salts, scale solvents, and feeding compounds. Her notes show that she serves voluntarily and imagines eventually harvesting Sovarath's law-gem.

### Roof Procession

Gorun Many-Wing patrols open stone and hunts anything declared outside the court. Cover, bells, cloud, and broken procession markers can deprive it of the terrain it expects.

### Noon Chapel

The Noon Window consists of three bronze leaves, a roof aperture, and a polished court-stone. Ilyra coils around the mechanism and speaks titles from a vanished order. She can reveal the First Heat-Name if convinced that Sovarath's household is appetite impersonating court.

## Closing the Noon Window

The Window can be disabled by any coherent combination of:

- changing its noon alignment;
- breaking or removing the three bronze leaves;
- restoring the warning bell to its interrupting rhythm;
- reflecting the passage back through [[The Meridian Mirror]];
- using Othmar's grounding sequence;
- persuading Ilyra to pronounce the old dismissal.

Closing it prevents additional Sun-Court fauna from crossing, ends Sovarath's long-range projection through the abbey, and opens a safer servant route into [[The Open-Noon Lair]].
