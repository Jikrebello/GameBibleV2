# The Open-Noon Lair

The Open-Noon Lair is the surviving gold-drake nesting court beneath the high quarry and watcher ridge above [[Hellerbruck]]. It is the ancestral territory and present stronghold of [[Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon]].

The lair is neither a hoard cave nor a temple. It is a natural Faewild anchor altered by drake habitation: sun shafts, mineral mirrors, clutch hollows, warm fissures, molt ledges, and court-stone arranged by the pressure of Father Sun's law.

## Historical Layers

1. **Original court:** nesting hollows, court-stone, and the socket for [[The Meridian Mirror]].
2. **Sabine's battle:** the collapsed outer chamber, spear-cut bloodstone, and quarry charges from 1117 AR.
3. **Hellerbruck quarry:** drains, cut faces, worker marks, and a service stair that unknowingly approaches the court.
4. **Sovarath's return:** tribute shelving, captive galleries, Giltling passages, and a mortal audience shelf.

These layers prove both the old nesting claim and the fact that Sovarath has converted return into deliberate rule.

## Approaches

- **Tribute road:** broad, watched, and suitable for carts, allies, or deception.
- **Old quarry:** unstable but concealed, with access to the battle collapse and lower nest.
- **Abbey servant route:** reliable after the Noon Window closes; dangerous while it remains open.
- **Giltling fissure:** reaches the captives and cooling vents but restricts equipment and numbers.

No route is mandatory. Each preparation uncovers at least one approach, and failed entry leaves evidence that makes a later attempt possible.

## Key Zones

### Tribute Shelf

Sovarath arranges bells, shrine fittings, regalia, and prisoners according to invented court rank. Removing an object carelessly can sound an alarm or carry a command. The shelf also offers cover and improvised weapons to captives.

### Molting Arcade

Ancient scales replay heat, movement, and fragments of attention. [[Magister Othmar Veyl|Othmar's]] method can read them, while the First Heat-Name can force the arcade to answer the elder's juvenile pattern instead of its current command.

### Attendants' Gallery

Surrendered or kidnapped people are taught poses, routes, and service calls under threat. Rescue is possible before the final confrontation through the Giltling fissure, a staged tribute, or disabling the bell commands.

### Mirror Well

The Meridian Mirror's original socket aligns the entire ridge at noon. A mastered Mirror can redirect glare or reveal Sovarath's law-gem. An unmastered Mirror lets Sovarath project into Hellerbruck and move between prepared reflections.

### Upper Nest Memorial

Old clutch hollows and surviving court-stone establish that the site was genuinely a nesting ground. The area can be protected without surrendering Hellerbruck's inhabited lower ridge. Damage here destabilizes the law-field after Sovarath dies.

### Open-Noon Chamber

Sovarath aligns its heart-gem with the ridge here. Direct sunlight, heated metal, and exposed movement strengthen it. Shade, cooling water, grounded iron, broken bells, and the campaign's preparation assets change the field materially.

### Cooling Fissure

A deep crack vents toward the Weiss aquifer. It is an escape route and a possible way to quench the chamber. Collapsing it indiscriminately threatens captives, quarry workers, and the future stability of the protected nest.

## After Sovarath

Killing the elder does not erase the court. The law-gem continues to organize heat and glare until stabilized. Safe handling requires separating it from the corpse without breaking the nest-stone, seating it in a grounded socket, or entombing it under witnessed protection.

Extracting it for immediate sale, weapons, or private research risks fire, territorial collapse, and a struggle among authorities. The morally strongest settlement preserves the upper court, ends quarrying within the old boundary, and retains Hellerbruck below it.
