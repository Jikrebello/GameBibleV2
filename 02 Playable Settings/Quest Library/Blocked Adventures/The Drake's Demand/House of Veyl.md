# House of Veyl

The House of Veyl is the walled residence, laboratory, and court-fauna menagerie of [[Magister Othmar Veyl]] on the founder ridge above [[Hellerbruck]]. It is both a crime scene and a lawful estate whose absent heir has ordered its property inventoried and sold.

## Estate Mandate

The party is commissioned to rescue surviving servants, make the house safe, identify what Othmar was doing, and preserve recoverable property. Consumables required for survival may be used. Books, instruments, specimens, and valuables remain estate property until custody is settled.

This constraint tests judgement rather than rote procedure. Leaving a dangerous mirror active or a suffering creature confined merely because it is valuable is not responsible stewardship.

## Zones

### Receiving Hall

Heat-sensitive mirrors establish Othmar's warning language. Reflections turn toward nearby court influence; a bright rim reveals an active passage or spoken concealment. One panel killed a Giltling intruder and remains dangerously charged.

### Sun-Court Menagerie

Brass-backed burrowers, glare moths, a wounded heat-cat, and smaller court-fauna survive in cramped enclosures. Broken feeders and failed cooling make them immediate hazards. Their records expose Othmar's cruelty as well as the observations that let him identify Sovarath.

### Molt Archive

Founding trophy scales and recent molts sit in rotating frames. Matching fracture patterns establish that [[Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon]] survived. Later molts contain repeated ordered responses, evidence of the rare elder threshold.

### Servant Wing

Steward Ada Rinn and two apprentices have barricaded themselves against escaped fauna. Their testimony identifies missing keys, Othmar's final work, and the compensation he intended but never completed.

### Noon Chamber

Metal leaves focus ordinary sunlight into a controlled court pattern. Helka Brand left a delayed fire to destroy it. Preserving the chamber provides a First Heat-Name fragment and teaches the Mirror's safe alignment.

### Hidden Sun-Cave

A cistern counterweight opens a lower cave containing [[The Meridian Mirror]], copied pages from [[Dame Sabine Heller|Dame Sabine's]] journal, quarry maps, and Othmar's procedure for closing the Noon Window. Warm airflow or the [[The Bright-Tail Host|Giltling]] servant path provides an alternate entrance.

## What the House Establishes

- Othmar did not summon or awaken Sovarath.
- the founder and current drake are the same creature;
- old drake memory has become sapient selfhood;
- Giltlings murdered Othmar while searching for the Mirror;
- the Noon Window, founder arms, heat-name, and Mirror can materially reduce Sovarath's power.
