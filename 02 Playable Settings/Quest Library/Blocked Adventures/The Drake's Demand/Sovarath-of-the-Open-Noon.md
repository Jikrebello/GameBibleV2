# Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon

Sovarath is a rare sapient elder [[01 Canon Reference/Creatures/20 Faewild and Menhir/10 Drakes/Gold Drakes.md|gold drake]] and the principal antagonist of [[The Drake's Demand — Full Adventure]]. It is the same animal [[Dame Sabine Heller]] grievously wounded beneath the future site of [[Hellerbruck]] in 1117 AR.

## From Animal to Elder

Sabine fought Sovarath before it possessed language or reflective moral agency. Its law-gem nevertheless retained place, injury, glare, metal, and repeated seasonal return. During centuries of recovery in remote sun-cliffs, those impressions accumulated into ordered memory. Memory became a stable self capable of speech, planning, restraint, and moral choice.

The elder threshold did not turn Sovarath into a true dragon, patron, humanoid, or Menhir. It remains an embodied court-animal whose intelligence emerged through extraordinary age and heart-gem accumulation.

## Grievance and Choice

Hellerbruck occupies Sovarath's former nesting court, and Sabine removed the [[The Meridian Mirror|Meridian Mirror]]. Sovarath therefore possesses a true account of loss. It could demand protected nesting ground, return of surviving court objects, or an end to quarrying above the old boundary.

Instead it demands Hellerbruck's bells, shrine fittings, founder regalia, and twelve living attendants. The point is not repair. The point is making the town enact its own subordination. Sovarath understands the distinction and considers mercy a failure to prove rank.

It murdered [[Magister Othmar Veyl]] through its servants because he could expose its vulnerability. It burns farms selectively, leaving witnesses to carry terms. It calls these acts court discipline rather than predation.

## The Invented Court

Sovarath remembers fragments of Father Sun's predatory court-law without holding an office within it. It has assembled an imitation household:

- [[The Bright-Tail Host]] as messengers, thieves, and petty stewards;
- Gorun Many-Wing as hunt and punishment;
- Helka Brand as mortal artificer;
- Lukas Auen as coerced territorial guide;
- Ilyra-of-the-White-Eye as witness to titles and forgotten ceremony;
- captive attendants as the beginning of a permanent mortal household.

Ilyra's accusation that the household is counterfeit wounds Sovarath because it is true. The drake has transformed nesting instinct into a theory of sovereignty.

## Conduct

Sovarath is formal when dominant and brutally direct when contradicted. It does not lie about accepting tribute or granting a stated delay. It phrases deliberately unjust terms with precision and treats accurate enforcement as proof of righteousness.

It can recognize courage, beauty, and faithful service without loving the person who embodies them. Praise is therefore dangerous: Sovarath's admiration often becomes a reason to possess someone.

## Powers and Vulnerabilities

- glare and heat organized by territorial law;
- command transmitted through bells and prepared reflective surfaces;
- adaptive memory that changes its response to repeated tactics;
- perception and passage through the Meridian Mirror and Noon Window;
- a law-gem shelter aligned with the old nesting court;
- vulnerability to the First Heat-Name, Dame Sabine's arms, a grounded Mirror, a closed Window, and the loss of its invented household.

The First Heat-Name does not control Sovarath. It forces the elder to confront the difference between inherited reflex and current choice, briefly breaking its composure.

## Defeat

Sovarath may be killed, driven away, contained, or obeyed. It cannot be restored to innocent animality. Its sapience and crimes are now part of the self it has made.

If killed, its law-gem must be stabilized within the nesting court. Immediate harvesting risks fire, territorial collapse, and another struggle over power. Preserving the court does not require preserving Sovarath's rule.
