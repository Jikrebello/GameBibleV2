# The Bright-Tail Host

The Bright-Tail Host is a community of thirty-six adult [[Giltlings]] and fourteen young gathered by [[Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon]] from sun-cliff warrens and ruined gold-court sites. It serves as the elder's scouts, tunnelers, thieves, messengers, and intended household staff.

The Host is a divided community, not a single obedient mind.

## Kezra's Loyalists

**Kezra Seven-Rings** leads nineteen adults who believe Sovarath's return will raise Giltlings from hunted scavengers into recognized court servants. Kezra wears seven polished tail rings, each supposedly marking a rank granted by the elder.

Kezra planned the search of [[House of Veyl]], ordered the interception of Othmar's courier, and continued the assault after learning that the tower might collapse. Kezra did not strike the killing blow against [[Magister Othmar Veyl]], but accepts responsibility as proof of loyalty.

The loyalists steal records, mark charter homes, kidnap specialists, and prepare bells for removal. They understand that the twelve attendants are unwilling and support the demand anyway.

## Nirr's Breakaway

**Nirr Ash-Eye** leads eleven adults who served from fear and have watched Sovarath discard injured Giltlings. Nirr lost an eye to the reflected defense at Othmar's house and realized the elder had withheld knowledge of the danger.

Nirr helped kill Othmar's porter and carried stolen pages. The breakaway therefore needs mercy and a path to restitution, not instant absolution. It can:

- return records and identify the murder route;
- reveal the servant fissure into [[The Open-Noon Lair]];
- identify Kezra's signals and charter-house marks;
- help evacuate young Giltlings and mortal captives;
- testify publicly and accept bounded judgement after the crisis.

If rejected, the breakaway flees or hides. It does not automatically return to Sovarath.

## The Uncommitted

Six adults care primarily for the young and follow whichever faction can provide a credible escape. Protecting them from collective punishment makes Nirr's defection far more likely. Threatening the young confirms Kezra's claim that only the drake can keep the Host alive.

## Resources

- old quarry fissures and warm-air routes beneath Hellerbruck;
- hand mirrors, glare powder, smoke pellets, small chisels, and line traps;
- knowledge of the tower undercroft, abbey flues, and lair maintenance passages;
- stolen watch records and charter-house marks;
- access to lesser gold-court fauna supplied by Sovarath.

## Conduct and Consequence

Fear explains why many joined the court. It does not excuse murder, abduction, or knowingly selecting human tribute. Individuals who defect before further harm should be distinguished from Kezra's committed loyalists. Giltling personhood requires particular judgement in both directions: they are neither blameless animals nor a species of villains.

The Host may split, withdraw, submit to Sovarath, be destroyed, or become the beginning of a lawful Giltling quarter outside the protected nest. No outcome is canonical.
