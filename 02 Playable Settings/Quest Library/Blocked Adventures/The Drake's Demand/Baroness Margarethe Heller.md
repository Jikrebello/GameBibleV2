# Baroness Margarethe Heller

Margarethe Heller is the hereditary baroness of [[Hellerbruck]], a ducal vassal, and the principal civic patron of [[The Drake's Demand — Full Adventure]]. She is fifty-two, widowed, old Church in worship, and committed to the confessional articles that protect the town's Free Hearth.

## Office

Margarethe commands the town watch, chairs the charter council, holds the founder fort in the Duke's name, and settles disputes that cross guild or parish jurisdiction. She cannot lawfully surrender guild bells, shrine property, or charter dependants by personal command.

Her authority is strongest when she convenes the town's institutions and weakest when she tries to substitute family honor for public truth.

## Character

Margarethe is practical, controlled, and accustomed to choosing among incomplete remedies. She knows workers by household and remembers which roofs burned in the last foundry fire. She is less comfortable with symbols: the Heller legend has carried public trust for generations, and she initially fears that admitting Sabine's mistake will dissolve the town's loyalty during crisis.

She does not contemplate surrendering twelve people as an acceptable price. Her temptation is instead to surrender every object immediately, conceal the journal, and hope Sovarath stops before demanding lives.

## What She Knows

- Othmar Veyl sent an incomplete warning about the founder ridge.
- Dame Sabine's arms and final journal remain sealed in [[Crypt of Dame Sabine|the founder crypt]].
- the high quarry has produced unexplained heat and hollow sounds for three summers;
- Lukas Auen altered a bridge-watch entry but has served faithfully for years;
- ducal help cannot reach Hellerbruck before Sovarath's deadline.

## Development

Margarethe becomes a stronger leader if she publishes Sabine's doubt, lets the charter council share the decision, and distinguishes restitution from submission. If she protects the legend at all costs, Town Resolve becomes brittle and Sovarath can use the secret against her publicly.

She may die, resign, flee, submit, or lead the town through the attack. No outcome is fixed beyond the campaign's starting situation.
