# The Meridian Mirror

The Meridian Mirror is a fixed gold-court instrument removed from the nesting ground beneath [[Hellerbruck]] by [[Dame Sabine Heller]]. It consists of a polished golden alloy face, a dark mineral backing, and three pivot rings that align glare, passage, and heart-law.

It is not sentient, a patron, a soul vessel, or an object of contract magic. It organizes natural light and local Faewild court-law with dangerous precision.

## Original Function

Seated within the Mirror Well of [[The Open-Noon Lair]], the instrument served three functions:

1. **Clutch alignment:** distribute heat through nesting hollows without burning eggs.
2. **Territorial survey:** reveal movement and reflective surfaces across the sunlit ridge.
3. **Court passage:** align with the natural threshold later enclosed by the [[Abbey of the Vigilant Bell|abbey's]] Noon Window.

Removing it weakened the old court and allowed Hellerbruck's quarrying to proceed. Returning it ungrounded would give [[Sovarath-of-the-Open-Noon]] its strongest territorial reach.

## Three Positions

- **Veiled:** face-down against dark backing; safe for transport and storage.
- **Meridian:** catches direct noon light, reveals court influence, and exposes a drake law-gem's rhythm.
- **Returning:** directs captured glare toward a selected reflection or threshold. This can close a passage, redirect an attack, or open a route for Sovarath if misaligned.

[[Magister Othmar Veyl]] discovered the positions through dangerous testing. His Noon Chamber teaches their signs: a cooling rim for Veiled, a single white point for Meridian, and three moving gold lines for Returning.

## Adventure Uses

- expose the warm pattern in Othmar's founding scales;
- carry or complete part of the First Heat-Name;
- close the Noon Window by reflecting its passage into itself;
- redirect one major glare effect in Sovarath's lair;
- reveal where the law-gem aligns with the old nest;
- provide Sovarath with projection and passage if surrendered unmastered.

## Failure and Destruction

An ungrounded Mirror turns toward the strongest active gold-court law nearby. It can burn handlers, expose hiding places, or let Sovarath see through it. These effects are physical and Faewild, not possession.

Breaking the face ends its projection and reduces Sun-Court Reach, but molten fragments remain dangerously aligned until cooled and separated. Destruction removes both the threat and the party's strongest reflective tool. No campaign clue exists only inside the Mirror.

## Custody

The Mirror is both a removed court instrument and a Hellerbruck civic trophy. Neither history grants unrestricted ownership. A just settlement returns it to a grounded protected nest, destroys it safely, or places it under witnessed custody that prevents private exploitation.
