# Hellerbruck

Hellerbruck is a prosperous market and craft town on the central River Weiss in [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/60 Realms and Peoples/Duchy of Brauvalis/The Duchy of Brauvalis Overview.md|Brauvalis]]. A stone bridge, brewery quay, limestone quarry, pottery yards, and bell foundry support about 2,400 permanent residents. Several hundred bargemen, quarry hands, apprentices, and buyers join them during the autumn auction season.

The town grew around the ruined ridge-fort of [[Dame Sabine Heller]], remembered publicly as the drake-slayer who opened the river crossing in 1117 AR. Its charter distributes obligations among twelve founding households and institutions rather than reserving them to nobles alone. Bridge wardens, shrine custodians, brewers, potters, bargemen, quarry households, healers, bell founders, landholders, and the Heller family all hold inherited duties.

## Land and Roads

The Weiss runs eastward beneath a two-arched stone bridge. Brewery and pottery yards occupy the lower north bank, while the market, parish, Free Hearth, and foundry rise toward the southern ridge. The high quarry cuts into pale limestone west of the old castle ruins.

River barges connect Hellerbruck to other Brauvalian markets and eventually the Thalassian trade. The north road carries hops and cloth. The ridge road reaches an abandoned watcher abbey and smaller farming communities. Hellerbruck is central Brauvalian country, separate from the poorer eastern [[Wanemoor District]].

## Authority and Faith

[[Baroness Margarethe Heller]] governs with a town council of guild, parish, charter-house, and landholding representatives. Ducal tax and military obligations remain real, but the baroness cannot simply dispose of guild property, bells, shrines, or charter dependants.

Saint Ardel's old-Church parish and a protected Freeflame congregation called the **Free Hearth** disagree over images, preaching, and jurisdiction. Both confessions participate in bridge relief, fire watch, and the common poor fund. Neither accepts the surrender of living people as tribute.

## Economy

- **Brewing and hops:** river water, preserved grain, common ale, medicinal beers, and regional auction lots.
- **Ceramics:** pale clay and clean kiln sand produce household ware, roof tile, and glazed devotional pieces.
- **Quarrying:** limestone supports roads, bridges, kiln work, and church construction.
- **Bell-founding:** the Twelve-Bell Foundry supplies parishes, river posts, guild halls, and warning towers.
- **River trade:** barges carry finished goods outward and return with grain, metals, salt, and cloth.

## The Founding Ridge

The upper ridge contains the ruined Heller fort, [[Crypt of Dame Sabine]], [[House of Veyl]], high quarry, and the road to the [[Abbey of the Vigilant Bell]]. Old songs say Sabine killed a gold drake beneath the ridge and took a sun-bright mirror from its hoard.

Creator-facing truth and the unresolved present crisis are detailed in [[The Drake's Demand — Full Adventure]]. Public maps should mark the upper quarry as protected old ground without assuming the adventure's outcome.

## Story Uses

Hellerbruck supports craft rivalries, mixed-confession town politics, quarry accidents, river smuggling, guild succession, estate auctions, relic disputes, and stories about what a community owes to a past it inherited but did not choose.
