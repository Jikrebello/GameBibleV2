# Mara Tide-Knot

Mara Tide-Knot is the route captain and principal proprietor of [[The Turning Lantern Company]]. She was born among Saltborn keel-wrights and spent her early adulthood managing river barges, where weather, payroll, cargo, and frightened passengers taught her that leadership is mostly deciding which problem must be allowed to remain a problem.

## Public Manner

Mara is welcoming in a deliberate, professional way. She remembers children's names, stall boundaries, guard shifts, and which local official needs to be treated as important. Her hospitality is genuine and part of the business.

She performs only once during Lantern Week, descending the central mast through a wheel of lit river lamps. The act reminds the company that she still accepts some of the physical risk she assigns to others.

## Priorities

1. Keep permanent company members alive and together.
2. Preserve the portable credit chest and merchant balances.
3. Protect the company's ability to receive future charters.
4. Maintain enough public wonder that the road remains worth choosing.
5. Settle internal wrongdoing without surrendering members to collective punishment.

## What She Conceals

Mara initially withholds:

- the chest's value and location;
- the fact that some headline attractions are stagecraft;
- a list of local merchants moving balances out of Chainhouse custody;
- previous disputes caused by Osric's temper;
- small civil claims the company intends to settle in its next major town.

None proves murder. Concealing them makes the investigation harder and gives the party legitimate reasons to challenge her.

## Trust Track

- **0:** Mara provides public schedules only and prepares to leave Halmenth.
- **1:** she opens company inventories and permits supervised wagon searches.
- **2:** she reveals merchant settlement movements and internal disciplinary history.
- **3:** she reveals the credit chest, accepts the party's security plan, and lets company guards act under shared direction.

Trust rises when the party protects people while testing claims. It falls when the party demands belief without evidence, humiliates harmless performers, or treats every concealed fact as equivalent to murder.

## Continuation

Mara can become a recurring patron and transport connection. She remembers debts precisely but prefers obligations repaid through useful acts rather than coin. If Halmenth burns the fair, her later route decisions become more cautious and the company stops entering towns without a prepared escape road.
