# The Turning Lantern Company

The **Turning Lantern Company** is a traveling fair, market, and settlement partnership moving between Eclessian river towns, Saltborn ports, Sestaran roads, and Folk-adjacent fairs. It carries entertainment because crowds create trade, and trade because performances alone do not feed a road community through winter.

## Composition

The company includes roughly enough permanent members to function as a small moving village:

- performers, musicians, rope-workers, animal handlers, and lanternwrights;
- cooks, guards, wagon carpenters, healers, and drovers;
- Sestaran factors and temporary exchange clerks;
- Saltborn riggers and river pilots between voyages;
- Folk craftspeople and performers who prefer seasonal road life to a settled frontier;
- local merchants who attach themselves for one or two stages.

Not every attraction is genuine. Painted sea-beasts, articulated saints, impossible foreign animals, and “prophetic” lantern shows mix stagecraft with occasional real relics whose owners understand them poorly. The company sells wonder, not scholarly certainty.

## Governance

[[Mara Tide-Knot]] owns the principal wagons but cannot rule alone. Permanent shares belong to eight senior road households. Major route, debt, and expulsion decisions require five household marks. Daily authority remains practical: the person responsible for a wagon, animal, act, or account controls it.

The company protects members strongly because local authorities rarely distinguish offender from entourage. This solidarity can become secrecy. Mara will surrender a proven murderer; she will not hand someone over because a town wants a familiar shape for its fear.

## Trade Function

The company's portable credit chest lets road merchants settle authenticated Sestaran paper, pool specie, and carry balances between fairs. The chest makes the company more than entertainment and explains why warehouse monopolies treat it as a threat.

The company charges for settlement and stall protection. Its prices are lower than permanent bonded storage because it leaves after seven days and carries less overhead. This is useful competition, not charity.

## Important Members

- **[[Mara Tide-Knot]]:** proprietor and route captain.
- **Tessa Marin:** Sestaran treasurer, seal reader, and keeper of the credit chest.
- **Osric Sealsson:** Saltborn rigging-knife performer and senior wagon rigger.
- **Elof Reed-Step:** Folk rope-dancer, lamp-climber, and apprentice route scout.
- **Mother Hanne:** cookhouse leader and informal judge of company disputes.
- **Petr Voss:** head guard, formerly of an Eclessian road patrol.

## Faults and Pressures

- Some games are designed to keep players trying after the expected value is gone.
- The company moves before many small civil claims can catch it.
- Senior households protect profitable performers from consequences too long.
- Outsiders in the attached market receive less protection than permanent members.
- The credit chest concentrates enough value to attract criminals and enough secrecy to make honest officials suspicious.

These faults create investigation material without making the company responsible for crimes committed against it.

## Continuation Uses

The Turning Lantern Company can recur as:

- a movable quest hub with a known cast;
- transport between regions;
- the first place unusual news, relics, performers, and fugitives converge;
- a witness to how different cultures treat the same outsiders;
- a company whose survival changes according to the outcome at Halmenth.
