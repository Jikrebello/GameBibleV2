# The Chainhouse

The **Chainhouse** is Halmenth's dominant storage, cartage, bonded lending, and dock-labor partnership. Its public name comes from the iron chain stamped across warehouse seals. Under [[Hester Vallin]] and Gero Vallin, the partnership has absorbed weaker stores until most goods entering by the Celion pass through one of its hands.

## Legitimate Business

The Chainhouse performs necessary work:

- dry and cold storage;
- bonded custody for taxed goods;
- freight carts and dock cranes;
- short advances against cargo;
- security, weighing, and damage claims;
- casual labor hiring during sailing season.

Thousands of ordinary transactions are honest. Destroying every warehouse or ledger would harm merchants whose only mistake was needing storage.

## Criminal Operation

The Vallins use legitimate control to conceal:

- false weights and storage losses;
- coerced purchase of defaulted goods;
- untallied night cargo;
- copied warehouse keys and delivery tokens;
- assaults committed by dependent porters;
- forged notices that redirect goods into Chainhouse custody;
- a hidden undercroft beneath Warehouse Nine.

The organization is not a separate thieves' guild wearing the company as a mask. The abuse grows directly from commercial control. The same supervisors who schedule lawful carts can schedule murder transport after dark.

## Internal Layers

- **Hester's table:** forged seals, political payments, acquisition plans, and public relationships.
- **Gero's floor:** guards, foremen, carts, coercion, and disposal of evidence.
- **Compromised supervisors:** know some cargo is false but not that the Vallins committed murder.
- **Dependent workers:** obey because the Chainhouse controls wages, bunk space, food advances, or family debts.
- **Committed hands:** a small group willing to assault witnesses and burn records.

Separating these layers lets the party break the criminal operation without treating every dockworker as an enemy.

## Murder at Lantern Fair

The Chainhouse wants the Turning Lantern charter revoked because direct settlement weakens storage and lending income. The Vallins expect public fear to accomplish three objectives at once:

1. remove Gerhard Lenz's opposition to an exclusive concession;
2. expel [[The Turning Lantern Company]];
3. seize its portable credit chest and merchant balances.

The operation fails if the party links Cart Twelve, copied keys, Chainhouse lamp materials, the Rook House disguises, and Warehouse Nine records into one demonstrable sequence.

## Aftermath

Removing the Vallins leaves Halmenth with a real economic problem. The town still needs warehouses, cranes, carts, and bonded custody. Possible successors include a worker partnership, several smaller licensed stores, a council warehouse, or a Sestaran concession whose cleaner books bring different forms of dependence.
