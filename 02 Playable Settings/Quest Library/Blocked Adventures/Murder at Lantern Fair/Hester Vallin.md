# Hester Vallin

Hester Vallin is co-owner of [[The Chainhouse]] and architect of the Lantern Fair murders. She is a respected warehouse widow who remarried her late husband's foreman, Gero Vallin, then expanded one bonded store into Halmenth's dominant logistics partnership.

## Governing Belief

Hester believes trade belongs to the people who remain after festivals, ships, and officials move on. From that defensible premise she concludes that temporary competitors are parasites and that permanent infrastructure entitles its owners to control the town using it.

She does not murder because a supernatural force corrupted a good intention. She understands that Lenz, Alena, and Niko are people. She judges their deaths cheaper than losing Halmenth's trade.

## Public Method

- Offers complete hospitality in rooms where nothing incriminating is kept.
- Answers questions narrowly and accurately whenever possible.
- Lets Gero appear angry so her restraint reads as innocence.
- Speaks sympathetically about performers while treating the fair as an unsafe institution.
- Produces clean public ledgers because the hidden operation uses copied tokens and parallel rolls.

## Operational Method

- Builds frames from real facts: Osric's temper, fair secrecy, Folk travel marks, Sestaran paper, and public fear.
- Plants one obvious clue and one apparently incidental clue meant for experienced investigators.
- Keeps murderers and planners separated from ordinary workers.
- Preserves witnesses when false testimony is more useful than silence.
- Changes the target of the frame from the fair to the party once they become dangerous.

## Relationship with Gero

Gero supplies physical control, but he is neither a disposable thug nor the sole source of violence. He and Hester built the Chainhouse together. Hester plans; Gero knows what plans cost on the floor and accepts the cost.

If Gero dies or is captured, Hester does not collapse. She narrows the operation, destroys the seal room, and attempts to leave with portable credit letters. If Hester falls first, Gero turns the riot into open retaliation and tries to burn Lantern Meadow.

## Evidence Against Her

- Her contradictory knowledge of Cart Twelve's movement.
- Forged delivery rolls cut from paper held in her counting room.
- The copied seal cabinet beneath Warehouse Nine.
- Alena's note recording Hester's approach to Lenz.
- Rook House disguises prepared from her observers' descriptions.
- Written instructions for the chest raid and destruction of the records wagon.

No single item should carry the conviction. Together they establish motive, preparation, sequence, and command.

## Continuation

If Hester escapes, she abandons the Vallin name and builds another respectable settlement business. She should recur through familiar methods rather than impossible reach: clean public books, dependent workers, useful services, and a crisis whose obvious culprit is someone newly arrived.
