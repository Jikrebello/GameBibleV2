# The Ninth Marker

The Ninth Marker is a buried Atherian survey shaft beside an obsolete road bearing west of [[Cavora]]. Its surviving road warden rotates stones, repeats water exemptions, and classifies modern routes according to dead imperial definitions.

## Present Occupants

Independent salvage scholar **Doctor Esma Loran** occupies the upper shaft with four workers. She found the site before Orsino's search became public and regards its survey table as her lawful discovery. She is guarded and commercially motivated, but she has not joined [[Magister Orsino Vellaro]].

Esma will trade access for help stabilizing the shaft, a copy of the modern road line, or recognition of her work. Theft or violence can also take the key but may activate the warden's full obstruction routine.

## The Road Warden

The engine treats Cavora as an unauthorized obstruction and the Grey-Rein tracks as unregistered pasture. Its classifications are neither moral judgement nor a surviving Atherian official. It is a constructed adaptive pattern applying an obsolete civic writ.

Visitors can complete its survey, invalidate the local writ, isolate the route stones, follow a maintenance classification, or destroy its frames. Destruction risks losing the calibration chamber.

## Calibration Key

The inner table identifies [[Echo Seam Mine]] as a lesser extraction workshop closed for recursive contamination. Its removable calibration key can:

- detune one crystal buttress;
- close a connected industrial ward network;
- establish the original bearing between the mine and [[Sunscar Quarry]];
- confirm that the Echo draw was never a permanent anchor.
