# Sunscar Quarry

Sunscar Quarry is a failed modern stone claim southwest of [[Cavora]]. Its workers unknowingly cut into an Atherian exhaust line connected to [[Echo Seam Mine]]. Repeated accidents, bad stone, and the arrival of a wyvern ended regular work.

## Upper Quarry

A wyvern dens above the quarry lip. Bones dropped from height, long scrape marks, and missing pack animals advertise the danger. The animal hunts the open approaches but does not serve Orsino, guard Atherian secrets, or possess human motives.

The foreman's shelter contains ventilation plans, accident reports, and the final crew roster. It can be reached by climbing the sheltered wall, baiting the wyvern away, moving under quarry machinery, or driving off the predator.

## Lower Cuts

Standing water, mineral runoff, unstable ledges, clear slimes, iron-eaters, and ruin vermin occupy the lower quarry. Warm air pulses from a fitted stone vent after sunset. The pattern follows old workshop pressure rather than weather.

The exhaust line provides a concealed descent into the mine's processing level. It is narrow and vertical. Without the quarry plan, opening the wrong baffle causes collapse or vents contaminated air through the lower cuts.

## Adventure Value

Securing the route lets the party bypass the mine's first mortal barricade, evacuate captives without using the haulage adit, and vent one pressure event during the Rehearsal Forge finale.

Killing the wyvern is not required. A living predator left beside an unsecured evacuation route remains a practical danger that must be planned around.
