# Temur Grey-Rein

Temur Grey-Rein is an Ash Road peace-witness assigned to Cavora's section of the [[Castaran Long Road]]. He travels with [[Dario Marevi]] when the Broken Stakes ambush them and remains captive at Split-Reed Camp when Dario is moved onward.

## Office

Temur records declared caravans, witnesses the Grey-Rein grant, inspects alleged banner marks, and carries restitution claims to the relevant household. He can bind the Grey-Rein Banner's testimony and response. He cannot command every Ash rider or speak for the whole [[Ash Horde]].

He knows that a Castaran charter can be lawful without becoming territorial conquest. Cavora may maintain its named wells and approaches; it may not push fields, forts, or quarry claims beyond the boundary stones by calling the road a border.

## The False Marks

Temur recognizes the Broken Stakes' deception immediately:

- ash knots are tied backward;
- badges combine marks never worn together;
- arrow and tack styles come from unrelated road markets;
- the warning signs point toward settled camps rather than away from them.

He wants physical evidence shown at the Grey-Rein Fire. Quietly killing the raiders without exposing their method leaves the road vulnerable to retaliation and repeated imitation.

## In Play

Temur is a guide, witness, mounted scout, and political bridge rather than a universal cultural spokesman. He helps the party distinguish local rights from opportunistic claims on both sides. He opposes collective punishment and also refuses to excuse road violence merely because some offenders have experienced displacement or debt.

If Cavora survives, Temur supports a limited mine arrangement only when the mine's access, water use, spoil, and roads remain within an explicit local grant.
