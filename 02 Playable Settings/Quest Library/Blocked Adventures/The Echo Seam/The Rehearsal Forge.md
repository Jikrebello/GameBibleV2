# The Rehearsal Forge

The Rehearsal Forge is an Atherian industrial workshop inside [[Echo Seam Mine]]. It can temper one complete, bounded elemental script into compatible metal or ceramic. It does not create unrestricted magical objects and does not make elemental beings.

## Operating Principle

A valid production run requires:

1. a complete [[Warlock Rune Magic|warlock script]] defining source, shape, duration, conductors, release condition, and safety limits;
2. compatible metal, ceramic, or crystal prepared to receive the relation;
3. a rehearsal plate engraved and spoken in correct sequence;
4. a brief lesser draw of elemental force from the Shell;
5. all three crystal buttresses tuned to one baseline;
6. controlled cooling, grounding, and removal.

The resulting component holds one bounded activation or a small fixed number of repetitions. Examples include a plate that raises one heat barrier, a ceramic bolt that releases freezing mist, or a brace that hardens once under structural failure.

## Why It Echoes

The surrounding crystal records vibration exceptionally well. Repeated production left timing, source, and command fragments in the lattice. Later scripts began incorporating earlier relations:

- a cooling command repeats during a fire run;
- a door responds to a dead shift declaration;
- a prepared plate releases along an earlier bearing;
- familiar voices emerge from crystal after the speakers have left;
- Crystal Forms grow around contradictory elemental instructions.

These effects are stored vibration and Shell computation. They are not trapped workers, whole memories, ghosts, or conscious elementals.

## The Lesser Draw

The Forge's draw admits bounded force but no traveler or independent inhabitant. It is not one of the fourteen active permanent anchors and cannot become a fifteenth through ordinary repair. Opening it without all three buttresses aligned produces pressure, heat, and mixed-script failure rather than a portal.

## Stabilization

Immediate stabilization requires:

- closing the temporary draw;
- removing six contaminated rehearsal plates without fully sounding them;
- retuning all three crystal buttresses together;
- collecting propagating crystal seeds;
- venting stored pressure before dismantling conductor channels.

The Closing Cadence from [[Hushfield]], Calibration Key from [[The Ninth Marker]], ventilation route from [[Sunscar Quarry]], and Toma Marevi's field book each solve a different part of the problem. No single magical tradition bypasses the whole process.

## Limited Reopening

A responsible reopening is deliberately small. Every script is tested away from workers, each material batch is traceable, and anyone operating the floor can stop a run when recurrence appears. Cavora workers, outside technicians, and a Grey-Rein land witness each hold a practical veto over unsafe use or expansion beyond the roadholding.

This arrangement reduces danger; it does not make the Forge harmless. Production remains slow, material-intensive, and vulnerable to contamination.
