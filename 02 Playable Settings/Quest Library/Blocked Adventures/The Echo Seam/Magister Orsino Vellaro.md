# Magister Orsino Vellaro

Orsino Vellaro is an expelled Castaran metallurgist and the principal antagonist of [[The Echo Seam — Full Adventure]]. He is brilliant at conductor alloys, recursive vibration, and the physical preparation of warlock scripts. He also knowingly treats other people as expendable proof material.

## The First Deaths

At the University of Castara, Orsino tested whether an alloy could retain a second elemental relation after discharge. Instruments showed that an earlier heat sequence remained inside the chamber. He accelerated the next run anyway. Three assistants died when the old command repeated after the room had been sealed.

Orsino altered the log, blamed a junior engraver, and attempted another run. Surviving plates and testimony exposed him. He was expelled and barred from University workshops. Senior patrons later suppressed parts of the scandal while retaining useful portions of his research, giving Orsino a real grievance but no excuse for his crimes.

## The Echo Seam Plot

Orsino recognizes one of Nella Marevi's copied cadences and follows the surveyors to [[Cavora]]. He pays [[The Broken Stakes]] to seize Dario and the route plate, then hires [[The Copper Mantles]] to occupy the town and divert miners, ceramic blanks, and prisoners toward [[Echo Seam Mine]].

He understands the recursive contamination because it resembles the failure that killed his assistants. He conceals the similarity and calls every warning institutional cowardice.

## In Confrontation

Orsino uses space and machinery. He raises finite barriers, redirects conductors, opens or closes shutters, and makes opponents choose between pursuing him and protecting captives. He does not command Crystal Forms as servants. He predicts some responses and deliberately provokes them.

He may surrender only after the Forge is disabled, his armed support is broken, and evidence can establish his crimes. Surrender means releasing captives, identifying every copy, relinquishing his notes and plates, and submitting to judgement. He cannot negotiate himself back into technical command.

If those conditions do not hold, Orsino fights, bargains for time, or escapes with one contaminated plate. A later confession can be genuine, but it does not erase the deaths, coercion, or restitution owed.
