# Red Mile Watch

Red Mile Watch is a ruined Atherian road fort on a stony rise west of [[Cavora]]. Later travelers repaired its walls, cistern, and horse yard without restoring the imperial road network that once supplied it. [[The Broken Stakes]] now use it as a fortified claim room and prisoner site.

## Layout

- **Outer gate:** repaired timber gate behind a shallow ditch and removable road barrier.
- **Horse yard:** remounts kept ready for evacuation rather than a prolonged siege.
- **Collapsed signal side:** broken upper wall accessible by a difficult exposed climb.
- **Dry cistern:** concealed lower entry connected to the old kitchen and route room.
- **Route room:** surviving Atherian wall table, copied claims, stolen badges, and Ressa Pike's messages.
- **Prison chamber:** [[Dario Marevi]] or other captives awaiting sale or transfer.

## Signs

False Ash knots face approaching caravans, several cultures' arrow styles share one parapet, and the fort's survey fires burn behind closed shutters. The deliberate mismatch rewards observation before combat.

## Adventure Function

Rescuing Dario and recovering the Marevi route plate gives the direct line to [[Echo Seam Mine]]. The messages and stolen markings prove that the road ambushes were staged. Ressa's copied fragment can survive even if the main plate is destroyed.

The Watch can be entered by negotiation, false purchase, public exposure of the marks, dry cistern, signal-side climb, or direct assault. Failed entry moves prisoners, spends horses, and increases readiness rather than removing the evidence.
