# Echo Seam Mine

Echo Seam Mine is a late Atherian industrial site beneath a low crystal ridge west of [[Cavora]]. Its engineers worked a naturally resonant deposit and built [[The Rehearsal Forge]] around a lesser Shell draw. The site is a mine and workshop, not a permanent anchor.

## Original Function

The outer workings produced pale conductor crystal, kiln mineral, and ordinary structural stone. Deeper processing halls cut, washed, and sorted compatible material. The inner workshop rehearsed finite elemental scripts into prepared metal or ceramic components.

The mine closed after recursive contamination made later runs repeat fragments of earlier scripts. Atherian overseers removed several master plates, shut the draw, and sealed the inner works. Collapse later buried the haulage road and removed the site from maintained maps.

## Modern Discovery

Dario, Toma, and Nella Marevi reconstructed the route from spoil, survey stones, and an old drainage cut. They entered far enough to recover one inactive rehearsal plate. Their copies attracted [[Magister Orsino Vellaro]], whose hired groups seized the route and captives needed to reopen the workshop.

Toma Marevi dies in the outer mine after refusing to help Orsino's crew cross an active crystal gallery. His remains and field book lie beyond a partial collapse. Nella is held in the inner transcription booth.

## Levels

### Abandoned Workings

Collapsed braces, flooded cuts, ore piles, natural slimes, ruin vermin, and the remains of modern camps dominate the outer level. Nothing supernatural is required to make it dangerous.

### Processing Halls

Crushers, kilns, conductor baths, route stones, and [[Atherian Ward and Trial Engines|Atherian industrial ward engines]] remain. Their apparent judgement is a procedural simulation based on dead worker classifications.

### Crystal Galleries

Facets grow toward sound and preserve vibration. At high activity the galleries produce [[Seam and Mixed-Script Manifestations|Crystal Forms]], delayed reflections, moving barriers, and fragments of old commands. These patterns have no subjective continuity or moral agency.

### Rehearsal Floor

Three crystal buttresses surround a tempering bed, removable plates, conductor channels, and the closed Shell draw. The room can be stabilized, sealed, or destroyed. No outcome is fixed in canon.

## Entrances

- **Haulage adit:** broad, direct, partly collapsed, and easy to guard.
- **Sunscar ventilation works:** narrow and vertical, entering near processing.
- **Atherian maintenance channel:** concealed behind a route classification controlled from [[The Ninth Marker]].

## Canon Limits

The mine is not one of the fourteen active permanent anchors described in [[Shell Anchors and Extraction]]. It cannot admit travelers, contain a native elemental population, or open a route through the Shell. Its stored voices are vibration records rather than people or souls.

Public maps should show restricted old workings west of Cavora without naming the Forge or asserting the mine's outcome.
