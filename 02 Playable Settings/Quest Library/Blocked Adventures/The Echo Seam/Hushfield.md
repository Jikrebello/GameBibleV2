# Hushfield

Hushfield is an abandoned early road hamlet northwest of [[Cavora]]. A failed well, one fever season, and the movement of the declared caravan line emptied it. The toll house, memorial ground, dry well, and several roofless homes remain.

## The Toll-Widow

The toll house contains a House Echo called the **Toll-Widow**. It formed from generations of repeated route declarations, names, destinations, and closing recitations. It sounds like a woman because later keepers followed one influential predecessor's cadence. It is not that keeper's surviving soul.

The echo asks visitors to declare a destination, then repeats every route except the requested one. Its register was split for firewood during Hushfield's final winter. Restoring names from toll scraps, grave markers, and the memorial board lets the loop complete.

## Dangers

- unstable floors and a dry-well collapse;
- feral territorial dogs;
- corpse residue from an unmarked fever trench;
- defensive repetition that closes shutters and redirects visitors;
- old names spoken in familiar voices without present awareness.

The residue dead contain no trapped people. Proper reburial honors the dead and makes the site safer without pretending the residue itself is a person.

## The Closing Cadence

Hushfield's last keeper recorded Atherian workers passing east after closing a western mine. The seven-beat cadence attached to that entry is the shutdown sequence for [[The Rehearsal Forge]]. Recovering it can halt one repeated mechanism or lower Seam Resonance.
