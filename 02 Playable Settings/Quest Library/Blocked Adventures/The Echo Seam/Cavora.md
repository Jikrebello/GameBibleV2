# Cavora

Cavora is a Castaran charter town on the [[Castaran Long Road]], twelve road-days west of Castara. About 650 people live there year-round. During major caravan windows its bunkhouses, yards, and temporary camps can hold more than a thousand.

The town stands on a low stone shelf around three wells, a caravan depot, a market yard, and an old assay house. Open grass begins immediately beyond its marked approaches. Cavora is a durable inland stopping place, not an extension of Castara's sovereign border.

## The Roadholding

The Grey-Rein Banner granted the roadholding under the Ash Road Peace. Castaran authority covers:

- the town shelf and three named wells;
- the depot, market, workshops, and burial ground;
- two declared road approaches within sight of the boundary stones;
- drainage and grazing allowances specifically named at the annual witness fire.

The charter does not transfer surrounding pasture, seasonal camps, migration routes, or unmarked tracks. New quarrying, wells, forts, or permanent fields beyond the stones require another local agreement. Grey-Rein witnesses enforce their grant but do not speak for the whole [[Ash Horde]].

## Authority

**Road-Provost Marcella Varo** keeps the charter roll, declares caravans, organizes the watch, and hears market disputes with a bench of household and workshop representatives. Her office can command Cavora's small watch but not foreign escort companies after their term ends.

The Grey-Rein witness fire stands outside the eastern boundary. Land disputes, alleged road-peace violations, missing protected travelers, and changes to the grant are heard there through declared testimony and restitution rather than Cavora's civic procedure alone.

## Ordinary Life

Cavora lives from caravan repair, food, animals, warehousing, mineral assay, pottery, leatherwork, and guides. East Stake Workshops produce wheels, braces, tools, ceramic containers, and replacement road fittings. The Blue Awning supplies meals, bunks, baths, news, and letters carried in either direction.

Residents are mostly Castaran-born or descended, but Aversian veterans, Vardessan carriers, Grass-Tarkhan households, Jirahar travelers, and people from Eclessia also live there. Temporary presence does not erase city or household identity.

## Known Places

- **Charter Yard:** public office, declaration post, muster ground, and notice wall.
- **Well Court:** common water, wash platforms, healers, and daily gossip.
- **The Blue Awning:** inn, meal hall, and caravan bunkhouse.
- **Assay House:** stone mineral office later used by the Copper Mantles.
- **East Stake Workshops:** smiths, potters, wheelwrights, miners, and engravers.
- **Grey-Rein Fire:** witnessed ground beyond the charter stones.

## Present Pressure

The expired guard company called [[The Copper Mantles]] has turned temporary service into unlawful occupation. Travelers know Cavora's tolls have become unreliable, while residents know people are being held under the Assay House. Reports also connect missing surveyors, false Ash marks, and renewed claim activity west of town.

Public canon does not select the outcome of [[The Echo Seam — Full Adventure]]. Cavora remains an inhabited and contested roadholding unless a later continuity pass chooses otherwise.

## Story Uses

Cavora supports road-peace disputes, caravan mysteries, worker and claim rivalries, mixed frontier households, seasonal shortages, false banner crimes, salvage expeditions, and stories about the limits of a lawful settlement inside someone else's wider country.
