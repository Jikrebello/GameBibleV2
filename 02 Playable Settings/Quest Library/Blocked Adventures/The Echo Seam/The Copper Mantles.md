# The Copper Mantles

The Copper Mantles are an Aversian-origin contract company occupying [[Cavora]] under Captain Leandro Vann. They were hired for six months after road attacks threatened a major caravan window. Their useful service ended, their term expired, and they remained under force.

## From Guard to Occupier

Vann first demanded emergency extensions, then invented protection tolls on already declared caravans. The company seized the Assay House, began approving mineral claims, intimidated the town watch, and imprisoned people who challenged its authority.

[[Magister Orsino Vellaro]] pays Vann to suppress search parties, supply labor and ceramic blanks, and keep other claimants away from [[Echo Seam Mine]]. Vann expects forge-made weapons and a permanent share of Cavora's security. Orsino cannot safely provide either.

## Strength

- twenty-four reliable soldiers under direct company discipline;
- eight local recruits serving through fear, wages, or family pressure;
- the Assay House, wagon court, sample cellars, and two street posts;
- seized caravan goods and claim records;
- crossbows, shields, road barricades, and ordinary military training;
- two unstable prepared components supplied by Orsino as demonstrations.

## Divisions

Some Mantles believe their service remains lawful because Marcella paid two extensions. Others know the company is extorting the town and expect a claim share. Local recruits can defect when prisoners are protected. Vann's core officers will fight or flee to preserve the company as an armed estate.

The Mantles are one rogue company, not the Aversian army or every contract soldier. Their original service and internal differences do not make continued occupation legitimate.

## Defeat and Aftermath

The company can be broken through public charter challenge, rescue of its prisoners, loss of Vann, internal defection, direct assault, or proof that Orsino's promised arms are contaminated. Surrendered soldiers remain answerable for specific acts rather than being killed or absolved as a group.

No outcome is fixed. Surviving Mantles may reinforce Orsino, submit to Cavora, flee toward Aversian employers, or become a later claimant to the mine.
