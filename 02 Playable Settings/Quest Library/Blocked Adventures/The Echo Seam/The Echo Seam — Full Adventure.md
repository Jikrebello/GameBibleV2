# The Echo Seam — Full Adventure

## Adventure at a Glance

**Premise:** The party escorts survey equipment toward [[Cavora]] after lead surveyor [[Dario Marevi]] and Ash peace-witness [[Temur Grey-Rein]] ride ahead. Their abandoned mounts and a road ambush reveal that both men have been taken. Temur can be rescued from the first camp, but Dario and the route plate to [[Echo Seam Mine]] have been sent deeper into the road country.

**Beginning:** Follow the false Ash marks to Split-Reed Camp, rescue Temur, and reach Cavora. The town's expired garrison, [[The Copper Mantles]], now collects unlawful tolls and suppresses anyone searching for the mine.

**Middle:** Break the occupation and pursue four regional leads in any order. [[Hushfield]], [[The Ninth Marker]], [[Sunscar Quarry]], and [[Red Mile Watch]] each provide a way to find or survive the mine. Two leads can locate it; all four make the finale substantially safer.

**Turn:** The evidence identifies expelled metallurgist [[Magister Orsino Vellaro]] as the organizer. Orsino has already entered the mine, where he holds Nella Marevi and is forcing captives to restore [[The Rehearsal Forge]]. His first activation raises the Seam's resonance across the roadholding.

**End:** Enter the mine through one of three routes, cross collapsed workings and contaminated crystal halls, rescue Nella, stop Orsino, and decide whether the Rehearsal Forge can be stabilized without repeating the crimes that brought everyone there.

**Tier:** starter town-and-delve campaign, level 1-5 analog.

**Expected length:** 8-12 substantial sessions: one opening road session, two sessions in Cavora, three or four regional operations, and two or three sessions in Echo Seam Mine.

## Who, What, Where, Why, and How

- **Who:** the player party; the three Marevi surveyors; Temur Grey-Rein; Road-Provost Marcella Varo; Cavora's households; the Copper Mantles; the Broken Stakes; Orsino Vellaro; and ordinary road people whose different interests do not make kidnapping or occupation morally ambiguous.
- **What:** rescue the missing surveyors, expose false Ash markings, free Cavora, assemble the route and safety knowledge needed to enter the mine, prevent an uncontrolled activation, and choose the workshop's immediate custody.
- **Where:** a bounded Castaran roadholding twelve days west of Castara, the adjacent grass and ruined road sites, and an Atherian industrial complex beneath a low crystal ridge.
- **Why the party acts:** Dario hired them; the ambush makes them witnesses; Temur asks them to prevent a retaliatory road feud; Marcella commissions them after their arrival; and Orsino's activation eventually threatens every well and structure built over the same stone shelf.
- **Why the crisis exists:** the Marevis found a valuable site. Orsino learned of it, decided that his technical brilliance entitled him to control it, and hired separate criminal groups to seize the route and paralyze the nearest town.
- **How it escalates:** mundane road violence leads to armed civic occupation; the regional search exposes older dangers; Orsino enters the mine; and each disturbed script makes the non-conscious crystal lattice more active and less predictable.

## Canon Boundaries

Cavora, its bounded charter, the Grey-Rein grant, the Marevi discovery, Orsino's plot, the Copper Mantle occupation, the Broken Stakes, the four regional sites, Echo Seam Mine, and the Rehearsal Forge are local canon.

No outcome is canon. Public references should describe Cavora as a pressured but inhabited road town and the crystal ridge as old restricted ground. They should not state whether Orsino dies, the Marevis survive, the mine opens, or the Forge changes custody.

The Castaran Long Road remains Ash-Horde country outside specifically granted ground. A Castaran charter, University interest, and regular caravan traffic do not make the surrounding pasture Castaran territory. The Grey-Rein Banner speaks only for its own grant and obligations, not for every Ash household or Horde.

The adventure is independent from [[No Word from Claim Seventeen]] and [[The Star over Dustfeld]]. It contains no Nythalum machinery, memory extraction, falling probe, or required outcome from another Castaran adventure.

## Moral Ruling

Cavora has a legitimate right to defend its households, wells, and declared roadholding. The Grey-Rein Banner has an equally legitimate right to prevent the charter from spreading into pasture and undeclared water. Those rights can coexist because the original grant was bounded rather than fraudulent.

Orsino was expelled for real misconduct. University patrons later attempted to bury the scandal while retaining useful portions of his research, which gives him a genuine grievance against their hypocrisy. It does not justify murdering witnesses, abducting surveyors, falsifying Ash responsibility, using captives as technical labor, or treating Cavora as an expendable field test.

The Copper Mantles once performed useful guard service. Their term expired and their continued tolls are extortion backed by force. The Broken Stakes contain dispossessed claimants and failed workers, but they knowingly kidnap, murder, and risk a road war for private gain. Neither organization represents an entire culture.

The strongest resolution frees the town and captives first, disables the contaminated Forge, tells the truth about Orsino's earlier deaths, and permits only limited reopening with workers able to stop unsafe production. Prosperity does not require pretending that technical danger or human guilt has disappeared.

## The Central Truth

### The Atherian Workshop

Late Atherian engineers found a seam of pale crystal beneath the low ridge west of modern Cavora. The material held vibration unusually well. They cut a mine around it, opened a small industrial draw from the Elemental Shell, and built a workshop that could rehearse a complete elemental script into prepared metal or ceramic.

The site was never a permanent anchor. Its draw could admit force but not travelers, inhabitants, or an independent elemental being. The workshop produced bounded components: a plate that released one wall of heat, a ceramic bolt that burst into freezing mist, a brace that hardened once during structural failure. Each object still required a complete script, compatible material, and a deliberate activation.

The mine closed after repeated work left old timing and command relations inside the crystal. New scripts began acquiring fragments of previous ones. Doors answered to dead shifts, cooling channels repeated a fire sequence, and Crystal Forms grew toward spoken commands. The last overseer shut the draw, removed the main rehearsal plates, and sealed the processing halls. Later collapse buried the route.

### The Marevi Discovery

Dario, Toma, and Nella Marevi are Castaran survey siblings from a modest road-and-quarry household. They found Atherian spoil in an abandoned drainage cut, triangulated three survey stones, and entered by the collapsed haulage adit. They recovered one inactive rehearsal plate and heard their own voices repeat from deeper crystal long after they stopped speaking.

They withdrew and divided their evidence. Dario carried the route plate. Toma kept mineral samples. Nella took copied inscriptions to Castara for comparison. Their caution delayed the discovery becoming public but did not keep it from Orsino, who recognized a copied cadence in a University reading room.

### Orsino's Plot

Orsino Vellaro was once a gifted University metallurgist studying how conductor alloys retain warlock scripts. He accelerated a test after instruments showed recursive vibration. Three assistants died when the material repeated an earlier heat command inside a sealed chamber. Orsino altered the log, blamed a junior engraver, and tried to run the experiment again. Surviving evidence led to his expulsion.

Orsino tracked the Marevis to Cavora. He paid Ressa Pike's Broken Stakes to seize Dario and the route plate. Separately, he paid Leandro Vann to keep Cavora frightened, suppress rival claims, and feed him prisoners who knew mining or script work. Neither group knows the whole plan, and they dislike one another.

Toma returned to the mine through a partial route before the ambush. Orsino's advance crew found him there. Toma refused to help open the workshop and died during a forced crossing of the crystal galleries. Nella was captured later while searching for her brothers. Orsino now needs her transcription skill and Dario's plate to finish the activation.

## Cavora in Play

Cavora has about 650 permanent residents and can hold more than a thousand people during major caravan windows. It is large enough to support several competing interests but small enough that occupation changes every ordinary journey.

Use six recurring places:

- **Charter Yard:** Marcella's office, caravan declarations, public notices, and the safest open meeting ground.
- **The Blue Awning:** inn, meal hall, bunkhouse, rumor exchange, and the party's likely base.
- **Assay House:** former mineral office, now the Copper Mantles' headquarters and prison.
- **Grey-Rein Fire:** a witnessed camp just outside the charter boundary where land and road disputes are heard.
- **Well Court:** common water, wash platforms, healers, and the place most vulnerable to public intimidation.
- **East Stake Workshops:** smiths, wheelwrights, potters, and miners capable of helping stabilize the Forge if they trust the party.

The town is not merely a quest dispenser. Between operations, show food prices, workers returning to public spaces, shutters closing after reprisals, caravans deciding whether to stop, and families asking what the mine will do to rent and labor if it opens.

## Campaign States

### Town Courage: 0 to 4

Begin at **1** when the party reaches Cavora.

- **0 - Subdued:** households avoid public aid, witnesses disappear, and the Mantles recruit local auxiliaries.
- **1 - Watching:** people share rumors privately but will not stand beside the party.
- **2 - Stirring:** workshops hide fugitives, provide supplies, and report gang movement.
- **3 - Standing:** the Charter Yard can openly defy the Mantles and protect witnesses.
- **4 - Free town:** Cavora can hold prisoners, guard approaches, and support the mine expedition without outside soldiers.

Public rescue, exposing false marks, returning seized goods, protecting witnesses, and treating town workers as decision-makers raise Courage. Collective punishment, looting, abandoned prisoners, or replacing the Mantles with another armed claimant lower it.

### Rival Progress: 0 to 4

Begin at **1** after Dario is moved to Red Mile Watch.

- **0 - Disrupted:** Orsino has no complete route and cannot begin activation before the party arrives.
- **1 - Searching:** agents hold fragments but still need Dario, Nella, or regional evidence.
- **2 - Entered:** Orsino reaches the mine through the haulage route and begins clearing it.
- **3 - Rehearsing:** captives are forced to restore the workshop; Seam Resonance activates at 1.
- **4 - First Run:** Orsino completes an unstable production cycle, raises Resonance by 1, and arms his remaining crew with flawed prepared components.

Major delay, retreat from a rival-held site, or allowing Ressa to sell her fragment advances Progress. Rescuing Dario, taking the route plate, breaking the Mantles' messages, or turning one criminal group against the other can reduce it.

### Seam Resonance: 0 to 4

This state begins at **0** and becomes active when Orsino opens the lesser draw or the party disturbs the inner workshop.

- **0 - Quiet:** old crystal holds only faint mechanical vibration.
- **1 - Repetition:** spoken words return later from the wrong direction; crystal grows toward sustained sound.
- **2 - Crosstalk:** doors and tools execute fragments of prior commands; delayed images obscure movement.
- **3 - Manifestation:** Crystal Forms grow in active galleries and seed new recurrences when shattered carelessly.
- **4 - Recursive run:** the Forge combines current and historic scripts, threatening a pressure and heat failure across the mine.

Using the closing cadence, calibration key, ventilation route, and Toma's notes can reduce Resonance. Uncontrolled script use, loud repeated commands, destructive mining, or failed activation raises it.

### Survivor Roster

Track Dario, Nella, Temur, and the six prisoners beneath the Assay House individually. A failed rescue first changes location, health, willingness to testify, or captor readiness. Death follows only when a danger is ignored, a captor deliberately kills, or an already wounded person is abandoned during escalation.

## Act I - False Marks on the Road

### The Empty Tack

The party escorts two wagons of survey tools, food, and replacement pump parts toward Cavora. Dario and Temur left the previous fire one morning ahead to register the equipment and inspect a reported boundary-stone disturbance.

At a narrow reed crossing, the party finds both mounts alive but stripped of saddles. Signs include:

- a grey ash-knot tied backward on a thorn branch;
- short-shafted road arrows from three different makers;
- wagon drag marks prepared before the victims arrived;
- one of Dario's brass measuring pins pressed into mud;
- blood leading toward reeds, then stopping where a captive was carried;
- two attackers arguing in Castaran road cant before the ambush begins.

The reversed ash-knot is the first fair warning that the marks are imitation. A character familiar with Ash practice recognizes it immediately. Anyone else can learn by comparing the knot to Temur's abandoned tack or asking at the next declared fire.

### The Ambush

The Broken Stakes attack to seize the equipment, not to fight to extinction. They use concealed pits, ordinary bows, sling stones, and a weighted net across the road. One attacker carries a stolen Grey-Rein badge and tries to be seen fleeing west.

If the party loses, the gang takes one wagon, binds rather than executes survivors, and withdraws. The party can escape, be released by an injured captive, or track the stolen wagon. Rival Progress rises, but the story continues.

### Split-Reed Camp

The trail leads to a temporary camp built around an old drainage culvert. It holds twelve claim-jumpers, three prisoners, and the surviving survey tack. Ressa Pike has already moved Dario and the route plate to Red Mile Watch. Temur remains because she wants the names of protected caravans and the Grey-Rein response signs.

Approaches include:

- follow the dry culvert beneath the lookout bank;
- enter as buyers using the stolen-wagon dispute;
- free the gang's hobbled mounts and create a stampede;
- attack the visible barricade;
- convince two unpaid recruits that Ressa means to blame them as Ash raiders.

Temur can identify every false mark and names Red Mile Watch as an old road fort beyond Cavora. He asks the party to carry one captured badge publicly rather than quietly killing everyone. Evidence, not rumor, is what prevents retaliation.

### Act I Results

- Temur rescued and badges preserved: Town Courage begins at 2 and the Grey-Rein Fire receives the party openly.
- Temur wounded or left behind: Courage remains 1; he is moved toward Red Mile Watch and remains recoverable.
- Ressa warned: Red Mile Watch fortifies and Rival Progress rises by 1.
- Equipment preserved: East Stake Workshops can supply one free mine-preparation asset.
- Equipment lost: replacement costs time or a favor in Cavora but never blocks the search.

## Act II - Cavora Under Copper

### Arrival

Cavora's gates are open, but Copper Mantles stand at the Charter Yard, Well Court, and Assay House. They demand an invented protection toll on goods already declared under the Ash Road Peace. Town residents watch from shaded doors.

Marcella Varo meets the party privately in the Blue Awning. The company's original six-month service ended eleven weeks ago. Marcella paid two emergency extensions from private merchant contributions after Vann threatened to abandon Cavora during a caravan window. Vann instead expanded the toll, seized the Assay House, and began deciding which claims could be registered.

Marcella asks for three things:

1. protect witnesses willing to describe the extortion;
2. find six people held under the Assay House;
3. remove Vann's ability to retaliate against the town in one coordinated action.

### Town Pressures

Run two or three before the Assay House assault:

- **Well Court beating:** Mantles punish a potter who refused a second toll. Intervention gains public trust but alerts Vann.
- **Seized caravan:** a Vardessan factor has papers proving payment, but the Mantles hold the wagons until the driver signs over a mineral share.
- **Missing apprentice:** an East Stake engraver was taken after recognizing Orsino's rehearsal notation.
- **Grey-Rein accusation:** young riders arrive after finding a false badge beside a robbed family. Temur or physical evidence can prevent escalation.
- **Mantle recruit:** a local guard wants out but fears Vann will kill a prisoner identified as his sister's partner.

These are ordinary human problems. No secret supernatural author controls them.

### The Assay House

The Assay House is a stone counting office, sample cellar, wagon court, and disused drainage tunnel. Vann keeps twenty-four reliable soldiers, eight frightened local recruits, and six prisoners. He expects attack after dark but assumes the town will not rise publicly.

Possible entries:

- the front yard during a public charter challenge;
- a roof bridge from the abandoned weigh shed;
- the old sample chute from East Stake Workshops;
- the drainage tunnel discovered through the Blue Awning cellar;
- negotiated inspection backed by Temur and caravan witnesses.

The cellars contain seized goods, Orsino's payments, messages to Ressa, a copied Atherian cadence, and a delivery order for miners and ceramic blanks. One iron-eating slime has entered through damp ore storage. It is an independent hazard that threatens prisoners and structural braces during the confrontation.

Vann bargains if isolated. He offers Orsino's route, the town's seized silver, and service against the Broken Stakes in exchange for continued command. Accepting his armed rule does not free Cavora. He can surrender, flee, be killed, or force a street withdrawal.

### Cavora Freed or Frightened

If Courage reaches 3, workshops close against the Mantles, townspeople guide prisoners out, and Marcella can hold the Charter Yard. At 4, Cavora supplies guards and a field workshop for later expeditions.

At Courage 0-1, defeating Vann still removes the occupation, but witnesses hide, stores remain closed, and a surviving Mantle faction follows Orsino into the mine. The party wins space rather than instant civic confidence.

### Downtime in Cavora

Between regional operations, allow the party to:

- restore confiscated claims and equipment;
- help the Well Court recover from rationing and intimidation;
- compare Atherian samples at East Stake Workshops;
- hear Marcella, Temur, miners, carriers, and Castaran agents argue over the mine without treating the argument as the adventure's only content;
- learn which households will be economically displaced if a large outside company takes the Seam;
- recruit two local guides or one technical assistant when Courage permits.

## Act III - The Four Road Leads

The party needs any two independent location clues to triangulate Echo Seam Mine. Dario or the complete route plate counts as two. Every lead also grants a finale asset.

### Hushfield - The Closing Cadence

Hushfield was an early road hamlet abandoned after a dry well and fever season. The intact toll house contains an anchored persona echo called the **Toll-Widow**, formed from repeated declarations spoken by several generations of record-keepers. It is not any one keeper's soul.

**Signs:** a shutter opens whenever someone names a destination; dust records footsteps that occurred years apart; a woman's voice asks for declarations from empty rooms; the memorial board has been split for firewood.

**Problem:** the Toll-Widow repeats every route except the one requested because its name-and-destination register is broken. Loud demands strengthen the loop. Restoring names from grave markers, toll scraps, and the surviving board lets it complete its last working sequence.

**Opposition:** collapse, territorial dogs gone feral, residue walkers from an unmarked fever trench, and the House Echo's defensive repetition. The walkers contain no trapped people.

**Information:** the last toll keeper recorded Atherian workers closing a western mine and sang the workshop's seven-beat shutdown cadence to remember it.

**Asset - Closing Cadence:** once during the mine, halt a repeating mechanism, lower Resonance by 1, or create a safe pause during the recursive run.

### The Ninth Marker - Calibration Key

The Ninth Marker is a buried Atherian survey shaft whose road warden still rotates route stones and rejects modern boundaries. Doctor Esma Loran occupies the upper chambers with four salvagers. She is not Orsino's servant and will trade, argue, or defend her find.

**Signs:** mile stones turn overnight; dry channels hum after speech; bronze masks repeat obsolete water exemptions; survey lines point beneath the western ridge.

**Problem:** the warden classifies Cavora as an unauthorized obstruction and the Grey-Rein track as unregistered pasture. Destroying it is possible but loses the most reliable mine-bearing. Completing or invalidating its local civic writ opens the calibration chamber.

**Opposition:** rotating barriers, maintenance tools, a collapsing shaft, and one adaptive road engine. Its apparent judgement is procedural simulation.

**Information:** the survey table identifies the Echo works as a lesser extraction workshop and proves it was deliberately closed for recursive contamination.

**Asset - Calibration Key:** detune one crystal buttress automatically, disable one ward-engine network, or reduce the difficulty of every technical stabilization action.

If Esma survives and trusts the party, she will advise Cavora after the finale but refuses to enter the contaminated inner halls.

### Sunscar Quarry - Ventilation Route

Sunscar Quarry is a failed modern stone claim cut into an older Atherian exhaust line. A wyvern dens in the upper quarry while clear slimes, iron-eaters, and ruin vermin occupy the damp cuts below.

**Signs:** bones dropped from height, long scrape marks above the quarry lip, missing tool heads, translucent trails across standing water, and warm air pulsing from a stone vent after sunset.

**Problem:** the party needs the ventilation plan from the foreman's shelter and must inspect the old exhaust without collapsing it. Killing the wyvern is not mandatory; baiting it away, sealing its approach, or moving through the lower cuts can work.

**Opposition:** the natural predator, unstable ledges, ordinary swarms, and oozes attracted to mineral runoff. None serves Orsino.

**Information:** the exhaust maps reach the mine's processing level and bypass the haulage collapse.

**Asset - Ventilation Route:** enter the mine above the first rival barricade, evacuate captives during high Resonance, and vent one pressure event in the finale.

### Red Mile Watch - Route Plate and Dario

Red Mile Watch is an Atherian road fort repaired repeatedly by later travelers. Ressa Pike holds it with about twenty Broken Stakes, Dario, stolen badges, and the route plate. She plans to sell the plate to Orsino and then sell a copy to a Castaran claim factor.

**Signs:** false Ash warning knots face the road; three cultures' arrow styles appear on one parapet; horses are kept ready inside the outer court; survey fires burn behind shuttered stone.

**Approaches:** pose as Orsino's buyers, climb the collapsed signal side, enter through a dry cistern, incite unpaid recruits, or assault the gate.

Ressa values escape over loyalty. She offers Dario for safe passage while hiding the copied fragment. If publicly exposed as the author of the false marks, she loses the protection of ambiguity and several followers surrender.

**Information:** Dario knows the haulage entrance, the route plate provides the exact line, and Ressa's messages prove Orsino ordered the seizure.

**Asset - Marevi Route Plate:** choose the direct adit or Atherian maintenance channel, recognize false mine markings, and prevent Orsino relocating the entrance evidence.

If Dario dies or is moved, the plate remains recoverable. Hushfield plus the Ninth Marker or Sunscar can still locate the mine.

## Act IV - Echo Seam Mine

### Approaches

The party can enter through:

- **Collapsed haulage adit:** direct and broad enough for equipment, but watched by Orsino's crew and vulnerable to renewed collapse.
- **Sunscar ventilation works:** concealed and close to the processing halls, but narrow, vertical, and unsafe without the quarry plans.
- **Atherian maintenance channel:** opened with the Ninth Marker key or route plate; it bypasses mortal guards but enters an active ward classification.

Every entrance remains usable after a failed attempt. Failure raises Resonance, alerts the rival crew, costs equipment, or separates the party from an evacuation route.

### Zone 1 - The Abandoned Workings

The outer mine contains rotted braces, clear slimes, iron-eaters, flooded cuts, and signs of two modern expeditions. Orsino's people have marked safe routes in white ceramic dust and trapped alternatives with ordinary deadfalls.

Toma Marevi's field camp lies beyond a partial fall. His body is present; his soul has routed. His field book records three critical truths:

1. the workshop draw is a temporary industrial tap, not a permanent anchor;
2. shattering singing crystal seeds new recurrences;
3. the three buttresses must be tuned together before the draw is reopened or closed under load.

Recovering the book permits controlled stabilization. Leaving the body does not curse the mine, but returning it matters to the Marevis and Cavora.

### Zone 2 - Processing Halls

Ore crushers, ceramic kilns, conductor baths, and self-moving maintenance tools fill the second level. Atherian ward engines classify entrants as workers, supervisors, material, or obstruction according to dead civic categories.

The party can:

- use the route plate to claim a maintenance function;
- use the Ninth Marker key to close the work shift;
- physically isolate the engine's route stones;
- follow Orsino's crew through a dangerous worker classification;
- destroy individual frames while risking kiln and water failures.

Six captives work here if they were not rescued in Cavora or if later reprisals supplied replacements. Saving them reduces Orsino's labor and provides witnesses. A failed rescue moves them to the rehearsal floor rather than killing them immediately.

### Zone 3 - Crystal Galleries

Facets grow toward sound, reflected movement arrives late, and fragments of old commands emerge in familiar voices. These are stored vibration and Shell-pattern response, not human consciousness.

At Resonance 1-2, the galleries create false routes and moving barriers. At 3, Crystal Forms use recorded sounds to separate intruders and protect the active relation. At 4, every major sound can seed a new growth.

Permanent control requires detuning the local lattice, collecting propagating seeds, and removing the active relation. Simply smashing forms buys time but worsens the return unless the fragments are gathered.

Nella is held in an isolated transcription booth overlooking the galleries. She can be reached by a maintenance bridge, a cut through quiet crystal, or Orsino's guarded lift. If the party is delayed, she sabotages one rehearsal line, raising immediate danger but preventing a clean first run.

### Zone 4 - The Rehearsal Floor

The workshop surrounds a circular tempering bed, three crystal buttresses, removable rehearsal plates, material channels, and the closed Shell draw. Orsino needs Nella's missing line or the Closing Cadence to control shutdown. He is willing to substitute prisoners and accept casualties rather than stop.

At Rival Progress 4, Orsino has produced several flawed components:

- a plate that raises one heat barrier before cracking;
- two ceramic bolts that release freezing mist along the wrong bearing;
- a brace that hardens violently and may trap the structure it supports;
- a glare lens carrying fragments of spoken commands.

These are finite tools and dangerous evidence, not an industrial stockpile of unrestricted magic.

## Confronting Orsino

Orsino fights through the room rather than standing alone. He activates prepared plates, changes conductor routes, orders remaining soldiers to hold Nella, and threatens to open the draw fully if denied the workshop.

He argues that:

- the University used his work after expelling him;
- Cavora will be swallowed by a Merchant Prince if it cannot bargain from strength;
- only someone willing to accept casualties can master an ancient industrial system;
- his victims would have died meaninglessly if he stops now.

The first two claims contain facts. The last two reveal his chosen moral collapse.

Orsino may surrender only when all three conditions hold:

1. the Rehearsal Forge is disabled or beyond his immediate control;
2. the Copper Mantles and Broken Stakes can no longer rescue him by force;
3. Dario, Nella, Temur, Toma's notes, or recovered records can establish his crimes.

He must release captives, surrender every plate and notebook, identify contaminated copies, and submit to public judgement. A promise of employment, secrecy, or technical authority is not repentance. If the conditions do not hold, he fights, bargains for delay, or escapes through the maintenance channel with one copied plate.

## Stabilizing the Rehearsal Forge

Safe stabilization requires three tasks. They may occur during combat or immediately afterward.

### Close the Temporary Draw

Use a complete counter-script, interrupt every conductor path, or physically drop the insulated draw gate. Closing it under Resonance 4 without venting causes a pressure and heat release.

### Remove the Contaminated Plates

Identify and extract six rehearsal plates without speaking their embedded sequences in full. Destroying them in place releases their stored relation. The party can ground, cool, or isolate them before removal.

### Retune the Three Buttresses

All three must share one baseline. Toma's notes, the Calibration Key, careful acoustic testing, or controlled physical wedges can establish it. Tuning only one or two causes the third to rewrite their relation.

No single character tradition is mandatory. Warlock counter-scripts, engineering, careful physical work, controlled environmental collapse, and recovered Atherian tools can contribute. White Flame can protect and steady living workers but does not transform a Shell computation into corruption or replace technical closure.

## Fail-Forward Rulings

- **Temur is not rescued:** false-mark evidence can be proved at Red Mile Watch, but Grey-Rein reaction begins harsher and slower.
- **Cavora remains frightened:** the Assay House can still be cleared; surviving Mantles reinforce the mine and no town field team follows.
- **Dario dies:** the route plate and two regional clues still locate the mine.
- **Nella is moved:** her deliberate mistranscriptions mark the route to the Rehearsal Floor.
- **Toma's book is destroyed:** the Ninth Marker and experimentation can reconstruct safe closure; controlled reopening remains unavailable during the immediate aftermath.
- **The wyvern remains:** the ventilation entrance is possible but cannot serve as a reliable evacuation route.
- **A regional lead is missed:** Orsino or the environment retains the corresponding finale advantage.
- **Resonance reaches 4:** the recursive run begins. The party must stabilize, evacuate, or collapse the workshop while manifestations spread through active galleries.
- **The party retreats:** Orsino completes another run, relocates captives, and raises Rival Progress. Cleared routes and rescued people remain cleared and rescued.
- **Orsino escapes:** the mine can still be stabilized, but Echoes Abroad becomes an immediate pursuit and containment problem.

## Ending States

### The Measured Forge

Orsino is removed, the captives are free, the false Ash marks are exposed, and the workshop is stabilized. Limited work resumes only after Cavora workers, University technicians, and a Grey-Rein land witness can each stop an unsafe run. Production remains small, finite, and tied to declared material and water limits. This is the morally strongest ending.

### The Quiet Seam

The draw is closed and the mine sealed while Cavora seeks safer expertise. The town survives without immediate industrial revival. Claim speculation and outside pressure continue, but no one is sacrificed to prove the Forge can work.

### The University Claim

Castara assumes technical custody, sends specialists and guards, and invests in Cavora. The site is safer than under Orsino, but decisions and profits move east while the town becomes dependent on an institution that once concealed parts of the original scandal.

### The Company Mine

The Copper Mantles, a successor company, or another armed investor controls the site. Roads improve and paid work appears, but Cavora becomes an extraction town whose public safety depends on the force profiting from it.

### The Broken Rehearsal

The party collapses the workshop or destroys the draw relation. The immediate danger and temptation end. The natural crystal remains beneath unstable ground, the mine's economic promise is lost, and fragments must still be accounted for.

### Echoes Abroad

Orsino or an accomplice escapes with a contaminated rehearsal plate and copied script. Cavora and the mine may survive under any other ending, but unstable prepared components begin appearing on the road market.

## Session Rhythm

1. Empty tack, false marks, and road ambush.
2. Split-Reed Camp and Temur's rescue.
3. Cavora pressures and first public resistance.
4. Assay House and the Copper Mantles.
5. Hushfield or the Ninth Marker.
6. Sunscar Quarry or Red Mile Watch.
7. Remaining regional lead and preparations.
8. Mine approach and abandoned workings.
9. Processing halls and Toma's camp.
10. Crystal galleries and Nella's rescue.
11. Orsino and the Rehearsal Floor.
12. Stabilization, custody, and Cavora aftermath.

For an eight-session campaign, combine Sessions 1-2, 3-4, and pairs of regional leads. Longer play should deepen Cavora relationships and road consequences rather than adding unrelated metaphysical machinery.
