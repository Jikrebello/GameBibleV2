# Dario Marevi

Dario Marevi is a Castaran road surveyor and the missing patron who begins [[The Echo Seam — Full Adventure]]. He works with his siblings Toma, a practical mine surveyor, and Nella, an epigrapher and technical copyist.

## The Discovery

The Marevis find pale Atherian spoil in a drainage cut west of [[Cavora]]. Dario reconstructs the haulage bearing, Toma tests the old workings, and Nella copies inscriptions from the first sealed hall. They enter once, recover an inactive rehearsal plate, and withdraw after the crystal repeats their voices.

Dario divides the evidence among them because no one person should be able to sell or reopen the site alone. He carries the route plate and plans to register a protected local claim after confirming the danger.

## Error and Responsibility

Dario delays informing the Grey-Rein Banner. The mine lies beyond Cavora's ordinary work ground, and he fears a land objection will cost the family its discovery before Castaran sponsors arrive. His delay is a real breach of trust, but it does not justify his abduction or transfer ownership to his captors.

After rescue, Dario accepts that the Grey-Rein grant must be addressed before work expands. He wants Toma and Nella recovered before discussing profit.

## In Play

Dario knows the direct haulage entrance and can identify the Marevis' original markings. He does not know the Sunscar ventilation route or the Ninth Marker's calibration function. Injury, fear, and guilt make him impatient, but he does not demand that the party abandon other captives.

If Toma dies and Nella survives, Dario supports limited reopening only after Toma's warnings are public. If both siblings die, he may argue for sealing the mine or become dangerously attached to proving their discovery worthwhile.
