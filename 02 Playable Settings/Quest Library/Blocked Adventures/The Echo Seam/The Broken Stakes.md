# The Broken Stakes

The Broken Stakes are mixed road criminals, failed claimants, deserters, and caravan thieves led by Ressa Pike. They kidnap [[Dario Marevi]], hold [[Red Mile Watch]], and use stolen Ash markings to shift blame for their raids.

## Composition

The gang includes Castaran claim-jumpers, Vardessan carriers, former Aversian soldiers, Grass-Tarkhan exiles, and people with no stable road affiliation. This mixture is practical rather than virtuous. Membership rests on chosen participation in robbery, kidnapping, and false-mark violence.

Ressa keeps the group divided into small crews that know different camps. She pays with seized goods and promises a share in undiscovered claims. Most members believe they are selling a route. Only her inner circle knows Orsino wants captives with technical skills.

## False Ash Marks

The Broken Stakes use reversed knots, combined badges, mismatched arrows, and tracks aimed toward lawful camps. The deception has three purposes:

- delay pursuit while travelers argue over responsibility;
- provoke Castaran pressure against Ash households;
- make Ressa useful as an apparent intermediary who can sell protection from the violence she arranged.

[[Temur Grey-Rein]] can expose the fraud, but physical evidence allows anyone to prove it.

## Holdings

- **Split-Reed Camp:** temporary ambush base and first prisoner site.
- [[Red Mile Watch]]: fortified headquarters, horse yard, copied claim room, and signal post.
- three hidden remount strings and several caches along undeclared tracks.

## Relationship with Orsino

[[Magister Orsino Vellaro]] pays for Dario, the Marevi route plate, and misinformation. Ressa retains a copied route fragment because she expects betrayal. She intends to sell it again once Orsino enters the mine.

The Broken Stakes and [[The Copper Mantles]] are not one organization. They compete for Orsino's payment and may attack one another if their messages or promises are exposed.
