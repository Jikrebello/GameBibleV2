# Brass Jackals

The Brass Jackals are a licensed desert salvage company led by **Daro Vessan**. They recover caravan wrecks, abandoned siege works, exposed ruins, and goods from routes that officials will not insure without proof someone has already returned.

They are fast, well equipped, and willing to enter first. Their jackal badge refers to finding what the desert leaves behind, not to a supernatural patron.

## Crew

The Glass-Risen expedition includes:

- **Daro Vessan:** captain, negotiator, and experienced ruin reader;
- **Tala Vey:** quartermaster who records shares and distrusts unpriced risks;
- **Orren Pike:** rune-cutter able to brace doors and crack stone seals;
- **Nasra and Halim:** sibling climbers and load riggers;
- six guards, porters, and glass-road laborers.

## Operating Ethic

The Jackals do not abandon living crew for ordinary treasure. They do believe that ownerless buried wealth belongs to those who risk recovery, and Daro has no patience for institutions that arrive after danger to claim the result.

This ethic fails at the Glass-Risen Estate because the wealth is neither morally ownerless nor metaphysically inert.

## Adventure Behavior

Their conduct follows Rival Trust:

- **-2:** steal proof, cut a retreat line, and attempt to leave with marked treasure.
- **-1:** conceal discoveries and compete for every portable object.
- **0:** keep their agreement exactly and offer no unpriced help.
- **+1:** share routes, carry wounded, and accept a negotiated division.
- **+2:** entrust the party with their dead and abandon treasure to save named records or people.

Greed can corrupt individual Jackals without making the entire crew secretly cultist. Sethrak's most effective pressure is the honest fear that everyone else will profit from their restraint.

## Continuation

The company can leave as respected specialists, bitter rivals, bearers of a contagious estate claim, or survivors trying to recover companions buried under Glass.
