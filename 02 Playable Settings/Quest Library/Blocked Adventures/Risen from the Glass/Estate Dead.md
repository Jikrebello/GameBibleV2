# Estate Dead

Estate Dead are human remains animated by a funerary role rather than by the free continuation of a whole soul. Ishkatar receivers created them by stripping a sacrificed servant's birth name, embalming the body beneath a property title, and making that title part of a tomb's perpetual inventory.

The soul should route onward unless another force separately obstructs it. What remains is memory residue, bodily habit, imposed office, and an ancient claim strong enough to make them move.

## Encounter Role

Estate Dead are guardians, laborers, attendants, and witnesses trapped inside the same role. They are most interesting when the party can identify and release them during an encounter rather than treating them as ordinary corpses to destroy.

## Signs

- human figures labeled by function instead of name;
- burial shelves arranged like storeroom inventory;
- tools placed where grave goods would normally rest;
- dead voices answering only to titles;
- the same duty repeated despite decay, danger, or obvious uselessness;
- faces removed from art while hands and uniforms remain precise.

## Behavior and Tactics

- repeat the task attached to their title;
- restrain and return “misplaced property” before killing;
- move in household groups directed by title plaques;
- become confused when addressed by a recovered birth name;
- lose coordination when personal objects are returned;
- defend the inventory system that holds them even while fragments of memory resist it.

They do not hunger for life or flesh. A released Estate Dead may speak one coherent memory before the animating residue fails.

## Release

Reliable methods include:

- recovering and speaking the birth name before witnesses;
- breaking the title plaque while returning a personal object;
- proving the estate or office extinct;
- severing the receiver seal that owns the household;
- sanctifying remains after the imposed identity is removed.

Destroying the body stops one servant but does not necessarily remove the title. A functioning estate may place that role onto another corpse or vulnerable living claimant.

## Ecology and Distribution

Estate Dead do not reproduce naturally. They occur in Ishkatar tombs, confiscation vaults, temple estates, and administrative burials where human beings were inventoried with objects.

Not every Ishkatar undead servant is Estate Dead, and later cultures may have copied the principle imperfectly.

## Materials and Faction Use

- title plaques help scholars reconstruct erased offices and crimes;
- embalming cloth may retain command clauses and must be handled as a binding object;
- Covenant jurists study them as contract fossils;
- Jirahar Keepers seek names and reburial rather than specimens;
- Avarix cultists prize intact household seals as tools for inherited bondage.

## Quest Uses

- a museum opens a sealed servant register and its exhibits begin resuming old duties;
- an Estate Dead asks the party to find a name no surviving archive records;
- a modern heir inherits an Ishkatar title and wakes every servant attached to it;
- rival scholars disagree whether a speaking residue is the person or only evidence of that person;
- a tomb can be cleared by combat but only healed through restitution.
