# Breach-Assessor Samir Veyad

Samir Veyad is a field assessor attached to the breach-court of Qasr Ilyun. He examines abandoned estates, ruin claims, disputed caravan losses, and contract hazards too unstable to bring into an ordinary courtroom.

He accompanies the expedition to [[The Glass-Risen Estate]] because an ancient ownership rite can attach itself to salvaged objects before a later hearing can undo the damage.

## Character

Samir is dry, patient, physically brave only after admitting that he is afraid, and deeply offended by law used to erase personhood. He believes a good court gives the weak a place where power must explain itself.

He is neither a secret rebel nor an apologist for every Covenant institution. He has enforced harsh but lawful judgments, defended debtors against governors, and avoided examining how much of Qasr Ilyun's prosperity depends on inherited obligations.

## Expedition Role

Samir carries four practical instruments:

- **Notice of Vacant Estate:** prevents first entry from automatically becoming a claim.
- **Inventory Clasps:** preserve declared custody over a small number of recovered objects.
- **Witness Cord:** records the names and spoken standing of an expedition party.
- **Closure Writ:** can end the estate only after its receiver seal, dissolution tablet, and confiscation register establish authority and extinction.

These instruments do not disarm traps, defeat wardens, interpret Ishkatar art, or rescue the dead. Samir remains one specialist in a larger crew.

## Pressure Points

- He wants dangerous relics in court custody even when the Jirahar reasonably distrust that custody.
- He is tempted by a ruling that would let the breach-court claim other Ishkatar ruins.
- He will risk his life for a properly named witness.
- He refuses to falsify an inventory, but may accept a temporary omission to get living people out.
- A direct encounter with Sethrak forces him to distinguish justice from mere enforceability in public.

## Continuation

Samir can become a recurring desert contact, a reformist jurist, a state claimant grown too comfortable with precedent, or the person who recognizes Sethrak's escaped title in an apparently ordinary inheritance case.
