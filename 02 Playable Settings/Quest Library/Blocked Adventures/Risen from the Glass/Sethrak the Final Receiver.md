# Sethrak the Final Receiver

Sethrak was a human priest of Avarix and provincial receiver in the late [[The Serpent Kingdom of Ishkatar|Serpent Kingdom of Ishkatar]]. He converted homes seized by the Naga Giants into temple estates, renamed enslaved people by function, and erased heirs who could contest confiscation.

He called himself **Final Receiver of Houses Without Heirs**. Most of those houses had no heirs because his office murdered, enslaved, or renamed them.

## Doctrine

Sethrak believed ownership was the lesser being's path to immortality. Bodies failed, dynasties ended, and gods abandoned their worshippers, but an uncontested title could persist in archive, law, and violence.

His tomb was designed to make him the final owner of:

- every grave good placed within it;
- every servant sacrificed to maintain it;
- every intruder who accepted standing as claimant or representative;
- and every later possession carried by that representative.

## What Wakes

Sethrak's complete soul does not remain in the tomb. The speaking dead is a conscious residue made from his embalmed body, office, repeated appetite, and Avarix's recognition of the estate.

He remembers his life and understands suffering. He is not an innocent soul trapped by machinery. His continued enslavement of the Estate Dead is a present choice.

## Manner

Sethrak is composed, exact, and insulting through classification rather than volume. He never uses an intruder's personal name if the estate has assigned a role. He speaks as though violence is merely the execution of a conclusion already reached.

His central claim is that all later courts also rest on force and inheritance. He expects hypocrisy to make opponents surrender the distinction between legitimate custody and enslavement.

## Powers Through the Estate

- command [[Estate Dead]] still bound to property titles;
- animate or recall inventoried objects;
- act through [[Gilded Wardens]] and the Processional Coil;
- mark hidden grave goods and their bearers;
- transfer his receiver title through the ancient seal;
- strengthen himself through every accepted claim.

These powers diminish when names are restored, the gold tally mirror is blinded, property is openly inventoried, and proof of the cult's extinction is recovered.

## Defeat and Continuation

Destroying Sethrak's body is insufficient if the receiver seal or a living representative remains bound. The cleanest defeat closes the estate, restores the servants' names, and leaves no bearer at full Estate Hold.

If his title escapes, Sethrak prefers counting houses, inheritance courts, salvage companies, and wealthy families to open battlefields. He needs institutions willing to call appetite custody.
