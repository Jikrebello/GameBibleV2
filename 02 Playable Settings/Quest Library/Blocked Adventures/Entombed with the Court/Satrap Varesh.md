# Satrap Varesh

**Varesh the Unbowed** was a Giant satrap of Anshara, master of a plateau court controlling wells, caravan approaches, and cultivated paradise-gardens. He was a provincial ruler beneath the Court of Courts, powerful enough to imitate imperial eternity and too proud to accept that Anshara could end.

## Governing Belief

Varesh believes freedom is the terror of not knowing one's place. His court assigns every person a role, provision, appearance, duty, and death. He calls this order merciful because no subject is left without purpose.

This is not misunderstood benevolence. It is the Sovereign's pride expressed as administration of the soul: Varesh cannot recognize a person except as a function beneath him.

## Primordial Vampirism

Varesh predates the specialized modern bloodlines. His blood can preserve life, impose obedience, alter memory, and cultivate dependent creatures. The later Regalis recognize their hierarchy in him; the Sanguine recognize the blood-memory rites; both inherit only fragments of what he was.

His primary expressions are:

- **Blood investiture:** grants strength and longevity while making the recipient responsive to court commands.
- **Throne-wax:** preserves bodies and memories while replacing personal identity with office.
- **Audience dominion:** compels anyone who accepts a court title to behave according to it.
- **Household hunger:** feeds through every marked servant rather than hunting as a solitary vampire.

## The Folded Court

During Anshara's destruction, Varesh sealed his satellite palace inside a paradise-garden fold. Four office-bearers sustained it while he slept. The court can be recalled only by the Processional Face, which tells the palace that new petitioners have arrived.

The fold is failing. Its garden repeats one sunset, its water has mineralized, and its household memories are fragmenting. Varesh needs living entrants because the court cannot imagine itself without subjects.

## Encounter Logic

Varesh should not be fought at full strength. The adventure is about dismantling his claimed authority before confronting his body.

- Each intact office-seal grants him one court-wide power.
- Each group of restored names denies him servants and weakens Household Hunger.
- The Processional Face can end the audience if its wearer is free of imposed titles.
- A character who accepts Varesh's offer becomes a new office-anchor and prolongs the fold.

Varesh addresses heroes by the role their actions suggest: Herald, Keeper, Hound, Witness, Rebel, or Heir. He remembers personal names only to demonstrate that he considers them irrelevant.

## Defeat and Survival

Destroying Varesh's body without breaking the court lets throne-wax rebuild him through the most heavily marked explorer. Breaking every office starts the fold's collapse but does not kill him immediately. His final defeat requires ending both body and recognized sovereignty.

If the throne-seal survives, Varesh can persist as a command-memory inside a Regalis claimant. This should be an explicit bad continuation, not a surprise that negates the party's success.
