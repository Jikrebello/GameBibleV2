# The Gilded Step

The **Gilded Step** is a four-person antiquities crew specializing in sealed ruins, hostile collections, and recoveries where the patron expects competition. They are professional rivals, not murderers hired to stand in the party's way.

Their present contract comes through the Meridian Antiquities Venture. Captain Vasco Ren believes a private collector is paying for first access. He suspects the collector is vampiric but does not know that Lord Cassian Veyr intends to erase witnesses after securing [[Satrap Varesh|Varesh's]] throne-seal.

## Members

### Vasco Ren — Captain and Delver

Vasco plans routes, negotiates salvage, and keeps the crew moving under pressure. He has lost people on previous recoveries and will accept humiliation or lost treasure to avoid another needless death.

**Breaking point:** proof that Cassian ordered the crew abandoned.

### Nadia Sere — Linguist and Infiltrator

Nadia reads funerary registers and enters competing expeditions under plausible identities. She steals information and access, not keepsakes for cruelty. Her family depends on her income, making exposure frightening but not enough to turn her suicidal.

**Breaking point:** being rescued after the Hall of First Radiance fixes her false identity onto her face.

### Yara Flint — Rune-Warlock

Yara uses compact elemental scripts to break seals, control passages, and cover retreats. Cassian's intermediary separately ordered her to recover primordial blood. She recognizes that the sample could be militarized and is looking for a reason to disobey.

**Breaking point:** seeing throne-wax overwrite a living person's will.

### Olek Marr — Shield and Porter-Master

Olek handles expedition security, loads, casualties, and camp discipline. Cassian controls a promised pardon for Olek's imprisoned brother. Olek will betray a salvage agreement before he abandons a wounded companion.

**Breaking point:** learning that the pardon was never lodged and exists only as Cassian's promise.

## Using the Crew

Track **Rival Trust** from 0-4.

- Share route knowledge, rescue a member, or divide necessary equipment: +1.
- Public humiliation, deliberate abandonment, or taking a relic after agreeing otherwise: -1.
- Revealing Cassian's actual plan and helping break his coercion: +1.

At low trust, the Gilded Step shadows the party, withholds discoveries, and attempts to leave first. At moderate trust, the crews divide tasks and exchange rescues. At high trust, they reject Cassian together and can become recurring expedition allies.

The crew should never act with perfect unity. Vasco values lives, Nadia values security, Yara values professional judgment, and Olek values his brother. Cassian's leverage pulls those motives in different directions.
