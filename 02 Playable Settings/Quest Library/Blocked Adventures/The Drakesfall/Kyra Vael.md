# Kyra Vael

Kyra Vael is the last warden of the [[The Drakesfall Vale|Cyrian Skete]] and, at twelve years old, the last keeper of the Drakesfall in [[The Drakesfall]]. She is a child oblate who was being raised to the wardens' slow vocation and is now, through a massacre she survived by being small and fast, the only one left who knows any of the keeping-offices at all.

## The Vocation She Inherited

The Cyrian Skete's work is slowness: walking the bone-field, singing the keeping-offices, and letting each drake's heart-gem return its law to the Faewild at a pace the vale can bear. It is not glamorous and not martial — it is a lifetime of patient tending that keeps a dangerous holy place safe. Kyra was years from finishing her instruction when the [[The Vharo Concession|concession]] came up the road.

She knows the daily offices and the shape of the greater ones. She does not yet know all of them, and she is old enough to understand exactly what that gap means now that everyone who could fill it is dead.

## What She Carries

Kyra is grieving, frightened, and trying very hard to be the thing the vale needs, which is a great deal to ask of a child. She has Elder Sabbas, dying, to guide her, and the scattered office-books if they can be recovered. She is not a mascot and not a helpless victim; she is a small person doing real, dangerous, necessary work she was not ready for, and whether she can hold it is a genuine open question.

She is wary of rescue that treats her vocation as a strangeness to be saved from. She has also, in her most honest moments, wondered whether she wants to spend her whole life keeping a field of the dead — and is ashamed of the thought.

## In the Adventure

- **Kyra's Footing** tracks whether she is a terrified survivor, a guide who can lead the party through the vale, or a warden able to sing the keeping-offices again.
- Sabbas's teaching, the party's trust, and the recovered office-books raise it.
- At high Footing she can lead the keeping that quiets the [[The Unquiet Field]] — a child finding her vocation, the strongest image the adventure offers.
- At low Footing the party must stand in for the missing wardens under Sabbas's failing guidance, buying the field time rather than truly settling it.

## What She Needs

- someone to keep her alive without deciding her life for her;
- the office-books recovered from the sacked Skete;
- new oblates, or a keeper to stand with her, so she is not the last;
- Sabbas's greater offices written down before he dies;
- permission — from someone she trusts — to grieve, and to be unsure.

## Continuation

Kyra can become a keeping warden who re-peoples the Skete, a Church ward taken down to Kalzaret when the Patriarchate claims the vale, a child who chose to leave a vocation that was never fairly hers, or the last warden of a vale that ended on her watch. Which one is the truest measure of how the adventure went.
