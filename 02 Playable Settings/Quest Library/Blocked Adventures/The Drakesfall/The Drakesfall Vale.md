# The Drakesfall Vale

The Drakesfall Vale is a highland valley in the Kingdom of Vlastrava, above Kalzaret, where the Faewild courts' [[Drakes Overview|drakes]] have come to die since before men — the drake canon's holiest and most dangerous site, and the setting of [[The Drakesfall]]. It is floored with heart-gems slowly returning their crystallized law to the Faewild, and it has been kept quiet for centuries by the ascetic wardens of the Cyrian Skete. Left untended, it is a dam with a crack.

## What the Vale Is

Every drake carries a heart-gem — crystallized Faewild law, colored to its court, condensing over the beast's long life. When the greatest of the courts' beasts near their end, some come here, to the Drakesfall, to die where their law can return to the Faewild slowly and safely. Over ages this has floored the vale with gems in every state of return, and made the ground itself a place where Faewild law runs close to the surface: a bone-field of the courts' dead, quietly unmaking itself the way it should.

"Slowly and safely" is the whole point. The law wants to return; tended, it returns at a pace the vale can bear. Untended — or torn out in bulk — it spills, stacks, and animates.

## The Cyrian Skete

Above the vale stand the cells of the Cyrian Skete, named for the ascetic Cyrion who first built here and taught that the drake-dead must be *kept quiet, not cleansed*. The wardens' vocation is slowness: walking the field, singing the keeping-offices, and letting each gem return its law at the right pace. They are a tiny, ancient order, folded into the Thalmyric Church as a tolerated hermitage — half-suspect to the Patriarchate for tending a Faewild site rather than a shrine of the Redeemer, and left alone because someone must, and because what they contain is real.

The Skete is now sacked: the office-books scattered, the chapel-cave of the greater offices broken open, the wardens dead but for [[Kyra Vael]] and the dying Elder Sabbas.

## The Bone-Field

The vale floor is the bone-field: centuries of drake-dead settling their law into the ground. Normally a slow, safe return; now, untended and torn at by the [[The Vharo Concession|concession]], destabilizing into the hazards of [[The Unquiet Field]] — stacked seasons, circling game, leaking moon-law by night and sun-law by the great gold drake's half-harvested body, and the [[The Gemfallen]] walking out of it toward the villages.

## Notable Features

- **The Great Gold Drake:** a generations-old [[Gold Drakes|gold drake]] that came to the vale to die and was cracked open fresh by the concession; its half-harvested heart is the richest prize and the worst hazard, and the keystone of any quieting.
- **The Silver Grave:** a century-dead [[Silver Drakes|silver drake]] whose moon-law now bleeds up into the nights, the source of the unnatural moonlight and wandering-sleep down the passes.
- **The Chapel-Cave:** where the Skete keeps its greater keeping-offices; broken open in the sack, and the place the field must be quieted from.
- **The Warden Road:** the single road up the passes from Kalzaret, along which the wardens died and the wagons came.

## The Vale's Law — Rules of the Place

- the heart-gems want to return their law to the Faewild, and will, whether tended or not;
- tended, the return is slow and safe; torn out in bulk, it spills as hazard;
- the keeping-offices set the pace — they are the vale's only real control, and they need a keeper;
- what leaks takes the color of its court: silver as moon-law, gold as sun-law, and so on;
- re-seating a great gem slows the whole field; harvesting one speeds its collapse.

## Aftermath Uses

The Drakesfall Vale can become a re-peopled and quieted holy site under a keeping order, a Patriarchate-claimed vale with all the danger of Church custody over a Faewild place, a legally recognized "kept site" protected from the next concession, or a broken field let go on purpose — the Drakesfall ended safely rather than shattered.
