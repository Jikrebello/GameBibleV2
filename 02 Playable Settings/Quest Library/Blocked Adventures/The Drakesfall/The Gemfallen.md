# The Gemfallen

The Gemfallen are drake-dead that animate when a tended death-place loses its keepers — the "eternal guardians gone wrong" of the [[The Drakesfall]], re-rooted in drake canon. They are not drakes, not undead in the residue sense, and not evil. They are Faewild law walking: the crystallized law of dead drakes, released unpaced from an [[The Unquiet Field|Unquiet Field]], taking rough shape and drifting toward wherever unfinished law drifts — which is, unfortunately, downhill toward people.

## What They Are

In a tended drake death-vale, each heart-gem returns its law to the Faewild slowly, quietly, under the keepers' offices. Kill the keepers or tear the gems out in bulk, and the law returns anyway — but unpaced, and some of it takes a body on the way out. A Gemfallen is a knot of released drake-law wearing the bones, scales, or gem-shards of the dead it came from: a walking fragment of a court's law with no keeper to point it home.

They are closest, in the wider bestiary, to the residue-animate dead — but where a rattle-bones is a shed human echo, a Gemfallen is shed *Faewild law*, and it cannot be put down for good while the field that feeds it is unquiet.

## Signs

- shapes of bone, scale, and gem-shard moving with a court's characteristic law — silver ones still and cold, gold ones bright and hoarding;
- animals and weather behaving lawfully-strange in their presence, as around a living drake's territory;
- a drift always *outward and downhill*, toward the passes and the villages, never back toward the bone-field on their own;
- re-forming after being put down, so long as the field remains unquiet;
- a settling — a return to stillness — when the keeping-offices are sung nearby.

## Encounter Role

The Gemfallen are a range-based threat, not a dungeon population: the further the [[The Unquiet Field|Field Strain]] climbs, the further they walk from the bone-field, and the more ordinary people they endanger. Fighting them buys time and space; it does not solve them. The real answer is quieting the field, which calls them home. A party that treats them as monsters to be cleared will fight forever; a party that treats them as a symptom will go and fix the cause.

## Behavior and Tactics

- drift outward and downhill toward inhabited country, following released law like water finding a slope;
- fight with their court's law as much as with tooth and claw — silver stillness and sleep-pull, gold glare and hoarding-madness;
- do not pursue cleverly; they are law running, not minds hunting;
- re-form while the field is unquiet, no matter how thoroughly they are put down;
- settle and go still when a keeper sings the offices, or when the gem that feeds them is re-seated.

## Handling and Settling

Iron and silver put a Gemfallen *down*; only keeping puts it to *rest*. The wardens' offices settle them; re-seating the great gem that anchors the field calls the whole drift home at once. Destroying the gem-shards a Gemfallen wears can end a particular one for good, but scatters its law into the field and raises strain — a trade the desperate make and the wardens never would.

## Canon Boundary

The Gemfallen are drake-canon law-animates, not a breeding species, not true undead, and not a new kind of drake. They exist only where a tended Faewild death-place has lost its keepers, which should be rare and should feel like a wound in the world's holy order — the drake canon's version of a dam breaking. Where the field is kept, there are no Gemfallen; that absence is the wardens' whole work made visible.

## Quest Uses

- a village reports "the dead walking" and the truth is a death-vale whose keepers were killed;
- a party clears Gemfallen for days before realizing they must quiet the field instead;
- a released Gemfallen carries a great drake's court-law downhill into a town that court has never touched;
- a desperate keeper destroys gem-shards to buy a night, and pays for it in strain;
- the settling of the last Gemfallen is the sign a quieted field has truly returned to rest.
