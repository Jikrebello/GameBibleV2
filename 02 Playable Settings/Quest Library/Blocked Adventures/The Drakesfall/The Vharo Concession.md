# The Vharo Concession

The Vharo Concession is the antagonist structure of [[The Drakesfall]]: a legal grant to harvest the drakes' death-vale, and the Vorthara-backed company working it. It is named for the Vlastravan military governor who sold it, and it is dangerous less for its blades than for its paper — a lawful-looking claim over a holy site, armored by frontier law and financed by the appetite of the [[The Concord of Weights|weights-houses]].

## The Grant

Governor Konstantin Vharo is the military governor of the Vlastravan highland district that includes the [[The Drakesfall Vale|Drakesfall]]. Under the Kingdom of Vlastrava's frontier land-for-service custom, a military governor may grant crown-land and its rights in exchange for service and fees — an emergency power meant for settling soldiers and supplying campaigns, which corrupt governors stretch into personal rule.

Vharo sold a harvesting concession over "unclaimed mineral rights in vacant highland crown-land." The description is legally defensible and factually a lie: the vale is neither vacant (the Cyrian Skete tends it) nor mineral (heart-gems are crystallized Faewild law, not ore). Vharo either did not ask or did not want to know — and the evidence suggests he knew exactly what he was selling.

## The Company

Behind the grant is a Vorthara acquisitions house — the kind that files drake heart-gems "under acquisitions" and moves them to the Concord of Weights in standardized lots. It sent Harvest-Master [[Corvane Sisk]] up the warden road with pick-crews, warded wagons, and hired blades to work the concession. When the wardens stood in the road, Sisk's crew killed most of them and dug in.

The company is not a warband. It is a business with a legal department, and its worst weapon is the grant nailed to a post at the dig. It expects any objection to be slow, out-lawyered, and eventually bought off.

## Structure

- **Governor Konstantin Vharo:** the grantor; sold rights he arguably held, and wants no Church inquiry into how he uses emergency authority. Off-site, but the source of the concession's legal cover.
- **Harvest-Master [[Corvane Sisk]]:** the on-site chief; a professional emptying the vale who reads its collapse backwards and digs faster.
- **The pick-crews:** hired labor, not committed zealots; they did not sign up to break a holy site and be blamed for a village of sleepwalkers.
- **The hired blades:** the muscle that killed the wardens and holds the camp.
- **The parent house and its Concord broker:** off-stage, patient, and the reason the grant exists — Vorthara appetite and weights-house demand.

## Why It Is Legal, and Why That Isn't a Defense

A lot of drake heart-gems is a Concord-of-Weights-grade fortune, standardizable and fungible, which is why the weights-houses want the vale emptied and will defend the grant to the last clause. The concession's whole strategy is to make the harvest a paperwork fact before anyone with standing can stop it. That the paper is valid is a truth about Vlastrava's frontier law — not an excuse for emptying a holy site, breaking its slow return, and getting its keepers killed.

## How It Can Be Broken

- **The true description:** proof that the "vacant mineral crown-land" is the tended, inhabited Drakesfall voids the grant's premise.
- **The military governor's knowledge:** proof that Vharo knew what he was selling turns a bad grant into an abuse of office — exactly what the Church court wants.
- **The road-deaths:** the killed wardens make the concession liable regardless of the grant's validity.
- **A split crew:** the pick-crews can be turned with evidence that the leaking law — and the blame — will fall on them.
- **A better deal:** Sisk himself runs a cost-benefit, and a buy-out that beats his cut without the risk can end the dig even while the paper stands.

## The Larger Danger

Even a total defeat here — the dig stopped, the grant voided, Vharo disciplined — does not end the appetite. The weights-houses still want the gems; Vorthara still files such sites under acquisitions; another military governor can sell another grant. Protecting the Drakesfall past this season means winning the vale a standing in imperial or Church law, so the next concession cannot simply buy it again.
