# Birchcross Imperial Stair

The Birchcross Imperial Stair is a buried Atherian service route beneath [[First Shelter Watch-Barrow]]. The upper landing survives; a bronze shutter seals every deeper level from the current adventure.

## Original Function

The stair connected a minor signal relay to road inspection galleries, drainage works, and at least two refuge stations. It allowed maintenance crews to move beneath exposed sections of the old frontier road.

It was infrastructure, not a Way-Gate or ritual descent. Its mapped route remains physically plausible even where collapse or later building has erased the surface road.

## First Landing

The accessible landing contains:

- a floor mosaic showing the local branch, bridge, refuge, and north spur;
- three rotatable road stones marking routes as open or closed;
- a brass route tablet held in a wall clamp;
- a dormant bronze-mask road warden;
- an unpowered lower shutter set into the descending passage.

The mosaic also marks two vanished refuge stations. Their modern surface locations are not yet identified.

## Road Warden

The warden belongs to the Road and Aqueduct Warden form in [[Atherian Ward and Trial Engines]]. It wakes if someone removes the route tablet while a route remains marked open or attempts to force the lower shutter.

When active, it:

- raises waist-high stone barriers;
- labels intruders as route obstructions;
- deploys maintenance arms to recover the tablet;
- attempts to return every route marker to its last registered state.

It does not speak as a person, bargain, pursue beyond the crypt when all roads are closed, or possess moral agency.

## Legible Safety

An instruction mosaic depicts the safe sequence: close branch, bridge, and refuge; cover the receiving lens; then remove the tablet. Replacing the tablet before full activation also resets the engine.

## The Sealed Shutter

The lower shutter has no functioning local power source. Physical force opens only a handspan before counterweights lock. A warlock script can produce the same temporary gap but fully wakes the warden and reveals only cold steps descending into darkness.

The adventure does not define a lower complex, hidden civilization, Shell breach, or required sequel beyond this point.

## Canon Boundaries

The stair and landing exist. No outcome of [[The First Writ — Full Adventure]] is fixed: the landing may remain undiscovered, be copied and resealed, lose its tablet, or contain an awakened warden.

The stair is not one of the permanent Shell anchors, a soul-route, patron threshold, Menhir road, or transport gate.

## Future Uses

- a later map identifies one vanished shelter beneath a settlement;
- a copied route tablet reveals a forgotten drainage route during siege;
- someone steals the original tablet and wakes wardens along the north spur;
- subsidence opens a lower section from a different location without requiring the Watch-Barrow outcome.
