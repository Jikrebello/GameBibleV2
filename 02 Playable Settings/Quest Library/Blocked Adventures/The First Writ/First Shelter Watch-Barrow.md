# First Shelter Watch-Barrow

First Shelter Watch-Barrow is a reused Atherian road relay half a day west of [[Birchcross]]. The upper structure served the [[Fraternity of the First Shelter]] as a refuge and memorial hall after the Folk Wars. A crypt and the [[Birchcross Imperial Stair]] remain hidden beneath it.

## Site History

### Imperial Relay

Atherian engineers cut the first chambers into a low stone ridge beside a service road. The relay maintained signals, water, route records, and emergency shelter. Its load-triggered inner passage restricted armored groups approaching the signal room.

### First Shelter Refuge

In 1012 AR, Chapel-Folk survivors and Eclessian marcher soldiers restored the dry upper rooms. They added hearths, sleeping ledges, the open-door statue, wall graves, and [[The First-Shelter Writ]].

The fraternity sealed its refuge as roads moved north and its final members died or joined Birchcross households. Local people continued to clear brush from the threshold until two generations ago.

## Surface Signs

- a low grassed ridge with one squared stone face;
- birches growing in a line above the buried signal wall;
- an open-door emblem visible beneath lichen;
- a spring issuing from a culvert below the western slope;
- fragments of an Atherian mile marker on the abandoned spur;
- a roof vent disguised as a collapsed cairn.

## Approaches

- **Road threshold:** direct, defensible, and visible from the revetment.
- **Signal vent:** a climb from the ridge into the refuge-hall gallery.
- **Spring culvert:** a cold, narrow route emerging beside the load passage.
- **North service cut:** a rubble-filled exit from the Oath Hall, usable by one or two people without baggage.

## Upper Layout

1. **Threshold Hall:** benches, dry cistern, sentry recesses, and later wire alarms.
2. **Refuge Hall:** hearth, sleeping ledges, heavy tables, fraternity carvings, and Writ inscription.
3. **Load Passage:** Atherian weight plate, sweeping blade arm, inspection curb, and visible counterweight.
4. **Oath Hall:** open-handed statue, signal cord, acoustic lock, and concealed crypt door.
5. **Hidden Crypt:** six wall graves, final register, open-door standard, and the imperial threshold.

## The Load Passage

The passage is mechanical rather than magical. Weight on the central stone releases a blade arm across the corridor. A raised maintenance curb, exposed counterweight chain, and accessible pivot supply several solutions. The fraternity once marked the safe route on a wooden board; the current occupiers removed it.

## The Hidden Door

The statue and refuge-hall inscription preserve an acoustic sequence based on the First-Shelter Writ. Speaking the sequence vibrates a plate and releases the counterweight behind the dais. The mechanism can also be opened by manipulating its speaking tube and lockwork.

It does not detect belief, ancestry, moral worth, or metaphysical obligation.

## Burial Danger

The six dead are ordinary human remains. Grave-script and repeated defensive habit can animate them as [[Corpse Residue]] if their seals, goods, remains, or open-door standard are disturbed. Their souls are absent.

## Present-Day Boundary

The deserter occupation that begins [[The First Writ — Full Adventure]] is canon. Its outcome is not. Later references must not assume the upper site was cleared, damaged, reopened, or returned to Birchcross.
