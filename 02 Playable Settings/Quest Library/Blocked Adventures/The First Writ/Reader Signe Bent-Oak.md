# Reader Signe Bent-Oak

Signe Bent-Oak is Birchcross's chapel reader and acting emergency road-speaker. She organizes the response to the mill raid and asks the party to recover [[Klara Mettler]] in [[The First Writ — Full Adventure]].

## Offices

Birchcross lacks a resident priest. Signe leads daily prayers, reads scripture, records births and burials, manages the poor chest, and prepares the community when an itinerant priest visits.

The previous road-speaker died two winters ago. No successor has gathered enough support, so Signe coordinates urgent repairs, missing travelers, winter refuge, and messages to Grenzburg without claiming permanent civil rule.

## Bent Oak Line

Signe descends from a daughter household of the Bent Oak. She treats Chapel-Folk identity as inheritance and obligation rather than proof of superiority. Her faith rejects sacrifice and court worship while preserving Folk kinship, walked boundaries, songs, and winter duties.

## Knowledge of First Shelter

Signe knows:

- the surface route to [[First Shelter Watch-Barrow]];
- the public history of the [[Fraternity of the First Shelter]];
- the five duties of [[The First-Shelter Writ]];
- that the iron open-door token identifies a rescue party's purpose;
- that old stories mention a road below the graves.

She does not know the crypt's exact lock, the blade passage's current state, or the [[Birchcross Imperial Stair|imperial landing's]] machinery.

## Why She Remains

Signe does not send others into danger because she considers herself above it. She remains because:

- three badly wounded people require organized care;
- armed relatives will leave separately unless someone holds them together;
- the ransom goods must be guarded or prepared as a fallback;
- a messenger must leave for Grenzburg;
- children and older residents need a defended refuge if the deserters return.

If the party has too few members, Signe can travel as a noncombatant guide after another elder assumes these duties.

## Guidance

Her instruction is simple: recover Klara if recovery remains possible; do not sacrifice a person to protect stores; accept surrender without mistaking it for innocence; and leave the human dead as human dead.

She does not demand that the party open, loot, or investigate the barrow after the rescue.

## Responses to Outcomes

- **Klara rescued:** secure care and testimony before celebration.
- **Goods lost:** organize rationing and petition neighboring refuge halls without blaming Klara.
- **Deserters captured:** record names and crimes, then seek a larger marcher court.
- **Graves disturbed:** require recovery and reburial rather than hiding what happened.
- **Stair discovered:** record the report privately and prevent immediate treasure hunting.
