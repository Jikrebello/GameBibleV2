# Klara Mettler

Klara Mettler is a twenty-year-old miller's apprentice from [[Birchcross]] and the captive at the center of [[The First Writ — Full Adventure]]. She understands grain, water, carts, household accounts, and the local road better than most people who call her merely the miller's daughter.

## Life Before the Raid

Klara works beside her father Marten at Mettler Mill. She manages tally sticks, watches the leat, bargains with neighboring households over milling shares, repairs flour cord, and accompanies grain carts to Lakewatch twice each autumn.

Her late mother served as a household reader. Klara therefore knows the public history of the [[Fraternity of the First Shelter]] and the order of duties in [[The First-Shelter Writ]], although she has never entered the hidden crypt.

## During the Raid

Klara recognizes three deserters from muster day. She:

- strikes one with a mill hook;
- cuts a pack strap nearly through;
- shouts the names and faces she recognizes;
- knots apron cloth around the dropped hook;
- begins marking the trail as soon as she is carried away.

Rass takes her because the band wants ransom leverage and cannot leave a witness who can identify all eleven after seeing their barrow deployment.

## Trail Work

Klara leaves evidence that does not depend on improbable access to equipment:

- pieces of the waxed flour cord already looped around her belt;
- broken mill tally sticks dropped at forks;
- thread tied at eye height where boot prints become unclear;
- a deliberate scuff through the stolen horse's distinctive shoe mark;
- a blood smear from the deserter she wounded.

## Captivity

Klara listens more than she speaks. She learns:

- all eleven deserters support Rass's plan;
- two will leave for the ransom stone at dusk;
- Dieter carries the written execution order;
- the patrol returns through the road threshold;
- Rass hopes the hidden door opens onto a northern escape route.

She loosens one wrist against the rough edge of a refuge bench. At higher Alarm, Rass moves her to the Oath Hall, but that also puts her within reach of the signal cord, brazier, and loose benches.

## Rescue Actions

Klara can:

- slip one hand free when the party creates a distraction;
- contradict Rass during negotiation with names and numbers;
- pull the signal cord to reveal her position;
- overturn the brazier between herself and an attacker;
- use a tally shard or dropped tool against a restraining hand;
- call out the safe curb beside the load-triggered blade.

These actions create opportunities. They do not make her invulnerable or replace the party's rescue.

## Character

Klara is observant, impatient with condescension, and frightened without being helpless. She values Birchcross's people more than its grain but knows exactly what losing the winter reserve means. She refuses any account in which her life makes the deserters' crimes less serious or the hamlet's hunger imaginary.

## Possible Continuations

No outcome is canon. If she survives, Klara may:

- return to the mill and become Birchcross's road-speaker in time;
- give detailed testimony against the deserters;
- help copy the imperial route mosaic;
- refuse all connection to the Watch-Barrow after the rescue;
- join future supply expeditions because she now understands how fragile the road is.
