# The Nine-Mile Deserters

The Nine-Mile Deserters are eleven former levy soldiers led by [[Sergeant Volker Rass]]. They leave Grenzburg's first autumn muster at the ninth mile marker, murder their quartermaster, raid [[Birchcross]], and occupy [[First Shelter Watch-Barrow]].

They are a short-lived criminal band, not a social class, reform movement, mercenary nation, or representative sample of frontier soldiers.

## Shared Choice

The levy column's wages were seven weeks late and its officers distributed food unjustly. Every future deserter knew those facts. They also knew alternatives existed: leave individually, seek kin shelter, petition a chapel, expose the officers, or accept arrest for ordinary desertion.

All eleven instead agree to Rass's plan for armed theft. After [[Klara Mettler]] is taken, all hear and support the order that she will be killed after payment. None remains an innocent camp follower.

## Crimes Before Birchcross

- murdering the levy quartermaster during departure;
- stealing two military horses, bows, food, and route papers;
- assaulting one soldier who refused to join;
- concealing their movement under an authorized patrol sign.

## Birchcross Raid

At Mettler Mill the band:

- enters under the appearance of hungry stragglers;
- kills Hagen Lutz when he bars the gate;
- wounds Marten Mettler and two workers;
- steals meal and a cart horse;
- abducts Klara after she identifies members;
- demands winter stores and pack animals under a false release promise.

## Deployment

- **Rass, Dieter Salk, and one bowman:** Oath Hall and captive.
- **Three hall fighters:** refuge barricade and ransom collectors.
- **Two threshold sentries:** public entrance and signal vent.
- **Three-person patrol:** trail, ransom stone, and rear approach.

At dusk, two hall fighters leave to collect the ransom. If the patrol is bypassed, it returns during a later confrontation.

## Equipment

The band carries ordinary levy gear:

- worn spears, short bows, hand axes, knives, and three shields;
- quilted coats and mismatched helmets;
- stolen route papers and one authorized patrol pennant;
- rope, lamp oil, chalk, meal sacks, and field dressings;
- no warlock scripts, relics, Bank equipment, or supernatural protection.

Their advantage comes from preparation, numbers, a hostage, and narrow terrain.

## Conduct Under Pressure

The deserters may retreat, hide, bargain falsely, or surrender after defeat. They do not voluntarily release Klara, turn on Rass to save her, or become allies.

A prisoner may reveal information to reduce his own punishment. That information remains useful without transforming self-interest into redemption.

## Legal Aftermath

Captured members face individual charges according to action and evidence. Shared commitment does not make every sentence identical, but no participant can truthfully claim ignorance of the kidnapping and intended killing.

Birchcross can hold prisoners briefly. Murder, organized desertion, and stolen military property eventually require a marcher court beyond the hamlet.
