# Quest Tier Doctrine

This is the setting-wide craft shorthand used by the conversion library and blocked adventures. It describes three useful scales of quest, not a law that every problem requires politics, contracts, or a late moral reversal. Grenzburg's production counts and metadata are controlled separately by [[Quest and Content Architecture]].

## Tier W: Authored Marquee Story

A Tier W quest has a specific human or communal stake, a place with its own history, several stages of discovery or action, and consequences the setting can remember. It may contain a moral knot, but the knot must arise from the premise. Sometimes the responsible answer is difficult; sometimes evil is clear and the difficulty lies in stopping it without losing everyone around it.

Required strengths:

- an immediate understandable hook;
- motives grounded in a particular culture and place;
- information learned through play;
- more than one viable method or consequential decision;
- a visible result.

## Tier F: Faction Line

A Tier F line contains eight to ten quests that teach an institution through its work. Admission leads to service, service exposes an internal fracture, and the final movement settles leadership, doctrine, method, or the player's relationship to the faction.

Rank or trust grants access and obligation. It should also reveal ordinary members, beneficiaries, sincere goods, and specific abuses. A faction line is not eight errands followed by a surprise promotion to supreme leader.

## Tier R: Gameplay Job

A Tier R job is a focused hunt, escort, rescue, bounty, patrol, clearing, recovery, delivery, defense, gathering trip, duel, or short investigation. Strong combat, travel, terrain, creature behavior, or time pressure can carry a simple structure.

Most placements are finite and authored around a named location. Selected hunts, escorts, and bounties may recur where repetition makes world sense. Repeatable work varies route, target, conditions, and reward without claiming permanent consequences it cannot retain.

## Escalation Rule

A job may reveal a larger story, but escalation is exceptional. Routine work must remain routine often enough that a real anomaly feels important. No fixed percentage, hidden Under-Ledger clause, or metaphysical twist is required.

## Conversion Use

The conversion library uses these tiers to understand a source adventure's playable skeleton. A fully blocked conversion still requires exact culture, geography, motives, beginning, middle, end, fail-forward states, encounters, and consequences. Tier labels never excuse generic translation.
