# The Sealed Star City

The Sealed Star City is the collective name for disconnected imperial observatories, temple substructures, senate vaults, command rooms, ritual avenues, and service corridors aligned to Atherian celestial planning.

It is not one intact underground metropolis. Surviving sites connect only under particular alignments, through lost service systems, or by routes later builders severed.

## Common Contents

Collapsed classrooms, mundane archives, broken instruments, mosaic floors, wall-wardens, dead mechanisms, and ordinary residue greatly outnumber functioning systems.

## Exceptional Contents

- observatories tracking a sky no longer visible;
- rune laboratories with active Shell pressure;
- civic devices recognizing obsolete offices;
- Idol architecture hidden inside public cult foundations;
- roads or rooms that connect by celestial condition rather than distance.

## Custody

Crown claims strategic and state material. Church claims converted sacred and martyr sites. The city claims republican structures. Finders claim shares. Private houses hold old entrances. Any major opening therefore becomes political before it becomes an expedition.

## Quest Uses

- authenticate a map without triggering a ruin rush;
- enter a complex only during a public celestial festival;
- stop a wall-warden enforcing a dead citizenship law;
- recover an archive that changes modern property;
- decide whether a working system should remain sealed;
- survive a temporary overlay between old and modern streets.
