# The Glass Courts Below

The Glass Courts Below are occupation-era legal chambers, customs stores, garrison archives, contract engines, name vaults, and infrastructure controls beneath Glass Court, Caravan Ward, the Quays, and parts of the sacred core.

## Why They Survive

Occupation government converted Atherian rooms rather than replacing the city. Many courts were sealed during 957 because immediate destruction would have cut water, erased title, killed bound workers, or destroyed evidence needed for amnesty.

## Present Condition

Most contracts are dead records. Some remain active in narrow ways: recognizing names, maintaining water, guarding property, or awaiting termination. Coercive provisions are morally and legally void, but a void obligation can still be metaphysically dangerous before it is dismantled.

## Inhabitants and Claimants

Glass Court associations, Crown custodians, Church investigators, foreign jurists, smugglers, and families named in old records all seek access. Some people live in protected chambers below and are not dungeon occupants.

## Quest Uses

- terminate a contract without destroying the service it sustains;
- answer a trial suspended since reconquest;
- protect residents from collective blame after a hostile discovery;
- find a name removed from every surface register;
- expose a modern faction secretly using occupation machinery;
- prevent the whole population from being classified by an ancient engine.
