# The Martyr and Ossuary Roads

The Martyr and Ossuary Roads connect execution sites, early congregation chambers, hospitals, parish crypts, plague galleries, military memorials, and the metropolitan necropolis. They are burial infrastructure maintained by living workers.

## Ordinary Work

Registrars identify bodies, families keep vigils, porters move remains, priests perform rites, masons open and close niches, and sextons clear residue. The system is busy, human, and usually reliable.

## Hazards

- rattle-bones, barrow-shades, keeners, corpse beetles, and flooded passages;
- wrong or damaged labels;
- plague-era collapses;
- illegal relic extraction;
- anonymous bodies and disputed custody;
- rite corruption by an Undertow Wrecker.

## Theological Ruling

Residue is not the routed person. Destroying a barrow-shade does not harm the dead. An apparent speaking dead person must be treated as residue, Shade, patron mimicry, trapped fragment, or rare saintly intervention until independently established.

## Access

Martyrs' Steps contains the densest working entries. Ember Forum entries are highly restricted. Outer Crown grave roads lead to older and less supervised galleries.

## Quest Uses

- body-count discrepancy and missing remains;
- a funeral route diverted through an active thin space;
- false relics cut from ordinary bones;
- a hospice crypt protecting refugees;
- an old martyr site beneath a living occupation-descended chapel;
- a mass Undertow interception attempt.
