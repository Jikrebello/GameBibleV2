# Haunted Evidence and Possession Procedures

## Intake

1. Record where, when, and by whom the object or person was found.
2. Separate witnesses before accounts converge.
3. Test mundane causes, poison, illness, fraud, and coercion.
4. Establish which magical or spiritual access method could produce the effect.
5. Protect the subject before attempting confrontation.

## Possession Categories

- **Shade occupation:** appetite or identity pressure from the Shadowlands.
- **Patron tenancy:** an Astral power operating through pact, rent, or anchor.
- **Contract enforcement:** imposed behavior or role through name and obligation.
- **Relic overshadowing:** rare contact with sanctified memory or purpose; not assumed benign without discernment.
- **Ancient interface:** a person treated as operator, officeholder, component, or subject by a functioning system.

Residue imitation is not true possession, although repeated exposure can overwhelm judgement and habit.

## Response

- remove the subject from the enabling place or object when safe;
- identify consent, contract, anchor, appetite, command, or repeated pattern;
- contain without promising that restraint equals cure;
- bring the correct institution rather than the most politically convenient one;
- preserve independent evidence;
- plan recovery, because removal does not erase injury, guilt, memory, or public fear.

## Charged Antiquities

Use isolation, stable storage, handling logs, material inspection, and observation before ritual testing. Do not clean inscriptions, separate fitted pieces, speak unknown formulae, restore power, or place the object near relics merely to see what happens.

## Public Communication

Authorities should distinguish confirmed danger, working theory, and rumor. Secrecy may protect a victim or operation; it cannot be used indefinitely to avoid restitution to harmed families.
