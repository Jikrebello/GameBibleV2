# The Deep Hill City

The Deep Hill City is the oldest confirmed settlement beneath Caleran: hill walls, cisterns, ancestor chambers, shrines, and civic spaces predating the Celestial Compact.

## Canon Boundary

Its builders are not yet assigned a single modern ethnicity, religion, or supernatural patron. Atheria inherited and altered the site. Later imperial claims that Caleran began with the Twelve Cities are therefore false, but the deeper truth remains incomplete.

## Access

Only a few routes are authenticated, all difficult: beneath the dry seventh aqueduct, behind a sealed Star temple foundation, through a collapsed Ash Hall shaft, and along an ancestor road reached from the metropolitan necropolis.

## Character

The site is worked stone, not incomprehensible alien architecture. Its danger comes from age, burial, local law, later intrusion, and the possibility that some civic mechanisms still distinguish resident, guest, enemy, and dead.

## Restraint

Most chambers are empty, collapsed, looted, or reused. Giant survivals, Menhir influence, and early rune work may appear only where a specific site establishes them.

## Quest Uses

- identify a pre-Atherian civic law still affecting the surface;
- find why later builders avoided one hill;
- mediate with a community that has lived below outside modern recognition;
- prevent modern factions from inventing ancestry claims;
- discover a mechanism older than Atheria but still locally lawful;
- decide whether public knowledge would preserve or destroy the site.
