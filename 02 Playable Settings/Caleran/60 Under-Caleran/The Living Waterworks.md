# The Living Waterworks

The Living Waterworks combine seven aqueduct lines, settling pools, pressure towers, mill races, storm drains, cisterns, bath feeds, and river intakes. They are the largest continuously maintained Atherian system in Caleran.

## What Is Ordinary

Most galleries contain flowing water, inspection walks, mineral buildup, repair scaffolds, vermin, old marks, and exhausted workers. Public survival, not mystery, is their main purpose.

## What Is Dangerous

- sudden pressure changes and flood gates;
- bad air in dry branches;
- collapsed masonry and false floors;
- contaminated lines;
- illegal taps and hidden settlements;
- wall-wardens protecting obsolete boundaries;
- impressions carried by water through sites of repeated violence.

## Control

The Keepers of the Aurin operate the system under civic charter. Crown engineers may intervene in strategic emergencies; the Church can close a line affecting consecrated sites but cannot safely reroute it.

## Deep Truth

The seven lines were built in different periods. Their apparent unity may be a later adaptation of a wider Atherian alignment whose original purpose was not solely water. This remains a high-tier question, not common keeper knowledge.

## Quest Uses

- contamination hunt where the danger is social information;
- repair under district pressure;
- rescue in a filling gallery;
- an illegal community reopening a dead line;
- a coordinated attack intended to justify emergency government;
- discovery that several aqueducts are responding to one ancient command.
