# Occult Investigation in Caleran

Caleran does not introduce a new psychic magic family. Occult practice is the disciplined investigation of effects produced by existing metaphysics.

## Séances

Ordinary séances do not summon routed souls. A response may come from:

- residue repeating memory or habit;
- a Shade using the expected identity;
- an Astral patron or agent;
- a trapped fragment held by unusual magic;
- an active contract, relic, or Atherian system;
- rare authenticated saintly intervention.

The investigator asks what is responding, what permits the response, what it wants, and whether independent evidence agrees.

## Psychometry

Psychometry interprets impressions left by intense use, grief, violence, ritual, or supernatural contact. It may reveal emotion, repeated gesture, sensory fragments, handling sequence, or a location association.

It is vulnerable to:

- contamination by later handlers;
- deliberate staging;
- several events layered together;
- the examiner's expectations and grief;
- an active entity presenting chosen impressions.

An impression is a lead, not testimony.

## Haunt Sources

1. mundane residue;
2. thin-space recursion;
3. Idol architecture;
4. persistent contract;
5. relic manifestation;
6. active Atherian machinery.

Identify the source before choosing cleansing, termination, repair, containment, or destruction.

## Ritual Investigation

A ritual states its source, operators, materials, boundaries, cost, failure mode, and stopping condition. It cannot acquire power merely by being mysterious. Mixed rites are dangerous because participants may not agree which system is answering.

## Mindscape-Like Events

Shared visions and symbolic environments are temporary Shadowlands or Astral overlays, structured residue, patron spaces, or active ancient interfaces. They are not a separate universal mental plane.
