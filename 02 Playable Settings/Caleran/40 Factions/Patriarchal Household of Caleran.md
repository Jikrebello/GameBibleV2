# Patriarchal Household of Caleran

## Public Identity

the spiritual government surrounding the Patriarch and coordinating the Holy Synod from Caleran.

## Private Truth

Sophron's restraint holds together contemplatives, administrators, and cautious bishops, but it also postpones decisions his enemies are already making for him.

## Leadership

Patriarch Sophron, advised by Abbess Nadezhda and competing Synod blocs.

## Resources

doctrinal authority, clergy, archives, hospitals, monasteries, public trust, and the ability to authenticate or condemn sacred claims.

## What It Offers

access to archives, clergy, hospitals, sacred sites, expert discernment, and public legitimacy.

## What It Demands

discretion, protection of worshippers, respect for sacramental jurisdiction, and evidence that can survive hostile examination.

## Internal Fracture

restraint versus militant urgency over Zanthir, succession, and public miracles.

## Rivals

Imperial Prefecture, Court of Five Lamps, old houses, and secret networks exploiting devotion.

## Escalation States

0. Sophron's coalition controls the agenda.
1. Synod blocs force public concessions.
2. a disputed miracle divides the Church.
3. a militant or captured Synod governs through crisis.

## Quest Uses

- protect a witness whom every Synod bloc wants to question.
- recover letters proving a bishop manufactured a popular prophecy.
- prevent a true miracle from becoming a pretext for violence.
