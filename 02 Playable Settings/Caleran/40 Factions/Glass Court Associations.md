# Glass Court Associations

## Public Identity

occupation-descended, Velian, mixed-family, craft, parish, and legal associations defending the Glass Court community.

## Private Truth

they preserve vulnerable people and sometimes protect compromised elders because exposure would empower collective punishment.

## Leadership

Advocate Samira Velen and a rotating council of neighborhood houses.

## Resources

translation, customary law, dense family networks, contract expertise, community funds, and occupation archives.

## What It Offers

local trust, interpreters, hidden history, safe houses, legal expertise, and access to guarded occupation sites.

## What It Demands

no ancestry-based guilt, consultation before entry, protection of civilians, and recognition of valid local rights.

## Internal Fracture

integration into Caleran versus guarded autonomy.

## Rivals

return purists, old houses, Prefecture hardliners, and Covenant agents exploiting grievance.

## Escalation States

0. recognized civic partner.
1. defensive mobilization.
2. street separation and retaliatory closures.
3. foreign-backed autonomy crisis.

## Quest Uses

- prove a respected elder's crime without validating collective blame.
- protect a termination rite from both purists and Covenant saboteurs.
- recover an archive the Associations fear as much as their enemies want.
