# Caravan Ward Compacts

## Public Identity

the negotiated customary protections linking Caleran's Jirahar, Eclessian, Sestaran, Velian, Covenant, and itinerant communities.

## Private Truth

plural law keeps the ward peaceful, but jurisdictional gaps are deliberately sold to spies, traffickers, and fugitives.

## Leadership

the Hall of Compacts, represented by Keeper Hadassa ben Or and several consular heads.

## Resources

languages, foreign contacts, couriers, caravan routes, safe conduct, consular funds, and community intelligence.

## What It Offers

translation, travel, foreign access, discreet mediation, and shelter recognized by multiple communities.

## What It Demands

respect for custom, neutral hearing, protection from mob politics, and reciprocal obligation.

## Internal Fracture

shared ward defense versus national and confessional pressure.

## Rivals

Prefecture foreign officers, criminal brokers, hostile preachers, and foreign intelligence services.

## Escalation States

0. working compact.
1. one community withdraws cooperation.
2. competing courts and reprisals.
3. ward-wide protection pact or violent fragmentation.

## Quest Uses

- resolve a case with two valid laws and one endangered person.
- find a courier erased from civic but not customary records.
- stop a foreign assassination without exposing the refuge network hiding its target.
