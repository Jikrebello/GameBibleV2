# Secret Networks of Caleran

These networks are objective hostile structures. Knowledge of their full shape is creator-facing and should emerge only through play.

## Undertow: The Missing Measure

Body counts in hospitals, ossuaries, and pilgrim hostels diverge by ones and twos. Undertow wreckers exploit rushed burial traffic, anonymous pilgrims, and old soul-route channels. Their goal is interception, not profit, though they pay body thieves and false sextons.

## Fleshweaver Procurement: The Quiet Anatomy

Independent buyers purchase cadavers, medical waste, condemned remains, and charged tissue through apparently ordinary schools and workshops. The procurement chain overlaps Broken Arch salvage and Martyrs' Steps funerary labor. Its products leave Caleran more often than they remain.

## Under-Ledger: The Beneath Account

Predatory capital is mixed into ordinary grain, excavation, and construction finance. Defaults produce despair, betrayal, and social corrosion useful to Greed's network. The Under-Ledger prefers legitimate contracts whose surrounding conditions it secretly controls.

## Idol Operation: The Borrowed Halo

A Weeping Courts and Devoted collaboration cultivates a celebrated healing reputation around selected hospices and patrons. Genuine charity creates the trust; manipulated comparison, jealousy, and dependency turn that good into food. The operation cannot tolerate an authentic saint who refuses celebrity.

## Interlock

- Undertow buyers need bodies; Fleshweavers can hide removal but want material rather than souls.
- Under-Ledger money finances compromised hospitals and digs but resents unrecoverable destruction.
- The Borrowed Halo generates vulnerable devotees and public cover while fearing exposure by relic testing.
- Each network will betray another to preserve its own supply chain.

## Signs

Repeated one-person discrepancies, provenance that is correct but too complete, rescue loans offered before disasters are public, devotional communities measuring holiness by access to one patron, and burial routes altered for trivial administrative reasons.
