# Council of the Old City

## Public Identity

Caleran's municipal government of district delegates, guild voices, magistrates, and old civic privileges.

## Private Truth

the Council defends real self-government while its wealthy blocs use civic language to preserve their own access.

## Leadership

First Councillor Theon Vardas.

## Resources

markets, roads, fire response, district coordination, civic courts, public hearings, permits, and popular visibility.

## What It Offers

city access, public platforms, district introductions, municipal contracts, and the power to make a private grievance public.

## What It Demands

visible accountability, service to affected residents, and concessions that can survive the next election.

## Internal Fracture

district democracy versus old-family and guild control.

## Rivals

Imperial Prefecture, Patriarchate when processions override civic life, and Sestaran creditors.

## Escalation States

0. working coalition.
1. open bloc politics.
2. paralyzed council and district freelancing.
3. extraordinary civic assembly or collapse.

## Quest Uses

- secure votes without buying them.
- prove a district delegate is coerced rather than corrupt.
- keep a public hearing alive while someone engineers a riot.
