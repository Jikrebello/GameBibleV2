# Keepers of the Aurin

## Public Identity

the hereditary and trained engineers maintaining Caleran's aqueducts, cisterns, drains, mills, and contamination watches.

## Private Truth

they know the city cannot survive political interference, but some families treat necessary secrecy as inherited ownership.

## Leadership

Water-Master Petru Voda.

## Resources

water maps, valve access, engineering crews, district trust, flood powers, and knowledge of the safest undercity routes.

## What It Offers

movement through waterworks, emergency supply, engineering expertise, flood control, and evidence carried by the city's water.

## What It Demands

obedience to closures, protection of infrastructure, apprenticeship knowledge, and no unsupervised rune work near pressure systems.

## Internal Fracture

public service versus keeper-family monopoly.

## Rivals

mill guilds, Prefecture engineers, illegal settlers, and anyone diverting old lines.

## Escalation States

0. stable service.
1. district rationing and political pressure.
2. keeper strike or sabotage panic.
3. multi-line failure and civic emergency.

## Quest Uses

- trace a contaminant that is information rather than poison.
- mediate between keepers and a district reopening an old valve.
- defend an inspection team in a water gallery that remembers an execution.
