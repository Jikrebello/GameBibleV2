# Broken Arch Antiquities Rings

## Public Identity

the illicit diggers, fences, landlords, labor gangs, collectors, and corrupt specialists moving unregistered antiquities.

## Private Truth

the rings exploit tenants and graves, but they also finance housing repairs and recover objects institutions would leave buried beneath dangerous buildings.

## Leadership

Vesna Kord, foremost among several competing salvage brokers.

## Resources

cellars, labor, bribed access, buyers, forged provenance, neighborhood fear, and routes through unstable strata.

## What It Offers

fast excavation, cash, rare goods, illegal maps, disposable crews, and information unavailable to official finders.

## What It Demands

silence, exclusivity, tolerance of unsafe practice, and a share before institutional seizure.

## Internal Fracture

neighborhood survival versus predatory extraction.

## Rivals

Licensed Finders, Prefecture, Court of Five Lamps, rival rings, and Fleshweaver buyers.

## Escalation States

0. contained illicit market.
1. ring war and rising collapses.
2. institutional crackdown displaces thousands.
3. a broker cartel governs the district beneath the city.

## Quest Uses

- recover an object before a buyer realizes it is only bait.
- protect tenants from both a criminal dig and a state demolition order.
- trace bodies disappearing through an antiquities route.
