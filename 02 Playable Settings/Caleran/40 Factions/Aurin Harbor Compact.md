# Aurin Harbor Compact

## Public Identity

the working alliance of customs, shipowners, dockworkers, warehouse guilds, ferries, and Saltborn ward captains.

## Private Truth

the Compact keeps food moving by making informal accommodations that range from sensible discretion to organized smuggling.

## Leadership

Harbor Marshal Thaleon Sar and a council of dock guilds.

## Resources

ships, warehouses, labor, food flows, ferries, foreign information, and the ability to stop the port.

## What It Offers

transport, cargo access, sailors, dock intelligence, hidden movement, and emergency supplies.

## What It Demands

fast decisions, protection of crews, practical compensation, and tolerance for customs that keep trade moving.

## Internal Fracture

labor security versus merchant profit and customs control.

## Rivals

Imperial customs, Credit Houses, antiquities smugglers, and quarantine authorities.

## Escalation States

0. working compact.
1. selective slowdown.
2. port strike or customs blockade.
3. grain panic and rival dock government.

## Quest Uses

- find why a lawful cargo terrifies veteran dockworkers.
- protect a strike from an engineered food riot.
- choose which contraband must move during a quarantine.
