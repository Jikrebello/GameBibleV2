# Caleran Delver Companies

## Public Identity

independent and chartered crews who enter dangerous sites after survey, during rescue, or when institutions need deniable expertise.

## Private Truth

the companies romanticize independence while depending on guild maps, creditor equipment, district labor, and institutional buyers.

## Leadership

Mara Ashkey, elected speaker of the Company Table.

## Resources

experienced crews, monster knowledge, tactical flexibility, recovered maps, reputation, and access to dangerous specialists.

## What It Offers

employment, expedition partnership, practical training, emergency rescue, and a route from street work to deep sites.

## What It Demands

shares, rescue obligations, crew autonomy, and honest disclosure of known hazards.

## Internal Fracture

mutual-aid profession versus celebrity captains and patron-owned crews.

## Rivals

Licensed Finders, Credit Houses, Prefecture teams, criminal salvagers, and rival companies.

## Escalation States

0. cooperative Company Table.
1. competition and poaching.
2. fatal scandal divides licensed and free crews.
3. one patron or captain dominates deep access.

## Quest Uses

- rescue a hated rival company because the Table's oath demands it.
- expose a famous captain staging supernatural discoveries.
- choose whether to publish a map that will start a ruin rush.
