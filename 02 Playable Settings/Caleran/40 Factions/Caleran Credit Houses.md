# Caleran Credit Houses

## Public Identity

the banks, factors, insurers, and exchange houses financing grain, shipping, construction, noble estates, and excavation.

## Private Truth

credit prevents collapse while contracts quietly transfer future customs, collections, and district property into creditor influence.

## Leadership

Factor Cassian Vetti speaking for several Sestaran and Thalmyric lenders.

## Resources

coin, debt records, insurers, foreign courts, informants, auctions, and the ability to make projects possible or impossible.

## What It Offers

capital, insurance, introductions, quiet settlements, information, and purchase of dangerous claims.

## What It Demands

security, repayment, privileged information, collateral, and influence over the project financed.

## Internal Fracture

ordinary profitable credit versus Under-Ledger capture and speculative antiquities finance.

## Rivals

Council, harbor labor, old houses in debt, and Church critics.

## Escalation States

0. competitive ordinary finance.
1. coordinated creditor terms.
2. public debt crisis.
3. secret predatory architecture controls essential services.

## Quest Uses

- protect a solvent debtor from a deliberately manufactured default.
- identify which loan pool contains Under-Ledger money.
- prevent a grain rescue from becoming permanent port ownership.
