# Court of Five Lamps

## Public Identity

the city's highest relic, miracle, and sacred-custody tribunal beneath the Synod.

## Private Truth

its caution prevents catastrophe and can also bury inconvenient sanctity beneath procedure and fear.

## Leadership

Bishop Kyrillos of the Five Lamps.

## Resources

authenticated experts, relic chambers, theological investigators, laboratory access, public rulings, and Church guards.

## What It Offers

authentication, safe custody, expert testimony, sanctioned access, and protection from false cult accusations.

## What It Demands

complete provenance, controlled testing, silence before ruling, and surrender of dangerous objects.

## Internal Fracture

discernment versus institutional self-protection.

## Rivals

licensed finders, private collectors, popular shrines, and Idol networks.

## Escalation States

0. trusted cautious tribunal.
1. backlogged and politically pressured.
2. public confidence breaks.
3. captured court authenticates power rather than truth.

## Quest Uses

- test a relic while three factions attempt to contaminate the evidence.
- locate a missing examiner before a public ruling.
- expose a false negative issued to protect a powerful house.
