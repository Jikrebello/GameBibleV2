# Imperial Prefecture of Caleran

## Public Identity

the Crown's civil, security, military, and state-antiquities authority in the sacred old capital.

## Private Truth

the Prefecture is genuinely necessary and permanently suspected of reducing Caleran to a Veridun property store.

## Leadership

Prefect Helena Kallistrate.

## Resources

professional watch, garrison support, seizure powers, engineers, Crown assessors, state archives, and direct communication with Prokopia.

## What It Offers

warrants, protection, access to sealed state sites, legal immunity for approved operations, and material support.

## What It Demands

reporting, chain of custody, obedience during emergencies, and priority for strategic discoveries.

## Internal Fracture

public service versus centralizing ambition inside Helena's own officer corps.

## Rivals

Council of Old City, Patriarchal Household, old houses, and illicit finders.

## Escalation States

0. limited cooperative prefecture.
1. expanded emergency authority.
2. district resistance and selective enforcement.
3. military occupation that cannot be sustained.

## Quest Uses

- escort a state assessor into a district that considers him a thief.
- find who is forging Helena's closure orders.
- decide whether a functioning defense system belongs to Crown custody.
