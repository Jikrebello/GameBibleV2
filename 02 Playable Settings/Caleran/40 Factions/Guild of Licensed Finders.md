# Guild of Licensed Finders

## Public Identity

the chartered surveyors, excavators, assessors, shoring crews, and salvage advocates who make lawful delving possible.

## Private Truth

the Guild saves lives and routinely hides minor finds to survive institutional claim-taking.

## Leadership

Guildmistress Dara Petren.

## Resources

maps, crews, equipment, licenses, claim knowledge, ventilation expertise, and access to hundreds of cellars.

## What It Offers

work, training, equipment, salvage shares, maps, and practical authority underground.

## What It Demands

guild shares, site discipline, declaration of dangerous finds, and solidarity against predatory patrons.

## Internal Fracture

professional safety versus profitable concealment.

## Rivals

Crown and Church assessors, Broken Arch rings, private collectors, and delver companies that reject guild control.

## Escalation States

0. credible professional guild.
1. strike against claim rules.
2. split between safety and free-salvage blocs.
3. license system collapses into a ruin rush.

## Quest Uses

- rescue a crew whose declared site was deliberately misclassified.
- prove a master finder is hiding an intact complex.
- defend a safety closure against residents who will lose their homes.
