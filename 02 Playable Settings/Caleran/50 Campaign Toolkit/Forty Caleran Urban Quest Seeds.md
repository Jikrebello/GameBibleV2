# Forty Caleran Urban Quest Seeds

## Street and District Work, Levels 1-4

1. **The Wrong Procession:** two pilgrim groups hold valid permits for the same narrow route, while a third party needs the collision to become violent.
2. **Warm Grain:** a Quays warehouse heats from within; the cause is a mundane germination scheme concealing one charged crate.
3. **The Fallen Tenement:** a house settles into an ancient street with residents alive inside rooms no longer matching their doors.
4. **Water Whispers:** Cistern Ward taps carry private confessions spoken nowhere near the pipes.
5. **The Missing Guide:** a pilgrim guide vanishes after recognizing a supposedly unknown shrine.
6. **Finder's Rescue:** an illegal digger is trapped beneath a lawful site and both crews refuse the other's claim.
7. **Five Authentic Letters:** a dead courier carries contradictory orders, all with genuine seals.
8. **The Unloadable Relic:** dockworkers hear formation bells from a certified cargo and stop the harbor rather than touch it.

## Faction and Investigation Work, Levels 5-8

9. **The Beautiful Dead:** devotees of a working healing shrine begin dying in carefully staged repose.
10. **The Returning Ration:** children recover fresh military food stamped 957 AR from a sealed tunnel.
11. **The Public Falsehood:** a councillor's lie prevented a massacre and now sustains an unjust charter.
12. **The Name Valve:** an occupation water contract stops serving households after marriages change their names.
13. **The Cold Furnace:** a licensed workshop contains elemental pressure no script created.
14. **The Empty Bishop:** a missing Synod voter continues sending authenticated letters and instructions.
15. **The Borrowed Collection:** a noble gallery contains an object listed in the last Atherian palace inventory.
16. **Two Funerals Too Many:** hospital and ossuary registers differ by two bodies, but both physical counts appear correct.

## Citywide Stakes, Levels 9-12

17. **The Census Beneath Bronze:** a republican roll redefines citizenship and property across three districts.
18. **The Court Reopens:** a sealed occupation chamber summons witnesses for a trial suspended in 957.
19. **The Three-Line Threat:** someone coordinates failures across aqueduct lines to force emergency rule.
20. **The Saint Nobody Wants:** an authentic manifestation occurs through a person rejected by every useful political narrative.
21. **The Grain Position:** contaminated finance and a real shipping shortage combine into a preventable famine panic.
22. **The Living Archive:** a state command room recognizes descendants as officeholders and issues binding access.
23. **The Ward Without Records:** Caravan Ward residents disappear from civic rolls while remaining in every customary register.
24. **The Procession of One Hundred:** a guarded crowd loses one hundred people between adjacent squares.

## Imperial and Hidden Networks, Levels 13-16

25. **The Quiet Anatomy:** body procurement routes converge on an academy protected by useful medical breakthroughs.
26. **The Beneath Account:** destroying an Under-Ledger loan pool would also close hospitals and granaries.
27. **The Halo War:** competing Idol networks fight over a genuinely charitable institution.
28. **The Ash Hall Device:** an imperial defense system works and classifies modern Caleran as occupied territory.
29. **The Zanthir Archive:** evidence beneath Glass Court could justify both liberation claims and present autonomy.
30. **The Adopted Order:** an ancient succession instrument recognizes a living commander before Caedros chooses an heir.
31. **The Drowned Customs Fleet:** the Aurin exposes ships impounded in a legal and metaphysical state since occupation.
32. **The Rival Below:** Mara's abandoned possessed rival has built a community around people lost underground.

## Endgame, Levels 17-20

33. **The Ember's Custodian:** the Eternal Ember refuses every recognized authority and responds to an unknown city resident.
34. **The City Routes Left:** the Undertow opens a mass interception route through plague ossuaries.
35. **The First Caleran:** the Deep Hill City awakens a civic law older than Atheria and incompatible with modern settlement.
36. **The Seven Aqueduct Alignment:** all historic lines activate as one Atherian mechanism whose purpose is not water.
37. **The Forum Under Stars:** the sealed Star city overlays the surface during a celestial recurrence.
38. **The Names of 350,000:** an occupation engine attempts to classify the entire population into enforceable roles.
39. **The Empty Throne's City:** succession war reaches Caleran and every faction demands sacred legitimacy.
40. **The Last Custody:** simultaneous Crown, Church, civic, and living-community claims over a world-changing discovery must be resolved before the site fails.
