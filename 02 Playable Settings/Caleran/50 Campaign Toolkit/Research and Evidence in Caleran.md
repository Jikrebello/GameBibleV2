# Research and Evidence in Caleran

## Four Evidence Families

- **Archive:** registers, correspondence, maps, rulings, accounts, and provenance.
- **Witness:** direct observation, community memory, confession, and expert interpretation.
- **Material:** bodies, tools, architecture, residue, objects, damage, and environmental signs.
- **Metaphysical:** authenticated relic response, contract persistence, soul-route anomaly, Shade pattern, or operational ancient system.

No family automatically outranks the others. Archives can be forged, witnesses coerced, material staged, and supernatural phenomena imitated.

## Research Tracks

A research question has four to six findings. Every essential conclusion must be reachable through at least two routes. A failed search costs time, alerts an interested party, or yields partial context; it never makes the campaign depend on guessing the correct archive.

## Proof Thresholds

- **Personal certainty:** enough to act privately.
- **Professional finding:** enough for a guild, watch, or district decision.
- **Public ruling:** evidence that can survive adversarial hearing.
- **Sacred or imperial authority:** independent provenance and specialist verification for claims affecting doctrine, relics, state inheritance, or soul routes.

## Haunted Evidence

Psychometric impressions and haunt behavior identify leads, emotional pressures, or repeated acts. They are not direct testimony. Record who handled an object, where it was stored, which impressions recur, and which expectations the examiner brought.

## Research Consequences

Every important discovery changes at least one:

- custody of an object or site;
- legitimacy of a person or faction;
- access to a deeper archive or location;
- public memory;
- danger to witnesses;
- the policy an institution can defend.
