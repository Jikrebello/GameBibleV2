# The Ossuary Heresy - Caleran Adventure

## Adventure at a Glance

**Premise:** Mercy Hospital prepares a dockworker for burial and discovers that the body bearing his tag is not him. Sister Calista's records show several small discrepancies over recent months. Beneath the hospital, an Undertow Wrecker and a Fleshweaver procurement agent have independently penetrated the ossuary roads, discovered one another, and formed a temporary market.

**Beginning:** The party is asked to identify the exchanged body, find the missing dockworker, and determine whether the failure is isolated before the next funeral convoy enters the galleries.

**Middle:** Investigation crosses the hospital intake, working ossuary, flooded carry road, and an abandoned washing chamber. Evidence reveals two crimes with different purposes: bodies are being stolen for material, while burial rites are being altered to divert souls.

**End:** During a feast-night convoy, both operations converge. The party must protect the living porters and dead in their custody, stop a bulk Undertow rite, prevent the Fleshweaver agent from escaping with the bodies, and decide how Caleran tells harmed families the truth.

**Suggested band:** levels 1-3 or equivalent starter investigators.

## Why the Party Is Involved

Use any origin:

- Sister Calista hires licensed delvers for a narrow undercroft search.
- Ilarion Vale requests independent witnesses before Church offices begin blaming one another.
- a party member knew the missing dockworker;
- the Finders' Guild assigns a crew after masonry near the ossuary is disturbed;
- Vesna Kord asks the party to prove her salvage routes are not responsible;
- a foreign or district advocate is called because the unidentified replacement body belongs to their community.

No origin makes the party servants of the Church. The hospital needs people able to move between institutions without immediately turning the discrepancy into a factional accusation.

## The Two Operations

### Undertow Route

Davor alters one response in the burial office and washes the deceased's name through a flooded side channel before the final rite. Most attempts fail. A few create the characteristic flat hymn and absence sensed only by specialized holy witnesses. The feast convoy will give him twelve closely timed deaths and enough ritual repetition to open a durable route.

### Fleshweaver Procurement

Livia purchases bodies through corrupt institutions elsewhere. In Caleran she found it easier to replace selected remains with unclaimed dead, damaged residue shells, or bodies diverted from pauper galleries. She wants flesh, medical information, and charged tissue. She does not care where souls route and considers Davor's work dangerous superstition.

### The Market

Davor needs missing bodies to hide his altered rites. Livia needs his access and funeral schedule. Each thinks the other temporary. Their disagreement is exploitable: Davor will expose her clients to protect the route; Livia will provide physical proof of his rite to buy escape.

## Investigation Structure

The party needs three conclusions: bodies were exchanged, rites were altered, and the two crimes share access but not purpose. Each conclusion has multiple routes.

### Hospital Intake

- Mikel's tag is genuine, but the replacement body lacks his union tattoo and old hand injury.
- A porter remembers Davor insisting on handling several late arrivals personally.
- Linen counts show bodies were wrapped twice on four nights.
- A medicine-room door opens onto a service stair absent from current hospital plans.

### Receiving Ossuary

This is a working place, not an abandoned dungeon. Most rooms contain registrars, masons, mourners, niches, and ordinary residue controls.

- rattle-bones animate where exchanged bodies were temporarily hidden;
- a niche sequence was reopened using a legitimate master key;
- Sister Calista's corrected slips identify the same shift and wash station;
- one unclaimed body carries Caravan Ward burial cords removed from another person.

### Ledger Gallery

The gallery preserves movement and rite records. Davor changed no names on paper. He changed the route between recorded stations.

- timing proves selected bodies spent too long at the old washing chamber;
- hymn responses copied by junior porters omit the same syllable;
- a wet handprint recurs on dry shelves;
- Livia's payment marks appear as purchases of lampblack and anatomy linen.

### Flooded Carry Road

The road links Martyrs' Steps to an older grave route. Hazards include bad footing, corpse beetles, drowned walkers, cold water, and a repeating call using voices familiar to listeners.

The voices are not the dead. Answering them strengthens the route and raises Davor's awareness.

### Abandoned Washing Chamber

Livia stores preserved bodies behind false masonry. Davor has inscribed the water channel with altered rite responses. Evidence here establishes the two operations beyond professional doubt.

The chamber also contains Toma and Reni's families' names, used as threats rather than magical bonds. Rescuing or protecting those families can turn both porters into witnesses.

## Pressure Tracks

### Route Breach, 0-4

Increases when altered rites proceed, voices are answered, Davor gains warning, or bodies enter the washing chamber. At 4, the feast convoy can be diverted in bulk.

### Body Chain, 0-4

Measures how much evidence and material Livia can remove. It falls when exits close, bodies are identified, porters turn witness, or buyers are exposed.

### Public Alarm, 0-4

Rises through visible violence, uncontrolled accusation, interrupted funerals, or rumor. High alarm brings grieving families and faction intervention into the galleries. This is not automatically bad, but it makes discreet rescue impossible.

## Finale: The Feast Convoy

Twelve dead from several districts enter the ossuary roads during a compressed feast schedule. Davor redirects the convoy toward the washing chamber while Livia prepares carts beyond an old service exit.

The party must address four simultaneous problems:

1. living porters and mourners are caught between routes;
2. residue and drowned walkers respond to the breach;
3. Davor performs the altered office at the water;
4. Livia removes bodies and evidence.

Possible methods include disrupting the repeated response, restoring names to their proper route, closing or flooding the chamber, turning Toma and Reni, exposing the two criminals to one another, evacuating the convoy, or fighting through the residue to seize the ritual point.

Davor can be defeated physically, but killing him without correcting the rite leaves the open pattern. Livia can be captured, bought into testimony, or driven off; her client records matter more than her personal guilt alone.

## Resolutions

### Full Closure

The route is corrected, the convoy completes lawful burial, missing remains are recovered, the procurement list survives, and the coerced porters testify. This is the strongest result.

### Route Closed

The soul diversion ends, but Livia escapes with bodies or records. Caleran is safe from the immediate breach while the Fleshweaver chain remains active.

### Body Chain Broken

The missing dead are recovered and buyers exposed, but Davor or his altered office survives elsewhere in the ossuary roads.

### Quiet Repair

Authorities close the route and suppress the wider failure. Public order holds, but families receive false remains or incomplete answers. Nadezhda and Samira will later press for restitution.

### Bulk Diversion

The feast convoy is intercepted. The campaign escalates immediately from a burial crime into a citywide Undertow investigation. The dead are not irretrievable by authorial fiat, but recovery is now a major arc rather than a starter resolution.

## Disclosure and Restitution

The best aftermath does not dump unverified horror on grieving families or hide wrongdoing indefinitely. Identify remains, notify families privately with advocates present, return bodies where possible, fund new rites and compensation, protect coerced witnesses, and publish the institutional failures once immediate danger is contained.

## Encounter Register

- ordinary rattle-bones, drowned walkers, corpse beetles, and keeners;
- Davor and compromised funeral porters;
- Livia and hired removal labor;
- flooded galleries, moving convoy, unstable masonry, and the Undertow call.

No enemy requires a new creature family. The adventure's escalation comes from human coordination discovering a genuine soul-route predator.

## What Changes

- the party gains or loses influence with Church, Glass Court, finders, and affected districts;
- Caleran's burial procedures may become more transparent or more secretive;
- the Fleshweaver client list opens later urban and foreign investigations;
- Undertow activity becomes a known campaign pressure;
- surviving porters and families become recurring contacts.
