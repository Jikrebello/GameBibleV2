# Caleran Party Origins

Caleran does not require a shared employer. A group needs one shared reason to cooperate and two starting contacts drawn from its members' origins.

## Origins

| Origin | Starting access | Starting obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed or aspiring delver | Finders, Broken Arch, shallow sites | declare dangerous discoveries and answer rescue calls |
| Pilgrim or pilgrim guide | Martyrs' Steps, hospitals, sacred precinct | protect vulnerable travellers and respect restricted rites |
| Church agent or student | Synod Close, parish network, archives | submit supernatural claims to discernment |
| Civic retainer or advocate | Bronze Forum, district councils, hearings | remain publicly accountable to a constituency |
| Veteran or military household | Returning Ward, garrison, Prefecture | answer lawful emergency muster |
| Criminal or informal salvager | Broken Arch routes, fences, street intelligence | repay protection and avoid exposing the network |
| Foreign visitor or caravan member | Caravan Ward, consular protection, trade | observe customary obligations and sponsor law |
| Independent investigator | watch contacts, evidence specialists, public cases | preserve evidence and accept scrutiny |
| Artisan or student | guilds, academies, workshops, printers | protect professional reputation and masters |

## Shared Bonds

Choose one:

- survived the same collapse, riot, fire, or pilgrimage disaster;
- share a patron but not loyalty to the patron's faction;
- jointly own a disputed salvage claim;
- owe a rescue debt to one another;
- are named together in an old document none understands;
- belong to different communities trying to prevent the same public crisis.

## Starting Reach

A new party begins trusted by no major faction. It may possess one useful district contact, one professional contact, and one relationship complicated by obligation. These are access points, not command authority.
