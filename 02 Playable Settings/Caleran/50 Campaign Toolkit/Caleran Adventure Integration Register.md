# Caleran Adventure Integration Register

Existing conversions are ingredients, not a required campaign sequence.

## Direct Fit

- [[The Ossuary Heresy - Caleran Adventure]]: optional level-1 opening in Martyrs' Steps and the ossuary roads.
- [[The Ossuary Heresy]]: source-conversion record retained in the Module Conversions library.

## Adaptable Structures

- [[The Saint of the Morning Quarter]]: adapt its false healing cult into the Borrowed Halo investigation without importing its Eclessian institutional assumptions.
- [[The Gallows Court]]: place its procedural haunt in a Glass Court or Bronze Forum chamber and preserve the ruling that residue is not the routed victim.
- [[The Severin Panels]]: use only if the panels travel to a Caleran private auction; Fiorenzia remains their origin.
- [[The Horror Beneath Blackspire]]: adapt the modular hunt structure to an Atherian anchor, not its Vortharan corporate identity.
- [[The Midnight Glass]]: a suitable Old Gardens private-collection mystery if its existing regional version is not simultaneously active.

## Conversion Rule

Do not relocate an adventure merely because it is urban. It must gain something specifically Caleran: sacred custody, district consequence, layered archaeology, civic legitimacy, or the collision between living population and buried system.
