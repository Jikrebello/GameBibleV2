# Caleran Faction Ladders

These six ladders are independent campaign spines. Each contains competence, internal fracture, choice, and capstone beats. Public factions not given a separate ladder intersect at least two of these six.

## Holy Synod and Five Lamps

1. Guard witnesses during a minor relic test.
2. Expose staged evidence without humiliating sincere pilgrims.
3. Investigate a healer whose charity works but whose following is predatory.
4. Choose whether to reveal Kyrillos's old false negative.
5. Prevent Synod blocs from turning a true manifestation into succession propaganda.
6. Break the Borrowed Halo network while preserving ordinary hospital care.
7. Capstone: determine the public doctrine and custody of a city-changing sacred discovery.

## Council of the Old City

1. Solve a district service failure before a public hearing.
2. Protect a delegate from coercion without deciding their vote.
3. Build a coalition across three districts with incompatible demands.
4. Expose civic corruption tied to a service people still need.
5. Choose emergency powers during riot, flood, or pilgrimage panic.
6. Capstone: win, revise, or reject a new Caleran civic charter.

## Licensed Finders and Delver Companies

1. Certify a shallow ruin and rescue an unlicensed crew.
2. Resolve a claim among landholder, Church, Crown, and finders.
3. Trace forged site classifications causing fatal work.
4. Choose between publishing and sealing Dara's Star-complex map.
5. Hold the profession together during a ruin rush.
6. Confront Mara's possessed abandoned rival.
7. Capstone: establish who controls deep access and what duties accompany it.

## Keepers of the Aurin

1. Repair a district line amid blame and rationing.
2. Investigate information carried through water.
3. Reopen or close a line serving an illegal settlement.
4. Expose keeper-family concealment without destroying expertise.
5. Defend the aqueduct hills against political sabotage.
6. Capstone: survive a three-line failure and decide future public control of the water system.

## Glass Court and Caravan Compacts

1. Resolve a customary-law conflict protecting one endangered person.
2. Terminate a coercive contract without cutting district water.
3. Expose a purist provocation and a foreign agent exploiting it.
4. Decide whether a compromised elder faces public or community justice.
5. Prevent one community's withdrawal from collapsing the Compacts.
6. Capstone: establish whether Caleran defines belonging through ancestry, loyalty, law, or protected plurality.

## Harbor, Credit, and Broken Arch

1. Move necessary cargo through a lawful dispute.
2. Trace antiquities passing through an aid route.
3. Protect a grain rescue without conceding permanent port ownership.
4. Identify Under-Ledger capital in ordinary loans.
5. Break body traffic using Vesna's routes without destroying Broken Arch livelihoods.
6. Decide which informal markets survive a port and excavation crackdown.
7. Capstone: restructure the city's food, credit, and salvage compact before panic empowers a hostile network.
