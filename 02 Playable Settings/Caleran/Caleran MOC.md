# Caleran MOC

Caleran is the World Egg setting's great-city sandbox: the recovered sacred capital of Atheria, a functioning metropolis of 350,000 people, and a place where a campaign can rise from street work to conflicts over the city's soul routes, imperial inheritance, and survival.
