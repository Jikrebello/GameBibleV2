# Theon Vardas

## Core Concept

**First Councillor of the Old City.**

## Public Face

a witty civic reformer from an established Bronze Forum family.

## Private Truth

he genuinely wants district government strengthened and quietly relies on family patronage he publicly condemns.

## Goal

win a durable civic charter before the imperial succession gives either Crown or Church an excuse to narrow city privilege.

## Resources

Council agenda, public hearings, advocates, printers, ward delegates, and an instinct for popular mood.

## Vulnerability

his family collection contains objects removed from a public excavation during his childhood.

## Relationships

rivals Helena, depends on Dara, admires Samira, and owes Cassian money.

## Quest Use

social patron, coalition builder, compromised reformer, and citywide influence gate.
