# Caleran Recurring Cast Register

These eighteen supporting characters recur across districts and factions without requiring individual dossiers.

| Character | Role | District | Immediate pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rada Tide-Born | Saltborn ferry captain | Aurin Quays | her refugee route has been compromised |
| Brother Orest | hostel keeper | Martyrs' Steps | recognizes a missing pilgrim no record admits existed |
| Magda Flint | fire inspector | Lampwrights' Ward | political patrons want a dangerous workshop reopened |
| Tomas Varek | district watch sergeant | Broken Arch | protects tenants while taking Vesna's information |
| Eleni Sarant | junior Synod clerk | Synod Close | receives letters from an absent bishop |
| Jakov Red-Hand | veteran claimant | Returning Ward | his own founder's amnesty defeats his land claim |
| Miriam bat Tal | Jirahar scroll advocate | Caravan Ward | safeguards a prophecy fragment she believes mundane |
| Lucan d'Aurelle | Eclessian envoy | Old Gardens | seeks schism leverage through a relic dispute |
| Farid Qasren | Zanthiri physician | Glass Court | treats people both empires prefer to classify first |
| Ana Velesh | mill representative | Cistern Ward | accuses the Keepers of inventing shortages |
| Pavo Kest | young finder | Broken Arch | found a sealed room and lied about where |
| Sister Calista | ossuary registrar | Martyrs' Steps | notices the one-by-one body discrepancy |
| Doro the Reed | delta guide | Aurin Delta | hears bells from a drowned customs stair |
| Basil Arkenos | academy historian | Old Gardens | can authenticate a politically ruinous census |
| Zoe Lampson | icon painter | Lampwrights' Ward | paints the same unknown door in every commission |
| Marko Seven-Loaves | camp provisioner | Outer Crown | can feed pilgrims only by breaking allocation rules |
| Selka Vran | Company Table medic | Returning Ward | knows Mara's abandoned rival was possessed |
| Niko Pale-Ear | antiquities runner | Broken Arch | wants out before body buyers claim his debt |

## Use

Each supporting character should cross at least two faction or district stories. Promotion to a full dossier occurs only when a campaign makes that character structurally important.
