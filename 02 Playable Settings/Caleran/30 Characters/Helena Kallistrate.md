# Helena Kallistrate

## Core Concept

**Imperial Prefect of Caleran.**

## Public Face

a disciplined provincial administrator sent by Grand Chancellor Prokopia to protect Crown interests without provoking the sacred city.

## Private Truth

she believes Caleran needs stronger emergency coordination and fears that every crisis she handles successfully makes permanent centralization easier.

## Goal

keep the city governable through the succession and prevent strategic antiquities from entering private hands.

## Resources

prefectural watch, Crown assessors, sealed warrants, garrison cooperation, and direct correspondence with Veridun.

## Vulnerability

her younger brother finances illegal digs through an intermediary she has not identified.

## Relationships

respects Petru, distrusts Theon, bargains honestly with Sophron, and considers Vesna dangerous but useful.

## Quest Use

warrant patron, principled antagonist, or ally forced to choose between legality and the city.
