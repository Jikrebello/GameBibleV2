# Samira Velen

## Core Concept

**Advocate of the Glass Court Associations.**

## Public Face

a patient contract jurist representing mixed and occupation-descended communities.

## Private Truth

her grandfather enforced coercive name custody before secretly helping families escape it; both halves of the record are genuine.

## Goal

win recognition that present loyalty and lawful personhood outweigh inherited accusation.

## Resources

community trust, multilingual archives, customary courts, safe houses, and formidable legal memory.

## Vulnerability

purists and Covenant agents each possess only the half of her family history useful to them.

## Relationships

allied with Theon, respected by Thaleon, watched by Helena, and opposed by return claimant lobbies.

## Quest Use

community patron, guide to living occupation history, and central figure in belonging conflicts.
