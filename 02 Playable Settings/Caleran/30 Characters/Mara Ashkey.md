# Mara Ashkey

## Core Concept

**Delver-captain and elected speaker of the Company Table.**

## Public Face

a Broken Arch native famous for bringing every living crew member home from the Red Cistern collapse.

## Private Truth

she left a trapped rival behind after he was already possessed and has allowed the story to call it an unavoidable cave-in.

## Goal

make company rescue obligations enforceable and keep patrons from owning crews through equipment debt.

## Resources

veteran delvers, monster knowledge, public reputation, hidden routes, and the loyalty of young crews.

## Vulnerability

the abandoned rival may still be active beneath the city.

## Relationships

ally of Dara, enemy of Vesna, frequent contractor for Helena, and reluctant subject of popular songs.

## Quest Use

expedition patron, field companion, faction leader, and delayed possession consequence.
