# Nadezhda of the Quiet Steps

## Core Concept

**Abbess, hospital patron, and contemplative adviser to Sophron.**

## Public Face

a beloved monastic superior known for refusing wealthy patrons who demand public gratitude.

## Private Truth

she has identified the Borrowed Halo network but delays accusation because its compromised hospitals still keep thousands alive.

## Goal

separate genuine charity from predatory devotion without collapsing care for the poor.

## Resources

monasteries, nurses, confessors, hospital records, contemplative authority, and Sophron's trust.

## Vulnerability

every day of delay feeds the Idol operation; a premature strike closes beds and kills ordinary patients.

## Relationships

trusted by Sophron, tested by Kyrillos, watched by hostile donors, and quietly assisted by Samira.

## Quest Use

moral anchor, hospital patron, source of high-stakes discernment, and leader of anti-Idol investigation.
