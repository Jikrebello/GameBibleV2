# Thaleon Sar

## Core Concept

**Harbor Marshal and speaker for the Aurin Harbor Compact.**

## Public Face

a Saltborn-descended navigator who can settle a dock fight faster than customs can record it.

## Private Truth

he permits a controlled smuggling route used for refugee medicine, and the same route now carries antiquities and bodies without his consent.

## Goal

keep food moving and prevent merchants, customs, or criminals from breaking dockworker solidarity.

## Resources

pilots, ferries, warehouse access, dock intelligence, union pressure, and sea contacts.

## Vulnerability

closing his route condemns people who depend on it; leaving it open feeds worse traffic.

## Relationships

rivals Cassian, knows Vesna's methods, protects Rada Tide-Born, and respects Samira.

## Quest Use

harbor patron, transport gate, labor ally, and morally constrained smuggling investigation.
