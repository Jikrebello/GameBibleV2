# Dara Petren

## Core Concept

**Guildmistress of Licensed Finders.**

## Public Face

a former shoring master who turned a fatal collapse into the city's strongest excavation safety code.

## Private Truth

she conceals a map to a sealed Star complex because every lawful claimant would open it too quickly.

## Goal

keep delving a profession rather than a patron's disposable labor market.

## Resources

guild crews, maps, tools, claim advocates, district contacts, and rescue obligations.

## Vulnerability

survivors of the collapse believe she chose who was rescued first for political reasons.

## Relationships

works with Mara, fights Vesna, bargains with Helena, and trusts Petru's engineering judgement.

## Quest Use

delver mentor, work source, custodian of forbidden access, or institutional reform ally.
