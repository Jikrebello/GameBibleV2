# Petru Voda

## Core Concept

**Water-Master of the Keepers of the Aurin.**

## Public Face

an unadorned engineer whose refusal can halt a Synod procession or military project.

## Private Truth

he reopened one ancient line without permission to save Cistern Ward during a drought and something has listened through it ever since.

## Goal

secure public funding and train enough outsiders that keeper-family deaths cannot end essential knowledge.

## Resources

water maps, valve keys, crews, pressure authority, district trust, and access beneath most of Caleran.

## Vulnerability

the illegal reopened line could justify Prefecture seizure of the whole system.

## Relationships

trusted by Helena and Dara, challenged by younger keepers, and disliked by mill owners.

## Quest Use

infrastructure patron, practical conscience, and gateway to non-combat undercity crises.
