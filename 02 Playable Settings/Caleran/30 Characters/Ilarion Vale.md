# Ilarion Vale

## Core Concept

**Deacon-investigator and specialist in haunted evidence.**

## Public Face

a quiet examiner attached to the Court of Five Lamps who teaches that impressions are evidence, not testimony.

## Private Truth

he experiences psychometric impressions more strongly than he admits and cannot always distinguish an object's residue from his own grief.

## Goal

create reliable occult investigative practice before charlatans and frightened judges discredit the field.

## Resources

Church access, evidence protocols, a small laboratory, trained assistants, and Kyrillos's reluctant protection.

## Vulnerability

an old family object produces a recurring impression that contradicts accepted history.

## Relationships

student of Kyrillos, covert ally of Cassian, respectful skeptic of Nadezhda, and professional irritant to every collector.

## Quest Use

occult mentor, unreliable-but-honest witness, research patron, or investigator in need of grounding.
