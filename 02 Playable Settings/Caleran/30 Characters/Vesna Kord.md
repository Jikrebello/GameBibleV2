# Vesna Kord

## Core Concept

**Antiquities broker of Broken Arch.**

## Public Face

a secondhand dealer and neighborhood benefactor who can find materials official channels abandoned.

## Private Truth

she runs the city's strongest illicit salvage network and draws a firm line against body traffic that her subordinates have begun crossing.

## Goal

control the illegal market, keep Broken Arch housed, and destroy the buyer turning salvage routes into cadaver routes.

## Resources

dig crews, fences, landlords, hidden cellars, foreign buyers, bribes, and neighborhood dependence.

## Vulnerability

her wealth rests on unsafe extraction and the buyer has evidence linking her routes to missing bodies.

## Relationships

enemy of Dara, informant to Thaleon, uneasy protector of Broken Arch, and target of Ilarion.

## Quest Use

criminal patron, antagonist with boundaries, black-market access, and route into Fleshweaver plots.
