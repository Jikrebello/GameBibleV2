# Cassian Vetti

## Core Concept

**Sestaran factor and spokesman for the Caleran Credit Houses.**

## Public Face

a cultivated lender who specializes in financing useful projects everyone else calls impossible.

## Private Truth

he is trying to identify Under-Ledger capital inside his own pooled loans before it consumes the house that trusted him.

## Goal

keep ordinary credit legitimate, profitable, and indispensable while quietly severing predatory positions.

## Resources

money, insurers, auctions, foreign contacts, debt records, and invitations into elite homes.

## Vulnerability

revealing the contaminated loans could trigger the defaults and panic he is trying to prevent.

## Relationships

creditor to Theon, rival to Thaleon, useful to Helena, and covertly dependent on Ilarion.

## Quest Use

ambiguous financier, intelligence patron, heist target, or ally against predatory capital.
