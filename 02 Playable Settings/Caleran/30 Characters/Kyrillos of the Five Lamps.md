# Kyrillos of the Five Lamps

## Core Concept

**Bishop and chief judge of the Court of Five Lamps.**

## Public Face

a severe but respected examiner who has rejected miracles desired by both court and mob.

## Private Truth

he once issued a false negative to prevent a relic from becoming the cause of a civil conflict and still does not know whether he was right.

## Goal

keep authentication credible while preventing sanctity from becoming political ammunition.

## Resources

relic laboratories, trained examiners, guarded vaults, doctrinal standing, and access to Sophron.

## Vulnerability

the suppressed object has resurfaced and remembers his touch.

## Relationships

mentor to Ilarion, wary of Nadezhda, hostile to false healers, and perpetually at odds with finders.

## Quest Use

investigation authority, moral pressure, source of difficult tests, or target of exposure.
