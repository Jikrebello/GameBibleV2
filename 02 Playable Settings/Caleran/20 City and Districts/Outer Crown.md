# Outer Crown

Outer Crown is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 28,000
- **Primary authority:** gate captains, suburban councils, monastery estates, cemetery wardens, market officers, and nearby military forts.
- **Economy:** livestock, wholesale produce, carts, kilns, cemeteries, caravan staging, pilgrim camps, and heavy craft.
- **Visual identity:** walls and gatehouses above sprawling markets, tent fields, grave roads, brick kilns, orchards, workshops, and irregular suburbs.
- **Dominant historical layer:** Reconquest siege belt and later expansion over camps, cemeteries, and abandoned villas.
- **Undercity access:** grave roads, siege mines, villa basements, and aqueduct inspection houses lead toward every undercity family.

## Living Conflict

the district absorbs people and hazards excluded from the inner city without receiving equal services or representation.

## Quest Hooks

- A pilgrim camp elects its own magistrate after the city refuses water and protection.
- An old siege mine collapses beneath a cemetery and exposes soldiers buried by both armies.
- A return village inside the suburban belt refuses annexation after discovering its refuge charter.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
