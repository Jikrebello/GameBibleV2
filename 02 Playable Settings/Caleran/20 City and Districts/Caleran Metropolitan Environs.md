# Caleran Metropolitan Environs

The Caleran sandbox extends one to two days beyond the walls without becoming a separate region.

## Aurin Delta

Ferries, fisheries, reed islands, warehouses, quarantine stations, monasteries, and old harbor defenses occupy a changing river landscape. Flooding exposes and buries ruins faster than formal archaeology can follow.

## Seven Aqueduct Hills

Four full aqueducts, two partial lines, and one dead line descend from guarded catchments. Inspection houses, maintenance villages, old estates, and sabotage points make the hills strategically indispensable.

## Old Siege Belt

Reconquest camps, buried engines, mass graves, forts, and abandoned approach trenches ring the old walls. Much of the modern Outer Crown stands over this belt.

## Metropolitan Necropolis

Formal cemeteries, martyr grounds, plague fields, military graves, and family mausoleums line several grave roads. The dead usually remain quiet because thousands of people work to keep them so.

## Abandoned Villas and Return Villages

Estates and villages survive in every condition from prosperous orchard community to roofless legal fiction. Their claimants include old houses, occupation-descended families, monasteries, veteran lines, tenants, and the Crown.

## Travel

- city gate to aqueduct inspection house: half a day;
- city gate to outer delta islands: two to six hours by boat;
- full circuit of the old siege belt: two days on foot;
- nearest return villages: one to four hours;
- farthest metropolitan site: two days with a cart.

## Quest Uses

- waterline defense and engineering expeditions;
- grave-road disappearances and residue control;
- villa excavations tied to living inheritance;
- river quarantine, smuggling, and drowned infrastructure;
- village resettlement where safety, title, and memory disagree.
