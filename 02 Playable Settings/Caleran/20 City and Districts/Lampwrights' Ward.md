# Lampwrights' Ward

Lampwrights' Ward is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 31,000
- **Primary authority:** craft guilds, fire inspectors, icon workshops, licensed rune supervisors, and parish councils.
- **Economy:** metalwork, icons, lamps, printing, glass, relic containers, instruments, and controlled rune manufacture.
- **Visual identity:** bright shopfronts, soot-dark upper floors, tiled furnaces, hanging signs, public water barrels, and workshops built into old baths.
- **Dominant historical layer:** imperial artisan quarter continually occupied through conversion, occupation, and return.
- **Undercity access:** kiln drains and bath service corridors connect to workshop vaults and disused heat channels.

## Living Conflict

innovation supports the city but every workshop fire or illicit script renews demands for Church or Crown control.

## Quest Hooks

- A printer produces accurate pages from a plate no one engraved.
- A licensed warlock disappears after reporting elemental pressure beneath a Church-owned workshop.
- Icons from five unrelated studios begin depicting the same unknown doorway behind their saints.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
