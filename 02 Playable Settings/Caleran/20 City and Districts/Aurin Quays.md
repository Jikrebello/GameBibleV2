# Aurin Quays

Aurin Quays is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 42,000
- **Primary authority:** the Harbor Compact, imperial customs, the Sea-Marshal's officers, dock guilds, and Saltborn ward captains.
- **Economy:** grain, fish, oil, wine, timber, passengers, ship repair, warehousing, customs, and smuggling.
- **Visual identity:** stone quays on two banks, mast forests, grain towers, salt-white warehouses, ferries, shrines to drowned saints, and crowded waterside tenements.
- **Dominant historical layer:** successive republican ports, imperial granaries, occupation customs houses, and Reconquest naval yards.
- **Undercity access:** flooded culverts enter old harbor magazines and drowned river shrines.

## Living Conflict

food security requires fast trade while customs, creditors, smugglers, and sacred cargo rules slow or redirect it.

## Quest Hooks

- A grain ship arrives with a correct manifest, an absent crew, and cargo warm enough to germinate in winter.
- Dockworkers refuse to unload a relic convoy after hearing bells from sealed crates.
- A newly exposed river stair leads to an occupation warehouse whose inventory still considers several living families cargo.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
