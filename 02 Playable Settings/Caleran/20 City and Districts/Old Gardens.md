# Old Gardens

Old Gardens is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 12,000
- **Primary authority:** old houses, academy governors, diplomatic immunities, private guards, and the civic council.
- **Economy:** landed wealth, education, salons, diplomacy, private collections, luxury craft, and patronage.
- **Visual identity:** walled gardens, cypress avenues, restored villas, private chapels, small academies, and fragments of imperial sculpture used as landscape ornament.
- **Dominant historical layer:** senatorial estates and imperial villas repeatedly divided, confiscated, restored, and rebuilt.
- **Undercity access:** family crypts, private museums, sealed garden temples, and villa service tunnels.

## Living Conflict

elite collections preserve priceless material while removing it from public scrutiny and lawful claim.

## Quest Hooks

- A salon guest is murdered during a debate in which every attendee used a false social identity.
- A private statue garden is actually an intact Star alignment device activated by seasonal pruning.
- An old house offers the party access to a sealed villa if they first discredit the family legally entitled to it.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
