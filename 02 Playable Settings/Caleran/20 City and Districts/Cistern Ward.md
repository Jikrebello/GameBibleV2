# Cistern Ward

Cistern Ward is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 38,000
- **Primary authority:** the Keepers of the Aurin, mill guilds, bathhouse councils, district watch, and civic sanitation officers.
- **Economy:** water distribution, milling, brewing, washing, pottery, food processing, and repair labor.
- **Visual identity:** pressure towers, public fountains, bath courtyards, mill races, damp stone housing, and painted water-level marks.
- **Dominant historical layer:** republican and imperial water infrastructure maintained through every later regime.
- **Undercity access:** the largest supervised entrances to the Living Waterworks and numerous illegal service hatches.

## Living Conflict

the people who keep Caleran alive have less formal prestige than institutions that can overrule their engineering decisions.

## Quest Hooks

- A water line carries whispered confessions into homes without contamination or visible magic.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
