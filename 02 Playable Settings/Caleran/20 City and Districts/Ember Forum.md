# Ember Forum

Ember Forum is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 12,000
- **Primary authority:** the Cathedral chapter, the Court of Five Lamps, and civic procession officers.
- **Economy:** pilgrimage offerings, sacred hospitality, certified relic services, books, candles, and ceremonial craft.
- **Visual identity:** broad stone stairs, converted forums, bronze screens, whitewashed hospices, and the Cathedral rising around the still-visible Pillar of Concordance.
- **Dominant historical layer:** Atherian civic and execution forum converted into the supreme sacred precinct.
- **Undercity access:** sealed martyr stairs beneath the Pillar precinct and supervised cathedral ossuary lifts.

## Living Conflict

Sophron's restraint clashes with preachers and patrons who want public miracles and a harder Zanthir message.

## Quest Hooks

- A credible miracle occurs during a politically useful sermon, and every authority wants custody of the witnesses.
- A paving collapse exposes an execution trench containing victims from two mutually hostile historical periods.
- A pilgrim procession loses one hundred people between two guarded squares without panic or visible violence.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
