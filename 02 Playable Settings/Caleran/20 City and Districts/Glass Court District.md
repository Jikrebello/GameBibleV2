# Glass Court District

Glass Court District is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 25,000
- **Primary authority:** district associations, customary Velian judges, imperial observers, parish clergy, and the keepers of sealed occupation sites.
- **Economy:** translation, dyeing, ceramics, contract interpretation, medicine, food trade, and specialist restoration.
- **Visual identity:** glazed courtyards, shaded arcades, mixed church forms, water gardens, contract stones turned into benches, and heavily inhabited old buildings.
- **Dominant historical layer:** the best-preserved occupation administrative district, continuously inhabited before and after 957.
- **Undercity access:** guarded contract courts, name vault approaches, and water channels whose obligations were never fully terminated.

## Living Conflict

residents defend lawful protections and living heritage against purists who treat every occupation survival as collaboration.

## Quest Hooks

- A protective water contract begins excluding residents whose family names changed after marriage.
- Purists stage evidence of patron worship inside a community archive containing records that would defeat their land claims.
- A sealed court requests a lawful witness for a case suspended since the night of reconquest.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
