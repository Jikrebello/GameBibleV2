# Ash Hall District

Ash Hall District is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 10,000
- **Primary authority:** the imperial prefect, Crown assessors, palace custodians, and old service households.
- **Economy:** state administration, antiquities assessment, court supply, diplomatic hospitality, and controlled access to imperial collections.
- **Visual identity:** blackened palace walls, formal courtyards, guarded galleries, old triumphal reliefs, and Reconquest offices inserted into monumental halls.
- **Dominant historical layer:** the last Atherian palace and Corvenas III's relic gallery, occupied, fortified, and reused by return governments.
- **Undercity access:** palace service tunnels reach sealed treasury corridors and an unverified imperial command chamber.

## Quest Hooks

- An object listed in Corvenas III's final inventory appears in a private Old Gardens collection.
- A state archive opens only for a clerk descended from an occupation administrator whom the Crown refuses to employ.
- Renovation uncovers a command room that still issues harmless but legally unsettling orders to dead provinces.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
