# Bronze Forum

Bronze Forum is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 22,000
- **Primary authority:** the Council of the Old City, civic magistrates, advocates, guild delegations, and public notaries.
- **Economy:** courts, petitions, wholesale exchange, professional services, printing, public auctions, and civic contracts.
- **Visual identity:** arcaded markets, bronze statues with repeatedly altered inscriptions, packed court steps, speaker balconies, and notice walls.
- **Dominant historical layer:** republican assembly ground beneath imperial civic buildings and modern municipal chambers.
- **Undercity access:** old vote tunnels, record vaults, and a buried republican drainage court.

## Living Conflict

the Council wants meaningful self-government while Crown and Church treat it as a service office when convenient.

## Quest Hooks

- A buried voting roll proves that a modern district charter was granted to the wrong successor community.
- A popular councillor is blackmailed with a genuine crime committed to prevent a massacre.
- Three public statues change their inscriptions overnight to accuse living families of the same ancient betrayal.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
