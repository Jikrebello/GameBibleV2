# Caravan Ward

Caravan Ward is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 27,000
- **Primary authority:** customary community councils, imperial foreign officers, caravan guilds, consular houses, and city magistrates.
- **Economy:** long-distance trade, translation, inns, animal markets, banking, courier work, and imported food.
- **Visual identity:** large courtyard inns, multilingual signs, covered markets, prayer houses, stables, consular compounds, and warehouses built along the River Gate road.
- **Dominant historical layer:** imperial foreign quarter expanded under occupation and retained through return because the city could not function without it.
- **Undercity access:** merchant vaults connect to courier tunnels, occupation customs chambers, and a disused eastern necropolis road.

## Living Conflict

customary protections shelter vulnerable visitors and occasionally shield criminals, spies, or traffickers.

## Quest Hooks

- Two customary courts issue opposite lawful judgements over the same missing child.
- A courier dies carrying five sealed letters, each authentic and addressed to a different version of the same official.
- Foreign residents vanish from civic records while remaining present in their own community rolls.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
