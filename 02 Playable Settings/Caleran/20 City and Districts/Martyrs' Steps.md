# Martyrs' Steps

Martyrs' Steps is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 25,000
- **Primary authority:** hospital houses, shrine chapters, hostel guilds, funeral confraternities, beggar wardens, and city watch.
- **Economy:** pilgrim lodging, medicine, food, burial work, candles, guides, alms, and devotional goods.
- **Visual identity:** steep processional streets, crowded hostels, courtyard hospitals, roadside icons, funeral kitchens, and rooftops filled with drying linens.
- **Dominant historical layer:** execution roads and early cemeteries overbuilt by hospitals, shrines, and pilgrimage infrastructure.
- **Undercity access:** martyr crypts, plague ossuaries, hospice burial lifts, and old roadside culverts.

## Living Conflict

real charity, relic commerce, desperate fraud, and predatory devotion operate within a few streets of one another.

## Quest Hooks

- A hostel's guests share the same dream and wake with memories belonging to a dead pilgrim.
- A beggar recognized as a holy fool is being kept ill because donations depend on his condition.
- The funeral guild's body counts diverge from the hospital registers by one or two each month.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
