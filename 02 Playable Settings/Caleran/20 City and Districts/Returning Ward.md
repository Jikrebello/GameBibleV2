# Returning Ward

Returning Ward is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 28,000
- **Primary authority:** veteran assemblies, military household officers, memorial brotherhoods, and the imperial prefect.
- **Economy:** military supply, pensions, horse and leather work, lodging, arms repair, and claimant advocacy.
- **Visual identity:** straight Reconquest streets, regimental lamps, reused barracks, memorial walls, kitchen gardens, and houses marked by returned-province names.
- **Dominant historical layer:** occupation housing cleared and resettled by return armies, then expanded around veteran grants.
- **Undercity access:** siege tunnels, supply vaults, and sealed execution pits from both occupation and return.

## Living Conflict

veteran service claims collide with occupation-descended title, later tenants, and a government unable to satisfy every promised grant.

## Quest Hooks

- A veteran house receives an order to surrender property based on the founder's own forgotten amnesty oath.
- A memorial wall adds the names of soldiers who are alive and stationed nearby.
- Children enter a sealed supply tunnel and return carrying fresh rations stamped 957 AR.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
