# Synod Close

Synod Close is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 15,000
- **Primary authority:** the Holy Synod, seminary rectors, monastery representatives, and the Patriarchal household.
- **Economy:** clerical administration, education, manuscript copying, hospitality, legal advocacy, and ecclesiastical patronage.
- **Visual identity:** quiet courts, cypress walks, tiled libraries, narrow bridges between archives, and guarded doors without signs.
- **Dominant historical layer:** late converted-imperial schools and noble houses rebuilt as the refuge Church's administrative quarter.
- **Undercity access:** archive basements link to converted temple schools and a closed observation gallery.

## Living Conflict

doctrinal administrators, contemplatives, provincial bishops, and frontier militants are assembling blocs around succession and war.

## Quest Hooks

- A provincial bishop vanishes before a decisive vote but continues sending authenticated letters.
- Students uncover that a required theology text contains occupation-era marginal instructions addressed to future readers.
- A foreign delegation offers evidence of a false saint in exchange for a doctrinal concession unrelated to the case.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
