# Caleran Anchor Location Register

These seventy locations are the first recurring stage set for Caleran. Promote a location to its own dossier only when a selected adventure requires room-level detail.

## Ember Forum

- **Cathedral of the Eternal Ember:** Patriarchal seat built through converted imperial halls around the Ember's guarded precinct.
- **Pillar of Concordance:** the Redeemer's execution site and Caedros I's death-place, open only under layered sacred security.
- **Court of Five Lamps:** tribunal chambers, testing chapels, laboratories, and sealed relic stores.
- **House of Pilgrim Measures:** assigns hostels, routes processions, tracks capacity, and mediates pilgrim disputes.
- **Impounded Ledger Gallery:** displays occupation custody records, including the meticulous account of the Ember.

## Synod Close

- **Hall of the Holy Synod:** council chamber where absence, seating, and procession order carry political meaning.
- **Seminary of the Standing Hymn:** clergy school divided between contemplative formation and ambitious doctrinal careers.
- **Archive of Provincial Letters:** reports from every bishopric, including sealed files on miracles, failures, and abuses.
- **House of the Unsleeping:** Sophron's austere residence, audience court, chapel, and small private garden.
- **Three Cypress Guesthouse:** neutral lodging for visiting bishops and a natural intelligence exchange.

## Ash Hall

- **Ash Hall:** Corvenas III's palace and relic gallery, now state museum, working office, and sealed ruin.
- **Prefect's Court:** Helena's public hearing room and operational center for Crown authority.
- **Crown Assay House:** identifies imperial arms, strategic mechanisms, minting material, and state antiquities.
- **Gallery of Returned Standards:** stores Reconquest banners and receives contested new claims.
- **Closed West Wing:** sound palace rooms sealed because inventories, doors, and internal dimensions disagree.

## Bronze Forum

- **Council House:** municipal chamber where district blocs, guilds, and old civic privilege collide.
- **Open Petition Court:** outdoor hearings where publicity can enforce a judgement or destroy a reputation.
- **Bronze Exchange:** wholesale goods, public auctions, salvage sales, and municipal price reporting.
- **Hall of Altered Names:** statues and tablets showing successive regimes rewriting public memory.
- **Advocates' Arcade:** honest counsel, witnesses, printers, and expert manipulators under one colonnade.

## Aurin Quays

- **Great Grain Stairs:** primary staple-grain landing and the first visible measure of food security.
- **Hall of Tides and Measures:** Harbor Compact council, customs arbitration, pilot licensing, and labor negotiation.
- **Naris Lantern:** drowned-saint shrine and navigation light maintained by Saltborn families.
- **Salt Bridge Market:** food, sailors' goods, day labor, rumors, and the city's fastest illicit exchange.
- **Sunken Customs House:** occupation warehouse reachable at low water with cargo still classified as impounded.

## Lampwrights' Ward

- **Guildhall of Lamps and Lines:** shared chamber for metalworkers, instrument builders, and rune supervisors.
- **Icon Court:** studios, material sellers, apprentice housing, and review of public commissions.
- **Red Kiln Market:** ceramics, glass, furnace parts, alchemical vessels, and fire-damaged goods.
- **Printers' Gallery:** theology, civic argument, cheap devotion, gossip, and prohibited copies.
- **Cold Furnace:** imperial heat chamber that cannot be lit and sometimes produces elemental pressure while cold.

## Returning Ward

- **Hall of Returning Standards:** veteran assembly, grant archive, memorial court, and mobilization site.
- **Widows' Wool Court:** cooperative market funding pensions, hospitals, orphans, and political lobbying.
- **Caedros Gate Barracks:** active garrison built into the return army's first secure city gate.
- **Forty Tables:** kitchens and meeting halls named for units lost during the recovery.
- **Field of Unclaimed Names:** memorial ground for dead whose family, allegiance, or burial remains disputed.

## Glass Court

- **Court of Common Names:** customary tribunal for marriage, inheritance, translated identity, and terminated obligations.
- **House of Two Liturgies:** church preserving Velian practice under uneasy Patriarchal toleration.
- **Glass Garden Market:** dyes, ceramics, medicine, water foods, translation, and neighborhood news.
- **Termination Office:** dismantles occupation contracts without destroying attached infrastructure.
- **Qabir Gate Chapel:** mixed-family chapel repeatedly accused of foreign loyalty and repeatedly cleared.

## Martyrs' Steps

- **Mercy Hospital:** vast charitable hospital whose intake, burial, and teaching touch every class.
- **Hundred-Bed Hostel:** name retained from its founding despite now sleeping nearly a thousand pilgrims.
- **Steps of Jareth:** processional stair serving scribes, advocates, the blind, and accused forgers.
- **Market of Small Reliquaries:** cases, keepsakes, fraudulent fragments, and legitimate low-order relic trade.
- **Quiet Kitchen:** funeral and alms kitchen accepting neither payment nor public donor names.

## Old Gardens

- **Academy of the Twelve Roads:** history, law, engineering, rhetoric, and astronomy school.
- **Garden of the Last Senator:** public garden around a contested republican tomb and misidentified statues.
- **House Vardas Salon:** civic and elite meeting place where reform is discussed by people insulated from its cost.
- **Embassy Crescent:** foreign residences, diplomatic immunity, servants, hospitality, and surveillance.
- **Unopened Villa:** legally inherited by six families and physically sealed since an occupation banquet.

## Cistern Ward

- **Seven-Valve House:** coordinates aqueduct lines and issues emergency water orders.
- **Great Settling Court:** reservoirs and filtration beds becoming a public square during maintenance.
- **Common Baths of Velos:** affordable baths, clinic, hiring ground, and political forum.
- **Millers' Reach:** channel-side mills whose owners can affect bread prices within a day.
- **Dry Mouth:** sealed seventh-aqueduct entrance surrounded by warnings and unauthorized offerings.

## Broken Arch

- **The Broken Arch:** half an imperial gate holding tenements, shrines, scaffolds, and a warning bell.
- **Rope Market:** salvage tools, household goods, labor hiring, stolen material, and rescue supply.
- **Saint Nadezhda Parish:** parish, tenant refuge, school, and organizer against unsafe demolition.
- **Ashkey Yard:** delver compound with training wall, infirmary, stores, and memorial boards.
- **Night Quarry:** illegal after-dark extraction whose stone supports much of the ward's repair.

## Caravan Ward

- **Hall of Compacts:** chamber where customary courts negotiate jurisdiction and safe conduct.
- **River Gate Caravanserai:** lodging, stable, warehouse, and hiring complex for inland arrivals.
- **Jirahar Scroll Court:** library, legal school, worship space, and secure Keeper archive.
- **Sestaran Exchange House:** currency, insurance, shipping letters, auction notices, and financial intelligence.
- **Velian Pilgrim Chapel:** lodging and worship for believers pressured by both great churches.

## Outer Crown

- **Four Gates Market:** livestock, produce, carts, bulk fuel, rural hiring, and low-toll trade.
- **Camp of the Rekindling:** service ground transformed into a tent city during the great feast.
- **Metropolitan Necropolis:** cemetery administration over grave roads, plague fields, and military belts.
- **Cartwright Field:** heavy workshops, yards, animal care, and freight assembly.
- **Saint Ioan Outer Fort:** garrison, road station, refuge house, and final secure aqueduct-hill post.
