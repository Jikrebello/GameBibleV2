# Broken Arch

Broken Arch is one of Caleran's fourteen playable surface districts.

## District Profile

- **Permanent population:** 35,000
- **Primary authority:** weak district magistrates, tenant associations, informal salvage bosses, parish networks, and irregular watch patrols.
- **Economy:** construction labor, quarrying, secondhand goods, illicit salvage, lodging, street markets, and repair trades.
- **Visual identity:** crowded tenements against broken monumental masonry, scaffold streets, quarry pits, patchwork roofs, and cellar doors marked with private warning signs.
- **Dominant historical layer:** collapsed imperial gate district damaged in 905, rebuilt poorly, and repeatedly undermined by quarrying.
- **Undercity access:** hundreds of cellars reach unstable mixed strata; few are surveyed or mutually connected.

## Living Conflict

residents need housing and work while institutions condemn the dangerous salvage economy that makes both possible.

## Quest Hooks

- A tenement settles intact into an older street, trapping residents in rooms aligned with ancient houses.
- A salvage crew finds only ordinary coins, but their stamped dates would overturn a noble genealogy.
- A local boss suppresses news of a genuine dungeon because evacuation would make thousands homeless.

## Use in Play

Routine scenes should establish work, worship, food, gossip, travel, and class before a hidden layer intrudes. District consequences change access, prices, public cooperation, watch attention, and faction influence.
