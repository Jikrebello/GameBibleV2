# Caleran Level 1-20 Progression

This progression describes changes in responsibility and consequence, not a mandatory plot.

## Levels 1-4: A Name on One Street

The party handles missing people, pilgrim trouble, guild errands, shallow residue, illegal digs, neighborhood crimes, and minor faction work. Success creates district contacts and the first institutional access. Failure should damage a relationship or place rather than end the campaign.

## Levels 5-8: A Name Across Several Districts

Faction ladders open. The party investigates public scandals, relic disputes, heists, murders, water failures, and intermediate underworks. Its decisions can replace district leaders, close a shrine, expose a noble, or give one faction a durable advantage.

## Levels 9-12: A Name the City Must Answer

The party enters Synod politics, sealed occupation systems, great archives, and citywide conspiracies. Discoveries can alter doctrine, property orders, diplomatic relations, or the legal status of a community. Violence draws institutional and foreign responses.

## Levels 13-16: A Name Beyond the Walls

Caleran's problems connect to the imperial succession, Zanthir, Sestaran finance, Left Hand supply chains, and deep Atherian systems. The party can change who governs major institutions but cannot do so without creating enemies and displaced dependents.

## Levels 17-20: A Name in the City's History

Threats concern the Eternal Ember, Caleran's soul routes, an operational Atherian network, mass possession, or the physical survival of the water and burial systems. Endgame choices determine what Caleran becomes, not merely whether a final enemy dies.

## Escalation Rule

Higher-tier play should reopen earlier relationships. A street contact becomes a district leader; a rescued novice becomes a Synod witness; an ignored water dispute becomes a city crisis. Scale grows through accumulated consequence rather than replacing the city with unrelated cosmic stages.
