# Caleran Great-City Dossier

## Playable Thesis

Caleran is a city where history is inhabited rather than displayed. People pray in converted forums, rent rooms in Reconquest barracks, draw water through republican masonry, litigate occupation-era contracts, and discover old cities when a cellar wall gives way. Its central tension is not old versus new, but **which inheritance is allowed to govern the living**.

Intrigue leads play. Archaeology, occult investigation, crime, and dungeon exploration create evidence and leverage that change public relationships. A ruin is rarely isolated from the property above it, the institution claiming it, the district endangered by it, or the history it can disprove.

## What Makes Caleran Distinct

- **Sacred without being the imperial court:** the Patriarch governs from Caleran; the emperor governs from Veridun.
- **Ancient without being a ruin:** most Atherian structures have been repaired, converted, quarried, occupied, or built over.
- **Supernatural without casual magic:** ordinary citizens know procedures, warnings, and institutions more often than they witness miracles.
- **Politically plural:** Church, Crown, city, guild, district, family, and customary foreign law overlap without one authority fully replacing the others.
- **Vertically playable:** every district has access to different historical strata, but the undercity is an irregular web rather than a clean stack.

## Campaign Promise

A party can begin as unknown troubleshooters, pilgrims, delvers, students, retainers, foreigners, criminals, veterans, or investigators. It can remain within Caleran and its metropolitan belt through a complete career:

- local work establishes contacts and district reputation;
- faction service grants access while creating obligations;
- discoveries alter city policy and institutional alliances;
- deep sites reveal truths with imperial or cosmological consequences;
- high-level play determines whether Caleran's sacred and material systems survive intact.

No employer, opening quest, faction victory, or campaign ending is mandatory.

## Three-Layer Adventure Rule

Every major Caleran adventure should pressure:

1. **a living constituency** that will bear the immediate cost;
2. **an authority conflict** over who may decide or profit;
3. **a historical or metaphysical truth** that cannot be safely reduced to treasure.

Combat can solve immediate danger. It cannot by itself settle legitimacy, custody, public memory, or trust.

## Restraint Rules

- Common finds are coins, tiles, bones, broken tools, masonry, and rusted weapons.
- A functioning Atherian system, authenticated relic, intact archive, or true supernatural breach is exceptional.
- Not every intrigue is an audit, contract, or conspiracy. Vanity, love, fear, accident, incompetence, ambition, grief, and ordinary crime remain sufficient causes.
- Objective evil remains objective: soul predation, slavery, body trafficking, false sanctity, and corruption are not morally neutral faction positions.
