# The Red Brass Papers

Player-readable documents for [[Tavio Companion Campaign]]. Both are quoted whole and read on screen; neither is summarized in dialogue before the player has read it.

- **The Challenge Notice** — filed publicly at the opening of [[The Fair Defeat]].
- **Maro's Letter** — arrives with the Bench's settlement offer, before the bout.

---

## The Challenge Notice

*Posted at the Great Forge and copied to the Crucible register. Lio Varcen's own hand, which is steady.*

> **CHALLENGE, FILED**
>
> Lio Varcen, Lancewright, formerly of the Red Brass Bench, to Tavio Meran, Lancewright, formerly champion of the same.
>
> One bout. I ask it on the following terms and no others.
>
> 1. Filed equipment, inspected by an examiner neither of us employs.
> 2. Permitted marks declared in writing before the day, and no substitution after.
> 3. Gauntlet capacity stated and measured. Mine at two and a brace. His at what he can prove.
> 4. **Each fighter names his own second, and either second may halt the bout. Neither fighter may overrule his second.**
> 5. Brace warnings, pain, and equipment noise are treated as information and not as forfeiture.
> 6. No wager on the outcome may be held by any person present in an official capacity.
> 7. **The recorded result remains what happens.** It is not to be improved by either party afterward, in speech or in writing.
> 8. I may withdraw. He may withdraw. Withdrawal is recorded as withdrawal.
> 9. I am not asking for an apology and will not accept one in place of any term above.
>
> I have filed a copy with the examiner and a copy with the Crucible. If the terms are refused I will publish them and consider the matter closed.
>
> Lio Varcen

*Design note: the whole man is in the numbering. A person denied fairness once builds it out of clauses; item 7 is his own idea and everything downstream of the bout depends on it holding. Note that he files it before he has ever spoken to Tavio in Grenzburg — the terms are not negotiable and were never meant to be a conversation.*

## Maro's Letter

*Arrives folded inside the Bench's settlement instrument, in the coach's own hand, unsealed.*

> Tavio, boy —
>
> They will have shown you the paper first, which is how these men do things, so let me put the human part after it where you will actually read it.
>
> You were nineteen and you could not hold a guard through three exchanges. I have coached forty fighters and I have never seen anyone learn as fast as you did that winter, and I have never told you so, because you were insufferable enough already.
>
> Now. The Bench is not the six men whose names are on the warranty. It is the drill hall, the apprentices, the widows we pay out of the tour purse, and about two hundred people in Vorthara who eat because our fittings are trusted. That is what your examiner's paper puts in the water, and she is not wrong about the fittings, and I am not asking you to say she is.
>
> I am asking you to carry it. One more time. The way you carried it for six years while men older than you took the credit.
>
> Say the ring was yours. It was — you put it on, you knew what it was, you have never once pretended otherwise, and that is why the words will be believed coming from you and from nobody else. Say it alone, and the branch survives, and in two years you can be back under supervision doing exhibitions, which is not what you were, but it is a floor and it is more than the Blackjacks will ever give you.
>
> You will want to say that this protects the men who cut the housing. It does. I am not going to insult you by pretending it doesn't. I am telling you the price and asking you anyway, because I have never lied to you about a load in my life and I am not starting now.
>
> Come home, Tavio. Carry it once more.
>
> — Maro

*Design note: it must read as love, because it is. No threat, no legalese, no denial of the facts — only loyalty, correctly aimed, asking him to spend his one true thing on the institution that spent him. This is the campaign's strongest gravity and the sole-blame temptation's real voice.*
