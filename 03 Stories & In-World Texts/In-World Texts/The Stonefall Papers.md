# The Stonefall Papers

Player-readable documents for [[Dismas Companion Campaign]]. Both are quoted whole; neither is paraphrased in dialogue before the player has read it.

- **The Claim** — retrieved from the Korrheim registry through [[Claims-Factor Marco Bellandi]] during [[The Name on the Charge]].
- **The Cofounder's Letter** — arrives by ordinary courier once the inquiry becomes known.

---

## The Claim

*Korrheim civil registry, form of particular loss. Filed against persons unknown. Eleven years unactioned. A clerk's hand throughout except the last line.*

> **CLAIMANT:** Etten, Sella. Passenger, Korrheim post-coach, Stonefall Cut.
>
> **PARTICULARS OF LOSS, ITEMISED AS REQUIRED:**
>
> Item: one travelling case and contents, not recovered. Four shillings.
>
> Item: one winter coat, cut off me. One shilling and six.
>
> Item: physician at Korrheim town, three visits, and the setting of the left leg, which was done badly and again in the spring. Two pounds four.
>
> Item: eleven weeks of work not done. I was a dairymaid at the Vester holding and the place was not kept for me. Nine pounds, being the season.
>
> Item: the cart I had to buy afterward because I could not walk to the town and back. Six pounds, and I still have it.
>
> Item: burial contribution toward the girl Bekk, subscribed among the passengers who could. Two shillings. I would like this one back if the road company is ever found, and not for me.
>
> **TOTAL CLAIMED:** seventeen pounds, seventeen shillings, six.
>
> **AGAINST:** persons unknown.
>
> *(Below, in a different hand — hers, unsteady, added at the desk:)*
>
> Item: I was under the stone from midday until the soldiers came. I claim nothing for this.

*Design note: the accounting register is the whole character. She was handed a form that asked for expenses and she filled it in correctly, which is the most anyone asked of her for eleven years. The last line is the only thing she wrote herself, and it must never be quoted back to her, explained, or repeated by another character.*

## The Cofounder's Letter

*Ordinary courier paper, no seal, no return. Arrives once the Korrheim inquiry becomes known — which takes about three weeks, because road people talk.*

> Dis —
>
> Word came down the road that you are paying a Fiorenzian to go digging at Stonefall. I laughed. Then I stopped.
>
> Here is what I can do for you, and I am offering it once because we were friends and because I am the only man alive who knows where you were standing.
>
> **It was me.** I will put it in writing to whoever you like — that I set the anchors, that I gave the release, that you were on the upper track with the glass and never touched it. There is no warrant on either of us for the coach and there never will be, and a paper from me closes the whole business without a court ever asking you a question. You keep your service, you keep the road, you keep whatever it is you have got yourself in Grenzburg that makes you want to be tidy about 1355.
>
> Take it, Dis. You have carried enough for that band and I am not going to be caught in this lifetime.
>
> And if you don't take it — if you go and hand yourself a bigger charge than the one they gave you, out of your own pocket, for two people whose names you did not know until you paid to learn them — then I never understood you and I have been telling your stories wrong for eleven years.
>
> There is a boat at Reed Landing on the fourteenth. There is always a boat.
>
> — you know who

*Design note: no threat, no blackmail, and the offer is real. It reads as loyalty and it is the fled ending in a gift's wrapping — an exit that costs nothing, dressed as a friend's sacrifice. Accepting it maps to `gone`. Note the last line: the letter also offers him the older escape, and does not push it.*
