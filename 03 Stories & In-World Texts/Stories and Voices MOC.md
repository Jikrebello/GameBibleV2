# Stories and Voices

This library contains histories, letters, sermons, diplomatic instruments and other works written from within the World Egg. Their words belong to particular authors, institutions and moments. A text may preserve valuable testimony while misunderstanding an event, serving a ruler or repeating a later legend.

The existence of each document is canonical at its stated authority level. Its claims remain subject to the reliability and provenance recorded in the article. The [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/Reliability Register.md|Reliability Register]] explains these distinctions, while the [[01 Canon Reference/History/World Historical Atlas MOC.md|World Historical Atlas]] provides the controlling historical account.

## Competing histories

These broad accounts show how later societies explain the Atherian past, the Redeemer, the Fall and the legitimacy of successor states. Their agreements reveal common inheritance; their omissions and accusations reveal the institutions for which they were written.

- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/10 Comparative Histories/The Eclessian Account.md|The Eclessian Account]] — Brother Malvian's devotional chronicle of a scattered eastern inheritance.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/10 Comparative Histories/The Thalmyric Account.md|The Thalmyric Account]] — an imperial history presenting Thalmyria as the bearer of restored Atherian authority.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/10 Comparative Histories/The Sestaran Account.md|The Sestaran Account]] — a Concord archivist's interpretation of imperial collapse and the independent cities.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/10 Comparative Histories/World History.md|World History]] — an older synthetic outline preserved as an apocryphal account rather than controlling chronology.

## Witnesses and letters

Personal testimony brings great events into the experience of soldiers, landholders and compromised officials. These texts record fear, self-justification and details often absent from public histories.

- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/20 Witnesses and Letters/A Footsoldiers Account of the Retaking of Caleran.md|A Footsoldier's Account of the Retaking of Caleran]] — a farmer-conscript remembers the march and the cost of restoration.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/20 Witnesses and Letters/A Letter from Count Rhael Tovarin of Velos.md|The Letter of Count Rhael Tovarin]] — a disputed defence written by an Atherian count who served the occupying government.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/20 Witnesses and Letters/A Soldiers Testament of the First Crusade.md|A Soldier's Testament of the First Crusade]] — an exiled commander recalls the collapse of a provincial defence.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/20 Witnesses and Letters/The Confession of Sir Renwalt of Dornheim.md|The Confession of Sir Renwalt]] — a knight's account of battle, loss and religious warfare.

## Sermons and sacred testimony

Sacred speech interprets political action through providence, judgement and inherited religious claims. These works are evidence for what their authors urged audiences to believe as well as for the events they describe.

- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/30 Sermons and Sacred Testimony/A sermon of Aqid Qalim ibn Haraz.md|The Broken Contract and the Sword of the Faithful]] — a Covenant sermon delivered on the eve of an Unsealing.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/Historical Accounts/30 Sermons and Sacred Testimony/Caedros and the Birth of Thalmyria.md|Caedros and the Birth of Thalmyria]] — a monastic foundation narrative written under imperial patronage.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/In-World Texts/The Aquilan Epistles — Selected Passages.md|The Aquilan Epistles]] — passages attributed to Saint Aquilan, preserved in rival collections and annotations.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/In-World Texts/The Hundred Grievances — Selections.md|The Hundred Grievances]] — Frater Ansgar's attack on the sale of routing assurances and the institutions profiting from them.

## Laws, safe-conducts and political counsel

Some texts acquired significance through the uses made of them. A safe-conduct can become a memorial of betrayal; a banned handbook can circulate among the rulers who denounce it.

- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/In-World Texts/The Bir Qassim Safe-Conduct — Annotated.md|The Bir Qassim Safe-Conduct]] — the instrument under which Husan ibn Raiq and his household travelled to arbitration, with rival traditions of annotation.
- [[03 Stories & In-World Texts/In-World Texts/The Mirror for Sovereigns — Selected Maxims.md|The Mirror for Sovereigns]] — Nicanor Vetti's maxims on courts, churches, money and rule.
