## **“Of Flame Reforged: A Thalmyric Account of the Fall and Return of Empire”**

_By High Scholar Arvalon of Caleran, Archivist of the Basilican Athenaeum_  
_Written under the patronage of His Radiant Majesty, Thalmyric Emperor Auren IV_

---

> _“It is no small thing to carry the burden of destiny. Where others fell, we endured. Where the light dimmed, we became its torchbearers. The Atherian flame lives, not in memory—but in Thalmyria.”_

---

### **I. The Divine Ascent of the Atherian Empire**

In the time before fire was tempered by faith, the **Atherian Empire** rose from the shores of the **Thalassian Sea**, uniting brilliant but fractious city-states into one radiant dominion. Led by emperors whose rule was sanctioned by the heavens, Atheria spread like dawn across a darkened world—first north into the steppe, then south into volcanic mountains, and at last east, into wild and scattered territories that would later be known as **Eclessia**.

The **Star Pantheon**, revered then as the celestial arbiters of mortal fate, was enshrined within every aspect of Atherian life. Temples gleamed with astral gold, and the night sky was mapped with reverence. Through this devotion—and through steel—the empire forged its greatness.

---

### **II. The Flame Enters the World**

But even the brightest star pales before true light.

From the dust of the **Jirahar caravans** in the distant east came **Isfir the Redeemer**, a figure born outside the palaces of power, yet whose words would outlive them. He preached **love**, **sacrifice**, and **the coming of a new kingdom**, not of gold, but of spirit. He performed miracles. He cast out demons. He lit hearts aflame.

The imperial court did not understand him—until they feared him. Brought in chains to **Caleran**, he was executed by decree of both **Atherian officials** and **Jirahar priests**. But the grave held him not.

Three days later, he returned.

His **Ascension** shattered the old world. From the void he left behind bloomed the **Holy Flame**, a divine presence that does not flicker. His followers—saints, martyrs, visionaries—would carry that flame through persecution, war, and empire.

---

### **III. The Burning of the Old and the Birth of the New**

As fire consumes dross to reveal the pure, so too did the Faith of the Redeemer consume the star-forged husk of Atheria. Even as its emperors persecuted his followers, the Redeemer’s message bloomed—first in the east, then across the empire. The **Sanctum Divinitatis** was founded in **Aurelia** to manage the growth of the faith, while temples of the Star Pantheon were emptied, repurposed, or abandoned.

At last, the emperors themselves knelt. The Redeemer’s Faith became the state religion, and for a time, a golden harmony returned.

But as always, faith burns away falsehoods.

The **Jirahar**, refusing to repent for their role in the Redeemer’s death, were cast out. Their Tower-Towns fell. Survivors fled west, into the desert. There, amidst forgotten gods and twisted pacts, they sowed the seeds of what would become the **Ahl-e-Ahd**.

---

### **IV. The Twilight of Empire**

Following its conversion, the Empire turned inward. It had faith—but not vision. A line of **weak emperors** squandered its strength. Raiders from the **Enaidfae** and **Ättar** harried the borders. The **Concord of Sestara**, fat with coin and trade, drifted from imperial grasp. Meanwhile, in the desert, something darker stirred.

When the **Ahl-e-Ahd** rose, they did so like a storm—terrible and sudden. Their **Unsealing** into the west brought **siege golems**, **demonic pacts**, and **enslaved spirits of war**. Whole provinces were lost in days. The **Velian States** fell. The capital trembled.

The **First Crusade** stemmed the tide, but only barely. Legions were drawn from the east, leaving **Eclessia** defenseless. There, bandits, heretics, and the Enaidfae descended. The imperial grasp faltered. Provinces declared autonomy. Rebellion brewed.

Then came the worst blow of all: the **fall of Caleran**.

The jewel of the empire—burned. The emperor—slaughtered. Holy sites—defiled. The blood that ran through the city streets has never truly washed away. It was the end of Atheria.

---

### **V. In the Ashes, the Ember Survives**

But fire does not die. It hides. It waits.

In the **southern volcanic ranges**, among basalt cliffs and fire-choked valleys, survivors endured. There, under the banner of a forgotten noble house, a **rebel general** kindled resistance. The **Third Crusade**, launched with desperate hope and shadowed by warlocks of **Vorthara**, held the line at **Caldria**. A **valley ambush** crippled the Ahl-e-Ahd elite. The battle at **Virelos** was the turning point.

One by one, the great siege golems fell. The desert juggernaut had overreached. Their cracks became faults. Their fury could not stop the fire rising again.

---

### **VI. The Flame Reforged – The Birth of Thalmyria**

With Caleran retaken and **the Holy Flame restored**, the rebel general was crowned as the **First Emperor of Thalmyria**, and the world knew: Atheria had not died. It had been reborn.

The **Fourth Crusade**, sanctioned by the **Thalmyric Redeemer Church**, swept through the lands like divine wildfire. Collaborators were purged. Betrayers hanged. But it was not vengeance that drove them—it was **restoration**.

Thalmyria, though smaller than old Atheria, is purer, sharper. Where once Atheria grew decadent, Thalmyria is forged by necessity, by sacrifice, by flame. It retains Atherian knowledge, Atherian roads, Atherian spirit—but tempered by trial.

---

### **VII. A World Not Yet Healed**

The world remains fractured.

- **Eclessia**, once the heart of the Redeemer’s ministry, is now a broken echo—faithful but rudderless. Its Sanctum proclaims that unity is near, but cannot enforce it.
- **Sestara**, for all its wealth, hides behind diplomacy and gold, its independence won at the cost of obligation.
- **The Ahl-e-Ahd**, while diminished, still hold the northern reaches—though even they show signs of rot.

And Thalmyria? We stand.  
Proud. Tempered. Unyielding.

---

### **VIII. The Flame Endures**

We do not claim to remake the old empire. We do not need to. **We are its continuation**, its divine refinement. Where others debate succession, we embody it. The Redeemer walked among us. His flame burns in our sanctums. His will shapes our swords.

Ours is the burden of peace and the duty of fire.

So let the world remember:  
**We are Thalmyria.**  
**We are the flame that endured.**
