# Reliability Register

## Physical Library

- **10 Comparative Histories:** competing Eclessian, Thalmyric, Sestaran, and synthetic accounts.
- **20 Witnesses and Letters:** testimony from soldiers, nobles, and confessors.
- **30 Sermons and Sacred Testimony:** doctrinal witness and foundation narratives.

The backlog item from [[Needs Work]], executed: every in-world account rated on the authorial scale — **reliable** (ground truth with ordinary bias), **disputed** (substantially true, contested framing), **propaganda** (institutionally shaped, useful mainly as evidence about its author), **apocryphal** (garbled or partly invented; may still contain buried truth). Ratings are authorial; in-world, of course, everyone rates them differently.

The properties-driven register is embedded below; the prose entries preserve useful nuance.

## Historical Accounts

- **A Footsoldier's Account of the Retaking of Caleran** — *reliable.* Ground-level truth of 957 AR; wrong about strategy, right about everything he saw.
- **A Soldier's Testament of the First Crusade** — *reliable*, with trauma distortion in the later passages.
- **The Confession of Sir Renwalt of Dornheim** — *reliable and self-serving*; a confession is testimony about two people, and one of them is always the confessor.
- **A Letter from Count Rhael Tovarin of Velos** — *disputed.* Genuine document; the Count is arguing a land claim while narrating history.
- **A Sermon of Aqid Qalim ibn Haraz** — *propaganda* in the technical sense: sincere Covenant homiletics; invaluable for how the Unsealing is remembered, useless for how it happened.
- **The Eclessian Account** — *propaganda.* The Sanctum's official memory: the fall as Thalmyric failure, the Split as Thalmyric pride.
- **The Thalmyric Account** — *propaganda.* The Patriarchate's official memory: the fall as Eclessian betrayal, the Split as Thalmyric fidelity. Its collective language about Jirahar guilt and claim that the exiles founded the Ahl-e-Ahd are confessional errors, not authorial facts.
- **The Sestaran Account** — *disputed, and often nearly honest*, because Sestara profits from accuracy; it is cynical about everyone equally, which is its own distortion. Its account compresses the 65 AR destruction and Vestian persecutions and repeats the false Jirahar-origin theory of the Covenant.
- **Caedros and the Birth of Thalmyria** — *disputed.* Founding narrative with hagiographic pressure; the Valley Ambush details check out, the omens do not have to.
- **Codex of the Era of Blood and Ash** — *propaganda* in the technical sense: an Eclessian devotional compilation of the 1190s AR, reliable for the twelfth-century feast calendar and unreliable for the Underground. Its Years of the Ember cannot be converted to AR. It omits informers and recanters, makes Velmion the founder of the White Flame Doctrine and invents a pommel of Calira's at Ironvale. (Still filed with the Redeemer and Conversion articles; see [[Codex of The Era of Blood and Ash]].)
- **World History (.md/.pdf)** — *apocryphal-synthesis.* An older compiled draft; its single tower-destruction sequence is superseded by [[Historical Spine of the Jirahar]] and the AR chronology.

### Causality Pass check (14 November 2026)

- **Corrected as accidental errors** in eyewitness documents: *A Soldier's Testament* and *Count Rhael Tovarin's letter* no longer put siege golems, bound devils or Janissaries into the First Unsealing; their towns fall to breach-courts, released garrison oaths and Qatada's horsemen. The *Footsoldier's Account* no longer sees a golem in 957 (Iqashar refused Durayd engines) and reports Caedros I's end at the Pillar as hearsay. Stray drafting lines were removed from the Tovarin letter and Sir Renwalt's confession.
- **Left as the author's bias:** the Thalmyric, Eclessian and Sestaran Accounts, World History and the sermon of Aqid Qalim still give the Unsealing (568–612) siege golems, bound devils and Ahdanite Guard formations. Canon has the Covenant fight without devils or great engines until the Ruling on Parties (885–886). *Caedros and the Birth of Thalmyria* still has the Flame missing at Caleran and Caedros crowned there with a tomb; canon finds the Ember burning under Covenant custody, crowns him at Virelos (922) and leaves no body.
- **Older problems the pass did not cause:** the Footsoldier joins the host after 905 and fights in 957; Sir Renwalt calls a 907 battle "the Valley Ambush", a name canon gives to Caelmount (917), and dates his record "1173 Post-Ascension"; Count Rhael's Velos is a Zanthiri emirate c. 614, while canon keeps Velos imperial; the sermon is dated to the eve of the Unsealing, though its golems and Guard would fit Idris's campaign of 902–905.

## Related In-World Sources (03/In-World Texts)

- **The Aquilan Epistles — Selected Passages** — *scripture*; variance between recensions is itself canon.
- **The Hundred Grievances** — *reliable as document*, incendiary as theology.
- **The Mirror for Sovereigns** — *reliable about everything small* (per its own censor).
- **The Bir Qassim Safe-Conduct** — *reliable*; the most damning documents are the authentic ones.
- **The Traveller's Reckoning of the Known World** — *disputed*, and openly partisan. Ugo Tessarin, a Sermoran chartmaker writing in 1360 AR, is dependable on roads, tolls, debts and recent politics and wrong in a merchant's way about oaths, wonders and peoples; the Thalmyric marginalia of Akakios of Kolonna correct several of his errors and add their own bias. The deliberate errors are listed in the article's author-only note.
- **Mono-Myth Overview** (01/Prehistory) — *apocryphal-synthesis, partially true*; see its authorial preface for the hybrid ruling.
