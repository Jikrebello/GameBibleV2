#### Ocean Aspect: Thalassor

- **Appearance:** A pressure-being perceived as abyssal scale, current-tentacle outlines, and cold vent-eyes; truest form expressed as undertow, black tide, and depth stillness.
- **Followers:** The Deep Ones and other depth-adapted ecologies that maintain trenches, currents, and pressure anchors; surface contact treated as lure-and-harvest.
- **Domains:** Depth-pressure, tide secrecy, abyssal ecology, shipwreck-law.
- **Rituals:** Tide-marking, weight-offerings, and dream-dives used to align timing and stabilize passages; the sea itself does not bargain, but conditions sometimes respond.
- **Sacred Sites:** Trench mouths, maelstrom eyes, sunken anchor-nodes (wreck-cities, coral-lodes, whale-fall cathedrals).
- **Human Worship:** Coastal bargaining cultures that practice survival-alignment rather than true devotion; sacrifice-cults are human desperation grafted onto an impersonal sea-law.

#### Land Aspect: Sylvorn

- **Appearance:** A forest briefly wearing a face—bark-skin, root-muscle, antlers like branching deltas, fungal glow; truest form expressed as path-editing and ecological insistence.
- **Followers:** Forest-born predators and symbionts (fae courts, grove-spirits, thorn-life) behaving as ecosystem with social tools.
- **Domains:** Succession, territorial memory, fertility-pressure, predation/symbiosis, metamorphic land.
- **Rituals:** Seed-burial oaths, ring-walks, and molt feasts used to bind to place, learn living routes, and abandon rigid identities.
- **Sacred Sites:** Old growth nodes, moving groves, stone circles, root-cairns, fungal rings that map living corridors.
- **Human Worship:** Hedge-wardens and border druids practicing land etiquette; atrocity-cults represent human misuse of fertility-pressure, not Sylvorn’s requirement.

#### Sky Aspect: [[Zephyraxis]]

- **Appearance:** Living pressure-front and storm-architecture; perceived as cyclone-eye, anvil-front, or turbulence silhouette; truest form expressed as organizing weather geometry.
- **Followers:** Aerial storm-ecologies (gust-wraiths, cloudlings, lightning-eels, flock-shadows) emerging within persistent systems.
- **Domains:** Pressure and wind-law, exposure/altitude, storm migration, aerial ecology.
- **Rituals:** Front-watching vigils, sky-needle ascents, and open-air oaths performed as readiness and timing discipline.
- **Sacred Sites:** Lightning fields, sky-needles (spires/towers), wind corridors in passes and coastal cliffs.
- **Human Worship:** Sailors and highland orders treating the sky as authority of preparation and humility; blood-sacrifice is cultural panic, not celestial appetite.

#### Unified Form: [[Trium]]

- **Appearance:** The rare phase-lock state where sky/land/sea synchronize; perceived as layered composite because convergence cannot be held by a single image.
- **Followers:** Not a cult—synchronized convergence ecologies across domains (aerial swarms, forest courts, abyssal emissaries) exploiting the same locked conditions.
- **Domains:** Convergence, boundary failure, world-scale ecological reshuffle, mythic recomposition events.
- **Rituals:** Threefold vigils and confluence markings used to predict and survive; no rite reliably “summons” Trium.
- **Sacred Sites:** Breakpoints where domains already overlap—sea-caves that breathe wind, cliff-groves above black surf, river mouths that flood backwards.
- **Human Worship:** Primarily defensive responses; opportunistic cults are parasites riding catastrophe.

### Additional Elements:
- **Core Frame:** Third Circle Menhir are not moral gods or civic rulers. They are sovereign natural engines: land, sea, and sky operating at mythic saturation, with Trium as their rare synchronized operating state.
- **Setting Utility:** Their “worship” is best treated as alignment behavior, survival culture, predatory ecology, and opportunistic misinterpretation—never as proof that these forces require belief.
