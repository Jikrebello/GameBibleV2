# Tavio Rewrite Spec

Execution spec for actioning [[Companion Critical Review - Tavio and Ilyana]], Tavio scope. Companion document to [[Elara Rewrite Spec]] and [[Bernard Rewrite Spec]] — shared prose rules and verification method are inherited and restated only where they differ. This spec is the Tavio half of the pending Ylva-Tavio paired pass; pair-cohesion items are marked ⟨PAIR⟩ so the Ylva spec can pick them up.

## Locked Decisions

1. **The Lio Clause: Lio arrives.** The Constitution gains a narrow, named exception (text in 2.1); Lio's arrival in [[The Challenger's Road]] and presence at [[The Fair Defeat]] become fully sanctioned. His file's self-contradiction is fixed, and he finally gets written lines. The clause applies once — Marta Etten and the Salt-Reeds remain remote per their reviews.
2. **Secondary archetype: Goofball** — Tavio becomes Jock-primary / Goofball-secondary ("Lovable Captain"). Payload: the stress register (jokes more, then turns controlling when humour fails) and the secret desire that is his thesis verbatim — *continued love after defeat or injury*. Differentiation from Bernard stays bright: Bernard's humor makes room; Tavio's recruits.
3. **The tremor and the motive: both adopted.** Nerve scarring in his gauntlet hand from the Fifth Ring's discharge, concealed for four years — narrative beats only, **zero mechanical change** to the Lancewright package. And the motive: the bout record shows Lio was ahead when the ring fired. He cheated from fear of decline, not hubris.
4. **Structure: Measure on the March is archived; Meran-Style replaces it.** The new Act III operation stages the replacement figure and the public stop that feels like defeat. Its knockoff supply chain re-sources `tavio_field_inspection_ledger`; the Muster-plate beat migrates into [[Horn for Leak-Day]]'s front.

# Part 1 — New Canon Elements

## 1.1 The tremor and the motive

**The body.** The Fifth Ring's discharge scarred the nerves of Tavio's right gauntlet hand. Symptom: a fine tremor at maximum load — invisible in ordinary work, real at the top of his strength. He has concealed it for four years with technique, equipment habits, and charm. Canon anchors to add:

- Bio: one paragraph in the Fifth Ring section; one new tell in Company Routine (he re-wraps the gauntlet lining more often than wear explains); the existing tell ("polishes the Fifth Ring's empty place on his gauntlet") now reads doubled — he is checking the housing *and* the hand.
- **Zero mechanical change.** A design note in [[Tavio Lancewright Package]]: the tremor is authored-beat material only; it never nerfs stats, never fires as RNG, never becomes a debuff. Its entire existence is scenes.
- **The mirror rule** (the point of the device, stated in the bio): he is hiding a weakness in exactly the architecture the Bench used to hide the ring — a concealed over-capacity problem inside a certified housing. His bio's private contradiction ("believes every body deserves fair limits and built his identity on being the exception") now has flesh.

**The motive.** Add to [[The False Ring]]'s evidence set a fifth, human fact: the bout cadence record (his old seconding card, or the written bout rhythm) shows **Lio ahead on exchanges when the discharge came.** Tavio wasn't protecting a championship; he was losing one — to a rival who had improved between meetings, which his own appetite list says he loves. The confession line this buys (placed late Act II, exact wording at scene pass): *"You were better that season. I knew it in the third exchange."* Cheating from fear of decline is the Jock reference's replacement-terror made act; "I carried the risk and still won" survives as the story he told himself afterward, which is more damning, not less.

**Thread map (the tremor appears exactly four times — budget enforced):**

1. **Seeded** — The False Ring: during Hella's control demonstration, an observant player (Insight/medical) notices his grip; he laughs it off in Lovable Captain register.
2. **Named** — Challenger's Road or pre-bout: the filing mirror (see 2.3) forces the question of disclosure.
3. **Spent** — The Fair Defeat: fighting honestly with the hand is genuinely dangerous; if he filed it, the rules accommodate; if he concealed it, one bout beat turns on it.
4. **Passed** — Horn for Leak-Day: the mobile vent needs "one person at the proof gauntlet." Resolved Tavio does not silently take the station — he names the hand and assigns or shares it. The concealment pattern ends where the campaign ends.

## 1.2 Lio's voice

Lio currently has zero written lines — a rights-document where a person should be. The Petra-letter standard applies, extended because he is *present*:

- **The challenge notice** (in-world text, quoted whole): his public filing, in his own hand — dry, precise, free of grievance-performance. It should read like a man filing paperwork about the most important thing in his life *because paperwork is the fairness he was denied*. One human detail surfaces in the phrasing (he numbers his conditions; condition four is about his second's authority; condition seven is that "the record remains what happens" — his signature idea, now sourced to him).
- **Weigh-in lines** — the three sentences he says to Tavio at first meeting. Register: no anger performed, no absolution offered, an athlete's directness about equipment and terms. He talks to Tavio the way professionals talk to opponents, which is itself devastating — the relationship Tavio broke was *collegial*.
- **Branch lines** — one authored beat each for win / loss / draw / withdrawal / refusal. His win is not triumph (he checks his brace before he looks at the crowd). His loss is not tragedy (he confirms the record stands). His withdrawal is information, per his own filed rules. His refusal branch keeps its review-guaranteed dignity: refusal is a valid outcome, and Tavio may not narrate himself denied redemption.
- **The cane and the mornings.** One texture beat, non-negotiable: a man who does pain management every morning has a specific relationship to small talk and schedules. Lio arrives early to everything; the file should say why without saying it twice.
- ⟨PAIR⟩ Lio and Ylva share one incidental scene opportunity (two professionals comparing brace/cold-injury management without drama) — flag for the Ylva spec; do not build here.

## 1.3 Maro Vezan's letter — the martyr's discount

Maro (coach, cover-up architect, father-figure) is currently inert. He does not arrive; he *writes*. One letter (in-world text, quoted whole, ~200 words), delivered with the Bench's settlement offer before the bout:

- Voice: warm, gruff, proud, monstrous — the coach who made him, asking him to carry the Bench "once more, like you carried it for six years." It should read as *love*; that is the trap. No threats, no legalese: loyalty-language weaponized.
- The offer it wraps: accept sole blame, preserve the branch, receive a path back to supervised exhibition.
- **The crowd's appetite staged** (new beat in Fair Defeat's middle): a Bench-fed bout bill or broadsheet naming Tavio the lone cheat circulates *and is popular* — one villain is easier to love a sport through than a corrupt institution. Vendors sell it outside the Great Forge. Tavio watches people he likes buy it with relief on their faces.
- **Hella's counter-line** (the scene that names the trap; wording at scene pass): taking all the blame is one more performance — the martyr buys the arena's innocence at a discount and calls the purchase honesty.

## 1.4 Steffen — the replacement figure

**File:** new character note. **Role:** the fear the campaign never staged: someone younger, wearing his style, owning his crowd.

- Hammer Ward crane-hand, nineteen, fighting unlicensed pit nights in "Meran-style" — spear and proof gauntlet, arena flourishes learned from bout bills and one touring exhibition he saw at eleven. Working name **Steffen** — surname at execution *after a name-registry check* (the review flagged Mika/Nika, Heda×2, Marta×2; nothing close to Mara Senn/Marta).
- **His gear is the campaign's own loose thread:** the private demonstration gauntlet that escaped the Old Market auction in [[The Signed Measure]] ("it returns through the field inspection chain in Act III" — this is that return), plus knockoff Red Brass rings from the recalled batches. Tavio's name made it trusted; Tavio's legend is now a safety hazard with a fan club.
- **Character rules:** he is not a villain, not a dupe, and not humble. He is *good* — genuinely gifted, which must be visible — and his counter-argument has real teeth: an old champion preaching limits to the young reads as a man closing the door behind himself. His line-register: cocky-warm, quotes Tavio's old bout bills back at him ("You said a halt is part of the bout. You never called one.").
- **Seeding (the Perle pattern):** Steffen appears in [[Games Without a Crown]] as a fair competitor — wins the spear-vault demonstration, glows when Tavio praises him, worship fully visible. One optional Yard-fair embed (TV-E09) reference. The player likes him before the gear does.
- **He is not disposable:** `steffen_state` persists to summer (examiner-apprentice, licensed fighter, injured, or estranged — per Meran-Style outcomes). In the resolved coda he appears at the road games, fighting filed and clean, still a little insufferable. The replacement fear resolves not by removal but by succession Tavio can bear.

## 1.5 Red Brass priced, and the betting subplot given a beneficiary

One paragraph into the Red Brass Bench faction file (read first): **why Grenzburg proof endangers a Vortharan bench.** The Crucible's certification travels; a proven substitution voids Red Brass warranties from Vorthara to the border marches and invites Crucible sanction on the branch's licenses. Hella's documentation isn't local embarrassment — it is warranty poison across every market the Bench sells into. This prices their saboteurs and bounds their spend (agents and bribes, never armies).

The Fair Defeat's orphan noir gets its beneficiary: the "impossible betting positions" are **Seraf's and Maro's personal exit money** — men who know the branch is dying, hedging its collapse. Cynical, human, small. One ledger line ties it off; no new conspiracy.

## 1.6 Hella's texture

Hella keeps the incorruptible-gatekeeper register (she is its origin; the clones elsewhere get differentiated in their own specs). She gains one object: **the wall of liars** — every failed or fraudulent proof ring she has ever caught, nailed above her bench in rows, tagged in her hand. Scenes gain a place to point; her register gains a body. The Fifth Ring's eventual place on or absence from that wall becomes a quiet outcome-state the coda can glance at.

# Part 2 — Structural Surgery

## 2.1 The Lio Clause (Constitution amendment)

Add to [[Companion Constitution]], Archetype and Emotional Contract section, after the remote-claims sentence:

> **Exception (the Lio Clause):** a wronged party may enter the base-game worldspace only under their own power, for their own aims, with authored refusal rights, independent protection and lodging, and no reconciliation requirement. The test is never "may the past arrive?" but "whose act is the climax?" — arrival must never replace the companion's volition, and absence must never replace the wrong's reality. This exception currently sanctions exactly one arrival: [[Lio Varcen]].

Then fix [[Lio Varcen]]: delete "Lio never enters the base-game worldspace" and the pending-migration framing; state his arrival under the Clause; remove the frontmatter `needs` item about replacing bout placeholders (superseded by this spec); add the voice section (1.2). Update [[The Challenger's Road]] and [[The Fair Defeat]] frontmatter from `remote_claim_status: pending-migration` to `compliant` *citing the Clause*.

## 2.2 Meran-Style (new Act III operation, replaces Measure on the March)

Working title **Meran-Style** (the kid's fighting style, named after Tavio — the replacement theme in two words; alternates: *His Name on It*, *Exhibition*). Normal party, ~0.75–1.0h, Act III window (Open Thaw / Spring Reclamation).

**Premise:** the first spring since the siege brings the reclamation games — and pit nights. Steffen is winning them in Meran-style with knockoff gear, and a crowd that used to chant Tavio's name has learned a new one. Hella traces a cracked ring from a pit injury to the recalled endorsement batches; the supply chain must be found and the kid must be stopped — and Tavio has no authority to stop him with.

**Beat structure:**

1. **The crowd, lost.** Tavio and the party attend a pit night. Steffen is *good* — the scene must sell his gift, not just his risk. Tavio watches someone else own his crowd; the player watches Tavio watch. One beat of the Lovable Captain stress register: he jokes, the jokes thin, and when Steffen publicly needles him ("You said a halt is part of the bout. You never called one.") the humour fails and something controlling comes out of Tavio's mouth before he can stop it — drill-sergeant, rank-pulling, ugly for three sentences. The crowd sides with the kid. (R4 staged.)
2. **The chain.** Investigation through Hammer Ward pit culture — bout brokers, a fence, the auction gauntlet's paper trail — yields `tavio_field_inspection_ledger` (re-sourced from the archived quest) and identifies which fighters carry batch gear. Ordinary vocation-neutral play; no new locations required (Hammer Ward anchors + Old Market).
3. **The refusal.** Steffen rejects the recall. His gear is his ladder out of the crane yards; Tavio's warning sounds like the door closing. No social check converts him — the review's chorus rules hold; his refusal is self-interested and *reasonable-shaped*, not seminar.
4. **The challenge — and the stop that feels like defeat.** Steffen challenges Tavio publicly: beat me and I'll hand the rings over. The trap is total: fighting is the thing Tavio wants most (and the ban he must accept forbids); winning proves nothing and might cripple the boy through the very gear at issue; the tremor makes it dangerous for Tavio too — and he cannot say so without an excuse-shaped truth. **Authored beat, all routes: Tavio declines, in front of the crowd, and lets them read it as cowardice or decline.** No speech rescues it. "A halt is part of the bout. Anyone can continue." performed at maximum personal cost — bio promise 3, finally staged. The player's role is response, not prevention (the Elara-joke pattern: the act is his).
5. **The teach.** The resolution path that exists because he declined: proof the gear honestly with Hella (it fails, publicly, on the test frame — the wall of liars grows); offer Steffen the fair ladder — filed bouts, a sponsor's word, examiner training. Steffen may take it, spurn it, or take it *resentfully*; `steffen_state` records. Direction moves on whether Tavio teaches or performs teaching.
6. **Residue:** the crowd does not come back to Tavio's side. The piece ends with the fair path open and the cheap glory gone — dethronement held, not reversed.

**Outputs:** `tavio_field_inspection_ledger`; `steffen_state`; `pit_gear_state`; `tavio_direction`; the declined-challenge flag for coda/banter reactivity. **Autonomous resolution:** Tavio confronts Steffen without the player, is baited, and half-accepts the challenge before Hella intervenes — gear partially recalled, Steffen estranged, ledger incomplete. (The played route must carry at least this much risk — the Bernard played≥autonomous test applies.)

**Migration:** archive [[Measure on the March]] to 90 Archive & Drafts; its unique Muster-cadence-plate beat (the contaminated forge plate) moves into Horn for Leak-Day's opening movement or the `tavio_muster_cadence_reading` asset's source description; the arsenal/convoy texture is released. Backlink sweep required (campaign doc, Horn, Narrative State Register, registers).

## 2.3 The Fair Defeat — rebuild

Keep: the second's-role structure, filed rules, Lio's total control of participation, sabotage investigation, the no-thrown-bout rule, refusal as a valid outcome, Lancewright unlock mechanics.

**Change:**

1. **Delete the agnosticism.** "Tavio loses: the campaign advances normally. Tavio wins: the campaign advances normally" is replaced by authored texture per branch:
   - **Fair loss** (the branch the campaign privileges with its richest content): the crowd does the cruelest available thing — retroactively voids his career. *"He was never good without the ring."* An honest loss indicts every honest win he ever had, and Lio's own rule — the record remains what happens — now protects the reading that damns him. Tavio must leave the result alone. This is the campaign's deepest test and should cost the most and pay the most.
   - **Fair win:** differently barbed — beating the man he maimed wins nothing; the cheer feels obscene to him; Lio confirms the record stands and means it, which is worse than anger.
   - **Draw / withdrawal / refusal:** each gets one authored beat (Lio's branch lines, 1.2) and distinct public reading. No branch "advances normally"; every branch advances *specifically*.
2. **The filing mirror.** During the rules filing, Lio files his brace warnings as information. The scene now holds a beat where the symmetry is on the table: does Tavio file the hand? Filing it = the resolved read (limits as part of the contest, applied to himself at last, and the bout rules accommodate). Concealing it = the ring pattern in miniature, live, with consequences if the tremor surfaces mid-bout. The player, as second, can know or not know depending on Act I–II attentiveness; a second who knows and stays silent gets one line of reckoning later.
3. **The martyr's discount staged** (1.3): Maro's letter arrives before the bout; the bout bill circulates in the middle; Hella's counter-scene sits before the reckoning. The reckoning choice ("expose the Bench / accept sole blame / minimize / attack Lio's credibility") now has a face, a document, and a visible public appetite behind its second option — the sole-blame temptation graduates from bullet point to the quest's strongest gravity.
4. **The confession beat** (1.1): "You were better that season." lands here — to Lio, at the weigh-in or after the result, branch-dependent. Lio's response is his own; the spec reserves him the right to find it useless.
5. Betting subplot tied off per 1.5.
