# Ylva Rewrite Spec

Execution spec for actioning [[Companion Critical Review - Ylva and Zafir]], Ylva scope. The last of the eight, companion to all prior specs — shared prose rules and verification inherited. This is the Ylva half of the pending Ylva-Tavio paired pass; pair items are marked ⟨PAIR⟩. Her structure is sound and her finales are among the roster's best; the work is **variety** — one lesson currently taught seven times gets three of its deliveries reassigned to the facets of her wound the campaign never fires.

## Locked Decisions

1. **Three delegation-beats reassigned, sins authored-fixed.** [[A Signal Shared]] becomes the inverted-paternalism piece; [[Signals Over Lantern Ward]] becomes her Box Hill (the contempt scene); [[Routes for the Living]] becomes the being-wrong piece. The contempt and the wrong call fire in every playthrough — the Elara-joke/Bernard-relapse pattern: preparation mitigates cost, never prevents her act. Both carry do-not-regress flags.
2. **Secondary archetype: Cheerleader** — Ylva becomes Tomboy-primary / Cheerleader-secondary ("Field Captain"): action combined with social command; conflict, directness versus diplomacy; shadow, **controlling protector** — her file's own sentence, arriving as a named formation. The Tavio mirror-secondary symmetry is registered ⟨PAIR⟩.
3. **The co-signer is built** — the White-Sled driver at the Ledger Quays, the fifth local face, fifth valence: he insists on standing beside. In the resolved ending he signs the route board as one name among several, next to hers.
4. **The coda renames: "Relieved."** One word, both meanings hers. The "The Road…" collision set closes.

# Part 1 — New Canon Elements

## 1.1 The slow reader — inverted paternalism given a person

**File:** new minor character note, for the rebuilt [[A Signal Shared]]. Working name registry-checked. Design: an old road-reader — a retired carter or ferry hand, forty years on these roads — who reads weather by joint-ache, cloud, and animal behavior; chatty, unhurried, physically past his prime; everything her competence-register discounts. He volunteers for a relay station. **She blocks him** — kindly, firmly, in exactly the officer's register from her own banter anger scene, inverted onto her ("the route needs legs"). His informed choice is the point: he knows his limits better than she has bothered to learn them, and his knowledge turns out load-bearing — he knows which station's flooding lies, which the quest's final test needs. The piece's decisive beat: whether she reopens the roster to a man she has already ruled out, on his terms. His voice key: unhurried, associative, right by a route the player can verify; never wounded-dignity seminar — he has been underestimated for years and finds it *boring* rather than painful.

## 1.2 The trainee — her Box Hill

**File:** minor character note, for the rebuilt [[Signals Over Lantern Ward]]. Working name registry-checked. A debtor trainee in her siege signal-school: earnest, limited, and honest about his limits — he reports them early and asks for relief on time. **He does exactly what her campaign preaches, and her gut answer is contempt.** The authored-fixed sin: mid-drill, exhausted, she says something quiet and unforgivable — one sentence, in her compressed register, the doctrine under the competence showing for three seconds (final wording at scene pass; the working shape: a comparison between what he asked for and what she was carrying at his age). The player witnesses; cannot prevent; responds. His response is not seminar: he keeps training, performs well, **and stops asking her questions** — the cost is precise and quiet. The repair is harder than the distress call, per the review: she must ask him to ask again, which requires saying why he stopped, which requires repeating what she said, out loud, to his face. Direction moves on whether she does. `trainee_state` persists; in the resolved winter he holds a station well, and the two facts — her cruelty and his competence — are both left standing.

## 1.3 The wrong call

For the rebuilt [[Routes for the Living]]: the authored-fixed error. During the corridor work, Ylva's read of one route conflicts with its local holder's — her method against what she once called romantic vagueness. She is confident, methodical, and **wrong in every playthrough**: the local's tradition encoded real knowledge her survey missed (spring behavior of a slope, a thaw pattern, a ford's lie). The cost is real and preparation-scaled but never zero — a cache lost, a delay with a named injury, a corridor entering the finale under pressure. Design guards: no sabotage, no withheld information that excuses her — the diligence was real and insufficient (the Posted Load rule, ⟨PAIR-ADJACENT⟩ with Zafir's scene; one cross-reference line permitted, no more). Her banter object with Zafir — "the right of field evidence to defeat theory" — turns on her, unnarrated. The aftermath beat: she re-marks the route in the local's notation, not hers.

## 1.4 The co-signer

**File:** new character note. The fifth local face. A White-Sled convoy driver — working name registry-checked — now a freight hand at the Ledger Quays (it was a Medori convoy; the quays are where its people ended up). He tried to co-sign the surety; the papers were already closed. His grievance is the campaign's thesis from the victim's side: **not harm — exclusion.** "You decided I couldn't afford my own conscience" (register; final wording at scene pass, plainer if possible). He is not angry in any usable way; he simply will not let her carry his gratitude, which is the one weight she wants. Behavior rules per the pattern: never a quest; never blocks; one fixed exchange (between Acts I and II, at the quays); `cosigner_state` never moves. Ambient presence in her Quays-adjacent content; one silent beat at the Icehouse Steps liability filing in [[What the Ice Takes]] — he is in the room when she chooses whether to sign alone again, and says nothing, and she sees him. **The payoff:** in the resolved [[The Last Marker]] ending, the northern route board records its names — and he signs as one member of the route, next to her, uncommented. That is the entire transaction.

## 1.5 The three loaded guns

- **The spoon scene** — her "Without the Powder." One authored quiet scene, post-Act I window (camp or Yard). The player notices the carved spoon kindly. She gets defensive on schedule — then the admission, short enough to miss (working register, final at scene pass): *"I was nine. The knife was for gutting fish."* — pause — *"It is a spoon."* She made a beautiful useless thing on purpose and has hidden it for twenty years; the scene never says so. Canon lock: she carved it herself; it is the only object she owns that serves nothing. No state output beyond a familiarity beat — the scene is the reward.
- **The legibility scene** — Act II, a shelter: a Grenzburg matron hands her mending, assuming; the room waits; her response is neither shame nor speech but exact, visible discomfort — and the player's response options do the scene's work (take the mending, name the assumption, let it stand). One scene; the Tomboy reference's compulsory-legibility problem honored without a dress or a lesson.
- **The Grey-Wolf/Medori rhyme, said once** — placed in [[The Fourth Marker]], after the roster abuse is established, in her own dry register (working shape: *"Koss is generous. At home I could do this work but not sign for it. Here I may sign four times."*). One line; no one responds to it; the campaign never says it again.

## 1.6 Named evacuees

The finale name budget (registry-checked): [[What the Ice Takes]] — two or three named among the nineteen (the ferry hand who takes the litter role she wanted; one wounded soldier; one child whose crossing beat the player runs); [[The Last Marker]] — two or three named at Last Hearth (the elderly miller already half-exists; a carer; one route-crew member from the causeway relief choice). Each gets one beat; no crowd-counts at climaxes.

# Part 2 — Structural Surgery

## 2.1 A Signal Shared — rebuilt around the slow reader

Keep: the three-station relay premise, the incompatible local signal cultures, the shared-code layer, the spoofer thread, the final storm test, `ylva_shared_signal_code`.

Change: the emotional engine is no longer "will the relay work without her" (that lesson is kept by the finales) but **whose risk she is entitled to rule on.** The slow reader (1.1) threads through all three stations; her blocking of him is the mid-piece beat; the storm test's decisive information is his; the decision beat is the roster reopened or held. The audit's reframe brief ("inexperienced partners, patronization, and the question of who is trusted to judge field risk") is satisfied with a person instead of a category. Prose pass; template scaffolding stripped.

## 2.2 The Rearguard Road — audit reframe honored

Light retrofit per the audit's own brief: reduce the repeated signal machinery; make her injured body, her authority, and the distress call the intimate pressure. The banter shame-scene's arithmetic — *"Seven minutes to shelter." / "How long have you known?" / "Eleven."* — is the register standard for the injury beats (the four stolen minutes as the design note says: the shame is the time she took from the team's decision). Structure, fail-forward, and the Honest Distress Call decision tree are otherwise protected. Named relief workers where currently counted.

## 2.3 Signals Over Lantern Ward — rebuilt around the contempt

Keep: the siege signal-school premise, station-holder training, the network serving the winter defence.

Change: the piece's centre is 1.2 — her school as the site of her cruelty. Beat order: the training montage establishing her as a good teacher (she is; that's what makes it land); the trainee's early honest limit-report; **the sentence** (authored-fixed, all routes); the witnessed cost; the drill continuing; the repair attempt, harder than any signal work in the piece. The station-holder training the audit asked for remains the material spine — the quest still produces the Act II asset — but the lesson the piece teaches is no longer delegation; it is that her doctrine has a body count of small refusals, and the first one was hers. Do-not-regress flag on the fixed sin.

## 2.4 What the Ice Takes and The Last Marker — protected, populated

Both finales stand structurally — they are among the roster's best. Additions only: named evacuees (1.6); the co-signer's silent presence at the liability filing (2.4a: one beat, no line); the co-signer's route-board signature in the resolved Last Marker end (1.4); the relief-column image threaded (the shape statement's roster motif — who signs, who answers, which column her name lands in). The sacrifice gating, the distant-choice beat (the receiving team's informed decision), the liability-filing scene, and the full resolution tree are **protected verbatim** in structure. Prose pass only.

## 2.5 Routes for the Living — rebuilt around the wrong call

Keep: the three corridors, the atlas layers, the duplication requirement, the final drill, the Muster interference rules, the Act III assets.

Change: the piece's centre is 1.3. The audit's brief ("delegation and training operation rather than a third mapping exercise") is satisfied by making the mapping itself the site of her failure — the delegation lesson arrives not as doctrine but as consequence: the atlas must be plural *because she was wrong*, and everyone including her knows it by the end. The final drill keeps its shape with one addition: the simulated failure assigned to her team is her own error, anonymized, and she recognizes it. Do-not-regress flag on the fixed error.

## 2.6 Everything protected

[[The Fourth Marker]] (plus the rhyme line, 1.5); [[No Quarry Today]] — the roster's best friendship episode, byte-protected including the emergency-portions beat and "Nothing has to die for us to know who read the ground"; [[A Hearth Chosen]] including "she does not know how to ask for private time without making it useful"; [[The Sister Watch]] entire; the arrow progression and recipe independence; the Tavio foil arc ⟨PAIR — jointly owned with the executed Tavio spec⟩; the banter file entire ("Your hands said yes."; "Name the injury. Weather later."; "Then I am improving."; "That was not my decision alone. I made it alone."; "Wake me if the wind turns."); the White-Sled Rescue file (gains only the co-signer's one-line existence).

## 2.7 The coda — "Relieved"

[[The Road She Chooses]] renames to **Relieved**; rename propagation swept. Content stands with additions: the route board's relieved column shown; the co-signer beat reflected; the spoon visible once, uncommented, in her kit; on the resolved path she is asleep when the player arrives and nobody wakes her — the banter quiet-scene's promise kept at campaign scale. The Brina wink lands here or at any late readiness docket (one line, optional, at the executor's judgment: the player's five prepared assets earn *"You are doing the thing she does."*).

- **The delegation count is four:** intro plus three finales; the three operations carry contempt, paternalism, and error; no fifth delegation-beat exists anywhere.
