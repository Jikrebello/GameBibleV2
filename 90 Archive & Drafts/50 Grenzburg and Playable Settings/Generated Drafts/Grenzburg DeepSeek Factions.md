### **The Duchy of Grenzburg: Power Structure & Key NPCs**

**The Duke:** **Alaric von Grenzmark**, Lord-Warden of the Southern Marches. A pragmatist in his late 50s, wearied by decades of balancing on a knife's edge. He rules not by divine right, but by the consent of the powerful—because he's the only one who can keep them from killing each other.

**His Inner Council (The Manse):**
These are the people Alaric must face every day. Each represents a pillar of his power, and each contains the internal "Good, Bad, Ugly" dynamic.

1.  **The Marschal (Military & Defense):** **Sir Gerhold "The Iron-Bear" Brandt.**
    *   **Role:** Commander of the garrison, wall defenses, and the Marchwardens (professional soldiers). Responsible for winter survival.
    *   **Internal Rule of Three:**
        *   **Good (Loyalist):** Sir Gerhold himself. Fiercely loyal to the Duchy as an *idea*—the last bastion of order. Wants a strong, professional military under the Duke's command.
        *   **Bad (Militarist):** **Captain Valdemar Koss.** Commander of the Blackjacks. Sees the Debtors' Corps as the future: expendable, effective, and loyal only to their paymaster (the Bank, *through* him). Dreams of a militarized state run by "those who get their hands dirty."
        *   **Ugly (Survivalist):** **Sergeant Liese.** A veteran scout from a marcher family. Argues for treaties with specific Grobi tribes and Folk clans. "My grandfather traded with the Stone-Tooth grobi for thirty years. Enemies change. Geography doesn't." Her loyalties are to her people in the hamlets, not the Duke's throne.

2.  **The Kämmerer (Chamberlain, Steward & Finance):** **Magister Leviticus of Sestaren.**
    *   **Role:** Manages the Duke's treasury, collects taxes (in coin, grain, or lumber), and is the official liaison to the Medori Bank. A foreign-born bureaucrat.
    *   **Internal Rule of Three:**
        *   **Good (Reformist):** Magister Leviticus. Believes Grenzburg's chaos is inefficient. Wants to formalize laws, banking, and trade to build a *lasting* state. Sees the Duke as the necessary authority.
        *   **Bad (Embezzler):** **Treasurer Baldric.** Skimming funds and selling information on artifact hauls to rival Eclessian agents. He's bleeding the Duchy dry for personal gain.
        *   **Ugly (Bank's Creature):** **Clerk Anya.** Quiet, meticulous. She *is* the Medori Bank's auditor within the treasury. Every debt, every hidden asset, every secret payroll is known to her. She ensures the Bank's interests are always served first.

3.  **The Landvogt (Chief Magistrate & Law):** **Baron Anders of Hochfeld.**
    *   **Role:** The highest judge in the Marches. Settles disputes between guilds, merchants, and nobles. Commands the few town watches and bailiffs.
    *   **Internal Rule of Three:**
        *   **Good (Justiciar):** Anders. Believes in the rule of law as the only thing separating Grenzburg from the Grobi. Harsh but fair.
        *   **Bad (Corrupt):** **Bailiff Hagen.** Runs protection rackets, ensures guild masters "win" disputes, and looks the other way for the right price. Law is a weapon for the powerful.
        *   **Ugly (Vigilante):** **"The Gravedigger."** An anonymous figure who "dispenses justice" to those who escape Anders' court. Is it a Folk sympathizer killing ruthless loggers? A rival guild member? A disgraced noble? Their actions destabilize the fragile legal order.

4.  **The Geistlicher Rat (Spiritual Advisor):** **Bishop-Prelate Caestus.**
    *   **Role:** The Duke's link to the Church of the Redeemer. Officially advises on morality and legitimacy; unofficially, he is the Church's political agent.
    *   **Internal Rule of Three (mirroring the larger Church):**
        *   **Good (Shepherd):** Caestus himself. Genuinely believes in bringing spiritual solace to this hard place. Seeks to reform the Church from within Grenzburg, making it a force for community.
        *   **Bad (Zealot):** **Deacon Ignatius.** Leader of the "Ashen Band," a militia of fanatics. Wants to purge the Folk as heathens, refuse deals with Grobi, and launch a crusade into the ruins to reclaim them for the Redeemer.
        *   **Ugly (Politician):** **Sister Marcellina.** The Church's intelligence head. Blackmails sinners, brokers information between factions, and ensures the Church's temporal power grows, regardless of doctrine. Faith is a tool.

### **The Major "Ugly" Factions**

#### **The Church of the Redeemer in Grenzburg**
*   **Leader:** **Bishop Konrad,** a shrewd political operator sent to this backwater as punishment, now seeking to make it his power base.
*   **Internal Rule of Three:**
    *   **Good (The Hospitallers):** Led by **Brother Clemens.** Run the city's only true hospital. Advocate for aiding all wounded (even Grobi prisoners) and giving refuge. Pure, draining charity.
    *   **Bad (The Ashen Band):** Led by **Deacon Ignatius** (see above). Millenarian zealots who see the frontier as a testing ground for the faithful. Their purges create more enemies.
    *   **Ugly (The Chancery):** Led by **Sister Marcellina.** The real power. Controls relics, interprets "miracles," and manages the Church's vast land holdings in the Marches. They'll support anyone who increases Church property and influence.

#### **The Medori Bank (The "Iron Ledger")**
*   **Factor (Head Agent):** **Silas Medori,** a cold, calculating young scion proving his worth.
*   **Internal Rule of Three:**
    *   **Good (The Venture Capitalists):** **Ledger-Master Elara.** Funds infrastructure: better sawmills, fortified granaries. Believes a stable, prosperous Grenzburg yields higher long-term returns. Wants to reform the Blackjack system.
    *   **Bad (The Collection Division):** **Enforcer-Captain Vorl.** Runs the Blackjacks. Views debtors as livestock. Pushes for riskier artifact delves and harsher terms to maximize short-term profit. Might instigate conflicts to create more debtors.
    *   **Ugly (The Silent Partners):** Unknown figures in Sestaren. They use Grenzburg as a deniable testing ground. They fund *both* the Duke's wall repairs *and* the aggressive Folk chieftain's weapon shipments via intermediaries, profiting from perpetual conflict.

#### **The Frontier Industry Lords (Vassals & Guild Masters)**
*   **Lumber (The Timber-Cartel):** **Guild Master Holger Strun.** Controls the lifeline of the Marches.
    *   **Good:** Veteran loggers who want sustainable harvesting to preserve their way of life.
    *   **Bad:** "Clear-Cut" crews who ignore treaties with the Folk, sparking raids.
    *   **Ugly:** Strun himself. He sells "protected" lumber to rival kingdoms and pays off Bailiff Hagen to look away.
*   **Mining & Artifacts (The Delvers' Union):** **Lady Katarina "the Veiled,"** a noble exile who owns the largest mine.
    *   **Good:** Geologists and engineers who just want to find ore and make the mines safe.
    *   **Bad:** Ruthless "tomb-breakers" who plunder ruins, setting off ancient curses that affect everyone.
    *   **Ugly:** Lady Katarina. She hoards the most dangerous artifacts, not to sell, but to study for personal, magical power.
*   **Furs & Fisheries (The Trappers' League):** **A council of older Marcher families.**
    *   **Good:** Families who follow ancient, sustainable trapping grounds.
    *   **Bad:** Poachers who encroach on Folk sacred hunting grounds.
    *   **Ugly:** The Council elders. They secretly trade luxury furs to the Folk for rare herbs the Bank desires, bypassing Duke's taxes.

#### **The Seasonal Village Headmen**
*   **Lakewatch (Fishing):** **Jaromir,** an old sailor. **Problem:** His village is first hit by Grobi ice-raids.
*   **Highlake (Mining):** **Berti,** a pragmatic foreman. **Problem:** His town sits on a Folk burial site; constant "accidents."
*   **Timberfalls (Logging):** **Gunther,** Strun's nephew. **Problem:** Arrogant, ignores Folk warnings, bringing danger to his people.
*   These leaders are the **ultimate "Ugly"** for the Duke. Helping them is essential, but each request pulls resources and triggers conflict with another faction (the Guild, the Folk, the Church).

### **The Folk Clans (Fasgrangar) - Not a Monolith**
*   **The Good (Potential Allies):** **The Moss-Crown Clan.**
    *   **Leader:** **Anya Moss-Crown.** Seeks a lasting peace. Will trade healing herbs and guide safe paths through forests in exchange for a *guaranteed* sanctuary forest, untouched by axes.
    *   **Faustian Bargain:** This alliance would cripple the lumber industry, enrage Strun and his workers, and cost the Duke vital revenue and stability.
*   **The Bad (Active Enemies):** **The Shattered-Tusk Clan.**
    *   **Leader:** **Jorund Shattered-Tusk.** Blames all marchers and friendly Folk for a past massacre. Engages in total war: burning camps, poisoning wells. Wants civilisation and all the Eclessians gone.
    *   **Faustian Bargain:** The Duke could hire the Blackjack mercenaries to hunt them, but that would blur the lines of "civilization" he claims to uphold and empower the bank.
*   **The Ugly (Manipulators):** **The Ghost-Foot Clan.**
    *   **Leader:** **The Weavers (a council of three).** They deal in secrets and ancient magic. They might offer to curse a Grobi horde or cause a rival Eclessian lord to fall ill.
    *   **Faustian Bargain:** Their price is always specific and disturbing: "the first-born child of the next season," "a night to dance in the Duke's great hall wearing the faces of his guards," or "the right to take a 'wrongdoer' from your stocks each year." Their help always creates a deeper, weirder debt.

### **The Grobi - The External "Bad" with Internal Dynamics**
*   **The Good (Traders):** **The Stone-Tooth Tribe.** A small, cunning clan. They trade worked stone, southern metals, and intelligence on larger hordes in exchange for grain and wool. Led by **Grakka Stone-Tooth.** Their allegiance is fragile; if the Duke appears weak, they'll join the siege.
*   **The Bad (The Main Horde):** **The Bloody-Sun Warband.** A massive, chaotic coalition of dozens of tribes united under **Warlord Ghor the Consumed,** a Grobi possessed by a ruin-demon. They want total destruction and the warm city for themselves.
*   **The Ugly (The Schemers):** **The Pale-Skin Clan.** Grobi who live in the deepest ruins. They are sickly, intelligent, and wield strange magic. They whisper to Ghor, urging war, but their goal is not conquest—it's to drown Grenzburg in enough blood to perform a ritual to awaken their sleeping "god-machines" in the ruins. They are the *true* long-term threat.

### **Putting It All Together: A Sample Winter Crisis**
The **Bloody-Sun Horde** is massing. The Duke (**Good**) cannot defeat them head-on.
*   He could appeal to the **Church (Ugly)** to bless the walls and rally the people. But Bishop Konrad will demand the right to burn "heathens" (including friendly Folk and the Stone-Tooth grobi) and seize Strun's lumber lands for the Church.
*   He could take a massive loan from **Medori Bank (Ugly)** to hire Eclessian mercenaries. Factor Silas will demand the Blackjacks be given command of the walls (undermining Sir Gerhold) and a monopoly on all artifact recovery.
*   He could make a deal with the **Ghost-Foot Clan (Ugly)** to curse Warlord Ghor. The Weavers will demand a "wrongdoer" – who turns out to be the Duke's own, loyal but corrupt, Bailiff Hagen. Delivering him undermines Baroness Solveig's authority and exposes the Duke's own justice as a sham.

Every choice saves the city while mortgaging its soul, empowering a dangerous ally, and setting the stage for the next, more complex crisis. This is the relentless, gritty politics of the Rule of Three in action.
