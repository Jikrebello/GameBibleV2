# **ACT III: SPRING'S BITTER ROOTS**
## **The Thaw of Betrayal, The Seed of Tomorrow**

The ice cracks, the snow melts into mud and blood. Grenzburg is wounded, victorious, and reeling from the theft of the Heart-Stone. Spring doesn't bring renewal; it exposes the rot.

Act III is the reckoning phase: the siege is over, but every wartime bargain now demands payment. The player follows the stolen Heart-Stone back beneath the Barrow of the First Chieftain and decides what kind of peace Grenzburg deserves.

---

## **PART 1: THE UNEARTHED BETRAYAL (The First Thaw)**

The city is a tangle of grief, suspicion, and opportunistic power grabs. With the external siege broken, the internal siege begins.

**The Aftermath:**
*   **The Traitor's Shadow:** Depending on who stole the Stone, a cold purge begins.
    *   **If the Bank Lost It:** **Silas Medori** is recalled in disgrace. **Captain Valdemar Koss** is dead—killed "resisting" the Silent Partners' extractors. **Ledger-Master Lucia Medori** is now the acting Factor, her reformist dreams poisoned by the need for brutal security. The Blackjacks are under a cloud of distrust.
    *   **If the Church Lost It:** **Bishop Konrad** declares a state of spiritual emergency. **Sister Marcellina** is missing, presumed a traitor or martyr. **Deacon Ignatius** is excommunicated in absentia, his Ashen Band now a rogue heretical cult. **Prelate Caestus** is elevated, but his church is shattered.
    *   **If the Folk Lost It:** Retaliation sweeps the Marches. **Holger Strun** and the settlers demand a crusade against "all tree-worshippers." The **Moss-Crown Clan** retreats, treaty broken. **Elof of the Chapel-Folk** is imprisoned by **Baron Anders of Hochfeld** as a hostage. The land itself feels hostile.
    *   **If the Duke Lost It:** Gerhold locks down the Manse while Leviticus remains missing. The valid authorization on the vault makes him appear to be a willing traitor. The Duke grants the player one final warrant to determine whether law, coercion, or corruption opened the door.

**The Investigation - A Web of Lies:**
The player is one of the few who understands the true scale of the threat. The Stone must be found before the Pale-Skins' ritual is complete. But every faction has its own agenda.

**1. The Thread of Silver (The Bank's Ledger).**
*   **If the Bank was the victim,** Lucia Medori needs deniable agents to track the Silent Partners' operatives. The trail leads through shell companies and false manifests to a seemingly abandoned mine shaft known to **Lady Katarina**.
*   **If the Bank was the thief,** the player must navigate a city where the Medori act with impunity. The counting-house route can be breached through Clerk Vesta's secret files or Enzo Voss's smuggler tunnels.

**2. The Thread of Faith (The Church's Schism).**
*   **If the Church was the victim,** Prelate Caestus, now acting Bishop, tasks you with finding Marcellina or Ignatius. The search leads to hidden catacombs beneath the cathedral, where you find **Marcellina**—alive, but her mind scoured by a Pale-Skin psychic attack. She babbles clues in the form of liturgical references that point to the **Ghost-Foot Clan**.
*   **If the Church was the thief,** the city is gripped by Ignatius's fiery sermons broadcast from hiding. He claims the Stone as a "divine scourge." To find him, you must follow the trail of his "miracles"—places where the populace has been whipped into a fervor so extreme it leaves them physically drained, a perversion of the Stone's power.

**3. The Thread of Stone (The Land's Anger).**
*   **If the Folk were blamed,** you must brave settler lynch mobs to find the truth. The **Ghost-Foot Weavers** contact you. They did not take the Stone; they tried to stop the theft and failed. One of their own is dying from the encounter. They offer a guide to the "place where the earth's blood turns cold"—the Pale-Skin nexus—in exchange for saving their kin (requiring the **Drake's Heart Gem** for healing).
*   **General Clue:** All investigations reveal the same geographical focal point: the region around and **beneath** the **Barrow of the First Chieftain**. The land is sick. Plants grow twisted, animals are born malformed, and the few remaining Folk report "the song from below" is now a deafening scream.

**4. The Thread of Blood (The Duke's Justice).**
*   **If the Duke was betrayed,** his personal warrant permits investigation of the Council, Iron Ledger, and ducal vault staff.
    *   Leviticus is found alive in Katarina's abandoned service tunnel with the forged escrow order and the Bank's threat to cancel grain credit.
    *   Elara or Zafir can prove the "neutral custody" clause is fraudulent. Leviticus admits he authorized the transfer, believing compliance would prevent famine.
    *   Katarina accepted payment to provide a covert route. The Silent Partners double-crossed both of them and delivered the Stone to Pale-Skin agents.
    *   Publishing the evidence and allowing Leviticus to testify redeems him and preserves the Duke's Path. Executing or concealing him remains playable but weakens the final coalition.

**The Converging Path:** All evidence leads to the same horrific conclusion: The Pale-Skins are not in some distant ruin. They are directly below the Barrow, in a pre-human geothermal cavern system—the **Dragon's Tomb**. They are using the stolen Heart-Stone as a psychic drill to crack the tomb's seal. The ritual is in its final stages.

---

## **PART 2: DESCENT INTO THE CHANTING DARK**

Time has run out. The player must rally a final expedition to descend into the underworld and stop the apocalypse. Who they can bring depends entirely on the alliances they've forged and the trust they've earned.

**Assembling the Company:**
*   **The Military:** **Sir Gerhold** will give you a squad of his best Marchwardens, but only if you've proven loyal to the Duchy over any faction. **Sergeant Liese** and her scouts will guide you, but only if you spared the Folk and valued the land.
*   **The Factions:** You can request aid, but it comes with strings.
*   **The Bank (Lucia):** Offers a squad of ruthless, well-equipped Blackjacks answerable to [[Ledger-Master Lucia Medori]]. The price: any non-Stone artifacts recovered are Bank property.
    *   **The Church (Caestus):** Sends a contingent of sober, determined Hospitallers (not zealots). The price: you must recover and sanctify any stolen relics.
    *   **The Folk:** The **Ghost-Foot** will send a single, silent Weaver. The **Moss-Crown** will send a guide. The price: a sworn vow to destroy the Stone, not repurpose it.
    *   **The Industry Lords:** **Lady Katarina**, if exposed and desperate, offers her personal delve team and their expertise with ruins. The price: immunity for her crimes and the right to study the Tomb (non-negotiable).
*   **The Duke:** If you retain his mandate, he gives you his signet ring and orders the Stone destroyed. On the canonical route, Leviticus accompanies the support column as a witness rather than a condemned traitor.

The field party contains the player plus three active companions. Additional loyal NPCs and faction forces support the approach but do not occupy companion slots.

**The Descent:**
The journey into the **Dragon's Tomb** has four locked encounter layers.
1.  **The Corrupted Barrow:** physical breach and warped guardians; Runa or Skrit can reveal safer approaches.
2.  **The Ossuary of Despair:** soul-route pressure and false absolution; Bernard's Chalice provides the clean solution.
3.  **The Geothermal Veins:** ritual clauses, machine locks, and psychic fractures; Elara or Zafir can open the lawful route.
4.  **The Machine-God's Carcass:** Dismas, Lotte, or allied forces hold the approach while the active party enters the heart-cage.

**The Ritual:**
The **Hollow-Kings**, ancient Pale-Skin ruler-intelligences now more spectral than solid, surround the stolen **Heart-Stone**, suspended in a nexus fed by hundreds of implanted **blood-stones**. They are not Grobi and not Ogres; they have cultivated that confusion while weaponizing Grobi hunger. The ritual has two stages:
*   **Stage 1:** Use the amplified death-energies of the siege (stored in the Stone) to crack the Dragon's core.
*   **Stage 2:** Feed on the released primal power to achieve metamorphosis.

The chamber is filled with Pale-Skin fanatics and psychic projections of their ideal future—a silent, grey world where only their transcendent hunger exists.

---

## **PART 3: THE DRAGON'S HEART, THE DUKE'S PEACE**

The final battle is a three-front war: **Physical** (against the Pale-Skin guardians), **Psychic** (against the disorienting projections and fear effects), and **Ritual** (disrupting the blood-stone network).

**The Choice of Weapons:**
*   **The Drake's Heart Gem:** The perfect counter. It can be used to shatter blood-stones, create zones of purifying life-energy, or be detonated to disrupt the ritual entirely (destroying this priceless artifact).
*   **The Duke's Authority:** The signet ring can be used to command the loyalty of any Grenzburg soldiers present, steadying their minds against fear.
*   **Factional Powers:** Each allied faction provides a unique boon. Bank Blackjacks can sabotage the stone's physical housing. Hospitallers can create wards. Folk Weavers can unravel the psychic weave. Katarina's delvers understand the machine-structure.

**The Confrontation with the Hollow-Kings:**
They are not brute monsters. They are intellectuals of hunger. They offer a final, terrifying bargain as you fight: **"Join us. We are the next step. The Duke will use you and discard you. The Bank will own you. The Church will fear you. We will make you eternal. Give us the Drake's Gem, and we will share the feast of godhood."**

**The Climax:**
To win, the players must shatter the Heart-Stone or sever its connection to the Dragon's core. This likely requires a sacrifice:
*   Destroying the **Drake's Heart Gem**.
*   One of the allied NPCs (a loyal Marchwarden, a brave Hospitaller, the Ghost-Foot Weaver) sacrificing themselves to overload the circuit.
*   **Optional Katarina Hijack:** available only if Industry concession is 3 or higher, Katarina remains unexposed, and she joins the descent. It is an alternate corruption branch, never part of the canonical Duke's Path.

As the Stone shatters, the Hollow-Kings shriek and unravel, their formless hunger dissipating. The Dragon's carcass shudders and goes permanently, peacefully dark. The psychic scream silencing is almost deafening.

**The Return & The Reckoning:**

You emerge into the spring sun. The land feels different—the twisted sickness is receding.

**Epilogue - The New Balance:**
The ending is defined by your alliances, your final choices, and the state of the factions.

*   **The Duke's Path (The Sovereign's Peace, canonical):** The Drake's Heart Gem is sacrificed and the Heart-Stone destroyed. Leviticus testifies and remains in office under audit. The Duke limits Bank privileges, recognizes the Great Sanctuary, confines Church coercive power to ordinary law, and reforms Blackjack service through ducal courts. The player is freed from unilateral Bank control and offered the wardenship of the Southern Marches.
*   **The Bank's Path (The Iron Peace):** With Koss and the Silent Partners checked, **Lucia Medori** governs the branch. Grenzburg becomes a ruthlessly efficient mercantile city-state. Debt remains law, and the player receives rank in the Blackjack Executive Corps—well-paid, respected, and still owned.
*   **The Church's Path (The Redeemed Peace):** **Caestus**, as Bishop, leads a reformation. The Church turns inwards, focusing on charity and community. The Ashen Band is disbanded. A new chapel is built in partnership with **Elof's people**. Grenzburg becomes a beacon of hard-won faith and tolerance. Your sins are absolved, not just legally, but spiritually. You are granted a plot of land and a quiet life, should you want it.
*   **The Folk's Path (The Old Peace):** With the Pale-Skins and the Heart-Stone destroyed, the land begins to heal. The treaties are rewritten on Folk terms. Human expansion is strictly limited. Grenzburg shrinks, becoming a trading outpost rather than a conquering city. You are granted the freedom of the forests, a friend to the clans, but a stranger in the city you saved. Your debt is forgotten by men, but the land remembers what you owe it.
*   **The Solitary Path (The Warden's Exile):** If you played all sides against the middle, betrayed everyone, and seized power or destroyed the Stone for yourself, the outcome is bleakly pragmatic. No faction trusts you, but all fear you. You leave Grenzburg with your freedom, your scars, and the knowledge that you saved a city that will never thank you. You become a legend on the frontier—a ghost they blame for bad luck and hail in desperate times.

**Final Scene:**
The camera pulls back on Grenzburg. The walls are scarred but stand. The lake is thawed. Smoke rises from chimneys. In a field outside the walls, a single, normal spring flower pushes through the charred earth. It is bright yellow, and its centre holds a drop of clean dew.

The story of Grenzburg is not over. Winter will come again. Grobi will hunger. Factions will scheme. But for this season, there is peace. You earned it. You defined it.

**THE END**
