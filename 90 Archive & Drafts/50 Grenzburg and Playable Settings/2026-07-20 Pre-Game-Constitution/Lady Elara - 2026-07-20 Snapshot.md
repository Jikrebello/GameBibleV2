# Lady Elara

## Core Concept

Elara is a minor noble widow-to-be whose betrothed, Lord Martyn Valerius, died while investigating the Bank's hidden contract clauses. She joins to pursue justice without destroying the city that depends on the institution she suspects.

## Five Decisive Beats

1. **The Widow's Charge:** investigate Martyn's death and enter a Bank vault under warrant or deception.
2. **The Map Fragment:** survive a Pale-Skin attempt to silence the investigation.
3. **The Gilded Shroud:** recover the artifact beneath Highlake and learn it reveals the true terms of magic and contract.
4. **The Final Suspects:** test the Bank, Church, and Duke without allowing any faction to seize the Shroud.
5. **Controlled Exposure:** prove Lucia Medori ordered Martyn's death to conceal the soul clause, then expose the Silent Partners while ring-fencing ordinary deposits and grain credit.

## Canonical Outcome

Elara chooses justice with continuity rather than silence or indiscriminate collapse. Her evidence breaks the Silent Partners' local authority and helps lower Bank Leverage, while lawful credit survives under ducal audit.

## Final Dungeon Solution

The Gilded Shroud reveals false clauses, concealed ritual anchors, and the exact weakness in the Heart-Stone's imposed covenant.
