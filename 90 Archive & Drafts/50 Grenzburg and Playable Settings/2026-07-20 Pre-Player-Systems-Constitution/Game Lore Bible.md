BASE PLAYER CORE SKILLS 

## UNARMED 

All unarmed attacks deal non-lethal damage so reducing an enemy’s health bar with unarmed attacks will knock them out in the same way that the knock-out stealth skill works. The player can turn this non-lethal damage into lethal damage by equipping certain gloves, gauntlets or rings. 

LIGHT ATTACK 

One-Two Punch: Performs a right-hook left-hook string uppercut overhead swing combo string. 

Jaw Breaker: An advanced form of One-Two Punch that strengthens the chain of one-handed attacks. 

## HEAVY ATTACK 

Three-Four Kick: Performs a right leg sweep. 

Ankle Breaker: An advanced form of Knee Breaker that adds a right mid kick after the sweep and then a left leg mid quickly afterwards. 

BLOCK 

Tightens guard and slows movement but blocks most damage received. If blocking perfectly, receives no damage and parries an attack back to the attacker and staggers them. 

SHEATHE 

Guard Up: Raises arms up in a defensive boxer pose. 

UNSHEATHE 

Guard Down: Lowers arms from the defensive boxer pose. 

## GRAB 

Grab: When near monsters, walls or objects, the player will perform a Grab like action that will attach the player to it. Grabbing will allow for climbing or holding down of the monster, or hold items such as explosive barrels that can then be thrown in front of them. 

## JUMP 

Vault: Executes a jump to a higher location. 

## SPRINTING (FREE RUNNING) 

Sprint: Dashes forwards with much more speed than a regular run, automatically sheathing any weapon if unsheathed and limiting rotation whilst adding momentum in a forwards direction. 

Forward Bound: Whilst Sprinting will launch into a forward jump that on land will turn into a roll and then go back to normal walking. If Bum Slide is pressed whilst in the air, then the forward roll transitions into a Bum Slide with the same momentum as the sprint instead of the normal walk. 

Bum Slide: Drops into a Bum Slide that over time will decrease in speed and momentum on the horizontal but maintain it if on a slope. It costs no stamina to perform. If sliding Vault is pressed, then the player pushes back on the ground and starts to Sprint again. 

Wall Run: When Sprinting near a wall parallel to the player and Grabbing, the player will attach to the wall and proceed to run along it in an arch. If Sprinting towards a wall that’s perpendicular to the player if the player goes into a Forward Bound, the player will run up the wall and try and to automatically grab at any ledges in reach before dropping down. If Vault is pressed again whilst running up, then the player will push away from the wall and in the opposite direction to where they came. 

## STEALTH 

Knock-out: When in stealth and behind an enemy undetected, the player will bash the target with their fists, knocking out the target and not killing them. The wakeup time of the target is scaled by the level of the user’s stealth proficiency. 

Assassinate: When in stealth and behind an enemy undetected, the player if unarmed will strike the target/ attack with their weapon, instant-killing most humanoid and small enemies, and dealing large amount of damage to bigger targets and monsters. The damage is determined by your stealth skill and a percentage of your Strength. 

Pickpocket: When in stealth and near an enemy undetected, the player can attempt to pick the targets pocket or perform a special action like tying their feet together, fastening their weapons making it difficult for them to draw them or planting an item on them (like a timed bomb or a poison with various effects like slow or sleep) or take an item away from them. The type of items that can be lifted from targets will change (from small things like keys and rings, to larger things like weapons) and the chance of being discovered decreased in accordance with pickpocket proficiency.

## CLASS IMPLEMENTATION (COMBAT) 

In character creation, the player will choose a class to play as. Each class has restrictions on what can and cannot be equipped and used in combat, as well as base stats that increases on each level up. These base stat level ups are geared towards the intended play style of the class in question and will favour certain stats more than others. For example, the fighter class is going to focus more on the Strength and Health base stats compared to the warlock who’s going to focus more on the Magick Defence and Stamina base stats. Each class also has its own level up rank that is levelled independently from the players global experience amount and is levelled based on a percentage of all experience earned by the player. 

The player will be allowed to unlock the other classes using the same currency as what is used to unlock new skills and augments. Once a new class has been unlocked, all associated proficiencies with the class will be permanently unlocked and will be able to be equipped and used. This will lead to the option of players being able to wield a sword and shield as a warlock for example. However, stats on level up will still be on the warlock class, leading to less and less effectiveness in swords and shield as the base stats aren’t in favour of these weapons that require strength. Another thing would be penalties applied to casting time if wearing heavy armour as a warlock. This can be somewhat mitigated by choosing to select the option of a “mixed” 

level up. In this case, between Warlock and Fighter, the mixed level up will be a rounded average of the stat gains on level up, rounded down. This mixed option will only be available between 2 classes. 

For example, the player starts as a Warlock, gaining War-wands and light armour as proficiencies. After some levels in Warlock, and having used some of the class currency to unlock some further upgrades on skills and some new skills, the player decides to save up so they can unlock the fighter class. Upon unlocking the fighter class, the player can now equip swords and shields, as well as equip heavy armour. The player now also has 3 options for how stat levelling should work. Either they choose to continue the Warlock stat gains, which are heavily focused on Magick Defence and Stamina, or they can swap over to the Fighter stat gains, which focus more on Strength and Physical Defence. However, the third option would be to choose the hybrid level option, in which the gains for all 4 stats are lower, but more balanced, allowing for the player to still make their character build work, but not be as strong stats wise as they would’ve been if they focused on a single class. After reaching certain milestones within a particular class, the player will be presented with the option to select particular talents, which will slightly augment or change how the class is played, modifying how equipment is used or adding additional effects to skills. Talents can also be gained as a part of certain quest rewards, by reading certain books and training with special NPC’s. 

Miscellaneous proficiencies cannot be increased with class currency, but instead require a trainer to unlock the rank and then use to level up. For example, a fighter can go to a lock-smithing trainer and pay money to unlock the first rank of lock-smithing to be available. In order to level up to the next rank, the player will then have to go out into the world and pick locks and traps of that level or lower. These trainers will give a special quest lines that will test the player on their skills and successfully completing it will reward the player with a special item or ability.

## SOCIAL STATS, SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE 

At character creation, the player will be prompted to select a primary and secondary stat as a focus for Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma or Luck or decide to choose a single stat to focus on. For example, Intelligence being the primary and Wisdom being the secondary, or the player choosing a single stat like having Luck as both the primary and secondary stat. 

The base number of the stat will passively increase every odd level by an amount marked by the multiplier, with 2 on the primary and 1 on the secondary or 3 if a single stat is chosen. The stats that aren’t selected for primary or secondary won’t increase passively, but can still be increased through certain quest rewards or by reading certain books. When a situation demands a roll needs to be made, either actively such as when in dialogue or passively such as when out in the world, the threshold for success or failure for that particular roll will be based off one of the four stats and sometimes multiple separate rolls will be made. 

After choosing your stats, the player will then choose a background knowledge and a culture knowledge. These knowledge’s will unlock certain dialogue options to explore and the depth of these options is determined by an active Intelligence roll and then a Luck roll afterwards. 

## ACTIVE ROLLS 

Any time a player is able to use (Intimidate/ Coercion), (Persuasion/ Diplomacy), (Bluff/ Deception), (Intuition/ Insight), (Background Knowledge) or (Cultural Knowledge) in dialogue it’ll be understood that an active stat roll will be made. 

Passing active rolls will usually open up a new branch in dialogue or add a new solution that can be pursued, sometimes even bypassing certain things that would’ve been tricky or annoying to deal with otherwise. For example, passing the Charisma check could mean convincing the town guard that there’s a fight at the tavern and therefore he needs to leave his post, giving you more time to sneak around the museum uninterrupted before the next shift. Failing these rolls means having options locked or limited in a way that will 

inconvenience the player. For example, not being able to convince the guard to leave his post, he tells you to piss off and also quietly alerts the other guards that something is suspicious and therefore should be extra weary in the museum. 

Charisma rolls will be made to influence others and bend them to the players will/ or requests by using social skills like (Intimidate/ Coercion), (Persuasion/ Diplomacy) or (Bluff/ Deception). 

Wisdom rolls will be made using (Intuition/ Insight) to discern information about what NPC’s haven’t said in dialogue or to understand the double speak being said, along with non-verbal communication like body language etc. 

Intelligence rolls will be made when using (Background Knowledge), (Cultural Knowledge) or general logical solutions, reasoning, as well as general knowledge, with the success of the (Background Knowledge) or (Cultural Knowledge) roll determining how much information is revelled to add additional context to conversations or solutions that can be logically solved. 

Luck rolls are different in that they will always be rolled after an Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma roll has been rolled. If the first roll was a fail, then the Luck roll will roll afterwards, and if that’s a success it will lessen the first failed rolls repercussions, but if it also failed then allowing the full result of the failure to happen. If the first roll was a success, then the Luck roll afterwards on a success will enhance the result to be even more desirable but if it fails then there is just the normal success. 

## PASSIVE ROLLS 

When travelling around in the world there will occasionally be situations where a roll will automatically be made. Luck rolls will be linked to finding secret areas, short cuts, spotting rare loot and other environmentally contextual points of interest. Other times, when a quest objective is presented or completed, there will be either a Wisdom or Intelligence roll to determine other possible objectives and additional secondary objectives or ways to complete the quest that would’ve been locked out otherwise. 

## PERSONALITY QUIRKS 

At character creation the player can decide whether or not they wish to begin being neutral between the seven sins and virtues or have a tendency towards either the one or the other. These quirks will affect dialogue, quest’s primary and secondary objectives, progression and rewards by having a chance to lock out options that are not congruent with the tendency it relates to. For example, if the character has a tendency towards being greedy, then the option to do something for free or accept a lower pay or a reward later has a chance to be locked out, thereby enforcing the player to choose the options that align with being greedy like extorting the quest giver for more money or taking the fetch quest object as well as the normal reward as payment. 

If the player decided not to choose a tendency at the beginning, then all options in both dialogue and quests will be open, and the choices made will then slowly increase tendency either way. These quirks will be based off the seven deadly sins and their seven virtuous counterparts as follows. 

- Lust or Chastity 

- Gluttony or Temperance 

- Greed or Charity 

- Sloth or Diligence 

- Wrath or Forgiveness 

- Envy or Kindness 

- Pride or Humility

## ARROW CRAFTING 

By collecting certain materials from enemies and the world, the hunter can craft various types of special arrows that are limited compared to the unlimited base arrows, but have different behaviours that give the hunter the edge they need. 

1) Magick Infused Arrow: This arrow has been infused with basic magick, and now deal magick damage. 

2) Blast Arrow: An explosive pack of gunpowder has been attached to the end of this arrow, resulting in a huge explosion of physical damage and knockback, with a chance to set enemies on fire. 

3) Blunt Head Arrow: This arrow has a rounded end to it, dealing double damage to armour as well as temporarily knocking out humans if hit on the head from stealth. 

4) Sapper Arrow: This arrow deals no damage upon impact but once lodged into an enemy, slowly drains the life force of the target for a certain amount. Once the arrow is “ripe”, can be shot again for an explosive blast dealing the same amount of damage in health taken. 

5) Petrifying Arrow: This cursed arrow deals minimal damage, but has a chance to afflict the target with the petrified status effect, after which will slowly turn the enemy to stone. Once an enemy has been turned to stone, a single hit of either magick or physical damage will kill them instantly. 

6) Flare Arrow: Fires an arrow of magickal light to illuminate the area. The flash it creates surprises foes and damages the undead.

# QUICK SLOT INVENTORY 

The Quick Slot Inventory is a slot of various items that are organised according to the required functionality. Will be able to move between the different slots with the up and down arrows on the d-pad, and the left and right arrows to cycle between the items in the slot. Long pressing on the down arrow will equip/ use/ activate the item, and long pressing the up will unequip/ deactivate it. The slots will revolve cycle. 

## CONSUMABLES SLOT 

This slot will contain all the health/ stamina/ buffs and antidote consumables in the characters belt bag. Will be organised in clumps and organised by weakest to strongest. 

## SKILL LOADOUT SLOT 

This slot will hold the currently activated skill loadout, and the player will be able to cycle between it and a second one. A skill loadout holds the button layout and selection of weapon skills chosen by the player, and by allowing them to swap between two makes it possible to drastically adapt to their current situation and allow for more skills to be used from the available unlocked skills they’ve acquired.

This slot will exclusively be available to the hunter vocation and will be used to craft/ equip special arrows. Equipping the currently selected arrows will be a long down press and to unequip them a long up press. To craft more of that type will be another long press of the button.

THE GROBI 

OVERVIEW 

The Grobi race can be roughly split into roughly 4 groups, and within each group a further division is made between those “born as” and those who “grew into”. The Grobi race is defined by many things, but its hunger and voracious appetite usually comes to mind for the humans who have traditionally fought against them. 

Grobi will eat almost anything and anyone, and whole villages and small human towns have disappeared to the appetite of a successful Grobi attack, all the more terrifying to see that the next town to face them will face an even stronger force due to the Grobi’s unique ability to grow entire size category’s due to their fast breeding and metabolism. 

## GOBLINS 

The proto-race of the Grobi, a fully grown goblin is roughly the same height as a prepubescent human child and weighs slightly less. Goblins are the weakest of the all Grobi, but also the hardiest. A goblin can survive on the least amount of food out of the four subraces, and can meaningfully metabolize almost anything edible including rotting flesh and vegetation. They also breed at an exception rate, and are in extreme situations observed to cannibalise themselves in order to survive. 

The nature of a goblin is cowardly, and will only directly attack something bigger and stronger than themselves with overwhelming numbers or from ambushes and traps where their target is unaware or incapacitated. A well-fed goblin, like all members of their race, can regenerate limbs and recover from otherwise fatal wounds in a very short manner of time, often surprising their assailant by recovering and subsequently counterattacking after a seemly fatal blow cut off an arm or a gash disembowelled them moments prior. This is due to the metabolism of the Grobi being so efficient that they can convert muscle and fat mass on their body into usable resources for regeneration far more efficiently than any other creature otherwise observed. 

If a goblin is consistently able to consume enough food to surpass their baseline bodily needs to function, any food eaten after is stored onto or built onto the body. A 1kg of meat can be converted into roughly 0.5kg of muscle with little to no physical effort required. A goblin that eats enough to build their body in such a manner can grow into what is called a Hobgoblin. A Hobgoblin is the biggest a natural born goblin can grow and stands on par with an average human and weighs slightly more. If a Goblin that has grown into a Hobgoblin is unable to continue eating to maintain the hobgoblin baseline, the body will go into starvation mode and begin shrinking itself back to the smaller goblin. This can also be a conscious choice where the process is accelerated and the body shrinks itself very quickly, turning the Hobgoblin back into a Goblin after passing out and then waking up surrounded in faeces as the body rapidly ejects all waste products during the transformation. 

If a Goblin that has grown into a Hobgoblin breeds with another Goblin that has also grown into a Hobgoblin or another true born Hobgoblin, then the offspring will be a fully-fledged Hobgoblin and cannot ever be a goblin. 

## HOBGOBLINS 

Hobgoblins are either born as full-fledged Hobgoblins or emerge as grown goblins with an abundance of food. They are bigger and stronger than Goblins and are the closest match physically and mentally to the average human. Although only second weakest out of all Grobi, they make up for it in being arguably the smartest compared to the other sub-groups and the most capable in terms of technology, warfare arms and armaments an even a basic form of savage magic called Voodoo. Most Grobi warbands consist of majority Hobgoblin born rank and file soldiers with Goblins used as scouts and cannon fodder and being supplemented by Orc and Ogre shock troops. 

Hobgoblin culture is focused around the military as its only with a decent military and training of soldiers that they can forage and hunt for food that sustains them. It is usually during this point that various tribes will develop and fight among each other for control of food and resources, with the successful war chiefs and generals being able to eat enough food to grow into Orcs. 

## ORCS 

Orcs only appear in successful Hobgoblin and Goblin warbands as the amount of food needed to become them only comes from successful group work to collect food. Growing into an Orc turns a Hobgoblin into a powerful killing machine, taller than the average human by half a metre to a metre taller and weighing double to triple the weight of an average human male. Of course, the same thing that applies to Hobgoblins and Goblins applies to Hobgoblins and Orcs, and “grown into” Orcs can reverse back to Hobgoblins and can also breed true Orcs with other Orcs. 

An Orc can easily fight up to 3 human men at once without an issue due to their strength, size and speed. As a result of eating as much food as they need to maintain their bodies, born Orcs spend most of their time fighting, eating or sleeping whilst “grown into” Orcs are the heads of the Hobgoblin warbands as war chiefs and Generals and are much more dangerous due to their intellect and strategy that came from their Hobgoblin origins. 

## OGRES 

If an Orc is ever able to consume enough food to grow into an Ogre, then it is truly a sight to behold. These literal giants tower 3 or even 4 metres above an average human and weigh close to a ton. At this point they reach a critical point, and become the least intelligent of all Grobi as their insatiable hunger forces them to devote all their time and energy to eat almost half their own body weight every other day or so and sleeping. Ogres can breed with other Ogres to breed true Ogres, and many have speculated that there is another almost mythological 5<sup>th</sup> subrace of Grobi giant that has yet to be encountered by humans.

Major world events timeline 

- The Lord creates Heaven and the prime elemental planes. 

- The Lord creates the angels and Eden. 

- The creates the animals and Man. 

- The Adversary rebels over the creation of humans (being proud and refusing to defer to beings less perfect than himself) and starts a war that divides heaven. 

- The Lord creates hell and banishes most of the adversaries forces there. The Adversary splits himself into multiple aspects and so only certain parts of him get banished as well. 

- The Adversary convinces/ deceives man to sin and fall out of spite as well, forcing the lord to remove Man from Eden and instead into the Mundane world, a no man’s land between heaven and hell. 

- Angels and demons flock to the material world as well, to escape from the lord and banishment into hell, at the behest of the adversary or for their own personal reasons (like feeling sorry for Man and trying to help). 

- The Menhir and the Idols attach themselves to aspects of the physical world and become involved in the affairs of man, with certain Menhir and Idols becoming Pretenders. 

- Certain Pretenders sin against The Lord and teach man the art of construction, agriculture and war. 

- Other Pretenders also profligate among themselves as well as sleep with and have children with mankind, eventually creating a hierarchy of primary, secondary and even tertiary Pretenders. 

- The Pretenders deceive and cajole Man into worshiping them as gods instead of the Lord, granting those who do power, wealth and other physical trappings in exchange for physical and spiritual sacrifices that sustain them. 

- The Grobi, Chimera and Grigori are created at this time as the interbreeding between humans, animals and themselves reaches record high levels. 

- Eventually the Lord appears to one of the last bloodlines of Man that has stayed pure and still follows and worships him and proclaims that the Lord will bless his bloodline with a son who will bring war to the Adversary and return Man back to the Lord but in order for that day to happen they must lead all those who still love the Lord through the on a great trek through the Great Desert, as the false Reign of the Pretenders is ending and they need to escape its destruction. 

- The Great Prophet brings as many of those who’ll listen to him together and prepares to lead them through the great desert to escape the destruction. 

- This Exodus triggers a civil war between the Pretenders over their coming day of judgement and fall into factions that call in favours with the Adversary in an attempt to try and save themselves, ask for forgiveness from the Lord and repent, and those who attempt to save themselves via their own means.
