# Greater-Power Garden Balance and Trace Promotion Review

## Purpose and Result

This review compares all sixteen historically placed but unpromoted Infernal, Menhir and Idol episodes against the sixty-five existing roots in [[The Historical Seed Garden]]. It asks whether each candidate adds a new historical pressure and evidence carrier without authenticating a complete supernatural person, realm, court, relic or biography.

Exactly five light traces pass:

- one of six Infernal placements;
- two of six Menhir placements;
- two of four Idol placements;
- no Long-Frontier trace;
- no complete court, dominion, Archmask, Saint, ruler or sovereign event.

Eleven candidates are withheld, not rejected. The five additions bring the Garden from sixty-five to exactly **seventy roots**.

## Carrier and chronological findings

- **Dense carriers:** legal clauses, registers, names, prison records, saint claims, gates, doors, relic authentication and warning texts.
- **Dense period:** the Long Frontier remains closed to this harvest. Its companies, Great Persons, Patron traces and heavenly traces already supply approximately fifteen roots.
- **Underrepresented pressures:** Menhir seasonal law experienced through ordinary work; Menhir mine ecology; Idol harvest rivalry as concrete human history; early infernal custody distinct from copies and offices.
- **Useful physical carriers:** recut public stone, prisoner sale-tags, release marks on lintels, burial cloth, a reserved mine rib, warning rhythm, an unused family grave and incompatible posthumous foundations.
- **Remote-world limit:** a local-only trace beyond verified contact does not balance the known-world Garden merely by being novel. The distant mountain remains withheld until its geography exists.

Promotion therefore does not use a two-per-family quota. It selects the five candidates whose traces remain independently useful when every named supernatural interpretation is doubted.

# III. Five Promoted Traces

## GP-T01 / IP-01 — The Lower Roster Refusal

- **Humble aperture:** burial workers must close a dead specialist's ordinary duties while Giant officials continue issuing work in that person's name.
- **Durable trace:** a dispersed mortuary formula adds **“not for the lower roster”** after the complete name. One pressure-marked tablet tradition separately distinguishes speaker, copied skill and held witness; no surviving fraternity understands all three categories.
- **Historical pressure:** a lesser Giant court used an infernal custody route to hold dead specialists unlawfully and install copied habits into work offices. Burial fraternities attacked the roster house; captives were tortured and killed, and some remained behind a stair sealed from the Material side.
- **Loose growth:** the phrase may have spread through genuine survivors, copied manuals or later mortuary caution. Speaking an omitted full name at one unlocated stair may release only that old duty-chain, but no present site or response is promoted.
- **Identity and promotion boundary:** the formula proves no soul remains imprisoned, no route is open and no infernal owner. Human soul, copied skill, duty office, name record and manifestation remain distinct. It is not Nythalum continuity, Titan recurrence, UH-03's boundary prison or resurrection.

## GP-T02 / MN-P02R — The Marks of Necessary Release

- **Humble aperture:** corpse washers, seed keepers and stable hands need to declare what has been released after emergency winter preservation ends.
- **Durable trace:** related northern communities mark barn, corpse-house and seed-store lintels at midwinter; the same quarry-sign appears on burial cloth and on harnesses excluding animals from winter breeding. The marks state that release has occurred rather than repelling every supernatural rider.
- **Historical pressure:** rulers once prolonged a necessary preservation arrangement to protect stores and authority. A temporary High Hunt seized living people, corpses, seed, food and pregnant animals until the blocked seasonal route reopened. Starvation, abduction, killing and spoiled release followed.
- **Loose growth:** Folk and Ättar-related memories disagree whether one hall opened its stores, diverted the Hunt or sacrificed its neighbors. Current custom may combine several later winters while preserving one real release grammar.
- **Identity and promotion boundary:** no hall, declaration, Rider, Hound, captive, route or Menhir conjunction is promoted. Corpse, seed, egg, pregnancy and animal continuity never imply a quarry of unborn or departed human souls. The trace is not the Hunt of the Escaped Courts, Mother Moon's bargain or a permanent Wild Hunt.

## GP-T03 / MN-P05 — The Middle Knock Is Never Permission

- **Humble aperture:** miners must distinguish natural rock noise, an actual structural warning, a sapient court speaker and an official signal before cutting.
- **Durable trace:** Aversian crews retain a three-part warning rhythm whose middle answer is never accepted as permission to work. Old workings preserve portion niches and an untouched load-bearing rib beside a later illegal cut.
- **Historical pressure:** a Shield-House administration converted one local compact into production law, counterfeited answers, withheld portions and forced prisoners and strikers into dangerous galleries. Officials executed independent interpreters; workers were crushed, buried alive or killed by gas after refuge chambers were sealed.
- **Loose growth:** genuine court guidance, reflexive Stone-Voice responses, ordinary engineering and one courtier's lethal deception may all have occurred. Modern worker societies, engineers, Shield descendants and court claimants preserve interested interpretations.
- **Identity and promotion boundary:** no mine, Shield House, court person, Knocker species, Menhir title or present ownership is promoted. A real warning proves neither moral judgement nor court title. The trace is not the Country Beneath the Rains or a hidden mine nation.

## GP-T04 / ID-P01 — The Victory Recut Four Times

- **Humble aperture:** canal workers and water judges must decide whether later “free tenant” titles descend from conquest, prisoner sale or lawful repair after a river war.
- **Durable trace:** one family of levee reliefs preserves four recutting phases. Prisoner sale-tags fit attachment holes beneath later canal-title plaques, while descendant boat households omit one triumphal verse and read surrendered names before irrigation work.
- **Historical pressure:** a real water seizure became hereditary war. Commanders murdered surrendered people; victory cults erased allies; officials sold families and water rights; displaced descendants turned a true grievance toward later tenants. Separate Wrath, Pride, Greed and Envy architectures are authorial possibilities, not facts proved by the stone.
- **Loose growth:** later accounts compress the sequence into a war among river gods, a single tyrant's reign or a clean dynastic settlement. The original crowns, Masks, survivors and present overlays remain open.
- **Identity and promotion boundary:** no Mask, Idol, dynasty, river crown, casualty total or current property judgement is promoted. The trace does not establish the Sevenfold Feast War, the origin of the Red Tally or Under-Ledger, Ishkatar's fall or a Regnant succession.

## GP-T05 / ID-P03 — The Grave Prepared for No Relic

- **Humble aperture:** a quarry family prepares to receive an executed relative's body while guards, officials and competing burial workers traffic contradictory claims about the corpse.
- **Durable trace:** the family plot contains a deliberately prepared but unused grave. The quarry-town icon platform and road sanctuary were both built after the execution and use incompatible anchor foundations; hostile Lives nevertheless share one prison rota, torture date and set of kin names.
- **Historical pressure:** one obscure human was imprisoned, tortured, ambiguously recorded as recanting and executed. Officials denied the body. Later Pride and Envy cult architectures monopolized the death, coerced relatives, opened graves and fueled clashes that killed pilgrims and custodians.
- **Loose growth:** the executed person may have been a martyr, confessor, recanter, ordinary victim or Saint under a later bounded commission. The location and identity of the body remain open. Either cult may preserve genuine history alongside supernatural fraud.
- **Identity and promotion boundary:** the human soul, body, prepared grave, Lives, claimed relics, dependent Masks and any authentic Saintly manifestation remain separate. No sainthood, damnation, recantation, relic, church claim or Mask identity is promoted. This is not Bruckmoor, the Red Passage, Forty Flames, enemy gate or a lost Companion.
