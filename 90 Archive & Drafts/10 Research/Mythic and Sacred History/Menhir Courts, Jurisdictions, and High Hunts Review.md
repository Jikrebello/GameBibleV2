# Menhir Courts, Jurisdictions, and High Hunts Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Menhir Courts through the Ages]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Purpose and Status

This is the third family review arising from [[Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas]]. It develops the twelve `MN-R` leads without turning the Faewild into one fairy empire, treating court titles as proof of literal monarchy, or making the Menhir into mindless outer gods.

Nothing here is canon, named history, a new creature family or a promoted Garden seed. The review establishes seven `retain` and five incorporated `revise` dispositions. [[First Menhir Constellation - Historical Placement Matrix]] subsequently gives six leads provisional historical soil, and [[First Menhir Constellation - Collision and Disposition Review]] passes three as `retain` and three with incorporated `revise` rulings. The other six remain undated and unplaced; all twelve remain promotion-locked.

The governing result is simple:

> A Menhir court is a local ecology made legible through custom. It is not automatically a state, species, bloodline, church, army, moral faction or detachable piece of a sovereign.

# I. Controlling Distinctions

## Court anatomy

| Layer | Continuity and agency | What “court” means | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Menhir sovereignty** | one continuous intelligent `yolk-sovereignty`, morally blind after fusion | the deeper natural law shaping the jurisdiction | cannot enter Material creation in full, judge moral guilt, forgive sin or become a local king |
| **Sapient court person** | ordinarily `court-law`, with full moral agency once capable of understanding alternatives | household member, mediator, ruler-title, hunter, artisan, fosterer, herald or rival | Menhir pressure does not excuse chosen murder, abduction, enslavement, torture or betrayal |
| **Place-mind or law-organism** | `court-law`, with agency established by maturity and form | the attentive spring, mound, mine, road, cliff, coast or grove around which custom develops | ecological response is not moral verdict or proof of property title |
| **Mythic fauna** | `biological-pattern` or another catalogued continuity | animals bred, gathered or altered around the pressure | dangerous behavior is not automatically culpable; bodily death may be permanent |
| **Manifestation role** | a bounded court-law office or local sovereign expression | king, rider, daughter, guest, champion or beast-shape seen by witnesses | title and appearance do not prove one recurring person |
| **Human pact-lineage or practitioner** | complete `human-soul`, including altered or dream-linked humans | tenant, fosterling, spouse, guide, worker, captive, intermediary or accused witch | crossing, transformation and service do not divide or replace the soul |
| **Counterfeit occupant** | actual continuity: Mask, Patron proxy, residue, constructed pattern, Giant claimant or fraud | an actor using familiar court language | custom, beauty or supernatural power does not convert it into Menhir ecology |

The office-holder, cohort, Faewild ecology and natural law fused into each Menhir. A local courtier is not one surviving angel from that cohort unless a unique dossier establishes an exceptional created spirit. A court can preserve cohort-shaped roles without containing detachable pieces of an angelic army.

## Court title is observational

“King,” “queen,” “host,” “hall,” “household,” “court” and “people under the hill” describe what mortals can negotiate with. They may preserve real rank and relationship among sapient court persons. They do not establish a universal Faewild peerage.

Several courts may answer one Menhir pressure while sharing no government. One court may contain beings of several continuities because a repeated local custom brings them into the same encounter ecology. A court can split when its route moves, its place-mind dies, its ruler is defeated, a new Mask infiltrates the custom, or the underlying Menhir conjunction ends.

## Every bargain still needs four terms

Each reviewed lead must identify:

1. **Gift:** what the local domain can actually do.
2. **Price:** the service, loss, transformation, access or taboo it recognizes.
3. **Boundary:** the place, season, lineage, route or condition in which it operates.
4. **Failure:** the physical or social result when a term is broken or misread.

A courtier can lie about possessing authority. A place-mind may enforce only the boundary. A Menhir may keep the exact shape of a bargain without accepting the human meaning assigned to it. “The court agreed” remains evidence about consequences, never a moral defence.

## Menhir horror

Menhir horror comes from intelligence without moral comprehension, not mindlessness. A Menhir can remember, prefer, bargain, pursue, test and change tactics. It cannot understand why the weakest person's dignity should limit a valid exchange or why natural consequence should yield to mercy.

Mindless disaster-scale computation belongs to [[Great Coherences]], constructed systems and certain other separately classified patterns. MN-R11 therefore concerns an intelligent Menhir pressure deeper than a familiar court. MN-R12 concerns a nonperson Great Coherence whose Material manifestation overlaps a Faewild thin space and is mistakenly treated as an ancestor. They must never be merged.

# II. Source Review

## Irish supernatural polity and mound settlement

The Irish source shelf contributes multiple supernatural peoples, kings, skilled offices, hostages, maiming, battles, strongholds and negotiated coexistence. Mound traditions supply particular apertures and local addresses rather than proof of a planet-wide subterranean fairy nation. *Cath Maige Tuired*, the wider CELT corpus and mound material must remain distinct from Tech Duinn and its separate death-boundary associations. Sources remain indexed in [[Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas#Irish material]].

World Egg use preserves rival courts, changed land access, fosterage, hostage exchange and political aftermath. It does not import the Tuatha Dé Danann, Fomorians, named kings, divine genealogy or a complete conquest cycle.

## Welsh guarded-fortress cycle

*Preiddeu Annwfn* contributes an armed raid for guarded Otherworld spoils, dangerous approach, multiple fortresses and the failure of most raiders to return. It does not supply a generic Celtic Hell. The review removes the fixed “seven returned” title because it was both too close to the source refrain and too close to [[Whole-History Planting Matrix - Blocks I and II#II-H02 — The Seven Who Took the Wrong Road|Seven Who Took the Wrong Road]].

World Egg use preserves the raiders' culpability, the defenders' non-demonic personhood, the useful stolen object and the dead, drowned, captured or transformed majority.

## Scottish Elfland, processions and trial evidence

Scottish material contributes abduction, service, tribute, changelings, healing, nocturnal processions, trance experience, time loss and fatal encounter. “Seelie” is not an alignment category and does not prove benevolence.

Witch-trial records require a second distinction: testimony obtained through fear, leading questions, torture or threat is evidence of prosecution and belief, not transparent reportage of a supernatural event. A World Egg history may contain real court traffic, voluntary service, coerced service, fraud and false accusation at once. It must identify which claim is supported by which evidence.

## Cornish local ecologies

Cornish collections distinguish piskies, spriggans, knockers, Buccas, mermaids, giants and other Small People rather than one faction. The review keeps treasure guardians, mine beings and coastal powers in separate cards. It removes child substitution and general crop blight from the Spriggan card because those harms belong to the wider Cornish shelf and should not be stacked onto one borrowed element without a selected recension.

The Cornish source labels remain research handles. They are not default World Egg creature or polity names.

## Lovecraftian scale mismatch

“The Other Gods” contributes forbidden ascent, an inaccessible high place, familiar local powers fleeing a deeper danger and the catastrophic failure of a human claim to mastery. The transferable element is scale mismatch, not Lovecraft's theology or racial coding.

MN-R11 uses an actual intelligent Menhir pressure beneath a local court. MN-R12 uses a distributed nonperson Shell computation manifested through a Material frame at the same site as a Faewild thin space. Neither creates a blind Menhir, rival creator or universal outer pantheon.

# IV. Reviewed Court and Jurisdiction Nurseries

## MN-R01 — The Mounds of Several Kings

- **Court form:** several neighboring mound, grove or hill-door ecologies whose ruler-titles are held by local sapient court persons.
- **Primary pressure:** preferably one Sylvorn territorial-memory jurisdiction crossed by Hecatara entrances; a final placement may select another established conjunction.
- **Population:** court households, place-minds, fauna, fostered mortals, abducted artisans, hostages, tenant families and counterfeit visitors, each separately classified.
- **Conflict mechanism:** courts close one another's entrances, steal cattle or craft, redirect fertility, seize hostages and mutilate rivals so a public office can no longer be performed.
- **Hard edge:** maiming, abduction, hostage exchange, crop failure, burned settlements and retaliatory killing remain material consequences. A court person's law does not excuse choosing civilians as leverage.
- **World Egg torque:** all courts may truthfully experience one deeper land law while disagreeing about who holds local access. Defeating one king ends one office-holder or manifestation, not the Menhir sovereignty.
- **Collision ruling:** not [[The Court Beneath the Roots]], a Pale-Skin barrow state, the Country Beneath the Rains or a universal Irish-derived Faewild nation.
- **Possible trace:** neighboring mounds accept incompatible fosterage tokens; one maimed office remains vacant; cattle routes still obey a truce human descendants deny.
- **Open:** region, culture, mound number, primary Menhir, rulers, participants, war, human tenants and current access.
- **Source fidelity:** Irish supernatural-polity and mound grammar; retain battle, maiming, skilled offices, hostages and negotiated aftermath while changing names, genealogy, combatants, land and result.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked.

## MN-R02 — The Court That Lost the Surface

- **Court form:** a defeated local court whose Material dwellings were conquered, built over or cut off while its Faewild routes remained operative.
- **Primary pressure:** Hecatara access law joined to Sylvorn territorial memory, or another specifically established route-and-place conjunction.
- **Population:** displaced court persons, route organisms, fosterlings, toll collectors, human occupants above and people living near surviving entrances.
- **Conflict mechanism:** surface rulers possess land and buildings; the court still controls when old paths, wells, cellars or mounds become adjacent to its jurisdiction. It uses toll, seasonal access and fosterage to recover leverage.
- **Hard edge:** people disappear at thresholds, children or adults are taken into service, cattle fail to return, rescue attempts collapse routes and both court and human forces kill occupants treated as collaborators.
- **World Egg torque:** route control is real power but no moral deed. Losing Material land does not make every later occupant a thief; possessing the road below does not confer human title above.
- **Collision ruling:** not a Material underdark civilization, [[Pale-Skins and Hollow-Kings|Pale-Skin polity]], [[The Country Beneath the Rains - First Subterranean Region Chassis|Country Beneath the Rains]] or the existing Folk Court Beneath the Roots.
- **Possible trace:** cellar doors opening by season, fosterage genealogies on both sides, toll objects found far from their entrances and a surface deed that accurately records no underground route.
- **Open:** conqueror, defeated court, site, surviving route, pact, fosterlings, present inhabitants and whether coexistence is possible.
- **Source fidelity:** Irish síd settlement grammar; preserve dispossession, negotiated coexistence, dangerous thresholds and claims surviving political defeat without importing named peoples or a conquest theology.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked.

## MN-R03R — The Raid That Returned One Tool

- **Court form:** one guarded Faewild stronghold with a local population and a bounded tool necessary to its law.
- **Primary pressure:** Hecatara governs approach and return; the fort's tool must answer one separately selected domain rather than generic magic.
- **Human aperture:** a ruler or warband raids for the tool after negotiation fails, is rejected or is never honestly attempted. The expedition is not another signature company.
- **Conflict mechanism:** the raiders cross a dangerous sea, road or threshold, breach the fort and carry away the real tool. The fort closes return routes and captures, drowns, transforms or kills most of them.
- **Hard edge:** the dead remain dead, captives may be enslaved or retained as payment, defenders are killed in their own stronghold and the survivors' useful prize does not make the raid righteous.
- **World Egg torque:** the tool performs one court function. It does not grant apotheosis, command the Menhir, prove heavenly favor or make every later copy authentic.
- **Collision ruling:** not Seven Who Took the Wrong Road, not a retelling of *Preiddeu Annwfn*, and not automatically the same stronghold as MN-R04.
- **Possible trace:** one functioning tool with nonhuman wear, a victory song whose returning roster shrinks in earlier copies, defenders' custody marks and descendants of prisoners on the wrong side of the route.
- **Open:** era, region, raiders, fort, inhabitants, tool, motive, casualties, survivors and present custody.
- **Source fidelity:** *Preiddeu Annwfn* supplies the armed Otherworld raid, guarded spoil, difficult return and catastrophic losses. The number, place-names, cauldron, company and poem sequence do not transfer.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; promotion locked.

## MN-R04 — The Revolving Fortress

- **Court form:** a stronghold whose entrance becomes reachable only when several natural pressures align.
- **Primary pressure:** Hecatara supplies changing adjacency; tide, sky, root, moon or season supplies the selected alignment. Trium is not presumed.
- **Inhabitants:** a separately classified court household, workers, prisoners, guests, fauna and possible human descendants. The fort is not automatically one being.
- **Mechanism:** “rotation” is the ordered change of jurisdiction. The same door may face coast at one phase, high air at another and a forest interior at a third without mechanical movement.
- **Hard edge:** failed entrants drown, age through displaced duration, become physically adapted to the wrong domain, lose the return route or arrive after everyone they knew has died. Defenders may deliberately time expulsions to cause those outcomes.
- **World Egg torque:** knowledge of the sequence creates strategic power without ownership of the fort. Breaking one alignment may strand inhabitants as surely as attackers.
- **Collision ruling:** not a mobile Patron city, Shell platform, Giant engine or the raid-event in MN-R03R unless a later placement separately joins them.
- **Possible trace:** approach calendars, bodies altered by different failed entries, several doors with identical interior wear and a family whose legal age differs by jurisdiction.
- **Open:** pressures, sequence, inhabitants, owner, fort purpose, entry cost, captives and current phase.
- **Source fidelity:** Caer Sidi and guarded Annwfn fortresses supply inaccessible stronghold and dangerous approach; Dreamlands supplies scale and hostile route logic only. No proper nouns or exact expedition transfer.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked.

## MN-R05 — The Elfland Tithe

- **Court form:** one full-agency court household owing a recurring quota of embodied service to another specifically identified jurisdiction.
- **Obligation:** the debt may run to a rival court, Patron, infernal office or another declared actor. No generic “Hell tithe” enters the setting and no Menhir owns judgement.
- **Victims:** abducted humans, retained fosterlings, court dependants and substitutes selected because the agreement classifies them as equivalent service.
- **Mechanism:** at a named season the creditor route opens and accepts a stated number or kind of servants. Court rulers hide their own members, seize mortals and treat successful rescue as a vacancy to be filled.
- **Hard edge:** abduction, coerced service, family separation, transformation, time loss, threatened substitution and deaths during rescue remain real. Saving one named captive can condemn another person if the quota survives.
- **World Egg torque:** a contract can enforce transfer of custody or labor without owning a human soul, changing judgement or making the exchange morally valid. Court leaders remain culpable for choosing captives.
- **Collision ruling:** not [[Mother Moon's Bargain]], the Ashen Guest Court's general child-exchange ecology, or a universal rule governing every Faewild court.
- **Possible trace:** substitute lists, seasonal missing-person patterns, a rescue ballad omitting the person taken afterward and a creditor token that counts service but cannot identify souls.
- **Open:** court, creditor, original bargain, quota, captives, rescuer, substitutions, route and whether the obligation can be ended without another death.
- **Source fidelity:** Scottish Elfland and tithe-ballad grammar; preserve abduction, transformation, threatened payment and dangerous rescue while replacing Queen, Hell, named lovers and ballad sequence.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked.

## MN-R06R — The Night Procession and the Accused

- **Court form:** a nocturnal procession involving some real court persons and some living human practitioners, later reconstructed through hostile prosecutions.
- **Primary pressure:** one route, moon, hunt or hospitality jurisdiction chosen at placement. “Seely” never means morally good.
- **Human mechanism:** every historical case must choose bodily crossing, a dream anchored in real natural pressure, or conscious direction of a court-made proxy. A human soul does not leave the body as a detachable rider and cannot be divided between procession and sleeper.
- **Activity:** participants heal, warn, steal, carry messages, identify disease, raid rival stores, assault enemies or bring people to a court. Different participants possess different knowledge and culpability.
- **Hard edge:** court violence and theft remain real where evidenced. Earthly authorities imprison, torture and execute accused practitioners; some victims participated, some misunderstood dreams, and some were named to settle ordinary grudges.
- **World Egg torque:** a true supernatural event does not validate the prosecutor's cosmology or every confession. Torture can destroy the very evidence needed to distinguish human choice, coercion, dream and court substitution.
- **Collision ruling:** not one High Hunt, not the Ashen Guest Court, not a secret surviving witch religion and not a universal Seelie order.
- **Possible trace:** bodily injuries matching one procession route, trial statements with shared impossible geography and copied leading phrases, cures using stolen material and acquitted families preserving the least dramatic accurate testimony.
- **Open:** culture, court, mechanism, practitioners, crimes, false accusations, officials, victims and which testimony is recoverable.
- **Source fidelity:** Scottish seely-wight and witch-trial evidence supplies nocturnal service, healing, theft, danger and prosecution. Trial testimony remains a damaged source; torture and execution are not sanitized or converted into proof of guilt.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; promotion locked.

## MN-R07R — The Barrow-Treasury War

- **Court form:** a small treasure-guardian ecology attached to one barrow, ancient ruin or buried court cache.
- **Primary pressure:** Sylvorn territorial memory, a Stone-Voice, Morvalis grave stillness or another selected local law; the treasure itself may also attract an external Patron.
- **Actors:** full-agency guardians, place-mind, fauna, human custodians, grave robbers, commissioned delvers, rival courtiers and Patron agents, all separately classified.
- **Conflict mechanism:** treasure serves as office proof, maintenance mass, burial deposit or boundary payment rather than unowned loot. Human and supernatural claimants breach layers, seize keys and retaliate against one another's access communities.
- **Hard edge:** burial desecration, maiming, crushing collapse, lethal ambush, hostage-taking, mutilation of guardians and retaliatory killing remain possible. Nearby families cannot be treated as guilty merely because a court chooses collective reprisal.
- **World Egg torque:** guarding treasure does not prove rightful title; defeating a guardian does not make the cache ownerless; a Patron's anchor claim does not absorb the rights of bodies buried there.
- **Collision ruling:** not the Folk Court Beneath the Roots, Pale-Skins, Hollow-Kings, the Concord's Barrow engine-field or every Cornish Small-People tradition. Child substitution and generic crop blight are removed from this card.
- **Possible trace:** a cache divided among incompatible custody layers, enlarged guardian armor, delver bodies sealed beside people they robbed and Patron deeds that omit the graves providing the mass.
- **Open:** site, culture, treasure, guardians, burials, Patron, human claimants, violence, survivors and current custody.
- **Source fidelity:** Cornish spriggan treasure-guardian grammar; preserve territorial violence, dangerous treasure and supernatural retaliation while refusing to pile unrelated piskie, changeling, Bucca and knocker traits into one species.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; promotion locked.

## MN-R08 — The Compact of the Knockers

- **Court form:** a mine ecology of one or more sapient court persons, Stone-Voice responses, ordinary miners and lesser fauna interpreted under one customary name.
- **Primary pressure:** load, fracture, buried water, mineral seam and safe void under Sylvorn, Astaroth or another selected established conjunction.
- **Compact:** miners leave a stated portion, keep a warning rhythm, avoid one seam and answer knocks through a witnessed sequence. The court provides warning, route or limited labor rather than ownership of all minerals.
- **Conflict mechanism:** a mining state converts the custom into production law. Officials ignore warnings when politically useful, declare dead workers defaulters and force dissidents into the workings carrying the greatest court risk.
- **Hard edge:** collapse, gas, burial alive, mutilation, forced labor, executed strikers and deliberate exposure of prisoners remain explicit. Court persons who knowingly mislead workers remain culpable; reflexive Stone-Voices do not become criminals.
- **World Egg torque:** a warning can be accurate while the compact's political use is murderous. The state cannot transfer its duty to protect laborers onto a geological jurisdiction.
- **Collision ruling:** not the Country Beneath the Rains' Ash-Well Crown, a Giltling host, Tangle-Gremlin species or a hidden underground nation. A new knocker form requires its own bestiary dossier.
- **Possible trace:** shift ledgers recording ignored knocks, portion niches, survivor rhythms, sealed seams and state executions falsely described as court collection.
- **Open:** mine, culture, court actors, primary Menhir, compact, officials, workers, revolt, collapse and present operation.
- **Source fidelity:** Cornish knocker traditions supply mine warning, misleading sound, conditional assistance, demanded portions and underground labor. World Egg state coercion preserves bodily cost without making the source beings a generic evil species.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked.

## MN-R09 — The Two Buccas of One Coast

- **Court form:** two contrary coastal manifestations recognized by one human tradition under paired titles.
- **Identity models:** final promotion must choose between two rival full-agency court persons, two recurring modes of one place-mind, or two cultural readings of a larger Thalassor-aligned law. These models produce different culpability and defeat conditions.
- **Primary pressure:** Thalassor's coast, current and custody, possibly in conjunction with Zephyraxis storm or Sylvorn shoreline succession.
- **Human interface:** fishers, shore custodians, wreckers, farmers and rulers divide offerings between the two titles and weaponize uncertainty over which response followed.
- **Hard edge:** drowning, wreck, failed fishing, destroyed crops through salt or storm, coerced offerings and retaliatory killing remain real. Prosperity purchased through deliberate wreck is still human guilt.
- **World Egg torque:** apparent benevolence and hostility do not establish a good and evil Menhir pair. If one place-mind changes mode, it remains one person; if two courtiers compete, each answers for chosen acts; if both are readings of sea law, neither title is the sovereign.
- **Collision ruling:** not [[The Court of Salt and Teeth]], two Masks, Thalassor divided into persons or a universal coastal dualism.
- **Possible trace:** paired shrines with one underlying tide response, offerings accepted by the wrong title, contradictory drowning records and two witnesses receiving different faces from one storm.
- **Open:** coast, culture, identity model, court actors, offerings, storms, victims, human collaborators and current relation.
- **Source fidelity:** Cornish Bucca traditions supply coastal ambivalence, paired readings, offerings and danger. Names, exact local rites and modern harmonizations do not transfer as World Egg fact.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked until the identity model is selected.

## MN-R10R — The High Hunt of the Withheld Season

- **Court form:** one temporary High Hunt assembled when seasonal preservation blocks a required release across several local routes.
- **Primary pressure:** Nevaura is the strongest fit, possibly phase-locked with Morvalis, Mother Moon, Orionis or Hecatara. No placement may turn the conjunction into a permanent alliance.
- **Hunt anatomy:** a witnessed declaration identifies what the season claims, a route connects the relevant courts and a quarry-sign marks bodies or material carried out of the withheld condition.
- **Quarry:** living people, corpses awaiting burial, pregnant people, pregnant animals, eggs, seed grain and other embodied continuities may be claimed according to the declaration. The Hunt never identifies, pursues or owns an unborn soul or future person.
- **Hard edge:** Riders abduct living people, tear corpses from mourners, seize food needed for survival, hunt pregnant bodies and kill those closing routes. Hounds remain animal agents; sapient Riders remain responsible for unlawful substitution and chosen cruelty.
- **World Egg torque:** the deeper seasonal law is morally blind, but a Rider can still refuse to substitute an easier innocent quarry. Releasing one route may end the Hunt while also thawing disease, famine stores or a preserved enemy army.
- **Collision ruling:** a local episode within the reviewed War of Unfinished Seasons ecology, never the whole umbrella or one universal Wild Hunt. It remains separate from the Hunt of Escaped Courts.
- **Possible trace:** one quarry-sign preserved on burial cloth, seasonal missing-person rolls, seed stores that attract hounds and a Rider mask recording a refused substitution.
- **Open:** era, region, exact pressure, declaration, route, quarry, Riders, victims, release and aftermath.
- **Source fidelity:** European Wild Hunt families contribute terrifying seasonal pursuit and capture; World Egg High Hunt rules supply declaration, route and quarry-sign. Regional folklore requires named attribution before placement.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; promotion locked.

## MN-R11 — The Mountain the Court Abandoned

- **Court form:** a familiar high-place court whose lesser persons flee when the mountain's deeper Menhir pressure enters an overwhelming active phase.
- **Primary pressure:** Father Sun exposure, Zephyraxis altitude and storm, Astaroth fracture or another selected existing Menhir. The mountain is a strong thin place, not the Menhir's body or capital.
- **Population:** fleeing court persons, abandoned fauna and dependants, human pilgrims, summit custodians and communities asked to shelter refugees they once worshipped.
- **Mechanism:** court custom had made the lesser rulers appear sovereign. Activation strips away their control of weather, route or refuge; they close paths behind themselves and conceal the fact that they cannot command the deeper law.
- **Hard edge:** climbers die from exposure, fall, disappear or return bodily damaged; court rulers abandon dependants, seize human shelter or sacrifice guides to close pursuit. The Menhir pressure does not select victims by impiety.
- **World Egg torque:** the court can be ancient, powerful and deserving of moral judgement while still being tiny beside its associated sovereignty. The Menhir remains intelligent and capable of bargain; it is not the blind thing beneath MN-R12R.
- **Collision ruling:** not a Faewild Olympus, a Patron capital, Trium's seat, MN-R12R's Great Coherence or proof that human worship created the court.
- **Possible trace:** evacuated court regalia, dead ascent paths, a lower shrine occupied by supernatural refugees and summit weather that ignores every former royal token.
- **Open:** mountain, Menhir, court, activation, refugees, pilgrims, casualties, abandoned dependants and present access.
- **Source fidelity:** “The Other Gods” supplies forbidden ascent, fled local gods, inaccessible height and catastrophic scale mismatch. Lovecraft's pantheon, theology, names and racial coding do not transfer.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; promotion locked.

## MN-R12R — The Court Above the Unanswering Engine

- **Court form:** a local Faewild ecology gathered at a Material site where its thin space overlaps the manifestations of a dormant distributed Great Coherence. The realms and continuities do not merge.
- **Deeper continuity:** one `shell-pattern` Great Coherence with no interior person or moral agency. The card no longer permits a Sleeping Wrong alternative.
- **Court population:** full-agency court interpreters, place-minds, fauna, human pact-lineages and maintenance victims. Their customs may turn the Coherence's repeated outputs into an ancestor persona.
- **Mechanism:** offerings and procession alter temperature, pressure, ballast or conductor state, giving the Coherence inputs it classifies. When disturbed, it routes correction through court sites and settlements without recognizing kinship, rank, promise or worship.
- **Hard edge:** interpreters disappear dissenters, feed bodies into maintenance works, evacuate favored households and sacrifice settlements to preserve the pattern. When correction begins, structures collapse, weather kills and court manifestations are used as relay bodies. The nonperson computation does not absorb the interpreters' guilt.
- **World Egg torque:** a ritual may work for centuries without being a bargain. Speech can be repeated operator fragments. Awakening can expose priestly fraud and still kill everyone whose shelter depends on the same system.
- **Collision ruling:** not PT-R12R's Regent of Dormant Anchors, not a Sleeping Wrong, dormant Menhir, Idol, ancestor soul, Great Drift being or native Shell civilization.
- **Possible trace:** ritual phrases matching old control commands, identical corrections across distant court sites, sacrificed settlements excluded from genealogy and a node that responds to physical input while ignoring every name.
- **Open:** seam, Coherence, court, interpreters, system purpose, victims, present dormancy, failure sequence and whether safe dismantling is possible.
- **Source fidelity:** Lovecraftian cosmic scale supplies the court's false kinship with an incomprehensible deeper power. World Egg Great Coherence canon replaces blind deity with causally describable nonperson computation; cult atrocity and catastrophic helplessness remain.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; promotion locked.

# V. Interfaces with Existing Canon

## Existing Folk courts

The six registered [[Courts of the Folk Lands]] already demonstrate that a court can contain Menhir ecology, place minds, fauna, Patrons, residue and Idol Masks without becoming a unified state. The reviewed nurseries should deepen that model elsewhere, not rename the existing six.

- MN-R01 and MN-R07R remain separate from [[The Court Beneath the Roots]].
- MN-R05 and MN-R06R remain separate from [[The Ashen Guest Court]].
- MN-R09 remains separate from [[The Court of Salt and Teeth]].
- MN-R10R may use [[Hunt Hounds and Hunt Riders]] but cannot create one permanent Wild Hunt.
- MN-R08 may use Stone-Voice grammar but cannot silently add a reusable Knocker species.

## Subterranean interface

MN-R01, MN-R02, MN-R07R and MN-R08 use mounds, barrows, routes and mines as apertures. None establishes a universal underground realm. They remain distinct from Under-Caleran, Pale-Skins and Hollow-Kings, the Country Beneath the Rains, Dusk Barrows, Material mines and infernal Depth.

Physical descent and Faewild adjacency must be mapped separately. A tunnel may end in rock while a nearby mound door opens into a court; a court route may pass beneath a city without occupying the physical geology below it.

# VII. Promotion Gates

Before any reviewed lead becomes a historical seed:

1. assign era, region, culture, local site and the ordinary people bearing the cost;
2. select the primary Menhir or conjunction without inventing a new sovereignty;
3. classify sovereign, court persons, place-minds, fauna, humans, manifestations, counterfeits and objects separately;
4. state gift, price, boundary and failure for every actual bargain;
5. distinguish a courtier's claimed authority from authority the local law truly carries;
6. name the physical mechanism of disappearance, crop failure, storm, mine collapse, transformation, route loss or Hunt capture;
7. preserve maiming, abduction, hostage-taking, forced service, drowning, execution, burial violation, mine death and lethal exposure where load-bearing;
8. never let Menhir law prove guilt, property title, just war, rightful kingship or moral legitimacy;
9. never let dream travel, bodily crossing, pact alteration or court service divide or replace a human soul;
10. treat title recurrence as a role until continuity evidence establishes one person;
11. create a unique-person or creature dossier before adding a new reusable court being;
12. maintain specific Irish, Welsh, Scottish, Cornish or regional Wild Hunt attribution at source-fidelity level;
13. keep MN-R03R and MN-R04, MN-R11 and MN-R12R, and every adjacent Folk court separate unless a later collision review establishes shared identity;
14. leave a useful 1360 AR trace while keeping names, present owners and exact outcomes open;
15. apply the Forward Rule to any present manifestation, first contact or active Hunt.
