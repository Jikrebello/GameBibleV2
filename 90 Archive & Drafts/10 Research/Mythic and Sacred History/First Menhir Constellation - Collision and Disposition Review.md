# First Menhir Constellation — Collision and Disposition Review

## Purpose and Result

This ledger reviews the six provisional histories in [[First Menhir Constellation - Historical Placement Matrix]] against their source nurseries, neighboring historical roots, existing courts, cultural histories and metaphysical boundaries.

The result is:

- three `retain`: MN-P01, MN-P05 and MN-P06;
- three incorporated `revise`: MN-P02R, MN-P03R and MN-P04R;
- zero `defer`;
- zero `reject`;
- six historically placed Menhir episodes whose complete histories remain Garden-locked;
- no edit to the authoritative chronology, culture histories or [[The Historical Seed Garden]].

[[Greater-Power Garden Balance and Trace Promotion Review]] later promotes only **The Marks of Necessary Release** from MN-P02R and **The Middle Knock Is Never Permission** from MN-P05. MN-P01, MN-P03R, MN-P04R and MN-P06 remain withheld rather than rejected.

Shared source grammar is treated as a recurring pressure, never proof that two entries are the same event, court, route, object, population or ruler.

# II. Detailed Review Rulings

## MN-P01 — Court That Lost the Surface

### Collision findings

The strongest collision is superficial: mound doors, subterranean routes and a displaced court could be mistaken for [[The Court Beneath the Roots]] or a Material underdark society. The event instead concerns a Faewild adjacency surviving after its Material settlement is destroyed. Ordinary excavation reaches stone, graves and Giant drainage works rather than a continuous underground country.

The Giant estate supplies the political conquest but does not create the court, Menhir law or local human population. Later Green March and Brauvalian communities inherit different pieces of the site without becoming direct unchanged continuations of either conqueror or displaced court.

### Coherence and continuity check

The plain mechanism is adequate: Giant clients destroy surface anchors; Hecatara–Sylvorn adjacency persists through seasonally aligned wells, roots and thresholds; sapient court rulers use the remaining access to pursue toll and fosterage claims. Human disappearance is bodily crossing or route loss, never soul theft.

Surface deeds can accurately establish Material possession while saying nothing about a Faewild route. Court memory can accurately establish former occupation while saying nothing about just title over descendants of later survivors.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** Irish *síd* settlement and defeated-supernatural-polity grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** dispossession with surviving local access and dangerous negotiated coexistence.
- **Hard edge:** conquest killing, displacement, abduction, coerced service and rescue deaths remain direct.
- **State:** `intact at parity`.

### Disposition

`retain` — the selected placement distinguishes political landholding, Material geology and Faewild adjacency while preserving human and court victims. Promotion remains locked.

## MN-P02R — High Hunt of the Withheld Season

### Collision findings

The nursery's former “European Wild Hunt” attribution was too broad and risked allowing the Hunt of the Escaped Courts, the War of Unfinished Seasons and several regional traditions to collapse into one host. Norwegian Oskoreia supplies a sufficiently specific procession grammar: a dangerous, noisy winter ride associated with threats to farms and people. It does not supply the World Egg declaration, route or quarry law.

The Hunt of the Escaped Courts concerns supernatural pursuit during greater-power flight. This episode concerns a local contradiction between necessary preservation and refused seasonal release. It has different participants, route, quarry, period, evidence and consequence.

### Coherence and continuity check

The phase-lock is limited to Nevaura's preservation and Morvalis's embodied completion. Existing Hecatara-aligned routes carry the Hunt but do not make Hecatara a permanent allied sovereign. The declaration marks embodied quarry only: people, pregnant animals, corpses, eggs, seed and stored food. Pregnancy does not expose or create an unborn soul for the Hunt, and corpses do not contain the departed human person.

Riders with moral agency remain culpable for substituting innocents. Hounds follow the sign as animals. The deeper pressure can be exact without declaring the rulers, victims or resistance morally right.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** Norwegian Oskoreia/Åsgårdsreia tradition.
- **Borrowed element:** midwinter procession, danger outside and at the farm threshold, capture and protective custom.
- **Hard edge:** abduction, violent intrusion, bodily danger and seasonal terror remain direct.
- **State:** `restored through named attribution`.
- **Exclusions:** dead human souls, Asgard, source leaders and generic pan-European synthesis are not imported.

### Disposition

`revise` — incorporate the named Norwegian recension, the exact phase-lock and embodied-quarry firewall. The result remains one local High Hunt. Only **The Marks of Necessary Release** later enters the Garden as a light trace; the complete episode stays locked.

## MN-P03R — Raid That Returned One Tool

### Collision findings

The original root risked collision with two existing early-history structures: the doomed route company and the twice-founded harbor. Fixing the attackers as one sea-king's changing warband prevents a new signature-company bed. Placing the attacked stronghold away from the selected harbor history prevents the raid from becoming a hidden foundation narrative.

The sounding-weight also distinguishes the event from a generic magical prize. It reveals and stabilizes one reef adjacency under one current sequence. It cannot command Hecatara or Thalassor, open every sea route or validate later imperial claims.

### Coherence and continuity check

The raid has an ordinary political cause—blockade and threatened food supply—but the sea-king still chooses theft and killing after permanent custody is refused. Defenders possess moral agency and are not demons. Raiders remain human souls through coercion or transformation. Prisoners retained by the court do not become nonhuman merely through time or service.

The working tool proves one bounded response. It cannot prove the heroism of the possessor, the wickedness of the defenders or the truth of the victory song.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** Welsh *Preiddeu Annwfn*.
- **Borrowed element:** armed Otherworld raid, guarded useful spoil, difficult return and catastrophic loss.
- **Hard edge:** defenders killed, raiders drowned, captured or transformed and victory morally compromised.
- **State:** `intact after transformation`.
- **Changed axes:** prize, region, motive, company, fort population, route mechanism and historical consequence.

### Disposition

`revise` — incorporate the sounding-weight, pre-imperial island corridor and non-company status. Promotion remains locked.

## MN-P04R — Elfland Tithe

### Collision findings

Leaving the creditor type open would allow the episode to drift into Mother Moon's firstborn bargain, a generic tithe to Hell, an infernal custody claim, a Patron ferry or the child-exchange ecology already associated with the Ashen Guest Court. The review therefore selects a rival offshore court under a bounded Hecatara–Thalassor winter-road compact.

The two courts share a route without sharing government, persons or universal law. Neither is [[The Court of Salt and Teeth]]. The event predates Saltborn formation and may contribute a song or caution without supplying Saltborn ancestry or identity.

### Coherence and continuity check

The creditor token counts embodied rowing and watch service. It cannot establish consent, distinguish a fosterling from a prisoner, own a person, transfer judgement or identify a human soul. The shore rulers and provincial officials remain culpable because they knowingly exploit that limitation.

Later Christian polemic contains real persecuted believers but falsifies the whole victim population when it makes every disappeared believer a martyr and every other victim a willing pagan. A genuine martyr cannot be established by disappearance alone.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** Scottish Elfland and tithe ballads.
- **Borrowed element:** abducted substitute, recurring embodied obligation, transformation, dangerous rescue and the vacancy left by incomplete liberation.
- **Hard edge:** abduction, coerced labor, separation, death and the possibility that saving one person leaves another exposed.
- **State:** `intact after creditor selection`.
- **Changed axes:** rival court replaces Hell; maritime labor replaces received tithe imagery; provincial persecution and later polemic create the historical transmission.

### Disposition

`revise` — incorporate the rival-court creditor, winter crossing and mixed victim history. Promotion remains locked.

## MN-P05 — Compact of the Knockers

### Collision findings

The mine setting could falsely merge Cornish knockers, Stone-Voices, an invented creature species and the Country Beneath the Rains. The placement expressly uses a mixed local ecology: sapient court interpreters, nonpersonal or minimally attentive Stone-Voice responses, ordinary fauna and human laborers. No shared vernacular label establishes shared species or personhood.

The historical Shield-House administration is not the present Soran regime. The older event can inform current mine politics without making the modern dynasty responsible for every death or turning the compact into the secret source of Aversian industry.

### Coherence and continuity check

The physical chain is recoverable through the cut load-bearing rib, transferred fracture, gas path and sealed refuge galleries. Real supernatural warning does not replace engineering evidence. Officials counterfeit and suppress warnings; a sapient court interpreter also knowingly misdirects workers. Reflexive Stone-Voices do not inherit either party's guilt.

The state cannot make dead workers contractual defaulters or transfer its safety duty onto a morally blind natural jurisdiction. Court possession of local access does not establish ownership of every mineral or surface settlement.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** Cornish knocker traditions.
- **Borrowed element:** underground knocks, warning and misdirection, conditional aid and demanded portion.
- **Hard edge:** dangerous labor and supernatural uncertainty remain; the World Egg history adds its own specifically Aversian coercion without attributing that cruelty to Cornish tradition.
- **State:** `intact and specifically attributed`.

### Disposition

`retain` — the placement produces a coherent local mine history while preserving actor distinctions and refusing a new species. Only **The Middle Knock Is Never Permission** later enters the Garden as a light trace; the complete episode stays locked.

## MN-P06 — Mountain the Court Abandoned

### Collision findings

The primary danger is conflating intelligent Menhir scale with MN-R12R's nonpersonal Great Coherence or turning the summit into a Faewild Olympus. The conjunction uses existing Zephyraxis and Father Sun pressures. The mountain is a thin place and physical environment, not their body, home, capital or rival creation.

The distant highland communities pre-exist the court's collapse. They are neither primitive scenery nor descendants of refugees from the known world. Their lack of verified 1360 contact preserves distant history without denying them institutions, conflicting memories or moral agency.

### Coherence and continuity check

The court formerly controlled a bounded breathable route and cloud-shelter arrangement. The deeper conjunction removes those conditions. Court rulers then choose concealment, abandonment, shelter seizure and guide sacrifice. Those acts—not the Menhir pressure—supply their moral guilt.

The altitude deaths have physical mechanisms: loss of breathable corridors, fixed exposure, wind loading and failed shelter. The court's regalia cease to work because the local arrangement ends, not because worship stops or a stronger god steals divinity.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** Lovecraft's “The Other Gods.”
- **Borrowed element:** inaccessible ascent, fled local powers and catastrophic scale mismatch.
- **Hard edge:** death, exposure, bodily damage, abandonment and helplessness before greater scale remain.
- **State:** `intact after theological correction`.
- **Exclusions:** blind gods, rival creators, racial coding, imported pantheon and human populations treated as degeneration.

### Disposition

`retain` — the placement gives the distant world a strong nonhuman history while preserving local human sovereignty and the no-contact boundary. Promotion remains locked.
