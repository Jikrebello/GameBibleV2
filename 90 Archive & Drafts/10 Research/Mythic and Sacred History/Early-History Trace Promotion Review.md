# Early-History Trace Promotion Review

## Promotion Ruling

[[Historical Seed Garden - Coherence and Balance Review]] passes all fifty-five existing roots: fifty-two retained and three Last Houses roots revised in place. None is removed. The Garden's principal chronological imbalance is now explicit: the Long Frontier is rich, while the human aftermath of the Prophet, the two fissions, early proto-Atherian settlement, Folk–Ättar divergence and the City-State/Star boundary remain comparatively sparse.

This pass therefore promotes exactly ten light roots into [[The Historical Seed Garden]]:

- six of the twelve reviewed Two-Fissions hooks;
- four of the six reviewed Unknown Histories;
- no new signature company;
- no complete historical biography, institution, supernatural realm or sovereign event.

Promotion means that the trace, pressure and moral wound become approved exploratory Garden material. It does not confer canon status. Exact personal names, polity names, sites, borders, descendant claims, supernatural identities and 1360 outcomes remain open.

## Garden Interface

Every promoted entry contains only:

1. **Humble aperture** — the ordinary need, work or legal problem through which the event becomes visible;
2. **Durable trace** — at least one material, architectural, bodily, archaeological, performed or customary carrier;
3. **Historical pressure** — the larger history touched without being caused by the seed;
4. **Loose growth** — the unresolved people, motives, transmissions and later uses;
5. **Identity and promotion boundary** — what the trace cannot authenticate or explain.

Documentary evidence may corroborate every root. It leads only in the Returners' Second Sack, where alteration of the records is part of the crime; even there the grave and building chronology remain independently necessary.
