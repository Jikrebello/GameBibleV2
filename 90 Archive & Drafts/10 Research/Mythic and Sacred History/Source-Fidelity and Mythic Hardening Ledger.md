# Source-Fidelity and Mythic Hardening Ledger

This is the controlling parity audit for the whole-history nursery. It does not canonize a card, name a participant, promote a succession, or override a card's `retain`, `revise`, or `defer` disposition.

## Ruling

**Severity parity** means preserving the causal weight, proximity, culpability, irreversibility, and consequence of the source element actually borrowed. It does not mean matching body counts mechanically, importing every horror named by a secondary source, or adding violence to a genuinely domestic, textual, hospitable, comic, or juridical element.

- Eastern Orthodox Scripture controls biblical borrowings. A load-bearing biblical act is preserved closely.
- Received Orthodox tradition controls saintly borrowings, while documentary history, received Life, liturgy, icon, miracle cycle, and folklore remain distinct layers.
- Familiar composites control Greek, Norse, heroic, and other non-Christian mythic grammar; the World Egg keeps equivalent severity while changing the setting expression.
- A living tradition still requires a named people, text, collection, or performance context. A vague regional analogy remains `research-blocked` and supplies no invented atrocity.
- Modern literary and game works supply structural grammar only.

`Current state` records the pre-hardening diagnosis. `Restoration` records the correction applied to the nursery or the reason none is required.

## Validation Lock

1. A hard act cannot survive only in a caution or sensitivity note when it is causal in the borrowed source.
2. Plain words such as rape, murder, torture, sacrifice, dismemberment, execution and cannibalism are used where accurate; anatomical spectacle is unnecessary.
3. A victim remains a person with relations, choices, memory and consequences rather than evidence decorating another person's arc.
4. A hero's supernatural distortion, coercion, repentance or later sanctity never makes the hero's own act unreal.
5. Cultural attribution prevents fabrication; it does not make a sourced tradition gentler than it is.
6. Severity restoration does not explain a Weird, promote a seed, change continuity, create a new apostle, or determine post-1360 history.
