# 350 BR to 0 AR — Imperial Expansion New Chat Handoff

## Purpose and correction

This note transfers the next history task to a new chat. Packages A–E now establish the world through **350 BR**. The next pass must explain how the Atherian Empire expands from that starting position to the world in which Isfir the Redeemer dies and rises in **0 AR**, while keeping the much later present-day successor geography in view.

An earlier answer wrongly treated the [[Package E - Harbours Islands and Braided Precursors/Package E - Sestaran City Origins and Imperial Handoff 350 BR to 0 AR|Sestaran city-origins handoff]] as though it described the entire transition. It is a **regional dependency**, not the empire-wide plan. The creator explicitly wants an account of imperial advances into the Qahuri Sacred Basin and the former Gateway country, across the Sestaran/Elmaric sea, deep into eastern and selected southern lands, and through the territories later associated with Thalmyria, Eclessia, the Velian frontier and Jirahar communities. This is a mandate for the next development pass, not a claim that every conquest has already been narrated in canon.

## What is already canon

The [[01 Canon Reference/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/Historical Atlas of the Atherian Age of Glory.md|Age of Glory atlas]] and [[01 Canon Reference/History/10 Chronology and Control/Age of Glory - Authorial History and Integration Review.md|Age of Glory integration review]] already establish **350 BR–0 AR** as a canon imperial period. They provide the dynastic sequence, provincial government, army, economy, public star religion and several local events. Do not replace that sequence or invent a rival emperor list. What remains thin is the connected political and geographic account of *which regions were conquered when, on what terms, and with what local consequences*.

Established imperial sequence: Thalion 350–318 BR; House of Thalion to 247 BR; Four Eagles 247–246 BR; Veloran house 246–151 BR; Serrat and later houses 151–0 AR; Othran II has reigned since 24 BR and is emperor at the Redeemer boundary. Isfir is born in 33 BR. His death and resurrection define 0 AR. The Redeemer's faith is **not** the empire's public religion at that boundary; that conversion belongs to later history.

The existing atlas says eastern provinces included much of the land later inherited by Eclessia; Sestaran harbours ranged from direct rule to alliance and privilege; Sarqen was an imperial Gateway port; southern forts and treaty districts met independent Folk societies; northern roads reached well kingdoms and mobile Tarkhan communities while most dry country remained outside imperial rule; and Jirahar tower towns retained their own institutions inside and beyond eastern imperial territory. Those statements are constraints and starting points, not a complete regional chronology.

## The 350 BR starting board

| Region or political world | Established position at the boundary |
|---|---|
| **Caleran, Aurin and Atherian-speaking cities** | Thalion's imperial settlement follows republican expansion, Casvilar's southern campaigns and the succession wars. The Caleran/Aurin system is the imperial core. |
| **Gateway country** | Qarzeth, the Carthage-coded Gateway Republic's capital, fell around **520 BR**. Its people and lesser towns survived under the Kingdom of Sarqen. Thalion conquered and annexed **Sarqen in 350 BR**. There is no intact Qarzethi republic still waiting to fall after 350. |
| **Qahur** | Three principal crowns—Tenaret, Qemaret and Sakhret—remain divided, with temple and canal jurisdictions beneath them. Vasharan kings influence claimants and collect tribute; Caleran controls the neighbouring Gateway approaches. Qahur is **not already an Atherian province** in 350 BR. |
| **Central Elmaric islands** | The Elmarai heartland, Seravon, Ilamar, eastern channels and outer approaches have different local governments. Caleran holds selected footholds and clients, not the whole archipelago. Marelith was destroyed in **355 BR**. |
| **Vashar and the eastern riverlands** | Orvaz II's Crown of Roads and Dawns remains an independent imperial peer. Thalion has secured Atherian-speaking ports and western clients; Vashar retains Avesh, the Ubar riverlands, Qiryath and its highland core. |
| **Jirahar** | Second Qiryath is a populous sanctuary and market under Vasharan supremacy, with internal communal institutions. Jirahar road, river, tower and sea communities are already dispersed across several states. |
| **Southern Folk and Ättar country** | Casvilar conquered a belt of southern approaches in c. 390–379 BR, but Thalion inherits an uneven frontier, allies and independent societies—not a completed southern province. The far-cold Ättar and deeper Folk worlds are not simply Atherian territory. |
| **Northern wells and grass** | Ahdari, Tarkhan and other local powers remain divided but capable of resisting. Ostar's failed expedition is recent memory. Imperial access and clientage do not amount to ownership of the whole dry belt. |

Start with [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Peoples before Atherian Unification/Peoples before Atherian Unification.md|Peoples before Atherian Unification]] and the respective regional atlases rather than projecting later national names backwards.

## What the 0 AR account must reconcile

| Conquest or relationship | Existing evidence | Development still needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Atherian core and Sarqen** | The imperial core and Sarqen's 350 BR annexation are secure. Sarqen remains an inhabited and useful Gateway provincial centre. | The longer provincial integration of Gateway towns and their relationship to northern desert approaches. |
| **Qahuri Sacred Basin** | Canon establishes the divided 350 BR crowns but offers no connected 350–0 BR conquest. The creator now directs that Atheria establishes provinces in this Egypt-coded country. | Campaigns, local alliances and resistance, order of the crowns' submission, degree of annexation by 0 AR, survival of Qahuri institutions and the Vasharan response. Do not silently write the gap as though already settled. |
| **Sestaran/Elmaric sea** | Later imperial incorporation of Fiorenzia and Sermora, Vardessa's charters, Aversia's colony, Orsena's station and Vorthara's limited accommodation are established at broad scale. | Date and sequence each change without assuming every later foundation occurred before 0 AR; distinguish the island core, outer approaches and Saronikan-facing east. Use the separate Package E handoff. |
| **Vashar, Arqesh and eastern coast** | Eastern imperial provinces and an Atherian Raveth river province are canon. The [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Jirahar.md|Jirahar historical spine]] says Atheria absorbs eastern riverlands. | Explain the long contest with Vashar, city-by-city allegiance, what becomes of House Tiravan and whether the highland core survives as a foreign peer, client or broken kingdom. The precise later fate of Vashar is still open in the programme. |
| **Qiryath and the Jirahar** | By Isfir's lifetime Atheria can try and execute him, and Qiryath is under Atherian rule with negotiated internal courts and sacrifice. The Great Tower Revolt and destruction occur **later, in 66–70 AR**. | Date the change from Vasharan to Atherian supremacy and explain its terms; preserve Jirahar institutions and the wider diaspora. Do not predate or predetermine the later revolt. |
| **Southern frontier** | The Age of Glory atlas has forts, treaty districts and independent Folk country. The creator wants substantial further imperial conquest southwards. | Define the particular river, forest, port and settlement corridors taken, and their provincial status, without annexing every Folk or Ättar homeland. Show supply, winter limits, local bargains and resistance. |
| **Northern inner-sea and desert margin** | Imperial roads, well contacts and Sarqen are secure; most dry belt remains outside rule. | Determine the western/northern inner-sea holdings and border arrangements that later feed the Velian States and Zanthir, without retroactively creating Velian or Covenant polities in 0 AR. |

The creator's intended overall shape is **large-scale imperial expansion**, not merely islands and not a static empire after Thalion. Existing statements about independent Folk country and the largely unconquered dry belt remain meaningful limits. “Deep conquest” should identify real controlled corridors and provinces while distinguishing garrisons, clients, tribute, trading rights and genuine occupation.

## Present-day territory: use as a backward-facing check, not an ancient map

The canon [[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR).md|present-day courts survey]] and [[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/Present-Day Political Board.md|political board]] control the **1360 AR** state system:

- **Thalmyria** occupies the west-central old Atherian heartland: Veridun, Saronika, Vlastrava, Elenara, Arkena and Kedrava. Caleran is the ancient capital; Veridun is the modern ruling seat. Saronika lies in Thalmyria, **not** in the Sestaran Concord.
- **Eclessia** inherits much eastern provincial country, now divided among Aurelia, Virelia, Ironvale, Caldria, Veridancia, Brauvalis, Korrheim, the Sylvan Marches and Grenzburg. Its present confessional and political map is much later than 0 AR.
- **The Sestaran Concord** consists of seven independent states—Fiorenzia, Sermora, Aversia, Vorthara, Castara, Orsena and Vardessa—not one ancient island kingdom. Sermora and Saronika are different cities/polities.
- **The Covenant sphere** includes the Iqashari centre, Al-Qahur, the independent Qazmarid counter-caliphate, autonomous Zanthir and the contested Velian client belt. The Ahl-e-Ahd confessional civilisation begins centuries **after** 0 AR. Qazmarid is not a synonym for the old Qarzethi/Gateway country.
- **The Jirahar** have living towers, routes, host-city quarters and Qiryath Reqa, but no enclosing modern territorial state.
- **The Folk and Ättar** retain distinct customary territories beyond later imperial and church frontiers; **Tarkhan** political range follows hordes, wells, pasture and routes rather than one surveyed state.

The [[01 Canon Reference/Geography/World Map/Political Map Border Proof Key.md|political border proof]] helps with relative geography but is explicitly **draft and awaiting creator approval**. Its silhouettes and exact borders must not be backdated or treated as finished canon.

## Work for the new chat

1. **Audit before drafting.** Build a region-by-region concordance of the 350 BR position, existing Age of Glory events, required 0 AR condition and 1360 AR inheritance. Flag contradictions and unsupported assumptions. Read the existing dynastic chronology and Age of Glory articles so new material fits rather than duplicates them.
2. **Propose an empire-wide sequence, 350 BR–0 AR.** Develop Qahur, Vashar/eastern riverlands and Qiryath, southern provincial advances, northern/Gateway approaches and the Sestaran sea together. Use named phases, rulers and local actors; specify motives, allies, food, fleets, roads, costs and durable provincial arrangements. The founding of the later states is **not** part of this ancient period.
3. **Pause at the review gate.** Present substantive choices and collisions to the creator before canon promotion. Use the agreed A/B/C decision format when a choice is genuinely open. Keep the first full sequence author-only until reviewed.
4. **Only after approval**, enlarge the existing encyclopedia articles and regional entries to the established [[01 Canon Reference/History/10 Chronology and Control/World Egg Encyclopedia - Editorial Style Guide.md|encyclopedia standard]], update chronology/navigation and run links, Atlas validation and compatibility checks. Do not treat a terse outline as a finished canon pass.

The first delivery in the new chat should be the **audit and proposed regional architecture**, not an immediate rewrite of all canon. The creator can then steer the precise campaigns and political settlements.
