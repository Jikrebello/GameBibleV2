# The Great Unbinding — Collapse, Remnant, and the Glass Ark

## Status

This dossier records the research and comparative reasoning behind the authorial causal backbone for the end of Giant rule, the Great Prophet's liberation, the preservation of a human remnant, the crossing of the Great Glass Desert, and the fate of the lands left behind.

The providential-convergence model, representative remnant, broad plague functions, one-Phoenix commission, Glass passage minimum, and differentiated imperial outcomes have now been promoted into [[The Great Unbinding]], which controls whenever this research dossier is more speculative or more specific. Names for plague complexes, individual actors, camps, battles, and stages remain working language. This note does not explain the Glass, settle the Year of Standing Stars, identify Anshara's final sovereign, convert Nythalum's lunar programme into Astral flight, or promote any historical hero other than the already established Great Prophet.

## Controlling Proposal

The fall of Giant civilization is a **providential convergence**, not one spell, battle, flood, rebellion, or synchronized disappearance:

> **The Prophet announced and led. The plagues severed the systems of bondage. Enslaved peoples rebelled. Giant houses consumed one another. The Lord's commissioned servants bound what mortals could not safely defeat. The ordered migrating host carried a representative human remnant through the ancient Glass while the old political world finished falling behind it.**

This makes the Noahic, Mosaic, Wisdom, and Enochic grammars complementary rather than interchangeable:

- **Noahic:** warning, preparation, an ordered refuge, preservation of life and craft, judgement, survival, and an intended covenantal landing.
- **Mosaic:** a commissioned human liberator, repeated demands for release, escalating signs, a mixed multitude, an impossible passage, pursuit, provision, complaint, law, and formation.
- **Wisdom:** the same created goods expose tyranny, punish predation, and become provision or passage for the oppressed.
- **Enochic:** Giant violence reaches a terminal crisis; the doomed houses turn upon one another; particular primordial offenders are bound; the wounded world requires healing after judgement.

The Great Glass Desert is not the catastrophe. It is the **dry ark-space** into which the remnant enters while the habitable Giant world undergoes a deluge of kingdoms.

## Source Hierarchy and Attribution

### Governing Eastern Orthodox and Septuagintal Lens

The theology should be governed by the Eastern Orthodox Old Testament received through the Septuagintal tradition:

- [Genesis 6–9](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/static/ioscs/pdf/nets/01-gen-nets.pdf): corruption and violence, Noah's favour and obedience, prepared refuge, preservation, judgement, disembarkation, and covenant.
- [Exodus 1–18](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/static/ioscs/pdf/nets/02-exod-nets.pdf): bondage, prophetic commission, plagues, warnings, household preparation, mixed flight, passage, pursuit, wilderness need, provision, and delegated government.
- [Wisdom of Solomon 10–19](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/static/ioscs/pdf/nets/29-wissal-nets.pdf): Noah and the arrogant Giants, the Exodus, reciprocal signs, measured judgement, creation serving liberation, and wilderness preservation.
- [Sirach 44–45](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/static/ioscs/pdf/nets/30-sirach-nets.pdf): Noah as the means by which an earthly remnant remains and Moses as a meek, commissioned servant rather than a divine source of power.
- [Psalms 76–77 and 104–106 LXX](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/static/ioscs/pdf/nets/24-ps-nets.pdf): later generations retelling deliverance together with rebellion, forgetting, intercession, and mercy.
- Deuteronomy 8: wilderness preservation as humbling and formation; later abundance creates the danger of forgetting the giver.

Wisdom supplies the most useful specifically Orthodox bridge. Wisdom 14 remembers arrogant Giants perishing while the hope of the world takes refuge upon a small raft and preserves the seed of a generation. Wisdom 10–11 then joins Noahic preservation to liberation through a prophet. Wisdom 11 establishes that what punishes the oppressor can benefit the oppressed, while Wisdom 19 describes creation being newly ordered so that an incredible journey becomes possible.

The World Egg translation is not that morally aware natural powers choose sides. The Lord acts through created conditions, direct signs, providence, and commissioned servants. Menhir remain morally blind natural sovereignties and cannot be made the judges of the Giant empires merely because their domains appear within a plague.

### Enochic and Tewahedo Comparative Grammar

The Book of Enoch is not part of the standard Eastern Orthodox Greek or Slavonic canon. It is canonical specifically in the Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo traditions, which belong to Oriental Orthodoxy; this should not be generalized to every Oriental Orthodox church. Compare the [Eastern Orthodox longer-canon summary](https://www.oca.org/questions/scripture/canon-of-scripture), the [official Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo canon list](https://ethiopianorthodox.org/english/canonical/books.html), and the [Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo canon list](https://english.eritreantewahdo.org/?sermons=canonized-books).

Accordingly:

- Genesis, Exodus, Wisdom, Sirach, and Psalms govern moral and theological interpretation.
- [1 Enoch 6–16](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/77935/77935-h/77935-h.htm#VI-XI) supplies a respected, specifically Tewahedo-attributed comparative grammar.
- Enochic material does not override World Egg soul, angel, Idol, Menhir, or Giant continuity rules.

The useful Enochic sequence is precise: the Watchers violate a boundary; their offspring consume produce, humans, animals, and one another; creation and the murdered cry out; Noah receives warning; commissioned angels bind particular higher offenders, turn the Giant offspring against one another, and begin healing the wounded land. The good angels do not behave like a rival pantheon. They petition, receive separate commissions, perform them, and do not seize government afterward.

The fragmentary Qumran *Book of Giants*—a separate Second Temple work represented by manuscripts such as [4Q531](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/4Q531-1?locale=en_US)—adds an optional court-level grammar: doomed Giants receive dreams of judgement, debate them, bargain, deny, and seek interpretation. It can inspire Giant perspectives during the Closing Courts without becoming a controlling source or making the dynasts innocent.

## Canon Already Supporting the Model

The setting already requires a plural collapse:

- [[Age of Exodus]] says Giant cities fall through plague, rebellion, divine judgement, internal rot, and the collapse of their binding systems.
- [[Real-History Concordance]] states that the plagues and the Bronze-Age-style systems collapse are one recontextualized event.
- [[The Empire of Qianglong]] ends in the War of the Four Horsemen and retreat into recurrence vaults.
- [[The Serpent Kingdom of Ishkatar]] ends through slave revolt, Giant succession war, and a final Giant ritual that dries the Serpent's Spine.
- [[The Blood Empire of Xolotl]] loses sacrificial supply, enters civil war, and destroys its own Blood Sun and volcanic system.
- [[The Lost Civilization of Nythalum]] launches physically toward the Moon, ruptures its island, and leaves a few genuine lunar survivors among many dead rulers and Reconstructions.
- [[The Fall of Anshara]] is not caused by the Prophet's plagues; human uprisings and mortal-vampire secession contribute to the Drinking Wars, during which the predatory courts consume themselves.
- [[Phoenixes]] explicitly permits a heavenly commission that preserves a remnant through catastrophe while ending the structure that trapped it.
- [[Host and Greater Angels]] permits a host to shield mortal withdrawal, defeat apex created enemies, close breaches, and destroy the mechanism sustaining a supernatural army.
- [[Prophet's Roads and the Underways]] establishes that the Exodus impressed stable threshold corridors and anchor camps into the world.

The controlling synthesis therefore joins existing pieces rather than replacing five collapse stories with a sixth universal explanation.

## The Noahic Inversion: A Deluge of Kingdoms

The setting does not need another primordial water flood. The Noahic function can be carried by an inverted image:

> **The fertile world becomes lethal while the sterile desert becomes traversable.**

Behind the remnant, different regions experience different forms of deluge:

- rivers leave their courses, irrigation fails, or destructive water is released;
- plague and failed medicine cross class boundaries;
- harvest, granary, and tribute systems cease feeding cities;
- Giant civil wars consume armies, heirs, estates, and captive populations;
- Xolotlan fire and ash bury a region;
- Nythalum's failed launch produces earthquake, eruption, drowning, and distant wrecks;
- folded Ansharan courts close upon themselves;
- failed Egg White anchors tear estates, palaces, and rulers away from their political worlds;
- refugees move in numbers that behave like a human flood;
- abandoned infrastructure kills after its rulers have fled.

Later cultures may call this the Flood Behind, the Drowning of Crowns, the Years the World Came Loose, or another local name. No one metaphor is authorially literal everywhere.

The Great Glass Desert remains what it was before the crisis: three hundred leagues of inexplicable glass that the Giant powers themselves had long avoided. The plagues do not create it, reveal its purpose, prove that it was designed against Giants, or explain why it exists. The miracle is narrower:

> **A survivable road was given through a place in which no imperial road, army, map, or calculation had established one.**

## The Human Remnant

### Representative, Not Exhaustive

The Great Prophet gathers the representative remnant from which the principal Scattering peoples descend. He does not collect every living human being upon the planet.

Existing canon already leaves people behind: Ishkatari and Xolotlan slaves revolt after the central procession has moved; the human subject-city beneath [[Necropolis of Nihil]] dies before the Exodus reaches the Glass frontier. Other human communities may survive in forgotten northern enclaves, client kingdoms, islands, folded estates, underground works, or separate flights.

The recommended ruling is:

> **The Great Exodus carries the largest deliberate gathering of living human communities and forms the principal ancestral convergence of the later Scattering. It is not the only place human communities survived the end of the Giant Age.**

### The Remnant Is Not a Purity Line

Genesis places violence, corruption, and wrongdoing at the center of judgement. It does not explicitly say Noah survives because his ancestry alone is biologically uncontaminated. The World Egg should reject the Giant courts' own error that blood concentration measures moral or creaturely worth.

Genesis establishes the negative point, not the whole positive rule. The controlling mixed-remnant principle joins Mosaic mixed-multitude grammar to the World Egg's already plural human world: admission to the moving remnant is based upon response to the call and the practical gathering of households, not racial purity. The host may include:

- enslaved people from many Giant horizons and lesser states;
- free subject peoples and client-city households;
- fugitives, debtors, soldiers, officials, and collaborators who turn and accept release;
- giant-blooded humans who possess complete human souls and the Image;
- people carrying languages, rites, skills, and moral failures that will later become different cultures;
- children born during the road who never saw a Giant capital;
- possibly a very small number of nonhuman dependents or morally repentant Giant defectors, only if later continuity work establishes their status separately. Such a Giant could receive temporal mercy or protection without acquiring the Image, a human soul-road, or status as an ancestor of humanity.

The remnant is preserved for a future it has not earned by collective innocence. It remains capable of fear, violence, hoarding, faction, idolatry, ingratitude, courage, repentance, and sanctity.

## The Great Unbinding in Eight Movements and a Prologue

The Great Unbinding spans approximately c. 2300–2260 BR inside the longer c. 2300–2160 BR Age of Exodus. These movements overlap and should not be reduced to eight equal years or eight canonical plagues. [[The Glass Sojourn]] controls the following century of refuge and people-formation.

### Prologue. The Closing-Court Crisis — before c. 2300 BR

Before the Prophet's public campaign, the Giant world is already strained by diluted dynasties, disputed successions, subject-city autonomy, Titan-fragment politics, competing continuity systems, and preparations for flight. This is the pre-liberation crisis, not a hidden forty-year prophetic ministry.

The five horizons do not peak simultaneously. Smaller crowns change allegiance. Ruling ancestors recognize that something is approaching but interpret it as a rival dynasty, a failed cycle, an Astral alignment, a launch deadline, or an opportunity for purification.

### 1. The Cry and the Warning

The Lord's response begins in the testimony of enslavement, bloodshed, stolen children, consumed lives, violated land, and buried dead. The Prophet receives two linked commissions:

1. confront the Giant order and demand release;
2. prepare a remnant capable of surviving the judgement that refusal will bring.

Preparation precedes the final flight. It includes hidden gathering places, household registers, seed stores, breeding animals, medicines, tools, water vessels, burial records, interpreters, route signs, carts, portable texts, and agreements among communities that do not yet trust one another.

The Prophet announces signs before they occur. Off-ramps are genuine. A Giant, client king, overseer, soldier, or household that obeys a bounded warning may shelter people from that sign without thereby being declared righteous in every other matter. A miracle validates only its commission.

### 2. The Plagues of Unbinding

The plagues are not ten arbitrary attacks reskinned from Egypt. They strike the systems through which the Giant horizons impersonate divine necessity:

- ownership of water and food;
- control of health, disease, fertility, and burial;
- blood as political fuel and genealogical proof;
- celestial calendars and artificial lights;
- recurrence bodies, copied memories, and inherited offices;
- military roads and supernatural formations;
- the claim that a ruler's survival is identical with the survival of the world.

Each sign has four parts:

1. a demand or warning;
2. a created good freed from the imperial lie, or an imperial mechanism exposed and disabled;
3. a protected act available to those who obey;
4. a further hardening or fracture among those who refuse.

The Prophet announces, interprets, intercedes, and performs only the acts commissioned to him. He is not the metaphysical source of the plagues and cannot redeploy them as personal sorcery.

### 3. The War of the Closing Courts

The plagues withdraw the surpluses and common lies that made dynastic cooperation possible. Giant houses then choose to fight over:

- remaining human labor and sacrificial populations;
- blame for failed signs;
- access to Titan fragments and ancestor bodies;
- marriage claims that now require incompatible loyalties;
- recurrence vaults and replacement bodies;
- berths in Nythalum vessels;
- Egg White gates, folded refuges, and portable anchors;
- whether to release captives, bargain, defect, or make one final example of them.

No angel needs to implant hatred. The Enochic “sending one against another” becomes a judgement in which concealed betrayals are exposed, common predatory supply is removed, and every old claim comes due at once. The Giant houses remain responsible for the wars they choose.

This phase includes the War of the Four Horsemen, Ishkatari succession wars, Xolotlan struggles over sacrificial supply, Nythalum berth wars and rushed launch decisions, numerous lesser dynastic conflicts, and the separate Ansharan Drinking Wars.

### 4. The Commission of Severance

At the apex of the crisis, mortal revolt encounters opponents it cannot safely overcome: Titan-Firstborn capable of reconstitution, supernatural armies that recur after defeat, generative routes producing fresh war-broods, Idol manifestations anchored into royal machinery, and escape systems attempting to carry captive populations as property.

The heavenly architecture is a regional judgement-and-restoration commission borne by one actual, presently unnamed Phoenix and supported by appropriate choirs and Host servants. Later traditions may conflate the Phoenix, supporting choirs, signs, and commission-bodies; that disagreement concerns testimony, not the underlying ontology or headcount.

The commission may authorize the servants to:

- separate captives from self-reproducing Giant structures;
- expose which ancestors, copies, Masks, offices, and fragments are actually present;
- destroy selected replacement bodies and reconstitution engines;
- bind a small number of Titan-Firstborn or permanently lock their reconstitution;
- close particular generative routes or illicit tethers;
- break supernatural pursuit long enough for mortal withdrawal;
- preserve one route while the human host completes the movement entrusted to it.

It does not authorize them to:

- conquer and administer every Giant state;
- kill every Giant or every person on the losing side;
- explain the Glass;
- serve as the Prophet's summonable army;
- prove every rebel, camp judge, or later nation righteous;
- remain behind as a Patron or new pantheon;
- turn Nythalum's physical Moon project into an Astral event.

The heavenly servants withdraw when the assigned powers are contained and the protected passage is complete. Humanity must still walk, carry, ration, judge, bury, repair, and rebuild.

### 5. The Breakout and Last Pursuit

The Exodus is not one column leaving one capital. Multiple human columns, defecting client communities, liberated estates, herds, carts, and rearward companies converge toward the Glass through changing war zones.

Some gates are opened by local rulers. Others are forced by rebels. Some Giant houses release slaves to save their cities from the next sign; others pursue because they cannot imagine political life without ownership. The central host absorbs people who arrive with incompatible calendars, languages, blood laws, skills, and memories.

A final coalition or sequence of pursuing armies reaches the Glass approaches. This provides the Red-Sea-like military climax without requiring a literal sea. The commissioned heavenly servants break the supernatural cohesion, recurrence, or apex commanders of the pursuit. The pursuers then face ordinary distance, Glass heat, reflection, thirst, storm, broken logistics, and their own collapsing alliances without the protected road granted to the remnant.

The exact sensory event remains contested. Traditions may remember:

- a moving darkness between host and pursuer;
- white fire that cast shade rather than heat;
- a wind that revealed one road and erased every neighboring track;
- ordered wings across the sky;
- a Glass storm that stopped at the rear camp;
- reflected armies attacking their own formations;
- the simple failure of every imperial instrument at the same boundary.

Authorially, the safe fixed claim is only that the Lord provided a bounded passage and prevented the apex pursuit from destroying the remnant. The underlying nature of the Glass remains unknown.

### 6. The Ark in Motion

The ark is not one ship. It is the **ordered camp itself**: a federated moving sanctuary whose people, animals, stores, duties, names, and law remain in right relationship long enough to cross what should kill them.

The host survives through a union of miracle and labor.

#### Carried preservation

- grain, dried food, salt, seed, and breeding stock;
- herd animals, working animals, and selected useful living ecologies rather than every species in the world;
- tools, portable forges, looms, medicines, written and oral technical knowledge;
- water vessels, tents, shade cloth, carts, harness, spare wheels, and burial equipment;
- records of persons and households so the missing cannot be converted into unowned numbers;
- songs, judgements, languages, ritual fragments, and memories that will later diverge.

#### High-sign provision

- a daily unfamiliar food associated with night cold or morning condensation, sufficient but difficult to hoard;
- a lawful exception connected to a communal rest cycle, preventing the miracle from abolishing preparation;
- appointed water sites, opened seams, purified bitter sources, or wells that cannot be made private property while the host remains;
- shade, wind, nocturnal orientation, or temporary shelter at otherwise unsurvivable stages;
- unusual endurance of essential clothing, tents, and containers without making material repair unnecessary;
- mercy commissions that contain particular outbreaks without granting universal immunity.

These signs should end when their commission ends. They cannot become a repeatable Glass industry in the present day.

#### Human institutions

- camp courts and delegated judges;
- quartermasters answerable to public measures;
- scouts, route-keepers, singers, translators, and signal crews;
- physicians, midwives, cooks, herders, water testers, undertakers, and sanitation workers;
- rearward carts responsible for elderly, pregnant, injured, unwanted, and slow-moving people;
- burial registers and rites that refuse Giant claims upon the dead;
- quarantine and washing law shaped by recent plague;
- rotating guard and rest obligations;
- procedures for joining, leaving, confessing theft, reversing a bad route decision, and hearing strangers.

The miracles act at impossible bottlenecks. Human competence keeps the miracle from being squandered. Many people still die; some leave the route; some hoard, rebel, exploit, or despair. Preservation never means ease or collective sainthood.

The camps and threshold corridors impressed by this passage later become the anchors underlying the Prophet's Roads and lawful Jirahar Way-Gates.

### 7. The Orphaned World

The lands behind the host do not become one empty ruin field. They fragment into many outcomes:

- human client cities become brief successor states;
- abandoned irrigation produces flood in one valley and desert in another;
- starving cities empty into refugee roads, predatory armies, or mass graves;
- lesser Giants inherit offices they cannot sustain;
- Titan fragments remain imprisoned inside public works;
- some High Giants die in dynastic war;
- some rulers enter genuine Egg White refuges;
- others leave only an office, residue, Reconstruction, Mask, or counterfeit Patron;
- Nythalum Continuants remain physically lunar;
- Ansharan courts fold, drink one another, flee, or vanish under their own unresolved chronology;
- Idols survive the loss of particular royal feeding architectures and develop new Masks around the grief, violence, hunger, and memory of collapse;
- Menhir remain morally blind natural sovereignties rather than defeated Giant gods;
- isolated human communities survive without joining the principal Scattering.

This is why later northern ruins can contain live machines, sealed courts, copied rulers, human graves, autonomous client archives, hungry Masks, and contradictory successor claims without one answer applying to all of them.

### 8. The Glass Refuge and Later Failed Sanctuary

The Noahic story does not end when the Ark survives the water. It ends with disembarkation, worship, covenant, cultivation, and the danger that the preserved family remains human and fallible.

The World Egg now separates two stages. The **Glass refuge** is commanded and substantially successful: over approximately c. 2265–2165 BR it enables the host to:

- treat plague and war trauma;
- compile camp rulings without making every emergency measure eternal;
- assign and limit the custody of Giant artifacts;
- separate military command from permanent kingship;
- teach children who had never known slavery;
- distribute seed, animals, crafts, and testimony among future peoples;
- prevent the first armed faction from claiming the whole liberated inheritance;
- permit the moving ark to become a formed people before it became many nations.

After that long formation, a distinct **southern sanctuary** is intended to hold the liberated people together for three generations during settlement. It is not successfully established. The Prophet dies near the outward threshold around c. 2165 BR as the last living direct witness to the Giant world, and Ark Scattering begins by c. 2160 BR.

The command and the failed landing may be fixed without simplifying the Prophet's death. Traditions can continue to dispute refusal, murder, exhaustion, panic, rival counsel, divine judgement, or mystery. No present seed should casually settle the responsibility.

The historical consequence is clear even if guilt is not: the Ark host forms a real common people and mature institutions in refuge, but that unity does not survive land, succession and possession after the Prophet's death.

The same method should later be applied to lesser states. A polity that obeys a release demand may survive one sign and still face political consequences. A polity that defects may save its people without becoming the chosen ancestor of every later culture.

## What the Angels Judge

The Enochic layer is strongest when angelic action is judicially specialized rather than spectacularly general.

| Mortal-scale work | Heavenly-scale work |
|---|---|
| organize revolt and escape | sever a supernatural army's recurrence |
| open gates and carry the vulnerable | hold an apex pursuer or Titan-Firstborn |
| expose human testimony | make erased evidence and counterfeit continuity legible |
| destroy an ordinary engine | burn a self-reproducing metaphysical structure while separating captives |
| choose whether to release slaves | close the generative or tether route that would make refusal endless |
| walk the Glass route | preserve the bounded possibility of passage at its impossible stages |

This preserves the Prophet's human vocation. He is the messenger, intercessor, lawgiver, organizer, and leader of the remnant. He is not a demigod commanding celestial troops, and the angels do not replace the decision and labor of the people he leads.

Potential bound results include:

- a Titan-Firstborn dismembered and sealed across several later polities;
- a generative manifestation denied further access to the Material Realm;
- an ancestral court cut off from descendants who still invoke it;
- a continuity palace made into the prison of the ruler it was designed to preserve;
- a Giant war-host deprived of replacement bodies but still present as dangerous residue;
- an Idol-fed royal machine stripped of its particular Mask without injuring the guilty Idol Throne itself.

These outcomes now feed the five provisional actor slots in the bridge matrix: one bound Titan-Firstborn, one bodily escaped Giant, one counterfeit escaped-ancestor or Patron claimant, one genuine lunar Continuant, and one repentant Giant defector. The slots are approved planning interfaces; their names, biographies, final polities, and ultimate fates remain unapproved.

## What Happens to Those Outside the Main Host

The world left behind needs a human as well as Giant ecology.

### People who remain deliberately

- client rulers who believe they can inherit Giant offices;
- collaborators afraid of judgement or loss of status;
- households tied to sick, imprisoned, missing, or geographically isolated kin;
- rebels who choose to hold liberated cities rather than enter the Glass;
- communities that distrust the Prophet, his camp law, or the reports reaching them;
- people promised places in folded courts, vaults, or lunar vessels.

### People unable to join

- cities destroyed before the gathering reaches them;
- remote estates and islands that receive warning too late;
- captives moved as hostages by retreating Giant houses;
- plague zones and besieged communities cut off from roads;
- people whose own liberators become local tyrants;
- northern communities outside the Prophet's principal route network.

### Plausible outcomes

- brief human successor kingdoms;
- later absorption into the returning or scattered peoples;
- independent northern human traditions now forgotten in the south;
- death through famine, disease, war, predation, exposure, or failed infrastructure;
- survival inside vaults or folded estates at terrible cost;
- transportation into the Egg White as servants, hostages, ballast, or descendants of escape courts;
- inclusion in Nythalum lunar stock, menageries, records, or failed receiving systems;
- loss within Shadowlands overlaps produced by mass death;
- preservation only through ruins, graves, tools, and hostile records.

None of these outside groups automatically becomes a morally inferior “rejected humanity.” Some never received a meaningful choice. Their histories are among the principal things later Giant-Age planting should recover.

## Giant Death, Binding, and Residue

Enoch's dead Giant spirits cannot be copied directly into the World Egg. The controlling ruling in [[Giant Descent and Creaturely Continuity]] instead distinguishes these settled categories:

- an ordinarily dismembered or scattered Titan remains one `titan-continuity`; only the Lord or specifically commissioned heavenly severance can end that continuity or permanently lock reconstitution;
- a High or Dynastic Giant may remain alive, die, or cross bodily according to `giant-continuity`;
- a royal office may continue without the person who held it;
- a copied memory may be a non-conscious Reconstruction;
- historical performance may accrete into a legend-persona;
- an Idol may grow a new Mask around a dead dynasty's fear, violence, or praise;
- a failed Egg White attempt may leave an anchor, false arrival, or Patron claimant;
- ordinary human victims remain human souls and are never converted into Giant residue by being remembered, consumed, copied, or buried beside a ruler.

The binding judgement prevents immediate danger; it does not make every sealed being dead, redeemed, or harmless. That provides ancient actors for later history without turning every monster, demon, Idol, or Patron into a dead Giant.

## Cultural Memory Architecture

The same event should produce several incomplete but meaningful recensions:

- **Proto-Jirahar memory:** names, commands, camps, burial registers, road songs, warning sequences, the Prophet's disputed failure, and which rulings truly descend from the road.
- **Proto-Ahl-e-Ahd memory:** water, bread, shade, rescue debt, hospitality, witness, release, and the rule that survival goods cannot be owned without obligation.
- **Early Folk memory:** principally Orphaned memory of local Giant collapse, coldward survival, rearward hearths, dangerous powers and refusal to trade freedom for easy protection; later Ark contact may add borrowed road material.
- **Proto-Atherian memory:** liberation increasingly translated into heroic civic victory, engineering, and the overthrow of tyrants, with the harder camp obligations softened.
- **Later Sestaran memory:** Braided harbor traditions combine inherited Ark lists, local survivor records, ship and cart manifests, translated testimony and political argument over who preserved which archive.
- **Giant and successor memory:** natural disaster, treason, lower-caste revolt, failed cycle, foreign sabotage, unjust heavenly invasion, necessary evacuation, or the final proof that the dynasty was betrayed by impure heirs.
- **Vampire memory:** Anshara's separate collapse, self-serving claims of prior liberation, and accusations that humans abandoned the courts before the Drinking Wars.
- **Nythalum memory:** technical failure, berth treason, labor desertion, launch sabotage, or proof that the terrestrial population was never worthy of Ascension.

No culture owns the entire Exodus, and no later people's possession of one genuine memory proves every element of its theology.

## Authorial Knowledge, Open Questions, and Hard Boundaries

### Promoted authorial facts

- The Great Prophet's commission and principal signs were genuine.
- The plagues were one cause within a multi-causal collapse, not retrospective metaphor alone.
- The Prophet prepared a representative remnant before the final breakout.
- The remnant was mixed, morally fallible, and not selected by pure blood.
- Giant civil wars were real and arose from responsible choices under exposed pressure.
- A bounded heavenly intervention preserved the withdrawal and judged selected apex structures.
- The Glass predated everything in the event.
- The entry and approximately century-long sojourn impressed real threshold corridors and occupied anchor camps.
- Miraculous provision and ordinary material competence were both necessary.
- The mixed host became one people in the Glass.
- The later southern sanctuary failed; the Prophet died near the outward threshold as the last living direct witness to the Giant world; the people scattered.
- The Giant lands experienced many outcomes rather than one disappearance.

### Facts to leave open until the next pass

- the Prophet's personal name and complete early life;
- the exact number and canonical titles of the plagues;
- the Phoenix's personal name, complete witnessed form, and later cultural titles;
- which Titan-Firstborn were bound and where;
- which Giant rulers morally repented, defected, received temporal mercy, escaped, or survived;
- the number of people and duration of particular Glass stages;
- which later culture preserves the closest version of each provision miracle;
- whether a single Last Pursuit occurred or several rearward battles became one story;
- the immediate human successor states north of the Glass;
- the precise extent to which independent survivors later joined Ark-derived, Orphaned or Braided peoples.

### Hard boundaries

- Do not explain what created the Glass, what it ultimately is, or why the Giants first avoided it.
- Do not connect the crossing to the Year of Standing Stars.
- Do not connect angelic bird imagery to the Bird-Marked Sites.
- Do not settle Anshara's final sovereign, capital, or supreme escape.
- Do not reinterpret Nythalum's imperial Ascension Project, Moonbase, or Continuants as Egg White flight or a concealed Second Ascension. Any later individual encounter with the Egg White requires a separate continuity ruling.
- Do not make Menhir moral judges or the source of the Prophet's revelation.
- Do not destroy, redeem, or replace an Idol through one royal collapse.
- Do not make the Prophet the source of divine power or a mortal god.
- Do not turn Giant ancestry into hereditary guilt or the remnant into a eugenic bloodline.
- Do not call every casualty personally condemned; systems collapse harms people who did not choose the crimes of their rulers.
- Do not make every Giant die, flee, bind, or receive the same judgement.
- Do not make one heroic company cause the liberation.
- Do not make the food, route, shade, or angelic aid repeatable modern techniques.
- Do not make the sanctuary dispute a casual explanation of the Prophet's death.
