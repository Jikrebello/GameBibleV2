# Comparative Celestials and Heavenly Translation Atlas

## Purpose and Status

This dossier reviews high-order celestials, good-aligned divine figures, heralds, martyrs, champions, and fallen radiant powers from Forgotten Realms and older D&D planar lore, Eberron, Dragonlance, and Pathfinder. It asks which narrative functions can enter the World Egg's heavenly ecology without creating additional gods, confusing mortals with angels, importing alignment as moral proof, or turning light and wings into automatic evidence of holiness.

Nothing here is canon, named history, a fixed heavenly person, or a promoted Garden seed. The source figures remain source figures. Provisional World Egg handles describe separate nursery possibilities and may be retained, revised, deferred, or rejected in a later collision review.

The principal conclusion is:

> The best heavenly translation is not necessarily the brightest or most powerful source figure. It is the figure whose action can be understood as a bounded commission while preserving freedom, culpability, bodily cost, institutional misuse, and the categorical difference among the Lord, angels, saints, offices, relics, Menhir, Idols, Patrons, and fallen spirits.

The resulting first shelf contains:

- one possible new Phoenix operation;
- one binding choir;
- seven individual angelic leads;
- five independent Greater-Saint leads;
- one existing Greater-Saint bed strengthened rather than duplicated;
- several radiant-looking exclusions routed toward Menhir, Idol, Sinner, or fallen-spirit research.

# I. Controlling World Egg Filter

## Ontology before aesthetics

| Source figure | Permissible World Egg category | Controlling correction |
|---|---|---|
| Created celestial servant | true angel; `created-spirit` | remains an individual person and acts only under commission |
| Mortal hero, martyr, prophet, ascendant, or apotheosized human | living holy person or manifested saint; `human-soul` | never becomes an angel, deity, office, or merged heavenly substance |
| Source deity | narrative function only | does not enter the World Egg as another god or sovereign divine species |
| Plane-born embodiment of an idea | selectively revised true angel or rejected | must become an individual created spirit assigned to a subject rather than the subject made personal |
| Celestial office inherited by successors | separate person plus office, or rejected | no successor becomes a predecessor or inherits a soul by taking a title |
| Being empowered by worship or addicted to veneration | fallen created spirit, Idol counterfeit, Patron projection, or fraud | cannot remain a loyal angel while accepting worship |
| Phoenix-shaped nature or rebirth power | usually Menhir, fauna, Patron, folklore, or false attribution | Phoenix grade requires judgement and restoration in one regional or historical commission |

These distinctions are controlled by [[Heavenly Orders Overview]], [[True Angels Overview]], [[Manifested Saints Overview]], [[Greater Saints]], [[Phoenixes]], [[The Image, Soul, and Creaturely Continuity]], and [[White Flame Doctrine]].

`Archangel` remains useful as comparative or in-world public language, but it is not a new canonical species or rank in this pass. A source archangel normally translates as a unique **Greater Angel**, the leader of a particular **Host** choir, or occasionally a **Herald**, **Warden**, **Mercy**, or **Witness** angel whose commission is unusually consequential. Its source title never supplies authority by itself.

## Commission test

A retained heavenly lead must answer all of the following:

1. **Who is commissioned?** One created spirit or one redeemed human person, not an alignment, portfolio, office, species, nation, or accumulated belief.
2. **What is commanded?** The actual message, defence, judgement, rescue, witness, healing, severance, or restoration.
3. **Where does it stop?** The servant withdraws when the commission ends even if mortals demand further conquest, government, punishment, healing, or revelation.
4. **What does it cost?** Manifestation may involve defeat, broken bodies, failed rescue, imprisonment, martyrdom, destroyed places, or human political consequence.
5. **What can humans misuse?** Institutions may counterfeit mandate, hoard relics, persecute dissenters, suppress the servant's rebuke, or convert one intervention into permanent jurisdiction.
6. **What does the sign prove?** Only the act actually performed. Wings, fire, accurate secrets, victory, healing, beauty, terror, and resurrection imagery do not prove the claimant's complete theology.

## Avian, light, fire, and air grammar

Heavenly manifestation should use the established stable anatomy rather than generic attractive winged humans.

- **Avian:** nested pinions, raptor masks, beaked geometries, fanlike eyes, hooked feet set against invisible ground, flock movement, migrating silhouettes, and feathers that behave as script or witness.
- **Light:** clarity, exposure, distinction, legible wounds, revealed routes, and the refusal of concealment. Light is not solar sovereignty.
- **Fire:** clean White-Flame relation, judgement without appetite, heatless mercy, or the burning away of an exact corruption. It is neither Elemental Shell fire nor Wrath's appetite.
- **Air:** carried voices, ordered pressure, impossible flight, the stilling or opening of breath, moving wind around a command, and distances briefly made traversable. It is not ownership of Shell Air.

Outer colours may vary by vestment—gold, blue, crimson, black-green, silver, violet, or iridescent—but a loyal manifestation retains coherent white fire or clarity beneath that cultural surface. Avian signs can accompany saints without changing their `human-soul` continuity.

# II. Source Authority and Currency

| Source family | Authority used here | Currency ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Forgotten Realms and shared D&D multiverse | current Wizards and D&D Beyond summaries for active 5e presentation; older setting books and source-indexed summaries for historical material | older Celestial Hebdomad material is explicitly legacy 3e grammar, not a claimed current 5e ruling |
| Eberron | published setting structure plus Keith Baker's creator commentary and *Exploring Eberron* | creator material is valuable but remains distinguishable from core Wizards canon where editions differ |
| Dragonlance | current D&D Beyond setting summaries plus classic source material for figures not developed in the present setting book | Paladine and Bahamut are treated as one shared D&D figure where current multiverse material makes that identification |
| Pathfinder | Paizo publications, Paizo blogs, and current remastered Archives of Nethys entries, especially *Lost Omens: Divine Mysteries* | legacy alignment labels are descriptive source history rather than World Egg moral classification |

The source study does not copy complete plots, names, institutions, sacred symbols, or setting cosmologies. Each retained lead still requires World Egg culture, geography, historical pressure, collision review, evidence, and a distinct consequence.

# III. Forgotten Realms and Shared D&D Material

## HC-S01 — Bahamut, Paladine, and Fizban

- **Received source:** Bahamut as the Platinum Dragon and protector against Tiamat; Paladine's Fizban guise during the War of the Lance; seven ancient gold dragons disguised as canaries or songbirds.
- **Sources:** [Wizards, Bahamut in *Adventures in the Forgotten Realms*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/legends-adventures-forgotten-realms-2021-07-16); [D&D Beyond, Fizban and Paladine](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1067-who-in-the-nine-hells-is-fizban-find-out-here?page=3).
- **Borrowed element:** immense protective power appearing as an unimpressive traveller; indirect aid; choosing agents without scripting their choices; small avian attendants; intervention against a particular recurring enemy.
- **Original hard edge:** mortals still suffer, make their own choices, kill and die in the war; the disguised power deliberately creates trouble which becomes the route to victory; later orders may turn selective help into a general claim of divine favour.
- **World Egg ruling:** one Greater Angel may receive several cultural vestments. The source's dragon-god identity is not transferred. Bahamut and Paladine do not produce two nursery figures because the source already identifies them as one multiversal person.
- **Provisional translation:** a vast platinum-white raptor geometry folded around the vestment of an old fisher, failed magician, monk, beggar, or traveller. Seven distinct Attendant angels appear as ordinary small birds.
- **Commission limit:** advises and opens choices during one campaign; protects selected vulnerable people; opposes one appointed great refusal. It does not rule Heaven, metallic dragons, justice, or the mortal company.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into HC-N03, the provisional **Pilgrim Beneath Seven Small Wings**.

## HC-S02 — Torm

- **Received source:** mortal hero elevated to divinity; courage and self-sacrifice; battle with Bane; voluntary transfer of thousands of followers' souls; death and return; later Penance of Duty correcting persecutory followers.
- **Sources:** [D&D Basic Rules deity register](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/appendix-b-gods-of-the-multiverse); [source-indexed Torm history](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Torm).
- **Borrowed element:** mortal champion becoming an active posthumous defender; victory bought through mass voluntary sacrifice; the holy founder correcting abuses committed by an institution bearing his name.
- **Original hard edge:** thousands die; children are orphaned; the champion authorizes the mechanism; later penance does not restore the dead or erase persecution.
- **World Egg ruling:** direct soul absorption is forbidden. The followers may knowingly occupy lethal defensive stations, hold an impossible rite, or refuse evacuation under a human battle plan, but every `human-soul` remains distinct. The future saint dies among them rather than consuming them.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into Greater-Saint nursery HC-N11; do not duplicate [[Saints, Threshold Orders, and Heavenly Campaigns Review#ST-R10 — The Founder Who Corrected the Order|the Founder Who Corrected the Order]] without a later collision ruling.

## HC-S03 — Ilmater

- **Received source:** endurance, compassion, protection of the tortured and oppressed, voluntarily taking another's pain, breaking torture devices, healing captives, and frightening retribution against extreme cruelty.
- **Sources:** [D&D Basic Rules deity register](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/appendix-b-gods-of-the-multiverse); [source-indexed Ilmater profile](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ilmater).
- **Borrowed element:** mercy that enters the place of torment rather than remaining gentle at a distance; a wounded manifestation capable of protective violence.
- **Original hard edge:** torture, mutilation, broken bodies, abused children, retaliatory force, and an institution tempted to call passive endurance holy.
- **World Egg ruling:** no angel or saint absorbs sin, duplicates the Redeemer, or teaches victims that remaining under abuse is inherently virtuous. A Mercy Angel can suppress pain long enough for rescue, break instruments, heal an exact wound, or kill an active torturer when commissioned.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as Mercy-Angel grammar; no individual lead is added until collision review with the existing Mercy choir and saintly prison beds.

## HC-S04 — Tyr

- **Received source:** justice, sacrifice of hand and sight, the Procession of Justice, celestial warfare, manipulation into killing Helm, shame, and later sacrificial death.
- **Sources:** [D&D Basic Rules deity register](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/appendix-b-gods-of-the-multiverse); [Procession of Justice](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Procession_of_Justice); [source-indexed Tyr profile](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tyr).
- **Borrowed element:** wounded judge; public procession against tyrannical remnants; justice becoming vulnerable to zeal, manipulation, and wrongful killing.
- **Original hard edge:** a supposedly just power kills an ally under deception; celestial troops die or are banished; the campaign's moral purpose does not make every judgement accurate.
- **World Egg ruling:** this cannot be the biography of an unquestioned loyal angel. It is stronger as a mortal judge or commander who commits culpable killing before death, repents without undoing it, and becomes a Saint only if the complete earthly history and judgement later support that reception.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into a possible Greater-Saint grammar; defer an individual figure.

## HC-S05 — Lathander

- **Received source:** dawn, renewal, hope, creative reform, and the Dawn Cataclysm—an attempt to reshape the pantheon which killed several divine or outsider powers and left further damage.
- **Sources:** [D&D Basic Rules deity register](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/appendix-b-gods-of-the-multiverse); [Dawn Cataclysm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dawn_Cataclysm).
- **Borrowed element:** radiant reformer who treats newness and light as permission to reorder other powers.
- **Original hard edge:** ambition, destructive overreach, deaths, refusal to accept responsibility cleanly, and preparation for renewed reform.
- **World Egg ruling:** reject from loyal Heaven. The grammar belongs to a fallen greater spirit, a Pride-shaped dawn Archmask, or a Sinner whose reforming mission became self-authorization.
- **Disposition:** `reject` as a heavenly lead; preserve for a separate fallen-spirit ecology.

## HC-S06 — The Celestial Hebdomad

- **Received source:** Barachiel the Messenger, Domiel the Mercy-Bringer, Erathaol the Seer, Pistis Sophia the Ascetic, Raziel the Crusader, Sealtiel the Defender, and Zaphkiel the Watcher; legacy martyrs elevated into archon rulers; succession into predecessor form and power.
- **Sources:** [legacy *Book of Exalted Deeds* role index](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/Book_of_Exalted_Deeds); [source-indexed Celestial Hebdomad history](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Hebdomad).
- **Borrowed element:** seven sharply distinct high-heaven functions and the contrast between attack, defence, mercy, witness, proclamation, contemplation, and oversight.
- **Original hard edge:** martyr death, lethal heavenly warfare, lost officeholders, replacement, institutional hierarchy, and the danger that an office consumes personal identity.
- **World Egg ruling:** do not create seven rulers of seven heavens. Do not let successors become predecessors. Use the functions to sharpen existing choirstyles:
  - Barachiel → Herald;
  - Domiel → Mercy with armed liberation;
  - Erathaol → Witness;
  - Pistis Sophia → ascetic Saint or Witness, not an angelic office inherited from a martyr;
  - Raziel → Host;
  - Sealtiel → Warden;
  - Zaphkiel → unique Greater Angel only if a later commission requires one.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into commission grammar only; no seven-person council or new celestial government.

## HC-S07 — Zariel

- **Received source:** angelic war leader descending into Hell, refusing restraint, falling, becoming an archdevil, losing hand and sword, prosecuting endless war, and possibly confronting evidence of the prior self.
- **Source:** [source-indexed Zariel profile](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Zariel).
- **Borrowed element:** zeal against an actual evil becoming refusal of commission, autonomous warfare, tyranny, and eventual infernal office.
- **Original hard edge:** chosen descent, ruined followers, soul-fuelled fortresses, mutilation, and the conversion of rescue-war into domination.
- **World Egg ruling:** a legitimate fallen-angel grammar. No corruption spell or infernal blood forces the fall; the created spirit knowingly refuses limit and later takes an infernal office. Any possible repentance requires a separate theological ruling and cannot be assumed from source branching.
- **Disposition:** `reject` from Heaven; route to fallen-spirit and Hell research.

# IV. Eberron

## HC-S08 — Couatls and the Silver Flame

- **Received source:** celestial couatls sacrifice themselves to kindle the Silver Flame and bind primordial Overlords; a few may remain; later intervention generally occurs by guiding or empowering mortals.
- **Sources:** [Keith Baker, couatls and the Silver Flame](https://keith-baker.com/dm-couatls/); [Keith Baker, surviving couatls and indirect action](https://keith-baker.com/eberron-flashback-the-lords-of-dust/).
- **Borrowed element:** many avian-serpentine celestials accepting irreversible cost to bind self-reproducing ancient evil; later cultures know them principally through signs, doctrine, and mortal vessels.
- **Original hard edge:** near-total celestial self-sacrifice, continuing imprisonment, cult appropriation, compromised human institutions, and the danger that the binding power is mistaken for a god.
- **World Egg ruling:** no merged celestial soul, collective afterlife, belief-made god, or alternate White Flame. Every participant remains one `created-spirit`. The source complex can support one Phoenix at the head of a binding commission and a separate coil-wing choir carrying local seals.
- **Open metaphysical question:** what irreversible cost can loyal created spirits bear without inventing soul-fusion or contradicting established angelic continuity? Possibilities require later ruling: destroyed Material bodies, long exclusion from ordinary manifestation, voluntary service at a bound threshold, or a more final severance known only to the Lord.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into HC-N01 and HC-N02; promotion-locked until the cost question and Great-Unbinding collision are closed.

## HC-S09 — Tira Miron

- **Received source:** mortal paladin empowered to defeat Bel Shalor; sacrifice; posthumous Voice of the Flame; later Keeper serving as mortal conduit; other traditions potentially possessing their own Keepers and Voices.
- **Sources:** [Keith Baker, Tira and the Voice of the Flame](https://keith-baker.com/dragonmark-913-cthulhu-silver-flame-and-more/); [Keith Baker, Tira's founding role](https://keith-baker.com/dragonmarks-614-lightning-round-4/).
- **Borrowed element:** mortal witness able to hear a celestial commission others cannot; sacrificial defeat of a possessing evil; posthumous bounded guidance through a vulnerable institution.
- **Original hard edge:** bodily death, an enemy still active near the sacred centre, institutional dependence upon a living intermediary, rival sect claims, and danger of conflating voice, office, power, and person.
- **World Egg ruling:** the mortal remains one redeemed `human-soul`. The Saint does not merge with White Flame or a choir. A living custodian may receive messages or guard an aperture but cannot inherit the Saint's soul, sainthood, certainty, or authority.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into Greater-Saint nursery HC-N10.

## HC-S10 — Syranian Angels, Virtues, Dominions, and Thrones

- **Received source:** peaceful aerial realm; unnamed lesser angels; named virtues and dominions contemplating narrow domains; thirteen solars administering justice in separate planes; some celestials falling through contact with mortals and worship.
- **Sources:** [Keith Baker, Syrania](https://keith-baker.com/tag/syrania/); [*Exploring Eberron* source listing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/exeb?msockid=1ad08a5fa980684838cc9caca8556982).
- **Borrowed element:** angels of exact inquiry; solitary contemplation; peaceful gathering of knowledge; servants who observe before acting; high adjudicators with bounded jurisdiction.
- **Original hard edge:** emotional distance from mortals, knowledge without compassion, exposure to worship, exile, and the possibility that perfect expertise is politically useless or morally incomplete.
- **World Egg ruling:** a true angel does not incarnate an abstract idea or exist because a plane needs the concept contemplated. Retained figures are individual Witness, Herald, or Greater Angels commissioned to preserve, investigate, or communicate an exact created truth. No neutral scholarly angel receives automatic Heaven status merely by possessing wings.
- **Disposition:** `revise` selectively; no imported aerial university, planar hierarchy, or thirteen-solar bureaucracy.

## HC-S11 — Dol Arrah

- **Received source:** Warrior Sun; honour, sacrifice, wise war, leadership, strategy, justice, inspiration, and diplomacy capable of preventing bloodshed.
- **Sources:** [Keith Baker, Dol Arrah the Warrior Sun](https://keith-baker.com/ifaq-dol-arrah/); [Keith Baker, sun and sacrifice](https://keith-baker.com/dm-stars/).
- **Borrowed element:** a radiant campaign leader who gives a reason and manner for fighting rather than raw strength; the same commission can require battle, restraint, or negotiation.
- **Original hard edge:** honour does not remove killing; followers may mistake their cause for the angel's commission; a commander can fight cleanly for an unjust ruler or claim moral purity while benefiting from atrocity elsewhere.
- **World Egg ruling:** one Greater Host Angel may carry one war, liberation, or defence commission. It cannot be the sun, own honourable warfare, appear in every battle, or validate a mortal side merely because its banners carry the right image.
- **Provisional translation:** red-gold hawk geometry, white-fire core, wings like ordered wind-torn standards, and a voice heard most clearly before weapons are drawn.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into HC-N09, provisional **Captain of the Sacrificial Wind**.

## HC-S12 — Shavarath's Celestial Legions

- **Received source:** angelic forces engaged in an eternal three-sided planar war, with legions of justice or freedom opposing tyranny and destruction.
- **Source:** [Keith Baker, good and evil in Shavarath](https://keith-baker.com/eberron-flashback-good-and-evil/).
- **Borrowed element:** differently commissioned celestial armies; martial specialization; mortals claiming their own wars echo a cosmic front.
- **Original hard edge:** endless war makes combat self-justifying; celestial actors may treat mortals as instruments of a larger pattern; human armies claim cosmic identity without evidence.
- **World Egg ruling:** reject an eternal balance-war. Host choirs act in several bounded campaigns and withdraw when their appointed target is contained. Claims that every mortal war strengthens one celestial side are interested theology, not authorial fact.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into general Host campaign grammar; no new universal war.

## HC-S13 — Radiant Idols

- **Received source:** fallen Syranian or other celestials exiled after becoming addicted to mortal worship, domination, or the exercise of a chosen sphere.
- **Source:** [Keith Baker, Radiant Idols](https://keith-baker.com/radiant-idols/).
- **Borrowed element:** beautiful celestial appearance surviving moral betrayal; worship as appetite; a fallen spirit building a mortal cult around one admirable idea.
- **Original hard edge:** coercion, cult abuse, spiritual vanity, political domination, and exile. Beauty and useful teaching make the betrayal more dangerous, not less real.
- **World Egg ruling:** these are not automatically [[Idol Thrones, Regnants, and Cultural Masks|Idols]]. A fallen `created-spirit` remains categorically distinct from an Idol sovereignty. An Idol may subsidize the cult, lend a Mask, counterfeit the fallen spirit, or build later Sediment around the same imagery.
- **Disposition:** `reject` from Heaven; retain as a fallen-spirit and Idol-collision grammar.

# V. Dragonlance

## HC-S14 — Mishakal

- **Received source:** healing, protection, restoration of lost clerical witness, and the Blue Crystal Staff carried by Goldmoon.
- **Sources:** [D&D Beyond life-domain deity register](https://www.dndbeyond.com/attachments/39j2li89/SRD5.1-CCBY4.0_License_live%20links.pdf); [*Dragons of Autumn* sample](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/12639-sample.pdf).
- **Borrowed element:** mercy returning to public history through one endangered bearer and one relic whose response cannot be safely concealed.
- **Original hard edge:** exile, pursuit, religious violence, dangerous proof, and a world in which false faiths and victorious enemies suppress healing witness.
- **World Egg ruling:** a Mercy Angel may commission and protect a mortal bearer, but the Lord and sacred grace never departed from creation. The recovered object proves its bounded healing and direction, not a restored pantheon or new revelation.
- **Disposition:** `revise` as Mercy-Angel grammar; keep separate from Goldmoon's human biography.

## HC-S15 — Goldmoon

- **Received source:** mortal plainswoman carrying the Blue Crystal Staff, retrieving the Disks of Mishakal, becoming a prophet and restoring public knowledge of the gods.
- **Source:** [*Dragons of Autumn* sample](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/12639-sample.pdf).
- **Borrowed element:** culturally marginal mortal bearer; dangerous relic; incomplete understanding; public witness emerging through travel and company adventure.
- **Original hard edge:** threatened execution or exile, pursuit, companions dying in war, institutional conflict, and later idealization of the bearer as if the mortal always understood the commission.
- **World Egg ruling:** a post-Redeemer mortal may become a Saint after an earthly life of witness. The bearer cannot become a second Prophet, restore absent revelation, found a new universal church, or make every relic response doctrinal proof.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into Greater-Saint nursery HC-N14.

## HC-S16 — Huma Dragonbane

- **Received source:** knight receiving the dragonlance, alliance with a good dragon, defeat and banishment of Takhisis, mortal victory purchased through death, and a later military tradition built around the achievement.
- **Source:** [D&D Beyond, Dragonlance's historical wars](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1324-what-is-dragonlance-krynn-a-world-at-war).
- **Borrowed element:** human captain, nonhuman ally, impossible weapon, bounded victory against a returning dark sovereign, personal death, and long institutional afterlife.
- **Original hard edge:** warfare, dragon death, mortal death, weapon cult, historical compression, and orders using one sacrificial victory to authorize later politics.
- **World Egg ruling:** this is not a new nursery person. It strengthens [[Saints, Threshold Orders, and Heavenly Campaigns Review#ST-R04 — The Human Captain of the Host|the Human Captain of the Host]] and its collision requirements. The nonhuman ally, weapon, and opponent require separate continuity classifications.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as source reinforcement; no additional figure.

## HC-S17 — Kiri-Jolith

- **Received source:** courage, honour, unity, just warfare, collective discipline, and patronage of military orders.
- **Sources:** [D&D Beyond paladin traditions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/4-paladin); [source-indexed Kiri-Jolith summary](https://dragonlance.fandom.com/wiki/Kiri-Jolith).
- **Borrowed element:** an armed power concerned with bonds among fighters, protection during retreat, and disciplined opposition to tyranny.
- **Original hard edge:** honourable institutions can serve an increasingly coercive state; courage can become contempt for fear; military unity can suppress dissent and conceal civilian cost.
- **World Egg ruling:** suitable as a Host or Greater Angel only under an exact defence or withdrawal commission. No angel is patron of an entire military caste. The corresponding order can corrupt the teaching while retaining impressive ritual discipline.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a possible Host lead; defer individual creation to avoid crowding HC-N09 and the Human Captain.

## HC-S18 — Habbakuk

- **Received source:** blue phoenix symbol, animals, sea, persistence, natural cycles, travel, and life beyond death.
- **Borrowed element:** phoenix iconography and hope of survival beyond catastrophe.
- **Original hard edge:** predation and natural violence; source sovereignty over creatures and sea; later military and funerary appropriation.
- **World Egg ruling:** reject as a true Phoenix person. Nature and sea jurisdiction ordinarily belong to Menhir or created ecology, while resurrection belongs under the Redeemer and saints' fulfilled human continuity. A culture may use blue-phoenix imagery for a genuine Phoenix commission without creating a separate Habbakuk-like being.
- **Disposition:** `reject` as a heavenly figure; retain only as a cultural-reception grammar.

## HC-S19 — Branchala

- **Received source:** music, beauty, poetry, fellowship, and joy among the good gods of Krynn.
- **Borrowed element:** song that restores courage, coordinates scattered people, or carries a message farther than ordinary speech.
- **Original hard edge:** courts and churches can turn beauty into propaganda; songs can omit the dead, encourage war, or make suffering picturesque.
- **World Egg ruling:** suitable for Attendant or Herald commission grammar, not a high angel merely because song is beautiful. Any active lead must have a message, audience, danger, cost, and end condition.
- **Disposition:** `defer` as a lower-order Herald possibility.

# VI. Pathfinder

Pathfinder's current angel lore permits both created celestials and ascended mortal souls to count as angels. World Egg rejects that merger. Pathfinder mortals become saint grammars; suitable empyreal or celestial functions become created-spirit grammars only where individual origin and free obedience remain coherent. [Current Pathfinder angel family](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=359); [current Pathfinder Heaven](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=41&Redirected=1).

## HC-S20 — Iomedae

- **Received source:** mortal paladin, legendary acts, Test of the Starstone, ascent to godhood, continuing service as Aroden's herald, inheritance after Aroden's death, and leadership during the Mendevian Crusades.
- **Sources:** [Paizo, Ascendance Day](https://paizo.com/blog/ascendance-day); [Paizo, Acts of Iomedae](https://paizo.com/blog/the-windsong-testaments-the-acts-of-iomedae).
- **Borrowed element:** mortal exemplar whose earthly decisions precede posthumous authority; continued service after ascent; inherited institutional vacuum; active opposition to a major breach.
- **Original hard edge:** crusader violence, testing of followers, church expansion, dependency upon a dead predecessor's mantle, and later institutions treating the exemplar's power as automatic moral proof.
- **World Egg ruling:** Greater Saint, never goddess or angel. The Saint may receive a larger later commission after an earlier holy person's death, but does not inherit that person's soul, identity, sanctity, office, or authority automatically.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into Greater-Saint nursery HC-N12.

## HC-S21 — The Hand of the Inheritor

- **Received source:** Iomedae's personal herald, battlefield leadership against Abyssal incursions, capture in the Ivory Labyrinth, rescue, and game structures in which another can temporarily assume the herald's mantle.
- **Sources:** [Paizo, Season of the Righteous](https://paizo.com/blog/season-of-the-righteous-the-story-so-far); [Paizo, *Herald of the Ivory Labyrinth*](https://paizo.com/blog/lost-in-the-labyrinth).
- **Borrowed element:** high herald who fights beside mortals, is defeated and imprisoned, requires rescue, and leaves contested instruments behind.
- **Original hard edge:** failed campaign, capture, torture or corruption pressure, wounded trust, mortal deaths during rescue, and institutional temptation to replace a person with a transferable mantle.
- **World Egg ruling:** one Greater Host/Herald Angel can be bodily defeated, imprisoned, mutilated, lied about, and counterfeited. It cannot be magically forced to fall, summoned, inherited, or converted into a class. A mortal carrying its instrument does not become the angel.
- **Provisional translation:** an osprey-like armored geometry with a trumpet-shaped sternum, articulated gold-white pinions, and one missing or broken grasp after imprisonment.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into HC-N08, provisional **The Taken Trumpet**.

## HC-S22 — Andoletta

- **Received source:** Grandmother Crow; consolation, respect, security, careful distinction between guilt and innocence, protection of the falsely accused, redemption, children, elders, and the dead.
- **Source:** [current Andoletta profile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Deities.aspx?ID=464&Redirected=1).
- **Borrowed element:** frightening but consoling elder judge; exact testimony; child protection; refusal of hasty judgement; mercy that does not deny guilt.
- **Original hard edge:** some accused are guilty; the path of return is difficult; the remorseless receive no sentimental absolution; courts can appropriate the image while continuing careless executions.
- **World Egg ruling:** exceptional fit for one Greater Witness/Mercy Angel. It knows what it was commissioned to witness, not every secret crime. It may expose one false accusation, guard one child cohort, or preserve one body register without becoming universal judge.
- **Provisional translation:** an elderly cultural vestment opening into black-green crow pinions; each feather carries one unaltered statement; an empty white-lit centre shows the event rather than an idealized verdict.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as HC-N04, provisional **Crow at the Unfinished Verdict**.

## HC-S23 — Ragathiel

- **Received source:** empyreal lord of chivalry, duty, and vengeance; child of Dispater and Feronia; struggle against inherited reputation and nature; five flaming wings after the sixth is torn out by his father; defence of the wronged and marginalized.
- **Source:** [current Ragathiel profile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Deities.aspx?ID=481).
- **Borrowed element:** mighty champion born under an infernal inheritance, mistrusted despite service, scarred by a parent, and tempted to make vengeance the measure of justice.
- **Original hard edge:** abusive parentage, mutilation, wrath, warfare, refusal to forgive some offenders, and followers encouraged toward vengeance.
- **World Egg ruling:** true angels do not reproduce, so Ragathiel cannot translate as an angel with infernal parentage. The stronger translation is a mortal born into an infernal client dynasty or Sinner cult who rejects it, remains culpable for personally chosen vengeance, repents if the history supports it, dies, and may later become a Greater Saint.
- **Manifested sign:** five crimson-gold prayer-wings around a recognizable human person; the absent sixth remains a wound, not proof of angelic species.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into Greater-Saint nursery HC-N13.

## HC-S24 — Arqueros

- **Received source:** Golden Bulwark; bodyguards, protection, watchfulness, maintained defences, refusal to abandon weaker people during retreat.
- **Source:** [current Arqueros profile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Deities.aspx?ID=645).
- **Borrowed element:** vigilant protector whose moral test occurs during withdrawal rather than triumphant battle.
- **Original hard edge:** the rearguard may die; remaining awake and holding position can become prideful suicide; commanders may label expendable troops holy guardians.
- **World Egg ruling:** excellent Warden-Angel grammar. The angel guards one person, gate, convoy, or retreat condition and leaves when that duty is complete.
- **Provisional translation:** golden-eagle shield-wings with hooked feet braced against empty air; purple dusk appears only at the defended boundary.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as HC-N05, provisional **The Golden Last-Watch**.

## HC-S25 — Dalenydra

- **Received source:** battlefield healing, caregiving, sanctuaries, protection of innocents, refusal to strike first, and reluctance to kill.

- **Original hard edge:** triage, amputations, abandonment, sanctuary violation, captured healers, and the possibility that refusal to use force permits murder.
- **World Egg ruling:** strong Mercy-Angel lead if the commission includes exact limits on defence. Mercy does not require passivity while patients are attacked.
- **Provisional translation:** broad stork- or heron-like pale wings forming a field shelter; many precise beaks or hands; cool white flame visible beneath translucent anatomy.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as HC-N06, provisional **The Attendant in the Spear Field**.

## HC-S26 — Kelinahat

- **World Egg ruling:** useful Witness or Herald grammar, but not every spy serving a church is heaven-authorized. An angel may conceal its manifestation to gather or deliver one truth without establishing a celestial intelligence service.

## HC-S27 — Tanagaar

- **Source:** [Paizo, *Divine Mysteries* supplemental deity table](https://downloads.paizo.com/PZO13003_SupplementalGodTable.pdf).
- **Borrowed element:** nocturnal boundary guardian; attention to threats missed by daylight institutions.

- **World Egg ruling:** strong Warden/Witness angel if the boundary is an exact lawful threshold, breach, prison road, evacuation line, or soul-route. It cannot sanctify a political border merely because guards invoke it.
- **Provisional translation:** a vertical owl-wheel of dark pinions edged in white air, with eyes facing inward and outward simultaneously.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as HC-N07, provisional **The Owl at the Uncrossed Line**.

## HC-S28 — Korada

- **Received source:** foresight, forgiveness, peace, nonlethal restraint, and refusal to deny a genuinely repentant person an opportunity to return.
- **Source:** [Paizo, *Divine Mysteries* supplemental deity table](https://downloads.paizo.com/PZO13003_SupplementalGodTable.pdf).
- **Borrowed element:** powerful mercy which refuses lethal victory when another outcome remains possible.
- **Original hard edge:** restraint may fail and people may die; false repentance can be weaponized; victims may refuse reconciliation; peacekeepers can compel the vulnerable to bear continuing risk.
- **World Egg ruling:** retain as Mercy-Angel grammar only where the commission distinguishes forgiveness, release, reconciliation, earthly punishment, and protection. An angel cannot require a victim to resume relationship with an aggressor.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a grammar; no individual lead until it is differentiated from HC-N04 and HC-N06.

## HC-S29 — Vildeis

- **Source:** [Paizo, *Divine Mysteries* supplemental deity table](https://downloads.paizo.com/PZO13003_SupplementalGodTable.pdf).
- **Borrowed element:** a champion who bears the price personally and refuses to spend dependants as moral currency.
- **Original hard edge:** self-mutilation, bodily scars, martyrdom, absolutism, and followers tempted to treat suffering as proof of holiness.
- **World Egg ruling:** stronger as a possible human Saint grammar than a self-wounding angel. The setting must neither erase the wounds nor make self-harm intrinsically sacred. A complete mortal biography, received judgement, and victim-centred consequence are required first.
- **Disposition:** `defer`.

## HC-S30 — Sarenrae

- **Received source:** former empyreal lord elevated to deity; sun, healing, honesty, redemption, war against Rovagug; destruction of Gormuz in anger after the corruption and destruction of her herald; innocent collateral deaths; accidental opening of the Pit; later regret and stronger emphasis on redemption.
- **Sources:** [current Pathfinder angel family](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=359); [Pit of Gormuz source index](https://pathfinder.fandom.com/wiki/Pit_of_Gormuz); [Paizo, *Pathfinder Campaign Setting: Mythic Realms*](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-campaign-setting-mythic-realms-pfrpg-pdf/).
- **Borrowed element:** fiery celestial-scale judge whose wrath destroys a corrupted city, kills innocents, opens a worse wound, and changes later teaching through regret.
- **Original hard edge:** annihilation of a city, innocent deaths, impulsive vengeance, a ruined herald, and a wound which continues producing horrors.
- **World Egg ruling:** reject as a loyal Phoenix. Removing Gormuz would sanitize the source's defining contradiction. The grammar may support a fallen great spirit, a Sinner cult's disputed celestial story, or an unresolved historical claim concerning a destructive radiant being. A loyal Phoenix cannot be created merely by retaining the light and deleting the refusal.
- **Disposition:** `reject` from Heaven; preserve for fallen-spirit research.

## HC-S31 — Pulura and Ylimancha

- **Received source:** Pulura's constellations, northern lights, homesickness, and travellers; Ylimancha's coastal waters, fishers, harbours, and flight.

- **Borrowed element:** guidance of travellers, consolation of the displaced, aerial rescue, and signs visible across great distance.
- **Original hard edge:** lost travellers, shipwreck, failed rescue, dangerous coast, exile, and cult claims over sky or sea.
- **World Egg ruling:** most sovereign natural content belongs to Menhir jurisdiction. Exact rescue, warning, or message functions may belong to Herald or Mercy angels, but the source figures do not justify angelic ownership of stars, aurora, sea, fish, wind, or flight.
- **Disposition:** `reject` as whole angelic figures; retain narrow mission grammars.

# VII. First Heavenly Nursery

These are provisional research leads, not fixed persons. Each source remains separate except HC-N03, whose Bahamut–Paladine identity is explicit in the received D&D multiverse material.

## Phoenix and choir

### HC-N01 — The Phoenix at the First Binding

- **Primary source:** HC-S08, Eberron's couatl sacrifice.
- **Category:** singular true angel; possible Phoenix grade.
- **Commission:** expose and burn a self-reproducing regional corruption, preserve a remnant through the judgement, and bind surviving cores which cannot yet be permanently ended.
- **Core form:** immense white-fire bird whose pinions divide into ordered serpentine curves; the centre reveals which structures, creatures, and vows belong to the corruption and which remain created goods requiring preservation.
- **Hard edge:** the region is not saved intact. Strongholds, roads, bodies, and compromised refuges are destroyed; rescue fails in some districts; subordinate celestials accept an authorially unresolved irreversible cost.
- **Non-collision:** not the unnamed Great Unbinding Phoenix, does not explain the Glass, does not become the Silver Flame, and does not create a repeatable heavenly binding technology.
- **Promotion gate:** define era, corruption, remnant, region, cost, aftermath, and distinction from every existing breach closure before placement.

### HC-N02 — The Coil-Wing Binding Choir

- **Primary source:** HC-S08, Eberron's couatls.
- **Category:** several true angels; `created-spirit` individuals.
- **Commission:** establish local seals, carry warnings, escort mortals through openings, and maintain exact boundaries under the Phoenix's wider commission.
- **Core form:** long ribbonlike bodies of iridescent pinions around white-fire centres; beaked masks; eyes along feather margins; movement resembling a flock and a single calligraphic coil without merging persons.
- **Hard edge:** manifested bodies are broken, routes close with people still outside, seals require long service, and human custodians later abuse fragments of the binding history.
- **Identity boundary:** choir coordination never creates a collective person, merged soul, inheritable office, or alternate White Flame.
- **Promotion gate:** settle the created-spirit cost and select no more than one historical binding complex.

## Individual true-angels

### HC-N03 — The Pilgrim Beneath Seven Small Wings

- **Primary source:** HC-S01, Bahamut–Paladine–Fizban.
- **Category:** unique Greater Angel with seven distinct Attendant angels.
- **Commission:** counsel a mortal company, obstruct one appointed great refusal, shelter selected victims, and preserve mortal freedom by creating opportunities rather than commands.
- **Core form:** platinum-white raptor geometry around an old traveller vestment; seven attendants appear as canaries, finches, swallows, or culturally familiar songbirds.
- **Hard edge:** the angel knowingly permits dangerous freedom; advice is incomplete; people die after choosing badly; later institutions fictionalize the pilgrim into a guarantor of their wars.
- **Boundary:** not a dragon, god, Menhir, Patron, king of angels, or permanent company member.

### HC-N04 — Crow at the Unfinished Verdict

- **Primary source:** HC-S22, Andoletta.
- **Category:** unique Greater Witness/Mercy Angel.
- **Commission:** preserve testimony in one prosecution, protect an endangered child or falsely accused person, and distinguish pardon from denial of guilt.
- **Core form:** elderly vestment opening into black-green crow pinions; each feather carries an unaltered statement; white-lit emptiness displays the event without commentary.
- **Hard edge:** some accused are guilty; one protected witness may still be executed by human authority; exposure of false testimony may provoke riot or revenge.
- **Boundary:** cannot judge every case, compel forgiveness, replace courts, or certify an institution.

### HC-N05 — The Golden Last-Watch

- **Primary source:** HC-S24, Arqueros.
- **Category:** Warden Angel.
- **Commission:** hold one gate, convoy rear, refuge perimeter, or withdrawal line until named dependants cross.
- **Core form:** golden-eagle shield wings, purple dusk at the defended edge, hooked feet braced against invisible ground, eyes facing every approach.
- **Hard edge:** the rearguard suffers real casualties; the angel may refuse a commander who orders dependants abandoned; the defended people remain vulnerable after the commission ends.
- **Boundary:** no national border, castle, regiment, or order owns the angel's protection.

### HC-N06 — The Attendant in the Spear Field

- **Primary source:** HC-S25, Dalenydra.
- **Category:** Mercy Angel.
- **Commission:** maintain one battlefield sanctuary, enable triage, distinguish protected wounded from active combatants, and defend patients when the sanctuary is violated.
- **Core form:** pale stork- or heron-like shelter wings; many precise hands or beaks; cool white fire under translucent anatomy; voice carried in the breath after pain.
- **Hard edge:** patients die under triage; armies steal medicine; healers are captured; the angel may kill attackers without healing everyone present.
- **Boundary:** no hospital can summon it, and its appearance does not absolve the army whose wounded it treats.

### HC-N07 — The Owl at the Uncrossed Line

- **Primary source:** HC-S27, Tanagaar.
- **Category:** Warden/Witness Angel.
- **Commission:** watch one dangerous threshold at night, identify the actual approaching intrusion, and preserve the names of those admitted, refused, lost, or killed.
- **Core form:** vertical owl-wheel of dark pinions edged in moving white air; eyes face both sides of the boundary; wingbeats silence false passwords.
- **Hard edge:** negligent guards have already caused deaths; emergency closure strands travellers; rulers use the apparition to justify later xenophobic law.
- **Boundary:** a political border receives no heavenly legitimacy merely by borrowing the story.

### HC-N08 — The Taken Trumpet

- **Primary source:** HC-S21, Hand of the Inheritor.
- **Category:** unique Greater Host/Herald Angel.
- **Commission:** announce one supernatural campaign, rally defenders, enter hostile territory, and expose a captivity or false victory after its own defeat.
- **Core form:** armored osprey geometry; trumpet-shaped sternum; articulated gold-white pinions; one missing or broken grasp after imprisonment.
- **Hard edge:** the angel is defeated, imprisoned, mutilated, and fraudulently represented; rescuers die; human commanders suppress the evidence of failure.
- **Boundary:** cannot be compelled to fall, inherited, summoned, replaced by a mantle-bearer, or reduced to a weapon.

### HC-N09 — Captain of the Sacrificial Wind

- **Primary source:** HC-S11, Dol Arrah.
- **Category:** unique Greater Host Angel.
- **Commission:** guide one campaign in which victory, disciplined retreat, and negotiated peace are all possible instruments of the same bounded justice.
- **Core form:** red-gold hawk geometry around a white-fire core; wings resemble ordered wind-torn standards; its clearest command is heard before battle begins.
- **Hard edge:** clean battle still kills; allied rulers commit crimes beyond the angel's commission; refusal to pursue a fleeing enemy permits some guilty actors to escape.
- **Boundary:** not the sun, patron of all honour, or proof that one side's entire cause is righteous.

## Human Greater-Saint leads

### HC-N10 — The Voice Beside the Binding

- **Primary source:** HC-S09, Tira Miron.
- **Category:** post-Redeemer Greater-Saint candidate; redeemed `human-soul`.
- **Earthly grammar:** mortal hears and accepts an otherwise unrecognized warning, gathers inadequate allies, confronts a possessing or whispering power, and dies completing one necessary binding act.
- **Active grammar:** later speaks or manifests only where the same bounded captivity, threatened custodians, or counterfeit voice falls within commission.
- **Hard edge:** martyrdom, compromised institution, hostile presence near the cult centre, and custodians who mistake access for ownership.
- **Boundary:** never merges with fire, choir, relic, office, or living intermediary.

### HC-N11 — The Martyr of the Voluntary Wall

- **Primary source:** HC-S02, Torm.
- **Category:** Greater-Saint candidate.
- **Earthly grammar:** former soldier or civic defender leads thousands who knowingly occupy fatal positions against a city-destroying power; dies among them; leaves orphaned households and unresolved command guilt.
- **Active grammar:** later commission corrects a persecutory institution which invokes the sacrifice while refusing its penance and restitution.
- **Hard edge:** mass death remains bodily real; voluntariness is disputed at household level; the commander bears responsibility for proposing and using the plan.
- **Boundary:** no soul absorption, resurrection-as-reward, or automatic sanctity through military victory.

### HC-N12 — The Inheritor Who Inherited No Soul

- **Primary source:** HC-S20, Iomedae.
- **Category:** Greater-Saint candidate.
- **Earthly grammar:** mortal champion serves beneath a morally mixed predecessor, performs famous but disputed acts, survives institutional ascent, and receives no divine office while alive.
- **Active grammar:** after death and judgement, a later commission addresses a crisis left by the predecessor's disappearance; churches call this inheritance even though the persons and authorities remain separate.
- **Hard edge:** crusader violence, tests that humiliate or endanger followers, institutional expansion, and suppression of evidence that the Saint did not ratify every campaign.
- **Boundary:** no apotheosis, Starstone, transferred mantle, or human divinity.

### HC-N13 — The Saint of Five Remembered Wings

- **Primary source:** HC-S23, Ragathiel.
- **Category:** Greater-Saint candidate.
- **Earthly grammar:** mortal born into an infernal client dynasty or Sinner household is maimed by a parent, becomes a formidable defender, wins trust through service, commits personally chosen vengeance, and must confront the difference between defending the wronged and making wrath sovereign.
- **Active grammar:** if genuinely received after repentance and judgement, later manifests with five prayer-wings against the specific infernal inheritance once escaped.
- **Hard edge:** abuse, mutilation, killing, vengeance, mistrust, and victims who need not forgive the champion.
- **Boundary:** infernal ancestry never removes the human Image; manifested wings do not make the Saint an angel.

### HC-N14 — The Staff-Bearer after the Long Silence

- **Primary source:** HC-S15, Goldmoon.
- **Category:** Greater-Saint candidate.
- **Earthly grammar:** culturally marginal bearer accepts a dangerous relic whose narrow response exposes an official religious fraud or forgotten duty; travels with an ordinary company while learning what the commission does and does not mean.
- **Active grammar:** later intercession may protect truthful bearers or endangered healers, not reopen revelation or found another church.
- **Hard edge:** exile, attempted execution, pursuit, companions killed, false miracle claims, and later erasure of the bearer's ignorance and errors.
- **Boundary:** no second Prophet, missing-gods return, universal scripture, or relic-based succession.

### Existing-bed reinforcement — The Human Captain of the Host

HC-S16, Huma Dragonbane, reinforces [[Saints, Threshold Orders, and Heavenly Campaigns Review#ST-R04 — The Human Captain of the Host|the Human Captain of the Host]] rather than creating HC-N15. Any later development must preserve:

- mortal origin and redeemed `human-soul` continuity;
- a separately classified nonhuman ally;
- an instrument which does not carry a soul or inheritable heavenly office;
- bodily death and failed rescues;
- a bounded host commission which ends before human conquest does;
- later orders whose claims exceed the historical intervention.
