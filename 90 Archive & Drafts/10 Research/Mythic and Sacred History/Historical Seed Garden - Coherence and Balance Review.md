# Historical Seed Garden — Coherence and Balance Review

> [!warning] Review scope
> This is an author-only audit of the fifty-five roots already planted in [[The Historical Seed Garden]] before the first early-history harvest. It does not make a root canonical, remove a root, promote a complete person or institution, amend chronology or resolve a protected Weird.

## Governing Ruling

The Garden is no longer thin. Its principal risk is repetition: records, names, roads, gates, relic claims and legal clauses can appear to perform the same historical work even where their underlying events differ. The correct response in this pass is **functional separation**, not pruning. Every existing root remains planted and receives either `retain` or an incorporated `revise` disposition.

A shared object family is not itself a collision. Two roads remain distinct when one carries an Exodus rearguard and another converts measurement into imperial conquest. Two name records remain distinct when one restores murdered prisoners and another tests corpse-identity fraud. A collision exists when two roots share the same event, actors, institution, evidence carrier and historical consequence, or when one can replace the other without losing anything.

The review therefore tests eight things for every root:

1. the ordinary human aperture remains understandable;
2. the cause-and-effect chain names actual decisions and consequences;
3. at least one 1360 AR access point survives;
4. the nearest similar root performs a different historical function;
5. human souls, Giant continuities, offices, Masks, copies, residues and Patron roles remain distinct;
6. heavenly action, where alleged, remains bounded by commission;
7. the source's load-bearing cruelty, guilt or mercy remains intact;
8. the root remains useful if later supernatural interpretation is false.
