# First Heavenly Constellation - Earth-First Historical Placement Pass

## Purpose and Result

This pass gives the twelve leads selected by [[Orthodox Heavenly Canon Reconciliation and Combined Celestial Collision Review]] their first independent historical apertures. It begins with human decisions, institutions, victims and material consequences. Only then does it state the bounded heavenly commission.

The twelve are not a celestial council, brotherhood, campaign generation or opposing pantheon. They do not share an order, revelation, relic set, enemy, genealogy, title system or hidden plan. Later scholars may compare them because all twelve involve heavenly claims; comparison does not establish contact.

The pass places:

- one pre-imperial high-order withdrawal;
- three separate Underground or Conversion histories;
- five unrelated Long Frontier histories;
- one Fall-era angelic defeat;
- one localized High-Noon judgement;
- one ordinary personal-guardian case close enough to the present to remain legally active.

Nothing here enters canon, the authoritative chronology, the named-figure register or a church synaxarion. [[First Heavenly Constellation - Chronological Placement Review]] subsequently passes all twelve placements after incorporating seven revisions, and [[First Heavenly Constellation - First Trace Promotion Review]] harvests only one light trace from each into [[The Historical Seed Garden]]. Every handle and locality remains provisional. The history must remain useful even if a later church misidentified the angel, denied the Saint, fabricated a relic or attached the wrong supernatural name.

## Placement Rules

1. **Earth first:** each event begins with a human pressure and a culpable human decision. Heavenly action does not replace ordinary logistics, command, testimony, burial, repentance or restitution.
2. **One commission:** every angel or manifested Saint receives one principal active intervention in this pass. It ends when its named work ends.
3. **Saint before manifestation:** the three Saints possess complete `human-soul` continuity. Their earthly guilt, repentance, death and judgement precede any later commissioned manifestation.
4. **Angel without mortal becoming:** every angel remains one `created-spirit`. Its form is a manifestation signature, not biology, incarnation, offspring or office succession.
5. **Hard consequence:** sexual exploitation, abandonment, torture, execution, siege death, coercive conscription, mutilation, plague and neglect remain direct where load-bearing.
6. **No total proof:** an intervention validates only the work actually performed. It does not validate a ruler, nation, army, church, cult, relic collection or later interpretation.
7. **Discoverable independence:** every placement carries at least three evidence classes and one useful 1360 AR trace. No single vision or relic proves the event.

# I. Age of Stars

## OBA-N07 — Wheels at the Withdrawing Glory

### Historical aperture

During the later Age of Stars, one inner-gulf city maintained a fortified refuge gate, granary and house of names founded after an earlier sack. The gate’s public covenant promised bread, temporary shelter and a hearing before any foreign household could be expelled. The city’s ruling league gradually turned the refuge register into a tax class and then, during famine and war fear, into a list of suspected traitors.

Magistrates closed the inner gate, separated mixed households, executed several registered foreigners and seized their grain. Others were expelled beyond the walls while hostile forces were already gathering. Priests and clerks altered the covenant text so that the dead appeared to have violated guest-law first. The killings were not a misunderstanding produced by supernatural ambiguity; named human officials ordered them and city guards carried them out.

### Heavenly intervention

The Lord commissioned one high-order choir, later remembered as intersecting white-gold wheels nested inside migrating pinions, to withdraw a particular protection from the refuge covenant the city had inverted. The choir passed through the gate stations in a witnessed order, but surviving recensions disagree about their number. Lamps extinguished without smoke, the protected register became ordinary parchment, and the pressure that had previously prevented hostile breach ceased. The choir did not attack the city or assist the besiegers. The subsequent siege, executions and exile remained human acts, and the invaders were not thereby declared righteous.

The commission ended when the protection was withdrawn and the surviving truthful copy of the covenant was carried out. Later cities adapted the contradictory station accounts into an ordered **unhousing rubric** for declaring that a former sanctuary no longer carries its old public charge. Engineers who reproduce the architecture achieve only mechanical closures. Star priests claimed the wheels were salvaged Nythalum machinery; later churches claimed the city had been their own before the Redeemer. Neither claim follows from the event.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** famine and military fear → magistrates weaponize the refuge register → guards segregate, execute and expel registered households → the commissioned choir withdraws the covenant’s bounded protection → the city loses people first to purge and then to siege → later civic law distinguishes consecration from mere possession of a gate.
- **Commission:** preserve and, if the covenant is deliberately inverted, withdraw protection from one created civic good; carry out one truthful register. It cannot judge every citizen, open the city to an enemy, found a later church or become the city’s resident guardian.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Ezekiel’s departure of glory and siege imagery supply visible withdrawal, execution and exile. The atrocity occurs before the withdrawal and is not softened into administrative misuse.
- **Evidence carriers:** differently scorched gate sockets whose count varies by surviving plan; a grain-and-execution tablet copied by an enemy clerk; the expelled households’ covenant recension; a siege layer beginning after the purge; incompatible wheel diagrams in engineering and liturgical manuscripts.
- **1360 AR trace:** unrelated cities still use an ordered public unhousing rubric before converting a sanctuary gate to military or commercial use. Its steps differ by city, and most users do not know the disputed origin.
- **Competing interpretations:** abandoned star machine, high-order angelic withdrawal, sabotage by expelled clerks, divine approval of the besiegers, or several sieges compressed into one civic lament.
- **Authorially fixed:** the city murdered and expelled people protected by its own covenant; a real loyal choir withdrew one bounded protection; the enemy was not commissioned by that act.
- **Open:** city, households, number killed, choir’s received title, covenant’s original consecration, fate of the carried register and whether any current gate descends institutionally from it.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Great Unbinding, Nythalum Ascension, Erasure of Orthea, War of the Closed Doors or a permanent guardian-city. No protected Weird is explained.
- **Promotion gate:** the Garden may use the variable unhousing rubric or a carried covenant phrase, never the complete city, siege or choir identity.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — remove the fixed five-stage procedure so it cannot collide with the Rule of Five Closures; retain the ordered withdrawal, purge, siege and technical misreading.

# II. Underground and Conversion

## HC-N10 — Voice Beside the Binding

### Earthly life

The future Saint was the child of a kiln tender in an inland prison-and-dye district during the Underground Centuries. As a young prisoner clerk under torture, the mortal supplied one false name to stop a beating. The named worker was arrested and died in custody. After release, the clerk recanted publicly, found the victim’s household, surrendered wages for their support and began copying the names of people whom the prison courts removed from official rolls. The family accepted the support but never declared the death forgiven.

Beneath the old civic court, officials maintained a Star-age containment around a dependent Pride Mask. The containment had once used sworn testimony freely given. Persecuting magistrates altered it so that erased prisoners’ names, forced confessions and dying breath sustained the boundary. Custodians demanded that the clerk become its permanent living mouth because the copied name-roll could stabilize it. The clerk refused, exposed the murders and helped move prisoners out through the kiln service ways. Officials sealed the clerk inside the chamber during the final failure. The mortal died as the breach closed; death, usefulness and later veneration did not by themselves establish sainthood.

### Later active history

Around 404 AR, custodians of a new reliquary began issuing commands in the dead clerk’s voice and used them to identify alleged enemies of the converted state. A genuine Saintly manifestation, if the authorial placement survives review, received a single commission: preserve the old containment long enough to evacuate its keepers, state that the relic voice was counterfeit, and repeat the victim’s name the Saint had once betrayed. The Saint did not speak through the binding, inhabit the reliquary or become its keeper. The manifestation ended when the people were out and the false conduit was broken.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** persecution corrupts an inherited containment → officials feed it erased prisoners and demand a human mouth → the repentant clerk refuses, preserves names and helps an evacuation → the clerk dies and the Mask is contained → the victim remains dead and custodians later counterfeit the martyr → one bounded correction separates Saint, voice, relic and binding.
- **Continuity:** the Saint remains one redeemed `human-soul`; the dependent Mask, prison-binding `infrastructure-office`, vocal residue, reliquary response and counterfeit persona are separate.
- **Commission:** at the later crisis, protect named keepers, preserve one containment during withdrawal and identify one false voice. No permanent oracle, prison jurisdiction or general authority over Pride follows.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Tira Miron’s mortal containment and later institutional conduit grammar is joined to Underground martyr testimony. Torture, fatal informing, prison deaths and the Saint’s own culpability remain personal and irreversible.
- **Evidence carriers:** a kiln payroll matching erased prison names; a victim household’s receipt-and-refusal document; mortar containing the clerk’s writing tool but no authenticated body; the 404 reliquary hearing; two incompatible voice transcripts.
- **1360 AR trace:** an annual reading places the betrayed worker’s name before the martyr’s provisional title. One church preserves the order; another reverses it.
- **Competing interpretations:** murderer turned useful informant, penitent martyr, prison cult invention, bound ghost, Mask ventriloquism or genuine later Saintly correction.
- **Authorially fixed:** the clerk betrayed one person under torture, repented before death, made restitution without obtaining forgiveness, died preserving others and did not merge with any supernatural mechanism.
- **Open:** gender, city, baptismal history, public recognition, number evacuated, exact Mask, relic custody and present authenticity claims. Within this nursery lead, reception as a redeemed Saint after judgement is fixed; no mortal institution proves it.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Saint of the Two Ferries, Three-Lock Prison Road, Witness at the Red Passage or one of the Redeemer’s Companions.
- **Promotion gate:** no trace may prove the voice, relic or sainthood; the safest root is the rule that the betrayed victim’s name precedes the martyr’s title.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — retain the earthly guilt and later correction, but make the victim-before-title rule the trace so it cannot collapse into the Red Door name-reading or another prison-road custom.

## OBA-N02 — Finder of the Cast-Out

### Historical aperture

Soon after legalization, a wealthy household with new public Christian ties feared the extinction of its line. Its lord and lawful wife compelled an enslaved stable worker into the lord’s bed under an inheritance arrangement the woman could not refuse. When she bore a son, the household treated mother and child as proof of continuity. After the wife later bore a legitimate child, she beat the enslaved woman, persuaded the lord to void the earlier promises and drove both mother and son into a salt-and-thorn waste with inadequate water. The man consented and sent riders to prevent their return.

The child was near death when an angel appeared in the vestment of a pale ground bird with a clear human voice. The angel did not create a sacred spring, promise the child a nation or declare the mother morally flawless. It exposed an ordinary covered cistern abandoned by road builders, named the coercion and expulsion as the household’s crime, and directed the mother toward a caravan route. Human carriers supplied the remaining water and transport. Mother and child survived; many other enslaved women on the estate did not receive a visible rescue.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** dynastic fear → a couple coerces an enslaved woman into reproductive use and expels her after obtaining an heir → riders block return and water fails → the Finder reveals an existing cistern and route → mother and child survive while the household retains wealth → survivor-built inheritance and refuge rules preserve an accusation later piety tries to soften.
- **Commission:** find and preserve these two cast-out persons, tell the truth about the expulsion and direct them to ordinary human aid. It ends at the caravan road and grants no dynasty, culture or shrine permanent guardianship.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Genesis 16 and 21 supply sexual exploitation, household domination, expulsion and a dying child. The acts remain close to the principals and are not replaced with an abstract employment dispute.
- **Evidence carriers:** a household account recording the coerced inheritance arrangement; a later petition in the mother’s own legal formula; the covered road cistern with a matching repair layer; an early orphan-house rule barring expulsion after a change of heir; hostile family genealogies that omit and then accidentally restore the child.
- **1360 AR trace:** several hospitals retain the clause, “need does not end when inheritance changes.” Most attribute it to a charitable widow and omit the enslaved mother.
- **Competing interpretations:** angelic rescue, lucky cistern, later abolitionist legend, saintly woman rather than angel, household slander or a disguised foundation marriage.
- **Authorially fixed:** coercive sexual access, beating, expulsion and child endangerment occurred; the lord and wife were both culpable; one loyal angel provided bounded aid through ordinary water and a human caravan.
- **Open:** household, region, names, later freedom, child’s adulthood, baptism, final judgements, angel’s title and whether the cistern is the one now claimed by any institution.
- **Collision boundary:** not a founding mother of a people, a wilderness Menhir, a saintly midwife network or a general emancipation miracle.
- **Promotion gate:** the hospital clause or one survivor legal formula may later enter the Garden; the alleged cistern and complete household genealogy may not.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — the event is chronologically sound, materially recoverable and distinct from a national mother, miraculous spring or saintly midwife cycle.

## OBA-N06 — Coal-Bearer at the False Choir

### Historical aperture

During the Vestian Persecutions, a converted civic hymn guild gained the confiscated house and salaries of an older temple choir. One ambitious singer bought advancement by identifying five former custodians, including two genuine converts whom local officials wished to dispossess. The captives were tortured for further names. All five were executed, and their families were barred from collecting the bodies. The guild then inserted their confiscation into a dedication hymn as proof that the city had been cleansed without innocent blood.

At the new sanctuary’s dedication, the informing singer broke off before the altered verse, spoke the first victim’s name and begged not to be allowed another profitable lie. A Witness Angel then manifested above the choir rail as a black-and-white rook geometry carrying a coal without smoke. The angel touched the singer’s mouth and writing hand. The pain was bodily and public but did not kill him. It made the concealed testimony clear; it did not make the singer willing. He chose to speak all five names and the manner of their deaths, confessed his role before the governor, surrendered the purchased office and was imprisoned. The intervention did not absolve him, restore the dead or declare the old temple cult true.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** compulsory conversion creates offices and confiscated wealth → a singer denounces rivals and helps falsify a hymn → torture and execution are hidden inside worship → a commissioned Witness painfully restores named testimony → the singer confesses and loses liberty → later choirs preserve both a sanitized purification Life and a suppressed victims’ recension.
- **Commission:** cleanse one culpable witness into truthful speech and expose five concealed killings during one dedication. It does not cleanse the guild, forgive the singer, restore the temple or create a repeatable truth-coal.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Isaiah 6 supplies the coal and painful cleansing; Orthodox martyr and conversion records supply informing, torture, execution, confiscation and liturgical suppression. Cleansing follows guilt and does not make prior complicity symbolic.
- **Evidence carriers:** guild promotion and confiscation ledgers; a prison deposition bearing burn-scarring on the singer’s writing hand; five family grave petitions; an altered hymn whose meter requires the missing names; the dedication wall’s heat change.
- **1360 AR trace:** the hymn survives with an intentional silent measure. Some choirs read the five names there; others call the silence reverence for the purified singer.
- **Competing interpretations:** miraculous confession, coercive church spectacle, curse from the old temple, political reversal, guilt-induced seizure or later martyr-family insertion.
- **Authorially fixed:** the singer knowingly informed; officials tortured and executed five people; a real Witness Angel compelled no belief but made one liar able to speak truth at cost.
- **Open:** city, singer, precise temple cult, later repentance, final judgement, victims’ faiths, body recoveries and whether the singer was killed in prison.
- **Collision boundary:** not Susanna’s courtroom grammar, the Coal of any Phoenix, the False Lady, Children of the Closed Temple or a general judgement upon Conversion.
- **Promotion gate:** a silent hymn measure may be promoted later; no coal relic or institutional innocence may be inferred.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — preserve the singer’s freely chosen confession, bodily cleansing, five executed victims and the silence that prevents institutional self-absolution.

# III. Long Frontier

## HC-N03 — Pilgrim Beneath Seven Small Wings

### Historical aperture

In the first decades of the Long Frontier, a border governor refused to release hostages taken during the preceding war and sealed the only winter pass through a half-abandoned Giant toll fortress. A dependent Pride Mask inhabited the fort’s hostage standard and tally stone. It turned a captive’s safe-conduct oath into acknowledgement that the governor owned the captive’s movement, labor and descendants. The governor knowingly exploited the claim while insisting that continued detention preserved peace.

A mixed relief force of soldiers, relatives, porters and hired engineers met an apparently foolish old traveller accompanied by seven small birds. The traveller exposed the ownership clause hidden in the safe conducts, opened three tactical choices and named the captives who must not be treated as expendable. He did not choose the commander, promise victory or disclose the enemy’s whole disposition. The mortal commander selected a night assault after the traveller refused to endorse any option. The assault shattered the tally stone and freed many prisoners, but a premature fire killed captives in one tower and soldiers executed several surrendering guards in revenge.

### Heavenly intervention

The old traveller was the bounded vestment of one Greater Angel; the seven birds were seven distinct Attendant angels, not detachable parts of him. Their commission was to expose the false oath, protect specified vulnerable captives where possible and oppose the dependent Mask’s use of the standard. They did not command the assault. The Greater Angel prevented the Mask from transferring its claim into the escaping households only long enough for the human force to break the Material tally; the commission ended when the surviving named prisoners crossed the pass.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** a governor uses a Giant tally to treat safe conduct as inherited ownership → a relief coalition chooses armed entry after receiving bounded counsel → humans break the tally while angels prevent the dependent Mask transferring its claim → prisoners cross → captives, guards and rescuers die through mortal tactical choices → later military cults call every choice the Pilgrim’s command.
- **Commission:** give truthful but incomplete counsel, expose one false ownership oath, protect named captives and oppose one dependent Pride Mask at one pass. No government, strategy, resurrection or permanent patronage follows.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** the Bahamut–Fizban grammar retains disguised overwhelming power, indirect intervention and dangerous mortal freedom. The traveller’s advice does not remove siege killing, revenge execution or a commander’s culpable choice.
- **Evidence carriers:** three contradictory campaign diaries agreeing on the old traveller; a prisoner roll with seven separate bird marks; the tower burn layer and captive remains; broken tally pieces preserving the hidden ownership clause; later military homilies that erase the rejected options.
- **1360 AR trace:** frontier officers still call an unendorsed set of tactical choices “the pilgrim’s table.” The phrase is useful precisely because command responsibility remains with the chooser.
- **Competing interpretations:** Greater Angel, disguised Saint, spy network using trained birds, Patron strategist, collective battlefield apparition or pious excuse invented by the commander.
- **Authorially fixed:** one Greater Angel and seven Attendants acted; the commander remained free; the assault caused preventable deaths; the hostile power was a dependent Pride Mask, not an angel or Patron.
- **Open:** pass, governor, commander, casualty count, names of the eight angels, later history of the dependent Mask and authenticity of every later traveller sighting.
- **Relationships:** no personal link to another selected celestial is fixed. Later comparison with OBA-N08 is scholarly, not evidence of a shared campaign.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Rule of Five Closures, Three-Lock Prison Road, War of the Last Map as a whole or a heavenly Great Crusade.
- **Promotion gate:** the command phrase or prisoner bird-marks may later become a light trace; the angelic identities and campaign theology may not.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — replace the corrupted signal fort and fallen gate-captain with a Giant hostage tally and dependent Pride Mask, separating this campaign from the Taken Trumpet’s signal-house captivity.

## HC-N05 — Golden Last-Watch

### Historical aperture

A frontier town changed hands under a negotiated withdrawal whose written terms protected soldiers and taxable households but omitted debtors, servants, the wounded, mixed families and children claimed by both sides. The departing commander ordered the last carts stripped of those who could not keep pace. Cart crews obeyed at first. Several abandoned people were killed by scouts; others hid in drainage cuts while the column moved on.

When one cart crew turned back, a Warden Angel appeared at the rear as a tall hooked shadow made of gold-white pinions. It did not carry the carts or kill the pursuers. For the length of one march it confused hostile range-finding, held one collapsing ford and made the names on the neglected household roll audible whenever someone was about to be left. Human guards still fought and died. The column crossed only because porters shared loads, soldiers surrendered mounts and families refused the commander’s second abandonment order.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** a peace treaty excludes inconvenient dependants → commanders abandon them to preserve speed → ordinary workers turn back → a commissioned Warden guards the moving rear while humans carry and fight → most named dependants cross → abandoned dead and rearguard casualties make the later rescue morally incomplete.
- **Commission:** protect the people named on one dependant roll until they cross the agreed boundary. It cannot revise the treaty, punish every commander, guarantee survival or become guardian of all refugees.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Arqueros-style threshold defence and evacuation grammar retains lethal abandonment, named dependants and human rearguard death. Angelic aid does not replace carrying bodies or fighting pursuers.
- **Evidence carriers:** the treaty’s omitted classes; the amended dependant roll; cart axle repairs made with surrendered spearheads; burial pits along the drainage cuts; hostile scouting reports describing failed range estimates.
- **1360 AR trace:** some caravan courts require evacuation treaties to append a **dependants’ annex** naming people who cannot be excluded by military convenience.
- **Competing interpretations:** Warden Angel, golden Menhir bird, atmospheric mirage, soldiers’ collective oath, later refugee saint or legal fiction created to shame commanders.
- **Authorially fixed:** abandonment was deliberate; people died before the return; one loyal Warden received a bounded moving charge; humans performed the rescue and paid most of its cost.
- **Open:** town, belligerents, numbers, commander’s later trial, named households, angelic title and whether any current last-watch practice descends directly.
- **Relationships:** none fixed with another constellation figure.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Long Watch, Saint of the Two Ferries, Choir at the Eastern Road, Pilgrim’s hostage campaign or a permanent refugee-road protection.
- **Promotion gate:** the dependants’ annex may enter the Garden without an authenticated angel or founding company.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — the moving rear-guard, deliberate abandonment and human carrying work remain distinct from the Long Watch and other route guardians.

## OBA-N08 — Prince Delayed at Three Borders

### Historical aperture

One dispersed minority of artisans, drovers and translators lived under three Long-Frontier governments without possessing a state of its own. After a failed revolt elsewhere, rulers arrested members of the minority as exchange prisoners and moved them across three borders so that no single court could hear the whole case. Families petitioned for years. Some captives died under torture, exposure or prison disease before help arrived; their deaths cannot be reclassified as an invisible rescue.

The people possessed a real Greater Guardian commissioned toward them and the created goods carried through their households: language, burial names, mutual ransom and truthful guest testimony. The guardian’s intervention was delayed by three different supernatural obstructions. One was a dependent Envy Mask cultivated by officials who profited from divided kin. One was a Patron route-magistrate enforcing a valid passage claim against the wrong guarantor with lethal indifference. One was a fallen angel bearing a captured border office and refusing delivery of testimony. None was a rival national angel, and the Patron was not a devil merely because its law was severe.

The Greater Guardian contested each obstruction under a separate limit. Before the first court, it made the duplicated petitions display their different human makers so advocates could establish which copy preceded the Envy Mask’s alterations. At the second border, it exposed the actual guarantor named in the Patron docket, allowing human petitioners to tender the lawful dispute without accepting the prisoners as collateral. At the third, it broke the captured office’s seal over one messenger’s mouth so the testimony could be delivered. Human advocates still bribed clerks, carried copies, recovered bodies and forced one court to receive the complete captive roll. The surviving prisoners were released only after that hearing. The commission ended there; it did not establish an ethnic state, permanent immunity or innocence of everyone in the community.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** rulers distribute prisoners to prevent a hearing → three nonhuman powers exploit or enforce different barriers → petitions and testimony are delayed while captives die → the Greater Guardian contests each obstruction and humans deliver a complete roll → one hearing frees survivors → the dispersed people inherit guardian memory alongside evidence that aid came late.
- **Commission:** protect one people’s continuity in burial names, ransom and testimony through this transfer; defeat only the obstructions preventing the named hearing. No state, dynasty or church inherits the charge.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Daniel 10 supplies real delay and hostile princes. Prison death, torture and political guilt remain, and every opposing power is classified rather than flattened into a demon.
- **Evidence carriers:** petitions dated in three calendars; prison remains matching the transfer lists; a Patron docket naming the mistaken guarantor; a confiscated border-office seal; three liturgical recensions which disagree about why the Guardian was delayed.
- **1360 AR trace:** diaspora courts require copies of a captive roll to travel by three unrelated carriers and forbid one ruler from holding every authenticated version.
- **Competing interpretations:** national archangel, delayed saint, three ordinary bureaucratic failures, one Patron lawsuit exaggerated into war, proof that the prisoners were abandoned by Heaven or proof that their people can do no wrong.
- **Authorially fixed:** the people-facing commission was real; the people had no angelic species or corporate soul; captives died before aid; the three obstructions were not one alliance or one kind of being.
- **Open:** people, jurisdictions, prisoner count, Guardian’s title, hostile actors’ later fates, why this people received this particular commission and whether any later apparition is authentic.
- **Relationships:** no contact with the Pilgrim, Last-Watch or another selected angel is fixed.
- **Collision boundary:** not a Menhir cultural court, national election, Long Rent War in total, permanent guardian dynasty or the Great Unbinding’s remnant protection.
- **Promotion gate:** the three-carrier captive rule may be promoted without naming the people or authenticating an apparition.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — retain the three separately classified delays but state the mechanism by which each was overcome; supernatural opposition no longer substitutes for a causal chain.

## HC-N11 — Martyr of the Voluntary Wall

### Earthly life

The future Saint began as a ditch digger and conscripted stone hauler in a frontier water-city. The mortal learned fortification from maintaining drains, not from noble schooling, and rose through militia command after exposing an officer who sold repair stone. Earlier in command, however, the future Saint authorized beatings, hostage seizure and one lethal exemplary punishment against workers who deserted a floodwall project. Later Lives remove these acts or transfer them to a deputy; the orders were personally given.

During a combined siege and structural failure, the commander devised a plan requiring thousands to hold an outer retaining wall while families crossed the inner causeway. Some defenders freely remained. Others were debtors, prisoners, foreign laborers or conscripts threatened under emergency law. The commander knew the distinction and used the coerced stations anyway because the inner city would otherwise fall. Before taking a place on the wall, the commander confessed the earlier killing, revoked inheritance penalties against deserters’ children and ordered the volunteer and compulsory rolls kept separately. The wall failed. The commander and most of those stationed there died; the city survived. Repentance did not make the coercion voluntary or restore the dead.

### Later active history

In c. 811 AR, a successor confraternity used the old emergency law to seize minority labor for an unnecessary prestige wall. A genuine manifestation of the Saint received one bounded commission: stop the confraternity from invoking the Saint’s name, reveal the separate rolls and require the living institution to hear the coerced households. The Saint did not demolish the wall, govern the city or dictate compensation. The manifestation ended when the seizure order was publicly revoked; restitution remained human work and remained incomplete.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** siege and structural failure threaten a city → a commander uses both volunteers and coerced labor in a lethal holding plan → the outer wall buys evacuation time → thousands and the commander die → separate rolls preserve unequal consent → a later Saintly correction prevents one institution from calling coercion holy.
- **Continuity:** every defender remains a distinct `human-soul`; their deaths do not become fuel, merged strength or the Saint’s power. The Saint remains one redeemed human person.
- **Commission:** in the later intervention, expose the rolls, stop one misuse of the Saint’s name and protect seized workers until hearing. No permanent municipal or military jurisdiction follows.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Torm’s mass sacrifice and corrective penance grammar is retained without soul absorption. Personal command guilt, coerced and voluntary death, orphaned households and later institutional persecution remain.
- **Evidence carriers:** distinct volunteer and compulsory rolls; worker bones in different station groups; the revoked inheritance order; orphan-compensation accounts; the later confraternity’s seizure warrants and public cancellation.
- **1360 AR trace:** descendants still demand that public memorials name the compulsory stations separately. The city pays a reduced orphan levy whose original purpose is politically contested.
- **Competing interpretations:** flawless savior, necessary tyrant, repentant martyr, fabricated worker rebellion, Saint who legalized sacrifice or later apparition invented to defeat a building faction.
- **Authorially fixed:** the commander personally used coercion and personally died at the wall; repentance preceded death; the dead did not all consent; the later commission corrected rather than ratified the institution.
- **Open:** city, gender, exact number, public recognition, compensation, cause of the structural failure and authenticity of relic claims. Within this nursery lead, reception as a redeemed Saint after judgement is fixed; coerced deaths do not become voluntary through that ruling.
- **Relationships:** none fixed with the other two selected Greater Saints.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Founder Who Corrected the Order, Warden of the Last Redoubt, Four Who Brought Water or one of the Redeemer’s Companions.
- **Promotion gate:** only the two-roll memorial dispute or orphan levy may later enter the Garden; the complete Saint biography remains locked.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — the separate consent rolls, personal command guilt, repentance before death and bounded later correction already distinguish the figure from nearby founder-corrector and redoubt histories.

## HC-N13 — Saint of Five Remembered Wings

### Earthly life

The future Saint was born into a Long-Frontier client dynasty whose ruler held an infernal inheritance compact. Children of the house were treated as hostages and possible vessels for a hereditary office. The parent beat and mutilated the future Saint, cutting five winglike scars across the shoulders and side after the child refused a custody rite. Servants helped the young heir escape; several were executed for it.

Years later the exile returned with an armed household and overthrew the parent. The heir killed the parent personally, then murdered surrendered relatives and an unarmed ritual custodian in vengeance. The infernal compact influenced fear and opportunity but did not choose the murders. After the victory, the heir refused the inherited office, confessed the killings before victims’ households, surrendered the throne and spent the remaining years securing freedom, burial and property for the house’s former servants and child hostages. Some accepted restitution. Others refused contact and continued to name the penitent as murderer. The future Saint died defending an evacuation from former allies who wanted the dynasty restored.

### Later active history

During the early Fall, an infernal claimant attempted to collect surviving children through the old blood-and-office clause. The manifested Saint received a single commission to testify that neither blood nor mutilation transferred a human soul, guard the children while the actual compact was destroyed and confront the office’s current bearer. The Saint’s five avian signs marked remembered wounds rather than angelic anatomy or hereditary power. The Saint did not return to kill the family again, claim the throne or compel victims to forgive.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** a client ruler subjects children to an infernal inheritance system → one maimed heir escapes and later returns → the heir freely commits vengeance murders, then refuses the office and repents → restitution frees many dependants but cannot restore victims → death follows a final evacuation → a later bounded commission breaks one claim that blood owns children.
- **Continuity:** the Saint, murdered kin, servants and children are distinct `human-soul` persons; the infernal inheritance is an `infrastructure-office` and contract architecture, not a blood-soul.
- **Commission:** defend named children, testify to continuity and oppose one current office-bearer while mortals dismantle the clause. It grants no continuing dynasty, vengeance campaign or family patronage.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** Ragathiel’s abused infernal parentage and vengeful defender grammar is translated through human continuity. Abuse, mutilation, personally chosen kin-killing, repentance and victims who refuse reconciliation remain intact.
- **Evidence carriers:** client-house custody ledgers; the five scars recorded by opposing physicians; surrender witnesses and victim petitions; manumission and restitution charters; the later child-claim compact and its broken seals.
- **1360 AR trace:** several custody courts preserve the clause **“No wound inherits the child.”** It bars blood, mutilation, household office and a parent’s compact from transferring ownership of a child’s soul or person.
- **Competing interpretations:** infernal bastard made holy by violence, repentant murderer, political fabrication, angel mistaken for a Saint, dynasty founder, victim of slander or continuing secret ruler.
- **Authorially fixed:** abuse did not remove freedom; the heir murdered surrendered people; repentance preceded death; sainthood, if retained, follows judgement and never converts vengeance into holiness.
- **Open:** house, region, gender, exact relations killed, public recognition, form of restitution, later claimant, surviving children and authenticity of all five-wing relics. Within this nursery lead, reception as a redeemed Saint after judgement is fixed; victims remain free to refuse reconciliation.
- **Relationships:** no common campaign with the Wall Martyr or Voice Beside the Binding is fixed.
- **Collision boundary:** not Caedros, the Ashen-Wings Saint, a Giant bloodline, an Idol child or a redeemable devil.
- **Promotion gate:** the no-inherited-custody clause may enter the Garden; no paired feast, five-part relic collection or hereditary Saint-order may be created.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — replace the paired feast with a custody clause so the trace no longer duplicates the Ashen-Wings liberation feast and victims’ reading.

# IV. Fall, High Noon and the Near Present

## HC-N08 — Taken Trumpet

### Historical aperture

In the first years after Caleran’s fall, an occupation governor held prisoners and negotiated surrenders through a mountain signal house. A Greater Host/Herald Angel was commissioned to announce one protected surrender and name the prisoners covered by it. Human warlocks serving the governor prepared a ward around the signal apertures. The ward could not seize the angel’s whole created spirit, corrupt its will or make it fall. It could trap the active manifestation path and projected commission-body inside a repeating false signal.

The Herald entered because the protected surrender could not otherwise be heard. The ward crushed and stripped the visible pinions, split the beaklike trumpet geometry and repeatedly demanded a proclamation that the prisoners had surrendered their names and property. The Angel refused. A human rescue party breached the lower room but was discovered; most rescuers and several prisoners were killed. Survivors damaged the ward enough for the Herald to complete the true proclamation and withdraw. The occupation then executed additional witnesses. Rebel commanders suppressed the rescue’s failure because they feared that a defeated angel would destroy morale.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** an occupier converts surrender signals into confiscation → the Herald accepts a commission requiring entry into the warded signal house → the ward traps the manifestation path and attempts forced false speech → the Herald refuses while humans damage the apparatus → one protected surrender is heard → rescuers, prisoners and witnesses die, and both regimes falsify the defeat.
- **Commission:** announce one exact surrender, identify the protected prisoners and refuse contradictory proclamation. It does not win the Reconquest, free every prison, validate every rebel or remain as a permanent war herald.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** the Hand-of-the-Inheritor grammar retains celestial defeat, captivity, mutilation and dead rescuers. CR-01 prevents a chosen later fall: every “fallen trumpet” is propaganda, counterfeit or imposed image.
- **Evidence carriers:** the governor’s interrogation transcript; lower-room rescue graves; signal plates cut for a repeated false phrase; rebel minutes suppressing public mention; independent prisoner copies of the true surrender terms.
- **1360 AR trace:** military signal law requires the protected persons in a surrender to be named before property terms. A broken three-note call marks any message suspected of coerced authorship.
- **Competing interpretations:** captured angel, failed rebel weapon, mutilated Saint, primordial fallen spirit, Patron signal-body, invented morale story or proof that Heaven abandoned the capital.
- **Authorially fixed:** a loyal Greater Angel was locally defeated but never corrupted; human rescuers died; one surrender was truthfully proclaimed; suppression occurred on both sides.
- **Open:** signal house, governor, prisoners, rescuers, Herald’s title, enemy ward’s ultimate source, later counterfeit identity and whether any metal fragment is a relic.
- **Relationships:** no personal rivalry with an archfiend or another selected angel is fixed in this pass.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Battle of the Shattered Engines, Thirty Sisters, Veil over Veridun, Glass General or the cause of the Reconquest.
- **Promotion gate:** the coerced-message signal rule may later be promoted; the battle, angel and fragments remain unproven.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — after the Pilgrim revision, this is the constellation’s sole signal-house history; local defeat, mutilated manifestation and suppressed rescue remain coherent without angelic fall.

## OBA-N04 — Angel at the Threshing Floor

### Historical aperture

Between the Thalmyric golden age and Qarath, a tributary river principality ordered a census whose concealed purpose was to seize dependants for road labor, identify taxable refugees and select hostages from politically doubtful households. The ruler had been warned that the enumeration exceeded the law but proceeded because a complete register would strengthen a claim to royal independence. Clerks and soldiers broke houses, dragged people to counting stations and killed resisters.

A sudden localized mortality followed. It was not the Grey Sweat: its geography, season, symptoms and documentary vocabulary differ, and it ended more than half a century before the great plague. Death advanced along the census couriers’ roads: count-households and nearby barracks developed the same rapid fever and breath failure within hours of each sealed schedule arriving. A Greater Judgement Angel became visible above the road’s leading edge, appearing as a dark, immense raptor outline filled with white fire. The mortality killed clerks, soldiers, nobles, laborers and children; its justice cannot be reduced to a list of individually proven crimes. The ruler first blamed foreigners, then confessed the unlawful purpose, released surviving hostages, destroyed the seizure schedule and came barefoot to an ordinary threshing yard owned by a household already bereaved by the census.

The Angel stopped there when the Lord’s bounded commission ended. The threshing floor was not magical, and the ruler’s confession did not purchase the end. Later royal Lives present the ruler as having heroically saved the people; victim records insist that the admission came only after mass death. Both can agree on the visible stopping place without settling why that particular moment was appointed.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** a ruler seeks control through an unlawful census → officials seize people and suppress resistance → the Lord commissions a bounded judgement manifested through one Greater Angel → a localized mortality kills across classes → the ruler confesses and dismantles the seizure machinery before the commission visibly ends → the dead remain dead and later law restricts census purpose.
- **Commission:** carry out one judgement over one polity and stop at the appointed place. It confers no recurring plague authority, dynastic patronage or licence for rulers to imitate it.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** 2 Kingdoms/2 Samuel 24 and 1 Chronicles 21 supply royal culpability, mass death, angelic judgement and the threshing-floor limit. Death is not reduced to economic disruption, and confession does not erase consequence.
- **Evidence carriers:** census drafts with concealed seizure columns; household breach records; burial trenches and mortality lists; the ruler’s revocation charter; three hostile accounts agreeing on the ordinary threshing yard; environmental evidence separating the event from the Grey Sweat.
- **1360 AR trace:** several law codes still require a census to state its permitted purpose and forbid later use for hostage or labor seizure without a new public act. Jurists call the limit “the floor beyond the count.”
- **Competing interpretations:** direct judgement, ordinary epidemic later moralized, assassination poison, Idol plague, proof of the ruler’s sanctity, proof that all enumeration is evil or a displaced Grey Sweat story.
- **Authorially fixed:** the ruler knowingly abused the census; people were seized and killed; a real judgement commission occurred and visibly ended; the event does not explain another plague.
- **Open:** principality, ruler, number dead, disease mechanism, exact prophetic warnings, why the stopping point was appointed and whether the current threshing site is authentic.
- **Relationships:** none fixed with another selected celestial or Saint.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Quiet Ninth, Grey Sweat, Census Chronicles, Caedros III’s reign as a whole or a Phoenix commission.
- **Promotion gate:** the census-purpose rule may later enter the Garden; the ruler, plague identity and site remain withheld.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — specify the mortality’s witnessed advance along the courier network while leaving its natural mechanism open and its divine justice irreducible to a human guilt ledger.

## OBA-N11 — Guardian Who Could Not Prevent the Grave

### Historical aperture

In the assurance era, a baptized tallyman falsified warehouse measures for personal gain. Several poor households lost grain, and a guard died during a riot the shortage helped provoke. The tallyman later confessed part of the fraud, returned what money remained and supplied evidence against a larger broker. He was still convicted, indebted for restitution and sent to a workhouse infirmary. Repentance, if genuine, did not erase the dead guard, hungry households or lawful punishment.

The workhouse ignored repeated warnings that the prisoner was dying. After death, a clerk exchanged the corpse tag with that of another debtor bearing a similar name. A contract broker intended to treat the wrong identity as proof that one infernal assurance claim had matured, transferring liabilities to innocent dependants. The tallyman’s personal Guardian Angel had not prevented the crime, imprisonment, illness or death. Its final visible act was narrower: throughout one night a pale, small-winged figure repeatedly displaced the false tag, woke a washer and caused the baptismal name to be spoken before both bodies were sealed.

The bodies were eventually identified through ordinary records and testimony. The identity fraud was exposed, but the infernal office still disputes whether the corrected register defeats its claim. The Angel did not reveal the tallyman’s judgement, declare him innocent or advocate a false name to save him from consequence.

### Causal and evidentiary interface

- **Cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence:** a guilty debtor dies after institutional neglect → a clerk swaps identities to mature an assurance claim → the personal Guardian makes the mismatch impossible to ignore → human witnesses compare bodies and records → the true name is restored → judgement remains hidden while a 1360 custody case continues.
- **Commission:** personal guardianship of one baptized human person, culminating in protection of truthful identity against fraud. It does not guarantee health, acquittal, visible comfort, salvation or posthumous immunity from lawful claims.
- **Hard edge / source fidelity:** guardian-angel and grave-custody tradition is tested by real guilt, hunger, riot death, imprisonment, neglect and corpse fraud. The guardian’s reality is not measured by whether suffering or death occurred.
- **Evidence carriers:** baptism and warehouse registers; victim restitution accounts; workhouse medical warnings; two corpse tags and washer testimony; the broker’s assurance claim; a bounded contract test which changes when the authenticated name is used.
- **1360 AR trace / access point:** the **Double-Tag Docket** preserves the corrected identity and is the central precedent in a current assurance hearing. The hearing can establish fraud and bounded contract behavior without proving the winged witness was an angel.
- **Competing interpretations:** personal Guardian, hospital saint, guilt dream, trained bird, clerk’s cover story, infernal interference against a rival office or compassionate fabrication by the washer.
- **Authorially fixed:** the tallyman harmed people and later attempted partial restitution; the workhouse culpably neglected him; a real personal Guardian protected his identity without preventing death or deciding judgement.
- **Open:** city, names, sincerity of repentance, final judgement, degree of broker knowledge, fate of the other debtor, angel’s identity and outcome of the 1360 hearing under the Forward Rule.
- **Relationships:** none fixed with another selected celestial.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Boatman, Ossuary Crown, Quiet Ninth, Keeper of the Contested Body, a repeatable soul-road escort or proof that baptism prevents infernal litigation.
- **Promotion gate:** only the already documented Double-Tag Docket may enter the Garden; the 1360 hearing, tallyman’s judgement and winged witness remain under the Forward Rule.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — promote the existing docket only as a starting hook; do not predetermine the current contract ruling or authenticate its angelic interpretation.
