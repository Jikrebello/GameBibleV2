# Orthodox Biblical Angels and Heavenly Powers Atlas

## Purpose and Status

This dossier gives the World Egg's heavenly ecology the biblical and Eastern Orthodox pass that the comparative fantasy atlas cannot supply. It distinguishes what the Orthodox biblical canon actually narrates, what later Orthodox theological synthesis teaches, what liturgy and iconography express, and what remains pious or regional tradition.

Nothing here creates a canonical angel, changes the heavenly bestiary, alters the Great Unbinding, or promotes a Garden seed. The biblical figures remain biblical figures. Provisional World Egg handles are separate nursery leads, not disguised copies or declarations that the same angel exists in both histories.

The selected biblical handles now have provisional earth-first apertures in [[First Heavenly Constellation - Earth-First Historical Placement Pass]]. The source studies remain distinct from those World Egg histories, and both layers remain exploratory pending review.

The governing result is:

> Biblical angels are not a bright counterpart to demons, a reusable species ladder, or a source of morally infallible super-heroes. They are created personal powers whose terrifying action is intelligible only through the Lord's command. Their messages, mercies, delays, refusals, battles, rescues and mass judgements prove the particular commission performed—not the permanent righteousness of the human ruler, army, city or institution that witnessed it.

This pass produces:

- thirty biblical and Orthodox-reception source studies;
- fourteen independent heavenly nursery leads;
- no new Phoenix candidate;
- one reinforcement of the Great Unbinding Phoenix's judgement-and-mercy grammar;
- a nine-rank vocabulary crosswalk which does not replace the existing service profiles;
- four major canon questions, now closed by the combined reconciliation review.

# I. Authority and Source Layers

## Source ladder

| Tier | Material | Authority in this dossier | World Egg use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Eastern Orthodox Scripture** | Greek Septuagintal Old Testament and the received New Testament | governing biblical witness | supplies the acts, limits, moral wounds and direct theological boundaries |
| **B — Liturgical reception** | feasts, hymns, sacramental prayers and established worship | authoritative ecclesial interpretation, though not a transcript of invisible anatomy | supplies titles, recurring relations and the Church's experienced grammar |
| **C — Patristic synthesis** | especially Saint John of Damascus and the Dionysian *Celestial Hierarchy* | major Orthodox theological interpretation | supplies created nature, freedom, bodilessness and the familiar nine-rank synthesis |
| **D — Synaxarial and iconographic tradition** | received Lives, miracle collections, icons and regional commemorations | real received tradition requiring historical-layer labels | supplies later identifications, local patronage and visual language |
| **E — Pious opinion and folklore** | ungraded angel names, private revelations, local stories and modern systematization | never sufficient alone | may shape a culture's belief but cannot establish authorial heavenly fact |

The [Orthodox Church in America overview of angels and evil spirits](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/doctrine-scripture/the-symbol-of-faith/angels-and-evil-spirits) is the principal modern Orthodox synthesis used here. It explicitly calls the bodiless powers created, distinguishes them from the uncreated Lord, presents the nine ranks, and warns that their scriptural bodily descriptions are symbolic rather than material anatomy. The [OCA Synaxis of the Archangel Michael and the Other Bodiless Powers](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/1989/11/08) supplies the received liturgical hierarchy, traditional archangel names, guardian traditions and later attribution of several biblical interventions to Michael. The [2025 OCA reflection on the Synaxis](https://www.oca.org/reflections/metropolitan-tikhon/reflection-synaxis-archangel-michael-other-bodiless-powers) confirms the continuing devotional use of seven or eight archangel names and their differentiated roles.

Saint John of Damascus describes an angel as an intelligent, free, incorporeal minister made immortal by grace rather than by nature; the relevant primary text is [*Exact Exposition of the Orthodox Faith*, book II](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/33042.htm). The Dionysian synthesis explicitly treats the nine names as interpretive designations ordered in three triads; see [*The Celestial Hierarchy*, chapter VI](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/dionysius/celestial.vii.html). These sources are important precisely because they prevent a monster-manual reading of the ranks.

## Canon and numbering cautions

- Tobit, the longer Daniel, 2 Maccabees and 3 Maccabees belong to this pass because the controlling Old Testament lens is the Orthodox Septuagintal tradition.
- `2 Esdras` is not a stable cross-tradition title. Any archangel name or episode taken from an Esdras text must record the exact Greek, Slavonic or English numbering used.
- First Enoch is not part of the ordinary Eastern Orthodox biblical canon. It remains important comparative and reception material, while its canonical home in the Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo Churches belongs to an Oriental Orthodox, not Byzantine Eastern Orthodox, canon. The distinction is recorded by the [Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church's own canon page](https://ethiopianorthodox.org/english/canonical/books.html) and the existing [[Orthodox Old Testament Lens]].
- Genesis 6 has received more than one Christian interpretation. This dossier does not retroactively declare the setting's Titan–Giant descent model to be the only Eastern Orthodox reading of the sons of God and daughters of men.
- Revelation belongs to the Orthodox New Testament but is not used in the ordinary Byzantine lectionary. It supplies images and moral grammar, not an encrypted chronology.

# II. Controlling Orthodox Filter

## Christ above every angel

Hebrews 1 is the controlling hierarchy before every angelic taxonomy: the Son is not the greatest angel. The angels worship Him; they are created ministers, while His kingship is unique. The OCA's [Hebrews 1 reading](https://www.oca.org/readings/daily/1994/12/23/2) preserves the fire-and-wind language while making the same categorical distinction.

**World Egg ruling:** no Phoenix, Greater Angel, first-created spirit, Menhir, Idol or saint is the Redeemer in another guise. No power grade approaches uncreated divinity.

## The theophany firewall

In several Old Testament passages the `Angel of the Lord` speaks with divine authority, bears the divine Name, receives a response appropriate to God, or is read Christologically in Orthodox worship. The fiery-furnace figure, for example, is identified in Orthodox iconographic and hymnographic reception as the **Angel of Great Counsel**, the Son of God; see the [OCA Life of the Prophet Daniel](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/2045/12/17/103559-prophet-daniel).

Therefore every apparent angel must first be classified as one of:

1. a created bodiless power;
2. a theophany or Christological type;
3. a prophetic symbol;
4. a later traditional identification;
5. an uncertain case which must remain uncertain.

**World Egg ruling:** a theophany can supply no created-angel biography. The Lord may act through a sign without that sign becoming a separate person, species or office.

## Nine ranks, not nine bestiary species

The received Orthodox ordering is usually presented as:

| Hierarchy | Ranks |
|---|---|
| Highest | Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones |
| Middle | Dominions, Powers, Authorities/Virtues |
| Lowest | Principalities, Archangels, Angels |

Translations vary especially around `Powers`, `Virtues`, `Authorities` and related Pauline terms. Scripture names the terms in different contexts; the complete three-by-three architecture is the later Dionysian synthesis received by Orthodox tradition.

**World Egg ruling:** retain [[True Angels Overview|Attendant, Herald, Warden, Mercy, Witness, Host, Greater Angel and Phoenix]] as encounter-facing service profiles. Orthodox rank names become theological, liturgical and cultural descriptions of relation and ministry. They do not create nine biological populations, nine realms, nine promotion levels or nine stat blocks. One created person may be described by a rank and a current service profile without either label consuming identity.

## Form is revelation, not anatomy lesson

Scripture gives six-winged seraphim, many-faced cherubim, wheels full of eyes, armies, flames, winds and men whose otherness becomes visible only gradually. Orthodox teaching treats these visible descriptions symbolically because angels are bodiless.

**World Egg ruling:** the current stable strange core form is a setting-specific manifestation rule, not a claim that Orthodoxy teaches hidden biological avian anatomy. `Commission-body`, `stable sign` and `cultural vestment` should be understood as how a created spirit is truthfully made perceptible within Material conditions.

## Veneration and petition are not summoning

The Council of Laodicea's received Orthodox memory condemns worship of angels as gods while affirming proper veneration. Orthodox prayer does directly address Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, guardian angels and the bodiless powers in petition and praise.

**World Egg ruling:** the current ban on command, binding and mechanical deployment remains correct. The narrower sentence in [[True Angels Overview]]—which permits address mainly while a servant is visibly present—does not fully express Orthodox practice. A future canon edit should permit petitionary address and requested intercession while maintaining that only the Lord commissions the response.

## Guardianship without national innocence

Matthew 18:10, Acts 12:15, Daniel 10 and received Orthodox baptismal and consecration traditions support guardian relations to people, nations and churches. The Synaxis tradition explicitly speaks of personal, national and church guardians.

**World Egg ruling:** guardian is a bounded commission, not ownership. A nation with a guardian can commit atrocity. A guardian may protect victims from the ruler it is popularly said to patronize. Borders, dynasties and denominations cannot inherit command of a guardian. Personal guardianship cannot erase freedom, consequence, illness or death.

## Saints, humans and the hosts

Hebrews 12 distinguishes innumerable angels from the spirits of the righteous made perfect while placing both in one heavenly assembly. Orthodox worship calls the Theotokos more honorable than the cherubim and beyond compare more glorious than the seraphim; sanctified humanity is not simply a lower celestial caste.

**World Egg ruling:** [[Manifested Saints Overview|saints]] remain `human-soul` and angels remain `created-spirit`. The existing rule that a saint may direct angels under a particular commission is compatible with the setting's theology, but it must not be presented as a universal fixed ladder in which every saint outranks every angel.

## The Phoenix boundary

Neither Scripture nor the standard Orthodox nine-rank synthesis contains a Phoenix order. Seraphic fire, cherubic guardianship, Michaelic warfare, the stopped destroying angel and apocalyptic judgement each contribute part of the World Egg grammar, but none establishes a biblical Phoenix species.

**World Egg ruling:** [[Phoenixes]] remain a distinctive World Egg synthesis: singular true angels commissioned where regional judgement and restoration must occur together. This pass proposes no second Phoenix. It only strengthens the existing Great Unbinding rule that judgement must distinguish captives from the structure being destroyed and must stop at its appointed boundary.

# III. Old Testament Source Atlas

## OBA-S01 — Cherubim at the East of Eden

- **Received source:** Genesis 3:22–24; cherubim and the flaming sword guard the way to the tree of life after expulsion.
- **Borrowed element:** a holy threshold closed for the sake of judgement and to prevent fallen humanity from making its condition endless.
- **Original hard edge:** exile, mortality, lost access and a guarded road which human need cannot reopen by force.
- **World Egg use:** a Warden choir can close one route because passage would preserve corruption rather than heal it. The gate is not a soul-hungry maze and the angels do not own what they guard.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N01.

## OBA-S02 — The Angel Who Finds Hagar

- **Received source:** Genesis 16 and 21; the Angel of the Lord finds an abused and expelled woman, names her suffering, gives a hard instruction in the earlier encounter, and later reveals water when the child is expected to die.
- **Borrowed element:** heavenly attention at the social edge; a message addressed to the cast-out person rather than the household which harmed her.
- **Original hard edge:** slavery, sexual exploitation within a fertility arrangement, domestic abuse, expulsion, thirst and the expected death of a child. The promise does not make the household innocent.
- **World Egg use:** a Mercy or Herald angel may locate an abandoned household, reveal one material means of survival and preserve a future which respectable chroniclers would erase.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N02.

## OBA-S03 — Mamre, Sodom and Dangerous Hospitality

- **Received source:** Genesis 18–19; three visitors receive Abraham's hospitality, announce a birth, hear intercession for a city, and two angels enter Sodom before its destruction.
- **Borrowed element:** disguised visitors, hospitality as revelation, investigation before judgement and rescue from a condemned polity.
- **Original hard edge:** threatened gang rape, Lot's offer of his daughters, coercive flight, the death of Lot's wife, destruction of settlements and the later incestuous exploitation of Lot. None may be softened into a charming guest-right tale.
- **Orthodox reception boundary:** the Hospitality of Abraham is a Trinitarian iconographic type. It does not require three created angels with private biographies.
- **World Egg use:** use separate created visitors only in a wholly different event, or use the grammar without fixing the visitors' ontology. No host's courtesy purchases immunity for the city.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a grammar; no nursery trio.

## OBA-S04 — The Stayed Knife

- **Received source:** Genesis 22; the Angel of the Lord stops Abraham's hand and the child survives.
- **Borrowed element:** intervention at the irreversible instant; command, obedience, terror and substitution.
- **Original hard edge:** a father binds his son and raises the knife. Later faith does not erase the child's terror or allow rulers to repeat the act as theatre.
- **Theophany boundary:** the speaker's identity belongs within the Angel-of-the-Lord problem and cannot be casually assigned to a created angel.
- **World Egg use:** loyal angels never authorize human sacrifice as a loyalty test. A created Warden may stop a counterfeit command and preserve evidence against the cult that issued it.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as recognition grammar; no individual lead.

## OBA-S05 — Ladder, Camp and Wrestler

- **Received source:** Genesis 28 and 32; angels ascend and descend at Bethel, Jacob meets God's camp, and the nocturnal wrestler leaves him wounded and renamed.
- **Borrowed element:** a road briefly revealed as crowded with heavenly movement; encounter that blesses and permanently wounds.
- **Original hard edge:** exile, fear of fratricidal revenge, deception, night struggle and lasting bodily impairment.
- **World Egg use:** a revealed heavenly road does not become a permanent portal or prove ownership of the land. The wrestler remains theophanically ambiguous and supplies no ordinary angel stat block.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as soul-road and encounter grammar; no new person.

## OBA-S06 — Burning Bush and the Name-Bearing Messenger

- **Received source:** Exodus 3 and 23:20–23; the Angel of the Lord appears in the bush while God speaks, and a messenger bearing the divine Name is promised before the people.
- **Borrowed element:** fire which does not consume; commissioned guidance; dangerous obedience to a word that is not the messenger's private policy.
- **Original hard edge:** forced labor, murdered infants, lethal imperial pursuit, threatened judgement and the terror of holy ground.
- **Theophany boundary:** Orthodox Christological reading prevents these passages from becoming proof of one created super-archangel.
- **World Egg use:** reinforce the difference between clean heavenly fire and Wrath or Shell fire. Do not turn the Prophet's guidance into service under an angelic co-redeemer.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a theological boundary.

## OBA-S07 — Exodus Angel, Destroyer and Divided Sea

- **Received source:** Exodus 12–14, 23 and 33; plague, the destroyer, pillar, angelic protection and the sea crossing appear in a complex account of deliverance and judgement.
- **Borrowed element:** a protecting presence moving behind the vulnerable column; judgement constrained to a particular historical liberation.
- **Original hard edge:** dead firstborn, devastated households, drowned pursuers, slavery, panic and survivors who still face hunger and rebellion.
- **World Egg use:** supports the Great Unbinding's distinction between protected withdrawal and general conquest. It does not supply plague names, explain the Glass or make the Phoenix a repeatable angel of national war.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a reinforcement of [[The Great Unbinding]]; no new lead.

## OBA-S08 — The Sword in Balaam's Road

- **Received source:** Numbers 22; a donkey sees the armed Angel of the Lord before the seer does, suffers three beatings, speaks, and exposes the rider's blindness.
- **Borrowed element:** an animal perceiving a blocked spiritual road; a commissioned enemy standing against someone who thinks himself authorized.
- **Original hard edge:** animal abuse, threat of death, purchased divination and a prophet willing to travel toward a politically useful curse.
- **World Egg use:** a Warden angel may make one road impassable, use a humble creature as witness and force a powerful traveler to admit what he intended. The animal neither becomes a Menhir oracle nor permanently acquires speech.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N03.

## OBA-S09 — Captain before Jericho

- **Received source:** Joshua 5:13–15 and the conquest sequence which follows; the commander of the Lord's army refuses Joshua's assumed binary and declares holy ground.
- **Borrowed element:** the heavenly commander who is not simply `on our side` and whose presence sharpens responsibility rather than baptizing a campaign.
- **Original hard edge:** Jericho's destruction, killing of inhabitants, Rahab's endangered household and the moral terror of a conquest text.
- **Theophany boundary:** the commander's identity is not reduced to Michael merely because later tradition makes that identification.
- **World Egg use:** a Host captain may support one commanded rescue or judgement while refusing the mortal general's wider war. No Atherian, Thalmyric, Eclessian, Jirahar or Ahdari conquest receives scriptural authorization by analogy.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as Host grammar; no separate lead until a historical war requires one.

## OBA-S10 — Gideon, Manoah and the Departing Flame

- **Received source:** Judges 6 and 13; strange visitors commission unlikely households, consume offerings in fire, disappear and leave witnesses afraid that seeing them means death.
- **Borrowed element:** the messenger who must be recognized through action; humble or compromised people receiving a narrow task rather than general sanctity.
- **Original hard edge:** occupation, raiding, childlessness, fear, later vow catastrophe and the violence of the Judges cycle.
- **World Egg use:** a sign validates an exact commission. It does not validate every later choice of the recipient or establish inheritable heroic blood.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as recognition grammar.

## OBA-S11 — Threshing Floor and Assyrian Camp

- **Received source:** 2 Kingdoms/2 Samuel 24; 4 Kingdoms/2 Kings 19:35; 1 Chronicles 21; one angel strikes during the plague after David's census and is stopped at the threshing floor, while another account records 185,000 Assyrian dead in one night.
- **Borrowed element:** terrifying commissioned judgement, visible stopping point and the ruler's culpability before the dead.
- **Original hard edge:** mass death, plague bodies, bereaved households, a king whose decision carries consequences and an enemy army annihilated without heroic combat.
- **World Egg use:** one Greater Angel may bear a judgement that visibly ceases when commanded. No angel develops an appetite for killing, and no later king inherits the right to direct it.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N04 and a Phoenix-limit reinforcement.

## OBA-S12 — Bread beneath the Broom Tree

- **Received source:** 3 Kingdoms/1 Kings 19; an angel touches the exhausted Elijah, provides food and water, and sends him onward after his desire for death.
- **Borrowed element:** bodily care before revelation; sleep, food and repeated attention rather than a spectacular cure.
- **Original hard edge:** political murder, flight, exhaustion, despair and the continuing danger which nourishment does not remove.
- **World Egg use:** an Attendant or Mercy angel may perform the small material act that makes later obedience possible. The recipient's despair is not treated as spiritual failure or solved by a speech.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N05.

## OBA-S13 — Council, Accuser and Lying Spirit

- **Received source:** Job 1–2; 3 Kingdoms/1 Kings 22; Zechariah 1–3; created heavenly attendants stand before the Lord, an accuser contests a human, a spirit volunteers to deceive Ahab's prophets, and an angel intercedes for Jerusalem.
- **Borrowed element:** heavenly deliberation and testimony without peer gods; hostile accusation; permitted trial; intercession over a devastated city.
- **Original hard edge:** Job's children and servants die, his body is afflicted, Ahab is lured toward a fatal battle, and political prophecy becomes an instrument of death.
- **World Egg use:** Heaven may contain counsel, report and assigned action, but the Lord neither learns facts nor bargains with equal sovereigns. The lying-spirit passage cannot normalize holy deception or turn every false prophet into an angelic puppet.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into court-and-witness grammar; no polity of angels.

## OBA-S14 — Seraph and Coal

- **Received source:** Isaiah 6; six-winged seraphim acclaim the Lord, the temple shakes, and one touches Isaiah's lips with a burning coal before his commission.
- **Borrowed element:** worship, exposure of uncleanness, painful purification and message-bearing.
- **Original hard edge:** terror, burning contact and a prophetic mission whose announced consequence includes hardened hearing, ruined cities and desolation.
- **World Egg use:** a Witness angel may expose and cauterize one corruption before testimony. The coal does not become a reusable confession relic or proof that every seraph is a fire-elemental.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N06.

## OBA-S15 — Cherubim and the Departing Glory

- **Received source:** Ezekiel 1, 9–11 and 43; living creatures, wheels full of eyes, ordered motion, execution through the city, the glory's departure from the defiled sanctuary and later return.
- **Borrowed element:** an immense coordinated throne-bearing manifestation; judgement beginning at a claimed sacred center; holy withdrawal as an event with civic consequence.
- **Original hard edge:** siege, killing in the city, sanctuary corruption, exile and a prophet forced to witness the loss of the place his people considered secure.
- **World Egg use:** a choir may carry or mark one departing commission. It does not transport the Lord, form a celestial vehicle species or prove that a church deserted by one sign has lost every grace.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N07.

## OBA-S16 — The Angel of Great Counsel in the Furnace

- **Received source:** the Septuagintal Daniel 3 and Orthodox Nativity reception; the three youths refuse the image, officials are killed by the furnace's heat, and a fourth figure preserves the condemned.
- **Borrowed element:** protection within rather than removal from lethal punishment; hymn and witness under state idolatry.
- **Original hard edge:** forced worship, execution by burning, officials killed by the apparatus they operate and a ruler who repeatedly threatens dismemberment and destruction of households.
- **Theophany boundary:** Orthodox reception identifies the saving figure Christologically. It is not available as a created angel lead.
- **World Egg use:** reserve this grammar for the Redeemer or a clearly commissioned created servant in a sufficiently transformed history which does not reproduce the furnace scene.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a firewall; no nursery figure.

## OBA-S17 — Gabriel, Michael and the Princes of Kingdoms

- **Received source:** Daniel 8–12; Gabriel interprets, Michael is `one of the chief princes` and the prince associated with Daniel's people, and a messenger is delayed by conflict with the princes of Persia and Greece.
- **Borrowed element:** a messenger whose arrival is delayed by real supernatural resistance; great guardians assigned to peoples; conflicts crossing imperial succession.
- **Original hard edge:** conquest, persecution, desecration, execution, delayed help and faithful people dying before the promised resolution.
- **World Egg use:** supports Greater Angels and bounded national or civilizational guardians. A guardian protects persons and a divine purpose, not every border or policy. Hostile `princes` require individual classification: fallen spirit, Idol Mask, Patron, infernal office or another created actor.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N08.

## OBA-S18 — Raphael on Tobit's Road

- **Received source:** Tobit, especially 3–12; Raphael travels under the name Azarias, guides Tobias, gives practical instructions, binds Asmodeus, heals Tobit and identifies himself as one of seven who present the prayers of the saints.
- **Borrowed element:** disguised angelic road companion; domestic rather than imperial scale; prayer, burial piety, medicine, marriage and demon conflict intertwined.
- **Original hard edge:** corpse denial, threatened execution for burial, blindness, suicidal prayers, seven murdered bridegrooms, fear of another wedding-night death and the danger of travel.
- **World Egg use:** a Mercy-Herald may accompany one household road without revealing identity, while humans still cook, negotiate, marry, bury, prepare medicine and accept risk. The angel does not become a Patron companion available for hire.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N09.

## OBA-S19 — Horsemen at the Violated Treasury

- **Received source:** 2 Maccabees 3, 5, 10 and 11; terrifying mounted and armed apparitions defend, punish or accompany the persecuted community, including the beating of Heliodorus at the treasury.
- **Borrowed element:** heavenly force defending entrusted property from state seizure; hostile witnesses surviving long enough to testify.
- **Original hard edge:** bodily beating, near death, massacre, enslavement, temple violation and retaliatory war.
- **World Egg use:** a Host intervention may stop one sacrilegious seizure without making every custodian innocent or every item authentic. `Heaven defended it once` is not perpetual immunity from audit.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N10.

## OBA-S20 — The Elephants Turned Back

- **Received source:** 3 Maccabees; mass registration, detention and intended execution by drugged elephants are reversed after prayer and angelic intervention.
- **Borrowed element:** bureaucratic persecution reaching the point of industrial killing; the weaponized machinery of death turning against the persecutor's plan.
- **Original hard edge:** families penned for execution, forced registration, humiliation, terror, drunken war animals and lethal reversal.
- **World Egg use:** an angelic intervention may break one mass-killing apparatus while mortals preserve names and organize survivors. It never proves that later rulers of the rescued people are immune from judgement.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as a choir grammar; no separate lead until a historical persecution is chosen.

# IV. New Testament Source Atlas

## OBA-S21 — Gabriel and the Annunciation

- **Received source:** Luke 1; Gabriel stands before God, announces John and the Incarnation, answers questions, strikes Zechariah mute for unbelief and departs after Mary's free assent.
- **Borrowed element:** a messenger of history-changing news who neither becomes the author of the event nor remains as its manager.
- **Original hard edge:** dangerous pregnancy, public shame, imperial vulnerability, Zechariah's months of muteness and the sword later prophesied for Mary.
- **World Egg use:** a Herald can announce a unique divine act, impose one bounded sign and withdraw. No second Annunciation, Theotokos or Incarnation analogue is planted.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as Herald grammar; no new figure in the protected Redeemer generation.

## OBA-S22 — Nativity Host, Wilderness Ministry and Gethsemane

- **Received source:** Luke 2:8–15; Matthew 4:11; Luke 22:43; angels proclaim peace, minister after temptation and strengthen Christ in agony without preventing arrest or crucifixion.
- **Borrowed element:** proclamation to low-status witnesses; aid which strengthens a person for suffering rather than removing the necessary path.
- **Original hard edge:** imperial census pressure, poverty, massacre in the Nativity cycle, wilderness hunger, temptation, bloody anguish, betrayal, torture and execution.
- **World Egg use:** Attendants may feed, steady breath, clarify a choice or bear witness while leaving the commissioned person free to endure the consequences. Rescue is not the only form of mercy.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as Attendant grammar; no duplicate lead beyond OBA-N05.

## OBA-S23 — Tomb and Ascension Witnesses

- **Received source:** the Gospel resurrection accounts and Acts 1; angelic or shining witnesses identify what God has done, direct frightened disciples and rebuke passive sky-gazing.
- **Borrowed element:** the heavenly witness who explains a transformed scene but does not cause the Resurrection.
- **Original hard edge:** execution, corpse handling, grief, terrified guards, disbelief, social danger to women witnesses and a mission that leads many witnesses to persecution and death.
- **World Egg use:** a Witness angel can authenticate one absence, preserve testimony and send survivors back to ordinary duty. No angel raises the Redeemer or owns resurrection.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as Witness grammar; no new central Redeemer figure.

## OBA-S24 — Guardians, Escorts and Harvesters

- **Received source:** Matthew 13:39–43, 18:10 and 24:31; Luke 15:10 and 16:22; angels rejoice over repentance, behold the Father, carry Lazarus and serve in final gathering and judgement.
- **Borrowed element:** personal guardianship, heavenly joy over one repentant person, escort after death and eschatological gathering.
- **Original hard edge:** neglected poverty at the rich man's gate, death, irreversible judgement and separation. Guardian care does not prevent every earthly abuse.
- **World Egg use:** personal guardians and soul-road escorts may be distinct commissions. Neither replaces [[The Boatman]] nor guarantees rescue from all Material harm.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N11.

## OBA-S25 — Angels in Acts

- **Received source:** Acts 5, 8, 10, 12 and 27; angels open prisons, direct Philip, instruct Cornelius, wake Peter, strike Herod and promise survival in shipwreck.
- **Borrowed element:** varied missions inside one historical movement: escape, road direction, household preparation, judgement and preservation through disaster.
- **Original hard edge:** imprisonment, beating, state execution, guards facing lethal punishment, Herod's gruesome death, shipwreck and prisoners threatened with execution.
- **World Egg use:** do not make one omnipresent campaign angel responsible for every event. The prison opener and the angel who strikes the acclaimed king may be different persons and functions.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N12 and OBA-N13.

## OBA-S26 — Thrones, Dominions, Principalities and Powers

- **Received source:** Romans 8:38; 1 Corinthians 15:24; Ephesians 1:21 and 6:12; Colossians 1:16 and 2:15; created visible and invisible powers are under Christ, while hostile spiritual powers oppose the faithful and are disarmed in His victory.
- **Borrowed element:** real supra-human jurisdictions and conflicts without independent divine sovereignty.
- **Original hard edge:** persecution, deceptive authority, spiritual warfare, institutional captivity and the cross as the form of victory.
- **World Egg use:** these titles can name angelic ranks, hostile offices or jurisdictions only after continuity is classified. Similar title does not imply same species, allegiance or person.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as the theological basis for the rank/service crosswalk.

## OBA-S27 — Hebrews and the Innumerable Assembly

- **Received source:** Hebrews 1–2, 12:22–24 and 13:2; Christ exceeds the angels, angels are ministering spirits, hospitality may receive them unknowingly, and angels and perfected human spirits gather without merging.
- **Borrowed element:** hidden guests, ministry to heirs of salvation and one worshipping assembly containing categorically distinct persons.
- **Original hard edge:** martyr suffering, shaking judgement, Abel's blood, danger of refusal and vulnerable strangers needing hospitality.
- **World Egg use:** directly supports `created-spirit` versus `human-soul`, disguised visits and the refusal of angel worship. Heaven is communion without collective-soul fusion.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as controlling metaphysics.

## OBA-S28 — Michael's Restraint and the Bound Rebels

- **Received source:** Jude 6 and 9; 2 Peter 2:4; rebellious angels are held for judgement, while Michael does not pronounce an abusive judgement in the dispute over Moses' body but appeals to the Lord's rebuke.
- **Borrowed element:** high power under verbal and juridical restraint; confinement of rebellious spirits; contested custody of a righteous person's body.
- **Original hard edge:** rebellion, binding, judgement, grave or corpse dispute and the danger of arrogant human teachers imitating powers they do not understand.
- **World Egg use:** a Greater Angel may refuse theatrical hatred even toward a real enemy. Heavenly restraint is not softness. The episode does not create a routine method for resurrecting, stealing or soulbinding saints.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as OBA-N14.

## OBA-S29 — Revelation's Hosts, Trumpets and Bowls

- **Received source:** Revelation 4–16 and 19–22; living creatures worship, angels guard winds, seal servants, sound trumpets, pour bowls, govern exact plagues, fight the dragon and refuse worship.
- **Borrowed element:** large-scale differentiated heavenly operations; angels assigned to winds, waters, fire, abyssal restraint, proclamation and war.
- **Original hard edge:** famine, poisoned waters, burning, darkness, sores, earthquake, massacre, martyr blood and final judgement. These cannot be reduced to luminous pageantry.
- **World Egg use:** an angel `over` wind, water or fire bears a commission concerning it and does not become an elemental, Menhir or owner of a Shell state. The visions do not provide a calendar or a standing apocalyptic army for human rulers.
- **Disposition:** `revise` into choir and campaign grammar; no direct timeline or universal war.

## OBA-S30 — Orthodox Synaxis, Guardians and Iconic Form

- **Received source:** the November 8 Synaxis; nine ranks; Michael, Gabriel, Raphael and additional traditional archangel names; guardian angels of persons, peoples and churches; winged human iconography and liturgical co-service.
- **Borrowed element:** a populated Heaven remembered through feast, prayer, art, place patronage and distinct ministries rather than only battlefield appearances.
- **Original hard edge:** the feast remembers rebellion and spiritual warfare; Michaelic traditions include mass death, invasion, civil war and contested national appropriation.
- **World Egg use:** cultures may possess incomplete but sincere angelologies, feast days and patronal claims. A single true angel can accumulate several cultural titles without becoming a belief-made Mask. Competing traditions require behavioral and commission evidence, not merely matching wings.
- **Disposition:** `retain` as the cultural-reception interface.

This table is deliberately non-exclusive. It translates useful narrative function, not essence.

| Orthodox rank language | Principal received emphasis | Strongest World Egg service interface | Prohibited simplification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seraphim | fiery love, ceaseless worship, purification | Witness, Mercy, rare Greater Angel | fire elemental, Wrath angel or automatic Phoenix |
| Cherubim | knowledge, many-eyed guardianship, throne imagery | Warden, Witness, high choir | gate-beast species or heavenly transport animal |

# VI. First Biblical Heavenly Nursery

These are source-shaped research leads. They are not canonical angels, biblical figures with renamed faces, fixed historical events or Garden traces.

## OBA-N01 — The Choir at the Eastern Road

- **Source root:** OBA-S01.
- **Function:** a small Warden choir closes one route whose use would make a specific corruption permanent.
- **Hard edge:** refugees die on lesser roads after the closure; an innocent household denied passage may never understand why.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the ward refuses kings, saints' relics and angelic-looking counterfeits equally. It opens only after the perpetuating mechanism is destroyed, not when a password is learned.
- **Collision boundary:** not [[The Boatman]], a Threshold Warden, the Glass, or HC-N05's Golden Last-Watch.

## OBA-N02 — The Finder of the Cast-Out

- **Source root:** OBA-S02.
- **Function:** one Mercy-Herald repeatedly appears only at abandonment sites created by the same institution.
- **Hard edge:** the first found household includes a sexually exploited bondwoman and a child already near death; rescue cannot restore the dead from earlier expulsions.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the angel reveals water, names the institutional crime and leaves the survivors to decide whether to return, flee or testify.
- **Collision boundary:** not a wilderness Menhir, fertility Patron, saintly midwife or permanent guardian of one ethnicity.

## OBA-N03 — The Sword in the Beaten Animal's Road

- **Source root:** OBA-S08.
- **Function:** a Warden blocks a hired curse-seer travelling to legitimize a massacre.
- **Hard edge:** the seer beats and permanently injures the pack animal before recognizing the armed obstruction; earlier contracted curses may already have caused deaths.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the animal receives one moment of truthful speech or perfectly intelligible witness. The seer must continue under narrowed speech, return in humiliation or knowingly rebel.
- **Collision boundary:** not a Menhir beast, talking-animal people, prophecy engine or pardon for the seer's prior work.

## OBA-N04 — The Angel at the Threshing Floor

- **Source root:** OBA-S11.
- **Function:** a Greater Angel bears one plague judgement produced through a ruler's culpable census, then visibly stays its commission at an ordinary work site.
- **Hard edge:** people die in numbers; abandoned bodies, triage and political terror remain visible; the ruler's confession does not restore them.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the chosen stopping place becomes contested because workers, crown and churches each tell a different reason the blade halted.
- **Collision boundary:** reinforces but is not a Phoenix, B4 plague explanation, Quiet Ninth solution or angel of death species.

## OBA-N05 — Bread beneath the Last Tree

- **Source root:** OBA-S12 and OBA-S22.
- **Function:** an Attendant provides sleep, water, food and one repeated touch to an exhausted fugitive whose work is unfinished.
- **Hard edge:** the fugitive has asked to die after colleagues were murdered; nourishment does not remove the persecutor or guarantee later courage.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** later pilgrims search for miraculous bread while ignoring the mortal household that baked it and was punished for the theft.
- **Collision boundary:** not HC-N14's Staff-Bearer, a healing saint, a food Patron or a proof that despair is cured by obedience.

## OBA-N06 — The Coal-Bearer at the False Choir

- **Source root:** OBA-S14.
- **Function:** a Witness angel exposes a sacred choir whose hymns conceal torture and purchased denunciation.
- **Hard edge:** the chosen human witness accepts a painful cleansing but still carries guilt for earlier silence; several accused singers were already executed.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the coal burns only lies the witness personally helped speak. It cannot cleanse the institution collectively.
- **Collision boundary:** not White-Flame kindling, sacramental absolution, seraph species proof or a weapon against unbelievers.

## OBA-N07 — The Wheels at the Withdrawing Glory

- **Source root:** OBA-S15.
- **Function:** a coordinated Cherubic/Witness choir makes the departure of one protection publicly legible before a city falls.
- **Hard edge:** executions, siege and exile follow; the choir neither evacuates everyone nor makes the invader righteous.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** later diagrams of the wheels are mistaken for machines, though the reproducible fact is only the order in which compromised gates cease responding.
- **Collision boundary:** not Nythalum engineering, Shell machinery, a Patron vehicle or a permanent heavenly city.

## OBA-N08 — The Prince Delayed at Three Borders

- **Source root:** OBA-S17.
- **Function:** one Greater Angel assigned to protect a people is delayed by three separately classified hostile sovereignties while mortal persecution continues.
- **Hard edge:** prisoners die before aid arrives; collaborators and faithful households exist in every involved culture.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the angel's eventual victory opens one route and exposes the supernatural affiliations of three courts, but validates none of the claimant regimes.
- **Collision boundary:** not a racial patron, national soul, eternal angelic empire or proof that every `prince` is an angel.

## OBA-N09 — The Physician under a Borrowed Road-Name

- **Source root:** OBA-S18.
- **Function:** a Mercy-Herald accompanies one indebted household through burial, marriage, medicine and a predatory spirit's attack.
- **Hard edge:** denied corpses, threatened execution, suicidal despair and previous wedding-night deaths remain central.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** every remedy uses an ordinary substance prepared correctly under instruction; the miracle lies in knowledge, timing, protection and commission rather than angel blood or touch.
- **Collision boundary:** distinct from HC-N03's seven-winged Pilgrim by household scale, medical mechanism, no attendants, no war and no great enemy cycle.

## OBA-N10 — The Riders at the Seized Treasury

- **Source root:** OBA-S19.
- **Function:** two or three Host manifestations stop one state confiscation of entrusted famine and burial funds.
- **Hard edge:** the intruding official is beaten nearly to death; soldiers have killed custodians; the treasury itself contains compromised deposits.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the surviving official becomes the most credible witness against his own court. Later custodians falsely claim the intervention sanctified every account.
- **Collision boundary:** not HC-N06's Spear-Field Attendant, a treasury Patron, Greed Mask or standing temple guard.

## OBA-N11 — The Guardian Who Could Not Prevent the Grave

- **Source root:** OBA-S24 and Orthodox guardian tradition.
- **Function:** a personal guardian commission protects freedom, prayer and truthful judgement but does not prevent every assault, disease, bad decision or death.
- **Hard edge:** the guarded person dies neglected after a life in which the guardian's warnings were often ignored and other humans refused ordinary aid.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the guardian's one visible posthumous act is to preserve the person's true name from an infernal custody fraud, not to reverse judgement.
- **Collision boundary:** not [[The Boatman]], a soul fragment, conscience, familiar, inherited household angel or saint.

## OBA-N12 — The Herald at the Unbarred Prison

- **Source root:** Acts 5 and 12.
- **Function:** a Warden-Herald opens one political prison, wakes captives and orders public testimony rather than flight into anonymity.
- **Hard edge:** prisoners have been beaten; earlier detainees died; guards face execution and some respond with further violence.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** doors open but chains do not fall from anyone detained for an unrelated crime. Mortals must distinguish prisoners rather than treating escape as automatic innocence.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Three-Lock Prison Road, a universal abolition miracle, Sinner extraction or repeatable key relic.

## OBA-N13 — The Angel Who Ended the Acclamation

- **Source root:** Acts 12:20–23.
- **Function:** a Witness-Judgement angel strikes a ruler at the instant public praise becomes accepted divine honor.
- **Hard edge:** the ruler dies painfully; his earlier execution of prisoners and manipulation of food politics remain part of the judgement; succession violence follows.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the angel exposes the exact transition from political praise into worship architecture, interrupting a dependent Pride Mask's access without destroying Pride's Throne.
- **Collision boundary:** not an anti-monarchy angel, assassination Patron, Pride Archmask or reusable test for legitimate sovereignty.

## OBA-N14 — The Keeper of the Contested Body

- **Source root:** OBA-S28.
- **Function:** a Greater Angel resists a hostile claim over one righteous person's corpse, relic-route or public memory while refusing self-authorized condemnation.
- **Hard edge:** the body has been hidden, desecrated or politically weaponized; rival custodians commit violence in the name of protection.
- **Distinctive World Egg torque:** the angel preserves custody long enough for judgement or burial but discloses neither the soul's location nor a complete biography.
- **Collision boundary:** not a relic-owning saint, saintly soul, funeral Patron, Ossuary Crown claimant or mechanism for commanding the dead.

# VIII. Canon Reconciliation Result

## OC-01 — Loyal angels are confirmed in good

- **Orthodox pressure:** the received patristic account normally treats the primordial angelic choice as decisive. Saint John of Damascus says the fall is to angels what death is to humans and denies repentance after that fall; broader Orthodox synthesis commonly regards the holy angels as confirmed in good.
- **Settled ruling:** the loyal host freely chose the Lord during the primordial rebellion and is confirmed in good. Active loyal angels cannot later fall. New hostile radiant figures must be primordial rebels, counterfeits, Idols, Menhir, Patrons, Masks or Sinners.

## OC-02 — Fallen angels cannot repent

- **Orthodox pressure:** the dominant Damascene formulation denies repentance after the angelic fall.
- **Settled ruling:** a fallen angel cannot repent or return to Heaven. False repentance, strategic defection, a broken devil office, a rescued human Sinner or liberation of a wrongly bound loyal angel can provide adjacent stories without changing the primordial rebel's judgement.

## OC-03 — Mortals may petition angels

- **Orthodox pressure:** established Orthodox worship directly petitions archangels, bodiless powers and guardian angels while rejecting their worship as gods.
- **Settled ruling:** petition and requested intercession are permitted whether or not the angel is visibly present. Summoning, binding, purchasing, inheriting and compelling remain impossible.

## OC-04 — Guardian commissions exist

- **Orthodox pressure:** Scripture and received tradition support all three.
- **Settled ruling:** personal, people-facing, consecrated-place and mission-facing guardianships exist under [[Guardian Angels and Consecrated Commissions]]. They do not create ownership, national innocence or guaranteed rescue.

## Settled non-collisions

- `created-spirit` versus `human-soul` is strongly reinforced by Hebrews 12.
- Commission before raw power is reinforced by messenger, guardian and judgement narratives.
- Angels rejecting worship is directly reinforced by Revelation 19:10 and 22:8–9.
- `Angel of Great Counsel` must remain a Christological title, not a Greater-Angel rank.
- Avian, fiery and many-eyed manifestation is compatible as World Egg visual grammar when not misrepresented as Orthodox material biology.
- Menhir and Idols remain transformed Yolk Exiles, a unique World Egg history rather than ordinary ranks in Orthodox angelology.
- No biblical passage creates a Phoenix species, a summonable saint, a heavenly alignment faction or an eternal equal war between Heaven and Hell.

# IX. Promotion Gates

Before any biblical heavenly lead is placed:

1. classify the received passage as created-angel narrative, theophany, vision, liturgical interpretation or unresolved case;
2. preserve the exact hard edge if it is load-bearing: death, exile, rape threat, slavery, animal abuse, plague, massacre, torture, desecration or judgement;
3. state who is commissioned, what is authorized, who is protected, what force is permitted and what ends the work;
4. give every human actor independent agency, guilt and practical work;
5. state what the sign proves and the wider claims it does not prove;
6. separate angel, saint, body, relic, office, road mechanism, Mask, Patron and residue;
7. prevent national guardianship from becoming racial election or permanent approval of a state;
8. prevent liturgical rank language from becoming species or power-level determinism;
9. keep the Redeemer, Incarnation, Resurrection, Theotokos and Angel of Great Counsel unique;
10. do not plant a newly fallen loyal angel or a repentant primordial rebel;
11. require at least two independent evidence classes for an unknown historical manifestation;
12. apply the Forward Rule to every 1360 AR appearance, relic response or institutional claim.
