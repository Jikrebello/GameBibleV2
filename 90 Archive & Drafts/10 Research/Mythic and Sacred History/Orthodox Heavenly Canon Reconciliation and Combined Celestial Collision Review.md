## Purpose and Result

This review closes the controlling Orthodox angelology questions, then collision-tests all fourteen leads from [[Comparative Celestials and Heavenly Translation Atlas]] and all fourteen leads from [[Orthodox Biblical Angels and Heavenly Powers Atlas]] as one nursery.

The pass produces:

# I. Settled Canon Reconciliation

## CR-01 — Loyal angels are freely confirmed in good

**Ruling:** the angelic host made its decisive creaturely choice during the primordial rebellion. Loyal angels retain intelligence, affection, freedom and individual agency, but cannot subsequently fall. Confirmation perfects their allegiance rather than replacing personhood with a species instinct.

**Consequences:**

- no spell, wound, captivity, infernal environment, blood contamination or bad command turns a loyal angel evil;
- a later `fallen angel` story must concern a primordial rebel only now revealed, not a new celestial fall;
- an angelic commission can be defeated, frustrated or refused by mortals without becoming malicious;
- an apparition claiming recent corruption is counterfeit, substituted, misremembered or lying about its original allegiance.

**Canon reconciled:** [[Heavenly Orders Overview]], [[Heavenly Commission and Manifestation]], [[True Angels Overview]], [[Attendant and Herald Angels]], [[Host and Greater Angels]] and [[Phoenixes]].

## CR-02 — Fallen angels cannot repent

**Ruling:** a fallen angel's primordial refusal is fixed. It may change strategy, defect from an infernal alliance, lose a mantle, surrender tactically, tell truth for interested reasons or help destroy a rival. None of these is repentance or restoration to Heaven.

**Consequences:**

- no routine or exceptional reconciliation dossier remains open;
- a penitent infernal figure must be a human Sinner where the established human judgement rules permit the story, not a fallen angel;
- breaking a devil office can liberate victims and alter jurisdiction without saving the spirit wearing it;
- apparent former-radiant tenderness can be memory, manipulation or natural created good still twisted toward refusal, not proof of return.

**Canon reconciled:** [[Fallen Angels and Broken Commissions]].

## CR-03 — Petition is permitted; summoning remains impossible

**Ruling:** mortals may directly petition known angels and the heavenly hosts for intercession whether or not a servant is visibly present. Prayer, icon, feast, hymn and title can be truthful relations of petition. They do not issue or renew commissions.

No mortal can summon, bind, buy, inherit, command, breed, harvest or contract for a loyal angel. A repeatable response may belong to an object, office, road mechanism, Patron agreement or counterfeit rather than the petitioned person.

**Canon reconciled:** [[True Angels Overview]] and [[Heavenly Commission and Manifestation]].

## CR-04 — Guardian commissions are real and plural

**Ruling:** personal, people-facing, consecrated-place and mission-facing guardianships exist under the rules in [[Guardian Angels and Consecrated Commissions]]. A baptized person receives a personal guardian. Other people may receive personal or situational angelic help without lesser humanity or grace being inferred from sacramental status.

Guardianship protects a created person or bounded good. It never proves state innocence, ethnic election, dynastic title, church perfection, guaranteed health, salvation or military victory.

## CR-05 — Angelic form is a manifested signature, not biology

**Ruling:** eyes, pinions, wheels, beaks, hands, flame, wind and human vestments are truthful Material expressions of a bodiless created person. Stable signs allow recognition, but `core anatomy` must not imply angelic flesh, mating, offspring or a zoological heavenly species.

Avian language remains the World Egg's strong visual grammar. Orthodox rank names remain theological and liturgical language; encounter-facing Attendant, Herald, Warden, Mercy, Witness, Host, Greater Angel and Phoenix remain service profiles.

# VI. Promotion and Counterfeit Boundaries

- A selected lead is not canon merely because its metaphysical category is canon.
- No title, feast, icon, relic, prayer or repeated sign proves identity by itself.
- A Saint cannot fall, be summoned or merge with a relic, office, fire or angel.
- A loyal angel cannot fall, repent from a fall that never occurred, or be corrupted into an enemy.
- A primordial fallen angel cannot repent or counterfeit loyal service indefinitely without contradiction becoming visible.
- A guardian protects a charge without possessing it.
- A national or people-facing guardian supplies no ethnic or political innocence.
- A defeated commission-body proves neither falsehood nor heavenly abandonment.
- A successful intervention validates only the exact work performed.
- No first-wave figure receives a private heavenly realm, inheritable order, automatic military cult or universal jurisdiction.
