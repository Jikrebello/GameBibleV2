# The First Imperial House Sources and Development Review

The companion [[The First Imperial House - Proposed Character Biographies|proposed biographies]] develops Thalion, Meren, Varelan, Cassara and Olarion as a connected family history. Its main proposal is that improvements in government and southern occupation create the provincial resources later used in the Painted Reign war. The first dynasty makes substantial territorial gains. The Veloran and later houses inherit the task of extending them.

First review **9 October 2026**, revised **13 November 2026** and **14 November 2026**. The creator's succession revision is incorporated in the dynasty articles. Under [[Causality Pass - 03 Age of Glory and the Bridge to 0 AR|Causality Pass 03]] the biographies were cut to one page: private characterisation and most new names were removed, Cassara's death date was left open, the southern general lost his name, and the 274 strike gained its cause (stone for the Star's share). Campaign placements remain proposals.

## Scope and governing decisions

The selected route is recorded in [[350 BR to 0 AR - Three Imperial Expansion Routes]] and its [[350 BR to 0 AR - Imperial Expansion Canon Constraint Sheet|constraint sheet]]. Broad southern provinces grow over generations, supported throughout by a naval network. Under [[Causality Pass - Direction]] decision 5, Qahur is three crowns inside the Atherian sphere at 33 BR and 0 AR, under no direct rule; Sarqen is the one annexed royal country. Major Atherian–Vasharan wars belong after 0 AR and before the Ahl-e-Ahd's rise from 541 AR.

This delivery covers the first house and its fall, ending in 246 BR. It develops character and causal history sufficiently to guide the regional sequence. The broad new campaign intervals below are proposed placements, with exact participating settlements and ancient borders still to be established. They should not be copied into a map or canon chronology as completed conquests.

## Family and supporting people

Thalion's family relationships follow [[Households of the Imperial Founding]]. Meren is both Thalion's adopted son and his biological nephew. His parents are Evara and Velsar. Talven and Lethia share his father and were raised within Evara's household after Sarqen's conquest. Cassara is Meren's daughter. She and Varelan are Olarion's parents.

The proposed c. 310 birth makes Cassara about twenty-four when Varelan accedes. Meren is about forty-eight at her birth. Olarion's proposed c. 283 birth makes Cassara about twenty-seven at his birth and makes him about twenty at accession and thirty-six at death. Cassara's death date remains open.

Brenna is the only new name kept, because she changes a result. Alessa Corven (a third Corven), Tavren Arel (collides with King Tavren of Sarqen), Maerdis, Avel Darsen (echoes Darsil) and Mirsa Tel (joins the Mira–Meren cluster) were dropped on 14 November 2026. Thalion's daughter Lysara is named after his mother Lysara Casvilar, a family custom now stated in [[The Later Reign of Aurelius Thalion]]. Death dates for Evara, Ilmara and Melvo are not supplied. Melvo's personal work is kept within Thalion's generation; later rulers inherit arrangements and trained institutions. Meren's canon racing interest is retained without introducing northern horse hordes or changing the later Tarkhan First Catch.

Casvilar's earlier conquest chiefly affected the Rauven-facing northern Folk approaches. The draft begins from that position. Kellovar and Darneth are already affected communities; neither is discovered or conquered for the first time by Meren. Morveth, Drenoc and Velcara are not carried forward as active figures across implausible lifespans.

The new gains contain occupied farms, towns and assessed districts, sufficient to begin the broad provincial transformation requested by the creator. Brael, Varena, Suroth, Tidewood and the Gale-Crown ranges receive no invented first-house annexation. Their individual relationships require subsequent development. The first-house frontier does not determine the final 0 AR line.

Qahur retains three crowns; Vashar remains a sovereign peer. Qiryath's transfer and Raveth's incorporation are now fixed by Causality Pass 03 (Raveth c. 160 BR; Qiryath under the late Halreths); Ortheia's eventual settlement remains open. Fleet agreements provide transport and access without making all the Elmaric islands imperial provinces. No present-day Eclessian, Sestaran or Covenant polity is projected into this period.

## Historical inspiration

Two focused primary-source comparisons supplement the canon. They supply questions about government and imperial representation. Fictional dates, names, personalities and events above are original proposals.

Augustus's *Res Gestae*, especially sections 3, 16–17 and 34, connects military settlement, expenditure and claims about restored civic government. Its account is a ruler's presentation of his achievements. It helps frame Thalion's concern with paying soldiers and making his supremacy appear durable and legitimate. The fictional land settlement and private motives are our additions. [Augustus, Res Gestae, translated by F. W. Shipley](https://www.livius.org/sources/content/augustus-res-gestae/).

Tacitus's *Agricola*, sections 19–22, links provincial exactions, campaigns, forts and cultivation of local elites. It is a laudatory biography with a Roman author's judgements about conquest. It supplies a useful comparison for Meren's dependence on local cooperation and Cassara's public works. The Ossen withdrawal, Brenna's bargaining and Dovren agreements arise from World Egg's particular institutions and geography. [Tacitus, Agricola, translated by A. J. Church and W. J. Brodribb](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/ancient/tacitus-agricola.asp).

Roman succession helps frame the revised household. Tacitus describes Augustus bringing Tiberius into his family and advancing him through military and public powers, as well as Livia's influence in the household and the Senate's treatment of her honours. The Atherian adoption of a son-in-law is a local choice. [Tacitus, Annals 1.3–8 and 1.14](https://classics.mit.edu/Tacitus/annals.1.i.html)

## Review and next handoff

The development review checks reigns, kinship, plausible ages, regional starting positions and the agreed later-war boundary. The added supporting names were searched against the canon and research shelf before drafting. Character coverage includes relationships, pleasures, faith, work, political choices and consequences. New conquest intervals remain broader than the final regional chronology will require.

Technical validation on 9 October 2026 passed all eighteen checks in the existing World Atlas validator. The three changed Markdown files had no unresolved new wikilinks, malformed frontmatter or generation artefacts; the validator also confirmed its existing history reachability and register checks. A rendered Atlas preview was not inspected for these author-only research files.

The proposed character emphases are succession security for Thalion, obligations secured through bargaining for Meren, dependable supply for Varelan and Cassara, and personal recognition for Olarion. Varelan bears responsibility for retaining compulsory labour after the quarry killings. Cassara shares responsibility for defending the works; her death date stays open, so she may outlive Varelan.
