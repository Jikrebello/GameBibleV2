# Scattering Ethnogenesis and Inheritance Matrix

> [!warning] Promotion ledger, not canon
> This matrix records the implications of [[The Two Fissions and the Atherian Long Formation]]. It identifies later canon edits but makes none. Current Canon Reference notes remain controlling until a separate promotion decision.

## 1. Classification Rules

| Category | Controlling meaning | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Ark-derived** | A people or early institutional lineage principally descends from the one people formed during the Glass Sojourn. | pure ancestry, superior souls, uniform appearance, perfect preservation, or direct participation in the original Exodus by every later member |
| **Orphaned** | A people principally descends from human communities which survived the Giant collapse outside the organized Glass host. | rejected by the Lord, one common remnant ancestry, cultural stasis, savagery, or immunity from later Ark admixture |
| **Braided** | A later people forms through historically significant joining of Ark-derived, Orphaned, and/or other established local populations. | equal contribution from every strand, peaceful mixture, absence of conquest, or lack of a real later identity |
| **Atherianized** | A person or community adopts Atherian language, civic law, cult, education, military service, provincial status, or citizenship. | descent from the earliest southern leagues |
| **Imperial successor** | A later polity or people inherits material, legal, ecclesial, linguistic, or political structures from Atheria. | uninterrupted state continuity or one ancient ethnicity surviving unchanged |

Ancestry, culture, law, language, religion, and citizenship must be recorded separately. A community can be principally Folk by ancestry, Atherian by imperial citizenship, Redeemer-faithful by religion, bilingual in domestic and public life, and later Eclessian without contradiction.

## 3. The Two Fissions Matrix

| Question | First fission | Northern fission |
|---|---|---|
| **Approximate span** | c. 2130–2050 BR | c. 1950–1750 BR |
| **Starting population** | one post-Prophet Glass people | Near-Glass camp continuum after the southern departure |
| **Primary geography** | fertile southern routes, river basins, hills, coasts, occupied ruins versus Glass-margin and northern roads | seasonal wells, desert margins, caravan routes, dry belt, older settlements, Tarkhan circuits |
| **Political problem** | which bounded office may direct settlement after the Prophet's death | whether portable adjudication or territorial oath-enforcement can secure life under divergent conditions |
| **Material pressure** | seed, land, defensible sites, metals, timber, food, hostile settlements, reusable works | water, pasture, seasonal access, raid defense, marriage, rescue, trade, burial, long-distance testimony |
| **Human choice** | southern companies make temporary expeditionary command into permanent settlement government | some communities retain translocal courts; others attach law to defended wells, protector houses, and public oath coalitions |
| **Bodily cost** | massacre, enslavement, hostages, displacement, reprisals, hunger, destroyed settlements | drought deaths, captive-taking, revenge killing, forced movement, coerced marriage, confiscation, kin division |
| **Result** | proto-Atherian war-settlement world | proto-Jirahar and proto-Ahdari/proto-Ahl-e-Ahd formations |
| **What it does not prove** | military success does not prove divine grant or cultural purity | either later people possessing a real inheritance does not prove exclusive succession to the Prophet |

## 4. Language and Institutional Inheritance

| Layer | Expected evidence | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Common camp public substrate** | archaic administrative, ration, burial, service, witness, route, and office terms shared by old Atherian, Jirahar, and Ahdari forms | shared vocabulary proves Glass contact, not identical later doctrine or pure descent |
| **Northern shared innovations** | well, caravan, guest, burial, testimony, and seasonal terms shared between archaic Jirahar and Ahdari but absent from earliest southern Atherian | supports a period of continued northern common life after the first fission; it does not identify one branch as the original |
| **Tarkhan and older desert loans** | pastoral, weather, camel, seasonal lake, kin, raid, mediation, and route words entering both northern traditions differently | contact is reciprocal; borrowing does not make Tarkhan Ark-derived |
| **Proto-Atherian contact vocabulary** | Folk and harbor words for trees, ore, tides, local crops, courts, diseases, ships, and terrain; Atherian legal and military loans moving the other way | dependence upon local knowledge coexists with conquest and does not make every exchange voluntary |
| **Atherian civic koine** | star, archive, game, magistracy, citizen, coin, levy, and urban-status terms shared across rival cities | shared civilization is not yet one state |
| **Republican and imperial Atherian** | citizenship, province, census, appeal, military grant, colony, tax, road, and state-cult language | legal Atherian identity can be acquired; it cannot be read as ancestry |
| **Later Church language** | Atherian-derived liturgical, legal, and educational vocabulary across Thalmyric and Eclessian worlds | ecclesial inheritance does not erase local speech or settle which successor is “more Atherian” |

## 6. Inheritance Topology

### Thalmyric inheritance

Thalmyria holds the densest physical concentration of early Atherian, city-state, republican, imperial, Redeemer, occupation, and Reconquest layers. This supports a Greek, eastern-Balkan, and eastern-imperial cultural register. It does not create an ancient proto-Thalmyric people. The modern people form after the Fall from several populations living among that inheritance.

### Eclessian inheritance

Eclessian realms inherit Atherian provincial law, roads, Latin-flavored names, military settlement, municipal government, and western Church institutions through populations already braided with Folk, Markish, Vesari, Arqeshite, and other local layers. Their Roman-western flavor is an institutional inheritance subsequently made ethnic through centuries of common history.

### Sestaran inheritance

Some Sestaran cities precede Atherian conquest; some are Atherian foundations; all acquire Atherian archival, legal, linguistic, commercial, and later Church layers unevenly. “Sestaran” remains primarily civic and diplomatic. No city may use Atherianization to prove that its first population was Atherian.

### Vesari inheritance

Vesari formation occurs in a separate eastern river world. Ark-descended migrants, possibly including Atherianized groups, join rather than originate the whole population. Later Atherian and Eclessian categories overlay a people with its own moot, princely, cultic, and regional formation.
