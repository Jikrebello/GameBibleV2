# Northern Foundations Deepening — Vathra, Qahur, the Gateway and Second Qiryath

## Purpose

This review joins four new lines of research without turning them into one civilisation. It identifies the Giant polity which shaped Tarkhan antiquity, gives the Sacred Basin a long dynastic history, deepens the Gateway Republic and carries Jirahar development through the Second Destruction and Vestian diaspora.

No proposal in this review changes canon. It prepares a later promotion pass alongside the older northern and eastern research.

## Recommended historical architecture

### 1. Vathra and the Tarkhan

Adopt [[Vathra and the Tarkhan Orphaned World - The Walking Court and the Great Unyoking|Vathra]] as the principal Giant crown whose fall shaped the early Tarkhan world. Its human subjects and neighbours become part of a plural Orphaned population after the Great Unyoking. They inherit routes, foster kinship, portable authority and animal craft. They do not inherit one royal bloodline or an intact horse empire.

The recommended Vathran founder is one Titan-Firstborn conceived through a bounded Hecatara manifestation. This is a metaphysical proposal rather than a requirement for the historical skeleton. The founder's fate must remain open until the five ancient actor slots are reconciled.

### 2. Qahur and the Sacred Basin

Adopt the long rhythm developed in [[Egypt from Unification to Foreign Rule - Dynasties Temples and the Qahuri Comparison]]:

- post-Giant river communities and local crowns;
- several periods of divided rule remembered as the Twin-Reed Crowns;
- reunification and mortuary estates;
- a mixed Delta dynasty and violent southern restoration;
- a Qahuri imperial phase;
- regional military houses and a failed exclusive royal cult;
- High-Road domination and later restorations;
- eventual Covenant Al-Qahur inheriting the country without being the same state.

The Twin-Reed Crowns should remain a historian's collective label. This allows changing dynasties, foreign rulers and periods of division while preserving one intelligible river tradition.

### 3. The Gateway Republic and Samyra's country

Adopt the social and political deepening in [[Phoenicia and Carthage in Depth - Cities Country Sacrifice and Royal Afterlives]]. The Republic is a complete country of ports, farms, subject towns and inland allies. Its armies include citizens, allied troops, subject levies and paid companies under distinct obligations.

The final Caleran war retains several causes. Strategic rivalry, land, debt, fear and elite ambition matter. So does sincere horror at a real institution which kills children and feeds a Gluttony Mask. The capital's destruction and salting are actual World Egg events. Lesser towns survive through surrender, resistance to sacrifice, military necessity and chance.

The queen's later kingdom grows from these spared communities. Nerethan rule joins a local Sarqen house with Hadrima's recommended junior Qahuri maternal relationship. Samyra can claim the country of the old Republic, the restoration of Sarqen and one basin lineage. None of those claims resurrects the capital or grants rule over Qahur.

### 4. Second Qiryath and the Peshara

Adopt the five-stage development proposed in [[From Second Qiryath to the Peshara - Revolt Destruction and Jirahar Religious Formation]]:

1. pre-70 plurality around sanctuary, road courts, diaspora assemblies and competing schools;
2. emergency local rulings after the Second Destruction;
3. a subject-ordered collection between c. 160 and 260 AR;
4. several regional conversations before Vestian;
5. diaspora redactions after 395.

This model keeps Jirahar life substantially settled before the revolt and makes later mobility a renewed historical response. It also preserves the diaspora which existed before 70.

Current chronology should not move. The pagan Atherian Empire destroys Qiryath in 70. The converted empire persecutes the rebuilt Jirahar institutional world in 377–395. These are distinct catastrophes.

## Collision rulings

1. Vathra remains a lesser Giant crown and does not become a sixth Imperial Horizon.
2. The Tarkhan remain principally Orphaned humans. Giant rule shapes their history but does not define their human nature.
3. The Qahuri basin remains separate from Ishkatar, Arqesh and the Gateway coast.
4. The Gateway Republic remains a mixed, pre-Covenant coastal civilisation.
5. Child sacrifice belongs to particular Gateway institutions and supporters. It creates no hereditary guilt.
6. The Salted Capital is destroyed; the country survives. The Nerethan kingdom descends from that surviving country.
7. Hadrima's Qahuri relationship provides kin, knowledge and prestige rather than territorial ownership.
8. The Great Tower Revolt is not caused by Atheria's later conversion. Vestian persecution is a later, separately culpable event.
9. The Peshara grows from several pre-existing traditions. No council instantly invents it after 70.
10. Atherian conquest and later Redeemer-faithful government remain capable of both real achievement and grave wrongdoing.

## Proposed future canon packages

### Package N1 — Vathra and Tarkhan antiquity

- expand [[Lesser Crowns of the Giant Age]] and [[The Making of the Tarkhan]];
- create a readable history of Vathra and the Great Unyoking;
- add the loss of royal horse infrastructure and long pre-horse interval;
- preserve the founder's final fate in author-only control.

### Package N2 — The ancient Sacred Basin

- create a Qahuri dynastic atlas from c. 2260 to 500 BR;
- establish recurring towns, craft settlements, river crowns and foreign houses;
- connect later Al-Qahur to the inherited landscape without retroactive Covenant identity.

### Package N3 — Gateway society and royal aftermath

- deepen the Republic's government, countryside and military composition;
- expand the three wars and the internal payment revolt;
- establish the saved-town league and generations leading to the Nerethans;
- integrate Hadrima's junior basin descent if separately approved.

### Package N4 — Second Qiryath and classical Jirahar formation

- develop settled Second-Qiryath society before 0 AR;
- expand the Great Tower Revolt with its factions and provincial war;
- create a history of the Peshara's regional recensions;
- connect the Vestian persecution to the migration of teachers and texts.

These packages can be promoted independently. N3 should precede the final Atherian imperial-founding cast because the queen's country is one of that drama's principal powers.

## Decisions recommended for approval

- **Vathra:** approve as the Tarkhan-associated Giant crown; defer only the exact founder and Titan fate.
- **Qahur:** approve the long dynastic rhythm and keep “Twin-Reed Crowns” as a collective historical name.
- **Gateway:** approve the complete country, real sacrificial institution, survival of lesser towns and combined local–Qahuri Nerethan rise.
- **Jirahar:** approve a settled Second-Qiryath phase, the existing 66–73 revolt, gradual Peshara formation and the 377–395 second institutional catastrophe.
