# I. The Human-Coherence Test

A historical statement is coherent only when it answers the following in ordinary language:

1. **Starting condition:** what material, political, bodily, legal, or ecological situation exists first?
2. **Choice:** which person, institution, or nonhuman actor does what?
3. **Trigger:** what exact condition permits the supernatural response, if any?
4. **Mechanism:** what physically, legally, socially, or metaphysically changes?
5. **Witnessed effect:** what can somebody present see, hear, measure, fail to access, or suffer?
6. **Direct cost:** who dies, is injured, loses property, time, family, status, safety, memory, or freedom?
7. **Later consequence:** what institution, migration, feud, landscape, custom, archive, cult, or law results?
8. **Evidence:** what did that process itself leave behind?
9. **Limit:** what tempting larger conclusion does the evidence not justify?

The shorthand test is:

> **Because X existed, A chose B. B met condition C, so mechanism D produced visible result E. E harmed or helped F, which produced consequence G and evidence H. H supports claim I but cannot prove J.**

If a card cannot be restated in that form, it is not ready to carry hidden history.

## Plain-language controls for recurring terms

| Term | Required ordinary meaning in a historical card |
|---|---|
| **metaphysical mass** | accumulated matter, repetition, memory, authority, labor, or relation that lets a place or pattern remain stable, resist Drift, support inhabitants, or produce one specified response; the card must name which |
| **anchor** | the object, person, site, office, repeated act, or combined structure upon which a made place or effect presently depends; destroying it must have a stated result |
| **route** | a repeatable way of moving between stated points under stated entry, hazard, refusal, and return conditions; “a route opens” must say where it leads and what a traveler does |
| **infrastructure-office** | a repeated created procedure that performs one bounded task when its real conditions are met; it has no general conscience and cannot infer private virtue |
| **court-law** | a Menhir or place response following natural relation or jurisdiction; the card must state the local action and material consequence, not merely say the land “recognizes” somebody |
| **correspondence** | a repeatable link between two things established by material relation, history, name, use, location, oath, or resemblance; the card must identify the link and its practical effect |
| **residue** | non-soul persistence left by repeated action, death, memory, emotion, or interrupted process; a card must state what it repeats and how witnesses distinguish it from a person |
| **feeding architecture** | human or Giant acts, offices, images, rewards, punishments, and habits that reliably provide an Idol Mask with access, imagery, victims, and occasions to manifest; emotion alone is insufficient |
| **Mask feeds** | the Mask gains more reliable access, stronger manifestation, wider proxy use, or more stable public identity because people keep performing the named vice through a repeatable institution |
| **Sediment** | accumulated attributed biography, image, ritual and expectation carried by an Idol pattern; it is not the memories or souls of worshippers |
| **recognizes** | an object, office, route, or court produces a specified response when evidence or conduct meets a stated condition |
| **refuses** | a stated physical or procedural action fails: a door remains shut, an object burns, a route returns the traveler, an office will not transfer, or a mechanism stops |
| **answers** | a specified voice, light, movement, manifestation, change of access, or other observable response follows a stated invocation or condition |
| **stabilizes** | prevents one named failure—collapse, drift, duplication, misrouting, loss of identity, or contradictory operation—for a stated duration and area |
| **thin place** | a location where two normally separate layers interact under identifiable conditions; the card must name the intrusion, danger, and sign of closure |

These definitions do not add new metaphysics. They restrict how existing metaphysics may be used in history.

# VI. Discovery Standard for Unknown History

The next historical generation should distinguish **unknown**, **lost**, **suppressed**, **misclassified** and **unrecoverable**:

| State in 1360 AR | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **unknown** | nobody currently possesses the correct combined conclusion, although recoverable evidence exists |
| **lost** | the former knowledge chain ended; investigators must reconstruct from primary remains rather than locate a secret surviving book |
| **suppressed** | somebody controls relevant evidence or punishes its circulation, but may still misunderstand it |
| **misclassified** | evidence is public but placed in the wrong category—saint, Mask, Patron, office, fraud, natural event, person or copy |
| **unrecoverable** | the evidence cannot answer the question; authorial truth may exist, but no honest investigation can establish it |

## Required discovery chain

Every recoverable unknown history must contain:

1. **hidden authorial fact** — one plain statement of what happened;
2. **partial contemporary witnesses** — at least two viewpoints with different access and limitations;
3. **primary remainder** — evidence caused by the event rather than planted to explain it to future investigators;
4. **transmission damage** — loss, mistranslation, inheritance, propaganda, reuse, looting, pious editing, legal copying or natural decay;
5. **1360 access point** — a concrete ruin, body, lawsuit, archive, route, person, market, ritual test, disaster or political crisis;
6. **reproducible test** — an action whose result supports or falsifies one bounded claim;
7. **plausible false reading** — a wrong answer that fits some real evidence;
8. **corroboration threshold** — at least two independent evidence classes before a major conclusion is accepted;
9. **remaining darkness** — facts no surviving evidence can establish.

## Evidence classes

Use at least two, preferably three, independent classes for consequential discoveries:

- **material:** body, tool, architecture, road scar, ecological layer, anchor damage, residue distribution;
- **documentary:** contemporary account, ledger, title, sentence, marginal sign, copied chronicle, enemy record;
- **procedural:** a rite, machine, office, route or object repeatedly responds under the same bounded condition;
- **testimonial:** living witness, retained soul where lawful, Giant Continuant, Driftkin person, manifested saint under commission, or separately classified copy;
- **negative:** an expected feature is absent in a way the competing account cannot explain;
- **comparative:** independent cultures preserve different pieces whose overlap is more specific than shared folklore convention.

No vision, archive, saint, Patron, Giant, relic or recovered city should provide omniscient exposition. Even a truthful supernatural witness knows only what that person observed and may have interests, damaged memory, bounded commission or the wrong categories.
