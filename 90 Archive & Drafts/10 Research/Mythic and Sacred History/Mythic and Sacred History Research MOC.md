## Purpose

This author-only workspace holds comparative research and provisional historical planting material. Its purpose is to give every age a reservoir of human-scale stories, sacred memories, folk heroes, companies, relics, contested chronicles, unexplained traces, and conflicts among created powers without prematurely fixing complete biographies or a secret cosmic master chronology.

Nothing in this folder is canon merely because it is written here. Promotion requires a separate decision and an edit to the relevant Canon Reference note.

## Governing Method

> Plant lightly and harvest late.

Research should preserve the difference between:

- a source tradition and the narrative grammar learned from it;
- a provisional seed and a canonical event;
- an underlying authorial truth and later cultural interpretation;
- a human soul and the residue, office, legend-persona, Mask, or institution that may survive under the same name.

The standard transformation formula is:

> **visible scriptural grammar + comparative narrative grammar + culture-specific historical pressure + World Egg metaphysical consequence + contested later memory = world-native historical seed**

A seed must change at least three major axes from any single source: culture and setting, company composition, inciting problem, event sequence, outcome, metaphysical mechanism, political function, or later moral interpretation.

Changing those axes may never remove the borrowed element's causal atrocity, principal culpability, or irreversible price. The standard is severity parity: preserve the load-bearing moral wound, but do not import unrelated cruelties from other cited sources or increase violence merely for atmosphere.

## Source Register Standard

Every dossier should distinguish:

1. **Primary source:** Scripture, oral account, saga, epic, chronicle, folktale, saint's life, or literary work.
2. **Community or textual identity:** the particular people, language, recension, church, manuscript tradition, teller, or performer where relevant.
3. **Status:** sacred, liturgical, canonical, appendix, folkloric, literary, scholarly, or disputed.
4. **Narrative function:** what the story accomplishes for its community or text.
5. **Transferable grammar:** structure that can inspire World Egg material.
6. **Non-transferable surface:** sacred names, ritual identities, community-owned details, and distinctive sequences that should not be transplanted.
7. **World Egg torque:** the setting-specific metaphysical or historical consequence that prevents a reskin.
8. **Confidence and cautions:** attribution problems, competing versions, living-tradition restrictions, or uncertain scholarship.

## Seed Statuses

- **Research lead:** promising source or motif not yet checked closely.
- **Nursery seed:** usable exploratory concept with no canonical claim.
- **Era candidate:** tested against a particular period and culture.
- **Promotion candidate:** passed originality, theological, historical, and cultural audits.
- **Canon:** only after explicit promotion outside this folder.
- **Deferred:** preserved for possible later use.
- **Rejected:** retained with the reason so the same mistake is not repeated.

## Promotion Gates

A proposed seed must answer all of the following before promotion:

1. Does it arise from the World Egg's theology, metaphysics, culture, geography, and historical pressure?
2. Can its humble beginning be told without announcing the grand fate?
3. Does more than one member or supporting figure possess a meaningful trajectory?
4. Does it cross history without secretly causing every major event in its age?
5. Does it leave a durable trace useful even if the full story is never written?
6. Do later accounts disagree for intelligible political, regional, liturgical, or personal reasons?
7. Is at least one ending ordinary rather than royal, saintly, damned, or catastrophic?
8. If every reported miracle were false, would the human story still function?
9. If a miracle were true, would it remain consistent with the Lord's unique uncreated divinity and the established soul-route?
10. Does the proposal preserve existing unresolved Weirds rather than explaining them by accident?
