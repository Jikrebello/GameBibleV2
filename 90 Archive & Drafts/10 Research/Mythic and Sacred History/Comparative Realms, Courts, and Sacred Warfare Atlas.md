# Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas

## Purpose

This is the broad source-atlas pass requested after the first [[Great-Power Mythic Ecology and Planting Matrix]]. That matrix proved the framework but compressed too many possible kingdoms, courts, dominions, wars, commissions, and refuge states into twelve representative beds.

This atlas deliberately does **not** combine three source traditions into one seed. It keeps source units separate long enough to discover what each actually contributes. Its nursery leads are smaller than historical seed cards and larger than casual notes. None is canon, none fixes a name or date, and none automatically expands the existing twelve beds.

The desired effect is a world in which human history is not the only history. Created powers possess old enemies, frontier systems, capitals, processions, vassals, refuges, proxy wars, offices, routes, sacred charges, and goals that may only incidentally involve humanity. Humans can be witnesses, tenants, prey, clients, hostages, champions, contraband, negotiators, or pieces in a conflict whose principal actors are not human.

## The First Correction: “Realm” Means Several Different Things

| Realm type | World Egg meaning | Characteristic politics | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Idol heartland** | A region of the Shadowlands organized by one guilty Throne and its Hunger | Mask rivalry, harvest raids, cult capture, Archmask courts, Regnant policy | not a neutral divine plane and not created by human belief |
| **Menhir jurisdiction** | Faewild and Material ecology behaving under one continuous natural sovereignty | convergence, Hunt, taboo, bargain, court mediation, environmental displacement | not a moral state, church, or ordinary royal dynasty |
| **Patron anchor-kingdom** | A made place in the Egg White stabilized by mass, law, upkeep, and tether | rent, route control, proxy war, foreclosure, refuge, trade, succession of owners | Patron is a role, not a species or proof of divinity |
| **Heavenly field of commission** | A temporary theater in which a saint or angel has been sent to accomplish one charge | protection, witness, siege-breaking, passage, exposure, rescue | no private celestial kingdom, territorial appetite, or summonable standing army |
| **Shell littoral** | Recognizable terrain imposed on sterile elemental pressure by an anchor or frame | refugee government, industrial rule, platform war, environmental dependence | pure Shell patterns are not persons and the Shell has no native nations |
| **Infernal dominion** | A mapped work of refusal in Hell maintained by a damned ruler, office system, Patron, or coalition | custody, contract, hierarchy, conscription, extraction, rebellion | no universal infernal government and no created power can annihilate a soul |
| **Abyssal wound-front** | A damaged relation in creation that repeatedly expresses hostile contradiction | propagation, occlusion, mutation, collapse of maps and mandates | not a demon nation, stable empire, or second evil creation |
| **Legendary or received realm** | A mortal account that may combine real place, cultural memory, dream, propaganda, and misclassification | varies with narrator and historical layer | its public story never overrides creator continuity |

The garden should contain many of each. It should not flatten all eight into “another plane ruled by another god.”

## Source Method and Severity Rule

Every extraction below records five things:

1. the source or source family;
2. the structure worth borrowing;
3. the original hard edge that makes the structure matter;
4. its likely World Egg home;
5. what must not be transferred.

The rule remains severity parity. Abduction, enslavement, sacrifice, torture, massacre, corpse use, damnation, mutilation, madness, starvation, kin-slaying, betrayal, and destruction of inhabited cities remain present where they are load-bearing. They are named directly without voyeuristic elaboration. A source is not “made mature” by importing unrelated atrocities from another tradition.

Modern game sources contribute structure, not protected characters, proper nouns, complete plots, or prose.

# I. Idols: The Great Game Without Equal Gods

## Source Findings

Official Warhammer material treats the Great Game as an endless rivalry in which the Chaos Gods temporarily cooperate but prevent any one from remaining dominant. Their daemons are reflections or extensions of a patron power; their mortal champions compete for gifts, command, and transformation; and powerful intermediaries such as Be’lakor can be empowered by several rival gods and then become a problem for all of them. The hard edge is not decorative corruption. Mortal populations are cultivated, enslaved, sacrificed, used up, and abandoned, while even apparently noble violence can feed the relevant power.

Sources: [Warhammer Great Game](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/xxjiyeid/building-battlefields-writing-books-and-playing-the-great-game-on-warhammer-tv/), [Chaos Daemons overview](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/ftkwC8YW/warhammer-40000-faction-focus-chaos-daemons/), [Be’lakor and competing patrons](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/u4qgowmf/belakor-returns-to-the-41st-millennium-and-he-can-basically-chop-a-tank-in-half/), [mortal emotion and Khorne](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/McZ753TM/warhammer-age-of-sigmar-faction-focus-blades-of-khorne/).

### Useful extraction

- The Idol equivalent of the Great Game is a **war over mortal architectures of choice**, not a battle for raw belief points.
- Dependent Masks function well as projected organs of the Throne. Archmasks supply the independent generals, rival courtiers, defectors, pretenders, and dangerous intermediaries.
- A heartland may embody its Hunger while containing many courts, fortresses, feeding routes, captive districts, ruined older identities, and rival Mask cultures.
- A champion may serve several Hungers, play them against one another, and survive as residue or a legend-pattern. A human soul still cannot become an Idol, Archmask, or Regnant.
- “Ascension” belongs to an autonomous `idol-pattern`, an acquired office, a Patron anchor, a Giant continuity mechanism, or propaganda—not to conversion of the Image-bearing soul into a god.
- A Mask can be defeated, mutilated, consumed, rewritten, bound into another network, or driven into historical Sediment without the Throne being killed.

### Non-transferable material

- The Idols are not emotion-generated deities. Humanity supplies imagery, biography, routes, and dense chosen vice; it did not create the seven guilty sovereignties.
- A daemon-like Mask is not automatically a detachable fragment of divine substance. Its exact continuity remains `idol-pattern`, dependent organ, or autonomous Archmask as classified.
- No human earns literal Idol species by accumulating atrocities.
- No Great Game makes the Lord one participant in a cosmic balance.

# II. Menhir: From Fairy Courts to Inhuman Natural Sovereignty

## Isles source findings

The Isles do not supply one generic fairy kingdom.

### Irish material

Irish texts preserve supernatural peoples with kings, strongholds, rivalries, battles, maiming, hostage relations, skilled offices, subterranean dwellings, and negotiated coexistence. The *Cath Maige Tuired* and related material give political aftermath as much weight as spectacle. The *Dindshenchas* account of Tech Duinn gives a boundary-place associated with Donn and the dead. These are separate grammars: supernatural polity, contested land, and a dangerous terminal place should not be collapsed into one court.

Sources: [UCC CELT Irish corpus](https://www.ucc.ie/en/research-sites/celt/), [Metrical Dindshenchas and Tech Duinn](https://celt.ucc.ie/document/T106500D/), [Irish texts including Cath Maige Tuired](https://celt.ucc.ie/irlpage.html).

### Welsh material

*Preiddeu Annwfn* presents an armed expedition into Annwfn for supernatural spoils, including a cauldron, from which only a few return. Caer Sidi and the other named fortresses give the Otherworld multiple strongholds rather than one pastoral fairyland. The grammar is raid, guarded treasure, perilous sea passage, difficult return, and uncertain interpretation.

Sources: [National Library of Wales, Book of Taliesin](https://www.library.wales/fileadmin/docs_gwefan/ymweld_a_ni/arddangosfeydd/ardd_pan_4llyfr_s.pdf), [University of Bristol study of Annwfn and Caer Sidi](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/files/435766529/Fulton_Space_FINAL_2.pdf).

### Scottish material

The neat modern binary of benevolent Seelie Court versus evil Unseelie Court should not be imported as ancient fact. Better-supported material gives seely wights, Elfland, nocturnal processions, trance journeys, changelings, abduction, service, tribute, healing, danger, and later Christian demonization. “Seelie” carries blessed, fortunate, or innocent senses; it is not a reliable alignment label. The source hard edge includes stolen children, loss of time, coerced attendance, supernatural injury, witch-trial prosecution, and fatal encounters.

Sources: [Dictionary of the Scots Language, wicht](https://dsl.ac.uk/entry/snd/wicht_n), [University of Edinburgh Walter Scott e-text register](https://www.walterscott.lib.ed.ac.uk/etexts/editedworks.html), [Julian Goodare, “The Cult of the Seely Wights”](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/0015587X.2012.682483), [National Library of Scotland edition of *Minstrelsy of the Scottish Border*](https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/8060/80600435.23.pdf).

### Cornish material

Cornish collections distinguish piskies, spriggans, knockers, Buccas, mermaids, giants, and the Small People. Their local functions differ: underground communities, mine warnings, treasure guardians, coastal powers, abduction, child substitution, dangerous feasts, enchanted labor, misleading lights, crop harm, and conditional assistance. These should yield several small court ecologies rather than one Cornish Menhir faction.

Sources: [Robert Hunt, *Popular Romances of the West of England*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/59033), [William Bottrell collection excerpt](https://www.artcornwall.org/features/Piskies_Spriggans_Knockers.htm), [Cornwall Heritage Trust discussion](https://www.cornwallheritage.com/ertach-kernow-blogs-2026-2027/cornish-piskies-and-their-place-in-folklore/).

## Lovecraftian finding

Lovecraft’s Dream Cycle distinguishes relatively local, capricious “gods of earth” from vastly more alien powers, inaccessible strongholds, forbidden ascents, and mindless or incomprehensible cosmic agencies. *The Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath* also gives trade cities, black galleys, moon traffic, enslavement, predatory peoples, forbidden monasteries, and a messenger with many forms. Its useful Menhir grammar is **scale mismatch**: familiar court ritual may be only the mortal-facing edge of a law whose full operation is terrifyingly inhuman.

The “blind idiot god” element does **not** fit the Menhir themselves. Canon Menhir are intelligent continuous sovereignties capable of memory, preference, tactics, and bargain. Mindless cosmic computation belongs instead to [[Great Coherences]], some [[Great Drift Beings|Sleeping Wrongs]], Before-Shapes, or Abyssal contradictions. Menhir horror comes from intelligence without moral comprehension and from law operating at a scale where human categories are not binding.

Sources: [“The Other Gods”](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/og.aspx), [“The Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath”](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/dq.aspx), [“Celephaïs”](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/c.aspx), [“Nyarlathotep”](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/n.aspx).

Lovecraft’s racialized descriptions and equations of human difference with degeneracy are non-transferable. The atlas takes alien scale, hostile geography, and dream-route logic—not ethnic caricature.

# III. Patrons and the Egg White: Dreamlands Meets Oblivion

## Dreamlands extraction

The Dream Cycle offers a genuinely inhabited otherworld: ports, markets, temples, laws, ships, underworld routes, cloud cities, moon traffic, ruined civilizations, local gods, dangerous traders, monsters, and mortal dreamers who become rulers. Its best contribution is not “anything can happen because dreams.” It is a **coherent travel world whose distances obey meaning, memory, taboo, and route knowledge**.

Useful structures include:

- cities created or stabilized by a dreamer’s memory and longing;
- ports that trade with cloud, moon, underworld, and ordinary regions;
- an inaccessible capital whose apparent rulers are absent, displaced, or protected by a more dangerous intermediary;
- travelers who can become permanent inhabitants without being dead;
- beautiful lands that are also predatory, enslaving, cannibalistic, or fatal;
- guides whose assistance creates dependence and whose destination differs from the traveler’s.

The Egg White remains explicitly **not** the Dreamlands. Dream, memory, and longing may provide metaphysical mass, but Drift, anchor, rent, and tether remain the controlling laws.

## TES extraction

TES supplies several distinct otherworld-political grammars:

- a realm expressing the obsession or sphere of its owner;
- a Prince attacking, annexing, or physically dragging mortal territory into its realm;
- mortal cults, champions, artifacts, heralds, and proxies acting where the owner cannot or will not enter directly;
- realm-internal fortresses, libraries, cities, client populations, and hostile factions;
- neutral portal hubs such as Fargrave, where several realms meet under a local rule system;
- names as instruments of authority over otherworld beings;
- a sovereign whose own realm can be besieged, infiltrated, or endangered.

Official examples include Coldharbour’s attempted annexation of Tamriel, Apocrypha’s endless library and name-fortresses, the Deadlands and its inventions, and Fargrave as a city between planes inhabited by mortals and Daedra. Sources: [Molag Bal and the Planemeld](https://help.elderscrollsonline.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/2659/~/who-or-what-is-molag-bal%3F), [ESO: Necrom and Apocrypha](https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/updates/chapter/necrom), [Necrom’s Bastion Nymic and names](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/635069/pc-mac-patch-notes-v9-0-5-necrom-update-38), [Deadlands and Fargrave](https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-gb/updates/dlc/deadlands).

The World Egg correction is that a Patron’s realm is not its body or proof of godhood. It is an anchor-work with a declared owner, law, upkeep, inhabitants, and failure state. A powerful Patron can resemble a Daedric Prince in practice while retaining very different continuity and limits.

# IV. Saints, Angels, and Sacred Frontier Work

## Pathfinder structural findings

Pathfinder’s Riftwardens are a network of specialists who protect planar boundaries and oppose incursions. Blackfire Adepts are their hostile counterparts: planar specialists who exploit, open, or weaponize breaches. *Wrath of the Righteous* adds wardstone frontiers, crusader cities, celestial heralds, demonic infiltration, refugee suspicion, torture, purges, failed crusades, corrupted guardians, and religious institutions whose genuine cause does not make every action righteous.

Sources: [Wrath of the Righteous Player’s Guide](https://downloads.paizo.com/WrathOfTheRighteous_PlayersGuide.pdf), [Riftwardens protecting planar boundaries](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-society-scenario-5-07-port-godless/), [Iomedae’s herald in the crusade](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-adventure-card-guild-adventure-1-1-herald-s-chosen-pdf/), [Worldwound aftermath](https://paizo.com/blog/march-2021-organized-play-scenario-previews).

The World Egg should split this grammar four ways:

1. living **threshold orders** that study and repair breaches;
2. living Sinner, warlock, or Patron-aligned **breach adepts** who open and sell them;
3. true angels commissioned to defend a named frontier or close a named wound;
4. manifested saints who may found, correct, rescue, command, or temporarily fight beside those orders under a bounded commission.

A saint is never a repeatable class feature, permanent planar police officer, or owner of a ward network.

## Orthodox received-tradition findings

The received Lives are much harder and more varied than a generic kindly apparition.

- Saint Demetrios is remembered as a martyr, teacher, myrrh-gusher, defender of Thessaloniki, terrifying apparition on the walls, and a saint who refuses removal of his relics.
- Saint Mercurius is a soldier aided by an angel, tortured and beheaded, later remembered as returning to military service against an oppressor.
- Saint Nicholas physically stops an execution, confronts a bribed governor, appears in dreams to an emperor, rescues captives and sailors, and relieves famine.
- Saints Cyrus and John and the unmercenary physicians join healing, prison visitation, torture, execution, relic translation, and posthumous intercession.

Sources: [Saint Demetrios of Thessaloniki](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/2020/10/26/103059-holy-glorious-demetrios-the-myrrh-gusher-of-thessaloniki), [Great Martyr Mercurius](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/1989/11/24/103383-great-martyr-mercurius-of-caesarea-in-cappadocia), [Saint Nicholas](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/2026/12/06/103484-saint-nicholas-the-wonderworker-archbishop-of-myra-in-lycia), [Cyrus and John](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/2026/01/31/100380-holy-wonderworkers-and-unmercenaries-cyrus-and-john-and-those-wi), [Cosmas and Damian](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/2012/11/01/103133-holy-wonderworkers-and-unmercenaries-cosmas-and-damian-of-mesopo).

These are received Lives and cult traditions, not automatically modern critical biography. The documentary, Life, liturgical, iconographic, relic, and folklore layers must remain visible.

## Making saints multidimensional without making Heaven factional

Canon already permits saints to command angels, defend cities, work wonders, guard thresholds, correct their own cults, and alter an age. The needed expansion is biographical and vocational variety.

- A saint may have been a soldier, ruler, physician, midwife, outlaw, judge, captive, slave, wealthy patron, fool, penitent killer, translator, controversial bishop, frightened survivor, former persecutor, or ordinary parent.
- Sanctity does not mean the earthly biography was clean, politically convenient, temperamentally mild, or correctly remembered by every later Life.
- Repentance does not resurrect victims, cancel unjust laws, return stolen property, or force reconciliation from survivors.
- Posthumous final communion does mean the saint cannot become corrupt, seek worship, annex territory, or prosecute a private feud.
- Apparent conflicts among saints should usually be conflicts among earthly followers, rival Lives, forged apparitions, misunderstood commissions, or two real but differently bounded tasks—not celestial ego politics.

# V. The Elemental Shell: Sterile Matter, Inhabited Littorals

## Source findings

D&D and Pathfinder make the elemental planes inhabited worlds. Their City of Brass is a cosmopolitan efreet capital and market; genies form major civilizations; elemental planes possess rulers, rival lords, and settlements. Mermaid and other sea-people can maintain cities such as Pathfinder’s merfolk trading city built into a titanic moving shell. The useful structures are environmental city engineering, elemental trade monopolies, tyrannical cosmopolitanism, moving refuge-cities, and polities whose survival depends upon manipulating an otherwise lethal state of matter.

Sources: [D&D SRD planar overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/attachments/39j2li89/SRD5.1-CCBY4.0License.pdf), [Pathfinder merfolk and Alohmab](https://2e.aonprd.com/Ancestries.aspx?ID=74), [Pathfinder fire elemental ecology](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=410&Redirected=1).

The original *Arabian Nights* City of Brass is a different source: a dead city, expedition, warnings about mortality, sealed jinn, automata, greed, and repeated fatal attempts to enter or plunder it. It should never be silently treated as the same story as D&D’s living marketplace. Sources: [1909 *Arabian Nights*, “The Story of the City of Brass”](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Arabian_Nights_%281909%29/The_Story_of_the_City_of_Brass), [Project Gutenberg edition](https://gutenberg.org/cache/epub/20916/pg20916-images.html).

Underwater-palace traditions provide further grammars. Hesiod places Triton with Poseidon and Amphitrite in a golden house beneath the sea. Japanese Ryūjin/Ryūgū traditions join a sea or dragon palace, tide control, hospitality, otherworldly time, and perilous return. Sources: [Hesiodic sea palace material](https://www.theoi.com/Pontios/Amphitrite.html), [Encyclopedia of Shinto, Ryūjin belief](https://d-museum.kokugakuin.ac.jp/eos/detail/?id=8979), [Japanese government account of Urashima Tarō](https://www.gov-online.go.jp/eng/publicity/book/hlj/html/201407/201407_09_en.html).

## World Egg ruling

The Shell itself remains sterile and mechanical. A recognizable city can exist only on a constructed littoral held by an anchor, Material frame, Astral ballast, environmental protection, and return relation.

- The inhabitants are refugees, colonists, engineers, altered biological peoples, Driftkin, Giant Continuants, constructs, or stranded extrarealm beings. They are not automatically “elementals.”
- Pure Shell patterns remain nonpersons. They can serve as infrastructure, labor patterns, hazards, fauna-like recurrence, and weapons without becoming an enslaved race.
- A mermaid, triton, salamander-person, or djinn-like ruler must receive an origin and continuity. Plausible homes include Material biology, Menhir alteration, Giant ancestry, Astral Anchor-Lord, constructed body, or another already permitted continuity.
- Menhir associated with sea, storm, earth, sky, forge, or season may ally with or attack a Shell littoral because its effects touch their natural jurisdiction. They do not own the corresponding plane-quadrant.
- A city’s collapse can kill thousands of persons even though the surrounding elemental manifestations feel nothing.

# VI. Hell, the Depths, and the Abyss: More Than One Evil Ecology

## D&D and Pathfinder findings

D&D separates devils, demons, and yugoloths by characteristic social grammar. Devils are hierarchical, contractual, quota-driven, and ambitious within a chain of command. Demons are destructive, mutable, numerous, and organized around violent lords and unstable layers. Yugoloths are mercenary, self-serving, expensive, and willing to change sides. The Blood War creates fronts, river routes, supply economies, propaganda, conscription, and the morally dangerous claim that one evil must be preserved to contain another.

Sources: [D&D Blood War](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/148-learn-about-the-blood-war-in-mordenkainens-tome-of), [devils and contracts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/595-how-to-play-a-devil-like-a-lawyer-from-hell), [demons as engines of chaos](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/583-how-to-play-a-demon-like-an-engine-of-chaos), [yugoloth mercenaries](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/601-how-to-play-a-yugoloth-like-the-ultimate-mercenary).

Pathfinder makes the tripartition still clearer: Hell for devil tyranny, Abaddon for daemon predation and nihilistic appetite, and the Abyss/Outer Rifts for demons. Its neutral-evil realm adds Horsemen, soul-harvesting, mercenary predation, plagues, and the desire to extinguish life rather than rule it. Sources: [Paizo’s *Book of the Damned* division](https://paizo.com/products/btpy8a6f/discuss%26page%3D1?Pathfinder-Chronicles-Book-of-the-Damned-Volume-1-Princes-of-Darkness=), [Paizo on Abaddon’s daemons](https://paizo.com/blog/an-accounting-of-abbadon), [Paizo on divs](https://paizo.com/blog/divs-daemons-of-the-desert).

## World Egg transformation

The setting should preserve three different hostile modes without importing three species or three equal afterlives.

### Dominion evil

Mapped Hell supplies hierarchy, mandate, contract, prison, army, jurisdiction, quotas, infernal promotion, and rival damned courts. Devil remains an `infrastructure-office`; its bearer retains separate continuity.

Hell's own metaphysical environment is cold black Earth and Water: mire, silt, flooded depth, burial pressure, drowning and crushing enclosure. Authentic fire and brimstone belong only to Wrath. A burning “Hell” therefore records a Wrath Mask or Archmask intrusion, a later conflation, or ordinary Material fire—not native infernal substance.

### Predatory evil

The Depths can support selfish mercenary powers, soul predators, ransom fleets, scavenger cities, hired armies, plague-brokers, and beings who prefer profit or consumption to either law or unmaking. These need individual provenance: damned human, Depth fauna, Patron, office-bearer, or another established continuity. They are not one neutral-evil race.

### Abyssal unmaking

The Abyss supplies wounds, contradictions, impossible recurrence, mutating battlefronts, occlusion, and collapse of relationship. A wound-being is not a citizen of an Abyssal empire. Killing its body does not close the contradiction.

Pathfinder’s literal destruction or consumption of souls is non-transferable. A soul may be captured, mutilated, stripped of memory, exploited, or hidden, but no created predator can annihilate it.
