# Patron Realm Garden Promotion Review

## Ruling

This review promotes **one light historical layer from each of the six reviewed Patron placements** into [[The Historical Seed Garden]]. It does not promote the six complete realms, their provisional titles, rulers, owners, routes, dates, populations, wars, or present conditions.

Here, “promotion” means movement from an external research nursery into the central exploratory Garden. The Garden remains author-only and non-canonical. A saying may enter while the event behind it remains disputed; a legal form may enter while the city that authored it remains unnamed; a damaged object may enter without proving the story attached to it.

## Selection Rule

Each approved layer must:

1. remain useful if the complete Patron placement is later revised or rejected;
2. provide a specific carrier discoverable in 1360 AR;
3. preserve the source's culpability and irreversible price;
4. avoid fixing a sovereign identity, complete route, final map, or present outcome;
5. keep every human soul distinct from names, offices, copies, cities, anchors, residue, and Patron title;
6. deepen an existing historical pressure instead of creating another signature company;
7. remain separable from the other five promoted layers.

# I. Approved Trace Roots

## PG-T01 — Petitions of the Cut-Loose Wards

- **Historical bed:** late Chains, Great Unbinding, and the later Hunt of the Escaped Courts.
- **Promoted root:** several Egg White tenant traditions use a common petition form: the ruler must identify every district considered expendable, every protected estate, and every person denied passage before claiming that abandonment was necessary rent.
- **Durable carrier:** damaged petitions, road scars that terminate at cleanly severed foundations, and contradictory “ballast lists” that protect different elite wards.
- **Hard edge retained:** families were divided, people were killed at closed gates, and dependent districts were deliberately sacrificed while rulers concealed unequal calculation.
- **Use in 1360 AR:** a petition can expose a Patron genealogy, support a tenant claim, open a cut road, or prove only that later communities borrowed the form.
- **Not promoted:** the Court Cast Loose, its Giant owner, imperial origin, precise victims, route, survival, or responsibility for any other Giant flight.
- **Continuity control:** the petition is a document and possible `infrastructure-office`; road residue is not a soul; all human victims and descendants remain complete `human-soul` persons; any escaped Giant retains `giant-continuity`.

## PG-T02 — The Second-Hearth Clause

- **Historical bed:** Scattering and early Hearths.
- **Promoted root:** one ancient refuge constitution is credited with the saying, **“The first hearth remembers; the second hearth admits.”** Later jurists paraphrase it as present witnessed dwelling outranking a founder's private affection.
- **Durable carrier:** evacuation songs, household admission clauses, two incompatible maps, and a disputed anchor shard that punishes people under obsolete domestic rules.
- **Hard edge retained:** a founder consumed memories belonging to living relatives; restorationist officials later excluded an unremembered workers' district, withdrew maintenance, killed inhabitants, and broke the city.
- **Use in 1360 AR:** refugee courts, Driftkin assemblies, and Material inheritance hearings quote the clause for incompatible purposes. Possession of the shard proves neither citizenship nor survival of anyone copied within it.
- **Not promoted:** founder, full city, exact collapse, surviving district, copied person's status, or descent into Chalkhaven or the Unfinished Shore.
- **Continuity control:** the founder, builders, excluded residents, refugees, and descendants remain distinct complete `human-soul` persons; city, copy, office, song, shard, and memory residue are separately classified.

## PG-T03 — The Borrowed-Standing Seal

- **Historical bed:** Long Frontier and Long Rent War.
- **Promoted root:** a multilingual seal states that a witness may possess standing for one declared transaction without becoming the client, subject, property, or moral guarantor of any Patron party.
- **Durable carrier:** seal impressions, extradition clauses, witness fees, and a surviving register that classifies the same kidnapped people as cargo in one language and citizens in another.
- **Hard edge retained:** officials knowingly preserved a fraudulent cargo classification to prevent compact withdrawal; captives, witnesses, guards, and bystanders died in the resulting dock violence, and whole districts were later displaced by anchor withdrawal.
- **Use in 1360 AR:** Vortharan and frontier advocates cite the seal in Patron negotiations, but no surviving copy contains the complete compact or proves descent from the original port.
- **Not promoted:** port location, founding owners, access cities, complete law, dock crisis sequence, route, or present polity.
- **Continuity control:** human witnesses and captives remain complete `human-soul` persons. Standing binds only the stated act; it cannot convey a soul, total identity, or permanent allegiance.

## PG-T04 — The Unreturned Ledger

- **Historical bed:** Fall of Caleran, refugee flight, and Long Reconquest.
- **Promoted root:** several Fall-era remittance books use paired columns: **what was carried out** and **who carried it**. Later creditor copies often preserve the first and erase the second.
- **Durable carrier:** paired title entries, refugee-credit warnings, service claims, and a formula stating that no room returns before the people who kept it standing receive a hearing.
- **Hard edge retained:** shelter became debt, debt became inherited service, dependants became collateral, wounded people were abandoned outside a closing refuge, and restoration claims threatened survivors and occupation-born families.
- **Use in 1360 AR:** courts, monasteries, descendants, creditors, and alleged portable rooms invoke incomplete copies in property disputes. The ledger supplies evidence, never an automatic verdict.
- **Not promoted:** treasury-city, Patron owner, client rulers, expedition, return programme, portable palace, current creditors, or authority over the Reconquest.
- **Continuity control:** every refugee, servant, debtor, claimant, collaborator, and occupation-born person remains a complete `human-soul`; ledgers, titles, rooms, offices, and Patron ownership remain distinct.

## PG-T05 — The Three-Night Exception

- **Historical bed:** Grey Sweat and later missing-person law.
- **Promoted root:** several inheritance courts preserve an exception for a person who can prove continuous bodily life in a place where three nights passed while years elapsed outside. The rule stays exceptional and proof-heavy.
- **Durable carrier:** sealed farewell gifts, contradictory age testimony, plague registers amended in different hands, and a warning that refuge time must be disclosed before admission.
- **Hard edge retained:** admission was unequal; people were beaten or killed at a refuge gate; saved parents returned to dead or aged children; spouses remarried; property passed; and stewards concealed a cost they partly understood.
- **Use in 1360 AR:** the exception can protect a genuine returnee, enable inheritance fraud, expose plague profiteering, or provoke a new asylum debate. It does not prove the alleged palace still exists.
- **Not promoted:** palace, Anchor-Lord, locality, entry object, time ratio, named returnee, gift contents, present route, or relation to the Quiet Ninth.
- **Continuity control:** temporal displacement changes neither species, moral judgement, nor human soul continuity. Gifts, testimony, offices, constructs, and palace residue are not persons by default.
- **Source control:** the retained grammar remains specifically attributed to Ryūgū and Urashima traditions in the research dossier; no final cultural recension may be promoted without the dedicated Japanese source pass.

## PG-T06 — The Nameless-Citizen Petition

- **Historical bed:** Reformation, Held-Proof climate, and 1360 AR Patron politics.
- **Promoted root:** a redacted petition asks whether people who erased their registered names to resist torture can regain civic protection without restoring the old commands, debts, and routes attached to those names.
- **Durable carrier:** erased veteran marks, a provisional restoration rubric, conflicting gatekeeper genealogies, and testimony concerning present kidnappings.
- **Hard edge retained:** gatekeepers were tortured, defenders erased biographies and family standing, children disappeared from civic protection, and later creditors exploited the unregistered households.
- **Use in 1360 AR:** the petition can reach Vorthara, a Material proof court, the Weighmoot, or several venues independently. Its existence creates claimants and danger without authenticating a castellan.
- **Not promoted:** fortress, owner, claimant, besiegers, archive contents, exact jurisdiction, ward failure, correct remedy, or any campaign result.
- **Continuity control:** every defender, gatekeeper, citizen, prisoner, dependant, and descendant remains a complete `human-soul`; a name is a bounded handle, not the person or soul.
