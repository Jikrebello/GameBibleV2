# Infernal Dominions and Black-Coil Archfiends Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Infernal Kings and the Wars of the Depth]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Purpose and Status

This is the fourth family review arising from [[Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas]]. It develops all twelve `HL-R` dominion leads and adds eighteen separate `AF-R` actor nurseries drawn from classic archdevil, demon-lord, bound-overlord and exiled-dark-sovereign grammars.

Nothing here establishes a canon ruler, new fiend species, universal Hell monarchy, second Adversary, named fallen angel, new true dragon or promoted historical event. The strengthened ruling that Hell has no native metaphysical fire or brimstone has been reconciled into controlling canon; every polity, actor and event in this dossier remains exploratory. The twelve dominion leads finish with seven `retain` and five incorporated `revise` dispositions. [[Infernal Dominions and Black-Coil Archfiends - Collision Selection and Historical Placement Review]] gives four actors and six dominion roots separate historical apertures. [[Black-Coil Archfiends - Source Signature and Full Ecology Review]] then restores the recognizable source signatures and graduates all fourteen formerly deferred figures into bounded active ecologies.

The governing result is:

> **Archdevil** is an in-world political grade for a power controlling several infernal offices and durable works. **Demon lord** is an imprecise in-world name for a great hostile power that appears unconstrained by those works. Neither term identifies a species, continuity or universal rank.

# I. Controlling Infernal Distinctions

## Hell is not one kingdom

[[Hell, Depth, and the Abyss]] remains controlling. Mapped Hell contains black mire, judged human souls, native Depth fauna, imposed offices, prisons, roads, dominions, Patrons and damaged approaches to the Abyss. Local rulers can conquer, administer and lose territory. None owns Hell, performs judgement or rules every devil office.

The Adversary remains the terminal principle and personal champion of refusal, not an infernal emperor issuing every order. An archdevil may claim to be its viceroy, heir, enemy or jailer. Such claims are propaganda unless a unique dossier establishes a real relationship.

## Creator taxonomy behind public names

| Public label | Possible creator classification | Continuity that must remain visible | Defeat boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **archdevil** | damned map-lord, Crowned Refuser, Patron ruler, autonomous Archmask, unique fallen angel, or coalition controlling several offices | underlying `human-soul`, `idol-pattern`, `created-spirit`, Patron continuity or other established person remains distinct from every `infrastructure-office` | destroying regalia or office-bodies removes command capacity; it does not annihilate, redeem or automatically kill the bearer |
| **demon lord** | sovereign `abyssal-wound`, autonomous Idol Archmask, unique fallen angel, damned tyrant outside stable office law, or deliberate counterfeit | exact continuity must be authorially selected | wound recurrence, Shadow anchors, soul manifestation, created-spirit manifestation and fraud all end differently |
| **devil** | imposed `infrastructure-office` and its current bearer | mantle and bearer never merge | invalidate mandate, standing, naming chain and transferable standards before addressing the bearer |
| **demon** | Church or folk term for a hostile spirit; popular speech also applies it to wounds, Masks, damned manifestations and predators | creator notes must correct the public category | no generic banishment works across all provenances |
| **black dragon / eel king** | appearance shared by unrelated great manifestations | morphology never proves kinship | true dragon, office-body, Archmask, Depth animal and symbolic spirit-form have separate anchors |

## The Black-Coil visual grammar

`Black-Coil` is an authorial design tag, not a creature family. Hell's pressure, submerged roads, black mud, narrow breaches and consuming offices encourage long eel, lamprey and serpent shapes. A ruler that occupies roads rather than rooms may appear as a coil passing through several fortresses at once. A mouth can be a gate, a throat a prison route and ribs a chain of offices.

Different Black Coils remain visibly distinguishable:

| Form | Visual language | What the body means |
|---|---|---|
| **dominion coil** | black flesh carrying human portraits, crowns, deeds and conquered masonry | a damned ruler's manifested body shaped by its local dominion; the human soul is not spread through the masonry |
| **office coil** | iron plates, seals, chains, rank standards, pressure gates and a visible bearer socket | a colossal `infrastructure-office`; repeated form may outlive several bearers |
| **Abyssal coil** | broken outline, missing intervals, impossible mouths, wound-light and causal discontinuity | the body of an `abyssal-wound`; destruction interrupts it until the enabling contradiction closes |
| **Archmask coil** | culturally unstable regalia, repeated vice imagery and faces borrowed from cult history | an autonomous `idol-pattern`, not a damned soul or Hell-native species |
| **fallen-spirit coil** | symbolic many-eyed, serpentine, beautiful or terrible manifestation | one `created-spirit`; anatomy is not biology and no reusable form follows |
| **Depth leviathan** | scarred eel, drake, worm or lamprey anatomy adapted to mire | a living animal or separately established sapient biological person; bodily death is ordinarily permanent |

Two Black Coils may hate, marry politically, devour one another's manifestations or claim a common parent. None of that proves shared blood or ontology.

## Fire and brimstone belong to Wrath

Hell is entirely a leftward Earth-and-Water reality: cold black water, saturated earth, silt, burial pressure, mire, drowning, crushing weight and the loss of clean boundaries. Hell possesses no native brimstone wastes, fire rivers, burning prison ecology, hellfire substance or infernal furnace tradition.

All authentic fire-and-brimstone imagery belongs to [[Wrath|Wrath's Red Marches]]. Where witnesses report fire inside an infernal dominion, one of the following is true:

1. a Wrath Mask or autonomous Archmask has imposed a bounded Red-Marches overlap;
2. an infernal ruler has allied with, leased power from or been partially occupied by a Wrath actor;
3. a mortal cult has collapsed two distinct hostile experiences into one later iconography;
4. ordinary Material fire is present and has been incorrectly treated as metaphysical evidence.

An Abyssal wound does not generate authentic fire merely to look demonic. A devil office cannot manufacture metaphysical flame. Fire-Shell leakage remains Shell physics rather than brimstone and never becomes infernal fire. Removing the Wrath route removes the supernatural flame even if the black dominion, damned ruler, office machinery or Abyssal wound remains.

Its archdevils are monstrous tyrants with differentiated layers; infernal dukes and malebranche widen the political ecology; demon lords carry intensely specific realms and cult practices; daemonic Horsemen model predation that seeks extinction rather than rule.

| Source shelf | Useful extraction | Required correction |

## Eberron

Eberron supplies two different grammars which must remain separate.

### Bound Overlords and the Lords of Dust

Overlords are regional, concept-specific powers. They can overlap without being one pantheon; their release depends on particular prophetic conditions; shattered prisons can spread weaker influence; ancient servants maneuver mortal history toward release; and released rivals may fight rather than unite.

World Egg use:

- apply conditional-release logic to a true dragon, unique fallen angel, Archmask or damned map-lord only after its own continuity is fixed;
- allow prison fragments to transmit influence without containing pieces of a human soul;
- build rival release cults whose prophecies are technical causal instructions rather than divine revelation;
- let an ancient servant understand the release mechanism without knowing final judgement or the Lord's purpose.

Do not create thirty native concept-fiends, one draconic prophecy governing all history, or sacrificial celestials whose deaths manufacture the cosmos. Keith Baker's descriptions emphasize regional range, conceptual overlap, conditional release and competing servants. Sources: [Lords of Dust and Overlords](https://keith-baker.com/eberron-flashback-the-lords-of-dust/), [regional distinctions among Overlords](https://keith-baker.com/dragonmarks-51016-planes-druids-and-faith/).

### Shavarath

Shavarath supplies eternal armies whose offices embody different reasons for war. World Egg can use this for recurring command mantles, fortress objectives and rival interpretations of duty. It cannot import a cosmic Balance in which Heaven must preserve Hell so reality survives. Loyal angels never defend an infernal regime as a metaphysical necessity; a commission may protect named souls, a route or creation from an Abyssal wound while leaving Hell's tyrants to answer for themselves.

## Dragonlance

Dragonlance is thinner as an archdevil catalogue but strong as a campaign grammar. Takhisis is an exiled dark sovereign who cannot simply enter the world, works through stolen portals, dragons, mortal champions and mass armies, and repeatedly converts other people's ambition into an invasion route. Lord Soth contributes the great damned champion who retains personal will and can be coveted, used or defied rather than becoming a faceless monster.

World Egg translation routes the sovereign grammar to an autonomous Pride Archmask exiled from a particular Material route. It may command constructed war-broods, false dragons, willing rulers and infiltrated temples. It is not a goddess, cannot steal the World Egg, is not the Adversary and cannot create human souls. The champion remains a damned `human-soul` distinct from every deathly body and office.

The setting's recurrent wars also warn against making every infernal story one return plot. Source: [official Dragonlance historical overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1324-what-is-dragonlance-krynn-a-world-at-war).

# III. Scriptural, Orthodox and Mythic Controls

## Orthodox and scriptural lens

Orthodox teaching treats Satan and demons as created bodiless spirits who knowingly rebelled, attack principally through deception and temptation, and remain creatures already defeated in principle by Christ. This forbids a dualist cosmos, necessary Blood War, redeeming infernal bureaucracy or claim that spectacular anatomy proves spiritual rank. Sources: [OCA on angels and evil spirits](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/doctrine-scripture/the-symbol-of-faith/angels-and-evil-spirits), [OCA on the devil](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/spirituality/orthodox-spirituality/the-devil).

World Egg already broadens popular `fiend` language beyond fallen angels. The correction is authorial clarity: true demons in the strict spiritual sense are unique fallen `created-spirit` persons; devil offices, damned humans, Idol Archmasks, Abyssal wounds and Depth animals are not additional fallen-angel species.

### Dragon and serpent imagery

Revelation's dragon identifies the Adversary and his warfare, not a reusable dragon species. Job and Isaiah use overwhelming Leviathan and twisting-serpent language to emphasize creaturely terror under the Lord's absolute sovereignty. This supports immense eel-draconic manifestations while preserving the central asymmetry: no archfiend is the Lord's equal, necessary rival or dark counterpart.

### Tobit

Asmodeus attacks Sara's marriages and kills her husbands; Raphael binds the hostile spirit under a specific commission. The useful grammar is intimate predation, a demon's narrow repeated claim and an angelic intervention that answers the commission rather than founding an exorcist empire. It does not supply an infernal king merely because later games reuse the name.

### 1 Enoch and Watcher traditions

The Watchers are bound after rebellion, while spirits associated with the slain giant offspring continue harming humanity. This remains a comparative source rather than a direct provenance retcon. World Egg Titan-Firstborn possess `titan-continuity`, not human souls, and no new roaming demon species is inferred from their destruction. Useful elements are binding, distributed aftermath, prohibited teaching and the difference between an imprisoned rebel and the harms it already released.

# V. Reviewed Dominion and Front Nurseries

## HL-R01 — The Quota Kingdom

- **Polity:** one mapped mire dominion whose ruler controls a Crown office and delegates custody, advocacy, collection and command mantles.
- **Ruler:** preferably a damned `human-soul` whose manifested dominion body resembles a seal-covered black eel passing through the kingdom's prisons. The Crown and body remain separate from the soul.
- **Mechanism:** each subordinate office receives measurable quotas: prisoners retained, labor extracted, breaches closed, defections punished and rival mandates captured. Failure strips protection and exposes the bearer to the next office below.
- **Hard edge:** prisoners are tortured into testimony, workers are mutilated to prevent flight, families and former comrades are forced to accuse one another, and successful officers expand the population subject to abuse.
- **Political lie:** because no official acts without a superior mandate, everyone claims the violence belongs elsewhere.
- **Collision boundary:** no quota measures the Lord's judgement, creates damned souls or reaches every dominion. The ruler is not an Asmodeus analogue governing Hell.
- **Possible trace:** several Material contract cults unknowingly pay the same quota through different intermediaries.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; unplaced and unpromoted.

## HL-R02R — The Claimed First Front

- **Polity:** a fortified dominion on one mapped boundary where an Abyssal contradiction repeatedly opens.
- **Ruler:** a damned marshal, Patron beneficiary or coalition of office-bearers which calls the front the oldest and most necessary defence in Hell.
- **Mechanism:** the fort closes one wound route, then engineers dependence by sabotaging rival seals, relocating refugees into exposed territory and classifying neutral roads as military depth.
- **Hard edge:** permanent conscription, hostage execution, punishment mutilation, abandoned civilians, prisoners used to test wound spread and whole districts drowned to simplify defence remain explicit.
- **Political lie:** every conquest is presented as the final wall between creation and the Abyss.
- **World Egg correction:** the local containment may be real and still never justify the ruler. Other closures remain possible; Heaven has no duty to preserve the regime.
- **Possible trace:** Material summoners receive maps whose safe routes all terminate at the claimant's recruiting prisons.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; “first” is propaganda and necessity is bounded.

## HL-R03 — The Black Companies

- **Polity:** several unrelated hired forces using similar charters, black-route markets and defection customs.
- **Possible companies:** damned soldiers preserving earthly unit identities; Patron-leased crews; office-bound heterogeneous troops; sapient Depth persons; coerced beasts with handlers; or mixtures whose continuities are individually recorded.
- **Mechanism:** contracts specify objective, permitted losses, route, payment and the price of switching sides. Companies sell tactical memory and access rather than loyalty.
- **Hard edge:** they abandon civilians to reprisal, sell prisoners twice, execute witnesses to a changed contract and deliberately prolong sieges to raise their rate.
- **World Egg correction:** no yugoloth ancestry or neutral-evil people is created. A company can keep its contract exactly and remain wicked.
- **Possible trace:** a Material dynasty's legendary infernal guard is actually three successive companies with incompatible provenances.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; each future company requires its own dossier.

## HL-R04R — The Hunger That Refuses Rule

- **Power:** one future actor whose desired outcome is not subjects or tribute but the destruction of usable life, memory and relation across a bounded region.
- **Classification gate:** placement must choose a damned human ruler, autonomous Idol Archmask, Patron predator or sovereign Abyssal wound. No new daemon species is permitted.
- **Mechanism:** it spoils seed, spreads a named disease, pays armies to destroy wells, teaches victims to burn records and makes suicide or abandonment appear like the only remaining choice.
- **Hard edge:** starvation, plague death, corpse stripping, despair, coerced euthanasia, cannibal survival and self-destruction remain available where the selected source element requires them.
- **Soul boundary:** it can hide, torment, intercept and mutilate a soul but cannot consume it into nonexistence.
- **Collision boundary:** natural famine and disease are not its moral agents; Morvalis and Veradia remain natural-law sovereignties rather than infernal accomplices.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; promotion waits on exact provenance and one bounded mechanism.

## HL-R05 — The Infernal Client City

- **Polity:** a city built and inhabited primarily by damned humans under protection from several devil offices.
- **Social reality:** residents preserve old languages, households, friendships, trades, festivals, hatreds and political loyalties. No children are born there and no infernal ethnicity replaces their human souls.
- **Mechanism:** the city purchases protection through labor, intelligence, captive exchange and maintenance of one map-lord's roads. Its council can negotiate locally while never escaping judgement or the coercive environment.
- **Hard edge:** dissidents disappear into office works, outsiders are enslaved, former earthly victims encounter their persecutors in positions of civic honor and loyal residents defend institutions that torture others.
- **Moral boundary:** affection, courage and civic beauty do not make damnation neutral. The ruler may be loved without becoming rightful.
- **Possible trace:** a Material ruin reproduces the city's street plan because an architect received it in dreams and omitted the prisons.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; no fixed city or ruler yet.

## HL-R06 — The Prison That Employs Its Captives

- **Polity:** a custody work whose cells, archives and roads function through the prisoners' skills, pain, memories or compelled testimony.
- **Population:** damned human captives, intercepted souls awaiting restored passage, office-bearers, constructed custodians and predators must be kept in separate registers.
- **Mechanism:** engineers maintain locks, witnesses authenticate sentences, singers stabilize names and mutilated navigators keep routes open. The prison preserves what it exploits because destruction would end the yield.
- **Hard edge:** torture, forced labor, sensory deprivation, mutilation, coerced accusation and prisoners made to maintain the cells of people they hate remain explicit.
- **Rescue boundary:** breaking the central office without opening a viable route can strand intercepted persons, collapse identities or leave predators uncontained. Care does not validate the prison.
- **Possible trace:** one Material institution unknowingly uses a legal procedure copied from the prison's work roster.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## HL-R07 — The Counter-Contract War

- **Polity:** rival Ash Advocate networks contest custody and dominion by attacking one another's standing rather than marching armies first.
- **Mechanism:** they steal witnesses, alter definitions, invalidate seals, redefine collateral and transfer offices to bearers their enemies cannot lawfully command.
- **Hard edge:** a changed clause moves real prisoners, exposes protected settlements, orders torture, removes food, authorizes execution or sends an office-body through a Material household.
- **Political texture:** advocates may expose a genuine fraud for an evil purpose. One correct judgement proves only the clause before it.
- **Collision boundary:** no document alters the Lord's judgement, owns a soul or makes consent retroactively valid.
- **Possible trace:** incompatible infernal precedents are cited by two Material legal schools that do not know their cases concern the same captive.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## HL-R08R — The Mutiny of Broken Mantles

- **Event:** bearers of several devil offices cooperate to attack the seals, beneficiaries and standards controlling them.
- **Participants:** innocent captive bearers, damned volunteers, sadists seeking better offices, sapient nonhumans, constrained created spirits and mindless constructed bearers may all appear. They are not one people.
- **Mechanism:** each conspirator can violate only one layer of the architecture. Success requires simultaneous refusal, sabotage and the theft of replacement mandates.
- **Hard edge:** former jailers murder prisoners to erase evidence, rebels abandon incompatible bearers, offices tear bodies apart when commands conflict and liberated predators attack those who freed them.
- **Moral boundary:** separation from an office reveals rather than decides culpability. A damned human remains damned; an innocent bearer remains itself; a mindless pattern does not acquire guilt.
- **Possible trace:** broken mantle-parts circulate as abolitionist relics and as tools for forcing new bearers.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; no mantle species, liberation race or automatic absolution.

## HL-R09R — The Wound Arsenal

- **Polity:** one dominion studies existing Abyssal contradictions as strategic weapons.
- **Mechanism:** handlers preserve openings, divide manifestation anchors, redirect signs, attach route beacons and reopen injuries under controlled conditions. They cannot breed wounds biologically or create anti-place from nothing.
- **Hard edge:** prisoners and occupied settlements become test sites; roads vanish, bodies lose sequence, names detach from testimony and failed handlers are fed into the next trial.
- **Strategic failure:** every directed wound damages the map that makes control possible. Rival dominions deliberately allow limited breaches to bankrupt the arsenal's custodians.
- **Fire boundary:** no experiment produces hellfire. Any authentic supernatural flame in the arsenal proves a separate Wrath intrusion.
- **Possible trace:** a Material weapon is actually a range marker telling a wound where to recur.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; “harvesting” means preservation and redirection, never reproduction.

## HL-R10 — The War of the Damned Kings

- **Conflict:** several human rulers continue earthly feuds after judgement using local dominions, inherited propaganda and devil offices.
- **Mechanism:** each king recruits former soldiers, coerces old subjects, fabricates dynastic courts and reopens Material anchors at battlefields, tombs and coronation sites.
- **Hard edge:** former victims are enslaved again, cities are punished for descendants' identities, hostages are executed, corpses are used as anchors and subjects who never supported the original war are conscripted.
- **Human boundary:** every ruler remains a distinct `human-soul`; crowns, bodies, legends and offices do not fuse their persons. Living descendants inherit consequences, not guilt.
- **Possible trace:** rival royal cults on the Material side are maintaining two fronts of the same infernal feud.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; no fixed kings or dynasties.

## HL-R11R — The Privateers of the Undertow

- **Conflict:** unrelated captains raid damaged soul-routes, infernal approaches and Patron passages under letters of seizure issued by interested powers.
- **Possible captains:** damned human, living Sinner manifesting through an anchor, Patron proxy, sapient route predator, Archmask agent or office-bearer. Each vessel likewise needs a continuity.
- **Mechanism:** crews capture persons, ransom names, sell memories, tow damaged souls into false destinations and change allegiance when another power offers better passage.
- **Hard edge:** captives are mutilated to defeat recognition, abandoned where routes fail, forced to guide raids against companions and sold repeatedly. No victim is annihilated.
- **Collision boundary:** [[The Undertow]] is an ecology and network, not one fleet. Loyal soul-road forces may destroy a privateer without defending the infernal port it attacks.
- **Possible trace:** a drowned Material bell rings whenever one old letter of seizure is exercised.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; every captain and craft remains separate.

## HL-R12 — The Last-Door Crusade

- **Conflict:** a damaged route is assaulted from one infernal custody work while a saint and loyal angels hold the final viable threshold under a bounded commission.
- **Human work:** living investigators must close the Material cause, identify the prisoners, open physical cells and carry people who cannot cross alone.
- **Hard edge:** defenders are killed, captives are tortured to produce false names, wounded people are abandoned by panicked rescuers and the infernal ruler attacks its own prisoners to deny testimony.
- **Commission boundary:** the saint may repel manifestations, preserve personhood and hold the route. The saint cannot empty Hell, reverse judgement, become permanent gate-governor or command unrelated angels.
- **Infernal boundary:** destroying the attacker proves nothing about the legitimacy of rival dominions asking to use the saved route.
- **Collision ruling:** this is an infernal-side front compatible with the reviewed Liberator of Captives, not a second saintly nursery.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; unplaced.

# VI. Black-Coil Archfiend Actor Nursery

These eighteen figures are separate actor leads, not a council, species, genealogy, pantheon or predetermined cast. Their titles are nursery handles. Several may never be promoted.

The plain one-to-one source key and final active-ecology rulings are collected in [[Black-Coil Archfiends - Source Signature and Full Ecology Review]]. Four actors possess provisional dated apertures; the remaining fourteen are active but undated rather than deferred.

- **Black-Coil form:** an immense blind eel whose scales are seals and whose human face appears only inside the mouth when an audit is pronounced.

- **Hard edge:** he promotes betrayal, audits torture as productivity and destroys successful subordinates before they can become independent.

# VII. Relationship and Collision Controls

## Archfiends may scheme without one Blood War

The actor nursery supports:

- archdevil against archdevil over office standing, roads, prisoners and quotas;
- damned ruler against Idol Archmask over who owns a cult or harvest;
- Archmask against Archmask across Hell where two Idols lease different routes;
- dominion armies against one Abyssal wound without becoming good;
- wound-sovereign against wound-sovereign where their contradictions cannot coexist;
- Patron or Undertow privateers selling movement to every side;
- saintly or angelic intervention only where a commission protects persons, truth, creation or a soul-route.

No hostile war is required to preserve cosmic balance. If two evils contain each other, mortals may exploit the pause without becoming obligated to maintain either forever.

## Idol interface

Only the following actor leads are provisional Archmasks:

- AF-R03 belongs to Wrath;
- AF-R06 belongs to Greed;
- AF-R12 and AF-R14 belong to Gluttony;
- AF-R15 belongs to Envy;
- AF-R18 belongs to Pride.

Each is one autonomous person rooted in one Throne. Alliances, leases and shared cults never create a blended Idol continuity. An infernal court may worship an Archmask as a demon lord, but the court does not thereby become part of the Shadowland heartland.

## Existing true dragons

AF-R10 and AF-R16 are sovereign Hell-side `abyssal-wound` actors, but [[Black-Coil Archfiends - Source Signature and Full Ecology Review]] rules that neither is presently a True Dragon. They remain separate from:

- [[The Shoreless King|refuge deliberately made impossible]];
- [[The Unwritten Worm|atrocity converted into enforced absence]];
- [[The General Below|eternal conscription]].

Similar eel-draconic morphology is expected and proves no kinship. Neither may acquire True Dragon classification merely because a campaign needs a boss; a later reclassification would require reopening this ruling and satisfying the complete dedicated dossier gate.

## Giant and Enochic interface

No actor nursery is secretly the disembodied soul of a Titan-Firstborn or High Giant. A Titan fragment remains `titan-continuity`; a Giant remains `giant-continuity`; a copied ancestor remains its actual constructed pattern. Enochic demon-after-giant grammar remains a comparative story source, not a continuity override.

# IX. Promotion Gates

Before any dominion or actor receives historical placement:

1. identify exact era, Material aperture, ordinary victims and 1360 AR evidence;
2. classify every human soul, office, body, Archmask, Patron, created spirit, Depth organism and wound separately;
3. explain who built each prison, road, city, seal, contract and command body;
4. state how the power obtains information, movement, labor, captives and manifestation mass;
5. state what bodily destruction accomplishes and what anchors remain;
6. preserve torture, enslavement, cannibalism, execution, corpse violation, starvation, sexual coercion and mutilation where source-load-bearing without adding spectacle for its own sake;
7. never let a created power annihilate, create, divide or change the judgement of a human soul;
8. never let office removal redeem a damned bearer or condemn an innocent one;
9. never let an Archmask cease belonging to one Idol Throne;
10. never let a fallen angel appear without original commission, exact refusal, present lie and unique defeat grammar;
11. never let a wound recur without an enabling contradiction that investigators can actually address;
12. keep Hell black, cold, wet, earthen and oppressive;
13. route every authentic fire-and-brimstone manifestation to Wrath and show what happens when that route is severed;
14. preserve bounded heavenly commission and mortal participation in every rescue;
15. leave present owners, names, final release and future victories open.
