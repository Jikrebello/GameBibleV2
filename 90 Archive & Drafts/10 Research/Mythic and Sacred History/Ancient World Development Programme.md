# Ancient World Development Programme

## Purpose

This programme develops the histories of the non-Atherian world from the Great Unbinding to Aurelius Thalion's rise. The existing Atherian chronology supplies a common calendar and a neighbouring political history. It does not turn Atheria into the measure of every civilisation. Each regional package follows households, rulers, settlements, work, faith, violence and exchange through changes generated within that region as well as pressures arriving from abroad.

The work proceeds from research to review and then to a separate canon delivery. A development dossier may give people and places proper names so that their relationships can be understood. Those names remain proposals until their package is approved and entered into the Canon Reference.

## The common periods

| Period | Dates | Shared historical question |
|---|---:|---|
| **Unbinding and Abandoned Worlds** | c. 2300–2165 BR | What survived Giant rule, and who acquired abandoned land, tools, roads and obligations? |
| **Reassembly and Fissions** | c. 2165–1900 BR | How did Orphaned communities rebuild while the Glass people divided and moved into an already inhabited world? |
| **Hearths and First Kings** | c. 1900–1400 BR | Which halls, wells, tower circuits, ports and cultivated districts became durable centres of power? |
| **Bronze Roads and City Worlds** | c. 1450–800 BR | How did exchange enlarge cities and kingdoms, and what survived the later breakdown of routes and palace cooperation? |
| **Eastern Empires and Maritime Republics** | c. 800–520 BR | How did Arqesh, Qiryath, the Qahuri crowns, the Gateway Republic and Caleran compete across rivers, roads and seas? |
| **Balance before Empire** | c. 520–350 BR | Which restoration states, dynasties and rival powers confronted the generation that made Thalion emperor? |

The periods overlap deliberately. The Breaking of the Bronze Roads began in some corridors while other regions still prospered. The Gateway capital fell in 520 BR, while the spared towns and Qahuri crowns immediately entered new histories rather than waiting for a neat change of age.

## Geography of the ancient world

The working geography follows an inverted-Mediterranean model. The inner sea joins more histories than it separates. Hotter and drier country lies principally to the north; colder forests, wet coasts and mountain systems lie farther south. River corridors, desert margins, straits, island chains and mountain approaches govern movement.

Development uses relational descriptions. A city may stand upriver from a delta market, behind an eastern pass, or across the strait from an allied harbour. Exact distances, coastlines and borders await a later cartographic revision. Existing named locations retain their positions unless a contradiction is demonstrated. Suggested map changes belong in the [[Ancient World Synchronisation Ledger|Synchronisation Ledger]] and acquire no authority through repetition in prose.

## Working method

Each regional package has two deliveries.

The **development delivery** reconciles canon and research, establishes a fully named history, and records proposed additions and collisions. It uses the approved encyclopaedic voice so that the proposed world can be read as history rather than as a planning document. Its technical controls remain in a separate review article.

The **canon delivery** follows approval. It expands the appropriate Canon Reference articles, creates substantial new entries, localises relevant legendary traditions and updates shared chronology and cultural navigation. Canon delivery includes a second consistency check against neighbouring packages.

Every developed event requires identifiable participants, motives, movement, supplies, direct costs and lasting consequences. The history also follows cultivation, craft, food, marriage, worship, games, songs and ordinary pleasure. Violence remains explicit where it shapes later institutions or memory. Supernatural action may alter weather, routes, bodies, fear or political confidence; human actors still choose campaigns, sacrifices, enslavement, revolt and accommodation.

## Delivery packages

### Package A — Sacred Basin and Gateway Country

The first package gives the Qahuri river world and the Gateway coast separate, connected histories. It develops the Twin-Reed dynasties, the mixed Delta house, southern restoration, Qahuri empire and later foreign interventions. The Gateway strand follows coastal settlement, republican government, the three wars with Caleran, the payment revolt, child sacrifice, destruction of Qarzeth, the spared towns and the rise of the Nerethans.

Development status: **approved and promoted to canon, 7 September 2026**. Begin with [[Ancient Qahur and the Twin-Reed Crowns]] and [[The Gateway Republic]], then read [[Qahur and the Gateway before Atheria]] and [[Package A - Sacred Basin and Gateway Canon Integration Review]]. The research shelf remains available through [[Historical Atlas of Ancient Qahur]], [[Historical Atlas of the Gateway Country]] and [[Package A - Sources, Collisions and Canon Review]].

### Package B — Qiryath and the Eastern Empires

This package develops the road houses, tower circuits, protector houses, First Qiryath, Arqeshite conquest, deportation, local survival, Vasharan restoration and eastern wars through 350 BR. The promoted history names the eastern-highland people **Vashari**, their country **Vashar**, their imperial state the **Crown of Roads and Dawns**, and their ruling dynasty **House Tiravan**.

Dependencies: Package A's Qahuri eastern frontier; the canonical dates 742, c. 690 and c. 660 BR; the later Peshara development remains outside this ancient package.

Development status: **approved and promoted to canon, 11 September 2026**. Begin with [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria.md]], [[01 Canon Reference/History/30 Exodus and First Civilizations/Arqesh - River Cities, Kings and Deportation.md]] and [[Vashar and the Crown of Roads and Dawns]], then read [[Eryndor III and the Ortheian Eastern Conquest]], [[Wars of the Inheritance]] and [[The Eastern Sea Wars]]. [[Package B - Qiryath and Eastern Empires Canon Integration Review]] records the final boundaries and source history.

### Package C — Wells, Open Country and Vathra

This package follows Vathra, the Great Unyoking, western wells, eastern herding and hunting societies, Narash, Glassward trade and the loss of Giant horse infrastructure. It establishes proto-Ahdari and Tarkhan relations with Qahur, Arqesh and Qiryath while keeping the Tarkhan Orphaned and the early Ahdari world pre-Covenant.

Dependencies: named routes from Packages A and B; the First Catch remains after 905 AR and cannot be anticipated as ancient horse mastery.

### Package D — Folk, Ättar and the Southern Cold World

This package develops the southern Orphaned populations, regional Folk worlds, halls, agriculture, hunting, trade, Menhir bargains, Grobi pressure and the gradual Ättar divergence. It places the War of the Empty Halls and early Atherian contact in a history with regional agency. The completed Tidewood corpus supplies the standard for detail and voice.

Dependencies: Atherian settlement chronology; Package E's maritime routes; existing Menhir placements.

### Package E — Harbours, Islands and Braided Precursors

This package develops the old island and coastal survivors among whom proto-Atherian and Gateway settlers made new communities. It follows shipbuilding, grain, metals, piracy, mercenary work, port government, mixed religious life and participation in the Gateway and Atherian wars. Sestaran, Vesari and Eclessian identities remain later Braided formations.

Dependencies: Packages A and D; existing Arkenan and Saronikan geography; the later Concord is excluded from the ancient account.

### Package F — Nsizwa and Distant Orphaned Worlds

This package follows the collapse of Giant power on the Nsizwa landmass, local survival, political reassembly, Izivukwa traditions, burial, kinship, technology and regional sovereignties. External contact remains limited to what established history supports. The Last Houses and other uncontacted peoples receive relative histories without invented contact with the core world.

Dependencies: distant-world metaphysical controls and source-specific cultural research. No Mediterranean analogy governs these histories.

### Package G — The World in 350 BR

The concluding package synchronises principal states, rulers, disputed territories, trade, sailing, dynastic marriages, active wars, religious centres and greater-power pressures. It explains the world Thalion could conquer, inherit, negotiate with or fail to reach. This survey becomes the bridge into the established Atherian imperial history.

Dependencies: approved development from Packages A–F and the canonical Atherian history through 350 BR.

## Authority and promotion

Existing cosmology, chronology and explicit canon decisions control this programme. Final collision rulings control the identity of earlier research leads. Source-fidelity rulings preserve their cruelty, guilt and irreversible cost. Human-coherence rulings govern cause and consequence. Superseded proposals remain visible as research history and cannot silently return through a later package.

Ordinary history may be promoted after review. Protected Weirds, true Idol successions, uncertain Titan fates and events after 1360 remain outside ordinary promotion. A functioning supernatural sign proves only the act it performs. It does not make a dynasty righteous, turn a human ruler into a god or identify two powers whose images resemble one another.

## Validation after each package

Each delivery checks the following:

- dates and lifespans agree with the common chronology;
- ancestry remains Ark-derived, Orphaned or Braided according to the history of the population concerned;
- geography is relational and compatible with existing routes;
- supplies and transport make wars and migrations possible;
- neighbouring peoples retain agency and their own names;
- Giant continuities, human souls, Menhir persons, Idol Masks and Patron offices remain distinct;
- no modern ethnicity, confession or state is projected into a period before its formation;
- links, aliases, headings and stable anchors resolve;
- the Atlas validator and whole-vault compatibility audit introduce no new failures.
