# Great-Power Mythic Ecology — Collision and Disposition Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Distant Lands and Otherworld Histories]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Scope

This review tests the twelve great-power beds together before any further nonhuman history is planted. It asks whether each title describes a useful recurring conflict ecology, whether dependent historical episodes remain separate, whether every actor retains the correct continuity, and whether the bed still works without becoming one hidden war behind human history.

The review changes no canonical chronology, named-figure register, culture history, Held Proof, Weird, or seventeen-bed human-company budget. The twelve duplicate umbrella summaries previously copied into [[The Historical Seed Garden]] have been removed so the umbrellas remain research-only. The independently reviewed Patron and Great-Person traces remain in the Garden. No new Garden trace is added here.

## Review Rules

1. An umbrella title may group recurring pressures; it does not prove a continuous war, capital, command, route, dynasty, relic, office or participant list.
2. Two episodes under one umbrella share no person, polity, object, evidence carrier or consequence unless a later dossier independently establishes that connection.
3. Continuity is categorical. A human soul, damned soul, devil office, Commission Husk, Mask, court-being, Giant, Patron role, copied ancestor, Astral person, residue and relic response remain distinct.
4. A Saint or loyal angel acts only under a bounded commission. Resemblance to an abandoned office does not transfer, inherit or revive that office.
5. Collision correction may narrow identity or scope but may not remove a load-bearing assault, murder, sacrifice, slavery, mutilation, torture, coercion, desecration, lethal abandonment or institutional guilt.
6. A reviewed bed remains exploratory until a separate promotion decision changes a Canon Reference note.

## Block I — First-War and Giant-Age Rulings

### GP-I-01 — The War of Broken Standards

The bed survives because its central idea is specific: obsolete affiliation continues to recruit bodies after the original armies and commissions have gone. It needed revision because “remnant” had begun to conceal four distinct metaphysical situations. Broken Standards do not suffer; Commission Husks do not contain abandoned angels; Unhoused Witnesses are new culpable persons; named fallen angels remain exceptional created spirits requiring individual dossiers.

The saint-facing trace is therefore a dangerous classification error by the Husk. A Saint's current commission can resemble part of an ancient instruction, causing the Husk to treat the Saint as a returning bearer. The resemblance grants no office, authority or continuity. The hard edge remains: full-agency persons can be coerced, manifestations can be destroyed, and Giant or human users can be recruited into compulsory battle.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

### GP-I-02 — The Sevenfold Feast War

The Giant cult material needs recurring Idol rivalry, but not a Warhammer-like council of seven co-present gods. The useful authorial truth is that different Masks repeatedly seized different consequences of the same human and Giant institutions. “Sevenfold” is the later historian's completed diagram. One feast may move from Pride to Greed; another marriage system may be fought over by Lust and Envy; a famine cult may never touch all seven.

This retains sacrifice, dynastic rape, mutilation, cannibal feast practice, kin murder and abandonment as historical acts. It also keeps human and Giant perpetrators culpable. The revision removes only the false claim that every atrocity was coordinated by a complete pantheon or constituted a hidden Regnant succession.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

### GP-I-03 — The Hunt of the Escaped Courts

The Hunt is a useful ecology precisely because the fugitives and pursuers do not share one motive. A Menhir court may pursue violated jurisdiction; an Idol proxy may seek feeding infrastructure; a Patron may seek anchor mass; abandoned tenants may seek the ruler that carried away their shelter. Those pressures do not make one hunt, one court or one route.

The Petitions of the Cut-Loose Wards and UH-01 now remain separate local crimes. Petition, ballast and severed-foundation evidence belongs to the former. Decoy itineraries, route carcasses, conflicting copied ancestors and pursuit records belong to the latter. The Last Houses remain mortal Orphaned institutions rather than refugees from either court. An Ossuary Mind may appear only if a separate dossier establishes an accretion and maturity history; it is no longer presumed at the Unbinding.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

## Block II — Scattering, Kings and Stars Rulings

### GP-II-01 — The War of Unfinished Seasons

This bed already contains the required correction: several regional Menhir conjunctions were later compressed into one moralized divine war. The actual pressure begins when abandoned Giant recurrence machinery keeps a season, plague garden, famine cycle or death office running without its old court. A Menhir completes, intensifies, preserves or opposes that process according to natural jurisdiction, not the guilt of the victims.

The reviewed Menhir family may later localize individual conjunctions, but no placement may assume that Veradia, Solrath, Morvalis, Nevaura or any other Menhir all met in one council or war. Mortal plague, starvation, exposure and landscape loss remain explicit.

**Final disposition:** `retain`.

### GP-II-02 — The War of the Last Map

UH-03, the Ninth Bastion, the Chain-Bearer's recurrence and the Saint of Ashen Wings all touch this ecology without becoming one campaign. Hell's map-lords need stable refusal, named boundaries and repeatable offices. Abyssal wounds destroy those distinctions. Each local tyrant therefore contains and weaponizes a contradiction while claiming to defend order.

The review makes two boundaries explicit. A damned human appointed after death still possesses one judged `human-soul`; its devil mantle remains a separate `infrastructure-office`. A Saint or angel does not contest ownership of a sealed wound with a devil marshal. A heavenly commission may instead protect named prisoners, close unlawful soul interception or preserve retreat, then withdraw.

The enslaved boundary-prisoners, mutilation, compulsory office-bearing and erased dominions remain intact. Neither infernal side becomes good or necessary to creation.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

### GP-II-03 — The Crown-Market of Shadows

The central grammar is sound: mortal claimants choose acts that let Masks and Patron claimants sell partial kingship. The collision arose when a First-Kings institution became the implied continuous home of the much later Glass Court. The revised bed treats “Crown-Market” as a scholarly family name. Local rites may share language or copied procedure without sharing an owner, route, demiplane or office-persona.

GS-11's Glass Court is one later imitation or recurrence. It is not the capital or surviving government of every ancient coronation bargain. Kin murder, spouse seizure, execution, archival erasure and sale of dependants remain the ruler's culpable acts rather than a Mask's excuse.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

## Block III — Martyrs, Closure and Frontier Rulings

### GP-III-01 — The Siege of the Martyrs' Names

This is already a distributed struggle over destroyed or confused bodies, names, graves, testimony and public memory. GS-01, the prison-road tradition and any later relic case remain local histories with independent evidence. The Last Houses' ancestral polity remains categorically different: civic transmission of mortal memory cannot make a Saint.

The bed preserves torture, informing, recantation, execution, corpse desecration, grave theft and retaliatory violence. A genuine manifestation, if commissioned, remains one redeemed person and may contradict the politically useful Life rather than perfect it.

**Final disposition:** `retain`.

### GP-III-02 — The War of the Closed Doors

The umbrella remains useful because one temple closure can simultaneously expose fraud, starve a Mask, violate a real Menhir compact, displace workers, release an office and expose anchor mass. Its local episodes must nevertheless inventory those functions separately. The Second-Gospel Preacher, Broken-Hands Smith and Sealed-Door Reader do not belong to one organization merely because their later histories cross this ecology.

The hard edge remains killing, smashed sanctuaries, forced conformity, looting and retaliatory fire. The correction concerns mechanism: “court-beings take bodies” was too vague. A court-being must use its own manifestation, an altered creature, a contracted proxy or a separately described coerced host. It does not become the human body or absorb the host's soul and guilt.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

### GP-III-03 — The Long Rent War

This bed has accumulated the most dependants: six Patron polity placements, several Great Saints and Sinners, two breachward traditions and the Last Houses all touch frontier route politics. Treating them as one war would create exactly the secret organization the programme forbids.

“Long Rent War” is now a later juristic umbrella for overlapping foreclosures, debt purchases, route seizures, anchor withdrawals and refuge failures. Every local case must identify its owner, speaker, contract, witness standing, upkeep mechanism, victims and route. No shared Creditor, capital or original default is presumed. Clear contracts may still kill nonsignatories, strand families, repossess leased bodies or memories and impose hereditary service.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

## Block IV — Split, Plague and Present Rulings

### GP-IV-01 — The War for the Common Saints

The bed already preserves the necessary distinction between one Saint and the many institutions, relics, images, counterfeits and contracts using that name. UH-06's landlocked enemy gate is not the Two Ferries, SB-P04 or any other common-saint dispute. The Judge Beneath the Black Eaves may exploit this ecology without determining the Saint's earthly identity or commission.

Imprisonment, beating, mob killing, shrine seizure and corpse or relic desecration remain human acts. The genuine Saint, if commissioned, may protect the people each church least wants protected and then withdraw without settling the Split.

**Final disposition:** `retain`.

### GP-IV-02 — The Ossuary Crown Dispute

Canonical Ossuary Minds become full-agency `astral-pattern` persons once mature. The prior card called this Mind a new person while leaving its agency open, which made the central dispute incoherent. The corrected version fixes that the Mind has matured by the time rival powers try to crown it. It is not the plague dead, their collective soul, their resurrection, their office or their archive.

The real uncertainty now lies elsewhere: whether the salvage crown is the Mind's coherence core or merely a manifestation; whether any human soul is separately trapped among the wreckage; which remains belong on Material ground; and whether destroying the crown would actually kill the new person. Plague bodies, abandoned kin, mass graves, carrier executions, grave theft and coerced memory remain intact. The Quiet Ninth remains unexplained.

**Final disposition:** `revise`, incorporated.

### GP-IV-03 — The Proof That Attracted Three Heavens

This remains a bounded present pressure around one of the eight existing Held Proofs. “Three heavens” is frightened folklore for three incompatible supernatural claimants, not a cosmological claim. Each claimant may know one truth while converting it into a false right of ownership.

The bed adds no ninth proof, guaranteed apparition, predetermined atrocity or post-1360 outcome. Its human work remains custody, testimony, reproducible testing, refusal and repair. A Saint or angel appears only if separately commissioned.

**Final disposition:** `retain`.
