# The Two Fissions — Canon Promotion Review

## Promotion Ruling

On 2026-08-31, the author approved the reviewed demographic skeleton in [[The Two Fissions and the Atherian Long Formation]] after the dispositions recorded in [[The Two Fissions - Collision and Disposition Review]]. This pass promotes the eight macro-history rulings into controlling canon. It does not promote any of the twelve stories in [[The Two Fissions - Twelve Nursery Hooks]].

The promotion changes sequence, institutional descent and early contact. It does not fix a founder, personal name, exact route, complete office list, relic, local war, first city, first tower, first well coalition or protected Weird.

## Promoted Macro Rulings

| ID | Promoted ruling | Canon effect |
|---|---|---|
| MF-01 | The Prophet leaves no universal human successor. | Commanders, judges, archivists, provisioners and household elders retain bounded work without inheriting his commission. |
| MF-02 | The first fission is gradual. | The failed common sanctuary and c. 2130–2050 BR southern separation replace an immediate three-way partition. |
| MF-03 | A later northern fission produces two formations. | The Near-Glass continuum divides c. 1950–1750 BR into proto-Jirahar and proto-Ahl-e-Ahd responses under geographic and reciprocal Tarkhan pressure. |
| MF-04 | Southern settlement is hard mixed conquest. | Massacre, slavery, hostages, expulsion and land seizure coexist with treaty, defection, adoption, intermarriage, local victory and failed Atherian forts. |
| MF-05R | Heroic-maritime recovery is plural. | Several connected recovery horizons and regional breakdowns replace one migration people, one Bronze Age or one universal collapse. |
| MF-06 | Atherian identity changes basis four times. | War-settlement, heroic-maritime, city-civilizational and republican-imperial membership cannot be treated as one pure lineage. |
| MF-07 | Ark, Orphaned and Braided classifications remain distinct. | Folk, Ättar, Tarkhan and Nsizwa remain Orphaned; Sestaran harbors, Vesari and later Eclessians remain Braided. |
| MF-08 | Successor inheritance is topological, not biological. | Thalmyric and Eclessian cultural registers arise through later regional, imperial, ecclesial and demographic inheritance. |

## Principal Canon Reconciliation

The promotion revises:

- [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]] and [[Historical Spine of the Known World]];
- [[The Glass Sojourn]], [[Age of Exodus]], [[The Great Unbinding]], [[Age of Scattering]], [[Ancient and Early Historic Ages]] and [[Age of Hearths and First Kings]];
- [[Proto-Jirahar Tower Towns]], [[The Making of the Jirahar]] and [[Historical Spine of the Jirahar]];
- [[Proto-Ahl-e-Ahd Desert Tribes]] and [[The Making of the Covenant Peoples]];
- [[The First Atherian City-States]] and [[The Rise of the Atherian Empire Overview]];
- early Folk, Ättar and Sestaran contact histories;
- the Eclessian successor boundary and the Scattering pressure attached to [[The Historical Seed Garden|the Seven Who Took the Wrong Road]].

[[Real-History Concordance]] now records the mixed maritime grammar as raiders, migrants, mercenaries, settlers and captive economies rather than one ethnic Sea Peoples analogue.

## Withheld Nursery Layer

TF-01 through TF-12 remain author-only nursery material. Their dispositions establish usability, not historical existence. In particular, promotion does not establish:

- an Empty Seat, Five Staffs or a complete post-Prophet constitution;
- a first southern gate, hostage cohort or twice-founded harbor;
- a Furnace League, three-law well, walking court or wall community;
- a Dry-Road bride, Grobi-facing hall, Oar-King or Caleran archive crime.

No Garden bed, named-figure register, relic pedigree or local chronology receives those particulars through this pass.
