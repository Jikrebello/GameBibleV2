# 350 BR to 0 AR — Imperial Expansion Canon Constraint Sheet

> [!warning] Author-only working sheet
> This is the first audit and endpoint proposal for the empire-wide expansion pass. **Fixed canon**, **creator direction**, and **proposed working targets** are identified separately. No campaign, annexation date, provincial border or new imperial maximum is canonised by this note.

**Creator direction selected after the first audit:** develop the southern-provinces-first route with a continuous Sestaran/Elmaric naval network. Keep Vashar an independent eastern power at 0 AR; place the principal Atherian–Vasharan wars **after 0 AR but before the Ahl-e-Ahd rise beginning in 541 AR**. Orent's established pre-0 eastern campaigns remain, but they should not be recast as a war that destroys Vashar. See [[350 BR to 0 AR - Three Imperial Expansion Routes]].

## The question this pass must answer

How did the state Thalion consolidated in 350 BR become the geographically large, institutionally mature empire in which Isfir was born in 33 BR, preached from about 3 BR and was executed at Caleran in 0 AR? The creator wants expansion across the Sacred Basin, the Sestaran/Elmaric sea, eastern riverlands and broad southern provincial country, with defensible northern approaches, reaching the empire's full territorial breadth. The comparison with Roman Egypt and Judaea is **structural inspiration**; the causes, alliances, supply routes and settlements must arise from World Egg's established peoples and geography.

The [[01 Canon Reference/History/10 Chronology and Control/Real-History Concordance.md|Real-History Concordance]] explicitly assigns Qahur a long Egypt-inspired river history while giving **Delkos**, not Qahur, the strongest Ptolemaic court grammar. It also requires transformed historical dynamics rather than one-to-one identities.

## Fixed chronology and imperial framework

The [[01 Canon Reference/History/10 Chronology and Control/Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating).md|authoritative chronology]], [[01 Canon Reference/History/10 Chronology and Control/Age of Glory - Authorial History and Integration Review.md|Age of Glory integration review]] and [[01 Canon Reference/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/Historical Atlas of the Atherian Age of Glory.md|Age of Glory atlas]] fix the following anchors. Expansion must fit them rather than replace the dynasties.

| Date | Fixed event or condition |
|---|---|
| 350 BR | Thalion annexes Sarqen and consolidates imperial government at Caleran. Qarzeth's capital had already been destroyed around 520 BR; the Gateway towns and people survived. |
| 350–318 BR | Thalion's settlement and later reign. |
| 318–247 BR | House of Thalion; Four Eagles civil war follows in 247–246 BR. |
| 246–151 BR | House of Veloran. Orent I's eastern campaigns and the Arcelon campaign/road around 190–182 BR are established; new eastern sequencing must account for them. |
| 151–61 BR | Serrat house. Raveth is an eastern imperial river province by its famine of 119–113 BR. |
| 61–42 BR | Doran I; his southern veteran settlements displace existing households. |
| 42–24 BR | Othran I. Isfir is born in **33 BR** in a Jirahar tower town of the eastern provinces. The canon does not name his birth town as Qiryath. |
| 24 BR–0 AR | Othran II reigns at the boundary. Isfir's public ministry begins around 3 BR. |
| 0 AR | Atherian authority sentences and executes Isfir at Caleran after a controlling Qiryath council faction presses for his death. He rises on the third night. Imperial Star religion is still public; conversion lies centuries ahead. |
| 66–73 AR | The Great Tower Revolt and destruction of Second Qiryath occur **after** Isfir. |

The imperial age continues to 905 AR. Canon describes weakening after about 400 AR and major losses during the First Unsealing of 568–612 AR, but the sources checked here do **not** explicitly fix the date of Atheria's all-time greatest territorial extent. The creator's present direction supplies a **working target** of full imperial breadth by 0 AR. Its precise high-water claim needs review against post-0 history before canon promotion. The creator now reserves the major wars with independent Vashar for 0–540 AR; if 0 AR is the strict all-time territorial maximum, those wars must not create a larger durable imperial province beyond that boundary.

## Proposed territorial architecture at 0 AR

This is the **proposed endpoint to test**, not a record of conquests already dated in canon. "Direct" means an imperial governor or recognised provincial administration can assess revenue and enforce decisions locally; it does not erase municipal, temple or communal law.

|---|---|---|
| Aurin/Caleran core, established Atherian-speaking cities and Sarqen/Gateway cultivated towns | Direct imperial rule, though cities keep unlike charters and old local households. | No revived Qarzethi republic; no assumption that every culturally Atherian harbour joined at the same time. |
| Ortheian uplands and Delkosi harbours | Proposed direct rule over the main upland-to-sea routes and principal harbours by 0 AR, after a separately explained settlement of the independent Ortheian crown and Delkosi city governments. | Do not infer early Caleran ownership from Atherian language or erase older Ortheian kings and mixed Delkosi households. |

Jirahar communities outside the province retain their own host relationships. |

This endpoint gives the empire a large, governable central belt without equating the edge of imperial influence with a surveyed border. The southern/eastern proposal follows [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Eclessia/10 Foundations/The Making of the Eclessians.md|The Making of the Eclessians]], which names an old eastern provincial base and Folk communities ruled unevenly by Atheria, and [[01 Canon Reference/Peoples and Cultures/Folk/70 History and Conflicts/Historical Spine of the Folk.md|the Folk historical spine]], which preserves autonomous old-rite territory beyond consistent rule. Exact districts and the period of maximum area are still review choices.

## The 33 BR and 0 AR tests

1. **Birth setting, 33 BR — fixed:** Othran I reigns. Isfir is born in a Jirahar tower town in the eastern provinces; road, caravan, sanctuary, craft and legal networks already exist. The precise tower town and the date of Qiryath's own incorporation remain open.
2. **Qahur, 33 BR — proposed:** It should already be a major Atherian provincial possession, following the Roman-Egypt structural inspiration chosen by the creator. The Egyptian/Ptolemaic sequence does not determine its dynasty or conquest mechanism; Qahur begins this period with three living crowns and Vasharan involvement.
3. **Ministry, c. 3 BR–0 AR — fixed:** Imperial roads and ports permit movement. Jirahar law and sacrifice continue, while imperial Star theology and local authorities give Isfir distinct institutions to confront. No Redeemer-faith public empire exists yet.
4. **Execution, 0 AR — fixed:** A specific senior Qiryath council faction seeks an imperial execution it cannot impose itself. High Augur Severan and the court choose the sentence; imperial soldiers execute Isfir at Caleran. Atherian authority over Qiryath must have an intelligible legal and political history before this point.
5. **Greatest extent — working target:** At or by 0 AR the empire should have its largest durable network of provinces, protected ports and controlled corridors. A client or treaty partner should be labelled as such rather than counted as annexed land. Post-0 canon must be checked before asserting a unique all-time territorial maximum.

## Present-day inheritance is a check, not the 0 AR map

[[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR).md|The 1360 AR court survey]] and [[01 Canon Reference/History/70 Later History and Present Day/Present-Day Political Board.md|political board]] show descendants and later holders of old imperial lands: Thalmyria in the west-central heartland; Eclessia across much eastern provincial country; the independent Sestaran cities; Covenant-held Al-Qahur, Zanthir and the Velian belt; and living Folk, Ättar, Jirahar and Tarkhan worlds. Their current confessions, rulers and borders cannot be backdated. The [[01 Canon Reference/Geography/World Map/Political Map Border Proof Key.md|political border proof]] is still draft and cannot fix ancient coastlines or boundaries.

## Decisions and checks before a campaign sequence

1. **Territorial high-water mark:** test the proposed 0 AR apex against the 0–400 AR imperial history. The sources read here establish later weakening, but no explicit maximum date. If a later expansion is required, define the 0 AR empire as the mature pre-conversion state and reserve the later gain rather than silently contradicting it.
2. **Qahur's minimum outcome:** confirm the proposed 33 BR provincial condition and determine whether all three historic crown regions or a large, continuous cultivated core must be under direct rule by then. The annexation date, local collaborators and Vasharan response are for the sequence pass.
3. **Eastern sovereignty:** determine when Atheria holds Qiryath and the Raveth/Arcelon and western river corridors while Vashar remains sovereign. Reserve the principal Atherian–Vasharan wars for the post-0, pre-541 AR programme; do not turn the Arcelon campaign into Vashar's collapse.
4. **Control vocabulary:** use province, charter city, client, allied harbour, garrison corridor, tribute and trade access consistently. Draw the maximum from the first category and defensible occupied corridors, not from every place receiving an imperial envoy.
5. **Cost and continuity:** each chosen advance must explain food, fleets, roads, local partners, coercion, resistance and durable administration, then pass the 33 BR and 0 AR tests above.

This sheet stops before assigning new conquest dates or rulers to particular campaigns. Its next companion should be a synchronized regional sequence with alternative settlements where a genuine authorial choice remains.
