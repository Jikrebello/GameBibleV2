# Great Saints and Sinners — Chronological Placement Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Great Saints and Sinners - Lives and Afterlives]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Review Scope

This ledger reviews the fourteen visible placements and two erased-person mysteries in [[Great Saints and Sinners - Historical Placement and Relationship Matrix]]. Review proceeds earth-first. A mortal life must possess a coherent cause, decision, cost and historical residue before its later Saintly commission or Sinner project is admitted.

The review does not name a person, fix a locality, assign an existing order, alter chronology, authenticate a present apparition or determine either erased person's judgement. An incorporated `revise` is a passing disposition, not a rejection.

[[Great Saints and Sinners - Primarch Source-Fidelity Hardening Review]] subsequently restores nine source wounds that this placement review had described too softly. The revised causal chains below incorporate those restorations without changing any disposition.

## Review Interface

Every ruling tests:

1. chronological and cultural fit;
2. cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence;
3. separation from named figures, companies, orders and prior seeds;
4. source hardness and World Egg transformation;
5. soul, commission, office, Mask, Patron, relic, residue and copy boundaries;
6. independent evidence and 1360 discoverability;
7. justified mortal or posthumous contact for rivalries;
8. whether the proposed Garden trace remains useful if every supernatural interpretation is false.

# Group I — Underground and Conversion

## GS-01 — Witness at the Red Passage

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** the Underground supports prisons, burial names and martyr-family memory, but the earlier river-port wording pressed too close to the Three-Lock Prison Road and Saint of the Two Ferries.
- **Causal chain:** a porter abandons one yard during a riot → the escape cell murders captives and innocent prisoners → the porter hides the killings to preserve the network → guilt produces years of service and an attempt to restore the names → a later purge traps households at one service passage → the porter knowingly holds it, is tortured and killed → later cults suppress both the abandonment and the network's murders while repeating retaliatory killing.
- **Collision ruling:** move the earthly core to one inland prison manufactory and its transfer courtyard. It is not a road network, ferry tradition, apostolic prison or founder of later visitation systems.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** the redeemed soul is distinct from red-door cults, herald figures and rage residue. Encounters with GS-06 and GS-11 occur only through later bounded commissions.
- **Discoverability and trace:** prison lists, courtyard fabric, letters and hostile trial minutes corroborate the mortal core. An unhinged red door and name-reading custom remain useful even if every later apparition is false.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — incorporate the inland single-prison boundary and retain the abandonment, service, execution, cult violence and bounded later manifestation.

## GS-02 — Preacher of the Second Gospel

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** post-legalisation temple closure and displaced workers provide a distinct setting for a charismatic preacher without adding a Companion, apostle or Mujalib analogue.
- **Causal chain:** a temple copyist joins charitable congregations → successful preaching becomes coerced private revelation → a justified disciplinary removal is interpreted as persecution → the preacher consults responsive hostile powers → the Preacher presides over captive sacrifice, orders a child possession trial and has the disciplinary witness murdered → the counter-sacramental church repeats those acts as proof that effective power is true.
- **Collision ruling:** unlike Mujalib, the Preacher knowingly receives contradictory hostile answers and freely treats effective power as worthy. Unlike Aquilan, correction does not initiate repentance.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** the damned soul, Second Gospel, heresiarch office, speaking copies and cults remain separate. Later contests with GS-07 and GS-14 do not require mortal contemporaneity.
- **Discoverability and trace:** disciplinary records, shared early sermons, murder correspondence and sacrificial remains establish the becoming independently of infernal recensions. Hospital use of one harmless fragment does not authenticate the counter-church.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

# Group II — Early Long Frontier

## GS-04 — Mason of the Ninth Bastion

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** early frontier quarry works and disposable prison labour provide a human origin for later infernal fortress politics without creating another Patron city.
- **Causal chain:** a measurer saves a town → rulers exploit the talent while honouring patrons → resentment becomes a doctrine of necessary sacrifice → the Mason decimates surviving work crews after sabotage → evacuation is shortened to preserve a perfect foundation → workers and prisoners die sealed inside → the Mason refuses guilt and later accepts an office that turns repeated casualty architecture into route jurisdiction.
- **Collision ruling:** the Ninth Bastion is one historical project; the later linked works are a bounded operation within the War of the Last Map, not the map of Hell or a World Egg version of the Infinite Citadel in total.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** the human soul, infrastructure office, command-body, fortress mandate and any Patron contribution remain separate. GS-12 contests one link only.
- **Discoverability and trace:** death rolls, plans, worker graffiti and casualty-chamber route failure establish mechanism. Sealed ninth rooms remain compelling as fraud, ordinary atrocity or genuine office geometry.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## GS-06 — Chain-Bearer of Sarrat

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** slavery, quarry war and portable breach courts belong to the Long Frontier's coercive legal economy without making one culture uniquely guilty.
- **Causal chain:** a slave healer receives an imposed pain engine → captives organise revolt → authorities remove the healer from the companions' final stand → later command turns pain into entitlement → surrendering enemies and a neutral household are murdered → the commander refuses removal and chooses an infernal recurrence office.
- **Collision ruling:** the figure is neither a Wrath Mask, Titan nor generic patron of slave revolt. The fixed chain-of-custody recurrence is an infernal rule, not an Idol cycle.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** imposed machine, damaged mortal body, damned soul, office and recurrence bodies remain categorical. GS-01 opposes one prisoner transfer after both mortal histories are complete.
- **Discoverability and trace:** infirmary tablets, imitator scars, revolt testimony, graves and repeated custody formula corroborate separate phases. Broken-chain tokens retain abolitionist meaning even if recurrence stories are denied.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## GS-07 — Clerk of a Hundred Wells

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** Banu Mizan-era municipal water law fits the period, but the current formulation risked making the Clerk a fifth member or hidden source of the Four Who Brought Water.
- **Causal chain:** a low clerk records elite seizure during drought → a common register prevents immediate deaths → the Clerk signs armed seizures and an emergency procedure that kill defenders, labourers and a petitioner → confiscation and labour become permanent dependency → survivors challenge the peacetime continuation → the Clerk restores hearings and adds expiry clauses → later states preserve the powers and omit the ending.
- **Collision ruling:** the “hundred wells” are an administrative district, not the repaired well of B3-12. The Clerk neither meets nor inherits from that company; the episode occurs in another municipality and legal generation.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** seal, manual, office and Saint remain distinct. GS-08 is a mortal policy opponent; GS-02 is encountered only in later active history.
- **Discoverability and trace:** layered registers, dispossession petitions and revised clauses establish the history without a miracle. “The Clerk's ending” works as a legal phrase even if the Saint claim is false.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — incorporate the separate-district and no-descent rulings; retain emergency success, coercive afterlife, restitution and bounded later correction.

## GS-08 — Bitter Reaper

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** poison estates, quarantine farms and caravan clinics fit frontier labour and medical pressures without touching the later Grey Sweat's cause.
- **Causal chain:** a corpse carrier learns practical medicine → workers overthrow elevated poison lords → the liberator mandates exposure rites → deaths and treatment dependency follow → refusal loses water and burial → during an uncaused outbreak the Reaper knowingly makes permanent control the price of refuge and accepts damnation.
- **Collision ruling:** this is neither B3-13's wandering physician nor a hospital saint. Material medicine remains useful; dependency, not healing, is the chosen leftward mechanism.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** disease, medicine, clinic, dependency contract, incarnation and damned soul remain separate. GS-07's mortal dispute concerns permanent emergency power.
- **Discoverability and trace:** estate plans, burials, prescriptions and correspondence establish the human history. The bitter cordial can be medically effective without validating forced exposure or the Sinner cult.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

# Group III — Late Long Frontier

## GS-03 — Hunter of the Shutwood

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** mixed forest jurisdictions suit hostage guides and route intelligence, but the route-hearing custom must not prove descent from the Hunter or rewrite the Long Watch.
- **Causal chain:** a hostage-guide becomes a secretive commander → infiltration fear produces one lawful execution and one unlawful sentence → an innocent scout demands an open hearing → the Hunter kills the scout personally and falsifies all three deaths as one traitor case → exposure forces release of records and resignation → families of collaborators and victims are evacuated together → later guides turn the scandal into a hearing custom.
- **Collision ruling:** the hearing fellowship postdates the Hunter and merely claims the founder. No present order is an authenticated institutional heir.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** forest correspondence is neither Menhir identity nor private Saintly domain. GS-13 is encountered in one later threshold commission.
- **Discoverability and trace:** execution orders, coerced testimony, graves and evacuation registers establish the mortal core. The empty hearing-place criticises secret judgement even if the founder attribution is false.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — make the custom later and non-genealogical; retain wrongful execution, resignation, rescue and bounded manifestation.

## GS-05 — Smith Who Broke His Hands

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** later frontier foundries supply a distinct craft-and-disability history separate from Saintmaker's Forge and the Iron Martyrs.
- **Causal chain:** a bondservant survives lethal heat → success becomes a doctrine that weakness deserves exposure → an apprentice rings the stop bell but the Smith personally holds the test shut until the apprentice dies → maimed witnesses are expelled → the Smith breaks the famous gauntlets, confesses and funds restitution → adherents attack the reformed workshop → a later order preserves the discarded doctrine and requires correction.
- **Collision ruling:** this is one workshop family and one later strength-order, not the source of all forge-convents or holy smithing.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** gauntlets, tools, workshop persona, order and redeemed soul remain separate. The Glass Court conflict occurs only through a later commission.
- **Discoverability and trace:** guild roll, restitution contracts, adaptive tools and two workshop rules establish reform without needing the apparition. Rival gauntlets cannot invalidate the charter.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## GS-09 — Reader Behind the Sealed Door

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** the breach-warning problem fits the later frontier, but an underpaid textual corrector was too close to the Fifth Translation and Open Cinder.
- **Causal chain:** a low maintenance reader detects pressure through heat, sound and seal distortion → custodians accept the danger but forbid the demonstration that would complete the path → a preliminary test maims a student → the Reader conceals the injury and overrules students who withdraw consent → coerced receivers complete the forbidden address → the warning arrives and the route opens → students, households and custodians die → the Reader makes rejection, rather than the prohibited act, the centre of the story and chooses a Sinner office.
- **Collision ruling:** the Reader is an aperture diagnostician, not a translator, Fifth Translation member or Open Cinder founder. Later schools steal observations without common descent.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** copies preserve selected memories but never soul fragments. GS-10 is a mortal friend and later bounded opponent.
- **Discoverability and trace:** maintenance logs, seal geometry, death rolls and incompatible copies establish cause. An unread warning remains dangerous evidence without proving its alleged author.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — replace textual-copy origin with low-status aperture maintenance and retain the knowing demonstration, deaths, refusal and warning office.

## GS-10 — Smith of the Refuge Bell

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** mobile refuge workshops fit the later frontier, but the exact nine-tool/five-recovered structure remained too visibly dependent upon Vulkan's source surface.
- **Causal chain:** a refugee repairer gains military patronage → a heat weapon is built for an allegedly empty approach → the first discharge reveals civilians → the Smith personally authorises and fires a second discharge rather than risk an enemy breakout → civilians burn with fighters and commanders conceal the order → the Smith testifies, dismantles copies and builds evacuation tools → patrons retaliate against workshops → a later commission activates duties encoded in particular tools during one closure.
- **Collision ruling:** there is no authoritative tool count, collection quest or relic-triggered return. Different communities organise unrelated tools into changing devotional sets.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** tool response, craft tradition, relic claim, commission-body and redeemed soul remain separate. GS-09 begins as a mortal friend.
- **Discoverability and trace:** commission, burn pattern, testimony and duty marks establish the history. Tools remain useful obligations even if no manifestation occurred.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — remove the number nine and recovery tally; retain culpable invention, restitution, refugee craft and bounded closure commission.

## ER-01 — Institutionally Erased Person

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** late-frontier command redistribution fits competing states and orders without requiring a central imperial memory purge.
- **Causal chain:** a catastrophe leaves useful trained followers and politically dangerous testimony → a human coalition removes one name → practices and personnel are redistributed → witnesses are imprisoned, coerced or executed → later institutions inherit identical exceptions without an acknowledged source.
- **Collision ruling:** not Ischar, an erased Companion, hidden order-founder or explanation of the Long Frontier's end.
- **Continuity:** censorship affects records and social standing, never the human soul or Heaven's knowledge.
- **Discoverability:** ration sequences, recut monuments, exemptions and a hostile sentence prove coordinated suppression but cannot recover identity or judgement.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; no Garden promotion in this pass.

## ER-02 — Supernaturally Name-Wounded Person

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** a bounded attack on identity carriers fits the Long Rent or Closed-Door ecology, but must not become a universal memory plague.
- **Causal chain:** a hostile operation targets one person's public identity → names, portraits, testimony and route signatures fail differently → institutions punish inconsistent witnesses and families lose rights → later copies, personas, Masks and frauds occupy the vacancy → investigators can establish attack-pattern but not attacker or original identity.
- **Collision ruling:** limit damage to carriers participating in one historical network. Unconnected records may be missing ordinarily; the phenomenon explains no Weird and no other erased figure.
- **Continuity:** the human soul remains complete. True name, social identity, copied memory and claimant are not the person.
- **Discoverability:** two independently damaged carrier classes plus one predating material consequence establish the core attack.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — incorporate the bounded-network limit; no Garden promotion in this pass.

# Group IV — Fall through High Noon

## GS-11 — General of the Glass Court

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** a refugee court and reconquest army can host aesthetic cleansing without duplicating the Six at the Cinder Inn or making the figure an imperial founder.
- **Causal chain:** a camp tailor earns trust through material competence → logistics and courage produce command → victory becomes aesthetic cleansing → mixed families and damaged veterans are removed → a loyal critic confronts the General over sexual abuse, theft and cleansing → the General personally murders the critic and fabricates a field death → the General chooses a court of perfected replacements and is damned.
- **Collision ruling:** the repeated Glass signature belongs to restoration medium and contract practice, not proof that every altered portrait came from one supernatural court. No Pride succession is implied.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** court, Patron support, dependent Masks, copies, perfected bodies and damned soul remain separate. GS-01 and GS-05 are later opponents only.
- **Discoverability and trace:** accounts, assault orders, correspondence, missing remains and restoration layers establish revision and possible court contact without authenticating any returner.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — narrow the portrait signature and retain the craft ascent, cleansing, sexual abuse of office, personal murder and fabricated field death, chosen court and damnation.

## GS-12 — Warden of the Last Redoubt

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** one provincial redoubt fits early Reconquest without duplicating Caedros I, but a genuine posthumous manifestation requires an authorially dead person.
- **Causal chain:** a refugee quartermaster becomes siege commander → an outer ward is sealed before evacuation completes → the inner fort survives while civilians and relief troops die → grief becomes wasteful punitive assaults → confession and later evacuation reforms follow → the Warden dies, while recovery and public death accounts remain contradictory.
- **Collision ruling:** authorial bodily death is fixed; only location, remains and public knowledge stay uncertain. The later defence proves one commission, not imperial legitimacy.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** remains, hand claims, measuring cord, visions and commission-body remain distinct from the redeemed soul. GS-04 is opposed in one later fortress event.
- **Discoverability and trace:** repair rolls, excluded names, casualty patterns and incompatible inventories establish the mortal history. The measuring cord supports refugee craft without proving sainthood.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — fix bodily death authorially and retain contradictory recovery, confession, redemption and bounded defence.

## GS-13 — Judge Beneath the Black Eaves

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** courts divided after the Great Split plausibly create space for predictive terror without reducing the Split to one Sinner.
- **Causal chain:** a warrant runner receives conditional visions → successful prevention creates authority → the Judge punishes future acts → destroyed relationships make feared crimes more likely → torture and collective execution become policy → the Judge permits assassination to present death as proof of inevitability.
- **Collision ruling:** neither crown, prophecy nor fear Mask contains the soul. The figure is not a neutral prophet corrupted only by followers.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** conditional visions preserve freedom; crown, office, residue, Mask and damned soul remain distinct. GS-03's later rescue is bounded to marked families.
- **Discoverability and trace:** pre-dated warrants, contradictory survivors, courthouse fabric and assassin testimony establish terror policy. “The future fact” remains a sound legal prohibition without proving the forecasts supernatural.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## GS-14 — Saint of Ashen Wings

- **Chronological and cultural fit:** High-Noon mines and prison estates support a liberation story outside Ansgar, the Reconquest and existing breachward founders.
- **Causal chain:** a slave courier helps lead revolt → threatened settlements authorise an experimental escape → the commander uses prisoners without consent → altered survivors are killed and the experiment concealed → confession, surrender and exile follow → later rescue work ends in death and redemption → bounded commissions target specific Second-Gospel routes.
- **Collision ruling:** not a founder of every covert order or permanent celestial hunter. Ashen wings signify the commission rather than corruption.
- **Continuity and rivalry:** earthly guilt, liberation persona, victim roll, commission-body and Saint remain distinct. GS-02 is encountered only under named later commissions.
- **Discoverability and trace:** courier marks, prisoner allocations, remains and victim-family confession copies establish the history. The paired feast remains morally useful even if the apparition tradition is false.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.
