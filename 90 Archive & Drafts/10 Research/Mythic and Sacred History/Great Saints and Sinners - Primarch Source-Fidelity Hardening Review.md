# Great Saints and Sinners — Primarch Source-Fidelity Hardening Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Great Saints and Sinners - Lives and Afterlives]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Controlling Ruling

This pass tests the fourteen visible exemplars and two erased-person mysteries against the particular Primarch function each borrowed. It restores the source's load-bearing guilt, bodily consequence, betrayal or institutional corruption where the World Egg translation had shifted responsibility onto unnamed followers or softened an atrocity into an administrative error.

Hardening does **not** mean importing every Primarch atrocity, matching body counts, reproducing a Legion or Heresy, or treating brutality as proof of importance. It means preserving parity in five places:

1. **severity:** the borrowed wound remains grave enough to cause the later life;
2. **proximity:** the wound reaches the principal person's own household, companions, subjects or victims;
3. **culpability:** where the source figure chooses the act, the translated person chooses it too;
4. **irreversibility:** repentance, good intentions and later miracles do not resurrect the dead or cancel victims' claims;
5. **institutional afterlife:** followers can inherit the founder's uncorrected wound, suppress it or turn it into doctrine.

Direct terms such as *murder*, *execution*, *massacre*, *torture*, *sacrifice* and *child death* are used where they describe the event. Anatomical spectacle is not required. A future Saint's culpable acts belong to the earthly life before final repentance, death, judgement and communion; a Saint does not fall after communion. Trauma and supernatural pressure can explain a future Sinner's choices but never make those choices morally involuntary.

# Individual Rulings

## GS-01 — The Witness at the Red Passage

- **Received source function:** Sanguinius as beloved doomed defender, the concealed violence within his own household, followers who kill innocents under the martyr's image, and uncertain posthumous heralds.
- **Original hard edge:** the founder knows that the household's rage can murder the undeserving and helps conceal it because exposure may destroy the whole body.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. The Witness abandoned prisoners, but the later rage killing belonged only to admirers whom the person never knew.
- **Restoration:** during the transfer crisis, members of the escape cell lose control and murder bound guards, an informer already in custody and two prisoners wrongly believed to be collaborators. The porter prevents the killings from entering the escape register to protect the network. Later years of service include an unsuccessful attempt to restore the dead to the record before the final defence. The authentic Saint condemns the later cult precisely because it repeats the sin once concealed.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; later cult versions suppress the murdered prisoners or call every victim a traitor.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. This is not a genetic curse, substitute Passion or proof that the martyr apparition is authentic.

## GS-02 — The Preacher of the Second Gospel

- **Received source function:** Lorgar's rejected worship, responsive evil, authored counter-revelation and deliberate conversion of pastoral authority into damnation.
- **Original hard edge:** forced conversion, sacrifice, corruption of trusted companions and a leader who authors the system rather than merely attracting violent followers.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. Initiates performed the sacrifices and trials while the Preacher's own part remained grammatical.
- **Restoration:** the Preacher personally presides over the first captive sacrifice, orders that a child be used in the first possession trial after another teacher refuses, and orders the former companion killed to obtain the disciplinary record. These are chosen proofs of the doctrine that effective response establishes truth.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; hostile trial records, counter-church Lives and later infernal copies disagree over whether the Preacher wielded the knife but not over authorship and command.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. Just Church discipline remains neither torture nor the cause of the apostasy.

## GS-03 — The Hunter of the Shutwood

- **Received source function:** Lion El'Jonson as secretive knight-commander whose distrust wounds loyal followers and whose later mercy cannot make the broken household whole.
- **Original hard edge:** secrecy becomes fratricidal violence; the commander personally kills a loyal subordinate during a dispute and the house builds later policy around concealed shame.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. The Hunter signed three execution orders but did not personally commit the decisive wrongful killing.
- **Restoration:** one guilty scout is executed after trial and one coerced scout dies under an unlawful sentence. When the third refuses a secret interrogation and demands an open hearing, the Hunter kills the innocent scout in a sudden blow, then orders all three deaths entered as one lawful traitor case. Submission of the records and later rescue work begin only after that lie becomes impossible to sustain.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; founder Lives reduce the killing to battlefield necessity while the victim household preserves the refused-hearing account.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. Mercy rescues the guilty and innocent alike from immediate danger but does not pardon crime or restore the scout.

## GS-04 — The Mason of the Ninth Bastion

- **Received source function:** Perturabo as indispensable builder who turns humiliation into punitive expenditure of subordinates and eventually makes massacre part of architecture.
- **Original hard edge:** siege atrocity, collective punishment, expendable followers, enslaved builders and civilian death used as structural proof.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. The Mason sealed workers and prisoners, but punishment of the commander's own followers remained vague.
- **Restoration:** after sabotage delays the project, the Mason orders one worker in ten from the surviving crews killed by lot. The condemned include people who reported the sabotage and people who worked in unrelated courses. This decimation establishes the doctrine later used to shorten evacuation and seal the ninth foundation.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; engineering-school copies omit the lot list, while the infernal appendix preserves it as a design principle.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. No number beyond this local work force and no universal fortress network is implied.

## GS-05 — The Smith Who Broke His Hands

- **Received source function:** Ferrus Manus as strength-teacher whose contempt, anger and late desire to correct his sons are overtaken by death, leaving successors to absolutise the wound.
- **Original hard edge:** contempt for weakness becomes lethal; the founder's own anger and doctrine, not merely an overzealous successor, cause the irreversible loss.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. The Smith designed a fatal test, but the prose left room for accidental or subordinate failure.
- **Restoration:** when the apprentice rings the stop bell and begs release, the Smith personally holds the test mechanism closed to prove that fear can be mastered. The apprentice dies. The Smith then expels maimed workers who witnessed the refusal, making the later confession and restitution an admission of murderously enforced doctrine rather than an abstract change of philosophy.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; one workshop rule deletes the stop bell, while the victim families' copy names it.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. Later repentance is real, and the founder lives long enough to begin the correction that the source figure never completed; that changed axis remains deliberate.

## GS-06 — The Chain-Bearer of Sarrat

- **Received source function:** Angron as enslaved empath, mutilated rage engine, abandoned companion and later willing author of the same wound in others.
- **Original hard edge:** slavery, bodily mutilation, denied solidarity, massacre after liberation and compulsory reproduction of the founder's injury.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. The Chain-Bearer already kills surrendering overseers, orders imitation engines and massacres a neutral household after escape. The chosen recurrence office does not absorb those decisions.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact with later revolutionary, state and cult propaganda.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity`.

## GS-07 — The Clerk of a Hundred Wells

- **Received source function:** Guilliman as saving administrator whose conquest, emergency paternalism and inheritable systems outlive their reasons and injure people in his name.
- **Original hard edge:** administration is coercive power backed by soldiers, confiscation and death, not merely an imperfect filing system.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. Enforcement deaths were listed without a decision connecting them to the Clerk.
- **Restoration:** the Clerk personally signs armed well-seizure warrants after being warned that several households will resist. The seizures kill household defenders and impressed labourers; a petitioner is executed under an emergency procedure the Clerk wrote. The Clerk initially defends all deaths as the price of saving the district and only later admits that necessity did not make every enforcement choice just.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; official code commentaries count water delivered but omit enforcement deaths, while petitions and burial rolls preserve them.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. The common register still genuinely saves thousands.

## GS-08 — The Bitter Reaper

- **Received source function:** Mortarion as liberator from poison tyrants who recreates their coercion through endurance, dependency and refusal of helplessness.
- **Original hard edge:** killing, forced hardiness, child death, abandonment of the vulnerable and freely accepted damnation.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. The exposure regime already injures children, kills adults, withholds water and burial, and makes survival dependent upon the Reaper's clinics. The outbreak is not the Reaper's fault; the coercive compact is.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact with competing worker, clinic, state and cult accounts.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity`.

## GS-09 — The Reader Behind the Sealed Door

- **Received source function:** Magnus as brilliant scholar who correctly perceives a threat but treats knowledge and good intention as exemptions from prohibition, consent and bounded means.
- **Original hard edge:** the warning itself destroys the barrier; followers and civilians die for the scholar's certainty; later grievance minimises the original choice.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. Student experimentation appeared in the cost list but not in the causal event.
- **Restoration:** a preliminary address maims one student and proves that a living receiver bears the route's return pressure. The Reader conceals the severity, overrules students who withdraw consent and stations them as receivers for the final demonstration. They die with custodians and nearby families when the warning opens the path.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; prohibition minutes prove the custodians believed the threat, while altered student rolls reveal the concealed preliminary test.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. The Reader remains correct about the distant threat and guilty for the chosen means; the human soul never shards.

## GS-10 — The Smith of the Refuge Bell

- **Received source function:** Vulkan as compassionate protector and maker of terrible weapons who nevertheless kills civilians personally and cannot be excused by humane reputation.
- **Original hard edge:** personal civilian killing and catastrophic weapon use by the compassionate conqueror; remorse cannot resurrect the victims.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. Commanders fired the weapon, leaving the Smith culpable mainly as inventor and supplier.
- **Restoration:** after the first discharge reveals that civilians remain in the approach, the Smith receives a chance to lock the weapon. Believing an enemy breakout will kill more refugees, the Smith personally authorises and triggers the second discharge. Civilians burn with the fighters. Later testimony names that choice before addressing the commanders' concealment.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; military copies merge the two discharges and attribute both entirely to an absent operator.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. Later restitution and sainthood remain possible only through repentance, death and judgement, never through a weapon relic or collection quest.

## GS-11 — The General of the Glass Court

- **Received source function:** Fulgrim as sincere artist turned perfectionist tyrant whose fall becomes personal and irreversible when he murders the beloved peer whose honest judgement matters most.
- **Original hard edge:** intimate betrayal, murder by the principal, humiliation and cruelty made beautiful, followed by counterfeit bodies and reconciliations.
- **Previous state:** `attenuated`. The General arranged the loyal officer's death at a distance.
- **Restoration:** during a private confrontation over sexual abuse of office, refugee theft and the cleansing programme, the General personally murders the loyal officer. Afterwards the General fabricates assault orders and a field-death record, causing the body to be placed among soldiers abandoned in an unsupported action.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; triumphal Lives preserve the false field death, while repair records, private correspondence and the body's later movement expose the murder.
- **Result:** `restored-at-parity`. Patron influence and dependent Masks intensify appetite but never possess away the General's choice.

## GS-12 — The Warden of the Last Redoubt

- **Received source function:** Rogal Dorn as defender who sacrifices people within inflexible defence and turns grief into punitive, self-consuming warfare inherited by followers.
- **Original hard edge:** civilians and relief forces die behind a deliberate gate decision; later penance spends subordinates as well as the commander.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. The Warden knowingly seals the outer ward, then leads wasteful proving assaults that kill followers. Confession, evacuation reform and a later bounded defence do not make those deaths necessary after the fact.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact with contradictory recovery and relic traditions.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity`.

## GS-13 — The Judge Beneath the Black Eaves

- **Received source function:** Konrad Curze as accurate but fatalistic terror judge who commits feared atrocities first and arranges death as proof that choice was impossible.
- **Original hard edge:** torture, mutilation, collective punishment, exemplary killing and willing assassination used as propaganda.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. The Judge already tortures, mutilates and executes people for uncommitted acts, creates self-fulfilling massacres and permits assassination as the final legal argument.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact; prophetic, judicial, cult and victim records contest interpretation rather than the existence of terror.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity`.

## GS-14 — The Saint of Ashen Wings

- **Received source function:** Corvus Corax as liberator who violates liberation through expendable prisoners and failed creations, destroys the survivors and abandons a wounded polity before a frightening later pursuit.
- **Original hard edge:** insurgent killing, non-consensual experimentation, murder of failed followers and abandonment.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. The commander already authorises non-consensual breach experiments, personally kills altered survivors, conceals the programme and leaves a vulnerable polity. Confession and later rescue work precede redemption; the later Saint does not continue a private vendetta.
- **Historical layer:** authorial earthly fact with official liberation, victim-family and later received-Life layers.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity`.

## ER-01 — The Institutionally Erased Person

- **Received source function:** the Primarch II absence as deliberate human suppression after catastrophe, with followers and useful practices redistributed rather than cleanly erased.
- **Original hard edge:** the missing founder's uncertainty is surrounded by real war, death, coercive silence and institutions willing to punish witnesses.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. The catastrophe already kills captives, workers and noncombatants; suppression includes imprisonment, coerced silence and witness execution.
- **Historical layer:** authorial fact that suppression occurred; identity, guilt, judgement and final fate remain genuinely open.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity` and unpromoted.

## ER-02 — The Supernaturally Name-Wounded Person

- **Received source function:** the Primarch XI absence as damaged identity carriers and incompatible replacements occupying a vacancy without a settled canonical crime or fate.
- **Original hard edge:** identity destruction harms witnesses, descendants and institutions even when the missing person's guilt cannot be known.
- **Previous state:** `intact`.
- **Restoration:** none. Witnesses already suffer memory injury, disappearance and institutional killing; families lose inheritance and burial rights.
- **Historical layer:** authorial fact that a bounded supernatural attack occurred; attacker, allegiance, judgement and later manifestation remain open.
- **Result:** `intact-at-parity` and unpromoted.

# Consequences for the Garden

The fourteen existing light traces remain the right promotion level. Hardening changes the pressure beneath nine traces but does not promote a complete biography:

- the Red Door reading must include people murdered by the escape network, not only people abandoned by officials;
- the Second-Gospel fragments descend from a preacher-authored sacrificial system even though innocent hospital users remain innocent;
- the empty hearing place remembers a killing personally committed by the alleged founder;
- the ninth chambers carry a history of decimation as well as sealed labour;
- the restitution charter answers a test the founder knowingly refused to stop;
- the Clerk's Ending answers armed enforcement and execution, not mere bureaucratic overreach;
- unopened warnings carry the names of coerced student receivers;
- the refuge tools redirect a craft tradition whose maker personally fired the civilian-killing weapon;
- the restored portraits conceal the General's personal murder of the loyal critic.

The other five visible traces already preserve their source wounds. Neither erased-person mystery receives a Garden trace.

## Validation

- Exactly fourteen visible exemplars and two erased positions receive one hardening ruling.
- Nine visible translations are restored and five remain intact; both erasures remain intact.
- No new figure, company, universal war, Legion analogue or Heresy is created.
- No act is added merely to increase a body count; every restoration belongs to the borrowed Primarch function.
- The principal person owns the decisive act wherever the source grammar requires personal guilt.
- Saints repent, die and are judged before any later commission; no Saint falls after communion.
- Sinners remain damned human souls distinct from office, Mask, Patron, body, copy and legend-persona.
- Repentance, service and miraculous commission do not erase victims, restore the dead or authenticate followers.
- ER-01 and ER-02 remain different mysteries and disclose no invented Primarch biography.
- No protected Weird, chronology entry, named order, present outcome or canon ruling changes.
