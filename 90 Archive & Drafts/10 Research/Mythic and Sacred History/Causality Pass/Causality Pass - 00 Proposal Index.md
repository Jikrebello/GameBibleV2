## The proposals

### [[Causality Pass - 01 Deep Past and the Ancient East]] — 9 items

- **01.1 The provision ends.** The Glass provision stops at the threshold. A host that has lost its farmers fights over the seed reserve, which is the Vacant Commission. The Southern Seizures take farmers as well as land, and the Atherians turn towards stars that still answer. Also: an author-only host size (about 40,000 entering, 100,000–120,000 leaving), a shared camp layer under three languages, and the Ark/Orphaned grievance.
- **01.2 Giant dilution.** Thinning blood made every continuity system cost more human bodies, and that is the root of the collapse. Includes a plan for the legacy Ishkatar, Xolotl and Qianglong dossiers.
- **01.3 First kingship through named-petitioner pacts.** A Menhir bargain binds the petitioner's line (Meya of Narash, *proposed*). Ark refusal of bargains keeps Jirahar and Ahdari leaders judicial.
- **01.4 The Prophet in public tradition.** Four incompatible public deaths; the authorial truth stays sealed.
- **01.5 Qiryath's sign, the Withholding (743–654 BR).** The sign stops before the sack and returns at an offering by Yaelith's mixed households. Its cause stays unresolved even in the author's notes. This gives the 1360 Jirahar a precedent for a silence that ended.
- **01.6 Eryndor's corpse.** A failed White Measure rite lets the Crowned Countenance speak through the corpse at Delkos, which prolongs the Wars of the Inheritance.
- **01.7–01.9** Causes for the Aramen empire's end, Eryndor's motive and the Bronze Roads failure; a reworked Siege and the Returnings; rulings on writing, iron, coins and the lost Vathran cities. Plus a **Cuts** table of copied incidents.

### [[Causality Pass - 02 Gateway Wars and the Late Republic]] — 7 items, re-sequenced

- **02.1 League into hegemony.** The twelve Compact members, all existing names, and three episodes that turn the league into Caleran's hegemony. The Oar League's ship money withholds harbour refuge from cities in arrears.
- **02.2 The Gateway Wars re-sequenced.** The first war is fought over grain from the Mask's festival tables. Zamaret's invasion fails because towns that eat his gift grain lose their own stores.
- **02.3 The Mask provides.** The Three Collections are the bill coming due. Qarzeth falls as a Gluttony defeat sealed by a shared meal, whose rules become the Spared Towns' **Table Oath**, the root of the Nerethan and Samyra legitimacy. The salt and Sarqen as royal seat gain world-native reasons.
- **02.4 Caleran's own sacrifice (609 BR).** The killing of Gateway child hostages for Astaroth breaks his taboo and creates the **Warden of the Red Field**, an office its holder cannot lay down.
- **02.5 The late republic re-sequenced.** Damar's crisis turns on a Sun-answered boundary sighting, and the Social War fuses with the Oar revolts. Harod dies in office, **Calvena lives**, Veyr dies at Ilamar, and Casvilar's killers strike through the Red Field rite. The 370, 367, 355 and 350 BR anchors are kept.
- **02.6 Kelarion and Orthea reconnected.** Kelarion's Roll is the ancestor of sea-refuge law; the Unwritten Worm's loose years make the records of Qarzeth's sack irreconcilable. Orthea is renamed **Ambreth** to end the clash with Ortheia.
- **02.7** A merge plan for the island and Vorthara files, with nine human events.

### [[Causality Pass - 03 Age of Glory and the Bridge to 0 AR]] — 7 items

- **03.1 The Year the Portrait Spoke (247 BR).** Mott governs through the speaking portrait, and its words lead to the granary searches and the market killings. Samor covers it with a soldier's cloak. Afterwards the Face is kept veiled and unveiled last at each accession, and the Mask feeds through provincial copies.
- **03.2 The Threshold Law.** Caelor dies under a hall that was blessed but never tested. Thalion makes Astaroth's tests compulsory, priced, witnessed and personally liable. This explains the 274 quarry strike, the Arcelon rule and Velen's exempted riverbanks.
- **03.3 Four pressures.** Raveth becomes an integration famine instead of Joseph's. Elian's citizenship grant carries an oath before the image, with a veiled form for Jirahar scribes; that is where Aquilan's citizenship comes from. The Mask's appetite; the eastern frontier; the 151 BR vacancy tied to the memory of 247.
- **03.4 The Charter of Referral.** A Vasharan succession war sells confirmation of Qiryath's sanctuary office. The sign answers at the interim Keeper's offering, the losers appeal to Atheria, and the sign refuses a trial. The governor rules on the House of Qoreth's written record, and Ezrahel's grandfather drafts the charter whose clauses return in 0 AR and 61 AR.
- **03.5 The bridge narrative, 151 BR–0 AR** (about 1,500 words).
- **03.6 One Egypt.** Sarqen is kept and the Qahuri annexation withdrawn, against the current working target of Qahur under direct rule by 33 BR. Ostar's crucifixions and Velsar's 358 defeat are de-duplicated.
- **03.7 The Low Door** supplies Doran's march in 61 BR, and his officers enter by the low door instead of swearing before the image.

### [[Causality Pass - 04 Who May Authenticate a Sign, 0 to 411 AR]] — 9 items, re-sequenced

- **04.1 Why proof does not compel belief.** Grace acts by commission; a light on the dead might be Flame, Shell-fire, a Menhir omen or a Mask; and only a licensed augur college can make a sign exist in law. At 0 AR there are **Three Silent Days** with no recorded answer, after which augury resumes.
- **04.2 The Great Purge as a war over records.** It leaves a statute against unregistered signs, which is why early accounts understate their own miracles.
- **04.3 249–398 AR re-sequenced.** The firm dates become **377, 385 and 398**, and no beat keeps its AD year. One civil war remains, against a Star-recognising eastern command left by Astrion's 350 answer.
- **04.4 The Second Silence.** Compelled professions fail to kindle; the Church records it and seals the record.
- **04.5–04.9** A sworn statement about the Forty Flames read by Cassian's camp in 292; the Man-Bearer roots and Nasriel's career; "before the Mesakha" as Vestian's own clause; the Four Nails' route to the Pillar (the Third Nail stays sealed); recasting the four AE-dated legacy files.

### [[Causality Pass - 05 The Guttering, 400 to 905 AR]] — 8 items

- **05.1 A dynastic spine.** Four houses: Vestianic, Saldane (*proposed*), Leovarid and Corvenid. Bishops trade backing for relic-estate tax exemptions, and after 612 come the frontier marches that prefigure Thalmyria's military provinces.
- **05.2 The Guttering.** Court rites stop kindling; the court hires warlock fire and then fakes kindlings with it, and the fakery counts as a breach. This explains Caleran 905, Veridun 906, the refugee kindling of the 950s and both sides of the Kindling Question.
- **05.3 Vashar's end.** A toll-road war (514–523), then drought and murrain (546–552) during which the 4,444 dig as famine relief. Vashar is taken on a ruling that its own king broke his road-oath, and the Qahirids descend from one of its converted houses.
- **05.4 The Unsealing as law.** The abolition of the Star rites left augur-witnessed town charters with a dead witness. Re-seals before the Flame and the Fourth Seal schism stop the conquest. **The Suspension of 612** freezes both sides' untested proofs.
- **05.5–05.8** The founding clause that bars the 4,444 from conquest, and Qahir's loophole; the eastern drift and Simeon's refusal of a court relic (711), the source of "Simeon's primacy"; Corvenas II's Second Crusade; Corvenas III trapped by tests he could not afford to fail.

### [[Causality Pass - 06 The Covenant after Caleran, 905 to 1190 AR]] — 6 items

- **06.1 The Widowed Seals.** Idris bound army and cities to himself by name. His heir drowned in 929 and Idris died in 931 before anyone re-swore, so every oath lapsed. The **Sublime Threshold**, an office that cannot die, is the remedy. This explains the Basin military-household leases, why the Cull began as binding rather than killing, Qazmarid's 933 proclamation, and one foundry left from three.
- **06.2 The Long Reach (951).** Hymn-blessed Grey Sister horses do not shy from bound things, so a Covenant frontier built on panicking enemy animals fails. The Tarkhan act from their own motives.
- **06.3 Caleran, 957.** The commander who swore the south to himself surrenders it in his own name. The Ember is a deposit held for the final audit; Veridun's payment releases its keepers' names.
- **06.4 Why Thalmyria won to 1052.** The south fragments into separately sworn districts, and the Covenant has its own distractions.
- **06.5 The classical age.** The Vault splits into two houses; Mazrakan is founded; the War of the Southern Deeds (1131–39) is lost when the persuasion engines misread the settlers; a court voids Suhrab's renunciation.
- **06.6 The Grey Sweat as a custody crisis,** "the Emptying of the Benches", with the Quiet Ninth left untouched.

**What the re-read shows.**

- Causes are now the best-disguised part of every sequence. The readers' own "what broke my prediction" lists are the bends: Zamaret lynched at the store doors with no great battle, the cast of the late republic outliving their death scenes, Astrion living on as a rival emperor for twenty years, the legate drawing the blank at Drossfeld, Ansgar drowning before his theses are read.
- Qiryath's kings, the Age of Glory, 78–288 AR and the late republic moved most. Eryndor, High Noon and the road to 1360 did not move.
- **The clock is now the weakest disguise.** Where anchor dates were kept, a sequence still sits at a near-constant distance from its original, and a specialist who finds the distance can date the next event. The road to 1360 is the clearest case: bought conclave 1274, the *Grievances* 1297, the council 1319 and the Accord 1343 all sit 212 to 226 years below their originals, which makes 1360 read as about 1578.
- **Names are the second.** They add half a point to a point wherever a ruler's name carries the original's sound. [[Causality Pass - 12 Rename Proposals]] gathers the proposals; the rows marked Apply went in on 15 November, after this re-read.
- Some of what remains is deliberate and stays: the Isfir and Companions parallels to the Gospels and Acts, which carry the First Church article, and the Jirahar parallels in the Exodus and returnings cycles.
- The re-read also reported twelve small contradictions between articles. Ten were real and are fixed (the siege of Qarzeth begins in 525, Saint Naris is a man, the marriage at the store-house falls in 92, and so on). Two were not contradictions: one misread BR dating, and one took an acclamation for a coronation.

**What it shows.**

- **The clock and the names both fell by about a point.** The clock fell most before the Redeemer (4.5 to 2.8 across those ten sequences): the Gateway Wars from 6 to 2, the founding from 7 to 3, Eryndor from 6 to 3. Names fell wherever they were renamed, most on Cassian to Vestian (6.5 to 3).
- **Overall predictability fell less, by about a third of a point,** and almost all of that is before the Redeemer (4.95 to 4.4). From the Age of Glory to 1360 it did not move (5.15 to 5.1).
- **The skeleton is now what a specialist follows.** Where the overall score did not move, the readers scored the order of events and the fates at 7: Cassian to Vestian, Two Crowns, the road to 1360 and the siege and the returnings. A third bend of those four sequences is the lever that is left.
- **What moved most:** Eryndor III (6 to 4), the imperial founding (5.5 to 4), the Great Tower Revolt (6.5 to 5), the Long Frontier (4.5 to 3.5), the late republic and the Eastern Sea Wars (6 to 5).
- **What did not move, as record 13 predicted:** the road to 1360 (7), High Noon (5) and the 1360 board.
- **Two Crowns kept its clock.** The reader set it by the four dates that could not move (993, 1017, 1019 and 1104, about 205 above), so moving Kaltfurt and Drossfeld did not change what the reader saw.

**New things the readers found.**

- **The first imperial house.** One reader paired the House of Thalion's reigns with the Julio-Claudians' and found them about 323 years apart by the sum of the two years (350 BR with 27 BC, 324 with AD 4, 286 with AD 37, 247 with AD 68). Record 13B paired the same reigns differently and found no line. This is why the Age of Glory's clock score rose from 2 to 5. It has not been checked by a researcher.
- **78–288 AR sits close to the real calendar.** The reader matched 119 with 117, 233 with 235, 245 with 250 and 248 with 251. The 233 is Tervan's new accession year; the third century has an emperor every few years, so any year there lands near one.
- **Fifteen possible contradictions between articles.** Five were real and are fixed: Mahdar is eight in 931, not six; the Celebrants hold the veiled Face from 245, not 247; Iltara did not direct a Qahuri intervention two generations before her time; the Queen's country is "most of a century" old at Samyra's accession; and Eclessian rule goes round the lake country, which is how the First Southern Crusade could pass south of it. None was caused by the date moves. The rest were loose wording or readings that hold.

**Limits.**

- One reader for each half, and not the readers of the second re-read.
- The reader of the earlier half reported seeing commit titles that said sequences had been moved "off their clocks". The reader knew dates had moved, not which analogues were meant.
- Two file names still carried old year ranges (292 to 316, 348 to 377). The reader was told to ignore years in file names.
