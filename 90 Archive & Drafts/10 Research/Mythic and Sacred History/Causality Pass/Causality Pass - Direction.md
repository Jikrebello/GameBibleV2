# Causality Pass — Direction

The creator's brief for the rest of the Causality Pass (14 November 2026), and the standing rules every writer in the pass follows. Index: [[Causality Pass - 00 Proposal Index]].

## The brief

The World Egg's history should be **causally realistic and fun to read**: a fictional history people want to learn more about. The touchstone is the older Elder Scrolls lore — Daggerfall, Morrowind, the original *Pocket Guide to the Empire*, Project Tamriel — not the sanitised later style.

- **A land, not a courthouse.** Contracts, domains and sworn instruments are real parts of the cosmology, but history told through them reads like a court record. Readers should feel they are standing in a country: roads, rivers, weather, hunger, smoke, songs, graves.
- **Magic shapes history.** Roughly half to three-fifths of the major turning points should be driven or decisively bent by the supernatural. Every such turning point still obeys cause and effect and passes through messy people.
- **Saints, sinners and the strange belong on the page.** The research on great saints, great sinners, Idol wars, Menhir hunts and prodigies should reach reader-facing canon.
- **Real history is a springboard.** Start from a real-history pressure where it makes good story, then branch into the World Egg's own chain. The closer to 1360, the more native the chain must be. The present must be explained: why and how the world reached 1360.

## Standing rules

1. **Land before law.** Tell every turning point first through what people saw, ate, lost, feared and sang. Law, charters, oaths and contracts appear as things people fight over, break and fake, not as the engine narrated in procedural vocabulary. In reader prose, keep legal and procedural words as seasoning. The Covenant may use more, because contract is its culture, but even there show the bound devil digging, the dry well and the screaming engine rather than the docket.
2. **The blend.** In each era, about half to three-fifths of major turning points have a supernatural cause or consequence that changes an outcome. The rest are human: famine, greed, love, error, weather, disease, pride. Supernatural causes always carry costs, side-effects and human misreadings; people exploit them, fake them, misunderstand them and fight over them.
3. **Messy people.** Each turning point has at least one person who chose well or badly for reasons of their own. Contingency is allowed: a drowned heir, a bad winter, a drunk sentry, a misheard order.
4. **Saints and sinners in the open.** Each era's reader articles name its great saints and sinners and what they did to history. Report in-world disagreement about them (cult, sceptic, rival church); keep the authorial truth in author-only files.
5. **Strangeness is welcome.** Prodigies, cursed places, holy fools, monsters as historical forces, uncanny events. The Weird of History register stays scarce for the truly unexplained; strange-but-explained events may be common.
6. **Analog as springboard.** Borrow a real pressure, then branch by the second or third beat. After 1052 AR, history must be readable without real-history knowledge and unpredictable from it.
7. **Unsanitised, not gratuitous.** Atrocity, slavery, religious violence, corruption and saints with ugly flaws stay on the page, told plainly. Moral contrast stays sharp (Commandment 4).
8. **Fixed anchors.** The 1360 present-day board (states, rulers, faiths and the Locked Rulings in [[Current Focus Board]]) is fixed. The path to it may change. The Weird of History entries stay unexplained.
9. **Writing style.** Vivid encyclopaedia prose for reader articles: concrete nouns, named places, people who act. No negation clutter (Style Guide), no "X benefited; Y suffered" closers, no lists of abstractions standing in for events.

## Decisions on the cross-proposal questions

Made on the creator's instruction to proceed on best judgement.

1. **Vestian's dates** move to accession 377, compulsion 385 and death 398. Anything that assumed 380 or 395 follows.
2. **Astaroth.** His structural answers to builders and siege engineers are a standing practice (Darneth precedent). The Torque Register line becomes: Astaroth answered **Astrion's public test of legitimacy** once.
3. **Calvena survives** (02.5). The Casvilar–Veyr break needs and receives another cause; the founding biographies follow.
4. **Names.** 01.5 keeps *the Withholding*; 03.4's Vasharan succession war is renamed.
5. **Qahur** is not under direct Atherian rule in 33 BR; Sarqen is the one annexed "Egypt". Flight into Qahur at 0 AR is flight into contested country.
6. **Controlling rulings** amended by approved items (the Great Unbinding table, the Wars of the Inheritance corpse line, Three Collections, the Palace review) are amended with a dated note.
7. **Ottoman beats in 06.** The Cull of Brothers and Suhrab's executed son are bent into native forms. "Sublime Threshold" stays as an established translation convention.

## How items are decided

Writers approve items that make history both causal and engaging; revise items whose engine is procedural so that the same cause is told through the land; and reject items that add bureaucracy without story. Each decision is marked in the proposal file with a one-line reason.
