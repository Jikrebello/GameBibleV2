# Causality Pass — 11B1 Second Bend: Eryndor, Vashar and Arqesh

> [!note] Record of applied changes
> Written 14 November 2026. The first bend ([[Causality Pass - 10B1 Bending Eryndor and the Eastern Sea Wars]]) made the mechanisms native; an adversarial reading still called beats in advance because the order of fates and several dates survived. This pass applies Concordance rules 6–8 (quarry not script; clocks never align; skeletons, not only mechanisms). The changes below are already in the reader articles and the two control files this writer owns. The `## Integration patch` gives final wording for files this writer may not edit. No one and no place has been renamed; proposals are in the table. Rules: [[Causality Pass - Direction]].

## What stays fixed

First Qiryath destroyed by Arqesh in 742 BR. Arqesh falls in 690 to its own succession war, Arezan's Vashari and Eryndor. Eryndor dies in 684 and his conquests break into Vashar, Ortheia, Delkos, the Qahuri crown towns and the farther-eastern garrisons. The Voice at Delkos, the loan from the dead (695), the closed wood (687–686), the images changing voice (688) and the water books sealed at Durqesh. Mehrava's line in Vashar. Vashar as the lasting eastern power, holding Qiryath until the late Age of Glory and ending in 555–560 AR. The League of the Eastern Oar formed at Velathene in 497; Ebris's three sentences (495); the holds that took the tide and the deserters admitted under Kelarion's Roll (493); the withdrawal of 491; ship money and the closed chains after it.

### Arqesh (was 4/10)

| Adversary beat | Now |
|---|---|
| Saruq as Sargon: pitch-sealed basket, gardener, rival birth tales, a later dynasty stealing him | A boy found asleep on Teluq's regulator beam the night the head gate opened unbidden. The alliance and the marriage of 694 stand: Mehrava depends on them. |
| "Tenth to seventh centuries"; Neshkar II dies 728 | Neshkar II reigns 758–731 and dies of marsh fever at Nemeru. The century phrase is gone from both articles. The span itself cannot move (see the clock check). |

A river portent at the fall was considered and rejected: gates of a river opening on a doomed capital is Nahum on Nineveh, and soldiers walking a drained channel is Herodotus on Babylon.

### Clock check

- **Achaemenid years** (559, 539, 530, 522, 499, 494, 490, 486, 480, 479, 478, 465, 431, 424, 404, 401, 358, 330): no Vasharan accession, death or battle now falls on one. Reign lengths are 33, 33, 9, 35, 42, 24, 37, 52, 30 against 29, 8, 36, 21, 41, 19, 46. Intervals in the sea wars are 3, 5, 9, 2, 2, 2 (514–511–506–497–495–493–491) against the source's 5, 4, 10, 1, 1.
- **Macedonian years** (348, 336, 335, 334, 332, 331, 330, 328, 327, 326, 323, 317, 310): none matched. Offsets from the nearest analogues now run 370, 368, 363, 360, 360, 361, drifting with the fixed anchors (706, 702, 690, 684) and never constant; the order of beats differs.
- **Assyrian years** (745, 727, 722, 705, 701, 689, 681, 612, 609): 731 clears 727. Three near misses remain and are not analog events: the demand of 746 (a fixed Qiryath date in other writers' files), the succession war "c. 706", and the fall in 690 (fixed).
- **Residual.** Arqesh's span, c. 958–690, still lies in Neo-Assyria's centuries. 742 and 690 are fixed anchors, and Kassur I's dates (c. 918–879) carry Qahur's Dead Landlords (c. 903–858), so the founding cannot move without rewriting Qahur. The phrase "tenth to seventh centuries" is removed from reader prose. A later pass could move Merod I if Qahur's chain is reopened.
- **Noted in other writers' files, not changed:** Velsar's defeat in the Avesh approaches is dated 358, the year Artaxerxes II died; it is not an analog event.

## Protected, withdrawn and decided

- **Protected (new or restated):** what opened Teluq's gate; whether Demeran Kavos stood at the Pelatha table; the half-brother's name; whether the king nodded to the bearers; which harbour, if any, holds the tomb; the later history of the garrisons beyond the wood; what Ebris knew.
- **Withdrawn:** the undersea spring, the bell and what struck it; the Qahuri title and the adoption story; the posthumous birth and the conception at the gorge; the army assembly and the waived bow; the garrisons' coins; the Atherian squadron of 513; the fevered beach of 503; the cousin's revolt of 403; the death of Orvaz I at the acclamation; Saruq's basket, gardener and royal descendants; the delegation terrace and the trilingual inscriptions.
- **Authorial truths decided** (recorded in [[Authorial Notes from Reader Articles - Ancient East]]): the images' new voice is Mask behaviour feeding on the king's shame, with no part of the dead companion present; the Ended Acclamation is a Witness-Judgement angel closing the mouths of those present on one formula, with nobody struck; Ilyessa's tide court answered the anchoring in 506 as it did in 493 and answers acts, not pilots; the seal of the sleeping child binds no one and works by custom; Nemeru's rising and Tiraz I's drowning are ordinary.
