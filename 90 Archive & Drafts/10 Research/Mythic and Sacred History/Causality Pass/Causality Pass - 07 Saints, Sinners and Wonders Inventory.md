# Causality Pass — 07 Saints, Sinners and Wonders Inventory

> [!note] Inventory, not proposal
> Nothing here changes canon. This file maps every saint, sinner, heavenly, infernal, Idol, Menhir and Patron intervention, monster-war, prodigy and strange event found in the Mythic and Sacred History research, the two control ledgers, the author-only Atlas hubs and the archived drafts. For each one it records how far it got. The ranking in section 3 applies the Consequence Rule in [[The Divergence Pass — Torque Register]]. Index: [[Causality Pass - 00 Proposal Index]].

**Status key.**
- **PUB**: a public reader article states the wonder, or hedges it.
- **PUB-thin**: a public article exists but the saint, angel, fiend or Mask has been removed or reduced to "some say".
- **AO**: author-only canon.
- **RO**: research only, never promoted.
- **PROT**: protected-dormant or ⊘ SEALED.
- **SUP/REJ**: superseded draft, or rejected or cut.

**Cool** (1–5) rates how strange, memorable and morally sharp an item is, measured against the older Elder Scrolls lore. **Weight** says what the item could decide: who wins, rules, starves or believes.

The research produced a world crowded with saints, sinners and powers. Almost all of it was carried through review and the Ledger's reserve closure ("132 integrate, 57 graft, 15 protected-dormant, zero unresolved ordinary holds"), and almost all of it ended up in author-only files.

What readers get follows a clear slope. Before 350 BR the public legends keep their wonders: walking houses, High Hunts, Masks eating a victory. From the Redeemer onward they hedge. After 905 AR they mostly go flat:
- Public [[G-47 - Three Notes before the Spoils]] is about a horn player, and "the human players remain sufficient". Its author-only parent, [[HC-N08 - Taken Trumpet]], has occupation warlocks whose ward "crushed and stripped the visible pinions" of a Herald angel.
- Public G-48 offers "Some say a blazing messenger stood". OBA-N04 has Death advancing "along the census couriers' roads".
- The public era articles for 905–1360 AR name about a dozen wonders. They name no saint with a Life and no named sinner.

### Why good material got stuck

1. **Visibility by design.** The Ledger's authority strings route every figure away from readers: "earthly history and author-only afterlife" (GS), "regional tradition; not a new authenticated angel" (the fourteen deferred HC/OBA leads), "author-only truth; incomplete public knowledge" (UH), "author-only past and active person; future open" (AF). Integrate and graft both meant a paragraph in an author-only hub. Even [[Great Saints and Sinners - Lives and Afterlives]] is author-only, yet the public High Noon and Great Split articles link to it.
2. **The light-trace gate.** Research could send only "promote-light" traces: "promote no complete person, institution, relic or active campaign"; "name no saint until an individual earthly-life dossier exists". Every Saint and Breachward card ends "Promotion remains locked."
3. **The harvest stopped at 350 BR.** The 15 September 2026 harvest "makes no ruling on G-10 or any later-period root". No supernatural claim after 350 BR has had a harvest decision, so the later legends fell back on hedging.
4. **The Q1 test.** The Seed Garden asks whether a tale still works "if every supernatural interpretation is false". That is a sound test for a legend. Applied to every public article, it trained the public layer to delete the wonder.
5. **Consequence limits written before November.** Examples: "does not explain an age" (ID-P01); infernal states "exploited the Giant collapse without causing it" (IP-01); "No placement causes an age" (Menhir); Gate 11, "cannot secretly cause the entire human chronology". The Consequence Rule supersedes these, but no research frontmatter was updated. None of Causality Pass 01–06 cites a GS, HC, OBA, UH or AF figure.
6. **Crowding and quotas.** The Saints and Sinners distribution lock is 2 / 8 / 2 / 2, with eight figures in the Long Frontier. Deferral reasons include "already crowded", "not presently scarce", "Long Frontier density is intentional but closed to the next harvest" and "no more than three promoted accessions".
7. **The Weird gate and the Forward Rule.** "No seed explains the Bells of the Split, Quiet Ninth, Third Nail, Averted Legion"; "No outcome is prewritten." Both rules are sound, but they hold back most of the 1290–1360 material.
8. **Source fidelity and provisional names.** The figures are Primarch, D&D archdevil and Orthodox-saint grammars. Every title is a "provisional authorial handle", and readers cannot be given a nameless saint.
9. **Losses at integration.** The canon text of IP-06 dropped its commissioned saint and loyal angels ("Local defenders rescued captives"). Three public graft destinations lack the bed grafted to them: Creation and the First Rebellions (GP-I-01), The Conversion of the Empire (GP-III-02) and G-16 (GP-IV-02).

**Bottom line:** promotion is mostly a matter of visibility and framing, not new invention. The authorial truth exists; it needs a public "the chronicles claim" version that keeps the wonder.

## 4. Gaps

**962–1360 AR in public.** The public era articles carry Caedros I's assumption (957), Wolfsmere (993), Strazgorod (1061), the Knockers, the Grey Sweat, Bruckmoor and the Quiet Ninth (1232–1247), G-22, Ansgar's Psalter (1305), the Pyre of Masks and an unnamed healing spring. There is no named saint with a Life and no named sinner. There is no public Idol war, no infernal war, and no Menhir event apart from the Knockers. The Torque Register has no saint or sinner entry after Strazgorod.

**962–1360 AR even in author-only canon.** Four holes remain:
- **1052–1087.** Only GS-13 and ID-R10R.
- **1200–1232.** Only AF-R17 and HC-N14.
- **1250–1305 is the deepest hole.** It holds only OBA-N14 and the mundane Road That Paid Twice. The Qarath Disaster has no wonder at all. The Black Wind years have no saint, monster or Mask.
- **1330–1350.** Only the Pyre of Masks and OBA-N11.

**Regions after 905.** The Ahl-e-Ahd Covenant has no wonder anywhere in 905–1190. Causality Pass 06 is entirely political, although a Vault of Names invites an ER-02-style name-wound. The Jirahar have none after OBA-N09 (c. 720–760). The Tarkhan have only the First Catch and Qarayel's Grave (1303). The Sestaran coast is the only region with several (SB-P05, MN-R09, the Thirty Sisters).

**Earlier ages.** The Atherian Imperial Age has no saints by rule and only two wonders (the Sleepwalkers' Summer, B3-04). Causality Pass 03's Portrait is still a proposal. The Age of Stars has one angel and little else. Before 2165 BR the material is rich, but it is all Giant, Mask and Patron; no human saint or sinner has a name.

**The Long Frontier is saturated.** Eight GS lives, nine angelic leads, the breachward orders and five infernal episodes all fall in 411–905. Moving one deferred Primarch grammar out would help. The Winter Executioner (1030–1090) or the Two Envoys (1160–1200) could be reworked as a full GS-style Life for 1250–1305.
