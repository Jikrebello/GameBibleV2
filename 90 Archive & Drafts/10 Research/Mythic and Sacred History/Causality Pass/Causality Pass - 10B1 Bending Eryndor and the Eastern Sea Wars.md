# Causality Pass — 10B1 Bending Eryndor and the Eastern Sea Wars

## Summary

Two sequences were still legible as their analogues: Eryndor III as Alexander from Philip's murder to the romances, and Xarun I's war as the Persian Wars from the Ionian Revolt to the Delian League. Both are now bent by the second or third beat. The outcomes the later world depends on are unchanged: Eryndor's conquests break at his death into Vashar, Ortheia, Delkos, Qahuri garrisons and a farther-eastern successor; the Wars of the Inheritance follow and the Voice at Delkos governs them; Vashar lasts; Vashar fails to rule the Atherian leagues; the League of the Eastern Oar forms in 497, takes ship money after 491 and drives the republic's later crisis. The speaking corpse and the Arezan alliance are kept. Every recorded victim is kept; where an event's mechanism changed, its dead were carried into the new event.

Blend: about half of the Eryndor turning points turn on something only this cosmology could produce (the loan from the dead, the images' change of voice, the closing wood, the seep, the deathbed rite and the Voice, with the Countenance behind the bowing killings). The Xarun sequence is more human, but its decisive turn is supernatural twice over (the tide court's terms and their breach; the refuge custom descended from Kelarion's Roll). Taken together the two sequences sit near the Direction's half. The rest are human: pay, greed, hunger, fever, a family's price, a diver's sale, a quarter that opened a gate for bread, a wind that did not come.

### What was bent and why

- **Philip's assassination.** A lone assassin at a festival became an army killing its paymaster over a short count, in the decade the world first struck coin. The beat now grows from a world-native first and sets the reign's engine: an army that has to be paid. The killers are known as a body; who began it is protected.
- **Thebes.** The rebel city destroyed as an example became the city whose withheld silver started the riot, sold to pay the men who killed the king. The order is reversed (the city's act precedes the death), the mechanism changes (opened on terms, treasury short) and Demeran's later hold on Ortheia gains a cause: he was left there with the companies and the mints in 704.
- **Tyre.** The causeway and the king's diving bell became a hunt for a spring under the sea. The bell survives as the defenders' water-bell and the besiegers' forced divers; the king never goes down except in late court song.
- **Siwa.** The oracle and divine paternity became a loan of grain from a Patron dead four centuries, against the right to embalm the borrower. Romance later reads the pledge as an adoption. It gives the deathbed rite a cause eleven years deep.
- **Darius and Bessus.** The satrap's murder and the conqueror's royal burial became the Mask choosing a new face: the images go over, the king is left faceless, surrenders to the ally, seals the canal books and outlives his conqueror. This explains why the Ubar's water offices served Vashar and why Vashar, rather than Delkos, lasted as the eastern power. The Countenance learns Eryndor's voice while he lives, which makes the Voice at Delkos less of a coincidence.
- **Cleitus and Callisthenes.** The feast, the drink, the saved life and the remorse are cut. Both deaths stay; the companion now dies for mimicking the images' voice, and the exemption is won by the companies.
- **The Hyphasis.** No mutiny. A Root-Mind wood closed the road because the king refused its price and broke its taboo. The farther-eastern Successor now has an origin: garrisons nobody could reach.
- **Babylon.** Death in the conquered capital while planning another campaign became death aboard ship at Nemeru while trying to go home and keep the war's founding promise to the Terys families. The rite in a ship's hold, with no house, is why it failed. Serevan's possession of the body needs no theft.
- **Roxane's son.** The child lives: a daughter, Mehrava, carried by Vashar as a contested claim and married into the Tiravan line. The paternity dispute arises natively from the White Measure's fasts and the season at the gorge. Only the half-brother is murdered.
- **The romances.** The five *Alexander Romance* episodes are replaced by five native incidents: the spring under the sea, the lions who followed, the faces that answered, the wood that closed and the water that would not travel. The refusal is now the spring-keeper's.

### What a reader of Greek history would now fail to predict

The father dies at his own pay table. The rebel city is the army's wages. The great siege turns on a spring. The oracle is a loan from a corpse. The enemy king is abandoned by his own gilded images and lives. The army is stopped by a wood. The king dies at sea, trying to go home, and his posthumous child grows up to marry the next eastern king.

### What was bent and why

The Ionian springboard is kept for two beats (514, 513), as the Direction allows. The branch comes at the third.

- **Miletus.** The breach became a gate opened by the hungry for bread, and the deportation to the canals (exactly the Milesians at Ampe) became sea families sent to keep highland relay stations. The station families give the Dawn Road a human population that matters again in 554 AR.
- **Athos.** No storm and no cape. Siege fever from Tasar travels with the conscripts, and the admiral's choice to put subject crews ashore with the sick seeds the desertions of 493.
- **Salamis and Plataea.** No fleet battle in narrow channels and no decisive land battle. The royal granary fleet spoils at anchor because the king called the sea his and asked it to stand still after it had carried him; its own subject crews desert to an Atherian refuge fire; the League takes ships that are already lost. The land army starves while a governor's family sells the king his own road. The Darius-in-Scythia details (wells guarded, the weak abandoned at night) are cut; the dead of the withdrawal are kept as people who died when the animals failed.
- **The Delian League.** Its shape is a fixed outcome. It gains its native irony: a league that won by admitting its enemies' hulls in distress was raising its chains against its own members within a generation, which is the road to Pyrassa in 466.
- **Vasharan internal politics.** The Aresh house's price makes the Avesh command a family possession, which is the cause of the governor of Avesh's revolt in 462.
- **The ideology.** Xarun's claim as heir of the Far Road through Mehrava gives the invasion a cause beyond punishment, and makes Mazdan's use of a Delkos grant a delicious hypocrisy: the voice he cited had disowned his king's ancestress.

### What a reader of Herodotus would now fail to predict

The punitive fleet arrives intact and dies of fever. The great fleet is never beaten in battle. The sea takes back the king's bread in sound hulls. The king's subject rowers decide the war by rowing to the enemy's refuge fire. There is no Plataea; a governor's family sells the king his own road.

### The Divergence Pass — Torque Register

**Replace the Voice at Delkos entry with the two entries below, in this order.**

**The Wood that Closed (687–686 BR — the army's refusal at the Hyphasis).** TORQUE: no army refused Eryndor; a wood did. Beyond the Vashari highlands a Root-Mind grove offered passage for the valley terraces it wanted back. Eryndor refused the price, felled the speaking trees and left the cut unused, and the Root-Mind's law ran: roots crossed the cut, worked timber sprouted, and the road behind the army edited itself into a different valley each morning. He cut his way out, leaving prisoners, the villages that had fed him and the farther garrisons on the far side; those garrisons, beyond anyone's reach, became the farther-eastern Successor. The wound he took in the cutting sent him to the seep that heals only those who stay, and, because he would not stay, to the White Measure. [[Eryndor III and the Ortheian Eastern Conquest]] · [[The Five Limits of Eryndor]]

**The Voice at Delkos (695–c. 655 BR — Siwa, the end of Darius and Ptolemy with Alexander's body).** TORQUE: the corpse that governed was assembled over eleven years. In 695 Eryndor fed his army on grain lent by Khaseret's House of the Returning Cargo, a Qemaret Patron four centuries dead, against her masters' right to prepare his body. In 688 Kassur III's portable images, in which the Crowned Countenance was anchored, began to answer in the voice of the king most bowed to; their bearers carried them to Eryndor, and the Mask learned his voice while he lived. Kassur, a king with no face, surrendered to Arezan and sealed the Gate House's water books to him, which is why the Ubar's canal offices served Vashar. In 684 Eryndor died aboard Serevan's flagship at Nemeru, sailing for home; the masters performed the final rite in a ship's hold with no house to close him into, bound his name into an anchor his soul had already left, and the Countenance filled it.
