# Causality Pass — 12 Rename Proposals

The rows marked **Applied** were applied on 15 November 2026 with the creator's approval. The rows marked Optional or Keep are unchanged and still wait on the creator.

The two bends changed what happens and when. They left the names alone, because "one step sideways" naming is the creator's standing decision. After the bends, names were the loudest remaining pointer at the real analogues: a reader who meets *Xarun* crossing a strait, or *Astrion the Apostate*, has the parallel before the first event. This page gathers the eight writers' rename tables ([[Causality Pass - 11A1 Second Bend - Gateway Wars and Late Republic|11A1]] to [[Causality Pass - 11F Second Bend - The Road to 1360|11F]]) into one list with a recommendation on each row.

**How to read the tables.** *Mentions* is whole-word mentions and files across the vault on 15 November, not counting the Causality Pass records. *Call* is the recommendation:

- **Applied** — the name gave the analogue away and the change was affordable. Done on 15 November 2026.
- **Optional** — a weaker echo, or a matter of taste.
- **Keep** — the cost outweighs the echo, or the name belongs to someone else's decision.

**How to apply the rest.** Mark the Optional or Keep rows wanted and ask for them to be applied in one pass. Notes whose file name changes are renamed so that links follow; names in prose are replaced as whole words; the registers are updated in the same commit. Grenzburg files are left alone and listed where they mention a changed name.

**Locked registers.** The Jirahar and Covenant naming registers are locked. Every suggestion below for those cultures stays inside the register's existing pattern, but each is still a change to a locked list and needs the creator's yes.

## What was applied on 15 November 2026

The creator approved the rows marked Apply, and they now read **Applied**. Sixty-seven rows went in:

- **Text.** About 2,100 whole-word replacements in 226 notes across canon, the stories and the research notes, then 53 sentences fixed by hand where a straight swap read badly or a gloss had to change (*al-Qari* is "the Reader"; Qatada is "Keeper of the Roads").
- **Notes renamed.** [[The Painted Reign]], [[A Soldiers Testament of the War of the Seals]], [[The Brenholt Margins — Selections]] and [[Of Princes as Borrowers — Selected Maxims]]. Every link to them was updated, including the links in these records.
- **Two suggestions were taken and replaced.** "The Two Winters" is already the famine of 1101–1103, so the Year of Four Eagles took its writer's alternate, the Painted Reign. Brennar of Stoneden is an Ättar chief, so Neral Vardun became Garen Vardun. The lead of the Painted Reign article was rewritten to match, and its section "The four eagles" is now "Three standards and a painting".
- **Registers.** The [[Real-History Concordance]] lists every pair under "Renames applied on 15 November 2026".

What the pass left alone:

- **Grenzburg and Caleran** (`02 Playable Settings`). Two lines still say "Saint Ioan Outer Fort", in *Caleran Anchor Location Register* and *Outer Crown*; the saint is now Dovrin of Velia. Tavian Sorel, Ivo Renn and Livia Serrat are different people and keep their names.
- **Dated snapshots** under `90 Archive & Drafts/30 Cultures`. Five lines keep old names there, because those folders are copies of the vault before earlier passes.
- **The other records in this folder.** They keep the old names in their text as a record of what was proposed.
- **Real names in the real-history research notes:** Simeon and Levi, the historian Carmel Cassar, the Venetian *Serrata* and Cato the Elder.
- **The word "crusade".** Only the four numbered wars were renamed. "Crusade" and "crusader" remain ordinary words, and the First Southern Crusade (1060–1067) keeps its name because it was not in the approved rows.

One thing to weigh: Hadram, the new name for Amesar, now sits near Hadrath (the town), Hadrun (the Ättar ancestor) and Hadran Pell (a Grenzburg survivor).
