# Causality Pass — 12 Rename Proposals

Nothing on this page has been applied. Every name in the vault is as it was.

The two bends changed what happens and when. They left the names alone, because "one step sideways" naming is the creator's standing decision. Names are now the loudest remaining pointer at the real analogues: a reader who meets *Xarun* crossing a strait, or *Astrion the Apostate*, has the parallel before the first event. This page gathers the eight writers' rename tables ([[Causality Pass - 11A1 Second Bend - Gateway Wars and Late Republic|11A1]] to [[Causality Pass - 11F Second Bend - The Road to 1360|11F]]) into one list with a recommendation on each row.

**How to read the tables.** *Mentions* is whole-word mentions and files across the vault on 15 November, not counting the Causality Pass records. *Call* is the recommendation:

- **Apply** — the name gives the analogue away and the change is affordable.
- **Optional** — a weaker echo, or a matter of taste.
- **Keep** — the cost outweighs the echo, or the name belongs to someone else's decision.

**How to apply.** Mark the rows wanted and ask for them to be applied in one pass. Notes whose file name changes are renamed so that links follow; names in prose are replaced as whole words; the registers are updated in the same commit. Grenzburg files are left alone and listed where they mention a changed name.

**Locked registers.** The Jirahar and Covenant naming registers are locked. Every suggestion below for those cultures stays inside the register's existing pattern, but each is still a change to a locked list and needs the creator's yes.

## Collisions that are not analogue tells

- Two Nerals (Neral Vardun; Neral daughter of Sapha): cleared by the Brennar rename above.

- Arest Corven and Corven Ilar; Ardel Veyr and Miretha Ardel; Merath and Celor Marath; Aster Vey and Veyr; Hadrath, Hazreth and Harzun; Tamim and Tahmur; Ilarion and Ilaron; Neram bar Elior and Elior bar Hadan; Thalion's two Lysaras.
