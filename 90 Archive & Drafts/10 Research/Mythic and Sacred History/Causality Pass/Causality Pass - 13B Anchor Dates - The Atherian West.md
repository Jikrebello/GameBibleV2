> - "The brief" in the text below is the instruction this research was written to: find every sequence that still sits a near-constant number of years from its real analogue, count what each date move would cost, check ages and reigns, and propose a Minimal and a Full tier without changing what happens.

## The Atherian west (c. 660–350 BR)

Research and design only. Nothing in the vault was edited. Names are the ones canon uses after the renames of 15 November 2026.

**The short answer.** The Gateway Wars' clock can be broken outright by moving one whole war and one death (five dates). In the late republic two beats, the partnership and Rethmar, can leave their line, and the war of the closed chains can be re-spaced. The thirty years from Darneth to the Unification (380–350 BR) cannot be taken off their clock by any re-spacing: 350 is fixed, the queen's childbearing limits how long the succession war can run, and twenty-six dated beats share a band seventeen years wide. Moving the killing of Casvilar would not break that band, so I do not propose it. The Age of Glory carries no line.

**Conventions.** Offset = BR year minus the analogue's BC year. "Bent" marks a beat whose fate or form the two bends already changed (records 11A1 and 11A2 call these gone, removed or reversed); I show its offset but test lines on the beats a reader can still recognise without the clock. A line is three or more beats within 8 years of one offset. Every subtraction below was computed by script and re-checked against the finished file.

### What pins the dates

- **350 BR.** Epoch of Atherian old-style reckoning; about 958 canon lines in 209 files. It fixes the end of the founding at +320.
- **The band arithmetic.** From Darneth (380) to the Unification (350) canon has 30 years against the source's 21 (51–30 BC). Every beat between must fall in order between those ends, so its offset is held between +313 and +329. Twenty-six dated beats, fifteen of them still recognisable, share that band; the rule allows two per nine years. No re-spacing inside the band can pass the test.
- **Darneth, 380.** Held as the precedent for Astaroth's answers to engineers in `Astaroth.md`, `The Divergence Pass — Torque Register.md` (the 350 AR Astrion line), `Age of Glory - Authorial History and Integration Review.md` and the Folk files (`Folk Canon Register.md`, `Historical Spine of the Folk.md`, `The Folk before Atheria.md`, `Historical Atlas of the Southern Cold World.md`): 17 lines in 15 files. It is the hinge: the chain Darneth, Ostar's ruin (379), the voiding and Velos (377), four years of two governments, Sarqen (373), Veyr's death (371) and the killing (370) has no slack, so 370 cannot move earlier unless 380 does.
- **The queen's children.** Samyra (b. 392) bears Hadram to Casvilar in 372 and Lethia to Velsar in 351; in 350 Lethia is "on her hip" and Talven "by the hand" (`The Wars of the Atherian Succession.md`). Hadram must be born before or just after his father's death. If the killing moved to 375 and Hadram's birth with it to 377, a queen born in 395 would be 18 at his birth and 44 at Lethia's. That caps any lengthening of the succession war at about five years.
- **Ages stated in prose.** Casvilar "at sixty-one" (b. 431); Selvena sixteen and Veyr forty-six at their wedding in 389, Veyr "thirty years older" (`Marovan Veyr.md`), Selvena "born 405" (`Imperial Founding - Authorial History and Integration Review.md`); Ostar "born five years before the tent came down" and "sixty-seven" in 379; Pel born 418; Thalion "twenty years old" in 370, "a man of forty" in 350, "aged seventy-two" in 318, his father dead in 384; Melvo and Thalion "close in age"; Venn sixty-eight in 366 and eighty-four in 350; Varroth sixty-four in 350; Hadram twenty-two in 350 and ten when Seneth came (362); Meren eight in 350.
- **Counted spans.** Harod's twenty-five years in the tent (466–441); the Field open thirteen years (383–370; 12 lines in 10 files); the succession war twenty years and the discharge four (350–346); two governments four years (377–373); Seneth's twelfth spring at the table (362–350); Sallas eight years in sanctuary (to 359); Miretha's ten autumns (366–356); Merath seven years behind the stair (358–351); Tashqar's "eleven years" (5 lines) and "no battle for six years"; the Rope Camp's five years; the siege's "five campaigning seasons".
- **The hostage cycle.** Cohorts are renewed every seventh year from the peace and the last sails in 611, two years before it is killed. The first war's peace must therefore fall on 611 plus a multiple of seven (646, 653, 660, 667).
- **609 and 520.** "The names of 609" are read in the debate of c. 529 and at Caleran in 354 (14 lines in 13 files; 12 lines in 8 files use the phrase "of 609"). 520 carries the Table Oath, the spared towns' chronology, the Erasure of Orthea (c. 520–513) and Marelith's child-buying (520–518): 137 lines in 71 files.
- **466 and 461.** The gale and the Chain Peace are used again in the founding (Marelith as "the harbour without a chain", Pyrassa's wardens' oath, refuge law, Ilamar's copy of the Roll) and in `The Refusal at Kelarion.md`: 23 lines in 17 files and 19 in 14.
- **Kellovar, 383.** The ford's river-court returns in 377 AR (Lucar's army) by name, not by year; the year itself is in 29 lines in 22 files, including `Signs and Proof - Authorial Truth.md`, the Torque Register and the Folk files.
- **The Kept Doors, 410.** Cited in the founding ("had held their doors against Lethren Ostar's agents in 410") and the Vorthara files: 13 lines in 10 files.
- **Kept by instruction.** 370, 367, 355 and c. 350 (`Imperial Founding - Authorial History and Integration Review.md`, the 10A and 11A2 notes). 11A1 lists the Gateway and late-republic dates it left unchanged.
- **Shared with other stretches.** The League of the Eastern Oar (497–469), its ship money (491–469), the refused hulls (c. 480) and the pilots' families (c. 475) belong to the eastern sea; the Marenthe seat (c. 680–672) to the Successors; the Second Qiryath (c. 660) to the Jirahar.
- **Registers.** `The Weird of History — The Unexplained Register.md` has no dated entry between 660 and 350 BR (its nearest is the Sleepwalkers' Summer, c. 200). The Torque Register dates the Unreckoned Days (c. 652), Tashqar's Bread (c. 612–601), the Lustration (609), the Unranked Table (c. 528–520), the Noon Sighting (488), the Closed Chains (466–461), the Red Field (466–350), the Ford at Kellovar (383), the Writing-In (367–364), the Guests of Pyrassa (355–353) and the Salt Field (353–350).
- **Play material.** No Grenzburg or Caleran play file cites any year proposed for a move.

Command to Kellovar becomes 9 years against 6, and Kellovar to Darneth 11 against 1. What it costs beyond the edits: "thirteen years" is a motif, and twenty-one sits close to Harod's twenty-five.

### Recommendation

Take **Minimal**. It removes the Gateway Wars' clock completely and lifts the two late-republic beats that have room to move, for about 98 lines in 40 files and no change to any event, order or fate. Full is worth its extra 82 lines only if the creator wants Kellovar off the band and accepts a twenty-one-year Field; its step between the two halves is nine years at the narrowest (eight if the rising of 410 is counted with the upper half), which is a thin margin for the price. Neither tier touches the run from Darneth to the Unification, and I advise leaving 370, 367 and 355 where they are: with 350 fixed, moving them cannot break that band (see Not proposed). The honest position to record in the Concordance is that the last thirty years of the republic keep their clock because the Unification keeps its date.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Is the core acceptable as the price of 350?** If not, three locks would all have to open: Darneth (380), the killing (370), and the ages of Talven and Lethia at the table in 350. Opening one or two buys nothing.
2. **Birth years.** Would you drop exact birth years from the lead lines of Veyr, Velsar, Varroth, Melvo and Samyra, whose ages do no work in the story? That takes five of the eleven births off the clock without moving a date. Do you want Thalion older (28 at the killing, 80 at his death)?
3. **The first war and the Marenthe seat.** Moving the war to c. 674–660 makes the dry springs overlap the seating of Marenthe (c. 680–672). I think that reads as cause; it needs the agreement of whoever proposes for the Successors, since that stretch may move 680–672.
4. **Tashqar's eight years.** Dying in the hostage year gives the date a reason, but the prose loses "eleven years" (five places). Acceptable?
5. **The closed chains.** 469 → 476 touches the League's dates (497–469, 491–469), which the eastern-sea proposal may also move; the two should be settled together. By raw year 466 and 461 also sit one and two years from the Thasian revolt of 465–463 BC, which the Torque Register's own label ("a naval league's revolts") invites a reader to notice.
6. **Kellovar.** If you take the Full rows, is a twenty-one-year Field beside Harod's twenty-five what you want?
7. **Age of Glory.** Varelan I's 23 years equal Tiberius's and Olarion's 16 sit against Nero's 14. Moving 263 to about 272 (7 lines in 4 files, plus the reign lists) would clear both. It is outside this brief; say if you want it worked up.
8. **Harod's later dates.** The nine senators (452–449) and his death (c. 441) still slide from +370 to +363 behind the Chain Peace. I have treated them as bent. If you read Harod as still recognisable, they cannot be cleared without changing "twenty-five years".
