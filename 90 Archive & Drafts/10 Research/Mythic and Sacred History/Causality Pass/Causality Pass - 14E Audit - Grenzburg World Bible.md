# Causality Pass — 14E Audit: The Grenzburg World Bible, Region and Factions

> [!note] Status — 15 November 2026
> Detail for [[Causality Pass - 14 Grenzburg and Companions Audit]]. **Report only: nothing in the game folders or in canon was changed.** This is the auditor's report as written. Paths beginning `CMP-`, `C10`, `PH` and the like are short forms for folders under `02 Playable Settings/Grenzburg/`; each report explains its own.

Audit date: 15 November 2026. Read-only. Nothing in the vault was changed.

**Scope.** `02 Playable Settings/Grenzburg/10 Production Bible/` (all but the two archetype references), `20 Region and Locations/`, `40 Factions/`, `Maps/`, `Grenzburg MOC.md` and `02 Playable Settings/Playable Settings MOC.md`. The two MOCs are reported under Production Bible; the Maps folder is reported under Region and Locations.

**Paths.** Game paths are relative to `02 Playable Settings/Grenzburg/`. Canon paths are relative to `01 Canon Reference/`. "Focus Board" is `00 Start Here/Current Focus Board.md`.

**The short version.** This slice is almost free of dated history. It contains one year (1359 AR, Location Register:198), none of the seventy renamed names, and no mention at all of the Korrheim Accord, the Freeflame, the Sanctum, the Grey Sweat or Brauvalis. The rewrite therefore did little damage by date or name. It did damage in three places: the Wolfsmere country (canon now has a fort, a flood and a court judgement where the game has an "occupation fort"), church and order naming, and the paladin terminology that canon itself corrected on 14–15 November. The rest of what follows is older drift between the game and canon, and the game disagreeing with itself.

**Totals.** 1 at severity A, 7 at B, 15 at C.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **"Arcane Burn" or "Shell burn"?** The game uses Arcane Burn throughout. Canon uses "Shell burn" for the Warlock's overdraw (`Warlock Rune Magic.md`:78; `Magic Systems Overview.md`:24) and "Arcane Burn" only in the Lancewright line (:22) and in Harmonic Shellcraft (:67). Which is the setting's word?
2. **Paladin "techniques" and the new Thalmyric lesson.** The rewrite added that Thalmyric orders learned "the Fire answers whom it will and never for the asking" (`Eclessia vs Thalmyric Paladins.md`:38), and canon already says genuine workings "remain gifts rather than techniques" (:20). The Locked Ruling speaks of "Paladin techniques" gated by coherence. Is a Long Watch form that kindles on demand, given coherence, still what you want Bernard's tradition to teach?
3. **Paladin traditions.** Paladin.md:38 describes the two traditions by chivalry and relics against asceticism and hospitals. Canon now parts them by what they carry: Thalmyric lamps, Eclessian examined banners (:36–40). Should the vocation text follow?
4. **The Medium has no canon article.** Bard, Lancewright, Binder and Paladin each rest on a Magic & Systems note. The only Shadowlands family in `Magic Systems Overview.md`:28 is "Idol/Shade Magic", defined as predatory. Canon also uses "Shades" for agents (`The Reed Court of Wolfsmere.md`:36 "opportunistic Shades"), while the game's shades are tools. Does the imprint craft need its own canon note and a different word?
5. **Unsent dead.** The game states flatly that souls have routed (`Grenzburg MOC.md`:127; Medium.md:53). The rewritten Sweat says the walking dead were "residue and unsent souls at thin places" (`History/70 Later History and Present Day/The Grey Sweat - Authorial Truth.md`:22), and mass death is a named interception risk (`Soul Routing and the Vestibule.md`:37). After a siege winter, can any Grenzburg dead be unsent, or is the flat rule meant to stand for this game?

### Open questions for the creator

1. **How far south does the duchy lawfully run?** Canon says "Eclessia's southern frontier has stopped at the lake country ever since" 994 (`The Reed Court of Wolfsmere.md`:20) and that by 1232 the frontier was "a gradient from parish to clan-land, stopping at Wolfsmere" (`Historical Spine of the Folk.md`:55); it also says the First Southern Crusade (1060–1067) carried marcher rule south "around the lake country" (:52). The game has ducal forts, mines and survey markers six to eight days south of the city. Is the Upper Grenz corridor that crusade's work, and should the game say so?
2. **Chapel-Folk as waystone custodians.** `Road Keys and Menhir Paths.md`:59 lists a "Chapel-Folk reader" among those who keep a compact "aligned to Hecatara's route law" (:26). `Chapel-Folk.md`:24 says they "reject sacrifice and worship offered to local courts" but may "maintain an inherited boundary ... because prudence is not worship". Is keeping a waystone prudence or appeasement?
3. **Bells near the lake.** Under the charters of 999 no bell may sound within hearing of Wolfsmere. Bent-Oak Refuge has a "road bell" (`Bent-Oak Refuge.md`:30) and Long Lake has Ice Bell Rock. They read as far enough away; confirm.
4. **Migdal Talga.** Canon places a Jirahar tower "in the cold Eclessian marches nearest Grenzburg" whose charter "a winter siege will test" (`Peoples and Cultures/Jirahar/60 Realms and Peoples/Migdal Talga.md`:16, :40). The game's atlas and travel tables never mention it. Outside the map by design?
5. **Fort Tannbruck's "north-bank route"** (`Fort Tannbruck.md`:45). The Grenz flows north, so its banks are east and west. Is this a local bend or a slip?
6. **The Rain Vault's "blue-glass terraces"** (`Rain Vault.md`:26) are called Atherian. Canon has no Atherian blue glass. Local invention, or should it be older work?
7. **Does Grenzburg stand on the Schedule of Yards?** See Factions, question 1; it bears on Cathedral Close, the Paupers' Cloister and the camp burial grounds.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Where does the Church line stand under the rewritten Accord?** The Accord now turns on burial: "every lamp where its dead lie"; the lord "confirms its clergy"; "A yard whose dead go unsent for a year and a day is open to whichever rite will send them" (`The Korrheim Accord.md`:20, :44, :47). The faction's four settlements (bounded diocese, broad episcopal court, secular restriction, dual jurisdiction) never mention the Accord, the Freeflame or the Sanctum, though canon puts "Freeflame preachers among the winter labor camps" (`Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR).md`:43). Is Grenzburg a signatory land with yards on the Schedule? If so, a siege winter of unsung dead is a legal event the line should know about.
2. **Bishop-Prelate.** Canon's offices are Lord Primarch, bishops, parish priests and the orders (`Eclessian Church Overview.md`:24–29). "Bishop-Prelate" is the game's own. Keep as a local title?
3. **Blackjack contracts and Fiorenzian law.** Canon's lawful instrument has a "term ceiling" and "discharge conditions", and Fiorenzian advocates may challenge the arithmetic (`Debtors’ Corps.md`:26). The game's best settlement is that "Existing service is fixed and reviewable" (`Factions Overview.md`:30), which implies it is neither today. Is that the "foreign charters weaken oversight" case (:28), and should the faction notes name the Debtors' Corps, which they never do?
4. **Named Folk officers.** Canon names Speaker Bran of the River-Clans and Warlord Cenn of the Ash-Spear (`The Folk of the Frontier and the Wolfsmere Lineage.md`:20). The Compact gives the River-Clans "local speakers rather than a permanent chief" and the Ash-Spear no seat. Intended?
5. **Grenzburg's standing.** The game never names Brauvalis, so nothing here depends on its dukes or its settlement. Canon calls Grenzburg a "Small marcher enclave between the Sylvan Marches, Wolfsmere country and the Folk frontier" (`Geography/World Map/Political Map Border Proof Key.md`:82) and gives it no founding, no row in the `Eclessian Canon Register.md` matrix (:32–41) and no overlord. Which Drossfeld holding did it come from, and does its duke answer to anyone?

## What the new history offers

- **The Peace of the Bent Oak (999).** The ban on bell, boat and iron at Wolfsmere is ducal charter law, won by a baptised Folk lord in the bishops' own court. Useful to Elof's seat, Lake Gate politics and every Compact term.
- **The Lake Watch and the Standing of the Water (994).** A shore fort whose garrison was "found on the ice". A ready history for Bellless Hold.
- **The Schedule of Yards and the year and a day.** After a siege, each unsung grave is a deadline, and Freeflame elders in the camps "will bury anyone". It fits Bread and Burial, the Paupers' Cloister and the Church settlement.
- **Ford tokens.** Folk households crossed Hecatara's river-courts on tokens of iron, bone or hair; the older toll was a traveller delivered who could not return. A lineage for Road Keys and their custodians.
- **The Bank of the Dead.** The Medori hold the Chancery's registers of the unsung; a factor "in a debtor's coat" sold extracts in 1345 and was drowned for it. A precedent for Lucia's Long Book and for what a debtor can leak.
- **The dead are named.** Ash-Walkers, Saint Gerlind ("the dead are named, not bought") and Irmel ("named, not counted") give the game's pauper names, memorial readers and the Roll of the Drowned one shared custom.
- **Lamps and banners.** Thalmyric soldiers keep a door-lamp; Eclessian orders swear examined banners. Bernard's Open Lamp Hospice already sits on the Thalmyric side of that line.
- **The old registered forts.** Adalmar regranted imperial forts the clans had held for three generations; the Crusade of 1060–1067 went round the lake; the Purge of 1163 ended when "riders whose breath made no smoke" took an Aveline chapter. Origins for Tuskway Redoubt, Tannbruck and the commandery's memory.
