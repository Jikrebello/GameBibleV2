**In brief.** Neither companion's files contain any of the old names in the renames list (all were searched for), and neither contains a year other than 1360 or a span tied to a moved date. There are therefore no stale-name and no stale-date findings. The disagreements are of three kinds:

1. Elara's Virelian background was written against the older, plainer Grey Sweat and the "second serfdom". Canon has since given both a new cause (burial), and the game is silent where canon now speaks.
2. A few canon lines that predate the rewrite disagree with the game (Grobi signals, the Medori's people in Grenzburg).
3. Internal contradictions, most of them small, two of them not.

Counts: Elara 16 findings (A 1, B 7, C 8). Ilyana 9 findings (A 1, B 4, C 4).

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Which court convicted her, and of what?** In canon a lord's use of bound labour is lawful ("Estate theft is discipline, not law", `Lives of Virelia`:38). Desecration would fall to a relic court or to the Accord's yard rules. Were the diggers bound Velescar households or hired free poor? If the Tessaris were bound, their move to Grenzburg was flight, which canon punishes.
2. **Who are the "Velescar household delegates"** Mira Spoke, Olek Sar and Hanne Wirth (`E/Uninvited.md`:75)? `E/No Lady of Velescar.md`:175 has them "arrive from districts that went hungry", which reads as Grenzburg people; `PH/House Velescar.md`:31 and :66 rule out arrivals and new post. Who appointed them, and when?
3. **Enforcement.** By what Virelian law does a hearing in Grenzburg free bound households and transfer Virelian land? Canon: "Few magnates intend to free bound tenants regardless of confession" (`Politics … in Virelia`:43).
4. **"a household-backed crew"** on the broken route (`E/The First Honest Find.md`:111): are these Velescar people arriving in Highlake? That would breach `PH/House Velescar.md`:31.
5. **Roll 52** asks for "two ledgers of the dairy accounts in my mother's hand" (`VC`:97). Canon has serf literacy at "almost never" (`Lives of Virelia`:20). Intended exception?
6. **Names.** "Lord Marek Vardek" sits beside canon's living "Duke Marek Arvidan", a river-commerce magnate and royal candidate (`Politics … in Virelia`:33), and beside the ancient minister Vardek who "laid a table to kill a guest" in 373 BR (`CR/History/40 Atherian Imperial Age/Samyra Nerethan.md`:33–35). "Tessari" for a Virelian bound family sounds Sestaran and now sits beside the Sermoran chartmaker Ugo Tessarin (new on 14 November). Canon also has a "Lady Elara Soran" of Aversia (`House of Soran Overview`:20). Keep or change? Is House Velescar Vesari or Latinate-Markish? Canon tags Virelia's culture "Vesari"; the game uses "Virelian".
7. **Seals.** Vardek's cabinet holds "Velescar seals" and "the restoration seal" (`E/The Patron's Cabinet.md`:73, :118), while the title seal went to the Bank as collateral and is in the vault (`PH/House Velescar.md`:62–64). Two sets?
8. **"one Grobi bombardment"** (`E/The Winter Company.md`:88) against canon's "Grobi technology is materially poor" (`Grobi Overview`:72). This rests on the main quest (`The City in Siege`:47) and should follow whatever is decided there.
9. **Criminal surety.** Canon describes the Debtors' Corps as for "Bankrupt people" under Fiorenzian law (`Debtors’ Corps`:16). The game has the Bank buy a Virelian court's criminal surety. This premise is shared by all the companions.

### What the new history offers

- The bond of the clean village: the house's hold on its sixty-three households is a Sweat-era promise of "a priest, a consecrated cemetery and burial in rite", and its lady dug up the Sweat's dead (`The Grey Sweat`:52). Sealed priestless villages are a ready canon home for the six sites.
- The Korrheim Accord: "every lamp where its dead lie"; a yard unsent "for a year and a day is forfeit"; in Virelia the magnates "are patrons of their estate yards" (`The Korrheim Accord`:20, :70, :72). Opening graves touches title and rite, and Accord Wardens or Lantern investigators could be the "burial tracing".
- Vesari hearth-law: a house that relights from a greater fire becomes "the lender's client" (`Kingdom of Virelia Overview`:22). That is the shape of Vardek's restoration offer.
- Saint Gerlind's "the dead are named, not bought", and naming the dead aloud to quiet them (`The Grey Sweat`:74–76), beside the name tablets of *Names Beneath Lime*.
- The runaways' road to Grenzburg and the Virelian mother at Vessing in 1352 (`Lives of Virelia`:20; Chronology:340) for Aldi and Sandro; the Trade War of Orzova in 1355 (`Politics … in Virelia`:38), the year Elara inherited a river landing with disputed tolls.
