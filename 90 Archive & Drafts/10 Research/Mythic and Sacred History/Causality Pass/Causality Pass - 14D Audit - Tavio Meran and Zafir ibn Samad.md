### Checked and holds

- **The Crucible and the Bench.** Game: "The Crucible is larger than the Red Brass Bench" (`Comp/Tavio Meran.md` l.106). Canon: the Bench "is one corrupt arena branch, not a synonym for the Crucible as a whole" (`The Crucible.md` l.30). Agrees. The canon page lists Tavio and the Bench in its links and says nothing the game contradicts.
- **Lancewright craft.** Game: "Marks are bounded Warlock Rune Magic" (`Comp/Tavio Meran.md` l.289); spear, gauntlet, Yield. Canon: "a martial proof discipline within this family… one bounded release or safe Yield" (`Warlock Rune Magic.md` l.22); "one-handed war spears, fitted gauntlets, physical marks, and filed load limits" (`The Crucible.md` l.24). Agrees, and with Board l.88.
- **Road examination as a real tradition.** Canon: "road examination, siege inspection, foundry safety… are equally legitimate traditions" (`The Crucible.md` l.24). Supports the examiner ending.
- **The tremor.** Canon lists "nerve shock" among the effects of overdraw (`Warlock Rune Magic.md` l.78). The nerve scarring fits.
- **Harmonic Shellcraft.** The canon page does not mention Tavio or Lancewright. The package's Bard line ("times coordinated loading") does not conflict with it.
- **Horn and leak day.** Canon: in Vorthara "leak days (declared by horn)" close a district and "the liability courts convene within the week" (`Lives of Vorthara.md` l.20). The finale's title and the "injury claims attached to Tavio" (`Comp/Tavio Meran.md` l.157) both sit on this.
- **Arena career.** Canon: "the arena ladder — Vorthara's blood-sport circuit is examination, advertisement, and settlement of institutional disputes in one" (`Lives of Vorthara.md` l.30). Agrees with a touring champion whose name sells fittings.
- **City before Concord.** Board l.68: "'Sestaran' is secondary to city identity." The prose always calls him Vortharan. (Front matter lists "Sestaran" first in three files and second in the rest; harmless.)
- **Medori and the Blackjacks.** Canon: "distressed-debt purchase" (`Medori Bank.md` l.26); "Medori offices purchase and administer enough distressed paper to dominate overseas deployments" (`Debtors’ Corps.md` l.22). Agrees with "Medori purchased the surety and restitution exposure".
- **Shell-fire at the Great Forge.** "Shell-fire" is a canon term (Sestaran Canon Register l.51). States outside the anchor blocs "buy output, lease crews… or operate unsafe temporary draws" (`Shell Anchors and Extraction.md` l.65), and "venting into inhabited districts" is a named failure (l.74). The forge leak and the River Vent fit.
- **The Muster.** Canon names the Old River Arsenal as a regional anchor and rules "Human souls are not trapped in Muster soldiers" (`The Unending Muster.md` l.56, l.60). The plate "from the Old River Arsenal" and "not a soul, patron, idol" (`T/Horn for Leak-Day.md` l.73, l.77) agree.
- **Structure rulings.** Eleven pieces, ten per run, three duo finales, ten embedded opportunities: all match Board l.91. Hours add up to 10.25 as stated.
- **Ages and spans.** Thirty in 1360; nineteen when Maro took him on (Red Brass Papers l.62); the ring at twenty-six, four years ago; "six years" carrying the Bench; Steffen copying him for eight years since he was eleven. These agree with one another, provided his arena career ran on for two or three years after the Lio bout (see the open question on "nine years of work").
- **Stale names and dates.** None. No renamed name and no historical year appears in any Tavio file.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Why "Fifth" Ring?** The gauntlet has "three filed championship proof rings" and the hidden insert carries "a full fourth relation" (`T/The False Ring.md` l.88–89, l.110). Nothing in the slice explains the number five.
2. **"Nine years of work, six of them clean"** (`T/The Fair Defeat.md` l.212). Starting at nineteen (1349) and cheating at twenty-six (1356) gives six clean years and then about three as a champion on a rigged title, ending near 1358–59. Is that the intended reading, and when did the Ring evidence surface?
3. **Tuition-bond.** Canon says entry to the Vortharan schools is "by talent-test and tuition-bond… years of post-mastery service" (`Lives of Vorthara.md` l.26). Tavio's files mention no bond. Was it discharged, or is it part of what Medori bought?
4. **Who bans him?** "A permanent arena ban" (`T/Tavio Companion Campaign.md` l.229) needs an issuer in a city without common law. A Crucible panel is the likely answer; it is not said.
5. **Adjacent file, outside this slice.** `40 Factions/60 Secondary Chains/Red Brass Bench.md` l.52 says a finding "in one member city voids the warranty… everywhere". The Crucible has no member cities and the Concord has no common court (Board l.68). Worth a look by whoever audits factions.

### What the new history offers

- The Age of Held Proofs (1290–1360): the great powers "do not pull [a proof] out to see what happens" (`CR/History/70 Later History and Present Day/The Age of Held Proofs.md` l.16 onward). Hella running a proof in public, and the Bench spending everything to stop one examiner's paperwork, is that age in miniature.
- The Blackwater pit of 1290, where a tally-keeper read a dead chaplain's count to the widows, and the miners' twenty-three-year war: a ready echo for Ottir, Sesk and worker stop authority at Highlake.
- The Muster's hunger: "each repair demanded more bodies, armour and grain" (`The Unending Muster.md` l.28). The forge plate that treats "every failed part as something to be remade" is the same demand.
- The Kept Doors of 410 BR, "the oldest refuge oath Vortharan households still point to" (Sestaran Canon Register l.50): something for Maro's "Come home".

### Checked and holds

- **Peoples.** `culture: ["Ahl-e-Ahd", "Iqashari"]` and "Origin: Iqashar" match Board l.66: "Iqashar is the imperial center". His formation in "exact relation, witnessed procedure, technical petition" matches `The Iqashari.md` l.16–18: Iqashari "dominate imperial administration, long-distance finance… and the educated professions", with "formal debate, and fiercely competitive education".
- **Names.** "Zafir ibn Samad" and "Samira bint Nahal" follow the patronymic form canon itself uses (Zuhayr ibn Qays, Rumaitha bint Mujalib, Nadir ibn Haraz). Both names stand in the Locked Rulings (Board l.91 for Zafir). No name in the slice is on the rename list.
- **Slavery and humane instruments.** Canon: enslaved people "may petition over food, injury… withheld manumission… Such rights save lives but do not make ownership just" (`Status, Slavery, and Manumission` l.26); "the institution itself remains genuinely evil" (l.38). The game says the same of his instruments (`Comp/Zafir ibn Samad.md` l.119–121). Canon's "sound… fair… predatory instrument" (`Ahl-e-Ahd Cultural Philosophy Overview.md` l.24) is the ground his error stands on.
- **Enslaved copyist in a household.** Canon: "Enslaved and name-bound people live inside households" (`Households and Daily Life in the Covenant.md`). Agrees.

Its dependence on "witness, seal, or local anchor" echoes the Court of Breaches' "no page, no lodging" (`Historical Spine of the Covenant.md` l.66).
- **No souls.** Canon: "custody of a name never touches soul-routing" (Ahl-e-Ahd Canon Register l.71). The Binder rules and Board l.71 and l.88 agree with every Zafir file.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Samira's legal status.** She "escaped" (`Comp/Zafir ibn Samad.md` l.127). Canon's Court of Breaches ruled in 1326 that people whose ledgers were burned "remained bound in the Living Register"; "Iqashar calls them absconded" (`Historical Spine of the Covenant.md` l.70). Is Samira absconded, freed by a breach order (canon allows freedom "ordered for breach"), or released by her own inversion? This decides why an Iqashari court treats her instruction as something to authenticate.
2. **Why the court kept the inversion alive.** Canon's Court of Breaches voided Suhrab's renunciation "as coerced" in 1190. The game calls Samira's act coercive and still has a judgement recording its effects. Is that deliberate (the court could bound it but not dissolve it)?
3. **Copied restraints working in Grenzburg.** The Nine-Lever Chainworks "copied a family of Zafir's old living-person restraints into its safety interlocks" (`Z/The Ninth Lever.md` l.71), and Chain House holds "living-person plates". Canon says "Metaphysical enforcement is expensive" (`Covenant Economy and the Caravan World.md` l.28) and needs a witness and seal. Who made the copies operable in an Eclessian duchy, and what law do they break there?
4. **Zafir's confession.** As an Iqashari he would be of the Witnessed Seal. No file says so, and none says how a Covenant jurist stands in a Sanctum-loyal duchy.
5. **Secrecy rule.** `Medori Family - Authorial Truth.md` forbids public notes from stating the family's Covenant legal ancestry. The Zafir files do not state it. If ZAF-02 is fixed by leaning on Medori's skill with Covenant forms, keep to "foreign rumours" wording.

### What the new history offers

- The Vault of Names split in 1059; the Living Register at Iqashar; a Custodian who has no name (Ahl-e-Ahd Canon Register l.82). This is the real home of every name Zafir ever bound.
- Rab Yequtiel's *What the Register Keeps*: "a register holds the name and lets the person go" (l.71). It is the comfort a humane jurist would have been taught, and canon says it stopped being true in some cases after 885.
- The Emptying of the Benches: release needs a living witness, and in the Sweat the witnesses died first. A reason for his "formal release conditions" to matter, and to fail.
- The lending house that held the Custodian's own name from 1003 to 1011 and was burned for it (`The Qahirid Suzerainty and Iqashar.md` l.39). A precedent for what Iqashar thinks of a bank holding a jurist's cord.
- The people of the Clause and the Ruling on Parties (885–886): "a pure construct [is] party to nothing" (l.74). A native Covenant argument for a Binder craft limited to non-conscious forms.
