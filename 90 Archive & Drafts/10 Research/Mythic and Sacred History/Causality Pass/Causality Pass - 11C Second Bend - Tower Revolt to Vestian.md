# Causality Pass — 11C Second Bend: Tower Revolt to Vestian

> [!note] Record of applied bends
> 14 November 2026, under the [[Causality Pass - Direction|Direction]] and rules 6 to 8 of the [[Real-History Concordance]]. [[Causality Pass - 10C Bending Kellovar, Halmenth and the Last Fortress|Pass 10C]] made the mechanisms of this era native. An adversarial read found that the skeletons had survived: the same people met the same fates in the same order, and the Tower Revolt ran at a constant five years before its analogue. This record breaks those skeletons in the owned reader articles and in [[Signs and Proof - Authorial Truth]], and gives exact wording for every file outside this writer's ownership. Isfir, the Passion, the Companions and Aquilan's conversion are untouched by design. [[Vestian - Compulsory Faith and the Long Diaspora]] was read and not edited, and everything it relies on stands.

The nine kept are the ones the brief protects: Tervan's green lamps, the Forty, the Concord's restitution, the deathbed baptism, and the five Vestian beats that belong to the other developer's draft or were bent in 10C.

### The new sequence

1. **The altar before the war.** Since 0 AR every offering has smoked and the custodians of the three old houses have said aloud each morning that the stones are hot. By the charter the governor supplies the emperor's offering and its wood, and it goes up first.
2. **Spring 61, the Nine Mornings.** After three days of rain the emperor's bull will not burn. The Keeper has it laid again each day. The war houses say the Lord has spoken against the emperor. The augur Dessan Vole enters a fraud; on the tenth morning Governor Halen Corvath, sitting at the pilgrimage tribunal, demands the wood-carriers and sends soldiers up the stair with a bull and army pitch. They kill the custodians and the crowd behind them (the towers count 206). The garrison is driven out within the month.
3. **61–64, the city that kept its charter.** Corvath shuts the pilgrim roads; the Dawn Portion stops. The Keeper keeps every clause; the war captain Zevad bar Tirzan holds the walls. In autumn 62 the wood-carrier Gedor bar Ammi confesses that he wet the emperor's wood from the second morning, and Zevad's men kill him that night.
4. **62–64, the Dawn Tower.** The legate Karsen Maruth goes first to Shahar, to shut the road by which Qiryath could be fed or emptied. The 10C sequence stands, re-dated: gatehouse taken, two years of digging, the Red Star's "survey" dropping the gallery roofs, the bench's terms in spring 64, Eshkar's refusal, Yaltah's nine days, the broken keys, the killing in the lower halls.
5. **Autumn 64 to spring 67, Qiryath.** Maruth drops the cistern galleries by the same survey. Every bench that holds keys to Qiryath has learned from Shahar what receiving costs, and none will open for people. One, never named, will take what cannot be questioned: over two nights the Keeper sends the vessels and the custodians' books through. The gate has not opened since.
6. **Spring 67, the fire.** Vole lights the emperor's bull on the altar with the sanctuary's own cedar. The house burns. The treasury rooms hold shelves; the gate chamber is swept and the gate does nothing. The Keeper dies under torture at Arcelon the next winter without naming a bench.
7. **The empty litters.** Maruth's triumph is a joke in the street. He is the eastern commander who loses the succession to Tavian Dascen in 78.
8. **The Litter Debt, direct rule and exile.** Outcomes unchanged: charter annulled, garrison, diaspora, sacrifice ended, burial slope lost.

Authorial truth is in Signs and Proof §15 and §16. The first morning was green wood and rain; the other eight were Gedor. Nothing in the war was an answer and the Silence did not change. The vessels went lawfully to a consenting bench that this pass leaves unnamed. Why Qiryath's gate is silent stays unruled, as the Keepers' "silent, not dead" requires.

**Victims and disputes kept.** The dead on the stair; the burned villages and captives of the provincial campaign; the crushed in Shahar's galleries; the killing in its lower halls and the legate's claim that the rebels killed their own; the factional murders in Qiryath; thirst, hunger and disease in the refuge halls; the people killed or displayed trying to escape; the killing in the precinct; the captives sorted for sale. New: the flogging to death of Gedor's brother, Gedor's murder, and the Keeper's death under torture.

## Notes for the Vestian draft's developer

Nothing in [[Vestian - Compulsory Faith and the Long Diaspora]] was edited. Its fixed points stand: accession 377, the Velos register to the bishops in 377, the Dissolution 383, the last petition, the eastern war of 383–384, the Profession 385, the Second Silence, Raveth, the tower orders, the Empty Charter and *before the Mesakha*, death 398. Details that have moved under it:

1. **"After Lucar's defeat near Kellovar" and "dependants sold under Lucar".** The sold are children whom fort stewards were given to deliver at the fords and sold instead, entering them as delivered; receipts exist. The children actually delivered are beyond the rivers and beyond Vestian's law. Nobody was admitted at the forts, and no grain was traded for anyone.
2. **"The tribute rolls identified households."** They are the rolls of the **Litter Debt**, the head-charge laid in 67 for the sanctuary vessels that went through Qiryath's gate. Either wording works.
3. **"Astrion's abandoned survey and its petitions."** Abandoned because the Jirahar crews refused to work under the Red Star's wardens, not after a wall collapse. The survey's lists of cistern-keepers, houses, fields and graves are what the officers reuse.
4. **"Kin who had left after 70 AR."** Read "after 64 and 67 AR": the Shahar households crossed in 64 and Qiryath fell in 67.
5. **"Officers who had served under Astrion."** This is now literal. Astrion survived the Emperor's Cut and was emperor east of the depot line under the Accord until c. 372; Tellan, his brother, commanded in the field throughout and in his own right afterwards. That answers the draft's open question about the eastern command's leader ("Pellan") and about Savel, who was never emperor and ceded nothing.
6. **"The challenge of 355–357"** is the stand-off that ended in the Accord of the Two Standards.
7. **Two kinds of sacred authority.** Until c. 372 there were also two emperors. Vestian's reading of Kellovar is stronger for it.
8. **The Purge's edict.** Astrion revived it as the Entering; Lucar repealed the Entering and left the edict on the books, which is how it reaches the bishops in 383 and Nasriel in 411.
9. **Cassian's "household executions"** are now one execution, the High Augur's. The son was ordained by force and baptised his father. A compelled ordination at the root of the Cassian Wager sits well beside the Second Silence, if the draft wants it.
10. **Qiryath's gate "stayed shut".** It last opened in 67, to send the vessels out. The clearance of c. 396 found no vessels, and the draft may use that or ignore it.
