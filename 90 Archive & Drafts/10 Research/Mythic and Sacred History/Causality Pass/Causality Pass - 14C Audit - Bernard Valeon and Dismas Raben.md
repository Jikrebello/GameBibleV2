### Open questions for the creator

1. **The witnessed-stores rule at Froststep.** Canon says Long Watch "relief inventories require witnesses" (L54). The inquiry found "the… store count [was] reasonable" although one man's unrecorded removals falsified it for a month (`The Froststep Evacuation.md` L37–39, L57). Was the rule in force then, or is it what the house learnt?
2. **Drink no sworn water.** The rewrite gave the Kedravan Long Watch houses a rule that "a well, cistern or cup offered under a bargain binds whoever drinks", and parties that "carry their own water" and read well-curbs aloud (L48). Bernard never does either, and two of his quests open cisterns while sponsors offer help on conditions. Should the habit appear?
3. **A Thalmyric kindling in a Sanctum-loyal city.** The Kindling Question is what split the churches in 1015. A public kindling by a Thalmyric layman draws no relic court, Lantern examiner or Reliquarian in `Embers Without Excuse.md`. Canon also notes that "a wonder shows that something acted. It does not show what acted" (`Signs and Proof…` L29). Is the absence of any examination intended?
4. **A kit of repeatable kindlings.** Bernard has modest kindling "from recruitment" (`Bernard Long Watch Greatsword Package.md` L83–88) and Last Ember as a capstone. Canon's Thalmyric lesson is that the Fire "answers whom it will and never for the asking" (`Eclessia vs Thalmyric Paladins.md` L38). The Locked Ruling that "Paladin techniques require White-Flame coherence" covers this; I record it only so the wording can be kept on the right side of that line.
5. **Grobi dependants as a class.** Beyond Urra, the hospice admits "captured Grobi dependents" under guard, and other game files (Fenn Road Exchange, Frostfenn March, the Campaign Spine) use the same idea. Canon has breeding hearths that hosts abandon, and also "Human captives remain prey". Who guards a Grobi young among human patients, and does Sister Hanne's Church accept that care is owed?
6. **The Long Watch mark in his kit.** `A Blade Freely Given.md` L85 finds "a Kedravan Long Watch issue mark" under the repairs. Canon says "dismissal ends office and access to common property" (L32). How did the piece leave the house?
7. **Tracked or untracked.** `The Wagon in the Reeds.md` L190 says "Bernard still joins the later school trip"; `Bernard Companion Campaign.md` L159 says the stable colleague "does not privately lead the children's expedition". The frontmatter suggests the first applies only once the campaign is tracked. Confirm.
8. **Routes across the river.** The pursuit in `Little Things Missing.md` L183 runs from Old Market (east bank) down to Lantern Market (west bank) with no mention of Bridgehold, the "only permanent crossing"; the residue column bound for Tannward (east bank) meets West Refuge Hall (Longlake Ward, west bank) "across its route" (`The Open Hospice.md` L279). I did not read the city adjacency note, so these are questions, not findings.
9. **Names near the Order of the Lantern.** The rewrite made the Eclessian Order of the Lantern prominent. "Hospice of the Lantern Road" is Thalmyric and unrelated. Is the nearness wanted?

### What the new history offers

- The Kedravan refuge rule and the reader called "the Nikandros" for the day (`Thalmyric Military Orders.md` L48): a habit about what one may accept, held by a man whose trouble is what he takes.
- The lamp-houses: every lamp sealed away for safety, "their keepers sold the oil", and the household fires found dead (`The Holy Fire.md` L46). His own church's history of the hidden store.
- Thalmyric suspicion of visible sanctity in the living, the elder told "to wash his wrists and resume kneading bread" (`The Making of Saints.md` L30), against the West's hot charismatic piety. It gives Bernard native words for refusing the city's reading.
- The Kedravan monasteries that "refused the forfeited land offered to them" after Qarath's Well (`Principality of Kedrava Overview.md` L22).
- Saint Solana of the Last Ember, who carried a live coal "in a covered clay firepot" (`The Twelve Companions…` L114), and the Peace of the Bent Oak with its bell-law, for the road-school's lessons.

1. **Purchased service from a Korrheim court.** The rewrite says "the first cantons were made by slaves": convicts in the counts' mines who rose, swore the Oath of the Shafts in 1124 and still keep a liberation feast (`Confederation of Korrheim Overview.md` L22). A canton court that offers a convict "long imprisonment or purchased service" (`PH/The Korrheim Rockfall.md` L51), and lets that service be sold abroad, sits oddly beside that founding. Is the irony intended, or should the mechanism be a canton service contract?
2. **Which canton, which rite, what standing.** Canon makes "the canton rolls… the birthright" and separates citizens from the resident non-citizen class (`Lives…` L20). Dismas's canton, confession and standing are never given.
3. **The three voices.** By the fixed record the driver was "killed by the fall itself" and one passenger was "thrown clear" (`PH/The Korrheim Rockfall.md` L38–40). That leaves two people alive under the stone, yet he "heard people alive under the stone. He counted three voices" (L43), one of them a "him" (`CMP-06/The Easy Ambush.md` L119). Is the third voice the passenger thrown clear?
4. **Does she already suspect people?** Her claim asks for a refund "if the road company is ever found" (`IWT/The Stonefall Papers.md` L44). If "road company" means the band, she knows people did it, which weakens "she may not know that a person caused it".
5. **The dead and their rites.** In current canon the unsung dead walk, and a yard unsent for a year and a day changes hands (`The Korrheim Accord.md` L47, L70). The papers mention a burial subscription for Anke Bekk and nothing for Hedric Vane. Were both sung, and in which rite?
6. **The 1355 route book.** It comes "out of a Lowlands cache" (`CMP-06/The Name on the Charge.md` L106), though he was arrested in Korrheim and brought to Grenzburg under contract a year and a half later. How did it travel?
7. **Coin and weekdays.** "Pounds" and "shillings" appear only in `IWT/The Stonefall Papers.md`; canon names no coinage anywhere. Canon gives "a seven-day week ending in the Lord's Day" and no other day-names; "Tuesday" and "Thursday" are the game's.
8. **Residue and the package boundary.** `CMP-06/Dismas Quarry-Wise Package.md` L66 says "no technique interacts with residue… in a privileged way"; the finale has him spoil a dead formation's road (`CMP-06/The Road That Answers.md` L118). Presumably ordinary craft, but the two lines should not seem to disagree.
9. **The Fence route and the river.** The Lask stall is in Old Market (east bank); the laundry loft and watch platform are at Quarry Steps in Lantern Ward (west bank), and Bridgehold is the only permanent crossing. `CMP-06/The Decorative Fence.md` L101–104, L115 treat them as one roofline. Not checked against the city adjacency note.
10. **Anke Lask, Bekka Lask, Anke Bekk.** The family he saves and the girl he killed share their names. Deliberate?

### What the new history offers

- The cantons' founding: convict slaves, the Oath of the Shafts, and a liberation feast at which "the names of the Cold Way's victims are read". Their liberator "confessed before the canton assembly" and went into exile (`Confederation of Korrheim Overview.md` L22). A count of the dead read aloud, and a public confession nobody compelled, are already Korrheim customs.
- Falkendorf (1340) and the Night Count (1342) fall when he is fourteen and sixteen: commissioners sitting up in churchyards, and Accord Wardens escorting families who "cross the borders with their dead on handcarts" (`The Korrheim Accord.md` L32–34, L70).
- The Unsworn, left outside the Accord and hunted by both confessions (L62), and Virelian runaways who "walk south to Grenzburg" (`Present-Day Courts and Crowns (1360 AR).md` L33): people a free guide network would actually carry.
- A world in which a burial left unsung has consequences gives the two shillings for "the girl Bekk" more weight than a form can show.
- The standing rule since Falkendorf that canton companies may not take "both sides of one war", honoured "with lawyerly creativity" (`Lives…` L20): his native model of a rule that is lawful and evaded.
