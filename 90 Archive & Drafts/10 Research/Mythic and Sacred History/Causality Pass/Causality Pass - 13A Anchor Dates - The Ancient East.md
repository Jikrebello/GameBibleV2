# Causality Pass — 13A Anchor Dates: The Ancient East

> [!note] Status — 15 November 2026
> Research for [[Causality Pass - 13 Anchor Date Proposals]]. **Nothing on this page has been applied**; every date in the vault is as it was.
> - 96 offsets in the tables below were reproduced by machine, with four alternates given in brackets.
> - **Rows 11 and 12 of Full are dropped** (the invasion to 500 BR, the Narrows to 497). They were held until the neighbouring stretch reported. [[Causality Pass - 13B Anchor Dates - The Atherian West|13B]] moves neither Qarzeth's fall (520) nor the veterans' return (491), so the sea wars' line stays whatever is done inside it, and the two rows would only un-match intervals.
> - **The first Gateway war** is proposed for c. 674–660 BR in 13B. Under Full it then opens the year before Eryndor dies (673). The Marenthe seat keeps c. 680–672 with the rewording in open question 3, and the sentence in *Wars of the Inheritance* that has Caleran "already fighting" that war about 650 needs rewording.
> - Record 13 recommends Full, rows 1 to 10, and gives Minimal as the lighter choice.
> - "The brief" in the text below is the instruction this research was written to: find every sequence that still sits a near-constant number of years from its real analogue, count what each date move would cost, check ages and reigns, and propose a Minimal and a Full tier without changing what happens.

## The ancient east (c. 960–490 BR)

Research and design only. Nothing in the vault was edited.

- **Offset** = BR year minus BC year. "c." dates are taken at the year stated. A **line** is three or more beats of one sequence within 8 years of the same offset.
- Names are the ones in canon after 15 November 2026 (Ilbarun II, Tavrad I, Kazren I, Zadran Vezh, Belyra, Velanthe, Marenthe).
- Line counts come from `yearcount.py` plus a context filter of my own over the same zones. "Canon files" means `01 Canon Reference`; "drafts" means the older working copies under `90 Archive & Drafts/10 Research/Mythic and Sacred History/`, which the helper also counts as editable.
- No BR year in this stretch is cited in the Grenzburg or Caleran game material (play zone 0 for every anchor checked with `--need BR`).

### What pins the dates

**Calendar and the sign**

- The Jirahar count from the Great Prophet's death, c. 2165 BR (`Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating).md`, "The Dating System"; `The Glass Sojourn.md`; `The Four Deaths of the Prophet`). I found no Jirahar-count year number anywhere in the vault, so nothing converts. Not moved.
- The Withholding, 743 to c. 654. Its length is stated: "those eighty-nine years" (`Historical Spine of the Jirahar.md`), "about 89 years (743–c. 654)" (the Chronology's Sanity Checks), "nearly ninety years" (`Historical Atlas of Qiryath before Atheria.md`), "some ninety years" and "more than seven centuries before the Redeemer" (`The Traveller's Reckoning of the Known World.md`). Present-day Jirahar cite "the cool stones of c. 654 BR" (`The Jirahar and the Future.md`, `Theological and Mystical Traditions.md`); `Qiryath Reqa.md` and `Great Tower Revolt and the Second Destruction.md` lean on it. Both ends stay.
- 742 is welded to 743: the siege lasts "two harvests" and the sign fails in its first winter. Arezan is "born around 742 BR, the year Arqesh destroyed First Qiryath" (`Vashar and the Crown of Roads and Dawns.md`).
- c. 660 is tied to c. 654: "Six years later the stones went cool" (Torque Register), "Some six years after the consecration" (`Vashar…`). The Concordance states "Destruction to consecration is 82 years".
- c. 824 to 743: the Keresh woman's right was last used "eighty years earlier" (`Arqesh - River Cities, Kings and Deportation.md`). Untouched by anything below.

Full moves 684 and 687–686, and its rows 11 and 12 move 495 and the invasion's share of 497. Those were fixed for that writer; moving them is the creator's decision.

What Minimal achieves:

- **Q.** The four events sit at 156, 151, 135 and 144. No three are within 8. 690 → 673 is 17 years against 1; 673 → c. 660 is 13 against 22.
- **A.** 91, 78, 79. The run is gone; 28 years against 15. (c. 718 was chosen so that the result also holds if the source's civil war is counted from 631: 718 − 631 = 87, and 87, 78, 79 span 9.)
- **S.** Two of the five matching intervals go: 511 → 502 is 9 against 4, and 502 → 497 is 5 against 10. The line stays.

What it cannot do, and why:

- **Eryndor.** His source did in 13 years (336–323) what the test needs spread over 31. Six main beats avoid sharing an 8-year window in threes only if the first and last offsets differ by at least 18, which needs a reign of at least 13 + 18 = 31 years. He reigns 22. No one or two dates change that; only Full's re-spacing does.
- **Sea wars.** The revolt cannot be earlier than 519 (offset at most 20), and the withdrawal is fixed at 491 (offset 12). The King's Bread must fall within a year or two of the withdrawal, because an army that has lost its granary cannot sit for longer. So the revolt, the Bread and the withdrawal are always within 8 of one another, whatever is done inside the stretch.
- **Q's echoes.** 758, 746, 743, 704, 671 and 638 still fall at 145–155. They are reigns and a prelude hung on 742 and 690, and they cannot be cleared while those two stay (see Not proposed).

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Mehrava at eighteen.** Full makes her eighteen, not seven, when her father dies, and Demeran's agents hunt a marriageable heiress. 11B1 chose "seven and living" on purpose. Is eighteen acceptable? If not, Eryndor's line cannot be broken and Minimal is the answer.
2. **Four years at the seep, seven on the river throne.** Full spends its extra years there because the setting already supplies the reasons. The alternative is to move the whole late phase eleven years as one block (the bow in 677–676, the wood in 676–675, one season at the seep in 674). That keeps "the only season" and "for three years" word for word and still clears the six main beats (373, 368, 363, 359, 349, 350), but it leaves the wood two years before the death against the source's three. Which reads better?
3. **The Marenthe seat (c. 680–672).** Canon puts it "after Eryndor's death". Under Full he dies in 673. Either reword it (Caleran buys ships from his unpaid western commanders while he lies at the seep) and keep the dates, or move it to c. 672–664 and tell the Compact's writers. I would reword.
4. **The Decree's cause under Minimal.** Tying it to Kassur III's death adds a causal link that canon does not state. Acceptable? Under Full the question does not arise.
5. **The sea wars.** Their line can be broken only from outside: by moving Qarzeth's fall and the loose years (520–513), or the veterans' return and Damar (491, 488). Does either neighbouring stretch plan to move those? If so, redo this sequence then.
6. **746.** Do you count the demand as the analogue of 597? If so it stands at 149 beside 742 and 690, and only 744 clears it.
7. **The crossing.** Moving it from 702 to 703 would make the six-beat result hold on either reading of the analogues (see Clock after). It costs about 27 more lines and changes "three summers on the coast". Not proposed; say if wanted.
8. **The drafts.** About two-fifths of the lines to change are in older working copies under `90 Archive & Drafts/10 Research/`. Update them, or leave them as history?
9. **Neram bar Elior.** Is he the son of Elior bar Hadan, chief guardian in 746? If he is, he was probably born before about 725, and moving the Decree to 673 sends him to supervise the rebuilding in his fifties or sixties, not his forties or fifties. His age at the cool stones (c. 654) does not change.
