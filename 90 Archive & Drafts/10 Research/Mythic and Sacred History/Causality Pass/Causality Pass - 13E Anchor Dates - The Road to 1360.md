## The road to 1360 (1232–1360 AR)

**Verdict first.** This clock can be loosened but not broken by moving dates. Ten of the Reformation's beats sit between −226 and −212, and nine of them cannot leave the range −233 to −212 while the Accord stays at 1343, the Sweat stays where it is and the order of events stands. A range that wide can hold six beats with no three within 8 years of each other; it has to hold ten. What dates can do is break the opening run (funerals, book-trial, rising), fix every interval between consecutive beats, and remove three life-date tells. That needs the founding event to move, and the only shape that works is a **long breaking of seven years** (1297 → 1290). Nothing in this stretch changes what 1360 reads as: that comes from the Accord and the 1360 board, which are fixed.

Offsets are AR year minus AD year. A "line" is three or more beats of one sequence within 8 years of each other. Real-history dates are from memory and were not looked up.

### The three shapes

**Why no shape clears the band.** Each beat can move only so far before it breaks the order or a floor:

None can reach −203 or later (the Lantern would need a charter in 1337 or after, the Council an opening in 1342 or after), and none but the Council's opening can go below −233. Ten beats in 21 years of offset; six is the most that fit with no three within 8. A machine search of the order-preserving datings (conclave 1258–1276, funerals 1276–1299, Risings begun by 1317, Council closed by 1331) found none with no three beats within 8; in the narrower ranges of the table it found none even at 5. With the Risings held at 1306–1329 the best it found was **four beats within 8** under a long breaking and **five** with 1297 kept; today it is seven. The datings that reach those floors carry tells of their own (the synod one year after the funerals, so seventeen winters of distraint before the rising; Ferro dead at 53 to 55, Calvin's age), so they are not proposed.

**The opening run decides the shape.** The source packs theses, trial and rising into seven years. Their three offsets stay within 8 unless the rising begins at least 16 years after the funerals (13 if the synod sits the year after them). The Risings are pinned at 1306, so the funerals must be 1290 or earlier.

### Recommendation

Take shape (a), the long breaking by seven years, at the **Minimal** tier. It is the only move that breaks the Theses–Worms–Peasants' War run, it makes every interval between consecutive beats unlike the source's, and it removes three life-date tells (Severin, Vetti, Meshalem), for about 88 mostly mechanical line edits and no change to any event, order or game file. Do not expect it to change the re-read's sentence: the four dates it named will sit 212 to 227 below their originals instead of 212 to 226, because the Accord sets that clock and cannot move. The Falkendorf move is worth adding only if the endgame reading in Line 2 bothers the creator.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Is a loosened clock enough?** Dates cannot do more on this stretch. Breaking it needs either a free Accord date or event work that takes two to four beats out of recognition (the bought conclave, the rising, the Council or the chartered order are the candidates). Both are outside this brief.
2. **May Alessior be alive at the funerals?** If his death stays at 1296 and only the funerals move to 1288–1290, about 29 lines are saved and the ten quiet years have a stronger reason (the Primarch who canonised Gerlind will not prosecute people for singing their dead). It reverses the order of two events and means rewriting the Fortieth margin and two sentences in `Freeflame Reformation.md`, so I have not proposed it.
3. **Where should the Age of Held Proofs begin?** The 25 label lines can follow the funerals (1290–1360) or be re-anchored to the first held proof, the psalter vaulted at Aldersbruck (1299–1360). The Chronology's own section already starts at 1290.
4. **Present-day dates on the −212 line** (listed, not designed around): Alistair III's election and Celestin IX's death, 1354; Empress Vasilia's death, 1357; Prince Nikandros's death, 1358; Marzuq III's accession, 1349; Virelia's pending election against 1572–1573. These, with the Accord, are what make 1360 read as the 1570s.
5. **Marzuq II (1301–1349).** His accession is not a present-day fact, but it cannot leave the line: he must reign by 1304 to hire the Iron Horde and Marzuq I must reign to 1300. Only 11F's proposal to split the reign would change it.
6. **Severin's four years and the Pyre.** The last Savonarola interval. Lengthening the Vigil moves the duchy's founding date and one game line; moving the Pyre back to 1342 costs about 45 lines and puts Fiorenzia's crisis before the Accord.
7. **Falkendorf as Passau.** Does the creator read the endgame that way? If not, Full adds nothing.
8. **Ansgar's age.** At 30 he is a very young lecturer. Born c. 1255 he dies at 35, but "when Ansgar was seven" must become "twelve".
9. **Meshalem** belongs to the Jirahar thread; confirm this stretch may move his death.
