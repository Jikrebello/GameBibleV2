# Causality Pass — 13D Anchor Dates: Two Crowns and High Noon

> [!note] Status — 15 November 2026
> Research for [[Causality Pass - 13 Anchor Date Proposals]]. **Nothing on this page has been applied**; every date in the vault is as it was.
> - 50 offsets in the tables below were reproduced by machine, and the sorted runs under "Clock after" were re-added by hand.
> - **Record 13 recommends rows 1 to 5 and row 9, and leaves rows 6 to 8 as an option.** The High Noon line (1121, 1214, 1225) is three landmark dates a century apart; no three *successive* beats of that sequence share an offset. Breaking it moves Qarath's Well, halves the War of the Seal ("ten years in a monastery cell"), touches three present-day files, and has no year to spare. Record 13 sets the choice out for the creator.
> - **Record 13 adds a re-spacing of the Nikandran reigns after 1225** (Nikandros I's death to 1249, Evandros's to 1296), which answers residual 5 and the first half of open question 7. The line this page found running on through 1263 and 1290 is where a reader can in fact date the next event.
> - Row 2 improves one Grenzburg date: with Drossfeld in 1027 the abbey of 1028 is founded a year after Brauvalis forms, not thirteen years before.
> - "The brief" in the text below is the instruction this research was written to: find every sequence that still sits a near-constant number of years from its real analogue, count what each date move would cost, check ages and reigns, and propose a Minimal and a Full tier without changing what happens.

## Two Crowns and High Noon (962–1232 AR)

Conventions used below. **Offset** = AR year minus AD year. Offsets are sorted and every **run** of three neighbours is measured; a run that spans 8 or less is a **line**. "Soft" beats are echoes I list with their offsets but do not count, and I say why. Real-history years are the standard reference dates, given from memory and not looked up in this session. Nothing in the vault was edited.

### Open questions for the creator

1. **Will you move Qarath?** Every arrangement that breaks Line 4 moves it. It is the largest single cost (34 lines in 26 files for the year alone) and it reaches three present-day files, including the rule "since 1215 no emperor signs a Covenant instrument", which would read "since 1220".
2. **How long should the War of the Seal be?** The Concordance and the Torque Register both quote "ten years in a monastery cell". Full leaves five. There is a third arrangement with fourteen: crown Caedros III in 1124 (+148), Funeral 1155 (+130), Qarath 1210 (+139). It has the same seven offsets as Full, so no line. Its gains: Caedros III reigns 31 years against 49; Qarath to the Poured Cup is 15 against 10; "by 1120" needs no rewording; Caldria's 1217 and the "c. 1220" legends stay. Its costs: Funeral to Qarath stays 55 against 46, as now; Markellos's accession moves to about 1203 and he is 45 at the Poured Cup; Nikandros is nearly 80 at his death in 1263 unless the next stretch shortens his reign.
3. **Is Drossfeld's year part of the fixed board?** No border, ruler or state changes, but every 1360 border suit cites that afternoon and `The Kingdom of Aurelia Overview.md` cites 1041.
4. **Do you count the Examination of 1006 as the coronation's shadow?** If not, Kaltfurt need not move, and the crown's lines break with rows 2 and 3 alone at slightly different years (Drossfeld 1030, Wolmar's death 1036: +187, +196, +205, +207, +215, +217; runs 18, 11, 10, 10). I count it, because 975 + 31 = 1006 and 768 + 32 = 800.
5. **Adalmar at seventy-six.** Row 1 makes him seven years older at every beat. A gentler version is Kaltfurt 963 and acclamation 964 (+196): he is 73 at his coronation, the runs are 12, 11, 10, 10, 11, and acclamation to Examination is 42 against 32, just inside a third.
6. **Residual 1.** The only complete cure for the Adalmar clock is structural: the king who drowned Wolfsmere and the emperor crowned after the Split would have to stop being the same man, or the Two Winters and the vaulting would have to move. I recommend neither.
7. **For whoever takes 1232–1360.** 1263, 1290 and c. 1329 sit at +145, +147 and +149 from the deaths of Alexios I, John II and Manuel I, and the reign lengths match within two years. c. 1329 is Caedros IV's coronation and cannot move. If it is judged recognisable, it makes a run of 5 with the Poured Cup and Qarath under Full (+144, +148, +149). I searched for an arrangement of this stretch that clears both it and the Refusal of the Lamp and found none; one of the two would have to be accepted as a kept echo.
8. **Row 4 loses a synchronism.** Tamim's accession and the Impounding now share 1029. Putting the Impounding in 1033 with the Audit would keep one, at +218 and a run of 9.
