## From the Tower Revolt to the Reconquest (0–1052 AR)

Research and design only; nothing in the vault was edited. Offsets are AR year minus AD year. Names are the ones now in canon (after the renames of 15 November).

Two terms are used throughout. A **line** is the brief's: three or more beats of one sequence whose offsets lie within about 8 years of each other. A **run** is a line whose beats follow one another, with no beat of another offset between them. A run is what lets a reader date the *next* event; a line that is not a run only shows up when somebody tabulates the whole sequence.

**One finding governs the rest.** The strict test (no three beats within 8 years of one offset) can be met in the Tower Revolt and in the Covenant's rise, and both designs below meet it. It cannot be met between 288 and 398 AR. Twenty-seven dated beats with a recognisable analogue sit between Cassian's acclamation and Vestian's death; 292 and 377–398 are pinned at −20 and at −2 to +5; every beat between them must therefore carry an offset between about −30 and +11, and that span has room for ten beats at two per 8-year band. So in that stretch the designs break every *run* and say plainly which lines remain. The same holds for the imperial side of the Long Frontier, where the sequence is 500 years long.
