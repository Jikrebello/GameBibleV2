# Great Saints and Sinners — Collision and Selection Review

## Review Ruling

The Primarch material is useful as a library of **eighteen distinct figure grammars and two distinct absence grammars**. It is not useful as a literal roster, divine family, fixed set of factions, universal rebellion, or equal division between good and evil.

This review selects fourteen visible World Egg exemplars for provisional placement and defers four named-source leads. The two missing designations become separate erased-person mysteries. No selected lead enters canon, chronology or [[The Historical Seed Garden]].

## Scoring Method

Each named lead receives a score from one to five in seven categories:

| Code | Category | Five means |
|---|---|---|
| D | distinctiveness | supplies a historical or supernatural function not already prominent |
| H | historical fit | occupies a genuine pressure or gap without forcing chronology |
| O | origin strength | supports a humble mortal beginning before the later scale is visible |
| A | active use | supports bounded saintly or Sinner action beyond biography |
| T | trace value | leaves useful 1360 AR evidence, institutions or conflict |
| C | collision separation | can be distinguished cleanly from existing people and beds |
| M | metaphysical fit | preserves soul, commission, office, Mask, Patron and relic boundaries |

Scores rank the first planting only. A deferred lead is not inferior fiction; it currently adds less than the material it risks duplicating.

## Deferred Leads

### Jaghatai Khan grammar

Retain the source study for a later road-and-freedom pass. Current planting already contains wandering duos, wrong-road founders, Menhir paths, pursuit beyond mapped roads and sleeping return expectations. A future culture dossier may provide a genuinely distinct mobile political pressure.

### Leman Russ grammar

Retain for a later war-founder and repentance pass. The sleeping champion, Menhir hunt, returning standard and severe military saint fields are presently crowded. The valuable unplanted element is not the wolf aesthetic but the executioner recognizing that obedient excess remains his responsibility.

### Horus grammar

Retain charisma, trusted mediation and false inheritance for a later individual biography. Do not use the universal rebellion, equal faction split or single civil war. The programme's plural campaigns deliberately remove the structure that currently makes this grammar distinct.

### Alpharius/Omegon grammar

Retain for a dedicated identity-and-office pass after the two erased persons are stable. Any future version must begin with two complete human souls and one shared office, not soul division, Mask succession or a secret explanation of contradictory history.

## The Two Absence Rulings

## ER-01 — The Institutionally Erased Person

- **Source grammar:** Primarch II and the surviving Legionaries adapted to new circumstances.
- **Historical placement:** later Long Frontier, within human institutions affected by one catastrophic breach or campaign.
- **Fixed:** a human person existed; authorities deliberately removed the person's name; followers and practices were redistributed; altered records remain detectable.
- **Open:** name, gender, culture, guilt, judgement, catastrophe, suppressing coalition and whether suppression protected victims or perpetrators.
- **Non-identity:** not Ischar's empty chair, an omitted Companion, the Unmirrored Interregnum, an erased Giant ancestor or a secret founder of every breachward order.
- **Evidence boundary:** archive alteration can prove suppression but not final judgement. Heaven is neither ignorant nor bound by the purge.
- **Disposition:** `retain as erased position`.

## ER-02 — The Supernaturally Name-Wounded Person

- **Source grammar:** Primarch XI, contradictory remnants and a vacancy occupied by later theories.
- **Historical placement:** late Long Frontier, in one bounded Long Rent or Closed-Door conflict.
- **Fixed:** a human person existed; hostile action damaged names, images and testimony; the soul remained whole; later claimants include non-identical copies, personas, Masks and frauds.
- **Open:** name, gender, culture, allegiance, judgement, attacker, original conflict and any authentic later manifestation.
- **Non-identity:** not a Name-Shearer victim whose soul was stolen, not a Borrowed Saint automatically, not the institutionally erased person and not an explanation for a protected Weird.
- **Evidence boundary:** patterned damage can establish an attack; no surviving claimant proves original identity through secret knowledge alone.
- **Disposition:** `retain as erased position`.
