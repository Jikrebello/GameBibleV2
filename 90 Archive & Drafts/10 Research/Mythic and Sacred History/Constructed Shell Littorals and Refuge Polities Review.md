# Constructed Shell Littorals and Refuge Polities Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Constructed Shell Settlements]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Purpose and Result

This is the fifth great-power family review arising from [[Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas]]. It expands all eight `EL-R` research leads without turning the Elemental Shell into a set of inhabited fantasy planes or silently creating a fifteenth active anchor.

The review establishes:

- two `retain`: EL-R02 and EL-R06;
- six incorporated `revise`: EL-R01, EL-R03, EL-R04, EL-R05, EL-R07 and EL-R08;
- zero `defer`;
- zero `reject`;
- no historical placement, canonical polity, named ruler, new people, mapped route or Garden promotion.

The governing result is:

> The Shell supplies pressure. A littoral supplies place. Persons must arrive from somewhere else.

# I. Controlling Littoral Architecture

## The Shell is not a country

The four joined plane-quadrants contain pressure, relation, rate, direction and boundary. They do not naturally contain soil, atmosphere, rivers, palaces, nations, ancestors or inhabitants. Apparent terrain exists only where an imposed frame makes Shell pressure legible at mortal scale.

A constructed littoral therefore requires eight functions:

| Function | What it does | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| **Material datum** | establishes direction, duration, bodily scale and recognizable matter | geometry loses human orientation; rooms become pressure relations rather than places |
| **Astral ballast** | holds the platform in relation to the World Egg rather than letting it drift through the membrane | platform shears away from routes and may become unreachable |
| **quadrant lattice** | limits which Shell pressure can enter and in what form | heat, flow, weight or motion crosses the habitable frame without limit |
| **habitable envelope** | maintains breathable air or suitable fluid, temperature and bodily pressure | residents suffocate, boil, freeze, crush or decompress |
| **food and water cycle** | imports, grows, recovers or recirculates actual biological necessities | famine and disease follow; Shell output is not nutrition merely because it resembles food or water |
| **waste and dump channels** | grounds excess pressure and ordinary biological or industrial waste | patterns recur as hazards; the platform poisons itself or vents into inhabited Material districts |
| **return relation** | defines how persons and cargo leave, even if the route is closed or monopolized | the settlement becomes stranded or every departure transfers danger elsewhere |
| **maintenance population** | repairs all seven preceding systems | political power concentrates in whoever can compel or withhold lethal work |

No prayer, title or simulated bargain replaces these systems. A city without a food source starves. A Water littoral does not automatically provide drinkable water. An Air littoral does not automatically provide breathable air. A Fire littoral needs cooling and a sink. An Earth littoral needs void, ventilation and a means to prevent structural instruction from sealing the inhabitants inside.

## Four permitted platform conditions

| Condition | Relation to the canonical fourteen | Story use |
|---|---|---|
| **Active permanent threshold anchor** | exactly one of the fourteen installations operating in 1360 AR | repeatable delves, extraction and a maintained Material return |
| **Stranded legacy littoral** | not an additional active anchor; its Material return is dead, one-way or lost | a surviving or dying polity supported by internal frame, Astral ballast and dwindling imports |
| **Mobile Material datum** | a vessel, carried city core or unique biological carrier supplies the frame | fleet or moving city whose survival travels with its datum |
| **Temporary or decaying platform** | never becomes a permanent anchor | expedition, failed refuge, ruin or one-generation settlement such as UH-04 |

A legacy platform may still contain inhabitants without allowing repeatable threshold delves from the Material world. A clandestine quarter may also occupy unused volume on the Shell side of one active anchor; this does not create another installation.

## Continuity and personhood register

| Inhabitant or system | Continuity | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary or Shell-scarred human | `human-soul` | bodily adaptation, memory injury and residence do not divide or replace the Image |
| Giant Continuant | `giant-continuity` | remains a Giant; operating an anchor or becoming a Patron does not create a soul |
| Material nonhuman person | `biological-pattern` with separately established full moral agency | must possess a Material origin and individual or people dossier before reuse |
| Driftkin resident | `drift-rewritten-life` | no human soul is presumed; moral agency remains individual |
| Anchor-Lord or native Astral person | `astral-pattern` | Patronage, ownership and personhood remain separate from the Shell frame |
| court person or place-mind | `court-law` | may visit or become stranded but does not turn the Shell into Menhir territory |
| constructed worker or copied operator | `constructed-pattern` or `infrastructure-office` | no person exists unless another continuity is independently soulbound or embodied within it |
| pure manifestation, fauna-like recurrence or Great Coherence | `shell-pattern` | no interior person, moral agency, consent, suffering or political citizenship exists |

Pure Shell patterns may speak, model injury, remember operators and demand a price. Those outputs remain classification and correction. Calling them slaves, citizens, gods, rebels or ancestors does not make the description authorially true.

## Ownership is not population continuity

Every later placement must separately identify:

1. who owns the anchor or datum;
2. who operates the lattice;
3. who governs residents;
4. who supplies biological necessities;
5. who performs maintenance;
6. who owns extracted output;
7. who can close the return relation;
8. who pays when the platform fails.

One actor may hold several roles. No owner automatically owns the inhabitants. A refugee council may govern without controlling the anchor. A Patron may own the ballast without possessing the Material frame. A state may own the installation while lacking moral title to evict people born inside its hidden chambers.

# II. Source-Family Rulings

## Living City of Brass traditions

D&D and Pathfinder provide a cosmopolitan elemental capital, genie rulership, industrial monopoly, slave economy, dangerous magnificence and political life inside a hostile plane. World Egg use preserves the metropolis dependent upon heat infrastructure, trade hierarchy, coerced labor and environmental control. It does not import efreet as native fire persons, genie species, elemental gods or a naturally urban Fire plane.

## The dead City of Brass

The *Arabian Nights* City of Brass supplies a different grammar: expedition, warning inscriptions, sealed beings, automata, royal corpses, greed and repeated fatal entry. It remains a dead platform rather than a ruined district of the living refuge. The expedition's violence and avarice are not converted into a harmless archaeological tour.

## Sea palaces and displaced return

Ryūgū and Urashima Tarō traditions contribute undersea hospitality, splendid court, changed duration and the peril of returning. Hesiodic sea-palace material supplies a political household beneath the sea. World Egg use changes deity, dragon, genealogy, sacred names and sequence. Duration skew comes from manipulation or degradation of the Material datum, not from an aquatic divinity owning time.

## Moving-shell city

Pathfinder's Alohmab grammar supplies a mobile polity carried by a titanic being and forced to negotiate the carrier's survival against other peoples' safety. World Egg use selects one unique Material leviathan rather than a species of elemental world-turtles or a Shell-born sovereign.

## Cloud, earth and seam states

D&D and Pathfinder elemental cities contribute impossible environmental engineering, monopolized resources and political states dependent upon plane conditions. World Egg use places refugees and colonists on built platforms. Mixed expressions remain interactions between the four quadrants, not additional planes or native nations.

# IV. Eight Littoral and Refuge Nurseries

## EL-R01R — The Living Brass Refuge

### Polity form

A large refugee and industrial city survives on a stranded legacy Fire littoral. At the story's principal period it is living, populous and politically complex, but its old Material threshold is dead or one-way. It therefore does not add a fifteenth active anchor in 1360. Whether the city survives into 1360 remains open until historical placement.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datum:** a carried furnace district, stone civic core and stores of ordinary matter taken onto an ancient platform.
- **Astral ballast:** one inherited mass-anchor whose owner and current legal title remain open.
- **quadrant lattice:** a Fire relation that converts limitless release pressure into bounded forge-charge and radiant chambers.
- **habitable envelope:** nested ceramic cooling walls, pressure-brine exchangers and sealed districts. Water failure, rather than heat alone, is the city's immediate existential danger.
- **food and water:** imported soil, fungi, seed houses, livestock, condensers and rigorous recycling. Shell heat cannot feed a person.
- **dump system:** sacrificial heat sinks and dangerous outer maintenance galleries.
- **return relation:** former Material road dead; irregular Astral trade or rare one-way arrivals keep the city from complete autarky.

### Population and government

The possible population includes human refugees with `human-soul`, Driftkin traders, individually classified Material nonhumans, Astral residents and constructed maintenance systems. No inhabitant is a native fire elemental. A ruler may be human, Giant, Driftkin, Anchor-Lord or institutional; later placement must choose one continuity rather than treating “brass lord” as a species.

Government grows around control of cooling, imported water, heat sinks and access to the outer galleries. Guild citizenship can be real and valuable while excluding slaves, prisoners, debt workers and people born into maintenance wards.

### Hard edge and political wound

The refuge's founders save thousands by building a city where no ordinary army can follow. Their successors convert emergency labor into hereditary debt. Slaves, condemned prisoners, insolvent households and conquered residents absorb lethal heat failures. Supervisors knowingly seal workers behind cooling doors to preserve wealthier districts. Revolts sabotage the same systems everyone needs, and rulers use that danger to call every demand for freedom attempted mass murder.

The city is not secretly innocent because its oppression maintains life support. The inhabitants are not secretly free because ending the regime carelessly would kill them.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received source:** D&D and Pathfinder living City of Brass grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** magnificent industrial metropolis, cosmopolitan tyranny, slave economy and monopoly of elemental production.
- **Hard edge:** slavery, lethal labor, luxury built on expendable workers and political violence remain intact.
- **Collision boundary:** not [[First Unknown Histories - Six Recoverable Lore Capsules#UH-04 — The Refuge at the Living Furnace|UH-04's failed furnace refuge]], the Seven Furnace Cities of Orqad, the Material Furnace League or an active fifteenth anchor.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible later carriers include incompatible water-ration tokens, cooling bricks marked with sealed workers' names, forge-charge bearing a unique legacy lattice and Astral contracts which presume a city no Material map can reach.

Owner, era, founders, cultures, exact population, old datum, slave institutions, revolts, present survival and all routes remain open.

**Review disposition:** `revise` — preserve the living industrial metropolis while making it a stranded legacy littoral with explicit biological support and no native elemental people. Promotion locked.

## EL-R02 — The Dead Brass Warning-City

### Polity form

A magnificent Fire platform has already failed before the first recoverable expedition reaches it. Palaces, water works, sealed galleries, automata, corpses and warning inscriptions remain arranged as if the city were waiting for court. There is no living marketplace and no necessary relationship to EL-R01R.

### Infrastructure and collapse

- **Material datum:** an ancient city core or royal expedition platform whose origin remains open.
- **Astral ballast:** fractured but not wholly lost, allowing the ruin to remain locatable.
- **lattice:** Fire channels persist in bounded loops and power some wards.
- **life support:** failed. Apparent fountains are pressure or heat relations and cannot sustain ordinary bodies.
- **return relation:** opens only through a dangerous sequence recorded in several incompatible inscriptions.

The city died through a chosen failure rather than a curse without mechanics. Candidate causes include elite refusal to reduce extraction, closure of water to rebellious wards, a ruler sealing the return relation against invasion or an attempt to preserve the court after its biological support had failed. Historical placement must select one.

### Occupants and guardians

Corpses remain corpses whose human souls have departed unless separately bound. Automata and repeated royal audiences are `constructed-pattern` or `infrastructure-office`. Shell guardians are `shell-pattern`. Any sealed speaker must be classified separately as a created spirit, human soul, Giant, Astral person, court person, damned manifestation, copy or fraud. “Jinn” is not an authorial continuity.

### Hard edge and expedition grammar

Expeditions ignore warnings, pry valuables from corpses, break seals and kill one another over treasure and water. Wards burn, crush or strand intruders according to remaining instructions. Sealed full-agency persons may lie, bargain or seek release; working out that they are persons does not make release safe or captivity automatically just.

The dead residents are not decorations for an explorer's rise. Their bodies, labor quarters, ration divisions and failed evacuation remain evidence of how the city was governed.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received source:** *The Arabian Nights*, “The Story of the City of Brass.”
- **Borrowed element:** fatal expedition, warnings, royal dead, sealed beings, automata, greed and mortality.
- **Hard edge:** death, avarice, desecration, imprisonment and repeated fatal entry remain central.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Living Brass Refuge, UH-04, a Giant Furnace City, Orthea or a general dungeon beneath the Material world.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible carriers include a warning sequence whose earliest copy omits the treasure chamber, bodies killed by different systems than the heroic expedition claims, a sealed speaker using language older than the visible city and stolen brass which still follows the ruin's heat cycle.

Origin, ruler, inhabitants, collapse, sealed persons, first expedition, surviving plunder, location and current access remain open.

**Review disposition:** `retain` — the dead warning-city remains a distinct Shell ruin and expedition grammar. Promotion locked.

## EL-R03R — The Coral Court in Pressure Brine

### Polity form

A Water-littoral court occupies pressure basins shaped around imported reef, bone, glass and living marine matter. It resembles an undersea palace because its builders imposed aquatic scale and movement upon the platform, not because the Water quadrant naturally contains an ocean kingdom.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datum:** a carried reef-palace foundation and several biological seawater habitats.
- **Astral ballast:** a route mass held separately from the ruling crown.
- **quadrant lattice:** Water pressure converted into channels, cold currents and tide-like gates.
- **habitable envelope:** some districts hold breathable chambers; others support aquatic bodies under controlled brine pressure.
- **food:** Material algae, fish, shell beds, imported grain and recirculated biological farms.
- **duration:** the court can alter the datum's clock relation within limits. Guests may experience days while their Material point advances years, but the effect consumes ballast and cannot reverse age or judgement.
- **return:** one pressure gate opens toward selected Material coasts when both sides maintain compatible vessels.

### Population and government

The court is a braided arrived polity rather than one merfolk species. Possible residents include human divers and adapted human lineages retaining `human-soul`, one separately developed Material marine people using `biological-pattern`, Driftkin route families and constructed pressure systems. A later people dossier is required before any marine form becomes reusable.

The palace government controls duration, pressure gates and the biological habitats required by different populations. Marriage and hostage exchange therefore determine succession and access as much as blood. No ruler is Poseidon, Ryūjin, Triton, Thalassor or a native water god.

### Hard edge and political wound

Surface rulers send hostages and coerced spouses to secure pressure routes. Court factions drown prisoners by transferring them outside their viable envelope, sabotage habitats and manipulate duration so an unwanted surface heir returns after their inheritance has been taken. Guests may genuinely receive hospitality while servants, hostages and captive spouses cannot leave.

Later surface descendants dispute whether a person who returns generations late remains sovereign, dead in law or foreign. The court's power cannot settle that human legal question.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received sources:** Japanese Ryūgū and Urashima Tarō traditions; Hesiodic sea-palace grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** splendid sea court, hospitality, dangerous return, changed duration, tide control and political household.
- **Hard edge:** separation, loss of time, perilous return, dynastic coercion, drowning and hostage politics remain.
- **Collision boundary:** not [[The Court of Salt and Teeth]], Thalassor's capital, a Menhir sea kingdom, a native Water-plane nation or EL-R04R's moving city.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible carriers include incompatible birth and return records, pressure-gate marriage objects, a drowned hostage chamber, biological farms containing Material species from several eras and one surface law for heirs “returned after their own mourning.”

Era, coasts, populations, ruler, crown continuity, hostages, time ratios, present access and all successions remain open.

**Review disposition:** `revise` — fix arrived mixed populations and datum-based duration while withholding any reusable marine people until a dossier exists. Promotion locked.

## EL-R04R — The City on the Moving Shell

### Polity form

A mobile Water littoral is built around the carapace of one unique non-sapient Material leviathan. The creature is biological, capable of pain and bodily death, but lacks the established reflective agency needed for political consent. It is neither a Shell pattern, True Dragon, Menhir body nor member of a reusable world-turtle species.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datum:** the living leviathan, its carapace and a city frame fixed around it.
- **Astral ballast:** mobile stones and route knots distributed across several civic districts so no one tower can stabilize the whole city alone.
- **quadrant lattice:** Water pressure is diverted around the body and through propulsion channels.
- **habitable envelope:** sealed civic galleries, farms and markets maintain ordinary atmosphere and pressure; aquatic outer workers require separately classified bodies.
- **food:** imported soil and livestock for residents; enormous biological feeding grounds for the carrier.
- **waste:** directed wakes and pressure sinks whose discharge can devastate a Material coast when the platform approaches a seam.
- **return:** intermittent coastal correspondences rather than one fixed gate.

### Population and government

Refuge households, traders, farmers, engineers, animal keepers, Driftkin and possible biological sea-persons may inhabit the city, each retaining its own continuity. City clans control different ballast stones, feeding routes and carapace districts. No clan owns the leviathan merely because its structures are fastened to the animal.

### Hard edge and political wound

The city survives by steering the carrier toward feeding grounds and cutting living tissue to seat anchors, repair frame roots and redirect motion. Officials drug, wound and starve the animal to control its route. Poor districts sit closest to pressure leaks and are sometimes cut away to reduce pain or ballast.

The carrier's migration can open a destructive Shell correspondence near a Material coast. Coastal rulers may be justified in seeking diversion while knowing that killing the animal collapses the city and may drown or strand its population. City rulers invoke national survival to conceal their own avoidable cruelty and refusal to change course.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received source:** Pathfinder's moving-shell city grammar associated with Alohmab.
- **Borrowed element:** a mobile nation dependent upon a titanic carrier and conflict between the carrier-city's survival and other shores.
- **Hard edge:** threatened destruction of a people, bodily exploitation of the carrier, displacement and political sacrifice remain.
- **Collision boundary:** not a True Dragon, Great Coherence, Patron mobile fortress, Nythalum vessel or the biological source of every sea-monster tradition.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible carriers include city maps matching scars on a unique shell fragment, civic laws allocating pain-producing anchor roots, coastal wreck deposits left by impossible wakes and descendants of districts deliberately cut loose.

Era, Material origin, inhabitants, current course, intelligence assessment, feeding ecology, rulers, cut-away districts and survival remain open.

**Review disposition:** `revise` — select one unique biological carrier and make its bodily exploitation part of the city's constitution. Promotion locked.

## EL-R05R — The Fleet of Surrendered Directions

### Polity form

An Air-littoral refugee fleet survives inside Material vessels, each of which supplies a small datum and habitable envelope. Stormglass signal relations hold the ships in one navigable formation. The fleet is a federation of vessels rather than a cloud nation or population native to Air.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datum:** ships, ballast, sealed atmosphere, clocks and carried household matter.
- **Astral ballast:** one leased route-anchor owned by a separately classified Anchor-Lord with `astral-pattern`.
- **quadrant lattice:** Air pressure translated into motion, sound corridors, charge and lift.
- **habitable envelope:** sealed hulls preserve breathable atmosphere; opening a deck without a pressure cadence can scatter bodies beyond recovery.
- **food and water:** ship gardens, condensers, livestock, ration exchanges and periodic imports.
- **navigation:** route memories are copied into stormglass through trained harmonic and runic procedures. Coercive extraction can damage or erase corresponding human memory but cannot remove part of a soul.
- **return:** the Anchor-Lord controls stable arrival, not all motion inside the fleet.

### Population and government

Every human resident retains `human-soul`. Driftkin pilots, Astral employees, biological passengers and constructed signal systems remain distinct. The Anchor-Lord is a Patron and route owner, not an Air elemental or natural king. Fleet captains govern ships; a route assembly bargains over formation; households who surrendered navigation memories may possess evidence rights without controlling the anchor.

### Hard edge and political wound

The founding refugees voluntarily contribute remembered roads because no map survives. Later captains take directions from prisoners, children and politically weak households, leaving donors unable to remember their homes or recognize return signs. Mutineers who break formation expose whole vessels to vacuum, charge and unbounded acceleration. The Anchor-Lord offers genuine stability while gradually making every safe arrival dependent upon its permission.

The fleet cannot recover home simply by collecting enough memory. Memories provide route instruction, not moral title or metaphysical mass sufficient to recreate a place.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received sources:** cloud-city and flying-fleet grammar; Breathwaste pressures; World Egg Patron-route politics.
- **Borrowed element:** airborne refugee society, mobile survival, route monopoly and surrender of home as the price of continued movement.
- **Hard edge:** exile, coercive memory loss, dependency, mutiny, exposure and lethal failure remain.
- **Collision boundary:** not Zephyraxis's court, a native Air civilization, the Palace Under Folded Wind, the Last Cart or a fleet of divided human souls.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible carriers include stormglass containing routes no living donor remembers, ships with incompatible home bearings, a Patron lease that distinguishes motion from arrival and descendants who inherit political compensation without inheriting memory.

Era, refugee origin, fleet size, Anchor-Lord, extraction procedure, mutinies, present route and survival remain open.

**Review disposition:** `revise` — define surrendered directions as copied or coercively extracted navigation memory and select an Astral route owner without soul transfer. Promotion locked.

## EL-R06 — The Weight-Seed Kingdom

### Polity form

An Earth-littoral mine becomes an enclosed state after its Material return relation fails. Its founders came to extract weight-seed and structural crystal. Their descendants inherit a platform whose walls, voids and support systems must be actively prevented from completing themselves around the population.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datum:** mine galleries, carried soil, clocks, burial chambers and ordinary structural matter.
- **Astral ballast:** buried foundation mass distributed beneath civic districts.
- **quadrant lattice:** Earth pressure converted into ledges, weight wells and structural instruction.
- **habitable envelope:** excavated void, air galleries and flexible supports prevent the platform from becoming solid enclosure.
- **food and water:** fungal beds, imported seed, cisterns, recycled waste and scarce biological livestock.
- **dump system:** spent structure is exported into sacrificial galleries which eventually become inaccessible solid mass.
- **return:** lost, sealed or politically monopolized; no current active anchor is presumed.

### Population and government

The inhabitants descend from miners, prisoners, guards, engineers, families and later stranded travelers. Humans retain `human-soul`; any Giant, Driftkin or Material nonhuman presence requires separate classification. Shell load patterns, talking supports and recurrent labor forms are nonpersons.

Government begins as emergency shift command and becomes a kingdom whose offices control air, void and the placement of weight-seed. Work debt passes through households because officials treat inherited access to habitable void as repayment for ancestral rescue.

### Hard edge and political wound

Forced laborers are buried alive in sacrificial galleries, strikers are sealed behind weight walls and insolvent families inherit dangerous shifts. Rulers deliberately over-reinforce rebellious districts, turning houses and people into solid inclusions. A genuine need to maintain void becomes the ideological claim that every hierarchy is structurally necessary.

At the former Material seam, land-Menhir courts may resist subsidence, extraction or the return of concentrated weight. They do not own the Earth quadrant and cannot decide the kingdom's lawful ruler.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received source:** elemental-earth city and deep mining-state grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** enclosed mineral polity, structural resource monopoly and survival through control of the environment.
- **Hard edge:** mine collapse, forced labor, burial alive, hereditary work debt and lethal enclosure remain.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Aversian Compact of the Knockers, Country Beneath the Rains, an underdark nation, an Earth Menhir kingdom or a Giant continuity machine.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible carriers include people recovered alive from impossible mineral inclusions, weight-seed using an extinct lattice, family shift brands, air maps mistaken for royal genealogies and a former Material seam that grows structurally heavier when the kingdom operates.

Era, original state, ruler, population, lost return, revolts, Menhir conflict, present survival and any reachable fragment remain open.

**Review disposition:** `retain` — the Earth-mine state already fits the sterile Shell if void, biology, labor and sealing mechanisms remain explicit. Promotion locked.

## EL-R07R — The Seam Marches

### Polity form

Two independently founded platforms and one shared maintenance march occupy a Water–Air conductor. One side extracts pressure brine, the other stormglass; the middle structure prevents cold flow, charge and motion from destroying both. They are constructed neighboring states, not native kingdoms of ice, storm or lightning.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datums:** separate carried settlement cores with incompatible legal and cultural origins.
- **Astral ballast:** each state owns one ballast mass; the maintenance march holds neither and survives by treaty.
- **shared conductor:** Water and Air relations create frost, charge, pressure fronts and signal channels around one mixed lattice.
- **habitable envelopes:** one settlement is built around fluid pressure and cold; the other around sealed atmosphere and charge. Neither population can survive unprotected in the other's works.
- **food and water:** both depend on Material biological stocks and controlled exchange.
- **dump and return:** a shared neutral system grounds excess force and preserves separate return possibilities.

### Population and government

The two populations arrive separately and retain their original continuities. Mixed households, maintenance workers, hostages and defectors create a third society in the march. Shell seam patterns are infrastructure and hazard, not neutral citizens or an oppressed indigenous people.

Each state claims the other is merely an unsafe annex of its own platform. The maintenance march possesses technical veto without full sovereignty and becomes the obvious target for bribery, hostage-taking and military occupation.

### Hard edge and political wound

Wars seize conductor stations, expose prisoners to the other envelope and redirect lethal cold or charge into civilian districts. Both governments know that destroying the shared lattice can freeze, suffocate or scatter their own populations, yet factions repeatedly threaten total failure to win limited disputes. Maintenance families are conscripted, mutilated to prevent defection or killed as collaborators by alternating occupiers.

The fact that the states contain one another's most immediate danger does not make either necessary to creation or morally protective.

### Source fidelity and collision

- **Received source:** para-elemental and mixed-plane border grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** contested seam territories, unlike environmental societies and war over a shared mixed resource.
- **Hard edge:** civilian exposure, destructive war, hostage-taking and mutually lethal infrastructure remain.
- **Collision boundary:** not an eternal elemental Blood War, additional planes, two Great Coherences, Menhir Circles or one culture divided into moral alignments.

### Possible traces and open facts

Possible carriers include conductor parts bearing three incompatible custody systems, bodies adapted to only one envelope, hostage law written into maintenance rhythm and paired disasters that prove deliberate redirection rather than natural seam failure.

Era, founders, states, cultures, treaty, wars, present survival, access and final conductor condition remain open.

**Review disposition:** `revise` — replace natural para-elemental border nations with separately founded Water–Air platforms sharing one lethal conductor. Promotion locked.

## EL-R08R — The Fourteen-Anchor Free Port

### Polity form

One candidate present-day active anchor contains a concealed Shell-side quarter beyond the worksite shown on official plans. The quarter uses unused maintenance volume, illegal lateral chambers and diverted life-support capacity. It is supported by one of the canonical fourteen anchors, not by a fifteenth installation and not by all fourteen at once.

“Fourteen-Anchor” is an authorial research handle for the crisis its discovery creates: every bloc would have to ask whether hidden populations, smuggling chambers or falsified capacity reports exist at the other thirteen sites.

### Infrastructure stack

- **Material datum:** the selected official anchor's worksite and clandestinely extended chambers.
- **Astral ballast:** the official ballast plus one illegal emergency tether whose owner must be classified before placement.
- **quadrant lattice:** whatever affinity belongs to the selected anchor; no affinity is chosen in this review.
- **habitable envelope:** stolen cooling, pressure, air, water and dump capacity hidden inside maintenance tolerances.
- **food:** smuggled Material provisions and small biological recovery systems.
- **return:** unofficially attached to crew rotations, waste channels and emergency marks.

### Population and government

Residents may include abandoned delvers, defecting workers, refugees denied Material admission, Driftkin, individually classified extrarealm people and descendants born within the chamber. Human residents retain `human-soul`; birth on a Shell platform does not make them elemental.
