## Real-world comparative sources

### Ancient Egypt

The Qahuri sequence draws upon the political grammar of Egyptian unification, periods of divided rule, Middle Kingdom restoration, Hyksos rule, New Kingdom empire, the Amarna religious experiment, Third Intermediate plurality and later foreign dynasties. Egyptian history contributes durable river institutions, temple wealth, mortuary estates, craft settlements and the ability of local society to continue through changes of crown.

The principal modern syntheses already recorded in the source dossier are the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Old, Middle and New Kingdom essays, its material on Nubia and later periods, UCL Digital Egypt's work on kingship and literature, and archaeological studies of workers and households. These sources describe Egyptian evidence. They do not authenticate the fictional Qahuri names or chronology.

### Phoenicia and Carthage

The Gateway history draws upon independent Phoenician cities, mixed western settlement, Carthage's cultivated African hinterland, civic magistrates, councils, assemblies and varied military recruitment. The three wars use the strategic sequence of the Punic Wars and the Mercenary War: contested intervention, fleet building, unpaid troops, an overseas military base, an alliance-breaking invasion, recovery and annihilation.

Polybius, Livy, Appian, Aristotle and archaeological evidence remain unequal sources. Greek and Roman texts dominate the surviving narrative. Child sacrifice at Phoenician and Punic tophets remains disputed in frequency, age profile and interpretation. World Egg makes a defined fictional ruling: Qarzeth's civic establishment killed living children. Caleran's salting of Qarzeth is also a fictional event; the familiar salting of historical Carthage lacks ancient evidence.

## Proposed geography

### Sacred Basin

- Tenaret stands on the middle river at the junction of several cultivated districts.
- Qemaret occupies the lower fan and commands cart routes towards the Gateway country.
- Sakhret controls the southern narrows, quarry approaches, pasture and mining roads.
- Isethar lies between the principal crown centres and prospers through transport.
- Aseret is an inland garrison and craft town enlarged by the Single Radiance court.

The river's ancient proper name remains open. None of these placements fixes coordinates or modern borders.

### Gateway country

- Qarzeth, the destroyed republican capital, stands at a deep harbour in the broad Qabir–Zanthir gateway.
- Sarqen is a separate sheltered harbour, later the royal seat.
- Esharat lies inland among springs, grain fields and carrying routes.
- Belqara is a cultivated district tied unequally to the capital.
- Hazreth watches the outer coast.
- Tammur is an island or strait anchorage on the inner-sea side.
- Oresh is an overseas coastal district rather than part of the Gateway homeland.
- Hadrath lies inland along a disputed border and road system.

The proposed map revision should add these relationships after canon promotion. It should not redraw the coast until the wider inverted-Mediterranean survey is complete.

## Identity and metaphysical rulings

1. Ancient basin populations are principally Orphaned humans. Later migrants and conquerors create mixed households without turning the civilisation Ark-derived.
2. Gateway peoples are a Braided ancient population formed from older shore communities, proto-Ahdari migrants, maritime settlers, captives and freed people.
3. The Qahuri Sacred Basin is neither Ishkatar's Serpent's Spine nor the Arqeshite riverlands.
4. Claims of Ishkatari ancestry by human crowns do not create Giant continuity.
5. The dead Qahuri rulers possess human souls. Tomb offices, copied personas, residues and Masks remain separate.
6. The Bronze-Faced Provider is a dependent Gluttony Mask. Its destruction at Qarzeth ends one feeding architecture without destroying the Idol Throne.
7. Ithren III's Radiance cult is exploited by a Pride Mask. It introduces no true Idol succession.
8. Flood and shore responses belong to existing Menhir relationships or court persons and prove only their actual operation.
9. The Boatman remains the commissioned psychopomp and belongs to neither civilisation's pantheon.
10. Hadrima's Qahuri relationship provides kinship, knowledge and prestige. It grants no title to Qahur.

## Later comparative follow-up: the White Measure

[[Druma and the Prophecies of Kalistrade - Wealth, Necropolis and the Qahuri Dead]] proposes a distinct mortuary-economic movement within ancient Qahur after comparing the Prophecies of Kalistrade with the Basin's mortuary estates and Ishkatar's funerary technology. Its provisional **White Measure** belongs to a later research layer and is not silently included in the Package A proposals above.

The follow-up recommends a long development: first formulation among mortuary professionals during the divided Senareth world, commercial expansion under Yasharet and great necropolises under the Aramen empire. In a successful rite, the founder's complete human soul is bound to the embalmed necropolis and becomes owner of a private Egg White demiplane. The founder may then act as a Patron while remaining a self-bound human held from the Boatman and final judgement. Failed and parasitised rites remain separately classified as residue, infrastructure offices, constructed patterns, dependent Greed Masks or Giant claimants.

[[The White Measure and the Second Houses of Ancient Qahur]] develops this proposal as an integrated Package A history. It names Meresha, Khaseret, Tamekha, Hesenu and Nemeret; establishes three successful Second Houses and one counterfeit; connects the Copper Orchard to the already proposed stoppage of 903 BR; and traces the movement through the Single Radiance and foreign-rule periods. These details remain a separable review group and enter no canon through their inclusion here.

### White Measure identity and collision rulings

1. Meresha, Khaseret and Tamekha are self-bound human souls. Patron is their role and lich is creator shorthand; neither term changes their humanity.
2. Hesenu and other successful attendants remain distinct human persons. They do not become extensions of the founder or inhabitants manufactured by the realm.
3. Nemeret's soul departed normally. His estate-office and the later Greed Mask are not Nemeret.
4. The White Measure descends from human study of Ishkatari instruments. It is not a surviving Ishkatari government, Giant lineage or proof that Qahuri crowns descend from Naga rulers.
5. The Second Houses are made demiplanes with anchors, local laws, upkeep and failure states. They possess no authority over Heaven, the Vestibule, the Boatman or final judgement.
6. Successful soulbinding is objectively evil even when voluntary. Material usefulness, affection or protection does not alter that classification.
7. The movement introduces no new Idol, Archmask or Regnant succession. A dependent Greed Mask exploits Nemeret's funerary architecture.
8. The Copper Orchard deepens the existing 903 BR stoppage rather than creating a second workers' revolt.
9. Khaseret's coastal tethers deepen Qahuri–Gateway trade without making the Gateway country Qahuri or White Measure territory.
10. The proposed figures do not replace Estate Dead, Gilded Wardens or the established Patron taxonomy.
