# Whole-History Mythic Planting Programme

This is the control note for the first author-only planting pass from Creation through 1360 AR. It records what has been built, what remains provisional, and what may move into the nursery. It is not a chronology and does not canonize a seed by listing it.

## Governing Lens and Interfaces

- [[Orthodox Old Testament Lens]] — the governing moral, theological, and sacred-historical source atlas.
- [[Idol Thrones, Regnants, and Cultural Masks]] — the controlling Idol sequence and succession ontology.
- [[The Image, Soul, and Creaturely Continuity]] — the hard boundary among a human person, memory, residue, office, legend, and constructed identity.
- [[Names of the Menhir, Masks of the Idols]] — Menhir continuity and cultural refraction versus Idol identity accretion.
- [[Historical Seed Card Template]] — the complete seed interface and validation checklist.
- [[The Historical Seed Garden]] — the nursery into which reviewed material may be promoted.

The transformation formula is:

> scriptural moral pattern + primary comparative grammar + culture-specific pressure + World Egg torque + contested residue

Every visible source parallel must change at least three major axes. Comparative traditions supply narrative grammar and local research questions; they do not override the Orthodox scriptural lens or become one-to-one culture mappings.

## Four-Lane Audit Rule

Every historical band is inspected independently for:

1. human hero or company;
2. land, folklore, and local encounter;
3. institution, memory, and relic;
4. Idol, Menhir, or Patron pressure.

An intentional empty lane is a valid audit result. Creation, the Great Rebellion, and other protected dense or sacred periods retain deliberate empty beds where new participants would diminish or overexplain them.

## Succession Ledger and Hard Cap

Each block may place no more than one true Idol succession into authorial review. Other throne-shaped ideas must remain Archmask revolts, failed accessions, Mask regime changes, Sediment, or mistaken historical theories unless the block's sole slot is explicitly reopened.

No more than **three true successions across recorded human history** may be promoted from the entire first pass. The four block candidates therefore cannot all become true. B3-16 and B4-18 may be two memories of one attempted or completed Pride convulsion and must be reconciled before either is promoted.

Any retained succession must preserve all of the following:

- one continuous guilty `yolk-sovereignty`;
- an autonomous Archmask before any attempt at accession;
- no human soul entering or becoming the Idol;
- no redemption, innocence, or neutral Throne;
- the original betrayed commission still accusing every Regnant through its Counter-Countenance.

## Trace and Loss Rule

The default is a discoverable 1360 AR trace: object, custom, bloodline, institution, enemy, landscape, ruin, or disputed text. A trace proves only its own bounded behavior and provenance.

Loss remains real. A surviving custom need not preserve the people who made it; several cards deliberately leave an individual name, biography, or original account unrecoverable. B4-14 has no trace yet because it is a present-day bed governed by the Forward Rule. Later review may designate additional cards as genuinely lost, but should not manufacture a surviving clue merely to make every seed playable.

## Block Review Procedure

For each block, in chronological order:

1. read the complete four-lane matrix;
2. mark every card `retain`, `revise`, `defer`, or `reject`;
3. approve source attribution, sensitivity limits, and theological classification;
4. check geographic and cultural distribution;
5. reconcile any retained succession candidate with the whole-history cap;
6. promote only selected roots into [[The Historical Seed Garden]];
7. leave names, membership, miracles, and ultimate fates provisional unless separately chosen for canon.

Promotion proceeds from lightest to most constraining:

1. sayings, names, and local customs;
2. relics, graves, roads, and disputed texts;
3. local heroes and episodic cycles;
4. institutions and historical companies;
5. true Idol successions or sovereign convulsions.

## Validation Lock

A card fails review if it:

- explains a protected Weird;
- makes a heroic company the secret cause of an age;
- merges a human soul with legend, residue, office, Mask, or Patron persona;
- treats humanity as the creator of an Idol's original Hunger or sovereignty;
- gives an Idol succession moral innocence or a route to redemption;
- treats Menhir cultural refraction as ordinary Regnant succession;
- makes a miracle prove more than its actual commission;
- lets repentance erase victims, law, property, or historical consequence;
- maps a World Egg people wholesale onto a scriptural or comparative source culture;
- predetermines post-1360 history.

## Later Enrichment Queue

Retained and revised cards can later be deepened through specifically attributed dossiers for the Isles; western and central Europe; eastern Europe and the Balkans; northern and Baltic traditions; Greek, Norse, and heroic-adventure cycles; Hindu epic and cosmological material; sleeping-hero traditions; Indigenous American narrative grammars; and distinct African traditions.

Indigenous, African, Hindu, and Sámi material requires named-community or named-text attribution at expansion time. Sacred names, ceremonies, living ritual identities, and signature sequences are not transferable surface decoration.

## Initial Structural Audit

- **Coverage:** seventy-two unique developed cards span all four chronological blocks; the Faewild Schism and Black Wind have explicit non-headline audit results.
- **Nursery discipline:** all seventeen existing Garden beds are deepened before duplication; the Company of the Ransomed Standard is the pass's only completely new signature company.
- **Transformation fields:** every developed card records a scriptural lens, comparative grammar, World Egg torque, continuity classification, present residue, open material, source restriction, and review state through either the full or compact interface.
- **Trace density:** seventy-one of seventy-two cards have an explicit 1360 AR trace; B4-14 intentionally has none yet under the Forward Rule. The Praise roll additionally preserves people whose identities are genuinely lost despite surviving customs.
- **Succession control:** four blocks expose four candidate slots; no succession is promoted, and noncandidate throne conflicts are fixed as Archmask or Mask events.
- **Protected boundaries:** no card explains a registered Weird, creates a second Prophet or central apostle, makes a company cause an age, or turns a human soul into a Mask, office, Patron persona, or Idol.
- **Navigation:** the research notes, canonical continuity controls, seed template, and Garden links resolve through the vault.

This audit established readiness for author review. Block I and the Last Cart have since received nursery dispositions, while all story material remains exploratory until separately promoted.
