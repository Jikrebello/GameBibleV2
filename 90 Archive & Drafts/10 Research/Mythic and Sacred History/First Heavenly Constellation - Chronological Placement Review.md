# First Heavenly Constellation - Chronological Placement Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Heavenly Campaigns and the Human Dead]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Review Scope

This ledger reviews all twelve placements in [[First Heavenly Constellation - Earth-First Historical Placement Pass]] before any Garden harvest. Each event must remain coherent as human history, preserve its source wound, fit the established chronology and maintain the categorical distinctions among `human-soul`, `created-spirit`, Saintly manifestation, angelic commission-body, Idol Mask, Patron, infernal office, relic, residue and institutional procedure.

An incorporated `revise` is a passing disposition. Nothing here canonizes a person, dates an event in the authoritative chronology, proves a miracle or grants a church, nation or army permanent heavenly favor.

## Review Interface

Every placement is tested for:

1. chronological and cultural fit;
2. cause → decision → mechanism → effect → cost → consequence;
3. collision with existing companies, Great-Person traces, saint histories and great-power ecologies;
4. parity with the borrowed biblical, Orthodox or comparative hard edge;
5. commission and continuity discipline;
6. independent evidence and 1360 discoverability;
7. a trace that remains useful if its supernatural attribution is false;
8. no explanation of a protected Weird or predetermined present outcome.

# Group I — Age of Stars

## OBA-N07 — Wheels at the Withdrawing Glory

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 610–570 BR supplies a pre-imperial civic covenant capable of being inverted without creating a Church sanctuary before the Redeemer. Magistrates turn a refuge register into a purge list; guards kill and expel protected households; a commissioned choir withdraws the covenant’s bounded protection; siege follows without making the besiegers righteous.
- **Collision finding:** the proposed fixed five-stage procedure pressed too close to the Long-Frontier Rule of Five Closures and encouraged a mechanical reading already prohibited by the Nythalum collision boundary.
- **Correction incorporated:** surviving recensions now disagree about the number of gate stations. The durable descendant is a variable public **unhousing rubric**, not five universal supernatural operations.
- **Continuity and evidence:** the choir remains a high-order created-spirit complex; the gate, covenant and rubric remain created goods and procedures. Gate fabric, purge records, expelled copies and siege layers establish the history without authenticating the wheel diagrams.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — retain the purge, visible withdrawal, siege and technical fraud; remove the fixed numbered system.

# Group II — Underground and Conversion

## HC-N10 — Voice Beside the Binding

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 244–267 AR supports a local prison containment corrupted through forced testimony, while c. 404 AR places the counterfeit correction after imperial conversion without adding an apostle. A clerk betrays one worker under torture, repents, preserves erased names, refuses to become a living conduit and dies during evacuation; a later commissioned Saint distinguishes one false voice from the old containment.
- **Collision finding:** the victim-first annual reading remained too close to the Red Door Names and other prison memorial rolls, even though the event itself is neither the Three-Lock Road nor the Red Passage.
- **Correction incorporated:** the trace becomes the narrower rule **the name before the title**: the person killed through the martyr’s sin is named before the martyr’s honorific. The Saint’s redeemed judgement is fixed within the nursery lead, while public recognition remains open.
- **Continuity and evidence:** Saint, dependent Pride Mask, binding office, vocal residue and reliquary claimant remain separate. Kiln payroll, victim-family refusal, chamber fabric and later voice transcripts corroborate the mortal core.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — pass the placement with the narrower trace and explicit Saintly continuity.

## OBA-N02 — Finder of the Cast-Out

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 304–323 AR places the crime amid legalized Christian respectability rather than biblical pastoral society. A lord and wife coerce an enslaved woman into reproductive use, beat and expel her when a lawful heir arrives, and send riders to prevent return; an angel exposes ordinary water and a caravan route; later survivor law prevents the same inheritance excuse.
- **Collision finding:** no collision with a national mother, saintly midwife network or wilderness Menhir survives. The cistern does not become a shrine that produces repeatable rescue.
- **Continuity and evidence:** mother and child retain complete human souls; the Mercy-Herald neither founds their descendants nor becomes their Patron. Household accounts, the mother’s legal formula, cistern repair and inheritance exclusions provide independent classes.
- **Trace test:** the no-new-heir non-expulsion clause remains useful even if the cistern story is denied.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## OBA-N06 — Coal-Bearer at the False Choir

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 382–391 AR sits inside Vestian coercion. A singer knowingly informs on five custodians, officials torture and execute them, and a guild hides the murders inside a dedication hymn; the singer begins a confession freely, the Angel painfully clarifies the testimony, and imprisonment follows.
- **Collision finding:** the episode concerns liturgical complicity rather than Susanna-style forensic procedure, a Phoenix coal, Children of the Closed Temple or a judgement on conversion as a whole.
- **Continuity and evidence:** angel, coal manifestation, singer, guild office, temple history and silent measure remain distinct. Ledgers, deposition, grave petitions, meter and altered stone corroborate the killing and confession.
- **Trace test:** the five-name rest prevents institutional self-absolution without proving the Angel or declaring the former cult true.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

# Group III — Early Long Frontier

## HC-N03 — Pilgrim Beneath Seven Small Wings

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 626–641 AR provides an early truce, disputed hostages and a Giant toll fortress. A governor uses a hostage tally to turn safe conduct into inherited ownership; a Greater Angel supplies three choices without endorsing one; mortals assault the fort, break the tally, free prisoners and also kill captives and surrendering guards through their own decisions.
- **Collision finding:** the original corrupted signal fort duplicated the Taken Trumpet’s later signal-house imprisonment and created a second false-proclamation episode.
- **Correction incorporated:** the hostile mechanism is now a dependent Pride Mask using a Giant hostage standard and tally stone. The Pilgrim exposes ownership language and prevents one claim transfer; it neither fights a fallen gate-captain nor controls military signals.
- **Continuity and evidence:** one Greater Angel, seven Attendants, one dependent Mask, the commander and every prisoner remain distinct. Diaries, bird-marked rolls, tally fragments, remains and hostile homilies establish the choice structure.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — pass after separating antagonist, apparatus and evidence from HC-N08.

## HC-N05 — Golden Last-Watch

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 653–668 AR supports negotiated territorial withdrawals during the Frontier peace. Commanders omit debtors, servants, wounded people and mixed families; people die after deliberate abandonment; cart crews return; a Warden guards the moving rear while humans carry loads and fight; survivors cross at real cost.
- **Collision finding:** the title could be confused with the Long Watch, but the event has no watch-house, institutional lineage, stationary border or saint-founder. Its legal carrier is therefore called the **dependants’ annex**, not a last-watch roll.
- **Continuity and evidence:** the Warden remains one created spirit with a roll-bounded charge. Treaty text, amended annex, cart repairs, graves and enemy scouting reports establish the human event.
- **Trace test:** the annex protects omitted dependants even if no golden manifestation is accepted.
- **Disposition:** `retain` — the carrier clarification does not alter the event.

## OBA-N08 — Prince Delayed at Three Borders

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 692–706 AR belongs to the Long Frontier’s overlapping jurisdictions. Rulers distribute one minority’s prisoners to prevent a complete hearing; an Envy Mask duplicates petitions, a Patron claim is attached to the wrong guarantor, and a fallen border office seals testimony; captives die while human advocates and the people-facing Guardian pursue the case.
- **Coherence finding:** “the Guardian contested the borders” was insufficiently concrete and risked mystical word salad.
- **Correction incorporated:** the Guardian exposes the human makers of duplicate petitions, identifies the proper Patron guarantor so mortals can tender a lawful dispute, and breaks one captured seal over a messenger’s speech. Human bribery, copying, burial recovery and advocacy remain necessary.
- **Continuity and evidence:** Guardian, Mask, Patron, fallen angel, office, people and prisoners are separately classified. Three calendars, remains, Patron docket, office seal and rival liturgies corroborate delay without proving national innocence.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — pass with the three mechanisms stated plainly.

# Group IV — Late Long Frontier

## HC-N11 — Martyr of the Voluntary Wall

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 744–752 AR supports a mixed frontier water-city under siege. A ditch digger rises to command, personally authorizes earlier beatings and a lethal punishment, later uses both volunteers and coerced stations to preserve the inner city, distinguishes the rolls, repents and dies with the wall; a c. 811 AR commission corrects one successor institution.
- **Collision finding:** the person is neither the Founder Who Corrected the Order nor Warden of the Last Redoubt. The defining evidence is unequal consent, not an institution’s claim of descent.
- **Continuity and evidence:** every dead defender remains a distinct soul; no mass death becomes Saintly fuel. Rolls, station graves, revocation orders, orphan accounts and later seizure warrants establish guilt and correction.
- **Trace test:** separate free and compulsory death rolls remain morally useful if the Saintly manifestation is false.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## HC-N13 — Saint of Five Remembered Wings

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 781–826 AR supports one infernal client dynasty. An abused and mutilated heir escapes, returns, personally kills the parent and surrendered kin, rejects the inherited office, repents and makes incomplete restitution; after judgement, one c. 907 AR commission protects children from the old custody clause.
- **Collision finding:** the paired liberation feast and victim reading duplicated the already promoted Ashen-Wings feast-and-reading structure.
- **Correction incorporated:** the trace becomes the custody rule **“No wound inherits the child.”** It bars blood, scars, household office and parental compact from owning a human person. Victims remain free to refuse reconciliation.
- **Continuity and evidence:** human souls, inherited infernal office, contract, wounds, later manifestation and five-wing iconography remain separate. Custody ledgers, medical descriptions, surrender testimony, petitions and manumission charters establish the mortal history.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — pass with a distinct legal trace rather than a second paired commemoration.

# Group V — Fall, High Noon and Near Present

## HC-N08 — Taken Trumpet

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 910–913 AR fits an occupied mountain prison during the rebellion without entering Caelmount or Shattered Engines. An occupier turns surrender into confiscation; a Herald accepts the bounded proclamation, its manifestation path is trapped and mutilated, human rescuers die, the true surrender is completed and both sides suppress the defeat.
- **Collision finding:** after HC-N03’s revision, this remains the only selected signal-house history. It neither explains the Veil over Veridun nor becomes an allegory for the whole Reconquest.
- **Continuity and evidence:** the loyal angel is locally defeated but cannot be corrupted. Created spirit, projected body, signal ward, coerced image and counterfeit “fallen trumpet” remain distinct. Transcripts, graves, plates, rebel minutes and prisoner terms corroborate the event.
- **Trace test:** the protected-name signal rule addresses coerced authorship even if no relic fragment or angelic identity is accepted.
- **Disposition:** `retain`.

## OBA-N04 — Angel at the Threshing Floor

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 1171–1173 AR lies more than half a century before the Grey Sweat and after the strongest reign of the Thalmyric golden age. A tributary ruler abuses a census for labor, hostage and property seizure; death advances along the courier network; confession, release and destruction of the seizure schedule precede the visible end at an ordinary threshing floor.
- **Coherence finding:** the first draft named a mortality but did not state what witnesses actually observed between the schedules and the deaths.
- **Correction incorporated:** count-households and nearby barracks develop the same rapid fever and breath failure within hours of sealed schedules arriving; the angelic manifestation is seen at the leading edge. The natural mechanism remains open, and divine justice is not reduced to a human ledger of individual desert.
- **Collision and evidence:** chronology, symptoms and records distinguish the event from the Grey Sweat and Quiet Ninth. Census drafts, breach records, burial trenches, revocation and hostile floor accounts establish the bounded event.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — pass after making the witnessed mechanism concrete.

## OBA-N11 — Guardian Who Could Not Prevent the Grave

- **Fit and causal chain:** c. 1327–1331 AR fits assurance expansion. A guilty tallyman makes partial restitution, dies through workhouse neglect, and is assigned another debtor’s corpse tag so a broker can mature an infernal claim; a personal Guardian makes the mismatch visible, while humans restore the name through records.
- **Forward-Rule finding:** the underlying death and attempted fraud are historical, but the 1360 assurance ruling remains present action and cannot be predetermined.
- **Correction incorporated:** only the already documented **Double-Tag Docket** may become a Garden hook. It proves a disputed identity correction and bounded contract response, not the current verdict, the tallyman’s judgement or the winged witness.
- **Continuity and evidence:** human soul, Guardian, corpse, tag, assurance office and contract response remain distinct. Baptism, warehouse loss, restitution, medical warnings, tags and witness testimony provide independent carriers.
- **Disposition:** `revise` — pass as a bounded present hook under the Forward Rule.
