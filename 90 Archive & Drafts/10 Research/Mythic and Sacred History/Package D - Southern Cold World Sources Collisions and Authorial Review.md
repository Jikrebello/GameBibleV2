# Package D — Southern Cold World Sources, Collisions and Authorial Review

## Review purpose

This review controls the Package D development delivery and the response to the author's sixteen atlas comments of 12 September 2026. It records the proposed history, source transformations, chronology, identities, metaphysical boundaries, name checks and possible canon destinations. Its events and names were exploratory at this stage; [[Package D - Folk Ättar and Southern Cold World Canon Integration Review]] records their later promotion on 13 September 2026.

The delivery consists of:

1. [[Historical Atlas of the Southern Cold World before Atheria]] — the connected history from the Giant crownland to 350 BR;
2. [[Nhaldur - The Penal Crownland and the First Rewriting]] — the Council of Keys, six principal fortresses, their satellite prisons, human revolts and Kharvos;
3. [[The Folk before Atheria - Rivers Forests and Upland Gords]] — three constellations, period peoples, institutions and Atherian contact;
4. [[The Ättar before the Great Keel-Taking]] — hall development, the Empty Halls campaign and the pre-350 maritime world;
5. [[The Deep Voice - Masters Wars and the Quiet Measure]] — Veyrun origins, named masters, heroic war, catastrophe and a fringe mountain-druid order;
6. [[Grobi before Atheria - Hearths Hosts and Winter Roads]] — reproduction, crude technology, predatory pack institutions, annual winter hosts and political history.

The [[Southern Peoples Polar Giants and Grobi - Comparative Research for Package D]] remains the source record. The new histories apply its selected architecture in the encyclopaedic voice established by Tidewood.

## Controlling canon

The following facts have not been reopened:

- Folk and Ättar are related, principally Orphaned peoples whose ancestors survived outside the Glass Ark.
- Fäskogarfolk is a reconstructed ancestral language-and-custom continuum, not a lost state or pure race.
- Proto-Atherian contact begins around 2050 BR and includes conquest, slavery, hostage-taking, alliance, trade, intermarriage and mutual absorption.
- The Ättar develop from related southern populations through halls, coasts, boats, defence and political choice.
- The War of the Empty Halls occurs around 1600 BR in an already established hall world.
- Mother Moon's bargain creates four paternal pact lines, hybrid chiefly forms, inherited rage and a firstborn price in every generation.
- G-61, the Hall That Faced the Grobi, remains one earlier local tragedy and causes neither the whole divergence nor the lunar bargain.
- Wrath alone performs the First Rewriting. The first victims' human souls separate and route towards judgement.
- Grobi are a true-breeding `wrath-rewritten-life` lineage with five food-driven bodily stages, individual continuity, intelligence and suffering.
- Humans do not become Grobi through ordinary captivity, adoption, diet, blood or descent. Pale-Skins and Hollow-Kings retain separate origins.
- Teren Casvilar's principal southern campaigns run c. 390–379 BR. Kellovar falls in 383 and Darneth in 380. His 385 cold-coast voyage creates no lasting occupation.
- Tidewood's dry-fleet history remains local and separate.
- South is coldward. Grobi winter movement runs principally northward and westward from deeper southern country.
- Exact borders, map distances, protected Weirds, true Idol successions and all post-1360 outcomes remain untouched.

The author has reopened one current canon statement: Grobi full moral agency. Package D now proposes intelligent predatory life without human moral conscience. Grobi can learn, plan, remember, suffer, form packs and select tactics; domination, fighting and consumption remain their creaturely ends. This conflicts with the existing metadata and prose of [[Grobi Overview]], [[Goblins]], [[Orcs]], [[The Image, Soul, and Creaturely Continuity]] and related bestiary entries. The draft records the new direction without altering those Canon Reference files. A later canon-integration delivery must reconcile every occurrence explicitly.

## Comparative sources and their use

### Celtic, Brittonic and Goidelic histories

The western development uses farms, fortified centres, cattle wealth, fosterage, learned professions, island lordship, bardic standing, raiding, captivity and dangerous ritual. It refuses a single Celtic nation or universal Druid church. Belathi, Caerethi, Dovren and Morlach are original World Egg names, not codes for Gaul, Wales, Ireland or Scotland.

The War of the Three Fosters transforms heroic cattle and foster obligations into a dispute driven by boats, storm loss, inheritance and repair labour. Its resolution depends upon ship crews and supply, not a substituted real-world hero.

### Continental Germanic histories

The middle-forest development uses agricultural longhouses, public assemblies, compensation, warrior followings, frontier coalitions and weapon deposits. Hartheni, Ossen and Rauven remain regionally distinct. The War of the Three Crossings gives one victory bog a political cause and retains the killing of surrendered fighters. It does not make sacrificial violence universal.

The reviewed draft adds an annual All-Thing shared in local forms by the Folk constellations and Ättar halls. Þingvellir provides the useful distinction between local district assemblies, a general open-air gathering and a smaller law council. Package D retains male formal voice and chiefly influence without importing Iceland's exact constitution or creating one parliament for the whole south. The Package D comparative dossier records the sources.

Uneven Atherian occupation draws on the cost of supplying armies through forest, river and wet ground. This explains strategic limits without treating forest people as naturally unconquerable.

### Slavic and Baltic comparisons

The eastern development uses timber villages, ovens, mixed farming, beekeeping, river settlement, gords, assemblies, princely followings and selected cult centres. Odrani, Kreseni and Vezhari are separate period peoples inside an overlapping eastern constellation. No modern Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Baltic or other national identity is projected backwards.

The Vardeni relationship is resolved as old southern kinship, work vocabulary and marriage across approaches followed by political separation before mature Atherian state formation. Kreseni are not the lost ancestors of every later Thalmyric mountain community.

### Scandinavian and heroic-hall histories

The Ättar sequence distinguishes farm-and-hall society, the c. 1600 BR covenant, heroic jarls, early sea-kings and the post-400 AR great keel-taking. Hall warmth, feast, gift and protection operate beside slavery, coercion and recurring child loss.

Varik Stone-Hand translates the heroic defender whose victory cannot supply stable succession. Ravna Blue-Oar translates maritime leadership through ships, shares, repairs and raids without making a female ship owner a skinwalker or creating an ancient national monarchy.

Changeling research supports the revised Moon bargain. The Pale Household takes a human child and leaves a substitute; the original returns only when the changeling reaches adolescence, with fairy time explaining discontinuous memory and the apparent age at return. Northern animal-skin transformation provides a comparative form-boundary without being copied as a removable pelt. The hybrid gift follows the exact words spoken by four named petitioners and therefore attaches to their bodies, offices and paternal descent. The lesser rage follows the separate request concerning every spear sworn beneath them and spreads through the broader oath population.

### Elder Scrolls comparisons

The Reachfolk comparison supplies varied local covenants rather than a single Folk religion. It does not transfer Reachmen, Daedra or clan names.

Vivec's *Sermon Nine* supplies the heroic range of distinct named voice masters. Jurgen Windcaller's received Red Mountain interpretation now supplies a narrower silhouette: after Dramovar, Sarvek withdraws to a high exposed sanctuary and establishes a fringe order of druids devoted to Zephyraxis. It does not create a reformation obeyed by every Deep-Voiced school. The Deep Voice remains World Egg resonance rather than dragon language or a renamed Thu'um.

Yevra, Dorovan and Ravka possess different arts and culpabilities. Dramovar produces Sarvek's mountain withdrawal and religious discipline. Defeat itself is not divine judgement.

### Kaer Maga

Kaer Maga supplies the grammar of a ring structure repurposed as prison and later inhabited by many communities. Nhaldur enlarges that function into a regional crownland of six principal fortresses governing scores of mine prisons, labour camps, hostage houses, road towers and harbour compounds. No Pathfinder name, mystery or plot is transferred.

### Warhammer Greenskins

Warhammer supplies size-and-status loops, accumulating war-hosts, crude material culture, violent exuberance and a slave-warrior project turning against its makers. The Package D development rejects fungal spores, species-wide psychic unity, biologically installed technology and belief as universal machine power.

The reviewed Grobi are intelligent apex predators rather than moral neighbours. Their tools and weapons remain crude; goblins compensate with traps, poison and evasion, while seven-to-nine-foot orcs compensate with the strength of at least five human men and rapid regeneration. Every winter produces converging warbands built from packs which survived the previous cold and grew through the warmer months. The Nine-Drum Host is one unusually large attempt to make that seasonal scourge permanent. Grobi consume a settlement during the attack and keep human captives only as future food or for an immediate task before slaughter.

## Proposed regional geography

The development uses relationships rather than coordinates:

- Nhaldur lies near the southern polar country beyond the principal inhabited ranges;
- Kharvos anchors the largest ancient Grobi ruin-and-market zone;
- Veyrun guards an eastern road towards the Odrani and Kreseni country;
- Avarn opens towards outer cold-water coasts;
- the Belathi occupy western rivers, estuaries, islands and Atlantic-facing districts;
- Hartheni districts occupy the middle woodland and marshes between western and eastern systems;
- Rauven coalitions lie nearer the northern Atherian approaches affected by Casvilar;
- Odrani rivers connect eastern forests, Kreseni uplands and Vezhari shores;
- the Ättar ranges lie far coldward, with Grey-Wolf approaches most exposed to the principal Grobi routes;
- Tidewood remains one western coastal neighbourhood and not the Belathi capital.

No existing map location moves. Package E retains authority over the full island, Sestaran-precursor and inner-sea route network.

## Plain causal chains

### Nhaldur's fall

**Cause:** distant courts use a cold prison system dependent upon imported grain, heat and skilled captives.

**Decision:** local Giant houses answer imperial collapse by opening selected cells, executing hostages and fighting for stores.

**Mechanism:** human clerks, heatworkers, miners and pilots control the labour systems needed for survival and coordinate revolt.

**Effect:** six principal fortresses and their dependent prisons fall differently; refugees carry skills, Giant ancestry and claims into the southern world.

**Cost:** starvation, execution, abandoned dependants, mine fire, flooding, massacre and new local slaveries.

### The First Rewriting

**Cause:** the collapse of Giant armies gives Wrath an opportunity to plant a self-reproducing Material host; Urdazar and Nashara concentrate 2,014 people in a starvation-and-violence programme during civil war.

**Decision:** Nashara accepts the regimen supplied by a disguised Red Reckoner; guards seal the barracks, kill at ration doors and open the full store to select survivors.

**Mechanism:** the Reckoner arranges their choices into a Wrath architecture; Furor separates the human souls and directly rewrites the vacated bodies around food, regeneration and predation.

**Effect:** human souls route; the first intelligent but nonmoral Grobi predators awaken and reproduce.

**Cost:** every victim loses embodied life; people outside the Rewriting die in the breakout; later Grobi inherit Wrath's predatory nature without inheriting the victims' human identities.

### Folk constellations

**Cause:** dispersed Orphaned communities reconnect across different landscapes after Giant collapse.

**Decision:** households build forts, assemblies, river markets, gords, learned houses and local defence agreements.

**Mechanism:** food, work, marriage, fosterage, captivity, migration and foreign contact widen some names while preserving local belonging. Lesser Giant and giant-blooded Nhalduran prisoners enter the mixture, spreading bodily and magical traits chiefly through human descendants while rare true Giant births retain Giant continuity.

**Effect:** three related constellations become historically recognisable without a common state.

**Cost:** local kingship, raiding, slavery, unequal compensation, sacrifice and foreign alliance expose people within as well as beyond each community.

### Empty Halls

**Cause:** every winter drives enlarged Grobi packs north after spring, summer and autumn growth; several abundant years and sudden early freezes produce an exceptional concentration.

**Decision:** Gharuk forms the Nine-Drum Host to seize a permanent Warm Road; divided halls fail to muster together.

**Mechanism:** Varka organises a moving predatory host; Grobi consume captured settlements until Urzha betrays Gharuk, human supply holds and the four lunar transformations cut his food road.

**Effect:** the host breaks, Gharuk's own orcs eat him alive and four pact-kindreds form.

**Cost:** consumed villages, prisoners kept as marching meat, dead Grobi brood, human reprisals and a firstborn price borne by every later old-rite ruling generation.

### Deep Voice mountain withdrawal

**Cause:** heroic war makes masters politically prestigious and normalises force at a prince's deadline.

**Decision:** nine masters attempt a combined earth wake at unfamiliar Dramovar despite Sarvek's warning.

**Mechanism:** the cadence enters a wet seam behind the allied camp.

**Effect:** military defeat and civilian catastrophe drive Sarvek into the high mountains, where he founds the fringe Quiet Measure druids of Zephyraxis while martial schools continue.

**Cost:** at least nine hundred people die; survivors lose kin, voice, hearing and political trust.

## Material and logistics review

- Nhaldur survives through imported grain, local fish, geothermal heat, mines, roads and harbour shipping. Loss of supply drives its civil war.
- Refugee movements use barges, smaller coast craft, roads and opened eastern bridges rather than an unexplained mass march.
- Early Folk and Ättar societies rely on mixed farming, livestock, fishing, hunting, storage and repair.
- Grobi growth and host size are directly constrained by food, transport, weather and bodily consumption. Every winter therefore creates a new convergence of bands which grew through the warmer months.
- The Nine-Drum Host carries crude sledges, rough smiths, breeding hearths, animals and humans kept as food. Cutting the sled road explains its loss of cohesion.
- Human victory requires boats, grain, gates, scouts and intelligence sold by a Grobi betrayer in addition to four transformations.
- Gords depend upon villages and river landings. The Seven Gords war is fought over grain boats, tolls, mines, winter quarters and hostages.
- Deep Voice effects depend upon known terrain, prepared structures, acoustic testing, trained breath and bodily cost. Far calls relay speech; storm turning redirects existing weather; neither is an unexplained lightning attack.
- Atherian reach remains strongest along rivers, roads and allied crossings and weakest in dispersed forests, outer coasts and winter refuges.

## Collision review

### Nhaldur and the Five Imperial Horizons

Nhaldur is a shared lesser crownland and extraction prison. Its Council of Keys depends upon houses connected to larger powers, while the Imperial Horizons and numerous lesser southern crowns use its many subordinate prisons. It has no continental civilisational system, universal cult or claim to peer imperial status.

### Nhaldur and Vathra

Vathra is a confederation of flying crown-cities above a ground economy. Nhaldur is a fixed polar prison system of ring-fortresses. Veyrun uses Material pressure and sound; it neither flies like Vathra nor pursues Nythalum's physical Moon project.

### First Rewriting and Giant body craft

Nemek programmes can starve, drug, injure and alter bodies. They cannot remove the human soul or author a new creaturely continuity. A dependent Red Reckoner directly guides the programme as part of Wrath's plan, and Wrath alone performs the Rewriting. This remains the most important promotion lock.

### Grobi and human captives

Grobi do not adopt human children or maintain human slaves as household members. Humans taken in raids remain human and survive only as stored food, guides, interpreters or specialists for an immediate task before slaughter. Grobi children raised in human captivity remain Grobi and retain their predatory nature. Diet, training and household declaration change neither continuity.

### Grobi moral agency

Current Canon Reference material assigns Grobi full moral agency and explicitly allows mercy, restraint and cooperation. The author's Package D review replaces that ruling with intelligence and tactical choice without human moral conscience. This is a deliberate proposed retcon, not a stylistic paraphrase. The canon-integration pass must change metadata as well as prose in the Grobi stage dossiers, Grobi Overview, soul-and-continuity control and any linked encounter guidance. Package D cannot be promoted selectively while leaving both rulings active.

### War of the Empty Halls and G-61

G-61 remains an earlier local conflict about incompatible duties. The Nine-Drum Host and firstborn bargain are later, larger events. No G-61 protagonist is silently turned into one of the four chiefs.

### Mother Moon and the Deep Voice

The Moon bargain grants monstrous hybrid forms to four named paternal office lines and the lesser rage to the broader oath population. The Deep Voice originates in eastern work and resonance schools. Sarvek's fringe devotion to Zephyraxis is later and local. Neither Menhir causes or owns the other tradition.

### Deep Voice and Harmonic Shellcraft

Both address elemental relations through resonance. Deep Voice is an older, principally unaccompanied eastern tradition using meaningful speech, bodily stance and known chambers. A far call transmits intelligible words along a tested path; weather turning redirects existing local air or precipitation. Neither phrase implies lightning. Later Harmonic Shellcraft uses a broader conductor, refrain, lead and cadence grammar. Canon integration should cross-link them without making one institution a renamed version of the other.

### Kreseni and Vardeni

The two peoples possess old southern kinship and continued contact. Vardeni formation remains within the Aurin and Atherian history already promoted. Kreseni do not replace them or become ancestors of every mountain-refuge population.

### Tidewood

Tidewood remains one local Atlantic Folk history. Brael, Dovren and Morlach widen the western context without placing the Three Beaches at its centre. Aulon's c. 1260 dry fleet and Casvilar's 385 voyage remain separate.

### Casvilar

The proposed 350 BR balance reproduces all named participants, dates, acts and limits in the Southern Campaigns. New Rauven placement is regional context, not a change to Kellovar, Darneth, Morveth, Drenoc or Velcara.
