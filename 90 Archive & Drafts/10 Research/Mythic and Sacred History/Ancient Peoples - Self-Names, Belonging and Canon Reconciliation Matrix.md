## Classification interface

| Field | Meaning in this pass |
|---|---|
| **People** | A durable community recognising shared descent, belonging and inherited cultural obligations. |
| **Regional culture** | Related communities connected by speech and practice without requiring one government or ancestry. |
| **Civic population** | People whose public identity and rights belong to a particular city. |
| **Political identity** | Membership or subjection within a kingdom, republic, league or empire. |
| **Dynasty** | A ruling household. It is never used as an ethnic category. |
| **Legal status** | Citizen, ally, subject, dependant, freed person, enslaved person, hostage or exile. These conditions may shape culture but are not peoples. |
| **Principal descent** | Ark-derived, Orphaned or Braided at population scale. It makes no claim of personal purity. |
| **Formation mode** | The historical process—branching, continuity, fusion, diaspora, assimilation or civic incorporation—through which the identity became durable. |

Intermarriage does not by itself create a Braided people. Braiding requires a durable shared society inherited by descendants of previously distinct communities. A person may have mixed ancestry while belonging fully to one established people.

## Difficult-case reconciliation

### Enathi, Serathi and Vardeni within Atheria

Their Orphaned ancestry remains fixed. Atherian speech, Common Sky worship and Caleran citizenship can be acquired without requiring renunciation of local kin. A person may use both identities. Canon should not describe these peoples as disappearing when their governments or public language changed.

### Ortheian and Atherian

Ortheian speech belongs to the Atherian family. Ortheia possessed its own crown and civic life. Shared language did not give Caleran authority, and Valeron's hegemony did not make every Atherian city Ortheian. The relationship provides the clearest ancient example of language without common citizenship.

### Jirahar service in Arqesh

An exile could become an Arqeshite-speaking scribe, soldier or water official while remaining a recognised Jirahar. A descendant could also leave Jirahar communal life and enter a local household. Neither outcome was automatic. Deportation remains coercive even where later generations prospered.

### Vashari and Vasharan

Vashari is a people and cultural family. Vasharan describes the empire, its government, service and subjects. The empire was founded by a Vashari house and governed many non-Vashari populations. No canon table should list all Vasharan subjects as Vashari.

### Qahuri and the Twin-Reed crowns

Qahuri civilisation crossed political divisions. Tenareti, Qemareti and Sakhreti allegiance remained meaningful without creating three ancestries. Foreign occupation changed rulers and taxes while leaving Qahuri households and institutions in place.

### Gateway, Qarzethi and Sarqeni

Gateway describes the older Braided regional society. Qarzethi was a civic identity centred on the destroyed capital. Sarqeni became a later civic and royal identity centred on a surviving harbour. Neither capital owned the ancestry of the entire country.

### Samyra's four inheritances

Samyra's Gateway country, Hadrima's Esharati house, junior Qahuri maternal kin and Delkosi court culture remain four relationships. Together they explain her political resources. They do not create universal Qahuri descent, restore Qarzethi citizenship or make the Nerethans descendants of Eryndor.

## Proposed ordinary additions

The following details are proposed because the current history requires them to explain belonging. They create no new headline event:

- *Atherian* becomes a broad maritime-cultural name before it becomes a Caleran political designation.
- *Qahuri* becomes most useful in foreign, royal and cross-Basin settings while local self-identification remains strong.
- Tenareti, Qemareti and Sakhreti are available as transparent reader demonyms; exact ancient linguistic forms remain open.
- Qarzethi descendants retain a diasporic origin after 520 BR without inheriting office or guilt.
- *Sarqeni* develops separate civic, subject and court meanings under the Nerethans.
- *Jirahar* becomes a broad communal name as houses recognise shared obligations across routes; no fabricated primordial endonym is supplied.
- Arqeshite identity survives the Ubar dynasty as an urban and river culture.
- Vasharan is fixed as political and imperial, never ethnic.
- Delkosi expands from city affiliation into a mixed fleet-and-court identity.
- Tasar, Ilyessa and Avesh retain city identities; no common eastern coastal ethnonym is invented.

## Items requiring no canon change

- Ark-derived, Orphaned and Braided ancestry rulings remain intact.
- Every human population possesses complete human souls.
- Giant descent, Menhir persons, Idol Masks, Patron roles and human offices remain distinct.
- Existing massacres, sacrifice, enslavement, deportation, rape, hostage-taking and forced labour remain as written.
- The Common Sky does not merge supernatural persons or make conquest divinely commanded.
- The White Measure remains a minority Qahuri practice rather than a people.
- The Crowned Countenance and Witness of Dawn remain dependent Pride Masks, not ethnic gods.
- The Bronze-Faced Provider remains a dependent Gluttony Mask and does not define all Gateway worship.

## Review disposition

All twenty-five profiles were approved. Related profiles were merged into shared articles where separate entries would have repeated most of the same history. The review accepted *Atherian* as a widening maritime-cultural name, Tenareti, Qemareti and Sakhreti as reader-facing regional demonyms, a standalone Hadrathi article and the bounded use of *Delkosi* for city, fleet and court communities.
