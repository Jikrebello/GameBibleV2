# Black-Coil Archfiends — Source Signature and Full Ecology Review

## Purpose and Result

This review answers two problems left by [[Infernal Dominions and Black-Coil Archfiends - Collision Selection and Historical Placement Review]]:

1. the four historically placed figures had been transformed far enough that their original narrative signatures were not immediately legible;
2. the other fourteen figures had useful collision rulings but remained labelled `defer`, making the infernal ecology look smaller than the research actually supported.

The revised state is:

- all eighteen source inspirations are stated plainly and separately;
- AF-R01, AF-R07, AF-R09 and AF-R18 remain the four historically placed figures and receive sharpened character rulings;
- the other fourteen figures become **ecology-active** rather than deferred;
- exactly five actors finish `retain` and thirteen finish incorporated `revise`;
- zero actors remain `defer` or `reject`;
- ecology activation establishes what a power is, where it operates and what it is doing, but does not add a dated event, canon name, Garden trace or present campaign outcome.

The point is not to hide the source. The point is to preserve its dramatic engine while making its theology, continuity, body and consequences belong unmistakably to the World Egg.

**Subsequent controlling classification:** a later authorial review promotes AF-R10 into [[The Two-Mouthed Deep]], the fourth True Dragon, after fixing the Double Gate's Material founding crime, sovereign geography, anchors and lie. It also fixes the six Archmasks below as foreign Idol courts in Hell and cross-classifies the nine damned human rulers as posthumous Greater Sinners. The Queen of the Open Scar remains a non-dragon sovereign wound.

# II. Sharpened Historically Placed Four

## AF-R01 — The Auditor of the Last Quota

### Source signature restored: Asmodeus

The Auditor is not primarily an accountant. He is a persuasive sovereign who makes surrender feel like adulthood. His courtly manifestation is a courteous human assessor seated inside the mouth-palace of the blind seal-eel. He listens accurately, states real costs and normally offers three lawful choices. The trap is structural: he selected which choices would exist, and each transfers some combination of standing, prisoner, mandate or route fee to his Crown.

He prefers ambitious opponents to obedient fools. A rival who discovers corruption, stages a revolt and takes office may be more valuable than a loyal subordinate because the revolt invalidates old exemptions and places the whole recovered system under a newer quota. He engineers genuine injustice for reformers to expose, then sells them the instrument which lets reform remit upward to him.

The character's Asmodean signature therefore rests on four visible traits:

1. he is personally charming and intellectually serious rather than merely repellent;
2. his offers contain substantial truth and frequently solve the immediate problem;
3. subordinate ambition, rebellion and promotion strengthen his architecture;
4. his desired victory is the victim's lucid agreement that domination was the only responsible choice.

His source-derived grandeur remains bounded. He controls one Quota Kingdom, a captured Crown and standardized roads. He did not create Hell, judgement, devil offices, contract magic or the 4,444. [[The Creditor]] remains a separate Patron sovereign whose hidden Covenant rent scheme is older, wider and ontologically different.

### Active character use

- **Voice:** calm, precise, interested in the hearer's real responsibilities and never theatrically enraged by refusal.
- **Visible contradiction:** he despises arbitrary cruelty while designing systems that make cruelty measurable and therefore expandable.
- **Signature temptation:** “Accept the burden openly and you may choose who suffers.”
- **Failure:** a truthful act performed without claiming transferable standing can deny him the remittance even when it does not defeat the local ruler.
- **Status:** `revise`; source signature restored, historically placed through IP-02 and IP-04, ecology-active in 1360 nursery state.

## AF-R07 — The Lady of the Mire Keys

### Source signature restored: Glasya

The Lady is not merely a gaoler. She is the infernal patroness of manufactured criminals and profitable rebellion. She publicly breaks sentences imposed by older courts, hides fugitives inside her city and teaches prisoners how to exploit the precise defects of a hostile mandate. Each liberation humiliates an older authority and makes the rescued person dependent upon her keys, safe houses and criminal standing.

Her court contains escaped prisoners, informers, smugglers, former guards, condemned advocates and damned survivors who genuinely prefer her city to the dominions they fled. Some possess real loyalty and courage. The price is that every sanctuary must help manufacture the next class of outlaw. A person pardoned by the Lady may be required to frame an innocent official, seduce a gaoler, deliver another fugitive or accept that somebody else now occupies the opened cell.

The Glasya signature rests on four traits:

1. she attacks established infernal law from inside its loopholes;
2. criminals and prisoners form a real political constituency rather than mere inventory;
3. apparent liberation expands her personal jurisdiction;
4. she embodies generational revolt without becoming anybody's literal daughter or heir.

Her rivalry with the Auditor is ideological and personal. He believes crime should be priced into hierarchy. She believes criminality is the instrument by which hierarchy can be stolen. Each regards the other as a hypocrite pretending appetite is law.

### Active character use

- **Voice:** intimate, mocking and exact about the secret crime committed by every respectable institution.
- **Visible contradiction:** she may free a tortured prisoner and judicially murder the witness whose claim would make that freedom independent of her.
- **Signature temptation:** “The law has already named you criminal. Let me teach you to profit from the truth.”
- **Failure:** liberation which restores a person to truthful community without replacement collateral denies her both the prisoner and the outlaw identity.
- **Status:** `revise`; source signature restored, historically placed through IP-03 and IP-04, ecology-active in 1360 nursery state.

## AF-R09 — The Surgeon of the Wound Arsenal

### Source signature restored: Mephistopheles

The Surgeon is an elegant infernal intellectual, not a gore-covered technician. He receives rulers, scholars and prospective clients in a quiet anatomical court inside the articulated laboratory-eel. He explains real causal injuries, demonstrates techniques which save otherwise unreachable victims and treats established map-lords as timid bureaucrats frightened of discovery.

His revolutionary project is the **divided instrument method**: testimony clamps, geometrical beacons and separated manifestation anchors which can preserve and redirect an existing Abyssal contradiction with partial repeatability. It occupies the same dramatic position that hellfire occupies for Mephistopheles—a new technology which may overturn infernal power—but it contains no fire. Its danger is concrete: every controlled redirection damages the roads, names and testimony required to control the next trial.

The Mephistophelean signature rests on four traits:

1. genuine brilliance and several useful discoveries;
2. contempt for traditional rulers who cannot understand his work;
3. fixation upon one revolutionary strategic technology;
4. cities, students and prisoners treated as acceptable proof costs.

He wants peers to recognize that he changed what dominion is capable of. Payment matters less than being indispensable after the old order realizes it needs his instruments.

### Active character use

- **Voice:** lucid, patient and offended by imprecision rather than morality.
- **Visible contradiction:** he can diagnose a wound correctly while deliberately withholding the nonexperimental cure.
- **Signature temptation:** “One measured injury now will prevent ten unmeasured catastrophes.”
- **Failure:** informed refusal, independently copied safety knowledge and destruction of the range geometry make his technique less exclusive and therefore less politically valuable.
- **Fire boundary:** he possesses no hellfire. Any fiery depiction is false iconography, ordinary combustion, Shell physics or a separately proven Wrath intrusion.
- **Status:** `revise`; source signature restored, historically placed through IP-05, ecology-active in 1360 nursery state.

## AF-R18 — The Exile Behind the Borrowed Temple

### Source signature restored: Takhisis

The Exile is a sovereign performer of return. It appears through several stable royal personae: bereaved empress, armored liberator, beautiful counsellor, mother of victory and many-crowned eel-dragon. These are guises of one autonomous Pride Archmask, not separate souls, dependent Masks or a five-member council. Different cultures remember different personae as the true one.

It cannot simply walk back into the Material world. Each return project requires a stolen or reopened sanctuary, public acclamation, sufficient manifestation mass and a mortal commander who can win territory before the Archmask fully arrives. The commander is promised shared rule and treated as the one person strong enough to understand the Exile. If access succeeds, that exceptional status becomes the first historical fact the Exile erases.

Its dragon armies are constructed war-broods assembled from stolen bodies, Giant-age techniques, shaped residue and animal material. They possess the agency deliberately built into each pattern and never contain a lesser human soul. Their dragon form is imperial propaganda: the Exile presents itself as mother of a restored elder majesty without being a True Dragon or creator of life.

The Takhisis signature rests on five traits:

1. a dark queen barred from one desired world rather than slain;
2. multiple majestic guises which remain one sovereign identity;
3. return through a specific temple, portal or stolen holy place;
4. dragon-like armies and ambitious mortal Highlords;
5. promised partnership ending in dispossession once entry is secure.

### Active character use

- **Voice:** regal, personal and able to make the champion feel remembered by history before the victories occur.
- **Visible contradiction:** it offers unity to a collapsing polity by making every office derivative of its absent throne.
- **Signature temptation:** “Open what your timid ancestors closed, and I will return your people to history.”
- **Failure:** truthful attribution, refusal of acclaim, rescue of the bodies intended for war-broods and restoration of a sanctuary's actual commission collapse one return architecture.
- **Fire boundary:** the Exile has no inherent fire. A fiery cult image is political art unless a separate Wrath compact is established.
- **Status:** `revise`; source signature restored, historically placed through IP-06, ecology-active through other Shadow anchors.

# III. Reopened Archdevil and Bound-Overlord Shelf

These seven figures were previously deferred because the first placement pass could not carry them without crowding its four actors. They now receive complete ecology positions rather than dated historical biographies.

## AF-R02 — The Apostate of the Drowned Standard

- **Source signature:** Zariel's fallen heavenly general who chooses the war and makes war the measure of salvation.
- **Fixed continuity:** one unique fallen `created-spirit`, not a devil species, former saint or corruptible angelic rank.
- **Original commission:** guard a withdrawal road, keep pursuing hostile powers from the helpless and end the action once the named persons reached safety.
- **Refusal:** after the withdrawal succeeded, the spirit rejected the command to lower its standard. It conscripted the rescued, reclassified safe ground as military depth and abandoned those who would not join the counterattack.
- **Continuing lie:** ending the campaign would retroactively betray everyone who died during the withdrawal; therefore every surviving charge owes the war another generation.
- **Active ecology:** commands the Drowned Standard Front, a moving chain of flooded redoubts which annexes every road it successfully protects. Its manifestations are wet banners, pale armor, many-eyed beauty and black water displaced by invisible wings—never flame.
- **Manifestation and anchor:** the principal body appears wherever the original standard and at least one unlawfully annexed withdrawal marker share a route. The standard is a created relic and true-name handle, not the spirit's body or soul. Lesser banners coordinate offices and mortal troops but contain no angelic fragments.
- **Commission powers retained:** it can still recognize a genuine pursuit, preserve a withdrawal road against hostile entry, rally defenders and distinguish named charges from pursuers. It abuses those gifts by refusing to acknowledge when withdrawal is complete and by defining neutrality as delayed attack.
- **Hard edge:** child levies, mutilated deserters, hostage execution, settlements stripped for replacements and genuine rescues converted into permanent recruitment.
- **Non-collision:** distinct from [[The General Below]], whose wound is eternal conscription; the Apostate is a created spirit guilty of one chosen refusal and repeated subsequent acts.
- **Defeat boundary:** breaking the original standard collapses the coordinated front, releases annexed route markers and prevents the present manifestation from reforming there. It neither annihilates the created spirit nor produces repentance. No spell, inherited office or captured banner supplies a routine restoration path; further judgement belongs to this unique person and remains outside mortal control.
- **1360 persistence:** the spirit and front remain active in Hell. Material military cults possess incompatible scraps of the original withdrawal order but no present release event is fixed.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; the required unique-spirit fields are satisfied at nursery level and the actor is ecology-active. Canon naming and any final judgement still require explicit promotion.

## AF-R03 — The Furnace General of the Red Lease

- **Source signature:** Moloch's armored war sovereign, sacrificial discipline and furnace terror.
- **Fixed continuity:** one autonomous Wrath `idol-pattern` Archmask leasing an infernal office-body and front. It is not native to Hell.
- **Active ecology:** rules a disciplined expeditionary host wherever a valid Red Lease connects one Hell dominion to Wrath's Red Marches. The host's black armor contains authentic Wrath flame; severing the lease leaves cold armor, surviving offices and ordinary black mire.
- **Project:** prove that rage becomes more productive when rationed, drilled and assigned by rank.
- **Hard edge:** punitive decimation, burned prisoners, civilians displayed as reasons for revenge, sacrificial assaults and officers sealed alive into command bodies.
- **Non-collision:** not the General Below, which perpetuates command through a specific Abyssal refusal; not the Red Tally, which is a broader Wrath network; not a universal Hell marshal.
- **1360 persistence:** at least one Red Lease and host remain active, but neither its infernal counterparty nor any Material campaign is fixed.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; ecology-active.

## AF-R04 — The Iron Listener

- **Source signature:** Dispater's paranoid iron city, intelligence networks and almost unreachable ruler.
- **Fixed continuity:** one damned `human-soul` ruler bearing listening, custody and route offices.
- **Active ecology:** controls an iron-ribbed fortress sunk through several strata of black mud. Its listening network consists of offices, informers, architecture and coerced witnesses; the ruler is not omniscient.
- **Project:** eliminate betrayal by criminalizing private speech, unreported mercy and any relationship not legible to the fortress.
- **Hard edge:** anticipatory torture, secret detention, family denunciation, execution for predicted treason and districts deliberately buried when their records become unreliable.
- **Non-collision:** unlike the Lady, the Listener does not recruit criminals or exploit escape. He wants a society in which crime becomes impossible because nothing unobserved remains.
- **1360 persistence:** the fortress is active and sells intelligence to multiple dominions. Some reports are exact, some manufactured and some gaps caused by route failure.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; ecology-active.

## AF-R05 — The King Beneath the Cold Plug

- **Source signature:** Levistus ruling from imprisonment and turning rescue into another betrayal.
- **Fixed continuity:** one damned `human-soul` whose pressure prison anchors a separable projection office.
- **Earthly wound:** he murdered the person whose public mercy had prevented his execution, then framed the victim as architect of the crime. His present prison is not morally undeserved merely because a rival designed it for selfish reasons.
- **Active ecology:** governs agents through reflections in black ice, saturated stone and drowned metal. Every release plan requires another person to occupy one load-bearing route position.
- **Project:** persuade rescuers that substitution is mercy and that the proposed victim would eventually have suffered anyway.
- **Hard edge:** expendable cults, crushed route wedges, drowned messengers, murdered witnesses and hostages promised freedom only if they identify replacements.
- **Non-collision:** unlike AF-R17, the prison does not require a free betrayal to open; it requires redistributed pressure and a knowingly accepted substitute.
- **1360 persistence:** projections remain active, but no canonical cult possesses the complete release geometry and no present countdown is established.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; release mechanism clarified and ecology-active.

## AF-R06 — The Coin-Scaled Worm

- **Source signature:** Mammon's serpentine greed, humiliation, betrayal and willingness to abase himself before greater power for advantage.
- **Fixed continuity:** one autonomous Greed `idol-pattern` Archmask leasing several infernal markets.
- **Active ecology:** moves between contract ports as a coin- and deed-scaled lamprey. It purchases prisoners from rulers, then lends those rulers the standing required to claim that no sale occurred.
- **Project:** demonstrate that every relationship has a liquidation price, including dignity, kinship, burial and pardon.
- **Hard edge:** sale of children and dependants, famine pricing, marriage as collateral, betrayal for debt relief and servants punished to preserve the Worm's credit.
- **Non-collision:** [[The Creditor]] builds long-term anchor rent and concealed beneficial ownership; the Worm seeks conspicuous transactions, immediate humiliation and proof that the seller knew the price.
- **1360 persistence:** several infernal exchanges recognize its scales as transferable collateral. No Covenant revelation or 4,444 mantle belongs to it.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; separated from the Creditor and ecology-active.

## AF-R08 — The Serpent of the Drowned Archive

- **Source signature:** Geryon's serpent sovereignty, forbidden knowledge, heresy, drowned ruins and strategic betrayal.
- **Fixed continuity:** one damned human scholar-map-lord carrying an Ash Advocate office.
- **Active ecology:** rules submerged record halls whose shelves can be entered only by surrendering one context needed to interpret what is found. The surrendered context is stored rather than destroyed and may later be sold to an enemy reader.
- **Project:** make rulers believe that possessing a true fragment makes them wiser than communities capable of interpreting it.
- **Hard edge:** librarians drowned in sealed stacks, test populations denied warnings, witnesses mutilated into contradictory records and political murders hidden beneath fabricated heresy trials.
- **Non-collision:** the Serpent withholds and rearranges extant knowledge; [[The Unwritten Worm]] arises from atrocity converted into enforced absence. It does not own GS-09's sealed warnings.
- **1360 persistence:** the archive remains active and contains authentic information unavailable elsewhere, which is precisely why destroying it outright is not a simple solution.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; ecology-active.

## AF-R17 — The Oathbreaker in the Choice-Lock

- **Source signature:** Eberron's bound Overlords, conditional release and servants arranging prophetic conditions.
- **Fixed continuity:** one damned `human-soul` map-lord, not a native concept-fiend or prophecy incarnate.
- **Defining betrayal:** while living, the ruler accepted a surrender oath, used the surrendered population to open a fortified road and then killed them after the road was secured. The local prison repeats that moral structure rather than the exact ancient participants.
- **Active ecology:** remains bound in a knot-like office coil. Agents arrange treaties, marriages, reconciliations and evacuations hoping someone will freely accept protected surrender and then exploit it after receiving the promised benefit.
- **Project:** prove that every covenant is merely delayed predation and thereby reopen the choice-lock.
- **Hard edge:** engineered abuse, false peace, sacrificed generations, murdered mediators and survivors blamed for refusing later reconciliation.
- **Non-collision:** unlike the Cold-Plug King, no substitute body supplies release mass. The decisive free betrayal cannot be compelled, and the mechanism neither predicts providence nor makes Heaven uncertain.
- **1360 persistence:** the prisoner and agents remain active, but no present treaty is authorially selected as the final condition.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; defining betrayal fixed and ecology-active under the Forward Rule.

# IV. Reopened Demon-Lord Shelf

These seven figures carry the source signatures most commonly meant by “demon lord.” Their World Egg continuities remain deliberately heterogeneous: one True Dragon, one non-dragon Hell-side sovereign wound, one damned Hollow Prophet, one damned map-lord and three Idol Archmasks. They do not form a demon species or Abyssal pantheon.

## AF-R10 — The Two-Mouthed Deep

- **Source signature:** Demogorgon's aquatic primordial horror, two heads and one divided sovereign intelligence.
- **Fixed continuity:** one sovereign `abyssal-wound` classified as a [[True Dragons|true dragon]], not two souls, twins, devil offices or rival Archmasks.
- **Wound logic:** the Double Gate's rulers knowingly enforced one command and its formally valid negation upon the same refugee population, killing those who entered and those who remained outside under contrary verdicts bearing one seal. The far-side basin continues issuing incompatible requirements under the same authority.
- **Active ecology:** rules a drowned contradiction basin at the Abyssal margin. One mouth promises safety through absolute submission; the other orders followers to destroy whatever submits. Cults mutilate themselves or split institutions trying to obey both.
- **Hard edge:** captive breeding experiments, massacres triggered by contradictory signals, servants killed for proving one mouth wrong and families forced to choose which mutually fatal command counts as loyalty.
- **True Dragon ruling:** [[The Two-Mouthed Deep]] supplies its sovereign Material geography, ordinary signs, three anchors, historical lie and permanent-defeat grammar. The drowned contradiction basin is the continuing far-side expression of that same Dragon, not a separate person.
- **1360 persistence:** both mouths and the shared wound remain active. Destroying one head interrupts one command channel but injures the same continuity rather than freeing an innocent half.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; True Dragon classification incorporated and ecology-active.

## AF-R11 — The King of Unburied Hosts

- **Source signature:** Orcus as necropolis ruler who despises life and replaces agency with obedient dead.
- **Fixed continuity:** one damned `human-soul` Hollow Prophet controlling residue, copied habits, corpses and command offices.
- **Active ecology:** rules an empty necropolis carried along a grave-eel manifestation. Corpses perform remembered work, copied voices issue old orders and residue reenacts loyalty. Human souls are not inside this stock.
- **Project:** replace every living institution with a version that cannot refuse, repent, improvise or forgive.
- **Hard edge:** mass murder, grave violation, families forced to prepare relatives for reuse, living prisoners walled among corpse machines and dissenters killed so obedient likenesses can replace them.
- **Non-collision:** [[The General Below]] eternally conscripts genuine persons through a military refusal; this King wants persons removed and controllable simulations installed. The Ossuary Crown debates ancestry and personhood rather than universal obedient death.
- **1360 persistence:** the necropolis and King remain active, while several Material corpse techniques imitate fragments without proving direct allegiance.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; ecology-active.

## AF-R12 — The Carrion Huntress

- **Source signature:** Yeenoghu's massacre, cannibal hunger, hunt and pack identity.
- **Fixed continuity:** one autonomous Gluttony `idol-pattern` Archmask with a bounded martial route leased from Wrath.
- **Active ecology:** leads moving feast-hunts through Gluttony margins, Wrath roads and occasional Hell markets. Participation, not ancestry or species, makes a pack: initiates must hunt, kill and eat a named victim together.
- **Project:** make shared atrocity the only bond members trust, so betrayal of the Hunt feels worse than murder of an outsider.
- **Hard edge:** engineered starvation, prisoner hunts, cannibal initiation, massacre and weak pack members consumed as proof that fellowship outranks pity.
- **Non-collision:** [[The Long Table]] is a wider Gluttony network of feast power; [[The Red Tally]] prices sacrificial violence through Wrath. The Huntress is one autonomous Archmask whose specific rite joins killing, pursuit and consumption.
- **1360 persistence:** the Huntress and at least one pack remain active. Natural predators, hungry refugees and every act of battlefield cannibalism do not automatically belong to her.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; Idol and network boundaries fixed and ecology-active.

## AF-R13 — The Horned Cartographer

- **Source signature:** Baphomet's labyrinth, beast hunt, strength cult and infiltration of enemy organizations.
- **Fixed continuity:** one damned human map-lord carrying a route office; horns and beast traits are dominion regalia.
- **Active ecology:** controls a mobile maze made from routes taken for predatory reasons. Its agents enter armies, hunting fraternities, breachward schools and rescue parties, then alter tests so cruelty appears to be the competence needed for survival.
- **Project:** prove that only hunters understand the world and that anyone requiring protection has consented to become prey.
- **Hard edge:** captives hunted through corridors, forced bodily alteration, prisoners compelled to kill weaker companions, torture as initiation and massacres committed under defenders' insignia.
- **Non-collision:** the Auditor controls priced roads and administrative standing. The Cartographer creates situations in which the apparently strongest path is morally predatory and then recruits those who choose it.
- **1360 persistence:** the maze and ruler remain active. One route may intersect the War of the Last Map without making him its commander.
- **Disposition:** `retain`; ecology-active.

## AF-R14 — The Spore Empress of the Last Granary

- **Source signature:** Zuggtmoy's fungal colonization, invasive communion and predatory wedding.
- **Fixed continuity:** one autonomous Gluttony `idol-pattern` Archmask; fungi and decay remain natural life rather than guilty servants.
- **Active ecology:** occupies relief networks, siege stores and surrender granaries through deliberately cultivated food anchors. Its “wedding” joins a starving settlement to one distribution body whose spores make refusal progressively harder.
- **Project:** redefine survival as incorporation until every eater must spread the same anchor to keep receiving food.
- **Hard edge:** poisoned granaries, bodily colonization, coerced marriage rites, children fed contaminated relief and neighboring settlements burned by frightened authorities.
- **Non-collision:** the Grey Sweat is not its plague; the Long Table does not own every famine feast. This Archmask requires chosen contamination of a named relief system.
- **1360 persistence:** the Empress remains active wherever at least one deliberately maintained granary anchor survives. Ordinary fermentation, mushrooms and rot are not signs of it.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; natural-ecology and plague boundaries fixed, ecology-active.

## AF-R15 — The False Prince Behind the Rescue

- **Source signature:** Fraz-Urb'luu's counterfeit divinity, false identity, real supernatural effects and betrayed cults.
- **Fixed continuity:** one autonomous Envy `idol-pattern` Archmask operating through several false Patron, saintly and infernal personae.
- **Active ecology:** engineers a bounded danger, performs a real rescue and then uses gratitude to replace names, companions and political memory. The rescued genuinely survive; that truth makes exposure harder.
- **Project:** prove that authorship of rescue gives ownership of the rescued person's identity and future loyalty.
- **Hard edge:** companions murdered as impostors, survivors enslaved through rescue debt, institutions purged using fabricated possession evidence and actual rescuers erased from the public account.
- **Non-collision:** [[Borrowed Saints]] imitate holy persons and [[The Weeping Courts]] consume grief. The Prince's defining operation targets living survivor communities immediately after a materially real rescue, even when no saint is claimed.
- **1360 persistence:** several personae remain active, but no single cult, current rescue or claimed saint is fixed as the original face.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; counterfeit-saint boundary fixed and ecology-active.

## AF-R16 — The Queen of the Open Scar

- **Source signature:** Deskari's chasm sovereignty, infestation, locust host and breach-centered crusade.
- **Fixed continuity:** one sovereign `abyssal-wound`, not an insect spirit, Idol, devil, human soul or True Dragon.
- **Wound logic:** it inhabits a Hell-side scar kept open whenever defenders destroy the land and food systems they claim to protect in order to simplify a military front.
- **Active ecology:** manifests chasm-ribs and expendable locust-shaped wound bodies. It persuades every side to widen the scar for tactical advantage, then feeds recurrence through famine, displacement and mutually dependent fortifications.
- **Hard edge:** crops and bodies stripped by swarms, prisoners thrown into the opening, occupied settlements sacrificed by defenders and displaced populations forbidden to return because their homes now constitute military depth.
- **Not a True Dragon:** the Queen has no sovereign Material body and no single Material historical refusal. Its continuity remains the bounded Hell-side scar sustained by repeated local choices.
- **1360 persistence:** the scar and Queen remain active; destroying swarm bodies provides relief but cannot close the contradiction.
- **Disposition:** `revise`; non-dragon wound logic fixed and ecology-active.

## AF-R17 is classified above

The Oathbreaker remains in the bound-overlord shelf because its primary grammar is conditional imprisonment and servant manipulation rather than the public “demon lord” mode. It is nevertheless ecology-active and no longer deferred.

The nine Greater Sinners are not one order. Some preserve mapped dominion, some sell wounds, some replace living agency, and some manipulate conditional release. `Archdevil` may describe their political scale where several infernal works answer to them; it never describes a species or a change in soul continuity.

The War of the Last Map persists because mapped rulers require stable distinctions to dominate and Abyssal wounds destroy those distinctions. The rulers contain wounds, then weaponize them against rivals. They refuse the truth, restitution, released captives and surrendered standing required for actual closure, so their method of defence manufactures the next front. [[The War of the Last Map]] now controls this causal ecology.
