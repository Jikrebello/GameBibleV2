# Idol Heartlands, Masks, and Hunger Wars Review

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[Idol Kingdoms and the Hunger Wars]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

## Purpose and Status

This is the sixth and final family review arising from [[Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas]]. It develops all ten `ID-R` leads into usable political and historical nurseries without turning the seven Idols into emotion-created gods, equal cosmic nations or one council of evil divinities.

Nothing here is canon, dated history, a named Archmask, a true Regnant succession or a promoted Garden seed. Four leads are retained and six receive incorporated revisions. The revisions chiefly replace vague “belief feeding,” unclassified divine personalities and apparent succession with concrete institutions, separately classified actors and observable consequences.

[[First Idol Constellation - Historical Placement Matrix]] subsequently gives four of these leads provisional soil: a Late-Chains river war, a City-State Archmask claimant, an Underground-to-Legalization counterfeit-martyr conflict and a post-Split biography purge. [[First Idol Constellation - Collision and Disposition Review]] retains two and incorporates revisions into two. [[Greater-Power Garden Balance and Trace Promotion Review]] later promotes one light trace from the river war and one from the counterfeit-martyr conflict while withholding the two major autonomous-Archmask histories. All four complete histories remain unnamed in canon, succession-neutral and Garden-locked. The other six leads remain reviewed but undated and unplaced.

The controlling result is:

> Idol warfare is a struggle to make chosen human corruption repeat itself through bodies, law, property, memory, household and public rite. Culture shapes the face and route; it does not create the guilty Hunger behind them.

# I. Controlling Anatomy

## One sovereignty, several kinds of actor

| Layer | Continuity and agency | Political function | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Betrayed Commission** | the original created good and permanent witness against the rebellion | defines what the Hunger counterfeits | cannot be rewritten, inherited by a claimant or made guilty |
| **Hunger** | the invariant chosen predatory economy | determines what conduct can nourish the Throne | not an emotion, natural necessity or neutral portfolio |
| **Throne** | one guilty `yolk-sovereignty`: fused cohort remnants, heartland, realm-law, accumulated feeding architecture and culpable continuity | the durable sovereign whose routes and harvests are contested | cannot enter Material creation in full, become innocent or be killed by destroying a Mask |
| **Regnant** | the executive personality organizing one Throne | coordinates routes, suppresses rivals and gives deep policy to the Hunger | ordinarily one at a time; true accession is exceptionally rare and never established by a cult coup |
| **Dependent Mask** | a projected `idol-pattern` organ dependent on a title, object, institution, dream-route, dominion condition or mortal practice | provides a culturally real face, biography and local method | may speak and scheme within its role but has no independent continuity and cannot seize a Throne |
| **Autonomous Archmask** | a full-agency `idol-pattern` Shadow person with durable anchors | can found a court, betray a Regnant, bargain with rivals and pursue a local project | rebellion is not repentance; it remains rooted in one guilty ecology unless a dedicated accession dossier proves otherwise |
| **Sediment** | a category containing stored memories, discarded biographies, absorbed cult histories, residue, suppressed routes and possibly separately surviving former-Regnant pressure | supplies old claims, contradictory memories and dormant anchors | is not automatically a person, chorus, dead soul or former Regnant; every speaking layer requires classification |
| **Mortal network** | humans with complete `human-soul`, plus separately classified altered creatures, patterns and proxies | supplies law, custom, images, chosen acts, inheritance and physical infrastructure | membership does not erase coercion, ignorance, resistance, victimhood or personal guilt |
| **Legend-pattern** | an `idol-pattern`, residue or copied cultural construct wearing a human biography | lets a Mask claim a dead ruler, hero, martyr or saint | never is the human soul whose life supplied the image |

The heartland is part of the Throne's guilty sovereignty. It is not the collective body of its worshippers, the human afterlife, an independent continent or a neutral landscape subsequently occupied by evil. Its borders move because Material history opens and closes routes by which the Hunger can make one kind of chosen distortion durable.

## Feeding without “belief points”

Every later Idol episode must show the complete chain:

1. **Good in use:** protection, provision, rest, nourishment, dignity, union or gratitude is genuinely present.
2. **Chosen distortion:** a person or institution recognizes enough of that good to bend it toward retaliation, withholding, abdication, consumption, self-worship, possession or replacement.
3. **Repeatable architecture:** law, household, army, market, shrine, title, road, room, ration system, archive or public rite makes the choice reproducible.
4. **Mask route:** a dependent Mask, fauna ecology or Archmask gains a stable way to appear, bargain, punish or recruit.
5. **Dominion effect:** a bounded Material site or Shadow route begins obeying part of the Hunger's logic.
6. **Parasitic return:** the Idol teaches the culturally shaped form back to its users, making the next chosen distortion easier, more prestigious or harder to escape.

Unchosen fear, desire, grief, anger, hunger, exhaustion, intrusive thought, dream imagery and cultural expectation can shape weather, image and route. They do not by themselves supply guilt or dense harvest. A victim's pain is not consent. Trauma does not make a person an Idol worshipper. Repetition carried out under coercion must be distinguished from the knowing choices of the person who designed, benefits from or continues the coercion.

An Idol does not literally consume a human soul through ordinary feeding. It harvests the metaphysical density of chosen distortion and uses mortal architecture to enlarge its access. Captivity, bodily death, damnation and unlawful soul interception require their own mechanisms; they cannot be smuggled into the word “feeding.”

## Five fronts of an Idol war

The seven cannot kill one another's Thrones through ordinary conflict. They fight by changing who receives the next human choice and what that choice becomes.

| Front | What the actor does | What witnesses can observe |
|---|---|---|
| **Target classification** | changes who counts as enemy, property, guest, beloved, inferior, rival or abandoned responsibility | new lists, marriage categories, ration classes, titles, exclusions and punishments |
| **Institutional capture** | takes control of a court, army, guild, household, shrine, archive, market or relief system | appointments, altered procedure, disappearances, confiscations, executions and beneficiaries |
| **Route seizure** | attaches a Mask or court to an existing architecture and displaces a rival manifestation | recurring signs, changed thresholds, fauna, physical overlays and failed rival rites |
| **Biography war** | steals, suppresses or rewrites the person through whom a culture understands the Hunger | repainted images, incompatible Lives, destroyed graves, forged genealogies and copied voices |
| **Harvest raid** | takes the bodily and institutional consequences produced by another vice and reclassifies them for its own Hunger | prisoners sold, dead consumed, victory monuments seized, neglected districts claimed and victims turned into rivals |

“The Idols fought” must therefore be followed by an answer to four questions: which Mask or Archmask acted, which humans chose what, what physical institution carried the act, and who was harmed. Heartland weather may accompany the event; it cannot replace causation.

## Succession firewall

None of the ten reviewed leads establishes a true Regnant succession, divided regnancy or interregnum.

- Mask rivalry is not succession.
- An Archmask protectorate is not a new Idol.
- Serving several Hungers does not combine Thrones.
- A Sediment voice is not presumed to be a former Regnant.
- Destroying a biography does not alter the Throne's guilty continuity.
- A human soul cannot become a Mask, Archmask, Regnant or Idol.

Any later accession proposal still requires the dedicated dossier specified by [[Idol Thrones, Regnants, and Cultural Masks]] and remains subject to the whole-history ceiling: no more than one candidate per chronological block and no more than three promoted accessions across recorded human history in the first pass.

# II. Source and Severity Ruling

The principal modern source is Warhammer's Great Game: rival predatory powers poach champions, servants, territories and the fruits of one another's wars. TES contributes culturally real supernatural identities, mantle pressure, biography as an anchor and the danger that an office or repeated story can outlast an individual. Greek division-of-honours grammar contributes conflict over who owns victory and its spoils. Orthodox martyr reception and Wisdom 13–15 contribute the difference among a murdered person, received Life, image, cult machinery, false miracle and predatory power using the image.

Modern game sources contribute structure only. No protected character, proper noun, complete plot or cosmology transfers. The World Egg revision is decisive: the Idols pre-exist humanity as guilty created sovereignties; repeated chosen vice feeds them, while culture supplies their normal local faces.

Severity parity remains in force. The cards retain murder, enslavement, sexual coercion where selected, corpse theft and use, sacrificial killing, torture, family punishment, starvation, institutional betrayal and displacement where those acts carry the borrowed element. They do not stack every source atrocity into every lead. Humans remain capable of perpetrating evil without supernatural compulsion and of resisting a structure without being morally pure.

# IV. Reviewed Heartland and Hunger-War Nurseries

## ID-R01 — The War of Stolen Victories

- **Conflict form:** one human or Giant war whose consequences are successively captured by Wrath, Pride, Greed and Envy through different Masks and mortal networks. It is not a four-Throne council or a permanent war among their full sovereignties.
- **Actors and continuities:** human or Giant rulers, soldiers, captives and civilians; separate dependent Masks or, where later established, one autonomous Archmask per intervention; the four Thrones remain distant guilty sovereignties.
- **Feeding architecture:** Wrath expands the enemy class and makes surrender insufficient. Pride edits monuments, rolls and victory rites so one ruler becomes the sole author. Greed converts prisoners, seized land and relief into transferable property. Envy teaches excluded allies and dispossessed survivors that another population's continued existence stole their rightful victory.
- **Causal chain:** a defensible war begins → commanders authorize collective retaliation → the Red March route opens → victors monopolize honor and sell people and land → rival networks attach to those institutions → peace becomes slavery, dispossession and renewed feud.
- **Observable effect:** trophies warm when named enemies approach; victory inscriptions shed collaborators' names; deeds travel more readily than freed prisoners; later memorials identify mutually exclusive betrayers.
- **Hard edge:** massacre, execution after surrender, enslavement, family separation, confiscation, destroyed settlements and revenge killing remain. The original justice of a cause does not sanctify later acts.
- **Source fidelity:** Warhammer Great Game and Greek division-of-honours grammar supply rival seizure of one victory's fruits. World Egg changes the actors, metaphysics, institutions, culture and aftermath.
- **Collision ruling:** may deepen [[Great-Power Mythic Ecology and Planting Matrix#GP-I-02 — The Sevenfold Feast War|Sevenfold Feast War]] but is not that entire umbrella, the Red Tally's universal origin or a hidden cause of any named imperial war.
- **Possible trace:** a battlefield with four incompatible overlay scars; prisoner sale-tags beneath triumphal stone; a veterans' feast that preserves the name erased from the monument; land claims whose oldest valid seal belongs to the defeated population.
- **Open:** era, war, culture, initial just cause, rulers, Masks, sequence, survivors, present claimants and whether any intervention was recognized at the time.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R02R — The Parliament of Borrowed Faces

- **Conflict form:** several dependent Masks of one Throne compete to become the culture's preferred expression of the same Hunger. “Parliament” is the later scholar's name for their contradictory cult programmes, not a chamber containing several independent gods.
- **Actors and continuities:** one Throne and Regnant; multiple dependent `idol-pattern` Masks anchored in separate institutions; human priests, dynasts, artists, spouses, victims and officials. A face that can refuse the Regnant and survive loss of its mortal anchor must be reclassified through an Archmask dossier.
- **Feeding architecture:** each faction defines the same distortion differently. A Pride recension may say rank comes through blood, another through beauty, another through office and another through acclaimed achievement. Their human sponsors force marriages, suppress rival shrines, assassinate witnesses and rewrite succession law so their version reproduces.
- **Causal chain:** one Hunger receives several cultural biographies → institutions compete for prestige and access → human sponsors make one biography legally or ritually compulsory → the Masks gain or lose routes → defeated adherents and dependants are killed, married away, mutilated, exiled or absorbed → the surviving face is later mistaken for the Idol's only ancient identity.
- **Observable effect:** different rites trigger the same deeper heartland signature; related images contradict one another's genealogy; destroyed shrines cause one route to fail while another strengthens; old cult titles recur in new offices.
- **Hard edge:** assassination, coerced marriage, sexual access where the selected marriage system entails it, shrine destruction, punishment of children for lineage, exile and retaliatory killing remain.
- **Source fidelity:** TES supplies culturally real identities and biography pressure; Warhammer supplies rivalry among manifestations serving one predatory sovereignty. Neither supplies literal World Egg succession.
- **Collision ruling:** not a Regnant council, an Idol civil society, Menhir title drift, the Crown-Market or proof that contradictory masks are equal moral perspectives.
- **Possible trace:** mutually exclusive marriage dispensations; four images made from the same altered foundation; court records in which changing a title changes who may inherit; one dependent Mask still answering a rite its cult officially denies.
- **Open:** Idol, culture, Mask number, biographies, institutions, victims, outcome and whether one face ever acquired autonomy.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R03 — The Archmask Protectorate

- **Conflict form:** one autonomous Archmask breaks with its Regnant and occupies a bounded district. It protects the population because living, choosing tenants maintain the several anchors that make its autonomy possible.
- **Actors and continuities:** one `idol-pattern` Archmask rooted in one Throne; dependent Masks sent against it; humans with complete souls; any fauna, offices, relics and proxies separately classified.
- **Feeding architecture:** the Archmask requires a stable court, remembered title, repeated tribute and an institution capable of recognizing its judgements across mortal generations. It suppresses rival predators, repairs roads and punishes unauthorized killing because dead or scattered tenants cannot renew those anchors. In exchange it demands public deference, hostages, labor and chosen acts that still feed its native Hunger.
- **Causal chain:** a Regnant attempts to absorb or discard an autonomous servant → the Archmask seizes one Material-facing network → inhabitants accept protection under coercive terms → courts, roads and tribute stabilize its independence → rival Masks raid the district → the protector preserves its asset by killing invaders and rebellious tenants alike.
- **Observable effect:** rival manifestations fail at marked borders; tribute objects recur inside Shadow court architecture; decrees protect bodies while converting households into hereditary service; the Archmask can act after one cult or ruler dies.
- **Hard edge:** conquest, hostage-taking, execution, forced labor, mutilation, collective punishment and deliberate exposure of disloyal wards remain. Effective protection does not make the ruler benevolent.
- **Source fidelity:** Daedric client-realm and Be'lakor-like rebel grammar supplies the powerful intermediary whose independence creates real local politics. World Egg keeps it one culpable Archmask rather than a fallen god or redeemable antihero.
- **Collision ruling:** not a Patron lease, infernal dominion, Menhir court, human Sinner state or candidate Regnant. Its tenants do not become extensions of its person.
- **Possible trace:** border stones that repel one Mask but admit the protector's forms; hostage genealogies; a road genuinely safer inside the old tribute line; decrees whose humanitarian clauses and execution lists share the same hand.
- **Open:** Idol, district, era, anchors, Archmask, tenant cultures, rebellion, present court and whether the Regnant still hunts it.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R04R — The Champion of Four Doors

- **Conflict form:** one autonomous Archmask rooted in one Hunger accepts three foreign powers' incompatible gifts in addition to its native route. It remains one person and one continuity; it does not become a composite Idol.
- **Actors and continuities:** one full-agency `idol-pattern` Archmask; four separate Thrones; dependent foreign Masks, offices, tools, contracted fauna or leased routes; human servants and victims. No human soul is the claimant.
- **Feeding architecture:** each foreign gift is concrete. One opens a route only while prisoners remain property; one lends public acclaim while a named rival is erased; one directs violence while surrender never ends guilt; one preserves the claimant's own Hunger. Every power receives chosen acts through its own architecture and can withdraw only its own grant.
- **Causal chain:** a threatened Archmask seeks power outside its Throne → rival Regnants grant bounded tools in exchange for incompatible conduct → the claimant conquers several courts → each victory creates another victim class and contractual obligation → fulfilling one gift violates another → former beneficiaries, captives and creditor Masks attack through different doors.
- **Observable effect:** four distinct manifestation signatures never fuse; disabling one rite removes one capacity; conquered institutions keep incompatible target lists; the claimant's body or court changes by route rather than species.
- **Hard edge:** sacrifice, enslavement, mutilation, betrayal of allies, execution of surrendered opponents and abandonment of protected dependants remain where imposed by the selected gifts.
- **Source fidelity:** the multi-patron champion grammar supplies incompatible gifts and manipulation of rival powers. World Egg refuses mortal apotheosis, species conversion and one unified Chaos pantheon.
- **Collision ruling:** not a Regnant candidate, True Dragon, Sinner office, Giant continuity, four-soul merger or universal champion recurring across cultures.
- **Possible trace:** four doors in one ruined court that answer different repair acts; a body depicted with alterations that appear and vanish between recensions; four victim registers whose dates converge on one conquest.
- **Open:** native Hunger, foreign Hungers, gifts, court, victims, defeat, surviving anchors and whether the Archmask still exists.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R05 — The Feast-Raid Corridors

- **Conflict form:** Gluttony Masks invade the consequences produced by other Hungers through Material supply systems and the Shadow routes those systems open. The Eternal Feast does not bodily march across a fixed planar frontier.
- **Actors and continuities:** dependent Gluttony Masks, possible Banquet Tyrants, rival Masks, human provisioners, clergy, grave workers, officers, cooks, prisoners, starving households and corpse users; dead human souls remain distinct from bodies and residue.
- **Feeding architecture:** a funeral society treats the poor dead as saleable matter; a victory banquet classifies captives as courses; a famine office withholds full stores; a sacrificial kitchen kills named victims; an addiction market turns relief into dependency. Repeated chosen consumption links the sites into one corridor through which Feastlings, Gut-Maws or an Archmask court can move.
- **Causal chain:** another vice produces hoards, casualties, captives or neglected wards → officials deliberately make those people and goods consumable → Gluttony gains a repeatable supply route → Masks seize kitchens, roads and invitations → rival networks lose their harvest and retaliate → the population experiences famine, corpse theft, predation and war over the supply chain.
- **Observable effect:** stores vanish along one institutional chain rather than by distance; funeral tokens appear in banquet halls; the same serving law operates in separated places; closing one kitchen breaks only one section of the route.
- **Hard edge:** starvation, captive sacrifice, cannibal consumption or corpse processing where selected, killing of witnesses, addiction, grave violation and abandonment of excluded households remain direct.
- **Source fidelity:** Warhammer domain-war grammar supplies harvest raids; Wisdom 13–15 supplies human craft, status and cult machinery around a real predatory power. The victim's body is never confused with the person's soul.
- **Collision ruling:** not the Long Table's origin, one ghoul nation, Morvalis's death law, Veradia's disease ecology, an infernal corpse-road or a universal route joining every famine.
- **Possible trace:** matched funeral and ration implements; a closed kitchen whose destruction ends appearances at several waystations; graves emptied according to feast rank; a relief custom designed to keep food from crossing one old invitation line.
- **Open:** era, regions, initiating network, rival Hunger, corridor scale, Masks, body practices, survivors and remaining sections.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R06R — The Campaign of the Borrowed Martyr

- **Conflict form:** Envy and Pride cultivate separate dependent Masks using the name and image of one tortured and executed human. Their rival cults fight over who owns the death, while neither counterfeit is the murdered person's soul.
- **Actors and continuities:** one judged `human-soul`; the historical body and remains; family and witnesses; two separate dependent `idol-pattern` Masks; mortal cults and state officials; any authentic Saintly manifestation only if independently commissioned.
- **Feeding architecture:** Pride makes the victim's suffering proof of one hierarchy's exceptional glory. Envy gives ignored communities a replacement martyr who performs the signs they believe another city stole. Officials alter the Life, steal or divide the corpse, threaten surviving kin and punish recanters so each Mask receives a repeatable biography, relic claim and pilgrimage route.
- **Causal chain:** a person is tortured, recants or is accused of recanting, and is executed → authorities and relatives preserve incompatible testimony → cult operators edit the death into competing claims → two Masks attach to different images and rites → miracles, punishments and political benefits reinforce the cults → families, pilgrims and rival cities suffer violence over a person neither Mask contains.
- **Observable effect:** each image answers comparison or acclaim rather than need; relic responses cease when the supporting public claim is corrected; hostile Lives preserve matching torture details beneath different endings; family burial evidence contradicts both cults.
- **Hard edge:** torture, execution, informing, recantation, corpse theft, grave desecration, coercion of surviving family and retaliatory killing remain. The recanter is not made contemptible merely to improve a later martyr story.
- **Source fidelity:** Orthodox martyr reception supplies the bodily witness, contested Life, relic and cult layers; TES mantle grammar supplies a biography used by a new supernatural pattern. Received tradition remains evidence with layers, not a license for arbitrary debunking.
- **Collision ruling:** not [[Borrowed Saints]] as one reusable creature, the whole [[Great-Power Mythic Ecology and Planting Matrix#GP-III-01 — Siege of the Martyrs' Names|Siege of the Martyrs' Names]], a proof that the victim is or is not a Saint, or a Saint split into two persons.
- **Possible trace:** two hostile icon underlayers; a grave proven empty before either relic translation; descendants preserving the same private last words; one bounded test in which each Mask fails when the rival comparison or acclaim is removed.
- **Open:** person, sex, era, church status, truth of recantation, final judgement, cults, Masks, remains, genuine miracles and present claimants.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R07R — The Siege of Invitation and Consumption

- **Conflict form:** one Lust dominion overlay and one Gluttony overlay contest the same bounded settlement or Shadow-facing district. The narrower pairing replaces the earlier four-Hunger blur.
- **Actors and continuities:** dependent Masks and possible Archmasks of Lust and Gluttony; human hosts, cooks, household rulers, captives, beneficiaries, resisters and victims; every human remains one complete soul.
- **Feeding architecture:** Lust's faction makes entry, shelter, marriage, affection and household membership conditional on surrendering privacy and refusal. Gluttony's faction makes food, festival and protection conditional on becoming usable supply. Captives are physically installed at gates, kitchens or household thresholds and forced to repeat the competing formulas. Their coerced speech marks the border; the knowing choices of captors, officials and beneficiaries feed it.
- **Causal chain:** a settlement accepts predatory hospitality during scarcity → one network claims people through belonging while another claims them through consumption → officials assign households and prisoners to maintain the boundary → both overlays become spatially real → rescue or refusal opens gaps each side exploits → people are abducted, starved, assaulted, consumed, expelled or transferred without consent.
- **Observable effect:** a door admits a named “beloved” but a kitchen records the same person as ration stock; changing the forced formula moves the overlay by one room or street; freed captives stop being boundary signs although trauma and legal claims remain.
- **Hard edge:** abduction, forced intimacy, coerced marriage where used, starvation, bodily consumption, captivity, family separation and punishment for refusal remain. No victim's compliance under threat is treated as worship.
- **Source fidelity:** moving Chaos-realm borders supply the territorial struggle; World Egg places the mechanism in two specific Hunger architectures and retains human causal decisions.
- **Collision ruling:** not the Garden of Invitation in full, the Eternal Feast in full, an infernal border, the Elfland Tithe or a four-Idol hybrid realm.
- **Possible trace:** one building with incompatible household and ration plans; gate chains sized for living captives; testimony that the border moved when a prisoner refused; descendants maintaining a consent-and-ration custom whose original terror is forgotten.
- **Open:** era, settlement, crisis, Masks, rulers, captives, duration, breach, survivors and current adjacency.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R08 — The Cult-Colony Wars

- **Conflict form:** rival Masks establish bounded Material overlays inside different institutions of one polity. “Colony” means a predatory jurisdiction reproduced through local structures, not a foreign species settling empty land.
- **Actors and continuities:** dependent Masks, occasional separately established Archmasks, human officials, beneficiaries, coerced workers, reformers, soldiers, households and victims; the polity and population never become one Idol's body.
- **Feeding architecture:** one network captures provincial debt and land law; another captures officer promotion and retaliation; another captures dynastic marriage or public acclaim. Each uses existing buildings, titles, personnel, supply chains and punishments to make its Hunger repeat. Cult agents sometimes expose a rival's real crimes while concealing their own intended replacement.
- **Causal chain:** a state centralizes institutions → local actors deliberately bend them toward a Hunger → Masks gain stable signs and overlays → rival networks poach offices and populations → officials wage purges, strikes, lawsuits, riots, assassinations and military campaigns → removing one colony leaves victims, collaborators and usable institutions behind.
- **Observable effect:** different offices display distinct manifestation signatures; a law works normally outside its captured procedure; personnel transfers move the overlay; a rival cult's truthful exposure weakens one Mask without making the exposer righteous.
- **Hard edge:** dispossession, enslavement, coerced marriage, torture, execution, massacre, famine manipulation, household seizure and retaliatory purge remain only where the selected institutions perform them.
- **Source fidelity:** Chaos cult networks and TES mortal-agent grammar supply proxy competition inside human government. World Egg refuses universal possession, moral automatism and a hidden cult explanation for all political evil.
- **Collision ruling:** not one empire secretly ruled by all seven, the origin of the seven canonical mortal networks, a universal conspiracy or proof that secular institutions are inherently Idol-made.
- **Possible trace:** one courthouse with two incompatible overlay phases; guild tools marked by a former Mask route; purge pits containing both cult agents and innocent accused; reform law that works despite its compromised sponsor.
- **Open:** polity, era, institutions, Hungers, Masks, participant knowledge, victories, reforms and surviving networks.
- **Review disposition:** `retain`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R09R — The Insurrection of the Buried Faces

- **Conflict form:** suppressed historical layers inside or around one Throne begin disrupting the current Regnant's routes. “Insurrection” describes observable conflict among outputs and anchors; it does not presume that Sediment is one conscious opposition party.
- **Actors and continuities:** one Throne and Regnant; stored memories and discarded biographies with no presumed agency; dependent Masks produced from reactivated material; any autonomous claimant separately classified as an Archmask; any surviving former Regnant requiring its own dossier; human cults and witnesses.
- **Feeding architecture:** a present cult reuses an old title, image, sanctuary or rite because it once fed efficiently. The act reopens buried routes. Old instructions conflict with current policy, forgotten victims' names recur, dependent Masks speak obsolete biographies and human factions use the outputs to challenge present authorities.
- **Causal chain:** a Regnant suppresses an earlier feeding architecture without removing every anchor → later humans reactivate one anchor for practical or political reasons → stored memories shape new outputs → routes issue contradictory commands → cults kill, exile or sacrifice people to decide which face is authentic → an Archmask may emerge if several anchors acquire durable autonomous continuity.
- **Observable effect:** accurate memories appear alongside impossible claims of identity; old rites trigger bounded effects the current cult cannot control; destroying one anchor silences one voice but not the others; no output knows the full buried history.
- **Hard edge:** murder of witnesses, sacrificial tests, destruction of families associated with a former cult, grave violation, purge and retaliatory violence remain where used to settle authenticity.
- **Source fidelity:** TES identity accretion supplies layered biography and contested continuity. World Egg makes Sediment heterogeneous historical matter rather than reincarnation, soul chorus or automatic former god.
- **Collision ruling:** not the unpromoted Pride succession bundle, ER-02's name wound, copied ancestors, an Ossuary Mind, collective possession or proof that a former Regnant survived.
- **Possible trace:** one obsolete rite with a reproducible but narrow effect; several accurate voices that contradict each other's self-identification; an image physically older than the sanctuary housing it; purge graves arranged by discarded cult title.
- **Open:** Idol, Regnant, historical layers, agency, possible former Regnant, cults, location, violence, outcome and current activity.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; placement and promotion locked.

## ID-R10R — The Archmask That Ate Its Biography

- **Conflict form:** one autonomous Archmask destroys the records, bodies, monuments and kinship routes supporting its earlier identity because a rival is using those anchors to constrain, counterfeit or replace it.
- **Actors and continuities:** one full-agency `idol-pattern` Archmask rooted in one Throne; dependent rival Masks or another separately established Archmask; human priests, archivists, artists, witnesses, families, rulers and displaced settlements. No human memory or soul is part of the Archmask's person.
- **Feeding architecture:** the Archmask's old biography is spread across a cult calendar, hereditary offices, burial sites, portraits and phrases that determine how it may manifest. It orders human agents to burn archives, kill witnesses, destroy graves, rename children and relocate settlements. The purge weakens the rival anchor and lets the Archmask adopt a new local face, but also removes routes it once required.
- **Causal chain:** an old biography becomes usable against an Archmask → it chooses historical annihilation rather than relinquish rule → officials classify entire families and towns as dangerous evidence → killings and displacement sever most anchors → the Archmask survives through fewer, harsher institutions → later cultures mistake the lack of records for primordial mystery or proof that the victims never existed.
- **Observable effect:** missing settlements remain visible in roads and irrigation; later portraits cover a consistent older face; banned kin names still trigger a Mask response; the new cult cannot perform functions tied to the destroyed biography.
- **Hard edge:** execution of priests and witnesses, killing of children as lineage evidence where selected, destroyed graves, burned archives, forced renaming, deportation, settlement destruction and retaliatory murder remain. The victims do not exist merely to decorate the Archmask's reinvention.
- **Source fidelity:** mantle-control and *damnatio memoriae* grammar supply rule through destruction of biography. World Egg turns the ruler into one autonomous Mask-person rather than a Throne, human god or metaphysically rewritten past.
- **Collision ruling:** not a Regnant accession, the Crown That Replaced Its Wearer, Caleran's later archive crime, ER-01's human institutional erasure or ER-02's supernatural name wound.
- **Possible trace:** roads ending at erased foundations; genealogies with forbidden blank generations; archive ash containing repeated pigments; one former cult function that no current Mask can reproduce.
- **Open:** Hunger, Archmask, former identity, rival, era, culture, purge scale, surviving families, new face and present court.
- **Review disposition:** `revise`; placement and promotion locked.

# V. Interfaces with Existing Ecologies

## The Sevenfold Feast War

GP-I-02 remains a later umbrella for separate proxy conflicts. ID-R01, ID-R02R, ID-R05 and ID-R08 may each supply one local episode, but none proves a seven-sided war or a shared command. A historical placement must identify which Hungers actually appear and may use only the consequences belonging to that event.

## Mortal networks

The Red Tally, Under-Ledger, Diadem, Long Table, Apathetics, Weeping Courts and Devoted are canonical current networks, not timeless monoliths. A historical card may show an ancestor, predecessor, rival or analogous architecture without declaring that the present organization has existed since the Giant Age. Shared ritual language does not prove institutional continuity.

## Saints, Sinners and infernal offices

A Saint's human soul cannot be copied into a Mask. A Sinner remains a damned human soul distinct from any infernal office or Idol persona used in its campaigns. An Archmask may lease an infernal road or fight a devil without becoming damned. A devil may use an Idol cult without becoming a Mask. ID-R06R therefore requires the strongest identity audit before placement.

## Giant cults and old dynasties

Giant rulers can manufacture cultural Masks around real Hungers through sacrifice, caste, dynastic marriage, public image and inherited violence. That does not make every Giant ancestor an Idol child or every royal cult authentic. A repentant Giant remains `giant-continuity`; its repentance cannot redeem a Mask that survives its reign.

## Human guilt and resistance

Every human participant must be placed among knowing architect, beneficiary, coerced worker, ignorant participant, reformer, resister, captive or victim according to evidence. These positions can change. Miraculous power does not prove moral legitimacy, and truthful exposure of a rival does not make an Idol network good.

# VI. First Historical Placement Result

The first placement pass selects exactly four roots without treating the choice as one lead per Idol or as a demand to populate every era evenly:

1. **Late Chains:** ID-R01 becomes one secondary Sarrakai river war whose consequences are captured sequentially by four dependent Masks.
2. **City-States:** ID-R04R becomes one Envy Archmask using three separable foreign leases inside a fortified inner-gulf city.
3. **Underground and Legalization:** ID-R06R becomes one obscure provincial execution followed by competing Pride and Envy counterfeits.
4. **Two Crowns and post-Split consolidation:** ID-R10R becomes one bounded successor-court purge in which a Pride Archmask destroys its older biography to defeat an Envy rival anchor.

ID-R02R, ID-R03, ID-R05, ID-R07R, ID-R08 and ID-R09R remain unplaced. ID-R07R in particular should wait until a culture and bounded settlement can carry its captivity, forced intimacy and consumption history without becoming an abstract horror zone. No lead is reserved for 1360 AR, and the Forward Rule controls any presently active court or overlay.

# VII. Promotion Gates

Before any lead receives historical placement or a Garden trace:

1. name the betrayed good and exact chosen distortion;
2. identify the Throne, Regnant-facing policy, dependent Mask or autonomous Archmask separately;
3. state the material institution, route and repeatable act that supplies the feeding architecture;
4. identify what unchosen culture shapes and what chosen vice feeds;
5. classify every human soul, Mask, Archmask, office, residue, legend-pattern, corpse, relic response and Sediment layer separately;
6. state which human actors knew, chose, benefited, resisted, were coerced or were harmed;
7. show the physical effect of the overlay and the act that can reproduce or interrupt it;
8. preserve source-hard murder, slavery, torture, corpse use, sexual coercion, sacrifice, starvation, purge and dispossession where load-bearing;
9. refuse a culture-wide equation between one people and one Hunger;
10. prove non-identity with the canonical mortal networks unless institutional continuity is separately established;
11. create a dedicated dossier before naming an Archmask or alleging former-Regnant survival;
12. treat every apparent accession as Mask turnover until the succession gate and historical ceiling are separately satisfied;
13. leave a 1360 trace useful even if the claimed Mask biography is false;
14. explain no protected Weird and fix no post-1360 outcome.
