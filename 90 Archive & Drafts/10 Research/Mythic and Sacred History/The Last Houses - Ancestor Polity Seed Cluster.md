# The Last Houses — Ancestor Polity Seed Cluster

> [!info] Atlas integration
> Current canonical destination: [[The Last Houses and the Cypress Commonwealth]]. This dossier retains its research history; former holds and promotion gates are superseded only where the [[World Historical Atlas - Integration and Coverage Ledger]] records an explicit outcome. Preliminary or rejected variants do not override their corrected descendants.

> [!warning] Nursery material
> This cluster is an author-only World Egg translation of selected Aerenal functions. It is not a canon culture, mapped state, fixed history, or declaration that human institutions can preserve, recall, employ, or govern human souls.

## Purpose

The useful source grammar is not simply **benevolent undead rule**. It is a civilization that makes curated ancestry into constitutional authority: achievement-based Houses, magnificent mortuary infrastructure, living administrators beneath a supposedly ancestral court, a place-bounded preservation ecology, an order policing unlawful continuities, and a rival tradition which attempts a more literal conquest of death.

The World Egg translation divides those functions among six related but non-identical seeds. No voice called an ancestor is accepted without a continuity classification. The actual human dead remain beyond civic custody.

## Source and Transformation Ruling

Primary modern-fantasy references:

- [Aerenal overview](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Aerenal) — Giant-slave ancestry, civic lines, deathless ancestors, mortuary chronicles, living Sibling Kings and isolation.
- [Keith Baker, “IFAQ: Aerenal”](https://keith-baker.com/ifaq-aerenal/) — the Undying Court as a collective spiritual power, the place-bound reach of that power and the role of living intermediaries.
- [Deathguard overview](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Deathguard) — an institution devoted to destroying undead and hostile necromancy.
- [Tairnadal overview](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Tairnadal) — heroic ancestors emulated through the lives and deeds of later inheritors.

**Borrowed elements:** deed-based ancestral Houses; a living government advised by preserved ancestors; preservation tied to one landscape; competing mortuary schools; an anti-necromancy order; and heroic ancestors reenacted by living heirs.

**Original hard edge:** escape from Giant slavery; oligarchic selection of the worthy dead; institutional stagnation; destruction of a rival bloodline; state-sanctioned mortuary violence; and the danger that veneration turns living people into instruments of inherited wars.

**Current state:** `intact`. The translation retains slavery, massacre, coercive preservation, archive manipulation, soul imprisonment, collective punishment and identity pressure where those wounds support the borrowed functions. It does not reproduce Eberron's elves, positive-energy undeath, divine gestalt, dragon war, named Houses, exact government or complete historical sequence.

**Changed axes:** the people are a provisional Orphaned human civilization rather than elves; their founders survive the Giant collapse without another Exodus prophet; preservation, archive simulation, residue, Patron impersonation and saintly manifestation are separate mechanisms rather than one deathless species; the alleged ancestral court does not grant divine magic; and no permanent council of human souls governs the living.

| speaking corpse | imposed motion, residue, office, bound soul, hostile occupant or fraud | bodily preservation proves none of these by itself |
| Court ancestor | separately classified Patron-role bearer, proxy, archive-pattern, residue, copy or fraud | Patron is an anchor relation, not a species or proof of identity |

The civilization's public theology may collapse these categories under a term translated as **the Enduring**. Authorial notes never may.

## Provisional Historical Soil

The controlling placement is a remote Orphaned island, peninsula or enclosed forest country far beyond the core southern imperial world. There has been **no verified contact by 1360 AR**: no embassy, trade good, migrant lineage, missionary, military expedition, relic traffic, mapped Material route, stable Egg White route or intelligible report connects the Last Houses to the currently detailed setting. Their 1360 traces are local to their own civilization and cannot presently be collected by core-world investigators. A later first-contact project requires a separate authorial decision under the Forward Rule.

Its earliest human institutions descend from captive mortuary workers, record keepers, gardeners, embalming families and burial carriers of a secondary Giant polity. They survive the Great Unbinding inside an extensive mortuary landscape that the collapsing court abandons.

This is not another branch of the Glass people, another Great Prophet, the secret ancestry of the Nsizwa, or an unrecorded sixth Imperial Horizon. The Giant polity may have been a client, mortuary league or crownlet within an existing Horizon, but its owner, border, Horizon and fate remain open.

The cluster can grow from Orphaned Reassembly into a durable civilization without requiring uninterrupted technological superiority. Its mortuary specialisation may preserve documents and bodies far better than machines, roads, medicine or ordinary state capacity.

# The Six Seeds

## LH-01 — The Commonwealth of the Last Houses

- **Lane / scale / carrier:** institution and relic / civilization cluster / House law, grave and archive.
- **Humble origin:** abandoned burial workers must ration food, identify bodies, settle orphan custody and prevent armed Giant retainers from seizing the mortuary stores after the wider court collapses.
- **Historical pressure:** Orphaned Reassembly, recovery of useful Giant infrastructure and the transformation of work fraternities into cities.
- **Primary grammar:** Aerenal lines and living government beneath ancestral authority; Scriptural inheritance disputes, truthful witness and the warning that the fathers' sins do not transfer guilt automatically to descendants.
- **World Egg torque:** a House becomes a civic body made from kin, adoption, profession, service and shared dead rather than a supernatural bloodline. Extraordinary service can found a noble branch; hereditary birth cannot by itself prove worth, truth or divine favor.
- **Institutional form:** two living seats divide immediate government from record and inheritance. The alleged ancestral Court may advise, but a living magistrate remains responsible for every decree. “The ancestors commanded it” is not a defence without a classified speaker, a preserved instruction and evidence that the instruction applies.
- **Mortuary procedure:** one record is dictated or witnessed by the dying person, one is assembled adversarially by household members and victims, and one records the civic consequences of the life. The disagreement among the three is preserved rather than harmonized automatically.
- **Hard edge:** successful Houses turn dead estates into permanent voting power, force poor districts to maintain elite tombs and erase embarrassing servants, mixed children, victims and defeated founders from the public lineage. Expulsion from a House can remove housing, work, burial and legal standing at once.
- **Source-fidelity ruling:** received source: Aerenal civic lines, mortuary records and division between living government and ancestor authority; borrowed element: deed-based Houses and institutional rule by curated memory; original hard edge: an insulated oligarchy chooses which lives merit enduring authority and allows ancient priorities to govern later generations; current state: `intact`; restoration: none; historical layer: authorial civic history with later House propaganda.
- **Plain causal chain:** mortuary fraternities control food and records after collapse → they use those records to allocate people and property → emergency custodianship becomes hereditary House jurisdiction → the Houses preserve civic continuity but acquire power to manufacture respectable ancestry → later reformers create achievement, adoption and adversarial-testimony rules without ending oligarchy.
- **1360 AR traces:** three mutually inconsistent death records; noble Houses with provable adoption from laborers or foreigners; tomb endowments larger than living hospitals; and a legal rule that no preserved ancestor can cast a living vote.
- **Collision ruling:** separate from Ark-derived peoples and from Nsizwa clan, praise-song and Izivukwa institutions. No Last House founded, trained or secretly survives through an Nsizwa tomb.
- **Open:** proper name, region, language, date, Giant predecessor, form of the two seats, religion, degree of isolation and whether a unified commonwealth ever exists.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — the three records perform a distinct civic and mortuary function without claiming custody of the dead.
- **Status:** `garden-reviewed-retain`.

## LH-02 — The Still Cypress Vaults

- **Lane / scale / carrier:** land and encounter / local jurisdiction / forest vault, embalming craft and decay garden.
- **Humble origin:** embalmers discover that a particular root-vault preserves prepared tissue only while an adjoining tract is allowed to decompose without interruption.
- **Historical pressure:** survival of evidence after Giant collapse and later competition for mortuary land.
- **Primary grammar:** Aerenal's place-bounded preservation; Menhir natural law; Biblical grave custody without confusing a body with the immortal person.
- **World Egg torque:** **the Still Cypress** is a provisional human name for a local Menhir `court-law`, place-mind or jurisdiction—not a new Menhir sovereignty. Its controlling relationship to Sylvorn, Nevaura, Morvalis or a conjunction remains deliberately unresolved until the distant-land geography pass can establish which existing sovereignty actually reaches the site. Human embalming performs most of the physical work.
- **Mechanism in plain language:** workers prepare a body, seal it among living roots and maintain a paired return plot. Breakdown slows inside the vault while fallen wood, offerings and other organic matter in the return plot break down unusually quickly. Ending the maintenance allows ordinary decay to resume.
- **Hard edge:** Houses appropriate farmland for return plots, move poor graves into accelerated-decay ground and preserve elite bodies at the cost of soil, labor and the evidence carried by unfavored corpses. During famine, guards kill families entering reserved groves for food or burial.
- **Source-fidelity ruling:** received source: Aerenal's Irian-bound preservation ecology; borrowed element: exceptional preservation that weakens away from one place; original hard edge: the source element itself is principally geographical and nonviolent, while privileged access creates political inequality; current state: `intact`; restoration: none; historical layer: authorial natural law with competing Menhir cult explanations. The land seizure and killings arise from the World Egg polity's decisions rather than being attributed to Eberron.
- **Limit:** the vault preserves matter and sometimes residue. It does not preserve a soul, confer intelligence, make a corpse holy or establish moral approval by a Menhir.
- **1360 AR traces:** paired wood samples age at radically different rates only inside an intact vault arrangement; neglected elite bodies begin decaying; and petitions identify fields taken for mortuary return plots.
- **Collision ruling:** not Nevaura's universal winter law, Morvalis's soul-road, the salt archive of Nihil or proof of a new Menhir. The Garden may retain the observable preservation relation while every claim about its controlling sovereignty remains promotion-locked.
- **Open:** landscape, climate, botanical form, controlling sovereignty, original discoverers, extent and whether every city can reproduce the effect.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — retain the place-bounded material effect while explicitly gating its controlling existing Menhir or conjunction to the later geography pass.
- **Status:** `garden-reviewed-revise`.

## LH-03 — The Court Beyond the Cypress

- **Lane / scale / carrier:** Idol, Menhir or Patron pressure / Patron polity / legal opinion, route and archive error.
- **Humble origin:** living Houses ask an Egg White refuge to preserve duplicate testimony during wars and fires. The refuge gradually claims authority to interpret the people whose records it stores.
- **Historical pressure:** expansion of mortuary government, Egg White anchor politics and the conversion of archive custody into political sovereignty.
- **Primary grammar:** the Undying Court's long counsel and geographically limited reach; TES-like otherworld courts; World Egg Patron rent and proxy politics.
- **World Egg torque:** the Court is a real Egg White polity but not a court of human souls. Its anchor owners, speaking proxies, archive simulations, residue-born inhabitants and possible Giant or human continuants require separate classifications. No single category is assumed to govern the entire city, and no statement by “the Court” may be used historically until the particular speaker, carrier and continuity behind that statement have been classified.
- **Mechanism in plain language:** Material agents copy records and repeated legal performances into anchored correspondences. Court speakers compare this material, pay informants and run archive-patterns, allowing them to produce impressive answers. Their knowledge fails or repeats errors where the transmitted record fails.
- **Hard edge:** the Court auctions privileged access to ancestral personae, withholds routes from Houses that challenge its opinions and encourages living elites to preserve the versions of history most useful to stable rent. Families are dispossessed after Court judgements based on records their enemies curated.
- **Source-fidelity ruling:** received source: the Undying Court's long counsel, collective power and place-bounded influence; borrowed element: an ancestral authority working through living agents and thinking beyond ordinary lifetimes; original hard edge: the living accept durable rule from a selected ancestral elite and become instruments of projects they cannot fully inspect; current state: `intact`; restoration: none; historical layer: authorial Patron politics concealed beneath received ancestral theology.
- **Idol risk:** repeated praise of perfect founders supplies feeding architecture for Pride or Envy Masks, but no Idol is the secret founder or single owner of the culture. A Mask wearing an ancestor's public face remains an `idol-pattern`.
- **Saintly limit:** a genuine saint may manifest only under the Lord's commission, may rebuke the Court and cannot be retained as one of its members. A true miracle authenticates only its bounded act.
- **1360 AR traces:** Court judgements repeating rare scribal errors found in concealed Material exemplars; conflicting ancestor-patterns making mutually exclusive claims; and contracts in which access to a “grandmother” is priced as an archive service.
- **Collision ruling:** not the plague-born Ossuary Crown, a wandering escaped Giant court, the Court of the Martyrs' Names or the Table of the Last Name. It shares the Long Rent War ecology without becoming its capital.
- **Open:** city, anchor, owners, inhabitants, routes, present government, degree of deception and whether any speaker has ever been the bodily continuant it claims to be.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — preserve the Egg White polity while requiring case-by-case classification of every speaker and refusing to treat the Court as one species, person or continuity.
- **Status:** `garden-reviewed-revise`.

## LH-04 — The Unclosed Line

- **Lane / scale / carrier:** institution and relic / mortuary schism / burned cemetery, grafted genealogy and sealed body.
- **Humble origin:** physicians and mourners seek a way to keep dying people present long enough to finish testimony, reconcile households and expose false Court speakers.
- **Historical pressure:** fear of manipulated ancestry and the political advantage of possessing an indisputably living founder.
- **Primary grammar:** Aerenal's rival necromantic school and destruction of a bloodline; Saul's unlawful demand for the dead, false immortality and the World Egg's soul-route predation systems.
- **World Egg torque:** the school crosses from preservation into actual captivity. Its methods can produce `bound-human-soul` or `retained-human-soul` cases through infernal custody, Idol bargains, living descendant anchors and stolen funerary machinery. Other results are residue, occupants or empty simulations rather than successful retention.
- **Hard edge:** some victims remain conscious in dead or failing bodies. Others depart while a copy or hostile occupant speaks through the corpse. When the ruling Houses of one city and its dependent wards suppress the school, their forces kill practitioners, surrendered dependants and children, burn bodies needed as evidence, redistribute survivors and graft useful techniques onto victorious lineages. Necessary destruction of soul prisons becomes collective punishment and political theft.
- **Source-fidelity ruling:** received source: the Aerenal–Vol mortuary schism and destruction of the rival line; borrowed element: competing immortality methods followed by state eradication of the defeated tradition; original hard edge: extermination of a lineage and suppression of its continuity practice; current state: `intact`; restoration: none; historical layer: authorial atrocity divided among victorious orthodoxy, survivor memory and erased technical records.
- **Plain causal chain:** Court impersonations create demand for certain continuity → one city's school offers bodily retention → experiments imprison some souls and replace others with convincing false speakers → victims and rival Houses expose the work → the city government and forces from its dependent wards destroy laboratories but massacre whole households and falsify the settlement → refugees carry fragments outward while later orthodoxy inherits both real safeguards and stolen methods.
- **1360 AR traces:** interrupted genealogies reappearing under victorious House names; mass cremation pits beneath official gardens; sealed bodies with incompatible response layers; and technical vocabulary shared by condemned texts and orthodox embalming manuals.
- **Collision ruling:** not Ansharan vampirism, the Pale Hand, an Undertow invention, the Vol name or a secret origin for every retained soul. Each case requires its own route and continuity audit.
- **Open:** city and ward names, guilt of founders, exact mechanism, number of actual bound souls, identity of patrons, survivors and whether the city-scale purge ended the practice locally.
- **Review disposition:** `revise` — limit the purge to one city and its dependent wards; refugees and stolen techniques may travel, but the event is not a league-wide purge or general civil war.
- **Status:** `garden-reviewed-revise`.

## LH-05 — The Keepers of the Last Door

- **Lane / scale / carrier:** institution and relic / threshold order / warrants, grave seals and destroyed evidence.
- **Humble origin:** burial recovery crews need a repeatable way to distinguish a preserved body, ordinary residue, trapped soul, imposed office, Patron claimant and predatory occupant after the Unclosed Line catastrophe.
- **Historical pressure:** reconstruction after mortuary civil violence and later expansion into foreign grave disputes.
- **Primary grammar:** the Deathguard's war against undeath; Tobit's burial piety; testing spirits and claims without treating suspicion as proof.
- **World Egg torque:** the Keepers use a five-part grave warrant: identify the body; test for ordinary mechanism; establish whether a human soul is unlawfully present; identify any office, residue, copy, Mask or Patron anchor; and name the least destructive closure that protects victims and evidence.
- **Hard edge:** the order saves people from soul prisons and destroys predatory bodies, but state factions also execute custodians without adequate testing, burn foreign graves, suppress inconvenient authentic records and label rival ancestor customs criminal. Destroying evidence can make an apparent victory impossible to audit.
- **Source-fidelity ruling:** received source: the Deathguard's transnational pursuit of undead and hostile necromancy; borrowed element: a specialist order that treats corrupted immortality as an active battlefield; original hard edge: lethal campaigns against beings and practitioners whose destruction the order regards as necessary; current state: `intact`; restoration: none; historical layer: authorial mixed institutional record with heroic Lives, warrants and victim petitions.
- **Commission limit:** the Keepers are living humans, not angels, saints or Threshold Wardens. No saint is attached to their chain of command, and no seal compels heavenly action.
- **1360 AR traces:** contradictory five-seal warrants; graves bearing four seals because the final classification was disputed; and foreign restitution claims for bodies destroyed during Keeper campaigns.
- **Collision ruling:** independent of existing breachward orders, the impersonal Threshold Warden office, Nsizwa Spirit-Keepers and Vortharan corpse industries. Similar work does not establish descent or shared authority.
- **Open:** founder, creed, state relation, current factions, geographic reach, actual successes, atrocities and whether a reform movement still exists.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — the living forensic order remains distinct from breachward and heavenly offices while retaining both rescue and institutional crime.
- **Status:** `garden-reviewed-retain`.

## LH-06 — The Mantle Houses

- **Lane / scale / carrier:** human hero and institution / local heroic cycle / adopted name, repeated deed and disputed equipment.
- **Humble origin:** a frontier household without access to prestigious preserved bodies adopts living heirs to maintain the unfinished duties of dead defenders.
- **Historical pressure:** rivalry between corpse-centered Houses and mobile communities whose dead cannot be kept in the central vaults.
- **Primary grammar:** Tairnadal heroic emulation, TES mantling and World Egg legend accretion.
- **World Egg torque:** a mantle is first a human office and disciplined performance. Repeated stories and reenactments can produce `residue`, `legend-persona`, `infrastructure-office` or a feeding opportunity for an Idol Mask; none transfers the dead person's soul into the heir.
- **Hard edge:** Houses pressure heirs to surrender names, marriages and occupations in order to repeat an approved life. Some reenact inherited feuds or atrocities because custodians declare the deed essential to continuity. Refusing a mantle can mean exile; accepting one can give the living performer authority over victims who never consented to remember the ancestor as a hero.
- **Source-fidelity ruling:** received source: Tairnadal heroic ancestor emulation, with TES mantling used only as a secondary identity grammar; borrowed element: a living heir treating an ancestor's characteristic deeds as vocation and proof of continuity; original hard edge: martial identity and inherited duty can subordinate the heir's independent life and perpetuate ancient conflicts; current state: `intact`; restoration: none; historical layer: performed heroic tradition with legal offices, folklore accretion and occasional hostile Mask imitation.
- **Plain causal chain:** mobile communities cannot use central ancestor bodies → they preserve duties through trained living heirs → successful performances win land and legitimacy → custodians begin treating resemblance as identity → later heirs are coerced into repeating politically useful deeds → rival performers and victims force courts to distinguish person, office and legend.
- **1360 AR traces:** armour repeatedly altered for bodies of different size and sex; two living claimants with equally valid training records; contracts distinguishing inherited duty from inherited guilt; and an outlawed rite in which a performer abandons a birth name.
- **Collision ruling:** not Nsizwa Izivukwa, reincarnation, Giant Regnant succession, Idol mantling or proof that legend creates souls. It may interact with [[Names of the Menhir, Masks of the Idols]] only after a specific Mask is established.
- **Open:** whether the Mantle Houses are exiles, rivals, frontier branches or a later reform; number of mantles; named heroes; supernatural cases; wars and present legal standing.
- **Review disposition:** `retain` — the living-office and performed-legend grammar remains useful without transferring, merging or manufacturing souls.
- **Status:** `garden-reviewed-retain`.

## Garden Promotion Ruling

All six items are planted in [[The Historical Seed Garden]] as one **First Ancestor-Polity Seed Cluster**. This gives the Garden durable apertures without promoting:

- a mapped civilization;
- a culture name or ethnicity;
- a new Menhir;
- a complete Patron realm;
- a collective afterlife;
- a fixed soul-binding war;
- an international order;
- any specific saint, Sinner, ruler, founder or active 1360 outcome.

It also does not plant a hidden past contact with any known culture. Similar customs, legends or objects in the core setting cannot be attributed to the Last Houses without a later collision and first-contact review.

The six roots may later remain one cultural ecology, separate into neighbouring societies or be partially rejected. Their shared source grammar does not require shared final identity.

[[Historical Seed Garden - Coherence and Balance Review]] subsequently records three `retain` and three incorporated `revise` dispositions. The Still Cypress effect remains observable while its controlling existing Menhir or conjunction stays unresolved; every Court speaker requires separate classification; and the Unclosed Line purge is bounded to one city and its dependent wards rather than a league or civilization-wide war. No root was removed.
