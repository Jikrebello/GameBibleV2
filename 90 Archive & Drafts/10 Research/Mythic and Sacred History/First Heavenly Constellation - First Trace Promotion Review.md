# Trace Rulings

## OBA-N07 — Public Unhousing Rubric

- **Promote:** several cities require a witnessed public procedure before converting a sanctuary or refuge gate to military, commercial or punitive use. Recensions disagree about the number and order of stations.
- **Withhold:** original city, covenant, purge, besiegers, choir, gate plan and carried register.
- **Independent value:** the rubric distinguishes possession of a structure from continuation of its old public charge.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Rule of Five Closures, Nythalum machinery, Erasure of Orthea or a permanent city guardian.
- **Severity sync:** the originating pressure must retain murdered and expelled protected households; “change of use” cannot replace purge.

## HC-N10 — The Name Before the Title

- **Promote:** one martyr-memory tradition requires the name of a person killed through the martyr’s earlier betrayal to be read before the martyr’s honorific.
- **Withhold:** clerk, prison, victim household, Mask, binding, reliquary, voice and Saintly manifestation.
- **Independent value:** later service and possible holiness cannot erase personal guilt or force a victim’s family to pronounce forgiveness.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Red Door Names, Three-Lock Prison Road, Saint of the Two Ferries or a general martyrology rule.
- **Severity sync:** the prior act remains fatal informing under torture, not a harmless error in record order.

## OBA-N02 — No-New-Heir Clause

- **Promote:** refuge and hospital charters preserve a rule that shelter cannot be cancelled merely because a household obtains a preferred heir.
- **Withhold:** enslaved mother, child, coercing couple, estate, cistern, caravan and Angel.
- **Independent value:** it protects dependants against inheritance convenience whether the rescue tradition is historical, legendary or misattributed.
- **Collision boundary:** not a national mother, miraculous spring, abolition of all bondage or Saint-founded hospital network.
- **Severity sync:** the originating wound remains coerced sexual access, beating, expulsion and a child near death.

## OBA-N06 — The Five-Name Rest

- **Promote:** one dedication hymn contains a silent measure in which rival choirs either read five executed names or maintain deliberate silence.
- **Withhold:** singer, guild, old temple, confiscation, Angel, coal and final judgement.
- **Independent value:** meter and victim records expose a historical alteration without proving the supernatural confession.
- **Collision boundary:** not a Phoenix coal, Susanna hearing, Children of the Closed Temple or general condemnation of Conversion.
- **Severity sync:** the silence answers informing, torture, execution and denied bodies; it cannot become reverence only for the penitent singer.

## HC-N03 — The Pilgrim’s Table

- **Promote:** frontier command teaching calls a set of truthful but unendorsed tactical choices **the Pilgrim’s table** and insists that responsibility remains with the chooser.
- **Withhold:** traveller, seven attendants, pass, hostages, Giant tally, Pride Mask, assault and military cult.
- **Independent value:** the doctrine resists commanders who turn advice, omen or prophecy into transferred culpability.
- **Collision boundary:** not a signal code, heavenly war council, Rule of Five Closures or general strategy attributed to an angel.
- **Severity sync:** the source campaign retains captives killed by a chosen assault and surrendering guards executed in revenge.

## HC-N05 — The Dependants’ Annex

- **Promote:** evacuation agreements may require a separate annex naming wounded people, servants, debtors, mixed families and other dependants omitted from military and tax rolls.
- **Withhold:** town, belligerents, commander, cart crew, ford, Warden and exact dead.
- **Independent value:** the annex makes deliberate abandonment harder without requiring a miracle or a permanent rescue order.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Long Watch, a last-watch institution, Golden Road or universal refugee compact.
- **Severity sync:** the custom begins after commanders knowingly left people to be killed; the Warden does not perform the carrying work for humans.

## OBA-N08 — The Three-Carrier Register

- **Promote:** some diaspora and ransom courts require a captive roll to travel through three unrelated human carriers so one ruler, patron or archive cannot suppress every authenticated copy.
- **Withhold:** people, three jurisdictions, prisoners, Guardian, Mask, Patron, fallen office and liberation hearing.
- **Independent value:** distributed custody evidence answers disappearance even if the delay was entirely political.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Three Testimonies of the Last Houses, Three-Lock Road, a national angel or proof of ethnic innocence.
- **Severity sync:** prisoners died under torture, exposure and disease before the record reached a hearing.

## HC-N11 — The Two Death Rolls

- **Promote:** one wall memorial preserves separate rolls for people who freely stayed and people stationed under threat, debt or emergency law.
- **Withhold:** city, commander, siege, structural failure, confraternity, Saint and later correction.
- **Independent value:** it prevents later hagiography from calling unequal consent one unanimous sacrifice.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Warden of the Last Redoubt, Founder Who Corrected the Order or an authorization for mass-sacrifice rites.
- **Severity sync:** the commander personally used coercive stations and earlier ordered lethal punishment; repentance does not make the compulsory dead volunteers.

## HC-N13 — No Wound Inherits the Child

- **Promote:** several custody courts repeat the clause **“No wound inherits the child.”** Blood, scars, household office and a parent’s compact cannot transfer ownership of a human person or soul.
- **Withhold:** maimed heir, murdered kin, client dynasty, infernal office, restitution, surviving children, Saint and five-wing manifestations.
- **Independent value:** the rule remains sound even if the penitent figure was never canonized or the later intervention was misidentified.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Feast and the Reading, hereditary Saint-order, angel–human bloodline or rule that family obligation is unreal.
- **Severity sync:** the provisional founder personally killed surrendered relatives before repentance; the rule cannot turn vengeance into sanctity.

## HC-N08 — The Protected-Name Signal

- **Promote:** military surrender law requires protected persons to be named before property clauses, and one broken three-note call marks a message suspected of coerced authorship.
- **Withhold:** prison, governor, Herald, ward, mutilated manifestation, rescuers, fragments and counterfeit fallen trumpet.
- **Independent value:** the procedure protects a surrender against confiscatory rewriting even if the angelic event is denied.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Pilgrim’s tally, Shattered Engines, Veil over Veridun or a general heavenly military code.
- **Severity sync:** rescuers, prisoners and witnesses died, and both occupier and rebels suppressed the defeat.

## OBA-N04 — The Floor Beyond the Count

- **Promote:** several legal codes require a census to state its permitted purpose and call any later seizure use **the floor beyond the count**, requiring a new public act.
- **Withhold:** principality, ruler, mortality, Angel, threshing yard, warnings and exact dead.
- **Independent value:** the limit restrains hostage, labor and property seizure without proving divine punishment or forbidding all enumeration.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Clerk’s Ending, Grey Sweat, Quiet Ninth, Census Chronicles or recurring plague jurisdiction.
- **Severity sync:** the historical pressure includes house-breaking, killed resisters and a mass mortality affecting clerks, soldiers, nobles, laborers and children.

## OBA-N11 — The Double-Tag Docket

- **Promote:** one active assurance case begins from two exchanged corpse tags, a corrected baptismal identity and a bounded contract response that changes when the authenticated name is used.
- **Withhold:** tallyman’s name, full crime, final judgement, Guardian, washer, broker knowledge, other debtor and 1360 verdict.
- **Independent value:** investigators can prove neglect and attempted identity fraud without deciding the apparition or the contract case.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Nameless-Citizen Petition, Ossuary Crown, Keeper of the Contested Body or proof that baptism defeats every infernal claim.
- **Severity sync:** the dead man genuinely defrauded poor households and contributed to a fatal riot; workhouse neglect and corpse fraud do not make him innocent.
- **Forward Rule:** only the documented starting facts enter the Garden. Every ruling, remedy, casualty and supernatural response after the 1360 opening remains unfixed.

## Withheld Layer

Across all twelve traces, withhold:

- every final personal name and exact locality;
- every completed Saintly biography and public canonization;
- every angelic title as an authenticated identity;
- every relic body, feather, coal, wheel, trumpet fragment or speaking object;
- every institutional claim of direct succession;
- every later apparition not required by the controlling placement;
- every current verdict or campaign outcome.
