# First Idol Constellation — Collision and Disposition Review

## Purpose and Result

This ledger reviews the four provisional histories in [[First Idol Constellation - Historical Placement Matrix]] against their source nurseries, historical neighbors, existing Mask ecologies, erased-person mysteries and succession boundaries.

The result is:

- two `retain`: ID-P01 and ID-P03;
- two incorporated `revise`: ID-P02R and ID-P04R;
- zero `defer`;
- zero `reject`;
- four historically placed but Garden-locked Idol episodes;
- no edit to canon, chronology, culture histories or [[The Historical Seed Garden]];
- no use of the recorded-history Regnant-succession allowance.

# II. Detailed Review Rulings

## ID-P01 — The War of Stolen Victories

### Collision findings

The principal risk is that four Hungers appearing around one war could turn the later Sevenfold Feast label back into a literal pantheon campaign. The placement avoids this by giving each Hunger a different human architecture and a successive aperture. Wrath expands the target class during war; Pride seizes authorship after victory; Greed legalizes the captives and land; Envy redirects the excluded allies' later grievance.

The River Reckoner, Tall Crown, Keeper of the Drowned Deeds and Unavenged Sister are provisional dependent Masks. They share no person, council, route or command. Removing one architecture affects only its corresponding overlay.

The Reed Crowns of Sarrakai remain a provisional political nursery inside the Ishkatari horizon. The episode neither identifies Ishkatar's capital nor explains its fall, Titanic Naga ancestry or later serpent cults.

### Human coherence and continuity

The water diversion and worker deaths are ordinary political facts. Giant and human rulers then choose collective retaliation, enslavement, false glory and dispossession. Idol pressure makes those choices repeat and gives them Shadow routes; it does not cause the initial scarcity or remove mortal guilt.

Dynastic Giants retain `giant-continuity`. Giant-blooded humans, enslaved workers, soldiers and civilians retain complete `human-soul`. The dead are not Mask fragments, and sale-tags or deeds do not contain their persons.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received sources:** Warhammer Great Game; Greek division of honours and spoils; Wisdom 13–15 cult machinery.
- **Borrowed element:** several predatory powers seize different fruits of one victory.
- **Hard edge:** massacre after surrender, execution, enslavement, family separation, confiscation, grave denial and renewed feud remain direct.
- **State:** `intact after historical placement`.

### Disposition

`retain` — the episode provides a concrete Giant-age Hunger war while keeping its four routes and human crimes distinct. Promotion remains locked.

## ID-P02R — The Champion of Four Doors

### Collision findings

The source nursery could easily resemble the Crown-Market: a ruler accepts several supernatural offers and gains partial sovereignty. The selected claimant is instead already one autonomous Envy Archmask. Human magistrates do not buy kingship for themselves; they lease three foreign capacities to the Archmask through four existing city institutions.

The Shoreless King is a separate c. 1160 BR Menhir court event with a different region, continuity, disaster and seal. This city lies later in the City-State period, and neither its harbor nor its champion founds a pan-Atherian navigation tradition.

The placement also refuses a Sinner comparison. The claimant is not a damned human soul carrying several offices. It is one `idol-pattern` person whose native Envy continuity remains categorical while foreign Masks and routes supply bounded powers.

### Mechanism and succession firewall

Each door has its own architecture, price and reproducible interruption. Repairing a Greed title does not diminish Envy or close the Wrath gate. Restoring an erased commander breaks the Pride procession without altering the claimant's personal continuity.

The city's later claim that the Archmask “nearly became the god behind all four faces” is cult propaganda. Accession would require voluntary assimilation into one entire guilty Throne, not accumulated leases from several Thrones. No such assimilation occurs.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received source:** multi-patron champion grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** a powerful intermediary takes incompatible gifts from rival powers and becomes trapped by their prices.
- **Hard edge:** enslavement, sacrifice, execution of surrendered enemies, erased allies, betrayal, mutilation and abandoned dependants remain.
- **State:** `restored through continuity selection`.
- **Correction:** the champion is one Envy Archmask; three foreign powers provide separable routes rather than species change, soul merger or shared sovereignty.

### Disposition

`revise` — incorporate the Envy continuity, three lease model and non-founding city limit. Promotion remains locked.

## ID-P03 — The Campaign of the Borrowed Martyr

### Collision findings

The episode shares the Siege of the Martyrs' Names ecology but not one of its existing people, graves, prison roads or churches. Its human aperture is a local prison laundry and burial rota; its later anchors are one quarry-town civic icon and one separate road sanctuary. It has no relation to the Red Passage, the Forty Flames or the landlocked enemy gate.

`Borrowed Saints` remains a creature-family label for Envy counterfeits, not one recurring supernatural person. The road image may be classified as one dependent Envy Mask of that broad ecology. The Crowned Witness is separately a Pride Mask. Neither contains the executed soul or authenticates a relic.

Bruckmoor remains a much later forty-year Weeping Courts operation after the Grey Sweat. This episode is provincial and Conversion-era, uses two rival Hungers, and leaves hostile early evidence rather than a single engineered apparition site.

### Earth-first and saintly boundary

The torture, ambiguous recantation, execution and corpse denial must work as human history before either Mask appears. Officials and guards are culpable for the death and body trafficking. Later clergy, shrine custodians and violent pilgrims remain culpable for suppression, grave theft and retaliatory killing.

The human soul is judged once and remains whole. A genuine later Saintly intercession is neither required nor excluded. If it occurred, it would be the same redeemed person under a bounded commission and would not inherit either Mask's cult, image or violence.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received sources:** Orthodox martyr reception; TES mantle and cultural-biography grammar.
- **Borrowed element:** one bodily witness becomes the contested subject of Lives, relic claims, icons and supernatural identities.
- **Hard edge:** imprisonment, torture, informing, recantation, execution, corpse denial and theft, coercion of kin, grave desecration and retaliatory killing remain.
- **State:** `intact after earth-first placement`.

### Disposition

`retain` — source layers, two Mask tests and commission limits keep the episode useful without declaring or debunking a Saint. Promotion remains locked.

## ID-P04R — The Archmask That Ate Its Biography

### Collision findings

The nearest collision is not merely thematic. Several existing roots concern destroyed names and records:

- ER-01 is a human institutional erasure in which authorities redistribute followers and practices after a catastrophe.
- ER-02 is a supernatural attack that damages a human person's names, images and testimony without harming the soul.
- LH-04 is a far-distant ancestor-polity purge tied to soul-binding claims.
- Caleran's archive crime is a later civic manipulation of ancestral history.

ID-P04R instead concerns one autonomous Pride Archmask intentionally destroying **its own former cultural anchors** so a dependent Envy Mask cannot use them. The human prince collaborates for land, legitimacy and administrative uniformity. No human soul or Heaven's memory is erased.

The regional ruling is deliberately narrow. The purge affects one city and its directly administered wards. It does not create Virelian or Vesari identity, cause the Choosing at Strazgorod, explain the Great Split or implicate every household of either later people.

### Mechanism and succession firewall

Portraits, forbidden kin names, civic offices, graves and settlement plans determine how the Archmask can manifest locally. Destroying them removes routes and duties; it does not change past events or the Pride Throne. The autonomous Protector remains an Archmask before and after the purge.

The Elder Face is fixed as a dependent Envy Mask grown through displaced families' perfected comparison narrative. It contains real memories and names but is not a former Regnant, the Protector's soul, an ancestor collective or an innocent older god.

### Source-fidelity ruling

- **Received sources:** TES identity and mantle-control grammar; *damnatio memoriae*.
- **Borrowed element:** a supernatural ruler protects its present identity by destroying the people and carriers that preserve an earlier biography.
- **Hard edge:** executions, child and dependant killing, grave destruction, forced renaming, deportation, burned settlements, famine and retaliatory murder remain.
- **State:** `intact after scale and identity correction`.
- **Correction:** one city and dependent wards; one Pride Archmask; one dependent Envy rival; no Regnant or soul erasure.

### Disposition

`revise` — incorporate the bounded purge, Elder-Face classification and open Archmask origin and fate. Promotion remains locked.

# III. Constellation-Wide Checks

## No hidden campaign

The four episodes share no Mask, Archmask, human, dynasty, cult, road, relic, object or institution. Their dates are separated by centuries. ID-P01's four dependent Masks do not recur as ID-P02R's leased routes. ID-P03's two counterfeits are unrelated to ID-P04R's Envy rival.

The repeated presence of Pride and Envy is purposeful but not genealogical. Both Hungers naturally compete over authorship, comparison and public identity. That recurring pressure supplies thematic coherence without making one secret court.

## No culture ownership

One Sarrakai river war does not make Ishkatari peoples servants of four Idols. One inner-gulf city does not make Atherian synthesis demonic. One provincial cult conflict does not make the early Church counterfeit. One post-Split purge does not make Virelian or Vesari identity the work of Pride.

Every culture contains perpetrators, beneficiaries, coerced participants, resisters, victims and later interpreters. The Idol relationship belongs to specified choices and institutions.

## Discoverability

Each central event requires at least two independent evidence classes:

| Placement | Minimum corroboration for the central event |
|---|---|
| ID-P01 | river and grave archaeology plus monument recutting or prisoner-title evidence |
| ID-P02R | gate architecture plus at least two victim/office records showing separable prices |
| ID-P03 | prison and body evidence plus hostile Lives, family custom or separate anchor tests |
| ID-P04R | demolished settlement infrastructure plus underpaint, bodies or coordinated fiscal gaps |

No Mask, vision, relic, archive or family tradition knows the complete history. The bounded tests identify mechanisms, never whole biographies or final judgements.

# IV. Promotion Locks

The four placements remain outside the Garden. A later trace review must:

1. prefer one material, bodily, customary or architectural carrier per placement;
2. keep the trace useful if the proposed Mask identity is wrong;
3. authenticate no Archmask, Saint, relic, Regnant or complete cult genealogy;
4. preserve massacre, slavery, torture, corpse theft, execution, child killing, purge and dispossession where causal;
5. compare density against the existing sixty-five Garden roots;
6. promote no more than a small subset merely because all four placements passed.
