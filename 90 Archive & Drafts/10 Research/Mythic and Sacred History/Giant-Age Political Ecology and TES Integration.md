# Giant-Age Political Ecology and TES Integration

> [!info] Atlas integration

## Status and Controlling Proposal

This is an author-only expansion bed. The Giant descent rules, Five Imperial Horizons, many-state political model, categorical continuities, Nythalum lunar boundary, and five broad imperial genealogies have been promoted into controlling canon. The provisional polities, working chronology, rulers, borders, actor identities, and specific Egg White outcomes in this dossier remain exploratory. Nothing here settles a protected Weird, identifies Anshara's final sovereign or capital, or assigns a new polity as the ancestor of a later people.

> **Controlling ruling:** Qianglong, Ishkatar, Xolotl, Nythalum, and Anshara are the **Five Imperial Horizons**: the five largest surviving traditions of Giant hegemony, administration, monumental culture, and continuity engineering. They are not the complete list of states that existed during the Age of Chains.

Common liberation histories compress a thousand years of shifting crowns into the four rival god-kingdoms broken directly by the Prophet; some traditions remember only three. The authorial and reconstructed register restores Anshara as a **fifth imperial horizon** whose self-destruction ran parallel to the liberation. Those five systems left the largest ruins and imposed the archive categories through which smaller states were remembered, but the historical Giant lands should contain dozens of kingdoms, city-states, client crowns, cadet courts, mobile confederacies, temple estates, tributary leagues, and short-lived regional empires.

The five remain special because each possessed all of the following at imperial scale:

- a long-lived political and ritual vocabulary that survived several dynasties;
- a recognizable Giant ruling ecology and theory of sacred descent;
- a continental or maritime sphere of tribute, forced labor, trade, marriage, and imitation;
- a signature continuity project capable of outliving individual courts;
- a remembered catastrophe through which later people explained the end of the Chains.

This model preserves the existing five while making the Giant Age large enough to feel historical.

### Canon Reconciliation Completed

The load-bearing foundations now reside in controlling canon:

1. [[Giant Descent and Creaturely Continuity]] establishes bounded parentage, categorical birth, distributed Titan life, Giant death, and the distinction between a surviving person and a copy.

## The Historical Grammar

The useful ancient comparison is not five modern states with fixed borders. It is a layered system in which a few rulers claim equality with one another while many smaller rulers bargain, rebel, intermarry, change patrons, and survive conquest under altered titles. The [Amarna correspondence](https://www.metmuseum.org/it/essays/the-amarna-letters) preserves precisely the distinction between mutually recognizing great kings and the many rulers of small dependent kingdoms; the [Mesopotamian sequence](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/amgg/mesopotamianhistory/index.html) shows city-state competition, short hegemonies, imperial consolidation, collapse, and renewed dynastic rule in the same cultural country. Assyrian records also preserve the practical difference between provinces, hereditary vassal rulers, civic bodies, and useful [buffer states](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/saao/aebp/essentials/diplomats/representingassyria/index.html).

The World Egg translation is:

1. The five horizons are the remembered **great kingship systems**.
2. Their boundaries expand, contract, overlap, and sometimes disappear for generations.
3. A conquered kingdom may keep its dynasty, gods, language, and law while supplying hostages, troops, blood, labor, or launch material.
4. A province may become an independent kingdom without ceasing to use the conqueror's imperial title.
5. A marriage can be more politically important than a conquest because it combines Giant traits, ancestor access, cult routes, and claims upon relics.
6. An old imperial culture can survive centuries without political unity.
7. Later archaeologists can correctly identify an imperial material horizon while being wrong that every site within it belonged to one state.

## Six Political Layers

Not every layer must appear in every region, and a polity may move between layers over time.

1. **Great Imperial Horizon:** one of the five macro-traditions through which later history classifies the age.
2. **Apex Throne or Titan-Firstborn Domain:** a rare primordial ancestor, body-territory, or eldest sovereignty inherited, imprisoned, or claimed by later rulers.
3. **High Giant House or Palace League:** God-Kin dynasts with their own armies, marriages, cults, capitals, and capacity to treat an imperial ruler as a peer.
4. **Cadet Crown, Satrapy, or Marriage State:** a diluted but still powerful dynasty governing a region under disputed imperial seniority.
5. **Client Crown, Temple Estate, March, or City League:** locally governed communities owing tribute, troops, heirs, blood, labor, or technical service.
6. **Subject City, Household, Caravan Polity, Port, or Mobile Confederacy:** the ordinary human political communities most likely to disappear from imperial histories.

Affiliation is temporal rather than ethnic. The same kingdom may enter Qianglong's administrative horizon, accept an Ansharan marriage house, pay Ishkatar river tribute, and regain independence without its population becoming four different peoples.

## Blood, Birth, and Dynasty

The expanded political system requires one controlling distinction:

> **Blood is graduated; continuity is categorical. Blood dilutes. A soul does not fractionalize.**

- A **Titan or Titan-Firstborn** is the direct child of a human woman and either a bounded generative manifestation of a Menhir, the underlying Idol Throne acting through a dependent Mask, or a separately classified autonomous Archmask. It possesses `titan-continuity` and no human soul. An ordinary dependent Mask is an organ of its Idol rather than an independent parent; any exception requires its own continuity dossier.
- A **High Giant or God-Kin** descends from Titans through later unions and possesses a nonhuman Giant continuity.
- A **Dynastic Giant** is a later, usually weaker bearer of Giant continuity. Great age, stature, strength, inherited law, and supernatural capacities may remain far above the human range.
- A **giant-blooded human** possesses a complete human soul and the Image, however visibly altered, long-lived, or powerful the body may be.

No child is thirty percent human-souled or seventy percent Giant. Concentrated ancestry, parental continuity, blood craft, birth rites, and supernatural pressure may affect the likelihood or bodily expression of a birth, but the result must be one authorially declared continuity. Giant courts can influence birth and can lie about it; they cannot manufacture the Image or guarantee what only the Lord gives.

This uncertainty improves the dynastic history. A court that has practised close marriage for generations may unexpectedly produce a human-souled heir. A distant cadet house may produce a true Dynastic Giant whom the capital cannot dismiss. A giant-blooded human may be the most powerful ruler in centuries and remain fully human. A frail-looking child may be the only true bearer of an ancestral continuity. Blood registers therefore matter without becoming an infallible metaphysical caste test.

### What Dynasties Do with the Problem

Different houses respond differently:

- **close-kin concentration:** preserve traits and access at the cost of illness, rivalry, and increasingly narrow marriage pools;
- **conquest marriage:** take the defeated dynasty's heir into the victor's house and claim both ancestors;
- **hostage fostering:** raise rival heirs at court until affection, title, and dependence become indistinguishable;
- **first-night predation:** use subject populations as coerced reproductive stock in the hope of producing useful heirs;
- **fresh-blood rites:** attempt another direct Titan-Firstborn conception through a bounded Menhir manifestation, an Idol Throne acting through a Mask, or an autonomous Archmask;
- **cadet diffusion:** spread many lesser branches so that at least one survives civil war;
- **mortal administration:** retain an ancient Giant patron while government passes to human-souled descendants, priests, scribes, generals, and estate families;
- **ritual adoption:** use a crown, name, blood exchange, relic, or office to make a politically necessary outsider eligible for bounded ancestral powers.

None of these proves moral legitimacy. A rite may genuinely grant access while leaving the act coercive, the genealogy false, and the ruler unjust.

## A Working Chronology of the Giant Political World

Dates below are spacing tools, not proposed canon dates. The existing c. 3400-2300 BR Age of Chains remains the controlling outer frame.

### 1. The First Blood Domains — before c. 3400 BR

The direct Titan-Firstborn do not begin by drawing borders. Their bodies, households, captive populations, hunting grounds, rivers, mountains, and cult sites form personal dominions. Some are dismembered yet remain one distributed life. Some father or bear High Giants. Some are imprisoned and used as living sources of law or power.

The Titanomachy-like wars belong here: High Giants overthrow, bind, divide, or inherit their Titan-Firstborn parents. The subsequent Gigantomachy-like wars produce retaliatory Titan-Firstborn, War-Born broods, and at most a few singular Conjunction Children. These conflicts create the inherited prisoners, broken bodies, dynastic grudges, and disputed offices from which later kingdoms grow.

These conflicts—and the later Twin-Court marriage wars—are Giant dynastic history. They occur after the Faewild Schism and do not explain it, rename it, or introduce mortal participants into it.

### 2. The Many Crowns — approximately c. 3400-3150 BR

Dozens of God-Kin palace states replace personal Titan domains. A single valley, delta branch, island, lake, mountain stair, or caravan gate can sustain a kingdom. Two rulers may share one divine ancestor; one ruler may hold pieces of several. The first durable human scribal, mortuary, agricultural, and engineering institutions emerge beneath them.

The five imperial horizons exist only in early or regional forms. Ansharan court rank, Ishkatari river kingship, Qianglong caste claims, and older Xolotlan and Nythalum predecessor traditions compete with many alternatives.

### 3. The Great Marriage System — approximately c. 3150-2850 BR

Peer dynasties discover that marriage, hostage exchange, cult adoption, and relic sharing can bind powers conquest cannot reliably kill. Diplomatic houses classify one another as throne-equals, lesser crowns, client houses, or unrecognized monsters. Composite courts acquire several ancestor cults and deliberately contradictory genealogies.

This is the richest period for Æsir-Vanir-like dynastic wars: peers fight to exhaustion, exchange elite hostages, intermarry, share rites, and produce a pantheon whose members visibly came from different houses.

### 4. The Hegemonic Centuries — approximately c. 2850-2450 BR

The five traditions become the strongest archival and military systems, but their peaks need not coincide.

- **Anshara** can be the oldest continuously recognized court order: its dynasty, capital, and borders change while the Court of Courts claims that every replacement was proper procession.
- **Qianglong** repeatedly unifies and fractures among its four caste traditions. “Qianglong” names the ecumene and imperial project even when four or more courts compete.
- **Ishkatar** alternates between river unity, rival upper and lower crowns, foreign dynasties, and Pharaoh-Serpents who successfully reunify the title.
- **Xolotl** is best treated as a later and more violently centralized empire. Its war-calendar and Blood Sun absorb older jungle and highland city systems rather than creating civilization from nothing.
- **Nythalum** enters continental politics late through seaborne extraction, observatories, off-island annexes, and launch supply. Its island core remains unusually unified because the Ascension Project consumes every rival institution.

Secondary empires can rise, conquer several smaller states, and disappear entirely within one horizon's longer history.

### 5. The Closing Courts — approximately c. 2450-2320 BR

Blood is more diluted, but the political order is not gentler. Many thrones are now occupied by giant-blooded human dynasties ruling beneath an embodied ancestor, a hidden Titan fragment, a court office, an Idol Mask, a Menhir correspondence, or a claimed Astral patron. “Purification” movements intensify close marriage, coercive breeding, fresh Titan-Firstborn rites, and the destruction of mixed genealogies.

At the same time, imperial ancestors recognize that providential liberation cannot be defeated. Qianglong, Ishkatar, Xolotl, Anshara, and numerous lesser courts prepare bodily flight, folded refuge, counterfeit continuation, or Egg White secession. Nythalum alone commits its imperial programme to physical spaceflight and settlement of the Moon.

### 6. Systemic Crisis and the Unbinding — approximately c. 2320-2260 BR

From c. 2320-2300 BR, succession crises, failed flight projects, rebellious clients, unpaid troops, refugee movements, and compromised officials weaken the closing courts. From c. 2300 BR, the Prophet's advance does not knock over five untouched monoliths: it passes through that fractured world, where human burial networks and smaller rulers must decide whether to open a gate or become the last jailer of a dying order.

Four imperial systems are broken directly in liberation memory. Anshara destroys itself in the parallel Drinking Wars. Some lesser polities defect, some are annihilated, some join the Exodus, some survive briefly as human successor kingdoms, and some vanish so completely that later scholars file their ruins under the nearest great horizon.

- **Varesh's satellite court** proves that an Ansharan satrap could control wells, caravan approaches, paradise-gardens, tribute, and its own dependent households far from the unresolved imperial center.
- **The Unending Muster** proves a Qianglong western military march, road-and-depot network, or Qianglong-controlled client system reached the later Grenzburg frontier.
- Nythalum Return infrastructure securely occurs in the Emerald Expanse. A draft Xolotl step-tomb provides a useful but unpromoted foothold for overlapping colonial phases rather than exclusive continental blocks; retain that possibility pending its own canon review.
- Nythalum launch-support vessels, Return Annexes, and scattered facilities prove a material logistical network outside the drowned island.

The Giant-Age map should therefore use corridors, river systems, coastwise networks, marches, enclaves, tribute arrows, and time-slices. Five solid modern border colors would reproduce the problem this dossier is meant to solve.

## Diplomatic and Dynastic Systems

### Ranks of Recognition

Giant diplomacy should distinguish at least five relationships:

1. **Throne-equal:** exchanges royal kin, relics, technical specialists, and named gifts; addresses the other ruler as a peer.
2. **Protected crown:** retains a local dynasty and law while surrendering foreign policy, troops, tribute, or heirs.
3. **Client house:** rules through an imperial grant and can be replaced if the hegemon can enforce the decision.
4. **Temple or estate polity:** owes allegiance through one ancestor, Mask, Menhir correspondence, or continuity engine rather than ordinary territorial administration.
5. **Unrecognized power:** may be militarily formidable but is denied diplomatic personhood so that enslavement, conquest, and broken treaties appear lawful.

Status can change faster than territory. A minor king who gains a living Titan-Firstborn grandparent may demand equality overnight. A great empire whose ancestral patron stops answering may discover that yesterday's “servants” now call it brother.

### What Travels with a Royal Marriage

A Giant-Age marriage transfers more than a bride or groom:

- future claims upon children of either continuity;
- access to an ancestor's relics, manifestations, or Egg White tether;
- court engineers, physicians, priests, scribes, guards, servants, and captives;
- one culture's Idol Mask routes or Menhir correspondence;
- hostage obligations and sanctuary rights;
- rights to burial, reassembly, blood archives, and continuity machinery;
- a narrative explaining why the receiving dynasty always possessed what it has just acquired.

This creates genuine composite courts. Their pantheons are not necessarily fake because they were politically assembled. Real Menhir responses, Idol Masks, Giant ancestors, and relic tests can operate through an invented genealogy. The supernatural behavior validates only the bounded relationship that actually answers.

### Conquest Without Replacement

The default Giant conquest should be layered:

- the conqueror takes the imperial title;
- a local dynasty continues as client;
- a hostage heir grows up at the foreign court;
- local scribes retain the tax system;
- imperial priests rename local ancestors as cadet gods;
- one fortress, road, launch annex, or blood archive receives a direct governor;
- rebellion restores the old crown while leaving foreign law embedded in every record.

This is how the same city can be truthfully called Ishkatari, Ansharan, Kharadi, and independent in four sources dated only decades apart.

## The Late Flights and the Nythalum Exception

The late Giant flight should operate across the wider political ecology rather than through five single royal households. Titan-Firstborn fragments, God-Kin ancestors, cadet dynasties, hostage houses, court offices, and secondary kings all attempt different exits.

Possible Egg White outcomes remain:

1. **True Flight:** the original Titan under `titan-continuity`, or an uninterrupted Giant under `giant-continuity`, crosses and may become an anchor-owning Patron without changing creaturely kind.
2. **Folded Refuge:** the original survives in a bounded, failing, or dependent court.
3. **Counterfeit Survival:** only office, residue, copied memory, Mask, blood pattern, or reconstruction claims to be the ancestor.
4. **Catastrophic Failure:** the attempt scatters, imprisons, or dismembers the ancestor while creating dangerous anchors.
5. **Unknown Severance:** records prove an attempt but cannot establish whether the current speaker is ancestor, proxy, claimant, or fraud.

This produces an Egg White ecology of treaty houses, carcass thrones, shared marriage anchors, foreclosed courts, fragment keepers, counterfeit ancestors, wandering palace caravans, and human-souled descendants leasing power from the beings who once ruled their families.

### Nythalum Is Not Part of That Programme

Nythalum's defining difference is now a hard boundary:

> **Nythalum attempted physical escape from the planet to the physical Moon. It did not seek the Egg White, another realm, a translunar Astral current, or a concealed “Second Ascension.”**

- Launch-spires are physical cities and vehicles.
- The Moonbase is a physical installation on the World Egg's moon.
- Its Continuants persist through uninterrupted machine operation, not Astral anchoring.
- Reconstructions remain copies even on the Moon.
- Moon Harbors, receiving frames, supply colonies, orbital calculations, and return craft belong to material astronomy and engineering.
- A later individual Nythalum survivor could encounter the Egg White in the same way any sufficiently capable being might, but this must never be made the empire's secret goal or the explanation of Ascension.

The thematic result is stronger. Other Giant ancestors flee sideways into unfinished reality and become tenants. Nythalum tries to climb physically beyond the world that judges it, only to discover that distance does not end creaturely dependence. Both projects fail to escape Providence, but they fail in different directions.

## TES Concepts: Fit, Translation, and Refusal

The attached Elder Scrolls synthesis is most useful where it distinguishes identity, role, continuity, cultural expression, supernatural recognition, and the cost of becoming metaphysically definite. It is least useful where TES permits mortal divinity, cyclical worlds, authored reality, or uncertain afterlife routing that would contradict the World Egg's governing theology.

### Strong Fits

| **A political religious synthesis can be constructed and still receive real supernatural recognition** | an invented composite genealogy can create a functioning court because real ancestors, Menhir laws, Idol Masks, relics, and institutions accept bounded relations within it | Sarr-Vey, Kharad, conquest pantheons, marriage cults, and courts incorporating defeated gods |
| **An artifact can test a real metaphysical condition while the surrounding propaganda remains false** | a crown, throne, gate, weapon, or relic can recognize access, commission, continuity, or covenant without proving biological descent, holiness, or universal kingship | Meret's Empty Throne; Kharad's route rods; dynastic crowns that accept adopted or foreign claimants |
| **Nymic or true-name engineering** | Giant law can edit titles, access rights, bodily presentation, lineage claims, and office eligibility through true names; it cannot create the Image, forgive sin, or make a creature uncreated | ritual adoption, dynastic marriage, coerced renaming, court titles, identity surgery, and a claimant engineered to pass one imperial test |
| **Towers and tonal structures edit the local story or laws of reality** | Giant **Sovereign Works** impose bounded law through architecture, bodies, relics, names, calendars, and upkeep. They are local engines inside creation, never supports that mortals can use to rewrite Creation itself | Qianglong vault relays, Ishkatar river and pyramid estates, Xolotl's Blood Sun, Nythalum launch-spires, Ansharan processional courts, Orqad furnaces, and Ezhar's dam |
| **A divine heart or artificial substitute can power an impossible system** | an actual Titan organ remains part of one distributed living continuity; an artificial heart may imitate function without becoming the ancestor | Orqad's seven fragments, captive ancestor-engines, counterfeit royal hearts, and plots in which gathering fuel also reassembles a Titan-Firstborn |
| **Death is a stress test of identity** | bodily destruction reveals whether the claimant was a human soul, living Giant, distributed Titan, retained vampire, office, residue, Idol Mask, constructed copy, or an anchor-owning claimant whose underlying continuity still requires classification | every Giant funerary and continuity project; Urum's mortuary expertise; disputed escaped ancestors |
| **A realm and its ruler may be entangled without being identical in every case** | Menhir and Idol sovereignties already possess realm-scale continuity; a Titan body can become an anchor landscape; an ordinary Egg White demiplane remains made property unless a dedicated continuity says otherwise | carcass thrones, folded Ansharan courts, and Idol Thrones; do not universalize this to every Patron |

### Bounded Fits

| TES grammar | Safe use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Mantling** | use for Idol Mask or Regnant dynamics and for a Giant ruler converging with a royal office until subjects can no longer distinguish person from station | the office does not consume or manufacture a human soul; an ordinary Giant mantle does not create a god |
| **Several cultural gods as expressions of one underlying power** | use differently by origin: Menhir titles are readings of one continuous natural sovereign; Idol identities may be simultaneous active Masks; Giant ancestor names may be translation, propaganda, or office succession | never collapse Menhir, Idol, and Giant identity mechanisms into one universal aspect theory |
| **Composite god or oversoul traditions** | let chronicles merge several rulers, a captive ancestor, and an office into one legendary founder; a constructed pattern or Idol Mask may speak in the composite voice | no fusion of human souls and no mortal collective becoming the Lord or a true god |
| **Divine death without nonexistence** | use for Titans whose distributed continuity never ended and for uninterrupted Giant survival, including a Giant later acting as Patron | ordinary human death routes the soul; a remembered human ancestor is not made present by cult or residue |
| **The Prisoner as an underdetermined hero** | use as narrative grammar for enslaved humans whom Giant census, caste, prophecy, and office systems cannot completely classify. Their freedom is creaturely agency under Providence | do not create a player-character species outside causality, and do not imply that the Lord fails to know them |
| **Magnus-like withdrawal from entanglement** | use only as a structural analogy for Nythalum deciding that terrestrial dependence is intolerable and attempting material departure | the Moon is still inside Creation; physical distance does not escape the Lord, the soul order, mortality, upkeep, or moral responsibility |
| **Self-authorship becomes a cage** | a ruler collects contradictory titles, marriages, bodies, and myths to become universally legitimate, then loses the capacity to act outside them | this is accumulated constraint, not CHIM and not proof that belief creates reality without an anchor |

### Reject or Defer

| TES concept | Ruling for the World Egg |
|---|---|
| **CHIM as self-generated transcendence or reality-authoring** | reject as an objective route to godhood. It may exist as a Giant or Pride heresy whose practitioners mistake self-knowledge, power, or continuity for becoming uncreated |
| **Literal kalpas or replaceable worldskins** | do not import into Creation. Dynasties may call each regime a new world, and patrons may survive imperial collapses, but history is not thereby a recurring universe |
| **Dragon Breaks as a general solution to contradiction** | defer until Time has a controlling World Egg dossier. Do not explain the Year of Standing Stars, existing Weirds, or bad chronology through a TES-style temporal break |
| **Talos-like mortal apotheosis into true divinity** | reject. A human may become a saint by the Lord's grace, an Egg White Patron while alive, the face of a later Idol Mask, the subject of residue, or the bearer of an office. The human soul never becomes God, Menhir, Idol, or Giant |
| **Afterlife as the automatic cashing-out of patron, belief, race, or accumulated identity claims** | reject as final authority. Bonds can retain, intercept, obscure, or misdirect a human soul, but judgement and lawful routing remain the Lord's; Patrons and Idols possess no rightful terminal claim |
| **Universal mantling** | reject. Similarity, repeated title, worship, and successful imitation are evidence questions, not a setting-wide law that every sufficiently close performance becomes identity replacement |

## High-Value Story Seeds Produced by the Combined Model

1. **The treaty bride who founded three countries:** three archives record the same woman as captive, conqueror, and divine mother. Her human or Giant continuity remains open, but each account preserves a different real treaty right.
2. **The crown that accepts the wrong heir:** an ancient instrument recognizes a giant-blooded human whom every purity register excludes. It proves one bounded commission and detonates the official genealogy without declaring the claimant virtuous.
3. **The dowry that is a body:** a royal marriage transfers what both courts call a furnace relic. It is one piece of a Titan-Firstborn and has begun orienting toward six others.
4. **The two kings who must disagree:** descendants of Sarr-Vey seek to repair the Twin Diadem by installing one absolute ruler, which would destroy the very tension that makes it work.
5. **The berth still owed:** a Moon Harbor treaty promises physical passage to the descendants of a labor population. A returning Nythalum Continuant arrives to enforce the letter of the quota without recognizing later freedom, borders, or consent.
6. **The ancestor who remembers the wrong childhood:** an Egg White Patron knows every state secret but fails Urum's continuity tests. It may be an office reconstruction, Idol Mask, or later claimant rather than the founder it leases power as.
7. **The city with four truthful pasts:** Ishkatar, Anshara, Kharad, and a local king list all claim one ruin. Layered titles show that each ruled a different part of the city at the same time.
8. **The last Glassward gate:** liberation hymns call its ruler a saint, a traitor, a coward, and an anonymous porter. The present ruin preserves only a gate commission that opened lawfully once.
9. **The burial league's missing key:** without it, a Titan fragment, human mass grave, and royal reconstruction cannot be distinguished before an excavation proceeds.
10. **The moonward chart:** a Nine Bearings observatory proves Nythalum's destination was physical. Several modern cults and one Reconstruction need the chart destroyed because their authority depends on “Ascension” meaning divine escape.

## Promotion Order

If this bed is retained, promotion should proceed from least constraining to most constraining:

1. establish the **Five Imperial Horizons** sentence and the non-exhaustive political model;
2. approve only broad polity forms and relationship zones;
3. place traces, roads, archives, ruins, marriage customs, and disputed king lists;
4. select a small number of named kingdoms for full dossiers;
5. fix dynasties, rulers, borders, and exact chronology only when a story needs them;
6. assign Egg White escape outcomes last;
7. promote no Titan reconstitution, true ancestral Patron, or Idol succession without its own continuity dossier.

## Protected Boundaries and Reconciliation Notes

- **The Glass** predates Giant interest and remains unexplained. No Giant war, Sovereign Work, Nythalum launch, or TES-style time event creates it.
- **The Bird-Marked Sites** remain sealed for the future New World arc. This dossier may place nearby Giant politics but cannot identify the sites.
- **The Year of Standing Stars** cannot be explained through Dragon Break grammar, Giant calendars, Nythalum astronomy, or a failed Sovereign Work.
- **Anshara's final sovereign, capital, supreme escape, and complete collapse chronology** remain unresolved. Varesh's court and the Palace of the Last Audience are separate provincial sites.
- **The General Below** is an Abyssal wound, not a Giant ruler, dead general, collective soldier-soul, Titan-Firstborn, or escaped ancestor.
- **Pale-Skins and Hollow-Kings** are a separate prehuman stratum and must not be made an early Giant dynasty or product of Giant blood.
- **Nihil** and Bitter Crown Basin remain separate. Nihil's unidentified immediate Giant client should not be named incidentally by assigning a new polity nearby.
- Present-day maps do not establish Giant-Age borders.
- The Gazetteer's references to Giant lands beyond the Glass and Giant ruins in the Emerald Expanse should eventually be clarified as principal heartlands and great ruin fields, not claims that every horizon occupied one compact region.
- “Court of Courts” needs a later terminology ruling distinguishing Anshara as an imperial horizon, its sovereign household or moving court, and the still-unlocated capital or supreme audience complex.

## Validation

- The five imperial traditions remain the great powers of liberation memory.
- The Age of Chains now contains dozens of states without requiring dozens of simultaneous peer empires.
- Anshara, Qianglong, and Ishkatar can persist through multiple political phases while Xolotl and Nythalum rise later or differently.
- Marriage, conquest, fosterage, tribute, client rule, and cadet inheritance materially alter Giant blood and politics.
- Every birth has one continuity; no soul is diluted or manufactured.
- Direct Titan-Firstborn remain exceptional and require a bounded Menhir manifestation, an Idol Throne acting through a dependent Mask, or an independently classified Archmask.
- Smaller polities leave room for human heroes, collaborators, defectors, burial networks, and successor communities.
- Late Egg White flight belongs to many Giant courts and ancestors, not only five emperors.
- Nythalum remains a physical Moon and spaceflight civilization with no secret Astral Ascension.
- TES supplies identity grammar, local metaphysical engineering, and political-theological ambiguity without supplying the setting's theology.
- No mortal becomes the Lord, a Menhir, an Idol, or a Giant through fame or self-knowledge.
- No Weird is explained.
