> This review accounts for the principal reviewed-but-unselected nursery material after the narrative rebuild of [[The Historical Seed Garden]]. It changes no chronology, culture canon, protected Weird or post-1360 outcome.

## Disposition Rule

- `reactivate` creates a new story-facing Garden root because the lead contributes a distinct change, forward pull, human significance and mythic image.
- `graft` keeps the material as depth within a named existing root or research bed rather than pretending it is another story.
- `hold-trigger` preserves the lead and names the exact missing soil required before reconsideration.
- `protected-dormant` preserves material whose mystery, succession, first contact or future outcome must not be fixed by ordinary planting.
- `reject` removes a lead whose only function is duplicated lore. None is required in this review; several atlas precursors instead graft into their developed descendants.

“Review later” is not a disposition. Every hold below names the event, relationship, region, biography or proof it lacks.

## Atlas-Precursor Rule

The broad entries in [[Comparative Realms, Courts, and Sacred Warfare Atlas]] and [[Comparative Celestials and Heavenly Translation Atlas]] are source grammars for the reviewed family leads above. They inherit the disposition of their named descendant rather than becoming additional hidden reserves. This prevents a research citation from quietly multiplying into a second polity, Saint, angel or war.

## Outcome

- Five reserves become G-71 through G-75.
- Strong material is grafted into a named developed descendant when its independent story function is exhausted.
- Actionable holds now name their missing soil.
- Idol successions, erased persons, protected cosmological scars, remote first contact and Forward-Rule outcomes remain protected-dormant.
- No reserve is rejected merely for being uncomfortable, violent, morally uneven or temporally clustered.
