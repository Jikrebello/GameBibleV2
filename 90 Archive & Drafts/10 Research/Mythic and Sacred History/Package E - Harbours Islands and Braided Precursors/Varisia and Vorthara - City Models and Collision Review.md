# Varisia and Vorthara — City Models and Collision Review

> [!warning] Comparison boundary
> Pathfinder is a design comparison, not World Egg canon. PathfinderWiki served as an index; classic first-edition Paizo books control the comparison. This dossier does not alter Vorthara or claim that its Black Spire is Thassilonian, a prison or a Kaer Maga analogue in-world.

## Executive finding

Vorthara is recognisably in conversation with Kaer Maga: both are dangerous destinations built within or around ancient architecture, divided among local powers, sustained by tolerated illicit economies and only partially legible to surrounding states. The resemblance is useful at the level of **urban pressures**. Direct copying would weaken Vorthara because its best features are already distinct:

- the Black Spire’s original function is unknown;
- Atheria added roads, laboratories, quarantine forts and inspectors but never made the inner city;
- Vorthara has no common citizenship or citywide council;
- the Spire Truce protects ascent, shared water, external wards and foreign exclusion rather than governing ordinary life;
- Wardens of the Crown Road control logistical choke points without ruling wards;
- four permanent Shell anchors create interdependent industrial hazards;
- the Astral Conclave is temporary session law, not a parliament;
- Vortharan metaphysics distinguish tools, soul-prisons, Shades, patrons and Shell-force.

The classic 1E cities are most valuable as a **regional relationship system**: imperial Korvosa, dissenting Magnimar, criminal Riddleport and anomalous Kaer Maga occupy the same wider landscape but turn ruins, migration, law and trade into different civic identities.

## 1. Varisia as a regional design

Classic Varisia layers contemporary city-states over the remains of Thassilon. Shoanti and Varisian populations survive the ancient empire’s fall and later Chelaxian settlement; Korvosa, Magnimar and Riddleport are not natural successors but competing appropriations of land and ruins. Giant monuments and magical remnants keep deep history materially present.

The useful design principle is not “every city has a dungeon.” It is that each city answers the same inherited landscape differently:

| City | Foundational posture | Government | Economic identity | Relationship to ancient remains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Korvosa | colonial order and imperial inheritance | crown, bureaucracy and elite institutions | administration, trade, military and craft | disciplines and studies ruins within a lawful city |
| Magnimar | secession and civic ambition | lord-mayor and council, publicly republican | trade, monuments, migration and patronage | builds beside and beneath the Irespan’s shadow |
| Riddleport | haven consolidated under criminal power | overlord and competing crimelords | piracy, vice, smuggling and Cyphergate traffic | treats the Cyphergate as mystery, brand and danger |
| Kaer Maga | refuge inside an incomprehensible ring | ward autonomy and negotiated balances | destination trade, sanctuary, illicit services, passage | inhabits and repurposes the ancient structure itself |

**World Egg caution.** Package E already has older Arkenan and Saronikan cities. They must not be recast as empty colonial foundations simply to reproduce Varisia’s lineup. The transferable element is contrasting civic responses to inherited infrastructure and imperial pressure.

## 2. Kaer Maga in classic 1E

### Ancient ring and prison history

*City of Strangers* presents Kaer Maga as a massive ancient ring, older than Thassilon and not fully explained. Runelord Karzoug later used it as a prison. Earthfall ended that regime, and later populations repeatedly repurposed the structure. This produces a city whose current residents did not build the enclosing monument and whose known uses do not settle its first purpose.

The prison history matters because confinement leaves infrastructure, sealed populations, custodial routes and traditions of sanctuary. It does not make the modern city a functioning prison. Kaer Maga’s urban identity grows from generations choosing, or being forced, to live among inherited walls.

### Ward autonomy

Kaer Maga’s districts differ in population, custom, enforcement and built environment. Power is local: gangs, guilds, temples, families, commercial groups and stranger communities can govern within neighbourhoods without one civic law becoming effective everywhere. This is not the absence of rules. It is legal pluralism enforced by reputation, force, tolls, retaliation and negotiated boundaries.

The sharpest example for Vorthara is captivity. Paizo's [*City of Strangers* preview](https://paizo.com/blog/city-of-strangers) identifies the Bottoms with freed slaves pursuing abolition, while the [official book description](https://paizo.com/products/btpy8fda) presents Kaer Maga as an anarchic market where refugees and dangerous buyers coexist. The full 1E city treatment locates slave dealing in Downmarket. This is not a citywide consensus for or against slavery: neighbouring districts and factions can defend incompatible moral and economic orders. The portable structure is jurisdiction at neighbourhood scale, not the importation of the Freemen, the Bottoms, the Flesh Block or Kaer Maga's specific layout into Vorthara.

### Sanctuary and illicit destination trade

The city attracts exiles, prohibited practitioners, fugitives, traders and customers seeking goods or services restricted elsewhere. Its remoteness and dangerous reputation are economic assets only because routes, markets and protectors make arrival possible. “Anything can be bought” is promotional shorthand; every transaction still has suppliers, brokers, territorial protection and risk.

### Imported grain and logistical dependence

Kaer Maga’s rocky position and large population make imported food indispensable. River and caravan traffic therefore constrains the supposed freedom of ward powers. Whoever maintains approaches, water and bulk freight possesses structural leverage even without sovereign office.

### Undercity and incomprehensible architecture

The Duskwardens guide and protect routes through the Halflight Path beneath the city. The undercity creates employment, quarantine, delving and concealed movement while reminding readers that occupied space is smaller than inherited space. Unknown chambers remain dangerous because the city cannot simply inspect its own foundations.

### Faction law and Korvosan conflict

Kaer Maga’s resistance to external rule, including historic conflict and settlement with Korvosa, makes its autonomy relational. It survives not because no state wants it but because conquest, occupation and supply are expensive and because local powers can cooperate against a sufficiently threatening outsider.

## 3. Vorthara’s independent identity

### What Vorthara already does better

Vorthara’s city model is materially specific. It controls Crown Gate Pass and an ascent rather than a simple ring-road nexus. Shared water, warning bells, freight rules and external wards explain why rival factions tolerate Wardens. The Spire Truce defines catastrophic thresholds rather than pretending to legislate daily life. Shell anchors give the wards a dangerous common utility system. The Astral Conclave creates recurring temporary jurisdiction with proof, surety and exit obligations.

Its ordinary population—carriers, cooks, builders, laundresses, clerks, ward hands, miners, gardeners, healers, performers, servants, traders and families—also protects it from becoming a theme park of magical factions.

### Non-negotiable collision controls

| Vorthara canon | Comparison allowed | Comparison forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Black Spire is pre-Atherian and its original function unknown | ancient inhabited architecture generates uncertain rooms and competing interpretations | declare it a prison, Thassilon analogue, Giant engine or Kaer Maga ring |
| Atheria built roads, quarantine forts, laboratories and inspections | imperial overlay leaves procedures and infrastructure after imperial withdrawal | claim Atheria founded the city or fully governed its wards |
| no council, common citizenship or ordinary ruler | study ward autonomy and legal pluralism | add a secret ruling council to simplify exposition |
| Spire Truce protects shared survival systems | compare limited peace compacts | turn the Truce into comprehensive criminal law |
| Wardens control ascent, water stations, gates and warnings | compare Duskwarden route expertise and harbourmaster functions | give Wardens police sovereignty within wards |
| four Shell anchors are permanent interdependent utilities | compare ports dependent on cisterns, grain and shipyards | derive Shell anchors from mundane ancient machines |
| Astral Conclave has session-bounded rules | compare fairs, merchant courts and sacred truces | make the Conclave Vorthara’s government |
| human souls, Shades, tools and patrons remain distinct | compare factional taboo and professional liability | import Pathfinder undead or constructs without metaphysical translation |

## 4. Korvosa

Classic Korvosa is a former Chelaxian colonial centre whose monarchy, military, Acadamae, noble houses and civic bureaucracy promise order while producing hierarchy and corruption. Its identity depends on remembered connection to Cheliax and dominance over nearby territory. Shoanti displacement and conflict are not flavour; they are part of the city’s foundation and claim to land.

**Portable structures.** A colonial port treating imperial procedure as civilisation; elite magic tied to government and foreign prestige; a city whose legal clarity benefits some residents and makes others legible for taxation, policing or dispossession.

**Package E caution.** An Atherian-influenced port may inherit surveys, quarantine, weights or roads without becoming “World Egg Korvosa.” Existing local populations must remain participants, opponents and co-authors.

## 5. Magnimar

Magnimar was founded by people rejecting Korvosan rule. Its council and lord-mayor create a republican civic story, while patronage, corruption, district inequality and monuments complicate that story. The Irespan dominates the skyline and creates both wonder and shadow. Magnimar’s rivalry with Korvosa is ideological, commercial and regional rather than mere personal hatred.

**Portable structures.** Dissidents founding a rival port; public monuments producing civic identity; representative institutions captured through wealth and office; ancient ruins creating topographic class divisions.

**Package E caution.** Do not force an “old monarchy versus free republic” binary onto Arkenan and Saronikan cities if their histories do not support it. A dissident foundation must have migrants, land access, food and a relationship with existing inhabitants.

## 6. Riddleport

Riddleport grew at a natural cove beneath the Cyphergate, with pirates and criminal interests consolidated under an overlord and competing crimelords. Its harbour, vice economy, smuggling and ancient arch make it a destination. The city survives because criminal power becomes territorial administration: docks, protection, information, adjudication and violence must be organised enough for ships to return.

**Portable structures.** A pirate anchorage becoming a city when protection and market rules stabilise; criminal offices performing public functions; one inexplicable monument serving as navigational marker and civic brand.

**Package E caution.** Do not romanticise the port. Captivity, fencing, debt, prostitution, coercion and state complicity must remain visible. Tortuga and Mediterranean corsair comparisons in [[Corsair States Mediterranean Piracy and Tortuga]] provide the historical control.

## 7. Regional relationships worth carrying forward

The strongest classic-Varisia structure is four cities arguing over what legitimate urbanism means:

- order through inherited empire;
- liberty through civic secession;
- practical sovereignty through criminal protection;
- refuge through ward autonomy and shared survival compacts.

Package E can ask comparable questions of existing cities without duplicating the answers. A port can condemn Vorthara’s practices while buying its specialists; a city can claim old island legitimacy while depending on settler shipwrights; a commercial republic can use law to conceal oligarchy; a pirate market can exist because respectable merchants buy from it.

## 8. Vorthara research questions for later review

These are questions only. They do not authorise answers in ancient canon.

1. Which present wards or road institutions have demonstrably pre-Atherian predecessors, if any?
2. How did the continually inhabited refuge communities around the Black Spire obtain food, fuel and building materials through the Giant age, the Unbinding and later periods?
3. Which refuge customs or pass markets arose before Atherian quarantine, and which details are later civic myth?
4. Which institution first enforced a narrow predecessor of the Spire Truce, and what disaster made others accept it?
5. How did surrounding villages experience the city’s demand for carriers, fuel, food and bodies?
6. Which practices now described as “Vortharan” actually descend from distinct Arkenan, Saronikan, Atherian or refugee traditions?
7. What evidence would falsify a proposed ancient history of the Spire rather than merely decorate it?

## Final ruling

Kaer Maga remains the deepest external comparison for Vorthara, but only at the level of ancient inhabited structure, destination economy, ward power, sanctuary and logistical dependence. Vorthara’s pass, Black Spire, Atherian overlay, Spire Truce, Shell anchors, Conclave and metaphysical rules make it a different city. Future Package E work should strengthen those causal foundations rather than import Kaer Maga’s prison history or factions.
