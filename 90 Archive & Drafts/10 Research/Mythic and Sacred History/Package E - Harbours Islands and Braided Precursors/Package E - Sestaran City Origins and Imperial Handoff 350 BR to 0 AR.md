# Package E — Sestaran City Origins and Imperial Handoff, 350 BR to 0 AR

> [!warning] Author-only bridge, not the next period's history
> This records the **starting conditions and required transitions** for a later 350 BR–0 AR study. It does not promote Package E, assign new foundation years, move a border, establish an imperial province or write a new 350 BR–0 AR event. The present Concord is a much later defensive league, **not** the polity being formed in this interval. [[The Making of the Sestaran Cities]], [[Historical Spine of the Sestaran Concord]] and [[Chronology of the Known World (AR Dating)]] control the established sequence.

Canon identifies imperial acts at several future Concord sites but does **not** date each one relative to **0 AR**. The next study must decide which belong within 350 BR–0 AR and which require a later imperial phase; this handoff does not silently move them all before the Redeemer.

## The distinction that must survive the next pass

The seven present Concord members do **not** share one founding pattern. Package E's island and Vortharan histories supply pre-imperial depth to some places; they must not fill every later city's site with a completed ancient Concord institution. Conversely, an imperial foundation does not license describing its future site as empty of earlier human use without evidence.

These are **geographic and institutional relationships**, not an assertion that all seven cities existed under their later names and borders in 350 BR. Some place-names are used retrospectively so the reader can orient themselves.

## Sequence for the next 350 BR–0 AR study

1. **Starting frame, c. 350 BR.** Thalion has secured the imperial settlement at Caleran; its reach is uneven. The Elmaric heartland and important Sermoran-side polities are politically separate. Fiorenzia and Vardessa have their own pre-conquest urban histories. Vorthara is a refuge with a limited external arrangement, not an imperial school-city. Do not quietly attach Aversia and Orsena to this snapshot before dating their foundations.
2. **Expansion through unlike mechanisms.** For each place, identify whether Atheria conquers, negotiates an alliance, charters an older market, settles veterans, builds a military/customs station or places infrastructure outside an autonomous inner town. The canon fixes the **kind** of action for the six peninsula/island members above, but generally not its exact year, commander, original local counterpart or sequence relative to every neighbouring city.
3. **Provincial and municipal layering.** Imperial governors, assessments, roads, archives, military supply and star-cult ceremonies can make the places more mutually legible while preserving local councils, captains, shrine rights and unequal citizenship. Enslavement, requisition, land transfer and resistance belong in the causal account alongside infrastructure. The histories of [[Provinces and Municipal Life in Imperial Atheria]] and [[Historical Atlas of the Atherian Age of Glory]] provide the wider pattern; they do not supply missing local foundation dates.
4. **The boundary at 0 AR.** The empire is still politically intact when Isfir is executed and resurrected at Caleran. His ministry begins only near the end of this period. **The Redeemer's Church, the later old-Church city cultures and the Sestaran defensive compact are not already in place.** Imperial star religion remains the public establishment at the boundary. Conversion and the churches' local histories require their own post-0 treatment.

The broad imperial chronology has established reign blocks—Thalion **350–318 BR**, the Thalion house **318–247**, the Veloran house **246–151**, the Serrat line **151–61**, and the final pre-Redeemer houses through **0 AR**. These are **review bins**, not licences to insert Aversia's or Orsena's foundation into a convenient reign without evidence or authorial choice.

## Spatial reservations from Package E

- The [[Package E - Central Elmaric Circuit to 350 BR|Elmaric core circuit]], [[Package E - Sanctuary Island to 350 BR|independent sanctuary]], [[Package E - Drowned Predecessor Site to 350 BR|drowned predecessor]] and [[Package E - Black Reef at 350 BR|Black Reef]] are not blank future Sermoran properties. Atheria's later naval expansion must specify which harbours it takes, which pilots it employs, which island authorities survive and which sites remain outside it.
- The [[Package E - Sermoran Great-Island Zone to 350 BR|great-island zone]] must be able to produce later Sermora **without** requiring every inland dynasty or neighbouring island to become a republic at once. The modern capital and jurisdiction need a staged history after the pre-350 BR material.
- The [[Package E - Gateway-Facing Outer Approaches to 350 BR|Gateway-facing outer approaches]] already contain Caleran footholds, but they do not imply prior conquest of the island core. The [[Package E - Eastern Channels to 350 BR|eastern channels]] remain a separate contested theatre, with their own later imperial relationships.
- The Sestaran peninsula's **highland river city, Three Roads fair basin, Iron Foothills and eastern road-to-sea junction** are four different development settings. Package E's focus on islands and Vorthara must not accidentally collapse them into one “unsettled Sestaran hinterland.” Only the first two have established pre-Atherian cities; the latter two are places where imperial foundations will require a local-site history.
- Vorthara's inhabited Spire interior and exterior remain outside routine imperial ward government. Outer forts and inspectors should have a spatial relationship to the [[Package E - Vorthara Ascent and First Market to 350 BR|ascent and market]] and [[Package E - Vorthara Inhabited Spire and Foot to 350 BR|inhabited Spire]], not replace them.
- Detached Castara should receive a separate chronological and geographic bridge when relevant. Its later accession cannot be made the purpose of every pre-0 inner-sea development.

## Decisions still needed before a 350 BR–0 AR narrative

1. **Order, not invented precision:** which Sestaran city relationships change first under Thalion and his successors? Can any exact dates be supported, or should the narrative use broad phases?
2. **Fiorenzia:** what local houses, valley settlements and resistance survive conquest, and what is the mechanism of preserved municipal rule?
3. **Sermora:** which individual island harbours are conquered, allied or left independent; how does Atheria retain captains without immediately creating the later Admiralty?
4. **Vardessa:** what pre-imperial fair rules and communities does the charter formalise rather than invent?
5. **Aversia and Orsena:** who uses their future sites beforehand; what resource, route and military needs cause each distinct imperial foundation; and where do those events sit within the imperial reigns?
6. **Vorthara:** when do outer roads, quarantine works, laboratories and contracted specialist service emerge, and which arrangements remain uncompleted at 0 AR?
7. **Religion and identity:** how does imperial star worship coexist with older island, river, fair and Spire rites? Keep the Redeemer and post-0 conversion out of earlier stages.
8. **Castara:** decide whether any 350 BR–0 AR connection is needed at all; do not force a distant later seventh member into the peninsula's founding sequence.

These gates are for the **next** development package. The present document only prevents the Package E canon pass from erasing the space those histories require.
