# Package E — Eastern Channels to 350 BR

**Area.** These passages lead from the island heartland toward the older Arkenan–Saronikan port world and the Delkosi–Vashari-facing theatre. Saronika is a **later** kingdom and remains geographically western despite being at Thalmyria's eastern maritime edge in the present. Pre-350 BR harbours here are distinct cities and island communities, not provinces of that future state.

**Contested relationships.** Island pilots, mixed near-mainland households, Atherian-speaking settlers, Delkosi and Vashari claimants and other coast powers negotiate or fight over grain, ship service, forts and access. Caleran has thinner direct holdings here than on the Gateway-facing approaches: contracts, treaties and isolated stations are more characteristic, with exceptions possible. Older local communities are not erased by a foreign harbour claim.

**The 355 BR approach.** The moving city made an exceptional trek east of its ordinary central circuit to support Ardel Veyr. Its destruction and Ardel's defeat occurred in **one battle** in the eastern approaches of the **central Thalassian anchorages**. This relational placement does not identify an exact channel or turn the action into a distant second campaign. The [[Package E - Black Reef at 350 BR|Black Reef]] grounds along the eastward route.
