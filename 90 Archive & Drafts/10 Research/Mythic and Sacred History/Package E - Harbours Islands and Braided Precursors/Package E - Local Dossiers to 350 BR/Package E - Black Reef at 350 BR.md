# Package E — Black Reef at 350 BR

**What lies here.** The Black Reef is the grounded, burned **urban mass of the moving reefback city**, not the older drowned colonial capital. Its pearl-white living works were blackened when Istran Melvo's prize-ship stratagem delivered the first operational Atherian sea-fire in **355 BR**. The injured reefback separated and escaped; its fate remains uncertain.

**The battle.** [[The Wars of the Atherian Succession]] already records Melvo's defeat of **Ardel Veyr's main force among the central Thalassian anchorages**. The author has selected **one linked operation**: Ardel hired the independently governed moving city, Melvo burned that allied harbour, and his returning fleet broke Ardel's nearby force. The city and Ardel's fleet were allies, not the same polity. Ardel escaped east and was executed in 354 BR.

**By 350 BR.** The wreck is a grave, hazard and possible salvage site on or near the eastward route from those anchorages. Most city residents were killed or captured; smaller surface and deep diasporas survived. Later sightings of a “Black Bearer” cannot be treated as proof of a restored city.

**Open before promotion.** Chart the anchorage and reef separately, decide who can salvage or bury there, and distinguish documented events from survivor and imperial claims. Do not invent a second 355 BR battle. See [[Atlantis Azlant Absalom and the Drowned-City Problem|Decision 35]].
