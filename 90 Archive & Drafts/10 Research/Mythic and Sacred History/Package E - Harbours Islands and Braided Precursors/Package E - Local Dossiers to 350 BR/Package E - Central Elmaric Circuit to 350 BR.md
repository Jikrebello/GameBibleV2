# Package E — Central Elmaric Circuit to 350 BR

**Area, not jurisdiction.** The core route system links the [[Package E - Elmarai Isles to 350 BR|Elmarai Isles]], other specialised islands, the [[Package E - Sanctuary Island to 350 BR|sanctuary island]] and [[Package E - Shoal and Lagoon Islands to 350 BR|shoal communities]]. Related languages, marriages, fosterage, crews and shared markets make travel intelligible across boundaries; no common ruler commands the circuit.

**Material system.** Each island's water, crops, fish, timber access and landing conditions differ. Grain from outer islands and opposite shores, together with imported ship material, sustains denser harbours. The sanctuary's cargo measures and convoy court help rivals transact. Local captains also raid, ransom and seize people, so a safe route for one house can be a predatory corridor for another.

**The moving harbour.** The reefback city ordinarily travelled this central circuit during its later ages, selling refuge, exchange and eventually licensed violence. It undertook exceptional longer treks; its 355 BR destruction occurred **east of this ordinary circuit** near Ardel's central-anchorage campaign, not in an unrelated far-eastern war.

**Open before promotion.** Identify actual stages and sailing seasons. “Circuit” must not imply annual visits to every island, one polity, a literal circular geography or exact coordinates already chosen.
