# Package E — Gateway-Facing Outer Approaches to 350 BR

**Area.** These outer islands, channels and facing coasts connect the Elmaric world to the Gateway Republic, its Sarqeni successor and Caleran. They are **not** an Elmaric frontier owned by one state. Older islanders, mixed harbours, foreign settlers and dependent ports hold overlapping claims; one fort or customs quay does not control a whole island.

**Gateway Wars and aftermath.** During the wars of roughly **650–520 BR**, Elmaric captains served Caleran, the Gateway or their own ventures. Related households could be on opposite sides. After Qarzeth fell in 520 BR, Caleran selectively employed useful former opponents and pursued less protected crews it labelled pirates in waters where it had leverage. By 350 BR its direct island and harbour footholds are **strongest here**, while the independent core remains outside its rule.

**Why these positions matter.** Grain holdings, local cultivators, captive labour, opposite-shore farms, timber, repairs, pilotage and shelter make modest ports strategically valuable. Control can provoke war without requiring conquest of the entire archipelago.
