## Shared promotion controls

The **Elmaric** family is neither a single state nor a claim that every islander shares one ancestry. The original drowned capital, the mobile successor and the Black Reef are separate historical places or forms. The 355 BR destruction and Melvo's defeat of Ardel are one linked battle, but exact anchorage and reef coordinates remain unset. Vorthara has a recognisable death-working quarter and adjoining cult courts by 350 BR; its other working areas are spatial descriptions, not a final ward map. Do not import the later Spire Truce, four permanent Shell anchors, Astral Conclave, Concord, mature Atherian service schedules or present-day faction roster into these dossiers.

**Forward boundary:** [[Package E - Sestaran City Origins and Imperial Handoff 350 BR to 0 AR]] distinguishes older settlements from imperial foundations and reserves the separate future paths of all seven present Concord members. It is a planning bridge, not a continuation of these pre-350 BR dossiers.
