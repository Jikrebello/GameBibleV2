# Package E — Elmarai Isles to 350 BR

**Place and role.** The established **Elmarai Isles** are one central Elmaric heartland archipelago, not a synonym for all Elmaric peoples. Local Orphaned island survivors rebuilt water stores, terraces, fisheries and short crossings after the Giant collapse. Folk and halted Glass-descended arrivals entered particular communities; raiding, captive labour and coerced household incorporation also changed their populations. No single founding migration or ruler explains the chain.

**By 350 BR.** Separate harbours and island peoples share partly intelligible speech, fosterage, seasonal crews, pilgrimage and sanctuary ties, yet keep different laws and loyalties. Scarce grain and ship timber draw them into opposite-shore treaties, outer holdings and convoy disputes. Some crews raid as a profession; most island households are not thereby raiders. Civic councils, ship-owning associations and local leagues matter especially in the western, later Saronikan-facing world, but that later kingdom is not yet present. Caleran's footholds remain principally in an outer ring, not a conquest of this core.

**Open before promotion.** Identify the important individual isles and ports, their resources, which belong to the ordinary circuit of the moving city, and where later Saronikan geography meets older Elmarai institutions.
