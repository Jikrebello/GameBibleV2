# Package E — Sermoran Great-Island Zone to 350 BR

**Geographic relationship.** A large, varied island and neighbouring archipelago anchor the zone later associated with **Sermora**. That name locates this draft for the reader; the **Sermoran Republic does not exist by 350 BR**. Coastal ports, upland communities, pilot villages and inland dynasties are not one automatic polity.

**Population and power.** Locally rooted Elmaric peoples are the zone's most populous cultural family at the 350 BR boundary. Their dialects, cults, tenure and relations to the wider sanctuary network vary. Southern and Glass-descended settlers, captives and traders joined them through negotiated settlement and violence. Fortified settler quays and grain stores can command disproportionate wealth without making settlers the majority or founders of an empty land. Dynastic houses are especially prominent here; later republicanism is a different historical outcome.

**One consequential harbour.** [[G-58 - The Harbour Founded Twice]] records a predecessor whose settler landing and seizure of shore were later contested by an Elmaric-led recovery with returned exiles and defectors. It is **one harbour** later absorbed into a larger capital, not the whole island's origin. The neighbouring chain retains its own pilot communities and politics.

**Open before promotion.** Name and position major island polities, the neighbouring chain and the twice-founded harbour; determine which overseas grain dependencies and rival dynasties matter most.
