# Package E — Drowned Predecessor Site to 350 BR

**Ancient site.** A Nythalum-founded Giant–human colonial harbour displaced older human inhabitants and built living marine infrastructure. During the Great Unbinding its ruling dynasty's forced deep-migration order, revolt and failing biological foundations caused most of the capital to drown. The site is **not** the later Black Reef or Nythalum itself.

**Occupied margins.** Refugees reached high ground, salvage wharves and adjacent fishing places. A limited number transformed their existing bodies in living chambers; many others died or remained ordinary humans. Descendants of colonists, subordinated earlier inhabitants and workers continued near damaged systems without restoring the imperial polity. Dangerous submerged structures and local Deep Envoy relationships persisted, but neither gave one people total command over the ruin.

**Link to the moving city.** Around 1000–900 BR a Morlach follower of [[Thalassor]] arrived following a vision and worked with survivor knowledge and an injured reefback to establish a **new mobile harbour**. It did not raise the original city intact. The predecessor remained a separate, inhabited ruin through 350 BR.

**Open before promotion.** Name and map the original settlement, determine its surviving shore communities and decide what outsiders can reliably know of the dynasty's culpability. See [[Atlantis Azlant Absalom and the Drowned-City Problem]].
