# Package E — Vorthara Terraces and Workshops to 350 BR

**Material base.** Hidden refuge plots became ridge terraces, herding places and water-dependent gardens. They supplement but do **not** replace imported grain. Some native and introduced magical plants are cultivated as valuable reagents; the Black Spire's anomaly is not automatically their source. Growers, gatherers and traders have their own claims and risks.

**Built work.** Repeated need for carts, doors, vessels, tools, medicines, preservation supplies and repairs draws workshops toward usable water, freight routes and customers. Some crafts are ordinary and some dangerous. Their proximity creates bargaining and disputes over smoke, waste, bodies, access and payment, but not a planned industrial district or common guild charter by 350 BR.

**Later institutions kept later.** Present-day [[The Crucible]], [[The Ironbound Guild]] and [[The Crafters’ Union and The Elixirium]] can inherit problems of supply and space without being established here in their modern form. The four permanent Shell anchors and the Spire Truce cannot be inferred from pre-350 BR craft and gardens.

**Open before promotion.** Place usable terraces and water in relation to inhabited Spire access; select which workshop clusters merit a separate named article. See [[Package E - Comparative Synthesis and Canon Readiness Questions|Decisions 51–52 and 69–70]].
