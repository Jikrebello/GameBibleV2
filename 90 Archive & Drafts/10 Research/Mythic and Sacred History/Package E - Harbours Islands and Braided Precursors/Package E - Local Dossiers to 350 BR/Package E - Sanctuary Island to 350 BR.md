# Package E — Sanctuary Island to 350 BR

**Provisional author-only name: Ilamar.** This independent island is the shared Elmaric meeting ground, not the capital of a pan-island nation. It belongs to the central network connecting the Elmarai Isles, shoal communities and Seravon-facing harbours; its exact position remains uncharted. Its resident community and custodians maintain a **plural sacred precinct** where different traditions witness oaths, arrange protected conduct, meet kin and negotiate ransom. Thalassor's followers may be important, but do not own the sanctuary or supply every rite.

**Material reason for authority.** The island cannot feed all its visitors reliably. Its custodians measure grain, record convoy obligations, arbitrate cargo losses and proclaim safe conduct because rival ports need credible transactions. Their rulings depend on reputation and participants' cooperation; the court has no general taxing power or common navy. Grain dependence, donor pressure and armed captains limit its independence.

**People at the threshold.** A fugitive reaching the precinct is protected from immediate seizure, not automatically freed. Return, exchange and ransom are ordinary outcomes; a wealthy sponsor or valuable skill may secure a fuller hearing. Raider-captains may enter under conditions, with influential ones bargaining for wider access. These unequal practices are part of the sanctuary's history, not evidence of impartial justice.

**Open before promotion.** Approve or revise Ilamar's name, place the island more exactly if evidence allows, specify its water supply and principal shrines, and distinguish resident law from customs merely honoured by visitors.
