# Package E — Near-Mainland Harbour Islands to 350 BR

**Selected environment.** These islands sit close enough to a mainland that one household may hold an island landing, opposite-shore fields and relatives or graves on both sides of a channel. Island and shore communities resumed contact soon after the Giant collapse; later forts and colonial charters did **not** begin their history.

**By 350 BR.** Elmaric, local shore, Folk-linked and Glass-descended people have made different mixed settlements. A dynasty, foreign fort or harbour league may hold one quay without governing the island interior or facing coast. Grain, timber, fishing, customs and the right to pilot a narrow passage make control attractive. Siege or blockade can cut residents off from farms and kin still visible across the water. Both Gateway-facing and eastern-channel theatres contain cases of this type, but their politics differ.

**Open before promotion.** Choose which individual island–shore pairs are significant, who controls each landing, and where treaty farming became coercive holding. Do not assume a single Elmaric identity or legal status for all the channel islands. See [[Package E - Comparative Synthesis and Canon Readiness Questions|Decisions 12–13 and 25–26]].
