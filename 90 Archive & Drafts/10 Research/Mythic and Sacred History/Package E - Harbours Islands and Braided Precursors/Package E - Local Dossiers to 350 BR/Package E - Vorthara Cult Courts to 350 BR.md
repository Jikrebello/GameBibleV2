# Package E — Vorthara Cult Courts to 350 BR

**Formation.** Scattered refuge-house rites accumulated around familiar paths; later travellers, interpreters, witnesses and paying clients attracted small patron rooms and cult houses. By 350 BR this is a **distinct, adjoining quarter** beside the death-working streets, not a branch of the Pale Hand or a single temple administration.

**Visible plurality.** Menhir-associated courts, Idol houses, Egg White patron brokers, infernal claimants and human groups invoking angels can advertise their claimed allegiance openly. Individual houses conceal bargains, fraud or crimes even when their sign is public. They may provide powers, training, protection or merely a persuasive story. A public sign does **not** prove the patron exists as described or grants magic. Loyal angels cannot be hired, summoned or made to accept worship; Menhir, Idol and Egg White relationships have different metaphysics.

**Street politics.** Each house protects a doorway through followers, clients or hired force. Rivals can combine against a would-be monopolist without forming a cult parliament or permanent truce. The shared edge with corpse workers serves funerary clients and Qahuri patron rooms but remains contested. A cult does not gain authority over every neighbour by invoking an outside power.

**Open before promotion.** Choose a few consequential houses and their actual partners; map a modest cluster without making every present cult ancient. The later Astral Conclave and Redeemer interdict are outside this period. See [[Vorthara before 350 BR - Refuge Cults and the Dead]].
