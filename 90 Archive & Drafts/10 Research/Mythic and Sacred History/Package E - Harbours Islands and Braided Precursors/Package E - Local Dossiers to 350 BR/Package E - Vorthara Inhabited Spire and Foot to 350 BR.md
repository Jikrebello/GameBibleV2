# Package E — Vorthara Inhabited Spire and Foot to 350 BR

**Continuity.** Human fugitives sheltered **within or against** the pre-Atherian Black Spire while Giant powers avoided it. Their descendants and later arrivals occupied further accessible spaces, lower exterior fabric, the foot and ascent. Ridge hamlets supplied plots, herds and warning routes. By 350 BR these are parts of one substantial settlement in daily use, not a planned citadel founded by an emperor.

**Access and authority.** Public passages carry food, freight and clients into and alongside inhabited Spire space. Specialist households control side rooms and workshops; dangerous practitioners and gangs guard some deeper **occupied** thresholds. Passage is graded through custom and effective force, not three legal rings. A defended doorway does not confer jurisdiction over the whole Spire. Water and supplies create cooperation, but there is no common government.

**Mystery retained.** The Spire's original builders and function remain unknown. Its Giant-selective effect is bounded: it discouraged Giants and strongly active Giant inheritance, not ordinary humans or every strange practitioner. It does not explain all Vortharan magic or allow the entire interior to be declared explored. The later Spire Truce, Shell anchors and Astral Conclave are not present by inference.

**Open before promotion.** Map only the demonstrably inhabited accesses, working foot and ridge connections; keep hidden reaches unknown. See [[Package E - Comparative Synthesis and Canon Readiness Questions|Decisions 38–45 and 69–70]].
