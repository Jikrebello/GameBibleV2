# Package E — Vorthara Ascent and First Market to 350 BR

**Working area, not a formal ward.** Paths first used by fugitives and ridge households become the most public route into the inside-and-around Spire settlement. By 350 BR strangers find water, carriers, cheap lodging, food, repairs and brokers there. Imported grain is crucial despite nearby plots and herds. Open passage reflects repeated practical bargains, not ownership by a city council or a guarantee of safety.

**Threshold politics.** A traveller can buy a service at one frontage and cross into a different household's protection or coercive power on the next passage. People fleeing outside owners may pass through the market, but those owners have no automatic right of recovery inside; local captive dealers pose a separate danger. Temporary pressure to keep food and cargo moving can constrain fights without creating general police or emancipation law.

**At the 350 BR edge.** The emerging Atherian state publicly calls Vorthara a small lawful market and watches surrounding routes. A temporary local gathering negotiates limited external terms while interior jurisdictions persist. Present-day [[Wardens of the Crown Road]] and their defined duties are **not** automatically ancient offices.

**Open before promotion.** Place gates, water stations, safe approaches and market thresholds; decide who actually maintains them in this period. See [[Vorthara before 350 BR - Refuge Cults and the Dead]].
