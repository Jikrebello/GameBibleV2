# Package E — Vorthara Refuge and Captive-Trade Fault Line to 350 BR

> [!note] Cross-neighbourhood issue, not a named district
> This conflict cuts through the arrival routes, lodgings, markets and guarded thresholds of several Vortharan areas. Do not draw one free ward opposite one slave ward simply because both interests are present.

**Two attractions.** Descendants of people who escaped Giant slavery and later fugitives maintain the town's reputation for disappearance and shelter. Buyers, sellers and coercive brokers are also drawn to a substantial market without a citywide ban on captive labour. An outside owner's old claim has no general force inside Vorthara; that does **not** prevent a new local seizure or guarantee any fugitive's protection.

**By 350 BR.** Refuge households and captive-trade interests rescue, buy out, kidnap, recapture and retaliate against one another. A family's safety can change at the next doorway. Other factions, gangs and traders sometimes intervene when fighting blocks grain, freight or customers. Their limited business agreement is neither abolition, a general peace nor the later Spire Truce. This is potentially the town's most serious internal flashpoint, but no single climactic crisis is selected yet.

**Open before promotion.** Decide whether one large pre-350 BR confrontation occurred, and trace its actual people and thresholds without making either side uniform or romanticising the trade.
