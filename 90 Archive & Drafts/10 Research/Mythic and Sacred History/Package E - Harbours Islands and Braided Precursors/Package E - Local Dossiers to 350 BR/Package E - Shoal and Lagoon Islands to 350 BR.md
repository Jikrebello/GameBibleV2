# Package E — Shoal and Lagoon Islands to 350 BR

**Selected environment.** Low islands, shoals and tidal channels form one of the three core Elmaric homeland environments. Fishing and difficult-water pilotage give these communities a livelihood different from the rocky open-water isles. They are inhabited homelands, not merely obstacles on a foreign chart.

**By 350 BR.** Knowledge of channels, seasons and safe anchorages gives pilot households leverage over grain convoys, raiders and war fleets. It does not make every pilot neutral: kinship, debt, captive claims and patronage can divide neighbours, and a captain may seek to monopolise passage by holding a household or landing. Fisheries and small cultivation sustain part of the population, but grain and ship materials still enter from elsewhere. Low land also makes wells, storage and storms political problems.

**Open before promotion.** Select the main inhabited shoals, the seasonal passages they command and their distinct local governments. Do not turn all pilots into one hereditary guild, make every island a pirate base or assign exact tides without a map.
