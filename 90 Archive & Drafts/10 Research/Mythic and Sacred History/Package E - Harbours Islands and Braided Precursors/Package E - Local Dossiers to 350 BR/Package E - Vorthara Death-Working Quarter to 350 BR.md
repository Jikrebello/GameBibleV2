# Package E — Vorthara Death-Working Quarter to 350 BR

**Origin and work.** Several Qahuri mortuary migrations brought preservation, funerary contracts, estate instruments and disputed death-working to Vorthara. Local practitioners adapted these arts to steep freight, dangerous disposal and paid body care; smaller Folk and other Orphaned traditions joined without becoming Qahuri. No single Qahuri colony or exact arrival year is established.

**By 350 BR.** The **Pale Hand** leads a recognisable quarter of mortuaries, guarded workshops, client rooms and corpse-labour routes. It recruits skilled outsiders and hires independents. Most animated porters are **soulless bodies**, not trapped people. The Hand publicly prohibits human soul-binding, while a concealed circle or protected clients practise it. A stolen corpse, a witnessed postmortem pledge and a bound soul are different wrongs and claims; they should not be flattened into “undead labour.”

**Neighbours and limits.** The adjacent [[Package E - Vorthara Cult Courts to 350 BR|cult courts]] share clients, witnesses and disputed entrances with the Hand without falling under its rule. The faction's lead in death craft is not citywide government. Individual ancient vampires may seek its services but are not ordinary zombies or proof of a vampire ward.

**Open before promotion.** Determine the quarter's ancient entrances, body procurement, burial rights and when the Pale Hand took its name. Do not backdate every present-day technique. See [[Vorthara before 350 BR - Refuge Cults and the Dead]].
