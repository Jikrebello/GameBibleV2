# Package E — Pre-Canon Editorial Rulings

> [!warning] Selected review policy, not canon promotion
> These are the author's pre-promotion choices for turning [[Package E - First Historical Draft to 350 BR]] and [[Package E - Local Place Dossiers to 350 BR]] into encyclopedia articles. **The four new place-names below are provisional author-only names**, not yet public canon or final map labels. The history still stops at **350 BR**. Existing chronology and geography control any later promotion.

## 1. Name the essential places; keep the rest relational

**Selected:** give the central narrative's indispensable places distinct names and fix their broad relationships now. Individual minor islands, precise coastline, coordinates and the full route chart can wait for a dedicated geographic pass. A name below cannot silently turn an island into a state, identify two separate ruins as one place or backdate a modern polity.

| Provisional working name | Place or form | Relative placement and control |
|---|---|---|
| **Seravon** | The populous multi-polity great island in the zone that later produces Sermora. | East of the western Thalmyric/Saronikan maritime region; in the central Sestaran island zone; **west of its neighbouring archipelago** and west or north-west of most Eclessian territory below. Sermora's later capital and jurisdiction do not cover all ancient Seravon by anticipation. |
| **Ilamar** | The independent plural sanctuary-market island. | Within the central Elmaric route network that connects the Elmarai Isles, shoal-and-lagoon communities and Seravon-facing harbours. It is **not** identified with Seravon, the original drowned capital or a later imperial sanctuary. Its precise side of any one island or channel remains uncharted. |
| **Caelovar** | The Nythalum-founded Giant–human colonial predecessor city that drowned during the Unbinding. | Its ruined core and inhabited margins remain in the **central island world**, distinct from Ilamar and the later Black Reef. This is a working site-name; whether it was the ancient dynasty's own name or a later remembered name is undecided. |
| **Marelith** | The later **mobile harbour-city** built around the altered reefback and survivor communities. | Its ordinary range is a central island circuit connecting some of the above communities; it is **not** Caelovar raised intact. Its 355 BR campaign carries it east of that ordinary circuit toward Ardel's central Thalassian anchorages. This is a working common name, not yet a documented constitutional self-name. |
| **Black Reef** | The grounded burned urban mass left by Marelith's destruction in 355 BR. | On or near the **eastward route from the central anchorages** after Melvo's one linked battle against the city and Ardel. It is separate from Caelovar's original drowned site. Retain “Black Reef” as a descriptive common name; no additional formal place-name is needed now. |
| **Elmarai Isles** | Already established name for one western/central Elmaric heartland archipelago. | Keep the existing name. It is neither all Elmaric islands nor the whole future Saronikan kingdom. Its individual isles and precise relationship to Ilamar remain for later cartography. |

**Relative route sequence only:** Elmarai and the other central heartland islands connect through the shared circuit to Ilamar and Seravon-facing harbours; Marelith ordinarily works this network. On its exceptional final trek it travels **eastward from its normal circuit** toward Ardel's central anchorages, and the Black Reef remains along that eastward route. This is **not** a fixed itinerary, proof of annual visits to every island or a coordinate map. The position of Caelovar within the central island world is intentionally less precise than its separateness from Ilamar and the Black Reef.

The working names were checked against current vault usage before selection. They require a final linguistic, alias and map pass when promoted; until then, older descriptive dossier filenames remain stable.

## 2. Public history, attributed tradition and protected authorial truth

**Selected:** the public encyclopedia states well-supported and externally knowable history in an ordinary reference voice, attributes disputed traditions at the point of use, and keeps genuinely hidden or metaphysically protected facts in author-only controls. It must not turn every authorial explanation into something a 1360 AR reader could discover. The [[World Egg Encyclopedia - Editorial Style Guide]] governs article leads, provenance and uncertainty.

| Subject | Reader-facing treatment at promotion | Retain in author-only control unless separately justified |
|---|---|---|
| **Elmaric formation and captivity** | Explain the plurality of island peoples, old local roots, settlement, maritime livelihoods and the coercion involved in captivity and household incorporation. Do not euphemise victims into merely “foreign brides.” | Unproved individual genealogies, invented demographic counts and claims that every island followed one marriage or captive rule. |
| **Caelovar's fall** | Establish that an old colonial city drowned and that occupied margins survived. Attribute rival accounts of royal culpability, revolt and supernatural judgement where the evidence is contested. | The complete internal chain of dynastic override, control-graft failure and selected transformation access as universally known public fact. Preserve the author's selected causal account in the control record. |
| **Marelith and its founder** | Describe the mobile city's existence, changing governments, civic inequality, raiding and the remembered Thalassorian covenant. Report the founder's vision and supposed fate as traditions with identifiable tellers. | A final verdict on the founder's fate: it is permanently unresolved even authorially. Do not make Thalassor morally approve child tribute or treat every Deep Envoy as a child-taker. |
| **355 BR battle and sea-fire** | Explain the one linked Melvo–Ardel battle and Marelith's destruction where the historical evidence supports it. Distinguish survivor, Atherian and later sailor accounts of the stratagem and Black Reef. | The formula, production chain, tactical technicalities and precise surviving stores of restricted sea-fire. Its **355 BR** first operational use is authorial control; how much later historians know requires an evidence check. |
| **Vorthara's dead and cults** | Describe visible soulless corpse labour, Qahuri-rooted craft, early Pale Hand leadership, an adjoining plural cult quarter and disputes over bodies and patron claims. | The identity and acts of the Hand's concealed soul-binding circle or protected clients unless an article is explicitly author-only. Allegations may appear publicly only when attributed and not presented as proof. |
| **The Black Spire** | State the pre-Atherian structure, long refuge history and observed selective danger to strongly active Giant inheritance in appropriately bounded language. | Its original builders, function or a universal spell-amplifying explanation: none has been established. |

**Application test:** for every new public claim, ask *who in the world could know this, by what evidence, and whether the article has represented uncertainty honestly*. A hidden truth may guide prose without being disclosed. Conversely, visibility is not a licence to erase documented enslavement, child-taking, massacre or other harms from readable history.

## 3. Vorthara has precincts, not a finished ward map

**Selected:** by 350 BR Vorthara contains recognisable working areas and locally defended thresholds, **not** a set of formally named, fixed-boundary wards inherited unchanged by the present. The [[Package E - Vorthara Death-Working Quarter to 350 BR|Pale Hand's death-working quarter]] and the [[Package E - Vorthara Cult Courts to 350 BR|adjoining cult courts]] are distinguishable; the [[Package E - Vorthara Ascent and First Market to 350 BR|ascent market]], [[Package E - Vorthara Inhabited Spire and Foot to 350 BR|inhabited Spire/foot]] and [[Package E - Vorthara Terraces and Workshops to 350 BR|terraces/workshops]] describe functions that can overlap. The [[Package E - Vorthara Refuge and Captive-Trade Fault Line to 350 BR|refuge–captive-trade conflict]] crosses several areas and is **not** a discrete district.

Recurring rescues, seizures, reprisals and limited intervention when trade is obstructed are selected. **No singular decisive pre-350 BR uprising, massacre or citywide settlement is established.** This is a restraint on the current account, not a metaphysical claim that no such local event could ever be developed. No early ward map may backdate the Spire Truce, four Shell anchors, present school roster, Astral Conclave, common police or Concord seal.
