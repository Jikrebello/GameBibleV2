# Historical Seed Garden — Mythic Voice, Variety, and Localisation Review

> [!warning] Authorial control ledger
> This ledger carries the technical machinery deliberately removed from [[The Historical Seed Garden]]. It is not public mythology and promotes nothing into chronology or canon.

## Controlling Story Ruling

The supplied storytelling resource is used as a diagnostic framework rather than a plot formula:

1. a story is a connected sequence in which something changes;
2. momentum is the reason to want the next beat, not speed or violence;
3. significance is the reason to care, not the objective size of the stakes;
4. conflict, goals, transformation, surprise and mystery are optional techniques serving those functions.

Every legacy root therefore receives a connected beat chain, a before-and-after state, one principal momentum engine and one principal significance anchor. A root may keep a steadfast protagonist, reveal a foreknown tragedy or concern a small domestic loss. It may not remain a static bundle of evidence.

## Disposition and ID Rules

- `G-01` through `G-70` are permanent legacy IDs.
- A renamed root keeps its ID and old title as an alias.
- A merged or retired ID remains recorded here and is never reused.
- Reactivated reserve material begins at `G-71`.
- No legacy root is deferred. Final legacy-audit totals are sixty-eight `recast` and two `retain-and-revoice`; zero relocate, merge, retire or reject. None of the seventy had to be removed or merged. A separate reserve review subsequently added G-71 through G-75 without changing those legacy dispositions.

## Control Fields

The tables compress the full control card. Existing source and hardening dossiers remain authoritative for received source, exact violence, continuity, commission and discovery chains.

- **Beats** gives three to six connected actions.
- **Change** states the before-and-after movement.
- **Momentum** names the principal forward pull.
- **Significance** names why the outcome matters.
- **Remainder** is the story-facing primary carrier.
- **Readiness** is `trace-ready`, `needs-regional-anchor`, `needs-event-development` or `protected-reserve`.
