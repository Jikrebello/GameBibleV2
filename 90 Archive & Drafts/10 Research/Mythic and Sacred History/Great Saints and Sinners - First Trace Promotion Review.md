## Promotion Ruling

The earth-first review in [[Great Saints and Sinners - Chronological Placement Review]] passes all fourteen visible exemplars: seven retained and seven revised with their corrections incorporated. This pass promotes exactly one light trace from each into [[The Historical Seed Garden]].

Promotion means that the trace becomes an approved exploratory root in the Garden. It does not promote the provisional person, title, locality, lifespan, soul-state, miracle, office, cult, order, rivalry, campaign or complete causal sequence. The Garden remains a nursery rather than canon.

[[Great Saints and Sinners - Primarch Source-Fidelity Hardening Review]] subsequently restores nine softened source wounds. The Garden may name the resulting historical pressure directly without thereby authenticating the provisional person's identity, sainthood, damnation or complete biography.

ER-01 and ER-02 receive no trace. Their absence mechanisms remain separate research material.

## Trace Rulings

## GS-01 — Red Door Names

- **Promote:** one unhinged red-painted prison door and a custom of reading abandoned people, rescued people and victims murdered by the escape network itself.
- **Withhold:** original prison, porter, martyrdom, riot, apparition, cult lineage and relic authenticity.
- **Independent value:** the reading preserves institutional failure and service together even if the door came from another prison or no Saint ever manifested.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Three-Lock Prison Road, Saint of the Two Ferries or a Companion relic.
- **Severity sync:** the custom must not omit the network's captive and innocent victims merely to preserve a spotless martyr memory.

## GS-02 — Hospital Fragment of the Second Gospel

- **Promote:** incompatible Second-Gospel texts circulate; one fragment survives in charitable hospital use among people unaware of its hostile textual family.
- **Withhold:** preacher, original recension, counter-church, responsive powers, sacrifices, office and damnation.
- **Independent value:** a compromised source can preserve a genuinely useful instruction without making the source or current users guilty by contamination.
- **Collision boundary:** not Mujalib's revelation, Aquilan's Epistles or a lost canonical Gospel.
- **Severity sync:** innocent reuse does not soften the source text's connection to captive sacrifice, child possession trials and ordered murder.

## GS-03 — Empty Place at the Route Hearing

- **Promote:** a later guide fellowship leaves one place empty for an innocent scout killed by the person it claims as founder.
- **Withhold:** Hunter identity, execution case, route manuals, fellowship descent and later manifestation.
- **Independent value:** the custom restrains secret judgement even if founder attribution is propaganda.
- **Collision boundary:** no inheritance from the Long Watch or universal route jurisdiction.
- **Severity sync:** the alleged founder personally killed the innocent scout; “founder error” may not replace murder.

## GS-04 — Sealed Ninth Chambers

- **Promote:** otherwise unrelated fortresses contain sealed ninth rooms omitted from public plans; engineers and Sinner cults disagree about opening them.
- **Withhold:** original bastion, Mason, office, linked-fortress project, casualty mechanism and current command-body.
- **Independent value:** each room can be ordinary concealed atrocity, copied military superstition, fraud or genuine office geometry.
- **Collision boundary:** no room proves one universal infernal fortress network.
- **Severity sync:** the chambers' pressure includes workers killed by lot as collective punishment before people were sealed into the construction.

## GS-05 — Broken Gauntlets and Restitution Charter

- **Promote:** rival cities display incompatible broken gauntlets while an independently transmitted charter records restitution to injured workers.
- **Withhold:** Smith identity, authentic gauntlets, workshop, order, sainthood and correction manifestation.
- **Independent value:** material restitution remains historically meaningful even if every relic is false.
- **Collision boundary:** not Saintmaker's Forge, Iron Martyrs or the source of all adaptive craft.
- **Severity sync:** the charter answers a test the founder personally refused to stop after the apprentice begged release.

## GS-06 — Broken Chain and Missing Link

- **Promote:** abolitionists wear open chain links; recurrence cults seek a joining link they claim can close the chain.
- **Withhold:** Sarrat, healer, engine, revolt, massacre, office, recurrence conditions and cult success.
- **Independent value:** the open chain remains a valid emancipation symbol even if the cult story is invented.
- **Collision boundary:** no token is a Wrath relic or fragment of a Sinner's soul.

## GS-07 — The Clerk's Ending

- **Promote:** courts ask whether an emergency order contains “the Clerk's ending,” meaning a fixed point at which coercive powers must justify themselves again.
- **Withhold:** Clerk identity, hundred-wells district, original register, sainthood, seal and manifestation.
- **Independent value:** the phrase carries a workable legal safeguard without requiring holy authorship.
- **Collision boundary:** not founded by the Four Who Brought Water and not universal Covenant law.
- **Severity sync:** the expiry doctrine answers armed seizures, enforcement deaths and an execution authorised through the emergency code.

## GS-08 — Bitter Cordial

- **Promote:** a bitter frontier cordial remains medically useful in bounded doses and is also abused in coercive dependency rites.
- **Withhold:** Reaper identity, estate, exposure regime, outbreak, compact, clinics and damnation.
- **Independent value:** neither therapeutic usefulness nor cult abuse settles the medicine's origin or moral classification.
- **Collision boundary:** does not cause the Grey Sweat, make illness sinful or invalidate material medicine.

## GS-09 — Unopened Warnings

- **Promote:** several archives preserve sealed warnings that may complete a hostile route if opened under the wrong conditions.
- **Withhold:** Reader identity, maintenance college, first breach, warning office, copies and current destination.
- **Independent value:** archives must solve a real evidence problem whether the warnings are supernatural mechanisms, dangerous instructions or elaborate frauds.
- **Collision boundary:** not the Fifth Translation, Open Cinder archive or universal rule for written messages.
- **Severity sync:** the first breach used students as receivers after a concealed injury and withdrawn consent; “dangerous scholarship” may not replace those victims.

## GS-10 — Duty-Marked Refuge Tools

- **Promote:** scattered tools bear duty marks associated with evacuation, hospice work, dismantling weapons or proving workmanship; no authoritative set or count exists.
- **Withhold:** Smith identity, original workshop, heat weapon, tool authenticity, sainthood and closure commission.
- **Independent value:** communities inherit practical obligations rather than a collection quest.
- **Collision boundary:** assembling tools cannot summon their maker, kindle White Flame or manufacture a Saint.
- **Severity sync:** the duty tradition responds to a maker who personally authorised and triggered the second civilian-killing discharge, not only to an innocent inventor's stolen design.

## GS-11 — Portraits Beneath the Victors

- **Promote:** restoration of victory portraits reveals wounds, mixed marriages and displaced households painted out of the official generation; several share one medium-and-contract signature.
- **Withhold:** General identity, army, sexual abuse of office, personal murder, Glass Court, Patron and perfected bodies.
- **Independent value:** coordinated historical revision can be proved without concluding that every portrait belongs to one supernatural operation.
- **Collision boundary:** no Glass signature proves Pride succession or membership in the Six at the Cinder Inn.
- **Severity sync:** the concealed death is the General's personal murder of the beloved critic, followed by a fabricated field record.

## GS-12 — Repaired Measuring Cord

- **Promote:** several shrines claim hands or hand-bones from a vanished defender, while the most credible associated object is an ordinary measuring cord repaired by refugee children.
- **Withhold:** Warden identity, fort, sealed ward, death place, remains, sainthood and later defence.
- **Independent value:** the cord preserves refugee maintenance and evacuation craft without needing bodily relic authentication.
- **Collision boundary:** not Caedros I, the Returning Standard or proof that the Reconquest was uniformly holy.

## GS-13 — The Future Fact

- **Promote:** public law prohibits conviction for “the future fact,” while illicit magistrates and private buyers still trade in Black-Eaves forecasts.
- **Withhold:** Judge identity, town, visions, torture regime, crown, office, assassination and damnation.
- **Independent value:** the prohibition remains just whether forecasts are supernatural, probabilistic intelligence or fraud.
- **Collision boundary:** no forecast makes a possible act guilty or explains the Great Split.

## GS-14 — The Feast and the Reading

- **Promote:** a public liberation feast is followed by an unofficial reading of prisoners and altered survivors omitted from the heroic account.
- **Withhold:** liberator identity, revolt, experiment, killings, confession, exile, sainthood and later commissions.
- **Independent value:** communities can celebrate liberation while preserving crimes committed by liberators; neither observance cancels the other.
- **Collision boundary:** not Ansgar, a Reconquest feast or the charter of a universal breachward order.

## Withheld Erasures

- **ER-01:** no recut monument, unnamed exemption or redistributed practice enters the Garden. Promotion would prematurely make the institutional erasure a public signature.
- **ER-02:** no failed name-space, claimant or damaged portrait enters the Garden. Promotion would allow the mystery to dominate other identity anomalies before its attacker and network boundaries are better tested.
