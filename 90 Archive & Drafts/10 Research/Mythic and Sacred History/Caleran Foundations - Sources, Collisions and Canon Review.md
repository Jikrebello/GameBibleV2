# Caleran Foundations — Sources, Collisions and Canon Review

## Scope and authority

The [[90 Archive & Drafts/10 Research/Mythic and Sacred History/Caleran Foundations - Giants, Inland Peoples and Royal Beginnings.md|historical account]] develops a proposed regional foundation for Caleran. This review supplies the source distinctions, identity rulings and future integration destinations behind its prose. Neither document promotes material into canon.

The delivery contains two new research documents and an addition to the research MOC. It does not revise the canonical rollout programme, chronology, culture histories, Garden, campaigns or application. The current encyclopedia remains the controlling published account until a separate integration decision.

Three kinds of statement require different handling:

| Kind | Examples here | Treatment at this review |
|---|---|---|
| Existing canon | Aurin geography; Orphaned survival; gradual Atherian formation; twelve-city Compact before republic; Thalion around 350 BR | Preserve and use as constraints. |
| Proposed authorial history | Arsuvan; the three peoples; Ravessa's rescue and limited scheme; Avren's murder; successive households; Common Sky settlement | Submitted for approval as events and relationships; presently exploratory. |
| Proposed received tradition | The complete household telling; hostile and royal variants; bowls and wolf images | Approve the existence and bearers of a tradition separately from its literal claims. |

The narrative's descriptions of Sarven's terraces and other remains are proposed World Egg evidence, not real archaeological discoveries or newly established campaign locations. No inference from real-world archaeology proves a fictional event.

## Comparative chronology and its limits

The real-world sources describe interacting histories over many centuries. They support a layered foundation rather than a single migration in which Bronze Age Greeks arrive, become Etruscans and immediately found Rome.

| Real-world layer | Useful temporal distinction | World Egg application |
|---|---|---|
| Mycenaean civilisation, approximately 1600–1100 BCE | Palatial production and exchange precede the main Archaic colonial movement. The destruction of palaces around the end of the Bronze Age has several regional histories. | Large households, workshops and vulnerable exchange networks help frame different portions of Arsuvan and heroic-maritime Atheria. Their causes of collapse remain World Egg events. [Met, Mycenaean civilisation](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/mycenaean-civilization). |
| Greek overseas settlement, especially eighth–sixth centuries BCE | Colonies, trade and movement belong to a later world of distinct cities and established neighbours. | Separate maritime households establish footholds in occupied country; exchange, coercion and local alliances produce varied settlements. [Met, Greek colonisation and trade](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/ancient-greek-colonization-and-trade-and-their-influence-on-greek-art). |
| Etruscan societies of first-millennium BCE Italy | Rich urban, religious and craft traditions coexist with Greek and other Mediterranean connections. | Serathi hill cities provide the strongest comparison, while the Enathi and Vardeni have their own overlapping economies and institutions. [British Museum, Etruscan world](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/galleries/etruscan-world). |
| Rome's kings and foundation traditions | Ancient literary accounts are later tellings of a remote past. Their dates and episodes require different treatment from excavated settlement evidence. | The wolf narrative has an explicit proposed authorial account and a separately written received telling. [Livy, Book I](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/19725/19725-h/19725-h.htm). |
| Roman republican expansion | Unequal access to land, political standing and the profits of conquest contributes to civic conflict and military patronage. | These pressures develop through Atheria's already established Compact-to-republic sequence. [Met, Roman Republic](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-roman-republic). |

No BCE date is converted mechanically into BR. Modern Thalmyric regional diversity is also not explained as Greeks biologically becoming Slavs. Ancient upland settlement is one inheritance among later imperial, religious, migratory and successor developments, scheduled for subsequent packages.

## Source bibliography and borrowed contributions

Research checked 3 September 2026. Museum interpretations, archaeological objects, ancient literary narratives and fictional in-world books are distinguished below. No modern historical reference is represented as the source of our invented names or events.

### Archaeology and historical synthesis

1. **Department of Greek and Roman Art, Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Mycenaean Civilization.”** Palace centres, craft organisation, exchange and their archaeological survival supply a material comparison. This does not establish an Arsuvan architectural plan or a single historical explanation for every Bronze Age collapse. [Article](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/mycenaean-civilization).

2. **Department of Greek and Roman Art, Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Ancient Greek Colonization and Trade and their Influence on Greek Art.”** Used for movement between distinct communities and the circulation of objects, techniques and styles. It informs the maritime layer after the earlier palace comparison. [Article](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/ancient-greek-colonization-and-trade-and-their-influence-on-greek-art).

3. **British Museum, “Etruscan world.”** Used for religious learning, metalwork, music, banquets and the prominence of some women in the surviving material. Elite tomb evidence does not describe every household or prove equal power across society. Those limitations inform the Serathi profile. [Gallery account](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/galleries/etruscan-world).

4. **Museo Nazionale Etrusco di Villa Giulia, Pyrgi gold tablets; Italian national catalogue, record 1200951713.** Late sixth-century BCE dedications connect Thefarie Velianas with Uni/Astarte through Etruscan and Phoenician texts. They provide direct material evidence for a ruler's religious dedication across cultural connections. Their imperfect correspondence prevents treating them as a complete parallel translation. Our Common Sky is an invention; the tablets neither describe a whole pan-Etruscan reform nor provide our couple's biography. [Museum object account](https://www.museoetru.it/works/lamine-doro-da-pyrgi); [museum discussion](https://www.museoetru.it/masterpieces/lamine-doro-da-pyrgi); [national catalogue](https://catalogo.beniculturali.it/detail/ArchaeologicalProperty/1200951713).

5. **Department of Greek and Roman Art, Metropolitan Museum of Art, “The Roman Republic.”** Used for the connection between political privilege, land, debt, conquest and increasingly powerful commanders. Atheria's twelve-city Compact is existing setting history, not a claim about a Roman predecessor constitution. [Article](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-roman-republic).

### Ancient literary traditions

6. **Livy, *History of Rome*, Book I, especially 4–7, 18–21 and 34–35.** The first group supplies the wolf-foundation and fatal sibling rivalry. Numa's traditions supply sustained royal organisation of worship. Tanaquil and the earlier Tarquin traditions supply religious interpretation, migration, marriage and a woman's substantive role in royal advancement. These are separate contributions. Our couple does not inherit their complete lives, and their stories are not treated as contemporary reports. The accessible text is D. Spillan's nineteenth-century English translation. [Text and numbered chapters](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/19725/19725-h/19725-h.htm).

The Roman episode of the Sabine women, Lucretia's rape, Numa's Egeria relationship and later Tarquin succession murders are not incorporated in this delivery. Their absence is a scope decision about unborrowed episodes. The borrowed fratricide remains deliberate, bloody and irreversible. We do not attach every atrocity in Livy to this one foundation.

### TES in-world texts

7. **“Shezarr and the Divines,” attributed in-world to Faustillus Junius.** The text describes Alessia's religious accommodation between inherited Aldmeri worship and Nordic allies. It supplies the political usefulness of a shared religious settlement among constituencies with different sacred histories. It is an in-world explanation, not an authorial proof that political agreement creates or merges divine beings. [In-game text transcription](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Shezarr_and_the_Divines); [Imperial Library text location](https://www.imperial-library.info/content/shezarr-and-divines). Full-page retrieval was intermittent in this research environment; indexed passages support the accommodation motif. The older “Shezarr and the Eight Divines” text is identified by its host as superseded and is not used as an independent controlling version.

8. **“The Last King of the Ayleids,” attributed in-world to Herminia Cinna.** Used to distinguish an initial coalition and accommodation from subsequent persecution and rewritten memory. It guards against describing Alessia's entire era as one uniform ethnic confrontation. We do not transplant the Ayleid royal list, Alessian Order, Dragonfires or later anti-elven campaign into Caleran. [Imperial Library transcription](https://www.imperial-library.info/content/last-king-ayleids).

The transcription hosts are fan-maintained access points for game texts. Source authority belongs to the identified text and its in-world perspective. No fandom theory about mantling, the Amulet of Kings or planetary metaphysics is promoted here. The Common Sky has seven currently named Star cults; it is not required to acquire an eighth to match TES.

## Geography and ancestry review

Arsuvan is one regional kingdom in the Aurin basin, with power along agricultural and transport connections. It does not occupy all later Thalmyria, replace an Imperial Horizon or imply that all southern ruins share its origin. Its reach towards uplands follows approaches, estates and mines. Present borders and map coordinates are unchanged.

Sarven and Nerava are upstream from the estuary; Thesra is in a defensible intermediate district; Olveth lies in an upland basin. These descriptions preserve the river's direction from the Veridun highlands to Caleran. They do not place the later city of Veridun or its post-Fall political role in the Giant Age. Farther south remains coldward.

Connections with the Copper Stair kingdoms use intermediate trade. Kharad supplies knowledge arriving with caravans, while Ulmaris supplies indirect maritime contacts. No direct frontier, shipping itinerary or previously uncharted road is fixed. Those kingdoms' named rulers and signature events remain independent.

The Enathi, Serathi and Vardeni are related **Orphaned** peoples formed from multiple households. None is Folk, Ättar, a lost Ark branch or a single Giant's biological human creation. Each contains farmers, workers, traders, religious specialists and leaders. Their predominant environments do not turn them into occupational castes.

Southern newcomers are Ark-derived proto-Atherians whose identity changes through centuries of settlement. Later mixed Aurin communities can be described as braided in their formation without making them identical to the later Sestaran, Vesari or Eclessian peoples. Language, civic standing, ancestry and religion remain separate questions. No conquest by these settlers is divinely commissioned.

The three peoples do not become three modern nationalities on a fixed genealogical chart. Upland contributions to later Thalmyric cultures pass through further historical changes. Those later inheritances belong in the scheduled regional packages.

## Giant ancestry and continuity

The authorial proposal makes **House Iskaren remotely descended from a Titan-Firstborn whose nonhuman parent was a bounded generative manifestation associated with Astaroth**. **House Velketh remotely descends from a separate Titan-Firstborn of a Typhonius manifestation**. These are two distinct exceptional ancestral conceptions. Their human mothers, circumstances, dates and the later fates of the Titan ancestors remain unwritten. Neither sovereignty divides, incarnates in full or authorises hereditary rule.

This establishes actual remote ancestry for the ruling houses without accepting their stories of stars descending into bodies. No ruler in the main narrative is an immediate child of a Menhir. The histories do not imply that repetition of a rite can produce another Firstborn.

| Actor or category | Authorial continuity | Specific ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Veskar, Tarvessa, Arveth and the Giant rulers described as their dynastic peers | `giant-continuity` | Embodied High/Dynastic Giants; personal agency and inherited power; ordinary bodily death can end their lives. |
| The two remote Firstborn ancestors | `titan-continuity` | Ancestral existence proposed; no return, destruction, fragment location or present activity established. |
| Human service households, artisans and captives | `human-soul` | Complete Image-bearing persons, including any who might have Giant ancestry. No fractional souls or caste tests. |
| Royal office and astronomical regalia | Office and objects, not personal continuities | No inherited office becomes the dead king. |
| Sarven's royal visions | Human interpretation plus the bounded activity described below | Not proof of stellar birth, divine approval or a returned ancestor. |
| Arveth's remains | Remains of a bodily dead Giant | Not Titan fragments, a resurrection set or a Nythalum-style Continuant. |

No machine escape, Egg White evacuation or ancestor reconstruction is added to Arsuvan's collapse. The wider canon contains these possibilities, but this kingdom does not need to repeat each of them. Unspecified cadet survivors remain unassigned rather than silently converted into Patrons.

## Faewild persons and the wolf's scheme

**Ravessa and Orsul** are individually sapient `court-law` persons in Orionis-associated Faewild ecology. Their households comprise persons and dependants whose membership is not a new reusable species. Ordinary wolves, human attendants, household roles and nonpersonal features of their environment remain distinct categories. Orionis is the existing Menhir sovereignty; Ravessa is neither his complete manifestation nor a dependent Idol Mask.

Ravessa's route connects one woodland approach in the lower Aurin country with the vicinity of Istren's refuge. It can be crossed under local conditions known to her household. This pass establishes the ancient crossings needed for the rescue and failed expedition, not a permanent present-day route. Neither the Egg White nor the soul-road is involved. The children age normally and remain human throughout their upbringing.

Her practical scheme has limited aims:

1. Preserve children whose human kin could support a future estuary dynasty.
2. Retain access to the woodland through their settlement's goodwill.
3. Obtain shelter, metal equipment and a limited body of fighters for the struggle against Nereth.
4. Recover the lost hunting basin and approach, strengthening Istren against one rival household.

Ravessa can rescue and guide people through the route, supply food and affect the local struggle. She cannot compel love, guarantee inheritance, create a city, order the Menhir sovereignty or predetermine Caleran's republican and imperial future. Avren's promise is politically important because he chooses to honour it and attempts to impose its cost on others. It is not a soul-binding contract or a magical explanation of his murder.

Her affection is real within the proposed account, alongside her ambition and possessiveness. Alvessa and Meral provide essential human care, teaching and relationships. The fosterage therefore has a daily history instead of a miraculous image standing in for the work of raising children.

The scheme partly fails. Caleth's death deprives it of provisions and allies. A smaller force recovers one refuge at the cost of lives; the basin remains under Orsul. This is one dynastic conflict within a local court ecology. Its later outcome, Ravessa's ultimate fate and any authentic presence in 1360 remain open.

### Actual powers and errors of identification

- **Astaroth** is the Menhir of collision, resistance and structural force. Human war theology does not make him a commissioned military patron or identify him with Qianglong's War Horseman.
- **Lyraxis** concerns adaptive variation, change and creative play. Music and trickery are cultural expressions, not an exhaustive biological or moral description.
- **Orionis** concerns pursuit and predation. Ravessa's personal attachments do not convert the sovereignty into a universal guardian of children.
- **Hecatara** concerns routes and adjacency. Civic claims about moral choices, fate or dead souls exceed that domain.
- **Typhonius** concerns recombination and bodily transformation. Breeding and healing cannot manufacture the Image or return a dead human soul.
- **Arachnara** concerns hidden, distributed networks and related creaturely pressures. Her cult supplies neither omniscient archives nor a second lord of the human afterlife.
- **Thanatos**, in this tradition, falsely interprets **the Boatman** as a Star Child. The Boatman remains the existing psychopomp. Payments, inherited rank, civic membership and temple promises cannot bribe his routing or divert souls into an Astratheon afterlife.

The six Menhir do not become seven celestial siblings through collective belief. Human identifications, regalia and stories do not merge supernatural persons. Sun, Moon, land powers and other cults retain independent existence even when public theology maligns, marginalises or excludes them.

### Idol involvement at Sarven

The proposal classifies a limited portion of the flattering royal visions at Sarven as activity of **one unnamed dependent Mask of Pride**, exploiting ceremonies of comparison, humiliation and sovereign superiority. Other visions may be dreams, staged wonders or misread observations. The Mask depends on the existing Pride Throne; the ceremony supplies imagery and feeding opportunities, not the Idol's creation.

This Mask is separate from Astaroth, the Giant rulers, their ancestors and their regalia. It receives no autonomous Archmask status, Regnant succession or continuous secret rule over Caleran. Its post-collapse fate is left unassigned. The name **Crowned Reflection** was considered during drafting and excluded because it already belongs to a protected succession hypothesis. No alias or connection to that hypothesis is introduced.

The Common Sky may preserve images once exploited by a Mask without carrying the original Mask into every later ceremony. A public religious synthesis is not itself a supernatural merger. No member of the ruling couple is unknowingly made into the Mask's incarnation by this review.
