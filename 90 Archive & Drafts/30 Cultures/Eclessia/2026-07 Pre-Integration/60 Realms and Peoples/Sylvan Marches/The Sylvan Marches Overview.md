## **1. Overview**
The **Sylvan Marches** are a vast, untamed region that serves as **Eclessia’s southern frontier**, acting as a natural barrier between **civilized lands and the uncharted wilds of the Fäskogarfolk**. It is a land of **rugged woodlands, mist-choked marshes, and ancient ruins**, where scattered settlements cling to survival amidst the constant threats of **raiders, monstrous creatures, and eldritch remnants of a lost pre-Atherian civilization**.

Unlike the structured duchies of Eclessia, the Marches are **a land of outcasts and survivors**, populated by those who have either been **exiled, fled from persecution, or chosen to carve out a life away from the eyes of the Church and noble courts**. While technically a part of Eclessia, the region is **loosely governed**, with authority shifting between **warlords, rangers, and village elders** who hold power by necessity rather than divine right.

Among the dangers of the Sylvan Marches are the **raiding warbands of the Fäskogarfolk**, the **migratory Grobi hordes**, the **cunning but feral drakes**, and the **hidden horrors that dwell within the blackened ruins of a lost, Adversary-worshipping civilization**.

---

## **2. Political Structure & Governance**
The Marches have no **centralized government**, functioning instead as **a loose confederation of settlements, ranger enclaves, and frontier lords**.

### **Marchburg – The Closest Thing to a Capital**
- Marchburg is the **largest and most fortified settlement**, a walled frontier town that serves as **a hub for rangers, mercenaries, and traders**.
- Ruled by **High Warden Elias Dreymann**, a former noble who abandoned Eclessian court life to govern the frontier.
- Maintains the **only real standing force in the Marches**, the **Marchburg Rangers**, tasked with patrolling the wilderness and keeping the roads safe.

### **Independent Settlements & Frontier Lords**
- **Grimmholt:** A fortress-town ruled by **Baroness Katarina Wolfsden**, a fierce warrior-lord whose knights defend the western forests.
- **Thornhollow:** A gathering of exiled scholars and alchemists, rumored to conduct **forbidden experiments on the ruins of the lost civilization**.
- **Wolfsheim:** A **hunter’s settlement**, home to skilled woodsmen who tame and ride **lesser drakes as hunting mounts**.
- **Blackbriar:** A lawless town ruled by **bandit lords and ex-mercenaries**, serving as a hub for black-market trade and fugitive hideouts.

### **Ranger Enclaves & Warrior Bands**
- The **Marchburg Rangers** act as the **only real law enforcement**, trained in **archery, ambush tactics, and survivalism**.
- **The Bloodwood Brotherhood**, a group of rogue knights and exiles, functions as **both protectors and mercenaries**, enforcing frontier justice.
- **The Hollowborn**, a secretive sect of mystics and druids, live deep in the **haunted groves**, guarding knowledge that predates even the Atherian Empire.

## **3. The Ever-Present Threats of the Marches**

### **The Fäskogarfolk Raids**
- The **Fäskogarfolk**, the untamed tribes that dwell in the deep southern forests, launch frequent **raids on settlements, seeking captives and plunder**.
- Their warriors **ride giant elk and dire wolves**, and their shamans **wield strange, primal magic** unlike anything sanctioned by the Church.
- They see the Eclessians as **invaders**, vowing to drive them back across the rivers and reclaim the woodlands for their ancient gods.

### **The Seasonal Grobi Invasions**
- The **Grobi**, cursed creatures driven by hunger, swarm into the Marches **during the winter months**, desperate for food and warmth.
- These attacks can **wipe out entire villages**, and only **fortified towns like Marchburg** can withstand their relentless assaults.
- Many mercenary companies make a living by **slaughtering Grobi warbands for coin**.

### **The Drakes of the Deepwood**
- These **four-legged, wingless dragons** are cunning hunters, preferring **ambush tactics** and attacking isolated travelers.
- Some settlers have learned to **coexist with drakes**, taming **lesser breeds for hunting and war**.
- Rumors persist of **a great drake, the Ashen Maw**, an ancient beast said to have survived since before the Atherian Empire.

### **The Ruins of the Forgotten Civilization**
- Scattered throughout the Marches are **crumbling ruins of a lost empire**, believed to have been **wiped from history for their devotion to the Adversary**.
- These ruins contain **dungeons filled with traps, lingering curses, and unearthly horrors that have not seen the sun in centuries**.
- Scholars and treasure hunters frequently **disappear** when they venture too deep, fueling superstitions that **something ancient still watches over the ruins**.

## **4. Religion, Mysticism, and Forbidden Knowledge**

### **The Church’s Limited Influence**
- While the Eclessian Church claims the Marches as part of its domain, **it has little direct control** over the frontier.
- Missionaries and inquisitors occasionally **venture into the Marches**, attempting to **root out heretical cults and unearth dangerous relics**.
- Many settlers practice **a blend of Church doctrine and old-world superstitions**, believing in **forest spirits, ancient curses, and omens**.

### **The Hollowborn & Their Secrets**
- The Hollowborn are **a secretive order of mystics, druids, and scholars** who dwell within the Marches’ deepest groves.
- They guard **forbidden knowledge**, including **ancient texts and rituals predating the Atherian Empire**.
- Some believe they **protect the world from whatever lies beneath the ruins**, while others fear they **seek to reclaim the lost power of the Adversary’s followers**.

---

## **5. The Future of the Sylvan Marches**
As the **conflict between civilization and the wild** continues, key questions shape the future of the Sylvan Marches:

- **Will the Marches be fully integrated into Eclessia, or will they remain a land of exiles and frontiersmen?**
- **Can the raiders and monstrous threats be subdued, or will the wild reclaim what civilization has built?**
- **What secrets lie buried in the lost ruins, and will they bring power or doom to those who uncover them?**

The Sylvan Marches stand on the edge of both **Eclessian civilization and the unknown**, where every shadowed grove, forgotten ruin, and mist-laden valley **holds stories yet to be told**.
