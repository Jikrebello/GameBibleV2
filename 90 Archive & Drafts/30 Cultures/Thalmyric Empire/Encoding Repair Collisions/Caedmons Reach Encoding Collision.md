# Principality of Caedmon's Reach Overview

Caedmon's Reach is a Thalmyric frontier principality built around reclaimed mountain passes, crusader roads, and fortified pilgrimage corridors. It is the hand Thalmyria stretches toward lost imperial territory.

## Role in the Setting

The principality is a staging ground for reconquest and a pressure cooker for settlers, veterans, clergy, engineers, and border nobility. Its people live with one eye on old imperial glory and the other on the next raid.

## Conflicts

- Veterans demanding land grants promised during crusades.
- Church officials claiming relic sites and pilgrim taxes.
- Local governors balancing settlement with military readiness.
- Ahl-e-Ahd agents probing for contract vulnerabilities.

## Game Use

Use Caedmon's Reach for fort-building, border skirmishes, holy road protection, veteran unrest, and quests around whether reconquest is justice, ambition, or both.
