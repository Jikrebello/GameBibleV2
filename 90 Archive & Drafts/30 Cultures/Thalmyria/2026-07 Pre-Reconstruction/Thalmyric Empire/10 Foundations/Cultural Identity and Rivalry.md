# Cultural Identity and Rivalry

Thalmyric identity is built from faith, fire, loss, endurance, and the claim that old Atheria did not die so much as retreat into the mountains and return armed.

## Rivalries

- **Eclessia:** seen as fragmented, rural, and insufficiently disciplined, but still kin in the faith.
- **Ahl-e-Ahd:** seen as occupiers, contract tyrants, and the great proof that holy war remains unfinished.
- **Sestara:** admired for sophistication and distrusted for opportunism, finance, and archive politics.
