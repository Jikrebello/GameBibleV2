# The Velian States Overview

The Velian States are contested successor territories: partly occupied, partly liberated, partly collaborator-ruled, and symbolically central to the long war between Thalmyria and the Ahl-e-Ahd.

## Role in the Setting

The Velian States are the wound that will not close. Every faction can claim them: Thalmyria as lost inheritance, Ahl-e-Ahd as lawful protectorate, local nobles as ancestral homelands, merchants as trade corridors, and the Church as souls under siege.

## Conflicts

- Puppet rulers versus rebel networks.
- Mixed communities accused of collaboration by all sides.
- Contract-bound infrastructure that cannot simply be destroyed without killing civilians.
- Relic sites and old Atherian ruins used as propaganda anchors.

## Game Use

Use the Velian States for spy work, insurgency, liberation politics, moral ambiguity, border towns, and quests where breaking a contract may also break a city.

Define constituent states, current borders, major cities, resistance cells, and which powers control which roads.
