# The Kingdom of Saronis Overview

The Kingdom of Saronis is a Thalmyric constituent realm whose identity should center on coastal defense, shipyards, maritime saints, and contested sea-lanes.

## Role in the Setting

Saronis gives Thalmyria reach beyond the mountains. It builds ships, moves soldiers, protects pilgrim routes, and watches for smuggling between Sestaran merchants, Ahl-e-Ahd agents, and imperial officials who prefer flexible bookkeeping.

## Conflicts

- Admirals versus landed nobles over funding and prestige.
- Smugglers moving relics, contracts, and forbidden texts.
- Coastal towns that resent imperial requisitions.
- Sestaran financiers entangled in dockyards and naval debt.

## Game Use

Use Saronis for naval missions, port intrigue, blockade-running, sea relics, saint shrines, and investigations into contraband that could destabilize the empire.

## Needs

Define capital port, ruling dynasty, fleet structure, patron saints, and relationship to Sestara.
