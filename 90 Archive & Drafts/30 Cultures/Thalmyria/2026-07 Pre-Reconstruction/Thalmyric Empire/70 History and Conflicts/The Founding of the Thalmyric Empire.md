### **I. [[Prelude to Collapse]] (c. Year 0 - Year 20)**

- The Atherian Empire, though vast and ancient, begins to show signs of spiritual stagnation and political fatigue.
- The ruling emperor consolidates power in Caleran, relying heavily on religious symbolism while ignoring growing tensions at the empire’s periphery.
- Ahl-e-Ahd emissaries and scouts are reported near the eastern borders, but warnings go unheeded.

---

### **II. [[The Invasion and the Fall]] (Year 21 - Year 27)**

- The Ahl-e-Ahd invade with overwhelming force, using contract-bound armies and arcane siegecraft to rapidly conquer eastern provinces.
- The Atherian Emperor orders the Mustering of All Flame, calling upon manpower and resources from across the empire, including distant satrapies.
- Despite valiant defense, the Ahl-e-Ahd breach Caleran’s walls in Year 27.
- The **Day of Black Flame**: The capital is sacked. The imperial family is slaughtered. Surviving nobility and civilians flee or are enslaved.

---

### **III. [[The Refuge and the Rise of Veridun]] (Year 28 - Year 30)**

- A coalition of survivors, including noble houses, priests, and commonfolk, escape into the Veridunian Highlands.
- In secret, they establish the **Kingdom of Veridun**, a militant theocracy rooted in old Atherian flame tradition.
- **Altharic of House Virelion**, a general of noble blood, emerges as the leader of the exiled forces.

---

### **IV. [[The Battle of Caelmount]] (Year 31)**

- Ahl-e-Ahd forces attempt to snuff out the Veridunian resistance.
- Altharic leads a coalition of monks, militia, and noble retainers into a volcanic pass sacred to the Redeemer.
- Through sacrificial pyromancy and tactical genius, the Veridunians achieve a decisive victory.
- The battle is seen as a divine turning point and proof of the Redeemer’s continued favor.

---

### **V. [[The Coronation at Virelos]] (Year 32)**

- The noble houses and clergy gather in the high city of Virelos.
- Altharic is crowned **First Flame-Emperor of the Thalmyric Empire**, marking the spiritual rebirth of Atherian legacy.
- The empire is formally renamed to signify the **new covenant of flame and struggle**.

---

### **VI. [[The War of the Burning Banners]] (Year 33 - Year 51)**

- Rival claimants to the imperial title emerge in neighboring provinces.
- Altharic fights a series of skirmishes and diplomatic wars to unite the fractured remnants.
- Notable moments include the Synod of Ash Oaths and the defeat of the False Heir of Tervas.
- By Year 51, all major threats to his legitimacy are vanquished.

---

### **VII. [[The Reclamation of Caleran]] (Year 52 - Year 54)**

- With the highlands secure and rival banners subdued, Altharic marches to reclaim the old capital.
- A protracted siege begins, including arcane purification of the city’s corrupted walls.
- The gates are finally breached during a fiery storm coinciding with a rare celestial alignment.
- Caleran is retaken. Though scarred, it becomes the **throne-city of the new empire**.

---

### **VIII. [[The Founding Flame]] (Year 55 - Year 65)**

- Altharic reigns for another decade, restoring law, religious order, and infrastructure.
- He is entombed beneath the **Sanctum Pyrae**.
- His reign is remembered as the **Founding Flame**, and his example is revered throughout Thalmyric history.

> _“When blood failed, the flame chose. When law shattered, the sword spoke. And when hope flickered, Caelmount burned bright.”_ — Inscription at the Shrine of the Rekindled Path, Caleran
