# Hold Until Relieved

## Role in the Arc

This is Ilyana Veshkar's quest 4 of four. It is playable in Early Spring and Muster Crisis. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Ilyana Veshkar duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

A spring residue surge traps a mixed repair platoon beneath the reclaimed Tannbruck works while a dead-command column advances. Command orders Ilyana to seal the breach and save the road.

Hold the position long enough to extract the trapped people without sacrificing the wider defense through indecision.

## Player Actions

Build fallback lines, direct Blackjack specialists, descend for survivors, and decide when to vent the captured elemental load into the dead column.

## Opposition and Pressure

Residue soldiers obey old patterns, the structure is failing, and frightened officers invoke both her past failure and the immediate strategic cost.

## Information Paths

Sound through the wall, the repair roster, and a survey gap reveal three rescue routes with different costs. Ilyana's prior method determines which she recognizes first.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Commit to a rescue doctrine and bear its military cost. A deliberate late sacrifice may be offered by Ilyana only if her broken state and player choice make it explicit; it is never automatic.

## Fail-Forward Results

Lost time destroys artillery or road sections before it kills trapped personnel. Failed extraction leaves wounded captives reachable through a harder combat route.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

Committing to the Deep Muster closes ordinary resolution. The companion reaches a specified compromised summer state unless the quest has already produced a broken or departed outcome.

Completes her arc and determines whether she becomes a life-preserving field commander, a secretive expert, or a returned sacrificial officer.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
