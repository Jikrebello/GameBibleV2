# The Sermoran Republic Overview

Sermora is a canal metropolis and fortified island republic commanding the central Thalassian routes. Its jurisdiction includes the capital islands, naval stations, pilot villages, dockyards, and protected privateer havens rather than a broad mainland.

## Formation

Island harbors and pilot communities predate Atherian conquest. Atheria preserved local captains to move fleets and collect customs. During the Decadence those captains kept the revenue and built an independent Admiralty. Sermora joined the first defensive compact in 908.

## Government and Citizenship

**Doge Terenzo Vescari** represents the republic and brokers among the Admiralty, merchant houses, guilds, shipowners, privateer captains, and the **Veiled Office**. Male guild masters and registered ship-share citizens vote; widows retain house votes they inherit. Only Houses of the First Mooring may supply the Doge and senior Admiralty.

The Veiled Office directs foreign intelligence and state secrecy. The **Night Oar** performs deniable retrieval, sabotage, and killing but remains a contested instrument rather than an all-powerful assassin guild.

## Society and Faith

Canal folk, arsenal hands, sailors, pilots, courtesans, craftsmen, servants, captives, foreigners, and resident merchant communities outnumber enfranchised houses. Public faith centers on Saint Naris, ship blessings, confraternities, and harbor processions.

## Economy and Defence

Shipping, convoy service, insurance, naval stores, luxury trade, pilotage, salvage, and privateering sustain the republic. The Admiralty fleet protects recognized routes; licensed captains raid enemies and occasionally discover after the fact who the republic considers an enemy. During a muster Sermora concentrates fleets and relieves blockades.

## Present Tensions

Castaran routes threaten old trade, Ättar raiding blurs into licensed service, the Veiled Office accumulates secrets no Doge controls, and privateer violence risks wars the Concord will not defend.
