# Grenzburg RPG Experience Constitution Pass Archive Index

This collection preserves the active control layer immediately before Grenzburg was reframed around three equal experience layers: the seasonal campaign, the blank-slate debtor, and eight autonomous companion campaigns.

## Snapshot Groups

- `Pre-Pass/Controls`: game, player, quest, season, companion, familiarity, reactivity, chapter-window, and narrative-state authorities.
- `Pre-Pass/Indexes`: Grenzburg, main-campaign, companion, player-system, and current-focus navigation.
- `Pre-Pass/Templates`: the former quest and character templates.
- `Pre-Pass/Bases`: the former Companion and Main Campaign Bases.
- `Pre-Pass/Summer`: the two summer quest references before the postwar seasonal cycle was established.
