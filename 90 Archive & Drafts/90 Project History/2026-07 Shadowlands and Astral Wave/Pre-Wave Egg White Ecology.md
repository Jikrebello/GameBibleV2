## 1) The Egg-White's people: the Driftkin

### What they are

The Driftkin descend biologically from humans whose communities survived thin spaces, demiplane failures, patron-islands, and long exile in the Egg-White. Across generations, Astral drift, rent dependence, and metaphysical ballast rewrote the lineage until it ceased to bear the Image. Fully developed Driftkin are therefore **not metaphysically human and do not receive routable human souls at birth**.

Where Material humans survive food, weather, and predators, Driftkin bodies survive place, stability, and coherence.

### Continuity

Driftkin identity persists through embodied memory, true-silt, names, route-patterns, and personal ballast. At death, those patterns ordinarily disperse into Drift. A pattern may be copied, anchored, harvested, or imitated, but that persistence is not a human afterlife and does not produce an arrival at the Vestibule. [[The Image, Soul, and Creaturely Continuity]] controls the authorial ruling.

Driftkin remain capable of intelligence, loyalty, cruelty, culture, and moral choice. None of those qualities manufactures the Image.

### What makes them unique

**(A) Their bodies carry “metaphysical ballast.”**
They don’t anchor reality by raw strength; they anchor it the way a deep-sea creature anchors against current—by **structure**. Driftkin bones accumulate mineral-like deposits called _true-silt_ (congealed vow-residue, name-fragments, memory-ash). This gives them just enough “weight” to keep a personal bubble of geometry consistent for a few hours or days—longer with tools.

**(B) Their nervous system is a navigation engine.**
In the Egg-White, travel is not “walking”—it is **directed arrival**. Driftkin develop disciplined cognition: focus-rituals, shared chanting mathematics, mnemonic maps, and “route-language” (speech designed to preserve intention). Their “magic” looks like mindcraft because it is literally **the skill of insisting on a destination**.

**(C) Their societies are mobile by necessity.**
If they stop paying upkeep—through labor, ritual, tribute, or extracting mass—**their home frays**. So Driftkin cultures are defined less by territory and more by **maintenance economies**.

### Social forms (three useful baselines)

1. **Carcass-Cities (Mass-Sitters):** built into the ribs, scales, hollows, or cavities of dead-god debris. The city persists because the corpse is heavy.
2. **Procession-Clans (Rent-Payers):** nomadic fleets of raft-islands that travel between mass nodes, paying rent by service, tribute, or carefully rationed sacrifices of memory/time.
3. **Chalk-Orthodoxies (Pattern-Keepers):** groups who can’t afford mass and therefore survive through **recursive pattern stability**—endless repetition of geometry, song, and rite to keep their “place” from dissolving.

### Why they _rarely_ enter yolk-space

They can, but it’s costly and strategically rare:

- **Biological mismatch:** yolk-space has “too much rule” (too many fixed physics). Driftkin bodies, adapted to drift-management, often experience sickness: insomnia, time-lag, sensory overload, compulsive anchoring behaviors.
- **Risk of losing their core:** crossing usually requires hauling a ballast object (core-stone, contract tablet, relic shard). Lose it in yolk-space and you may not be able to “get home” because home is not a coordinate—it’s a maintained claim.
- **The primary motivation is “mass acquisition,” not conquest:** they intrude when a rare opportunity exists—an unguarded relic-lode, a thin-space leak, a catastrophic battlefield saturated with repeated oaths, etc.

### What they want from yolk-space

- **Metaphysical mass** (relics, saint-remains, true-names, oath-anchors, menhir splinters).
- **Skilled agents** (warlocks, oath-bound specialists) who can act inside stable physics without suffering drift-sickness.
- **Access points** to enforce routes, monopolize crossings, or harvest thin-space “leaks.”

This dovetails cleanly with your warlock economy: Egg-White patrons are powerful mainly because they **own stability**, and Driftkin are the most natural brokers/clients of that stability.

## 2) The Egg-White’s “animals”: Protons (native drift-fauna)

### What they are

Protons are the Egg-White’s baseline life: **organisms of partial rule**. They are not “summoned” and not “psychological.” They arise the way fungi arise in damp wood: wherever drift, residue, and minimal structure meet.

If Driftkin are “people who learned to be heavy,” Protons are “life that learned to live while light.”

### How they behave (ecological logic)

Protons fill niches created by your three laws:

- **Drift-scavengers:** eat abandoned geometry, frayed demiplane edges, decaying contracts, stale ward-lines.
- **Mass-symbionts:** cling to heavy objects and feed on the “shedding” of rule; some stabilize, some destabilize.
- **Rent-predators:** hunt maintained pockets by sensing upkeep cycles (when rituals lapse, they surge).
- **Route-parasites:** infest intention—causing mis-arrival, false destinations, or “near-enough” landings that get travelers killed.

### A practical bestiary set (useful, distinct, non-humanoid)

1. **Lacuna Rays:** flat, manta-like things that swim through empty “between,” drinking unused possibility; their passing causes rooms to forget corners.
2. **Anchor Leeches:** barnacle-worms that attach to cores (relic-stones, contract tablets). They make a demiplane feel safer—until they’ve eaten enough “rule” that it collapses all at once.
3. **Mnemonic Kites:** ribbon-bodied fliers that trail memory-filaments. Driftkin hunt them to harvest usable recollections (a literal upkeep fuel).
4. **Causality Crabs:** armored scavengers that collect “because” and “therefore” the way crabs collect shells; their nests become pockets where events re-order to fit the crab’s stolen logic.
5. **Glass-Spawn Shoals:** swarms of tiny shard-fish that form around divine debris; they are both hazard and resource (excellent for wardcraft).
6. **Vow-Wasps:** insects that build hives out of spoken promises; when disturbed they sting with compulsions to “finish what you started.”

This keeps “protons” firmly in **nature/ecosystem territory**, not Idol territory: they’re not moral, not sin-coded, not dream-coded—just the Egg-White doing ecology.

## 3) Major landmarks and “big players” that don’t freeze the setting into static maps

### Landmark types (repeatable tools for you)

- **Corpse-Reefs:** asteroid-fields of lesser fragments—teeth, rib-arches, petrified wings, nameless organs—clumped into navigable belts. Great for resource wars and scavenger politics.
- **The Sleeping Wrong:** a vast failure-state entity that is _not dead_—it generates loops, false awakenings, repeated days, and “almost exits.” It’s a region hazard and a theological problem.
- **Rent-Wells:** places where upkeep is unnaturally cheap (a “subsidy zone”). Everyone wants them; nobody trusts them.
- **Route-Sutures:** stable corridors between stable masses—high-value trade lanes that exist only while certain observances are met.
- **Thin-Space Bruises:** places where the Material projection fails and Egg-White drift “bleeds through” (your doors-to-nowhere, rooms that don’t remember shape, paths that unspool).

### Player archetypes (the movers)

- **Anchor-Owners (Patrons):** beings whose “power” is primarily that they sit on stable mass and can lease it.
- **Pattern-Engineers (Archmages / conclaves):** groups that maintain demiplanes through recursive ritual and geometry.
- **Salvage-Saints and Relic Reavers:** factions that strip mass from carcasses, battlefields, and oath-sites.
- **Displaced higher-realm manifestations:** a heavenly servant, damned office-holder, Depth creature, or Abyssal form may become stranded in the Drift. Astral pressure can distort embodiment and access to power, but it does not turn good and evil into neutral temperament.

## 4) Vorthara’s Black Spire as a “dual-realm anchor” (your bridge into the Egg-White)

From your Vorthara primer: the city is built on an **ancient black spire** and contains competing magical factions constrained by a strict internal rule-set (no infighting; disputes settled in arenas).

That is _exactly_ the profile of an Egg-White anchor behaving like:

- **Recursive Pattern Stability:** the “no infighting + arena adjudication” is not only politics; it can be the _ritualized loop that pays rent_.
- **A Self-Paying Rent Loop:** the city’s constant regulated conflict (contained, repeated, codified) becomes upkeep—energy that the Spire converts into stability.

### What that means in practice

- Vorthara can have an **“upper city”** in the Material and a **“shadow annex”** in the Egg-White: not a mirror, but a _co-occupancy_.
- Certain factions (elemental war-magic, shadowbinding, construct guilds, etc.) naturally become the Spire’s “organs,” each unknowingly maintaining a different stabilizing subsystem.
- The city becomes the most believable “gateway hub” for:

    - patron contracts,
    - demiplane engineering,
    - Driftkin diplomacy,
    - and rare yolk-to-eggwhite migration events.

## 5) How this stays cleanly separated from Idols/Menhir

- **Idols** remain tied to the Dreamlands and sentient vice generation.
- **Menhir** remain your Faewild “forces of nature wearing faces,” organized in circles (Sun/Moon + star children; Trium split history; horsemen).
- **Egg-White** becomes the third thing: not vice, not wild-cycle—**entropy, ballast, and lease**.

That gives you a stable three-way contrast:

- Shadowlands = psychic negative / vice ecology (Idols).
- Faewild = mythic natural cycles / predatory law (Menhir).
- Egg-White = drift ecology / mass economics / patron leasing.
