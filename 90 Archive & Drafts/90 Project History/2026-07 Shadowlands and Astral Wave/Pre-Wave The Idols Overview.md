# The Idols Overview

The seven Idols are objective predatory powers of the Shadowlands. They are not misunderstood gods, neutral psychological archetypes, or necessary halves of a balanced cosmos. Each turns a human capacity away from its proper object and builds an ecology that rewards repetition, imitation, and harm.

`Native follower` is an unreliable mortal phrase. An Idol may grow a Shadowlands pattern, alter a living lineage, organize residue, impose a mask, or feed a Material institution. Those relationships do not imply literal biological descent.

## The Seven Economies

### [[Greed]]: Avarix

- **Realm:** The Hoarded Vaults, where possession replaces use and every apparent prize creates a stronger claim upon its holder.
- **Material harvest:** hoarding, enslavement, predatory acquisition, price manipulation, and the conversion of persons into assets.
- **Creature ecology:** claim-mites, hoardlings, gilded counterfeits, and vault-imposed conditions such as [[Vault-Claimed]]. Reptilian masks occur in Avarix cults, but [[Drakes Overview|drakes]] are Menhir court-fauna and do not serve Greed by nature.
- **Moral action:** Avarix turns stewardship and provision into ownership without obligation.

### [[Wrath]]: Furor

- **Realm:** The Infernal Pits, a conflict ecology in which injury must produce retaliation and every victory creates the next enemy.
- **Material harvest:** revenge, massacre, humiliation, feud, militarized appetite, and violence that reproduces itself.
- **Creature ecology:** [[Grobi Overview|Grobi]] are Wrath's principal self-sustaining Material harvest. Burning armor, rage-residue, and death-knight forms exist as minor manifestations, not as the defining lineage.
- **Moral action:** Furor corrupts courage and just anger into domination, indiscriminate vengeance, and delight in destruction.

### [[Sloth]]: Lethargos

- **Realm:** The Endless Dusk, where delay becomes surrender and the will loses the ability to choose the good it still recognizes.
- **Material harvest:** neglect, despairing passivity, abdicated duty, numbing comfort, and institutions that learn not to act.
- **Creature ecology:** [[Nightmares]], hush-forms, sleep parasites, and abandoned duties given predatory repetition.
- **Moral action:** Lethargos corrupts rest, patience, and contemplation into refusal of necessary action.

### [[Gluttony]]: Voracia

- **Realm:** The Eternal Feast, where consumption cannot nourish and appetite destroys the thing it seeks.
- **Material harvest:** overconsumption, corpse economies, addiction, devouring hierarchy, and the treatment of every good as fuel.
- **Creature ecology:** [[Ghouls]], appetite conjunctions, residue feeders, and feast-grown forms. A mature ghoul is a culpable residue intelligence after the original human soul has departed.
- **Moral action:** Voracia corrupts nourishment and celebration into endless consumption.

### [[Pride]]: Vaingloria

- **Realm:** The Shimmering Palace, where every surface reflects rank and every relationship becomes proof of superiority.
- **Material harvest:** self-worship, domination, isolation, false sovereignty, and the refusal to receive truth or correction.
- **Creature ecology:** mirror doubles, heraldic masks, and court counterfeits. Spectral peacocks are a recurring mask, not the principal lineage. [[Vampire Origins|Vampires]] are Pride's greatest Material inheritance through Ansharan Giant blood: genuine human souls retained and bent toward predatory sovereignty.
- **Moral action:** Vaingloria corrupts dignity, excellence, and authority into self-deification.

### [[Lust]]: Luxuria

- **Realm:** The Garden of Temptation, where desire separates pleasure from fidelity, personhood, and the good of the beloved.
- **Material harvest:** coercion, possession, cultivated obsession, exploitation, and the reduction of persons to appetite.
- **Creature ecology:** siren masks, desire doubles, invitation predators, and garden-grown forms. `Succubus` and `incubus` are mortal classifications for several such manifestations, not biological species.
- **Moral action:** Luxuria corrupts attraction, union, beauty, and fertility into consumption and control.

### [[Envy]]: Invidia

- **Realm:** The Shadowed Glade, where another person's good is experienced as personal injury.
- **Material harvest:** resentment, comparison, stolen identity, counterfeit sainthood, and communities built around grievance.
- **Creature ecology:** shades, grief doubles, replacement forms, and [[Borrowed Saints]].
- **Moral action:** Invidia corrupts admiration and the desire for justice into hatred of another's good.

## Moral and Creature Rules

- An Idol's power is real, but its interpretation of reality is false.
- An altered creature does not become morally innocent merely because its compulsion has a supernatural source; agency must be assessed explicitly.
- A creature associated with an Idol does not automatically contain a human soul.
- The Idols can alter, counterfeit, feed, and enslave. They cannot create the Image or manufacture a human soul.
- Menhir fauna, ordinary animals, and human institutions must not be reassigned to an Idol merely because their appearance resembles its symbols.

## Entry Through Local Courts

Idols do not always announce themselves as universal powers. A mask may enter an established local court, imitate a useful role, and turn a bounded custom toward habitual predation. Folk seers therefore encounter Idol activity without reliably identifying its source. [[Courts of the Folk Lands]] records the authorial classification of the first six such court ecologies; [[Folk Faith and Appeasement]] records what ordinary practitioners believe.
