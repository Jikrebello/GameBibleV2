# Shadowlands and Astral Wave Migration Manifest

## Scope

This wave established exactly **42 Shadowlands forms** and **18 Astral forms**, bringing the active bestiary to **212 genuine catalogued forms**. It also locked the Idols' origin in the Great Rebellion, their seven dominion ecologies, the distinction between Hell and the Shadowlands, the Astral anchor regimes, patron roles, and fourteen substantial demiplanes.

Indexes, overviews, and reference notes contribute no artificial catalogue coverage.

## Shadowlands Coverage

- Existing Grobi stages: 5.
- Existing vampire bloodlines: 5.
- Existing Ghouls, Grave-Runners, Nightmares, and Borrowed Saints: 4.
- Seven Idol ecologies with three native forms each: 21.
- Dreadforms: 3.
- First-War remnants: 3.
- Before-Shapes: 1.

Total: **42**.

Native life is separated into fauna, Masks, and Archmasks. A sovereign Idol never enters the Material Realm. Mortal institutions matter because chosen vice, inherited harm, and scalable systems provide denser nourishment than repetitive Shadowlands behavior.

## Astral Coverage

- Driftkin: 1.
- Protons: 6.
- Route and anchor predators: 4.
- Patron servitors: 3.
- Anchor-Lords: 1.
- Great Drift beings: 3.

Total: **18**.

Patron is a role rather than a species. Anchor-Lords are native `astral-pattern` persons, not elementals. Driftkin remain `drift-rewritten-life` and produce no routed human soul. Every lich-like being must state the continuity behind its apparent identity.

## Atlas Consolidation

The Astral atlas now uses seven navigational regimes: Ossuary Verge, Cairnmarket of the Claim-Ribs, Lantern Registry, Index-Shelves of Knots, Knot-Sleeper Basin, Root-Glass March, and Breathwaste. Earlier brainstorm locations survive as subregions, mobile settlements, or hazards within those regimes.

[[Demiplane Atlas]] records fourteen canon exemplars across mortal-made realms, artifact interiors, patron kingdoms, Anchor-Lord palaces, refuges, institutions, and failures. Each entry states its anchor, law, upkeep, access, owner continuity, inhabitants, exports, failure state, and story uses.

## Canon Corrections

- Mortal dreams did not create either the Idols or the Shadowlands.
- The future Menhir and Idol cohorts deserted the Adversary after the Great Rebellion and became yolk-sovereignties through forced fusion.
- Idols neither rule Hell nor serve the Adversary.
- Hell remains cold, wet, and pressure-heavy; Wrath owns the principal fire-and-slaughter imagery.
- Veradia retains infection and proliferating life. Morvalis retains bodily death, decomposition, and return.
- Counter-Countenances are rare remnants of original commissions, not redeemable alter egos or routine patrons.
- The Ansharan Vampire Giants were altered practitioners under Pride's influence, not literal children of an Idol.
- Wild Shadowlands life includes dread ecologies, First-War remnants, and primordial Before-Shapes independent of all seven Idols.

## Outward Reconciliation

The seven Left Hand networks now link to their matching Idol ecologies without reducing every cult plot to a monster incursion. Caleran references distinguish mortal networks from Masks. Conversion seeds received family links or demiplane routes only where their existing causes already supported them; no adventure outcome or hidden cause was newly fixed.
