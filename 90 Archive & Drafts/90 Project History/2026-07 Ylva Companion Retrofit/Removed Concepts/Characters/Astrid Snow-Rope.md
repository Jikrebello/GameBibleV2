# Astrid Snow-Rope

## Core Concept

A senior Rimewing rescuer who witnessed Svala strike after yield and helped move her into foreign service before vengeance consumed the fellowship.

## Narrative Function

Astrid values Svala and still testifies against her. She protects rescue stores, carries the weregild record, and supports reform that keeps the Sisters useful without preserving secret rage license.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Astrid Snow-Rope has interests independent of [[Svala Last-Beacon]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
