# Last Beacon, First Road

## Role in the Arc

This is Svala Last-Beacon's quest 4 of four. It is playable in Early Spring and Muster Crisis. The current note remains an outline; its deep block must use the fixed player-plus-Svala Last-Beacon duo format in [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Hook and Immediate Stake

The dead march extinguishes Fenn Lantern while refugees and wounded Grobi noncombatants converge on Last Hearth from opposite roads.

Hold a winter-road sanctuary, preserve surrender and refuge rules, and choose Svala's life after exile.

## Player Actions

Relight route beacons, escort stranded groups, defend the shelter from residue troops, and negotiate who may enter when stores cannot cover everyone without rationing.

## Opposition and Pressure

Old hatreds, scarcity, and Svala's rage all threaten the sanctuary. A committed Grobi raiding party attacks separately and receives no immunity from the civilians' claim to refuge.

## Information Paths

Road signs, refugee counts, and scout reports show which groups are vulnerable and which carry weapons. Svala's prior recall method changes the final defense.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Create an open road-warden service, return Svala to conditional hall authority, or let her choose a glorious last stand. Sacrifice requires explicit player confirmation after alternatives are known.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the beacon remains dark, the player can guide groups manually at greater casualties. If Last Hearth falls, survivors establish a smaller protected camp at Fenn Road Exchange.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
