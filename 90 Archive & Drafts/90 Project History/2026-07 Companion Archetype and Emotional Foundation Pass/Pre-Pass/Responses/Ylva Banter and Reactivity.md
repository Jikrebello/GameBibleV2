# Ylva Banter and Reactivity

This note controls Ylva's paired banter, faction opinions, regional observations, and reactive campaign scenes. Other companions may react before or after her duo finales but do not enter their central conversations.

## Voice Rules

- Ylva uses short concrete sentences.
- She notices temperature, footing, hands, breath, stores, and exits before abstractions.
- Affection arrives as preparation, not a sudden speech.
- Her dry humour never treats injury or frightened civilians as a joke.
- As she moves toward `shared`, she increasingly names what she needs and asks another person to choose.
- As she moves toward `self-spent`, her speech becomes falsely reassuring: "I have it" means she has stopped reporting the cost.

## Elara Velescar

**Early**

> **Elara:** You have looked at my boots three times.
>
> **Ylva:** The left sole is opening.
>
> **Elara:** You might begin with hello.
>
> **Ylva:** Hello. Your left sole is opening.

**Act I**

> **Elara:** A sealed chamber and you are interested in the drainage.
>
> **Ylva:** The treasure will still be there after the roof decides.
>
> **Elara:** That is a very ugly kind of wisdom.

**Act II**

> **Elara:** I found the owner before I took it.
>
> **Ylva:** Good.
>
> **Elara:** You could sound more impressed.
>
> **Ylva:** I am saving that for when you return it.

**Act III**

> **Ylva:** Two copies of the route.
>
> **Elara:** And two names on the finding record.
>
> **Ylva:** You have learned something.
>
> **Elara:** We have both become intolerably ethical.

## Ilyana Veshkar

**Early**

> **Ilyana:** A signal is only music with fewer notes.
>
> **Ylva:** A signal is music people must survive misunderstanding.

**Act I**

> **Ilyana:** I can carry the warning farther.
>
> **Ylva:** Then teach the next station the phrase.
>
> **Ilyana:** You distrust solos.
>
> **Ylva:** In a blizzard, yes.

**Act II**

> **Ylva:** Ask before you make them part of the refrain.
>
> **Ilyana:** Even when the wall is open?

**Act III**

> **Ilyana:** Call.
>
> **Ylva:** Answer.
>
> **Ilyana:** Again?
>
> **Ylva:** Until someone else can lead it.

## Ashira bath Melan

**Early**

> **Ashira:** Your route marks assume fear.
>
> **Ylva:** They assume people.

**Act I**

> **Ashira:** A recognizable shade would draw pursuit away.
>
> **Ylva:** It would also put a dead face in every survivor's sleep.
>
> **Ashira:** That is not a tactical objection.
>
> **Ylva:** It is if you plan to bring them home.

**Act II**

> **Ylva:** Fog hides a lane. It should not hide the price.
>
> **Ashira:** You are speaking about me.
>
> **Ylva:** You noticed.

**Act III**

> **Ashira:** I have copied the route without the faces.
>
> **Ylva:** Then I will carry one copy.
>
> **Ashira:** Only one?
>
> **Ylva:** I am learning.

## Tavio Meran

Tavio is Ylva's principal foil.

**Early**

> **Tavio:** The fourth mark was within my capacity.
>
> **Ylva:** So is staying awake for three days.
>
> **Tavio:** An unflattering comparison.
>
> **Ylva:** It was not a comparison.

**Act I**

> **Tavio:** The test needs someone willing to hold the load.
>
> **Ylva:** It needs someone empowered to stop it.
>
> **Tavio:** You volunteer for both.
>
> **Ylva:** That is the problem.

**Act II**

> **Ylva:** When your second calls halt, you stop.
>
> **Tavio:** When your relief calls change, you leave.
>
> **Ylva:** I dislike this friendship.
>
> **Tavio:** It is doing excellent work.

**Act III**

> **Tavio:** Horn first?
>
> **Ylva:** Relief first.
>
> **Tavio:** Then the impossible thing?
>
> **Ylva:** Only if it remains necessary.

## Zafir ibn Samad

**Early**

> **Zafir:** Your contract assigns loss without apportionment.
>
> **Ylva:** I signed it.
>
> **Zafir:** That explains its force. It does not establish justice.

**Act I**

> **Ylva:** I do not need a clause to carry someone.
>
> **Zafir:** No. You need one to stop an institution charging only you for it.

**Act II**

> **Zafir:** Shared liability?
>
> **Ylva:** Shared decision.
>
> **Zafir:** Better. The first is paperwork. The second is freedom.

**Act III**

>
> **Zafir:** And if they arrive?
>
> **Ylva:** I go.
>
> **Zafir:** I will believe you when I see your footprints north.

## Faction Reactivity

### Ducal and Marcher

Ylva supports:

- published evacuation authority;
- staffed watch rotations;
- village petitions over arbitrary requisition;
- officers who abandon ground before spending civilians.

She opposes indefinite emergency service, punitive hold orders, and prestige defences with no living purpose.

### Blackjacks and Medori

Ylva trusts Brina's transparent rosters and collective debtor relief. She distrusts Koss's habit of assigning extra work to whoever does not complain.

She supports a chartered free company or debtor cooperative. Continued Medori control pushes her toward `managed`; Koss's predatory route assignments can push her toward conflict or departure.

### Church

Ylva values Mercy Hospital, staffed shelters, burial parties, food lines, and quiet courage. She respects Caestus when he asks what a route needs before blessing it.

She rejects Church courts claiming authority over rescued people and any suggestion that survival creates spiritual debt.

### Folk

Ylva treats route rights as local and particular. She will learn Moss-Crown, River-Clan, Chapel-Folk, and Ghost-Foot signals separately rather than invent a universal Folk code.

She opposes attacks on civilians and Jorund's total-war followers without treating every hostile claim as false.

### Underworld

Ylva can support Low Lantern routes that move threatened debtors, witnesses, or refugees. She insists their shelter stores and signals remain protected.

She rejects false beacons, predatory tolls, and Black Bell execution as substitutes for rescue or civil judgement.

## Regional Observations

- **Grenz Lowlands:** notices overloaded carts, exposed ditches, and which mills can shelter travelers.
- **Longlake Country:** reads ice, ferry load, reed movement, and settlement signals.
- **Wolfsmere Woods:** defers to local place-law and refuses to mark sacred inner paths for convenience.
- **Southwood Barrowlands:** distinguishes a hunter's trail from a burial approach and treats both as held places.
- **Highlake Uplands:** watches wind shear, mine exits, and signal failures before ore.
- **Upper Grenz:** treats bridges, watch stations, and relief rosters as one route system.
- **Frostfenn March:** becomes quieter, more exact, and most vulnerable to the belief that she alone must remain.

## Seasonal Arc

- **Autumn:** competence appears as solitary endurance; she begins publishing signals.
- **Winter:** rescue under siege tests whether she can share liability and informed risk.
- **Spring:** duplicated routes and independent teams test whether her work can survive her absence.
- **Summer:** her dialogue reflects an independent pathfinder, supervised scout, solitary departure, or explicit memorial.
