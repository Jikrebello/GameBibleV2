# Elara Companion Retrofit Archive Index

This collection preserves Elara's active character bible, four-adventure deep block, relationship and combat controls, Vasko's original continuity role, and the shared registers immediately before the companion-campaign retrofit.

## Snapshot Groups

- `Pre-Retrofit/Characters`: Elara and Vasko as previously controlled.
- `Pre-Retrofit/Campaign`: the four quests, Field Familiarity, Delver-Duelist package, and reactivity register.
- `Pre-Retrofit/Controls`: the companion MOC, expansion and reactive registers, Narrative State Register, and Companion Base.

The active campaign replaces the old four-quest-only release structure. These files remain historical provenance and must not drive active Bases, navigation, or narrative state.
