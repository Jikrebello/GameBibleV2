# Elara Field Familiarity and Relationships

This note controls Elara's approval, personal-quest release, romance, sworn bond, departure, and player rewards. Shared rules come from [[Companion Field Familiarity and Duo Quest Standard]].

## Recruitment and Home Base

Elara becomes available at the [[Blackjack Yard]] during Early Autumn after [[Warrant at the Gate]]. Koss presents her as a useful delver, but the player recruits or dismisses her without asking his permission each time.

At the Yard she maintains her equipment, appraises questionable salvage for other debtors, powders her face using a polished mess-tin lid, and keeps her bunk screened with a repaired Velescar travel curtain. In other hubs she favors inns, delver stations, archives, and any table from which she can watch both doors.

## What She Notices

Elara responds positively to:

- brave action that does not spend another person's life for spectacle;
- clever entry, climbing, exploration, and discovery;
- returning named property or dividing salvage honestly;
- protecting servants, laborers, prisoners, and people overlooked by rank;
- confronting powerful people without humiliating the powerless around them;
- admitting culpability while still attempting practical repair;
- wagers whose stakes belong to the people making them.

She responds negatively to:

- looting active graves or treating human remains as crafting material;
- stealing food, keepsakes, or tools from poor households;
- class humiliation used as entertainment;
- cowardice renamed prudence after someone else is left in danger;
- blind deference to title, Church office, or wealth;
- forcing her confession into public view merely to control her;
- taking an exciting risk with an uninformed bystander as the price.

She can approve a theft from a corrupt official, a rescue through trespass, or salvage from an abandoned civic ruin. She judges ownership and harm rather than treating legality or illegality as the whole moral answer.

## Field Familiarity Gates

### Quest One: Names Beneath Lime

Required before the offer:

- `elara_ruin_observed`: discover or meaningfully explore one named ruin or dungeon with Elara active;
- `elara_property_choice_observed`: witness a decision about salvage, burial, debt, rank, or rightful possession;
- `elara_shared_danger_observed`: complete a dangerous rescue, fight, or traversal sequence together;
- approval is **Open**, or a named Strained conflict has been repaired.

At the next hub she asks the player to accompany her to Customs Hall after recognizing a handling mark on an impound notice.

### Quest Two: A House Fed by Bones

Required before the offer:

- [[Names Beneath Lime]] completed;
- `elara_rescue_priority_observed`: the player has visibly chosen people over a simpler material reward at least once;
- `elara_delving_choice_observed`: the duo has entered another named underground or ruined space;
- `elara_companion_intervention_observed`: Elara has used an authored field intervention or post-quest observation;
- Open Autumn or its transformed Early Winter window.

Ashfield Lodge's message reaches the next camp or hub rather than appearing while the player is inside another operation.

### Quest Three: The Patron's Cabinet

Required before the offer:

- [[A House Fed by Bones]] completed;
- `elara_winter_city_observed`: Elara has witnessed Grenzburg's refugee, siege, or emergency-privilege pressure;
- `elara_authority_choice_observed`: she has witnessed one meaningful ducal, Bank, Church, Folk, or underworld authority decision;
- approval is **Open** or **Trusted**;
- Deep Winter or its transformed Early Spring window.

The quest itself is never romance-gated. Relationship commitment is considered only after its reckoning.

### Quest Four: No Lady of Velescar

Required before the offer:

- [[The Patron's Cabinet]] completed;
- `elara_reclamation_observed`: Elara has witnessed a spring recovery, return, burial, or Muster consequence;
- no unresolved departure confrontation;
- Early Spring, Open Spring, or the transformed Muster Crisis window.

No romance or bond is required. A player who remains an ordinary trusted friend can complete Elara's strongest resolution.

## Conflict and Departure

A major conflict begins if the player knowingly helps Vardek destroy claims, repeatedly desecrates graves after Elara's warning, or uses her testimony to coerce her into surrendering property to the player.

Elara names the act at the next hub and asks for a concrete repair. Repair may require returning property, protecting a witness, repudiating Vardek's bargain, or accepting a material loss. Refusal marks the relationship Strained. Reaffirming the wrong during [[No Lady of Velescar]] produces the broken/departed outcome.

## Attraction and Bond Rhythm

Elara is experienced, comfortable with flirtation, and not frightened by desire. Her difficulty is allowing herself to be known when charm, powder, rank, and daring no longer control the scene.

Two pre-commitment scenes are required:

1. **The Wager:** after quest one, the player may accept, redirect, or decline a playful wager about the next impossible entrance. Romance interest uses mutual attraction; bond interest treats the wager as the beginning of deliberate trust.
2. **Without the Powder:** after quest two, Elara speaks while repairing equipment and cleaning away the day's powder. The scene is about culpability and fear of ordinariness, not a revelation that her public femininity was false.

## Commitment After Quest Three

Commitment requires:

- approval **Trusted**;
- both earlier relationship scenes completed without a closed refusal;
- no active major conflict;
- the global romance or sworn-bond slot remains available.

A male player may enter romance. Elara proposes an equal relationship with no patron, rescued lady, or inherited claim between them. Mature intimacy fades to black.

A female player may enter a sworn bond. They make a witnessed promise of mutual aid, truthful spoils, and return from danger without turning the oath into ownership.

Declining either path preserves friendship and quest access.

## Romance Reward

### Equal Footing

After [[No Lady of Velescar]], a resolved or compromised committed romance grants **Equal Footing**, a permanent non-slot campaign perk.

When the player exploits a weak point or stagger opening created by any companion, or a companion exploits one created by the player, the first alternating follow-up extends the opening and restores a bounded amount of Stamina. It triggers once per opening and does not create a weak point by itself.

### Crossed Measure

Commitment after quest three unlocks this Elara-only duo move. Against a properly staggered elite or large target, Elara pins, levers, or marks the vulnerable point and opens a vocation-specific player finisher. It cannot bypass required preparation or instantly kill a healthy boss.

## Sworn-Bond Reward

### Witness at My Back

After [[No Lady of Velescar]], a resolved or compromised sworn bond grants **Witness at My Back**, a permanent non-slot campaign perk.

The first flank or off-screen attack in an encounter receives an authored warning and wider defensive response window. A successful guard, dodge, or counter exposes the attacker and restores a bounded amount of Stamina. The perk never guarantees immunity.

### Back-to-Back

Commitment after quest three unlocks this Elara-only duo move. Elara can intercept one contextual attack while the player climbs, revives, operates a mechanism, or is surrounded, allowing an immediate defensive response. Cooldown and encounter limits remain provisional.

## Persistence

Relationship perks occupy neither vocation augment slots nor equipment slots. The player may possess one romance perk and one sworn-bond perk in a campaign.

Once earned, the permanent perk remains learned if Elara later dies through a chosen sacrifice or leaves. Her duo move and field interventions become unavailable without her. Relationship perks do not become account-wide New Game unlocks.
