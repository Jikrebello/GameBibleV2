# Faewild and Hell Wave Migration Manifest

## Scope

This wave established exactly thirty Faewild and Menhir forms and twenty-four Hell and Abyss forms. It also locked Menhir agency, mixed Hag continuity, final damnation, heterogeneous devil bearers, indestructible human souls, rare individual fallen angels, and the first two sealed true dragons.

New active folders were populated for Faewild mythic fauna and High Hunts, plus five numbered Hell provenance sections. `01 Canon Reference/Geography/10 Hidden Sites` holds the author-only remains of Kelarion and Orthea.

## Faewild Coverage

- Seven drake forms remain active and unchanged as the established court-fauna ladder.
- Nine court-being forms now include small court folk, Gremlins, Revel Hosts, and the two distinct Hag continuities.
- Four place forms distinguish natural place minds from Oathwood formation.
- Seven mythic-fauna forms cover storm, depth, griffon, noble-beast, basilisk, hydra, and linnorm roles.
- Three High-Hunt forms cover Hounds, Riders, and Way-Weavers.

Total: **30**.

## Hell Coverage

- Five damned forms retain real human souls and final judgement.
- Six native Depth animals use biological continuity and do not form a moral hierarchy.
- Four devil mantles use imposed-office continuity with heterogeneous bearers.
- Three soul predators can injure and intercept without annihilating souls.
- Six Abyssal wound forms include the sealed Shoreless King and Unwritten Worm.

Total: **24**.

## Canon Corrections

- Menhir are created natural and mythic laws, not moral gods.
- Sapient court-beings remain responsible for chosen cruelty.
- Worked iron is a situational boundary stabilizer rather than a universal fey poison.
- Deep Envoys are local ecologies and communities, not a worldwide Deep Ones civilization.
- Morvalis governs bodily decline and ecological return; the Boatman alone holds the soul-road commission.
- Skinbound Hags retain human souls and require broken bargains plus restitution for restoration.
- Damnation is final after judgement, while human souls remain beyond creaturely annihilation.
- Devil mantles do not overwrite their bearers. The Covenant's 4,444 are heterogeneous under the Creditor's author-only architecture.
- Fallen angels remain rare named created spirits rather than a bestiary ladder.
- The Shoreless King and Unwritten Worm are historically sealed and create no automatic present-day outcome.

## Adventure Reconciliation

- Ghor now operates through Hollow-King blood-stones and the Heart-Shard rather than demon possession.
- Grobi and Troll bodies carry no routable souls.
- Zafir's captive "angel" is a Way-Weaver misrepresented by a corrupt canon court.
- Malphas is an Ash Advocate mantle tied to the Silent Partners and Creditor architecture, not a Kuthite species or infernal legion.
- Existing Grenzburg acts, choices, and canonical continuation remain otherwise unchanged.
