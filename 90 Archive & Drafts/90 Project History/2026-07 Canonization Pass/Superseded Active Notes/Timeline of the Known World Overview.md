## **I. [[The Atherian Empire – Age of Glory]]**

> _The world is united. The stars rule the heavens, and the emperors rule the earth._

- Atherian Empire dominates the continent (like Rome-at-peak).
- Star Pantheon is the dominant religion.
- Grand roads, cities, aqueducts, and temples built.
- The eastern frontier (modern Eclessia) is seen as rustic and rural, with growing cultural mingling with Fäskogarfolk and minor spiritual sects.

---

## **II. [[The Age of the Redeemer]]**

> _A carpenter’s son from a Tower Town begins to whisper of mercy, freedom, and flame._

- **Isfir the Redeemer** is born among the Jirahar in a Tower Town (Eastern Empire).
- Ministry confronts both Jirahar legalism and Atherian imperial theology.
- Performs miracles, casts out demons, gathers a band of followers.
- Executed in Caleran (modern Thalmyria) by a coalition of Atherian officials and Jirahar priests.
- Resurrection → Ascension → Leaves behind the **Holy Flame**.
- Persecutions follow, but faith spreads underground.

---

## **III. [[The Conversion of the Empire]]**

> _Martyrs become saints. The stars go dark. The empire is baptized in fire and faith._

- A long period of imperial persecution. Countless saints and martyrs.
- Eventually, the faith of the Redeemer overtakes the old star cults.
- **Star Pantheon outlawed**.
- The Atherians begin an internal renaissance: architecture, theology, identity.
- Pogroms against the Jirahar escalate; their Tower Towns are razed.
- Survivors flee northeast into the deserts of what will become **Ahl-e-Ahd** territory.

---

## **IV. The Age of Decadence**

> _Without war, without need, without purpose—the empire begins to rot._

- Empire turns inward. No expansion, little pressure.
- A succession of weak emperors.
- Barbarian raids from the **southern Enaidfae**.
- Coastal raids from the **Ättar**.
- The Concord City-States in Sestara grow richer and more independent.
- Rumors of a unifying prophet among the desert tribes begin to spread.

---

## **V. The Invasion of the Ahl-e-Ahd**

> _From the deserts came men of fire and law, of demons bound and oaths sealed in blood._

- Ahl-e-Ahd launch a sudden, overwhelming **Unsealing** from the northwest.
- Massive losses; empire unprepared for golems, bound devils, and Janissaries.
- First Crusade (Defensive) is called—initially slows the advance.
- The **Emirate of Zanthir** is formed and establishes the **Velian States** as puppet regions across occupied eastern provinces.
- A fragile peace is negotiated.

---

## **VI. Fall of Atherian Empire**

> _One last empire shatters under its own weight and foreign steel._

- Second Crusade is called to retake the Velian States—**a disaster**.
- Ahl-e-Ahd seize the moment, marching all the way to **Caleran**, capital of Atheria.
- Caleran falls, occupied and desecrated.
- Refugees flee to the **volcanic mountains** in the south (modern Thalmyria).
- Third Crusade stops an attempted Ahl-e-Ahd push into Eclessia at the **Duchy of Caldria**.

---

## **VII. Rebellion in the Mountains 

> _In the ash and stone, a flame is kindled again._

- Atherian rebel general from a minor house leads a **guerrilla defense** in the mountains.
- Legendary **Valley Ambush** cripples Ahl-e-Ahd elite forces.
- Victory at **Virelos** cements the rebels' success.
- Rebel commander crowned as the **First Thalmyric Emperor**.
- **Thalmyria is born.**

---

## **VIII. The Fourth Crusade – The Reconquest**

> _Let the Holy Flame burn anew._

- Church-sanctioned crusade led by Thalmyria, now with **weaponized Holy Flame**.
- Siege of **Caleran** is won—city retaken, invaders and collaborators executed.
- Other rebel cells across the continent rise up, some succeeding, others crushed.
- Eclessia fights its own battles in the west but remains fractured.

---

## **IX. The Present Day**

> _The empire is dead, but its ghosts still march._

- Borders between **Eclessia**, **Thalmyria**, **Sestara**, and **Ahl-e-Ahd** stabilize.
- **Eclessia**: Religious, fragmented, yearning for unity.
- **Thalmyria**: Militarized, mystical, proud heirs of the Atherian legacy.
- **Sestara**: Rich and independent, mercantile and artistic, a renaissance zone.
- **Ahl-e-Ahd**: Still powerful, but cracks are forming from within.
- **Fäskogarfolk** remain wild and mostly unconquered, yet deeply entangled.
