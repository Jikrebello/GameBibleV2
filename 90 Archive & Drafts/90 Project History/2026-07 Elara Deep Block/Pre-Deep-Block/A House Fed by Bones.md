# A House Fed by Bones

## Role in the Arc

This is Elara Velescar's quest 2 of four. It is playable in Open Autumn and Early Winter and advances the arc without requiring Elara Velescar in the active field party outside explicitly companion-owned scenes.

## Hook and Immediate Stake

Hunters catch a Velescar-marked delving crew preparing to open one of the Seven Mounds using Elara's old notes.

Stop a second grave economy before winter seals the barrows and determine who copied Elara's field book.

## Player Actions

Track the crew from Ashfield Lodge, cross trapped spoil trenches, rescue coerced diggers, and recover correspondence linking the operation to Vardek's Grenzburg agent.

## Opposition and Pressure

Paid delvers defend their claim, while frightened laborers believe abandoning the job will transfer its fines to their families. Natural residue and a damaged burial ward complicate the site without becoming imprisoned souls.

## Information Paths

The foreman carries copied diagrams; a surviving worker knows who recruited them; Elara recognizes corrections only Vardek's cabinet possessed.

Required understanding never rests on a single social check. Cultural, background, Insight, exploration, and companion observations alter clarity and cost rather than deciding whether the plot continues.

## Decision or Mastery Point

Preserve the mound and accept lost evidence, stage a controlled recovery under burial witnesses, or let Elara seize the correspondence while the player protects the workers. Looting the mound drives her toward compromise.

## Fail-Forward Results

If the foreman flees, his payment trail remains in Old Market. If the ward breaks, the party must contain residue and the mound becomes scarred rather than losing the quest.

Failed combat, stealth, or persuasion changes injuries, evidence, access, public trust, or the later encounter. It does not erase the companion line.

At the spring transition, unresolved danger produces a named casualty, loss, or policy change and the reckoning quest inherits it.

Establishes whether Elara can place living workers and burial duty ahead of the proof she wants.

Completion grants a companion technique or field synergy, qualitative approval movement, and a concrete late-game or summer state. Exact numerical tuning remains provisional.
