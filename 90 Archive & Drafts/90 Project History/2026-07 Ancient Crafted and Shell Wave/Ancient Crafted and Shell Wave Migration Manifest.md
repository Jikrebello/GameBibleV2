## Canon Corrections

- The Shell is one membrane with four joined quadrants and no elemental peoples.
- Pure Shell and constructed patterns never acquire interior consciousness or moral agency.
- Apparent machine cognition is recorded as observable cunning, not personhood.
- Human soulbinding is always an evil imprisonment, including voluntary binding.
- Biological fleshworks and captured Depth animals can suffer even without human souls.
- Only uninterrupted Nythalum Continuants and continuously embodied Ansharan Vampire Giants are genuine surviving Giant individuals.
- Qianglong, Ishkatar, Xolotl, ordinary Nythalum backups, and Ansharan machines reconstruct performance rather than preserve their makers.
- Shell extraction is morally neutral technology whose labor, concealment, negligence, and use determine culpability.

## Strategic Infrastructure

Fourteen permanent anchors are active in 1360 AR:

- Vorthara: four.
- Ironvale: three.
- Qahirid Covenant: four.
- Thalmyria: three.

Public culture and military notes acknowledge strategic outputs. [[Shell Anchors and Extraction]] retains exact access geometry at author-only visibility.

The Qahirids retain three operational great engines, two dormant frames, and one slow replacement foundry. Depth-Core, Slave-Soul, and Surrender technologies now have separate continuities and defeat conditions.

## Adventure Reconciliation

- The Examiner and Curator are non-conscious adaptive systems; living human captives remain genuine rescue subjects while copied memories are records.
- Dustfeld's emissary is a genuine Nythalum Continuant with full responsibility.
- Fallen Spire's command system is a Reconstruction; its human expedition leader carries present moral culpability.
- Reborn Arsenal follows self-maintenance directives installed by a human Black Forger rather than developing survival instinct.
- Cinder-Bound patterns can be released or decommissioned without treating them as enslaved persons.
- Fane, Echo Seam, Tower, Step-Tomb, Nythalum, Ansharan, and Ishkatar conversions now route to their completed bestiary families.
