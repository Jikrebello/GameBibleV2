# Bernard Long Watch Greatsword Package

Bernard uses the authored [[Paladin]] vocation through a companion-specific **Long Watch Greatsword** package. It preserves the Paladin's heavy weapon, interposition, protection, and White-Flame coherence without creating a ninth vocation or changing the player's native equipment rules.

## Field Role

Bernard is a close-range protector and recovery-window maker. He is strongest at a doorway, narrow road, patient line, or endangered ally where committed positioning matters more than pursuit.

His combat rhythm is:

1. identify the person or route that must hold;
2. enter the attack line before it closes;
3. brace, deflect, or stagger the decisive threat;
4. create time for movement, treatment, or recovery;
5. use modest kindling to steady rather than dominate;
6. remain with the endangered instead of chasing a fleeing target.

He is not a shield tank, artillery caster, full healer, or guaranteed answer to corruption. His greatsword attacks are slower than a Fighter's one-handed forms, and his support depends upon proximity and moral coherence.

## Equipment

| Element | Function |
|---|---|
| **issued or freely gifted greatsword** | broad committed blows, line denial, bracing, and anti-armour pressure |
| **right-leading plate** | protects the side most exposed during entry and interposition |
| **left scale and padding** | preserves two-handed reach, prayer-rope use, carrying, and treatment movement |
| **wooden prayer rope** | focuses breath, memory, and supportive prayer without compelling the White Flame |
| **field treatment roll** | stabilizing supplies, bandage work, splints, and ordinary medicine |

Bernard may loosen the fingers of his left hand around one bead while the sword remains braced. He never fires the rope like a wand, binds enemies with it, or treats it as expendable equipment.

At recruitment the sword is inherited company steel. During [[A Blade Freely Given]] Bernard returns it to the common armoury and may accept a lawfully acquired sword from the player as his first personally owned weapon. The new sword gains no hidden relic property and remains unnamed until an eligible resolved [[The Open Hospice|Open Hospice]] outcome, when Bernard calls it **Open Hand**.

## Core Actions

### Long-Watch Guard

Bernard enters a threatened ally's line and braces the greatsword across the attack. A successful guard reduces displacement and gives the protected character a wider response window. He cannot intercept attacks he cannot physically reach or perceive.

### Road-Clearing Stroke

A low, controlled sweep breaks light formations, legs, shields, or narrow-road pressure. It favors stagger and space over raw execution. Bernard avoids using it through civilians or surrendered enemies.

### Shelter at My Back

Bernard plants his stance and names a short protected side behind him. Allies moving, treating, reviving, or operating a mechanism there receive reduced interruption pressure. The shelter moves only when Bernard deliberately resets his position.

### Mercy Between Blows

Bernard uses an opening to stabilize recoverable injury, stop bleeding, or clear a modest bodily affliction. Ordinary medicine supplies the material work. White Flame may steady the patient but does not regrow tissue or replace treatment.

### Burden Shared

Bernard takes immediate pressure meant for an ally: a physical strike, fear surge, corruption exposure, or brief affliction transfer where metaphysically valid. The burden remains real and may down him. Human guilt, moral responsibility, and permanent curses cannot simply be transferred.

### Last Ember

The capstone creates a bounded sanctuary interval through greatsword guard, truthful prayer, and coherent protection. It steadies allies, disrupts active corruption or residue command, and preserves one route of withdrawal or rescue. It is not invulnerability, resurrection, artillery, or proof that Bernard's judgement is correct.

## Modest Kindling Before Embers Without Excuse

From recruitment, Bernard may:

- stabilize a downed ally enough to permit rescue;
- clear ordinary fear through voice and presence;
- resist a minor corruption pulse;
- create a brief protective warmth during authored exposure scenes.

These effects remain restrained. [[Embers Without Excuse]] does not make the Lord begin caring about Bernard; it establishes that his mature field form can join truth, restitution, and protection without self-excuse.

## Companion AI

- Prioritize downed allies, endangered civilians, casters threatening a rescue route, and enemies about to break the line.
- Do not chase a wounded enemy beyond the protected space unless commanded.
- Reserve Mercy Between Blows for meaningful injury rather than consuming supplies after every scratch.
- Use Burden Shared only when the transferred consequence remains survivable or prevents a worse immediate loss.
- Treat surrendered opponents as protected unless they resume active threat.
- Do not use visible kindling as a dialogue claim or faction badge.

## Custom-Vocation Transfer

[[Little Hands, Long Road]] records the Long Watch rescue augment and compatible greatsword guard parts for [[Custom Vocation Workshop]]. Completing [[The Open Hospice]] personally with Bernard resolved or compromised adds the **Long Watch Field Form** blueprint.

The blueprint uses a great weapon, heavy armour, interposition, treatment support, and White-Flame parts. Sacred parts retain coherence requirements. The prayer rope remains Bernard's personal devotional object and does not become an account-wide item skin or compulsory player focus.

## Presentation Limits

- The Holy Fire appears white-gold and restorative, never as orange Shell artillery.
- Prayer animations remain short enough to preserve action-combat control.
- Bernard does not shout sermons through every encounter.
- His armour remains visibly pieced together after moral resolution; virtue does not reward him with immaculate plate.
- His initial greatsword is useful inherited steel; the later gifted weapon is honest craft rather than a secretly chosen relic.
- Last Ember prayers before major danger remain narrative and grant no generic pre-fight buff.
