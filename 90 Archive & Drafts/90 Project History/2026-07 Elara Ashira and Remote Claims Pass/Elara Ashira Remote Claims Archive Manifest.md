# Elara, Ashira, and Remote Claims Pass — Archive Manifest

This collection preserves the state immediately before the first paired emotional campaign rewrite.

- **Pre-Pass Character Bibles:** all eight companion bibles before the remote-only claims doctrine.

## Retired Campaign Cast

Vasko Dren, Daria Keln, Kata Belyn, Danara Veln, and Keeper Othara bath Qesin were useful development devices in earlier campaign structures. They no longer travel to Grenzburg or control an active companion resolution. Their prior functions remain readable here rather than being deleted.

Marek Vardek and the important remote anchors for other companions remain active world-canon people. [[Companion Debt and Remote Claims Standard]] controls their remote-only presence.
