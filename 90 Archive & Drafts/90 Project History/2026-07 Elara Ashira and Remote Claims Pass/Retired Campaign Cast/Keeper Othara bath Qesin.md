# Keeper Othara bath Qesin

## Core Concept

The keeper who preserved Qesheth Tower from collective punishment by placing full civil liability on Ashira bath Melan.

## Narrative Function

Othara opposes both human-imprint terror and the destruction of innocent tower households. She brings the original judgement, demands exact ontology, and can sponsor a nonhuman Medium discipline.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Keeper Othara bath Qesin has interests independent of [[Ashira bath Melan]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
