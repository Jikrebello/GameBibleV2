# Kata Belyn

## Core Concept

Kata Belyn is a thirty-one-year-old Virelian shoring hand and former Velescar household laborer whose apparent paid work on Elara's grave expeditions was secured through family fines she could not safely refuse.

She is a claimant, witness, and capable temporary expedition ally. She is not present to forgive Elara on behalf of every worker.

## History

Kata learned lime work, timber shoring, drainage, and winter foundation repair on the Velescar estate. Vasko recruited her by advancing payment against estate fines attached to her mother's household. Leaving an expedition would have transferred the balance to her family.

She survived four of the six digs. After discovery, Vardek's evidence described her as an independent thief. She accepted a labor sentence and later followed seasonal work toward Grenzburg, where Vasko's agents again found leverage through the unsettled balance.

## Character

Kata is practical, unsentimental, and unwilling to perform gratitude. She values tools that work, people who return when a tunnel moves, and promises whose cost is stated before acceptance. She can laugh with Elara and still refuse reconciliation.

Her anger has specific objects:

- Elara called the crew paid while knowing why they could not leave;
- Vasko made pressure look like ordinary hiring;
- Vardek converted every participant below him into disposable proof;
- courts treated title as direction when assigning blame but as innocence when tracing profit.

## Quest Use

- [[Names Beneath Lime]] identifies Kata in the surviving manifest and establishes that her household fines were transferred.
- [[A House Fed by Bones]] places her among the coerced Seven Mounds crew. Once freed, she can guide shoring and rescue outside the companion limit.
- [[The Patron's Cabinet]] allows her testimony to authenticate labor schedules without making it the only evidence path.
- [[Sixty-Three Claims]] lets a surviving and willing Kata organize three claimant delegates, state their different priorities, and produce `elara_kata_delegation_ready`. She does not participate if injured, missing, or unwilling; other delegates preserve the quest.
- [[No Lady of Velescar]] gives her one of several household petitions. She may seek land, cash, or freedom to leave depending prior survival and trust.

## Agency and Outcomes

Kata may survive, be injured, disappear under renewed pressure, or die through visible quest failure. Her loss never deletes the whole evidence chain. If she survives, she chooses whether to work with Elara again; the player cannot purchase forgiveness through the estate settlement.

In the resolved outcome, Kata may become a paid expedition shoring master, take a Velescar parcel, or accept a cash share and leave. Her choice is independent of Elara's preferred image of repair.
