# Danara Veln

## Core Concept

Danara Veln is a Virelian burial clerk who traced one of Elara's recovered Grey Sweat heirlooms to a missing emergency inventory. Her work exposed the grave operation but failed to reach Vardek's ownership of its profits.

## Background

Danara spent two decades reconciling parish death rolls, estate records, emergency plague inventories, and family petitions. She is neither an inquisitor nor a treasure expert. Her authority comes from patient records and the families who continued asking where named property went.

Vardek discredited her first report as a confessional attack on sabre-right burial custom. When evidence became undeniable, he supplied Elara and the workers as a complete criminal chain while preserving his cutouts.

## Character

Danara is formal, stubborn, and careful not to promise what a record cannot prove. She believes burial property matters because names, kinship, and testimony matter, not because every object must remain underground forever.

She does not hate Elara. She refuses Elara's early attempts to turn Danara into the severe official whose cruelty would make the crime understandable.

## Grenzburg Presence

Danara joins Vardek's winter relief delegation as its burial and household-record clerk. Vardek includes her because excluding the woman who exposed the crime would look worse than controlling her access.

She can:

- authenticate objects and certified copies;
- identify missing names in the cabinet;
- explain which records preserve relief warrants after seizure;
- authenticate the casket sequence uncovered in [[Eight Honest Warrants]], producing `elara_danara_authentication` when her copy remains independent of Vardek;
- match grave inventories against the petitions protected in [[Sixty-Three Claims]];
- testify at the Hall of Petitions;
- accompany a noncombat investigation segment outside the squad limit.

She cannot authorize ducal seizure, decide the fate of House Velescar, or replace the claims of workers and families.

## Outcomes

Danara may be protected, silenced, injured, or removed from Grenzburg by Vardek. Every necessary fact has another source. If she survives, she oversees returned inventories or delegates that work to Paupers' Cloister without becoming Elara's personal absolver.
