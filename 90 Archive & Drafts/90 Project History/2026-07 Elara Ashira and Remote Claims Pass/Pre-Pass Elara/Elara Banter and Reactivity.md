# Elara Banter and Reactivity

This note calibrates Elara's voice and reactive life. It does not supply final scripts for every quest.

## Voice Key

- Uses names to confer attention or quietly apply pressure.
- Turns shared work into an occasion.
- Praises in public and criticizes through proximity, phrasing, and changed formality.
- Makes wagers when direct vulnerability feels too exposed.
- Becomes ceremonially polite under shame.
- Starts arranging people and exits under fear.
- Rarely asks plainly for inclusion.

## Calibration Scenes

### Joy — After the Bridgehold race

Elara removes one boot and tips out a pebble.

**Elara:** “Do not look at the clock.”

The player looks.

**Elara:** “Faithless. By how much?”

If she lost badly, she demands the pebble be entered as a material witness.

### Competence — A sealed delver door

Elara runs one fingertip along the lintel, then moves the temporary guide two steps left.

**Elara:** “Stand where the builder expected a servant. Yes. Less flattering, much safer.”

She gives the player the visible mechanism and keeps the collapsing brace for herself without turning the act into a speech.

### Embarrassment — The yard succeeds without her

The player returns to find a debtor supper already arranged.

**Elara:** “You used the blue cups.”

**Brina:** “They hold soup.”

Elara looks around the full tables, then starts carrying bowls.

**Elara:** “A controversial defence. I shall observe the results.”

### Anger — A porter is omitted

An official praises the delvers and ignores the porter who held the fire door.

**Elara:** “You have forgotten Jonas Feld.”

The official tries to continue.

**Elara:** “No. Begin the sentence again.”

Her anger is frightening because she knows exactly how much public discomfort the room will tolerate.

### Care — A frightened new debtor

Elara begins a grand introduction, sees the debtor recoil, and changes course.

**Elara:** “This is Neda. She has had enough of being looked at. The stew is there; the quiet bench is mine, but I am lending it.”

She keeps the rest of the room occupied until Neda chooses whether to join.

### Shame — A familiar survey cut

Elara recognizes her own mark on a disturbed burial wall.

**Elara:** “That is Velescar work.”

She starts to say “we.”

**Elara:** “I marked it. Someone else swung the pick because I paid them to.”

No companion supplies the lesson. The next action determines whether the admission becomes repair.

### Apology — Credit returned

Elara has a public notice corrected before approaching the person omitted.

**Elara:** “Your name is on the board. Mine is no longer before it.”

**Worker:** “Is that the apology?”

**Elara:** “No. That was the correction. I am sorry I made you ask for either.”

She does not request reassurance.

### Quiet — Camp above Southwood

Elara polishes the delver's pick long after it is clean.

**Elara:** “There is a view half a mile east.”

The player does not move.

After a while, she sets the pick down.

**Elara:** “Tomorrow, then.”

## Foil Arc — Ashira

### Autumn

Elara repeatedly introduces Ashira by expertise. Ashira asks whether she may someday enter a room without being announced as its interesting danger.

Elara is offended, then notices she has never asked what Ashira wants people to call her.

### Winter

Ashira identifies a person Elara's morale plan has quietly excluded. Elara secures that person's place publicly without naming Ashira as the source.

Ashira later leaves a sweet beside Elara's map and refuses to explain.

### Spring

Elara asks Ashira to review a public settlement before it is announced. Ashira declines to become her private conscience and instead sits beside the petitioners while Elara hears them.

Their mature friendship permits criticism without either woman becoming audience, ornament, or corrective device.

## Other Companion Objects

- **Ilyana:** parties, clothes, audiences, and the disagreement between hosting art and owning its room.
- **Ylva:** Elara's praise initially feels like display; they bond over teaching shy people how to enter hostile spaces by different means.
- **Bernard:** he punctures her social perfection and she takes his hidden competence seriously.
- **Dismas:** she knows how institutions may be pressured from within; he knows when her belonging has become capture.
- **Tavio:** fellow public captains who must learn how to lose status in private.
- **Zafir:** she translates difficult systems socially; he refuses to let charm replace exact terms.

## Faction Reactivity

- Publicly supports useful ducal leadership and opposes permanent emergency prestige.
- Treats Brina as the person who makes the Yard a real company; distrusts Koss's cultivated dependence.
- Respects burial care and Caestus's pastoral authority; opposes secular immunity through holiness.
- Defers to living Folk custodians only after learning that refusal is not failed hospitality.
- Enjoys Low Lantern social craft and rejects Black Bell execution.

## Temperament Use

Use [[Companion Archetype Chemistry Matrix]] for primary dynamics.

- A grey player receives Elara's complete social-equal path.
- Secondary temperament may change one joke, invitation, or objection.
- Do not make Cheerleader players automatically popular with her.
- Do not make Rebel players automatically more honest than her.

## Failure Modes

Reject any Elara line that:

- could be transferred to Ilyana by replacing “house” with “company”;
- makes her constantly confess class guilt;
- treats femininity as camouflage for competence;
- resolves conflict through one perfect statement about equality;
- makes every interaction a lesson in provenance.
