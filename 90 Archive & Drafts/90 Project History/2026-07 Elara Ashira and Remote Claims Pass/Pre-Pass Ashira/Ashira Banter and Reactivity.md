# Ashira Banter and Reactivity

This note calibrates Ashira's voice and response pattern without pretending every severe person speaks in funeral quotations.

## Voice Key

- Notices exact physical behavior before naming emotion.
- Uses pauses as part of the answer.
- Asks private questions once; repeated pressure is a boundary violation.
- Offers dark humor without advertising cleverness.
- Becomes more intimate, not louder, when angry.
- Overexplains categories when ashamed.
- Allows silence to remain silence.

## Calibration Scenes

### Joy — The puppet death

A market puppet loses its head three beats before the execution scene.

Ashira laughs once, sharply, and covers it with a cough.

**Player:** “Was that funny?”

**Ashira:** “No. It was structurally perfect. The scratching began after they sealed it.”

She buys the broken puppet after the show.

### Competence — A supposed haunting

Ashira watches the window latch repeat its movement.

**Ashira:** “Not a soul. Not residue. The warped frame records the wind and returns it after the gust.”

She opens the wall and finds the hidden pressure channel.

**Ashira:** “The carpenter may still be guilty of theatre.”

### Embarrassment — The sweet

The player leaves black cherries beside her rod case.

**Ashira:** “Who told you?”

**Player:** “You did.”

**Ashira:** “I would remember that error.”

She takes the cherries only after the player looks away.

### Anger — A confidence repeated

Ashira waits until the gossiping clerk is alone.

**Ashira:** “She told you because you were small enough to fit beside the bed.”

The clerk begins to defend the public interest.

**Ashira:** “Do not enlarge yourself with what she said while dying.”

### Care — Identification

A woman cannot look at the recovered body.

Ashira covers the damaged hand and leaves only a repaired bracelet visible.

**Ashira:** “You may identify the bracelet. Nobody is owed more courage than that.”

She remains by the door, not beside the witness.

### Shame — The retained face

Ashira names every technical reason the imprint is not a soul.

The player asks who consented to its likeness.

She closes the case.

**Ashira:** “No one.”

The answer ends the explanation rather than beginning another.

### Apology — A secret used

Ashira returns a copied note and burns her transcription in front of its owner.

**Ashira:** “I was accurate. I was not entitled.”

She waits.

**Owner:** “You may go.”

**Ashira:** “Yes.”

She goes.

### Quiet — Rain at the threshold

Ashira and the player watch rain fill the yard ruts.

After several minutes:

**Ashira:** “You are not required to improve this.”

Nothing else is said.

## Foil Arc — Elara

### Autumn

Elara presents Ashira as fascinating expertise. Ashira punctures the display and then discovers that Elara's introduction stopped a frightened official from excluding her.

Neither apologizes correctly.

### Winter

Ashira identifies somebody missing from Elara's public circle. Elara corrects the exclusion without publishing Ashira's confidence.

Ashira's gratitude appears as a practical warning about Elara's route, not praise.

### Spring

Ashira refuses to become Elara's private moral examiner. She sits with the petitioners and forces Elara to hear unsorted testimony.

Later, Elara saves Ashira a place without announcing why it matters.

## Other Companion Objects

- **Ilyana:** mourning song, spectacle, beauty, and whether pain becomes material.
- **Ylva:** the person who acts and the person who witnesses; each must respect the other's boundary.
- **Bernard:** he makes darkness breathable; she knows when the joke is a locked door.
- **Dismas:** shared distrust of respectability, endangered by contempt becoming their only bond.
- **Tavio:** public warmth and private shadow without rescue or exoticization.
- **Zafir:** hidden-knowledge partnership that must leave the library and touch actual people.

## Faction Reactivity

- Accepts bounded ducal evidence and opposes emergency secrecy becoming ordinary convenience.
- Uses Blackjack protection while rejecting Koss's attempt to turn useful terror into doctrine.
- Respects bedside faith and burial truth; opposes pious panic and institutional concealment.
- Learns that Folk place-law may be disciplined custody rather than mystical vagueness.
- Values Low Lantern refuge while distrusting gossip, aestheticized crime, and Black Bell judgement.

## Temperament Use

- Grey writing treats the player as a person who neither performs fascination nor recoils.
- Goth players do not receive automatic intimacy; similarity can produce gatekeeping and co-rumination.
- Goofball humor stops immediately when Ashira states a boundary.
- Secondary temperament may alter one question or silence, never her consent.

## Failure Modes

Reject any Ashira line that:

- sounds like generic morbid poetry;
- treats depression or cruelty as sophistication;
- explains Medium ontology in every scene;
- makes her secretly soft in a way that invalidates severity;
- uses another person's confidence only to prove she sees deeply.
