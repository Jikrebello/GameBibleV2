# Menhir and Ghoul Geography Migration Manifest

## Scope

This wave reconciled every major Menhir with the [[The Great Rebellion and the Yolk Exiles|Great Rebellion]], established the post-fusion [[The Faewild Schism|Faewild Schism]], and placed the three great ghoul cities within a discontinuous Material underworld and Gluttony-shaped Feast-shadows.

The locked bestiary coverage did not change. Faewild remains exactly **30 forms**, Shadowlands remains exactly **42 forms**, and the active registry remains exactly **212 genuine forms**.

## Menhir Ruling

- Every major Menhir is the fused sovereignty of an angelic office-holder, its cohort, and a Faewild domain.
- Joining the Rebellion and choosing yolk-fusion were culpable acts committed before fusion.
- Completed fusion retained intelligence, intention, memory, preference, and choice while removing comprehension of good and evil as binding categories.
- Menhir law establishes domain consequence, never moral justice.
- Idols remain distinct because they retained moral comprehension and knowingly designed evil feeding economies.
- Courtiers are judged by their own continuity and agency. Court association does not erase culpability.
- Direct mortal dealings with a sovereign remain rare, domain-bounded, and non-repeatable.

## Schism and Circles

The historical Trium attempted to place land, sea, and sky beneath one integrated sovereignty. The other yolk powers shattered that permanent union during the Faewild Schism. Sylvorn, Thalassor, and Zephyraxis survived separately; modern Trium is a rare temporary phase-lock.

Second, Third, and Fourth Circle are Atherian names for recurring ecological blocs. They are neither governments, ranks, alliances, nor literal divine kinship. The active [[The Mythology of The Menhir]] preserves the family story as canonical Atherian source material with disputed reliability.

## Ghoul Geography

The physical underworld is discontinuous. Mines, caverns, tombs, buried settlements, and temporary corpse-roads do not form a worldwide subterranean realm. Jirahar Underways, Hell, the Vestibule, the Abyss, and the Shadowlands remain separate categories.

Each great ghoul city has a Material body and a corresponding Feast-shadow:

| Creator title | Public regional name | Function |
|---|---|---|
| [[Necropolis of Nihil]] | Hollow City; City After the Last Name | Stolen names, grave archives, and pre-Exodus ruins |
| [[Warrens of Woe]] | Fever Deeps | Fever cultivation, surface hunters, and corpse-route logistics |
| [[Dusk Barrows]] | Under-Cairns | Frozen burial shafts, relic retrieval, and elite grave-runners |

The cities are rival predatory societies rather than one ghoul nation. The Devourer shapes their Feast-shadows but does not govern them as a resident monarch. Destroying one layer permits eventual reconstruction from the other.

## Outward Reconciliation

- [[The Wane-Warren]] is an independent Fever Deeps splinter, and the Maw-Road is a damaged western feeder rather than a global highway.
- [[The Long Table]] may trade with individual ghoul lineages but has no stable necropolis treaty or surface franchise.
- [[The Bright-Fire Carnival — Full Adventure]] now distinguishes genuine human guilt, amoral Menhir law, culpable court choices, and the Rider's chosen predation.
- [[Mother Moon's Bargain]] no longer treats exact enforcement as proof of moral legitimacy.
- Qianglong's Seasonal alignment is patronage and political imitation, not divine descent or moral mandate.
- Public Qahuri, Jirahar, Brauvalian, and Folk references preserve contradictory warnings without revealing exact entrances.
