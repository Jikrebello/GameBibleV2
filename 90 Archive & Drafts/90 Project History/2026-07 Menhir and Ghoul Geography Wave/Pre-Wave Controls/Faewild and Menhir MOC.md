# Faewild and Menhir MOC

The Faewild is creation under concentrated natural and mythic law. Its beings are not automatically benevolent, corrupt, or divine. Menhir express real created laws; their courts are ecologies and observed affiliations rather than universal governments.

## Governing Rules

- **Natural law is not moral truth.** Hunger, winter, tide, metamorphosis, and pursuit can be lawful and still become terrible for human beings.
- **Agency creates responsibility.** A sapient court-being remains accountable for chosen cruelty even when appetite or compulsion explains the desire.
- **Court is an observational word.** Folk courts may include courtiers, place minds, animals, residue, patrons, or hidden counterfeits. They are not states or reliable theological categories.
- **Iron is situational.** Worked iron stabilizes some Material boundaries and disrupts some manifestations. It is not a universal poison or instant answer.
- **Continuity controls defeat.** Court-law must be satisfied, displaced, exposed, or broken. Biological court-fauna die as living creatures, though the law around their territory may remain.
- **No hidden nations.** Sapient families form households, local communities, retinues, and solitary powers without adding a second geopolitical map.
