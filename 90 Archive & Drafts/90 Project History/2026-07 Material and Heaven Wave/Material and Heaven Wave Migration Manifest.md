## Material and Residue Additions

Populated sections:

- `10 Predators and Megafauna`
- `20 Residue Dead`
- `30 Vermin and Swarms`
- `40 Oozes and Decay`
- `50 Plants and Fungi`

The controlling catalogue in [[The Mundane Layer — Residue, Vermin, and the Delver Trade]] records all 34 forms and the exact family count. Alchemical oozes, Shell seepage, black mud, Menhir plants, and engineered fungi remain deferred to their native origin waves.

## Heaven and Soul-Road Additions

Populated sections:

- `10 True Angels`
- `20 Manifested Saints`
- `30 Soul-Road`

The sixteen profiles consist of eight true-angel services, five manifested-saint services, and three soul-road services. [[Heaven and Soul-Road MOC]] is the section hub.

## Canon Corrections Applied

- Added `created-spirit` to the continuity register.
- Distinguished true angels from redeemed human saints.
- Established the Boatman as a unique created angel and psychopomp, not a judge or neutral god.
- Recast route guides, separators, hunters, and wardens as impersonal sacred infrastructure.
- Removed Thanatos from authorial Menhir genealogy while preserving Atherian misclassification as in-world doctrine.
- Established four residue grades and separated every ordinary haunting from the routed deceased person.
- Added `catalogued_forms` to the creature schema and registry coverage view.
- Linked the wave to the requested adventure conversions without selecting outcomes.

## Deferred Work

- Fallen-angel ecologies belong to the Astral/Hell wave.
- Menhir plants, noble beasts, and court ecologies belong to the Faewild wave.
- Shell, alchemical, Astral, black-mud, and engineered decay forms remain in later origin waves.
- System-specific statistics remain outside the world bible.
