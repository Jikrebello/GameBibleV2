# Rune Fist

## Player Fantasy

The Rune Fist is a close-range counter-caster who catches physical and elemental force through disciplined guards, steps, and inscribed gauntlets, then returns it as a short bounded script. It fights in the dangerous distance between a weapon blow and a completed spell.

The vocation rewards timing, enemy knowledge, nerve, and controlled release. It is not an unarmed brawler with decorative magic and not a spellcaster who happens to stand nearby.

## In the World

The discipline descends from Vortharan martial warlock schools that trained bodyguards, duel assessors, laboratory wardens, and counter-saboteurs. Grenzburg's version is a practical adaptation shared between the [[Hammer Ward#Anchor Locations|Warlock Hall]] and [[Hammer Ward#Anchor Locations|Great Forge]]. Marcher smiths have made it heavier, plainer, and better suited to armour and winter gloves.

`Rune Fist` is the gameplay umbrella. Individual schools use their own names, inscriptions, postures, and proof rituals. The discipline still obeys [[Warlock Rune Magic]]; it does not introduce chi, inner divinity, or a fifth elemental source.

## Native Kit

| Element | Rune Fist rule |
|---|---|
| Weapons | paired inscribed gauntlets |
| Armour | medium |
| Primary statistics | Stamina, Stagger Power, Physical Defence, Magick Defence |
| Signature interaction | catch force, hold a short relation, return it, vent Burn |
| Movement advantage | counterstep, recoil vault, grounded landing, close pursuit |

The gauntlets are conductor, shield, and script surface. They reinforce hands and forearms but do not make fists immune to blades, crushing force, or failed scripts.

## Counter-Script Grammar

Rune Fist does not steal a complete spell. A successful guard or evasion samples one usable relation from the incoming force: heat, chill, charge, pressure, momentum, erosion, or mineral impact. The gauntlets hold that relation briefly and qualify it into a fixed return script.

| Return family | Captured relation | Typical return |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | heat and expansion | burning impact, short cone, denial ring |
| Rime | cold and adhesion | anchoring guard, slowing strike, brittle break |
| Storm | charge and pressure | redirecting step, interruption, chained contact |
| Stone and bitter earth | momentum, mineral force, erosion | armour break, ground shock, stabilizing brace |

Physical attacks can charge momentum returns. Elemental attacks offer their own family when caught safely. An attack that exceeds the gauntlets' proof or the player's timing breaks through instead of becoming free power.

## Arcane Burn

Rune Fist shares Warlock Arcane Burn because both conduct Shell instructions. Its cadence differs:

- catching force creates little or moderate Burn;
- holding several relations without release increases Burn rapidly;
- completed impact returns vent part of the captured relation;
- grounded strikes, disciplined guard sequences, and safe discharge vent Burn;
- a failed release can drive Burn into the arms, nerves, armour, and floor.

The vocation lives near working Burn and repeatedly clears it through contact. It cannot cast indefinitely by punching the ground.

## Core Combat Loop

1. Enter the range where an enemy must commit.
2. Guard, evade, or meet the attack with the correct inscription face.
3. Capture one bounded relation.
4. Choose immediate return, stored combination, or defensive vent.
5. Break stance or elemental preparation with the return.
6. Close with disciplined impacts before Burn or enemy distance resets the exchange.

## Working Skill Families

These functions are original working concepts; names remain subject to ordinary presentation review.

| Family | Intended function |
|---|---|
| Inscribed Guard | timed catch against physical or elemental force |
| Counterstep | short evasive shift that samples a passing relation |
| Grounding Form | defensive posture that vents Burn and resists displacement |
| Impact Return | basic captured-force release through a strike |
| Ember Reversal | converts held heat into a close burning answer |
| Rime Brace | turns cold into anchoring armour and a slowing counter |
| Storm-Thread Step | redirects charge through a fast line change |
| Bitter Palm | corrosive or mineral return that attacks armour and wards |
| Breaker's Ring | radial stagger generated by a grounded impact |
| Backlash Ward | spends a held relation to interrupt an incoming script |
| Recoil Vault | uses a safe release for height or lateral escape |
| Fourfold Reversal | master sequence that cycles several prepared returns without pretending they are one unrestricted spell |

## Mastery Shape

- **Opening:** Inscribed Guard, Counterstep, basic impacts, one family return, and emergency grounding.
- **Practiced:** all four return families, recoil movement, deliberate Burn venting, and physical-force capture.
- **Veteran:** stronger counter windows, stored relations, armour breaking, counterscripts, and large-creature anchoring.
- **Master:** Fourfold Reversal, safe multi-relation sequencing, and a school proof taught jointly by a master and gauntlet-maker.

## Augment Themes

Perfect-catch timing, guard Stamina, Burn venting, Staying Power, Stagger Power, elemental resistance, counter damage, grounded movement, and gauntlet durability.

## Training and Reputation

Training requires both script literacy and bodily proof. The Warlock Hall controls safe inscription and counterscript theory; the Great Forge controls gauntlet fitting, conductor repair, and impact testing. Neither half alone produces a responsible practitioner.

Vortharan visitors may regard Grenzburg's form as crude but recognize its ancestry. Ordinary citizens tend to find it less frightening than wand artillery until a practitioner cracks a street or vents lightning through a crowd.

## Lore and Moral Risks

- Catching an effect does not transfer ownership of another person's will or spell knowledge.
- Counter-scripts hold elemental relations, never souls, identities, or divine grace.
- Nerve shock, burns, mineralization, shattered fingers, gauntlet fracture, and dangerous grounding are credible failures.
- White Flame cannot be captured as an elemental charge.
- Patron, Idol, Menhir, and contract workings cannot be freely returned merely because they produce visible force.

## Custom Vocation Parts

Mastery contributes inscribed gauntlets, medium armour, counter timing, captured-relation parts, impact venting, movement counters, and the Rune Fist growth profile to [[Custom Vocation Workshop]]. Using Rune Fist and Warlock parts together does not create two independent Burn meters.
