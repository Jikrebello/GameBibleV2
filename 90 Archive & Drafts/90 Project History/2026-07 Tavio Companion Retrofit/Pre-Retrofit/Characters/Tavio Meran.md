# Tavio Meran

## Companion Contract

A Vortharan Crucible champion who knowingly used his coach's illegal conductor insert, maimed a trusted sparring partner, and accepted a title while his school buried the evidence. Tavio Meran is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. He can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

Tavio's coach supplied an insert that stored more Shell force than arena rules allowed. Tavio understood what it did and used it. Lio Varcen expected a fair match and was permanently injured. The school paid liability, called Tavio's tuition surety, and sold it to Medori while preserving its own reputation.

The coach offered the illegal advantage; Tavio knowingly made it decisive and accepted glory afterward. Institutional corruption explains his opportunity, not his hand.

## Person Before Problem

A competitive showman who loves fair challenges, loud crowds, and teaching someone the trick that finally beats him. He can yield gracefully in games and poorly in life. His instinct is to confess just enough to preserve the next bout.

## Combat and World Use

A Rune Fist in medium armor, using inscribed gauntlets to catch force through guards and evasions, return short elemental scripts, and vent Arcane Burn through disciplined impacts.

Tavio Meran contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. He is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens in **Early Autumn**. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** He exposes the Crucible school, accepts a permanent arena ban, settles a fair reckoning with Lio, and becomes a leak-warden protecting Grenzburg's foundries.

**Compromised:** He takes sole public blame to protect the school and receives a private teaching license. The rigged culture survives.

**Broken or departed:** He wins another fraudulent contest or silences Lio. After warning and repair he may depart to reclaim fame through an unregulated circuit.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Tavio opens through fair competition, disclosed rules, skillful risks, and honoring yield. He strains at rigged contests, contempt for craft, or declaring any victory legitimate because it was difficult.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **rivals who can yield**. A female player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **Fair Challenge**, turning one partner's perfect answer into an immediate empowered reply for the other. Sworn bond grants **Fair Return**, sharing captured force with the oath-partner who did not make the counter.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Svala Last-Beacon]] challenges Tavio Meran's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
