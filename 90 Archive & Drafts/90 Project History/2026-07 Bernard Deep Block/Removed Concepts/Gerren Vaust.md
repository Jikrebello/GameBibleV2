# Gerren Vaust

## Core Concept

An ordinary murderer who uses pilgrim routes, hospitality, and changing names to find isolated victims. Bernard's stolen keepsake once let him escape identification.

## Narrative Function

Vaust has no supernatural protection and no redemptive grievance. His danger comes from patience, social camouflage, and knowing that ashamed institutions prefer quiet mistakes.

This character enters Grenzburg through a credible convoy, delegation, court record, protected passage, or refugee route. Their presence does not add an off-map playable zone.

## Agency and Limits

Gerren Vaust has interests independent of [[Bernard Valeon]]. The player can protect, persuade, expose, defeat, or lose them as the relevant quest allows, but cannot convert them into proof that the companion is innocent.
