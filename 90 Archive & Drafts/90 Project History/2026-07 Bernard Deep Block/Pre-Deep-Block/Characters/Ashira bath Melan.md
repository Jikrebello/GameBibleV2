# Ashira bath Melan

## Companion Contract

A Qesheth Tower scholar who used recognizable human combat imprints to terrorize the families of caravan killers and accepted a crushing civil liability to prevent punishment of her whole tower. Ashira bath Melan is an outsider debtor and one of Grenzburg's eight full companions. She can join the field party of the player plus three companions and remains optional to critical-path completion.

## Debt and Culpability

After raiders massacred a Qesheth caravan, Ashira captured non-conscious combat patterns from the killers' traces and made those faces hunt their home camp. She targeted families who had not committed the massacre. Keeper Othara secured a narrow judgement against collective punishment by placing personal liability on Ashira; Medori administers it. Ashira secretly retained the named human roster.

The raiders chose murder; their families did not. Simulating familiar faces to terrorize them was chosen collective punishment even though no human soul was captured.

## Person Before Problem

Precise, skeptical, and dryly funny. She enjoys hard questions, neat instruments, and people who can change their mind without pretending they never held the first view. She is generous with teaching and ruthless with euphemism, except when discussing the terror she called useful.

## Combat and World Use

A Medium using a rod, shadow fields, marks, and a limited roster of non-conscious imprints. Her resolved arc eliminates recognizable human imprints and develops creature, construct, and environmental patterns instead.

Ashira bath Melan contributes alternate methods, observations, and late interventions. She is never a mandatory key for traversal, investigation, or the main ending.

## Recruitment

Recruitment opens in **Early Autumn**. A declined invitation remains repairable until the end of Open Autumn. Recruitment establishes the debt and immediate field role without unloading the entire foreign history in one conversation.

## Outcome Range

**Resolved:** She destroys the human-derived roster, documents the harm without metaphysical falsehood, and remains in Grenzburg as a scholar of nonhuman field patterns.

**Compromised:** She seals the human roster under joint custody for emergency research and promises not to manifest it. The dangerous archive survives.

**Broken or departed:** She weaponizes the roster during the Muster or hides copies. After a warned confrontation she may leave rather than surrender what she still calls necessary.

Death is possible only through an explicit, late, telegraphed sacrifice after the player is shown a viable alternative. It is never an unseen punishment for low approval.

## Approval and Confrontation

Ashira opens through intellectual honesty, measured curiosity, protection of noncombatants, and rejecting superstition without contempt. She strains at collective punishment, calling imprints souls, or destroying evidence merely because it is uncomfortable.

Approval is shown as **Guarded**, **Open**, **Trusted**, or **Strained**, plus named unresolved conflicts. Permanent departure requires a warned confrontation and one clear repair opportunity.

## Relationship

The relationship theme is **intellectual equals**. A male player may pursue romance; a same-sex player may pursue a sworn bond. Commitment becomes available only after quest three, is explicit, and does not follow automatically from approval. Romance grants **Unmasked Pattern**, revealing a true behavior cue and shared weak point on enemies studied by both partners. Sworn bond grants **Clear Pattern**, preventing ordinary constructs or imprints from deceiving either oath-partner about personhood and exposing their command cue.

Romance and sworn bond have distinct scenes and promises but equivalent power. A playthrough may contain one committed romance and one committed sworn bond.

## Principal Foil

[[Bernard Valeon]] challenges Ashira bath Melan's central habit without becoming a constant antagonist. Their disagreements change after personal quests and include warmth, shared competence, and occasions when each is plainly right.
