## **1. The Mercenary Lords of Korrheim**

### **Overview**
Korrheim’s warriors are renowned throughout Eclessia, with **mercenary captains, warlords, and battle-hardened leaders** shaping both its history and its economy. These mercenary companies are not just soldiers for hire—they are **political power players**, capable of **deciding wars, toppling rulers, or defending city-states from invasion**.

### **Famous Mercenary Leaders and Their Legacies**
#### **Sir Brandt Falkenwald – The Immovable Bastion**

- A **legendary fortress commander** and former Grand Captain of the **Blackmount Guard**.
- Known for **holding the fortress of Dornkessel for three years** against an overwhelming enemy force.
- Created **the Falkenwald Doctrine**, a defensive military strategy still used in Korrheim today.

#### **Klara “The Stormbearer” Ostvald – Mistress of the Winterfang Riders**
- A **ruthless cavalry commander** specializing in **hit-and-run ambush tactics in mountain terrain**.
- Allegedly **tamed a frost-bred warhorse from the high peaks**, earning the title “Stormbearer.”
- Led a famous **raid against an invading force**, cutting down an army **five times her size** before vanishing into the snow.

#### **Ulrich Dornhelm – The Iron Wolf**
- Founder of **the infamous Dornhelm Free Company**, a mercenary band known for **unwavering discipline and brutal battlefield tactics**.
- Once hired to **defend a city-state**, then switched sides when offered **a higher bid**, cementing his legacy as a **mercenary kingmaker**.
- His betrayal sparked **The Siege of Falkendorf**, a brutal conflict that shaped mercenary laws in Korrheim.

#### **Father Gregor the War-Priest**
- A former warrior-monk who abandoned the cloister to **lead a holy army of mercenaries**.
- Combined **faith and warfare**, making his troops **zealots who refused to retreat**.
- His death at the **Battle of Saint’s Crossing** is now **commemorated in Korrheimer war-prayers**.

## **2. Internal Feuds and Power Struggles**

### **Ongoing Conflicts Within Korrheim**
Despite their unity in war, Korrheim’s city-states and warrior-cantons are **rife with internal rivalries and power struggles**. The decentralized nature of Korrheim’s government often leads to feuds between **mercenary captains, noble lineages, and guild leaders**.

### **Notable Feuds and Their Causes**

#### **The Siege Engineers’ Uprising (30 years ago)**
- The **Guild of Siege Engineers** revolted after **the Council of Heldenheim refused to fund new fortifications**.
- Led to the **brief occupation of Grimhart**, where engineers seized control of the city’s foundries and demanded reforms.
- The rebellion ended when **military leaders negotiated better wages and research funding** for siege innovations.

#### **The Ostwald Blood Feud**
- A **century-long rivalry** between the **Winterfang Riders and the Ironclad Brotherhood**, two of Korrheim’s most powerful mercenary orders.
- Originated from **a betrayal in a foreign war**, where one side allegedly **abandoned their allies for better pay**.
- To this day, **mercenaries from these groups refuse to fight on the same battlefield**.

#### **The Falkendorf Rebellion (20 years ago)**
- A failed coup by **mercenary warlords** who attempted to **seize control of the Council of Heldenheim**.
- Sparked by **a dispute over mercenary contract regulations**, where independent companies opposed new restrictions.
- Resulted in the **massacre of rebellious leaders**, solidifying Council authority over Korrheim’s contracts.

## **3. The Legendary Fortresses of Korrheim**

### **Overview**
Korrheim is **filled with impenetrable fortresses**, constructed over centuries to protect against invasion. These fortresses are **built into mountain passes, nestled in high valleys, or hidden within cavern networks**, making them **incredibly difficult to besiege**.

### **Most Notorious Fortresses**

#### **Heldenheim – The Bastion of the Confederation**
- The **largest and most politically powerful fortress-city**, home to **the Council of Heldenheim**.
- Built into a **cliffside overlooking a major trade route**, with **a nearly unbreachable gate system**.
- Famous for repelling **multiple invasions**, earning its nickname: **“The Unyielding Gate.”**

#### **Dornkessel – The Grave of Sieges**
- A fortress built **within a massive mountain ravine**, protected by **natural cliffs and layered defenses**.
- Has **never been successfully taken**, despite at least **ten major siege attempts**.
- Known for its **hidden tunnels and collapse-triggering rockslides**, making assaults **a death trap for invaders**.

#### **Sturmgard Keep – The Skyward Stronghold**
- Perched on **a sheer mountain peak**, accessible only by **a single, winding road**.
- Houses **Korrheim’s most elite warriors**, who live in **near isolation, training endlessly for war**.
- Used as a **final refuge in times of great crisis**, and rumored to have **ancient secrets hidden within its vaults**.

## **4. Korrheim’s Role in Past Wars**

### **The Battle of Sturmgard Pass (110 years ago)**
- A Korrheimer army **held off a massive invasion force** using **rockslides, ambush tactics, and fortifications**.
- Ended in a **decisive victory**, cementing Korrheim’s reputation as **masters of defensive warfare**.

### **The Siege of Heldenheim (60 years ago)**
- The only recorded attempt to **conquer Korrheim’s capital**.
- Invading forces were forced to **retreat after being trapped in a counter-siege**, where Korrheim’s forces **cut off their supply lines**.

### **The War of the Coin (30 years ago)**
- A war fought **entirely between mercenary companies**, as different factions fought to control **lucrative military contracts** in a foreign conflict.
- Led to the creation of **strict codes governing mercenary conduct**, preventing similar all-out wars between hired armies.

### **The Border Skirmishes of the Eastern Marches (Ongoing)**
- Korrheim continues to defend **the eastern mountain passes from incursions by pagan raiders and rogue mercenary bands**.
- Many young warriors **earn their place in the Iron Hosts** by fighting in these battles.

## **5. The Future of Korrheim**
As Korrheim stands at a crossroads, critical decisions will shape its destiny:

- **Will the mercenary lords gain even greater influence, or will the Council of Heldenheim rein them in?**
- **Can Korrheim’s warrior-brotherhoods and free cities maintain their fragile unity, or will political feuds lead to internal strife?**
- **How will the rise of new siege technologies impact Korrheim’s defensive supremacy?**

With **a history of legendary warriors, fortress cities that have never fallen, and a people who refuse to submit to any ruler**, Korrheim remains one of the most formidable forces in Eclessia. But whether it will **stand together or fracture under the weight of its own rivalries remains to be seen.**
