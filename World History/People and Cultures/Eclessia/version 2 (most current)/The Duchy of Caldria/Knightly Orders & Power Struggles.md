Caldria’s military might is built upon its **elite knightly orders**, each with distinct traditions, rivalries, and battle doctrines.

#### **The Order of the Silver Cross**
- The most **prestigious and elite** of all Caldrian knightly orders.
- Tasked with leading **Church-sanctioned holy wars** and maintaining discipline among other orders.
- Highly traditional, resisting **modernization** in favor of **time-honored cavalry tactics and chivalric values**.
- Their Grandmaster, **Sir Leopold von Hohenfeld**, is **both a warrior and a political strategist**, ensuring the Order retains its influence over the War Council.

#### **The Brotherhood of Stagfort**
- A militant monastic order known for **fanatical devotion** and brutal tactics against heretics.
- Often acts as the **right hand of the Inquisition**, rooting out corruption and dissent within the duchy.
- Noted for **shock infantry and siege warfare**, they specialize in **storming fortresses and conducting executions**.
- Viewed as extreme even within Caldria, some factions **fear their growing power and radical beliefs**.

#### **The Iron Banner Knights**
- A more **secular order**, prioritizing **discipline, military strategy, and tactical innovation** over religious fervor.
- Their rivalry with **The Order of the Silver Cross** has led to **several near civil wars**, including the infamous **War of the Broken Lance**.
- Their Grandmaster, **Dame Isolde Rensfeld**, is **one of the few female commanders in Caldrian history**, known for her **brilliant siege tactics and reformist views**.

#### **The Lance of Dornheim**
- A cavalry-focused order, specializing in **shock warfare, field maneuvers, and mobile engagements**.
- Once among the most powerful orders, their **influence has declined due to battlefield innovations** favoring mixed-arms tactics.
- They now struggle to **maintain their relevance**, leading to **internal power struggles** between traditionalists and reformers.

### **4. Legendary Warriors of Caldria**

#### **Sir Reinhardt “The Bastion” von Varendor**
- A legendary **defender and tactician**, best known for holding **The Siege of Dornheim** against overwhelming odds.
- His **tactical manuals** are still studied in Caldrian war colleges today.

#### **Dame Isolde Rensfeld – The Iron Strategist**
- Grandmaster of the **Iron Banner Knights**, she revolutionized **siege tactics** and fought to modernize Caldria’s military doctrine.
- Her reforms were met with fierce resistance from **traditionalist factions** within the knightly orders.

#### **Sir Wilhelm the Black**
- Once a hero, he turned against the Church and **led a rebellion of disillusioned knights**, sparking **The Schism Wars**.
- His name is still **cursed by many**, but some secret sects **venerate his ideals of knightly autonomy**.

### **5. Pivotal Military Campaigns**

#### **The Great Pagan War (150 years ago)**
- A massive campaign against the **Fäskogarfolk**, where Caldria established **fortified outposts deep in enemy lands**.
- Marked by **brutal winter warfare and prolonged sieges**.
- Ultimately led to the foundation of **several military-monastic outposts**, which still hold today.

#### **The Schism Wars (50 years ago)**
- When a faction within the Eclessian Church attempted to form a **separate military-religious state**, Caldria was called upon to crush the rebellion.
- A highly **controversial war**, as some knights sympathized with the rebels.
- The aftermath saw **a tightening of Church oversight over knightly orders**, causing lingering resentment.

#### **The War of the Broken Lance (25 years ago)**
- A **bitter internal conflict** between **The Order of the Silver Cross and the Iron Banner Knights**.
- Nearly resulted in a full **civil war within Caldria**.
- Ultimately settled through **a trial by combat**, reinforcing **the duke’s authority** but exposing the fractures within the duchy.
